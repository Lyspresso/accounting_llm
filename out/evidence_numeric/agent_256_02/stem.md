### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Change to LIFO when retrospective is impracticable (prospective COGS schedule)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Prospective application when retrospective is impracticable; LIFO base layer = beginning inventory; subsequent COGS schedule with mixed methods across comparative years; no cumulative RE JE; period-end Year 3 measurement under LIFO  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Retail Partners Inc.** reported inventory under **FIFO** for Year 1 and Year 2. On **January 1, Year 3**, Lakeshore changes to **LIFO**. After every reasonable effort, management **cannot reconstruct LIFO layers** for prior periods (purchase-price history for old layers is unavailable). Lakeshore can apply LIFO only from Year 3 forward. Year 3 ending inventory under LIFO is determined to be **$64,000**.

Data:

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO going forward) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $58,000 | $72,000 |
| Purchases | 200,000 | 235,000 | 260,000 |
| Ending inventory | 58,000 (FIFO) | 72,000 (FIFO) | 64,000 (LIFO) |
| Sales | 250,000 | 310,000 | 340,000 |
| Operating expenses | 42,000 | 45,000 | 47,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Is retrospective application required? Explain using ASC 250 impracticability.  
b. Prepare any **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (or state that none is made).  
c. Prepare the **three-year comparative COGS subsequent measurement schedule** under the approach Lakeshore actually uses.  
d. Compute **Year 3 net income** under the prospective LIFO approach (period-end measurement).  
e. Optional contrast: If Year 3 ending inventory under FIFO would have been **$90,000**, what would Year 3 COGS and NI have been under continued FIFO?  
f. **Presentation / disclosure:** How do comparative Year 2 vs Year 3 principles differ on the Year 3 report? What must be disclosed about impracticability?
