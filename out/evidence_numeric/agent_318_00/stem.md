### Q1 — CORE — Ashwick Merchandising: period-end COGS adjusting JE, inventory bridge, multi-year ratios (period-end adjusting JE emphasis)
**LO:** LO 9-9  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting journal entry under the periodic system that establishes ending inventory and residual COGS; initial recognition of purchases/freight; AP settlement and sales; multi-year inventory turnover, days in inventory, and gross profit % with interpretation  
**Scenario:**  
**Ashwick Merchandising Co.** is a wholesale merchandiser using a **periodic inventory system**. Fiscal year ends **December 31**. Selected balances:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory — Dec 31, Year 0 | $400,000 |
| Inventory — Dec 31, Year 1 | 440,000 |
| Net sales — Year 1 | 2,800,000 |
| Cost of goods sold — Year 1 | 1,680,000 |
| Net sales — Year 2 | 3,150,000 |

**Year 2 activity (chronological summary):**  
1. Purchased merchandise on account, **$1,950,000** (invoice gross).  
2. Paid freight-in of **$48,000** cash on Year 2 purchases (f.o.b. shipping point; inventoriable).  
3. Returned defective merchandise to suppliers; accounts payable reduced **$32,000** (purchase returns).  
4. Paid the remaining Year 2 accounts payable related to these purchases in cash.  
5. Sold merchandise on account for **$3,150,000** (periodic system: no COGS entry at sale date).  
6. Year-end physical count of ending inventory: **$465,000**.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **initial recognition** journal entries for Year 2 purchases and freight-in (periodic accounts).  
(b) Prepare the journal entries for the **purchase return** and for **settlement** of the related accounts payable.  
(c) Prepare the journal entry for **Year 2 sales**.  
(d) Compute **Year 2 cost of goods sold** using the inventory bridge (subsequent measurement schedule). Prepare the **combined period-end adjusting journal entry** that (1) removes beginning inventory, (2) closes Purchases, Freight-in, and Purchase Returns and Allowances, (3) records ending inventory, and (4) records residual COGS. Prove that Dr = Cr.  
(e) Prepare a **ratio analysis schedule** for **Year 1 and Year 2** showing: (1) average inventory, (2) inventory turnover, (3) average days in inventory, (4) gross profit, and (5) gross profit percentage. Round turnover and days to **two** decimals; round percentages to **two** decimals. (Compute turnover to two decimals first, then divide 365 by that rounded turnover for days.)  
(f) Briefly **interpret** the Year 1 → Year 2 trend (efficiency and margin).
