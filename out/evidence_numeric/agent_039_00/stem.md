### Q1 — CORE — Loan compensating balance, cash-equivalent life cycle, restricted funds, and year-end classification (initial recognition emphasis)
**LO:** LO 8-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash; Dec 31 classification schedule and presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Peak Manufacturing Co.** has a **December 31** year-end. Company policy: short-term, highly liquid investments with **original maturities of three months or less** (to Summit) that present insignificant interest-rate risk are reported as **cash equivalents**.

**Transactions during Year 1:**

1. **July 1, Year 1:** Borrowed **$300,000** from First Prairie Bank on a **one-year, 8%** note payable. The note **legally requires** a **12%** compensating balance (**$36,000**) to remain on deposit at First Prairie during the loan term. Summit deposited the full proceeds to its First Prairie checking account and immediately reclassified the legally restricted amount.

2. **November 1, Year 1:** Purchased **Atlas Power commercial paper** for **$54,000** cash. Original term to Summit: **60 days**. The paper matures on **December 31, Year 1** for **$54,810** (discount accretion / interest **$810**). Summit classifies qualifying commercial paper as a cash equivalent.

3. **December 1, Year 1:** Purchased a **U.S. Treasury bill** for **$24,900** cash when the bill had **two months** remaining until maturity (matures January 31, Year 2; face **$25,000**). Summit treats the T-bill as a cash equivalent.

4. **December 20, Year 1:** Transferred **$14,000** from general cash to a **restricted payroll fund** bank account (payroll will be paid January 5, Year 2).

5. **December 22, Year 1:** Deposited **$60,000** with a trustee for a **bond sinking fund**. Related bonds mature in **five years**.

**Additional items on hand / on books at December 31, Year 1** (after the transactions above, and after maturity of the commercial paper):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Checking—First Prairie Bank (balance **includes** the $36,000 legally restricted compensating balance; restricted amount is still in this total pending presentation split) | $71,200 |
| Checking—Heartland Credit Union (includes an **NSF check of $650** from a customer that was returned and has **not** yet been reclassified) | $28,400 |
| Savings account | $12,000 |
| Petty cash | $600 |
| Cashier’s check payable to Summit (dated Dec 29, not yet deposited) | $1,800 |
| Cash equivalents—U.S. T-bill (Dec 1 purchase, still held) | $24,900 |
| Money market fund (highly liquid; original maturity to Summit 1 month; low credit risk) | $33,000 |
| Restricted cash—Payroll fund (already segregated Dec 20) | $14,000 |
| Restricted cash—Bond sinking fund (already segregated Dec 22) | $60,000 |
| T-bill purchased **June 1, Year 1** when it had **seven months** remaining to maturity; cost still on books (now two months left) | $16,000 |
| Travel advance to employee for January sales trip | $2,200 |
| Customer check dated **January 20, Year 2** (postdated) | $1,100 |
| Overdraft at Lakeside Bank (Summit has **no** other account at Lakeside) | $3,500 |

**Assume:** The July 1 reclass of the compensating balance was **reversed for bookkeeping simplicity** into the First Prairie checking total shown above (i.e., the full $71,200 sits in Cash—First Prairie and must be split for balance-sheet presentation). Payroll fund and sinking fund remain in separate restricted-cash accounts.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **July 1** borrowing entry and the **initial recognition** reclassification of the legally restricted compensating balance.  
(b) Record the **November 1** purchase of commercial paper as a cash equivalent.  
(c) Record the **December 31** maturity/settlement of the commercial paper (cash proceeds and interest).  
(d) Record the **December 1** purchase of the cash-equivalent T-bill.  
(e) Record the **December 20** payroll-fund transfer and the **December 22** bond-sinking-fund deposit.  
(f) Prepare a **December 31 classification schedule** assigning every item above to: Cash; Cash equivalents; Restricted cash—current; Restricted cash—noncurrent; Other current asset (specify); Short-term investment; Accounts receivable; or Current liability. Show the **period-end adjusting/reclassifying** entry for the NSF check.  
(g) Report balance-sheet totals for: **Cash**; **Cash equivalents** (or Cash and cash equivalents combined); **Restricted cash—current**; **Restricted cash—noncurrent**; and the **current liability** from the overdraft.  
(h) Briefly state note-disclosure treatment if the compensating balance had **not** been legally restricted.
