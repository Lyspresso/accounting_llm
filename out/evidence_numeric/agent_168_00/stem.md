### Q1 — CORE — PPE cash/noncash schedule, note subsequent measurement, corrected SCF, noncash + cash-paid disclosures
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent-measurement equipment additions schedule; note interest accrual path; noncash disclosure; corrected investing/financing; interest and taxes paid  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Industrial Systems Corp.** is finalizing its statement of cash flows for the year ended **December 31, 20X8** (indirect method for operating activities). Staff prepared draft investing and financing sections that treated several noncash items as cash. You are asked to rebuild the analysis from the accounts and required disclosures.

**Equipment (gross) activity for 20X8:**

| | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Balance, January 1, 20X8 | 720,000 | |
| Purchases during year (total additions) | 245,000 | |
| Cost of equipment sold | | 55,000 |
| Balance, December 31, 20X8 | 910,000 | |

**Additional information:**

1. Of the **$245,000** equipment additions:  
   - **$70,000** was acquired by issuing common stock (fair value of shares issued = $70,000).  
   - **$45,000** was acquired on **October 1, 20X8** by signing a **3-year, 8% note payable** (interest payable annually each **September 30**; principal due at maturity).  
   - The **remainder** was paid in **cash**.  
2. Equipment sold during 20X8 had cost **$55,000** and accumulated depreciation of **$38,000**. Cash proceeds were **$14,000**. The **$3,000** loss is already included in net income.  
3. On **January 1, 20X8**, Harborline commenced a finance lease and recorded Right-of-Use Asset and Lease Liability of **$112,000** (no cash at commencement). During 20X8, Harborline paid **$25,000** cash on the lease, of which **$7,000** was interest and **$18,000** was principal.  
4. Other **cash** investing/financing items:  
   - Cash issuance of common stock (unrelated to equipment): **$60,000**  
   - Cash proceeds from issuance of bonds payable: **$200,000**  
   - Cash retirement of other notes payable: **$80,000**  
   - Cash dividends paid: **$42,000**  
5. **Staff draft (incorrect — noncash amounts treated as cash):**

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **initial recognition journal entries** for: (i) the equipment acquired for stock, (ii) the equipment acquired for the October 1 note, and (iii) the finance-lease commencement. Prove each entry balances.  
(b) Prepare an **equipment additions subsequent-measurement schedule** separating cash purchases from each noncash purchase component; state the **investing cash outflow** for equipment purchases. Prove the equipment T-account rolls forward.  
(c) Prepare the **December 31, 20X8 period-end adjusting JE** for interest on the October 1 equipment note; compute 20X8 interest expense on that note.  
(d) Prepare a **note subsequent-measurement schedule** for the October 1 note through the first cash interest payment date (show 20X8 accrual and the 20X9 first annual coupon components).  
(e) Prepare the **corrected** investing and financing sections of the 20X8 statement of cash flows (cash only). Show that the **sum of net investing + net financing** is unchanged by removing noncash items from the staff draft (or explain if the draft’s combined total must be adjusted for items that never should have been cash).  
(f) Prepare the **noncash investing and financing activities disclosure** (schedule form), clearly relating cash and noncash aspects of similar items.  
(g) Compute **interest paid** (net of capitalized interest) and **income taxes paid** for 20X8. Include lease interest paid and the October 1 note (cash interest on that note in 20X8). Also state the **restricted cash** disclosure requirement.  
(h) List the five general SCF reporting/disclosure requirements highlighted for LO 22-5 (overview checklist).
