### Q1 — CORE — Classify lease, initial recognition, liability schedule, full finance-lease JEs
**LO:** LO 17-1  
**Concept:** Lease identification & classification → finance-lease initial recognition and subsequent measurement  
**Scenario:** On **January 1, Year 1** (lease commencement), **Northline Logistics Corp.** enters a **4-year noncancellable** lease with **Cascade Equipment Partners** for a delivery-dock conveyor system. Facts:

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Annual lease payment | **$32,500**, due **beginning** of each year (Jan 1 Year 1–4) |
| Lessor’s implicit rate | **5%**, **known** by Northline |
| Northline’s incremental borrowing rate | 6% (not used when implicit rate is known) |
| Fair value of conveyor at commencement | **$125,000** |
| Economic life of conveyor | **4 years** |
| Residual value / guaranteed residual | **$0** / none |
| Ownership transfer at end of term | **No** — asset reverts to Cascade |
| Purchase / renewal options | **None** |
| Specialized asset / alternative use | Conveyor is **standard** equipment Cascade re-leases to others |
| Identified asset | Serial number **CLX-4419** is specified; Northline directs use and obtains substantially all economic benefits during the term |
| Northline’s year-end | December 31 |

**Required:**  
(a) Does the contract contain a **lease**? Cite the two control criteria briefly.  
(b) Apply all **five** lessee classification criteria (use 75% and 90% bright lines). Classify as **finance** or **operating**.  
(c) Compute the **lease liability** and **right-of-use (ROU) asset** at commencement (no incentives, no initial direct costs).  
(d) Prepare the complete **lease liability schedule** (payment, interest, liability change, ending liability).  
(e) Prepare Northline’s journal entries for **Year 1** (commencement, first payment, Dec 31 adjusting).  
(f) Report Dec 31, Year 1 **current** and **noncurrent** lease liability and net ROU asset.  
(g) Prepare journal entries for **Year 2** and **Year 3** (payment + Dec 31 interest and amortization).  
(h) Prepare **Year 4** settlement entries (final payment Jan 1; Dec 31 final amortization). Confirm ending ROU and liability balances are zero.
