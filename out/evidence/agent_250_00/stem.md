### Q1 — CORE — WA shares with buyback, issuance, 2-for-1 split + cumulative preferred (emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule)
**LO:** LO 20-5  
**Concept:** Weighted-average common shares schedule with mid-year buyback and issuance and retroactive 2-for-1 stock-split restatement; numerator reduction for cumulative preferred (undeclared); initial recognition JE for cash common issuance; treasury repurchase settlement JE; period-end closing of net income  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Logistics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company. Its capital structure for Year 1 is **simple** other than **nonconvertible preferred** (no options, warrants, or convertibles). No noncontrolling interest exists.

**Beginning of Year 1 (January 1):**
- **100,000** shares of **$2 par** common stock outstanding
- **5,000** shares of **8%, $20 par cumulative** preferred stock outstanding (issued in prior years; still outstanding all Year 1)

**Year 1 share activity (common):**
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| March 31 | Purchased **4,000** common shares for the **treasury** at **$15** cash per share (cost method) |
| July 1 | Issued **20,000** new shares of **$2 par** common for **$12** cash per share |
| October 1 | Effected a **2-for-1** common stock **split** (par adjusted; memorandum treatment only — no formal equity transfer JE) |

**Other Year 1 facts:**
- Net income = **$436,000**
- **No** preferred dividends were **declared** during Year 1
- **No** common cash dividends were declared
- After the October 1 split, **232,000** common shares are outstanding (116,000 pre-split × 2)

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **March 31 treasury stock repurchase** journal entry (settlement of shares from the outstanding pool). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare the **July 1 initial recognition** journal entry for the cash common stock issuance. Show Dr = Cr.  
c. State the **October 1 stock-split** accounting treatment (entry or memorandum).  
d. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule** for Year 1, applying the **retroactive restatement factor of 2.0** to all periods **before** the split (**subsequent measurement schedule — emphasis**).  
e. Prepare a **basic EPS computation schedule** (numerator, denominator, per-share). Show the preferred dividend claim.  
f. Prepare the **December 31 period-end closing entry** that transfers net income to retained earnings (use Income Summary).  
g. Present **net income** and **basic earnings per share** as they would appear on the Year 1 income statement.
