### Q1 — CORE — Convertible bonds at premium: issuance, period-end interest/amort, schedule, partial conversion

**LO:** LO 16-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of convertible debt as straight debt; subsequent SL premium amortization schedule; **period-end adjusting JE** for interest and amortization (emphasis); partial conversion into equity at book value (no G/L); presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Inc.** (calendar year-end) raises capital with **convertible bonds**. Company policy and U.S. GAAP treat the issue as **straight debt** at issuance (no separate equity for the conversion feature). Premium is amortized **straight-line**. Interest is paid **annually on December 31**.

**Facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Meridian issues **$400,000** face of **6%**, **10-year** convertible bonds for **$424,000** cash. Each **$1,000** bond is convertible into **20 shares** of Meridian **$5 par** common stock.
2. Cash interest is paid each **December 31**. Straight-line amortization of the premium is used.
3. On **December 31, Year 3**, after the year-end interest payment and amortization entry, holders of **50%** of the bonds exercise conversion. Meridian common stock is trading at **$48 per share** on the conversion date (market price is given as a distractor only).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for cash interest and premium amortization (emphasis).  
c. Prepare a **premium amortization schedule** for Years 1–3 showing beginning unamortized premium, amortization, ending unamortized premium, and **carrying amount** at each December 31.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** conversion JE for the **50%** converted (after the Year 3 period-end interest/amort entry).  
e. State the **Year 3 balance-sheet presentation** of any remaining convertible bonds and the **equity accounts** credited on conversion. Explain whether a gain or loss is recognized and whether the **$48** market price enters the conversion entry.
