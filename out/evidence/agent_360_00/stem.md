### Q1 — CORE — Period-end AJs for litigation (best estimate + recognized subsequent event) and multi-year assurance warranty
**LO:** LO 15-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs for probable/estimable litigation; recognized subsequent-event remeasurement dated year-end; settlement JE; assurance-type warranty period-end accruals and subsequent measurement liability schedule  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironwood Consumer Products** is a calendar-year company. Year 1 financial statements are **issued March 10, Year 2**. Ignore income taxes and COGS.

**Litigation**  
In **October Year 1**, a delivery-vehicle accident injured a third party. A lawsuit seeking **$350,000** was filed in **December Year 1**. Counsel concludes a loss is **probable**. At December 31, Year 1, the reasonably estimable range is **$90,000 to $280,000**, and counsel’s **better estimate** within the range is **$120,000**.

On **February 18, Year 2** (before FS issuance), the parties agree to settle for **$145,000** cash, payable **May 1, Year 2**.

**Assurance-type warranty**  
Ironwood’s products include a **two-year assurance-type warranty** against manufacturing defects (not sold separately). Estimated warranty cost = **2.0% of product sales**. Actual claims are paid in cash. Accruals are recorded as **period-end adjusting entries** each December 31.

| Year | Product sales (cash) | Actual warranty claims paid |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $3,200,000 | $18,000 |
| 2 | 3,600,000 | 52,000 |
| 3 | 4,000,000 | 61,000 |

Warranty rate remains 2.0% of that year’s sales each year.

**Required:**  
a. **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** to accrue the litigation contingency using the **better estimate** (ignore the February settlement for this part only).  
b. **(Emphasis)** Record the **recognized subsequent event** remeasurement as a **December 31, Year 1 adjusting entry** after the February 18 settlement agreement.  
c. Record the **May 1, Year 2 settlement** payment.  
d. Record Year 1 **product sales**, the **December 31, Year 1 period-end warranty accrual**, and **Year 1 claims** settlement.  
e. Prepare the complete **warranty liability subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 (beginning, accrual, claims, ending).  
f. **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting entry** for the Year 2 warranty accrual.  
g. In one sentence: if at December 31 Year 1 the litigation loss were only **reasonably possible** (estimate still $120,000), what period-end reporting would apply?
