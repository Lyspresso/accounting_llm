### Q2 — CORE number variant — Subsequent measurement schedule for EI understatement
**LO:** LO 10-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — multi-year subsequent measurement schedule for understated ending inventory; flipped correcting JEs; two-year net $0  
**Scenario:**  
**Redline Cycle Parts Co.** discovers that its **December 31, Year 1** ending inventory was **understated by $15,600** because two storage bays were omitted from the count. Purchases were correct both years. **Year 2 ending inventory is correct**. Ignore taxes.

Amounts **as recorded with the error**:

| | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $52,000 | $61,000 (understated) |
| Net purchases | 198,000 | 215,000 |
| Ending inventory | 61,000 (understated) | 70,000 (correct) |
| Net sales | 310,000 | 355,000 |
| Other expenses | 48,000 | 51,000 |

**Required:**  
(a) Complete the two-year **subsequent measurement schedule** (as recorded / correct / effect) for EI, COGS, pretax income, and cumulative pretax income.  
(b) State Inventory and RE misstatement direction/amount at Dec 31, Year 1 and Dec 31, Year 2 (books uncorrected).  
(c) Correcting entry if discovered **December 31, Year 1**.  
(d) Correcting entry if discovered **January 1, Year 2**.  
(e) Entry (if any) if discovered **January 1, Year 3**.  
(f) Is the error counterbalancing? Two-year net pretax effect?
