### Q2 — CORE number variant — Life extended, residual cut, disposal with loss
**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial PPE recognition; SL schedule; prospective change in useful life and residual; updated period-end dep; disposal with loss  
**Scenario:**  
**Coastal Glassworks Inc.** (calendar year) places a tempering furnace into service on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cash purchase cost | **$240,000** |
| Original residual value | **$24,000** |
| Original useful life | **8 years**, straight-line |
| **January 1, Year 5** change in estimate | Total useful life revised to **12 years**; residual revised to **$12,000** |
| Disposal | Sold for **$95,000** cash on **December 31, Year 6** (after Year 6 depreciation) |

**Required:**  
a. Initial recognition JE on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Original annual depreciation; depreciation schedule for Years 1–4.  
c. Carrying amount at January 1, Year 5; updated annual depreciation; Year 5 period-end JE.  
d. Schedule Years 5–12 (spot-check that residual is reached at end of Year 12).  
e. Disposal JE on December 31, Year 6.  
f. One-sentence classification: is this a change in principle, error correction, or estimate?
