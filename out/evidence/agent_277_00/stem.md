### Q1 — CORE — Premium issue, effective-interest schedule, interest JEs, period-end accrual (emphasis), maturity, presentation
**LO:** LO 16-5  
**Concept:** Bonds issued at a premium — initial recognition; effective-interest amortization schedule; **period-end adjusting JE** when year-end falls between interest dates; maturity settlement; BS/IS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar & Flint Ironworks Inc.** (calendar-year reporter) authorizes and issues **$250,000** face of **6%** bonds on **April 1, Year 1**. The bonds mature on **April 1, Year 4** (3-year term) and pay cash interest **semiannually** on **April 1** and **October 1**. The bonds are sold to yield a **market rate of 4%**. Cedar & Flint uses the **effective interest method** and rounds present values and schedule amounts to the nearest dollar (final period plugs residual unamortized premium). Because interest dates are April 1 and October 1, **December 31 year-end requires an adjusting accrual** for interest and premium amortization since the last payment date.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **issue price** and prepare the **initial recognition JE** on April 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** for all six semiannual periods.  
c. Prepare the **October 1, Year 1** interest payment and premium amortization JE.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** accruing interest and premium amortization for the three months October 1–December 31. Base the accrual on the next full semiannual schedule row (prorate **3/6**), or equivalently: Interest expense = carrying amount after Oct 1 × 2% × 3/6; Interest payable = face × 6% × 3/12; Premium amort. = Interest payable − Interest expense.  
e. Show the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet excerpt** (bonds payable, net, and related interest payable) and **Year 1 interest expense**.  
f. Prepare the **April 1, Year 4** entries to (1) record the final interest payment and premium amortization and (2) **derecognize** the bonds at maturity (assume no Interest Payable balance immediately before the final interest entry).
