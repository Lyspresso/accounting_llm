### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — DDB schedule (residual floor) + SYD Year 1 + UOP Year 1 + disposal
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Double-declining-balance subsequent measurement schedule with residual-value floor (final-year plug); SYD and units-of-production Year 1 expense; period-end dep JE; disposal after three years under DDB  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverton Machine Co.** buys production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$50,000 cash**. Residual value **$5,000**; useful life **5 years**; estimated total productive output **10,000 units**.

Actual production Year 1: **1,800 units**.

Riverton wants to understand all four LO 12-1 methods, then elects **double-declining-balance (DDB)** for books.

On **December 31, Year 3**, after recording Year 3 DDB depreciation, Riverton **sells** the equipment for **$12,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute Year 1 depreciation under **straight-line**, **sum-of-the-years’-digits**, **double-declining-balance**, and **units-of-production**. Record the **Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** assuming DDB is used.  
b. Prepare the full **DDB subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–5. Show the residual-value floor / plug in the final year.  
c. Record the **initial recognition JE** and the **December 31, Year 3 disposal settlement JE** under DDB (after Year 3 depreciation). State gain or loss.  
d. In one sentence, explain why DDB does **not** subtract residual value when applying the rate, yet still ends at residual.
