### Q2 — CORE number variant — Liability SARs twin with expense reversal year
**LO:** LO 20-10  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: four-year liability SARs schedule including a year with **negative** compensation expense; settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**SummitRidge Logistics Inc.** grants **8,000 SARs** on **January 1, Year 1** to its COO. Each SAR pays cash equal to the excess of the market price at exercise over the **grant-date market price of \$15**. The SARs vest after **four years** (first exercise expected **December 31, Year 4**); requisite service period = **4 years**. The **employee** may require **cash** settlement → **liability** classification.

Year-end fair values per SAR:

| Date | Fair value per SAR |
|---|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | $2.00 |
| December 31, Year 2 | $5.00 |
| December 31, Year 3 | $3.00 |
| December 31, Year 4 | $6.00 |

On December 31, Year 4, the employee exercises all SARs when the stock price is **\$21**. Cash paid = (\$21 − \$15) × 8,000 = **\$48,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **four-year subsequent measurement schedule** (aggregate FV, % accrued, cumulative liability, annual expense).  
b. Prepare **all period-end adjusting JEs** for Years 1–4 (including the Year 3 **reversal**).  
c. Prepare the **settlement JE** at exercise.  
d. Prove that total compensation expense over the service period equals cash paid.
