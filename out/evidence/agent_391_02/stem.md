### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end adjusting and settlement reconstructed entries only
**LO:** LO 22-6  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting worksheet JEs (WC, depreciation, bond premium amortization); disposal of equipment; initial recognition of bonds at a discount; early bond retirement (settlement) — classify each lower-half amount O/I/F  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverside Caliper Co.** is assembling the **cash flow worksheet** for the year ended December 31, 2025 (indirect method). For each item below, prepare the **reconstructed worksheet entry** (or entries) that would appear on the worksheet. Label lower-half lines with the **section** (Operating, Investing, or Financing). Do **not** prepare a full formal SCF.

**Items:**
1. **Period-end WC:** Accounts receivable decreased **$12,000**; allowance for doubtful accounts increased **$2,000**.  
2. **Period-end WC:** Inventory increased **$6,500**.  
3. **Period-end adjusting:** Depreciation expense **$28,000** (equipment).  
4. **Disposal:** Equipment sold — original cost **$90,000**, accumulated depreciation **$55,000**, cash proceeds **$30,000**.  
5. **Initial recognition:** Issued **$80,000** face bonds payable for cash at **95** (discount **$4,000**).  
6. **Subsequent measurement / period-end:** Amortization of bond discount for the year **$800**.  
7. **Settlement:** Retired remaining bonds early — face **$80,000**, unamortized discount at retirement **$2,400**, cash paid **$79,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the reconstructed worksheet entry for each item (1)–(7). Show Dr = Cr checks.  
b. For items (1)–(7), list each amount that hits the **lower half** and classify it as **Operating add**, **Operating deduct**, **Investing inflow/outflow**, or **Financing inflow/outflow**.  
c. Compute the **net effect on operating cash flows** (indirect) from items (1)–(4) and (6)–(7) only (exclude the pure financing initial recognition in item 5’s cash proceeds from this subtotal’s “operating” count; include only the O section pieces).
