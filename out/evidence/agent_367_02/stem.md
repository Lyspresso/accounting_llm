### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Disposal of discontinued component + permanent & temporary differences; period-end tax JE
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Disposal (settlement) journal entry for a discontinued component; intraperiod tax allocation when continuing operations includes a permanent difference and an originating taxable temporary difference; period-end tax adjusting JE and income statement  
**Scenario:**  
**QuarryBend Foods Co.** provides the following information for the year ended December 31, Year 3:

- Revenues $900,000; cost of goods sold $400,000; operating expenses $150,000.  
- Operating expenses include **regulatory fines of $3,000** that are **never tax-deductible** (permanent difference).  
- One originating timing difference produces a **future taxable amount of $36,000** during Year 3 (book income currently higher than taxable income by $36,000 for this item).  
- On **October 1, Year 3**, QuarryBend **sold** a component that meets the discontinued-operations criteria. Cash proceeds were **$156,000**. The component’s net assets (carrying amount) removed at disposal totaled **$180,000**. The **$24,000 pretax loss on disposal** is **fully tax-deductible** in Year 3 and is **not** included in the revenue/expense amounts above. No other disc. ops results.  
- Enacted tax rate for all years: **25%**. No beginning deferred tax balances. No valuation allowance issues.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **disposal journal entry** on October 1, Year 3 for the sale of the discontinued component (ignore tax in this entry).  
b. Compute **pretax income from continuing operations**.  
c. Compute **taxable income** and **income taxes payable**.  
d. Compute the **deferred tax liability** originating in Year 3 and the **intraperiod split** of tax between continuing operations and discontinued operations.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting journal entry** to record income taxes (intraperiod allocation). Prove Dr = Cr.  
f. Prepare an **income statement** (through net income) using intraperiod tax allocation.  
g. Briefly explain how tax related to the **disposal loss** is presented.
