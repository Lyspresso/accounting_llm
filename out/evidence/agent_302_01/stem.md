### Q2 — CORE number variant — Gross method twin with 3/15 terms and different year-straddle amounts

**LO:** LO 8-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: gross-method initial recognition; early/late settlement; **period-end adjusting JE** for expected discounts; AR/allowance schedule; post-YE settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperfinch Merchants Co.** (calendar year-end) uses the **gross method**. Credit terms are **3/15, n/45**. Beginning AR for these customers is **$0**. Year-end estimates use **Allowance for Sales Discounts**.

| Date | Customer | Event |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 12, Year 1 | Arden Supply | Credit sale **$90,000**; cost **$54,000** |
| Dec 20, Year 1 | Arden Supply | Pays **40%** of its gross invoice (**within** the 15-day discount period) |
| Dec 30, Year 1 | Bristol Outlet | Credit sale **$60,000**; cost **$36,000** (discount window through **Jan 14, Year 2**) |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | — | YE: expect **Bristol** takes the **3%** discount on **100%** of its open balance. **Arden’s** remaining balance is **past** the discount window (ended Dec 27) — **no** discount expected. |
| Jan 10, Year 2 | Bristol Outlet | Pays **in full** and takes the cash discount |
| Jan 25, Year 2 | Arden Supply | Pays remaining balance after the discount period |

**Required:**  
a. Dec 12 and Dec 30 sale and COGS entries.  
b. Dec 20 collection entry.  
c. Subsequent measurement schedule of gross AR by customer, YE allowance, and AR net at Dec 31.  
d. **December 31 period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis).  
e. Jan 10 and Jan 25 settlement entries.  
f. Year 1 net sales excerpt and Dec 31 net AR for these customers.
