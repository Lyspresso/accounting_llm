### Q4 — CORE — HTM → AFS transfer and subsequent AFS period-end adjustment
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** HTM → AFS transfer at fair value through OCI (no NI); subsequent AFS FVA period-end JE  
**Scenario:**  
Lakeshore Mutual Fund holds the following **HTM** portfolio at **December 31, Year 3**. All bonds were purchased at **par**.

| Security | Amortized cost | Fair value (Dec 31, Y3) | Unrealized gain (loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Quill Corp. bonds | $90,000 | $96,300 | $6,300 |
| Dune Partners bonds | 60,000 | 55,800 | (4,200) |
| **Total** | **$150,000** | **$152,100** | **$2,100** |

On **January 1, Year 4**, because of a significant deterioration in the issuers’ credit standings, Lakeshore changes intent and **transfers both securities from HTM to AFS**. Fair values on the transfer date equal the December 31, Year 3 amounts above.

At **December 31, Year 4**, fair values are: Quill **$94,000**; Dune **$57,000**. Lakeshore adjusts AFS securities to fair value at year-end through OCI.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 4** transfer entries (reclassify HTM → AFS and recognize transfer-date FV through OCI).  
b. State the transfer-date effect on NI and OCI.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 4** AFS fair-value adjusting entry.  
d. Briefly state what footnote disclosure should accompany a transfer of this type.
