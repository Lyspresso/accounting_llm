### Q1 — CORE — Convertible bonds: period-end interest accrual AJ (emphasis), interest schedule, if-converted EPS, partial-year, conversion settlement
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE for convertible-bond interest accrual (emphasis); initial recognition at par; subsequent-measurement interest schedule; basic and diluted EPS under the if-converted method (full year and mid-year issuance); conversion settlement JE after paying accrued interest; income-statement EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Robotics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company. Its only potentially dilutive instrument is convertible debt (complex capital structure). Common stock has a **$2 par** value. No preferred stock and no options/warrants.

**Facts for 20X5 (full-year bonds):**
1. On **January 1, 20X5**, Harborline issued **1,200** convertible bonds, each with **$1,000** face (**$1,200,000** total face), **7%** stated annual interest, for **cash at par**. Each bond is convertible into **25** shares of common stock. Maturity is December 31, 20Y4. Interest is paid **annually every January 1** for the **preceding calendar year** (so 20X5 interest is paid January 1, 20X6).
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding during 20X5 (before if-converted assumptions): **350,000**.
3. Net income for 20X5: **$980,000** (already after interest expense on the convertible bonds and after income taxes).
4. Enacted tax rate: **25%**.

**Alternate fact (part g only):** Same bonds are issued on **October 1, 20X5** (not January 1). Interest for the three months outstanding is accrued at December 31, 20X5, and paid January 1, 20X6. Net income for 20X5 under this alternate fact is **$1,027,250** (higher than $980,000 because only three months of after-tax interest is incurred). Weighted-average common shares remain **350,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, 20X5 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible bonds at par. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, 20X5 period-end adjusting JE** to accrue a full year of interest on the bonds. Prove the entry balances.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement interest schedule** for 20X5 (face, rate, interest expense, cash paid in 20X5, interest payable end, carrying amount).  
d. Compute **basic EPS** for 20X5 (full-year bonds facts).  
e. Prepare the **diluted EPS reconciliation schedule** using the **if-converted method**. State whether the bonds are **dilutive** or **antidilutive**.  
f. **Classification / presentation:** What EPS amount(s) does Harborline report on the face of its 20X5 income statement?  
g. Using the **October 1 issuance alternate facts**, recompute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** under the if-converted method with **partial-year weighting**. Show the reconciliation schedule and the **December 31 period-end AJ** for three months of interest.  
h. On **January 1, 20X6**, Harborline (i) pays the accrued interest from the full-year facts and (ii) all bondholders convert their bonds into common stock. Prepare both JEs (payment then conversion).
