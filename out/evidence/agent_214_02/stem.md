### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — multi-asset recoverability screen + year-end impairment + post-write-down schedule
**LO:** LO 12-7  
**Concept:** Period-end multi-asset recoverability (pass vs fail); combined impairment JE only for failed assets; **subsequent measurement schedule** with nonzero residual on the written-down asset  
**Scenario:**  
At **December 31, Year 3**, **Redwood Circuits LLC** identifies impairment indicators for three plant assets **currently used in operations**. Management provides:

| Asset | Cost | Accumulated depreciation | Recoverable cost (undiscounted) | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A — Precision press | $400,000 | $100,000 | $350,000 | $280,000 |
| B — Curing oven | $700,000 | $200,000 | $420,000 | $310,000 |
| C — Assembly conveyor | $900,000 | $150,000 | $750,000 | $600,000 |

After any required write-down, Asset B will be used **5 more years** with a revised residual of **$10,000** (straight-line). Assets A and C continue under their pre-existing policies (no change requested here).

**Required:**  
a. For each asset, compute carrying amount, apply the **recoverability test**, and state any **impairment loss**. Assume indicators are present for all three.  
b. Record a **single December 31, Year 3** journal entry for all impairment losses required (if any).  
c. For **Asset B only**, prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** of depreciation for the five years after the write-down (Years 4–8). Record the December 31, Year 4 depreciation entry for Asset B.  
d. Briefly explain why an asset with fair value **below** carrying amount might still have **no** impairment loss.
