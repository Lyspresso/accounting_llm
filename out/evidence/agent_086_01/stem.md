### Q2 — CORE number variant — Classification + initial recognition twin
**LO:** LO 13-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: classify items; initial recognition JEs; amortization schedule; period-end adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Robotics** (calendar year) has the following **Year 1** activity. Finite-life assets acquired **January 1**; residual values zero.

| # | Intangible item / transaction | Amount / terms |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cash purchase of a **copyright** (printing rights); useful life **12 years** (legal life much longer) | **$72,000** |
| 2 | Cash purchase of a **franchise**; renewable annually, management expects renewals **indefinitely** with no foreseeable limit | **$110,000** |
| 3 | Cash purchase of a **patent**; remaining legal life 14 years; **useful life 7 years** | **$56,000** |
| 4 | **External counsel fees** to successfully **register** an internally developed **trade name** expected to be renewed indefinitely | **$8,400** (April 1) |
| 5 | **Internal engineer salaries** and lab supplies creating an invention later patented | **$145,000** |
| 6 | Acquisition of a competitor: cash paid **$1,250,000**; FV of **identifiable net assets** **$980,000** | Residual = goodwill |
| 7 | **External legal fees** to **successfully defend** the purchased patent (added to patent cost on **July 1**) | **$11,200** |
| 8 | **Two-year municipal license** paid in cash January 1 | **$24,000** |

**Required:**  
a. Classify each item as finite-life IA, indefinite-life IA (not goodwill), goodwill, or expense. For item 7, state the effect on the patent’s classification and subsequent measurement.  
b. Record **initial recognition** (and the July 1 patent defense) journal entries.  
c. Prepare the **Year-1 amortization schedule** (prorate partial-year amortization for the patent defense costs added July 1; full year for Jan 1 acquisitions).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1** amortization adjusting entry.  
e. Compute **December 31, Year 1 carrying amounts** for the patent (including defense costs), copyright, franchise, trade name, license, and goodwill.
