### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Straight-line discount schedule, interest JEs, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Straight-line interest method for discount amortization (constant amort. per period); interest JE pattern; maturity when discount fully amortized; contrast with effective interest  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Brewing Co.** issues **$100,000** face of **8%** annual interest bonds on **January 1, Year 1**. Interest is paid **annually** each **December 31**. The bonds mature **December 31, Year 3**. Bonds are sold for **$95,026** (market yield **10%** at issuance). Cascade amortizes the discount using the **straight-line interest method** (assume results are not materially different from effective interest for this illustration).

- Initial discount = $100,000 − $95,026 = **$4,974**
- Annual cash interest = 8% × $100,000 = **$8,000**
- Straight-line discount amortization per year = $4,974 ÷ 3 = **$1,658**
- Annual interest expense = $8,000 + $1,658 = **$9,658**

**Required:**  
a. January 1, Year 1 issuance JE.  
b. Straight-line amortization schedule for Years 1–3.  
c. December 31, Year 1 and Year 2 interest/amortization JEs.  
d. December 31, Year 3 final interest JE and maturity settlement JE.  
e. **Classification / method note:** Under GAAP (ASC 835-30), when is straight-line acceptable relative to the effective interest method? Where is the unamortized discount reported?
