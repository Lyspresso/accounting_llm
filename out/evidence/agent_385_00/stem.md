### Q1 — CORE — Rank four dilutives; period-end AJs finalize NI; sequential diluted EPS; conversion settlement
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JEs for common, convertible preferred, and two series of convertible bonds; period-end adjusting JEs that finalize interest, tax, and preferred claim (emphasis); ranking by earnings per incremental share; sequential diluted EPS schedule with one antidilutive exclusion; period-end closing; dual EPS presentation; post-period conversion settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironridge Analytics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company with a **complex capital structure**. On **January 1, Year 1**, Ironridge establishes the following capital structure (all instruments outstanding **the entire year**):

| Instrument | Terms |
|---|---|
| Common stock | Issues **120,000** shares of **$2 par** common for **$15** cash per share |
| Convertible preferred | Issues **4,000** shares of **6%, $100 par cumulative** preferred for **$105** cash per share; each share converts into **3** common shares (**12,000** common total) |
| Series B convertible bonds | Issues **$500,000** face of **5%** bonds **at par**; each **$1,000** bond converts into **40** common shares (**20,000** common total) |
| Series A convertible bonds | Issues **$300,000** face of **7%** bonds **at par**; each **$1,000** bond converts into **20** common shares (**6,000** common total) |
| Employee stock options | **3,000** options outstanding all year; exercise price **$10**; Year 1 **average market price $15** |

**Additional facts**

- Income tax rate is **20%**.
- Before **period-end adjusting entries**, Ironridge’s books show **pretax income of $480,000** that **does not yet include** Year 1 interest on Series A and Series B convertible bonds or income tax.
- Cumulative preferred dividends for Year 1 have **not yet** been declared or accrued.
- No share issuances, buybacks, stock dividends, or splits during Year 1.
- On **January 8, Year 2**, all Series B bondholders convert their bonds into common stock (settlement of the convertible).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entries** for (1) common stock, (2) convertible preferred, (3) Series B bonds, and (4) Series A bonds. Show Dr = Cr on each.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting journal entries** for: Series B interest, Series A interest, income tax, and declaration of the full Year 1 cumulative preferred dividend. Show Dr = Cr on each.  
c. Prepare an **income-finalization schedule** ending in Year 1 **net income** and **income available to common stockholders**.  
d. Prepare the **ranking schedule** of potentially dilutive securities by **earnings per incremental share** (most dilutive → least dilutive). Show numerator effect, incremental shares, and the ratio for each.  
e. Prepare the **sequential diluted EPS schedule** (basic row, then each security in rank order, mark dilutive/antidilutive). State final basic and diluted EPS (nearest cent).  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end closing entry** that transfers net income to retained earnings.  
g. Show **Year 1 income-statement presentation** of basic and diluted EPS and identify the antidilutive security disclosed as excluded.  
h. Prepare the **January 8, Year 2 settlement (conversion) journal entry** for Series B bonds. Show Dr = Cr.
