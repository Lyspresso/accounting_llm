### Q2 — CORE number variant — Interest-bearing short-term note twin
**LO:** LO 15-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition JE; period-end interest accrual; subsequent interest schedule; maturity settlement; current presentation  
**Scenario:**  
On **August 1, Year 1**, **Oakridge Supply Inc.** borrows cash on a **10-month**, **interest-bearing** note. Interest is paid **in full at maturity** with principal. Stated rate equals market. Fiscal year-end is **December 31**.

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Cash proceeds / face of note | $120,000 |
| Stated (and market) annual interest rate | 8% |
| Issuance date | August 1, Year 1 |
| Maturity date | May 31, Year 2 |
| Interest payment pattern | All cash interest at maturity |
| Fiscal year-end | December 31 |

**Required:**  
a. Record the **August 1, Year 1 issuance** (initial recognition).  
b. Prepare the **interest schedule** for Year 1 accrual months, Year 2 remaining months, and total term interest.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1 adjusting** entry.  
d. State Year 1 **balance sheet** amounts for Notes Payable and Interest Payable.  
e. Record the **May 31, Year 2 settlement** entry.
