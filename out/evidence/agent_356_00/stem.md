### Q1 — CORE — Period-end sales-tax adjusting JE (emphasis) + AP purchase, discount settlement, remittance, rollforward
**LO:** LO 15-1  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis) — reclassify embedded sales taxes from Sales; initial recognition of AP (gross method + freight); settlement within discount; remittance of sales taxes; subsequent measurement schedules  
**Scenario:**  
**Fox Valley Outfitters LLC** uses a **perpetual** inventory system and the **gross method** for purchases. Company policy is to **not** record sales taxes payable at the point of sale. Cash and receivables are recorded for the **total** amount received/billed and credited entirely to **Sales**; at **month-end** an **adjusting entry** isolates Sales Taxes Payable by reducing Sales. Fiscal year-end is December 31.

Beginning balances on **March 1, 2026** (before March transactions):

| Account | Beginning balance |
|---|---:|
| Accounts Payable | $11,600 |
| Sales Taxes Payable | $0 |

The March 1 AP balance relates to a **February** purchase on **n/30** terms (discount window already expired; due **March 14**). February sales taxes were remitted in full before March 1.

| Date | Transaction |
|---|---|
| Mar 2 | Purchased merchandise for resale on account. Invoice price **$32,000**; terms **2/10, n/30**. Paid freight-in in cash **$480** the same day (FOB shipping point; control passes at shipment). |
| Mar 5 | Cash sales for the day: customers paid **$19,080** total (this amount **includes** 6% sales tax). Cost of goods sold **$10,500**. Per policy, the entire **$19,080** was credited to **Sales** (no tax liability recorded yet). |
| Mar 11 | Paid the Mar 2 supplier invoice **in full** within the discount period. |
| Mar 14 | Paid the February AP balance of **$11,600** in full (no discount available). |
| Mar 19 | Credit sales: customers billed **$12,720** total (**includes** 6% sales tax). Cost **$7,200**. Entire **$12,720** debited to Accounts Receivable and credited to **Sales**. |
| Mar 31 | Month-end: prepare the **adjusting entry** to reclassify the sales-tax portion of the Mar 5 and Mar 19 sales from Sales to Sales Taxes Payable. (No other taxable sales in March.) |
| Apr 22 | Remitted March sales taxes to the state taxing authority. |

**Required:**  
a. Record the **Mar 2** purchase and freight-in (initial recognition of AP).  
b. Record the **Mar 5** cash sale and COGS **as recorded under company policy** (tax not broken out).  
c. Record the **Mar 11** AP settlement within the discount period (gross method).  
d. Record the **Mar 14** settlement of February AP and the **Mar 19** credit sale + COGS under policy.  
e. Record the **Mar 31 period-end adjusting JE** for sales taxes; show the **computational schedule** (total ÷ 1.06 for each sale date).  
f. Record the **Apr 22** remittance.  
g. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / rollforward schedule** for Accounts Payable (Mar 1–Mar 31) and for Sales Taxes Payable (Mar 1–Apr 22). Prove ending balances.  
h. Classify ending (or post-adjustment) obligations on the balance sheet and briefly support.  
i. Comparison: if Fox Valley **had** recorded tax at the point of sale on Mar 5 and Mar 19, what would those sales entries have been, and what would the Mar 31 adjusting entry be?
