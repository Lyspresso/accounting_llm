### Q1 — CORE — Northvale Outfitters: interim period-end adjusting JEs, multi-period GP schedule, storm loss & insurance

**LO:** LO 10-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs for interim inventory under the gross profit method (emphasis); markup-on-cost conversion; subsequent measurement schedule; casualty write-off and insurance settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Northvale Outfitters LLC** uses a **periodic** inventory system and a **December 31** year-end. A full physical count is taken only at year-end. For **interim** reporting, management estimates inventory with the gross profit method. Past experience shows a consistent **markup of 25% of cost**.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory, January 1 (physical count) | $360,000 |

Quarterly activity (net purchases and net sales):

| Period | Net purchases | Net sales |
|---|---:|---:|
| Q1 (Jan 1 – Mar 31) | $420,000 | $500,000 |
| Q2 (Apr 1 – Jun 30) | 390,000 | 480,000 |

**July 12, Year 1:** A severe storm destroys the warehouse inventory. Records for **July 1–12** show net purchases of **$48,000** and net sales of **$90,000**. Undamaged goods salvaged and still salable are valued at **cost of $40,000**. Expected insurance recovery of the book inventory loss is **probable** and reasonably estimable at **$300,000**. On **August 5**, the insurer pays **$300,000** cash in full settlement (equal to the receivable previously recognized).

Assume each interim quarter’s estimated ending inventory is booked via a **period-end adjusting entry** and becomes the beginning inventory carrying amount for the next period’s estimate. Purchases for each period are recorded in **Purchases** (periodic). Ignore income taxes.

**Required:**  
(a) Convert the markup on cost to **gross profit as a percentage of sales** and state the **cost percentage of sales**.  
(b) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** rolling forward **estimated ending inventory** for Mar 31 and Jun 30 (show GAS, estimated COGS, and estimated EI for each quarter).  
(c) Prepare the **period-end adjusting journal entries** for **March 31** and **June 30** that recognize estimated COGS and set ending inventory (**emphasis**).  
(d) Using the Jun 30 estimated inventory as beginning inventory for July, prepare the **gross profit method schedule** estimating inventory on hand at the **July 12 storm date** (before salvage), and compute the **inventory loss**.  
(e) Prepare the **July 12 initial recognition journal entry** to reclassify salvage inventory, write off destroyed inventory, and record the casualty loss (**before** insurance).  
(f) Prepare the **July 12 journal entry** to recognize the **insurance receivable** for the expected recovery.  
(g) Prepare the **August 5 settlement journal entry** when cash is received.  
(h) Report inventory on the balance sheet immediately after the July 12 write-off (before insurance cash is received). State **one use** and **one limitation** of the gross profit method.
