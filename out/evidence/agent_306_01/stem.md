### Q2 — CORE number variant — Secured borrowing life cycle (all numbers changed)

**LO:** LO 8-6  
**Concept:** Same path as Q1 — initial secured-borrowing JE, loan schedule, **period-end interest/remittance adjusting JEs**, final settlement — with all different amounts and rate  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarpoint Metals Corp.** assigns **$200,000** of AR as collateral on **March 1, Year 1**.

**Contract terms:**  
- Advance = **75%** of assigned AR  
- Flat finance fee = **$2,250** (1.5% of face AR, deducted at inception)  
- Interest = **12%** per year on unpaid balance, paid monthly at period-end  

**Subsequent events:**  
1. **March 31, Year 1:** Collected **$74,000 cash** on **$82,000** assigned AR after **$5,000** sales returns (Refund Liability) and **$3,000** sales discounts. Remits **$74,000** to the finance company.  
2. **April:** Write off **$2,500** assigned AR against AFDA; collect another **$50,000** on assigned accounts.  
3. **April 30, Year 1:** Pays remaining loan **in full** (including April interest).

**Required:**  
a. March 1 initial recognition JE.  
b. Loan amortization schedule (Mar 1 → final payoff).  
c. March 31 period-end collection + remittance JEs.  
d. April collection/write-off JE.  
e. April 30 settlement JE.  
f. Total financing cost.
