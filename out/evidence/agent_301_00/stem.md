### Q1 — CORE — Multi-date classification path: initial recognition, CE cycle, subsequent schedule, period-end reclass
**LO:** LO 8-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JEs for borrowing/compensating balance and cash equivalents; subsequent measurement schedule; period-end NSF reclass; maturity/settlement of commercial paper; Dec 31 classification totals  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Peak Manufacturing Co.** has a **December 31** year-end. Policy: short-term, highly liquid investments with **original maturities of three months or less** (to Summit Peak) that present insignificant interest-rate and credit risk are reported as **cash equivalents**.

Immediately before **October 1, Year 1**, Summit Peak’s cash-related balances for classification tracking are:

| Caption | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Unrestricted cash | $168,000 |
| Cash equivalents | $0 |
| Restricted cash—current | $0 |
| Restricted cash—noncurrent | $0 |

**Events during Year 1 (classification-affecting only):**

1. **October 1, Year 1:** Borrowed **$360,000** from Crestview National Bank on a **one-year, 7%** note payable. The note **legally requires** an **8%** compensating balance (**$28,800**) to remain on deposit at Crestview during the loan term. Full proceeds are deposited to the Crestview checking account; Summit Peak immediately reclassifies the legally restricted amount.

2. **October 15, Year 1:** Purchased **commercial paper** for **$54,000** cash. Original term to Summit Peak: **45 days**. The paper matures on **November 29, Year 1** for **$54,486** (discount interest **$486**). Policy: qualifying commercial paper is a cash equivalent.

3. **November 20, Year 1:** Purchased a **U.S. Treasury bill** for **$36,800** cash when the bill had **two months** remaining until maturity (matures January 20, Year 2). Treated as a cash equivalent. Still held at December 31.

4. **December 5, Year 1:** Transferred **$14,200** from general cash to a **restricted dividend fund** (dividends paid January 15, Year 2).

5. **December 12, Year 1:** Deposited **$63,500** with a trustee for a **bond sinking fund**. Related bonds mature in **five years**.

**Additional items on hand / on books at December 31, Year 1** (after commercial-paper maturity; after the transfers above):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Checking—Crestview National (balance **includes** the $28,800 legally restricted compensating balance; split for presentation) | $76,400 |
| Checking—Prairie Credit Union (includes an **NSF check of $1,150** from a customer not yet reclassified) | $42,800 |
| Savings account | $18,600 |
| Petty cash | $650 |
| Cashier’s check payable to Summit Peak (dated Dec 29, not yet deposited) | $3,400 |
| Cash equivalents—U.S. T-bill (Nov 20 purchase, still held) | $36,800 |
| Money market fund (highly liquid; original maturity to Summit Peak 1 month; low risk) | $33,000 |
| Restricted cash—Dividend fund (segregated Dec 5) | $14,200 |
| Restricted cash—Bond sinking fund (segregated Dec 12) | $63,500 |
| T-bill purchased **August 1, Year 1** when it had **four months** remaining to maturity; cost still on books | $27,000 |
| Travel advance to employee for January trip | $2,250 |
| Customer check dated **January 12, Year 2** (postdated) | $1,880 |
| Overdraft at Redrock Bank (Summit Peak has **no** other account at Redrock) | $3,750 |

**Assume:** For Crestview presentation, the full $76,400 sits in the Crestview checking subtotal and must be split between unrestricted cash and restricted compensating balance. Dividend fund and sinking fund remain in separate restricted-cash accounts.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **October 1** borrowing entry and the **initial recognition** reclassification of the legally restricted compensating balance.  
(b) Record the **October 15** purchase of commercial paper as a cash equivalent.  
(c) Record the **November 29** maturity/settlement of the commercial paper.  
(d) Record the **November 20** T-bill purchase, the **December 5** dividend-fund transfer, and the **December 12** bond-sinking-fund deposit.  
(e) Prepare a **subsequent measurement / classification schedule** after each event date in (a)–(d), rolling forward columns: Unrestricted cash; Cash equivalents; Restricted cash—current; Restricted cash—noncurrent. Start from the pre–October 1 balances. Treat interest on commercial-paper maturity as an increase to unrestricted cash only (ignore other operating cash movements not listed).  
(f) Record the **December 31 period-end reclassifying entry** for the NSF check (**emphasis**).  
(g) Prepare a complete **December 31 classification schedule** for every item in the year-end table (after NSF reclass).  
(h) Report balance-sheet totals for **Cash**; **Cash equivalents**; **Restricted cash—current**; **Restricted cash—noncurrent**; and the **current liability** from the overdraft.  
(i) Briefly state note-disclosure treatment if the compensating balance had **not** been legally restricted.
