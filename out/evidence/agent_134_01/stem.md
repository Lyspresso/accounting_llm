### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same TS FV-NI path with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Harborpeak Capital LLC** purchased for **par $40,000** of **Redwood Corp. 6%** bonds that mature on December 31 of Year 8. Cash interest is paid **annually** each December 31. Harborpeak classifies the bonds as **trading securities (TS)** under **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA—TS is zero. No other TS activity except as stated.

| Date | Fair value / activity |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$38,500** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Still held; fair value **$41,200** |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Sold entire holding for **$41,000** |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Cash interest entry on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Fair value adjustment on December 31 of Year 1 (include FVA schedule).  
(d) Year 1 BS / IS presentation for this investment.  
(e) December 31, Year 2 FVA rollforward schedule and adjusting entry.  
(f) January 1, Year 3 sale: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust; (ii) sale eliminating FVA.  
(g) Holding-period unrealized G/L schedule proving cumulative NI = proceeds − cost.
