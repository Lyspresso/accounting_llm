### Q1 — CORE — Convertible bonds: interest schedule, if-converted EPS reconciliation (emphasis), partial-year, conversion JE
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedules for convertible-bond interest and diluted EPS under the if-converted method; initial recognition at par; period-end interest JEs; full-year and mid-year if-converted weighting; conversion settlement JE; income-statement EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Precision Instruments Inc.** is a calendar-year public company. Its only potentially dilutive instrument is convertible debt (complex capital structure). Common stock has a **$1 par** value. No preferred stock and no options/warrants.

**Facts for Year 1 (full-year bonds):**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Riverbend issued **1,500** convertible bonds, each with **$1,000** face (**$1,500,000** total face), **6%** stated annual interest, for **cash at par**. Each bond is convertible into **30** shares of common stock. Maturity is December 31, Year 10. Interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31.
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding during Year 1 (before if-converted assumptions): **250,000**.
3. Net income for Year 1: **$750,000** (already after interest expense on the convertible bonds and after income taxes).
4. Enacted tax rate: **25%**.

**Alternate fact (part g only):** Same bonds are issued on **July 1, Year 1** (not January 1). Semiannual interest is paid December 31 for the six months outstanding. Net income for Year 1 under this alternate fact is **$783,750** (higher than $750,000 because only six months of after-tax interest is incurred). Weighted-average common shares remain **250,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible bonds at par. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement interest schedule** for Year 1 (face, rate, interest expense, cash paid, carrying amount). Prepare the **June 30** and **December 31** cash interest payment JEs.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** for Year 1 (full-year bonds facts).  
d. Prepare the **diluted EPS reconciliation schedule** using the **if-converted method** (emphasis). State whether the bonds are **dilutive** or **antidilutive**.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** What EPS amount(s) does Riverbend report on the face of its Year 1 income statement?  
f. On **January 1, Year 2**, all bondholders convert their bonds into common stock. Prepare the **conversion (settlement) JE**. (Ignore any interest on January 1.)  
g. Using the **July 1 issuance alternate facts**, recompute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** under the if-converted method with **partial-year weighting**. Show the subsequent measurement / reconciliation schedule.
