### Q1 — CORE — Full R&D cost cycle (emphasis: initial recognition)
**LO:** LO 13-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of R&D costs (expense vs capitalize multi-use assets); Year-1 R&D depreciation; multi-year depreciation schedule; disposal of multi-use equipment; R&D expense disclosure total  
**Scenario:**  
**ApexNova Therapeutics Inc.**, a calendar-year company, conducts extensive research and development. During **Year 1**, ApexNova incurred the following (all amounts paid in cash unless noted):

| # | Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Materials purchased and **consumed** solely on current R&D projects (**no** alternative future use) | **$240,000** |
| 2 | Laboratory equipment purchased **January 1, Year 1** with **significant alternative future uses** (multiple future R&D projects and production support); residual value **$0**; **straight-line** over **6 years**; placed in service Jan 1 for R&D | **$480,000** |
| 3 | Specialized testing apparatus purchased **April 1, Year 1** for a **single** R&D project only; **no** alternative future use | **$96,000** |
| 4 | Salaries and wages of scientists and technicians engaged in R&D | **$1,150,000** |
| 5 | Fees paid to an outside university lab for contract research services | **$85,000** |
| 6 | Reasonable allocation of indirect facility costs clearly related to R&D (utilities, insurance, security of R&D wing) | **$72,000** |
| 7 | Corporate headquarters G&A (executive, HR, central accounting) **not** clearly related to R&D | **$310,000** |
| 8 | Troubleshooting and repair of breakdowns on an **existing commercial production** line | **$41,000** |
| 9 | Design and construction of **preproduction prototypes** for a new product under development | **$55,000** |
| 10 | Legal fees paid **July 1, Year 1** to outside counsel to **register a patent** on a successfully developed process; remaining legal life 20 years; estimated **useful life 10 years**; residual **$0**; amortize straight-line from July 1 | **$12,000** |

On **January 1, Year 4**, after recording three full years of depreciation, ApexNova sells the multi-use laboratory equipment (item 2) for **$250,000** cash. No impairment was recognized in Years 1–3.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **Year 1 initial recognition** journal entries for items 1–10 (group by accounting treatment as appropriate). Emphasize which costs are immediately expensed as R&D versus capitalized.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** adjusting entries for (1) depreciation of multi-use laboratory equipment and (2) amortization of the patent.  
c. Prepare a complete **straight-line depreciation schedule** for the multi-use laboratory equipment for Years 1–6 (beginning book value, depreciation expense classified as R&D, ending book value).  
d. Compute **total research and development expense** to be **disclosed** for Year 1. Briefly list what is **excluded** from R&D.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4 disposal** journal entry for the laboratory equipment (after Year 1–3 depreciation already recorded).  
f. State how total R&D cost is **presented/disclosed** under ASC 730.
