### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Self-constructed equipment: CIP accumulation, FV ceiling settlement, period-end dep AJ (emphasis), disposal

**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Self-constructed asset cost accumulation in CIP; completion JE with **fair-value ceiling** (cost > FV → loss); **period-end depreciation adjusting JE** (emphasis); subsequent CV schedule; disposal settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Quillridge Components Inc.** (calendar year-end) constructs specialized production equipment for its own use during **Year 1**. Costs are accumulated in Construction in Process as incurred. On **July 1, Year 1**, construction is complete and the equipment is placed in service. Quillridge applies full costing (materials, labor, incremental overhead, and applied general overhead).

**CIP costs through July 1, Year 1:**

| Cost | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Direct materials | $120,000 |
| Direct labor | 280,000 |
| Incremental overhead | 35,000 |
| Applied general overhead | 25,000 |
| **Total CIP** | **$460,000** |

The fair value of a similar equipment unit of equal capacity and quality on July 1 is **$440,000**.  
Depreciation: straight-line; salvage **$40,000**; useful life **8 years**.  
On **December 31, Year 2**, after recording Year 2 depreciation, Quillridge sells the equipment for **$355,000** cash.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **summary initial recognition JE** to accumulate all construction costs in CIP (cash / payable assumed paid in cash).  
b. Prepare the **July 1, Year 1 settlement JE** transferring CIP to Equipment, applying the fair-value ceiling. Prove the entry balances.  
c. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for depreciation.  
d. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of cost, accum. dep., and carrying amount at Dec 31 Year 1 and Dec 31 Year 2 (before disposal).  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** period-end dep JE and the **disposal JE**.
