### Q1 — CORE — Multi-use R&D assets: initial recognition, full subsequent measurement schedules, period-end R&D charges, disposal
**LO:** LO 13-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of R&D (expense vs capitalize multi-use assets); full straight-line **subsequent measurement schedules** for multi-use equipment and multi-project intangible (amortization classified as R&D); period-end adjusting JEs; disposal of multi-use equipment; Year-1 R&D disclosure total  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Photonix Corp.** (calendar year-end) conducts research and development of optical sensors. During **Year 1**, Northline incurred the following (paid in cash unless noted):

| # | Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Materials purchased and **consumed** solely on current R&D projects (**no** alternative future use) | **$185,000** |
| 2 | Laboratory equipment purchased **January 1, Year 1** with **significant alternative future uses** (multiple future R&D projects and limited production support); residual value **$30,000**; **straight-line** over **5 years**; placed in service Jan 1 and used in R&D | **$600,000** |
| 3 | Specialized testing chamber purchased **March 1, Year 1** for a **single** R&D project only; **no** alternative future use | **$72,000** |
| 4 | Salaries and wages of scientists and technicians engaged in R&D | **$980,000** |
| 5 | Fees paid to an outside university lab for contract R&D services | **$125,000** |
| 6 | Reasonable allocation of indirect facility costs clearly related to R&D (utilities, security of R&D wing) | **$48,000** |
| 7 | Finite-life technical database / know-how license acquired **January 1, Year 1** for use across **multiple** R&D projects (alternative future uses); residual **$0**; amortize **straight-line** over **4 years**; amortization classified as R&D | **$40,000** |
| 8 | Design and construction of **preproduction prototypes** for a new sensor under development | **$44,000** |
| 9 | Quality control and troubleshooting during **commercial production** of existing products | **$35,000** |
| 10 | Corporate headquarters G&A **not** clearly related to R&D | **$200,000** |

No impairment is recognized in Years 1–3. On **January 1, Year 4**, after three full years of depreciation have been recorded, Northline **sells** the multi-use laboratory equipment (item 2) for **$240,000** cash.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **Year 1 initial recognition** journal entries for items 1–10 (group by treatment: expense as R&D, capitalize asset, or non-R&D expense).  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JEs** for (1) depreciation of multi-use laboratory equipment and (2) amortization of the multi-project technical license.  
c. Prepare the complete **subsequent measurement (straight-line depreciation) schedule** for the multi-use laboratory equipment for Years 1–5 (beginning book value, depreciation classified as R&D, ending book value).  
d. Prepare the complete **subsequent measurement (amortization) schedule** for the multi-project technical license for Years 1–4 (beginning CV, amortization classified as R&D, ending CV).  
e. Compute **total research and development expense** to be **disclosed** for Year 1. List major items **excluded** from R&D.  
f. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4 disposal** journal entry for the laboratory equipment (after Years 1–3 depreciation already recorded).  
g. Briefly state why depreciation of multi-use equipment is R&D expense while the equipment **purchase** is not fully expensed in Year 1.
