### Q1 — CORE — Meridian Fabrication: initial recognition, dep schedule, period-end dep update (emphasis), commercial-substance exchange with loss + cash paid, subsequent dep, later disposal

**LO:** LO 11-9  
**Concept:** Full lifecycle for a **nonmonetary exchange with commercial substance** (here a **loss** after residual-aware SL dep) — initial recognition, subsequent measurement schedule, **period-end adjusting JE** to update AD through exchange date (emphasis), exchange JE, subsequent dep on new asset, later disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Fabrication Co.** (calendar year) acquired a hydraulic press on **January 1, 20X1** for **$90,000** cash. The press has an estimated residual value of **$9,000** and a **6-year** useful life. Meridian uses **straight-line** depreciation and records depreciation to the nearest month.

On **September 30, 20X4**, Meridian exchanges the press (fair value **$38,000**) plus **$25,000** cash for a used CNC mill. The fair value of the mill is not more clearly determinable than the fair value of the press. The exchange **has commercial substance**. The mill is placed in service on **October 1, 20X4**, residual value **$3,000**, useful life **5 years**, straight-line.

On **June 30, 20X7**, Meridian sells the CNC mill for **$28,000** cash. Depreciation is updated through the disposal date.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, 20X1 initial recognition** of the hydraulic press.  
(b) Prepare a **subsequent measurement (depreciation) schedule** for the press from acquisition through the exchange date (show annual and partial-year amounts and cumulative AD).  
(c) **(Emphasis)** Record the **September 30, 20X4 period-end / pre-exchange adjusting JE** updating depreciation on the press through the exchange date.  
(d) Compute book value of the press at September 30, 20X4, the **gain or loss** on exchange, and the **cost assigned to the CNC mill**. Record the **exchange JE**. Prove Dr = Cr.  
(e) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for the CNC mill from October 1, 20X4 through June 30, 20X7. Record **December 31, 20X4** and **June 30, 20X7** depreciation adjusting entries.  
(f) Record the **June 30, 20X7 disposal JE**. Compute gain or loss. Prove Dr = Cr.
