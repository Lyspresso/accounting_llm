### Q3 — CORE — Multi-item classification grid + depreciation origin schedule + warranty settlement
**LO:** LO 18-1  
**Concept:** Classify a portfolio of common book-tax differences; subsequent measurement schedule for accelerated tax depreciation; period-end settlement JE  
**Scenario:** **Lakeview Medical Devices LLC** has the following **independent** items that may differ for pretax GAAP income and taxable income. The enacted rate is **25%** where amounts matter.

**Part A — Classification grid.** For each item, classify as **taxable temporary**, **deductible temporary**, **permanent (nontaxable)**, **permanent (nondeductible)**, or **no book-tax difference**. Briefly justify using origin/reversal or “never reverses.”

| # | Item description |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Installment sale receivable recognized in full for GAAP; taxed as cash is collected |
| 2 | Customer advances deferred for GAAP; taxed when cash is received |
| 3 | Straight-line depreciation for GAAP; MACRS (higher early years) for tax |
| 4 | Life insurance proceeds received on officer’s death (GAAP gain) |
| 5 | Premiums on officer life insurance (company is beneficiary) — GAAP expense |
| 6 | Unrealized holding gain on equity securities (FV-NI) for GAAP; tax at sale |
| 7 | Estimated for environmental remediation accrued for GAAP; deductible when paid |
| 8 | Interest expense on loan used to carry municipal bonds (tax rules disallow) |
| 9 | Percentage-of-completion revenue for GAAP equals completed-contract tax revenue this year (same amount) |
| 10 | Inventory written down to NRV for GAAP; loss not deductible for tax until inventory is sold |

**Part B — Depreciation temporary difference schedule.** On January 1, Year 1, Lakeview places equipment costing **$80,000** in service (no salvage). GAAP depreciation is straight-line over **4 years** ($20,000 per year). Tax depreciation: Year 1 **$32,000**; Year 2 **$24,000**; Year 3 **$16,000**; Year 4 **$8,000**. Pretax GAAP income each year (already after GAAP depreciation): Y1 **$200,000**; Y2 **$210,000**; Y3 **$210,000**; Y4 **$205,000**. No other book-tax differences. Rate **25%**. Beginning deferred taxes = $0.

Required for Part B:  
(a) For each year, compute tax depreciation vs GAAP depreciation, the annual origin/(reversal) of the taxable temporary difference, ending cumulative taxable temporary difference, and ending DTL.  
(b) Compute taxable income and income tax payable each year.  
(c) Record the income tax journal entry for **Year 1** and **Year 4** only.

**Part C — Settlement JE.** Lakeview’s Accrued Warranty Liability beginning balance is **$18,000** (tax basis $0). During Year 2 it pays **$7,000** cash on warranties and records **no new** warranty expense. Record the settlement entry and state the effect on the deductible temporary difference and DTA (25%).
