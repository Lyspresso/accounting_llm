### Q2 — CORE number variant — Northvale Retail: trademark, goodwill, crypto, schedule, disposal

**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition JE for indefinite-life trademark and goodwill residual; period-end crypto FV AJ; subsequent-measurement schedule (no amort); disposal JE  
**Scenario:**

5. **April 1, 20X2:** Sells the **February 1, 20X1 purchased trademark** for **$110,000 cash**.  
6. **December 31, 20X2:** Crypto still held; fair value **$68,000**.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute trademark cost and goodwill; record initial recognition JEs for trademark, acquisition, and crypto. Prove balances.

(c) Prepare a 20X1–20X2 carrying-value schedule for: purchased Trademark, Acquired Brand, IPR&D, Goodwill, Crypto.  
(d) Record the April 1, 20X2 disposal JE and the December 31, 20X2 crypto AJ. Prove balances; compute gain/loss.
