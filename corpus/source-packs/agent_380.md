# Agent 380 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-4

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute earnings per share (EPS) with a simple capital structure  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Basic EPS formula:** net income available to common stockholders ÷ weighted-average common shares outstanding
- **Simple capital structure:** equity consists only of common stock (one or more classes); no instruments that could convert into common and dilute EPS
- **Initial recognition JE:** cash issuance of common stock that establishes shares outstanding for the EPS denominator
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** weighted-average common shares schedule when shares are constant all year; multi-line basic EPS computation schedule (numerator / denominator / per-share)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end adjusting entries that finalize pretax income, income tax, and **net income** before EPS is computed; period-end closing of Income Summary to retained earnings (EPS itself is a **presentation** measure—no separate EPS ledger account)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** cash common dividend declaration and payment (settlement of distributions) — **no** reduction of net income available to common for basic EPS
- **Net loss per share:** net loss ÷ weighted-average common shares
- **Classification / presentation:** simple capital structure reports **basic EPS only** (single EPS presentation) on the face of the income statement

### Q1 — CORE — Basic EPS after period-end AJs that finalize NI (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for common stock issuance; period-end adjusting JEs that finalize net income (emphasis); weighted-average shares and basic EPS computation schedules; period-end closing; simple-structure income-statement presentation of basic EPS  
**Scenario:**  
**Northvale Circuit Works Inc.** is a calendar-year public company with a **simple capital structure** (only common stock; no preferred stock, convertible debt, options, warrants, or other potentially dilutive instruments). No noncontrolling interest exists.

On **January 1, Year 1**, Northvale issues **160,000 shares** of **$5 par** common stock for **$25** cash per share. All 160,000 shares remain outstanding for the **entire year**. There are no additional share issuances, buybacks, stock dividends, or stock splits during Year 1.

Before **period-end adjusting entries**, Northvale’s books show **income before year-end adjustments of $1,050,000** (credit balance of temporary income accounts net of expenses already recorded during the year). The only remaining items needed to finalize Year 1 net income are the following **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjustments**:

| Adjustment | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Depreciation expense (manufacturing equipment) | **$90,000** |
| Accrued interest expense on notes payable | **$30,000** |
| Income tax expense (20% of pretax income **after** the two adjustments above) | *compute* |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the common stock issuance. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the three **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting journal entries** (depreciation, accrued interest, and income tax). Show Dr = Cr on each entry.  
c. Compute **Year 1 net income** after the period-end adjustments. Present a short **income-finalization schedule**.  
d. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule** for Year 1.  
e. Prepare a **basic EPS computation schedule** showing numerator, denominator, and per-share amount.  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end closing entry** that transfers net income to retained earnings (use Income Summary).  
g. Show how **net income** and **basic earnings per share** appear on the face of the Year 1 income statement.

**Answer key:**

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of common stock issuance**

Cash proceeds = 160,000 × $25 = **$4,000,000**  
Common stock (par) = 160,000 × $5 = **$800,000**  
APIC—Common = $4,000,000 − $800,000 = **$3,200,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 4,000,000 | |
| Common Stock ($5 par) | | 800,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 3,200,000 |
| *Issue 160,000 shares of $5 par common at $25* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **4,000,000**; Cr = 800,000 + 3,200,000 = **4,000,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

*(1) Depreciation*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 90,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 90,000 |
| *Record Year 1 depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **90,000**. **Balanced.**

*(2) Accrued interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 30,000 | |
| Interest Payable | | 30,000 |
| *Accrue interest at year-end* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **30,000**. **Balanced.**

*(3) Income tax*

Pretax income after adjustments (1) and (2):  
$1,050,000 − $90,000 − $30,000 = **$930,000**  
Income tax expense = $930,000 × 20% = **$186,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 186,000 | |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 186,000 |
| *Accrue income tax at 20% of pretax income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **186,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Income-finalization schedule → Year 1 net income**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Income before year-end adjustments | $1,050,000 |
| Less: Depreciation expense | (90,000) |
| Less: Interest expense | (30,000) |
| **Pretax income** | **930,000** |
| Less: Income tax expense (20%) | (186,000) |
| **Net income** | **$744,000** |

**d. Weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule (Year 1)**

| Period outstanding | Shares | Fraction of year (months/12) | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 – December 31 (entire year) | 160,000 | 12/12 = 1.00 | 160,000 |
| **Weighted-average common shares outstanding** | | | **160,000** |

Because no shares were issued or reacquired **during** the year after January 1, weighted-average common shares equal **160,000**.

**e. Basic EPS computation schedule**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income available to common stockholders (numerator) | $744,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (denominator) | 160,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$4.65** |

Computation:  
\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$744{,}000}{160{,}000} = \mathbf{\$4.65}
\]

*Check:* \(160{,}000 \times \$4.65 = \$744{,}000\).

**f. December 31, Year 1 — period-end closing of net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary | 744,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 744,000 |
| *Close Year 1 net income to retained earnings* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **744,000**. **Balanced.**

*Note:* Basic EPS is computed from finalized net income and weighted-average shares for **presentation**; there is no journal entry that “books” EPS itself.

**g. Financial statement presentation (simple capital structure)**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $744,000 |
| Basic earnings per share | **$4.65** |

Only **basic** EPS is presented (single EPS amount) because the capital structure is **simple**.

**Key insight:** Under LO 20-4, **basic EPS** = net income available to common ÷ weighted-average common shares. **Period-end adjusting JEs** finalize depreciation, interest, and tax so that the **numerator** is complete before EPS is computed. When only common stock is outstanding all year and no dilutive instruments exist, the company has a **simple capital structure** and reports a **single** basic EPS figure on the face of the income statement. Closing NI to RE does not change the EPS formula.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Period-end AJs + basic EPS twin
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: common stock issuance JE; period-end adjusting JEs that finalize net income; weighted-average shares and basic EPS schedules; period-end closing; simple-structure presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpine Logistics Corp.** has a **simple capital structure** (common stock only; no convertible or option-type instruments). No noncontrolling interest.

On **January 1, Year 1**, Silverpine issues **250,000 shares** of **$2 par** common stock for **$14** cash per share. All 250,000 shares remain outstanding **the entire year**. No share activity occurs during the year after issuance.

Before year-end adjustments, income is **$1,500,000**. December 31 period-end adjustments:

| Adjustment | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Depreciation expense | **$100,000** |
| Accrued interest expense | **$25,000** |
| Income tax expense (20% of pretax income after the two adjustments above) | *compute* |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1** common stock **initial recognition** journal entry.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the three **December 31 period-end adjusting** journal entries.  
c. Compute **Year 1 net income** (income-finalization schedule).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Net loss per share; period-end dividend settlement; closing
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Basic EPS when the company has a net loss; period-end common cash dividend declaration and settlement JEs do not change the EPS numerator; period-end closing of net loss; simple capital structure presentation of loss per share  
**Scenario:**  
**Glacier Peak Outfitters Ltd.** is a calendar-year public company with a **simple capital structure**. Throughout Year 1 it has **140,000 shares** of common stock outstanding (issued in a prior year; **no** Year 1 share issuances, buybacks, stock dividends, or splits). There is **no** preferred stock and **no** potentially dilutive securities. No noncontrolling interest.

For Year 1, Glacier Peak reports a **net loss of $280,000** (already finalized after all income-statement adjustments). On **December 20, Year 1**, the board declares a **cash dividend of $0.10 per common share**, payable **December 30, Year 1**. The dividend is paid on the payment date.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 20 declaration** and **December 30 payment** journal entries for the common cash dividend. Show Dr = Cr on each entry.  
b. Prepare a **weighted-average common shares schedule** for Year 1.  
c. Compute **basic earnings (loss) per share** for Year 1. Explicitly state the numerator and whether the common cash dividend affects it. Present a full **EPS computation schedule**.  
d. Prepare the **December 31 period-end closing entry** for the net loss (use Income Summary).  
e. Show the **income statement presentation** of the net loss and basic EPS for Year 1.  
f. In one or two sentences, explain why Glacier Peak reports **only basic EPS** on the face of the income statement.

**Answer key:**

**a. Common cash dividend — declaration and settlement**

Cash dividend = 140,000 × $0.10 = **$14,000**

*December 20 — declaration*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Dividends Declared) | 14,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 14,000 |
| *Declare $0.10 cash dividend on 140,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **14,000**. **Balanced.**

*December 30 — payment (settlement)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 14,000 | |
| Cash | | 14,000 |
| *Pay common cash dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **14,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Weighted-average common shares schedule**

| Period outstanding | Shares | Weight | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 – December 31 | 140,000 | 12/12 | 140,000 |
| **Weighted-average common shares outstanding** | | | **140,000** |

**c. Numerator and basic EPS (net loss case) — EPS computation schedule**

Net loss for the year = **$(280,000)**  
Common cash dividends **do not** change net income (loss) available to common for EPS. EPS measures earnings or loss **per common share**; distributing retained earnings as a common dividend does not increase the loss attributable to common for the EPS calculation.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income (loss) available to common stockholders (numerator) | $(280,000) |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (denominator) | 140,000 |
| **Basic earnings (loss) per share** | **$(2.00)** |

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$(280{,}000)}{140{,}000} = \mathbf{\$(2.00)}
\]

*Check:* \(140{,}000 \times \$(2.00) = \$(280{,}000)\).

**d. December 31 — period-end closing of net loss**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 280,000 | |
| Income Summary | | 280,000 |
| *Close Year 1 net loss to retained earnings* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **280,000**. **Balanced.**

**e. Income statement presentation**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net loss | $(280,000) |
| Basic earnings (loss) per share | **$(2.00)** |

**f. Classification / presentation reason**  
Glacier Peak has a **simple capital structure** (only common stock; no instruments that could convert into common and dilute EPS). Therefore a **single** EPS presentation—**basic** EPS (here, a loss per share)—is appropriate on the face of the income statement; diluted EPS is not required.

**Key insight:** A **net loss** is still divided by weighted-average common shares to produce a **loss per share**. **Common** cash dividends reduce retained earnings when declared (declaration + settlement JEs) but **do not** adjust the EPS numerator. Period-end **closing** transfers the loss to RE without changing the EPS formula. (Preferred dividends would reduce income available to common—that refinement is developed with other capital-structure complications; this item stays on the LO 20-4 simple-structure path.)

### Q4 — MC — Simple capital structure EPS presentation
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Classification of which EPS amount(s) appear on the face of the income statement when the capital structure is simple  
**Question:**  
A company has only common stock outstanding for the entire year and has no convertible debt, convertible preferred stock, options, warrants, or other potentially dilutive securities. Which EPS amount(s) must it present on the face of the income statement?

- A) Basic EPS and diluted EPS with equal prominence  
- B) Diluted EPS only  
- C) Basic EPS only (a single EPS presentation)  
- D) Neither basic nor diluted EPS is required on the face of the income statement  

**Answer:** C.  
A **simple capital structure** (only common stock; no potentially dilutive instruments) requires a **single** EPS presentation—**basic** earnings per share—on the face of the income statement. Diluted EPS is required when the structure is complex (potentially dilutive securities exist).

### Q5 — MC — Elementary basic EPS computation
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Classification of the correct elementary basic EPS computation for a simple capital structure  
**Question:**  
Tilden Marine Group has a simple capital structure, net income of $612,000, and 180,000 common shares outstanding for the entire year. There is no preferred stock and no noncontrolling interest. What is basic earnings per share?

- A) $2.40  
- B) $3.40  
- C) $3.60  
- D) $4.20  

**Answer:** B.  
\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$612{,}000}{180{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.40}
\]  
Net income available to common equals net income when there is no preferred stock claim. Shares outstanding all year equal weighted-average common shares.
