# Agent 396 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-2

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** trading debt securities purchased at par (including between interest dates with Interest Receivable for accrued coupon)
- **Interest revenue:** full coupon cash received; reverse accrued Interest Receivable; recognize only interest earned while held
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortized cost vs fair value; required FVA; existing FVA; amount of increase (decrease) to FVA needed
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Fair Value Adjustment—TS ↔ Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** (FV-NI), not OCI
- **Disposal / sale JE (emphasis):** **first** adjust FVA to sale-date fair value through NI; **then** debit Cash, eliminate Investment at amortized cost, and clear associated FVA—**no separate realized G/L** once FV-adjusted
- **Holding-period income rollforward:** period unrealized G/L in NI sum to (sale proceeds − amortized cost) over ownership
- **Multi-period FVA true-up:** when an FVA balance already exists, adjust only the **change** required to reach the new required FVA
- **Fair value option:** otherwise HTM (or AFS) debt measured at FV-NI with same period-end / sale-date FVA mechanics as TS
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** TS typically **current** assets; investment at fair value on BS; interest revenue and unrealized holding G/L in **net income** (other income); SCF investing vs operating for trading by purpose
- **Number-variant twin:** same TS FV-NI path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — TS purchase between interest dates, YE FVA, presentation, **sale-date FV-NI disposal (emphasis)**
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE; subsequent_measurement_schedule; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **September 1 of Year 1**, **Lakeshore Asset Management** purchased **at par** **$60,000** face of **Pinnacle Energy** **7%** bonds (bonds dated **July 1 of Year 1**; mature **June 30 of Year 7**). Cash interest is paid **semiannually on June 30 and December 31**. Lakeshore classifies the investment as **trading securities (TS)** and measures it at **FV-NI**. Lakeshore’s accounting period ends **December 31**. Brokerage fees are zero. Beginning Fair Value Adjustment—TS is **zero**.

| Date | Event / fair value |
|---|---|
| Sep 1, Year 1 | Purchase $60,000 face at par + accrued interest |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Collect semiannual coupon; fair value of bonds = **$57,600** |
| Feb 5, Year 2 | Entire position sold for **$58,200** cash (clean exit price for the investment account—no additional accrued-interest complexity beyond the post–December 31 coupon cycle) |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **September 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (include accrued interest).  
(b) Record receipt of cash interest on **December 31, Year 1**.  
(c) Prepare the **subsequent measurement (FVA) schedule** and record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end fair value adjusting JE**.  
(d) Report the investment on the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet** and the related **Year 1 income statement** effects (interest revenue and unrealized holding G/L).  
(e) **(Emphasis)** On February 5 of Year 2, sell the entire holding for $58,200: **(i)** adjust to fair value at the sale date using **FV-NI**; **(ii)** record the sale, eliminating the associated Fair Value Adjustment balance. Show Dr = Cr on both entries.  
(f) Prepare a **holding-period income schedule** for unrealized G/L in net income and prove the cumulative amount equals sale proceeds minus amortized cost.  
(g) Briefly state **balance sheet classification**, income-statement placement of unrealized G/L (NI vs OCI), and SCF classification logic for trading debt.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) September 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (purchase between interest dates)**  
Accrued interest from July 1 to September 1 = **2 months**:  
\(60{,}000 \times 0.07 \times 2/12 = \$700\)  
Cash paid = \(60{,}000 + 700 = \$60{,}700\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Pinnacle Energy Bonds | 60,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 700 | |
| Cash | | 60,700 |
| *Purchase $60,000 face 7% Pinnacle bonds at par between interest dates; classify TS (FV-NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 60,000 + 700 = 60,700 = Cr 60,700. **Balanced.**

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Receipt of semiannual cash interest**  
Cash (full 6 months) = \(60{,}000 \times 0.07 \times 6/12 = \$2{,}100\)  
Interest revenue earned while held (4 months, Sep–Dec) = \(60{,}000 \times 0.07 \times 4/12 = \$1{,}400\)  
Reverse Interest Receivable \(700\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,100 | |
| Interest Receivable | | 700 |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,400 |
| *Collect full semiannual coupon; reverse purchase-date accrued interest; recognize 4 months earned* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,100 = Cr 700 + 1,400 = 2,100. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Subsequent measurement schedule and period-end FV-NI adjust**

| TS Investment | Amortized cost | Fair value YE | Unrealized holding G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pinnacle Energy bonds | $60,000 | $57,600 | $(2,400)$ |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | 0 |
| **Amount of increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$(2,400)$** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 2,400 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 2,400 |
| *Mark TS portfolio to FV through net income (FV-NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,400 = Cr 2,400. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = amortized cost − credit FVA = \(60{,}000 - 2{,}400 = \mathbf{\$57{,}600}\) = year-end fair value.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**

**Balance sheet (December 31, Year 1) — Current assets**  
Investment in trading securities …… **$57,600**  
*(Investment in TS $60,000 less Fair Value Adjustment—TS credit $2,400)*

**Income statement (year ended December 31, Year 1) — Other revenues (expenses)**  
Interest revenue ……………………………… **$1,400**  
Unrealized holding loss on trading securities … **$(2,400)**

**(e) February 5, Year 2 — Disposal under FV-NI (emphasis)**

**Sale-date FVA worksheet**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost | $60,000 |
| Fair value (sale proceeds) | 58,200 |
| Required FVA balance at sale date | $(1,800)$ credit |
| Existing FVA account balance | $(2,400)$ credit |
| **Amount of increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$600** (debit FVA) |

**(i) Sale-date fair value adjustment (FV-NI) — required before the sale JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 600 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 600 |
| *Roll FVA to sale-date fair value through net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr 600 = Cr 600. **Balanced.**  
FVA now credit $1,800; carrying amount = \(60{,}000 - 1{,}800 = \$58{,}200\).

**(ii) Record sale and eliminate associated FVA (no separate realized G/L—already in earnings via FV-NI)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 58,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 1,800 | |
| Investment in TS—Pinnacle Energy Bonds | | 60,000 |
| *Remove investment at amortized cost; clear FVA; cash = exit FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 58,200 + 1,800 = 60,000 = Cr 60,000. **Balanced.**

**(f) Holding-period income schedule (unrealized G/L in NI)**

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in NI |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 (period-end FVA) | $(2,400)$ |
| Year 2 (sale-date FVA) | 600 |
| **Net G/(L) over ownership period** | **$(1,800)$** |

Proof: Amortized cost $60,000 − sale proceeds $58,200 = **$1,800 loss**. ✓  
(Interest revenue is separate; holding G/L tracks only cost-to-exit fair value.)

**(g) Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
- **Balance sheet:** Trading securities are typically **current assets**; report at **fair value** (amortized cost ± FVA).  
- **Income statement:** Unrealized holding gains/losses on TS are in **net income** (other income), **not OCI**. Interest revenue is also in NI.  
- **SCF:** Cash outflows to acquire and inflows from sale of debt securities of other entities are generally **investing**; trading securities held specifically for short-term profits may be classified as **operating** depending on purpose (ASC 230).  
- Sale of a TS does **not** create a freestanding realized G/L entry after sale-date FV adjust—earnings already reflect FV changes as they occur.

**Key insight:** Under **debt FV-NI (TS)**, disposal is a two-step sequence: **(1)** roll FVA to sale-date fair value through **Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income**, then **(2)** debit Cash, credit Investment at **amortized cost**, and eliminate the **FVA** balance. Cumulative unrealized G/L in NI equals proceeds − amortized cost; no separate “realized gain on sale” is booked after the sale-date FV adjust.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — same TS FV-NI path (purchase → interest → YE FVA → **disposal**)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same trading-debt FV-NI path with all new amounts (including disposal sequence)  
**Scenario:**  
On **October 1 of Year 1**, **Crestview Holdings Inc.** purchased **at par** **$90,000** face of **Summit Logistics** **8%** bonds (bonds dated **July 1 of Year 1**; mature **June 30 of Year 9**). Cash interest is paid **semiannually on June 30 and December 31**. Classified as **TS (FV-NI)**. Year-end is **December 31**. Brokerage fees are zero. Beginning FVA—TS is zero.

| Date | Event / fair value |
|---|---|
| Oct 1, Year 1 | Purchase $90,000 face at par + accrued interest |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Collect coupon; fair value = **$93,150** |
| Jan 28, Year 2 | Sold for **$92,700** cash (clean exit price for the investment account) |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **October 1, Year 1** purchase (include accrued interest).  
(b) Record the **December 31, Year 1** interest receipt.  
(c) Prepare the **period-end FVA schedule** and record the **December 31, Year 1** adjusting entry.  
(d) State the December 31, Year 1 balance-sheet carrying amount and Year 1 income-statement effects.  
(e) **(Emphasis)** Record the **January 28, Year 2** sale: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust; (ii) sale eliminating FVA. Reconcile holding-period G/L.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) October 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition**  
Accrued interest (3 months, July 1–October 1) = \(90{,}000 \times 0.08 \times 3/12 = \$1{,}800\)  
Cash paid = \(90{,}000 + 1{,}800 = \$91{,}800\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Summit Logistics Bonds | 90,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 1,800 | |
| Cash | | 91,800 |
| *Purchase $90,000 face 8% Summit bonds at par between interest dates; TS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 90,000 + 1,800 = 91,800 = Cr 91,800. **Balanced.**

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest receipt**  
Cash (6 months) = \(90{,}000 \times 0.08 \times 6/12 = \$3{,}600\)  
Interest revenue earned (3 months, Oct–Dec) = \(90{,}000 \times 0.08 \times 3/12 = \$1{,}800\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,600 | |
| Interest Receivable | | 1,800 |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,800 |
| *Full coupon cash; reverse accrued; 3 months interest revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,600 = Cr 1,800 + 1,800 = 3,600. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end FVA schedule and adjusting JE**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost | $90,000 |
| Fair value | 93,150 |
| Required FVA (debit) | 3,150 |
| Existing FVA | 0 |
| **Increase to FVA needed** | **$3,150** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 3,150 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 3,150 |
| *FV-NI period-end gain through net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,150 = Cr 3,150. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = \(90{,}000 + 3{,}150 = \mathbf{\$93{,}150}\).

**(d) Presentation**  
**BS (current assets):** Investment in trading securities **$93,150**  
**IS other income:** Interest revenue **$1,800**; Unrealized holding gain on trading securities **$3,150**

**(e) January 28, Year 2 — Disposal under FV-NI (emphasis)**

Required FVA at sale = \(92{,}700 - 90{,}000 = \$2{,}700\) **debit**  
Existing FVA = \(3{,}150\) **debit**  
**Decrease to FVA needed** = **$(450)**

**(i) Sale-date fair value adjustment**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 450 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 450 |
| *Reduce FVA from $3,150 debit to required $2,700 debit* | | |

**Check:** Dr 450 = Cr 450. **Balanced.**  
FVA now debit $2,700; carrying amount = \(90{,}000 + 2{,}700 = \$92{,}700\).

**(ii) Record sale and eliminate FVA**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 92,700 | |
| Investment in TS—Summit Logistics Bonds | | 90,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 2,700 |
| *Remove AC and debit FVA; cash = exit FV; no separate sale G/L* | | |

**Check:** Dr 92,700 = Cr 90,000 + 2,700 = 92,700. **Balanced.**

**Holding-period G/L reconciliation:**  
Unrealized gain in Year 1 income …… $3,150  
Unrealized loss in Year 2 income …… (450)  
**Net gain over ownership period** …… **$2,700**  
= Proceeds $92,700 − amortized cost $90,000. ✓

**Key insight:** Number-variant twin of Q1—same disposal sequence (sale-date FV-NI adjust, then clear Investment and FVA). When sale FV is **below** prior YE FV but still **above** cost, Year 2 books a partial **unrealized loss** that trims (but does not reverse all of) the Year 1 unrealized gain.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — multi-period FVA true-ups + **disposal emphasis**; fair value option note
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** subsequent_measurement_schedule; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure (incl. fair value option parallel)  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Amberfield Capital LLC** purchased **at par** **$100,000** face of **Cobalt Industrial** **5%** bonds. Cash interest is paid **annually each December 31**. Bonds mature **December 31 of Year 6**. Amberfield classifies the bonds as **trading securities (TS)** measured at **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA—TS is zero. No brokerage fees.

Fair values and activity:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value of Cobalt bonds = **$97,000** (after recording cash interest) |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Fair value = **$99,500** (after recording cash interest) |
| Mar 15, Year 3 | Entire position sold for **$101,000** cash (clean exit price) |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
(b) Record **December 31, Year 1** cash interest and the **period-end FV-NI adjusting JE**.  
(c) Prepare a **subsequent measurement / FVA rollforward schedule** for Dec 31 Y1 and Dec 31 Y2 (amortized cost, FV, required FVA, existing FVA, adjusting amount). Record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE**.  
(d) **(Emphasis)** Record the **March 15, Year 3 disposal package**: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust; (ii) sale eliminating FVA. Reconcile cumulative unrealized G/L in NI to proceeds − cost.  
(e) **Classification / fair value option:** How would the **same period-end and sale-date FVA mechanics** apply if Amberfield had classified the bonds as **HTM** but **elected the fair value option** on acquisition? What disclosure is required for the election? Where do unrealized G/L still appear?

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition at par**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Cobalt Industrial Bonds | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 100,000 |
| *Purchase $100,000 face 5% Cobalt bonds at par; classify TS (FV-NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000. **Balanced.**  
Amortized cost remains **$100,000** each year (purchased at par; ignore any premium/discount amort).

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest and period-end FVA**

Cash coupon = \(100{,}000 \times 0.05 = \$5{,}000\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 5,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 5,000 |
| *Collect annual coupon on Cobalt bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000. **Balanced.**

Required FVA YE Y1 = \(97{,}000 - 100{,}000 = \$(3{,}000)\) credit; existing FVA = 0.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 3,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 3,000 |
| *Year 1 FV-NI loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,000. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount Y1 = \(100{,}000 - 3{,}000 = \$97{,}000\).

**(c) Subsequent measurement / FVA rollforward schedule**

| Date | Amortized cost | Fair value | Required FVA (Dr/(Cr)) | Existing FVA (Dr/(Cr)) | Adjusting amount Dr/(Cr) to FVA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Dec 31, Y2 | 100,000 | 99,500 | $(500)$ | $(3,000)$ | **$2,500** |

December 31, Year 2 cash interest (same as Y1):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 5,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 5,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000. **Balanced.**

December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE (true-up only the **change**):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 2,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,500 |
| *Reduce credit FVA from $3,000 to required $500* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,500. **Balanced.**  
Ending FVA credit = \(3{,}000 - 2{,}500 = \$500\); carrying amount = \(100{,}000 - 500 = \$99{,}500\).

**(d) March 15, Year 3 — Disposal package (emphasis)**

**Sale-date FVA worksheet**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost | $100,000 |
| Fair value (sale proceeds) | 101,000 |
| Required FVA at sale date | $1,000 **debit** |
| Existing FVA | $500 **credit** |
| **Amount of increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$1,500** (debit FVA) |

**(i) Sale-date fair value adjustment (FV-NI)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 1,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 1,500 |
| *Flip FVA from $500 credit to $1,000 debit so carrying = $101,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,500. **Balanced.**  
FVA after entry: \(1{,}500 - 500 = \$1{,}000\) debit; carrying = \(100{,}000 + 1{,}000 = \$101{,}000\).

**(ii) Record sale and eliminate FVA**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 101,000 | |
| Investment in TS—Cobalt Industrial Bonds | | 100,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 1,000 |
| *Remove AC and debit FVA; cash = exit FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 101,000 = Cr 100,000 + 1,000 = 101,000. **Balanced.**

**Holding-period unrealized G/L in NI:**

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in NI |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | $(3,000)$ |
| Year 2 | 2,500 |
| Year 3 (sale-date) | 1,500 |
| **Cumulative** | **$1,000** |

Proof: Proceeds $101,000 − amortized cost $100,000 = **$1,000 gain**. ✓

**(e) Classification / fair value option**  
If Amberfield had classified the bonds as **HTM** but **elected the fair value option** on the **acquisition date** (ASC 825), the **same FV-NI FVA mechanics** apply: period-end and sale-date adjustments use Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option (or equivalent) with Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income**. The election is generally **irrevocable**, applied **instrument-by-instrument**, and must be disclosed (ASC 825-10-50-28). Unrealized G/L still hit **net income**, not OCI. TS is already FV-NI, so the fair value option is not needed for TS, but accounting treatment is **similar**. Trading securities remain typically **current**; HTM under FVO classification (current vs noncurrent) follows management’s expected holding period / ability, but measurement is still fair value through NI.

**Key insight:** Multi-period FV-NI always adjusts FVA for the **change** (required − existing). Disposal still requires a **sale-date true-up first**—even if FV has recovered from a prior unrealized loss into an unrealized gain—so that the sale JE clears Investment and FVA with **no freestanding realized G/L**.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of unrealized holding G/L on trading debt (FV-NI)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — unrealized holding G/L on trading debt securities reported in net income  
**Question:**  
**Driftwood Securities LP** holds corporate bonds classified as **trading securities**. At year-end, fair value is below amortized cost. How should the year-end fair-value change be reported?

- A) Unrealized holding loss in **other comprehensive income** (AOCI), not net income  
- B) Unrealized holding loss in **net income** (other income); investment reported at fair value  
- C) No entry until sale; bonds remain at amortized cost like HTM  
- D) Debit Allowance for Credit Losses and credit Investment (CECL model applies the same as HTM)

**Answer:** **B.** Trading debt securities are measured at **FV-NI**: unrealized holding gains and losses are included in **earnings** (ASC 320-10-35-1a). The investment is reported at fair value (amortized cost ± FVA). Option A describes **AFS (FV-OCI)**. Option C describes pure **HTM amortized cost**. Option D confuses **credit-loss** models for HTM/AFS with routine TS fair-value accounting.

### Q5 — MC — Sale sequence under FV-NI (disposal emphasis)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE — sale-date FV adjust then eliminate FVA (no separate sale G/L)  
**Question:**  
**Pinecrest Advisors** holds trading bonds with amortized cost **$40,000**, existing Fair Value Adjustment—TS **credit balance $1,200**, and sells the bonds for **$39,400** cash. Immediately before the sale entry, what is the correct **two-step** sequence under LO 14-2 (FV-NI with fair value adjustments at sale date)?

- A) Debit Cash $39,400, credit Investment $39,400; leave FVA untouched  
- B) Debit Cash $39,400, debit Loss on Sale $600, credit Investment $40,000; reverse the entire $1,200 FVA to OCI  
- C) First adjust FVA so required FVA = $(600)$ credit (debit FVA $600, credit Unrealized G/L—Income $600); then debit Cash $39,400, debit FVA $600, credit Investment $40,000  
- D) First reverse FVA $1,200 to Unrealized G/L—OCI; then debit Cash $39,400, debit Loss $600, credit Investment $40,000

**Answer:** **C.** Required FVA at sale = \(39{,}400 - 40{,}000 = \$(600)\) credit. Existing FVA = $(1,200)$ credit → **increase** FVA by **$600** (debit FVA / credit Unrealized G/L—**Income**). Then record the sale by debiting Cash and the remaining FVA and crediting Investment at amortized cost. No freestanding “Loss on Sale” after the sale-date FV adjust; changes hit NI as they occur. OCI (A/B/D mix-ups) is the AFS path, not TS FV-NI.
