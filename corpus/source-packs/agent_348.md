# Agent 348 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-1

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Identify and classify intangible items  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Classification:** finite-life intangible asset vs indefinite-life intangible asset (other than goodwill) vs goodwill vs expense (acquired vs internally generated)
- **Useful life factors:** legal/contractual limits, expected use, obsolescence, renewals; finite → amortize; indefinite → do not amortize
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize acquired intangibles and **external** registration/successful defense costs; expense internal R&D, internal brand-building, and unsuccessful defense
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-period straight-line amortization of finite-life intangibles; carrying-value roll-forward; mid-life successful defense reamortized over remaining useful life
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** amortization expense with credit to the intangible (or Accumulated Amortization); multi-year period-end AJEs
- **Disposal / maturity or settlement JE:** update amortization to disposal date; remove carrying amount; gain or loss vs proceeds; fully amortized license at end of contractual life
- **Presentation:** goodwill separate from other intangibles; amortizing vs non-amortizing
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all amounts, lives, and dates changed

### Q1 — CORE — Classify portfolio; initial JEs; multi-year amortization; period-end AJEs (emphasis); disposal

**Concept:** Classify intangible items (finite / indefinite / goodwill / expense); initial recognition JEs; subsequent measurement schedule with mid-life patent defense; multi-year period-end adjusting JEs; disposal (derecognition) JE

### Q4 — MC — Classification of intangible items
**LO:** LO 13-1  
**Concept:** Finite vs indefinite vs goodwill vs expense; crypto asset classification when ASC 350-60 criteria are met  

**Question 1:**  
On March 1, **Redline Aero Parts** pays **$6,500** cash to **outside counsel** to register a **trademark** that management expects to renew for successive 10-year periods with **no foreseeable limit** on its contribution to cash flows. Internally, Redline also incurred **$41,000** of designer salaries creating the mark. How should Redline classify these costs at initial recognition?

- A) Capitalize **$47,500** as a finite-life intangible asset  
- B) Capitalize **$6,500** as an indefinite-life intangible asset; expense **$41,000**  
- C) Capitalize **$41,000** as goodwill; expense **$6,500**  
- D) Expense the entire **$47,500** because the trademark was developed internally  

**Answer:** **B.** External legal/filing fees to establish or register an identifiable intangible are **capitalized** even when the underlying mark is internally developed. Because renewals are expected with no foreseeable limit, the trademark is an **indefinite-life** intangible (not amortized). Internal designer salaries are **internally generated** costs and are **expensed**. Goodwill is recognized only as a residual in a **business combination**, not from internal brand-building.

**Question 2:**  
**Northfork Auto Group**, a large vehicle retailer that occasionally accepts digital assets as payment, purchases **three fungible cryptocurrency units** (meeting ASC 350-60 criteria: intangible definition, no enforceable claim on underlying goods/services, blockchain-based, cryptography-secured, fungible, not issued by Northfork or related parties) for **$27,000** including purchase fees, intending to hold them as an investment. How should the units be classified?

- A) Finite-life intangible asset amortized over 18 months  
- B) Inventory (always required for crypto holdings)  
- C) Crypto asset classified as an **intangible asset**, presented **separately** from other intangibles on the balance sheet  
- D) Goodwill, because crypto has no physical substance and an indefinite economic life  

**Answer:** **C.** When the ASC 350-60 criteria are met, crypto assets are classified as **intangible assets** and are stated **separately** from other intangibles. They are not goodwill (goodwill arises only in a business combination residual). Inventory treatment may apply in some specialized cases (e.g., certain RECs in other guidance), but for this investment holding meeting 350-60, separate intangible presentation is required. Amortization life is not automatically 18 months (that example in the chapter relates to certain environmental credits, not crypto).
