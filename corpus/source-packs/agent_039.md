# Agent 39 — CORE DEMO — LO 8-1

**Chapter:** 8  
**LO title:** Classify cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and compensating balances  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Cash: currency, demand deposits, savings, petty cash, cashier’s/certified checks, money orders
- Cash equivalents: short-term, highly liquid investments with **original maturity to the holder of 3 months or less**; policy election
- Restricted cash (legal or voluntary): report **separately** from cash; **current** if used within operating cycle, else **noncurrent**
- Compensating balances: **legally restricted** → separate current/noncurrent asset; **not legally restricted** → may remain in cash with **note disclosure**
- Items **not** cash: travel advances (prepaid/other current), postdated checks (AR), NSF checks (AR), postage, CDs/T-bills with original maturity > 3 months (STI), bond sinking funds (restricted noncurrent), bank overdraft with no offsetting balance at same bank (current liability)
- Initial recognition JEs: borrow with compensating balance reclass; purchase cash equivalents; transfer to restricted funds
- Subsequent classification schedule and period-end reclassifying adjustments
- Maturity/settlement: cash-equivalent maturity; release of compensating balance when related debt is paid

### Q1 — CORE — Loan compensating balance, cash-equivalent life cycle, restricted funds, and year-end classification (initial recognition emphasis)
**LO:** LO 8-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash; Dec 31 classification schedule and presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Peak Manufacturing Co.** has a **December 31** year-end. Company policy: short-term, highly liquid investments with **original maturities of three months or less** (to Summit) that present insignificant interest-rate risk are reported as **cash equivalents**.

**Transactions during Year 1:**

1. **July 1, Year 1:** Borrowed **$300,000** from First Prairie Bank on a **one-year, 8%** note payable. The note **legally requires** a **12%** compensating balance (**$36,000**) to remain on deposit at First Prairie during the loan term. Summit deposited the full proceeds to its First Prairie checking account and immediately reclassified the legally restricted amount.

2. **November 1, Year 1:** Purchased **Atlas Power commercial paper** for **$54,000** cash. Original term to Summit: **60 days**. The paper matures on **December 31, Year 1** for **$54,810** (discount accretion / interest **$810**). Summit classifies qualifying commercial paper as a cash equivalent.

3. **December 1, Year 1:** Purchased a **U.S. Treasury bill** for **$24,900** cash when the bill had **two months** remaining until maturity (matures January 31, Year 2; face **$25,000**). Summit treats the T-bill as a cash equivalent.

4. **December 20, Year 1:** Transferred **$14,000** from general cash to a **restricted payroll fund** bank account (payroll will be paid January 5, Year 2).

5. **December 22, Year 1:** Deposited **$60,000** with a trustee for a **bond sinking fund**. Related bonds mature in **five years**.

**Additional items on hand / on books at December 31, Year 1** (after the transactions above, and after maturity of the commercial paper):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Checking—First Prairie Bank (balance **includes** the $36,000 legally restricted compensating balance; restricted amount is still in this total pending presentation split) | $71,200 |
| Checking—Heartland Credit Union (includes an **NSF check of $650** from a customer that was returned and has **not** yet been reclassified) | $28,400 |
| Savings account | $12,000 |
| Petty cash | $600 |
| Cashier’s check payable to Summit (dated Dec 29, not yet deposited) | $1,800 |
| Cash equivalents—U.S. T-bill (Dec 1 purchase, still held) | $24,900 |
| Money market fund (highly liquid; original maturity to Summit 1 month; low credit risk) | $33,000 |
| Restricted cash—Payroll fund (already segregated Dec 20) | $14,000 |
| Restricted cash—Bond sinking fund (already segregated Dec 22) | $60,000 |
| T-bill purchased **June 1, Year 1** when it had **seven months** remaining to maturity; cost still on books (now two months left) | $16,000 |
| Travel advance to employee for January sales trip | $2,200 |
| Customer check dated **January 20, Year 2** (postdated) | $1,100 |
| Overdraft at Lakeside Bank (Summit has **no** other account at Lakeside) | $3,500 |

**Assume:** The July 1 reclass of the compensating balance was **reversed for bookkeeping simplicity** into the First Prairie checking total shown above (i.e., the full $71,200 sits in Cash—First Prairie and must be split for balance-sheet presentation). Payroll fund and sinking fund remain in separate restricted-cash accounts.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **July 1** borrowing entry and the **initial recognition** reclassification of the legally restricted compensating balance.  
(b) Record the **November 1** purchase of commercial paper as a cash equivalent.  
(c) Record the **December 31** maturity/settlement of the commercial paper (cash proceeds and interest).  
(d) Record the **December 1** purchase of the cash-equivalent T-bill.  
(e) Record the **December 20** payroll-fund transfer and the **December 22** bond-sinking-fund deposit.  
(f) Prepare a **December 31 classification schedule** assigning every item above to: Cash; Cash equivalents; Restricted cash—current; Restricted cash—noncurrent; Other current asset (specify); Short-term investment; Accounts receivable; or Current liability. Show the **period-end adjusting/reclassifying** entry for the NSF check.  
(g) Report balance-sheet totals for: **Cash**; **Cash equivalents** (or Cash and cash equivalents combined); **Restricted cash—current**; **Restricted cash—noncurrent**; and the **current liability** from the overdraft.  
(h) Briefly state note-disclosure treatment if the compensating balance had **not** been legally restricted.

**Answer key:**

**(a) July 1, Year 1 — Borrow and reclass legally restricted compensating balance**

Compensating balance = \(300{,}000 \times 0.12 = \mathbf{\$36{,}000}\).

```
Dr Cash ........................................... 300,000
   Cr Notes Payable ....................................... 300,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 300,000)

```
Dr Restricted Cash—Compensating Balance .......... 36,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 36,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 36,000)

**(b) November 1, Year 1 — Purchase commercial paper (cash equivalent)**

```
Dr Cash Equivalents—Commercial Paper ............. 54,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 54,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 54,000)

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Maturity/settlement of commercial paper**

Interest income = \(54{,}810 - 54{,}000 = \mathbf{\$810}\).

```
Dr Cash ........................................... 54,810
   Cr Cash Equivalents—Commercial Paper ................... 54,000
   Cr Interest Revenue ....................................... 810
```
(Dr 54,810 = Cr 54,810)

**(d) December 1, Year 1 — Purchase cash-equivalent T-bill**

Original maturity to Summit = **2 months ≤ 3 months** → cash equivalent.

```
Dr Cash Equivalents—U.S. Treasury Bill ........... 24,900
   Cr Cash ................................................ 24,900
```
(Dr = Cr = 24,900)

**(e) December 20 and December 22 — Restricted cash transfers**

```
Dr Restricted Cash—Payroll Fund .................. 14,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 14,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 14,000)

```
Dr Restricted Cash—Bond Sinking Fund ............. 60,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 60,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 60,000)

**(f) December 31, Year 1 — Classification schedule and NSF reclass**

**Period-end reclassifying entry — NSF check** (still in Heartland cash):

```

   Cr Cash—Heartland Credit Union ......................... 650
```
(Dr = Cr = 650)

**Classification schedule** (after NSF reclass):

| Item | Amount | Classification |
|---|---:|---|
| First Prairie checking, unrestricted (\(71{,}200 - 36{,}000\)) | $35,200 | **Cash** |
| First Prairie legally restricted compensating balance (ST loan) | $36,000 | **Restricted cash—current** (Other current asset) |
| Heartland checking after NSF (\(28{,}400 - 650\)) | $27,750 | **Cash** |
| Savings account | $12,000 | **Cash** |
| Petty cash | $600 | **Cash** |
| Cashier’s check on hand | $1,800 | **Cash** |
| U.S. T-bill (orig. maturity to Summit 2 mo.) | $24,900 | **Cash equivalent** |
| Money market fund (qualifying) | $33,000 | **Cash equivalent** |
| Restricted payroll fund (used Jan 5) | $14,000 | **Restricted cash—current** |
| Bond sinking fund (bonds due in 5 years) | $60,000 | **Restricted cash—noncurrent** |
| T-bill purchased June 1 (orig. maturity 7 mo. > 3 mo.) | $16,000 | **Short-term investment** (not cash equivalent) |
| Travel advance | $2,200 | **Other current asset** (prepaid/receivable from employee) |
| Postdated customer check | $1,100 | **Accounts receivable** |
| NSF check | $650 | **Accounts receivable** |
| Lakeside Bank overdraft (no offsetting balance at same bank) | $3,500 | **Current liability** (accounts payable / bank overdraft) |

**(g) Balance-sheet presentation totals**

| Caption | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| **Cash** | \(35{,}200 + 27{,}750 + 12{,}000 + 600 + 1{,}800\) | **$77,350** |
| **Cash equivalents** | \(24{,}900 + 33{,}000\) | **$57,900** |
| **Cash and cash equivalents** (if combined) | \(77{,}350 + 57{,}900\) | **$135,250** |
| **Restricted cash—current** | Compensating balance \(36{,}000\) + payroll fund \(14{,}000\) | **$50,000** |
| **Restricted cash—noncurrent** | Bond sinking fund | **$60,000** |
| **Bank overdraft (current liability)** | Lakeside | **$3,500** |

Also reported (not cash): short-term investment **$16,000**; AR (NSF + postdated) **$1,750**; travel advance **$2,200**.

**(h) Non-legally-restricted compensating balance**  
If the compensating balance were **not** legally restricted, Summit would report the full First Prairie balance in **Cash** and **disclose** the arrangement and amount in the notes (ASC/SEC compensating-balance guidance). Legally restricted balances are **separated** from cash on the face of the balance sheet.

**Key insight:** Cash and cash equivalents include only unrestricted demand funds and **qualifying** short-term investments (**original** maturity to the holder ≤ 3 months). Legally restricted compensating balances and other restricted cash are **carved out** and classified current vs noncurrent based on the related purpose/debt. Maturity of a cash equivalent simply returns cash (plus any discount interest).

### Q3 — CORE — Period-end reclassifying adjustments and settlement of compensating balance
**LO:** LO 8-1  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting/reclassifying JEs for cash-related misclassifications; settlement release of restricted compensating balance when short-term note is paid  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborview Plastics Inc.** is preparing December 31, Year 3 financial statements. The unadjusted trial balance shows a single **Cash** account of **$186,400**. Investigation reveals the following items **still included in Cash** (unless noted):

| Ref | Item | Amount in Cash (or related) | Fact |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Postage stamps in cash drawer | $120 | Not cash |
| 2 | Postdated customer checks (dated Jan 15, Year 4) | $2,800 | Not available for deposit |
| 3 | NSF checks returned Dec 28; no entry yet | $1,450 | Included in Cash |
| 4 | Travel advances to sales staff | $3,600 | Included in Cash |
| 5 | Legally restricted compensating balance against **$200,000** short-term note at City Bank (10% requirement) | $20,000 | Still in Cash—City Bank subtotal |
| 6 | Cash accumulated in **bond sinking fund** (bonds due Year 8) | $48,000 | Included in Cash |
| 7 | U.S. T-bill purchased Dec 10 when **50 days** remained to maturity; cost | $15,000 | Correctly could be cash equivalent; currently in **Short-term investments** and should be reclassed to cash equivalents per policy |
| 8 | Six-month CD purchased Oct 1 (original maturity 6 months); cost | $25,000 | Incorrectly left in Cash; not a cash equivalent |
| 9 | Bank overdraft at Westside Bank (no other Westside accounts); books show cash **credit** balance | $(4,200) | Currently netted inside the $186,400 Cash total as a reduction |

**Additional settlement facts (Year 4):**  
On **April 1, Year 4**, Harborview pays the **$200,000** City Bank note in full plus accrued interest of **$4,500**. The legal compensating-balance restriction is **released** the same day. Immediately before payment, Restricted Cash—Compensating Balance still equals **$20,000** (assume Q3 period-end reclass was booked on Dec 31, Year 3).

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare **all December 31, Year 3 period-end reclassifying/adjusting journal entries** needed so that Cash, Cash Equivalents, Restricted Cash, and related accounts are correctly stated. (May combine logical groupings.)  
(b) Compute **correct Cash** and **Cash Equivalents** balances after the entries in (a). Start from unadjusted Cash **$186,400**.  
(c) On **April 1, Year 4**, record (i) payment of the note and interest and (ii) **release/settlement** of the compensating-balance restriction (reclass restricted cash back to cash).  
(d) State how the **bond sinking fund** and the **six-month CD** should be presented on the Dec 31, Year 3 balance sheet.

**Answer key:**

**(a) December 31, Year 3 — Period-end reclassifying entries**

**(1) Postage stamps → supplies/prepaid**

```
Dr Supplies (or Prepaid Postage) .................... 120
   Cr Cash ................................................... 120
```
(Dr = Cr = 120)

**(2) Postdated checks → AR**

```
Dr Accounts Receivable ............................ 2,800
   Cr Cash ................................................. 2,800
```
(Dr = Cr = 2,800)

**(3) NSF checks → AR**

```
Dr Accounts Receivable ............................ 1,450
   Cr Cash ................................................. 1,450
```
(Dr = Cr = 1,450)

**(4) Travel advances → other current asset**

```
Dr Travel Advances (Other Current Asset) .......... 3,600
   Cr Cash ................................................. 3,600
```
(Dr = Cr = 3,600)

**(5) Legally restricted compensating balance → restricted cash (current)**

```
Dr Restricted Cash—Compensating Balance .......... 20,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 20,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 20,000)

**(6) Bond sinking fund → restricted cash noncurrent**

```
Dr Restricted Cash—Bond Sinking Fund ............. 48,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 48,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 48,000)

**(7) Qualifying T-bill: reclass from STI to cash equivalent**

```
Dr Cash Equivalents—U.S. Treasury Bill ........... 15,000
   Cr Short-Term Investments .............................. 15,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 15,000)

**(8) Six-month CD: reclass out of cash to short-term investment**

```
Dr Short-Term Investments—CD ..................... 25,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 25,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 25,000)

**(9) Bank overdraft: remove netting; report as current liability**  
Unadjusted Cash **already nets** the $4,200 overdraft (cash is $4,200 lower than gross positive balances). To present gross cash and a liability:

Gross positive cash before overdraft netting = unadjusted cash + overdraft amount = \(186{,}400 + 4{,}200 = 190{,}600\) of positive balances embedded; after other removals we still need a liability of $4,200.

Clean approach: reverse the netting by restoring cash and recording the liability:

```
Dr Cash ............................................ 4,200
   Cr Bank Overdraft Payable (or Accounts Payable) ........ 4,200
```
(Dr = Cr = 4,200)

**(b) Correct Cash and Cash Equivalents**

Start: unadjusted Cash **$186,400**

| Adjustment | Effect on Cash |
|---|---:|
| (1) Postage | (120) |
| (2) Postdated | (2,800) |
| (3) NSF | (1,450) |
| (4) Travel advances | (3,600) |
| (5) Compensating balance | (20,000) |
| (6) Bond sinking fund | (48,000) |
| (8) Six-month CD | (25,000) |
| (9) Gross-up overdraft | +4,200 |
| **Correct Cash** | **$89,630** |

Check: \(186{,}400 - 120 - 2{,}800 - 1{,}450 - 3{,}600 - 20{,}000 - 48{,}000 - 25{,}000 + 4{,}200 = \mathbf{\$89{,}630}\).

**Cash equivalents** = reclassified T-bill = **$15,000**.

**Cash and cash equivalents** = \(89{,}630 + 15{,}000 = \mathbf{\$104{,}630}\).

**(c) April 1, Year 4 — Note settlement and release of compensating balance**

```
Dr Notes Payable ................................. 200,000
Dr Interest Expense (or Interest Payable) .......... 4,500
   Cr Cash ............................................... 204,500
```
(Dr 204,500 = Cr 204,500)

```
Dr Cash ........................................... 20,000
   Cr Restricted Cash—Compensating Balance ............... 20,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 20,000)

(Order may be either way: restriction released upon payoff; net cash out for principal/interest is $204,500 while $20,000 of restricted cash becomes unrestricted.)

**(d) Presentation**  
- **Bond sinking fund $48,000:** Restricted cash—**noncurrent** asset (not part of cash and cash equivalents).  
- **Six-month CD $25,000:** **Short-term investment** (original maturity > 3 months; not a cash equivalent), current asset.

**Key insight:** Period-end work for LO 8-1 is largely **reclassification**—cash is a residual of unrestricted demand funds after carving out restrictions, noncash items, and nonqualifying investments. When restricted compensating balances are tied to debt that is **settled**, the restriction is released and cash is restored to the general cash caption.

### Q4 — MC — Cash and cash equivalents total
**LO:** LO 8-1  
**Concept:** Compute cash and cash equivalents from mixed cash-related balances  
**Question:**  
Dover-style balances for **Pinecrest Labs Inc.** on December 31, Year 1:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash deposit in escrow fund (legal restriction; plant purchase in 18 months) | $45,000 |
| Cash deposit in checking account | $82,000 |
| Money market fund (highly liquid; original maturity 2 months; low risk) | $25,000 |
| T-bill matures Feb 15, Year 2; purchased **Oct 15, Year 1** (original maturity to Pinecrest = 4 months) | $30,000 |
| T-bill matures Feb 15, Year 2; purchased **Dec 15, Year 1** (original maturity to Pinecrest = 2 months) | $15,000 |
| Legally restricted compensating balance against short-term loan (included in the $82,000 checking balance) | $8,000 |

What amount should Pinecrest report as **cash and cash equivalents** on December 31, Year 1?

- A) $107,000  
- B) $114,000  
- C) $122,000  
- D) $152,000  

**Answer:** **B. $114,000.**  
Cash = checking unrestricted \(82{,}000 - 8{,}000 = 74{,}000\).  
Cash equivalents = money market \(25{,}000\) + Dec 15 T-bill \(15{,}000\) = \(40{,}000\).  
Total cash and cash equivalents = \(74{,}000 + 40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$114{,}000}\).  

Exclude: escrow (restricted noncurrent), Oct 15 T-bill (original maturity **4 months > 3 months** → short-term investment), and the $8,000 compensating balance (restricted cash—current, separate from cash).

### Q5 — MC — Compensating balance against long-term debt
**LO:** LO 8-1  
**Concept:** Classification of a legally restricted compensating balance held against long-term debt  
**Question:**  
On December 31, **Elmwood Distributors** maintains a **$40,000** deposit at Lender Bank that is **legally restricted** as a compensating balance under a long-term loan agreement (principal due in four years). How should Elmwood report the $40,000?

- A) Cash and cash equivalents (current asset)  
- B) Other current asset—restricted cash  
- C) Noncurrent asset—restricted cash (or investment)  
- D) Reduction of the long-term note payable (contra-liability)  

**Answer:** **C.** Legally restricted compensating balances are reported **separately from cash**. Because the related debt is **long-term** and the funds are not expected to be available for current operations, the restricted deposit is a **noncurrent** asset. It is not netted against the note payable.
