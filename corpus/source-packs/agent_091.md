# Agent 91 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-6

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Account for impairment of indefinite life intangible assets and goodwill  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (impairment):** record Impairment Loss and write down the indefinite-life intangible or Goodwill when the quantitative test fails
- Qualitative assessment (optional): more-likely-than-not trigger; management may **bypass** and go straight to quantitative
- Quantitative test — **indefinite-life intangible (other than goodwill):** fair value vs carrying amount; loss = excess of CA over FV; **no recoverability (undiscounted CF) step** (contrast LO 13-3 finite-life)
- Quantitative test — **goodwill:** compare fair value of the **reporting unit (RU)** to carrying amount of the RU **including goodwill**; impairment = excess, **capped at goodwill allocated to that RU**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** new carrying amount = post-impairment basis; **no amortization**; **no subsequent recovery** of prior impairment under US GAAP
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** annual (or interim if indicators) impairment test and related write-down
- **Disposal / derecognition JE:** remove remaining carrying amount of indefinite-life intangible; gain/loss = proceeds − CA
- Classification / presentation: impairment loss in income from continuing operations; goodwill as separate BS line; aggregate goodwill and impairment disclosures

### Q1 — CORE — Trade-name and goodwill impairment (emphasis: impairment recognition JEs)
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of impairment losses on indefinite-life trade name and reporting-unit goodwill; post-impairment carrying schedule; disposal of trade name; presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Consumer Products Inc.** (calendar year-end, **public** company) holds two items tested for impairment at **December 31, Year 4**. Management elects to **bypass** the qualitative assessment and proceeds directly to the **quantitative** tests. No impairment losses have been recorded on these assets in prior years.

| Item | Facts at Dec 31, Year 4 |
|---|---|
| Trade name **“AuroraGlow”** (indefinite life; not amortized) | Carrying amount **$380,000**. Estimated fair value **$212,000**. |
| **Packaged Foods** reporting unit (includes goodwill) | Carrying amount of RU **including goodwill** **$45,000,000**. Of that amount, **goodwill allocated to the RU = $3,200,000**. Fair value of the RU (appraisal / market–income approach) **$42,600,000**. Other (non-goodwill) assets of the RU have already been tested for impairment under ASC 360; **no** additional write-downs are required before the goodwill test. |

On **March 15, Year 5**, Northline sells the AuroraGlow trade name (after the Year-4 impairment has been recorded) for **$195,000 cash**. Selling costs are negligible.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **Year-4 impairment loss** (if any) on the trade name. Prepare the **December 31, Year 4** journal entry to **recognize** the impairment (or state none).  
b. Compute the **Year-4 goodwill impairment loss** (if any) for the Packaged Foods RU. Prepare the **December 31, Year 4** journal entry to **recognize** the impairment (or state none). Explain why the loss is (or is not) limited.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent-measurement carrying-amount schedule** for (i) the trade name and (ii) Packaged Foods goodwill from **beginning of Year 4** through **immediately after** the Year-4 impairment entries (assume beginning CA equaled the Dec 31 pre-impairment amounts above; no other activity in Year 4).  
d. Prepare the **March 15, Year 5 disposal** journal entry for the trade name.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** (i) On which financial statement and in what broad category is the Year-4 impairment loss reported? (ii) How should the **aggregate carrying amount of goodwill** appear on the balance sheet? (iii) May Northline reverse the Year-4 goodwill impairment if the RU recovers in Year 5?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Trade name — quantitative impairment (indefinite life)**

\[
\text{Impairment loss} = \text{Carrying amount} - \text{Fair value} = \$380{,}000 - \$212{,}000 = \$168{,}000
\]

Because FV **\<** CA, the asset is impaired. (For indefinite-life intangibles other than goodwill there is **no** undiscounted-cash-flow recoverability step.)

*December 31, Year 4 — Recognize impairment loss on trade name (initial recognition of impairment)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Trade Name (AuroraGlow) | 168,000 | |
| Trade Name—AuroraGlow | | 168,000 |
| *Write indefinite-life trade name down to fair value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 168,000 = Cr 168,000. **Balanced.**  
New carrying amount = **$212,000**.

**b. Goodwill — quantitative RU test**

\[
\text{RU excess of CA over FV} = \$45{,}000{,}000 - \$42{,}600{,}000 = \$2{,}400{,}000
\]

Goodwill allocated to RU = **$3,200,000**.  
Impairment loss = **min(\$2,400,000, \$3,200,000) = \$2,400,000**  
(The measured excess is **less than** allocated goodwill, so the **full** \$2,400,000 is recognized; remaining goodwill = \$3,200,000 − \$2,400,000 = **\$800,000**.)

*December 31, Year 4 — Recognize goodwill impairment (initial recognition of impairment)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Goodwill | 2,400,000 | |
| Goodwill | | 2,400,000 |
| *Write down goodwill; loss limited to RU excess, not exceeding allocated goodwill* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,400,000 = Cr 2,400,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent-measurement / carrying-amount schedule (Year 4)**

| Asset | Beginning CA (1/1/Y4) | Amortization | Impairment loss | Ending CA (12/31/Y4) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Trade name—AuroraGlow | 380,000 | 0 | 168,000 | **212,000** |
| Goodwill—Packaged Foods RU | 3,200,000 | 0 | 2,400,000 | **800,000** |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
- Trade name: 380,000 − 168,000 = **212,000** (= FV).  
- Goodwill: 3,200,000 − 2,400,000 = **800,000**.  
Indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill are **not amortized** (public company; no PCC election).

**d. Disposal of trade name (Year 5)**

Carrying amount at disposal = **$212,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$195,000**  
Loss on disposal = 212,000 − 195,000 = **$17,000**

*March 15, Year 5 — Dispose of trade name*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 195,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Trade Name | 17,000 | |
| Trade Name—AuroraGlow | | 212,000 |

**Check:** Dr 195,000 + 17,000 = Cr 212,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Classification / presentation**  
(i) Year-4 impairment losses (**\$168,000** trade name + **\$2,400,000** goodwill) are recognized in **net income** as part of **income from continuing operations** (typically presented as a separate impairment line or within operating expenses per company policy / materiality).  
(ii) **Goodwill** is presented as a **separate line item** on the statement of financial position (aggregate amount). After impairment, Packaged Foods goodwill contributes **\$800,000** to that aggregate (plus any other RUs).  
(iii) **No.** Subsequent **reversal** of a previously recognized goodwill (or indefinite-life intangible) impairment loss is **prohibited** under US GAAP.

**Key insight:** For **indefinite-life** intangibles other than goodwill, impairment is a **one-step fair-value test** (after optional qualitative screen). For **goodwill**, compare **RU fair value** to **RU carrying amount including goodwill**; the loss cannot exceed **goodwill allocated to that RU**. The write-down is an **immediate period-end adjusting JE**; the reduced CA is the new basis.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin impairment cycle
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: quantitative impairment of indefinite-life trademark and RU goodwill; carrying schedule; disposal with gain; presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeway Outdoor Brands Corp.** (calendar year-end, **public**) tests the following at **December 31, Year 3**. Management **bypasses** the qualitative assessment. No prior impairments on these items.

| Item | Facts at Dec 31, Year 3 |
|---|---|
| Trademark **“SummitPath”** (indefinite life) | Carrying amount **$510,000**. Fair value **$340,000**. |
| **Apparel** reporting unit | Carrying amount of RU **including goodwill** **$62,000,000**. Goodwill allocated to RU **$5,000,000**. Fair value of RU **$58,500,000**. Non-goodwill long-lived assets already tested; no further ASC 360 losses required before goodwill testing. |

On **June 30, Year 4**, Ridgeway sells the SummitPath trademark for **$355,000 cash** (post Year-3 impairment basis still in place; no Year-4 impairment before sale).

**Required:**  
a. Compute and record the **Dec 31, Year 3** impairment entry for the trademark (or none).  
b. Compute and record the **Dec 31, Year 3** goodwill impairment entry (or none). State remaining goodwill.  
c. Prepare a Year-3 **carrying-amount schedule** for the trademark and Apparel goodwill (beginning CA = pre-impairment amounts; no other activity).  
d. Record the **June 30, Year 4 disposal** of the trademark.  
e. Compute **total Year-3 impairment loss** reported in continuing operations related to these two items.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Trademark impairment**

\[
\$510{,}000 - \$340{,}000 = \$170{,}000 \text{ impairment loss}
\]

*December 31, Year 3 — Impairment of trademark*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Trademark (SummitPath) | 170,000 | |
| Trademark—SummitPath | | 170,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 170,000. **Balanced.**  
New CA = **$340,000**.

**b. Goodwill impairment**

\[
\text{RU excess} = \$62{,}000{,}000 - \$58{,}500{,}000 = \$3{,}500{,}000
\]

Allocated goodwill = **$5,000,000** → loss = **min(3,500,000, 5,000,000) = \$3,500,000**  
Remaining goodwill = 5,000,000 − 3,500,000 = **\$1,500,000**.

*December 31, Year 3 — Impairment of goodwill*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Goodwill | 3,500,000 | |
| Goodwill | | 3,500,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,500,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Carrying-amount schedule (Year 3)**

| Asset | Beg. CA | Amort. | Impairment | End. CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Trademark—SummitPath | 510,000 | 0 | 170,000 | **340,000** |
| Goodwill—Apparel RU | 5,000,000 | 0 | 3,500,000 | **1,500,000** |

**Checks:** 510,000 − 170,000 = 340,000; 5,000,000 − 3,500,000 = 1,500,000.

**d. Disposal with gain**

CA = **$340,000**; proceeds = **$355,000**; **gain = \$15,000**.

*June 30, Year 4 — Sell trademark*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 355,000 | |
| Trademark—SummitPath | | 340,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Trademark | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr 355,000 = Cr 340,000 + 15,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Total Year-3 impairment loss**  
\$170,000 + \$3,500,000 = **\$3,670,000** (income from continuing operations).

**Key insight:** Same LO 13-6 mechanics as Q1 with **all numbers changed**. Disposal after impairment uses the **post-impairment carrying amount**; proceeds above that basis produce a **gain**, below produce a **loss**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Cap on goodwill loss, no-impairment case, period-end only, presentation
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Period-end quantitative tests: goodwill impairment **capped** at allocated goodwill; indefinite-life asset with FV ≥ CA (no loss); classification/presentation and qualitative option  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborlight Media LLC** is a **public** calendar-year company. At **December 31, Year 2**, it performs **period-end** impairment testing (management **bypasses** qualitative assessment for both items).

| Item | Facts |
|---|---|
| Indefinite-life **broadcast license** | Carrying amount **$90,000**. Fair value **$105,000**. |
| **Streaming** reporting unit | Carrying amount of RU **including goodwill** **$12,000,000**. Goodwill allocated to RU **$800,000**. Fair value of RU **$10,500,000**. Other assets of the RU already tested under ASC 360 with no remaining indicators requiring further write-down before goodwill testing. |

**Required:**  
a. Determine the **Dec 31, Year 2 impairment loss** (if any) on the broadcast license. Prepare the period-end JE or state that **no entry** is required.  
b. Determine the **Dec 31, Year 2 goodwill impairment loss**. Prepare the period-end JE. Explicitly show the **cap** calculation.  
c. After the Year-2 entries, what is the **carrying amount** of (i) the broadcast license and (ii) Streaming RU goodwill?  
d. **Classification / presentation / process:**  
   1. If Harborlight had performed a **qualitative** assessment and concluded it was **not** more likely than not that the license or RU was impaired, what further testing would be required?  
   2. Where is the goodwill impairment loss reported?  
   3. Briefly contrast the indefinite-life quantitative test with the **finite-life** two-step model (recoverability then FV) from LO 13-3.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Broadcast license — no impairment**

FV **\$105,000** **\>** CA **\$90,000** → **not impaired**.  
**No journal entry.**

**b. Goodwill — excess exceeds allocated goodwill (cap applies)**

\[
\text{RU excess of CA over FV} = \$12{,}000{,}000 - \$10{,}500{,}000 = \$1{,}500{,}000
\]

Allocated goodwill = **\$800,000**  
Impairment loss = **min(\$1,500,000, \$800,000) = \$800,000**  
(The loss is **limited** to the goodwill of the RU; the remaining \$700,000 of RU “gap” is **not** written off against other assets via the goodwill test.)

*December 31, Year 2 — Period-end goodwill impairment (capped)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Goodwill | 800,000 | |
| Goodwill | | 800,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 800,000. **Balanced.**  
Remaining Streaming goodwill = **\$0**.

**c. Post-test carrying amounts**  
(i) Broadcast license: **\$90,000** (unchanged).  
(ii) Streaming goodwill: **\$0**.

**d. Classification / presentation / process**  
1. If qualitative assessment concludes it is **not** more likely than not that the asset/RU is impaired, **no quantitative test** is required for that item/RU (unless management elects to perform one anyway).  
2. Goodwill impairment loss of **\$800,000** is recognized in **income from continuing operations** (often a separate line when material).  
3. **Finite-life** intangibles (LO 13-3): indicator → **recoverability test** (undiscounted net CF vs CA) → only if fail, measure loss as CA − FV. **Indefinite-life** (LO 13-6): optional qualitative → **direct FV vs CA** measurement (no undiscounted recoverability step). Goodwill uses **RU-level** FV vs CA, loss capped at allocated goodwill.

**Key insight:** When the RU’s CA exceeds FV by **more** than allocated goodwill, impairment equals **only the goodwill balance**—goodwill can be written to **zero**, but the goodwill test does not automatically write down other net assets of the RU. An indefinite-life asset with FV ≥ CA needs **no** write-down.

### Q4 — MC — Measurement and process choice
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Cap on goodwill impairment; optional qualitative assessment  
**Question:**  
At year-end, a public company has a reporting unit with a **carrying amount (including goodwill) of $20 million**, of which **goodwill is $1.5 million**. The fair value of the reporting unit is **$17.8 million**. Management already completed any required impairment tests of other long-lived assets in the RU. Which statement is **correct**?

- A) Impairment loss on goodwill = **\$2.2 million** (full RU gap).  
- B) Impairment loss on goodwill = **\$1.5 million** (capped at allocated goodwill).  
- C) No goodwill impairment because a qualitative assessment was not documented.  
- D) Impairment loss on goodwill = **\$2.2 million**, and the remaining \$0.7 million reduces other identifiable assets of the RU under the goodwill test.

**Answer:** **B.**  
RU excess = \$20.0m − \$17.8m = **\$2.2 million**, but goodwill impairment is **limited to the \$1.5 million** of goodwill allocated to the RU. The goodwill test does **not** allocate the leftover gap to other assets. Qualitative assessment is **optional** (may be bypassed); lack of a qualitative memo does not prevent recognizing a quantitative loss.

### Q5 — MC — Indefinite-life quantitative test vs finite-life
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Quantitative impairment model for indefinite-life intangible assets (other than goodwill)  
**Question:**  
A company holds a renewable **trade name** accounted for as an **indefinite-life** intangible asset (not amortized). At the annual test date, qualitative factors indicate it is more likely than not that the trade name is impaired. Carrying amount is **\$400,000**; fair value is **\$275,000**; expected **undiscounted** future net cash flows from the trade name are **\$450,000**. The impairment loss, if any, is:

- A) **\$0**, because undiscounted cash flows (\$450,000) exceed carrying amount (\$400,000).  
- B) **\$125,000** (\$400,000 − \$275,000).  
- C) **\$400,000** (full write-off always required once qualitative fails).  
- D) **\$175,000** (\$450,000 − \$275,000).

**Answer:** **B.**  
Indefinite-life intangibles use a **fair-value vs carrying-amount** quantitative test after a failed (or bypassed) qualitative assessment. The **undiscounted recoverability test applies to finite-life** (amortized) intangibles under ASC 360 / LO 13-3, **not** to indefinite-life assets under LO 13-6. Loss = CA − FV = **\$125,000**.
