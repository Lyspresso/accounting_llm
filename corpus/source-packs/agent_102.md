# Agent 102 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-12

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for debt settlement and restructuring  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Troubled debt restructuring (TDR):** creditor grants a concession it would not otherwise consider because of the debtor’s financial difficulties (debtor perspective)
- **Settlement by transfer of assets:** remeasure assets to fair value (gain/loss on disposal), derecognize debt and assets; **gain on restructuring** = carrying amount of payable − FV of assets transferred
- **Settlement by transfer of equity interest:** equity issued measured at **fair value**; gain on restructuring = carrying amount of payable − FV of equity
- **Modification of terms — total future cash payments &lt; debt carrying amount:** reduce payable to **undiscounted** sum of revised cash flows; recognize **gain** immediately; subsequent cash payments reduce carrying amount (no interest expense when all CF is “principal”)
- **Modification of terms — total future cash payments &gt; debt carrying amount:** **no** carrying-amount write-down at restructure; compute a **new effective interest rate** equating PV of revised CF to carrying amount; amortize with effective-interest schedule
- **Initial recognition JE at restructure date:** combine forgiven interest with principal into the restructured note when terms are modified
- **Subsequent measurement / period-end interest JE:** effective-interest entries when CF &gt; carrying amount
- **Maturity / final settlement JE:** pay remaining face (or remaining restructured carrying amount)
- **Classification / presentation:** gain (loss) on disposal of assets **separate** from gain on restructuring of payables; both in measuring net income for the period

### Q1 — CORE — Debtor TDR: asset settlement, equity settlement, two modifications (initial JEs + schedule + maturity)
**LO:** LO 16-12  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of troubled debt settlement/restructuring (asset transfer, equity transfer, terms with CF &lt; CA and CF &gt; CA); subsequent effective-interest schedule and maturity payment  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Riverton Glass Co.** (calendar-year debtor) issued a **9%, 3-year, $300,000** note payable to **Crestline Finance LLC** in exchange for merchandise of equal face amount. Interest is payable each **December 31**. Riverton cannot pay the **December 31, Year 1** interest. Riverton accrued that interest. On **January 1, Year 2**, the carrying amount of the payable is:

\[
\$300{,}000 + (\$300{,}000 \times 9\%) = \$300{,}000 + \$27{,}000 = \mathbf{\$327{,}000}
\]

Crestline grants concessions because of Riverton’s financial difficulties (troubled debt restructuring). Four **independent** alternatives are under consideration on January 1, Year 2.

**Alternative A — Settlement through transfer of assets**  
Riverton transfers **land** and a **building** to Crestline in full settlement:

| | Land | Building |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair value, January 1, Year 2 | $55,000 | $140,000 |
| Original cost to Riverton | 40,000 | 180,000 |
| Accumulated depreciation through January 1, Year 2 | — | 60,000 |
| Carrying amount, January 1, Year 2 | 40,000 | 120,000 |

**Alternative B — Settlement through transfer of equity interest**  
Instead, Riverton issues **4,000 shares** of its **$5 par** common stock in full settlement. Market price is **$40 per share** on January 1, Year 2 (issuance not expected to change the price appreciably).

**Alternative C — Modification when total future cash payments &lt; debt carrying amount**  
Instead of settling, terms are modified as follows:
- Face reduced to **$240,000**
- Accrued Year-1 interest is **forgiven**
- Maturity extended to **January 1, Year 4**
- Stated rate reduced to **4%**; interest due **December 31, Year 2** and **December 31, Year 3**

**Alternative D — Modification when total future cash payments &gt; debt carrying amount**  
Instead, terms are modified as follows:
- Accrued Year-1 interest is **forgiven** (combined into the note)
- Face remains **$300,000**
- Maturity extended to **January 1, Year 4**
- Interest continues at **9%** of face; payments due **December 31, Year 2** and **December 31, Year 3**

**Required:**  
a. **Alternative A:** Prepare the January 1, Year 2 entries for (1) adjusting assets to fair value and (2) recording the troubled debt settlement. Prove that each entry balances and compute the gain on restructuring.  
b. **Alternative B:** Prepare the January 1, Year 2 settlement entry through equity. Prove Dr = Cr.  
c. **Alternative C:** Compute the sum of restructured cash flows, the gain, and the January 1, Year 2 restructuring entry. Then prepare the **December 31, Year 2**, **December 31, Year 3**, and **January 1, Year 4** cash-payment entries (debtor).  
d. **Alternative D:** Show that total future cash payments exceed carrying amount. Compute the **new effective interest rate** (to six decimal places as a percent is fine). Prepare the **amortization schedule** (whole dollars) and all debtor entries from January 1, Year 2 through January 1, Year 4.  
e. **Classification:** For Alternative A, how should the **gain on disposal of assets** and the **gain on restructuring of debt** be classified/presented relative to each other?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Alternative A — Settlement by transfer of assets**

Write assets up (down) to fair value first:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land ($55,000 − $40,000) | 15,000 | |
| Building ($140,000 − $120,000) | 20,000 | |
| Gain on Disposal of Assets | | 35,000 |
| *Remeasure land and building to FV before transfer* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 + 20,000 = Cr 35,000. **Balanced.**

Settlement (assets removed at post-write-up amounts; building cost basis after write-up = $180,000 + $20,000 = $200,000):

Gain on restructuring = carrying amount of debt − FV of assets transferred  
= $327,000 − ($55,000 + $140,000) = $327,000 − $195,000 = **$132,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 300,000 | |
| Interest Payable | 27,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | 60,000 | |
| Land | | 55,000 |
| Building | | 200,000 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 132,000 |
| *Full settlement by transfer of assets (TDR)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 300,000 + 27,000 + 60,000 = **387,000**  
Cr 55,000 + 200,000 + 132,000 = **387,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Alternative B — Settlement by transfer of equity**

FV of equity = 4,000 × $40 = **$160,000**  
Common stock at par = 4,000 × $5 = **$20,000**  
APIC = 4,000 × ($40 − $5) = **$140,000**  
Gain on restructuring = $327,000 − $160,000 = **$167,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 300,000 | |
| Interest Payable | 27,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 20,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 140,000 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 167,000 |
| *Full settlement by grant of equity interest* | | |

**Check:** Dr 327,000 = Cr 20,000 + 140,000 + 167,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Alternative C — Modification; CF &lt; carrying amount**

Carrying amount of debt, January 1, Year 2 = **$327,000**

Sum of restructured cash flows (undiscounted):

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Face payable January 1, Year 4 | $240,000 |
| December 31, Year 2 interest (4% × $240,000) | 9,600 |
| December 31, Year 3 interest (4% × $240,000) | 9,600 |
| **Total future cash payments** | **$259,200** |

Gain = $327,000 − $259,200 = **$67,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable (old) | 300,000 | |
| Interest Payable | 27,000 | |
| Note Payable (restructured) | | 259,200 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 67,800 |
| *Reduce payable to total future cash payments; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr 327,000 = Cr 259,200 + 67,800. **Balanced.**

Because total future payments equal the new carrying amount, **no interest expense** is recognized after restructure; cash payments reduce the carrying amount:

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Y2 | Note Payable | 9,600 | |
| | Cash | | 9,600 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | Note Payable | 9,600 | |
| | Cash | | 9,600 |
| Jan 1, Y4 | Note Payable | 240,000 | |
| | Cash | | 240,000 |

**Roll-forward check:** $259,200 − $9,600 − $9,600 − $240,000 = **$0**. All entries balance.

**d. Alternative D — Modification; CF &gt; carrying amount**

Sum of restructured cash flows:

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Face payable January 1, Year 4 | $300,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 interest (9% × $300,000) | 27,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 interest | 27,000 |
| **Total future cash payments** | **$354,000** |

Excess of CF over carrying amount = $354,000 − $327,000 = **$27,000** → **no gain**; carrying amount unchanged in total. Combine interest payable into the note.

New effective rate \(i\) solves:

\[
\$327{,}000 = \frac{\$27{,}000}{(1+i)} + \frac{\$27{,}000}{(1+i)^{2}} + \frac{\$300{,}000}{(1+i)^{2}}
\]

\[
i = \mathbf{4.213624\%}\quad \text{(Excel: =RATE(2,−27000,327000,−300000))}
\]

**Amortization schedule (debtor; whole dollars, effective interest method):**

| Date | Cash (stated) | Interest expense (eff.) | Reduction of note | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 2 | | | | $327,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | $27,000 | $13,779 | $13,221 | 313,779 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 27,000 | 13,221 | 13,779 | 300,000 |
| **Totals** | **$54,000** | **$27,000** | **$27,000** | |

*Interest expense Year 2:* \(327{,}000 \times 0.04213624 \approx 13{,}779\) (whole dollars).  
*Reduction Year 2:* \(27{,}000 − 13{,}779 = 13{,}221\); CA becomes \(327{,}000 − 13{,}221 = 313{,}779\).  
*Year 3:* interest \(27{,}000 − 13{,}779 = 13{,}221\); reduction \(13{,}779\); CA → \(300{,}000\).

**Journal entries:**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y2 | Note Payable (old face) | 300,000 | |
| | Interest Payable | 27,000 | |
| | Note Payable (restructured CA) | | 327,000 |
| | *Combine principal and accrued interest; no gain (CF &gt; CA)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 327,000.

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Y2 | Note Payable | 13,221 | |
| | Interest Expense | 13,779 | |
| | Cash | | 27,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | Note Payable | 13,779 | |
| | Interest Expense | 13,221 | |
| | Cash | | 27,000 |
| Jan 1, Y4 | Note Payable | 300,000 | |
| | Cash | | 300,000 |

**Check each:** Dr = Cr. Note payable reductions \(13{,}221 + 13{,}779 + 300{,}000 = 327{,}000\).

**e. Classification / presentation (Alternative A)**  
- **Gain on Disposal of Assets $35,000** arises from remeasuring assets to fair value (FV vs carrying amount of land/building).  
- **Gain on Restructuring of Debt $132,000** is the excess of the **carrying amount of the payable** over the **fair value of assets transferred**.  
- Report them **separately** (not netted into one catch-all “gain”); both are included in measuring **net income** for the period of transfer (ASC 470-60 debtor guidance / Topic 220 presentation).

**Key insight:** Settlement always compares debt carrying amount to **FV of consideration** (assets or equity). Pure term modifications use **undiscounted total future cash payments**: if less than carrying amount → immediate gain and new CA; if more → keep CA and impute a **new effective rate** for subsequent interest.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Full TDR twin (all four debtor paths)
**LO:** LO 16-12  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: asset settlement, equity settlement, modification CF &lt; CA, modification CF &gt; CA with schedule and maturity  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Oakridge Fabricators Inc.** issued a **10%, 2-year, $400,000** note to **Harbor Credit Partners** for cash of $400,000. Interest is due each December 31. Oakridge fails to pay December 31, Year 1 interest and has accrued it. Carrying amount of the payable on **January 1, Year 2**:

\[
\$400{,}000 + \$40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$440{,}000}
\]

Four **independent** January 1, Year 2 alternatives (troubled debt restructuring, debtor perspective):

**A — Assets:** Transfer equipment: cost **$280,000**, accumulated depreciation **$95,000**, fair value **$200,000**; and inventory: cost **$70,000**, fair value **$85,000**.

**B — Equity:** Issue **6,000 shares** of **$2 par** common stock; market **$45** per share.

**C — Terms, CF &lt; CA:** Face reduced to **$320,000**; accrued interest forgiven; maturity **January 1, Year 4**; interest **5%** due Dec 31 Year 2 and Year 3.

**D — Terms, CF &gt; CA:** Accrued interest forgiven and combined; face remains **$400,000**; maturity **January 1, Year 4**; interest **10%** of face due Dec 31 Year 2 and Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Alternative A: asset FV entries + settlement entry; compute both gains.  
b. Alternative B: equity settlement entry.  
c. Alternative C: compute gain and restructuring entry; Dec 31 Year 2 payment entry.  
d. Alternative D: prove CF &gt; CA; new effective rate; amortization schedule (whole dollars); all JEs through maturity.  
e. State the **total pre-tax gain** Oakridge would report on January 1, Year 2 under Alternative A only (disposal + restructuring).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Alternative A**

Inventory write-up: FV $85,000 − cost $70,000 = **$15,000**  
Equipment write-up: FV $200,000 − CA ($280,000 − $95,000) = $200,000 − $185,000 = **$15,000**  
Gain on disposal of assets = **$30,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 15,000 | |
| Equipment | 15,000 | |
| Gain on Disposal of Assets | | 30,000 |

**Check:** 15,000 + 15,000 = 30,000.

FV of assets transferred = $200,000 + $85,000 = **$285,000**  
Gain on restructuring = $440,000 − $285,000 = **$155,000**  
Equipment after write-up (gross) = $280,000 + $15,000 = **$295,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 400,000 | |
| Interest Payable | 40,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 95,000 | |
| Equipment | | 295,000 |
| Inventory | | 85,000 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 155,000 |

**Check:** Dr 400,000 + 40,000 + 95,000 = **535,000**  
Cr 295,000 + 85,000 + 155,000 = **535,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Alternative B**

FV equity = 6,000 × $45 = **$270,000**  
CS = 6,000 × $2 = **$12,000**; APIC = 6,000 × $43 = **$258,000**  
Gain = $440,000 − $270,000 = **$170,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 400,000 | |
| Interest Payable | 40,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 12,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 258,000 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 170,000 |

**Check:** 440,000 = 12,000 + 258,000 + 170,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Alternative C**

Restructured CF: face $320,000 + interest \(2 \times \$320{,}000 \times 5\% = \$32{,}000\) = **$352,000**  
Gain = $440,000 − $352,000 = **$88,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable (old) | 400,000 | |
| Interest Payable | 40,000 | |
| Note Payable (restructured) | | 352,000 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 88,000 |

**Check:** 440,000 = 352,000 + 88,000. **Balanced.**

December 31, Year 2 interest “payment” (reduces carrying amount; no interest expense):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable ($320,000 × 5%) | 16,000 | |
| Cash | | 16,000 |

**d. Alternative D**

CF: $400,000 + $40,000 + $40,000 = **$480,000** &gt; CA **$440,000** → excess $40,000; **no gain**.

New rate: `=RATE(2,−40000,440000,−400000)` → **4.648707%**

**Amortization schedule (whole dollars):**

| Date | Cash | Interest expense | Reduction of note | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y2 | | | | $440,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | $40,000 | $20,454 | $19,546 | 420,454 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 40,000 | 19,546 | 20,454 | 400,000 |
| Totals | $80,000 | $40,000 | $40,000 | |

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y2 | Note Payable | 400,000 | |
| | Interest Payable | 40,000 | |
| | Note Payable | | 440,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | Note Payable | 19,546 | |
| | Interest Expense | 20,454 | |
| | Cash | | 40,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | Note Payable | 20,454 | |
| | Interest Expense | 19,546 | |
| | Cash | | 40,000 |
| Jan 1, Y4 | Note Payable | 400,000 | |
| | Cash | | 400,000 |

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Subsequent measurement, period-end interest, maturity, and gain presentation when terms change mid-life
**LO:** LO 16-12  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting / subsequent measurement under CF &gt; CA modification; maturity settlement; classification of restructuring gain when CF &lt; CA  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Outdoor Co.** has a note payable to **Summit Bank** with **carrying amount $165,000** on **January 1, Year 3** (includes $15,000 of unpaid accrued interest combined into the principal balance that day under a TDR). No gain was recognized at restructuring because total future cash payments exceed $165,000. New terms after restructure:
- Face to be paid **December 31, Year 4**: **$150,000**
- Cash interest **$12,000** due **December 31, Year 3** and **December 31, Year 4**
- New effective rate (given): **2.789805%**

Separately, **another** Pinecrest note (unrelated) is restructured on the **same date** with total future cash payments of **$98,000** against a carrying amount of **$120,000** (CF &lt; CA path).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **effective-interest amortization schedule** for the Summit Bank note from January 1, Year 3 through maturity (whole dollars).  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** and **December 31, Year 4** interest/payment entries and the final principal settlement (if not already embedded). Prove the note clears.  
c. For the **other** note (CF &lt; CA): prepare the **January 1, Year 3** restructuring entry and state whether **interest expense** will be recognized after restructure.  
d. **Presentation:** Is the $22,000 gain on the other note an ordinary “interest savings” accrual over time, or an **immediate gain on restructuring of payables**? Where does it appear?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Amortization schedule (Summit Bank note)**

| Date | Cash | Interest expense | Reduction of note | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 3 | | | | $165,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | $12,000 | $4,603 | $7,397 | 157,603 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 12,000 | 4,397 | 7,603 | 150,000 |
| Totals | $24,000 | $9,000 | $15,000 | |

*Computation:* \(165{,}000 \times 0.02789805 \approx 4{,}603\); reduction \(12{,}000 − 4{,}603 = 7{,}397\); CA \(165{,}000 − 7{,}397 = 157{,}603\).  
Year 4: interest \(12{,}000 − 7{,}603 = 4{,}397\); reduction \(7{,}603\) clears to face $150,000.

**b. Period-end / maturity JEs**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Y3 | Note Payable | 7,397 | |
| | Interest Expense | 4,603 | |
| | Cash | | 12,000 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | Note Payable | 7,603 | |
| | Interest Expense | 4,397 | |
| | Cash | | 12,000 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | Note Payable | 150,000 | |
| | Cash | | 150,000 |

**Check:** Each interest entry balances (7,397 + 4,603 = 12,000; 7,603 + 4,397 = 12,000).  
Note payable total reductions: \(7{,}397 + 7{,}603 + 150{,}000 = 165{,}000\). **Clears.**

(If face is paid with the final interest on the same day, the two Dec 31, Year 4 entries may be combined: Dr Note Payable 157,603; Dr Interest Expense 4,397; Cr Cash 162,000.)

**c. Other note — CF &lt; CA**

Gain = $120,000 − $98,000 = **$22,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable (old carrying amount) | 120,000 | |
| Note Payable (restructured) | | 98,000 |
| Gain on Restructuring of Debt | | 22,000 |

**Check:** 120,000 = 98,000 + 22,000. **Balanced.**

After restructure, because carrying amount equals total remaining cash payments, **no interest expense** is recognized; subsequent payments reduce the $98,000 carrying amount.

**d. Classification / presentation**  
The **$22,000** is recognized **immediately** as a **gain on restructuring of payables** (not deferred as a reduction of future interest expense). It is included in **net income** in the period of restructuring. It is **not** an ordinary multiperiod interest accrual; the “interest” labels on later cash payments under the new terms do not create interest expense when CF was set equal to the reduced carrying amount.

**Key insight:** After a CF &gt; CA modification, period-end entries always split cash between **interest expense** (effective rate × beginning CA) and **reduction of carrying amount**. After a CF &lt; CA modification, the gain is **front-loaded** at restructure; later cash is pure principal reduction.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of debtor accounting method under term modification
**LO:** LO 16-12  
**Concept:** Classification — when debtor recognizes immediate TDR gain vs computes new effective rate  
**Question:**  
On the restructure date, a debtor’s note has a carrying amount of **$500,000**. Under a troubled debt restructuring involving **only modification of terms** (no asset or equity transfer), total future cash payments specified by the new terms (including amounts labeled interest and principal) equal **$460,000**. What is the correct debtor accounting at the restructure date?

- A) Keep the $500,000 carrying amount and compute a new effective interest rate so that the present value of $460,000 equals $500,000  
- B) Reduce the carrying amount to **$460,000** and recognize a **$40,000 gain** on restructuring of payables  
- C) Reduce the carrying amount to the **present value** of $460,000 discounted at the original effective rate, with the difference as a gain  
- D) Recognize a $40,000 extraordinary gain and continue accruing interest at the original contractual rate on $500,000  

**Answer:** **B.** When total **undiscounted** future cash payments under the new terms are **less than** the carrying amount of the payable, the debtor reduces the carrying amount to that total and recognizes a gain equal to the reduction. A new effective rate is used only when total future cash payments **exceed** the carrying amount (prospective interest method; no write-down). Discounting at the original rate (C) and “extraordinary” classification (D) are not the ASC 470-60 debtor model illustrated in Demo 16-12.

### Q5 — MC — Settlement measurement (assets vs equity)
**LO:** LO 16-12  
**Concept:** Classification / measurement — FV basis for assets transferred vs equity granted in full settlement  
**Question:**  
A debtor settles a payable with carrying amount **$90,000** by transferring land (carrying amount **$50,000**, fair value **$62,000**). Separately, another debtor settles a **$90,000** payable by issuing stock with fair value **$62,000** (par $10,000). Ignoring taxes, which statement is correct?

- A) Both debtors recognize a **$40,000** gain on restructuring only; neither records a gain or loss on the consideration given  
- B) The asset-transfer debtor records a **$12,000** gain on disposal of land **and** a **$28,000** gain on restructuring; the equity debtor records a **$28,000** gain on restructuring (equity at FV $62,000)  
- C) Both debtors recognize only a **$28,000** gain on restructuring and leave land/stock at carrying amount  
- D) The asset-transfer debtor recognizes a **$40,000** gain on restructuring and no gain on disposal  

**Answer:** **B.** Assets transferred are first adjusted to **fair value** (gain on disposal $62,000 − $50,000 = $12,000). Gain on restructuring = debt CA − FV of assets = $90,000 − $62,000 = **$28,000**. Equity granted is measured at **fair value** $62,000, so gain on restructuring is also **$28,000** (no separate “disposal” of equity). Measuring equity or assets at book value for the restructuring gain (A, C, D) is incorrect under the Demo 16-12 / ASC 470-60 approach.
