# Agent 72 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-7

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for disposal of property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of PPE at historical cost (cash purchase ready for use)
- Subsequent measurement: straight-line depreciation and accumulated depreciation schedule through disposal date
- Period-end / disposal-date adjusting JE to update depreciation before derecognition
- Disposal JE: remove cost and related accumulated depreciation; record cash (or none); recognize gain or loss
- Voluntary sale (gain or loss) vs involuntary conversion / abandonment (full carrying-amount loss when no proceeds)
- Income statement classification: gain or loss on disposal is part of **income from continuing operations**
- Net proceeds concept when removal/selling costs reduce cash realized (optional angle in Q3)

### Q1 — CORE — Acquisition through mid-year disposal with gain
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Initial PPE recognition; depreciation schedule; update depreciation at disposal; sale with gain; IS classification  
**Scenario:**  
**Northspire Fabricators** (calendar-year firm) purchases a production machine that is ready for its intended use on **January 1, Year 1** for **$150,000** cash. Estimated residual value is **$15,000**; estimated useful life is **10 years**. The company uses **straight-line** depreciation and records depreciation at each December 31.

On **July 1, Year 5**, Northspire sells the machine for **$95,000** cash. Depreciation was last updated on **December 31, Year 4**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** acquisition journal entry.  
b. Prepare a **depreciation and carrying-amount schedule** from Year 1 through the disposal date (July 1, Year 5), showing annual depreciation, accumulated depreciation, and book value at each year-end and at disposal.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** depreciation adjusting entry (as the representative period-end entry).  
d. Record the **July 1, Year 5** entry to update depreciation through the disposal date.  
e. Record the **July 1, Year 5** entry to dispose of the equipment.  
f. State where the gain (or loss) is reported in the income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**Depreciable base and annual depreciation**  
Depreciable base = $150,000 − $15,000 = **$135,000**  
Annual depreciation = $135,000 / 10 = **$13,500**  
Half-year (6 months) depreciation = $13,500 × 6/12 = **$6,750**

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 150,000 | |
| Cash | | 150,000 |
| *To record purchase of production machine ready for use* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 150,000.

**b. Depreciation and carrying-amount schedule**  

| Date | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount (cost − AD) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 (acquisition) | — | $0 | $150,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $13,500 | 13,500 | 136,500 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 13,500 | 27,000 | 123,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 13,500 | 40,500 | 109,500 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 13,500 | 54,000 | 96,000 |
| July 1, Y5 (update, ½ year) | 6,750 | **60,750** | **89,250** |

**Rollforward check:** 4 full years × $13,500 = $54,000; + $6,750 = $60,750 AD.  
Carrying amount at disposal = $150,000 − $60,750 = **$89,250**.  
Proceeds $95,000 − carrying amount $89,250 = **gain $5,750**.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 13,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 13,500 |
| *Straight-line depreciation for Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 13,500.

**d. July 1, Year 5 — update depreciation through disposal date**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 6,750 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 6,750 |
| *Update SL depreciation for Jan 1–June 30, Year 5* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,750.

**e. July 1, Year 5 — disposal (sale with gain)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 95,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 60,750 | |
| Gain on Sale of Equipment | | 5,750 |
| Equipment | | 150,000 |
| *Derecognize cost and AD; recognize gain = proceeds − carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 95,000 + 60,750 = **155,750**; Cr 5,750 + 150,000 = **155,750**. Balanced.  
Post-entry: Equipment and related AD for this asset = **$0**.

**f. Classification / presentation**  
The **$5,750 gain** is reported in the income statement as part of **income from continuing operations** (not as an extraordinary item). Starbucks-style policy language is consistent: net gain or loss on disposal is recognized in net earnings.

**Key insight:** Before any disposal entry, update depreciation so the carrying amount is current. Then remove **both** the asset’s historical cost and its full accumulated depreciation; the plug to cash (and any residual to gain/loss) is proceeds minus updated book value.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Mid-period sale with loss
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: acquisition, multi-year AD schedule, disposal-date dep update, sale at a loss  
**Scenario:**  
**Westbrook Packaging** (calendar-year) buys a packaging line ready for use on **January 1, Year 1** for **$72,000** cash. Estimated residual value is **$8,000**; useful life is **8 years**. Straight-line depreciation; year-end is December 31.

On **October 1, Year 6**, Westbrook sells the line for **$22,000** cash. Depreciation was last updated on **December 31, Year 5**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the January 1, Year 1 acquisition entry.  
b. Compute annual depreciation and prepare an AD / book-value rollforward through October 1, Year 6.  
c. Record the October 1, Year 6 depreciation-update entry.  
d. Record the October 1, Year 6 disposal entry.  
e. Prove that Dr = Cr on the disposal entry and compute the loss.

**Answer key:**  

**Annual depreciation**  
Depreciable base = $72,000 − $8,000 = **$64,000**  
Annual depreciation = $64,000 / 8 = **$8,000**  
Nine months (Jan 1–Sept 30, Year 6) = $8,000 × 9/12 = **$6,000**

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 72,000 | |
| Cash | | 72,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 72,000.

**b. Schedule through disposal**  

| Date | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | $0 | $72,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $8,000 | 8,000 | 64,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 8,000 | 16,000 | 56,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 8,000 | 24,000 | 48,000 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 8,000 | 32,000 | 40,000 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | 8,000 | 40,000 | 32,000 |
| Oct 1, Y6 (9/12 year) | 6,000 | **46,000** | **26,000** |

**Proof:** 5 full years × $8,000 = $40,000; + $6,000 = **$46,000** AD.  
Book value = $72,000 − $46,000 = **$26,000**.  
Cash proceeds $22,000 − book value $26,000 = **loss $4,000**.

**c. October 1, Year 6 — update depreciation**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 6,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 6,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,000.

**d. October 1, Year 6 — disposal (sale with loss)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 22,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 46,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 4,000 | |
| Equipment | | 72,000 |

**e. Balance proof**  
Dr: 22,000 + 46,000 + 4,000 = **72,000**  
Cr: **72,000**  
Loss = carrying amount − proceeds = $26,000 − $22,000 = **$4,000**.

**Key insight:** Same mechanics as a gain sale: update AD, strip cost and AD, compare proceeds to carrying amount. When proceeds are **below** book value, the plug is a **loss** (debited) rather than a gain (credited).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Three disposal outcomes; net proceeds; presentation
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Sale at loss, sale at gain, and involuntary/abandonment disposal; optional selling costs; classification of gain/loss  
**Scenario:**  

**Lakeshore Toolworks** (calendar-year) purchased specialized equipment on **January 1, Year 3** for **$100,000**. Estimated residual value **$10,000**; useful life **8 years**; straight-line depreciation. Depreciation is recorded each December 31 and was last updated on **December 31, Year 6**.

On **July 1, Year 7**, the equipment is disposed of under **three separate, independent scenarios** (each scenario starts from the same pre-disposal books).

**Annual depreciation (all scenarios):**  
($100,000 − $10,000) / 8 = **$11,250**  
Full years Year 3–Year 6: 4 × $11,250 = **$45,000** AD at Dec 31, Year 6  
Update for first half of Year 7: $11,250 × 6/12 = **$5,625**  
**Total AD at July 1, Year 7 after update:** **$50,625**  
**Carrying amount:** $100,000 − $50,625 = **$49,375**

| Scenario | Facts at July 1, Year 7 |
|---|---|
| **A** | Sold for **$38,000** cash (no selling costs) |
| **B** | Sold for **$55,000** cash; company also pays **$1,000** cash for removal/selling costs |
| **C** | Destroyed in a flood; **no** insurance recovery; zero proceeds |

**Required:**  
a. Record the **common** July 1, Year 7 depreciation-update entry (same under A, B, and C).  
b. Record the disposal entry under **Scenario A**.  
c. Record the disposal entry under **Scenario B** (use **net** cash proceeds).  
d. Record the disposal entry under **Scenario C**.  
e. For Scenario B only, state the **net proceeds**, **gain or loss**, and income-statement classification.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Common depreciation update — July 1, Year 7**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,625 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,625 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,625. AD after entry = $45,000 + $5,625 = **$50,625**.

**b. Scenario A — sale for $38,000 (loss)**  
Loss = $49,375 − $38,000 = **$11,375**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 38,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 50,625 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 11,375 | |
| Equipment | | 100,000 |

**Check:** Dr 38,000 + 50,625 + 11,375 = **100,000** = Cr Equipment. Balanced.

**c. Scenario B — sale for $55,000 with $1,000 removal costs**  
Net proceeds = $55,000 − $1,000 = **$54,000**  
Gain = $54,000 − $49,375 = **$4,625**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash ($55,000 − $1,000) | 54,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 50,625 | |
| Gain on Sale of Equipment | | 4,625 |
| Equipment | | 100,000 |

**Check:** Dr 54,000 + 50,625 = **104,625**; Cr 4,625 + 100,000 = **104,625**. Balanced.

*(Equivalent two-step presentation: Dr Cash 55,000 / Cr Cash 1,000 for costs, with same net effect on cash and gain.)*

**d. Scenario C — involuntary disposal, no proceeds (abandonment / flood)**  
Loss = full carrying amount = **$49,375**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 50,625 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 49,375 | |
| Equipment | | 100,000 |

**Check:** Dr 50,625 + 49,375 = **100,000**. Balanced.  
Note: Gain or loss is recognized on involuntary conversion even if the firm later replaces the asset (ASC 610-30-25-3 concept from the chapter).

**e. Scenario B — presentation**  
- Net proceeds: **$54,000**  
- Gain on sale: **$4,625**  
- Classification: reported within **income from continuing operations** on the income statement.

**Key insight:** All three paths share the same pre-disposal work: update depreciation, then clear cost and AD. The **only** difference is the proceeds side—cash (gross or net of disposal costs) versus none—which drives gain, loss on sale, or full book-value loss on abandonment.

### Q4 — MC — Classification and disposal process
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Classification of disposal gains/losses; required sequence before recording disposal  

**Question 1:**  
On June 30, a calendar-year company sells equipment for cash at an amount greater than the equipment’s updated carrying amount. Where should the resulting gain be reported?

- A) As an extraordinary item, net of tax  
- B) As a direct adjustment to retained earnings  
- C) As part of income from continuing operations  
- D) As other comprehensive income until the cash is spent on a replacement asset  

**Answer:** **C.** Under the chapter’s core treatment (Demo 11-7), the gain or loss on disposal of PPE is reported in the income statement as part of **income from continuing operations**. It is not deferred solely because replacement is planned, and it is not an extraordinary item.

**Question 2:**  
Immediately before recording the entry to remove a depreciable plant asset that is sold on October 1, what must the company do if depreciation was last recorded at the prior December 31 year-end?

- A) Reverse all accumulated depreciation recorded in prior years  
- B) Update (accrue) depreciation expense from January 1 through the disposal date  
- C) Revalue the asset to fair value with an offsetting revaluation surplus in OCI  
- D) Transfer the asset’s cost to a “Assets Held for Sale” equity contra account  

**Answer:** **B.** If the asset is subject to depreciation, it is depreciated **through the date of disposal** to update the carrying amount before cost and accumulated depreciation are derecognized and gain or loss is measured.
