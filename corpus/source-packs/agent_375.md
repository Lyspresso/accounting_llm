# Agent 375 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-8

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Explain stockholders’ equity disclosures and key ratios  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Equity disclosures:** shares authorized / issued / outstanding / treasury; par; rights and privileges of securities (ASC 505-10-50)
- **Preferred stock disclosures:** liquidation preference (when significantly > par), call/redemption amounts, cumulative dividends in arrears
- **Restricted / appropriated retained earnings:** note disclosure vs formal appropriation within equity (ASC 505-10-45-3)
- **Period-end appropriation JE:** transfer unappropriated RE → appropriated RE (no change in total equity)
- **Settlement / release of appropriation JE:** reverse appropriation when restriction ends
- **Initial recognition JE:** common stock issuance that updates share counts used in disclosures and ratios
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year equity / ratio rollforward (BVPS, payout, ROE, P/E)
- **Book value per common share** = (total SE − preferred claims) ÷ common shares outstanding
- **Preferred claims** = liquidation preference + cumulative dividends in arrears (if any)
- **Payout ratio** = cash common dividends ÷ net income available to common
- **Return on equity** = NI available to common ÷ average common stockholders’ equity
- **Price-to-earnings** = market price per share ÷ EPS
- **NI available to common** = net income − preferred dividend claim for the period (declared or cumulative entitlement)

### Q1 — CORE — Stock issuance, period-end RE appropriation, equity disclosures, and Year-3 ratios
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE (common issuance); period-end appropriation JE; equity disclosure schedule; BVPS, payout, ROE, and P/E  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Inc.** (calendar year) reports the following stockholders’ equity balances at **December 31, Year 2**:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 7% cumulative, $100 par; authorized 5,000 shares; issued and outstanding 2,000 shares | $200,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Preferred | 16,000 |
| Common stock, $2 par; authorized 200,000 shares; issued 80,000; outstanding 75,000 | 160,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Common | 640,000 |
| Retained earnings (all unappropriated) | 890,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive loss | (22,000) |
| Treasury stock (5,000 shares, at cost) | (90,000) |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,794,000** |

**Additional facts**
- Preferred liquidation preference is **$110 per share** (significantly greater than par). No preferred dividends are in arrears at Dec 31, Year 2.
- **March 1, Year 3:** Issued **10,000** new shares of common stock for cash at **$24** per share (no change in treasury holdings).
- Year 3 net income: **$255,000**.
- Year 3 cash dividends declared and paid: preferred **$14,000** (full current-year entitlement); common **$51,000**.
- **December 31, Year 3 (period-end):** Board formally appropriates **$100,000** of retained earnings for a plant-expansion program required by a lender covenant. Appropriation is recorded in the accounts (not note-only).
- Market price of common stock, Dec 31, Year 3: **$29.75**.
- For basic EPS, use weighted-average common shares outstanding (assume the March 1 issue is outstanding for **10/12** of the year). AOCI and treasury cost are unchanged in Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **March 1, Year 3** journal entry for the common stock issuance (**initial recognition**).  
b. Prepare a **Year 3 equity disclosure schedule** showing, for preferred and common: authorized, issued, outstanding, treasury, and par; and state the preferred **liquidation preference** amount that must be disclosed in the equity section.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 3 period-end appropriation** journal entry.  
d. Prepare the **stockholders’ equity section** of the Dec 31, Year 3 balance sheet (with appropriated and unappropriated retained earnings shown separately).  
e. Compute for **Year 3**: (1) book value per common share, (2) payout ratio, (3) return on equity, and (4) price-to-earnings ratio. Show intermediate amounts (preferred claim, NI available to common, average common equity, weighted-average shares, EPS).  
f. Briefly list **three additional equity disclosures** (beyond share counts) that ASC 505-style guidance expects for outstanding securities.

**Answer key:**

**a. March 1, Year 3 — initial recognition of common stock issuance**  
Cash proceeds = \(10{,}000 \times \$24 = \$240{,}000\)  
Common stock (par) = \(10{,}000 \times \$2 = \$20{,}000\)  
APIC—Common = \(240{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \$220{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 240,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 20,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 220,000 |
| *Issue 10,000 common shares at $24* | | |

**Check:** Dr **240,000** = Cr 20,000 + 220,000. **Balanced.**

After issuance: issued **90,000**; outstanding **85,000**; treasury still **5,000**.

**b. Year 3 equity disclosure schedule (end of Year 3 / after Mar 1 issue)**

| Item | Preferred | Common |
|---|---|---|
| Par value per share | $100 | $2 |
| Authorized | 5,000 | 200,000 |
| Issued | 2,000 | 90,000 |
| Treasury | — | 5,000 |
| Outstanding | 2,000 | 85,000 |

**Liquidation preference disclosure (equity section):**  
Aggregate liquidation preference = \(2{,}000 \times \$110 = \mathbf{\$220{,}000}\) (must be disclosed in the equity section because preference is significantly greater than par).

**c. December 31, Year 3 — period-end appropriation JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 100,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Appropriated for Plant Expansion | | 100,000 |
| *Formal appropriation; total RE and total equity unchanged* | | |

**Check:** Dr **100,000** = Cr **100,000**. **Balanced.**

**d. Stockholders’ equity section — December 31, Year 3**

Retained earnings before appropriation split:  
\(890{,}000 + 255{,}000 - 14{,}000 - 51{,}000 = \$1{,}080{,}000\)  
Unappropriated RE = \(1{,}080{,}000 - 100{,}000 = \$980{,}000\)  
Appropriated RE = **$100,000**

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 7% cumulative, $100 par (authorized 5,000; issued and outstanding 2,000); liquidation preference $220,000 | $200,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Preferred | 16,000 |
| Common stock, $2 par (authorized 200,000; issued 90,000; outstanding 85,000) | 180,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Common | 860,000 |
| Retained earnings: | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;Appropriated for plant expansion | 100,000 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;Unappropriated | 980,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive loss | (22,000) |
| Treasury stock (5,000 shares, at cost) | (90,000) |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$2,224,000** |

**Proof of total SE:**  
\(200{,}000 + 16{,}000 + 180{,}000 + 860{,}000 + 1{,}080{,}000 - 22{,}000 - 90{,}000 = \$2{,}224{,}000\).

**e. Year 3 equity ratios**

| Component | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Preferred claim (liq. preference; no arrears) | \(2{,}000 \times \$110\) | $220,000 |
| Common equity, Dec 31, Year 2 | \(1{,}794{,}000 - 220{,}000\) | 1,574,000 |
| Common equity, Dec 31, Year 3 | \(2{,}224{,}000 - 220{,}000\) | 2,004,000 |
| Book value per share, Year 2 (for reference) | \(1{,}574{,}000 / 75{,}000\) | $20.99 |
| **(1) Book value per share, Year 3** | \(2{,}004{,}000 / 85{,}000\) | **$23.58** |
| NI available to common | \(255{,}000 - 14{,}000\) | 241,000 |
| **(2) Payout ratio** | \(51{,}000 / 241{,}000\) | **0.212 (21.2%)** |
| Average common equity | \((1{,}574{,}000 + 2{,}004{,}000) / 2\) | 1,789,000 |
| **(3) Return on equity** | \(241{,}000 / 1{,}789{,}000\) | **0.135 (13.5%)** |
| Weighted-average common shares | \(75{,}000 + 10{,}000 \times 10/12\) | 83,333.33 |
| Basic EPS | \(241{,}000 / 83{,}333.33\) | $2.89 |
| **(4) Price-to-earnings ratio** | \(29.75 / 2.89\) | **10.29** |

*(BVPS and ratio percentages rounded to two decimals / one decimal place as conventional; EPS rounded to two decimals before P/E.)*

**f. Additional equity disclosures (examples)**  
1. Rights and privileges of securities (dividend rate, participation, call prices/dates, conversion terms, voting rights).  
2. Aggregate or per-share amounts at which preferred may be called or is subject to redemption; five-year redemption requirements if fixed/determinable.  
3. Nature and amount of retained earnings restrictions / appropriations (here: plant expansion covenant of $100,000).  
(Also common: shares issued upon conversion/exercise during the period; cumulative preferred dividends in arrears if any.)

**Key insight:** Share-count and preferred-rights **disclosures** feed the inputs to **BVPS** and related ratios. Formal **appropriation** is a **period-end equity reclassification** (Dr unappropriated RE / Cr appropriated RE) that protects dividend capacity in form and note content—it does **not** change total stockholders’ equity or cash.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin pack (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — common issuance; period-end RE appropriation for sinking fund; disclosure schedule; four equity ratios  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarbrook Labs Inc.** reports equity at **December 31, Year 1**:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 6% cumulative, $100 par; authorized 10,000; issued and outstanding 2,500 | $250,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Preferred | 12,500 |
| Common stock, $1 par; authorized 150,000; issued and outstanding 90,000 | 90,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Common | 720,000 |
| Retained earnings (unappropriated) | 540,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive loss | (18,000) |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,594,500** |

**Additional facts**
- Preferred liquidation preference **$105 per share**; no arrears at Dec 31, Year 1. No treasury stock.
- **April 1, Year 2:** Issued **15,000** new common shares for cash at **$18** per share.
- Year 2 net income: **$252,000**.
- Year 2 cash dividends paid: preferred **$15,000**; common **$54,000**.
- **Dec 31, Year 2:** Appropriates **$45,000** of RE for a bond sinking-fund restriction (period-end formal appropriation).
- Market price of common, Dec 31, Year 2: **$32.00**.
- Weighted-average common shares: outstanding before issue for 3 months, after issue for 9 months.

**Required:**  
a. Record the April 1 common stock issuance JE.  
b. Record the Dec 31 appropriation JE.  
c. Prepare ending equity totals: unappropriated RE, appropriated RE, total SE; and the preferred claim used for book value.  
d. Compute Year 2: BVPS, payout ratio, ROE, and P/E (with EPS from weighted-average shares).  
e. State what note disclosure accompanies the sinking-fund appropriation.

**Answer key:**

**a. April 1, Year 2 — initial recognition**  
Cash = \(15{,}000 \times \$18 = \$270{,}000\); Common stock = \(15{,}000 \times \$1 = \$15{,}000\); APIC = **$255,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 270,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 15,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 255,000 |
| *Issue 15,000 common shares at $18* | | |

**Check:** Dr **270,000** = Cr 15,000 + 255,000. **Balanced.**

Outstanding after issue: **105,000** shares.

**b. Dec 31, Year 2 — period-end appropriation**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 45,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Appropriated for Bond Sinking Fund | | 45,000 |
| *Formal restriction for bond sinking fund* | | |

**Check:** Dr **45,000** = Cr **45,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Ending equity amounts**

Total RE before split:  
\(540{,}000 + 252{,}000 - 15{,}000 - 54{,}000 = \$723{,}000\)  
Appropriated RE = **$45,000**  
Unappropriated RE = \(723{,}000 - 45{,}000 = \mathbf{\$678{,}000}\)

Common stock end = \(90{,}000 + 15{,}000 = \$105{,}000\)  
APIC—Common end = \(720{,}000 + 255{,}000 = \$975{,}000\)

Total SE end Year 2:  
\(250{,}000 + 12{,}500 + 105{,}000 + 975{,}000 + 723{,}000 - 18{,}000 = \mathbf{\$2{,}047{,}500}\)

Preferred claim = \(2{,}500 \times \$105 = \mathbf{\$262{,}500}\)  
Common equity end Year 1 = \(1{,}594{,}500 - 262{,}500 = \$1{,}332{,}000\)  
Common equity end Year 2 = \(2{,}047{,}500 - 262{,}500 = \$1{,}785{,}000\)

**d. Year 2 ratios**

| Ratio | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| BVPS (Year 1 ref.) | \(1{,}332{,}000 / 90{,}000\) | $14.80 |
| **BVPS Year 2** | \(1{,}785{,}000 / 105{,}000\) | **$17.00** |
| NI available to common | \(252{,}000 - 15{,}000\) | 237,000 |
| **Payout ratio** | \(54{,}000 / 237{,}000\) | **0.228 (22.8%)** |
| Average common equity | \((1{,}332{,}000 + 1{,}785{,}000) / 2\) | 1,558,500 |
| **ROE** | \(237{,}000 / 1{,}558{,}500\) | **0.152 (15.2%)** |
| Weighted-average shares | \(90{,}000 \times 3/12 + 105{,}000 \times 9/12 = 22{,}500 + 78{,}750\) | 101,250 |
| Basic EPS | \(237{,}000 / 101{,}250\) | $2.34 |
| **P/E** | \(32.00 / 2.34\) | **13.68** |

**e. Note disclosure**  
Disclose the **nature** of the restriction (bond sinking-fund requirement under the debt agreement) and the **amount** of retained earnings appropriated (**$45,000**). Appropriation appears as a separate component of retained earnings within stockholders’ equity; total equity is unaffected.

**Key insight:** Changing every numeric input (shares, prices, NI, dividends, appropriation) still preserves the same measurement architecture: **preferred claim out of total SE → common equity → BVPS**; **NI to common → payout and ROE**; **market ÷ EPS → P/E**. Appropriation remains a pure equity reclass.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Cumulative arrears, multi-year ratio schedule, reverse appropriation, dividend settlement
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Preferred dividends in arrears in preferred claim and disclosures; multi-year subsequent measurement ratio schedule; period-end appropriation and later settlement (release) JE; cash dividend settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Peakline Systems Corp.** has the following equity accounts at **January 1, Year 1**:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 8% cumulative, $50 par; 4,000 shares issued and outstanding | $200,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Preferred | 30,000 |
| Common stock, $1 par; 60,000 shares issued and outstanding | 60,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—Common | 480,000 |
| Retained earnings (unappropriated) | 450,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive loss | (20,000) |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,200,000** |

**Additional facts**
- Preferred liquidation preference **$55 per share**. At Jan 1, Year 1, **one full year** of preferred dividends is **in arrears** (not yet declared). Annual preferred entitlement = \(4{,}000 \times \$50 \times 8\% = \$16{,}000\).
- Common shares outstanding remain **60,000** throughout Year 1 and Year 2 (no issuances, treasury, or splits).
- **Year 1:** Net income **$144,000**. Board declares and pays **only the current-year preferred dividend of $16,000** (arrears remain unpaid). No common dividend. At **Dec 31, Year 1**, board appropriates **$75,000** of RE for a pending litigation contingency.
- **Year 2:** Net income **$192,000**. On **Dec 15, Year 2**, board declares and on **Dec 28** pays: (i) preferred arrears **$16,000**, (ii) current preferred **$16,000**, and (iii) common cash dividends **$36,000**. On **Dec 31, Year 2**, the litigation is resolved favorably and the **$75,000 appropriation is released** (reversed).
- Market price per common share: Dec 31, Year 1 **$27.00**; Dec 31, Year 2 **$33.60**.
- For EPS and NI available to common each year, subtract **that year’s** preferred dividend entitlement (**$16,000**), whether or not arrears are paid.

**Required:**  
a. Compute preferred claim and common equity / BVPS at Jan 1, Year 1; Dec 31, Year 1; and Dec 31, Year 2.  
b. Record the **Dec 31, Year 1 period-end appropriation** JE.  
c. Record **Dec 15 declaration** and **Dec 28 settlement** of Year 2 dividends (preferred arrears + current preferred + common).  
d. Record the **Dec 31, Year 2 release (settlement) of the appropriation**.  
e. Prepare a **two-year subsequent measurement schedule** of BVPS, payout ratio, ROE, EPS, and P/E for Year 1 and Year 2.  
f. Draft a short **note disclosure** of preferred rights at Dec 31, Year 1 (include arrears).

**Answer key:**

**a. Preferred claim and book value**

| Date | Liq. preference | Arrears | Preferred claim | Total SE | Common equity | BVPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | \(4{,}000 \times 55 = 220{,}000\) | 16,000 | **236,000** | 1,200,000 | 964,000 | **$16.07** |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 220,000 | 16,000 | **236,000** | 1,328,000 | 1,092,000 | **$18.20** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 220,000 | 0 | **220,000** | 1,452,000 | 1,232,000 | **$20.53** |

**SE rollforward**  
Year 1 RE: \(450{,}000 + 144{,}000 - 16{,}000 = \$578{,}000\) (of which appropriated $75,000; unappropriated $503,000)  
SE end Y1: \(1{,}200{,}000 + 144{,}000 - 16{,}000 = \$1{,}328{,}000\)  

Year 2 RE: \(578{,}000 + 192{,}000 - 16{,}000 - 16{,}000 - 36{,}000 = \$702{,}000\) (all unappropriated after release)  
SE end Y2: \(1{,}328{,}000 + 192{,}000 - 68{,}000 = \$1{,}452{,}000\)

**b. Dec 31, Year 1 — period-end appropriation JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 75,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Appropriated for Litigation Contingency | | 75,000 |
| *Formal appropriation for pending litigation* | | |

**Check:** Dr **75,000** = Cr **75,000**. **Balanced.** Total equity unchanged.

**c. Year 2 dividend declaration and settlement**

*Dec 15 — declaration*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 68,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred | | 32,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common | | 36,000 |
| *Declare preferred arrears $16,000 + current preferred $16,000 + common $36,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr **68,000** = Cr 32,000 + 36,000. **Balanced.**

*Dec 28 — settlement (payment)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred | 32,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common | 36,000 | |
| Cash | | 68,000 |
| *Pay declared dividends* | | |

**Check:** Dr **68,000** = Cr **68,000**. **Balanced.**

**d. Dec 31, Year 2 — release / settlement of appropriation**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings—Appropriated for Litigation Contingency | 75,000 | |
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | | 75,000 |
| *Contingency resolved; reverse appropriation* | | |

**Check:** Dr **75,000** = Cr **75,000**. **Balanced.** Total equity still unchanged; only the RE composition changes.

**e. Subsequent measurement — two-year equity ratio schedule**

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| NI available to common | \(144{,}000 - 16{,}000 = 128{,}000\) | \(192{,}000 - 16{,}000 = 176{,}000\) |
| Cash common dividends | $0 | $36,000 |
| **Payout ratio** | \(0 / 128{,}000 = \mathbf{0.000\ (0\%)}\) | \(36{,}000 / 176{,}000 = \mathbf{0.205\ (20.5\%)}\) |
| Beginning common equity | 964,000 | 1,092,000 |
| Ending common equity | 1,092,000 | 1,232,000 |
| Average common equity | 1,028,000 | 1,162,000 |
| **ROE** | \(128{,}000 / 1{,}028{,}000 = \mathbf{0.125\ (12.5\%)}\) | \(176{,}000 / 1{,}162{,}000 = \mathbf{0.151\ (15.1\%)}\) |
| **BVPS (year-end)** | **$18.20** | **$20.53** |
| Basic EPS | \(128{,}000 / 60{,}000 = \$2.13\) | \(176{,}000 / 60{,}000 = \$2.93\) |
| Market price | $27.00 | $33.60 |
| **P/E** | \(27.00 / 2.13 = \mathbf{12.68}\) | \(33.60 / 2.93 = \mathbf{11.47}\) |

**f. Sample note disclosure at Dec 31, Year 1**  
*Preferred stock —* 8% cumulative, $50 par; 4,000 shares issued and outstanding. Liquidation preference $55 per share (**$220,000** aggregate). Annual dividend entitlement $16,000. **Dividends in arrears: $16,000** (one year). No participation rights. [Add call/conversion terms if applicable.] *Retained earnings —* $75,000 appropriated for litigation contingency; $503,000 unappropriated.

**Key insight:** **Cumulative preferred arrears** increase the **preferred claim** subtracted in BVPS and must be **disclosed**, even though arrears are not a liability until declared. **Appropriation** and its later **release** are offsetting equity reclasses. Multi-year **ratio schedules** show how ROE, payout, BVPS, and P/E move as earnings, dividends, and preferred claims change.

### Q4 — MC (disclosure / method choice)
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Classification of appropriated retained earnings and effect on total equity  
**Question:**  
On December 31, Redwood Circuits Co. transfers $200,000 from unappropriated retained earnings to retained earnings appropriated for plant expansion because of a loan covenant. Which statement is correct?

- A) Total stockholders’ equity decreases by $200,000; the appropriation is reported as a liability until the plant is built.  
- B) Total stockholders’ equity is unchanged; appropriated retained earnings is reported within stockholders’ equity and the nature/amount is disclosed.  
- C) Total stockholders’ equity increases by $200,000 because appropriated earnings are a form of paid-in capital.  
- D) The entry debits Appropriated Retained Earnings and credits Unappropriated Retained Earnings, and book value per share falls by $200,000 divided by shares outstanding.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Appropriation is an **intra-equity reclassification** (Dr Unappropriated RE / Cr Appropriated RE). ASC 505 permits appropriation **within** shareholders’ equity, clearly identified; total equity, assets, and liabilities do not change. Notes disclose nature and amount. Option A confuses appropriation with a liability; C misclassifies it as paid-in capital; D reverses the debit/credit and incorrectly asserts a BVPS decline (common equity is unchanged).

### Q4b — MC (ratio definition)
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Correct measurement of book value per common share with preferred stock  
**Question:**  
When computing book value per common share, common stockholders’ equity equals:

- A) Total assets minus total liabilities, with no adjustment for preferred stock.  
- B) Total stockholders’ equity minus the preferred stock **carrying amount** only (par + APIC-preferred), ignoring liquidation preference and arrears.  
- C) Total stockholders’ equity minus preferred stockholder claims such as **liquidation preference** and **cumulative dividends in arrears**, then divided by common shares outstanding.  
- D) Retained earnings only, divided by common shares authorized.

**Answer:** **C.**  
BVPS uses common equity after removing **preferred claims** (liquidation value and cumulative arrears when present), divided by **common shares outstanding**. Carrying amount of preferred alone may understate the claim when liquidation preference exceeds par or arrears exist. Authorized shares and RE-only measures are incorrect.
