# Agent 329 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-2

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Determine costs to capitalize for lump-sum purchases of property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Lump-sum (basket) purchase:** two or more PPE assets acquired for a single negotiated price (often less than the sum of individual fair values)
- **Cost allocation is required** so each asset is capitalized separately (land is not depreciated; building/equipment are depreciated over different lives)
- **Proportional method:** allocate the lump-sum cost using each asset’s **relative fair value** (fair value of asset ÷ total fair value of the group)
- **Incremental method:** when one asset’s fair value is **indeterminable**, assign known fair values to the measurable assets and capitalize the **remainder** to the unmeasurable asset
- **Directly attributable costs** (title insurance on land only; freight/installation on equipment only) are assigned **in full** to those assets, then the remaining lump-sum price is allocated
- **Initial recognition JE:** debit each PPE account for allocated + direct cost; credit Cash (or AP) for total cash outlay
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** rollforward of cost, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount by asset
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** straight-line depreciation on depreciable allocated costs (full year or partial year from acquisition date)
- **Disposal JE:** update depreciation to disposal date, remove cost and accum. dep., recognize gain/loss

### Q1 — CORE — Proportional lump-sum allocation, direct costs, multi-year dep schedule, period-end adjusting JEs, equipment disposal

**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Proportional relative-fair-value allocation of a PPE basket purchase; capitalize direct costs; subsequent measurement schedule; **period-end depreciation adjusting JEs** (emphasis); disposal of one allocated asset  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Manufacturing Co.** acquires a production site on **January 1, Year 1** for a single negotiated **cash** price of **$840,000**. The package includes land, a factory building, and packaging equipment. Independent appraisals (best available indicators of fair value) are:

| Asset | Appraised fair value |
|---|---:|
| Land | $210,000 |
| Building | 525,000 |
| Equipment | 140,000 |
| **Total fair value** | **$875,000** |

Same day, Harborline also pays **cash**:
- **$6,000** land title insurance (directly attributable to **land only**)
- **$4,000** equipment installation and trial-run testing (directly attributable to **equipment only**)

Depreciation policy (straight-line; land is not depreciated):
- Building: **40-year** useful life, residual value **$24,000**
- Equipment: **10-year** useful life, residual value **$8,400**

Harborline adjusts depreciation only at each **December 31** year-end (and on disposal date). On **July 1, Year 4**, Harborline sells the packaging equipment for **$85,000 cash**. Depreciation is updated for half of Year 4 before recording the disposal.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **proportional allocation schedule** of the $840,000 lump-sum price and compute **final capitalized cost** of each asset after direct costs.  
(b) Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for the basket purchase and direct costs.  
(c) Compute **annual straight-line depreciation** for the building and equipment.  
(d) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of cost, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount for land, building, and equipment at **December 31, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3**.  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 period-end adjusting JEs** for depreciation (**emphasis**).  
(f) Record the **July 1, Year 4** depreciation update and **disposal** of equipment. Compute book value and gain or loss.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Proportional allocation and final capitalized costs**

Relative fair-value fractions (of $875,000):  
- Land: \(210{,}000 / 875{,}000 = 0.24\) (24%)  
- Building: \(525{,}000 / 875{,}000 = 0.60\) (60%)  
- Equipment: \(140{,}000 / 875{,}000 = 0.16\) (16%)

| Asset | FV fraction | Allocated lump-sum cost | + Direct costs | **Capitalized cost** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 24% | \(0.24 × 840{,}000 =\) **201,600** | Title insurance 6,000 | **207,600** |
| Building | 60% | \(0.60 × 840{,}000 =\) **504,000** | — | **504,000** |
| Equipment | 16% | \(0.16 × 840{,}000 =\) **134,400** | Install/test 4,000 | **138,400** |
| **Total** | **100%** | **840,000** | **10,000** | **850,000** |

**Check:** Allocated portions \(201{,}600 + 504{,}000 + 134{,}400 = 840{,}000\). Final costs \(207{,}600 + 504{,}000 + 138{,}400 = 850{,}000\).

**(b) January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 207,600 | |
| Building | 504,000 | |
| Equipment | 138,400 | |
| Cash | | 850,000 |
| *Lump-sum PPE purchase allocated by relative FV; land title + equipment install capitalized to those assets* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(207{,}600 + 504{,}000 + 138{,}400 = 850{,}000\) = Cr 850,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Annual straight-line depreciation**

Building: \((504{,}000 - 24{,}000) / 40 = 480{,}000 / 40 =\) **$12,000** per year  
Equipment: \((138{,}400 - 8{,}400) / 10 = 130{,}000 / 10 =\) **$13,000** per year  
Land: **$0** (not depreciated)

**(d) Subsequent measurement schedule (carrying amounts)**

| Asset | Cost | Accum. dep. 12/31/Y1 | BV 12/31/Y1 | Accum. dep. 12/31/Y2 | BV 12/31/Y2 | Accum. dep. 12/31/Y3 | BV 12/31/Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 207,600 | 0 | **207,600** | 0 | **207,600** | 0 | **207,600** |
| Building | 504,000 | 12,000 | **492,000** | 24,000 | **480,000** | 36,000 | **468,000** |
| Equipment | 138,400 | 13,000 | **125,400** | 26,000 | **112,400** | 39,000 | **99,400** |
| **PPE, net** | **850,000** | **25,000** | **825,000** | **50,000** | **800,000** | **75,000** | **775,000** |

**Schedule math:** Building accum. \(12{,}000 × n\); equipment accum. \(13{,}000 × n\) for full years \(n = 1, 2, 3\).

**(e) Period-end adjusting JEs — depreciation (emphasis)**

**December 31, Year 1** (identical structure and amounts also at Dec 31, Year 2 and Dec 31, Year 3):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 12,000 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 13,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 12,000 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 13,000 |
| *Period-end only: SL depreciation on allocated capitalized costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(12{,}000 + 13{,}000 = 25{,}000\) = Cr 25,000. **Balanced.**  
Same entry is recorded at **12/31/Y2** and **12/31/Y3** (full years in service). After three years: Accum. Dep.—Building **$36,000**; Accum. Dep.—Equipment **$39,000**.

**(f) July 1, Year 4 — update depreciation and dispose of equipment**

Partial-year depreciation (6/12 of annual): \(13{,}000 × 6/12 =\) **$6,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 6,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 6,500 |
| *Update equipment depreciation through disposal date* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,500 = Cr 6,500. **Balanced.**

Accumulated depreciation at disposal: \(39{,}000 + 6{,}500 =\) **$45,500**  
Book value: \(138{,}400 - 45{,}500 =\) **$92,900**  
Cash proceeds: **$85,000**  
**Loss on disposal:** \(92{,}900 - 85{,}000 =\) **$7,900**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 85,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 45,500 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 7,900 | |
| Equipment | | 138,400 |
| *Remove equipment cost and accum. dep.; recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(85{,}000 + 45{,}500 + 7{,}900 = 138{,}400\) = Cr 138,400. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** The basket discount is shared proportionally by relative fair values, so each asset’s depreciable base (and future dep./gain-loss) is driven by its FV weight—not by residual “leftover” assignment. Directly attributable cash costs never go through the FV fraction; they attach only to the asset they benefit.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Proportional basket with warehouse and forklifts; period-end dep; mid-life disposal

**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of proportional relative-FV allocation, direct costs, subsequent measurement, period-end dep adjusting JEs, and disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Forming LLC** buys land, a warehouse, and a fleet of forklifts on **January 1, Year 1** for a **lump-sum cash** price of **$540,000**. Appraised fair values:

| Asset | Appraised fair value |
|---|---:|
| Land | $120,000 |
| Warehouse | 360,000 |
| Forklifts (equipment) | 120,000 |
| **Total fair value** | **$600,000** |

Additional cash costs same day:
- Land survey **$3,000** and land-title legal fees **$2,000** (land only)
- Forklift freight and installation **$5,000** (equipment only)

Straight-line depreciation:
- Warehouse: **30-year** life, residual **$24,000**
- Forklifts: **8-year** life, residual **$9,000**

Cascade records depreciation at each **December 31** and on disposal. On **October 1, Year 3**, Cascade sells **all** forklifts for **$72,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
(a) Proportional allocation schedule and final capitalized costs.  
(b) January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE.  
(c) Annual depreciation amounts; subsequent measurement schedule at Dec 31 Year 1 and Year 2 (and at disposal date for forklifts).  
(d) Period-end adjusting JEs at Dec 31 Year 1 and Year 2 (**emphasis**).  
(e) October 1, Year 3 depreciation update and disposal JE; compute gain/loss.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Allocation**

Fractions of $600,000 FV: Land 20%; Warehouse 60%; Forklifts 20%.

| Asset | Allocated (× $540,000) | + Direct costs | **Capitalized cost** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | \(0.20 × 540{,}000 =\) **108,000** | Survey + legal 5,000 | **113,000** |
| Warehouse | \(0.60 × 540{,}000 =\) **324,000** | — | **324,000** |
| Forklifts | \(0.20 × 540{,}000 =\) **108,000** | Freight/install 5,000 | **113,000** |
| **Total** | **540,000** | **10,000** | **550,000** |

**(b) January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 113,000 | |
| Building—Warehouse | 324,000 | |
| Equipment—Forklifts | 113,000 | |
| Cash | | 550,000 |
| *Basket purchase allocated by relative FV; land and forklift direct costs capitalized* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(113{,}000 + 324{,}000 + 113{,}000 = 550{,}000\) = Cr 550,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Depreciation and subsequent measurement**

Warehouse annual dep: \((324{,}000 - 24{,}000) / 30 = 300{,}000 / 30 =\) **$10,000**  
Forklifts annual dep: \((113{,}000 - 9{,}000) / 8 = 104{,}000 / 8 =\) **$13,000**

| Date | Asset | Cost | Accum. dep. | Carrying amount |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 | Land | 113,000 | 0 | **113,000** |
| 12/31/Y1 | Warehouse | 324,000 | 10,000 | **314,000** |
| 12/31/Y1 | Forklifts | 113,000 | 13,000 | **100,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Land | 113,000 | 0 | **113,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Warehouse | 324,000 | 20,000 | **304,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Forklifts | 113,000 | 26,000 | **87,000** |
| 10/1/Y3 (pre-sale) | Forklifts | 113,000 | 35,750* | **77,250** |

\*Forklift accum. at 10/1/Y3: \(13{,}000 × 2 + 13{,}000 × 9/12 = 26{,}000 + 9{,}750 = 35{,}750\).

**(d) Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

**December 31, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Warehouse | 10,000 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Forklifts | 13,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Warehouse | | 10,000 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Forklifts | | 13,000 |

**Check:** Dr 23,000 = Cr 23,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 2** — same accounts and amounts as Year 1.

**Check:** Dr 23,000 = Cr 23,000. **Balanced.** Ending Accum. Dep.—Warehouse **$20,000**; Accum. Dep.—Forklifts **$26,000**.

**(e) October 1, Year 3 — update dep and dispose of forklifts**

Partial-year dep (9/12): \(13{,}000 × 9/12 =\) **$9,750**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Forklifts | 9,750 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Forklifts | | 9,750 |

**Check:** Dr 9,750 = Cr 9,750. **Balanced.**

Book value: \(113{,}000 - 35{,}750 =\) **$77,250**  
Proceeds: **$72,000**  
**Loss:** \(77{,}250 - 72{,}000 =\) **$5,250**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 72,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Forklifts | 35,750 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 5,250 | |
| Equipment—Forklifts | | 113,000 |

**Check:** Dr \(72{,}000 + 35{,}750 + 5{,}250 = 113{,}000\) = Cr 113,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Changing the lump-sum price, FVs, lives, and disposal timing does not change the method: form relative-FV fractions, multiply by the basket price, add only asset-specific direct costs, then depreciate and dispose using those capitalized amounts.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Incremental method; mid-year acquisition; period-end partial-year adjusting JE; disposal

**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Incremental allocation when one PPE asset’s fair value is indeterminable; mid-year initial recognition; **period-end partial-year depreciation adjusting JE** (emphasis); subsequent disposal of specialized equipment  
**Scenario:**  
On **April 1, Year 1**, **Redwood Assembly Inc.** pays **$390,000 cash** for a package of land, a small plant building, and highly specialized finishing equipment. Reliable appraisals exist for **land ($100,000)** and **building ($250,000)** only. The specialized equipment has **no determinable market price** (no active market; no comparable sales).

Redwood therefore uses the **incremental method**. Depreciation (straight-line):
- Building: **25-year** life, residual **$10,000**
- Equipment: **5-year** life, residual **$4,000**

Fiscal year-end is **December 31**. Redwood takes a **full month** of depreciation for April (9/12 of annual in Year 1). On **December 31, Year 2**, after recording full Year 2 depreciation, Redwood scraps the specialized equipment for **$8,000 cash** (no further residual expected).

**Required:**  
(a) Allocate the $390,000 lump-sum price using the **incremental method** and record the **April 1, Year 1** purchase JE.  
(b) Compute annual depreciation for building and equipment.  
(c) Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for partial-year depreciation (**emphasis**). Show Year 1 carrying amounts.  
(d) Record the **December 31, Year 2** full-year depreciation adjusting JE.  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 2 disposal** of equipment (after Year 2 dep). Compute book value and gain/loss.  
(f) Briefly explain why the proportional method is **not** available here, and what would change if equipment FV later became measurable at acquisition (conceptual only).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Incremental allocation and initial recognition**

| Asset | Allocation basis | Capitalized cost |
|---|---|---:|
| Land | Fair value (determinable) | **100,000** |
| Building | Fair value (determinable) | **250,000** |
| Equipment | Remainder: \(390{,}000 - 100{,}000 - 250{,}000\) | **40,000** |
| **Total** | | **390,000** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 100,000 | |
| Building | 250,000 | |
| Equipment | 40,000 | |
| Cash | | 390,000 |
| *Incremental basket allocation: known FVs to land/building; residual cost to specialized equipment* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(100{,}000 + 250{,}000 + 40{,}000 = 390{,}000\) = Cr 390,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Annual depreciation**

Building: \((250{,}000 - 10{,}000) / 25 = 240{,}000 / 25 =\) **$9,600** per year  
Equipment: \((40{,}000 - 4{,}000) / 5 = 36{,}000 / 5 =\) **$7,200** per year

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (partial year; emphasis)**

Service fraction: April 1–December 31 = **9/12**.  
Building dep Y1: \(9{,}600 × 9/12 =\) **$7,200**  
Equipment dep Y1: \(7{,}200 × 9/12 =\) **$5,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 7,200 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 5,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 7,200 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,400 |
| *Period-end only: 9/12 SL depreciation from April 1 acquisition* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(7{,}200 + 5{,}400 = 12{,}600\) = Cr 12,600. **Balanced.**

Carrying amounts at 12/31/Y1:  
- Land **$100,000**  
- Building \(250{,}000 - 7{,}200 =\) **$242,800**  
- Equipment \(40{,}000 - 5{,}400 =\) **$34,600**

**(d) December 31, Year 2 — full-year period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 9,600 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 7,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 9,600 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 7,200 |

**Check:** Dr 16,800 = Cr 16,800. **Balanced.**

Equipment accum. dep. after Y2 entry: \(5{,}400 + 7{,}200 =\) **$12,600**

**(e) December 31, Year 2 — disposal of specialized equipment**

Book value: \(40{,}000 - 12{,}600 =\) **$27,400**  
Cash scrap: **$8,000**  
**Loss on disposal:** \(27{,}400 - 8{,}000 =\) **$19,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 12,600 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 19,400 | |
| Equipment | | 40,000 |

**Check:** Dr \(8{,}000 + 12{,}600 + 19{,}400 = 40{,}000\) = Cr 40,000. **Balanced.**

**(f) Method choice**

Proportional relative-FV allocation requires a **reliable fair value for every** asset in the group so fractions sum to 100%. Here equipment FV is indeterminable, so the **incremental method** assigns known FVs first and puts residual cost on the specialized asset. If equipment FV had been measurable at acquisition, Redwood would use the **proportional method** on all three FVs (and equipment capitalized cost would generally differ from the $40,000 residual).

**Key insight:** Incremental allocation protects measurable assets from absorbing residual “bargains/premiums” incorrectly—when only some FVs are known, those assets are recorded at FV and the unmeasurable asset takes whatever cost remains (which can be high or low relative to economic value).

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)

**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Choose proportional vs incremental allocation; compute land cost under proportional relative fair value  

**Question 4A:**  
Pinecroft Materials acquires land, a building, and equipment for one lump-sum price. Appraisals exist for land and the building, but the equipment is so specialized that no reliable fair value can be obtained. How should Pinecroft allocate the lump-sum cost?

- A) Divide the lump-sum price equally among the three assets  
- B) Capitalize the entire lump-sum price to land (nondepreciable) until equipment FV is known  
- C) Assign the land and building their appraised fair values and capitalize the **remainder** of the lump-sum price to equipment (**incremental method**)  
- D) Allocate the lump-sum price using only the land-to-building fair-value ratio and expensing equipment  

**Answer:** **C.** When one asset’s fair value is indeterminable, the incremental method assigns known fair values to measurable assets and residual cost to the unmeasurable asset. Equal split (A) ignores relative values; parking the whole cost in land (B) misstates all accounts; omitting equipment from capitalization (D) violates the cost principle for acquired PPE.

**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Compute allocated land cost under the proportional method  

**Question 4B:**  
On March 1, Vale Forge Co. pays **$360,000 cash** for land, building, and equipment as a package. Appraised fair values: land **$80,000**, building **$240,000**, equipment **$80,000**. No other acquisition costs. Using the **proportional method**, the amount capitalized to **Land** is:

- A) $80,000  
- B) $72,000  
- C) $90,000  
- D) $360,000  

**Answer:** **B.** Total FV = \(80{,}000 + 240{,}000 + 80{,}000 = 400{,}000\). Land fraction = \(80{,}000 / 400{,}000 = 0.20\). Land cost = \(0.20 × 360{,}000 =\) **$72,000**. Option A is appraised FV (not used as cost when the basket price differs); C and D misapply the total or ignore allocation.
