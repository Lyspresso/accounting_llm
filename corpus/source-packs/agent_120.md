# Agent 120 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-6

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS using if-converted method for convertible securities  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** issue convertible bonds at par (Dr Cash / Cr Bonds Payable); issue convertible preferred stock (Dr Cash / Cr Preferred Stock [± APIC])
- **If-converted method (convertible debt):** assume conversion at beginning of period (or issuance if later); **add back interest expense net of tax** to numerator; **add potential common shares** to denominator
- **If-converted method (convertible preferred):** add back preferred dividends deducted in basic EPS; add potential common shares
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** contractual interest (and semi-annual payments); diluted EPS reconciliation schedule (basic → effect of convertibles → diluted)
- **Period-end adjusting / cash interest JE:** record interest expense and payment (or accrual) on convertible debt
- **Partial-year weighting:** if convertibles issued mid-year, weight **both** after-tax interest add-back **and** incremental shares by time outstanding
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** actual conversion of bonds or preferred into common (remove debt/equity preferred; issue common + APIC)
- **Dilutive vs antidilutive test:** include in diluted EPS only if diluted EPS **decreases** (or loss per share becomes more negative is **not** dilutive—with a loss from continuing operations, potential shares are **excluded**)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** report **basic and diluted EPS** on the face of the income statement when capital structure is complex; disclose antidilutive convertibles omitted from diluted EPS

### Q1 — CORE — Convertible bonds: issuance JE, interest, if-converted EPS, conversion JE, presentation
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of convertible bonds at par; interest JEs; basic and diluted EPS under the if-converted method (full year and mid-year issuance); actual conversion settlement JE; income-statement EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northpine Analytics Corp.** is a calendar-year company with a **complex capital structure** solely because of convertible debt.

**Facts for 2025 (full-year bonds):**
1. On **January 1, 2025**, Northpine issued **2,000** convertible bonds, each with **$1,000** face amount (**$2,000,000** total), **5%** stated annual interest, for **cash at par**. Each bond is convertible into **20** shares of Northpine’s **$2** par common stock. Maturity is December 31, 2034. Interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31.
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding during 2025 (before any if-converted assumptions): **300,000**.
3. Net income for 2025: **$900,000** (already after interest expense on the convertible bonds and after income taxes).
4. Enacted tax rate: **25%**.
5. No preferred stock and no other potentially dilutive securities.

**Alternate fact (part g only):** Same bonds are issued on **July 1, 2025** (not January 1). Semiannual interest is still paid December 31 for the six months outstanding. Net income for 2025 under this alternate fact is **$937,500** (higher than $900,000 because only six months of after-tax interest is incurred). Weighted-average common shares remain **300,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, 2025 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible bonds at par.  
b. Prepare the **June 30** and **December 31, 2025** cash interest payment JEs. Prepare a brief **interest schedule** for the year (face, rate, interest expense, cash paid).  
c. Compute **basic EPS** for 2025 (full-year bonds facts).  
d. Compute **diluted EPS** using the **if-converted method**. Show the diluted EPS reconciliation schedule. State whether the bonds are **dilutive** or **antidilutive**.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** What EPS amount(s) does Northpine report on the face of its 2025 income statement?  
f. On **January 1, 2026**, all bondholders convert their bonds into common stock. Prepare the **conversion (settlement) JE**. (Ignore any interest on January 1.)  
g. Using the **July 1 issuance alternate facts**, recompute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** under the if-converted method (partial-year weighting).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — convertible bonds at par (January 1, 2025)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,000,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 2,000,000 |
| *Issue 2,000 of $1,000 face 5% convertible bonds at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,000,000 = Cr 2,000,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Interest JEs and subsequent measurement schedule**

Annual contractual interest = $2,000,000 × 5% = **$100,000**  
Semiannual cash interest = $100,000 × 6/12 = **$50,000**

*June 30, 2025 — pay semiannual interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 50,000 | |
| Cash | | 50,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 50,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, 2025 — pay semiannual interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 50,000 | |
| Cash | | 50,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 50,000. **Balanced.**

**Interest schedule (2025, bonds at par — no premium/discount amortization)**

| Period | Face outstanding | Rate | Interest expense | Cash paid | Carrying amount end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 issue | $2,000,000 | — | — | — | $2,000,000 |
| Jan 1–Jun 30 | 2,000,000 | 5% × 6/12 | 50,000 | 50,000 | 2,000,000 |
| Jul 1–Dec 31 | 2,000,000 | 5% × 6/12 | 50,000 | 50,000 | 2,000,000 |
| **Year total** | | | **100,000** | **100,000** | |

**c. Basic EPS (full-year bonds)**

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$900{,}000}{300{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.00}
\]

**d. Diluted EPS — if-converted method (full year)**

Assume conversion as of **January 1, 2025**:

- **Numerator add-back (interest, net of tax):**  
  \( \$100{,}000 \times (1 - 0.25) = \$75{,}000 \)
- **Denominator add (new common shares):**  
  \( 2{,}000 \text{ bonds} \times 20 \text{ shares} = 40{,}000 \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $900,000 | 300,000 | $3.00 |
| Effect of convertible bonds: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 75,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 40,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$975,000** | **340,000** | **$2.87** |

Math check: \( 975{,}000 \div 340{,}000 = 2.867647\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.87} \) (rounded to nearest cent).

**Dilutive test:** Diluted EPS **$2.87** < basic EPS **$3.00** → bonds are **dilutive**. Include in diluted EPS.

**e. Presentation**

Northpine has a **complex capital structure**. It reports **both**:
- Basic EPS **$3.00**
- Diluted EPS **$2.87**

on the face of the income statement for 2025.

**f. Conversion settlement JE (January 1, 2026)**

Shares issued = \( 2{,}000 \times 20 = 40{,}000 \)  
Common stock at par = \( 40{,}000 \times \$2 = \$80{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$2{,}000{,}000 - \$80{,}000 = \$1{,}920{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 2,000,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 80,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 1,920,000 |
| *Convert all bonds into 40,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,000,000 = Cr 80,000 + 1,920,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Partial-year issuance (July 1) — if-converted weighting**

Interest outstanding for 6/12 of the year:

- After-tax interest add-back = \( \$75{,}000 \times 6/12 = \$37{,}500 \)  
  (equivalently \( \$50{,}000 \times 0.75 = \$37{,}500 \))
- Incremental shares weighted = \( 40{,}000 \times 6/12 = 20{,}000 \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $937,500 | 300,000 | $3.13 |
| Effect of convertible bonds (6/12): | | | |
| Add back prorated interest, net of tax | 37,500 | | |
| Add prorated common shares | | 20,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$975,000** | **320,000** | **$3.05** |

Basic: \( 937{,}500 \div 300{,}000 = 3.125 \approx \mathbf{\$3.13} \)  
Diluted: \( 975{,}000 \div 320{,}000 = 3.046875 \approx \mathbf{\$3.05} \)

**Dilutive:** $3.05 < $3.13 → include. Report basic **$3.13** and diluted **$3.05**.

**Key insight:** Under the if-converted method, convertible debt is treated as if converted at the start of the period (or issue date if later): **add after-tax interest** and **potential shares**. Mid-year issues weight **both** adjustments. Actual conversion is a separate **balance-sheet settlement JE**; diluted EPS already assumes conversion for the period the debt was outstanding.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Convertible bonds if-converted twin
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: issue convertible bonds at par; interest; basic and diluted EPS if-converted; conversion JE; EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**SummitForge Industrial Inc.** (calendar-year) issued convertible debt on **January 1, 2025**.

**Facts:**
1. Issued **3,000** of **8%**, **$1,000** face convertible bonds (**$3,000,000** total) **at par** for cash. Each bond converts into **25** shares of **$1** par common stock. Interest is paid **annually** on December 31.
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding in 2025: **450,000**.
3. Net income for 2025: **$1,350,000** (after interest and taxes).
4. Tax rate: **21%**.
5. No preferred stock; no other dilutives.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** for the January 1 bond issue.  
b. Prepare the **December 31 interest payment JE**.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** (if-converted). Show the reconciliation schedule and dilutive test.  
d. State the EPS amounts reported on the income statement.  
e. Prepare the JE if **all** bonds convert on **January 1, 2026**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, 2025 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,000,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 3,000,000 |
| *Issue 3,000 of $1,000 face 8% convertible bonds at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,000,000. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, 2025 — interest payment**

Interest expense = $3,000,000 × 8% = **$240,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 240,000 | |
| Cash | | 240,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 240,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Basic and diluted EPS (if-converted)**

After-tax interest add-back = \( \$240{,}000 \times (1 - 0.21) = \$240{,}000 \times 0.79 = \$189{,}600 \)  
New common shares = \( 3{,}000 \times 25 = 75{,}000 \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $1,350,000 | 450,000 | $3.00 |
| Effect of convertible bonds: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 189,600 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 75,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$1,539,600** | **525,000** | **$2.93** |

Basic: \( 1{,}350{,}000 \div 450{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3.00} \)  
Diluted: \( 1{,}539{,}600 \div 525{,}000 = 2.932571\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.93} \)

**Dilutive test:** $2.93 < $3.00 → **dilutive**. Include in diluted EPS.

**d. Presentation**

Report **basic EPS $3.00** and **diluted EPS $2.93** on the face of the 2025 income statement.

**e. Conversion JE — January 1, 2026**

Shares issued = 75,000  
Common stock at par = \( 75{,}000 \times \$1 = \$75{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$3{,}000{,}000 - \$75{,}000 = \$2{,}925{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 3,000,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 75,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 2,925,000 |
| *Convert all bonds into 75,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,000,000 = Cr 75,000 + 2,925,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Same if-converted mechanics as Q1 with fully changed inputs (face, coupon, tax rate, conversion ratio, shares, income). Always recompute after-tax interest as \( \text{interest} \times (1 - t) \) and test dilution against basic EPS.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Convertible preferred if-converted; conversion settlement; loss-year antidilution & disclosure
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** If-converted method for convertible preferred stock; preferred issuance and conversion JEs; diluted EPS when company has a net loss (antidilutive exclusion and disclosure)  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore BioPharma Co.** (calendar-year) has convertible preferred stock and, in a separate scenario year, convertible bonds with a loss.

**Part A — Convertible preferred (Year 1, profitable)**  
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Lakeshore issued **8,000** shares of **6%**, **$50** par **cumulative convertible preferred stock** for cash at **par** (**$400,000**). Each preferred share is convertible into **4** shares of **$5** par common stock.  
2. Net income for Year 1: **$480,000**.  
3. Weighted-average common shares outstanding: **120,000**.  
4. Preferred dividends for Year 1 are declared and paid in cash (cumulative preferred — annual dividend is earned/deducted for basic EPS).  
5. Tax rate: **25%** (not needed for preferred if-converted numerator).  
6. No convertible debt in Part A.

**Part B — Actual conversion (January 1, Year 2)**  
All 8,000 preferred shares convert into common stock on January 1, Year 2.

**Part C — Net loss year with convertible bonds (antidilutive)**  
In a different year, Lakeshore reports a **net loss of $600,000**. Throughout that year it has outstanding **1,000** of **6%**, **$1,000** bonds issued at par, each convertible into **20** common shares. Weighted-average common shares: **200,000**. Tax rate **25%**. Assume sufficient taxable income so interest is deductible for tax purposes if one were to compute a hypothetical diluted figure; apply the **loss-from-continuing-operations rule** for reporting.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible preferred stock.  
b. Prepare the **period-end (or declaration/payment) JE** for Year 1 preferred dividends.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** (if-converted) for Year 1. Show schedule and dilutive test.  
d. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 conversion settlement JE** for the preferred stock.  
e. For Part C (loss year): compute the **hypothetical** if-converted diluted figure, explain why it is **antidilutive**, state the **reported** basic and diluted EPS, and describe the **disclosure** for the omitted convertibles.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — convertible preferred at par (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 400,000 | |
| Preferred Stock ($50 par) | | 400,000 |
| *Issue 8,000 shares of 6%, $50 par cumulative convertible preferred at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Preferred dividends Year 1**

Annual preferred dividend = \( 8{,}000 \times \$50 \times 6\% = \$24{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Cash Dividends — Preferred) | 24,000 | |
| Cash | | 24,000 |
| *Declare and pay annual preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000. **Balanced.**

(If declaration and payment are split: Dr Retained Earnings / Cr Dividends Payable 24,000; then Dr Dividends Payable / Cr Cash 24,000 — each balances.)

**c. Basic and diluted EPS — convertible preferred (if-converted)**

Income available to common (basic) = \( \$480{,}000 - \$24{,}000 = \$456{,}000 \)

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$456{,}000}{120{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.80}
\]

If-converted assumptions (conversion as of January 1, Year 1):

- **Numerator:** add back preferred dividends **$24,000** → income for diluted = \( \$456{,}000 + \$24{,}000 = \$480{,}000 \) (equivalently, start from NI and do not deduct preferred dividends)  
- **Denominator:** add \( 8{,}000 \times 4 = 32{,}000 \) common shares → \( 120{,}000 + 32{,}000 = 152{,}000 \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $456,000 | 120,000 | $3.80 |
| Effect of convertible preferred: | | | |
| Add back preferred dividends | 24,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 32,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$480,000** | **152,000** | **$3.16** |

Diluted: \( 480{,}000 \div 152{,}000 = 3.157894\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$3.16} \)

**Dilutive test:** $3.16 < $3.80 → **dilutive**. Report basic **$3.80** and diluted **$3.16**.

*(Note: Preferred dividends have **no tax add-back adjustment** — dividends are not tax-deductible.)*

**d. Conversion settlement JE — January 1, Year 2**

Common shares issued = \( 8{,}000 \times 4 = 32{,}000 \)  
Common stock at par = \( 32{,}000 \times \$5 = \$160{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$400{,}000 - \$160{,}000 = \$240{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock ($50 par) | 400,000 | |
| Common Stock ($5 par) | | 160,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 240,000 |
| *Convert 8,000 preferred shares into 32,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 400,000 = Cr 160,000 + 240,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Net loss year — antidilutive convertible bonds (Part C)**

Contractual interest = \( 1{,}000 \times \$1{,}000 \times 6\% = \$60{,}000 \)  
After-tax interest (hypothetical) = \( \$60{,}000 \times 0.75 = \$45{,}000 \)  
Potential shares = \( 1{,}000 \times 20 = 20{,}000 \)

| | “Income” (loss) available | Shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $(600,000) | 200,000 | $(3.00) |
| Hypothetical if-converted: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 45,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 20,000 | |
| Hypothetical “diluted” | $(555,000) | 220,000 | $(2.52) |

**Antidilutive analysis:** Including the convertibles **improves** (makes less negative) loss per share from **$(3.00)** to **$(2.52)**. That is **antidilutive**.  

**ASC 260 rule:** When there is a **loss from continuing operations**, potential common shares are **not** included in diluted EPS (they are always antidilutive to a loss).  

**Reported amounts:** Basic EPS = Diluted EPS = **$(3.00)**  

**Disclosure:** Disclose the convertible bonds (terms, conversion ratio, that they were **excluded** from diluted EPS because they were **antidilutive** for the period). Full terms are required even though the securities are omitted from the diluted computation.

**Key insight:** Convertible **preferred** uses if-converted by restoring **preferred dividends** (no tax gross-up) and adding shares. Convertible **debt** restores **after-tax interest**. In a **loss year**, potential shares are left out of diluted EPS; report the same loss per share for basic and diluted and **disclose** the antidilutive convertibles.

### Q4 — MC (method and dilution classification)
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Classification — which computational method applies to convertible securities; identifying dilutive vs antidilutive outcomes  

**Question 1:**  
In computing **diluted EPS**, the effect of **convertible bonds** and **convertible preferred stock** is incorporated using which method?

- A) Treasury stock method  
- B) If-converted method  
- C) Two-class method only (no conversion assumption)  
- D) Retrospective restatement method  

**Answer:** **B.** The **if-converted method** assumes conversion at the beginning of the period (or issuance if later), adjusts the numerator (after-tax interest for debt; preferred dividends for convertible preferred), and adds the resulting common shares to the denominator. The treasury stock method applies to options/warrants (LO 20-7), not to convertibles.

**Question 2:**  
Bayline Co. has basic EPS of **$2.50**. Including its only convertible bond issue under the if-converted method would produce EPS of **$2.62**. How should Bayline treat the bonds in diluted EPS?

- A) Include the bonds; report diluted EPS of $2.62  
- B) Include the bonds; report diluted EPS of $2.50  
- C) Exclude the bonds as **antidilutive**; diluted EPS equals basic EPS of $2.50 (absent other dilutives)  
- D) Exclude the bonds and do not disclose them  

**Answer:** **C.** A security is **dilutive** only if its inclusion **reduces** EPS (or increases loss per share). Here $2.62 > $2.50, so the bonds are **antidilutive** and are **excluded** from diluted EPS. Antidilutive convertibles still require **disclosure** of terms; they are not silently ignored in the notes.
