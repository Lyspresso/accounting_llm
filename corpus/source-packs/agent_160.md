# Agent 160 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-4

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Account for multiple tax differences and prepare a tax reconciliation disclosure  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** record Income Tax Payable, Deferred Tax Liability (taxable temporary differences), Deferred Tax Asset (deductible temporary differences), and Income Tax Expense when multiple book–tax differences originate
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year schedules of cumulative temporary differences × enacted rate → DTL/DTA ending balances; roll-forward of deferred balances each year
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** year-end income tax entries that update payable and adjust deferred accounts to schedule ending balances
- **Disposal / settlement / reverse JE:** reverse DTL when prepaid/asset basis difference settles; reverse DTA as warranty (or similar) cash payments become tax-deductible
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** rate reconciliation (statutory → permanent reconciling items → effective rate in $ and %); offset DTA/DTL for same tax-paying component and jurisdiction as one noncurrent amount; permanent items never create DTA/DTL
- **Number-variant twin:** same multi-difference skill path with all pretax amounts, temporary differences, permanent items, and rate changed

### Q1 — CORE — Riverton Analytics: multi-year DTL + DTA + municipal interest; schedules, JEs, rate recon
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedules for DTL and DTA over three years; initial recognition and period-end tax JEs; partial warranty settlement; rate reconciliation disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverton Analytics Corp.** is a calendar-year company. Pretax GAAP income (after all revenues and expenses, including the items below) is:

| Year | Pretax GAAP income |
|------|-------------------:|
| Year 1 | $150,000 |
| Year 2 | $175,000 |
| Year 3 | $140,000 |

The enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. Deferred tax accounts have **zero** beginning balances on January 1, Year 1. Accounting and tax periods both end December 31.

Book–tax differences:

1. **Prepaid insurance:** On December 31, Year 1, Riverton paid and recorded prepaid insurance of **$24,000** for Year 2 coverage. The cost is **deductible for tax when paid** and is expensed for GAAP in Year 2 when coverage is consumed.  
2. **Warranty accrual:** On December 31, Year 1, Riverton accrued warranty expense of **$45,000** (GAAP liability). Warranty costs are **not deductible for tax until paid**. Settlements are **$15,000 in Year 2, $15,000 in Year 3, and $15,000 in Year 4**.  
3. **Municipal bond interest:** Riverton earns **$1,200** of municipal bond interest revenue each year (Year 1–Year 3). Interest is **included in pretax GAAP income** but is **nontaxable** (permanent difference).

No other temporary or permanent differences exist. No valuation allowance is required.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **taxable income** and **income tax payable** for Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3.  
(b) Prepare **subsequent measurement schedules** of the **deferred tax liability** and **deferred tax asset** ending balances at December 31 of Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 (basis difference × enacted rate). Also prepare a **deferred-tax roll-forward** (beginning balance, increase/(decrease), ending balance) for each account across the three years.  
(c) Record the **period-end income tax journal entries** for Year 1 (initial recognition), Year 2, and Year 3.  
(d) Prepare the **rate reconciliation** (dollars and percentages) from the statutory rate to the effective tax rate for each of the three years.  
(e) At December 31, Year 3, state the **balance-sheet presentation** of deferred taxes (same component/jurisdiction) and the remaining warranty temporary difference expected to reverse in Year 4.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Taxable income and income tax payable**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pretax GAAP income | $150,000 | $175,000 | $140,000 |
| Insurance (prepaid origin / reverse) | (24,000) | 24,000 | — |
| Warranty (origin / cash settlements) | 45,000 | (15,000) | (15,000) |
| Municipal bond interest (permanent) | (1,200) | (1,200) | (1,200) |
| **Taxable income** | **$169,800** | **$182,800** | **$123,800** |
| Tax rate | × 25% | × 25% | × 25% |
| **Income tax payable** | **$42,450** | **$45,700** | **$30,950** |

Checks: \(169{,}800 \times 0.25 = 42{,}450\); \(182{,}800 \times 0.25 = 45{,}700\); \(123{,}800 \times 0.25 = 30{,}950\).

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedules**

**Deferred tax liability (prepaid insurance — taxable temporary difference)**

| | Dec 31, Y1 | Dec 31, Y2 | Dec 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GAAP basis of prepaid insurance | $24,000 | $0 | $0 |
| Tax basis of prepaid insurance | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Cumulative taxable temporary difference | 24,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Enacted rate | × 25% | × 25% | × 25% |
| **DTL ending balance** | **$6,000** | **$0** | **$0** |

**Deferred tax asset (warranty — deductible temporary difference)**

| | Dec 31, Y1 | Dec 31, Y2 | Dec 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GAAP basis of warranty liability | $45,000 | $30,000 | $15,000 |
| Tax basis of warranty liability | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Cumulative deductible temporary difference | 45,000 | 30,000 | 15,000 |
| Enacted rate | × 25% | × 25% | × 25% |
| **DTA ending balance** | **$11,250** | **$7,500** | **$3,750** |

Municipal bond interest creates **neither** a DTA nor a DTL (never reverses).

**Deferred-tax roll-forward (subsequent measurement emphasis)**

| Account | Beg. Y1 | Δ Y1 | End Y1 / Beg Y2 | Δ Y2 | End Y2 / Beg Y3 | Δ Y3 | End Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Liability | $0 | +6,000 | $6,000 | (6,000) | $0 | 0 | $0 |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 0 | +11,250 | 11,250 | (3,750) | 7,500 | (3,750) | 3,750 |

Δ amounts equal ending balance minus prior ending balance and are the deferred-tax effects in each period’s income-tax JE.

**(c) Period-end income tax journal entries**

**December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition**  
Income tax expense = payable + ΔDTL − ΔDTA = \(42{,}450 + 6{,}000 - 11{,}250 = \mathbf{\$37{,}200}\).  
Cross-check (permanent only): \(150{,}000 \times 25\% - 1{,}200 \times 25\% = 37{,}500 - 300 = 37{,}200\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 37,200 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 11,250 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 6,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 42,450 |
| *Record current tax and deferred taxes — multiple differences, Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(37{,}200 + 11{,}250 = 48{,}450\) = Cr \(6{,}000 + 42{,}450\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 2 — subsequent measurement / partial settlement**  
ΔDTL = \(0 - 6{,}000 = (6{,}000)\) (full reverse of prepaid).  
ΔDTA = \(7{,}500 - 11{,}250 = (3{,}750)\) (one-third warranty reverse).  
Expense = \(45{,}700 - 6{,}000 + 3{,}750 = \mathbf{\$43{,}450}\).  
Cross-check: \(175{,}000 \times 25\% - 300 = 43{,}750 - 300 = 43{,}450\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 43,450 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | 6,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,750 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 45,700 |
| *Update deferred balances to schedule; record current tax, Year 2* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(43{,}450 + 6{,}000 = 49{,}450\) = Cr \(3{,}750 + 45{,}700\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3 — continued warranty settlement**  
ΔDTA = \(3{,}750 - 7{,}500 = (3{,}750)\).  
Expense = \(30{,}950 + 3{,}750 = \mathbf{\$34{,}700}\).  
Cross-check: \(140{,}000 \times 25\% - 300 = 35{,}000 - 300 = 34{,}700\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 34,700 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,750 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 30,950 |
| *Partial reverse of DTA as warranty paid; current tax, Year 3* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(34{,}700\) = Cr \(3{,}750 + 30{,}950\). **Balanced.**

**(d) Rate reconciliation — statutory to effective**

| | Year 1 $ | Year 1 % | Year 2 $ | Year 2 % | Year 3 $ | Year 3 % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Statutory tax rate | $37,500 | 25.000% | $43,750 | 25.000% | $35,000 | 25.000% |
| Tax-exempt municipal interest | (300) | (0.200)% | (300) | (0.171)% | (300) | (0.214)% |
| **Effective tax rate** | **$37,200** | **24.800%** | **$43,450** | **24.829%** | **$34,700** | **24.786%** |

Supporting computations:  
- Statutory dollars = pretax GAAP × 25%.  
- Tax-exempt effect = \(1{,}200 \times 25\% = 300\) each year; % = \(300 / \text{pretax}\).  
- Effective rate = income tax expense / pretax GAAP income.  
Temporary differences affect **current vs deferred** components of tax expense but **do not** appear as reconciling items between statutory and effective rates when enacted rates are constant.

**(e) Presentation and remaining temporary difference (Dec 31, Year 3)**  
Same tax-paying component and jurisdiction: offset DTA and DTL → **net noncurrent deferred tax asset of $3,750** (DTL = $0; DTA = $3,750).  
Remaining deductible temporary difference = **$15,000** warranty liability expected to reverse in Year 4 → DTA \(15{,}000 \times 25\% = \$3{,}750\).

**Key insight:** With multiple differences, total tax expense = current tax payable ± changes in DTL/DTA measured from **subsequent measurement schedules**. Permanent differences change the **effective rate** and appear in the **rate reconciliation**; temporary differences drive deferred balances and reverse in future periods without creating permanent rate reconciling items (when enacted rates are constant).

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Silverpine Logistics: multi-year multi-difference twin
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — multi-year DTL/DTA schedules, period-end JEs, and rate recon with all amounts and rate changed  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpine Logistics Inc.** reports pretax GAAP income of **$400,000** (Year 1), **$450,000** (Year 2), and **$380,000** (Year 3). The enacted tax rate is **21%** for all years. Beginning deferred tax balances are zero on January 1, Year 1. Calendar year-end for books and tax.

Differences:  
1. **Prepaid insurance** of **$60,000** paid and recorded December 31, Year 1 (tax-deductible when paid; GAAP expense in Year 2).  
2. **Warranty accrual** of **$48,000** on December 31, Year 1; settled **$16,000** in each of Years 2, 3, and 4.  
3. **Municipal bond interest** of **$3,000** each year (Years 1–3), nontaxable permanent difference.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute taxable income and income tax payable for Years 1–3.  
(b) Prepare deferred tax liability and deferred tax asset **ending-balance schedules** and a **three-year roll-forward** of each deferred account.  
(c) Record the December 31 income tax journal entries for Years 1–3.  
(d) Year 1 rate reconciliation (dollars and percentages).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Taxable income and tax payable**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pretax GAAP income | $400,000 | $450,000 | $380,000 |
| Insurance | (60,000) | 60,000 | — |
| Warranty | 48,000 | (16,000) | (16,000) |
| Municipal interest | (3,000) | (3,000) | (3,000) |
| **Taxable income** | **$385,000** | **$491,000** | **$361,000** |
| **Income tax payable (×21%)** | **$80,850** | **$103,110** | **$75,810** |

Checks: \(385{,}000 \times 0.21 = 80{,}850\); \(491{,}000 \times 0.21 = 103{,}110\); \(361{,}000 \times 0.21 = 75{,}810\).

**(b) Deferred tax ending balances and roll-forward**

| | Dec 31, Y1 | Dec 31, Y2 | Dec 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cumulative taxable TD (prepaid) | $60,000 | $0 | $0 |
| **DTL (×21%)** | **$12,600** | **$0** | **$0** |
| Cumulative deductible TD (warranty) | $48,000 | $32,000 | $16,000 |
| **DTA (×21%)** | **$10,080** | **$6,720** | **$3,360** |

**Roll-forward**

| Account | Beg Y1 | Δ Y1 | End Y1 | Δ Y2 | End Y2 | Δ Y3 | End Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DTL | $0 | +12,600 | $12,600 | (12,600) | $0 | 0 | $0 |
| DTA | 0 | +10,080 | 10,080 | (3,360) | 6,720 | (3,360) | 3,360 |

**(c) Journal entries**

**December 31, Year 1**  
Expense = \(80{,}850 + 12{,}600 - 10{,}080 = \mathbf{\$83{,}370}\)  
Cross-check: \(400{,}000 \times 21\% - 3{,}000 \times 21\% = 84{,}000 - 630 = 83{,}370\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 83,370 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 10,080 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 12,600 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 80,850 |

**Check:** Dr \(83{,}370 + 10{,}080 = 93{,}450\) = Cr \(12{,}600 + 80{,}850\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 2**  
Expense = \(103{,}110 - 12{,}600 + 3{,}360 = \mathbf{\$93{,}870}\)  
Cross-check: \(450{,}000 \times 21\% - 630 = 94{,}500 - 630 = 93{,}870\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 93,870 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | 12,600 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,360 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 103,110 |

**Check:** Dr \(93{,}870 + 12{,}600 = 106{,}470\) = Cr \(3{,}360 + 103{,}110\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3**  
Expense = \(75{,}810 + 3{,}360 = \mathbf{\$79{,}170}\)  
Cross-check: \(380{,}000 \times 21\% - 630 = 79{,}800 - 630 = 79{,}170\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 79,170 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,360 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 75,810 |

**Check:** Dr \(79{,}170\) = Cr \(3{,}360 + 75{,}810\). **Balanced.**

**(d) Year 1 rate reconciliation**

| | Dollars | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Statutory tax rate (\(400{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | $84,000 | 21.000% |
| Tax-exempt municipal interest (\(3{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | (630) | (0.1575)% |
| **Effective tax rate** (\(83{,}370 / 400{,}000\)) | **$83,370** | **20.8425%** |

**Key insight:** Changing the rate and all temporary/permanent amounts scales current tax, deferred schedule balances, and the reconciling item, but the structure is identical: **subsequent measurement schedules** set ending DTA/DTL; permanent differences alone drive statutory-to-effective rate reconciling items when rates are constant.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Quarry Bend: multi-difference JE, settlement year, dual permanents, BS netting
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Single-period multi-difference recognition with nontaxable **and** nondeductible permanents; Year-2 settlement/partial reverse of temporary differences; rate reconciliation; net noncurrent deferred tax presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Quarry Bend Retail Inc.** has a December 31 year-end. Enacted tax rate is **21%** for all years. Deferred tax accounts begin at **zero** on January 1, Year 1.

**Year 1** pretax GAAP income: **$275,000**, including:  
- Life insurance **proceeds (gain)** of **$15,000** on a key-person policy (nontaxable permanent difference).  
- Premiums on executive life insurance of **$3,000** (nondeductible permanent difference).  

Temporary differences originating in Year 1:  
1. **Prepaid rent** (GAAP asset) **$36,000**; tax basis **$0** (fully deducted for tax when paid) → taxable temporary difference. Coverage is for Year 2 only.  
2. **Accrued warranty liability** (GAAP) **$50,000**; tax basis **$0** (deductible when paid) → deductible temporary difference. Settlements: **$25,000 in Year 2** and **$25,000 in Year 3**.

**Year 2** pretax GAAP income: **$310,000**. No permanent differences in Year 2. During Year 2: prepaid rent expires (fully expensed for GAAP); **$25,000** of the warranty liability is settled in cash (tax deductible when paid). No new temporary differences arise in Year 2.

**Required:**  
(a) For **Year 1**, compute taxable income, income tax payable, DTL, DTA, and income tax expense. Record the December 31, Year 1 income tax entry.  
(b) Prepare the **Year 1 rate reconciliation** in dollars and percentages (include both permanent items).  
(c) State December 31, Year 1 **balance-sheet presentation** of deferred taxes (same component and jurisdiction).  
(d) For **Year 2**, compute taxable income, tax payable, deferred tax changes (from schedules), and income tax expense. Record the December 31, Year 2 entry that settles/reverses temporary differences.  
(e) Briefly explain why temporary differences do **not** appear as reconciling items in Quarry Bend’s Year 1 rate reconciliation.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Year 1 — initial multi-difference recognition**

Taxable income:  
\[
275{,}000 - 36{,}000 + 50{,}000 - 15{,}000 + 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$277{,}000}
\]  
Income tax payable = \(277{,}000 \times 21\% = \mathbf{\$58{,}170}\).

| Item | Cumulative TD | × 21% | Deferred tax |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Prepaid rent (taxable TD) | $36,000 | $7,560 | **DTL $7,560** |
| Warranty accrual (deductible TD) | $50,000 | $10,500 | **DTA $10,500** |

Income tax expense = \(58{,}170 + 7{,}560 - 10{,}500 = \mathbf{\$55{,}230}\).  
Cross-check (permanents only):  
\(275{,}000 \times 21\% - 15{,}000 \times 21\% + 3{,}000 \times 21\% = 57{,}750 - 3{,}150 + 630 = 55{,}230\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 55,230 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 10,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 7,560 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 58,170 |
| *Year 1 current + deferred tax with dual permanent differences* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(55{,}230 + 10{,}500 = 65{,}730\) = Cr \(7{,}560 + 58{,}170\). **Balanced.**

**(b) Year 1 rate reconciliation**

| | Dollars | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Statutory tax rate (\(275{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | $57,750 | 21.000% |
| Tax-exempt life insurance proceeds (\(15{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | (3,150) | (1.145)% |
| Nondeductible life insurance premiums (\(3{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | 630 | 0.229% |
| **Effective tax rate** (\(55{,}230 / 275{,}000\)) | **$55,230** | **20.084%** |

**(c) Balance-sheet presentation — December 31, Year 1**  
Offset DTA and DTL for the same tax-paying component/jurisdiction:  
Net noncurrent **deferred tax asset** = \(10{,}500 - 7{,}560 = \mathbf{\$2{,}940}\).

**(d) Year 2 — settlement / partial reverse of temporary differences**

Taxable income:  
\[
310{,}000 + 36{,}000 - 25{,}000 = \mathbf{\$321{,}000}
\]  
(Insurance reverse adds to taxable; warranty cash payment is tax-deductible.)  
Income tax payable = \(321{,}000 \times 21\% = \mathbf{\$67{,}410}\).

Ending DTL = $0; ending DTA = \(25{,}000 \times 21\% = \$5{,}250\).  
ΔDTL = \((7{,}560)\); ΔDTA = \(5{,}250 - 10{,}500 = (5{,}250)\).  
Expense = \(67{,}410 - 7{,}560 + 5{,}250 = \mathbf{\$65{,}100}\).  
Cross-check: \(310{,}000 \times 21\% = 65{,}100\) (no permanent differences in Year 2).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 65,100 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | 7,560 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,250 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 67,410 |
| *Reverse prepaid DTL; partial reverse warranty DTA; current tax* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(65{,}100 + 7{,}560 = 72{,}660\) = Cr \(5{,}250 + 67{,}410\). **Balanced.**

**(e) Why temporary differences omit from the rate recon**  
With a constant enacted rate, originating/reversing temporary differences shift tax between **current** (payable) and **deferred** components but leave **total income tax expense** equal to pretax GAAP × statutory rate adjusted **only** for permanent items. Therefore the rate reconciliation explains only permanent (and other non-temporary) effects such as tax-exempt income and nondeductible expenses.

**Key insight:** Nontaxable items **lower** the effective rate; nondeductible items **raise** it. Temporary differences are tracked on deferred tax **subsequent measurement schedules** and reverse at settlement (cash payment, asset consumption) without permanent effective-rate reconciling items when rates do not change.

### Q4 — MC (classification / presentation)
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Rate-reconciliation classification and deferred tax balance-sheet presentation  

**Question 1:**  
**Lakeshore Products Co.** has pretax GAAP income of $200,000 and a 25% enacted rate. During the year it recorded (1) prepaid insurance of $16,000 that was tax-deductible when paid and (2) $2,000 of municipal bond interest included in pretax income. Which item(s) appear as reconciling items in the statutory-to-effective **rate reconciliation**?

- A) Prepaid insurance temporary difference only  
- B) Municipal bond interest only  
- C) Both the prepaid temporary difference and municipal bond interest  
- D) Neither item  

**Answer:** **B.** Only permanent differences (here, tax-exempt municipal interest) create differences between statutory tax dollars on pretax GAAP income and income tax expense when rates are constant. The prepaid creates a deferred tax liability and affects the split between current and deferred tax, but not the effective-rate reconciling line.

**Question 2:**  
For a single tax-paying component in one tax jurisdiction, **Cedar Point Systems Inc.** has a deferred tax asset of $22,000 and a deferred tax liability of $30,000 (no valuation allowance). How should Cedar Point present deferred taxes on a classified balance sheet under ASC 740?

- A) Current DTA $22,000 and noncurrent DTL $30,000  
- B) Noncurrent DTA $22,000 and noncurrent DTL $30,000 (gross)  
- C) Single noncurrent deferred tax liability of $8,000  
- D) Single current deferred tax liability of $8,000  

**Answer:** **C.** All deferred tax assets and liabilities for a particular tax-paying component within a particular jurisdiction are offset and presented as a **single noncurrent** amount. Net = \(30{,}000 - 22{,}000 = \$8{,}000\) noncurrent DTL.
