# Agent 325 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-7

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Explain accounting treatment of inventory errors  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Inventory error treatment depends on **three factors**: (1) **when** the error is discovered, (2) whether it affects the **balance sheet only** or **BS + income**, and (3) whether the error is **counterbalancing** (self-corrects within two consecutive periods) or **noncounterbalancing**
- **Discovered in the year of the error:** reverse/correct through the same accounts that are still open (typically **COGS** and **Inventory**); prior-period restatement is not required
- **Discovered in a subsequent period — counterbalancing, before self-correction:** record a **prior-period adjustment** (Dr/Cr **Retained Earnings—PPA** and Inventory); restate all comparative statements presented
- **Discovered after self-correction:** **no correcting journal entry** (books already correct), but **restate** comparative financial statements for the misstated years
- **Balance-sheet-only errors** (e.g., misclassification WIP vs FG): reclassifying entry if still on the BS; restate presentations
- **Noncounterbalancing** (e.g., purchase capitalized as depreciable equipment): prior-period adjustment that also unwinds wrong depreciation; restate all periods presented
- Classic **counterbalancing count error:** ending inventory overstated → Year 1 COGS understated / pretax income overstated; Year 2 beginning inventory overstated → Year 2 COGS overstated / pretax income understated (net zero over two years if both years closed)
- Combined errors: net COGS effect = effect of BI error + effect of EI error (BI over → COGS over; EI over → COGS under)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** correct physical-count / cutoff errors at year-end so inventory and COGS are right before statements are issued
- **Purchase/inventory cutoff pairings:** omitted purchase + omitted inventory often leave COGS correct but understate AP and inventory; counted-but-not-recorded purchase understates COGS and AP, overstates pretax income

### Q1 — CORE — Cedarline Trading: EI overstatement — discovery timing, effect schedule, period-end JE, PPA, post-self-correction

**LO:** LO 10-7  
**Concept:** Counterbalancing ending-inventory overstatement; **period-end adjusting JE** in year of error (emphasis); multi-year effect schedule; subsequent-period PPA before self-correction; no entry after self-correction (settlement)  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Trading Co.** uses a **periodic** inventory system and takes a physical count each **December 31**. Purchases are recorded correctly. On December 31, **Year 1**, a mathematical error in the physical count causes **ending inventory to be overstated by $8,400**. Year 2 ending inventory is **correct**. Pretax amounts on the books **with the error uncorrected** are:

| | Year 1 (as recorded) | Year 2 (as recorded) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $42,000 | $58,000 (Y1 EI; overstated) |
| Net purchases | 186,000 | 195,000 |
| Ending inventory | 58,000 (overstated $8,400) | 51,000 (correct) |
| Net sales | 240,000 | 285,000 |
| Other expenses (exclude COGS) | 25,000 | 28,000 |

Ignore income taxes. The company would restate any comparative statements that remain misstated.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **reported** COGS, gross profit, and pretax income for Year 1 and Year 2 as recorded with the error. Then prepare a **subsequent-period effect schedule** showing correct amounts and the over/under effect on BI, EI, COGS, and pretax income for each year.  
(b) Assume the error is discovered on **December 31, Year 1** before books close. Record the **period-end correcting journal entry** (emphasis). Show corrected pretax income for Year 1.  
(c) Instead, assume the error is discovered on **January 5, Year 2** after Year 1 books are closed and statements issued. Record the **correcting entry** in Year 2 (before self-correction). Describe restatement.  
(d) Instead, assume the error is first discovered in **Year 3** after Year 2 has closed (after self-correction). Record any journal entry in Year 3 and describe financial-statement treatment.  
(e) Map the three discovery timings to Exhibit 10-2 type (same period / counterbalancing before self-correction / after self-correction).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Reported amounts with error and multi-year effect schedule**

Reported COGS:  
- Year 1: \(42{,}000 + 186{,}000 - 58{,}000 = \mathbf{\$170{,}000}\)  
- Year 2: \(58{,}000 + 195{,}000 - 51{,}000 = \mathbf{\$202{,}000}\)

Reported gross profit:  
- Year 1: \(240{,}000 - 170{,}000 = \mathbf{\$70{,}000}\)  
- Year 2: \(285{,}000 - 202{,}000 = \mathbf{\$83{,}000}\)

Reported pretax income:  
- Year 1: \(70{,}000 - 25{,}000 = \mathbf{\$45{,}000}\)  
- Year 2: \(83{,}000 - 28{,}000 = \mathbf{\$55{,}000}\)

**Correct** amounts (EI Y1 should be \(58{,}000 - 8{,}400 = \mathbf{\$49{,}600}\)):  
- COGS Y1: \(42{,}000 + 186{,}000 - 49{,}600 = \mathbf{\$178{,}400}\)  
- COGS Y2: \(49{,}600 + 195{,}000 - 51{,}000 = \mathbf{\$193{,}600}\)  
- Pretax Y1: \(240{,}000 - 178{,}400 - 25{,}000 = \mathbf{\$36{,}600}\)  
- Pretax Y2: \(285{,}000 - 193{,}600 - 28{,}000 = \mathbf{\$63{,}400}\)

**Subsequent measurement / effect schedule (overstatement of EI Year 1 by $8,400)**

| Item | Year 1 effect | Year 2 effect | Two-year net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginning inventory | — (correct) | **Overstated $8,400** | Nets to 0 after Y2 opens correctly post-correction |
| Ending inventory | **Overstated $8,400** | Unaffected (count correct) | Self-corrects as BI of Y2 |
| Cost of goods sold | **Understated $8,400** | **Overstated $8,400** | **$0** (counterbalances) |
| Pretax income | **Overstated $8,400** | **Understated $8,400** | **$0** |
| Retained earnings (end of year, uncorrected) | Overstated $8,400 | Correct (offsetting income errors) | Correct after Y2 closes |

**Proof of counterbalancing:** reported pretax Y1 + Y2 = \(45{,}000 + 55{,}000 = 100{,}000\); correct = \(36{,}600 + 63{,}400 = 100{,}000\). Same cumulative pretax after two years.

**(b) Discovered December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 8,400 | |
| Inventory | | 8,400 |
| *Correct overstated physical-count ending inventory in year of error* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,400 = Cr 8,400. **Balanced.**

After correction:  
- Inventory = \(58{,}000 - 8{,}400 = \mathbf{\$49{,}600}\)  
- COGS = \(170{,}000 + 8{,}400 = \mathbf{\$178{,}400}\)  
- Pretax income = \(45{,}000 - 8{,}400 = \mathbf{\$36{,}600}\)  
Year 1 statements are **correct** as issued; no prior-period restatement.

**(c) Discovered January 5, Year 2 — before self-correction (PPA)**

Year 1 is closed; Income Summary / COGS for Year 1 cannot be used. Correct Inventory and open RE:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | 8,400 | |
| Inventory | | 8,400 |
| *Prior-period adjustment: remove overstated inventory from closed Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,400 = Cr 8,400. **Balanced.**

**Restatement:** All comparative financial statements presented that include Year 1 (and opening RE for Year 2) are **restated** so Y1 inventory, COGS, pretax income, and ending RE are correct. After the PPA, Year 2 beginning inventory is correct, so Year 2 current-period income is measured correctly going forward.

**(d) Discovered in Year 3 — after self-correction (settlement / no book entry)**

By the end of Year 2 the overstated BI has fully flowed through COGS; inventory on the books at 12/31/Y2 is already correct.  

**Journal entry in Year 3:** **None.**

**Financial statements:** Restate comparative statements for Year 1 and Year 2 so each year’s COGS and pretax income are correct (even though cumulative RE after Year 2 was already correct without an entry).

**(e) Exhibit 10-2 mapping**

| Discovery timing | Type | Correcting entry | FS impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Error discovered in **same** accounting period | Reverse/correct via COGS & Inventory | No prior-period impact |
| Jan 5, Year 2 | Subsequent period; BS + IS; **counterbalancing**, **before** self-correction | **PPA** to RE and Inventory | Restate all statements presented |
| Year 3 | Subsequent period; counterbalancing, **after** self-correction | **No entry** | Restate comparative statements |

**Key insight:** An EI count error counterbalances over two years. Correct **through COGS** if still in the year of the error; use a **RE prior-period adjustment** if discovered after close but before self-correction; **no JE** after self-correction, but always **restate** misstated comparative periods.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Harborview Supply: EI understatement twin (discovery timings + schedule)

**LO:** LO 10-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — counterbalancing **understatement** of ending inventory; period-end correcting JE; multi-year effect schedule; PPA; post-self-correction  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborview Supply Inc.** uses a **periodic** inventory system. At December 31, **Year 1**, goods in a remote warehouse were **omitted** from the physical count, understating ending inventory by **$12,750**. Purchases are correct. Year 2 ending inventory is correct. Amounts **as recorded with the error**:

| | Year 1 (as recorded) | Year 2 (as recorded) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $55,000 | $59,750 (Y1 EI; understated) |
| Net purchases | 248,000 | 261,500 |
| Ending inventory | 59,750 (understated $12,750) | 67,200 (correct) |
| Net sales | 390,000 | 425,000 |
| Other expenses (exclude COGS) | 41,000 | 44,500 |

Ignore taxes.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute reported COGS and pretax income for both years; prepare the **effect schedule** (direction and amount) for COGS and pretax income in Year 1 and Year 2; prove two-year cumulative pretax is correct.  
(b) Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end correcting JE** if discovered before books close. Show corrected pretax income Year 1.  
(c) Record the **January 1, Year 2 PPA** if discovered after Year 1 closed.  
(d) State the Year 3 treatment if first discovered after Year 2 closed.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Reported amounts and effect schedule**

Reported COGS:  
- Y1: \(55{,}000 + 248{,}000 - 59{,}750 = \mathbf{\$243{,}250}\)  
- Y2: \(59{,}750 + 261{,}500 - 67{,}200 = \mathbf{\$254{,}050}\)

Reported pretax:  
- Y1: \(390{,}000 - 243{,}250 - 41{,}000 = \mathbf{\$105{,}750}\)  
- Y2: \(425{,}000 - 254{,}050 - 44{,}500 = \mathbf{\$126{,}450}\)

Correct EI Y1 = \(59{,}750 + 12{,}750 = \mathbf{\$72{,}500}\).  
Correct COGS Y1 = \(55{,}000 + 248{,}000 - 72{,}500 = \mathbf{\$230{,}500}\).  
Correct COGS Y2 = \(72{,}500 + 261{,}500 - 67{,}200 = \mathbf{\$266{,}800}\).  
Correct pretax Y1 = \(390{,}000 - 230{,}500 - 41{,}000 = \mathbf{\$118{,}500}\).  
Correct pretax Y2 = \(425{,}000 - 266{,}800 - 44{,}500 = \mathbf{\$113{,}700}\).

| Item | Year 1 effect | Year 2 effect | Two-year net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ending / beginning inventory | EI **under $12,750** | BI **under $12,750** | Self-corrects |
| Cost of goods sold | **Overstated $12,750** | **Understated $12,750** | **$0** |
| Pretax income | **Understated $12,750** | **Overstated $12,750** | **$0** |

Cumulative reported pretax: \(105{,}750 + 126{,}450 = 232{,}200\).  
Cumulative correct pretax: \(118{,}500 + 113{,}700 = 232{,}200\). **Counterbalances.**

**(b) Period-end correcting JE — December 31, Year 1 (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 12,750 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 12,750 |
| *Add omitted warehouse goods to ending inventory; reduce COGS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,750 = Cr 12,750. **Balanced.**

Corrected pretax Y1 = \(105{,}750 + 12{,}750 = \mathbf{\$118{,}500}\).

**(c) Discovered January 1, Year 2 — PPA before self-correction**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 12,750 | |
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | | 12,750 |
| *Restore understated inventory; increase beginning RE for prior understated income* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,750 = Cr 12,750. **Balanced.** Restate Year 1 comparative statements.

**(d) Discovered in Year 3 after self-correction**

**No journal entry.** Restate comparative Year 1 and Year 2 income statement amounts (COGS and pretax) for disclosure; ending RE after Year 2 was already correct.

**Key insight:** Understated EI is the mirror image of overstated EI—directions reverse, but the same discovery rules apply (COGS correction in year of error; RE-PPA next year; no entry after counterbalancing).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Iron Peak Outfitters: cutoff error matrix, period-end JEs, noncounterbalancing equipment mispost

**LO:** LO 10-7  
**Concept:** Multi-error effect classification schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs** for cutoff/count errors (emphasis); balance-sheet-only reclass; noncounterbalancing purchase-as-equipment PPA settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Iron Peak Outfitters LLC** uses a **periodic** inventory system; year-end is **December 31**. Ignore taxes. Consider the following **independent** situations discovered in the course of year-end procedures or later audits.

**Situation set A — effects analysis (each independent; periodic system)**  
For each error, indicate the effect on **current-year COGS**, **current-year pretax income**, **current-year ending inventory**, **next-year COGS**, and **next-year ending inventory** (overstated / understated / no effect). Assume the next-year physical count is correct unless the error inherently continues.

| # | Error description |
|---|---|
| A1 | Ending inventory **overstated** $6,200 due to count math error (purchases correct). |
| A2 | Ending inventory **understated** $4,100 due to omitted goods (purchases correct). |
| A3 | Merchandise purchased on credit Dec 31 **included in ending inventory**, but purchase **not recorded** until January when invoice arrived. |
| A4 | Purchase **recorded** Dec 28, but goods **f.o.b. shipping point** still in transit were **excluded** from the physical count. |
| A5 | Purchase **f.o.b. shipping point** Dec 30: purchase **not recorded** and goods **not counted**. |
| A6 | Goods held on **consignment for a supplier** were **included** in the physical inventory. |
| A7 | Beginning inventory of the current year was **overstated** $3,000 because last year’s EI error was never corrected (count this year is correct). |

**Situation set B — period-end correcting entries (same company, Year 5)**  
Before statements are issued for Year 5, Iron Peak discovers:

1. Physical count **overstated inventory by $9,600** (count already booked to Inventory / COGS via the year-end inventory adjustment).  
2. Goods on consignment from **Summit Brands** of **$5,200** were included in the count (and thus in Inventory).  
3. Unrecorded **f.o.b. shipping-point** purchase still in transit: invoice cost **$7,800** on account; goods **not counted**.  
4. Work-in-process of **$14,000** was misclassified as finished goods (balance-sheet only; total inventory correct).

**Situation set C — noncounterbalancing error discovered Year 7**  
On January 1, Year 5, Iron Peak purchased merchandise inventory for **$40,000** cash but incorrectly debited **Equipment**. Straight-line depreciation was taken for Year 5 and Year 6: 10-year life, no salvage (\(40{,}000 / 10 = \$4{,}000\) per year). All of that merchandise was **sold in Year 5** (should have been COGS Year 5). The error is discovered **January 10, Year 7**. Accumulated depreciation on the bogus equipment is **$8,000**. Equipment still shows **$40,000** gross.

**Required:**  
(a) Complete the **effect matrix** for A1–A7 (use O / U / NE).  
(b) Record **period-end adjusting/correcting journal entries** for B1–B4 at December 31, Year 5. Prove each balances.  
(c) Record the **January 10, Year 7 prior-period adjustment** to settle Situation C (remove equipment and accum. dep.; adjust RE for the net equity error). Show the analysis of the RE effect.  
(d) Briefly classify A3 and Situation C under Exhibit 10-2 (counterbalancing vs noncounterbalancing; BS only vs BS+IS).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Effect matrix**

| # | CY COGS | CY Pretax | CY EI | NY COGS | NY EI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 EI over $6,200 | **U** $6,200 | **O** $6,200 | **O** $6,200 | **O** $6,200 | **NE** |
| A2 EI under $4,100 | **O** $4,100 | **U** $4,100 | **U** $4,100 | **U** $4,100 | **NE** |
| A3 EI in / purch not recorded | **U** (purch under & EI over net: COGS under by amount) | **O** | **O** | **O** (BI over; Jan purchase records when paid/booked later may reverse purch timing—standard result: CY COGS under, NY COGS over if purchase booked NY and BI over) | **NE** after next count |
| A4 Purch recorded / not counted | **O** (EI under) | **U** | **U** | **U** (BI under) | **NE** |
| A5 Neither recorded nor counted | **NE** (purch under and EI under cancel in COGS) | **NE** | **U** | **NE** for COGS if purch booked NY and count correct* | **NE** |
| A6 Consigned goods counted | **U** (EI over) | **O** | **O** | **O** | **NE** |
| A7 BI over $3,000; CY count OK | **O** $3,000 | **U** $3,000 | **NE** | **NE** (self-corrected when prior year closed into RE / BI no longer wrong next period after this year) | **NE** |

\*A5 detail: CY: Purchases understated and EI understated by same amount → COGS **no effect**; pretax **no effect**; but Inventory and AP both **understated** (BS misstated). Next year when purchase is recorded, if goods are no longer on hand, COGS timing depends on when sale occurs—classic textbook treatment for pure omission of both is **COGS correct both years**, inventory/AP wrong only until purchase is booked and count is correct.

Standard textbook answers for A3–A6 (periodic, as in Review 10-7 style):

| # | CY COGS | CY EI | NY COGS | NY EI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A3 | Understated | Overstated | Overstated | No effect |
| A4 | Overstated | Understated | Understated | No effect |
| A5 | No effect | Understated | No effect | No effect |
| A6 | Understated | Overstated | Overstated | No effect |

A7 (BI over only this year, EI correct): CY COGS overstated, CY EI no effect; NY no remaining inventory error.

**(b) Period-end correcting JEs — December 31, Year 5 (emphasis)**

**B1 — Overstated count $9,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 9,600 | |
| Inventory | | 9,600 |
| *Reduce overstated physical inventory; increase COGS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,600 = Cr 9,600. **Balanced.**

**B2 — Remove consigned goods $5,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 5,200 | |
| Inventory | | 5,200 |
| *Remove goods held for Summit Brands (not owned)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,200 = Cr 5,200. **Balanced.**  
*(If the year-end inventory close had not yet increased Inventory for the count, the correction would instead reduce the inventory-count adjustment; once Inventory is stated from the erroneous count, credit Inventory.)*

**B3 — Unrecorded FOB shipping-point purchase in transit $7,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 7,800 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 7,800 |
| *Record purchase and include in-transit goods controlled by Iron Peak* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,800 = Cr 7,800. **Balanced.**  
(Periodic alternative often used: Dr Purchases 7,800 Cr AP 7,800 **and** include goods in EI so COGS is correct. Net effect on pretax is zero if both Purchases and EI increase by $7,800. Either presentation is acceptable if COGS and inventory are correct.)

**B4 — BS-only reclass WIP vs FG $14,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory—Work in Process | 14,000 | |
| Inventory—Finished Goods | | 14,000 |
| *Reclassify mislabeled inventory; total inventory unchanged* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,000 = Cr 14,000. **Balanced.** No income effect.

**(c) Situation C — noncounterbalancing settlement January 10, Year 7**

**Analysis of prior equity error (ignore taxes):**  
- Year 5 should have increased COGS (decreased income) by **$40,000** when goods were sold, but instead recorded equipment.  
- Years 5–6 depreciation expense **overstated** total income reduction by \(4{,}000 \times 2 = \mathbf{\$8{,}000}\) (wrong expense that partially offset the missing COGS over time).  
- Net: pretax income over two closed years was **overstated by \(40{,}000 - 8{,}000 = \$32{,}000\)** (RE overstated $32,000).  
- Books still show Equipment $40,000 and Accumulated Depreciation $8,000 (net equipment **$32,000** that should be zero).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 8,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | 32,000 | |
| Equipment | | 40,000 |
| *Remove misposted equipment; reduce RE for net prior overstatement of income* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(8{,}000 + 32{,}000 = 40{,}000\); Cr 40,000. **Balanced.**

Restate Year 5 and Year 6 statements presented (Y5 COGS up 40,000, dep. expense down 4,000; Y6 dep. expense down 4,000).

**(d) Classification**

| Item | When discovered | BS only? | Counterbalancing? | Entry type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A3 (EI in, purch late) | If later year before/after self-correction | No (BS + IS) | **Yes** (typically self-corrects over two periods) | PPA if before self-correction; none after |
| Situation C | Subsequent periods | No (BS + IS) | **No** (depreciation spreads error) | **PPA required** until fully removed |

**Key insight:** Period-end work focuses on **ownership and cutoff** (count, consignment, FOB). Counterbalancing inventory errors fix themselves in income over two years; **noncounterbalancing** misposts (inventory vs long-lived asset) require a **PPA** that clears the residual BS accounts and the net RE error.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)

**LO:** LO 10-7  
**Concept:** Classification of counterbalancing discovery timing; combined BI/EI error effect on COGS  

**Question 1:**  
On December 31, Year 1, **Lakeshore Parts Co.** overstated ending inventory by $18,000. The error was not discovered until **March of Year 3**, after Year 2 statements had been issued and Year 2 books closed. Lakeshore’s Year 2 ending inventory was correct. Ignoring taxes, the March Year 3 correcting entry is:

- A) Debit Retained Earnings $18,000; credit Inventory $18,000  
- B) Debit Cost of Goods Sold $18,000; credit Inventory $18,000  
- C) Debit Inventory $18,000; credit Retained Earnings $18,000  
- D) No journal entry is required  

**Answer:** **D.** The Year 1 EI overstatement counterbalanced through Year 2 COGS. By Year 3, inventory and cumulative RE are correct; **no entry**. Comparative Year 1–Year 2 statements are still **restated** for presentation.

**Question 2:**  
**Ridgeway Merchants** reports beginning inventory **overstated by $15,000** and ending inventory **understated by $6,000** for the current year (purchases correct). Current-year cost of goods sold is:

- A) Understated by $9,000  
- B) Overstated by $9,000  
- C) Overstated by $21,000  
- D) Understated by $21,000  

**Answer:** **C.** COGS = BI + Purch − EI. BI overstated $15,000 → COGS **over** $15,000. EI understated $6,000 → subtracting too little → COGS **over** $6,000. Net COGS **overstated by \(15{,}000 + 6{,}000 = \$21{,}000\)**.
