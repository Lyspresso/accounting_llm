# Agent 184 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-6

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Explain and compute a LIFO reserve  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **LIFO reserve** = inventory at internal method (FIFO / average / standard) − inventory at LIFO
- **Initial recognition JE** (first year of LIFO external reporting): create **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis** (contra-inventory); Dr COGS for the **full** ending reserve
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year rollforward of internal EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve, COGS effect, and allowance balance
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** after Year 1, adjust the allowance **only for the change** in the reserve (not the full ending reserve)
- **Reverse / settlement JE** when the reserve **decreases**: Dr Allowance, Cr COGS (partial reverse of prior build-up)
- **Classification / presentation:** inventory reported net of allowance at LIFO cost; note disclosure of LIFO reserve for peer comparability
- **Outside-the-books conversion:** compute and disclose reserve without a formal JE
- **COGS bridge:** LIFO COGS = FIFO (or internal) COGS + increase in LIFO reserve (Δ reserve)

### Q1 — CORE — Hearthstone Outdoor: multi-year LIFO reserve schedule, create, adjust up, reverse down
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule of LIFO reserve over four years; initial recognition JE; period-end Δ-reserve adjusting JEs; reverse entry when reserve falls; BS net presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Hearthstone Outdoor Goods Inc.** keeps **FIFO** inventory records for internal control and converts to **LIFO** at each year-end for external financial reporting and income tax. Year 1 is the company’s first year of operations. Year-end is **December 31**. Conversion to LIFO is **recognized in the accounts** using **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis** (contra-inventory).

Inventory valuations at each year-end:

| Date | Ending inventory at FIFO | Ending inventory at LIFO |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $78,000 | $51,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 94,000 | 58,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 110,000 | 67,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 88,000 | 64,000 |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (emphasis) for Years 1–4 with columns: FIFO EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve (current − prior), effect on COGS, and ending allowance balance.  
(b) Record the **December 31, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry that creates the LIFO allowance.  
(c) Record the **period-end adjusting entries** on December 31 of Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4.  
(d) Present **Inventory** on comparative balance sheets at December 31, Year 3 and Year 4 (FIFO gross, allowance, net LIFO).  
(e) Compute the **cumulative net debit (or credit) to COGS** from all LIFO-reserve entries over Years 1–4 and reconcile it to the Year 4 ending reserve.  
(f) Briefly state why only the **change** in the reserve (not the full reserve) is booked after Year 1.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**

Formula: **LIFO reserve = FIFO inventory − LIFO inventory**  
Δ reserve = current-year reserve − prior-year reserve (Year 1: full create).

| Date | FIFO EI | LIFO EI | LIFO reserve | Δ reserve | Effect on COGS | Ending allowance (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $78,000 | $51,000 | **$27,000** | +27,000 (create) | Increase COGS $27,000 | **$27,000** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 94,000 | 58,000 | **36,000** | +9,000 | Increase COGS $9,000 | **36,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 110,000 | 67,000 | **43,000** | +7,000 | Increase COGS $7,000 | **43,000** |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 88,000 | 64,000 | **24,000** | −19,000 | Decrease COGS $19,000 | **24,000** |

Checks:  
- Y1: \(78{,}000 - 51{,}000 = \mathbf{27{,}000}\)  
- Y2: \(94{,}000 - 58{,}000 = \mathbf{36{,}000}\); Δ = \(36{,}000 - 27{,}000 = \mathbf{+9{,}000}\)  
- Y3: \(110{,}000 - 67{,}000 = \mathbf{43{,}000}\); Δ = \(43{,}000 - 36{,}000 = \mathbf{+7{,}000}\)  
- Y4: \(88{,}000 - 64{,}000 = \mathbf{24{,}000}\); Δ = \(24{,}000 - 43{,}000 = \mathbf{-19{,}000}\)  
- Allowance rollforward: \(27{,}000 + 9{,}000 + 7{,}000 - 19{,}000 = \mathbf{24{,}000}\)

**Allowance T-account (credit balance):**

| Debit | Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | Credit |
|---:|---|---:|
| | | 27,000 (Y1 create) |
| | | 9,000 (Y2 increase) |
| | | 7,000 (Y3 increase) |
| 19,000 (Y4 decrease) | | |
| | **Bal. after Y4: 24,000 Cr** | |

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Initial recognition (create LIFO allowance)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 27,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 27,000 |
| *Create year-end LIFO reserve: FIFO $78,000 − LIFO $51,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 27,000 = Cr 27,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Period-end adjusting entries — Years 2, 3, and 4**

*December 31, Year 2 — Increase in LIFO reserve*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 9,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 9,000 |
| *Adjust allowance for Δ reserve: $36,000 − $27,000 = $9,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(27{,}000 + 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$36{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 3 — Increase in LIFO reserve*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 7,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 7,000 |
| *Adjust allowance for Δ reserve: $43,000 − $36,000 = $7,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,000 = Cr 7,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(36{,}000 + 7{,}000 = \mathbf{\$43{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 4 — Decrease in LIFO reserve (reverse / settlement of prior build-up)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | 19,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 19,000 |
| *Adjust allowance for Δ reserve: $24,000 − $43,000 = $(19,000)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 19,000 = Cr 19,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(43{,}000 - 19{,}000 = \mathbf{\$24{,}000}\).

**(d) Comparative balance-sheet presentation (inventory section)**

| | Dec 31, Year 3 | Dec 31, Year 4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (at FIFO) | $110,000 | $88,000 |
| Less: Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | (43,000) | (24,000) |
| **Inventory, net (at LIFO)** | **$67,000** | **$64,000** |

(Alternatively, report a single line “Inventory (LIFO)” of $67,000 / $64,000 and disclose the LIFO reserve in the notes.)

**(e) Cumulative COGS effect (Years 1–4)**  
Net debit to COGS = \(27{,}000 + 9{,}000 + 7{,}000 - 19{,}000 = \mathbf{\$24{,}000}\).  
Equals Year 4 ending LIFO reserve of **$24,000** (cumulative amount by which COGS has been increased relative to FIFO).

**(f)** After Year 1 the allowance already holds the prior-year full reserve. The books must bring the allowance to the **current** reserve only, so only the **Δ** is recorded. Booking the full ending reserve each year would double-count prior periods.

**Key insight:** The LIFO reserve is the **full** FIFO−LIFO gap every year; the **journal entry** books only that year’s **change** (except the first-year create). A multi-year subsequent measurement schedule shows the allowance rolling to the current reserve, with COGS rising when the reserve grows and falling when the reserve shrinks.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Lakeshore Parts: average-cost internal method twin
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — subsequent measurement schedule of LIFO reserve (average cost vs LIFO); initial create JE; period-end Δ JEs including reverse; BS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Parts & Supply Co.** uses **weighted-average cost** for internal inventory records and **LIFO** for external reporting and tax. Year 1 is the first year of operations. Adjustments are recognized **in the accounts**. Year-end is **December 31**.

| Date | Ending inventory at average cost | Ending inventory at LIFO |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $156,000 | $102,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 180,000 | 115,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 165,000 | 128,000 |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** (average-cost EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve, COGS effect, ending allowance).  
(b) Journal entry December 31, Year 1 (create allowance).  
(c) Journal entries December 31, Year 2 and Year 3.  
(d) Show Inventory on the December 31, Year 3 balance sheet (gross internal method, allowance, net LIFO).  
(e) Compute cumulative net COGS effect over Years 1–3 from LIFO-reserve entries and reconcile to ending reserve.  
(f) Assume Year 2 **LIFO COGS** (before the reserve adjustment is isolated conceptually as the “LIFO-basis” COGS) is **$740,000**. Using the Δ reserve, compute **average-cost COGS** for Year 2.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule**

LIFO reserve = average-cost inventory − LIFO inventory

| Date | Avg-cost EI | LIFO EI | LIFO reserve | Δ reserve | Effect on COGS | Ending allowance (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $156,000 | $102,000 | **$54,000** | +54,000 | +COGS $54,000 | **$54,000** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 180,000 | 115,000 | **65,000** | +11,000 | +COGS $11,000 | **65,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 165,000 | 128,000 | **37,000** | −28,000 | −COGS $28,000 | **37,000** |

Checks:  
- Y1: \(156{,}000 - 102{,}000 = \mathbf{54{,}000}\)  
- Y2: \(180{,}000 - 115{,}000 = \mathbf{65{,}000}\); Δ = \(65{,}000 - 54{,}000 = \mathbf{+11{,}000}\)  
- Y3: \(165{,}000 - 128{,}000 = \mathbf{37{,}000}\); Δ = \(37{,}000 - 65{,}000 = \mathbf{-28{,}000}\)  
- Allowance: \(54{,}000 + 11{,}000 - 28{,}000 = \mathbf{37{,}000}\)

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Create allowance**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 54,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 54,000 |
| *Initial LIFO reserve: $156,000 − $102,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 54,000 = Cr 54,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Subsequent period-end adjustments**

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 11,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 11,000 |
| *Δ reserve +$11,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,000 = Cr 11,000. **Balanced.** Allowance bal. = \(54{,}000 + 11{,}000 = \mathbf{\$65{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 3 — Decrease (reverse)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | 28,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 28,000 |
| *Δ reserve −$28,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,000 = Cr 28,000. **Balanced.** Allowance bal. = \(65{,}000 - 28{,}000 = \mathbf{\$37{,}000}\).

**(d) Balance sheet — December 31, Year 3**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory (at average cost) | $165,000 |
| Less: Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | (37,000) |
| **Inventory, net (at LIFO)** | **$128,000** |

**(e) Cumulative COGS effect (Years 1–3)**  
Net debit to COGS = \(54{,}000 + 11{,}000 - 28{,}000 = \mathbf{\$37{,}000}\) = ending LIFO reserve.

**(f) Year 2 COGS bridge**  
Relationship: **LIFO COGS = internal-method COGS + increase in LIFO reserve**.  
Average-cost COGS = LIFO COGS − Δ reserve = \(740{,}000 - 11{,}000 = \mathbf{\$729{,}000}\).

**Key insight:** Whether the internal method is FIFO or average cost, the mechanics are identical: **reserve = internal EI − LIFO EI**; after Year 1 book **only Δ reserve**. Ending allowance always equals the current reserve, and cumulative COGS charges equal the ending reserve.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Pinnacle Fasteners: midstream multi-year schedule, period-end only, disclosure vs in-accounts, reverse
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule from a nonzero beginning allowance; period-end adjusting JEs; reverse when reserve falls; in-accounts vs outside-the-books conversion; COGS bridge  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinnacle Fasteners Corp.** uses **FIFO** internally and **LIFO** externally. At **January 1, Year 6**, the credit balance in **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis** is **$120,000** (equal to the LIFO reserve at December 31, Year 5). Year-end is **December 31**.

Year-end inventory valuations:

| Date | Ending inventory at FIFO | Ending inventory at LIFO |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 6 | $520,000 | $385,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 7 | 545,000 | 400,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 8 | 490,000 | 415,000 |

Additional information for the COGS bridge:  
- Year 6 **LIFO-basis COGS** = **$890,000**  
- Year 8 **LIFO-basis COGS** = **$920,000**

Management is evaluating two Year 8 reporting approaches:  
**(1) In the accounts** — adjust the allowance each year-end.  
**(2) Outside the accounts** — leave books on FIFO; compute LIFO amounts only for external statements and notes (no formal JE for the reserve).

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 6–8 starting from the January 1, Year 6 allowance of $120,000 (FIFO EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve, COGS effect, ending allowance).  
(b) Prepare the **period-end adjusting journal entries** for December 31 of Year 6, Year 7, and Year 8 under approach (1).  
(c) Under approach (1), show **balance-sheet classification** of inventory at December 31, Year 8.  
(d) Under approach (2) for Year 8, state: (i) whether a formal JE is made for the reserve; (ii) inventory amount on the LIFO-basis balance sheet; (iii) LIFO reserve disclosed in the notes; (iv) FIFO inventory an analyst would reconstruct.  
(e) Using the Δ reserve each year, compute **FIFO COGS** for Year 6 and Year 8 from the given LIFO COGS amounts.  
(f) Identify which year’s entry is a **reverse / settlement-type** adjustment of prior LIFO-reserve build-up, and state the income-statement effect.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 6–8; nonzero beginning allowance)**

Beginning allowance (Jan 1, Y6 / Dec 31, Y5 reserve) = **$120,000**

| Date | FIFO EI | LIFO EI | LIFO reserve | Δ reserve | Effect on COGS | Ending allowance (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Jan 1, Y6 (beg.) | — | — | 120,000 | — | — | **$120,000** |
| Dec 31, Y6 | $520,000 | $385,000 | **135,000** | +15,000 | +COGS $15,000 | **135,000** |
| Dec 31, Y7 | 545,000 | 400,000 | **145,000** | +10,000 | +COGS $10,000 | **145,000** |
| Dec 31, Y8 | 490,000 | 415,000 | **75,000** | −70,000 | −COGS $70,000 | **75,000** |

Checks:  
- Y6: \(520{,}000 - 385{,}000 = \mathbf{135{,}000}\); Δ = \(135{,}000 - 120{,}000 = \mathbf{+15{,}000}\)  
- Y7: \(545{,}000 - 400{,}000 = \mathbf{145{,}000}\); Δ = \(145{,}000 - 135{,}000 = \mathbf{+10{,}000}\)  
- Y8: \(490{,}000 - 415{,}000 = \mathbf{75{,}000}\); Δ = \(75{,}000 - 145{,}000 = \mathbf{-70{,}000}\)  
- Allowance: \(120{,}000 + 15{,}000 + 10{,}000 - 70{,}000 = \mathbf{75{,}000}\)

**(b) Period-end adjusting JEs (in the accounts)**

*December 31, Year 6*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 15,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 15,000 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve from $120,000 to $135,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 7*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 10,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 10,000 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve from $135,000 to $145,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 = Cr 10,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 8 — Reverse / decrease*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | 70,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 70,000 |
| *Decrease LIFO reserve from $145,000 to $75,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 70,000 = Cr 70,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(145{,}000 - 70{,}000 = \mathbf{\$75{,}000}\).

**(c) Balance-sheet classification — December 31, Year 8 (approach 1)**

| Current assets | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory (FIFO) | $490,000 |
| Less: Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | (75,000) |
| **Inventory (LIFO basis)** | **$415,000** |

The allowance is a **contra-inventory (valuation)** account — not a liability.

**(d) Outside-the-accounts conversion (approach 2) — Year 8**  
(i) **No formal journal entry** is recorded for the LIFO reserve.  
(ii) External balance sheet reports inventory at LIFO: **$415,000**.  
(iii) Note disclosure of LIFO reserve: **$75,000** (for comparability with FIFO peers).  
(iv) Reconstructed FIFO inventory = LIFO inventory + disclosed reserve = \(415{,}000 + 75{,}000 = \mathbf{\$490{,}000}\).

**(e) COGS bridge**  
Formula: **FIFO COGS = LIFO COGS − Δ LIFO reserve**

| Year | LIFO COGS | Δ reserve | FIFO COGS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 6 | $890,000 | +15,000 | \(890{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \mathbf{\$875{,}000}\) |
| Year 8 | 920,000 | −70,000 | \(920{,}000 - (-70{,}000) = \mathbf{\$990{,}000}\) |

**(f) Reverse / settlement-type year**  
**Year 8** is the reverse adjustment: Dr Allowance $70,000 / Cr COGS $70,000.  
Income-statement effect: **decreases COGS by $70,000**, increasing pretax income by $70,000 relative to leaving the allowance unadjusted. This partially **settles** the prior LIFO-reserve build-up that had increased COGS in earlier years.

**Key insight:** A subsequent measurement schedule works whether Year 1 starts at zero or the firm is midstream with a beginning allowance. Only Δ reserve hits COGS each period; a shrinking reserve credits COGS. Users need the disclosed LIFO reserve for cross-company comparability whether conversion is booked or done off-books.

### Q4 — MC — LIFO reserve measurement and allowance classification
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Compute ending LIFO reserve and Δ; classify Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis; identify correct period-end JE  

**Question:**  
**Windmere Toolworks LLC** keeps inventory on **FIFO** for internal purposes and reports **LIFO** externally. At the end of Year 4 the LIFO reserve (credit balance in Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis) is **$55,000**. At the end of Year 5, FIFO ending inventory is **$275,000** and LIFO ending inventory is **$210,000**. Windmere records the conversion **in the accounts**. Which statement is **correct**?

- A) Year 5 LIFO reserve is $65,000; the Year 5 adjusting entry debits Allowance $10,000 and credits COGS $10,000.  
- B) Year 5 LIFO reserve is $65,000; the Year 5 adjusting entry debits COGS $65,000 and credits Allowance $65,000.  
- C) Year 5 LIFO reserve is $65,000; the Year 5 adjusting entry debits COGS $10,000 and credits Allowance $10,000; the allowance is a contra-inventory account.  
- D) Year 5 LIFO reserve is $210,000; Inventory is reported at $275,000 with no allowance because LIFO is used only for tax.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Year 5 LIFO reserve = \(275{,}000 - 210{,}000 = \mathbf{\$65{,}000}\).  
Δ reserve = \(65{,}000 - 55{,}000 = \mathbf{+\$10{,}000}\) → Dr COGS $10,000 / Cr Allowance $10,000 (**only the change**, not the full reserve).  
The allowance is a **contra account to inventory**, not a liability.  
A reverses the direction of the entry. B incorrectly books the full ending reserve instead of the change. D misstates the reserve amount and presentation.
