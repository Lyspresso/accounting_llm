# Agent 244 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-8

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Explain stockholders’ equity disclosures and key ratios  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Equity disclosures (ASC 505):** shares authorized/issued/outstanding and par; changes in equity categories and share counts; rights and privileges of securities (dividend and liquidation preferences, participation rights, call prices/dates, conversion rates, sinking funds, unusual voting rights)
- **Preferred-specific disclosures:** liquidation preference (when significantly greater than par) in the equity section; call/redemption amounts; aggregate and per-share cumulative preferred dividends in arrears; redemption requirements for each of the next five years
- **Restricted / appropriated retained earnings:** contractual or legal restrictions disclosed in notes; formal appropriation reclassifies unappropriated RE to appropriated RE within equity (total equity unchanged)
- **Book value per common share (BVPS):** common stockholders’ equity ÷ common shares outstanding; common equity = total equity − preferred claims (liquidation preference + cumulative preferred dividends in arrears)
- **Payout ratio:** cash common dividends ÷ net income available to common (NI − preferred dividends / cumulative claim for the period)
- **Return on equity (ROE):** NI available to common ÷ average common stockholders’ equity
- **Price-to-earnings (P/E):** market price per share ÷ earnings per share
- **Initial recognition JE (this LO):** board appropriation of retained earnings
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** year-end formal appropriation
- **Settlement JE:** release of appropriation when the restriction purpose is completed
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year comparative schedule of BVPS, payout, ROE, and P/E

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-year equity ratio schedule + appropriation JE + disclosures (emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule)
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule of BVPS, payout, ROE, and P/E over two (with Year-1 equity for ROE averages); preferred liquidation claim; initial appropriation JE; ASC 505 equity disclosures  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar Ridge Industrial Corp.** (calendar-year public company) reports the following stockholders’ equity balances:

| Account | Dec. 31, Year 3 | Dec. 31, Year 2 | Dec. 31, Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 7% cumulative preferred stock, $25 par, 12,000 shares outstanding | $300,000 | $300,000 | $300,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—preferred | 30,000 | 30,000 | 30,000 |
| Common stock, $1 par | 200,000 | 200,000 | 180,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—common | 3,100,000 | 3,100,000 | 2,520,000 |
| Retained earnings (all unappropriated before any Year 3 reclass) | 2,450,000 | 1,900,000 | 1,480,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive loss | (95,000) | (70,000) | (55,000) |
| Treasury stock (at cost) | (200,000) | (200,000) | — |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$5,785,000** | **$5,260,000** | **$4,455,000** |

Additional facts:
- Preferred stock has a **liquidation preference of $28 per share** ($336,000 aggregate). No preferred dividends are in arrears at any year-end (current preferred dividends are paid each year).
- Common shares outstanding: **190,000** at Dec. 31, Year 3 and Year 2 (200,000 issued − 10,000 treasury); **180,000** at Dec. 31, Year 1.
- Net income: Year 3 **$680,000**; Year 2 **$490,000**.
- Annual preferred dividend requirement: 7% × $300,000 = **$21,000**.
- Cash common dividends paid: Year 3 **$131,800**; Year 2 **$93,800**.
- Market price per common share: Year 3 **$42.00**; Year 2 **$33.00**.
- Basic earnings per share: Year 3 **$3.47**; Year 2 **$2.47**.
- On **December 31, Year 3**, the board formally **appropriates $300,000** of retained earnings for a plant-expansion reserve required under a bank loan covenant (reclassification only; no cash movement).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 journal entry** to record the appropriation of retained earnings. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Compute **common stockholders’ equity** and **book value per common share** for Year 3, Year 2, and Year 1.  
c. Compute the **payout ratio** for Year 3 and Year 2.  
d. Compute **return on equity** for Year 3 and Year 2 (use average common stockholders’ equity).  
e. Compute the **price-to-earnings ratio** for Year 3 and Year 2.  
f. Present a complete **subsequent-measurement ratio schedule** summarizing BVPS (three years) and payout, ROE, and P/E (two years), with brief directional notes.  
g. List **at least five equity disclosures** ASC 505 requires that are relevant to Cedar Ridge’s capital structure.  
h. Does the appropriation change total stockholders’ equity or the Year 3 book value per share? Explain.

**Answer key:**  

**a. December 31, Year 3 — appropriation of retained earnings (initial recognition of appropriation within equity)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 300,000 | |
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Plant Expansion | | 300,000 |
| *Reclassify RE for bank-covenant plant expansion reserve* | | |

**Check:** Dr 300,000 = Cr 300,000. **Balanced.**

After reclass (total equity unchanged):  
Unappropriated RE = $2,450,000 − $300,000 = **$2,150,000**  
Appropriated RE = **$300,000**  
Total RE still **$2,450,000**; total SE still **$5,785,000**.

**b. Book value per common share**

Preferred claim (liquidation preference; no arrears) = **$336,000** each year.

| | Year 3 | Year 2 | Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total stockholders’ equity | $5,785,000 | $5,260,000 | $4,455,000 |
| Less: preferred liquidation claim | (336,000) | (336,000) | (336,000) |
| **Common stockholders’ equity** | **$5,449,000** | **$4,924,000** | **$4,119,000** |
| Common shares outstanding | 190,000 | 190,000 | 180,000 |
| **Book value per share** | **$28.68** | **$25.92** | **$22.88** |

\[
\text{BVPS}_{Y3} = \frac{\$5{,}449{,}000}{190{,}000} = \$28.68;\quad
\text{BVPS}_{Y2} = \frac{\$4{,}924{,}000}{190{,}000} = \$25.92;\quad
\text{BVPS}_{Y1} = \frac{\$4{,}119{,}000}{180{,}000} = \$22.88
\]

**c. Payout ratio**

NI available to common:  
Year 3: $680,000 − $21,000 = **$659,000**  
Year 2: $490,000 − $21,000 = **$469,000**

\[
\text{Payout}_{Y3} = \frac{\$131{,}800}{\$659{,}000} = \mathbf{0.200}\ (20.0\%)
\]
\[
\text{Payout}_{Y2} = \frac{\$93{,}800}{\$469{,}000} = \mathbf{0.200}\ (20.0\%)
\]

**d. Return on equity**

Average common SE Year 3 = ($5,449,000 + $4,924,000) / 2 = **$5,186,500**  
Average common SE Year 2 = ($4,924,000 + $4,119,000) / 2 = **$4,521,500**

\[
\text{ROE}_{Y3} = \frac{\$659{,}000}{\$5{,}186{,}500} = \mathbf{0.127}\ (12.7\%)
\]
\[
\text{ROE}_{Y2} = \frac{\$469{,}000}{\$4{,}521{,}500} = \mathbf{0.104}\ (10.4\%)
\]

**e. Price-to-earnings ratio**

\[
\text{P/E}_{Y3} = \frac{\$42.00}{\$3.47} = \mathbf{12.10}
\]
\[
\text{P/E}_{Y2} = \frac{\$33.00}{\$2.47} = \mathbf{13.36}
\]

**f. Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis) — comparative equity ratios**

| Ratio | Year 3 | Year 2 | Year 1 | Direction / note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Book value per common share | $28.68 | $25.92 | $22.88 | ↑ each year (~10.7% Y2→Y3; ~13.3% Y1→Y2) |
| Payout ratio | 0.200 | 0.200 | n/a | Stable at 20% of NI to common |
| Return on equity | 0.127 | 0.104 | n/a | ↑ — more income per average common equity dollar |
| Price-to-earnings | 12.10 | 13.36 | n/a | ↓ slightly — EPS grew faster relative to price |

**Roll-forward interpretation:** Common equity grew from $4,119,000 (Y1) → $4,924,000 (Y2) → $5,449,000 (Y3). BVPS tracks that growth after stripping the constant **$336,000** preferred liquidation claim. ROE uses the **average** of beginning and ending common equity; payout and P/E use period income and market/EPS data only.

**g. Relevant equity disclosures (examples)**  
1. Number of preferred and common shares **authorized, issued, and outstanding**, and par value.  
2. **Rights and privileges** of preferred stock (7% cumulative dividend; $28 liquidation preference).  
3. **Liquidation preference** of preferred ($336,000 aggregate / $28 per share) in the equity section when significantly greater than par.  
4. Aggregate and per-share amounts of **cumulative preferred dividends in arrears** (here: none — still relevant disclosure policy).  
5. Changes in each equity category and in **share counts** during the period (including treasury activity).  
6. Nature and amount of **retained earnings restriction / appropriation** ($300,000 plant expansion under loan covenant) in the notes (and appropriated RE shown separately within equity).  
7. Call/redemption prices and dates, conversion features, or sinking-fund requirements if applicable.  
8. Redemption requirements for capital stock redeemable at fixed or determinable prices/dates for each of the **next five years** (if applicable).

**h. Effect of appropriation**  
Appropriation is a **reclassification within equity**. Total stockholders’ equity and common stockholders’ equity are **unchanged**, so **Year 3 BVPS is unchanged**. Payout, ROE, and P/E are also unaffected (they use NI, dividends, market price, and equity totals — not the unappropriated vs appropriated split).

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the core Demo 19-8 deliverable: strip preferred **liquidation preference** (and any arrears) for common equity, then roll BVPS, payout, ROE, and P/E across years. Appropriation of RE is a balanced reclass that does **not** change total equity or the ratios.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin ratio pack + appropriation JE
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: appropriation JE; multi-year BVPS, payout, ROE, P/E schedule with preferred claim; post-appropriation RE presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Precision Tools Inc.** reports:

| Account | Dec. 31, Year 3 | Dec. 31, Year 2 | Dec. 31, Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5% cumulative preferred stock, $100 par, 4,000 shares outstanding | $400,000 | $400,000 | $400,000 |
| Common stock, $5 par | 500,000 | 500,000 | 400,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital (common) | 2,200,000 | 2,200,000 | 1,400,000 |
| Retained earnings (unappropriated before Year 3 reclass) | 1,800,000 | 1,350,000 | 1,050,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income (gain) | 40,000 | 25,000 | 15,000 |
| Treasury stock (at cost) | (120,000) | (120,000) | — |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$4,820,000** | **$4,355,000** | **$3,265,000** |

Additional facts:
- Preferred **liquidation preference $105 per share** = **$420,000** aggregate. No preferred dividends in arrears.
- Common shares outstanding: **94,000** (Year 3 and Year 2: 100,000 issued − 6,000 treasury); **80,000** (Year 1).
- Net income: Year 3 **$550,000**; Year 2 **$400,000**.
- Annual preferred dividend: 5% × $400,000 = **$20,000**.
- Cash common dividends: Year 3 **$106,000**; Year 2 **$76,000**.
- Market price: Year 3 **$58.00**; Year 2 **$45.00**.
- Basic EPS: Year 3 **$5.64**; Year 2 **$4.04**.
- On Dec. 31, Year 3, board appropriates **$150,000** of RE for a legal capital restriction under state law related to treasury stock / dividend capacity (formal appropriation).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **appropriation journal entry** on Dec. 31, Year 3.  
b. Compute BVPS (Years 3, 2, and 1), payout ratio, ROE, and P/E for **Year 3 and Year 2**.  
c. Prepare a comparative **ratio schedule** (subsequent measurement).  
d. After appropriation, report **unappropriated RE**, **appropriated RE**, and **total equity**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Dec. 31, Year 3 — appropriation JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 150,000 | |
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Legal Restriction | | 150,000 |
| *Formal appropriation for legal dividend-capacity restriction* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 150,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Ratio computations**

Preferred claim = **$420,000**

| | Year 3 | Year 2 | Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total equity | $4,820,000 | $4,355,000 | $3,265,000 |
| Less preferred liquidation claim | (420,000) | (420,000) | (420,000) |
| **Common stockholders’ equity** | **$4,400,000** | **$3,935,000** | **$2,845,000** |

**Book value per share**  
Y3: $4,400,000 / 94,000 = **$46.81**  
Y2: $3,935,000 / 94,000 = **$41.86**  
Y1: $2,845,000 / 80,000 = **$35.56**

**NI available to common**  
Y3: $550,000 − $20,000 = **$530,000**  
Y2: $400,000 − $20,000 = **$380,000**

**Payout ratio**  
Y3: $106,000 / $530,000 = **0.200** (20.0%)  
Y2: $76,000 / $380,000 = **0.200** (20.0%)

**Return on equity**  
Avg CSE Y3 = ($4,400,000 + $3,935,000) / 2 = **$4,167,500**  
ROE Y3 = $530,000 / $4,167,500 = **0.127** (12.7%)  

Avg CSE Y2 = ($3,935,000 + $2,845,000) / 2 = **$3,390,000**  
ROE Y2 = $380,000 / $3,390,000 = **0.112** (11.2%)

**Price-to-earnings**  
Y3: $58.00 / $5.64 = **10.28**  
Y2: $45.00 / $4.04 = **11.14**

**c. Comparative ratio schedule (subsequent measurement)**

| Ratio | Year 3 | Year 2 | Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Book value per common share | $46.81 | $41.86 | $35.56 |
| Payout ratio | 0.200 | 0.200 | n/a |
| Return on equity | 0.127 | 0.112 | n/a |
| Price-to-earnings | 10.28 | 11.14 | n/a |

**d. Post-appropriation equity presentation (Dec. 31, Year 3)**  
Unappropriated RE = $1,800,000 − $150,000 = **$1,650,000**  
Appropriated RE = **$150,000**  
Total RE = **$1,800,000**  
**Total stockholders’ equity remains $4,820,000.**

**Key insight:** Changing every dollar amount does not change the model: strip **preferred claims**, use **NI to common** for payout and ROE, average beginning/ending common equity for ROE, and treat appropriation as a **zero-sum equity reclass**. The multi-year schedule shows BVPS and ROE rising while payout stays flat.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Dividends in arrears + period-end appropriation + settlement release + reverse payout
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Period-end appropriation JE; settlement (release) JE when restriction lifts; BVPS with liquidation preference and cumulative preferred dividends in arrears; reverse payout; disclosure vs ratio classification  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Peak Logistics Co.** (calendar year) has the following facts at **December 31, Year 4** (before any appropriation entry for Year 4):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total stockholders’ equity | $2,750,000 |
| 8% cumulative preferred stock, $50 par, 10,000 shares outstanding | 500,000 |
| Preferred **liquidation preference** | $55 per share ($550,000 aggregate) |
| Cumulative preferred **dividends in arrears** (2 full years) | $80,000 |
| Common shares outstanding | 150,000 |
| Net income, Year 4 | $320,000 |
| Preferred dividend requirement for Year 4 (8% × $500,000) | 40,000 |
| Cash common dividends paid, Year 4 | 84,000 |
| Market price per common share, Dec. 31, Year 4 | $22.00 |
| Basic EPS, Year 4 | $1.87 |

Events:
1. **December 31, Year 4:** Board appropriates **$350,000** of retained earnings because a term loan covenant restricts dividends until a distribution center is completed.  
2. **March 20, Year 5:** Distribution center is completed and the related loan covenant restriction is released; board **releases the entire $350,000 appropriation**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **common stockholders’ equity** and **book value per common share** at Dec. 31, Year 4 (include preferred arrears in the preferred claim).  
b. Compute what BVPS **would have been** if there were **no** dividends in arrears (liquidation preference only).  
c. Compute Year 4 **payout ratio**, **ROE** (use average common equity of **$1,900,000**), and **P/E**.  
d. Prepare the **Dec. 31, Year 4 appropriation JE** and the **March 20, Year 5 release (settlement) JE**. Both must balance.  
e. A peer company reports a payout ratio of **0.25** and NI available to common of **$360,000**. Compute cash common dividends.  
f. Classify each item as **required equity disclosure**, **ratio analysis metric**, or **neither**:  
   (i) liquidation preference significantly above par;  
   (ii) return on equity;  
   (iii) shares authorized/issued/outstanding;  
   (iv) market price of common stock alone (without EPS context);  
   (v) aggregate preferred dividends in arrears.

**Answer key:**  

**a. BVPS with liquidation preference + arrears**

Preferred claim = liquidation preference + dividends in arrears  
= $550,000 + $80,000 = **$630,000**

Common stockholders’ equity = $2,750,000 − $630,000 = **$2,120,000**

\[
\text{BVPS} = \frac{\$2{,}120{,}000}{150{,}000} = \mathbf{\$14.13}
\]

**b. BVPS without arrears (liquidation only)**

Common equity = $2,750,000 − $550,000 = **$2,200,000**  
BVPS = $2,200,000 / 150,000 = **$14.67**  

Difference: arrears reduce BVPS by **$0.53** per share ($80,000 / 150,000).

**c. Payout, ROE, P/E — Year 4**

NI available to common = $320,000 − $40,000 = **$280,000**  
(Note: cumulative arrears increase the **balance-sheet claim** for BVPS; the annual preferred **requirement** of $40,000 is subtracted for income available to common when computing payout/ROE for the period under the chapter model.)

\[
\text{Payout} = \frac{\$84{,}000}{\$280{,}000} = \mathbf{0.30}\ (30\%)
\]
\[
\text{ROE} = \frac{\$280{,}000}{\$1{,}900{,}000} = \mathbf{0.147}\ (14.7\%)
\]
\[
\text{P/E} = \frac{\$22.00}{\$1.87} = \mathbf{11.76}
\]

**d. Appropriation and settlement journal entries**

*December 31, Year 4 — period-end appropriation:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 350,000 | |
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Debt Covenant | | 350,000 |
| *Restrict RE under term-loan covenant* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 350,000. **Balanced.**

*March 20, Year 5 — release / settlement of appropriation:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Debt Covenant | 350,000 | |
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | | 350,000 |
| *Release appropriation; distribution center complete; covenant restriction lifted* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 350,000. **Balanced.**  
Neither entry changes total equity; both are pure equity reclassifications.

**e. Reverse payout**

\[
\text{Cash common dividends} = 0.25 \times \$360{,}000 = \mathbf{\$90{,}000}
\]

**f. Classification**

| Item | Classification |
|---|---|
| (i) Liquidation preference significantly above par | **Required equity disclosure** (equity section / notes) |
| (ii) Return on equity | **Ratio analysis metric** (Demo 19-8 analysis; not a required ASC 505 line formula) |
| (iii) Shares authorized/issued/outstanding | **Required equity disclosure** |
| (iv) Market price of common stock alone | **Neither** as an equity *disclosure requirement* under LO 19-8 (market price is an input to P/E, not an ASC 505 equity disclosure item itself) |
| (v) Aggregate preferred dividends in arrears | **Required equity disclosure** |

**Key insight:** Cumulative preferred **dividends in arrears** increase the preferred claim and **lower** common BVPS. Appropriation and its later **release** are balanced reverse reclassifications—period-end restriction imposed and “settled” when the covenant purpose is completed. Disclosures (claims, share counts, arrears) feed the inputs that make ratio schedules meaningful.

### Q4 — MC — Preferred claim in book value per share
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Classification of amounts subtracted from total equity to measure common equity for BVPS  
**Question:**  
In computing **book value per common share**, which set of amounts is subtracted from total stockholders’ equity to arrive at common stockholders’ equity when preferred stock is outstanding?

- A) Par value of preferred stock only, even if liquidation preference is substantially higher  
- B) Liquidation preference of preferred stock and cumulative preferred dividends in arrears  
- C) Market value of preferred stock and all future preferred dividends expected over five years  
- D) Additional paid-in capital on common stock and treasury stock at cost  

**Answer:** **B.** Common stockholders’ equity equals total equity less preferred stockholder claims such as **liquidation value** and **dividends in arrears** on cumulative preferred stock. Par alone understates the claim when liquidation preference exceeds par; market value and forecasted future dividends are not the chapter’s preferred claim for BVPS.

### Q5 — MC — Equity disclosure vs ratio
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Classification — required ASC 505 equity disclosure versus equity ratio metric  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is a **required financial statement disclosure** related to stockholders’ equity (as opposed to solely an analytical ratio computed by users)?

- A) Price-to-earnings ratio for each of the last two years  
- B) Return on equity using average common stockholders’ equity  
- C) Aggregate and per-share amounts of cumulative preferred dividends in arrears  
- D) Book value per share rounded to the nearest cent  

**Answer:** **C.** ASC 505 requires disclosure of **aggregate and per-share cumulative preferred dividends in arrears**, rights/privileges, share counts, liquidation preferences (when significantly above par), and related terms. P/E, ROE, and BVPS are **equity analysis ratios** illustrated in Demo 19-8, not the required disclosure items themselves.
