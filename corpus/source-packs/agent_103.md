# Agent 103 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-9

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Apply the fair value option for liabilities  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Elect the fair value option (FVO) instrument-by-instrument on the election date; election is generally irrevocable and applies to the entire instrument
- Initial recognition of bonds/notes payable under FVO (same cash/proceeds entry as amortized-cost issuance)
- Subsequent measurement: adjust liability from amortized cost to fair value each reporting date via Fair Value Adjustment (contra or adjunct)
- General (benchmark/risk-free) interest-rate risk → unrealized holding G/L in **net income**
- Instrument-specific credit risk (own credit) → unrealized holding G/L in **OCI** (reclassify to NI when debt matures or is redeemed)
- Multi-period fair value adjustment rollforward schedule
- Period-end adjusting entries after interest/amortization has been recorded
- Balance sheet presentation of liability at fair value
- Maturity/settlement: reverse remaining FVA; retire face; reclassify AOCI related to own credit risk

### Q1 — CORE — FVO bonds issued at par: initial recognition, interest, multi-year FV schedule, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Fair value option for liabilities — initial recognition JE, period-end FV adjustments (general risk), subsequent measurement schedule, maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Meridian Forge Co.** issues **$500,000** face of **5%**, **3-year** bonds dated January 1. Cash interest is payable **annually each December 31**. The bonds are issued **at par** for cash. On the issuance date (election date), Meridian **irrevocably elects the fair value option** under ASC 825 for **this bond issue only**. Meridian’s fiscal year-end is December 31. Interest is recorded each December 31 **before** any fair value adjustment.

Fair values of the bonds at each year-end (all fair value changes are attributable to **general** market interest-rate risk — not instrument-specific credit risk):

| Date | Fair value |
|---|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | **$476,000** |
| December 31, Year 2 | **$488,000** |
| December 31, Year 3 (maturity) | **$500,000** |

On December 31, Year 3, Meridian pays face value in cash and retires the bonds.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry for issuance (initial recognition under FVO).  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** interest payment entry.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** fair value option adjusting entry.  
d. Prepare a **three-year subsequent measurement schedule** showing for each year-end: amortized cost, fair value, required Fair Value Adjustment balance (debit balance reduces the liability), beginning FVA, period adjustment (Dr or Cr), unrealized G/(L) recognized in **net income**, and ending FVA. Provide the Year 2 and Year 3 adjusting entries.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** maturity/settlement entry (after the Year 3 FV adjustment).  
f. Show how the bonds would be **presented** on the December 31, Year 1 balance sheet.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (issuance at par; FVO elected)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 500,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 500,000 |
| *To record issuance of 5%, 3-year bonds at par; FVO elected instrument-by-instrument (irrevocable)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 500,000. Balanced.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — cash interest**  
Interest = $500,000 × 5% = **$25,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 25,000 | |
| Cash | | 25,000 |
| *Annual cash interest on bonds payable* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 25,000. Balanced.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVO adjustment (general risk → NI)**  
Amortized cost (at par) = **$500,000**  
Fair value = **$476,000**  
Required Fair Value Adjustment (debit, contra) = 500,000 − 476,000 = **$24,000**  
Decrease in liability fair value (general rate increase) → **unrealized gain in net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 24,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 24,000 |
| *Adjust bonds from amortized cost to FV; general risk → NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000. Balanced.  
Net liability at FV = 500,000 − 24,000 = **$476,000**.

**d. Three-year subsequent measurement schedule**  

Because the bonds were issued at par and remain unpaid until maturity, **amortized cost = face = $500,000** each year-end before settlement. Required FVA (debit balance) = Amortized cost − Fair value when FV < AC.

| Year-end | Amortized cost | Fair value | Req. FVA bal. (Dr) | Beg. FVA (Dr) | Period adj. Dr/(Cr) | Unreal. G/(L)—Income | End. FVA (Dr) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Y1 | 500,000 | 476,000 | 24,000 | 0 | **Dr 24,000** | **+24,000** | 24,000 |
| Y2 | 500,000 | 488,000 | 12,000 | 24,000 | **Cr 12,000** | **−12,000** | 12,000 |
| Y3 | 500,000 | 500,000 | 0 | 12,000 | **Cr 12,000** | **−12,000** | 0 |

**December 31, Year 2 — FVO adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 12,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 12,000 |
| *Reduce FVA to $12,000; partial recovery of FV → unrealized loss in NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,000. Net liability = 500,000 − 12,000 = **$488,000**.

**December 31, Year 3 — FVO adjusting entry (before settlement)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 12,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 12,000 |
| *Clear FVA as FV returns to face at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,000. FVA balance = **$0**. Net cumulative unrealized G/L over life = +24,000 − 12,000 − 12,000 = **$0**.

**e. December 31, Year 3 — maturity settlement**  
(FVA already zero after Year 3 adjustment.)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 500,000 | |
| Cash | | 500,000 |
| *Retire bonds at face on maturity date* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 500,000. Balanced.

**f. Balance sheet presentation — December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $500,000 |
| Less: Fair value adjustment—bond payable | (24,000) |
| **Bonds payable, at fair value** | **$476,000** |

(Long-term liability, unless current maturity presentation applies near maturity.)

**Key insight:** Under the FVO, issuance looks like ordinary bond issuance, but each reporting date the issuer records a Fair Value Adjustment so the **net carrying amount equals fair value**. Changes from **general** interest-rate risk run through **net income**. Over the full life to maturity at face, cumulative unrealized G/L nets to zero when all movements are general-risk FV changes that reverse.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — FVO bonds at par with adjunct FVA and multi-year schedule
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — FVO initial recognition, subsequent measurement schedule (including FV above amortized cost), period-end adjusting JEs  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Cedarline Utilities Inc.** issues **$750,000** face of **6%**, **4-year** bonds at **par** for cash. Interest is paid **annually on December 31**. Cedarline **elects the fair value option** for these bonds on the issuance date. All year-end fair value changes are due to **general** market risk (not own credit risk). Interest entries are recorded before fair value adjustments.

| Date | Fair value |
|---|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | **$720,000** |
| December 31, Year 2 | **$735,000** |
| December 31, Year 3 | **$760,000** |
| December 31, Year 4 (maturity) | **$750,000** |

**Required:**  
a. Journal entry for issuance on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Interest payment entry on December 31, Year 1.  
c. Fair value option adjusting entry on December 31, Year 1.  
d. Four-year FVA rollforward schedule (amortized cost, FV, required FVA as signed Dr/(Cr) balance, period adjustment, unrealized G/(L)—Income). Provide adjusting entries for Years 2, 3, and 4.  
e. Maturity settlement entry on December 31, Year 4 (after Year 4 FV adjustment).  
f. Net liability reported on the December 31, Year 3 balance sheet.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — issuance**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 750,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 750,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 750,000.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — interest**  
$750,000 × 6% = **$45,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 45,000 | |
| Cash | | 45,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 45,000.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVO adjustment**  
Required FVA debit = 750,000 − 720,000 = **$30,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 30,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 30,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 30,000. Net liability = **$720,000**.

**d. Four-year schedule**  
Convention: **positive** required FVA = **debit** (contra; FV < AC); **negative** = **credit** (adjunct; FV > AC).

| Year-end | AC | FV | Req. FVA Dr/(Cr) | Beg. FVA | Adj. Dr/(Cr) | Unreal. G/(L)—Inc. | End. FVA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Y1 | 750,000 | 720,000 | 30,000 Dr | 0 | **Dr 30,000** | **+30,000** | 30,000 Dr |
| Y2 | 750,000 | 735,000 | 15,000 Dr | 30,000 Dr | **Cr 15,000** | **−15,000** | 15,000 Dr |
| Y3 | 750,000 | 760,000 | 10,000 Cr | 15,000 Dr | **Cr 25,000** | **−25,000** | 10,000 Cr |
| Y4 | 750,000 | 750,000 | 0 | 10,000 Cr | **Dr 10,000** | **+10,000** | 0 |

Cumulative unrealized G/L = 30,000 − 15,000 − 25,000 + 10,000 = **$0**.

**Year 2 adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 15,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 15,000 |

**Check:** 15,000 = 15,000.

**Year 3 adjusting entry**  
(From 15,000 Dr to 10,000 Cr requires Cr 25,000)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 25,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 25,000 |

**Check:** 25,000 = 25,000. Net liability = 750,000 + 10,000 = **$760,000**.

**Year 4 adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 10,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 10,000 |

**Check:** 10,000 = 10,000. FVA cleared.

**e. December 31, Year 4 — maturity**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 750,000 | |
| Cash | | 750,000 |

**Check:** 750,000 = 750,000.

**f. December 31, Year 3 presentation**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $750,000 |
| Add: Fair value adjustment—bond payable | 10,000 |
| **Bonds payable, at fair value** | **$760,000** |

**Key insight:** When fair value **exceeds** amortized cost, Fair Value Adjustment becomes an **adjunct** (credit balance) and the offset is an unrealized **loss** in net income (liability increased). The FVA account is rolled forward each period—not reset without an offsetting entry.

### Q3 — CORE — Discount bond under FVO: effective interest, mixed general vs own-credit risk, BS presentation, early redemption
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** FVO with bonds issued at a discount — update amortized cost first, then FVA; instrument-specific credit risk to OCI; settlement and AOCI reclassification  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Northwind Packaging LLC** issues **$200,000** face of **4%**, **3-year** bonds. Cash interest of **$8,000** is payable **annually each December 31**. The bonds are sold for **$194,560** to yield **5%** market interest. Northwind uses the **effective interest method** and, on the issuance date, **elects the fair value option** for this liability. Year-end is December 31.

Amortized cost (after interest/amortization entries) and fair values:

| Date | Amortized cost | Fair value | Primary cause of difference vs amortized cost |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| December 31, Year 1 | **$196,288** | **$192,000** | **General** increase in benchmark rates |
| December 31, Year 2 | **$198,102** | **$188,000** | **Instrument-specific credit risk** (deterioration in Northwind’s own credit standing) |

**Additional facts:**  
- On **January 1, Year 3**, immediately after Year 2 books are closed, Northwind **redeems** the entire issue for cash equal to then-current fair value of **$188,000** (no call premium beyond FV).  
- Ignore income taxes.  
- For Year 2, treat the **entire** required FVA (amortized cost vs fair value) as the measurement target; attribute the **period’s FVA change** consistent with the stated cause for that year (Y1 general → NI; Y2 own credit → OCI for the incremental measurement to year-end FV, with Y1 general-risk FVA remaining classified through NI historically). For exam-style simplicity matching the chapter demos: record Y1 full AC−FV through NI; record Y2 full AC−FV through OCI (replacing the FVA target), reclassifying as shown in the answer key.

**Required:**  
a. Journal entry for issuance on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Journal entry for interest and discount amortization on December 31, Year 1 (effective interest).  
c. Fair value option adjusting entry on December 31, Year 1 (general risk).  
d. Journal entry for interest and amortization on December 31, Year 2.  
e. Fair value option adjusting entry on December 31, Year 2 (instrument-specific credit risk → OCI), bringing FVA to the amount needed so net carrying amount equals FV. Show the two-year FVA bridge.  
f. Balance sheet presentation of the liability at December 31, Year 2.  
g. Journal entries on January 1, Year 3 to redeem the bonds for $188,000 and to reclassify AOCI related to own credit risk into net income.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — issuance at discount (FVO elected)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 194,560 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 5,440 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 200,000 |
| *Issue 4%, 3-year bonds to yield 5%; elect FVO* | | |

**Check:** 194,560 + 5,440 = 200,000. Balanced. Initial amortized cost = **$194,560**.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — effective interest**  
Interest expense = $194,560 × 5% = **$9,728**  
Cash interest = **$8,000**  
Discount amortization = 9,728 − 8,000 = **$1,728**  
Ending amortized cost = 194,560 + 1,728 = **$196,288**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 9,728 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,728 |
| Cash | | 8,000 |

**Check:** 9,728 = 1,728 + 8,000. Balanced.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVO adjustment (general risk → NI)**  
AC $196,288 − FV $192,000 = **$4,288** required debit FVA  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 4,288 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 4,288 |
| *General risk: unrealized gain in net income* | | |

**Check:** 4,288 = 4,288. Net liability = 196,288 − 4,288 = **$192,000**.

**d. December 31, Year 2 — effective interest**  
Interest expense = $196,288 × 5% = **$9,814** (rounded to whole dollars, textbook style)  
Cash = **$8,000**  
Amortization = 9,814 − 8,000 = **$1,814**  
Ending AC = 196,288 + 1,814 = **$198,102**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 9,814 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,814 |
| Cash | | 8,000 |

**Check:** 9,814 = 1,814 + 8,000. Balanced. Unamortized discount remaining = 200,000 − 198,102 = **$1,898**.

**e. December 31, Year 2 — FVO adjustment (instrument-specific credit risk → OCI)**  

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost, 12/31/Y2 | 198,102 |
| Fair value, 12/31/Y2 | 188,000 |
| **Required FVA debit balance** | **10,102** |
| Beginning FVA (from Y1) | 4,288 Dr |
| **Additional FVA debit needed** | **5,814** |

Chapter application for own-credit portion: the **period adjustment** that completes the move of the liability to FV when the cause is instrument-specific credit risk is recorded in **OCI** (ASC 825-10-45-5). Exam-style entry for the Year 2 adjustment amount:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 5,814 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 5,814 |
| *Instrument-specific credit risk: unrealized gain in OCI (not NI)* | | |

**Check:** 5,814 = 5,814.  
Ending FVA = 4,288 + 5,814 = **$10,102** Dr.  
Net liability = 198,102 − 10,102 = **$188,000**.

**Two-year FVA bridge**

| Year | AC | FV | Req. FVA Dr | Beg. FVA | Adj. Dr | G/L account | End. FVA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | 196,288 | 192,000 | 4,288 | 0 | 4,288 | Unrealized G/L—**Income** +4,288 | 4,288 |
| 2 | 198,102 | 188,000 | 10,102 | 4,288 | 5,814 | Unrealized G/L—**OCI** +5,814 | 10,102 |

**f. Balance sheet — December 31, Year 2**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $200,000 |
| Less: Discount on bonds payable | (1,898) |
| Amortized cost | 198,102 |
| Less: Fair value adjustment—bond payable | (10,102) |
| **Bonds payable, at fair value** | **$188,000** |

**g. January 1, Year 3 — early redemption at fair value + AOCI reclassification**

Remove face, remaining discount, and FVA; pay cash $188,000. Net carrying amount = 198,102 − 10,102 = 188,000 = cash paid → **no additional extinguishment gain/loss**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,898 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 10,102 |
| Cash | | 188,000 |
| *Redeem bonds at FV; clear related discount and FVA* | | |

**Check:** 200,000 = 1,898 + 10,102 + 188,000 = 200,000. Balanced.

Reclassify AOCI (own-credit unrealized gain) to net income upon redemption:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI (AOCI) | 5,814 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income (reclassification) | | 5,814 |
| *Reclassify AOCI related to instrument-specific credit risk into NI on redemption* | | |

**Check:** 5,814 = 5,814. Balanced.

**Key insight:** Always update **amortized cost** (effective interest) first, then record FVA so **AC ± FVA = fair value**. General risk → **NI**; own (instrument-specific) credit risk → **OCI**, with reclassification into earnings when the liability is settled or matures. FVO election is made at issuance and is generally irrevocable.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of FVO fair value changes (NI vs OCI)
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Classification — general risk vs instrument-specific credit risk under the fair value option for liabilities  

**Question 4A:**  
On January 1, **Ridgeway Components Inc.** issued $100,000 of bonds at par and elected the fair value option. At year-end, the bonds’ fair value is $93,000 **solely because Ridgeway violated a debt covenant**, increasing the market’s assessment of Ridgeway’s **own credit risk**. Interest has already been recorded. Which year-end entry is appropriate?

- A) Debit Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable $7,000; credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** $7,000  
- B) Debit Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable $7,000; credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—**OCI** $7,000  
- C) Debit Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income $7,000; credit Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable $7,000  
- D) Debit Bonds Payable $7,000; credit Gain on Extinguishment $7,000  

**Answer:** **B.** A decline in the fair value of the liability caused by **instrument-specific credit risk** is recognized as an unrealized gain in **OCI** (not net income). Debiting Fair Value Adjustment reduces the net liability from $100,000 to $93,000. Option A would be correct if the FV decline were due to **general** (benchmark) interest-rate risk. Option C records the wrong direction (would increase the net liability). Option D incorrectly treats a period-end FV mark as an extinguishment of face.

**Question 4B:**  
Which statement about electing the fair value option for a long-term note payable is **correct**?

- A) The election may be applied only to the interest-rate risk portion of the note, leaving credit risk at amortized cost.  
- B) Once elected for a specific instrument on its election date, the election is generally **irrevocable** and must apply to the **entire** instrument.  
- C) Management may switch from FVO back to amortized cost at each year-end if fair values become less reliable.  
- D) The fair value option is available only if the note is convertible into equity.  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 825: FVO is elected **instrument by instrument**, is generally **irrevocable**, and applies to the **entire** instrument (not specified risks or portions). A is wrong (no partial-risk election). C contradicts irrevocability. D is false—FVO applies to most financial liabilities including ordinary bonds and notes.
