# Agent 221 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-5

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Determine the initial and subsequent measurement of indefinite life intangible assets and goodwill  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition of goodwill:** residual = purchase price − fair value of identifiable net assets acquired in a business combination
- **Identifiable intangibles first:** measure separable/contractual intangibles (brand/trade name, customer relationships, acquired IPR&D, licenses) at fair value **before** residual goodwill
- **Indefinite-life intangibles (non-crypto):** capitalize at cost; **do not amortize**
- **Acquired in-process R&D:** initially an **indefinite-life** intangible until project completion or abandonment
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-period carrying-amount roll-forward with **zero amortization** for goodwill and non-crypto indefinite-life intangibles; goodwill roll-forward (beg + acquisition − disposal; no amort column)
- **Crypto assets (Demo 13-5A path):** indefinite-life intangibles remeasured to **fair value** each reporting date; unrealized gains/losses in **net income**; multi-period FV schedule
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** crypto fair-value remeasurement only (no amortization entry for goodwill/indefinite non-crypto)
- **Disposal JE:** remove full unamortized carrying amount of indefinite-life intangible (or crypto at last FV carrying amount); difference vs proceeds = gain/loss
- **Presentation:** goodwill as a **separate** balance-sheet line; other indefinite-life intangibles not amortized; crypto separate with FV changes in income

### Q1 — CORE — Acquisition residual goodwill; multi-year subsequent measurement schedule; finite contrast; disposal
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE (goodwill residual + indefinite brand/IPR&D); multi-year subsequent carrying-amount schedule (zero amortization emphasis); period-end finite amort only; disposal of indefinite-life brand  
**Scenario:**  
**Solstice Media Group** (calendar-year, **public** company) acquires **100% of Tidewater Podcasts LLC** for **$875,000 cash** on **January 1, Year 1**. Solstice obtains control and records Tidewater’s identifiable assets and liabilities at fair value; any residual is goodwill.

Tidewater’s acquisition-date data and Solstice’s fair-value estimates:

| Account | Book value (as reported) | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | $55,000 | $55,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 140,000 | 125,000 |
| Inventory | 95,000 | 110,000 |
| Property, plant, and equipment (net) | 420,000 | 480,000 |
| Brand name (not on Tidewater’s books; renewable indefinitely) | — | 90,000 |
| Customer relationships (not on Tidewater’s books; 6-year finite life) | — | 48,000 |
| Acquired in-process research and development (incomplete project) | — | 65,000 |
| **Total assets at fair value** | | **$973,000** |
| Accounts payable | 80,000 | 80,000 |
| Long-term debt | 220,000 | 210,000 |
| **Total liabilities at fair value** | | **$290,000** |

Solstice does **not** elect the private-company goodwill amortization alternative. There is **no impairment** of goodwill or indefinite-life intangibles in Years 1–2 (impairment testing is LO 13-6). Customer relationships are amortized straight-line over **6 years** with zero residual (**finite contrast only**).

On **June 30, Year 3**, Solstice sells the **Tidewater brand name** to an unrelated party for **$78,000 cash**. No amortization was ever recorded on the brand.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **fair value of identifiable net assets** and **goodwill** recorded on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the acquisition. Prove Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare a **two-year subsequent measurement / carrying-amount schedule** (Year 1 and Year 2) for: (1) Brand name, (2) Acquired IPR&D, (3) Goodwill, and (4) Customer relationships. Include amortization (if any) each year and ending carrying amounts.  
d. Prepare any **December 31, Year 1 and Year 2 period-end adjusting journal entries** related to these four assets under LO 13-5 subsequent measurement.  
e. Prepare a **goodwill roll-forward schedule** from January 1, Year 1 through December 31, Year 2 (beginning balance, acquisitions, amortization, ending balance).  
f. Prepare the **June 30, Year 3 disposal journal entry** for the brand name sale.  
g. State Year-2 year-end **balance-sheet presentation** of goodwill versus the other intangibles.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Identifiable net assets and goodwill**

Fair value of identifiable assets = **$973,000**  
Fair value of identifiable liabilities = **$290,000**  
Fair value of **identifiable net assets** = $973,000 − $290,000 = **$683,000**

\[
\text{Goodwill} = \text{Purchase price} - \text{FV of identifiable net assets} = \$875{,}000 - \$683{,}000 = \mathbf{\$192{,}000}
\]

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of acquisition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 55,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | 125,000 | |
| Inventory | 110,000 | |
| Property, Plant, and Equipment | 480,000 | |
| Brand Name (indefinite life) | 90,000 | |
| Customer Relationships (finite life) | 48,000 | |
| Acquired In-Process Research and Development (indefinite until complete) | 65,000 | |
| Goodwill | 192,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 80,000 |
| Long-Term Debt | | 210,000 |
| Cash | | 875,000 |
| *Record Tidewater assets/liabilities at FV; residual to goodwill* | | |

**Check:**  
Dr = 55,000 + 125,000 + 110,000 + 480,000 + 90,000 + 48,000 + 65,000 + 192,000 = **1,165,000**  
Cr = 80,000 + 210,000 + 875,000 = **1,165,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis) — Years 1 and 2**

Annual amortization of customer relationships = $48,000 ÷ 6 = **$8,000**.  
Brand, IPR&D, and goodwill: **$0 amortization** each year.

| Asset | 1/1/Y1 CA | Y1 amort. | 12/31/Y1 CA | Y2 amort. | 12/31/Y2 CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Brand name (indefinite) | $90,000 | **$0** | $90,000 | **$0** | $90,000 |
| Acquired IPR&D (indefinite until complete) | 65,000 | **0** | 65,000 | **0** | 65,000 |
| Goodwill | 192,000 | **0** | 192,000 | **0** | 192,000 |
| Customer relationships (finite, 6-yr SL) | 48,000 | **8,000** | 40,000 | **8,000** | 32,000 |

**d. Period-end adjusting journal entries (Years 1 and 2)**

*Indefinite-life brand, IPR&D, and goodwill:* **no amortization adjusting entry** either year.

*Finite contrast only — December 31, Year 1 and Year 2 (same amount each year):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense—Customer Relationships | 8,000 | |
| Accumulated Amortization—Customer Relationships | | 8,000 |
| *Only finite-life intangible is amortized* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000 each year.

**e. Goodwill roll-forward schedule (Years 1–2)**

| Description | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Balance, January 1, Year 1 (before acquisition) | $0 |
| Acquisition of Tidewater Podcasts (1/1/Y1) | 192,000 |
| Amortization | **0** |
| Impairments / other adjustments | 0 |
| **Balance, December 31, Year 1** | **$192,000** |
| Amortization Year 2 | **0** |
| Impairments / other adjustments | 0 |
| **Balance, December 31, Year 2** | **$192,000** |

**f. June 30, Year 3 — disposal of indefinite-life brand name**

Carrying amount of brand = **$90,000** (never amortized)  
Cash proceeds = **$78,000**  
Loss on disposal = $90,000 − $78,000 = **$12,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 78,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Brand Name | 12,000 | |
| Brand Name | | 90,000 |
| *Remove indefinite-life brand at full carrying amount; recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 78,000 + 12,000 = Cr 90,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Presentation at December 31, Year 2**  
- **Goodwill $192,000** — separate line item on the statement of financial position (not combined with other intangibles).  
- **Brand name $90,000** and **Acquired IPR&D $65,000** — among intangible assets other than goodwill, **unamortized**.  
- **Customer relationships** — finite-life intangible, net of accumulated amortization ($48,000 − $16,000 = **$32,000**).

**Key insight:** After identifiable assets (including previously unrecognized brand, customer relationships, and IPR&D) and liabilities are measured at FV, **goodwill is pure residual**. Subsequent measurement for indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill is a **flat carrying-amount schedule** (no amortization); only finite-life intangibles reduce through periodic amortization. Disposal removes the **full** unamortized carrying amount.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Acquisition residual; subsequent schedule; disposal with gain
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial recognition of goodwill and indefinite-life intangibles; multi-year zero-amortization subsequent schedule; disposal of trade name with gain  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Outdoor Brands Corp.** (calendar-year, public) acquires **100% of Pinecrest Gear Co.** for **$1,240,000 cash** on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Account | Book value | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | $75,000 | $75,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 175,000 | 160,000 |
| Inventory | 130,000 | 145,000 |
| Property, plant, and equipment (net) | 555,000 | 610,000 |
| Trade name (not on Pinecrest’s books; indefinite life) | — | 125,000 |
| Renewable broadcast license (indefinite life) | — | 50,000 |
| Acquired in-process R&D (incomplete) | — | 80,000 |
| **Total assets at fair value** | | **$1,245,000** |
| Accounts payable | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| Notes payable | 265,000 | 250,000 |
| **Total liabilities at fair value** | | **$350,000** |

No private-company goodwill amortization election. No impairment in Years 1–2. On **March 15, Year 2**, Cascade sells the **trade name** for **$140,000 cash**. The renewable broadcast license and IPR&D remain held; IPR&D project is still incomplete at December 31, Year 2.

**Required:**  
a. Compute FV of identifiable net assets and **goodwill**.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 acquisition journal entry**.  
c. Prepare a **Year-1 and Year-2 carrying-amount schedule** for Trade name, Broadcast license, Acquired IPR&D, and Goodwill (show amortization each year).  
d. Prepare the **March 15, Year 2 disposal entry** for the trade name.  
e. After the disposal, compute **total unamortized indefinite-life intangibles other than goodwill** and **goodwill** at December 31, Year 2.  
f. State the **amortization expense related to goodwill and the three indefinite-life intangibles** for Year 1 and Year 2.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Goodwill**

FV identifiable assets = **$1,245,000**  
FV identifiable liabilities = **$350,000**  
FV identifiable net assets = $1,245,000 − $350,000 = **$895,000**

\[
\text{Goodwill} = \$1{,}240{,}000 - \$895{,}000 = \mathbf{\$345{,}000}
\]

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 75,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | 160,000 | |
| Inventory | 145,000 | |
| Property, Plant, and Equipment | 610,000 | |
| Trade Name (indefinite life) | 125,000 | |
| Renewable Broadcast License (indefinite life) | 50,000 | |
| Acquired In-Process Research and Development | 80,000 | |
| Goodwill | 345,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 100,000 |
| Notes Payable | | 250,000 |
| Cash | | 1,240,000 |
| *Assets/liabilities at FV; residual goodwill* | | |

**Check:**  
Dr = 75,000 + 160,000 + 145,000 + 610,000 + 125,000 + 50,000 + 80,000 + 345,000 = **1,590,000**  
Cr = 100,000 + 250,000 + 1,240,000 = **1,590,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — Years 1–2 (before considering Y2 disposal timing)**

Through December 31, Year 1 (full year held):

| Asset | 1/1/Y1 CA | Y1 amort. | 12/31/Y1 CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Trade name (indefinite) | $125,000 | **$0** | $125,000 |
| Renewable broadcast license (indefinite) | 50,000 | **0** | 50,000 |
| Acquired IPR&D (indefinite until complete) | 80,000 | **0** | 80,000 |
| Goodwill | 345,000 | **0** | 345,000 |

Year 2 (trade name disposed 3/15/Y2; others held full year):

| Asset | 1/1/Y2 CA | Y2 amort. | CA removed on disposal | 12/31/Y2 CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Trade name | $125,000 | **$0** | $125,000 | $0 |
| Renewable broadcast license | 50,000 | **0** | — | 50,000 |
| Acquired IPR&D | 80,000 | **0** | — | 80,000 |
| Goodwill | 345,000 | **0** | — | 345,000 |

**No amortization journal entries** for any of the above in Year 1 or Year 2.

**d. March 15, Year 2 — disposal of trade name**

Carrying amount = **$125,000**  
Proceeds = **$140,000**  
**Gain on disposal** = $140,000 − $125,000 = **$15,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 140,000 | |
| Trade Name | | 125,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Intangible Asset | | 15,000 |
| *Remove trade name at unamortized carrying amount; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr 140,000 = Cr 125,000 + 15,000. **Balanced.**

**e. December 31, Year 2 balances**  
Indefinite-life intangibles other than goodwill = License $50,000 + IPR&D $80,000 = **$130,000**  
Goodwill = **$345,000**

**f. Amortization expense (goodwill + three indefinite-life items)**  
Year 1: **$0**  
Year 2: **$0**

**Key insight:** Changing every number does not change the model: **FV net assets first**, residual **goodwill**, indefinite-life items and goodwill **held at cost with zero amortization** until disposal or impairment (impairment = LO 13-6). Multi-year schedules stay flat for these assets.

### Q3 — CORE — Crypto multi-period FV subsequent measurement schedule; standalone indefinite trademark; disposal
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Multi-period crypto fair-value subsequent measurement schedule and period-end adjusting JEs (Demo 13-5A path); standalone indefinite trademark at cost with no amortization; disposal of crypto  
**Scenario:**  
**LumenForge Analytics LLC** (calendar year) holds two types of indefinite-life intangibles in Years 1–3:

| Date | Transaction |
|---|---|
| January 1, Year 1 | Purchases **3 units** of a fungible crypto asset meeting ASC 350-60 criteria for **$42,000 per unit** cash (**$126,000** total). Classifies as indefinite-life intangible (crypto). |
| April 1, Year 1 | Pays **$28,000** purchase price plus **$3,500** external legal/registration fees cash for a **trademark** management concludes has an **indefinite** useful life (renewable indefinitely; no foreseeable limit on cash-flow contribution). |
| December 31, Year 1 | Still holds all 3 crypto units. Fair value is **$47,000 per unit**. Trademark still indefinite; no impairment indicated. |
| December 31, Year 2 | Still holds all 3 crypto units. Fair value is **$39,000 per unit**. Trademark unchanged; no impairment. |
| April 10, Year 3 | Sells **all 3** crypto units for **$121,000 cash** total. Trademark still held. |

LumenForge accounts for crypto assets at fair value each reporting date with changes in net income. The trademark is **not** a crypto asset.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry for the crypto purchase (**initial recognition**).  
b. Prepare the **April 1, Year 1** journal entry for the trademark (**initial recognition at cost**).  
c. Prepare a **multi-period subsequent measurement schedule** for the crypto holdings from acquisition through disposal (show units, cost basis, fair value, remeasurement gain/loss, and carrying amount at each reporting date and at disposal).  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 and Year 2 period-end adjusting entries** for crypto fair-value remeasurement.  
e. Prepare the **April 10, Year 3 disposal entry** for the crypto sale.  
f. State December 31, Year 2 **carrying amounts** of (1) crypto assets and (2) the trademark, and whether either was amortized in Years 1–2.  
g. Briefly contrast **income-statement and balance-sheet presentation** of crypto FV changes versus the trademark.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of crypto assets**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crypto Assets (Bitcoin/fungible crypto) | 126,000 | |
| Cash | | 126,000 |
| *3 units × $42,000; indefinite-life intangible at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 126,000.

**b. April 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of indefinite-life trademark**

Cost = $28,000 + $3,500 = **$31,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trademark (indefinite life) | 31,500 | |
| Cash | | 31,500 |
| *Purchase price + directly attributable legal/registration costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 31,500.

**c. Multi-period subsequent measurement schedule — crypto (emphasis)**

| Date | Event | Units | Cost basis (total) | FV per unit | FV total | Remeasurement gain (loss) | Carrying amount |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1/1/Y1 | Purchase | 3 | $126,000 | $42,000 | $126,000 | — | $126,000 |
| 12/31/Y1 | Period-end FV | 3 | 126,000 | 47,000 | 141,000 | **+$15,000** | **141,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Period-end FV | 3 | 126,000 | 39,000 | 117,000 | **−$24,000** | **117,000** |
| 4/10/Y3 | Sale | 3 | 126,000 | n/a | proceeds $121,000 | disposal gain **+$4,000** vs CA | **0** |

Computations:  
Y1 FV total = 3 × $47,000 = **$141,000**; gain = $141,000 − $126,000 = **$15,000**  
Y2 FV total = 3 × $39,000 = **$117,000**; loss = $117,000 − $141,000 = **$24,000**  
Y3 disposal gain = $121,000 − $117,000 = **$4,000**

**Trademark subsequent measurement (same periods):** carrying amount remains **$31,500** every date; amortization each year = **$0** (not a crypto asset; held at cost subject to impairment under LO 13-6).

**d. Period-end adjusting journal entries — crypto fair value**

*December 31, Year 1:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crypto Assets | 15,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain—Income ($141,000 − $126,000) | | 15,000 |
| *Remeasure crypto to fair value; gain in net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000.

*December 31, Year 2:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Loss—Income ($141,000 − $117,000) | 24,000 | |
| Crypto Assets | | 24,000 |
| *Remeasure crypto to fair value; loss in net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000.

*Trademark:* **no period-end amortization or FV adjusting entry** under LO 13-5 (cost model; impairment is LO 13-6).

**e. April 10, Year 3 — disposal of crypto assets**

Carrying amount immediately before sale = **$117,000** (last FV measurement)  
Proceeds = **$121,000**  
Gain = **$4,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 121,000 | |
| Crypto Assets | | 117,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Crypto Assets | | 4,000 |
| *Remove crypto at last carrying amount; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr 121,000 = Cr 117,000 + 4,000. **Balanced.**

**f. December 31, Year 2 carrying amounts and amortization**  
1. Crypto assets: **$117,000** (fair value); **not amortized** (remeasured, not amortized).  
2. Trademark: **$31,500** (cost); **not amortized**.

**g. Presentation contrast**  
- **Crypto:** reported as an intangible asset **separate from other intangibles**, at **fair value**; unrealized gains/losses in **net income** (presented separately from changes in carrying amounts of other intangibles).  
- **Trademark:** reported among other indefinite-life intangibles at **historical cost** (unamortized); no periodic income effect until impairment (LO 13-6) or disposal.

**Key insight:** Non-crypto indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill use a **cost-based subsequent schedule with zero amortization**. Crypto (core Demo 13-5A) is the exception: a **fair-value subsequent measurement schedule** with period-end adjusting entries each reporting date, and disposal compares proceeds to the **last FV carrying amount**.

### Q4 — MC — Subsequent measurement classification
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Classification of subsequent measurement — amortize finite patent only; not trademark, incomplete IPR&D, or goodwill (public company)  
**Question:**  
**Oakridge Precision Inc.** (public company; no private-company goodwill alternative elected) reports the following acquired assets at December 31, Year 1, immediately after acquisition (no impairment indicated):

| Asset | Carrying amount | Useful life assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Patent | $60,000 | Finite — remaining 10 years legal/useful life |
| Trademark | 95,000 | Indefinite — renewable; no foreseeable limit |
| Acquired in-process R&D | 40,000 | Incomplete project; indefinite until complete |
| Goodwill | 175,000 | Indefinite (public company) |

Under US GAAP for subsequent measurement **after initial recognition** (ignore impairment), which asset(s) should Oakridge **amortize** in Year 2?

- A) Patent, trademark, IPR&D, and goodwill  
- B) Patent only  
- C) Patent and goodwill  
- D) Patent, trademark, and IPR&D  

**Answer:** **B.** Only the **finite-life patent** is amortized systematically over its useful life. Indefinite-life trademark, incomplete acquired IPR&D, and goodwill (public company) are **not amortized**; they remain at cost under LO 13-5 subsequent measurement (subject to impairment testing under LO 13-6).
