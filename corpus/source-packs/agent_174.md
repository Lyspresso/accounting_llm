# Agent 174 — CORE DEMO — LO 8-5

**Chapter:** 8  
**LO title:** Noncurrent Note Receivable [Stated Rate = 0%; Market Rate = 10%]  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** noninterest-bearing (0% stated) noncurrent note received in exchange for inventory (or other noncash asset) is measured at the **more clearly determinable** amount—typically fair value of the asset sold or PV of future cash flows at the **market (imputed) rate**; face − initial carrying amount = **Discount on Note Receivable**; sales revenue (or gain/loss) is the **net** amount, not face
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** **effective interest method**—Interest revenue = beginning carrying amount × market rate; cash interest = face × 0% = **$0**; discount amortization = interest revenue; carrying amount rolls up to face at maturity
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** accrue interest revenue and amortize discount each reporting date (note-year or fiscal-year allocation when anniversary ≠ year-end)
- **Disposal / maturity settlement JE:** collect face cash; remove Note Receivable (discount is fully amortized by maturity so net CV = face)
- **Why discount exists when stated rate = 0%:** total cash received at maturity exceeds the cash-selling-price equivalent of the goods; the difference is **interest revenue over time**, not additional sales revenue
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all face, PV, rates timing, and company facts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Zero-interest NR: inventory sale, full amort schedule, annual accruals, maturity
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of 0% noncurrent note at FV of inventory; effective-interest subsequent measurement schedule; period-end discount amortization JEs; maturity collection of face  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Supply Co.** (calendar year-end) sells inventory on **January 1, Year 1**. The inventory’s cash selling price (fair value) is **$20,000**. In exchange, Meridian receives a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with a **face (stated) amount of $26,620**. The market rate for notes of similar risk is **10%**. Principal is due in a lump sum on **December 31, Year 3**. There are **no** periodic cash interest receipts (stated rate = 0%). Ignore cost of goods sold.

**Required:**  
a. Verify that the **present value** of the note at 10% equals the inventory fair value of $20,000, and prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **full subsequent measurement (effective interest) schedule** for the life of the note (columns: date/period, cash interest, interest revenue, discount amortization, ending carrying amount).  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting JEs** on **December 31, Year 1**, **Year 2**, and **Year 3** to record interest revenue and discount amortization.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 maturity / settlement JE** for collection of the face amount (after the Year 3 interest entry in part c).  
e. Briefly explain why sales revenue is **$20,000** rather than **$26,620**, and what the **$6,620** discount represents in total.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Present value check and initial recognition**

\[
PV = \frac{\$26{,}620}{(1.10)^3} = \$26{,}620 \times 0.7513148\ldots = \$20{,}000
\]

(Equivalently: \( \$20{,}000 \times (1.10)^3 = \$20{,}000 \times 1.331 = \$26{,}620 \).)

Discount on Note Receivable = Face − PV = $26,620 − $20,000 = **$6,620**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Receivable | 26,620 | |
| Discount on Note Receivable | | 6,620 |
| Sales Revenue | | 20,000 |
| *Record inventory sale for 3-year 0% note at FV / PV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 26,620 = Cr 6,620 + 20,000. **Balanced.**  
Initial net carrying amount = $26,620 − $6,620 = **$20,000**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (effective interest; market rate 10%)**

Cash interest each period = Face × Stated rate = $26,620 × 0% = **$0**  
Interest revenue each period = Beginning carrying amount × **10%**  
Discount amortization = Interest revenue − Cash interest = Interest revenue (since cash = 0)

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest revenue (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $20,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $2,000 | $2,000 | 22,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 2,200 | 2,200 | 24,200 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 0 | 2,420 | 2,420 | 26,620 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$6,620** | **$6,620** | |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Total amortization = total discount = $6,620  
- Ending CV at maturity = face $26,620  
- Interest Y1: $20,000 × 10% = $2,000; Y2: $22,000 × 10% = $2,200; Y3: $24,200 × 10% = $2,420  
- Sales revenue + total interest revenue = $20,000 + $6,620 = $26,620 = total cash collected

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 2,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 2,000 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount (0% stated → amort = interest rev.)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,000 = Cr 2,000. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $6,620 − $2,000 = $4,620; net CV = $26,620 − $4,620 = **$22,000**.

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 2,200 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 2,200 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,200 = Cr 2,200. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $4,620 − $2,200 = $2,420; net CV = **$24,200**.

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 2,420 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 2,420 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize remaining discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,420 = Cr 2,420. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $0; net CV = **$26,620** (face).

**d. December 31, Year 3 — maturity settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 26,620 | |
| Notes Receivable | | 26,620 |
| *Collect face amount of note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 26,620 = Cr 26,620. **Balanced.**

**e. Conceptual**  
Sales revenue is measured at the **cash-equivalent selling price** ($20,000), not face. The $6,620 discount is **implicit interest** (financing component) recognized as **interest revenue** over the three-year term under the effective interest method. Separating sales from interest prevents overstating revenue at the sale date when the note is noninterest-bearing.

**Key insight:** With a 0% stated rate, every dollar of effective interest revenue is **pure discount amortization**—there is no cash coupon. The amortization schedule is the engine: market rate × beginning CV drives interest revenue and rolls the net receivable up to face by maturity.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Zero-interest NR: equipment noncash exchange, full schedule, maturity
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: 0% noncurrent note measured at PV (market 10%); equipment sale with loss; effective-interest schedule; period-end amort JEs; face collection at maturity  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperline Equipment Inc.** (calendar year-end) sells used equipment on **January 1, Year 1** and receives a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$19,965**. The market rate for similar notes is **10%**. Present value of the note (and fair value of the consideration) is **$15,000**. The equipment’s original cost is **$42,000**; accumulated depreciation on the sale date is **$25,000**. Principal is due **December 31, Year 3**. Copperline does **not** regularly sell this type of equipment. No cash interest is received during the term.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **book value** of the equipment, the **loss (or gain) on sale**, and the **discount** on the note. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **3-year effective interest amortization schedule**.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** period-end interest / amortization JEs.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** interest JE and the **maturity collection JE**.  
e. State the **balance sheet presentation** of the note at December 31, Year 1 (net amount and remaining discount).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Book value, loss, discount, and initial JE**

Book value of equipment = $42,000 − $25,000 = **$17,000**  
PV / initial measurement of note = **$15,000**  
Loss on sale = $17,000 − $15,000 = **$2,000**  
Discount = Face − PV = $19,965 − $15,000 = **$4,965**  
Check: $15,000 × (1.10)^3 = $15,000 × 1.331 = **$19,965**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Receivable | 19,965 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 25,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 2,000 | |
| Discount on Note Receivable | | 4,965 |
| Equipment | | 42,000 |
| *Sale of equipment for 3-year 0% note at PV $15,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 19,965 + 25,000 + 2,000 = 46,965; Cr 4,965 + 42,000 = 46,965. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest revenue (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $15,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $1,500 | $1,500 | 16,500 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 1,650 | 1,650 | 18,150 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 0 | 1,815 | 1,815 | 19,965 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$4,965** | **$4,965** | |

**Schedule checks:** total amort = discount $4,965; CV at maturity = face $19,965; Y1: $15,000 × 10% = $1,500; Y2: $16,500 × 10% = $1,650; Y3: $18,150 × 10% = $1,815.

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,500 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,500 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,650 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,650 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,650 = Cr 1,650. **Balanced.**

After Year 2: unamortized discount = $4,965 − $1,500 − $1,650 = **$1,815**; net CV = **$18,150**.

**d. December 31, Year 3 — interest and maturity**

*Interest / amortization*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,815 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,815 |
| *Accrue final period interest; clear discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,815 = Cr 1,815. **Balanced.**

*Collect face*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 19,965 | |
| Notes Receivable | | 19,965 |
| *Collect face of zero-interest note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 19,965 = Cr 19,965. **Balanced.**

**e. Balance sheet at December 31, Year 1**  
Note receivable, net of unamortized discount of **$3,465** ($4,965 − $1,500) — **$16,500**.  
(Typically classified as **noncurrent** at Dec. 31, Year 1, with reclassification of the portion due within one year as current when appropriate under the company’s policy near maturity.)

**Key insight:** Same 0% / market-rate mechanics as inventory notes: measure at PV, plug discount, amortize with effective interest, collect face. For non-inventory PPE, the difference between asset book value and note PV is **gain or loss on sale**, not an adjustment to the discount.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Mid-year zero-interest note: allocate amort schedule to fiscal year-ends
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting when note anniversary ≠ fiscal year-end—build note-year amort schedule, then allocate discount amortization (interest revenue) to calendar reporting periods; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Harbor & Pine Trading Co.** has a **December 31** year-end. On **July 1, Year 1**, Harbor sells inventory with a fair value of **$10,000** and receives a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$13,310**. The market (imputed) rate is **10%**. Face is due **June 30, Year 4**. Harbor records adjusting entries only at fiscal year-end (and on maturity). Ignore cost of goods sold.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **July 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **note-year effective interest amortization schedule** (periods ending June 30 of Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4).  
c. Prepare an **allocation schedule** assigning each note-year’s interest to calendar years (half-year splits; use whole dollars as shown in the answer key).  
d. Prepare **all period-end interest JEs**: December 31 Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, and June 30 Year 4.  
e. Prepare the **June 30, Year 4 maturity JE** collecting face (after the final interest entry).

**Answer key:**  

**a. July 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

Discount = $13,310 − $10,000 = **$3,310**  
(\( \$10{,}000 \times 1.10^3 = \$13{,}310 \).)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Receivable | 13,310 | |
| Discount on Note Receivable | | 3,310 |
| Sales Revenue | | 10,000 |
| *Record inventory sale for 3-year 0% note* | | |

**Check:** Dr 13,310 = Cr 3,310 + 10,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Note-year amortization schedule (effective interest, 10%)**

| Date | Cash interest | Interest revenue | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| July 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $10,000 |
| June 30, Year 2 | $0 | $1,000 | $1,000 | 11,000 |
| June 30, Year 3 | 0 | 1,100 | 1,100 | 12,100 |
| June 30, Year 4 | 0 | 1,210 | 1,210 | 13,310 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$3,310** | **$3,310** | |

**c. Allocation of note-year interest to fiscal (calendar) periods**

Half of each note-year amount is recognized in the calendar year that contains the first six months of that note-year; the other half in the following calendar year (through maturity).

| Note-year interest (period ending) | Amount | Dec. 31, Y1 | Dec. 31, Y2 | Dec. 31, Y3 | June 30, Y4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| June 30, Year 2 | $1,000 | $500 | $500 | | |
| June 30, Year 3 | 1,100 | | 550 | $550 | |
| June 30, Year 4 | 1,210 | | | 605 | $605 |
| **Totals** | **$3,310** | **$500** | **$1,050** | **$1,155** | **$605** |

**Allocation check:** $500 + $1,050 + $1,155 + $605 = **$3,310** (same total interest as the note-year schedule).

**d. Period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 500 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 500 |
| *6 months: $10,000 × 10% × 6/12 = $500* | | |

**Check:** Dr 500 = Cr 500. **Balanced.**  
Net CV at Dec. 31, Y1 = $10,000 + $500 = **$10,500**.

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,050 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,050 |
| *Remaining half of Y2 note-year + first half of Y3 note-year* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,050 = Cr 1,050. **Balanced.**  
(Composition: $500 + $550 = $1,050; equivalently, roughly $10,500 × 10% for full year with schedule alignment.)

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,155 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,155 |
| *Allocate remaining Y3 note-year half + first half of final note-year* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,155 = Cr 1,155. **Balanced.**  
($550 + $605 = $1,155.)

*June 30, Year 4 — final interest accrual*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 605 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 605 |
| *Final 6 months; discount fully amortized* | | |

**Check:** Dr 605 = Cr 605. **Balanced.**  
Cumulative amort = $500 + $1,050 + $1,155 + $605 = $3,310; unamortized discount = $0; CV = face **$13,310**.

**e. June 30, Year 4 — maturity settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 13,310 | |
| Notes Receivable | | 13,310 |
| *Collect face of noninterest-bearing note* | | |

**Check:** Dr 13,310 = Cr 13,310. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** The amortization schedule follows **note interest periods**, not the fiscal calendar. When year-end falls mid-cycle, **split** each schedule line across reporting periods so total interest over the life is unchanged—only the **timing** within calendar years changes (Demo 8-5C part b logic).

### Q4 — MC — Measurement and interest when stated rate is 0%
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Classification / measurement — initial sales amount vs face; interest revenue equals discount amortization when stated rate is zero  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 1, Larkspur Co. sells inventory with a cash selling price of **$50,000** and receives a **four-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$73,205**. The market rate for similar notes is **10%**. Larkspur uses the effective interest method. Which statement is **correct**?

- A) Sales revenue is recorded at **$73,205**; no interest revenue is recognized over the note’s life because the stated rate is 0%.  
- B) Sales revenue is recorded at **$50,000**; Year 1 interest revenue is **$5,000** (and equals Year 1 discount amortization).  
- C) Sales revenue is recorded at **$50,000**; Year 1 interest revenue is **$0** because no cash interest is received.  
- D) The note is recorded only at net **$50,000** with no separate Discount on Note Receivable account, and face is never used in the journal entries.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Initial measurement of the receivable (and sales) is the cash selling price / PV of **$50,000**, not face. Discount = $73,205 − $50,000 = $23,205 (face is still recorded with a contra discount). Under effective interest, Year 1 interest revenue = $50,000 × 10% = **$5,000**, which equals discount amortization when the stated rate is 0%. Cash interest of zero does **not** mean zero interest revenue—the financing element is recognized over time.  
(A is wrong: that would overstate sales and ignore imputed interest. C confuses cash interest with interest revenue. D is incomplete presentation; companies typically record face and discount separately even though net CV starts at PV.)

**Check (optional verification):** $50,000 × (1.10)^4 = $50,000 × 1.4641 = **$73,205**.
