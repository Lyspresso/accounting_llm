# Agent 347 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-9

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Describe property, plant, and equipment disclosures and ratio analyses  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize invoice + freight + installation (ready-for-use costs) into PPE
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year straight-line cost / accumulated depreciation / book value rollforward; AD-to-gross-cost as a life-expired indicator
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Depreciation Expense → Accumulated Depreciation (single asset and multi-class compound entries; update dep through disposal date before settlement)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove cost and AD; recognize gain or loss on the income statement
- **ASC 360-10-50-1 required disclosures:** depreciation expense; balances of major classes of depreciable assets (by nature or function); accumulated depreciation (by class or in total); description of depreciation methods by major class
- **Commonly expected note items:** fully depreciated assets still in service; significant liens disclosed with **liabilities** (not as an asset offset)
- **Ratio analysis:** **Asset turnover** = Net sales ÷ Average total assets; **Return on assets** = Net income ÷ Average total assets; **Fixed asset turnover** = Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets (PPE net)
- **Depreciation method and asset age vs ROA:** ROA tends to rise as plant ages; when assets are new, SL often yields higher ROA than accelerated methods; relation can reverse as assets age

### Q1 — CORE — Ravenswood Fabrication: life cycle, period-end dep, disposal, note, ratios
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of heat-treat furnace; SL subsequent measurement schedule; **period-end adjusting dep JE (emphasis)**; mid-year disposal settlement; ASC 360 PPE note; asset turnover, ROA, fixed asset turnover  
**Scenario:**  
**Ravenswood Fabrication LLC** (calendar year-end) places a heat-treat furnace in service, records systematic depreciation, later disposes of it, and prepares year-end PPE disclosures and efficiency/profitability ratios for users of the financial statements.

**Part A — Heat-treat furnace life cycle**

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Acquisition / placed in service | January 1, Year 1 |
| Invoice price (cash) | $585,000 |
| Freight-in (cash) | $15,000 |
| Installation and testing (cash) | $20,000 |
| Estimated residual value | $20,000 |
| Estimated useful life | 10 years |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line (full months) |
| Disposal date | July 1, Year 5 |
| Cash proceeds on disposal | $340,000 |

**Part B — PPE note balances at December 31, Year 4** (furnace still held; included in machinery)

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | $360,000 | — |
| Buildings | 2,800,000 | $840,000 |
| Machinery and equipment | 2,100,000 | 945,000 |
| **Totals** | **$5,260,000** | **$1,785,000** |

Additional Year 4 facts: depreciation expense **$320,000**; all depreciable classes use **straight-line** (buildings 25–40 years; machinery 5–12 years); cost of **fully depreciated machinery still in service** **$110,000**; a bank note is secured by a first lien on the buildings (outstanding principal **$1,100,000**).

**Part C — Ratio inputs** (dollars)

| Date / period | PPE, net (fixed assets) | Total assets | Net sales | Net income |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning of prior year (1/1/Y3) | $3,100,000 | $9,200,000 | — | — |
| End of prior year (12/31/Y3) | 3,250,000 | 9,800,000 | $13,500,000 | $980,000 |
| End of current year (12/31/Y4) | 3,400,000 | 10,400,000 | 14,900,000 | 1,140,000 |

Treat **Year 3 = prior fiscal year** and **Year 4 = current fiscal year**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **capitalized cost** of the heat-treat furnace and prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry**. Prove Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–5 (full years) showing beginning book value, annual depreciation, ending accumulated depreciation, and ending book value. State annual SL depreciation and the depreciable base.  
c. **(Emphasis — period-end adjusting JE)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting entry for depreciation on the furnace. Also prepare a **compound multi-class** period-end adjusting entry for **Year 4** if annual depreciation by class is: buildings **$140,000**, machinery and equipment **$180,000** (total equals the $320,000 note amount). Prove each entry balances.  
d. Prepare the **July 1, Year 5** entries to (1) **update depreciation through the disposal date** (period-end-style adjusting JE for a partial period) and (2) record the **disposal**. Compute gain or loss. Journals must balance.  
e. List the **ASC 360-10-50-1 required disclosures** and prepare a **PPE note schedule** (cost, AD, net by class) with supplemental items (methods, dep expense, fully depreciated assets, lien placement). Compute **AD ÷ gross plant cost** at December 31, Year 4.  
f. Compute **(1) asset turnover**, **(2) return on assets**, and **(3) fixed asset turnover** for the **prior year** and the **current year** (round ratios to two decimal places). Briefly analyze whether efficiency and profitability of assets improved.

**Answer key:**

**a. Capitalized cost and initial recognition**  
Capitalized cost = $585,000 + $15,000 + $20,000 = **$620,000**

*January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition of heat-treat furnace*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Machinery and Equipment—Heat-Treat Furnace | 620,000 | |
| Cash | | 620,000 |
| *Capitalize invoice, freight, and installation into PPE* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 620,000. **Balanced.**

Depreciable base = $620,000 − $20,000 = **$600,000**  
Annual SL depreciation = $600,000 ÷ 10 = **$60,000**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (straight-line)**

| Year | Beg. BV | Depreciation | End. AD | End. BV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $620,000 | $60,000 | $60,000 | $560,000 |
| 2 | 560,000 | 60,000 | 120,000 | 500,000 |
| 3 | 500,000 | 60,000 | 180,000 | 440,000 |
| 4 | 440,000 | 60,000 | 240,000 | 380,000 |
| 5 (full year, if held) | 380,000 | 60,000 | 300,000 | 320,000 |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Annual dep constant $60,000; residual $20,000 reached after Year 10.  
- End. AD = year number × $60,000 for full years held.  
- End. BV = Cost − End. AD.

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (furnace)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Heat-Treat Furnace | 60,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Heat-Treat Furnace | | 60,000 |
| *Year 1 SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 60,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 4 — compound multi-class period-end adjusting JE*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 320,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Buildings | | 140,000 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery and Equipment | | 180,000 |
| *Allocate Year 4 depreciation by major class* | | |

**Check:** Dr 320,000 = Cr 140,000 + 180,000 = 320,000. **Balanced.**  
(This is the year-end entry that feeds **depreciation expense** and **accumulated depreciation** amounts disclosed under ASC 360-10-50-1.)

**d. July 1, Year 5 — update depreciation and dispose**

Depreciation for Jan 1–Jun 30, Year 5 (6/12):  
$60,000 × 6/12 = **$30,000**

*July 1, Year 5 — update depreciation through disposal date (partial-period adjusting JE)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Heat-Treat Furnace | 30,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Heat-Treat Furnace | | 30,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 30,000. **Balanced.**

Accumulated depreciation at disposal = $60,000 × 4 + $30,000 = **$270,000**  
Book value = $620,000 − $270,000 = **$350,000**  
**Loss on disposal** = $350,000 − $340,000 = **$10,000**

*July 1, Year 5 — disposal (settlement) of heat-treat furnace*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 340,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Heat-Treat Furnace | 270,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of PPE | 10,000 | |
| Machinery and Equipment—Heat-Treat Furnace | | 620,000 |
| *Remove cost and AD; recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 340,000 + 270,000 + 10,000 = **620,000** = Cr 620,000. **Balanced.**

**e. ASC 360-10-50-1 disclosures and PPE note**

**Required each reporting period (ASC 360-10-50-1):**  
1. **Depreciation expense** for the period  
2. **Balances of major classes** of depreciable assets, by nature or function  
3. **Accumulated depreciation**, by major class or in total  
4. **Description of depreciation methods** used by major classes of assets  

Also useful / commonly expected: postacquisition cost policy, idle plant assets, **fully depreciated assets still in service**, and **significant liens** (presented in the **liability** section / notes—not as an offset against the asset).

**PPE note schedule — December 31, Year 4**

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation | Net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | $360,000 | $— | $360,000 |
| Buildings | 2,800,000 | 840,000 | 1,960,000 |
| Machinery and equipment | 2,100,000 | 945,000 | 1,155,000 |
| **Total** | **$5,260,000** | **$1,785,000** | **$3,475,000** |

*Supplemental note items:*  
- Depreciation expense, Year 4: **$320,000**  
- Method: **straight-line** for buildings and machinery  
- Fully depreciated machinery still in service (cost): **$110,000**  
- Buildings are subject to a first lien securing notes payable of **$1,100,000** (disclose with **liabilities**, not as a deduction from plant assets)

**AD-to-gross plant cost (12/31/Y4)** = $1,785,000 ÷ $5,260,000 = **0.34 (34%)** — roughly 34% of recorded plant cost has been allocated through depreciation (an indicator of portion of service life expired, interpreted with care because land is nondepreciable and lives differ by class).

**f. Ratio analysis**

**Prior year (Year 3)**  
Average total assets = ($9,200,000 + $9,800,000) / 2 = **$9,500,000**  
Average fixed assets = ($3,100,000 + $3,250,000) / 2 = **$3,175,000**

| Ratio | Formula | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg total assets | $13,500,000 ÷ $9,500,000 | **1.42** |
| Return on assets | Net income ÷ Avg total assets | $980,000 ÷ $9,500,000 | **0.10** |
| Fixed asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg fixed assets | $13,500,000 ÷ $3,175,000 | **4.25** |

**Current year (Year 4)**  
Average total assets = ($9,800,000 + $10,400,000) / 2 = **$10,100,000**  
Average fixed assets = ($3,250,000 + $3,400,000) / 2 = **$3,325,000**

| Ratio | Formula | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg total assets | $14,900,000 ÷ $10,100,000 | **1.48** |
| Return on assets | Net income ÷ Avg total assets | $1,140,000 ÷ $10,100,000 | **0.11** |
| Fixed asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg fixed assets | $14,900,000 ÷ $3,325,000 | **4.48** |

**Analysis:** Asset turnover rose from 1.42 to 1.48, ROA from 0.10 to 0.11, and fixed asset turnover from 4.25 to 4.48. Sales and net income grew faster relative to average asset bases, so both **efficiency of asset use** and **profitability of assets** improved year over year.

**Key insight:** LO 12-9 pairs the **PPE measurement path** (capitalize → allocate via **period-end dep adjusting entries** → dispose) with **what users see**: major-class balances, AD, methods, dep expense, and the three core ratios. Always average **beginning and ending** balance-sheet amounts for ratio denominators; period-end dep JEs are what create the expense and AD figures that appear in the note and that reduce net PPE used in fixed asset turnover.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-class period-end dep, omitted-dep effect on ratios, disclosure checklist, SL vs accelerated ROA
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** **Period-end multi-class adjusting dep JE (emphasis)**; effect of omitted dep on PPE net, NI, and ratios; ASC 360 disclosure checklist and AD-to-cost; relation of depreciation method to ROA over asset age; disposal after period-end update  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Thermoformers Inc.** has a December 31 year-end. At the close of **Year 6**, the following depreciable assets require **period-end adjusting entries** before financial statements and ratio analysis are finalized. No Year 6 depreciation has been recorded yet.

| Asset | Cost | Residual | Useful life | Placed in service | Method |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Building | $2,400,000 | $200,000 | 40 years | Jan 1, Year 1 | Straight-line |
| Forming machine A | 360,000 | 40,000 | 8 years | Jan 1, Year 3 | Straight-line |
| Forming machine B | 180,000 | 0 | 5 years | **April 1, Year 6** | Straight-line |

**PPE gross and AD before any Year 6 depreciation** (includes building and both machines; land $250,000 is nondepreciable):

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation (before Y6 dep) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | $250,000 | — |
| Building | 2,400,000 | $275,000 |
| Machinery and equipment | 1,200,000 | 480,000 |
| **Totals** | **$3,850,000** | **$755,000** |

Additional facts:  
- Fully depreciated tooling still in service (cost): **$48,000**  
- Bank lien on the building: principal outstanding **$620,000**  
- Methods: straight-line for all depreciable classes  
- Draft (pre-closing) totals **before** recording Year 6 depreciation: total assets **$7,200,000**; PPE net = $3,850,000 − $755,000 = **$3,095,000**; pretax operating results before Year 6 depreciation yield **net income if no Year 6 dep is recorded** of **$540,000**; net sales Year 6 **$9,600,000**  
- Beginning of Year 6 balances (already after prior years’ dep): total assets **$6,800,000**; PPE net **$3,180,000**

On **December 31, Year 6**, after recording Year 6 depreciation, Lakeshore sold a delivery van (cost **$42,000**; AD after Year 6 update **$36,000**) for **$4,500** cash. The van’s Year 6 depreciation of **$3,500** is **included** in the multi-class dep total computed in part (a) (i.e., machinery dep includes the van).

**Selected multi-year data for ROA discussion (one plant asset group, ignore tax):**

| | Year 1 (new assets) | Year 6 (aged assets) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Operating income before depreciation | $480,000 | $480,000 |
| SL depreciation | 90,000 | 90,000 |
| Accelerated depreciation (same assets) | 150,000 | 36,000 |
| Average total assets if SL used | 1,800,000 | 1,260,000 |
| Average total assets if accelerated used | 1,710,000 | 1,152,000 |

(Net income for the ROA sketch = income before depreciation − depreciation; ignore other expenses.)

**Required:**  
a. **(Emphasis)** Compute Year 6 depreciation for the building, machine A, and machine B. Prepare the **compound period-end adjusting journal entry** that records all three (and state the total Year 6 depreciation expense related to these three assets). Prove Dr = Cr.  
b. After the Year 6 dep entry from (a), compute **updated** PPE note amounts (cost, AD, net by class — use the class AD given plus Year 6 dep allocated as: building $55,000; machinery $40,000 + $27,000 = $67,000). List the **four ASC 360-10-50-1 required items** and how **liens** and **fully depreciated assets still in service** should be handled. Compute **AD ÷ total plant cost**.  
c. Compute **Year 6 asset turnover, ROA, and fixed asset turnover** (two decimals) **after** recording Year 6 depreciation from (a). Show how NI and ending PPE net / total assets change if the period-end dep JE is **omitted**. (Assume the only effect of the $122,000 dep is to reduce NI and to reduce PPE net and total assets by $122,000.)  
d. Prepare the **disposal JE** for the delivery van (AD already updated). Where is the loss presented?  
e. Using the multi-year ROA data, compute **ROA under SL and under accelerated** for Year 1 and Year 6. Explain why ROA tends to **rise as plant assets age** and how **method choice** affects early vs later years.

**Answer key:**

**a. Period-end multi-class adjusting JE (emphasis)**

Building annual SL dep = ($2,400,000 − $200,000) / 40 = **$55,000**  
Machine A annual SL dep = ($360,000 − $40,000) / 8 = **$40,000**  
Machine B annual SL dep = ($180,000 − $0) / 5 = **$36,000**; Year 6 fraction = 9/12:  
$36,000 × 9/12 = **$27,000**

Total Year 6 depreciation on these three assets = $55,000 + $40,000 + $27,000 = **$122,000**

*December 31, Year 6 — compound period-end adjusting JE*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 122,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 55,000 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery and Equipment | | 67,000 |
| *Year 6 SL depreciation by major class (machine A $40,000 + machine B $27,000)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 122,000 = Cr 55,000 + 67,000 = 122,000. **Balanced.**

**b. PPE note after Year 6 dep and ASC 360 checklist**

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation | Net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | $250,000 | $— | $250,000 |
| Building | 2,400,000 | 330,000 | 2,070,000 |
| Machinery and equipment | 1,200,000 | 547,000 | 653,000 |
| **Total** | **$3,850,000** | **$877,000** | **$2,973,000** |

(Building AD: $275,000 + $55,000 = $330,000; Machinery AD: $480,000 + $67,000 = $547,000.)

**ASC 360-10-50-1 required:**  
1. Depreciation expense — **$122,000** (for these assets; entity-wide total may include other PPE)  
2. Major classes of depreciable assets — land / building / machinery balances as above  
3. Accumulated depreciation — by class or total **$877,000**  
4. Depreciation methods by major class — **straight-line** for building and machinery  

**Fully depreciated assets still in service:** disclose cost **$48,000**.  
**Significant liens:** report **$620,000** building lien in the **liability section / debt notes**, **not** as a contra-asset against PPE.

**AD-to-cost** = $877,000 ÷ $3,850,000 = **0.23 (23%)**.  
Users may read this as roughly **23% of recorded plant cost already allocated** to expense (proxy for portion of service potential expired), remembering land is not depreciated and class lives differ.

**c. Ratios after period-end dep; effect of omitting the adjusting JE**

After Year 6 dep of $122,000:  
Ending total assets = $7,200,000 − $122,000 = **$7,078,000**  
Ending PPE net = $3,095,000 − $122,000 = **$2,973,000**  
Net income = $540,000 − $122,000 = **$418,000**

Average total assets = ($6,800,000 + $7,078,000) / 2 = **$6,939,000**  
Average fixed assets = ($3,180,000 + $2,973,000) / 2 = **$3,076,500**

| Ratio | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| Asset turnover | $9,600,000 ÷ $6,939,000 | **1.38** |
| ROA | $418,000 ÷ $6,939,000 | **0.06** |
| Fixed asset turnover | $9,600,000 ÷ $3,076,500 | **3.12** |

**If the period-end dep JE is omitted** (incorrect):  
NI remains **$540,000**; ending TA **$7,200,000**; ending PPE net **$3,095,000**.  
Avg TA = ($6,800,000 + $7,200,000)/2 = **$7,000,000**  
Avg FA = ($3,180,000 + $3,095,000)/2 = **$3,137,500**  
Asset turnover = $9,600,000 ÷ $7,000,000 = **1.37**  
ROA = $540,000 ÷ $7,000,000 = **0.08**  
Fixed asset turnover = $9,600,000 ÷ $3,137,500 = **3.06**

**Interpretation:** Omitting the period-end adjusting JE **overstates** net income and asset bases and **overstates ROA** (0.08 vs correct 0.06). Asset turnover and fixed asset turnover are also misstated because average denominators are wrong. Exam emphasis: **period-end dep must be booked before** note amounts and ratio analysis are reliable.

**d. Disposal of delivery van**

BV of van = $42,000 − $36,000 = **$6,000**  
**Loss on disposal** = $6,000 − $4,500 = **$1,500**

**e. ROA and depreciation method over asset age**

| | Year 1 SL | Year 1 Accel. | Year 6 SL | Year 6 Accel. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NI (before dep − dep) | $390,000 | $330,000 | $390,000 | $444,000 |
| Avg total assets | 1,800,000 | 1,710,000 | 1,260,000 | 1,152,000 |
| **ROA** | **0.22** | **0.19** | **0.31** | **0.39** |

**Why ROA rises as assets age:** Numerator falls by only **one year’s** depreciation, while the denominator is reduced by **cumulative** depreciation to date (lower average assets) → ratio climbs over time regardless of method.  
**Method effect:** When assets are **new**, **straight-line** produces **higher** early earnings (and often higher ROA) than accelerated methods. As assets **age**, the relation **reverses** — accelerated methods have lower remaining dep, so later-year ROA can exceed SL (as in Year 6 above: 0.39 vs 0.31). Analysts comparing firms must consider **asset age mix** and **method choice**, not only the raw ratio.

**Key insight:** LO 12-9 is as much about **what goes in the notes and ratio analysis** as about the journal entries that feed those numbers. The **period-end adjusting JE** is the hinge: without it, dep expense, AD, PPE net, NI, and all three ratios are wrong. Disposal losses are IS items; liens stay with liabilities; AD/cost and method-vs-age effects stop naive cross-company ROA comparisons.

### Q4 — MC — Required PPE disclosure under ASC 360
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification of required depreciation and PPE disclosures (ASC 360-10-50-1)  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **required** to be disclosed for each reporting period related to depreciation and depreciable assets under ASC 360-10-50-1?

- A) Fair value of each major class of plant assets currently in use  
- B) Balances of major classes of depreciable assets, by nature or function  
- C) Budgeted capital expenditures for each of the next five years  
- D) Replacement cost of fully depreciated assets still in service  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 360-10-50-1 requires (1) depreciation expense, (2) balances of major classes of depreciable assets by nature or function, (3) accumulated depreciation by major class or in total, and (4) a description of depreciation methods by major classes. Fair value of PPE in use, multi-year capex budgets, and replacement cost of fully depreciated assets are **not** among the four required items (though cost of fully depreciated assets still in service is often **useful** voluntary information).

### Q4b — MC — Ratio identification and period-end effect
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification of PPE-related efficiency and profitability ratios; effect of period-end dep on ROA  
**Question:**  
**Fixed asset turnover** is computed as:

- A) Net income ÷ Average fixed assets  
- B) Average fixed assets ÷ Net sales  
- C) Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets  
- D) Net sales ÷ Average total assets  

**Answer:** **C.** Fixed asset turnover = **Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets** (activity/efficiency of PPE). Asset turnover uses **average total assets** in the denominator; ROA uses **net income** over **average total assets**. Recording the period-end depreciation adjusting JE reduces average fixed assets (and NI for ROA) and is a prerequisite for correct ratio computation.
