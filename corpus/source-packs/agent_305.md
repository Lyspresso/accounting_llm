# Agent 305 — CORE DEMO — LO 8-5

**Chapter:** 8  
**LO title:** Noncurrent Note Receivable [Stated Rate = 0%; Market Rate = 10%]  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** noninterest-bearing (0% stated) noncurrent note for inventory/noncash is measured at the **more clearly determinable** amount (FV of asset sold or PV of face at the **market/imputed rate**); face − initial CA = **Discount on Note Receivable**; sales (or other) revenue at **net**, not face
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** **effective interest**—Interest revenue = beginning carrying amount × market rate; cash interest = face × 0% = **$0**; discount amortization = interest revenue; CA rolls up to face at maturity
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** accrue interest / amortize discount at each reporting date; when note anniversary ≠ fiscal year-end, **allocate** each note-year interest amount across calendar reporting periods (time fraction of the note year)
- **Disposal / maturity settlement JE:** collect face cash; remove Note Receivable (discount fully amortized by maturity so net CV = face)
- **Economic split:** sales revenue (PV) + total interest revenue (full discount) = total cash collected at maturity
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all companies, faces, PVs, and calendar alignments changed

### Q1 — CORE — Mid-year zero-interest NR: initial JE, schedule, anniversary YE vs Dec 31 allocation, maturity
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of 0% / 10% noncurrent NR for inventory; note-year effective-interest schedule; **period-end adjusting** when fiscal YE matches anniversary vs when Dec 31 requires 9/3 allocation; face settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **April 1, Year 1**, **Northfork Outfitters Co.** sells inventory with a clearly determinable fair value of **$16,000** and receives a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face (stated) value **$21,296**. Principal is due in a single payment on **March 31, Year 4**. There are **no** periodic cash interest receipts (stated rate = **0%**). The market rate for notes of similar risk is **10%**. Ignore cost of goods sold.

Present value check:

\[
PV = \frac{\$21{,}296}{(1.10)^{3}} = \$21{,}296 \times 0.7513148\ldots = \mathbf{\$16{,}000}
\]

(Equivalently: \( \$16{,}000 \times (1.10)^{3} = \$16{,}000 \times 1.331 = \$21{,}296 \).)

**Required:**  
a. Record the **April 1, Year 1** journal entry for receipt of the note (initial recognition). Compute the initial **Discount on Note Receivable**.  
b. Prepare the **note-year effective-interest amortization schedule** (periods ending March 31 of Years 2, 3, and 4): cash interest, interest revenue, discount amortization, ending carrying amount.  
c. Assume Northfork has a **March 31** fiscal year-end (coincides with note anniversary). Record **period-end interest / amortization JEs** on March 31 of Years 2, 3, and 4, and the **maturity collection** entry on March 31, Year 4 (after the final interest entry).  
d. Assume instead Northfork has a **December 31** year-end. Initial recognition and final cash collection are unchanged. **Allocate** each note-year’s interest to reporting periods (9 months ending Dec 31 / 3 months ending Mar 31) and record interest accruals on **December 31 of Years 1, 2, and 3** and on **March 31, Year 4**. Verify total interest revenue equals the original discount.  
e. Briefly explain why sales revenue is **$16,000** (not face) and what the discount represents.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — April 1, Year 1**

Discount = face − PV = $21,296 − $16,000 = **$5,296**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Receivable | 21,296 | |
| Discount on Note Receivable | | 5,296 |
| Sales Revenue | | 16,000 |
| *Record inventory sale for 3-year 0% note at FV / PV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 21,296 = Cr 5,296 + 16,000. **Balanced.**  
Initial net carrying amount = $21,296 − $5,296 = **$16,000**.

**b. Note-year subsequent measurement schedule (market 10%; stated cash interest = $0)**

Cash interest each period = Face × 0% = **$0**  
Interest revenue = Beginning CA × **10%**  
Discount amortization = Interest revenue (cash = 0)

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest revenue (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $16,000 |
| Mar. 31, Year 2 | $0 | $1,600 | $1,600 | 17,600 |
| Mar. 31, Year 3 | 0 | 1,760 | 1,760 | 19,360 |
| Mar. 31, Year 4 | 0 | 1,936 | 1,936 | 21,296 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$5,296** | **$5,296** | |

**Schedule checks:**  
- $16,000 × 10% = $1,600; $17,600 × 10% = $1,760; $19,360 × 10% = $1,936  
- Total amortization = discount = $5,296  
- Ending CV at maturity = face $21,296  
- Sales $16,000 + interest $5,296 = cash $21,296

**c. March 31 fiscal year-end — period-end adjusting JEs and settlement**

*March 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,600 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,600 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount (0% stated → amort = interest rev.)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,600 = Cr 1,600. **Balanced.** Net CV = **$17,600**.

*March 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,760 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,760 |

**Check:** Dr 1,760 = Cr 1,760. **Balanced.** Net CV = **$19,360**.

*March 31, Year 4 — final interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,936 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,936 |

**Check:** Dr 1,936 = Cr 1,936. **Balanced.** Unamortized discount = $0; net CV = face **$21,296**.

*March 31, Year 4 — maturity settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 21,296 | |
| Notes Receivable | | 21,296 |
| *Collect face amount of zero-interest note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 21,296 = Cr 21,296. **Balanced.**

**d. December 31 year-end — allocate note-year interest to reporting periods (emphasis)**

The amortization schedule follows **note interest periods** (Apr 1–Mar 31), not the fiscal calendar. Each note-year amount is split **9/12** into the calendar year that contains April–December and **3/12** into the following January–March.

| Note-year interest (period ending) | Amount | Dec. 31, Y1 (9/12) | Jan–Mar next (3/12) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar. 31, Year 2 | $1,600 | $1,200 | $400 |
| Mar. 31, Year 3 | 1,760 | 1,320 | 440 |
| Mar. 31, Year 4 | 1,936 | 1,452 | 484 |
| **Totals** | **$5,296** | | |

**Reporting-period interest revenue:**

| Reporting date | Interest revenue | Build-up |
|---|---:|---|
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | **$1,200** | 9/12 × $1,600 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | **$1,720** | $400 + $1,320 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | **$1,892** | $440 + $1,452 |
| Mar. 31, Year 4 | **$484** | 3/12 × $1,936 |
| **Total** | **$5,296** | equals original discount |

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,200 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,200 |
| *9 months: $16,000 × 10% × 9/12 = $1,200* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,200 = Cr 1,200. **Balanced.** Net CV at Dec. 31, Y1 = $16,000 + $1,200 = **$17,200**.

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,720 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,720 |
| *Remaining 3 months of first note-year + 9 months of second note-year* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,720 = Cr 1,720. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,892 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,892 |
| *Remaining 3 months of second note-year + 9 months of final note-year* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,892 = Cr 1,892. **Balanced.**

*March 31, Year 4 — final interest accrual*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 484 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 484 |
| *Final 3 months; discount fully amortized* | | |

**Check:** Dr 484 = Cr 484. **Balanced.**  
Cumulative amort = $1,200 + $1,720 + $1,892 + $484 = **$5,296**; unamortized discount = $0; CV = face **$21,296**.

(Settlement Cash Dr / Notes Receivable Cr $21,296 same as part c.)

**e. Conceptual**  
Sales revenue is the **cash-equivalent selling price** ($16,000), not face. The $5,296 discount is **implicit interest** (financing component) recognized as interest revenue over the term under the effective interest method. Separating sales from interest prevents overstating revenue when the note is noninterest-bearing. **Total** interest revenue is the same under (c) and (d); only the **timing** within reporting periods differs.

**Key insight (period-end emphasis):** With a 0% stated rate, every dollar of effective interest is **pure discount amortization** (Dr Discount / Cr Interest Revenue). Build the schedule on **note periods**, then **time-allocate** to fiscal year-ends when anniversary ≠ year-end so cumulative interest still equals the original discount.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Oct. 1 zero-interest NR: full schedule, 3/9 fiscal allocation, maturity
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — 0% / 10% NR for inventory; note-year schedule; period-end adjusting with October issuance and December 31 YE (3/9 allocation); maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **October 1, Year 1**, **Steelhaven Components LLC** (December 31 year-end) sells inventory with a fair value of **$28,000** and receives a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$37,268**. Face is due **September 30, Year 4**. The market rate for similar risk is **10%**. No cash interest is received during the term. Ignore cost of goods sold.

\[
\$28{,}000 \times (1.10)^{3} = \$28{,}000 \times 1.331 = \mathbf{\$37{,}268}
\]

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **October 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** and state the initial discount.  
b. Prepare the **note-year effective-interest amortization schedule** (periods ending September 30 of Years 2, 3, and 4).  
c. Prepare an **allocation schedule** assigning each note-year’s interest to calendar reporting periods (3 months ending Dec 31 / 9 months ending Sept 30).  
d. Record **all period-end interest JEs**: December 31 Years 1–3 and September 30, Year 4.  
e. Record the **September 30, Year 4 maturity JE** collecting face (after the final interest entry). Prove sales + total interest = cash collected.

**Answer key:**  

**a. October 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

Discount = $37,268 − $28,000 = **$9,268**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Receivable | 37,268 | |
| Discount on Note Receivable | | 9,268 |
| Sales Revenue | | 28,000 |
| *Inventory sale for 3-year 0% note at PV $28,000* | | |

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest revenue (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Oct. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $28,000 |
| Sept. 30, Year 2 | $0 | $2,800 | $2,800 | 30,800 |

**Checks:** $28,000 × 10% = $2,800; $30,800 × 10% = $3,080; $33,880 × 10% = $3,388; sum = $9,268; CA at maturity = face.

| Dec. 31, Year 2 | **$2,870** | $2,100 + $770 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | **$3,157** | $2,310 + $847 |
| Sept. 30, Year 4 | **$2,541** | 9/12 × $3,388 |
| **Total** | **$9,268** | equals discount |

*December 31, Year 1*

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Equipment noncash exchange: initial JE with loss, calendar YE period-end accruals, maturity
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Zero-interest NR for equipment (not regularly sold); measure at PV; gain/loss = BV − PV; full-year period-end adjusting when anniversary = Dec 31; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Cedarline Fabrication Inc.** (calendar year-end) sells used equipment and receives a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$14,641**. The market rate for similar notes is **10%**. Present value of the note (and fair value of the consideration) is **$11,000**. Equipment original cost is **$40,000**; accumulated depreciation on the sale date is **$27,000**. Principal is due **December 31, Year 3**. Cedarline does **not** regularly sell this type of equipment. No cash interest is received during the term.

\[
\$11{,}000 \times (1.10)^{3} = \$11{,}000 \times 1.331 = \mathbf{\$14{,}641}
\]

**Required:**  
a. Compute equipment **book value**, **loss (or gain) on sale**, and **discount** on the note. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **3-year effective-interest amortization schedule**.  
c. Prepare **period-end adjusting JEs** on December 31, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 (interest / discount amortization only).  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 maturity collection JE** (after the Year 3 interest entry).  
e. State the **balance-sheet presentation** of the note at December 31, Year 1 (net amount and remaining discount) and at December 31, Year 2 (current vs noncurrent classification of the net receivable).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Book value, loss, discount, and initial JE**

Book value of equipment = $40,000 − $27,000 = **$13,000**  
PV / initial measurement of note = **$11,000**  
Loss on sale = $13,000 − $11,000 = **$2,000**  
Discount = Face − PV = $14,641 − $11,000 = **$3,641**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Receivable | 14,641 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 27,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 2,000 | |
| Discount on Note Receivable | | 3,641 |
| Equipment | | 40,000 |
| *Sale of equipment for 3-year 0% note at PV $11,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,641 + 27,000 + 2,000 = 43,641; Cr 3,641 + 40,000 = 43,641. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest revenue (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $11,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $1,100 | $1,100 | 12,100 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 1,210 | 1,210 | 13,310 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 0 | 1,331 | 1,331 | 14,641 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$3,641** | **$3,641** | |

**Checks:** $11,000 × 10% = $1,100; $12,100 × 10% = $1,210; $13,310 × 10% = $1,331; total amort = discount; CV at maturity = face.

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs (calendar year = note year)**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,100 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,100 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,100 = Cr 1,100. **Balanced.** Unamortized discount = $3,641 − $1,100 = **$2,541**; net CV = **$12,100**.

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,210 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,210 |

**Check:** Dr 1,210 = Cr 1,210. **Balanced.** Unamortized discount = $2,541 − $1,210 = **$1,331**; net CV = **$13,310**.

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount on Note Receivable | 1,331 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,331 |
| *Accrue final period interest; clear discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,331 = Cr 1,331. **Balanced.** Unamortized discount = $0; net CV = face **$14,641**.

**d. December 31, Year 3 — maturity settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 14,641 | |
| Notes Receivable | | 14,641 |
| *Collect face of zero-interest note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,641 = Cr 14,641. **Balanced.**

**e. Balance-sheet presentation**

- **December 31, Year 1:** Note receivable, net of unamortized discount of **$2,541** — **$12,100**. Entire amount is **noncurrent** (principal due December 31, Year 3 — more than one year away).  
- **December 31, Year 2 (after Year 2 accrual):** Net CA = **$13,310**. Face is due **December 31, Year 3** (within one year), so the **entire net receivable of $13,310** is classified as a **current asset** (Notes Receivable $14,641 less remaining discount $1,331).

**Key insight:** For non-inventory PPE, the difference between asset book value and note PV is **gain or loss on sale**, not an adjustment to the discount. When fiscal year-end matches the note anniversary, each period-end adjusting JE posts the **full** schedule interest line with no fractional allocation.

### Q4 — MC — Period-end interest when stated rate is 0%
**LO:** LO 8-5  
**Concept:** Classification / measurement — correct period-end adjusting JE for a 0% stated / market-rate noncurrent note under effective interest  
**Question:**  
On July 1, Year 1, Willowbark Co. (December 31 year-end) sells goods and receives a **two-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$12,100**. The note’s present value at the **10%** market rate is **$10,000**. Willowbark uses the effective interest method and makes adjusting entries only at year-end (and at maturity). Which entry correctly records **interest for the six months ended December 31, Year 1**?

- A) Debit Interest Receivable **$605**; credit Interest Revenue **$605**  
- B) Debit Discount on Note Receivable **$500**; credit Interest Revenue **$500**  
- C) Debit Discount on Note Receivable **$1,000**; credit Interest Revenue **$1,000**  
- D) No entry — stated rate is 0%, so no interest revenue is recognized until cash is collected at maturity  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Initial CA = $10,000. Full first note-year interest = $10,000 × 10% = **$1,000**, of which six months = $1,000 × 6/12 = **$500**. With stated rate 0%, cash interest is zero, so the entire amount amortizes the discount: **Dr Discount on Note Receivable $500 / Cr Interest Revenue $500**.  
(A is wrong: there is no cash coupon, so Interest Receivable is not used for this note’s “interest.” C books a full year although only six months have elapsed. D confuses zero cash interest with zero interest revenue—the discount is financing interest recognized over time.)

**Verification:** Face check \( \$10{,}000 \times (1.10)^{2} = \$12{,}100 \). After Dec. 31, Y1 entry, net CV = $10,000 + $500 = **$10,500**.
