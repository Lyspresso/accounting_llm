# Agent 106 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-1

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe and report key components of stockholders’ equity  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Five key components of stockholders’ equity:** paid-in capital (capital stock + APIC), retained earnings, accumulated OCI (AOCI), treasury stock (contra equity), noncontrolling interest
- **Initial recognition JE:** issuance of common and preferred stock (par to capital stock; excess to APIC)
- **Share terminology:** authorized vs issued vs outstanding; treasury shares reduce outstanding but not issued
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** statement of stockholders’ equity roll-forward (beginning → +/− activity → ending) for each major equity component
- **Period-end adjusting/closing JEs:** close net income to retained earnings; record OCI in AOCI; declare cash dividends (reduce RE)
- **Disposal / reacquisition JE:** purchase of treasury stock (cost method) and reissuance of treasury stock
- **Classification / presentation:** stockholders’ equity section of the balance sheet with par, authorized, issued, and outstanding share disclosures; APIC accounts aggregated; AOCI and treasury deducted when debit balances; NCI presented separately

### Q1 — CORE — Initial stock issuance JEs, period-end closes, treasury reacquisition/reissue, equity section & SOE schedule
**LO:** LO 19-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of paid-in capital (CS/PS/APIC); period-end RE and AOCI activity; treasury stock reacquisition and reissuance; prepare equity section and statement of stockholders’ equity  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Corp.** is a calendar-year corporation. Charter authorizations:

| Class | Par | Authorized |
|---|---|---:|
| Common stock | $2 | 200,000 shares |
| 6% Preferred stock | $10 | 50,000 shares |

**2025 transactions (summarized):**

1. **March 1 — Initial recognition:** Issued **40,000** shares of common stock for cash at **$18** per share.  
2. **March 1 — Initial recognition:** Issued **5,000** shares of preferred stock for cash at **$25** per share.  
3. **During 2025:** Net income **$95,000**.  
4. **December 10 — Period-end:** Declared and paid cash dividends: preferred annual preference **and** common **$20,000**. Preferred preference = 5,000 × $10 × 6%.  
5. **December 15:** Other comprehensive income (unrealized holding gain on AFS debt securities, **net of tax**) **$8,000**. No other OCI.  
6. **December 20 — Reacquisition:** Purchased **2,000** shares of its own common stock for cash at **$20** per share (cost method; held as treasury).  
7. **December 28 — Reissuance (settlement of treasury position in part):** Reissued **500** of the treasury shares for cash at **$22** per share.  
8. No beginning equity balances (company began operations in 2025). No noncontrolling interest.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **March 1 initial recognition journal entries** for common and preferred stock issuances.  
b. Prepare the **December 10** cash dividend journal entry (declare and pay as one combined cash dividend entry, or separate declare/pay — either form is acceptable if amounts are correct).  
c. Prepare the **period-end closing/adjusting entries** to close net income into retained earnings and to record OCI in AOCI.  
d. Prepare the **December 20 treasury purchase** JE and the **December 28 treasury reissuance** JE.  
e. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / statement of stockholders’ equity schedule** for 2025 showing beginning, each change, and ending balances for: Preferred stock, Common stock, APIC (combined), Retained earnings, AOCI, Treasury stock, and Total equity.  
f. Prepare the **stockholders’ equity section** of the December 31, 2025 balance sheet. Disclose par value and shares authorized, issued, and outstanding for each class of stock.  
g. Briefly identify which of the **five key equity components** appear (or do not appear) on Meridian’s December 31 equity section.

**Answer key:**  

**a. March 1 — initial recognition of common stock issuance**  
Cash = 40,000 × $18 = **$720,000**  
Common stock (par) = 40,000 × $2 = **$80,000**  
APIC—Common = 40,000 × ($18 − $2) = **$640,000**

**March 1 — initial recognition of preferred stock issuance**  
Cash = 5,000 × $25 = **$125,000**  
Preferred stock (par) = 5,000 × $10 = **$50,000**  
APIC—Preferred = 5,000 × ($25 − $10) = **$75,000**

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 23,000. Balanced.

**c. Period-end — close net income to retained earnings**

|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary (or revenue/expense nets) | 95,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 95,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 95,000. Balanced.

**Period-end — record OCI in AOCI**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| OCI—Unrealized Holding Gain on AFS (net of tax) | 8,000 | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. Balanced.  
(If OCI was recorded as a credit in comprehensive income during the period, the closing direction still credits the **AOCI** equity balance for a net gain.)

**d. December 20 — treasury stock reacquisition (cost method)**  
Cost = 2,000 × $20 = **$40,000**

**December 28 — reissuance of 500 treasury shares at $22**  
Cash = 500 × $22 = **$11,000**  
Treasury stock removed at cost = 500 × $20 = **$10,000**

**e. Statement of stockholders’ equity schedule — 2025 (subsequent measurement roll-forward)**

APIC combined = APIC—CS $640,000 + APIC—PS $75,000 + APIC—TS $1,000 = **$716,000**  
RE ending = $0 + $95,000 − $23,000 = **$72,000**  
Treasury stock ending cost = $40,000 − $10,000 = **$30,000** (1,500 shares remaining)

| AOCI | 0 | 8,000 | — | — | — | **8,000** |

**Schedule check:**  
50,000 + 80,000 + 716,000 + 72,000 + 8,000 − 30,000 = **896,000**.  
APIC from issuances only (before TS reissue) = 640,000 + 75,000 = 715,000; +1,000 reissue = 716,000.

**f. Stockholders’ equity section — December 31, 2025**

Shares issued common = 40,000; treasury held = 1,500; **outstanding common = 38,500**.  
Preferred issued and outstanding = 5,000.

| Stockholders’ Equity | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, $10 par value, 6%, 50,000 shares authorized, 5,000 shares issued and outstanding | $50,000 |
| Common stock, $2 par value, 200,000 shares authorized, 40,000 shares issued, 38,500 shares outstanding | 80,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par (including APIC—treasury stock) | 716,000 |
| Retained earnings | 72,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income | 8,000 |
| Less: Treasury stock, 1,500 shares (at cost) | (30,000) |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$896,000** |

**Presentation check:** 50 + 80 + 716 + 72 + 8 − 30 = **896,000**.

**g. Five key components present**

| Component | Present? | Amount / note |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Paid-in capital (capital stock + APIC) | Yes | $50,000 + $80,000 + $716,000 = $846,000 |
| 2. Retained earnings | Yes | $72,000 |
| 3. Accumulated OCI | Yes | $8,000 credit (income) |
| 4. Treasury stock | Yes | $(30,000) contra equity |
| 5. Noncontrolling interest | **No** | Not applicable (no consolidated subsidiary with outside owners) |

**Key insight:** Paid-in capital is **initially recognized** at issuance (par to capital stock; excess to APIC). **RE** and **AOCI** capture earnings and nonowner OCI over time. **Treasury stock** is a contra-equity deduction that cuts **outstanding** shares without changing **issued** shares. The **statement of stockholders’ equity** rolls each component; the **balance sheet equity section** reports ending balances with share disclosures (authorized / issued / outstanding).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Classify equity items; build equity section from trial balances; NCI presentation

### Q4 — MC (classification / presentation)
**LO:** LO 19-1  
**Concept:** Classification of treasury stock within the five key components of stockholders’ equity  
**Question:**  
On the balance sheet, treasury stock acquired for purposes other than retirement and accounted for under the **cost method** is reported as:

- A) An asset (investment in own shares)  
- B) A reduction of retained earnings only  
- C) A **contra equity** deduction from total paid-in capital and retained earnings (and AOCI) in arriving at total stockholders’ equity  
- D) A liability until the shares are reissued  

**Answer:** **C**.  
Treasury stock is one of the five key equity components: a **contra equity** account. Under the cost method it is deducted in the equity section; it is **not** an asset. Outstanding shares decline; issued shares do not change until retirement.

### Q4b — MC (method / share counts for presentation)
**LO:** LO 19-1  
**Concept:** Computing outstanding shares for equity-section disclosure and EPS  
**Question:**  
Lumen Trail Corp. has **100,000** common shares authorized, **60,000** issued, and holds **4,500** shares as treasury stock. How many common shares are **outstanding**, and which amount is used for **basic EPS**?

- A) Authorized 100,000 for both disclosure and EPS  
- B) Issued 60,000 outstanding; EPS uses 60,000  
- C) Outstanding **55,500** (= 60,000 − 4,500); EPS uses **outstanding** weighted-average shares  
- D) Outstanding 64,500; EPS uses authorized shares  

**Answer:** **C**.  
Outstanding = issued − treasury held = 60,000 − 4,500 = **55,500**. The equity section discloses authorized, issued, and outstanding; **EPS** is based on weighted-average **outstanding** (not authorized or issued) shares.
