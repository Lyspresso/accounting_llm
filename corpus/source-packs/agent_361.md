# Agent 361 — CORE DEMO — LO 15-6

**Chapter:** 15  
**LO title:** Explain liability, commitment, and contingency disclosures and analyses using liquidity ratios  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Current liability disclosures:** major components (and SEC 5% of total current liabilities threshold for separate presentation when applicable)
- **Commitments:** future obligations (plant acquisition, unused letters of credit, working-capital covenants, dividend restrictions) are **not present liabilities** — **disclose**, do not record a JE
- **Contingency disclosures:** nature of contingency; estimate of loss/range or statement that estimate cannot be made; certain remote items (e.g., guarantees) still require disclosure
- **Initial recognition JE:** accrue a loss contingency when **probable** and **reasonably estimable** (Dr Loss/Expense; Cr Liability); if range with no better estimate, accrue the **minimum**
- **Subsequent-measurement schedule:** warranty/contingency liability **rollforward** (beginning + provisions − payments/settlements = ending)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end warranty provision and litigation accrual AJs that increase current liabilities and affect liquidity ratios
- **Settlement JE:** settle accrued contingency for cash (gain/loss if settlement ≠ carrying amount)
- **Liquidity ratios:** **Current ratio** = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities; **Quick ratio** = (Cash + Marketable securities + Receivables) ÷ Current liabilities — short-run solvency, not profitability
- **Analysis:** how accruals and classifications change ratios and covenant headroom

### Q1 — CORE — Northpine Components: contingency lifecycle, commitment disclosure, liquidity ratios

**LO:** LO 15-6  
**Concept:** Settlement JE of prior contingency; warranty rollforward schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs** for warranty and litigation (emphasis); commitment disclosure (no JE); current and quick ratios before/after year-end accruals  
**Scenario:**  
**Northpine Components Co.** has a calendar year-end of **December 31, 20X5**. Management is finalizing year-end reporting of liabilities, commitments, contingencies, and short-term liquidity.

**Facts:**

1. **Prior litigation settlement.** On January 1, 20X5, Northpine had **Accrued Litigation Liability $80,000** from a 20X4 product case. On **March 1, 20X5**, the case settled for **$74,000 cash**.

2. **Assurance-type warranty (subsequent measurement).**  
   - Warranty liability, January 1, 20X5: **$48,000**  
   - 20X5 product sales subject to assurance warranty: **$4,000,000**  
   - Estimated warranty cost: **1% of sales**  
   - Actual warranty claims paid in cash during 20X5: **$43,000**  
   - Year-end adjusting entry for the warranty **provision** has **not** yet been recorded (claims payments during the year **have** been recorded against the warranty liability).

3. **New litigation — period-end accrual.** In October 20X5 a customer sued Northpine for alleged defects. Counsel concludes that a loss is **probable**. The estimated loss range is **$150,000–$300,000**, and **$210,000** is a **better estimate** than any other amount in the range. No accrual has been recorded.

4. **Commitments (not accrued).** On December 20, 20X5, Northpine:  
   - Signed a **firm commitment** to purchase a manufacturing plant for **$2,500,000** in March 20X6  
   - Has an **unused letter of credit** of **$350,000**  
   - Is subject to a bank covenant requiring **minimum working capital of $500,000**  
   None of these items has been recorded as a liability (correctly, except for analysis of the covenant using the year-end balance sheet).

5. **Selected balances after recording the settlement, warranty claim payments, and all other 20X5 activity, but *before* the two year-end contingency adjusting entries** (warranty provision and new litigation accrual):

| Current assets | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash | $540,000 |
| Marketable securities (short-term) | 160,000 |
| Accounts receivable, net | 300,000 |
| Inventory | 720,000 |
| Prepaid expenses | 80,000 |
| **Total current assets** | **$1,800,000** |

| Current liabilities (before YE contingency AJs) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accounts payable | $420,000 |
| Accrued expenses (excludes warranty and litigation) | 125,000 |
| Notes payable (current) | 200,000 |
| Warranty liability (after claims paid; provision not yet recorded) | 5,000 |
| Accrued litigation liability | 0 |
| **Total current liabilities (before YE AJs)** | **$750,000** |

*(Check: warranty after payments only = $48,000 − $43,000 = $5,000; prior litigation fully settled.)*

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **March 1, 20X5 settlement JE** for the prior litigation. Prove the entry balances.  
(b) Record: (1) the **period-end adjusting JE** for the 20X5 warranty provision; (2) the **period-end adjusting JE** for the new litigation accrual. Prove each balances.  
(c) Prepare a **warranty liability subsequent-measurement (rollforward) schedule** for 20X5. State the December 31, 20X5 warranty liability.  
(d) Identify which commitment items require **disclosure only** (no JE) and draft the **nature** of the Contingencies/Commitments note items (nature + amounts/ranges as applicable).  
(e) Compute the **current ratio** and **quick ratio** (1) **before** the two year-end contingency AJs and (2) **after** those AJs. Compute **working capital after** the AJs and assess the **$500,000 working-capital covenant**. Briefly analyze liquidity.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Settlement JE — March 1, 20X5**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accrued Litigation Liability | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 74,000 |
| Gain on Settlement of Litigation | | 6,000 |
| *Settle prior-year litigation for less than carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 80,000 = Cr 74,000 + 6,000 = 80,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

Warranty provision = $4,000,000 × 1% = **$40,000**.

(1) December 31, 20X5 — warranty period-end AJ:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warranty Expense | 40,000 | |
| Warranty Liability | | 40,000 |
| *Accrue estimated assurance-type warranty costs for 20X5 sales* | | |

**Check:** Dr 40,000 = Cr 40,000. **Balanced.**

(2) December 31, 20X5 — litigation period-end AJ (probable and estimable; use best estimate):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Litigation Loss (or Legal Contingency Expense) | 210,000 | |
| Accrued Litigation Liability | | 210,000 |
| *Accrue probable, reasonably estimable loss contingency at best estimate* | | |

**Check:** Dr 210,000 = Cr 210,000. **Balanced.**  
*(If no amount in the range were a better estimate, the **minimum** of the range would be accrued and the exposure above the minimum disclosed.)*

**(c) Warranty subsequent-measurement schedule**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning warranty liability, Jan 1, 20X5 | $48,000 |
| + Provision (period-end AJ): 1% × $4,000,000 | 40,000 |
| − Claims paid in cash during 20X5 | (43,000) |
| **Ending warranty liability, Dec 31, 20X5** | **$45,000** |

Proof: $48,000 + $40,000 − $43,000 = **$45,000**.  
Also: pre-AJ balance $5,000 + provision $40,000 = **$45,000**.

**(d) Commitments and contingencies — disclosure (no JE for commitments)**

| Item | Accounting | Disclosure content |
|---|---|---|
| Plant acquisition commitment $2,500,000 | **No JE** (future obligation, not a present liability) | Nature: firm commitment to acquire plant; amount **$2,500,000**; expected timing March 20X6 |
| Unused letter of credit $350,000 | **No JE** | Disclose unused LOC amount **$350,000** |
| Working-capital covenant minimum $500,000 | **No JE** for the covenant itself | Disclose obligation to maintain working capital of at least **$500,000** |
| Accrued warranty $45,000 | **Accrued** (already in CL) | Composition of current liabilities / warranty policy and rollforward as applicable |
| Accrued litigation $210,000 | **Accrued** | Nature of contingency; accrued amount; if material, range of reasonably possible loss **in excess of accrual** if any (here best estimate used) |
| Balance sheet caption | Optional line | “Commitments and Contingencies” on face of BS pointing users to the notes |

**(e) Liquidity ratios and covenant**

**Quick assets** (unchanged by the expense/liability AJs) = Cash + MS + AR  
= $540,000 + $160,000 + $300,000 = **$1,000,000**.

| Metric | Before YE contingency AJs | After YE contingency AJs |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current assets | $1,800,000 | $1,800,000 |
| Current liabilities | $750,000 | $750,000 + $40,000 + $210,000 = **$1,000,000** |
| **Current ratio** | $1,800,000 / $750,000 = **2.40** | $1,800,000 / $1,000,000 = **1.80** |
| **Quick ratio** | $1,000,000 / $750,000 = **1.33** | $1,000,000 / $1,000,000 = **1.00** |
| Working capital | $1,050,000 | $1,800,000 − $1,000,000 = **$800,000** |

**Covenant:** Required WC ≥ $500,000; actual after AJs = **$800,000** → **in compliance** (cushion $300,000).

**Analysis:** Period-end contingency accruals increased current liabilities by **$250,000**, reducing the current ratio from **2.40 to 1.80** and the quick ratio from **1.33 to 1.00**. Northpine can still cover current liabilities with current assets (and just meets a 1.0 quick-asset coverage). Inventory and prepaids support the gap between current and quick ratios. Commitments of **$2,500,000** plant purchase and **$350,000** unused LOC do **not** enter the ratios (off-balance-sheet) but are critical note disclosures for assessing future cash needs.

**Key insight:** LO 15-6 links **what hits the balance sheet** (accrued contingencies via period-end AJs and rollforwards) to **liquidity analysis**, while **commitments** and many **non-accrued contingencies** appear only in the notes—users must combine ratios **and** disclosures.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Silverbrook Manufacturing: same path, all numbers changed

**LO:** LO 15-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin—settlement JE; warranty rollforward; period-end warranty and litigation AJs; commitment disclosures; current/quick ratios before and after accruals  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverbrook Manufacturing LLC** (calendar year-end **December 31, 20X6**) is completing year-end liability, commitment, contingency, and liquidity reporting.

**Facts:**

1. **Settlement.** Accrued Litigation Liability on January 1, 20X6: **$120,000**. Settled **June 10, 20X6** for **$125,000 cash**.

2. **Warranty.**  
   - Beginning warranty liability: **$60,000**  
   - Sales subject to assurance warranty: **$5,000,000**  
   - Estimated warranty cost: **1.2% of sales**  
   - Claims paid in cash during 20X6: **$55,000** (already recorded against Warranty Liability)  
   - Year-end **provision not yet recorded**

3. **New litigation.** Probable loss; range **$100,000–$250,000**; **best estimate $180,000**. Not yet accrued.

4. **Commitments.** Firm commitment to acquire equipment for **$1,800,000** in February 20X7; unused letter of credit **$500,000**; debt covenant requires **minimum current ratio of 1.50**.

5. **Selected balances after settlement, claim payments, and other activity, but *before* YE warranty provision and litigation accrual:**

| Current assets | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash | $400,000 |
| Marketable securities | 150,000 |
| Accounts receivable, net | 250,000 |
| Inventory | 1,000,000 |
| Prepaid expenses | 200,000 |
| **Total current assets** | **$2,000,000** |

| Current liabilities (before YE contingency AJs) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accounts payable | $455,000 |
| Accrued expenses (excludes warranty and litigation) | 100,000 |
| Notes payable (current) | 200,000 |
| Warranty liability (after claims; before provision) | 5,000 |
| Accrued litigation liability | 0 |
| **Total current liabilities (before YE AJs)** | **$760,000** |

*(Check: warranty $60,000 − $55,000 = $5,000.)*

**Required:**  
(a) Settlement JE on June 10, 20X6; prove balance.  
(b) Period-end AJs for warranty provision and litigation; prove each balances.  
(c) Warranty liability rollforward for 20X6; ending balance.  
(d) Commitment/contingency disclosure treatment (no JE for commitments); note content.  
(e) Current ratio and quick ratio before and after YE AJs; working capital after; assess the **1.50 current-ratio covenant**. Brief analysis.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Settlement — June 10, 20X6**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accrued Litigation Liability | 120,000 | |
| Loss on Settlement of Litigation | 5,000 | |
| Cash | | 125,000 |
| *Settle prior litigation for more than carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 120,000 + 5,000 = 125,000 = Cr 125,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Period-end adjusting JEs**

Warranty provision = $5,000,000 × 1.2% = **$60,000**.

(1) Warranty YE AJ:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warranty Expense | 60,000 | |
| Warranty Liability | | 60,000 |
| *Accrue 1.2% of $5,000,000 sales* | | |

**Check:** Dr 60,000 = Cr 60,000. **Balanced.**

(2) Litigation YE AJ:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Litigation Loss | 180,000 | |
| Accrued Litigation Liability | | 180,000 |
| *Accrue probable, estimable contingency at best estimate* | | |

**Check:** Dr 180,000 = Cr 180,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Warranty rollforward**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning warranty liability, Jan 1, 20X6 | $60,000 |
| + Provision | 60,000 |
| − Claims paid | (55,000) |
| **Ending warranty liability, Dec 31, 20X6** | **$65,000** |

Proof: $60,000 + $60,000 − $55,000 = **$65,000**.

**(d) Disclosures (commitments — no JE)**

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Equipment commitment $1,800,000 | **Disclose only** — nature, amount, timing (Feb 20X7) |
| Unused letter of credit $500,000 | **Disclose only** |
| Current-ratio covenant ≥ 1.50 | **Disclose** covenant terms; test with post-AJ ratios |
| Warranty liability $65,000 | Accrued; disclose composition/policy/rollforward as needed |
| Litigation accrual $180,000 | Accrued; disclose nature; range of possible additional loss if material |

**(e) Liquidity ratios and covenant**

Quick assets = $400,000 + $150,000 + $250,000 = **$800,000**.

| Metric | Before YE AJs | After YE AJs |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current assets | $2,000,000 | $2,000,000 |
| Current liabilities | $760,000 | $760,000 + $60,000 + $180,000 = **$1,000,000** |
| **Current ratio** | $2,000,000 / $760,000 = **2.63** | $2,000,000 / $1,000,000 = **2.00** |
| **Quick ratio** | $800,000 / $760,000 = **1.05** | $800,000 / $1,000,000 = **0.80** |
| Working capital | $1,240,000 | **$1,000,000** |

**Covenant:** Required current ratio ≥ **1.50**; actual after AJs = **2.00** → **in compliance**.

**Analysis:** Contingency AJs add **$240,000** to current liabilities, cutting the current ratio from **2.63 to 2.00** and the quick ratio from **1.05 to 0.80**. Silverbrook remains comfortable on the **current-ratio covenant**, but the quick ratio below 1.0 signals reliance on inventory conversion for full coverage of current debts. The **$1,800,000** equipment commitment and **$500,000** unused LOC are off-balance-sheet and must be read with the ratios.

**Key insight:** Same LO 15-6 path as Q1 with new amounts—accruals move ratios; commitments stay in the notes.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Lakeworth Distributors: period-end classification matrix + AJs + liquidity

**LO:** LO 15-6  
**Concept:** **Period-end adjusting JE emphasis**—classify each item as accrue / disclose only / neither; record only required AJs; compute current and quick ratios; SEC-style current liability composition note  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeworth Distributors Inc.** (calendar year-end **December 31, 20X7**) is preparing period-end adjusting entries and note disclosures for liabilities, commitments, and contingencies. **No** contingency-related year-end AJs have been recorded yet.

**Items under review at December 31, 20X7:**

| # | Fact pattern |
|---|---|
| 1 | Assurance-type warranties on 20X7 sales: estimated remaining cost **$28,000** (not yet accrued). Warranty liability currently **$0** after all claims paid. |
| 2 | Pending lawsuit: loss is **reasonably possible** (not probable). Estimated range **$50,000–$90,000**; no amount is a better estimate. |
| 3 | Unasserted environmental assessment: possible fine **up to $200,000**. It is **not probable** that a claim will be asserted. |
| 4 | Guarantee of a major customer’s bank loan (remote likelihood of payment): maximum exposure **$400,000**. |
| 5 | Firm purchase commitment for inventory to be delivered in 20X8: **$75,000** (prices not unfavorable; ordinary purchase commitment). |
| 6 | One current liability component—“Customer deposits”—is **$95,000**. Total current liabilities **after** all required accruals will be **$800,000**. |

**Selected current assets (final, after any AJs that do not affect assets):**

| Current assets | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash | $220,000 |
| Marketable securities | 80,000 |
| Accounts receivable, net | 180,000 |
| Inventory | 360,000 |
| Prepaid expenses | 40,000 |
| **Total current assets** | **$880,000** |

**Current liabilities before any of the contingency/warranty AJs above:** **$772,000** (no warranty or litigation accruals included).

**Required:**  
(a) For each item 1–5, state **Accrue / Disclose only / Neither**, and briefly justify under ASC 450 / commitment guidance.  
(b) Record **all required period-end adjusting JEs** only. Prove each balances.  
(c) Compute ending total current liabilities, **current ratio**, and **quick ratio**.  
(d) For item 6: does SEC-style separate presentation of customer deposits apply (5% of total current liabilities)? Show the calculation.  
(e) Draft a brief **Commitments and Contingencies** note outline covering items that require disclosure.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Classification matrix**

| # | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Warranty $28,000 | **Accrue** | Probable (inherent in sales) and reasonably estimable assurance-type warranty loss contingency |
| 2 Reasonably possible suit | **Disclose only** | Not both probable and estimable for accrual; at least reasonably possible → disclose nature and range **$50,000–$90,000** |
| 3 Unasserted claim | **Neither** | Claim assertion **not probable** → no accrual and no disclosure |
| 4 Guarantee $400,000 | **Disclose only** | Guarantees require disclosure even if remote; no liability unless payment is probable and estimable |
| 5 Ordinary purchase commitment $75,000 | **Disclose** if material under commitment guidance / significant commitments; **no liability JE** (not a present obligation for the inventory yet). Treat as **disclose only** for exam purposes when material. |

**(b) Required period-end AJ only**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warranty Expense | 28,000 | |
| Warranty Liability | | 28,000 |
| *Period-end AJ — accrue estimated warranty costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,000 = Cr 28,000. **Balanced.**

No JEs for items 2–5.

**(c) Ratios**

Current liabilities after AJ = $772,000 + $28,000 = **$800,000**.  
Quick assets = $220,000 + $80,000 + $180,000 = **$480,000**.  
Current assets = **$880,000**.

| Ratio | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| **Current ratio** | $880,000 / $800,000 | **1.10** |
| **Quick ratio** | $480,000 / $800,000 | **0.60** |

**Analysis:** Current ratio modestly above 1.0; quick ratio **0.60** shows heavy reliance on inventory ($360,000) for short-term coverage. Off-balance-sheet guarantee (**$400,000**) and reasonably possible litigation (up to **$90,000**) are important for users even though they do not change the ratios.

**(d) SEC 5% current-liability component test**

5% × total current liabilities $800,000 = **$40,000**.  
Customer deposits $95,000 > $40,000 → **yes**, state separately on the balance sheet or in the notes (with other components exceeding 5% as applicable).

**(e) Note outline — Commitments and Contingencies**

- **Litigation:** Describe pending suit; state loss is reasonably possible; disclose estimated range **$50,000 to $90,000** (or that no amount within the range is a better estimate and the range is disclosed).  
- **Guarantees:** Disclose guarantee of customer loan; maximum exposure **$400,000**; remote likelihood.  
- **Commitments:** Disclose firm inventory purchase commitment **$75,000** (and any other significant commitments).  
- **Warranties:** Accrued warranty liability **$28,000**; briefly state estimation policy (and rollforward if presented).  
- **Unasserted environmental matter:** **Omit** (assertion not probable).

**Key insight:** Period-end work for LO 15-6 is a **decision tree**—only **probable + estimable** losses get AJs that change **current liabilities and liquidity ratios**; everything else is **note disclosure** (or silence for nonprobable unasserted claims).

### Q4 — MC — Quick ratio numerator components

**LO:** LO 15-6  
**Concept:** Quick (acid-test) ratio includes only the most liquid current assets  
**Question:** Which of the following is included in the **numerator** of the **quick ratio**?

- A) Inventory  
- B) Prepaid insurance  
- C) Accounts receivable (net)  
- D) Property, plant, and equipment  

**Answer:** **C.** The quick ratio = (Cash + Cash equivalents/marketable securities + Receivables) ÷ Current liabilities. Inventory and prepaids are current assets in the **current** ratio but are excluded from the quick ratio because they are less liquid. PP&E is noncurrent.

### Q5 — MC — Commitment vs liability recognition

**LO:** LO 15-6  
**Concept:** Commitments for future obligations are disclosed, not recorded as present liabilities  
**Question:** On December 31, a company signs a noncancelable commitment to purchase a plant for $3,000,000 in four months. No deposit is paid. How should the commitment be reported in the December 31 financial statements?

- A) Debit Plant $3,000,000; credit Notes Payable $3,000,000  
- B) Debit Loss Contingency $3,000,000; credit Contingent Liability $3,000,000  
- C) No journal entry; disclose the commitment in the notes  
- D) Report as a current liability only if the current ratio would otherwise exceed 2.0  

**Answer:** **C.** A commitment for a future plant acquisition is **not a present obligation**, so it is **not recorded** as a liability. Accounting guidance requires **disclosure** of significant commitments (including plant acquisition commitments). It is not a loss contingency accrual.
