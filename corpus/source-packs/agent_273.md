# Agent 273 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-1

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Identify types and features of bonds  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Bond **types**: corporate vs municipal; secured vs debenture; term vs serial; callable; convertible
- Bond **features** fixed in the indenture: face value, maturity date, stated (coupon) rate, interest payment dates, authorization date
- Market-dependent features: market (effective/yield) rate, bond selling price, issue date
- Stated vs market rate → issue at par, discount, or premium; **initial recognition JE**
- Subsequent measurement: **effective-interest amortization schedule** (carrying amount rollforward)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** when fiscal year-end ≠ interest payment date, accrue interest expense, amortize discount/premium, and credit Interest Payable
- Maturity / settlement JE (derecognize bonds payable; repay face)
- Classification / presentation: bonds payable net of unamortized discount (or plus unamortized premium); current portion of serial principal; interest expense classification

### Q1 — CORE — Types/features, premium issuance, schedule, period-end accrual (emphasis), maturity
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Identify bond types and features; initial recognition at premium; effective-interest schedule; period-end adjusting JE when YE ≠ interest date (emphasis); maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 31, Year 1**, **Harborline Specialty Chemicals Corp.** (a private manufacturing corporation) authorizes and issues bonds under the following indenture and market conditions. Harborline’s fiscal year-end is **December 31**.

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Face value | **$250,000** (250 bonds × $1,000) |
| Stated (coupon) rate | **8%** annual |
| Interest payment dates | **January 31** and **July 31** (semiannual) |
| Maturity date | **January 31, Year 4** (3-year **term** bonds) |
| Security | No liens on specific assets — general credit of issuer only |
| Call feature | Issuer may redeem after Year 1 at **101** |
| Convertibility | Not convertible |
| Market (yield) rate at issuance | **6%** annual |
| Authorization = issue date | January 31, Year 1 |
| Amortization method | **Effective interest** |

Because interest is paid on January 31 and July 31, **December 31 is not an interest payment date**. Harborline must record a **period-end adjusting entry** for accrued interest and premium amortization.

**Required:**  
a. Identify all applicable **bond type(s)** for this issue.  
b. Complete the **bond features** table (indenture features and market-dependent features), including face value, maturity date, stated rate **per interest period**, interest payment dates, authorization date, market rate **per interest period**, bond selling price (PV of cash interest + principal at the market rate), and issue date.  
c. Record the **January 31, Year 1** issuance journal entry.  
d. Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** for the bond term (all six semiannual periods). Round intermediate interest expense to the nearest dollar; plug the final period so unamortized premium reaches zero.  
e. Record the **July 31, Year 1** cash interest / amortization entry.  
f. **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** for **five months** of the next semiannual period (prorate 5/6 of the January 31, Year 2 schedule row). Show Dr = Cr.  
g. Show **balance sheet presentation** of the bonds at December 31, Year 1 (after the adjusting entry) and compute **Year 1 interest expense**.  
h. Record the **January 31, Year 2** entry that pays cash interest and clears Interest Payable (no reversing entries used).  
i. Record the **January 31, Year 4** entries for the **final interest payment** (with final amortization) and **maturity settlement** of principal. Assume no Interest Payable balance remains at maturity.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Bond type(s)**  
- **Corporate** (issued by a private corporation)  
- **Debenture** (backed only by issuer’s general credit; no asset lien)  
- **Term** (entire principal due on a single maturity date)  
- **Callable** (issuer option to redeem early at 101 after Year 1)  
- *Not* municipal, *not* secured, *not* serial, *not* convertible  

**b. Bond features**

| Feature | Harborline bonds |
|---|---|
| Face value | $250,000 |
| Maturity date | January 31, Year 4 |
| Stated rate per interest period | 4% (8% ÷ 2) |
| Interest payment dates | January 31 and July 31 |
| Bond authorization date | January 31, Year 1 |
| Market rate per interest period | 3% (6% ÷ 2) |
| Bond selling price | **$263,543** (see computation) |
| Bond issue date | January 31, Year 1 |

**Selling price computation** (n = 6; i = 3%; cash interest = $250,000 × 4% = **$10,000**):  
\[
PV = \$10{,}000 \times \frac{1-(1.03)^{-6}}{0.03} + \$250{,}000 \times (1.03)^{-6} = \$263{,}542.98 \approx \$263{,}543
\]  
Premium at issuance = $263,543 − $250,000 = **$13,543**.  
*(Market 6% < stated 8% → bonds sell at a premium.)*

**c. January 31, Year 1 — initial recognition (premium)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 263,543 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 13,543 |
| Bonds Payable | | 250,000 |
| *Corporate term debenture bonds sold at a premium (market 6% < stated 8%)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 263,543 = Cr 13,543 + 250,000 = **263,543**. **Balanced.**

**d. Effective-interest amortization schedule**  
Cash each period = $10,000. Interest expense = 3% × beginning carrying amount (rounded). Premium amort. = Cash − Interest expense. Final period plugged.

| Date | Cash (4% × face) | Interest exp. (3% × beg. CA) | Premium amort. | Unamortized premium | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 31, Y1 (issue) | — | — | — | 13,543 | 263,543 |
| July 31, Y1 | 10,000 | 7,906 | 2,094 | 11,449 | 261,449 |
| Jan 31, Y2 | 10,000 | 7,843 | 2,157 | 9,292 | 259,292 |

| Jan 31, Y4 | 10,000 | 7,574 | 2,426 | 0 | 250,000 |

**Rollforward checks:** Total premium amortized $13,543 = initial premium. Ending CA = face. Total interest expense $46,457 = total cash interest $60,000 − premium $13,543.

**e. July 31, Year 1 — cash interest and premium amortization**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 7,906 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,094 | |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr 7,906 + 2,094 = Cr 10,000. **Balanced.**

**f. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**  
Next full schedule row (January 31, Year 2): Interest exp. $7,843; premium amort. $2,157; cash interest $10,000.  
Prorate **5/6** (August–December; five of six months in the semiannual period):

**Check:** Dr 6,535 + 1,798 = Cr 8,333 = **8,333**. **Balanced.**

**g. Presentation and Year 1 interest expense**

Unamortized premium at Dec 31, Y1 = $11,449 − $1,798 = **$9,651**  
Carrying amount = $250,000 + $9,651 = **$259,651**

**Balance sheet excerpt — December 31, Year 1**

**Income statement — Year 1:** Interest expense = $7,906 + $6,535 = **$14,441**  
*(Bonds outstanding 11 months of calendar Year 1 after January 31 issue.)*

**h. January 31, Year 2 — pay interest and clear accrual (no reversing entries)**  
Remaining 1/6 of the Jan 31 Y2 row: Interest exp. $7,843 − $6,535 = **$1,308**; premium amort. $2,157 − $1,798 = **$359**; cash $10,000.

**i. Maturity — January 31, Year 4**

Final interest and amortization:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 7,574 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,426 | |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr 7,574 + 2,426 = Cr 10,000.

Derecognize bonds at maturity (settlement of face):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 250,000 | |

**Key insight:** LO 16-1 **features** drive measurement: the **stated rate** and **interest payment dates** fix cash coupons and when cash moves; the **market rate** at issuance sets the selling price and the constant effective rate. When the **fiscal year-end is not an interest date**, a **period-end adjusting JE** still recognizes interest expense and amortizes premium (or discount) for the elapsed months and credits **Interest Payable** for the accrued cash coupon.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Discount twin; 2-month year-end accrual (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — bond types/features; discount issuance; effective-interest schedule; period-end adjusting JE (2/6 of semi period); maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **October 31, Year 1**, **Twin Rivers Logistics Inc.** (a public transportation logistics corporation) authorizes and issues:

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Face value | **$400,000** |
| Stated rate | **5%** annual |
| Interest dates | **April 30** and **October 31** |
| Maturity | **October 31, Year 4** (term bonds) |
| Security | First lien on specified warehouse equipment |
| Other features | Not callable; not convertible |
| Market rate at issuance | **7%** annual |
| Authorization = issue date | October 31, Year 1 |
| Method | Effective interest |
| Fiscal year-end | **December 31** |

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-issue type/feature ID; between-date issuance; period-end current portion
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Classify bond types across issues; extract indenture vs market features; issuance between interest dates with accrued interest; period-end classification of serial current principal  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Peak Holdings** has three separate debt issues. None of the numbers match textbook demos.

**Issue A — “Lakeside Flood Control Series”**  
The **City of Northvale** issued $1,500,000 of bonds to finance a flood-control project. Principal repays in equal installments of **$300,000** each December 31 for five years. Bondholders have a **lien on project revenues**. Issuer may retire any remaining principal after year 2 at **102**. Interest **3%** paid annually each December 31. Sold on authorization date at **par**. Fiscal year-end = December 31 (interest and principal payment date).

**Issue B — “Cascade 7s Convertible Notes”**  
Cascade Peak Holdings Corp. issued **$600,000** face, **7%** bonds on **April 1, Year 1** (authorized April 1, Year 1). Interest payable **semiannually** on **April 1** and **October 1**. Bonds mature **April 1, Year 7** on a single date. No asset security; bondholders may convert each $1,000 bond into **15 shares** of Cascade common after Year 2. Sold for **$564,000** when the market yield was **8%** annual. Fiscal year-end = **December 31**.

**Issue C — “Cascade 5s Mid-Period Placement”**  
Cascade Peak Holdings Corp. authorized bonds dated **January 1, Year 1**: **$180,000** face, **5%** annual coupon payable each **January 1** and **July 1**, 5-year **term**, unsecured, not callable, not convertible. Bonds were **issued on February 1, Year 1** (one month after the authorization/interest date) at a **quoted price of 98** (98% of face). Buyers pay the quoted price **plus accrued interest** for January. Fiscal year-end = December 31.

**Required:**  
a. For **each** of Issues A, B, and C, list all applicable bond **type(s)** from: corporate, municipal, secured, debenture, term, serial, callable, convertible.  
b. For **Issue B only**, complete a features table with: face value; maturity date; stated rate per interest period; interest payment dates; authorization date; market rate per interest period; bond selling price; bond issue date; initial discount or premium.  
c. For **Issue C**, compute accrued interest at issuance, total cash proceeds, and record the **February 1, Year 1** issuance JE (quoted price excludes accrued interest; proceeds include it).  
d. **(Emphasis — period-end adjusting / classification)** For **Issue B** at December 31, Year 1: state how many months of the current semiannual period have elapsed since the last interest date (October 1), and record the **period-end adjusting JE** using **straight-line** approximation for this part only: annual cash interest = 7% × $600,000; amortize the **$36,000** initial discount evenly over **72 months**; accrue **3 months**.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** For **Issue A** immediately after the December 31, Year 1 principal installment is paid, show how the remaining principal should be split between **current** and **noncurrent** liabilities (four installments remain). For **Issue B** after the (d) adjusting entry, show long-term net presentation of the bonds and the Interest Payable line.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Types**

| Issue | Types |
|---|---|
| **A** | **Municipal**; **secured** (lien on project revenues); **serial** (principal in installments); **callable** (after year 2 at 102) |
| **B** | **Corporate**; **debenture** (no asset security); **term** (single maturity); **convertible** |
| **C** | **Corporate**; **debenture**; **term** (not municipal, secured, serial, callable, or convertible) |

**b. Issue B features**

| Feature | Amount / description |
|---|---|
| Face value | $600,000 |
| Maturity date | April 1, Year 7 |
| Stated rate per interest period | 3.5% (7% ÷ 2) |
| Interest payment dates | April 1 and October 1 |
| Authorization date | April 1, Year 1 |
| Market rate per interest period | 4% (8% ÷ 2) |
| Bond selling price | $564,000 |
| Bond issue date | April 1, Year 1 |
| Initial discount | $600,000 − $564,000 = **$36,000** |

Cash interest per period = $600,000 × 3.5% = **$21,000**.

**c. Issue C — issuance between interest dates**  
Quoted (clean) price = 0.98 × $180,000 = **$176,400**  
Discount = $180,000 − $176,400 = **$3,600**  
Accrued interest (1 month at stated rate) = $180,000 × 5% × 1/12 = **$750**  
Total cash proceeds = $176,400 + $750 = **$177,150**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 177,150 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 3,600 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 180,000 |
| Interest Payable (or Interest Expense)* | | 750 |
| *Issue at 98 plus one month accrued interest* | | |

\*Textbook approach: credit **Interest Payable** (or Interest Expense) for accrued interest received; the next July 1 full coupon payment then nets correctly to five months of interest cost for the new holder’s period.

**Check:** Dr 177,150 + 3,600 = 180,750; Cr 180,000 + 750 = 180,750. **Balanced.**

**d. Issue B — December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting (emphasis)**  
Last interest date = **October 1**; months elapsed to Dec 31 = **3 months** (of a 6-month semi period).  

Straight-line monthly discount amortization = $36,000 ÷ 72 months = **$500** per month.  
Three months amort. = 3 × $500 = **$1,500**.  
Three months accrued cash interest = $600,000 × 7% × 3/12 = **$10,500**.  
Interest expense = $10,500 + $1,500 = **$12,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 12,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,500 |
| Interest Payable | | 10,500 |
| *3-month year-end accrual; interest dates Apr 1 / Oct 1; YE = Dec 31* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr 1,500 + 10,500. **Balanced.**

**e. Classification / presentation**

**Issue A** after Dec 31, Year 1 principal payment of $300,000:  
Remaining principal = $1,500,000 − $300,000 = **$1,200,000** (four installments of $300,000).

| Classification | Amount |
|---|---:|
| **Current liabilities** — current portion of serial bonds payable | **$300,000** |
| **Long-term liabilities** — bonds payable (noncurrent) | **$900,000** |
| **Total remaining principal** | **$1,200,000** |

**Issue B** after Dec 31 adjusting entry:

| Long-term liabilities | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $600,000 |
| Less: Discount on bonds payable ($36,000 − $1,500) | (34,500) |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$565,500** |

| Current liabilities | |
|---|---:|
| Interest payable | $10,500 |

Income statement: Discount amortization is a component of **interest expense** (financing cost), not a separate extraordinary item.

**Key insight:** LO 16-1 identification (types + features, especially **interest payment dates** and **maturity pattern**) determines (1) whether a **year-end adjusting JE** is needed, (2) how much **Interest Payable** to accrue, and (3) whether serial principal must be **split current / noncurrent** on the balance sheet.

### Q4 — MC — Bond type and period-end accrual classification
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Classify bond types; identify correct period-end adjusting accounts when YE ≠ interest date  

**Question 1:**  
**Riverbend Transit Authority**, a governmental unit, issues bonds that mature in equal principal installments over 8 years, are backed by a pledge of toll revenues, and may be retired early by the issuer after year 2 at 103. Which classification set is **most complete and correct**?

- A) Corporate; term; debenture; convertible  
- B) Municipal; serial; secured; callable  
- C) Municipal; term; debenture; convertible  
- D) Corporate; serial; secured; not callable  

**Answer:** **B.** Issuer is a governmental unit → **municipal**. Principal in installments → **serial**. Pledge of specific revenues → **secured**. Issuer early-retirement option → **callable**. A and D misstate the issuer as corporate; C wrongly labels term/debenture/convertible.

---

**Question 2:**  
A corporation’s 6% term bonds pay interest every **June 30** and **December 31**. The bonds were issued at a **discount** on an interest date. The company prepares monthly financial statements. Which entry correctly describes the **July 31 period-end adjusting JE** (one month of the next semiannual period)?

- A) Debit Interest Expense; credit Cash for one month of coupon interest only (no discount amortization until the cash payment date).  
- B) Debit Interest Expense; credit Discount on Bonds Payable and Interest Payable (interest expense = accrued coupon + one month discount amortization).  
- C) Debit Interest Payable; credit Interest Expense (reverse the June 30 payment).  
- D) Debit Bonds Payable; credit Interest Expense for the monthly decline in face value.  

**Answer:** **B.** When the reporting date is not an interest payment date, accrue **Interest Payable** for the elapsed coupon and **amortize discount** for the same elapsed period under effective interest (or straight-line). Cash is not paid until the contractual interest date. Face value does not decline monthly for term bonds.
