# Agent 69 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-4

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Interest cost incurred during the construction of a fixed asset is considered to (Calculate capitalized interest)  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Qualifying asset and capitalization period (expenditures made, activities in progress, interest incurred)
- Weighted-average accumulated expenditures (WAAE) schedule
- Actual interest: specific (project) debt vs general debt
- Weighted-average interest rate on general debt
- Avoidable interest (specific rate first, then general rate on excess WAAE)
- Capitalize the **lesser** of actual interest and avoidable interest
- Initial recognition JEs: Construction in Process expenditures
- Period-end adjusting JE: capitalize interest / residual interest expense
- Subsequent measurement: CIP rollforward including capitalized interest
- Completion settlement: reclassify CIP → Building; capitalized interest depreciated over building life
- Land as building site: interest on land expenditures becomes part of **building** cost (not land)
- Disclosure: total interest incurred, amount capitalized, amount expensed

### Q1 — CORE — Helix Forge: WAAE, avoidable vs actual, capital expenditure JEs, period-end interest JE, CIP rollforward, disclosure
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Calculate and record capitalized interest (lesser of actual vs avoidable); initial CIP JEs; year-end interest JE; disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Helix Forge Industries** is constructing a manufacturing annex for its **own use** (a qualifying asset). Construction began **January 1** of the current year and is still in progress at **December 31**. Activities necessary to get the annex ready for use were in progress throughout the year, and interest cost was incurred all year. All listed debt was outstanding for the **full year**. Interest is paid in cash at year-end.

**Capital expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Amount |
|---|---:|
| January 1 | $1,200,000 |
| April 1 | 600,000 |
| July 1 | 900,000 |
| October 1 | 480,000 |
| December 31 | 300,000 |

**Outstanding debt at year-end:**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction note (project-specific) | $1,500,000 | 10% | Specific |
| Note payable | 800,000 | 8% | General |
| Bonds payable | 1,200,000 | 12% | General |
| Note payable | 400,000 | 9% | General |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **journal entry** for each capital expenditure date (initial recognition to Construction in Process).  
(b) Prepare the **weighted-average accumulated expenditures** schedule.  
(c) Compute **actual interest** for the year and the **weighted-average interest rate on general debt** (express the rate to three decimal places as a percent, and also use the exact fraction for avoidable interest).  
(d) Compute **avoidable interest** and the amount of interest to **capitalize** (lesser of actual vs avoidable).  
(e) Record the **December 31 period-end adjusting journal entry** for interest (cash settlement of full actual interest).  
(f) Prepare a **Construction in Process rollforward** for the year.  
(g) Show the **interest cost disclosure** for the December 31 financial statements.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Capital expenditure JEs (initial recognition)**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 | Construction in Process | 1,200,000 | |
| | Cash | | 1,200,000 |
| Apr 1 | Construction in Process | 600,000 | |
| | Cash | | 600,000 |
| Jul 1 | Construction in Process | 900,000 | |
| | Cash | | 900,000 |
| Oct 1 | Construction in Process | 480,000 | |
| | Cash | | 480,000 |
| Dec 31 | Construction in Process | 300,000 | |
| | Cash | | 300,000 |

Each entry balances (Dr = Cr). Total cash expenditures to CIP = **$3,480,000**.

**(b) Weighted-average accumulated expenditures**

| Date | Actual expenditure | Months outstanding | Weight | Weighted amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 | $1,200,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $1,200,000 |
| April 1 | 600,000 | 9 | 9/12 | 450,000 |
| July 1 | 900,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 450,000 |
| October 1 | 480,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 120,000 |
| December 31 | 300,000 | 0 | 0/12 | 0 |
| **Totals** | **$3,480,000** | | | **$2,220,000** |

**WAAE = $2,220,000**

**(c) Actual interest and general debt rate**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Construction note (specific) | $1,500,000 | 10% | $150,000 |
| Note payable (general) | 800,000 | 8% | 64,000 |
| Bonds payable (general) | 1,200,000 | 12% | 144,000 |
| Note payable (general) | 400,000 | 9% | 36,000 |
| **Total actual interest** | | | **$394,000** |

General debt principal = \(800{,}000 + 1{,}200{,}000 + 400{,}000 = \mathbf{\$2{,}400{,}000}\)  
General debt interest = \(64{,}000 + 144{,}000 + 36{,}000 = \mathbf{\$244{,}000}\)  
Weighted-average general rate = \(244{,}000 / 2{,}400{,}000 = 0.101\overline{6} = \mathbf{10.167\%}\) (three decimal places)

**(d) Avoidable interest and amount capitalized**

| Layer | WAAE applied | Rate | Avoidable interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Specific debt | $1,500,000 | 10% | $150,000 |
| Excess over specific | \(2{,}220{,}000 - 1{,}500{,}000 = 720{,}000\) | \(244{,}000/2{,}400{,}000\) | \(720{,}000 \times 244{,}000 / 2{,}400{,}000 = \mathbf{\$73{,}200}\) |
| **Total avoidable interest** | **$2,220,000** | | **$223,200** |

Actual interest = **$394,000**  
Avoidable interest = **$223,200**  
**Capitalize lesser = $223,200**  
Interest expensed = \(394{,}000 - 223{,}200 = \mathbf{\$170{,}800}\)

**(e) December 31 period-end interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 223,200 | |
| Interest Expense | 170,800 | |
| Cash | | 394,000 |
| *To record actual interest; capitalize avoidable (lesser) amount* | | |

Proof: Dr \(223{,}200 + 170{,}800 = 394{,}000\) = Cr. **Balances.**

**(f) Construction in Process rollforward**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning CIP | $0 |
| + Capital expenditures | 3,480,000 |
| + Capitalized interest | 223,200 |
| **Ending CIP** | **$3,703,200** |

Check: \(3{,}480{,}000 + 223{,}200 = \mathbf{3{,}703{,}200}\).

**(g) Interest cost disclosure (December 31)**

| Disclosure | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total interest cost incurred | $394,000 |
| Interest cost capitalized | 223,200 |
| Interest cost expensed | 170,800 |

**Key insight:** Capitalized interest equals the **lesser of actual interest and avoidable interest**. Avoidable interest applies the **specific** project rate to WAAE up to specific debt, then the **weighted-average general** rate to any excess. The capitalization entry increases **Construction in Process** and reduces **Interest Expense** relative to cash interest paid.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Crestline Packaging twin
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Calculate and record capitalized interest (number-variant twin of Q1)  
**Scenario:**  
**Crestline Packaging LLC** is building a bottling line facility for its own use. Construction and related activities ran the full calendar year; all debt was outstanding the full year; interest is paid in cash at December 31.

**Capital expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Amount |
|---|---:|
| January 1 | $1,500,000 |
| March 1 | 600,000 |
| June 30 | 900,000 |
| September 1 | 480,000 |
| December 31 | 420,000 |

**Outstanding debt:**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction loan (specific) | $1,000,000 | 8% | Specific |
| Note payable | 1,800,000 | 10% | General |
| Bonds payable | 1,200,000 | 15% | General |

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **WAAE**.  
(b) Compute **actual interest** and the **weighted-average general debt rate**.  
(c) Compute **avoidable interest** and the amount to **capitalize**.  
(d) Record the **December 31 interest journal entry** (full actual interest settled in cash).  
(e) Compute **ending Construction in Process** (expenditures + capitalized interest; assume beginning CIP = $0).

**Answer key:**

**(a) WAAE**

| Date | Expenditure | Months | Weight | Weighted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 | $1,500,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $1,500,000 |
| March 1 | 600,000 | 10 | 10/12 | 500,000 |
| June 30 | 900,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 450,000 |
| September 1 | 480,000 | 4 | 4/12 | 160,000 |
| December 31 | 420,000 | 0 | 0/12 | 0 |
| **Totals** | **$3,900,000** | | | **$2,610,000** |

**WAAE = $2,610,000**

**(b) Actual interest and general rate**

| Debt | Interest |
|---|---:|
| Specific construction loan \(1{,}000{,}000 \times 8\%\) | $80,000 |
| Note payable \(1{,}800{,}000 \times 10\%\) | 180,000 |
| Bonds payable \(1{,}200{,}000 \times 15\%\) | 180,000 |
| **Total actual interest** | **$440,000** |

General debt = \(1{,}800{,}000 + 1{,}200{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3{,}000{,}000}\)  
General interest = \(180{,}000 + 180{,}000 = \mathbf{\$360{,}000}\)  
WA general rate = \(360{,}000 / 3{,}000{,}000 = \mathbf{12\%}\)

**(c) Avoidable interest and capitalization**

| Layer | Base | Rate | Avoidable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Specific | $1,000,000 | 8% | $80,000 |
| Excess | \(2{,}610{,}000 - 1{,}000{,}000 = 1{,}610{,}000\) | 12% | 193,200 |
| **Total avoidable** | **$2,610,000** | | **$273,200** |

Actual **$440,000** vs avoidable **$273,200** → **capitalize $273,200**  
Interest expense = \(440{,}000 - 273{,}200 = \mathbf{\$166{,}800}\)

**(d) December 31 interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 273,200 | |
| Interest Expense | 166,800 | |
| Cash | | 440,000 |

Proof: Dr \(273{,}200 + 166{,}800 = 440{,}000\) = Cr. **Balances.**

**(e) Ending CIP**

\[
3{,}900{,}000 + 273{,}200 = \mathbf{\$4{,}173{,}200}
\]

**Key insight:** Same measurement model as Q1 with new facts: WAAE drives avoidable interest; specific debt is applied first; only the lesser of actual and avoidable is added to the asset.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Solara Components: land as building site, multi-period CIP, completion reclassification, depreciation start
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Interest on land-site expenditures capitalized to building; completion settlement (CIP → Building); subsequent depreciation of capitalized interest  
**Scenario:**  
**Solara Components Corp.** acquires land on **January 1, Year 1**, as the site for a new R&D building and begins construction immediately. The building is completed and placed in service on **December 31, Year 1** (ready for intended use that day; depreciation begins **January 1, Year 2**). All debt was outstanding the full year. Interest is paid in cash at year-end.

**Expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Description | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| January 1, Year 1 | Land (building site) | $400,000 |
| March 1, Year 1 | Construction costs | 600,000 |
| July 1, Year 1 | Construction costs | 800,000 |
| October 1, Year 1 | Construction costs | 200,000 |

**Debt (full year):**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction note (specific) | $900,000 | 10% | Specific |
| Note payable (general) | 2,000,000 | 9% | General |

Building useful life from January 1, Year 2: **20 years**, residual value **$50,000**, straight-line. Land is not depreciated.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1** land purchase and the **construction expenditure JEs** (land separate from CIP).  
(b) Compute **WAAE** (include land expenditures while construction activities are in progress).  
(c) Compute **actual interest**, **avoidable interest**, and the amount **capitalized**.  
(d) Record the **December 31, Year 1** interest JE.  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 1 completion/settlement JE** reclassifying CIP to Building. State the **Land** and **Building** balances immediately after completion.  
(f) Record the **Year 2 full-year depreciation** entry (capitalized interest is depreciated as part of building cost).  
(g) Briefly state the **ASC 835-20 disclosure** amounts for Year 1.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition JEs**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 | Land | 400,000 | |
| | Cash | | 400,000 |
| Mar 1 | Construction in Process | 600,000 | |
| | Cash | | 600,000 |
| Jul 1 | Construction in Process | 800,000 | |
| | Cash | | 800,000 |
| Oct 1 | Construction in Process | 200,000 | |
| | Cash | | 200,000 |

Each balances. Construction cash into CIP = **$1,600,000**. Land remains in **Land** at **$400,000**.

**(b) WAAE**

| Date | Expenditure | Months | Weight | Weighted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 (land) | $400,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $400,000 |
| March 1 | 600,000 | 10 | 10/12 | 500,000 |
| July 1 | 800,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 400,000 |
| October 1 | 200,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 50,000 |
| **Totals** | **$2,000,000** | | | **$1,350,000** |

**WAAE = $1,350,000** (land site expenditures are included once construction activities are underway).

**(c) Actual, avoidable, capitalize**

Actual interest:  
- Specific: \(900{,}000 \times 10\% = 90{,}000\)  
- General: \(2{,}000{,}000 \times 9\% = 180{,}000\)  
- **Total actual = $270,000**

Avoidable interest:  
- Specific layer: \(900{,}000 \times 10\% = 90{,}000\)  
- Excess: \(1{,}350{,}000 - 900{,}000 = 450{,}000 \times 9\% = 40{,}500\)  
- **Total avoidable = $130,500**

Capitalize lesser: **$130,500**  
Interest expense: \(270{,}000 - 130{,}500 = \mathbf{\$139{,}500}\)

**(d) December 31, Year 1 interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 130,500 | |
| Interest Expense | 139,500 | |
| Cash | | 270,000 |

Proof: Dr \(130{,}500 + 139{,}500 = 270{,}000\) = Cr. **Balances.**  
*All* capitalized interest is assigned to the **building** (including the portion theoretically related to land-site WAAE)—not to Land.

**(e) Completion / settlement JE — December 31, Year 1**

CIP before transfer = construction expenditures + capitalized interest:  
\[
1{,}600{,}000 + 130{,}500 = \mathbf{\$1{,}730{,}500}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 1,730,500 | |
| Construction in Process | | 1,730,500 |
| *To reclassify completed self-constructed building (incl. capitalized interest)* | | |

**Balances after completion:**  
- **Land** = **$400,000** (acquisition cost only; no capitalized interest)  
- **Building** = **$1,730,500**  
- **CIP** = **$0**

**(f) Year 2 depreciation (subsequent measurement of capitalized interest via depreciation)**

Depreciable base = \(1{,}730{,}500 - 50{,}000 = \mathbf{\$1{,}680{,}500}\)  
Annual SL depreciation = \(1{,}680{,}500 / 20 = \mathbf{\$84{,}025}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 84,025 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 84,025 |

Proof: Dr = Cr = 84,025. **Balances.**  
Capitalized interest of $130,500 is expensed over the building’s life as part of this depreciation (not as interest expense after capitalization).

**(g) Year 1 interest disclosure**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total interest cost incurred | $270,000 |
| Amount capitalized | 130,500 |
| Amount expensed | 139,500 |

**Key insight:** While land expenditures enter **WAAE** during construction of a building site, capitalized interest becomes part of **Building** cost—not Land. On completion, CIP (hard costs + capitalized interest) is reclassified to Building; thereafter the capitalized interest is recovered through **depreciation**.

### Q4 — MC — Capitalization rule and presentation
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Capitalize lesser of actual vs avoidable interest; classification of capitalized interest  

**Question 1:**  
During construction of a qualifying self-constructed plant asset, a company computes avoidable interest of $185,000 and incurs actual interest cost of $160,000 on all outstanding debt. The amount of interest that should be **capitalized** is:

- A) $185,000  
- B) $160,000  
- C) $25,000  
- D) $0  

**Answer:** **B.** Capitalized interest is the **lesser** of actual interest and avoidable interest. Avoidable interest cannot exceed actual interest incurred in the period (ASC 835-20). Here actual ($160,000) < avoidable ($185,000), so capitalize **$160,000** and expense **$0** of interest related to this computation (all actual interest is capitalized, subject to the overall actual-interest ceiling).

---

**Question 2:**  
Interest is capitalized on land expenditures while a building is being constructed on that land for the company’s own use. The capitalized interest related to those land expenditures should be:

- A) Added to the **Land** account and never depreciated  
- B) Added to the **Building** account and depreciated over the building’s useful life  
- C) Expensed immediately as interest expense  
- D) Deferred as a prepaid asset until the land is sold  

**Answer:** **B.** When land is a **building site**, interest capitalized on land expenditures is a cost of acquiring the **structure** (building), not land, and is depreciated over the building’s life.
