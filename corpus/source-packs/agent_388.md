# Agent 388 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-3

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Describe the accounting for a change in reporting entity  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Definition / scope:** A change in reporting entity produces financial statements that are, in effect, those of a **different reporting entity** (ASC Glossary). Limited mainly to: (a) consolidated or combined FS in place of individual-entity FS; (b) changing subsidiaries in the consolidated group; (c) changing entities in combined FS
- **Not a change in reporting entity:** Business combination under the **acquisition method**; consolidation of a **VIE** under Topic 810
- **Retrospective approach:** Restate **all prior periods presented** as if the new reporting entity had always been in place (ASC 250-10-45-21); restate previously issued interim information; do **not** change interest cost previously capitalized under Subtopic 835-20
- **Initial recognition / conversion JE:** Acquisition of the investee at book value; cost-to-equity conversion catch-up (Investment and beginning Retained Earnings) for post-acquisition undistributed earnings before consolidating
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** Multi-year schedule of previously reported (parent-only / separate) NI and EPS vs restated consolidated or combined NI and EPS; RE (equity) cumulative-effect bridge at the beginning of the earliest period presented
- **Period-end adjusting / eliminating JE (emphasis):** Year-end equity-method catch-up for the current year; eliminate reciprocal intercompany receivable/payable and unpaid intercompany dividends; eliminate investment against sub equity so consolidated totals are not overstated
- **Disposal / deconsolidation (composition change):** When an entity previously consolidated or combined **no longer meets criteria**, remove it from the reporting entity and apply the change **retrospectively**
- **Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-6):** Nature and reason; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, OCI, and related **EPS** for **all periods presented**; cumulative effect on RE (or other equity) as of beginning of **earliest period presented**; subsequent periods need not repeat the note
- **Number-variant twin:** Same consolidation-from-separate path with fully changed amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Begin consolidating a previously unconsolidated subsidiary: period-end elims (emphasis), conversion JE, restatement schedule, disclosure
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting and consolidating eliminating entries (emphasis); initial acquisition and equity-method conversion JEs; subsequent measurement restatement schedule of NI/EPS; RE cumulative-effect bridge; ASC 250-10-50-6 disclosure; contrast with acquisition method  
**Scenario:**  
**Ashgrove Partners Inc.** (calendar year-end) has owned **100%** of **Briarpoint Tooling LLC** since **January 1, Year 1**. Through Year 2, Ashgrove issued **parent-only** financial statements and accounted for Briarpoint under the **cost method** (dividend income only). Briarpoint was not consolidated.

Effective for the **Year 3** annual report (with **comparative Year 2**), Ashgrove determines that Briarpoint meets the criteria for consolidation and will present **consolidated** financial statements. There is **no** goodwill (acquisition was at book value). **Ignore income taxes.** Weighted-average common shares outstanding: **100,000** in all years (no preferred stock).

**Acquisition and Briarpoint equity data:**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash paid for 100% of Briarpoint, January 1, Year 1 (= book value) | $280,000 |
| Briarpoint common stock (constant) | 200,000 |
| Briarpoint retained earnings, January 1, Year 1 | 80,000 |

| Briarpoint | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $48,000 | $64,000 | $80,000 |
| Dividends declared | 12,000 | 16,000 | 20,000 |

**Ashgrove separate-entity results (cost method):**

| Ashgrove | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating income (excludes dividend income) | $400,000 | $440,000 | $480,000 |
| Dividend income from Briarpoint | 12,000 | 16,000 | 20,000 |
| Separate-entity net income | 412,000 | 456,000 | 500,000 |

**Additional facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Ashgrove recorded the acquisition of Briarpoint.
2. Briarpoint’s Year 1 and Year 2 dividends were paid in cash in those years. Briarpoint’s **Year 3** dividend of **$20,000** was **declared December 20, Year 3**, and remains **unpaid** at December 31, Year 3 (Ashgrove has Dividend Receivable; Briarpoint has Dividend Payable).
3. At **December 31, Year 3**, Ashgrove also has a **$27,000** open-account receivable from Briarpoint (reciprocal payable on Briarpoint’s books; will be settled in Year 4).
4. No intercompany inventory profits remain in ending inventory.
5. Through December 31, Year 3, Ashgrove’s **separate books still reflect the cost method** for Year 3 (dividend income recorded when declared). Management will convert the investment to the equity method for consolidation and record year-end consolidating elims.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for Ashgrove’s acquisition of Briarpoint.  
b. Classify the Year 3 change. State the required **accounting approach** (retrospective vs prospective) and what periods are affected.  
c. Prepare the **subsequent measurement / restatement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing: (i) previously reported parent-only NI and EPS; (ii) restated consolidated NI and EPS; (iii) period-specific effect of the change on NI and EPS.  
d. Compute the **cumulative effect on retained earnings** as of the beginning of the **earliest period presented** (January 1, Year 2) and as of January 1, Year 3. Show the undistributed-earnings bridge.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 conversion JE** that places the investment on the **equity method** for post-acquisition undistributed earnings through December 31, Year 2 (retrospective catch-up to beginning RE).  
f. Prepare the **basic consolidation eliminating entry** against Briarpoint’s equity and the investment account **as of January 1, Year 3** (after the conversion in e).  
g. **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Prepare Ashgrove’s **December 31, Year 3** books entry to pick up Year 3 equity-method earnings (net of the dividend already recorded under the cost method), so the investment reflects equity method through year-end.  
h. **Period-end consolidating eliminating JEs (emphasis):** Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** worksheet elims for (1) the intercompany open-account receivable/payable and (2) the intercompany dividend receivable/payable.  
i. **Classification / disclosure:** List the ASC 250-10-50-6 disclosures required in the Year 3 statements (include Year 2 NI and EPS effects as dollar amounts).  
j. **Contrast:** If instead Ashgrove had **first purchased** Briarpoint on January 1, Year 3 in an acquisition-method business combination, would that be a change in reporting entity? How would prior periods be treated?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — acquisition of Briarpoint (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Briarpoint Tooling LLC | 280,000 | |
| Cash | | 280,000 |
| *Acquire 100% of Briarpoint at book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 280,000 = Cr 280,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Classification and approach**

- **Type:** **Change in reporting entity** — presenting **consolidated** financial statements in place of **individual-entity** (parent-only) statements, and changing the composition of entities for which consolidated statements are presented (ASC Glossary; Demo 21-3 item 1).  
- **Approach:** **Retrospective.** Restate **all prior periods presented** (here Year 2 comparative, and any Year 1 history used in multi-year schedules/notes) as if Briarpoint had always been consolidated. Previously issued **interim** information is also presented retrospectively.  
- **Not** prospective; **not** a prior-period error correction. Interest cost previously capitalized under Subtopic 835-20 is **not** changed when restating.

**c. Restatement schedule (subsequent measurement of reported results under the new entity)**

Briarpoint RE rollforward (support schedule):

| Date | Computation | Briarpoint RE |
|---|---|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | given | 80,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 80,000 + 48,000 − 12,000 | 116,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 116,000 + 64,000 − 16,000 | 164,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 164,000 + 80,000 − 20,000 | 224,000 |

Consolidated NI (no unrealized intercompany profits)  
= Ashgrove **operating** income + Briarpoint NI  
(equivalently: parent separate NI − dividend income + Briarpoint NI)

| Year | Parent-only NI (as previously reported) | Parent-only EPS | Consolidated NI (restated) | Consolidated EPS | Effect on NI | Effect on EPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $412,000 | $4.12 | $400,000 + $48,000 = **$448,000** | **$4.48** | **+$36,000** | **+$0.36** |
| 2 | 456,000 | 4.56 | 440,000 + 64,000 = **504,000** | **5.04** | **+48,000** | **+0.48** |
| 3 (current) | 500,000 | 5.00 | 480,000 + 80,000 = **560,000** | **5.60** | **+60,000** | **+0.60** |

EPS checks: \(448{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 4.48\); \(504{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 5.04\); \(560{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 5.60\).  
Period effects equal Briarpoint NI − dividends (undistributed earnings of that year).

**d. Cumulative effect on beginning retained earnings**

Post-acquisition **undistributed** Briarpoint earnings = Briarpoint NI − dividends:

| Period | NI − Div | Undistributed |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | 48,000 − 12,000 | 36,000 |
| Year 2 | 64,000 − 16,000 | 48,000 |
| Years 1–2 cumulative | | **84,000** |

- **As of January 1, Year 2** (beginning of earliest period presented in the Year 3 comparative report): cumulative effect on RE = **$36,000** increase.  
- **As of January 1, Year 3:** cumulative effect on RE = **$84,000** increase.

**e. January 1, Year 3 — equity-method conversion (retrospective catch-up / consolidation setup on parent books)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Briarpoint Tooling LLC | 84,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 84,000 |
| *Recognize post-acquisition undistributed earnings of Briarpoint for Years 1–2 (cost → equity conversion for consolidation)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 84,000 = Cr 84,000. **Balanced.**  
Investment carrying amount after conversion = \(280{,}000 + 84{,}000 = \mathbf{\$364{,}000}\).

**f. January 1, Year 3 — basic consolidation eliminating entry**

Briarpoint equity at January 1, Year 3: CS \(200{,}000\) + RE \(164{,}000\) = **$364,000** (= investment after conversion).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock—Briarpoint | 200,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Briarpoint | 164,000 | |
| Investment in Briarpoint Tooling LLC | | 364,000 |
| *Eliminate 100% investment against Briarpoint’s equity at beginning of Year 3* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(200{,}000 + 164{,}000 = 364{,}000\) = Cr 364,000. **Balanced.**

**g. December 31, Year 3 — period-end equity-method catch-up (EMPHASIS)**

Under cost method Year 3, Ashgrove already recorded Dividend income **$20,000** (and Dividend Receivable **$20,000**). Equity-method earnings for Year 3 should be Briarpoint’s full NI **$80,000**. The **net** catch-up to the investment for Year 3 undistributed earnings is \(80{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$60{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Briarpoint Tooling LLC | 60,000 | |
| Equity in earnings of Briarpoint (or Investment income) | | 60,000 |
| *Period-end catch-up: Year 3 equity-method income net of dividend already recorded under cost method* | | |

**Check:** Dr 60,000 = Cr 60,000. **Balanced.**  

Investment after Y3 catch-up = \(364{,}000 + 60{,}000 = \mathbf{\$424{,}000}\).  
Check: sub equity at Dec 31 Y3 = CS \(200{,}000\) + RE \(224{,}000\) = **$424,000**.  
(Alternatively, reverse Dividend income $20,000 and record full Equity in earnings $80,000 with Dr Inv 80,000 / Cr Inv 20,000 for the dividend—net Inv increase still $60,000 and consolidated NI still $560,000.)

**h. December 31, Year 3 — period-end consolidating eliminating JEs (EMPHASIS)**

**(1) Intercompany open-account balances**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intercompany Payable (Due to Ashgrove) | 27,000 | |
| Intercompany Receivable (Due from Briarpoint) | | 27,000 |
| *Eliminate reciprocal intercompany trade balances in consolidation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 27,000 = Cr 27,000. **Balanced.**

**(2) Intercompany dividend receivable/payable (Year 3 dividend unpaid)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividend Payable (Briarpoint) | 20,000 | |
| Dividend Receivable (Ashgrove) | | 20,000 |
| *Eliminate reciprocal intercompany dividend balances in consolidation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000. **Balanced.**

**i. Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-6) — Year 3 (period of change)**

1. **Nature** of the change (Briarpoint now consolidated; previously parent-only) and **reason**.  
2. Effect of the change on **income from continuing operations**, **net income**, **OCI** (none here), and related **per-share amounts** for **all periods presented** (at least Year 2 restated and Year 3). Example: Year 2 NI higher by **$48,000**; EPS higher by **$0.48** (\(5.04 - 4.56\)). Year 3 NI higher by **$60,000**; EPS higher by **$0.60**.  
3. **Cumulative effect** on retained earnings as of the beginning of the earliest period presented: **+$36,000** at January 1, Year 2.  
4. Financial statements of **subsequent periods need not repeat** these disclosures.  
5. If the change is immaterial in the period of change but reasonably certain to be material later, still disclose nature and reason when presenting the period of change.

**j. Contrast — acquisition on January 1, Year 3**

**No.** A business combination accounted for by the **acquisition method** is **not** a change in reporting entity. Results are combined **prospectively from the acquisition date**; **prior periods are not restated** as if the acquiree had always been included (though certain pro forma disclosures may be required under other guidance). That is the opposite of the retrospective CRE treatment in parts b–i.

**Key insight:** Changing **who** is inside the reporting boundary is a **change in reporting entity** applied **retrospectively**. After conversion, the **period-end adjusting path** is critical: pick up current-year equity-method income, then eliminate reciprocal intercompany AR/AP and unpaid dividends so the consolidated balance sheet is not inflated. Buying a company under the acquisition method is **not** a CRE—prior periods stay as previously reported.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Common-control combination, period-end IC elim, deconsolidation settlement JE
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Combined financial statements under common control as a CRE; multi-year combined results schedule; period-end intercompany elim when first presenting combined FS (emphasis); deconsolidation settlement JE removing an entity from the reporting group; acquisition-method contrast; ASC 250 disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Valebrook Textiles Co.** and **Stonepath Accessories LLC** have been under **common control** of the same family holding company since Year 1. Through Year 3 each issued **separate** financial statements only. In **Year 4**, after a reorganization, management presents **combined** financial statements of Valebrook and Stonepath for Year 4 with **comparative Year 3** (and discloses Year 2 for the multi-year note).

| Separate NI | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Valebrook Textiles Co. | $250,000 | $280,000 | $320,000 |
| Stonepath Accessories LLC | 95,000 | 110,000 | 125,000 |

**Intercompany at December 31, Year 4 (period-end elim focus):**  
Valebrook has a **$41,000** receivable from Stonepath for inventory sold at cost (no profit in inventory). Stonepath has the corresponding payable.

**Part B — Deconsolidation / composition change (Year 5):**  
On **January 1, Year 5**, Stonepath **no longer meets the criteria** for inclusion in the combined group (common-control combination criteria fail). Valebrook will report **without** Stonepath. Stonepath remains a separate legal entity (not sold). At January 1, Year 5, Stonepath’s stand-alone balances are:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Identifiable assets (various) | $410,000 |
| Liabilities (various) | 95,000 |
| Equity (net assets) | 315,000 |

Assume the combined reporting entity had been keeping a single set of “combined books” that included Stonepath’s accounts. Management records a **deconsolidation entry** on January 1, Year 5, establishing an investment at carrying amount of net assets.

**Part C — Contrast fact:** On **April 1, Year 5**, Valebrook **acquires** unrelated **Harborline Packaging Inc.** for cash in an acquisition-method business combination.

**Required:**  
a. Classify the Year 4 change to combined reporting. State the accounting approach.  
b. Prepare a **combined results schedule** for Years 2–4 (combined NI) and the **period-specific effect** vs Valebrook-only NI.  
c. Cumulative effect on equity of the combined entity at **January 1, Year 3** (beginning of earliest comparative period in the Year 4 report) attributable to including Stonepath — assume Stonepath RE at Jan 1 Y2 was **$160,000** and Stonepath paid **no dividends** in Years 2–3 (so Y2–Y3 NI all retained). Show the bridge.  
d. **Period-end adjusting / eliminating JE (emphasis):** Prepare the **December 31, Year 4** consolidating (combining) elim for the intercompany receivable/payable.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 5 deconsolidation (settlement of combination relationship) JE** that removes Stonepath’s assets and liabilities and records Investment in Stonepath at net carrying amount.  
f. After the Year 5 composition change, how are **Year 4 comparative** figures presented in the Year 5 report?  
g. Is the **April 1, Year 5 acquisition** of Harborline a change in reporting entity? Explain prior-period treatment.  
h. **Disclosure:** What must Valebrook disclose in Year 4 regarding the change to combined statements?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**  
**Change in reporting entity** — presenting **combined** financial statements in place of financial statements of **individual** entities, and changing entities included in combined FS.  
**Retrospective** application to all prior periods presented.

**b. Combined results schedule**

| Year | Valebrook-only NI | Stonepath NI | Combined NI | Effect vs Valebrook-only |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | $250,000 | $95,000 | **$345,000** | **+$95,000** |
| 3 | 280,000 | 110,000 | **390,000** | **+110,000** |
| 4 | 320,000 | 125,000 | **445,000** | **+125,000** |

Checks: \(250{,}000 + 95{,}000 = 345{,}000\); \(280{,}000 + 110{,}000 = 390{,}000\); \(320{,}000 + 125{,}000 = 445{,}000\).

**c. Cumulative effect on equity at January 1, Year 3**

Stonepath RE at Jan 1 Y2 given **$160,000**. No dividends Years 2–3; Year 2 NI **$95,000** all retained.  
Equity of Stonepath at Jan 1 Y3 = \(160{,}000 + 95{,}000 = \mathbf{\$255{,}000}\).

When the combined entity first presents comparative Year 3, the **cumulative effect on equity at the beginning of the earliest period presented (Jan 1, Year 3)** from including Stonepath equals Stonepath’s equity at that date (**$255,000**), assuming Valebrook-only equity is the other component of combined equity and there is no new basis under common control (carryover basis).

**d. December 31, Year 4 — period-end IC elim (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intercompany Payable (Due to Valebrook) | 41,000 | |
| Intercompany Receivable (Due from Stonepath) | | 41,000 |
| *Eliminate reciprocal intercompany balances in combined statements* | | |

**Check:** Dr 41,000 = Cr 41,000. **Balanced.**  
(No profit elim needed—sales at cost with no remaining inventory profit.)

**e. January 1, Year 5 — deconsolidation / settlement JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Stonepath Accessories LLC | 315,000 | |
| Liabilities (various—Stonepath) | 95,000 | |
| Assets (various—Stonepath) | | 410,000 |
| *Remove Stonepath from the combined reporting entity; record investment at carrying amount of net assets* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(315{,}000 + 95{,}000 = 410{,}000\) = Cr 410,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Year 5 comparative presentation**  
Because removing Stonepath from the reporting entity is itself a **change in reporting entity**, Year 4 (and any other prior periods presented) are **restated retrospectively** to **exclude** Stonepath — as if the new (Valebrook-only) reporting entity had been in place in those periods (Demo 21-3 item 2). Combined Year 4 NI of $445,000 would be restated to Valebrook-only **$320,000** when presented comparatively in Year 5 (absent other adjustments).

**g. Acquisition of Harborline**  
**Not** a change in reporting entity. Acquisition-method business combinations are **excluded** from the CRE definition. Harborline’s results are included from **April 1, Year 5 forward**; **prior periods are not restated** to include Harborline.

**h. Year 4 disclosures (ASC 250-10-50-6)**  
Nature and reason for presenting combined statements of Valebrook and Stonepath; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, OCI, and EPS for all periods presented (e.g., Year 3 NI higher by **$110,000**); cumulative effect on equity/RE at beginning of earliest period presented (e.g., **+$255,000** equity of Stonepath at Jan 1, Year 3); subsequent periods need not repeat.

**Key insight:** CRE is about the **reporting boundary** (who is in the statements), applied **retrospectively**. When combined statements are first presented, **period-end eliminating entries** still apply so intercompany balances do not inflate combined assets and liabilities. **Common-control combination** and **criteria-based deconsolidation** are CREs; **acquisition-method purchases** are not.

### Q4 — MC — Accounting treatment for a change in reporting entity
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Retrospective application is required for a change in reporting entity (not prospective; not merely a prior-period adjusting entry without restatement)  
**Question:**  
A change in reporting entity is accounted for through:

- A) A prior-period adjusting entry only, with no restatement of comparative financial statements  
- B) Retrospective application of the new reporting entity to all prior periods presented  
- C) Restatement and reissuance of prior statements solely because an error was found  
- D) Prospective application of the new reporting entity to future periods only  

**Answer:** **B.**  
ASC 250-10-45-21 requires that when an accounting change results in financial statements that are, in effect, those of a **different reporting entity**, the change is **retrospectively applied** to the financial statements of **all prior periods presented**. Option A understates the requirement (comparative periods must be recast). Option C confuses CRE with **error correction**. Option D is the treatment for **changes in estimate**, not CRE.

### Q5 — MC — Identifying which event is a change in reporting entity
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Classify events as CRE vs acquisition-method combination vs estimate/principle; only CRE items get retrospective entity restatement under LO 21-3  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **both** a change in reporting entity **and** accounted for retrospectively?

- A) Parent acquires 100% of an unrelated company for cash on July 1 and consolidates from the acquisition date under the acquisition method  
- B) Parent changes from FIFO to weighted-average inventory costing for all inventories  
- C) Parent begins presenting consolidated financial statements that include a wholly owned subsidiary that previously issued only separate, unconsolidated parent-only statements (subsidiary now meets consolidation criteria)  
- D) Parent revises the useful life of manufacturing equipment from 10 years to 7 years  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Presenting consolidated statements in place of individual-entity statements (or changing which subsidiaries are consolidated) is a classic **change in reporting entity** and is applied **retrospectively**.  

- **A is wrong:** Acquisition-method business combinations are **explicitly not** changes in reporting entity; prior periods are **not** recast as if the acquiree were always consolidated.  
- **B is wrong:** Inventory method change is a **change in accounting principle** (LO 21-2), not a change in reporting entity.  
- **D is wrong:** Useful-life revision is a **change in estimate** (prospective) under LO 21-1.
