# Agent 276 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-4

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for Bonds Issued at a Discount  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** when market rate > stated rate, proceeds < face; Dr Cash, Dr Discount on Bonds Payable (contra-liability), Cr Bonds Payable at face
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** effective-interest amortization — Interest expense = beginning carrying amount × original market rate (per compounding period); Discount amort = Interest expense − cash coupon; carrying amount rises toward face
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** when the reporting date is not an interest date, accrue a pro rata share of the next semi-period’s cash interest, effective interest expense, and discount amortization (Dr Interest Expense; Cr Discount on BP; Cr Interest Payable)
- **Disposal / maturity JE:** final interest + remaining discount amortization, then pay face (Discount fully amortized; carrying amount = face)
- **Classification / presentation:** Bonds payable reported net of unamortized discount as a long-term liability; discount amortization reported as interest expense
- **Straight-line alternative (when not material):** constant discount amortization each period; total interest expense over life equals effective-interest total
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO mechanics with fully changed face, coupon, yield, and term

### Q1 — CORE — Discount bonds: issue, effective-interest schedule, interest JE, period-end accrual (emphasis), presentation, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Bonds issued at a discount (market > stated); initial recognition; full effective-interest schedule; **period-end adjusting JE** when year-end falls between interest dates; BS/IS presentation; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarvale Precision Tools Inc.** (calendar-year) authorizes and issues **$250,000** face of **6%** bonds on **May 1, Year 1**. The bonds mature on **May 1, Year 5** (4-year term) and pay cash interest **semiannually** on **May 1** and **November 1**. The bonds were sold to yield a **market rate of 8%**. Cedarvale amortizes the bond discount using the **effective interest method**, rounds present values and schedule amounts to the nearest dollar, and plugs residual unamortized discount in the final period. Because interest dates are May 1 and November 1, **December 31 year-end requires an adjusting accrual** for interest and discount amortization since November 1.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **issue price** and prepare the **initial recognition JE** on May 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** for all eight semiannual periods.  
c. Prepare the **November 1, Year 1** interest payment and discount amortization JE.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** accruing interest and discount amortization for the two months November 1–December 31 (prorate the next full semiannual row by **2/6**).  
e. Show the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet excerpt** (bonds payable, net, and related interest payable) and **Year 1 interest expense**.  
f. Prepare the **May 1, Year 5** entries to (1) record the final interest payment and amortization and (2) **derecognize** the bonds at maturity (assume no Interest Payable balance immediately before the final interest entry).

**Answer key:**  

**Setup**  
- Semiannual periods: \(4 \times 2 = 8\)  
- Semiannual market rate: \(8\% / 2 = 4\%\)  
- Semiannual cash interest: \(250{,}000 \times 6\% \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\)

**a. Issue price and initial recognition**  
Issue price \(= \mathrm{PV}(4\%, 8, -7{,}500, -250{,}000) = \mathbf{\$233{,}168}\).  
Discount \(= 250{,}000 - 233{,}168 = \mathbf{\$16{,}832}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 233,168 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 16,832 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 250,000 |
| *Issue $250,000 6% bonds at discount to yield 8%* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(233{,}168 + 16{,}832 = 250{,}000\) = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Effective-interest amortization schedule — discount**  
Cash each period \(= \$7{,}500\). Interest expense \(=\) beginning carrying amount \(\times 4\%\). Discount amort \(=\) interest expense \(-\) cash. Final period plugs residual unamortized discount to zero.

| Date | Cash (stated) | Interest expense (mkt) | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| May 1, Y1 (issue) | — | — | — | 16,832 | 233,168 |
| Nov 1, Y1 | 7,500 | 9,327 | 1,827 | 15,005 | 234,995 |
| May 1, Y2 | 7,500 | 9,400 | 1,900 | 13,105 | 236,895 |
| Nov 1, Y2 | 7,500 | 9,476 | 1,976 | 11,129 | 238,871 |
| May 1, Y3 | 7,500 | 9,555 | 2,055 | 9,074 | 240,926 |
| Nov 1, Y3 | 7,500 | 9,637 | 2,137 | 6,937 | 243,063 |
| May 1, Y4 | 7,500 | 9,723 | 2,223 | 4,714 | 245,286 |
| Nov 1, Y4 | 7,500 | 9,811 | 2,311 | 2,403 | 247,597 |
| May 1, Y5 | 7,500 | 9,903 | 2,403 | 0 | 250,000 |
| **Totals** | **60,000** | **76,832** | **16,832** | | |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
- Sum of amort \(= 16{,}832 =\) initial discount.  
- Total interest expense \(= 60{,}000 + 16{,}832 = 76{,}832 >\) total cash interest (discount case).  
- Carrying amount rises each period toward face.

**c. November 1, Year 1 — interest + amortization**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 9,327 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,827 |
| Cash | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr 9,327 = Cr \(1{,}827 + 7{,}500\). **Balanced.** Carrying amount after entry: \(233{,}168 + 1{,}827 = 234{,}995\).

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 1 (emphasis)**  
Next full semiannual row (May 1, Y2): Interest expense 9,400; discount amort 1,900; cash interest 7,500.  
Accrue **2 months / 6 months = 2/6**:

- Interest expense: \(9{,}400 \times 2/6 = \mathbf{3{,}133}\)  
- Discount amort: \(1{,}900 \times 2/6 = \mathbf{633}\)  
- Interest payable: \(7{,}500 \times 2/6 = \mathbf{2{,}500}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 3,133 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 633 |
| Interest Payable | | 2,500 |
| *Accrue 2 months bond interest/amort (Nov 1–Dec 31)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,133 = Cr \(633 + 2{,}500\). **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount after accrual: \(234{,}995 + 633 = \mathbf{235{,}628}\). Unamortized discount: \(15{,}005 - 633 = \mathbf{14{,}372}\).

**e. Presentation — December 31, Year 1**

Balance sheet excerpt:
| Liabilities | |
|---|---:|
| Interest payable | $2,500 |
| Bonds payable | $250,000 |
| Less: Discount on bonds payable | 14,372 |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$235,628** |

Income statement (Year 1): Interest expense \(= 9{,}327 + 3{,}133 = \mathbf{\$12{,}460}\)  
(6 months outstanding May 1–Nov 1 full semi + 2 months accrued Nov–Dec).

**f. Maturity — May 1, Year 5**

*Final interest + amortization*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 9,903 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,403 |
| Cash | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr 9,903 = Cr \(2{,}403 + 7{,}500\). **Balanced.** Unamortized discount → 0; carrying amount → face.

*Derecognize at maturity*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 250,000 | |
| Cash | | 250,000 |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Bonds sold when **market > stated** create a **discount** that is amortized as additional interest expense over the term so total interest expense exceeds total cash coupons. When the books close **between interest dates**, the period-end adjusting entry must accrue **cash interest, effective interest expense, and discount amortization** in the same proportion so the JE balances and carrying amount continues to roll toward face.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Discount bond twin (issue, schedule, interest, period-end accrual, maturity)
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: recompute issue price when market > stated; effective-interest schedule; **period-end adjusting JE** (3/6 of next semi row); interest payment and maturity settlement; net presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Briarwood Logistics LLC** (calendar-year) issues **$180,000** face of **5%** bonds on **April 1, Year 1**. Bonds mature **April 1, Year 4** (3 years), pay interest **semiannually** on April 1 and October 1, and were priced to yield a **market rate of 7%**. Briarwood uses the **effective interest method** (round to nearest dollar; plug residual amort in the final period). December 31 year-end falls three months after the October 1 interest date, so a period-end accrual is required.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **issue price** and prepare the **initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **full effective-interest amortization schedule** (6 periods).  
c. Prepare the **October 1, Year 1** interest + amortization JE.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (prorate the next full semiannual row by **3/6**).  
e. Report **bonds payable, net**, **interest payable**, and **Year 1 interest expense** at December 31, Year 1.  
f. Prepare the **maturity** entries on April 1, Year 4 (final interest + principal repayment; no prior Interest Payable immediately before the final interest entry).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Issue price and initial recognition**  
- Semiannual market rate: \(7\% / 2 = 3.5\%\)  
- Periods: \(3 \times 2 = 6\)  
- Semiannual cash interest: \(180{,}000 \times 5\% \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$4{,}500}\)  
- Issue price \(= \mathrm{PV}(3.5\%, 6, -4{,}500, -180{,}000) = \mathbf{\$170{,}409}\)  
- Discount \(= 180{,}000 - 170{,}409 = \mathbf{\$9{,}591}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 170,409 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 9,591 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 180,000 |

**Check:** Dr \(170{,}409 + 9{,}591 = 180{,}000\) = Cr 180,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Effective-interest amortization schedule**

| Apr 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 9,591 | 170,409 |
| Oct 1, Y1 | 4,500 | 5,964 | 1,464 | 8,127 | 171,873 |

**c. October 1, Year 1 interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,964 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,464 |

**Check:** Dr 5,964 = Cr \(1{,}464 + 4{,}500\). **Balanced.**

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 1 (emphasis)**  
Next full semiannual row (Apr 1, Y2): Interest expense 6,016; discount amort 1,516; cash interest 4,500. Prorate \(3/6 = 1/2\):

- Interest expense: \(6{,}016 \times 1/2 = \mathbf{3{,}008}\)

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Straight-line discount amortization + period-end accrual + maturity + presentation
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Straight-line interest method for a discount bond (constant amort); **period-end adjusting JE** between interest dates; maturity derecognition; classification of unamortized discount  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironclad Foundry Corp.** (calendar-year) issues **$400,000** face of **5%** bonds on **March 1, Year 1**, for cash proceeds of **$384,000** (sold at **96**). Bonds mature **March 1, Year 5** (4 years) and pay cash interest **semiannually** on March 1 and September 1. Management concludes that the difference between straight-line and effective-interest results is **not material**, so Ironclad amortizes the discount using the **straight-line interest method**. December 31 year-end falls four months after the September 1 interest date.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** on March 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the **straight-line amortization schedule** for all eight semiannual periods (show cash, interest expense, discount amort, unamortized discount, carrying amount).  
c. Prepare the **September 1, Year 1** interest + amortization JE.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (prorate the next full semiannual row by **4/6**).  
e. Show **bonds payable, net**, **interest payable**, and **Year 1 interest expense** at December 31, Year 1.  
f. Prepare the **March 1, Year 5** maturity entries (final interest + principal repayment; no prior Interest Payable immediately before the final interest entry).  
g. **Classification:** How is the unamortized discount presented on the balance sheet, and where is amortization reported in the income statement?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**  
Discount \(= 400{,}000 - 384{,}000 = \mathbf{\$16{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 384,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 16,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 400,000 |

**Check:** Dr \(384{,}000 + 16{,}000 = 400{,}000\) = Cr 400,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Straight-line amortization schedule**  
Semiannual cash interest: \(400{,}000 \times 5\% \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\).  
Straight-line discount amort each period: \(16{,}000 / 8 = \mathbf{\$2{,}000}\).  
Interest expense each period: \(10{,}000 + 2{,}000 = \mathbf{\$12{,}000}\).

| Date | Cash | Interest expense | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 16,000 | 384,000 |
| Sep 1, Y1 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 14,000 | 386,000 |
| Mar 1, Y2 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 12,000 | 388,000 |
| Sep 1, Y2 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 10,000 | 390,000 |
| Mar 1, Y3 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 8,000 | 392,000 |
| Sep 1, Y3 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 6,000 | 394,000 |
| Mar 1, Y4 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 4,000 | 396,000 |
| Sep 1, Y4 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 398,000 |
| Mar 1, Y5 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 2,000 | 0 | 400,000 |
| **Totals** | **80,000** | **96,000** | **16,000** | | |

**Checks:** Amort sum \(= 16{,}000\); total interest expense \(= 80{,}000 + 16{,}000 = 96{,}000 >\) cash interest. Carrying amount rises evenly toward face.

**c. September 1, Year 1 interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 12,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,000 |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr \(2{,}000 + 10{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 1 (emphasis)**  
Next full semiannual row: Interest expense 12,000; discount amort 2,000; cash interest 10,000. Prorate \(4/6\):

- Interest expense: \(12{,}000 \times 4/6 = \mathbf{8{,}000}\)  
- Discount amort: \(2{,}000 \times 4/6 = \mathbf{1{,}333}\) (rounded)  
- Interest payable: \(10{,}000 \times 4/6 = \mathbf{6{,}667}\) (rounded)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 8,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,333 |
| Interest Payable | | 6,667 |
| *Accrue 4 months bond interest/amort (Sep 1–Dec 31)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr \(1{,}333 + 6{,}667\). **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount after accrual: \(386{,}000 + 1{,}333 = \mathbf{387{,}333}\). Unamortized discount: \(14{,}000 - 1{,}333 = \mathbf{12{,}667}\).

**e. Presentation — December 31, Year 1**

| Liabilities | |
|---|---:|
| Interest payable | $6,667 |
| Bonds payable | $400,000 |
| Less: Discount on bonds payable | 12,667 |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$387,333** |

Year 1 interest expense \(= 12{,}000 + 8{,}000 = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\).

**f. Maturity — March 1, Year 5**

*Final interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 12,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,000 |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr \(2{,}000 + 10{,}000\). **Balanced.**

*Pay face*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 400,000 | |
| Cash | | 400,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Classification / presentation**  
- **Balance sheet:** Discount on Bonds Payable is a **contra-liability** deducted from Bonds Payable; the obligation is reported as **Bonds payable, net** (long-term until the year of maturity, when the current portion is reclassified as appropriate).  
- **Income statement:** Discount amortization is included in **interest expense** (not a separate “other” gain/loss line for routine amortization).

**Key insight:** Under straight-line, discount amortization and interest expense are **constant each period**, but total interest expense over the bond life still equals cash coupons + initial discount — the same total as under effective interest. Period-end accruals still **prorate** cash interest, amort, and expense so interim/year-end statements remain correct.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of discount and method choice
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Classification of Discount on Bonds Payable; when straight-line vs effective interest is appropriate  

**Question 1:**  
On the issuer’s balance sheet, **unamortized Discount on Bonds Payable** is best classified as:  
- A) A deferred charge (asset) amortized to interest expense  
- B) A contra-liability deducted from Bonds Payable  
- C) An adjunct account added to Bonds Payable  
- D) A component of accumulated other comprehensive income  

**Answer:** B. Under ASC 835-30, discount is a direct deduction from the face amount of the debt (contra-liability). It is not an asset; premium would be the adjunct account; AOCI is not used for the issuer’s own bond discount.

**Question 2:**  
Under GAAP, the **straight-line interest method** for amortizing bond discount may be used only when:  
- A) The bonds were issued between interest dates  
- B) The stated rate equals the market rate  
- C) Results do not differ materially from the effective interest method  
- D) The company elects fair value option for the liability  

**Answer:** C. ASC 835-30 requires the interest (effective interest) method; straight-line is acceptable only if results are not materially different from the effective interest method.
