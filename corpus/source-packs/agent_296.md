# Agent 296 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-2

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Prepare the operating activities section of the statement of cash flows using the indirect method  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Indirect method starts with net income** (or net loss) and reconciles to **net cash provided (used) by operating activities**
- **Noncash expenses and losses are added**; **noncash revenues and gains are subtracted**
- **Working-capital adjustments:** ↑ operating asset → subtract; ↓ operating asset → add; ↑ operating liability → add; ↓ operating liability → subtract
- **Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis):** depreciation, patent amortization, bond premium/discount amortization, and year-end accruals create the noncash and WC reconciling items on the SCF
- **Disposal / settlement JEs:** PPE sale gain/loss and bond retirement gain/loss are reversed in operating because related cash is **investing** or **financing**
- **Subsequent measurement schedules:** Accumulated depreciation, bond premium/discount, and WC change tables roll beginning balances to ending balances and prove SCF amounts
- **Classification / presentation:** full cash proceeds from asset disposal → investing; cash to retire debt → financing; only the G/L is adjusted in operating under the indirect method
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO 22-2 path with all company names and amounts changed

### Q4 — MC — Premium amortization and a WC decrease

**LO:** LO 22-2  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation — bond **premium** amortization and decrease in inventory under the indirect method  
**Question:** In preparing the operating section under the **indirect method**, which treatment is **correct**?

- A) Amortization of bond **premium**: **add**; decrease in inventory: **subtract**  
- B) Amortization of bond **premium**: **subtract**; decrease in inventory: **add**  
- C) Amortization of bond **premium**: **subtract**; decrease in inventory: **subtract**  
- D) Amortization of bond **discount**: **subtract**; decrease in inventory: **add**  

**Answer:** **B.** Premium amortization reduces interest expense relative to cash interest, so it **increased** NI without providing cash → **subtract**. A decrease in inventory means purchases < COGS on an accrual basis → cash paid for inventory is lower relative to COGS → **add**. A reverses both signs. C gets inventory wrong. D misstates discount amortization (discount is **added**).

### Q5 — MC — Period-end salaries accrual vs SCF

**LO:** LO 22-2  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE for accrued salaries and its single correct SCF effect  
**Question:** At year-end, Ridgeway records:  
`Dr Salaries Expense 8,000` / `Cr Salaries Payable 8,000`.  
Salaries Payable increased by **$8,000** during the year (no other salary-related reconciling complexity). Under the **indirect method**, the correct operating-section treatment is:

- A) Add salaries expense of $8,000 **and** add the $8,000 increase in salaries payable  
- B) Subtract salaries expense of $8,000 because it is unpaid  
- C) Add only the **$8,000 increase in salaries payable** (do not separately add the expense again)  
- D) Ignore the entry because no cash was paid  

**Answer:** **C.** Net income already includes the $8,000 salaries expense from the period-end AJ. The WC adjustment **adds the increase in Salaries Payable**, which converts accrual expense to cash basis. Adding the expense again (A) double-counts. B and D ignore that NI must be adjusted for the unpaid accrual through the payable change.
