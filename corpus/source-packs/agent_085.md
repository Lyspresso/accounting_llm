# Agent 85 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-9

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Describe property, plant, and equipment disclosures and ratio analyses  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE** for PPE: capitalize invoice + freight + installation (and similar) into the asset account
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** straight-line depreciable base, annual depreciation, multi-year cost / AD / book value rollforward
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** depreciation expense → accumulated depreciation
- **Disposal JE:** update depreciation through disposal date; remove cost and AD; recognize gain or loss
- **ASC 360-10-50-1 disclosures:** depreciation expense; major classes of depreciable assets; accumulated depreciation; depreciation methods by major class; fully depreciated assets still in service; liens in liability section (not as asset offset)
- **Ratio analysis:** asset turnover (Net sales ÷ Average total assets); return on assets (Net income ÷ Average total assets); fixed asset turnover (Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets / PPE net)
- **AD-to-cost ratio** as an indicator of percentage of useful life expired; aging of plant assets and depreciation method effect on ROA over time

### Q1 — CORE — Ironwood Packaging: initial recognition, dep schedule, disposal, disclosures, ratios
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for PPE; subsequent SL depreciation schedule; period-end dep JE; disposal with through-date dep update; ASC 360 disclosures; asset turnover, ROA, and fixed asset turnover  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironwood Packaging Co.** (calendar year-end) expands its production line and later disposes of the same asset. Separately, management prepares year-end PPE note disclosures and efficiency/profitability ratios.

**Part A — Asset life cycle (packaging line)**

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Acquisition date | January 1, Year 1 |
| Invoice price (cash) | $540,000 |
| Freight-in (cash) | $12,000 |
| Installation and testing (cash) | $18,000 |
| Estimated residual value | $30,000 |
| Estimated useful life | 6 years |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line, full months |
| Disposal date | April 1, Year 3 |
| Cash proceeds on disposal | $380,000 |

**Part B — PPE note balances at December 31, Year 2** (packaging line still held; included in machinery)

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | $400,000 | — |
| Buildings | 2,400,000 | $720,000 |
| Machinery and equipment | 1,800,000 | 675,000 |
| **Totals** | **$4,600,000** | **$1,395,000** |

Additional Year 2 facts for the note: depreciation expense **$285,000**; all classes use **straight-line**; cost of **fully depreciated machinery still in service** **$85,000**; a bank loan is secured by a first lien on the buildings (outstanding principal **$900,000**).

**Part C — Ratio inputs** (dollars)

| Date / period | PPE, net (fixed assets) | Total assets | Net sales | Net income |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning of prior year (1/1/Y1) | $3,200,000 | $9,500,000 | — | — |
| End of prior year (12/31/Y1) | 3,350,000 | 10,000,000 | $14,000,000 | $1,050,000 |
| End of current year (12/31/Y2) | 3,450,000 | 10,600,000 | 15,400,000 | 1,220,000 |

Treat **Year 1 = prior fiscal year** and **Year 2 = current fiscal year**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **capitalized cost** of the packaging line and prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry**.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing annual depreciation, accumulated depreciation, and book value (assume held full years for the schedule; residual and life unchanged).  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** for depreciation on the packaging line.  
d. Prepare the **April 1, Year 3** entries to (1) update depreciation through the disposal date and (2) record the **disposal** (must balance). Compute gain or loss.  
e. List the **ASC 360-10-50-1 required disclosures** and prepare a **PPE note schedule** (cost by class, AD, net) with the supplemental items users expect (methods, dep expense, fully depreciated assets, lien placement).  
f. Compute **(1) asset turnover**, **(2) return on assets**, and **(3) fixed asset turnover** for the **prior year** and the **current year** (round ratios to two decimal places). Compute the **AD-to-gross plant cost** ratio at December 31, Year 2. Briefly analyze whether efficiency/profitability improved.

**Answer key:**

**a. Capitalized cost and initial recognition**  
Capitalized cost = $540,000 + $12,000 + $18,000 = **$570,000**

*January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition of packaging line*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

| Cash | | 570,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 570,000. **Balanced.**

Depreciable base = $570,000 − $30,000 = **$540,000**  
Annual SL depreciation = $540,000 ÷ 6 = **$90,000**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (straight-line)**

| Year | Beg. BV | Depreciation | End. AD | End. BV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $570,000 | $90,000 | $90,000 | $480,000 |
| 2 | 480,000 | 90,000 | 180,000 | 390,000 |
| 3 (full year, if held) | 390,000 | 90,000 | 270,000 | 300,000 |

(At end of Year 6, BV would equal residual $30,000.)

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Line | 90,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Line | | 90,000 |
| *Year 1 SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 90,000. **Balanced.**

**d. April 1, Year 3 — update depreciation and dispose**

Depreciation for Jan 1–Mar 31, Year 3 (3/12):  
$90,000 × 3/12 = **$22,500**

*April 1, Year 3 — update depreciation through disposal date*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Line | 22,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Line | | 22,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 22,500.

Accumulated depreciation at disposal = $90,000 × 2 + $22,500 = **$202,500**

**Gain on disposal** = $380,000 − $367,500 = **$12,500**

*April 1, Year 3 — disposal (settlement) of packaging line*

**Check:** Dr 380,000 + 202,500 = **582,500**; Cr 570,000 + 12,500 = **582,500**. **Balanced.**

**e. ASC 360-10-50-1 disclosures and PPE note**

**Required each reporting period (ASC 360-10-50-1):**  
1. **Depreciation expense** for the period  
2. **Balances of major classes** of depreciable assets, by nature or function  
3. **Accumulated depreciation**, by major class or in total  
4. **Description of depreciation methods** used by major classes of assets  

Also useful / commonly expected: postacquisition cost policy, idle plant assets, **fully depreciated assets still in service**, and **significant liens** (presented in the **liability** section / notes—not as an offset against the asset).

**PPE note schedule — December 31, Year 2**

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation | Net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Buildings | 2,400,000 | 720,000 | 1,680,000 |
| Machinery and equipment | 1,800,000 | 675,000 | 1,125,000 |
| **Total** | **$4,600,000** | **$1,395,000** | **$3,205,000** |

*Supplemental note items:*  
- Depreciation expense, Year 2: **$285,000**  
- Method: **straight-line** for buildings and machinery  
- Fully depreciated machinery still in service (cost): **$85,000**  
- Buildings are subject to a first lien securing notes payable of **$900,000** (disclose with **liabilities**, not as a deduction from plant assets)

**f. Ratio analysis**

**Prior year (Year 1)**  
Average total assets = ($9,500,000 + $10,000,000) / 2 = **$9,750,000**  
Average fixed assets = ($3,200,000 + $3,350,000) / 2 = **$3,275,000**

| Ratio | Formula | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg total assets | $14,000,000 ÷ $9,750,000 | **1.44** |
| Return on assets | Net income ÷ Avg total assets | $1,050,000 ÷ $9,750,000 | **0.11** |
| Fixed asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg fixed assets | $14,000,000 ÷ $3,275,000 | **4.27** |

**Current year (Year 2)**  
Average total assets = ($10,000,000 + $10,600,000) / 2 = **$10,300,000**  
Average fixed assets = ($3,350,000 + $3,450,000) / 2 = **$3,400,000**

| Ratio | Formula | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg total assets | $15,400,000 ÷ $10,300,000 | **1.50** |
| Return on assets | Net income ÷ Avg total assets | $1,220,000 ÷ $10,300,000 | **0.12** |
| Fixed asset turnover | Net sales ÷ Avg fixed assets | $15,400,000 ÷ $3,400,000 | **4.53** |

**AD-to-gross plant cost (12/31/Y2)** = $1,395,000 ÷ $4,600,000 = **0.30 (30%)** — roughly 30% of recorded plant cost has been allocated through depreciation (an indicator of portion of service life expired, interpreted with care because land is nondepreciable and lives differ by class).

**Analysis:** Asset turnover rose from 1.44 to 1.50, ROA from 0.11 to 0.12, and fixed asset turnover from 4.27 to 4.53. Sales and net income grew faster relative to average asset bases, so both **efficiency of asset use** and **profitability of assets** improved year over year.

**Key insight:** Exam paths for LO 12-9 pair **PPE measurement entries** (purchase → depreciate → dispose) with **what users see**: major-class balances, AD, methods, dep expense, and the three core ratios (asset turnover, ROA, fixed asset turnover). Always average **beginning and ending** balance-sheet amounts for the ratio denominators.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Disclosure checklist, AD/cost, SL vs accelerated ROA, held-for-use presentation
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation of required PPE disclosures; AD-to-cost life-expired indicator; relation of depreciation method to ROA over asset age; period-end dep JE and classification of gain/loss on disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Northshore Metalworks LLC** presents the following incomplete Year 5 information. The controller asks for help finishing disclosures and interpreting ratios.

| Class (12/31/Year 5) | Cost | Accumulated depreciation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | $300,000 | — |
| Building | 1,500,000 | $600,000 |
| Equipment | 900,000 | 540,000 |

- Depreciation expense, Year 5: **$195,000** (all straight-line)  
- Equipment with original cost **$60,000** is fully depreciated and still used in production  
- On **December 31, Year 5**, Northshore sold a delivery truck (cost $48,000; AD after Year 5 dep update $39,000) for **$6,000** cash. Year 5 depreciation on the truck of **$4,800** has **not yet** been recorded (it is included in the $195,000 only after the update entry below).  
  *For parts (c)–(d), treat the $4,800 truck depreciation as the period-end update still needed before disposal; the $195,000 total dep expense is the amount after that update is recorded.*

**Selected multi-year data for ROA discussion (one plant asset group, ignore tax):**

| | Year 1 (new assets) | Year 5 (aged assets) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Operating income before depreciation | $500,000 | $500,000 |
| SL depreciation | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| Accelerated depreciation (same assets) | 160,000 | 40,000 |
| Average total assets if SL used | 2,000,000 | 1,400,000 |
| Average total assets if accelerated used | 1,880,000 | 1,280,000 |

(Net income for the ROA sketch = income before depreciation − depreciation; ignore other expenses.)

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **PPE disclosure schedule** (cost, AD, net by class) and state the **four ASC 360-10-50-1 required items**, plus how the **lien** rule and **fully depreciated assets still in service** should be handled.  
b. Compute **AD ÷ total plant cost** and interpret it.  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting JE** for the truck’s Year 5 depreciation ($4,800) and the **disposal JE** for the truck. Where is the loss presented?  
d. Using the multi-year data, compute **ROA under SL and under accelerated** for Year 1 and Year 5. Explain why ROA tends to **rise as plant assets age** and how **method choice** affects early vs later years (Exhibit 12-5 idea).

**Answer key:**

**a. Disclosure schedule and requirements**

| Class | Cost | Accumulated depreciation | Net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | $300,000 | $— | $300,000 |
| Building | 1,500,000 | 600,000 | 900,000 |
| Equipment | 900,000 | 540,000 | 360,000 |
| **Total** | **$2,700,000** | **$1,140,000** | **$1,560,000** |

**ASC 360-10-50-1 required:**  
1. Depreciation expense — **$195,000** (after truck update)  
2. Major classes of depreciable assets — land / building / equipment balances as above  
3. Accumulated depreciation — by class or total **$1,140,000**  
4. Depreciation methods by major class — **straight-line** for building and equipment  

**Fully depreciated assets still in service:** disclose cost **$60,000** (equipment).  
**Significant liens:** report in the **liability section / debt notes**, **not** as a contra-asset against PPE.

**b. AD-to-cost**  
$1,140,000 ÷ $2,700,000 = **0.42 (42%)**.  
Users may read this as roughly **42% of recorded plant cost already allocated** to expense (proxy for portion of service potential expired), remembering land is not depreciated and class lives differ.

**c. Period-end update and disposal**

*December 31, Year 5 — period-end dep on truck*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Truck | 4,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Truck | | 4,800 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,800. (After this entry, truck AD = $39,000 as given.)

BV of truck = $48,000 − $39,000 = **$9,000**  
**Loss on disposal** = $9,000 − $6,000 = **$3,000**

*December 31, Year 5 — disposal*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 6,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Truck | 39,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of PPE | 3,000 | |
| Equipment—Truck | | 48,000 |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 + 39,000 + 3,000 = **48,000** = Cr 48,000. **Balanced.**

**Presentation:** **Loss on Disposal of PPE $3,000** appears on the **income statement** in **other expenses** (other revenues and expenses), not as an operating revenue deduction from customers and not as a direct charge to retained earnings.

**d. ROA and depreciation method over asset age**

| | Year 1 SL | Year 1 Accel. | Year 5 SL | Year 5 Accel. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NI (before dep − dep) | $400,000 | $340,000 | $400,000 | $460,000 |
| Avg total assets | 2,000,000 | 1,880,000 | 1,400,000 | 1,280,000 |
| **ROA** | **0.20** | **0.18** | **0.29** | **0.36** |

**Why ROA rises as assets age:** Numerator falls by only **one year’s** depreciation, while the denominator is reduced by **cumulative** depreciation to date (lower average assets) → ratio climbs over time regardless of method.  
**Method effect:** When assets are **new**, **straight-line** produces **higher** early earnings (and often higher ROA) than accelerated methods. As assets **age**, the relation **reverses** — accelerated methods have lower remaining dep, so later-year ROA can exceed SL (as in Year 5 above: 0.36 vs 0.29). Analysts comparing firms must consider **asset age mix** and **method choice**, not only the raw ratio.

**Key insight:** LO 12-9 is as much about **what goes in the notes and ratio analysis** as about the journal entries that feed those numbers. Disposal losses are IS items; liens stay with liabilities; AD/cost and method-vs-age effects stop naive cross-company ROA comparisons.

### Q4 — MC — Required PPE disclosure under ASC 360
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification of required depreciation and PPE disclosures (ASC 360-10-50-1)  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **required** to be disclosed for each reporting period related to depreciation and depreciable assets under ASC 360-10-50-1?

- A) Fair value of each major class of plant assets  
- B) Balances of major classes of depreciable assets, by nature or function  
- C) Budgeted capital expenditures for the next five years  
- D) Replacement cost of fully depreciated assets still in service  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 360-10-50-1 requires (1) depreciation expense, (2) balances of major classes of depreciable assets by nature or function, (3) accumulated depreciation by major class or in total, and (4) a description of depreciation methods by major classes. Fair value of PPE in use, multi-year capex budgets, and replacement cost of fully depreciated assets are **not** among the four required items (though cost of fully depreciated assets still in service is often **useful** voluntary information).

### Q4b — MC — Ratio identification
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification of PPE-related efficiency and profitability ratios  
**Question:**  
**Fixed asset turnover** is computed as:

- A) Net income ÷ Average fixed assets  
- B) Average fixed assets ÷ Net sales  
- C) Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets  
- D) Net sales ÷ Average total assets  

**Answer:** **C.** Fixed asset turnover = **Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets** (activity/efficiency of PPE). Asset turnover uses **average total assets** in the denominator; ROA uses **net income** over **average total assets**.
