# Agent 352 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-5

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Determine the initial and subsequent measurement of indefinite life intangible assets and goodwill  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Indefinite-life intangible (other than goodwill):** initially measured at **cash-equivalent cost** (purchase price + transfer/legal/registration fees to ready the asset); **not amortized**; carrying amount remains cost until impairment (LO 13-6) or disposal
- **Goodwill:** residual = **purchase price − fair value of identifiable net assets** acquired in a business combination; record assets/liabilities at **FV** (including previously unrecognized acquiree intangibles such as brands, patents, IPR&D); present goodwill as a **separate** balance-sheet line item
- **Subsequent measurement of goodwill and indefinite-life intangibles:** **do not amortize**; evaluate for impairment at least annually (impairment mechanics are LO 13-6 — this pack stops at “no amortization / cost basis remains”)
- **Crypto assets (ASC 350-60):** indefinite-life class that is remeasured to **fair value each reporting date**, with unrealized gains/losses in **net income** (period-end adjusting JE — emphasis)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** FV remeasurement of crypto; **explicit zero amortization** AJ documentation for trademarks/goodwill
- **Carrying-value / subsequent-measurement schedule:** multi-period rollforward (cost → zero amort → ending CV; crypto cost → FV adjustments)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove carrying amount of indefinite-life trademark on sale; recognize gain or loss vs proceeds (no amortization to-date because none was recorded)
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all companies and amounts changed

### Q4 — MC — Subsequent measurement of indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill

**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement — indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill are not amortized  
**Question:**  
Under U.S. GAAP (public-company default model as presented in LO 13-5), which statement best describes the **subsequent measurement** of an **acquired indefinite-life trademark** and of **goodwill** after initial recognition (ignoring impairment, which is tested under a separate LO)?

- A) Both are amortized straight-line over a maximum of 10 years.  
- B) The trademark is amortized over its legal life; goodwill is amortized over its estimated useful life.  
- C) Neither is amortized on a periodic basis; both remain at cost until impairment or disposal (crypto assets are a FV exception).  
- D) Both are remeasured to fair value each reporting date with unrealized gains and losses in OCI.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Indefinite-life intangibles (other than crypto) and goodwill are **not amortized**. They are carried at cost and evaluated for impairment (LO 13-6). Crypto assets are remeasured to **FV through net income**, not OCI. Private-company optional goodwill amortization is an exception outside the core LO 13-5 public model emphasized here.

### Q5 — MC — Initial measurement of goodwill

**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Initial measurement of goodwill as residual of purchase price over FV of identifiable net assets  
**Question:**  
Pinnacle Co. pays **$900,000 cash** to acquire 100% of Trailhead Inc. Fair value of Trailhead’s identifiable assets is **$1,150,000**; fair value of identifiable liabilities is **$320,000**. Trailhead’s book value of equity was **$700,000**. What amount of **goodwill** does Pinnacle record, and what is the **initial measurement basis**?

- A) Goodwill **$200,000**, measured as purchase price minus book value of equity.  
- B) Goodwill **$70,000**, measured as purchase price minus fair value of identifiable net assets.  
- C) Goodwill **$250,000**, measured as fair value of assets minus purchase price.  
- D) Goodwill **$0**, because only book values are used in a business combination.

**Answer:** **B.**  
FV of identifiable net assets = 1,150,000 − 320,000 = **$830,000**.  
Goodwill = 900,000 − 830,000 = **$70,000**.  
Goodwill is a **residual** of consideration transferred over **fair value** of identifiable net assets — **not** book value of equity ($900,000 − $700,000 = $200,000 would be incorrect).

**Check:** 900,000 − 830,000 = 70,000.
