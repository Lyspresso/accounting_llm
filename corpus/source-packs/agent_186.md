# Agent 186 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-8

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Apply the dollar-value LIFO method  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Dollar-value LIFO measures **pools of inventory dollars** with **price indices**, not individual unit costs—reducing LIFO liquidation risk relative to unit LIFO
- **Four-step subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** (1) restate FIFO ending inventory to **base-year dollars** (FIFO EI ÷ year index), (2) arrange into **layers** (new layer only if base-year $ rises), (3) match each layer to its **originating-year index**, (4) restate layers to **dollar-value LIFO** (base-year layer × its index) and sum
- Base year = adoption / first year of DV LIFO; base-year FIFO EI is the base layer at index **1.00**
- Internal books stay on **FIFO** (or average cost); DV LIFO is a **year-end external/tax** measurement
- **LIFO reserve** = FIFO ending inventory − dollar-value LIFO ending inventory
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** increase reserve → Dr **Cost of Goods Sold**, Cr **Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis**; decrease reserve → reverse
- **Initial recognition:** purchases are recorded at actual cost under the internal method; conversion is not applied mid-year
- **Layer liquidation / settlement:** when base-year dollars fall, **newest layers liquidate first**; remaining older layers keep original indices; that year’s index is unused if no new layer is created
- Presentation: inventory (current asset) at DV LIFO, often FIFO less valuation allowance; disclose LIFO reserve

### Q1 — CORE — Northgate Outfitters: full multi-year DV LIFO schedule, purchase, LIFO-reserve JEs
**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule (four-step layer rollforward emphasis); base-year initial layer; period-end LIFO-reserve adjusting JEs; FIFO purchase remains unconverted mid-year  
**Scenario:**  
**Northgate Outfitters Co.** began operations on **January 1, Year 1**. It keeps **FIFO** inventory records for internal management and control. For **external financial reporting and income tax**, it uses the **dollar-value LIFO** method with a **single inventory pool**. Year 1 is the base year (price index **1.00**).

Year-end inventory and index data:

| Year | Ending inventory at FIFO (year-end prices) | Price index |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $75,000 | 1.00 |
| Year 2 | $99,000 | 1.20 |
| Year 3 | $117,000 | 1.30 |

During Year 2, on **April 8**, Northgate purchased merchandise on account for **$28,400**. The purchase was recorded at cost under the company’s FIFO system (no dollar-value LIFO conversion is made on the purchase date).

**Required:**  
(a) Explain why the April 8 purchase is **not** measured under dollar-value LIFO when recorded, and prepare the **initial recognition journal entry** for that purchase.  
(b) Prepare a complete **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing base-year dollars, inventory layers, layer indices, and **dollar-value LIFO ending inventory** each year (four-step method).  
(c) Compute the **LIFO reserve** at each year-end and the **annual adjustment** to the reserve.  
(d) Prepare the **period-end adjusting journal entries** on December 31, Year 2 and December 31, Year 3 (use **Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis**).  
(e) Present inventory on the **December 31, Year 3 balance sheet** (FIFO, allowance, net) and one **disclosure** point.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Mid-year purchase stays on FIFO books**  
Dollar-value LIFO is **not** applied transaction-by-transaction. Northgate continues FIFO for purchases during the year; only at **year-end** is ending inventory restated to DV LIFO and a **LIFO-reserve** adjusting entry recorded (Demo 9-8 / LO 9-6 linkage).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (or Purchases) | 28,400 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 28,400 |
| *Record April 8 merchandise purchase at cost (FIFO books)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,400 = Cr 28,400. **Balanced.**

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule (four steps) — emphasis**

**Step 1 — Restate ending inventory at base-year dollars**

| Year | FIFO EI | ÷ Index | = Base-year dollars |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $75,000 | 1.00 | **$75,000** |
| 2 | 99,000 | 1.20 | **$82,500** |
| 3 | 117,000 | 1.30 | **$90,000** |

Workings: \(99{,}000 / 1.20 = 82{,}500\); \(117{,}000 / 1.30 = 90{,}000\).

**Step 2 — Arrange into layers (oldest first; new layer if base-year $ increases)**

| Year | Base-year $ EI | Layers (base-year $) |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | $75,000 | Base layer: $75,000 |
| 2 | 82,500 | Base $75,000 + **Year 2 layer $7,500** (\(82{,}500 - 75{,}000\)) |
| 3 | 90,000 | Base $75,000 + Y2 $7,500 + **Year 3 layer $7,500** (\(90{,}000 - 82{,}500\)) |

No liquidation: base-year inventory rose each year.

**Step 3 — Match layers to originating price indices**  
- Base (Year 1) layer → index **1.00**  
- Year 2 layer → index **1.20**  
- Year 3 layer → index **1.30**

**Step 4 — Restate layers to dollar-value LIFO and sum**

| Year | Layers (base-year $) | × Index | Layer at DV LIFO | **Ending DV LIFO** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $75,000 | 1.00 | $75,000 | **$75,000** |
| 2 | 75,000 | 1.00 | 75,000 | |
|  | 7,500 | 1.20 | 9,000 | **$84,000** |
| 3 | 75,000 | 1.00 | 75,000 | |
|  | 7,500 | 1.20 | 9,000 | |
|  | 7,500 | 1.30 | 9,750 | **$93,750** |

Checks:  
- Y2: \(75{,}000 + 7{,}500 \times 1.20 = 75{,}000 + 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$84{,}000}\)  
- Y3: \(75{,}000 + 9{,}000 + 7{,}500 \times 1.30 = 75{,}000 + 9{,}000 + 9{,}750 = \mathbf{\$93{,}750}\)

**(c) LIFO reserve and annual adjustment**

| Date | FIFO EI | DV LIFO EI | LIFO reserve (FIFO − LIFO) | Adjustment to reserve |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $75,000 | $75,000 | $0 | $0 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 99,000 | 84,000 | **15,000** | **+15,000** |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 117,000 | 93,750 | **23,250** | **+8,250** |

Checks: \(99{,}000 - 84{,}000 = 15{,}000\); \(117{,}000 - 93{,}750 = 23{,}250\); \(23{,}250 - 15{,}000 = 8{,}250\).

**(d) Period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 2 — establish / increase LIFO reserve*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 15,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 15,000 |
| *Adjust LIFO reserve (FIFO $99,000 − DV LIFO $84,000)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3 — further increase LIFO reserve*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 8,250 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 8,250 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve by $8,250 ($23,250 − $15,000)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,250 = Cr 8,250. **Balanced.**

(No Year 1 reserve entry: base-year FIFO = base-year LIFO.)

**(e) Balance-sheet presentation — December 31, Year 3**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory at FIFO | $117,000 |
| Less: Allowance to reduce FIFO inventory to LIFO basis | (23,250) |
| **Inventory (dollar-value LIFO)** | **$93,750** |

**Disclosure:** Inventories are stated on a **dollar-value LIFO** basis; the **LIFO reserve** (allowance balance) is **$23,250**. Users may add the reserve to approximate FIFO inventory for cross-company ratio analysis.

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the engine of dollar-value LIFO: divide by the year index to strip price change, build **layers in base-year dollars**, then reprice each layer with **its own** originating index. Purchases stay on FIFO until year-end; the conversion JE hits **COGS** and the **inventory valuation allowance**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Clearwater Provisions twin
**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: four-step DV LIFO subsequent measurement schedule; LIFO reserve rollforward; period-end adjusting JEs  
**Scenario:**  
**Clearwater Provisions Inc.** uses FIFO for internal records and **dollar-value LIFO** (one pool) for external reporting and tax. Year 1 is the base year.

| Year | Ending inventory at FIFO | Price index |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $40,000 | 1.00 |
| Year 2 | $58,800 | 1.20 |
| Year 3 | $71,500 | 1.30 |

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **dollar-value LIFO ending inventory** for each year with a full layer schedule (all four steps).  
(b) Compute the LIFO reserve and the annual adjustment to the reserve.  
(c) Prepare the December 31, Year 2 and Year 3 adjusting entries to the LIFO allowance.  
(d) State inventory **net** of allowance at December 31, Year 3.

**Answer key:**

**(a) DV LIFO subsequent measurement schedule**

**Base-year dollars**  
- Year 1: \(40{,}000 / 1.00 = \mathbf{\$40{,}000}\)  
- Year 2: \(58{,}800 / 1.20 = \mathbf{\$49{,}000}\) → new layer \(49{,}000 - 40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}\)  
- Year 3: \(71{,}500 / 1.30 = \mathbf{\$55{,}000}\) → new layer \(55{,}000 - 49{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\)

| Year | Layers (base-year $) | × Index | = DV LIFO layers | **Ending DV LIFO** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $40,000 | 1.00 | $40,000 | **$40,000** |
| 2 | 40,000 | 1.00 | 40,000 | |
|  | 9,000 | 1.20 | 10,800 | **$50,800** |
| 3 | 40,000 | 1.00 | 40,000 | |
|  | 9,000 | 1.20 | 10,800 | |
|  | 6,000 | 1.30 | 7,800 | **$58,600** |

Checks: Y2 \(40{,}000 + 9{,}000 \times 1.20 = 40{,}000 + 10{,}800 = 50{,}800\); Y3 \(50{,}800 + 6{,}000 \times 1.30 = 50{,}800 + 7{,}800 = \mathbf{\$58{,}600}\).

**(b) Reserve schedule**

| Date | FIFO | DV LIFO | Reserve | Adjustment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec. 31, Y1 | $40,000 | $40,000 | $0 | $0 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 58,800 | 50,800 | **8,000** | **+8,000** |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 71,500 | 58,600 | **12,900** | **+4,900** |

Checks: \(58{,}800 - 50{,}800 = 8{,}000\); \(71{,}500 - 58{,}600 = 12{,}900\); \(12{,}900 - 8{,}000 = 4{,}900\).

**(c) Adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 8,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 8,000 |
| *Establish / increase LIFO reserve* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr 8,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 4,900 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 4,900 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve by $4,900* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,900 = Cr 4,900. **Balanced.**

**(d) Net inventory December 31, Year 3**  
FIFO \(71{,}500\) − allowance \(12{,}900\) = **$58,600** (equals DV LIFO EI).

**Key insight:** Changing every dollar amount and keeping the same four-step structure still isolates **quantity** change (base-year dollars) from **price** change (indices). Only layers that exist contribute to the total—and only at **their** originating indices.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Harborline Sporting: growth then layer liquidation / reserve settlement
**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement with multi-year layers then **liquidation** (newest layers drop first); period-end JE when LIFO reserve **decreases** (layer settlement / COGS credit)  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Sporting Goods LLC** uses FIFO for internal books and **dollar-value LIFO** (single pool) for external reporting and tax. Base year is Year 1.

| Year | Ending inventory at FIFO | Price index |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $120,000 | 1.00 |
| Year 2 | $151,200 | 1.20 |
| Year 3 | $175,500 | 1.30 |
| Year 4 | $137,500 | 1.25 |

**Required:**  
(a) Compute dollar-value LIFO ending inventory for **each** of Years 1–4. Explicitly identify which layers are **liquidated** in Year 4 and which index (if any) is unused.  
(b) Prepare a LIFO reserve rollforward (ending reserve and adjustment) for Years 1–4.  
(c) Prepare the **December 31, Year 4** adjusting entry only.  
(d) Briefly explain why Year 4’s index **1.25** does not enter the Year 4 DV LIFO total, and how inventory should be **presented/classified** at Year 4 year-end.

**Answer key:**

**(a) DV LIFO by year (subsequent measurement with liquidation)**

**Year 1**  
Base-year $: \(120{,}000 / 1.00 = 120{,}000\)  
DV LIFO: \(120{,}000 \times 1.00 = \mathbf{\$120{,}000}\)

**Year 2**  
Base-year $: \(151{,}200 / 1.20 = 126{,}000\)  
New layer: \(126{,}000 - 120{,}000 = 6{,}000\)  
DV LIFO: \(120{,}000 \times 1.00 + 6{,}000 \times 1.20 = 120{,}000 + 7{,}200 = \mathbf{\$127{,}200}\)

**Year 3**  
Base-year $: \(175{,}500 / 1.30 = 135{,}000\)  
New layer: \(135{,}000 - 126{,}000 = 9{,}000\)  
DV LIFO: \(120{,}000 + 7{,}200 + 9{,}000 \times 1.30 = 120{,}000 + 7{,}200 + 11{,}700 = \mathbf{\$138{,}900}\)

**Year 4 — layer liquidation (settlement of newer layers)**  
Base-year $: \(137{,}500 / 1.25 = 110{,}000\)  
Decline in base-year dollars: \(135{,}000 - 110{,}000 = \mathbf{\$25{,}000}\)  

Under LIFO, **newest layers liquidate first**:  
1. Entire **Year 3** layer of **$9,000** base-year $ — **liquidated**  
2. Entire **Year 2** layer of **$6,000** base-year $ — **liquidated**  
3. Base (Year 1) layer reduced by remaining **$10,000**: \(120{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$110{,}000}\) remaining  

Remaining layer restated:  
\(110{,}000 \times 1.00 = \mathbf{\$110{,}000}\) DV LIFO ending inventory  

Year 4 index **1.25 is not used** because **no new Year 4 layer** was created.

**(b) LIFO reserve rollforward**

| Year | FIFO EI | DV LIFO EI | Ending reserve | Adjustment |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $120,000 | $120,000 | $0 | — |
| 2 | 151,200 | 127,200 | **24,000** | **+24,000** |
| 3 | 175,500 | 138,900 | **36,600** | **+12,600** |
| 4 | 137,500 | 110,000 | **27,500** | **−9,100** |

Rollforward: \(0 + 24{,}000 = 24{,}000\); \(24{,}000 + 12{,}600 = 36{,}600\); \(36{,}600 - 9{,}100 = 27{,}500\).  
Checks: \(151{,}200 - 127{,}200 = 24{,}000\); \(175{,}500 - 138{,}900 = 36{,}600\); \(137{,}500 - 110{,}000 = 27{,}500\).

**(c) December 31, Year 4 adjusting JE (reserve decreases — settlement effect on COGS)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis | 9,100 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 9,100 |
| *Decrease LIFO reserve ($36,600 → $27,500); layer liquidation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,100 = Cr 9,100. **Balanced.**

*Context (optional):*  
- Dec. 31, Y2: Dr COGS 24,000 / Cr Allowance 24,000  
- Dec. 31, Y3: Dr COGS 12,600 / Cr Allowance 12,600  

**(d) Unused index and presentation**  
- **No new layer → no Year 4 index in the total.** Remaining inventory is entirely the reduced **base layer**, restated at the **base index 1.00**. Price change in Year 4 does not reprice older layers.  
- **Presentation:** Inventory is a **current asset** at dollar-value LIFO **$110,000**, often shown as FIFO \(137{,}500\) less valuation allowance **$27,500**. Disclose dollar-value LIFO method and LIFO reserve. Pooling still reduces liquidation risk vs unit LIFO (need a decline in the **whole pool** in base-year dollars), but a large real quantity drop (as in Year 4) **does** liquidate layers.

**Key insight:** Liquidation is diagnosed only after restating to **base-year dollars**. Newest layers settle first; survivors keep **original indices**. A **decrease** in the LIFO reserve **reduces COGS** (debit allowance, credit COGS)—the mirror image of the growth-year entries.

### Q4 — MC — Method application and classification under dollar-value LIFO
**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Classification of the LIFO allowance; applying the four-step DV LIFO schedule when base-year dollars are flat  

**Question 1:**  
A company maintains FIFO inventory records during the year and converts to dollar-value LIFO only at year-end. The credit balance in **Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO Basis** is best classified as:  
- A) A current liability for unpaid inventory costs  
- B) A valuation allowance (contra-asset) deducted from inventory  
- C) A component of accumulated other comprehensive income  
- D) Unearned revenue related to LIFO conformity  

**Answer:** **B.** The allowance converts FIFO carrying amounts to LIFO for external reporting; it is a **contra-inventory valuation** account, not a liability, AOCI item, or unearned revenue.

**Question 2:**  
Pinnacle Gear Co. reports the following for its first three years under dollar-value LIFO (Year 1 = base year):

| Year | FIFO ending inventory | Index |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $60,000 | 1.00 |
| 2 | $78,000 | 1.20 |
| 3 | $84,500 | 1.30 |

What is **Year 3** ending inventory under the dollar-value LIFO method?  
- A) $60,000  
- B) $65,000  
- C) $66,000  
- D) $84,500  

**Answer:** **C. $66,000.**  
- Year 1: base \(60{,}000\); DV LIFO \(60{,}000\).  
- Year 2: base \(78{,}000 / 1.20 = 65{,}000\) → layers \(60{,}000 \times 1.00 + 5{,}000 \times 1.20 = 60{,}000 + 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$66{,}000}\).  
- Year 3: base \(84{,}500 / 1.30 = 65{,}000\) → **no new layer** (base-year dollars unchanged from Year 2).  
- Year 3 DV LIFO remains \(60{,}000 + 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$66{,}000}\).  
(Year 3 index **1.30 is not applied** because no Year 3 layer exists. FIFO \(84{,}500\) is not the LIFO amount.)
