# Agent 78 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-2

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for depreciation in partial periods  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize PPE at acquisition cost when placed in service mid-period
- **Partial-period proration (exact fractional months):** apply months-in-service / 12 to time-based annual depreciation (SL, SYD layers, DDB first-year fraction)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year depreciation schedules that total exactly depreciable cost over the full service life (including stub final year)
- **Policy conventions:** full-year (beg/end of period), half-year, full-month — GAAP-acceptable if not materially different from exact fractional; applied consistently
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr Depreciation Expense / Cr Accumulated Depreciation for the partial or full year amount
- **Disposal with partial period:** update depreciation through disposal date, then remove cost and accumulated depreciation; gain/loss = proceeds − carrying amount
- **Units-of-production:** automatically adjusts for partial periods via actual output
- **Classification / presentation:** Depreciation Expense on the income statement (operating); Accumulated Depreciation contra-asset on the balance sheet; carrying amount = cost − AD

### Q1 — CORE — Mid-year acquisition: initial recognition, partial-period schedules (SL, SYD, DDB, UOP), year-end JE
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of PPE acquired mid-period; exact fractional-period depreciation schedules under multiple methods; period-end adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeway Fabrication LLC** (calendar-year reporter) places a CNC router into service on **April 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Acquisition cost (cash) | **$50,000** |
| Estimated residual value | **$5,000** |
| Estimated useful life | **5 years** |
| Estimated total productive output | **90,000 machine-hours** |
| Actual machine-hours, Year 1 | **8,000** |
| Actual machine-hours, Year 2 | **20,000** |
| Fiscal year-end | December 31 |
| Partial-period policy | **Exact fractional months** (prorate time-based methods) |

Depreciable base = $50,000 − $5,000 = **$45,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **April 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry for the purchase.  
b. Compute **Year 1** and **Year 2** depreciation under (1) **straight-line**, (2) **sum-of-the-years’-digits**, (3) **double-declining-balance**, and (4) **units-of-production**. Prorate partial periods for time-based methods.  
c. Prepare a complete **straight-line subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–6 (expense, AD, carrying amount each year-end). Confirm total expense = depreciable cost.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1** adjusting entry for depreciation under the **straight-line** method.  
e. Briefly state how depreciation expense and the related asset appear on the **Year 1 financial statements**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. April 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—CNC Router | 50,000 | |
| Cash | | 50,000 |
| *Place CNC router in service at acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 50,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Year 1 and Year 2 depreciation (exact fractional periods)**

**(1) Straight-line**  
Annual SL = $45,000 / 5 = **$9,000**  
Year 1 (Apr 1–Dec 31 = **9/12**): $9,000 × 9/12 = **$6,750**  
Year 2 (full year): **$9,000**

**(2) Sum-of-the-years’-digits**  
Digits sum = 5+4+3+2+1 = **15**  
Layers of annual full-year amounts, each allocated 9/12 to the year that begins Apr 1 and 3/12 to the following calendar year:

| Layer | Fraction | Full-year layer | 9/12 portion | 3/12 portion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5/15 | $15,000 | $11,250 (Y1) | $3,750 (Y2) |
| 2 | 4/15 | 12,000 | 9,000 (Y2) | 3,000 (Y3) |
| 3 | 3/15 | 9,000 | 6,750 (Y3) | 2,250 (Y4) |
| 4 | 2/15 | 6,000 | 4,500 (Y4) | 1,500 (Y5) |
| 5 | 1/15 | 3,000 | 2,250 (Y5) | 750 (Y6) |

**Year 1 SYD** = $11,250  
**Year 2 SYD** = $3,750 + $9,000 = **$12,750**

**(3) Double-declining-balance**  
DDB rate = 2 × (1/5) = **40%**  
Year 1: $50,000 × 40% × 9/12 = **$15,000**  
Carrying amount end of Year 1 = $50,000 − $15,000 = **$35,000**  
Year 2: $35,000 × 40% = **$14,000**  
Carrying amount end of Year 2 = $35,000 − $14,000 = **$21,000**

**(4) Units-of-production** (automatically partial)  
Rate = $45,000 / 90,000 hours = **$0.50 per hour**  
Year 1: 8,000 × $0.50 = **$4,000**  
Year 2: 20,000 × $0.50 = **$10,000**

**Summary — Year 1 / Year 2 depreciation expense**

| Method | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Straight-line | $6,750 | $9,000 |
| Sum-of-the-years’-digits | 11,250 | 12,750 |
| Double-declining-balance | 15,000 | 14,000 |
| Units-of-production | 4,000 | 10,000 |

**c. Straight-line subsequent measurement schedule (service life Apr 1 Y1 – Mar 31 Y6)**

| End of period | Depreciable cost | Rate | Partial period | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr 1, Y1 (in service) | — | — | — | — | $0 | $50,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $45,000 | 1/5 | 9/12 | **$6,750** | 6,750 | 43,250 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 15,750 | 34,250 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 24,750 | 25,250 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 33,750 | 16,250 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 42,750 | 7,250 |
| Dec 31, Y6 | 45,000 | 1/5 | 3/12 | **2,250** | 45,000 | **5,000** residual |

**Total depreciation over life** = $6,750 + $9,000×4 + $2,250 = $6,750 + $36,000 + $2,250 = **$45,000** = depreciable cost. ✓  
Exactly **5 full years** of SL depreciation are recorded across Years 1–6.

**d. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (straight-line)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC Router | 6,750 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC Router | | 6,750 |
| *Partial-year SL: $9,000 × 9/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,750. **Balanced.**

**e. Classification / presentation (Year 1)**  
- **Income statement:** Depreciation Expense **$6,750** (operating expense, unless capitalized into inventory under production costing).  
- **Balance sheet:** Equipment **$50,000** less Accumulated Depreciation **$6,750** → **carrying amount $43,250** (noncurrent asset, net of contra).

**Key insight:** When an asset is placed in service mid-period, **initial recognition** records the full acquisition cost; **depreciation** for time-based methods is **prorated** for months in service. Accelerated methods (SYD, DDB) apply the fraction to the **first layer / first DDB year**, then roll full years until the stub final period. UOP needs no separate proration—actual output does it. Total SL over the life still equals **depreciable cost**, and ending BV equals **residual**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Mid-period purchase twin (different dates/amounts); SL schedule + DDB + half-year vs exact
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition JE; exact fractional SL and DDB schedules; compare half-year convention to exact months  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Packaging Inc.** (calendar year) purchases packaging equipment on **October 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Acquisition cost | **$84,000** |
| Residual value | **$4,000** |
| Useful life | **5 years** |
| Placed in service | **October 1, Year 1** |
| Year-end | December 31 |

Depreciable base = $84,000 − $4,000 = **$80,000**.  
Annual SL = $80,000 / 5 = **$16,000**.  
DDB rate = 2/5 = **40%**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **October 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry.  
b. Under **exact fractional months**, prepare the **straight-line subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–6 and the **December 31, Year 1** depreciation adjusting entry.  
c. Compute **Year 1** and **Year 2** depreciation under **double-declining-balance** (exact fractional first year).  
d. Recompute **Year 1 through Year 6** SL depreciation under the **half-year convention** (ignore actual Oct 1 date). Compare Year 1 amounts: exact vs half-year.  
e. On **December 31, Year 1**, under half-year convention, record the adjusting entry.

**Answer key:**  

**a. October 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—Packaging Line | 84,000 | |
| Cash | | 84,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 84,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Exact fractional months — SL schedule**

Months in Year 1 = Oct–Dec = **3/12**.  
Service ends Sep 30, Year 6 → Year 6 = **9/12**.

| End of period | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Oct 1, Y1 | — | $0 | $84,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | **$4,000** ($16,000 × 3/12) | 4,000 | 80,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | **16,000** | 20,000 | 64,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | **16,000** | 36,000 | 48,000 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | **16,000** | 52,000 | 32,000 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | **16,000** | 68,000 | 16,000 |
| Dec 31, Y6 | **$12,000** ($16,000 × 9/12) | **80,000** | **4,000** residual |

**Total expense** = $4,000 + $16,000×4 + $12,000 = **$80,000**. ✓

*December 31, Year 1 — exact fractional SL*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Line | 4,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Line | | 4,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,000. **Balanced.**

**c. DDB (exact fractional Year 1)**

Year 1: $84,000 × 40% × 3/12 = **$8,400**  
BV end Y1 = $84,000 − $8,400 = **$75,600**  
Year 2: $75,600 × 40% = **$30,240**  
BV end Y2 = $75,600 − $30,240 = **$45,360**

**d. Half-year convention — SL over life**

Under half-year, Year of acquisition and Year of retirement each get **½** of annual SL regardless of Oct 1:

| Year | Half-year convention SL | Exact fractional SL |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | **$8,000** (½ × $16,000) | **$4,000** |
| 2 | 16,000 | 16,000 |
| 3 | 16,000 | 16,000 |
| 4 | 16,000 | 16,000 |
| 5 | 16,000 | 16,000 |
| 6 | **$8,000** (½ × $16,000) | **$12,000** |
| **Total** | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |

Year 1 difference: half-year records **$4,000 more** than exact months ($8,000 vs $4,000) because half-year ignores the late-year purchase date. Both conventions still depreciate exactly **$80,000** over the life.

**e. December 31, Year 1 — half-year convention adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Line | 8,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Line | | 8,000 |
| *Half-year convention: ½ × $16,000 annual SL* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** **Number-variant twin** of Q1 with a late-year acquisition. Exact months → small Year-1 expense (3/12); **half-year convention** deliberately records ½ year in Year 1 and ½ year in the retirement year. Policy conventions are acceptable under GAAP when not **materially different** from exact fractional results and are applied **consistently**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial-period update through disposal + disposal JE; policy conventions comparison
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Period-end / through-date partial depreciation before disposal; disposal JE (gain/loss); full-year and full-month conventions classification  
**Scenario:**  
**Northshore Tools Co.** owns a delivery van purchased and placed in service on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cost of delivery van | **$39,000** |
| Residual value | **$3,000** |
| Useful life | **6 years** |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line |
| Accumulated depreciation, January 1, Year 4 | **$18,000** |
| Disposal date | **September 1, Year 4** |
| Cash proceeds on sale | **$14,200** |
| Partial-period policy | **Exact fractional months** through disposal date |
| Fiscal year-end | December 31 |

Annual SL = ($39,000 − $3,000) / 6 = **$6,000**.  
Check AD at Jan 1, Year 4: 3 full years × $6,000 = **$18,000**. ✓

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **September 1, Year 4** entry to **update depreciation** through the disposal date (8 months).  
b. Compute the **carrying amount** at September 1 and the **gain or loss** on disposal.  
c. Prepare the **September 1 disposal journal entry**. Show Dr = Cr.  
d. Independently, assume instead a **different** asset acquired on **March 8, Year 1** (cost $39,000, residual $3,000, 6-year SL, annual $6,000). Without computing exact days, state **Year 1** depreciation under each policy convention: (1) full-year—beginning of period, (2) full-year—end of period, (3) half-year, (4) full-month.  
e. Classify: where is the gain/loss on disposal reported, and is Accumulated Depreciation a liability?

**Answer key:**  

**a. September 1, Year 4 — update depreciation through disposal**  
8/12 × $6,000 = **$4,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Delivery Van | 4,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Van | | 4,000 |
| *Partial-year SL through Sep 1: $6,000 × 8/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Carrying amount and gain/loss**  
AD at disposal = $18,000 + $4,000 = **$22,000**  
Carrying amount = $39,000 − $22,000 = **$17,000**  
Proceeds = **$14,200**  
**Loss on disposal** = $17,000 − $14,200 = **$2,800**

**c. September 1, Year 4 — disposal JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 14,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Van | 22,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of PPE | 2,800 | |
| Equipment—Delivery Van | | 39,000 |
| *Remove cost and AD; recognize loss (proceeds < BV)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,200 + 22,000 + 2,800 = **39,000**; Cr = **39,000**. **Balanced.**

**d. Policy conventions — Year 1 only (Mar 8 acquisition; annual SL $6,000)**

| Convention | Year 1 depreciation | Reasoning |
|---|---:|---|
| (1) Full-year — **beginning of period** | **$0** | Assets purchased during the year are **not** depreciated that year |
| (2) Full-year — **end of period** | **$6,000** | Assets purchased during the year are depreciated a **full year** |
| (3) Half-year | **$3,000** | ½ × $6,000 in year of purchase regardless of March 8 |
| (4) Full-month | **$5,000** (10/12 × $6,000) | Full month in March (month of purchase); 10 months Mar–Dec |

(Exact fractional from March 8 is nearly the same as full-month for practical purposes; textbook full-month = full dep in purchase month, none in disposal month.)

**e. Classification / presentation**  
- **Loss on Disposal of PPE $2,800** is reported on the **income statement** (typically other expense / not operating revenue).  
- **Accumulated Depreciation** is a **contra-asset** (credit balance reducing PPE), **not** a liability.  
- After disposal, both the van’s cost and its AD are **removed**; neither remains on the balance sheet.

**Key insight:** Before any mid-period **disposal**, always **update depreciation** for the fractional period used. The disposal entry clears cost and AD and plugs **gain or loss** so the entry balances. Policy conventions (full-year beg/end, half-year, full-month) are practical alternatives to exact days when applied consistently and results are not materially different.

### Q4 — MC — Partial-period method / convention classification
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Identify correct partial-period depreciation amount under a stated convention  
**Question:**  
On **November 12, Year 1**, Cedar Peak Co. purchased equipment for **$120,000**. Residual value **$12,000**; useful life **6 years**; straight-line. Cedar Peak’s policy is the **half-year convention**. What is **Year 1 depreciation expense**?

- A) $0  
- B) $9,000  
- C) $3,000  
- D) $18,000  

**Answer:** **B. $9,000.**  
Annual SL = ($120,000 − $12,000) / 6 = **$18,000**. Half-year convention → Year 1 = ½ × $18,000 = **$9,000**, regardless of the November 12 purchase date.  
- A would be full-year—beginning of period (no dep in year of purchase).  
- C is 2/12 × $18,000 (approximate exact months Nov–Dec) — not half-year.  
- D is a full year of SL — full-year—end of period, not half-year.

### Q5 — MC — SYD partial-period layer allocation
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Prorate first SYD layer when asset is acquired mid-year  
**Question:**  
Equipment cost **$65,000**, residual **$5,000**, life **4 years**, purchased **July 1, Year 1**. Company uses **sum-of-the-years’-digits** and **exact fractional periods**. What is **Year 1 depreciation**?

- A) $24,000  
- B) $12,000  
- C) $15,000  
- D) $6,000  

**Answer:** **B. $12,000.**  
Depreciable base = $60,000; digits sum = 10; first full layer = 4/10 × $60,000 = **$24,000**. July 1 → **6/12** of first layer in Year 1: $24,000 × 6/12 = **$12,000**.  
- A is the full first layer (no proration).  
- C would be if life were 5 years (5/15 × 60,000 × 6/12 = 10,000 — not listed) or other miscalc.  
- D is 6/12 of SL annual ($15,000 × 6/12) or half of wrong base.
