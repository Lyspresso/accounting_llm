# Agent 8 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-7

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of trading securities (TS) purchased between interest dates (cost + accrued interest)
- Interest revenue (cash interest, reverse receivable, earned period only)
- Period-end Fair Value Adjustment (FVA) to FV-NI (unrealized holding G/L in net income)
- Financial statement presentation of TS (current assets; other income)
- Disposal under **period-end-only** method: gain/loss = cash − amortized cost; **no** sale-date FVA
- Year-end cleanup of FVA after securities sold during the year
- Multi-security subsequent measurement / FVA rollforward schedule
- Fair value option election on HTM debt (FV-NI, similar mechanics to TS)
- Distinguishing LO 14-7 (period-end only) from LO 14-2 (sale-date and period-end FVA)

### Q1 — CORE — TS purchase between interest dates through sale (period-end-only FVA)

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On October 1 of Year 1, Cedar Ridge Capital purchased at par $50,000 face of Harborline Corp. 6% bonds (dated July 1 of Year 1) that mature on June 30 of Year 8. Cash interest is paid semiannually on June 30 and December 31. Cedar Ridge classifies the investment as **trading securities (TS)** and applies **FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end** (Appendix 14A / LO 14-7). Cedar Ridge’s accounting period ends December 31.

- Fair value of the Harborline bonds on December 31 of Year 1: **$47,500**
- On March 15 of Year 2, Cedar Ridge sells the entire investment for **$48,200** cash (no accrued interest is transferred separately; ignore brokerage).
- Cedar Ridge holds **no** other TS during Year 2.

**Required:**  
a. Record the entry to purchase the TS on October 1 of Year 1.  
b. Record the receipt of cash interest on December 31 of Year 1.  
c. Prepare the year-end FVA analysis and record the entry to adjust TS to fair value on December 31 of Year 1.  
d. Report the investment on the December 31, Year 1 balance sheet and the Year 1 income statement effects related to this investment.  
e. Record the sale on March 15 of Year 2 under the **period-end-only** method (gain or loss = cash received − amortized cost; do **not** adjust FVA at the sale date).  
f. Record the December 31 of Year 2 FVA entry (no other TS remain).  
g. Reconcile the total gain (loss) recognized over the ownership period to cash − amortized cost.

**Answer key:**

**a. October 1, Year 1 — Purchase (between interest dates)**  
Accrued interest paid (July 1–Sept 30 = 3 months):  
$50,000 × 0.06 × 3/12 = **$750**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Harborline Bonds | 50,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 750 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 50,750 |

*Check:* Dr 50,750 = Cr 50,750.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Cash interest**  
Semiannual cash: $50,000 × 0.06 × 6/12 = **$1,500**  
Interest revenue earned (Oct 1–Dec 31 = **3 months**):  
$50,000 × 0.06 × 3/12 = **$750**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,500 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest Receivable | | 750 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest Revenue | | 750 |

*Check:* Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVA (period-end only)**

| TS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Harborline bonds | $50,000 | $47,500 | $(2,500) |
| Existing FVA balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$(2,500)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 2,500 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 2,500 |

*Check:* Dr 2,500 = Cr 2,500. Carrying amount of TS = $50,000 − $2,500 FVA credit = **$47,500**.

**d. Financial statement presentation — December 31, Year 1**

**Balance sheet (excerpt)**  
Current assets  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in trading securities ……………… **$47,500**

**Income statement (excerpt) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
Other revenues (expenses)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest revenue ………………………………… **$750**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Unrealized holding loss on trading securities …… **$(2,500)**

**e. March 15, Year 2 — Sale (period-end-only method)**  
Gain/(loss) = cash received − amortized cost = $48,200 − $50,000 = **$(1,800)** loss.  
**No** FVA entry at sale date.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 48,200 | |
| Loss on Sale of Investment | 1,800 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in TS—Harborline Bonds | | 50,000 |

*Check:* Dr 50,000 = Cr 50,000.

**f. December 31, Year 2 — Eliminate FVA (no remaining TS)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required FVA ending balance | $0 |
| Less existing FVA balance (credit) | (2,500) |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$2,500** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 2,500 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,500 |

*Check:* Dr 2,500 = Cr 2,500. FVA ends at $0.

**g. Ownership-period reconciliation**

| Period | Income effect related to FV / sale |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 unrealized holding loss | $(2,500) |
| Year 2: reverse prior FVA via period-end entry | +2,500 |
| Year 2: realized loss on sale | (1,800) |
| **Net Year 2 income effect** | **+$700** |
| **Total loss over ownership** | **$(1,800)** |

Total: $(2,500) + $700 = **$(1,800)** = cash $48,200 − amortized cost $50,000. ✓

**Key insight:** Under LO 14-7, FVA is **not** touched at the sale date. Realized G/L uses **amortized cost**. Prior-period unrealized G/L remains in the FVA until the **next period-end**, when the FVA is remeasured for remaining holdings (or zeroed if none remain). Net income over the full holding period still equals cash − amortized cost.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — TS purchase, period-end FVA gain, mid-year sale

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin; initial_recognition_JE; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE  
**Scenario:**  
On November 1 of Year 1, Northwind Advisors purchased at par $40,000 face of Summit Ridge Inc. 9% bonds (dated July 1 of Year 1) that mature on June 30 of Year 6. Cash interest is paid semiannually on June 30 and December 31. The investment is classified as **TS**. Northwind adjusts the Fair Value Adjustment account **only at year-end** (not at sale dates). Year-end is December 31.

- Fair value on December 31 of Year 1: **$41,800**
- Sold February 1 of Year 2 for **$41,200** cash
- No other TS in Year 2

**Required:**  
a. Purchase entry (November 1, Year 1).  
b. Interest receipt entry (December 31, Year 1).  
c. Year-end FVA analysis and adjusting entry (December 31, Year 1).  
d. Sale entry (February 1, Year 2) under period-end-only method.  
e. Year-end FVA entry (December 31, Year 2).  
f. Reconcile total gain (loss) over the ownership period.

**Answer key:**

**a. November 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  
Accrued interest (July 1–Oct 31 = 4 months):  
$40,000 × 0.09 × 4/12 = **$1,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Summit Ridge Bonds | 40,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 1,200 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 41,200 |

*Check:* Dr 41,200 = Cr 41,200.

Cash (6 months): $40,000 × 0.09 × 6/12 = **$1,800**  
Interest revenue (Nov–Dec = 2 months): $40,000 × 0.09 × 2/12 = **$600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,800 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest Receivable | | 1,200 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest Revenue | | 600 |

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVA**

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Summit Ridge | $40,000 | $41,800 | $1,800 |

| **Increase (decrease) to FVA** | | | **$1,800** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 1,800 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 1,800 |

**d. February 1, Year 2 — Sale (no sale-date FVA)**  
Gain = $41,200 − $40,000 = **$1,200**

|---|---:|---:|

| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in TS—Summit Ridge Bonds | | 40,000 |

| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 1,800 | |

**f. Ownership-period reconciliation**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 unrealized holding gain | $1,800 |

**Key insight:** Same economics as LO 14-2, different **timing** of FVA: LO 14-7 leaves the year-end FVA alone until the **next period-end**, so Year 2 income mixes (1) reverse/remeasure of FVA and (2) realized G/L based on amortized cost.

### Q3 — CORE — Multi-security FVA schedule after mid-year disposal (period-end only)

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** subsequent_measurement_schedule; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; period_end_adjusting_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
Lakeview Holdings measures its **trading** debt securities at FV-NI and adjusts the **Fair Value Adjustment—TS** account **only at December 31** each year (not on sale dates).  

At January 1 of Year 2, the Fair Value Adjustment—TS account has a **credit balance of $3,200**.

During Year 2:  
- June 12: Sold the entire Crest 4.5% bond position (amortized cost **$35,000**) for cash **$33,800**.  
- No other purchases or sales of TS.

Securities still held at December 31 of Year 2:

| Security | Amortized Cost | Fair Value 12/31/Y2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dune 5% bonds | $25,000 | $24,100 |
| Pier 4% bonds | 60,000 | 61,500 |
| **Total remaining** | **$85,000** | **$85,600** |

**Required:**  
a. Record the June 12 sale of Crest under the period-end-only method.  
b. Prepare the year-end subsequent measurement / FVA schedule for the remaining portfolio.  
c. Record the December 31 of Year 2 FVA adjusting entry.  
d. Show how Investment in trading securities is reported on the December 31, Year 2 balance sheet.  
e. Compute the Year 2 income statement effect from (i) the Crest sale and (ii) the period-end FVA adjustment (ignore interest).

**Answer key:**

**a. June 12, Year 2 — Sale of Crest (no FVA at sale)**  
Loss = $35,000 − $33,800 = **$1,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 33,800 | |
| Loss on Sale of Investment | 1,200 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in TS—Crest Bonds | | 35,000 |

*Check:* Dr 35,000 = Cr 35,000.

**b. Subsequent measurement / FVA schedule — December 31, Year 2**

| Security | Amortized Cost | Fair Value | Required Unrealized G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dune 5% | $25,000 | $24,100 | $(900) |
| Pier 4% | 60,000 | 61,500 | 1,500 |
| **Portfolio total** | **$85,000** | **$85,600** | **$600** |

*Proof:* \(24{,}100 - 25{,}000 = -900\); \(61{,}500 - 60{,}000 = +1{,}500\); net \(= +600\) (not \(800\)).  
Also: \(85{,}600 - 85{,}000 = 600\).

| FVA rollforward | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required FVA ending balance (debit for net unrealized gain) | $600 |
| Existing FVA balance (credit) | (3,200) |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$3,800** |

(Algebra: target debit $600 − current credit $3,200 = need **+$3,800** to FVA, i.e., debit FVA $3,800.)

**c. December 31, Year 2 — Period-end adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 3,800 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 3,800 |

*Check:* Dr 3,800 = Cr 3,800.  
Ending FVA = $(3,200) + $3,800 = **$600 debit**.  
Carrying amount = $85,000 + $600 = **$85,600** = FV. ✓

**d. Balance sheet — December 31, Year 2**  
Current assets  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in trading securities ……………… **$85,600**  
*(amortized cost $85,000 + FVA debit $600; may also parenthetically disclose amortized cost)*

**e. Year 2 income effects (excluding interest)**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Loss on sale of Crest | $(1,200) |
| Unrealized gain—period-end FVA adjustment | 3,800 |
| **Net income effect (FV/sale items)** | **$2,600** |

**Key insight:** The period-end FVA entry is a **portfolio** plug to the **required** ending valuation allowance based on **remaining** securities only. Sold securities drop out of amortized cost and FV columns; their prior-period FVA is cleaned up as part of the same year-end plug (not at the sale date under LO 14-7).

### Q4 — MC — Method choice at sale date (LO 14-7 vs LO 14-2)

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE  
**Question:**  
Mariner Fund classifies certain debt bonds as trading securities and elects the **Appendix 14A** approach: fair value adjustments **only at period-end**. On April 10, Mariner sells a TS bond that had amortized cost of $90,000 and a related year-end Fair Value Adjustment credit of $4,000 from the prior December 31. Cash proceeds are $87,500. Which journal entry (or sequence) is correct on April 10?

- A) Debit Unrealized G/L—Income $1,500; credit FVA $1,500 to bring carrying amount to $87,500; then debit Cash $87,500 and FVA $2,500, credit Investment $90,000 (no separate gain/loss).  
- B) Debit Cash $87,500 and Loss on Sale $2,500; credit Investment in TS $90,000. Leave the FVA account unchanged until December 31.  
- C) Debit Cash $87,500 and Loss on Sale $6,500; credit Investment $90,000 and FVA $4,000.  
- D) Debit Cash $87,500; credit Investment $87,500. Recognize no gain or loss until year-end.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-7 (period-end-only), the sale-date entry records **realized** G/L as cash minus **amortized cost** and does **not** remeasure or eliminate FVA at the sale date. FVA is remeasured (including elimination of amounts related to sold securities) **only at period-end**.  
- A describes the LO 14-2 sale-date FVA approach (adjust to FV then remove investment and FVA with no separate G/L).  
- C incorrectly removes FVA at sale and double-counts economics.  
- D fails to remove the full amortized cost of the investment and ignores the realized loss.

### Q5 — CORE — Fair value option on HTM debt (period-end FV-NI)

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** period_end_adjusting_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure; initial_recognition_JE  
**Scenario:**  
On July 1 of Year 1, Prairie Ledger Co. purchased at par $75,000 face of Redfern 5% bonds that mature on June 30 five years later. Interest is paid semiannually on June 30 and December 31. Prairie classifies the bonds as **held-to-maturity (HTM)** but, on the acquisition date, **irrevocably elects the fair value option** for this investment (ASC 825). Prairie’s year-end is December 31. Assume all interest entries for Year 1 have already been recorded correctly.

Fair values:  
- December 31, Year 1: **$76,800**  
- December 31, Year 2: **$74,250** (still holds the entire position)

**Required:**  
a. Record the July 1, Year 1 purchase entry.  
b. Record the December 31, Year 1 fair value adjusting entry.  
c. Record the December 31, Year 2 fair value adjusting entry.  
d. Briefly explain (i) why the fair value option is unavailable for securities already classified as TS and (ii) one disclosure implication of the election.

**Answer key:**

**a. July 1, Year 1 — Purchase at par (on interest date; no accrued interest)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Redfern Bonds (or Investment—Fair Value Option) | 75,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 75,000 |

*Check:* Dr 75,000 = Cr 75,000.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Adjust to FV through NI**  
Unrealized holding gain = $76,800 − $75,000 = **$1,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option | 1,800 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 1,800 |

*Check:* Dr 1,800 = Cr 1,800. Carrying amount = $76,800.

**c. December 31, Year 2 — Remeasure FVA**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required FVA (FV $74,250 − AC $75,000) | $(750) credit |
| Existing FVA (debit) | 1,800 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$(2,550)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 2,550 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option | | 2,550 |

*Check:* Dr 2,550 = Cr 2,550.  
Ending FVA = $1,800 − $2,550 = **$(750)** credit.  
Carrying amount = $75,000 − $750 = **$74,250**. ✓

**d. Explanation**  
(i) TS are **already** measured at FV-NI; the fair value option is redundant for trading securities. It is useful for investments that would otherwise be at amortized cost (e.g., HTM) or FV-OCI (AFS).  
(ii) The election is made **instrument-by-instrument** at acquisition, is **generally irrevocable**, and requires disclosure of the fair value option election (ASC 825-10-50-28), including the reasons for electing and the carrying amounts / fair values of elected instruments.

**Key insight:** Fair value option accounting under LO 14-7 mirrors TS FV-NI: interest in NI, unrealized G/L in NI via FVA at **period-end**, investment presented at fair value.
