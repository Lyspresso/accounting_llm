# Agent 26 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-1

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Classifying Differences Between GAAP Income and Taxable Income  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** initial_recognition_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Book-to-tax differences: **taxable temporary**, **deductible temporary**, and **permanent** (nontaxable / nondeductible)
- Taxable temporary: originates when TI is lower than pretax GAAP (or equal after other items) and **increases future TI relative to GAAP** when it reverses (e.g., prepaid expensed for tax when paid; accelerated tax depreciation; installment receivable)
- Deductible temporary: **decreases future TI relative to GAAP** when it reverses (e.g., warranty accrual; bad debts; deferred revenue taxed on cash receipt)
- Permanent differences **never reverse** (muni bond interest, fines, life insurance premiums/proceeds) — no DTA/DTL
- Balance-sheet view: temporary difference = GAAP carrying amount ≠ tax basis of an asset or liability
- **Initial recognition JEs** for the underlying items that create the differences and the Year-1 **income tax** entry (current payable + DTA/DTL)
- Origin / reversal **schedule** of cumulative temporary differences
- **Settlement / maturity** of temporary differences (warranty paid; prepaid expires) and related period-end tax adjusting JE
- Classification and presentation: current vs deferred tax expense disclosure; DTA/DTL noncurrent

### Q1 — CORE — Cedar Point Fabrication: classify three differences, origin JEs, tax JE, reversal schedule
**LO:** LO 18-1  
**Concept:** Classify taxable temporary, deductible temporary, and permanent differences; record initial recognition JEs and Year-1 income tax entry  
**Scenario:** **Cedar Point Fabrication Inc.** begins operations in **Year 1**. Accounting and tax years both end **December 31**. Enacted tax rate for all years is **25%**. Deferred tax accounts begin at zero.

Amounts treated **identically** for GAAP and tax in Year 1:
- Other revenues: **$480,000**
- Other expenses: **$355,000**

Three book-to-tax differences in Year 1:

1. **Prepaid insurance.** On **December 31, Year 1**, Cedar Point pays **$24,000** cash for insurance coverage for all of Year 2. For GAAP, the payment is capitalized as Prepaid Insurance (asset). For tax, the cost is **deductible when paid**.
2. **Warranty accrual.** On **December 31, Year 1**, Cedar Point records **Accrued Warranty Liability** of **$36,000** (expected to be settled **evenly** over Years 2–4: $12,000 per year). For GAAP, full warranty expense is recognized in Year 1. For tax, warranty costs are deductible only when **paid**.
3. **Municipal bond interest.** Cedar Point earns **$1,200** of interest on municipal bonds in Year 1 (cash received). Interest is recognized in pretax GAAP income but is **never taxable**.

Assume no other book-tax differences. For **Year 2** (parts f–h), pretax GAAP income is **$110,000**. That amount already includes: municipal bond interest of **$1,200**, GAAP insurance expense of **$24,000** (amortization of the Year-1 prepaid), and **no new** warranty expense. In Year 2, Cedar Point **pays $12,000** cash on warranties related to the Year-1 accrual.

**Required:**

(3) Classification: taxable temporary, deductible temporary, nontaxable permanent, or nondeductible permanent?  
(d) Prepare a **cumulative temporary difference schedule** for prepaid insurance and warranty for Year 1 through Year 4 (origin and reversals; ending cumulative difference each year).  
(e) Compute Year 1 current tax payable, deferred tax asset, deferred tax liability, and total income tax expense. Record the **December 31, Year 1** income tax journal entry.  
(f) Record the Year 2 journal entries that **settle/mature** the temporary differences (warranty cash settlement; insurance amortization for GAAP).  
(g) Compute Year 2 taxable income and the December 31, Year 2 income tax journal entry (show DTA/DTL ending balances).  
(h) State how current and deferred tax expense are disclosed for Year 1 and how DTA/DTL are classified on the balance sheet.

Permanent items never reverse and never create deferred taxes. Initial recognition records both the **underlying book-tax item** and the period-end **tax entry** that establishes DTA/DTL.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Northwind Appliances: prepaid, bad debts, fines
**LO:** LO 18-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — classify prepaid (taxable temporary), allowance for doubtful accounts (deductible temporary), and regulatory fines (nondeductible permanent); initial JEs and Year-1 tax entry  
**Scenario:** **Northwind Appliances Co.** is in its **first year** of operations. Year-end is **December 31**. Enacted tax rate is **25%**. Zero beginning deferred tax balances.

Identical for GAAP and tax:
- Revenues: **$350,000**
- Other expenses (excluding items below): **$260,000**

Differences:

1. **Prepaid maintenance.** On December 31, Year 1, Northwind pays **$15,000** for a maintenance contract covering Year 2. GAAP capitalizes Prepaid Maintenance; tax deducts when paid.
2. **Bad debt expense.** GAAP records bad debt expense of **$25,000** via the allowance method on December 31, Year 1 (Allowance for Doubtful Accounts $25,000). ### Q3 — CORE — Multi-item classification grid + depreciation origin schedule + warranty settlement
**LO:** LO 18-1  
**Concept:** Classify a portfolio of common book-tax differences; subsequent measurement schedule for accelerated tax depreciation; period-end settlement JE  
**Scenario:** **Lakeview Medical Devices LLC** has the following **independent** items that may differ for pretax GAAP income and taxable income. The enacted rate is **25%** where amounts matter.

**Part A — Classification grid.** For each item, classify as **taxable temporary**, **deductible temporary**, **permanent (nontaxable)**, **permanent (nondeductible)**, or **no book-tax difference**. Briefly justify using origin/reversal or “never reverses.”

|---:|---|

| 4 | Life insurance proceeds received on officer’s death (GAAP gain) |

GAAP depreciation is straight-line over **4 years** ($20,000 per year). Tax depreciation: Year 1 **$32,000**; Year 2 **$24,000**; Year 3 **$16,000**; Year 4 **$8,000**. Required for Part B:

(b) Compute taxable income and income tax payable each year.

**Part C — Settlement JE.** Lakeview’s Accrued Warranty Liability beginning balance is **$18,000** (tax basis $0). During Year 2 it pays **$7,000** cash on warranties and records **no new** warranty expense. Record the settlement entry and state the effect on the deductible temporary difference and DTA (25%).

| 9 | **No book-tax difference** | Same amount in both measures this year |

### Q4 — MC — Classification of book-tax differences
**LO:** LO 18-1  
**Concept:** Identify taxable temporary vs deductible temporary vs permanent  

**Question 1:**  
On December 31, Year 1, **Summit Trail Outfitters** pays **$40,000** for a 12-month insurance policy covering Year 2. GAAP records Prepaid Insurance; tax deducts the premium when paid. There are no other differences. Relative to pretax GAAP income, how should this item be classified, and what deferred tax account (if any) originates at Dec 31, Year 1?

- A) Permanent nontaxable item; no deferred tax  
- B) Taxable temporary difference; deferred tax **liability**  
- C) Deductible temporary difference; deferred tax **asset**  
- D) Taxable temporary difference; deferred tax **asset**  

**Answer:** **B.** Tax deduction is taken in Year 1 before GAAP expense (Year 2). When the difference reverses, future taxable income is **higher** relative to pretax GAAP → **taxable temporary** → **DTL**. Prepaid asset GAAP basis > tax basis.

**Question 2:**  
Which item creates a **deductible temporary** difference?

- A) Municipal bond interest earned and included in pretax GAAP income  
- B) Product warranty expense accrued for GAAP; deductible for tax when claims are paid  
- C) Tax depreciation in excess of GAAP depreciation in the early years of an asset’s life  
- D) Regulatory fine expense recognized for GAAP  

**Answer:** **B.** Warranty: expense in GAAP before tax → future tax deductions when paid → future TI **lower** than pretax GAAP → **deductible temporary** (DTA).  
A and D are permanent (nontaxable / nondeductible). C is a **taxable** temporary difference.
