# Agent 336 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-9

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for exchange of property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Nonmonetary PPE exchanges** remove the asset given up (cost and accum. dep.), record cash paid/received, record the new asset, and recognize gain/loss subject to commercial-substance rules
- **Commercial substance** = future cash flows (risk, timing, or amount) change significantly because of the exchange → **full gain or loss** recognized
- **Lack of commercial substance** → **full loss** still recognized; **gain** is **fully deferred** if no cash or cash is **paid**, and only **partially recognized** if cash is **received**
- **Partial gain** = \(\frac{\text{Cash received}}{\text{Cash received + FV of other assets received}} \times\) total potential gain; if cash ≥ 25% of exchange FV, full gain is recognized (ASC 845)
- **New asset cost (has CS / loss):** FV of asset given up ± cash (or FV of asset received if more clearly determinable)
- **New asset cost (no CS, gain deferred):** carrying amount of asset given up + cash paid − cash received (adjusted for any partial gain recognized)
- **Period-end / pre-exchange adjusting JE (emphasis):** update depreciation through the exchange date **before** recording the exchange
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** roll forward accum. dep. on the old asset to exchange date and on the new asset after the exchange
- **Disposal / settlement:** later sale or retirement of the asset acquired in the exchange (derecognize cost and AD; gain/loss = proceeds − BV)

### Q1 — CORE — Meridian Fabrication: initial recognition, dep schedule, period-end dep update (emphasis), commercial-substance exchange with loss + cash paid, subsequent dep, later disposal

**LO:** LO 11-9  
**Concept:** Full lifecycle for a **nonmonetary exchange with commercial substance** (here a **loss** after residual-aware SL dep) — initial recognition, subsequent measurement schedule, **period-end adjusting JE** to update AD through exchange date (emphasis), exchange JE, subsequent dep on new asset, later disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Fabrication Co.** (calendar year) acquired a hydraulic press on **January 1, 20X1** for **$90,000** cash. The press has an estimated residual value of **$9,000** and a **6-year** useful life. Meridian uses **straight-line** depreciation and records depreciation to the nearest month.

On **September 30, 20X4**, Meridian exchanges the press (fair value **$38,000**) plus **$25,000** cash for a used CNC mill. The fair value of the mill is not more clearly determinable than the fair value of the press. The exchange **has commercial substance**. The mill is placed in service on **October 1, 20X4**, residual value **$3,000**, useful life **5 years**, straight-line.

On **June 30, 20X7**, Meridian sells the CNC mill for **$28,000** cash. Depreciation is updated through the disposal date.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, 20X1 initial recognition** of the hydraulic press.  
(b) Prepare a **subsequent measurement (depreciation) schedule** for the press from acquisition through the exchange date (show annual and partial-year amounts and cumulative AD).  
(c) **(Emphasis)** Record the **September 30, 20X4 period-end / pre-exchange adjusting JE** updating depreciation on the press through the exchange date.  
(d) Compute book value of the press at September 30, 20X4, the **gain or loss** on exchange, and the **cost assigned to the CNC mill**. Record the **exchange JE**. Prove Dr = Cr.  
(e) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for the CNC mill from October 1, 20X4 through June 30, 20X7. Record **December 31, 20X4** and **June 30, 20X7** depreciation adjusting entries.  
(f) Record the **June 30, 20X7 disposal JE**. Compute gain or loss. Prove Dr = Cr.

**Key insight:** Before any exchange, **update depreciation** so BV is current (SL base = cost − residual). Here residual-aware BV ($39,375) exceeds FV ($38,000), so a **$1,375 loss** is fully recognized. Later disposal of the acquired asset is a normal PPE disposal (remove cost and AD; residual gain/loss).

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cascade Transit: loss exchange with cash received (CS and no-CS same), dep schedule, period-end update, subsequent dep

**LO:** LO 11-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — **loss on exchange with cash received** (full loss whether or not commercial substance); initial recognition; subsequent measurement; **period-end adjusting JE** through exchange date; exchange JE; subsequent dep on new asset  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Transit LLC** acquired a passenger bus on **January 1, 20X2** for **$160,000** cash. Residual value **$16,000**; useful life **8 years**; straight-line; depreciation to the nearest month.

On **July 1, 20X5**, Cascade exchanges the bus (fair value **$88,000**) for a used coach and receives **$12,000** cash. The fair value of the coach is not more clearly determinable. Cascade places the coach in service on **July 1, 20X5** (residual **$6,000**, useful life **5 years**, straight-line).

**Required:**  
(a) Record **January 1, 20X2 initial recognition** of the bus.  
(b) Prepare a **depreciation / subsequent measurement schedule** for the bus through July 1, 20X5.  
(c) **(Emphasis)** Record the **July 1, 20X5 adjusting JE** updating depreciation on the bus through the exchange date.  
(d) Compute BV, loss, and cost of the new coach. Record the exchange JE **assuming commercial substance**. Prove Dr = Cr.  
(e) Explain and show the exchange entry if the exchange **lacked commercial substance**.  
(f) Record **December 31, 20X5** depreciation on the new coach and show the 20X5 half-year amount.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 1, 20X2 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—Bus | 160,000 | |
| Cash | | 160,000 |
| *Acquire passenger bus for cash* | | |

**Check:** Dr 160,000 = Cr 160,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule — bus**

Annual depreciation = \((160{,}000 - 16{,}000) / 8 = \mathbf{\$18{,}000}\) per year.

| Period | Dep. expense | Cumulative AD | Book value EOP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 20X2 (full year) | 18,000 | 18,000 | 142,000 |
| 20X3 (full year) | 18,000 | 36,000 | 124,000 |
| 20X4 (full year) | 18,000 | 54,000 | 106,000 |
| Jan 1–Jun 30, 20X5 (6/12) | 9,000 | 63,000 | 97,000 |
| **At exchange 7/1/20X5** | | **63,000** | **97,000** |

**(c) July 1, 20X5 — period-end / pre-exchange adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Bus | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Bus | | 9,000 |
| *Update AD through exchange date* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**  
BV at exchange = \(160{,}000 - 63{,}000 = \mathbf{\$97{,}000}\).

**(d) Exchange — loss, cash received, commercial substance**

- FV of bus given up: **$88,000**  
- BV: **$97,000**  
- **Loss** = \(97{,}000 - 88{,}000 = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}\) (fully recognized)  
- Cost of new coach = FV given up − cash received = \(88{,}000 - 12{,}000 = \mathbf{\$76{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—Coach | 76,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Bus | 63,000 | |
| Cash | 12,000 | |
| Loss on Asset Exchange | 9,000 | |
| Equipment—Bus | | 160,000 |
| *Nonmonetary exchange with loss and commercial substance* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(76{,}000 + 63{,}000 + 12{,}000 + 9{,}000 = 160{,}000\); Cr 160,000. **Balanced.**

**(e) Same exchange lacking commercial substance**

The entry is **identical** to part (d). **Losses are fully recognized whether or not the exchange has commercial substance** (avoids capitalizing the new asset above fair value). New coach still **$76,000**; loss still **$9,000**.

**(f) Subsequent measurement — coach; Dec 31, 20X5 period-end AJ**

Annual depreciation = \((76{,}000 - 6{,}000) / 5 = \mathbf{\$14{,}000}\).  
20X5 half-year (Jul 1–Dec 31): \(14{,}000 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Coach | 7,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Coach | | 7,000 |
| *Partial-year dep. on coach acquired July 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,000 = Cr 7,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** A **loss** on a nonmonetary exchange is **never deferred**. Cash received reduces the cost assigned to the new asset (FV given up − cash). Commercial substance does not change the loss entry.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Oakridge Packaging: lack of commercial substance (no gain / deferred gain / partial gain), period-end full-year dep update, contrast with commercial substance

**LO:** LO 11-9  
**Concept:** **Period-end adjusting JE** to update AD on exchange date (year-end), then **gain deferral rules** when the exchange **lacks commercial substance** (no cash, cash paid, cash received / partial gain) vs full recognition with commercial substance  
**Scenario:**  
**Oakridge Packaging Inc.** placed a packaging line in service on **January 1, 20X1** at cost **$250,000** (cash). Residual value **$25,000**; useful life **10 years**; straight-line; full-year convention for this asset (depreciation recorded each **December 31**).

On **December 31, 20X5**, after recording 20X5 depreciation, Oakridge exchanges the packaging line (fair value **$150,000**) under four **separate, independent** cases involving similar equipment (lacks commercial substance unless stated otherwise). Fair value of assets received is determinable when cash is involved as indicated.

**Case data (each independent after the same Dec 31, 20X5 dep update):**  
1. **No cash** — exchanges for similar packaging equipment (lacks CS). FV of old line **$150,000**.  
2. **Cash paid $20,000** — exchanges old line + $20,000 cash for similar equipment; dealer cash price without trade-in would be **$165,000** (use as FV of assets received for potential-gain analysis). Lacks CS.  
3. **Cash received $30,000** — exchanges old line for similar equipment with FV **$120,000** and receives **$30,000** cash. Lacks CS.  
4. **Same as Case 3 but has commercial substance.**

**Required:**  
(a) **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, 20X5 period-end adjusting JE** for depreciation on the packaging line. Compute AD and BV immediately after the entry.  
(b) For **Case 1** (no cash, lacks CS): compute potential gain and record the exchange JE.  
(c) For **Case 2** (cash paid, lacks CS): compute potential gain and record the exchange JE.  
(d) For **Case 3** (cash received, lacks CS): compute total potential gain, **partial gain**, deferred gain, cost of new equipment, and the exchange JE. Prove Dr = Cr.  
(e) For **Case 4** (cash received, has CS): record the exchange JE with **full gain**.  
(f) **Subsequent measurement:** Using the new equipment cost from Case 3 (**$110,000**), residual **$10,000**, remaining useful life **8 years**, straight-line — compute annual depreciation and record the **December 31, 20X6** period-end dep. JE.

**Answer key:**

**(a) December 31, 20X5 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

Annual depreciation = \((250{,}000 - 25{,}000) / 10 = \mathbf{\$22{,}500}\).  
After five years: AD = \(22{,}500 \times 5 = \mathbf{\$112{,}500}\); BV = \(250{,}000 - 112{,}500 = \mathbf{\$137{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Line | 22,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Line | | 22,500 |
| *Period-end dep. for 20X5 (update BV before exchange)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 22,500 = Cr 22,500. **Balanced.**

**(b) Case 1 — gain, no cash, lacks commercial substance**

Potential gain = \(150{,}000 - 137{,}500 = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\).  
**Gain fully deferred** (no cash exchanged). New equipment recorded at **BV of asset given up = $137,500**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—New Packaging Line | 137,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Old Packaging Line | 112,500 | |
| Equipment—Old Packaging Line | | 250,000 |
| *Nonmonetary exchange; gain deferred (no CS, no cash)* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(137{,}500 + 112{,}500 = 250{,}000\); Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Case 2 — gain, cash paid, lacks commercial substance**

FV of assets received (cash price) **$165,000** − cash paid **$20,000** = implied FV of old **$145,000** (or use given FV $150,000 of old if more reliable). Using the problem’s FV of old line **$150,000**:  
Potential gain = \(150{,}000 - 137{,}500 = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\).  
**Gain fully deferred** when cash is **paid** and exchange lacks CS.  
New equipment = BV of old + cash paid = \(137{,}500 + 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$157{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—New Packaging Line | 157,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Old Packaging Line | 112,500 | |
| Cash | | 20,000 |
| Equipment—Old Packaging Line | | 250,000 |
| *Nonmonetary exchange; gain deferred (no CS, cash paid)* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(157{,}500 + 112{,}500 = 270{,}000\); Cr \(20{,}000 + 250{,}000 = 270{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**(d) Case 3 — gain, cash received, lacks commercial substance (partial gain)**

Total potential gain = \(150{,}000 - 137{,}500 = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\).  
Partial gain recognized:
\[
\frac{30{,}000}{30{,}000 + 120{,}000} \times 12{,}500 = 0.20 \times 12{,}500 = \mathbf{\$2{,}500}
\]
Deferred gain = \(12{,}500 - 2{,}500 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\).  
Cost of new equipment = FV given up − cash received − deferred gain  
\(= 150{,}000 - 30{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$110{,}000}\)  
(equivalently: BV − cash received + partial gain = \(137{,}500 - 30{,}000 + 2{,}500 = 110{,}000\)).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—New Packaging Line | 110,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Old Packaging Line | 112,500 | |
| Cash | 30,000 | |
| Gain on Asset Exchange | | 2,500 |
| Equipment—Old Packaging Line | | 250,000 |
| *Nonmonetary exchange; partial gain (no CS, cash received)* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(110{,}000 + 112{,}500 + 30{,}000 = 252{,}500\); Cr \(2{,}500 + 250{,}000 = 252{,}500\). **Balanced.**

**(e) Case 4 — same facts as Case 3 but commercial substance (full gain)**

Full gain **$12,500**. New equipment = FV given up − cash = \(150{,}000 - 30{,}000 = \mathbf{\$120{,}000}\) (also equals FV of equipment received).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—New Packaging Line | 120,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Old Packaging Line | 112,500 | |
| Cash | 30,000 | |
| Gain on Asset Exchange | | 12,500 |
| Equipment—Old Packaging Line | | 250,000 |
| *Nonmonetary exchange; full gain (has commercial substance)* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(120{,}000 + 112{,}500 + 30{,}000 = 262{,}500\); Cr \(12{,}500 + 250{,}000 = 262{,}500\). **Balanced.**

**(f) Subsequent measurement — new line from Case 3; Dec 31, 20X6 period-end AJ**

Annual depreciation = \((110{,}000 - 10{,}000) / 8 = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—New Packaging Line | 12,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—New Packaging Line | | 12,500 |
| *Period-end dep. on equipment recorded at deferred-gain cost basis* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,500 = Cr 12,500. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** After the **period-end dep update**, apply the **gain matrix**: full loss always; full gain if commercial substance; if **no CS**, defer all gain when cash is paid or absent, and recognize only the **cash “boot” fraction** of potential gain when cash is received. Deferred gain **reduces the capitalized cost** of the new asset and thus future depreciation.

### Q4 — MC — Commercial substance / gain recognition classification

**LO:** LO 11-9  
**Concept:** Classify when full gain, partial gain, no gain, or full loss is recognized on a PPE nonmonetary exchange  

**Question 1:**  
A company exchanges old equipment (BV $40,000; FV $48,000) for similar equipment and pays **$5,000** cash. The exchange **lacks commercial substance**. What amount of **gain** is recognized, and at what amount is the **new equipment** recorded?

- A) Gain $8,000; new equipment $53,000  
- B) Gain $0; new equipment $45,000  
- C) Gain $0; new equipment $48,000  
- D) Partial gain $727; new equipment $44,273  

**Answer:** B. Potential gain = \(48{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 8{,}000\), but when the exchange **lacks commercial substance** and cash is **paid**, the **entire gain is deferred**. New equipment = BV + cash paid = \(40{,}000 + 5{,}000 = \mathbf{\$45{,}000}\). (A would be commercial-substance full gain; C uses FV without cash paid; D is the cash-received partial-gain formula, which does not apply when cash is paid.)

**Question 2:**  
Same facts as Question 1, except the company **receives $5,000** cash and the similar equipment received has FV **$43,000** (old equipment FV $48,000). The exchange still **lacks commercial substance**. What **gain** is recognized?

- A) $0  
- B) $8,000 full gain  
- C) $833 partial gain  
- D) $5,000  

**Answer:** C. Potential gain = \(48{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 8{,}000\).  
Partial gain = \(\frac{5{,}000}{5{,}000 + 43{,}000} \times 8{,}000 = \frac{5}{48} \times 8{,}000 = \mathbf{\$833}\) (rounded). Cash received + lacks CS → **partial** gain only, not full deferral and not full recognition.
