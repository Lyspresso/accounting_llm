# Agent 218 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-2

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Determine the initial and subsequent measurement of finite life intangible assets  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** finite-life intangibles recorded at cash-equivalent cost (purchase price + transfer, registration, and legal fees to place the asset in condition for intended use)
- Noncash acquisition: cost = present value of a zero-interest note (or FV of consideration / asset, whichever is more reliably measurable)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortize cost less residual over the **shorter** of remaining legal life and estimated useful life; straight-line unless another pattern better reflects consumption of benefits
- Successful legal defense costs: capitalize to the patent; recompute amortization over **remaining** useful life (prospective)
- Partial-period amortization when acquisition or disposal is mid-year
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr Amortization Expense; Cr Patent (direct write-down, most common) or Accumulated Amortization
- **Disposal JE:** amortize through disposal date; remove carrying amount; recognize gain or loss vs net proceeds (income from continuing operations)
- Basket / multi-asset and multi-intangible amortization roll-forwards (schedule emphasis)

### Q1 — CORE — Patent life cycle with amortization schedule, defense, mid-year disposal (emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule)
**LO:** LO 13-2  
**Concept:** Initial recognition at cash-equivalent cost; multi-year subsequent measurement (amortization) schedule with successful defense recomputation; period-end adjusting JEs; mid-year disposal with amortization to date  
**Scenario:**  
**AetherForge Dynamics LLC** (calendar year-end December 31) acquires a finite-life manufacturing patent. Company policy credits the **Patent** account directly for amortization (no Accumulated Amortization account). Residual value is **$0**.

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Acquisition date | **January 1, 2024** |
| Cash purchase price paid to seller | **$160,000** |
| Cash transfer, registration, and outside legal fees to secure title | **$8,000** |
| Remaining legal life at acquisition | 14 years |
| Estimated useful life at acquisition | **7 years** (expected technological obsolescence) |
| Amortization method | Straight-line |
| **January 5, 2025** | Successfully defends the patent; outside counsel fees paid in cash **$24,000** |
| **October 1, 2026** | Sells the patent for **$125,000** cash |
| Fiscal year-end | December 31 |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, 2024 initial recognition** journal entry (include all capitalizable acquisition costs). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement (amortization) schedule** from acquisition through disposal that incorporates the 2025 defense costs, recomputed amortization, and **partial-year** amortization to the disposal date. Show carrying amount after each event.  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting entries** for amortization on December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025.  
d. Prepare the **January 5, 2025** entry for the successful legal defense.  
e. Prepare the **October 1, 2026** entries: (1) amortize the patent through the disposal date, and (2) record the disposal (gain or loss).  
f. State the amortization life rule applied and the carrying amount immediately before disposal.

**Answer key:**

**a. January 1, 2024 — initial recognition (cash-equivalent cost)**  
Capitalized cost = purchase price + acquisition fees = $160,000 + $8,000 = **$168,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Patent | 168,000 | |
| Cash | | 168,000 |
| *Record finite-life patent at cash-equivalent cost (price + costs to place in use)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **168,000**. Balanced.

Useful life for amortization = shorter of remaining legal life (14) and estimated useful life (7) → **7 years**.  
Annual amortization (pre-defense) = $168,000 / 7 = **$24,000**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (amortization roll-forward) — EMPHASIS**

| Date / event | Computation | Amount | Carrying amount |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, 2024 — capitalized cost | $160,000 + $8,000 | 168,000 | **168,000** |
| Dec 31, 2024 — amortization | $168,000 ÷ 7 | (24,000) | **144,000** |
| Jan 5, 2025 — successful defense capitalized | | +24,000 | **168,000** |
| Dec 31, 2025 — amortization | $168,000 ÷ **6** remaining years | (28,000) | **140,000** |
| Oct 1, 2026 — amortization to disposal | $28,000 × **9/12** | (21,000) | **119,000** |
| Oct 1, 2026 — disposal (removed) | | (119,000) | **0** |

*After defense on Jan 5, 2025, remaining useful life from Jan 1, 2025 = 6 years; revised annual amortization = ($144,000 + $24,000) / 6 = $28,000.*

Roll-forward check through pre-disposal CA:  
\(168{,}000 - 24{,}000 + 24{,}000 - 28{,}000 - 21{,}000 = 119{,}000\).

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, 2024 — amortization*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense—Patent | 24,000 | |
| Patent | | 24,000 |
| *SL amortization: $168,000 / 7* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000.

*December 31, 2025 — amortization (after defense)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense—Patent | 28,000 | |
| Patent | | 28,000 |
| *Revised SL: ($144,000 + $24,000) / 6 remaining years* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000.  
Carrying amount Dec 31, 2025 = **$140,000**.

**d. January 5, 2025 — successful legal defense (capitalized)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Patent | 24,000 | |
| Cash | | 24,000 |
| *Capitalize successful defense costs as part of patent cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000.

**e. October 1, 2026 — amortize to date, then dispose**

*Amortize Jan 1–Oct 1, 2026 (9 months)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense—Patent | 21,000 | |
| Patent | | 21,000 |
| *9/12 × $28,000 annual amortization* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 21,000.  
Updated carrying amount = $140,000 − $21,000 = **$119,000**.

*Dispose patent*

Cash proceeds = **$125,000**  
Carrying amount = **$119,000**  
**Gain on sale of patent** = $125,000 − $119,000 = **$6,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 125,000 | |
| Patent | | 119,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Patent | | 6,000 |
| *Derecognize patent at updated carrying amount; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr 125,000 = Cr 119,000 + 6,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Life rule and pre-disposal CA**  
Amortize over the **shorter** of remaining legal life and estimated useful life (here 7 years, later remaining 6 after defense). Carrying amount immediately before disposal (after Oct 1 amort entry) = **$119,000**.

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the control document: start at cash-equivalent cost, amortize SL over the shorter life, capitalize successful defense and **prospectively** recompute remaining amortization, and **amortize to the disposal date** before removing the carrying amount. Gain/loss = proceeds − updated book value.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Patent schedule twin (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 13-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; amortization schedule with mid-life defense; period-end adjusting JEs; mid-year disposal with loss  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Circuits Inc.** (calendar year) purchases a finite-life software patent. Policy: credit **Patent** directly for amortization; residual value **$0**.

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|

| Cash purchase price | **$91,000** |

| Remaining legal life | 12 years |
| Estimated useful life | **6 years** |
| **January 8, 2024** | Successful infringement defense; cash legal fees **$20,000** |

a. Initial recognition JE on January 1, 2023 (balanced).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Two mid-year intangibles: year-end adjusting JE + multi-year schedule + franchise disposal
**LO:** LO 13-2  
**Concept:** Partial-period subsequent measurement for mid-year acquisitions; combined period-end amortization JE; multi-asset amortization schedule; disposal of one intangible after several years  
**Scenario:**  
**Solstice Media Group** (calendar year-end December 31) acquires two **finite-life** intangible assets during **2025**. Residual values are **$0**. Policy credits each asset account directly for amortization.

**Asset 1 — Patent (April 1, 2025)**

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Cash cost (including legal fees to secure) | **$48,000** |
| Remaining legal life (also its useful life) | **8 years** |

**Asset 2 — Franchise license (November 1, 2025)**

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Cash cost of franchise | **$60,000** |
| Estimated useful life | **5 years** |
| Remaining contractual/legal term | 10 years |

On **January 1, 2028**, Solstice sells the **franchise** for **$35,000** cash. Amortization for 2025–2027 has been recorded as required. The patent remains in use.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **initial recognition** journal entries for both acquisitions.  
b. Compute 2025 amortization for each asset and prepare the **combined December 31, 2025 period-end adjusting JE**.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for **both** assets for 2025, 2026, and 2027 (year-end carrying amounts).  
d. Record the **January 1, 2028 disposal** of the franchise (gain or loss).  
e. State the Dec 31, 2027 carrying amount of the **patent** that remains on the books.

**Answer key:**

**a. Initial recognition JEs**

*April 1, 2025 — patent*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Patent | 48,000 | |
| Cash | | 48,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 48,000.

*November 1, 2025 — franchise*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Franchise | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 60,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 60,000.

**Amortization parameters**  
- Patent: life = 8 years (legal = useful); annual = $48,000 / 8 = **$6,000**  
- Franchise: life = shorter of 5 (useful) and 10 (legal) → **5 years**; annual = $60,000 / 5 = **$12,000**

**b. 2025 amortization and period-end JE**

Patent 2025 (Apr 1–Dec 31 = 9/12): $6,000 × 9/12 = **$4,500**  
Franchise 2025 (Nov 1–Dec 31 = 2/12): $12,000 × 2/12 = **$2,000**  
**Total 2025 amortization** = $4,500 + $2,000 = **$6,500**

*December 31, 2025 — combined amortization adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense—Intangibles | 6,500 | |
| Patent | | 4,500 |
| Franchise | | 2,000 |
| *Partial-year SL amortization of mid-year acquisitions* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,500 = Cr 4,500 + 2,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule (both assets)**

| Year / asset | Beg. CA | Amortization | End. CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Patent** | | | |
| 2025 (9/12) | 48,000 | 4,500 | **43,500** |
| 2026 (full) | 43,500 | 6,000 | **37,500** |
| 2027 (full) | 37,500 | 6,000 | **31,500** |
| **Franchise** | | | |
| 2025 (2/12) | 60,000 | 2,000 | **58,000** |
| 2026 (full) | 58,000 | 12,000 | **46,000** |
| 2027 (full) | 46,000 | 12,000 | **34,000** |

Roll-forward checks:  
Patent: \(48{,}000 - 4{,}500 - 6{,}000 - 6{,}000 = 31{,}500\).  
Franchise: \(60{,}000 - 2{,}000 - 12{,}000 - 12{,}000 = 34{,}000\).

**d. January 1, 2028 — franchise disposal**  
Carrying amount of franchise = **$34,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$35,000**  
**Gain on sale of franchise** = $35,000 − $34,000 = **$1,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 35,000 | |
| Franchise | | 34,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Franchise | | 1,000 |
| *Derecognize franchise; recognize gain (income from continuing operations)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 35,000 = Cr 34,000 + 1,000. **Balanced.**  
(No amortization on Jan 1 because the full year 2027 entry was already recorded at Dec 31, 2027.)

**e. Patent carrying amount at Dec 31, 2027**  
**$31,500** (still reported among finite-life intangible assets, net).

**Key insight:** When intangibles are acquired mid-year, the **subsequent measurement schedule** prorates the first (and disposal) period. Each asset uses the **shorter** of legal and useful life. Year-end adjusting entries may combine multiple intangibles; disposal removes only the disposed asset’s updated carrying amount.

### Q4 — MC — Amortization period and partial year (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 13-2  
**Concept:** Classification of amortization life (shorter of legal vs useful) and partial-year period-end amortization  
**Question:**  
On **May 1, Year 1**, ValeChem Co. purchases a patent for **$96,000** cash. Remaining **legal** life is 16 years. Management estimates the patent will provide economic benefits for only **8 years** and will then be worthless. ValeChem uses straight-line amortization and a calendar year-end. Which amount of amortization expense is **correct** for Year 1?

- A) $96,000 / 16 × 8/12 = $4,000  
- B) $96,000 / 8 × 8/12 = $8,000  
- C) $96,000 / 8 = $12,000 (full year; ignore acquisition date)  
- D) $0 in Year 1; amortization begins only after a full year of ownership  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Finite-life intangibles are amortized over the **shorter** of remaining legal life (16) and estimated useful life (8) → **8 years**. Partial-year amortization from May 1 is 8/12 × ($96,000 / 8) = **$8,000**. A uses the longer legal life incorrectly. C ignores the mid-year acquisition. D is not GAAP for purchased finite-life intangibles placed in service during the year.

### Q5 — MC — Successful defense costs (classification)
**LO:** LO 13-2  
**Concept:** Classification — successful patent defense costs are capitalized and amortized over remaining useful life  
**Question:**  
At the start of Year 3, a patent has a carrying amount of **$70,000** and **7 years** of remaining useful life. During Year 3 the company **successfully** defends the patent and pays **$14,000** cash in legal fees. How should the defense costs and subsequent amortization be treated (straight-line, zero residual)?

- A) Expense $14,000 immediately; continue amortizing the $70,000 carrying amount at $10,000 per year  
- B) Capitalize $14,000 to Patent; amortize revised carrying amount $84,000 over the remaining 7 years ($12,000 per year)  
- C) Capitalize $14,000 to Patent; amortize only the $14,000 over 7 years and stop amortizing the original patent  
- D) Debit Goodwill $14,000; goodwill is not amortized  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Costs of a **successful** legal defense of a patent are **capitalized** as part of the patent’s cost. The new carrying amount ($70,000 + $14,000 = $84,000) is amortized prospectively over the **remaining** useful life (7 years) → $12,000 per year. Unsuccessful defense costs are typically expensed; that is not this fact pattern. Goodwill is not recorded for a successful patent defense.
