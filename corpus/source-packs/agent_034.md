# Agent 34 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-2

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Prepare the operating activities section of the statement of cash flows using the indirect method  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** initial_recognition_JE (supporting disposal / retirement / period-end JEs that feed indirect-method adjustments)

## Concept list for this pack
- Indirect method starts with **net income** (or net loss) and reconciles to **net cash from operating activities**
- Add **noncash expenses and losses**; subtract **noncash revenues and gains**
- Working-capital adjustments: **↑ operating asset → subtract**; **↓ operating asset → add**; **↑ operating liability → add**; **↓ operating liability → subtract**
- Gains (losses) on PPE disposal and nonoperating liability settlement are removed from operating because the related cash is **investing** or **financing**
- Bond **discount** amortization added (interest expense > cash interest); bond **premium** amortization subtracted (interest expense < cash interest)
- Reconstruct supporting **period-end** and **disposal/settlement** journal entries and T-account roll-forwards that justify SCF line items
- Other operating adjustments: equity-method excess of income over dividends, stock-based compensation, deferred taxes, unrealized G/L through net income
- Classification: operating vs investing vs financing presentation of the same economic event

3. On **January 1** of the current year, bonds with face **$30,000** were retired for **$30,000** cash. Remaining discount amortization for the year equals the reconciling change in Discount on Bonds Payable after removing the retired portion.  
4. No patents or other intangibles. Buildings and equipment purchases were for cash.

**Required:**  
(a) Reconstruct the **equipment disposal** journal entry and verify the reported gain.  
(b) Reconstruct the **bond retirement** journal entry and verify the reported loss.  
(c) Prepare the **period-end** journal entries implied for **depreciation** and **bond discount amortization** (show discount T-account roll-forward).  
(d) Prepare the **accumulated depreciation** T-account schedule proving depreciation expense of $32,000.  
(e) Prepare the **cash flows from operating activities** section for the current year using the **indirect method**.  
(f) Briefly classify where the **$28,000** equipment proceeds and the **$30,000** bond redemption cash would appear on the full statement of cash flows (not in the operating total).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Equipment disposal JE**  
Book value = $40,000 − $17,000 = **$23,000**.  
Gain = $28,000 − $23,000 = **$5,000** (matches income statement).

```
Dr Cash ......................................... 28,000
Dr Accumulated Depreciation — Equipment ......... 17,000
   Cr Buildings and Equipment ........................... 40,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Equipment .......................... 5,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **45,000**)

**(b) Bond retirement JE (January 1)**  
Carrying amount of retired bonds = $30,000 − $1,500 = **$28,500**.  
Loss = $30,000 − $28,500 = **$1,500** (matches income statement).

```
Dr Bonds Payable ................................ 30,000
Dr Loss on Bond Retirement ....................... 1,500
   Cr Discount on Bonds Payable .......................... 1,500
   Cr Cash .............................................. 30,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **31,500**)

**(c) Period-end adjusting JEs and discount schedule**

Discount on Bonds Payable roll-forward:

| | Debit | Credit | Balance (debit bal. in contra) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning | | | 4,000 |
| Retired with bonds (Jan 1) | | 1,500 | 2,500 |
| Amortization for year | | 1,000 | **1,500** end |

Amortization = $4,000 − $1,500 retired − $1,500 ending = **$1,000**.

```
Dr Depreciation Expense ......................... 32,000
   Cr Accumulated Depreciation — Equipment ............. 32,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **32,000**)

```
Dr Interest Expense .............................. 1,000
   Cr Discount on Bonds Payable .......................... 1,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **1,000**)  
(Discount amortization **increases** interest expense relative to cash interest → **add** $1,000 in the operating section.)

**(d) Accumulated depreciation schedule**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Beginning balance | $70,000 |
| Less: accum. dep. on equipment sold | (17,000) |
| Add: depreciation expense | 32,000 |
| **Ending balance** | **$85,000** |

**(e) Cash flows from operating activities — indirect method**

| Cash flows from operating activities | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $94,500 |
| Adjustments to reconcile net income to net cash provided by operating activities: | |
| Depreciation expense | 32,000 |
| Amortization of bond discount | 1,000 |

Computation check:  
\(94{,}500 + 32{,}000 + 1{,}000 - 5{,}000 + 1{,}500 - 9{,}000 - 6{,}000 + 2{,}000 + 8{,}000 - 2{,}000 + 3{,}000 = \mathbf{120{,}000}\).

Supporting change analysis (operating assets/liabilities):

| Account | Change | SCF effect |
|---|---:|---|
| AR (net) | +9,000 | Subtract |
| Inventory | +6,000 | Subtract |
| Prepaid expenses | −2,000 | Add |
| Accounts payable | +8,000 | Add |
| Accrued expenses payable | −2,000 | Subtract |
| Income taxes payable | +3,000 | Add |

**(f) Classification outside operating**  
- Equipment sale proceeds **$28,000** → **investing inflow** (gain already removed from operating).  
- Bond redemption cash **$30,000** → **financing outflow** (loss already added back in operating).  
Dividends paid $10,000 and common stock issuance $20,000 are financing (not required for the operating section). Equipment purchases $100,000 (= $260,000 − $200,000 + $40,000 sold) are investing.

**Key insight:** Under the indirect method, every gain/loss tied to investing or financing cash must be reversed in operating, and every noncash expense (depreciation, discount amortization) must be added so that net income is restated on a **cash** basis for operations only.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Harborview Analytics: equity method, SBC, deferred taxes, unrealized loss
**LO:** LO 22-2  
**Concept:** Other indirect-method adjustments — equity-method excess income over dividends; stock compensation; DTA/DTL; unrealized loss–income; gain on land sale  
**Scenario:** **Harborview Analytics Inc.** reports **net income of $150,000** for the year. Selected accounts and additional facts:

| Item | Amount / change |
|---|---:|
| Depreciation expense | $25,000 |
| Amortization of capitalized software | 8,000 |
| Stock-based compensation expense (equity-classified awards) | 12,000 |
| Equity in earnings of 30%-owned affiliate (equity method) | 21,000 |
| Cash dividends received from the affiliate (return on investment; cumulative earnings approach) | 6,000 |
| Unrealized holding **loss** on equity securities (FV-NI), included in net income | 3,000 |
| Gain on sale of land (cash proceeds reported in investing) | 9,000 |
| Increase in deferred tax **asset** | 4,000 |
| Increase in deferred tax **liability** | 7,000 |
| Increase in accounts receivable | 11,000 |
| Decrease in inventory | 5,000 |
| Increase in accounts payable | 8,000 |
| Decrease in salaries payable | 2,000 |
| Increase in interest payable | 1,000 |

No other noncash or WC items affect the operating reconciliation. Dividends from the affiliate do not exceed cumulative equity-method earnings (entire $6,000 is a return on investment / operating cash already embedded via the investment T-account analysis).

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **equity-method** investor entries for the year (pick-up of earnings and dividend). State the single net SCF adjustment for the equity-method investment.  
(b) Record the **stock-based compensation** journal entry.  
(c) Explain the SCF signs for the **deferred tax** changes and the **unrealized loss**.  
(d) Prepare the **operating activities** section (indirect method).  
(e) State where the land-sale **cash proceeds** are classified (section only; amount of proceeds not given).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Equity-method JEs**

```
Dr Investment in Affiliate ...................... 21,000
   Cr Equity in Earnings of Affiliate .................. 21,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **21,000**)

```
Dr Cash .......................................... 6,000
   Cr Investment in Affiliate ............................ 6,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **6,000**)

Equity income increased NI by $21,000 but cash was only $6,000 → deduct the excess **$15,000** (= $21,000 − $6,000) from net income in the operating section. (Equivalently: subtract equity income $21,000 and the $6,000 cash is already “in” via the net investment adjustment when using the excess method.)

**(b) Stock-based compensation**

```
Dr Compensation Expense ......................... 12,000
   Cr Paid-in Capital — Stock Options .................. 12,000
```
(Dr = Cr = **12,000**)  
Noncash expense → **add $12,000** in the operating section.

**(c) Deferred taxes and unrealized loss**  
- **↑ DTL $7,000:** tax expense > taxes currently payable → **add** $7,000.  
- **↑ DTA $4,000:** taxes paid (or payable pattern) exceed current tax expense component → **subtract** $4,000.  
- **Unrealized loss–income $3,000:** reduced NI with no cash → **add** $3,000. (An unrealized gain–income would be subtracted.)

**(d) Operating activities — indirect method**

| Cash flows from operating activities | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $150,000 |
| Depreciation expense | 25,000 |
| Amortization of software | 8,000 |
| Stock-based compensation expense | 12,000 |
| Excess of equity-method income over dividends | (15,000) |
| Unrealized loss on equity securities (FV-NI) | 3,000 |
| Gain on sale of land | (9,000) |
| Increase in deferred tax asset | (4,000) |
| Increase in deferred tax liability | 7,000 |
| Increase in accounts receivable | (11,000) |
| Decrease in inventory | 5,000 |
| Increase in accounts payable | 8,000 |
| Decrease in salaries payable | (2,000) |
| Increase in interest payable | 1,000 |

\(150 + 25 + 8 + 12 - 15 + 3 - 9 - 4 + 7 - 11 + 5 + 8 - 2 + 1 = \mathbf{178{,}000}\).

**(e)** Cash proceeds from the land sale are classified as an **investing inflow**. Only the **gain** is adjusted (subtracted) in operating activities.

**Key insight:** After the “standard” WC and depreciation adjustments, LO 22-2 still requires stripping noncash earnings (equity method, FV-NI unrealized items, SBC) and aligning tax expense with cash taxes via deferred tax changes—always so that operating cash reflects cash from operations, not accrual net income.

### Q4 — MC — Indirect-method treatment of PPE gain
**LO:** LO 22-2  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation — gain on sale of equipment in the operating section (indirect method)  
**Question:** Under the **indirect method**, how should a company report a **$12,000 gain on sale of equipment** (cash proceeds will be shown in investing activities)?

- A) Add $12,000 to net income in the operating section  
- B) Subtract $12,000 from net income in the operating section  
- C) Report $12,000 as a separate operating cash inflow  
- D) Ignore the gain entirely because equipment is a noncurrent asset  

**Answer:** **B.** The gain increased net income but the full cash proceeds are an **investing** inflow; subtracting the gain prevents double-counting and keeps operating cash free of the investing result. A is the treatment for a **loss**. C describes a **direct-method** style gross receipt, not the indirect reconciliation. D is incorrect—the gain sits inside NI and must be reversed.

### Q5 — MC — Working-capital and discount amortization signs
**LO:** LO 22-2  
**Concept:** Classification of WC change and bond discount amortization in the indirect operating section  
**Question:** In preparing the operating section under the indirect method, which pair of treatments is **correct**?

- A) Increase in accounts receivable: **add**; amortization of bond **discount**: **subtract**  
- B) Increase in accounts receivable: **subtract**; amortization of bond **discount**: **add**  
- C) Increase in accounts receivable: **subtract**; amortization of bond **premium**: **add**  
- D) Decrease in accounts payable: **add**; amortization of bond **discount**: **add**  

**Answer:** **B.** An AR increase means accrual sales > cash collections → subtract from NI. Bond **discount** amortization increases interest expense without a cash outlay → add to NI. (Premium amortization is subtracted; an AP decrease is subtracted, not added.)
