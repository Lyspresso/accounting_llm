# Agent 112 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-7

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe the components of comprehensive income and prepare a statement of comprehensive income and a statement of stockholders’ equity.  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Components of comprehensive income:** net income + other comprehensive income (OCI) = total comprehensive income
- **OCI common items (net of tax):** unrealized holding gains/losses on AFS debt securities; cash-flow-hedge gains/losses; foreign currency translation adjustments
- **Initial recognition JE:** period-end entries to record pretax OCI items and related deferred tax (emphasis angle)
- **Period-end closing JE:** close net income → Retained Earnings; close OCI → Accumulated OCI (AOCI)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** AOCI and Retained Earnings rollforwards; columnar statement of stockholders’ equity
- **Disposal / reclassification JE:** on sale of AFS debt securities, realize gain/loss in net income and reclassify the related amount out of AOCI (reclassification adjustment)
- **Classification / presentation:** single continuous SCI vs two-statement approach; separate SCI beginning with net income; equity section with AOCI as a distinct component
- **Number-variant twin:** same Demo 19-7 structure with all amounts changed

### Q3 — CORE — AFS disposal reclassification, AOCI schedule, presentation
**LO:** LO 19-7  
**Concept:** Disposal of AFS debt securities: realized gain in NI, reclassification adjustment out of AOCI, period-end OCI for remaining items, SCI with reclassification line, AOCI rollforward  
**Scenario:**  
**CedarVault Financial Inc.** (calendar-year, tax rate **25%**) holds a single portfolio of AFS **debt** securities and uses fair-value adjustments through OCI.

**January 1, Year 2 carrying amounts related to AFS**  
- Amortized cost of AFS debt securities: **$200,000**  
- Fair value adjustment (debit balance): **$30,000** → fair value **$230,000**  
- Related deferred tax liability: **$7,500**  
- After-tax unrealized gain embedded in AOCI from this portfolio only: **$22,500**  
- Total AOCI beginning balance (credit): **$22,500** (no other AOCI components)  

**During Year 2**  
1. On **March 15**, CedarVault **sells the entire AFS portfolio for $230,000 cash** (sale price equals beginning fair value; no additional unrealized change before sale).  
2. For the rest of Year 2, the company has a pretax foreign currency translation **loss** of **$4,000** (no other new AFS holdings).  
3. Net income for Year 2 (includes the **realized** pretax gain on the AFS sale and related tax on that gain within the NI tax provision): **$70,000**.  
4. Cash dividends: **$10,000**. No stock issuances.

**Other beginning equity (Dec 31, Year 1):** Common stock **$10,000**; PIC **$100,000**; Retained earnings **$150,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **disposal / settlement journal entries** on March 15 for (1) the sale at cost with realized gain to **net income**, (2) removal of the fair value adjustment, and (3) reverse of the related deferred tax / reclassification of the after-tax amount out of AOCI.  
b. Prepare the **period-end adjusting JE** for the pretax FX translation loss and its tax effect; then the **closing entry** of net-of-tax FX OCI to AOCI.  
c. Prepare a **separate statement of comprehensive income** for Year 2 that explicitly shows the **reclassification adjustment** related to AFS (so the same gain is not double-counted in comprehensive income).  
d. Prepare the **AOCI rollforward** and ending **stockholders’ equity section**.  
e. **Classification:** For each item below, state whether it is included in **(1) net income** or **(2) other comprehensive income**, and whether it ultimately accumulates in **(3) retained earnings** or **(4) AOCI**:  
   - Realized gain on sale of AFS debt securities  
   - Unrealized holding gain on AFS (before sale, prior periods)  
   - Reclassification adjustment (AFS) presented in OCI  
   - Foreign currency translation loss  
   - Cash dividends  

**Answer key:**  

**a. Disposal / reclassification JEs — March 15, Year 2**

*Sale of AFS debt securities (realized gain → net income)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 230,000 | |
| AFS Debt Securities (cost) | | 200,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investments (NI) | | 30,000 |
| *Realize pretax gain in earnings on disposal* | | |

**Check:** Dr 230,000 = Cr 200,000 + 30,000. **Balanced.**

*Remove fair value adjustment (gross reverse of prior unrealized)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Holding Gain—OCI (reclassification) | 30,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS Debt Securities | | 30,000 |
| *Clear FVA; reclassification will reverse prior OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 30,000 = Cr 30,000. **Balanced.**

*Reverse deferred tax and complete after-tax reclassification into AOCI closing*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Liability | 7,500 | |
| Unrealized Holding Gain—OCI (reclassification tax) | | 7,500 |
| *Reverse DTL; net reclassification out of AOCI = 22,500* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,500 = Cr 7,500. **Balanced.**

Net effect on OCI temporary account from reclassification = **$22,500 debit** (30,000 − 7,500), which will reduce AOCI when closed (prevents double-counting the $30,000 pretax gain already in NI).

*Optional combined view of reclassification close to AOCI (if closed at sale date):*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

**b. Period-end FX translation loss + close to AOCI**

Pretax loss $4,000; tax benefit $1,000; after-tax OCI loss **$3,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Foreign Currency Translation Loss—OCI | 4,000 | |
| Net Assets / CTA Clearing | | 4,000 |

|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Asset | 1,000 | |

| AOCI component | Amount |
|---|---:|

| Stockholders’ Equity, Dec 31, Year 2 | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock | $10,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par | 100,000 |
| Retained earnings | 210,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive **loss** | (3,000) |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$317,000** |

Total check: beg equity = 10 + 100 + 150 + 22.5 = **282,500**; + CI 44,500 − dividends 10,000 = **317,000**.  
(Alternatively: 10 + 100 + 210 − 3 = **317**.)

**e. Classification matrix**

| Item | NI or OCI? | Accumulates in |
|---|---|---|
| Realized gain on sale of AFS debt securities | **(1) Net income** | **(3) Retained earnings** |
| Unrealized holding gain on AFS (prior periods, before sale) | **(2) OCI** | **(4) AOCI** (until reclassified) |
| Reclassification adjustment (AFS) in OCI | **(2) OCI** (negative adjustment) | Moves amount **from AOCI → RE path via NI** |
| Foreign currency translation loss | **(2) OCI** | **(4) AOCI** |
| Cash dividends | **Neither** (owner distribution) | Reduces **(3) RE** directly |

**Key insight:** Sale of AFS **moves** previously unrealized amounts from AOCI into earnings (realized). SCI shows a **reclassification adjustment** so total comprehensive income is not double-counted. Dividends never appear in comprehensive income.

### Q4 — MC — Components: net income vs OCI
**LO:** LO 19-7  
**Concept:** Classification of gains/losses as net income vs other comprehensive income  
**Question:**  
Which of the following items is reported in **other comprehensive income** (not net income) under U.S. GAAP as illustrated in LO 19-7?

- A) Gain on sale of equipment used in operations  
- B) Unrealized holding gain on an **equity** investment that the investor elects (or is required) to measure at fair value through net income (no significant influence)  
- C) Unrealized holding gain on **available-for-sale debt** securities  
- D) Dividend revenue on equity securities  

**Answer:** **C.** Unrealized holding gains/losses on AFS **debt** securities are a classic OCI item (net of tax) until realized. Equipment sale gains, FV-NI equity unrealized gains, and dividend revenue are all components of **net income**.

### Q5 — MC — Closing destination of OCI
**LO:** LO 19-7  
**Concept:** Closing process — OCI closes to AOCI, not retained earnings  
**Question:**  
At period-end, after OCI items are recognized net of tax, the **closing entry** for a net other comprehensive **income** (credit) balance:

- A) Debits Income Summary and credits Retained Earnings  
- B) Debits the OCI temporary accounts and credits **Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income**  
- C) Debits Retained Earnings and credits AOCI  
- D) Is not required because OCI is already a permanent equity account  

**Answer:** **B.** Net income closes to **retained earnings**. OCI temporary accounts close to **AOCI**, a separate permanent equity component. AOCI is not closed to RE each period (except through reclassification when amounts are realized into NI).
