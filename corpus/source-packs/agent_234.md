# Agent 234 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-9

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Apply the fair value option for liabilities  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- Elect the **fair value option (FVO)** on the **election date** (usually first recognition); election is **instrument-by-instrument**, applies to the **entire** instrument, and is generally **irrevocable**
- **Initial recognition JE** under FVO looks like ordinary bond/note issuance (cash, face, discount/premium if any)
- **Subsequent measurement (emphasis):** each reporting date, measure liability at **fair value** using a **Fair Value Adjustment** contra/adjunct so that **amortized cost ± FVA = fair value**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-period rollforward of amortized cost, fair value, required FVA balance, period adjustment, and unrealized G/(L)
- **General (benchmark / risk-free) interest-rate risk** → unrealized holding G/L in **net income**
- **Instrument-specific credit risk** (own credit) → unrealized holding G/L in **OCI**; reclassify to NI when debt **matures or is redeemed**
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** record FVA after interest/amortization has updated amortized cost
- **Maturity / settlement JE:** retire face (and discount/premium/FVA); reclassify remaining AOCI for own-credit amounts into earnings
- Balance sheet: present liability **at fair value** (face − unamortized discount + unamortized premium ± FVA)

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-year FVO note: subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis), period-end FVA, early settlement
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Fair value option subsequent measurement schedule for a note payable (general risk → NI); initial recognition; period-end FVA adjusting entries; early settlement at fair value  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Thornfield Capital Partners** (calendar year-end) issues an **8%** note payable with **face $300,000** for **$280,400** cash to finance equipment expansion. Contractual cash interest of **$24,000** is paid **annually each December 31**. On the issuance date (election date), Thornfield **irrevocably elects the fair value option** under ASC 825 for **this note only**. Thornfield uses the effective interest method to update amortized cost.

Interest and amortization have been recorded for each year. After those entries, amortized cost (carrying amount before FVA) and fair value are:

| Date | Amortized cost | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | **$282,014** | **$291,000** |
| December 31, Year 2 | **$283,775** | **$286,000** |
| December 31, Year 3 | **$285,677** | **$284,500** |

All differences between amortized cost and fair value are attributable to **general** market interest-rate changes (not instrument-specific credit risk).

On **December 31, Year 3**, immediately after recording the Year 3 fair value adjustment, Thornfield **settles** the note by paying cash equal to then-current fair value of **$284,500**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry for **initial recognition** of the note (FVO elected).  
b. Prepare a complete **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing: amortized cost, fair value, required Fair Value Adjustment balance (show debit as positive / credit as negative or labeled), beginning FVA, period adjustment (Dr or Cr), unrealized G/(L) recognized in **net income**, and ending FVA. (**Emphasis.**)  
c. Prepare the **period-end fair value option adjusting entries** for December 31 of Years 1, 2, and 3.  
d. Show **balance sheet presentation** of the note at December 31, Year 1 and December 31, Year 2.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 settlement** entry (after the Year 3 FVA adjustment). Confirm no residual extinguishment G/L beyond amounts already in the FVA process.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (FVO elected)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 280,400 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 19,600 | |
| Note Payable | | 300,000 |
| *Issue 8% note at discount; elect fair value option (irrevocable, entire instrument)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 280,400 + 19,600 = Cr 300,000. **Balanced.**  
Initial amortized cost = **$280,400**. Unamortized discount = **$19,600**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**  

Convention: **required FVA** = amortized cost − fair value.  
- **Positive** required FVA = **debit** balance (contra; FV < AC) → prior gains or current gain if increasing  
- **Negative** required FVA = **credit** balance (adjunct; FV > AC) → prior losses or current loss if becoming more negative  

Period adjustment moves beginning FVA to the required ending balance.  
**Debit** to FVA (or decrease in credit FVA) with credit to Unrealized G/L—Income = **unrealized gain** (liability fair value fell relative to AC path).  
**Credit** to FVA (or increase in credit FVA) with debit to Unrealized G/L—Income = **unrealized loss**.

| Year-end | Amortized cost | Fair value | Req. FVA Dr/(Cr) | Beg. FVA | Period adj. Dr/(Cr) | Unreal. G/(L)—Income | End. FVA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Y1 | 282,014 | 291,000 | **(8,986) Cr** | 0 | **Cr 8,986** | **(8,986)** loss | 8,986 Cr |
| Y2 | 283,775 | 286,000 | **(2,225) Cr** | 8,986 Cr | **Dr 6,761** | **+6,761** gain | 2,225 Cr |
| Y3 | 285,677 | 284,500 | **1,177 Dr** | 2,225 Cr | **Dr 3,402** | **+3,402** gain | 1,177 Dr |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
- Y1 net liability = 282,014 + 8,986 = **291,000** = FV  
- Y2 net liability = 283,775 + 2,225 = **286,000** = FV  
- Y3 net liability = 285,677 − 1,177 = **284,500** = FV  
- Cumulative unrealized G/(L) through Y3 = −8,986 + 6,761 + 3,402 = **+1,177** (matches ending debit FVA still open before settlement)

**c. Period-end FVO adjusting entries**

*December 31, Year 1 — FVO adjustment (general risk → NI)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 8,986 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | | 8,986 |
| *FV > AC: adjunct FVA; unrealized loss in net income (general rate decrease)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,986. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2 — FVO adjustment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | 6,761 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 6,761 |
| *Reduce adjunct FVA from $8,986 Cr to $2,225 Cr; partial FV decline → unrealized gain in NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,761. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3 — FVO adjustment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | 3,402 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 3,402 |
| *Move from $2,225 Cr to $1,177 Dr FVA so net CA = FV $284,500* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,402. **Balanced.**  
Ending FVA = 2,225 Cr reversed + 3,402 Dr net movement → **$1,177 Dr**.

**d. Balance sheet presentation**

*December 31, Year 1*

| | |
|---|---:|
| Note payable | $300,000 |
| Less: Discount on note payable ($300,000 − $282,014) | (17,986) |
| Amortized cost | 282,014 |
| Add: Fair value adjustment—note payable | 8,986 |
| **Note payable, at fair value** | **$291,000** |

*December 31, Year 2*

| | |
|---|---:|
| Note payable | $300,000 |
| Less: Discount on note payable ($300,000 − $283,775) | (16,225) |
| Amortized cost | 283,775 |
| Add: Fair value adjustment—note payable | 2,225 |
| **Note payable, at fair value** | **$286,000** |

**e. December 31, Year 3 — settlement at fair value**

Unamortized discount at settlement = 300,000 − 285,677 = **$14,323**.  
FVA debit balance = **$1,177**.  
Cash paid = **$284,500**.  
Net carrying amount removed = 285,677 − 1,177 = **284,500** = cash → **no additional extinguishment G/L**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 300,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 14,323 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | | 1,177 |
| Cash | | 284,500 |
| *Settle note at FV; clear discount and FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 300,000 = Cr 14,323 + 1,177 + 284,500 = 300,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the control document under FVO: lock **amortized cost** after interest, compare to **fair value**, set **required FVA**, and post only the **period change**. General-risk marks go through **net income**. When settled at current FV after the mark, the settlement JE clears face, remaining discount/premium, and FVA with **no extra G/L**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Par bonds under FVO: multi-year schedule, adjunct FVA, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — FVO initial recognition at par; subsequent measurement schedule including FV above and below face; period-end adjusting JEs; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Lumenbridge Transit Co.** issues **$600,000** face of **7%**, **4-year** bonds **at par** for cash. Cash interest is payable **annually each December 31**. On the issuance date, Lumenbridge **elects the fair value option** for these bonds. All year-end fair value changes are due to **general** market risk (not own credit risk). Interest is recorded each December 31 **before** the fair value adjustment.

| Date | Fair value |
|---|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | **$575,000** |
| December 31, Year 2 | **$590,000** |
| December 31, Year 3 | **$615,000** |
| December 31, Year 4 (maturity) | **$600,000** |

On December 31, Year 4, Lumenbridge pays face value and retires the bonds (after the Year 4 FVA entry).

**Required:**  
a. Journal entry for **issuance** on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Journal entry for **cash interest** on December 31, Year 1.  
c. Fair value option **adjusting entry** on December 31, Year 1.  
d. Prepare a **four-year subsequent measurement schedule** (amortized cost, FV, required FVA Dr/(Cr), beginning FVA, period adjustment, unrealized G/(L)—Income, ending FVA). Provide adjusting entries for Years 2, 3, and 4.  
e. **Maturity settlement** entry on December 31, Year 4 (after Year 4 FV adjustment).  
f. Net liability reported on the **December 31, Year 3** balance sheet.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — issuance at par (FVO elected)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 600,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 600,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 600,000. **Balanced.** Amortized cost remains **$600,000** each year-end until maturity (issued at par; no discount/premium).

**b. December 31, Year 1 — cash interest**  
Interest = $600,000 × 7% = **$42,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 42,000 | |
| Cash | | 42,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 42,000. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVO adjustment (general risk → NI)**  
Required FVA debit = 600,000 − 575,000 = **$25,000**  
Decrease in liability FV → **unrealized gain in NI**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 25,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 25,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 25,000. **Balanced.** Net liability = **$575,000**.

**d. Four-year subsequent measurement schedule**

| Year-end | AC | FV | Req. FVA Dr/(Cr) | Beg. FVA | Adj. Dr/(Cr) | Unreal. G/(L)—Inc. | End. FVA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Y1 | 600,000 | 575,000 | 25,000 Dr | 0 | **Dr 25,000** | **+25,000** | 25,000 Dr |
| Y2 | 600,000 | 590,000 | 10,000 Dr | 25,000 Dr | **Cr 15,000** | **−15,000** | 10,000 Dr |
| Y3 | 600,000 | 615,000 | 15,000 Cr | 10,000 Dr | **Cr 25,000** | **−25,000** | 15,000 Cr |
| Y4 | 600,000 | 600,000 | 0 | 15,000 Cr | **Dr 15,000** | **+15,000** | 0 |

Cumulative unrealized G/L = 25,000 − 15,000 − 25,000 + 15,000 = **$0**.

*Year 2 adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 15,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 15,000 |

**Check:** 15,000 = 15,000. Net liability = 600,000 − 10,000 = **$590,000**.

*Year 3 adjusting entry*  
(From 10,000 Dr to 15,000 Cr requires Cr **25,000**)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 25,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 25,000 |

**Check:** 25,000 = 25,000. Net liability = 600,000 + 15,000 = **$615,000**.

*Year 4 adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 15,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 15,000 |

**Check:** 15,000 = 15,000. FVA cleared to **$0**.

**e. December 31, Year 4 — maturity settlement**  
(FVA already zero after Year 4 adjustment.)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 600,000 | |
| Cash | | 600,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 600,000. **Balanced.**

**f. December 31, Year 3 presentation**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $600,000 |
| Add: Fair value adjustment—bond payable | 15,000 |
| **Bonds payable, at fair value** | **$615,000** |

**Key insight:** When FV **exceeds** amortized cost, FVA is an **adjunct** (credit) and the period move that **increases** the net liability is an unrealized **loss** in NI. The schedule forces every period’s entry to be the **change** in required FVA—not a blind plug from face to FV ignoring the beginning FVA balance.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Instrument-specific credit risk (OCI), FVA schedule, early redemption + AOCI reclassification
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** FVO period-end adjustments when FV change is own (instrument-specific) credit risk → OCI; subsequent measurement schedule; disposal/settlement with AOCI reclassification into NI  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Silverpine Manufacturing Inc.** issues **$250,000** face of **4%**, **3-year** bonds **at par** for cash. Cash interest of **$10,000** is paid **annually each December 31**. Silverpine **elects the fair value option** for this bond issue on the issuance date.

Year-end fair values (interest already recorded each year before FVA):

| Date | Fair value | Primary cause of FV change vs amortized cost |
|---|---:|---|
| December 31, Year 1 | **$235,000** | **Instrument-specific credit risk** (market’s reassessment of Silverpine’s own credit standing after a debt-covenant concern) |
| December 31, Year 2 | **$242,000** | **Instrument-specific credit risk** (partial recovery of credit standing) |

Amortized cost remains **$250,000** each year-end (issued and still outstanding at par).

On **January 1, Year 3**, Silverpine **redeems** the entire issue for cash equal to fair value of **$242,000**.

Ignore income taxes.

**Required:**  
a. Journal entry for **issuance** on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Journal entry for **cash interest** on December 31, Year 1.  
c. Fair value option adjusting entry on December 31, Year 1 (**own credit → OCI**).  
d. Two-year **subsequent measurement / FVA schedule** and the December 31, Year 2 FVO adjusting entry (own credit → OCI).  
e. Balance sheet presentation at December 31, Year 2.  
f. January 1, Year 3 **redemption** entries: retire the liability at cash paid and **reclassify AOCI** related to instrument-specific credit risk into net income.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — issuance at par**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 250,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 250,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 250,000. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — cash interest**  
$250,000 × 4% = **$10,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 10,000 | |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,000. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 1 — FVO adjustment (instrument-specific credit risk → OCI)**  
Required FVA debit = 250,000 − 235,000 = **$15,000**  
Own-credit-driven decline in liability FV → **unrealized gain in OCI** (not NI)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 15,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 15,000 |
| *Instrument-specific credit risk: unrealized gain in OCI (ASC 825-10-45-5)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000. **Balanced.** Net liability = **$235,000**.

**d. Subsequent measurement schedule + Year 2 adjustment**

| Year-end | AC | FV | Req. FVA Dr | Beg. FVA | Adj. Dr/(Cr) | G/L account | End. FVA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Y1 | 250,000 | 235,000 | 15,000 | 0 | **Dr 15,000** | Unreal. G/L—**OCI** +15,000 | 15,000 |
| Y2 | 250,000 | 242,000 | 8,000 | 15,000 | **Cr 7,000** | Unreal. G/L—**OCI** −7,000 | 8,000 |

AOCI cumulative credit (own-credit unrealized gain) at YE2 = 15,000 − 7,000 = **$8,000**.

*December 31, Year 2 — FVO adjustment (own credit → OCI)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 7,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 7,000 |
| *Partial recovery of FV; reduce FVA and reverse portion of OCI gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,000. **Balanced.** Net liability = 250,000 − 8,000 = **$242,000**.

**e. Balance sheet — December 31, Year 2**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $250,000 |
| Less: Fair value adjustment—bond payable | (8,000) |
| **Bonds payable, at fair value** | **$242,000** |

(Related AOCI includes **$8,000** credit from instrument-specific credit risk on this liability.)

**f. January 1, Year 3 — early redemption + AOCI reclassification**

Remove face and FVA; pay cash $242,000. Net CA = 250,000 − 8,000 = 242,000 = cash → **no additional extinguishment G/L**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 250,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 8,000 |
| Cash | | 242,000 |
| *Redeem bonds at FV; clear FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 = Cr 8,000 + 242,000. **Balanced.**

Reclassify AOCI (own-credit unrealized gain remaining) into net income upon redemption:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI (AOCI) | 8,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income (reclassification) | | 8,000 |
| *Reclassify AOCI related to instrument-specific credit risk into NI on settlement* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Same FVA mechanics as general-risk marks, but **own-credit** fair value changes hit **OCI**, not earnings, while the debt is outstanding. On **maturity or redemption**, reclassify the remaining AOCI amount into **net income**. Review 16-9 style: covenant violation → OCI path.

### Q4 — MC — Classification under the fair value option (≤2)
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Classification — general risk (NI) vs instrument-specific credit risk (OCI); FVO election rules  

**Question 4A:**  
On January 1, **Ridgehaven Foods Inc.** issued $100,000 of bonds at par and elected the fair value option. At year-end, after interest was recorded, the bonds’ fair value is **$92,000** solely because the **risk-free (benchmark) rate increased**. Which year-end entry is appropriate?

- A) Debit Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable $8,000; credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—**OCI** $8,000  
- B) Debit Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable $8,000; credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** $8,000  
- C) Debit Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income $8,000; credit Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable $8,000  
- D) Debit Bonds Payable $8,000; credit Gain on Extinguishment $8,000  

**Answer:** **B.** A decline in the fair value of the liability caused by **general** (benchmark / risk-free) interest-rate risk is recognized as an unrealized **gain in net income**. Debiting FVA reduces the net liability from $100,000 to $92,000. Option A would be correct if the cause were **instrument-specific credit risk**. Option C records the wrong direction. Option D incorrectly treats a period-end mark as extinguishment of face.

---

**Question 4B:**  
Which statement about the fair value option for a long-term note payable is **correct**?

- A) Management may elect FVO only for the credit-risk portion of the note and leave interest-rate risk at amortized cost.  
- B) Once elected for a specific instrument on its election date, the election is generally **irrevocable** and must apply to the **entire** instrument.  
- C) The issuer may switch from FVO back to amortized cost at any year-end if quoted prices become unavailable.  
- D) Fair value option accounting is available only for convertible debt.  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 825: the fair value option is elected **instrument by instrument**, is generally **irrevocable**, and applies to the **entire** instrument (not specified risks or portions). A is partial-risk election (not allowed). C contradicts irrevocability. D is false—FVO applies to most financial liabilities, including ordinary bonds and notes.
