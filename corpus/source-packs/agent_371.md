# Agent 371 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-4

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe and account for preferred stock  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (equity preferred):** Dr Cash; Cr Preferred Stock at par; Cr Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock for excess (callable / convertible / redeemable solely at holder option — non-mandatory)
- **Initial recognition JE (mandatorily redeemable):** classified as a **liability** at issue proceeds / present value of fixed redemption (ASC 480-10); **not** equity Preferred Stock
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** when both redemption amount and settlement date are fixed, accrete carrying amount to redemption using the **implicit interest rate** at inception
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** multi-year Dr Interest Expense / Cr Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability; year-end current vs noncurrent presentation of the liability as maturity approaches
- **Disposal — call/recall:** remove Preferred Stock + related PIC—PS; cash at call price; **excess of cash over equity carrying amount → Retained Earnings**
- **Disposal — conversion (book value method):** remove Preferred Stock + PIC—PS; credit Common Stock at par of shares issued; remainder to PIC—Common Stock (or debit RE if common par exceeds preferred carrying amount); no gain/loss; net equity unchanged when remainder stays in equity
- **Settlement / maturity JE:** remove fully accreted liability; pay cash equal to fixed redemption amount
- **Classification:** mandatorily redeemable → **liability**; callable by issuer → **permanent equity**; redeemable solely at stockholder option (not mandatory) → equity (core textbook); cumulative feature described (arrears disclosed until declared — LO 19-5 for declaration)

### Q1 — CORE — Preferred issuance; multi-year period-end accretion AJEs (emphasis); call; conversion; maturity
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of callable and convertible preferred as equity and mandatorily redeemable as liability; subsequent accretion schedule; multi-year period-end adjusting interest JEs (emphasis); call, conversion, and maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpine Analytics Corp.** (calendar-year) completes three preferred-stock arrangements on **January 1, Year 1**, and settles them later as described.

**Issue A — Callable preferred (equity)**  
- 2,400 shares of **7%** callable preferred stock, **$40 par**, issued at **$92** per share for cash.  
- Issuer may call the shares at **$95** per share on or after January 1, Year 2.  
- On **April 10, Year 2**, Silverpine **calls all 2,400 shares** at the $95 call price.

**Issue B — Convertible preferred (equity)**  
- 1,200 shares of **6%** convertible preferred stock, **$30 par**, issued at **$75** per share for cash.  
- Each preferred share is convertible into **5** shares of **$2 par** common stock at the holder’s option.  
- On **August 20, Year 2**, holders convert **all** 1,200 preferred shares into common.

**Issue C — Mandatorily redeemable preferred (liability)**  
- On January 1, Year 1, Silverpine issues mandatorily redeemable preferred stock for cash of **$160,000**.  
- The instrument is **mandatorily redeemable** for a **fixed** cash amount of **$212,960** on **December 31, Year 3** (three years; redemption not solely upon liquidation).  
- Implicit effective interest rate at inception is **10%**. Ignore periodic preferred dividends for the accretion problem; focus on ASC 480 subsequent measurement of the liability.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JEs** for Issues A, B, and C.  
b. Prepare the complete **subsequent measurement (accretion) schedule** for Issue C for Years 1–3.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1**, **December 31, Year 2**, and **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JEs** for Issue C interest accretion (**emphasis**). State the balance-sheet classification of Issue C at December 31, Year 1 and at December 31, Year 2 (after the Year 2 AJE).  
d. Prepare the **April 10, Year 2 call/recall JE** for Issue A.  
e. Prepare the **August 20, Year 2 conversion JE** for Issue B (book value method).  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 maturity/settlement JE** for Issue C after the Year 3 accretion AJE.  
g. Briefly state what a **cumulative dividend preference** means (no JE required).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

*Issue A — Callable preferred (equity)*  
Cash = 2,400 × $92 = **$220,800**  
Preferred Stock = 2,400 × $40 = **$96,000**  
PIC in Excess of Par—PS = 220,800 − 96,000 = **$124,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 220,800 | |
| Preferred Stock—Callable ($40 par) | | 96,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 124,800 |
| *Issue 2,400 shares callable preferred at $92* | | |

**Check:** Dr 220,800 = Cr 96,000 + 124,800. **Balanced.**

*Issue B — Convertible preferred (equity)*  
Cash = 1,200 × $75 = **$90,000**  
Preferred Stock = 1,200 × $30 = **$36,000**  
PIC—PS = 90,000 − 36,000 = **$54,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 90,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Convertible ($30 par) | | 36,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 54,000 |
| *Issue 1,200 shares convertible preferred at $75* | | |

**Check:** Dr 90,000 = Cr 36,000 + 54,000. **Balanced.**

*Issue C — Mandatorily redeemable preferred (liability)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 160,000 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 160,000 |
| *Initial recognition of mandatorily redeemable preferred as liability (ASC 480)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 160,000 = Cr 160,000. **Balanced.**  
(Do **not** credit equity Preferred Stock for mandatorily redeemable shares.)

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — Issue C (fixed amount, fixed date; 10% implicit)**

| Year | Beginning carrying amount | Interest expense (10%) | Ending carrying amount |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 160,000 | 16,000 | 176,000 |
| 2 | 176,000 | 17,600 | 193,600 |
| 3 | 193,600 | 19,360 | **212,960** |
| **Total** | | **52,960** | |

**Roll-forward check:** 160,000 + 16,000 + 17,600 + 19,360 = **212,960** (equals fixed redemption). ✓  
**Interest total check:** 212,960 − 160,000 = **52,960**. ✓

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs — Issue C (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 1 — Accrete liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 16,000 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 16,000 |
| *Year-end accretion toward fixed redemption; interest cost under ASC 480* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,000 = Cr 16,000. **Balanced.** Ending liability = **$176,000**.  
**Classification at Dec 31, Year 1:** **Noncurrent liability** (settlement more than one year away).

*December 31, Year 2 — Accrete liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 17,600 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 17,600 |

**Check:** Dr 17,600 = Cr 17,600. **Balanced.** Ending liability = **$193,600**.  
**Classification at Dec 31, Year 2:** **Current liability** (redemption on Dec 31, Year 3 is within one year). No separate reclassification JE is required if the company simply presents the liability as current; if a formal reclass entry is used in the worksheet, Dr Noncurrent MRPS Liability / Cr Current MRPS Liability for $193,600.

*December 31, Year 3 — Final accretion (period-end AJE before settlement)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 19,360 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 19,360 |

**Check:** Dr 19,360 = Cr 19,360. **Balanced.** Carrying amount now = **$212,960**.

**d. Call/recall of callable preferred — April 10, Year 2**

Cash paid = 2,400 × $95 = **$228,000**  
Equity carrying amount removed = $96,000 + $124,800 = **$220,800**  
Excess of cash over carrying amount → **Retained Earnings** = 228,000 − 220,800 = **$7,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Callable | 96,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 124,800 | |
| Retained Earnings | 7,200 | |
| Cash | | 228,000 |
| *Call 2,400 shares at $95; excess of call price over equity carrying amount reduces RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr 96,000 + 124,800 + 7,200 = Cr 228,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Conversion of preferred into common — August 20, Year 2 (book value method)**

Carrying amount of convertible preferred equity = $36,000 + $54,000 = **$90,000**  
Common shares issued = 1,200 × 5 = **6,000** shares  
Common Stock = 6,000 × $2 = **$12,000**  
PIC—Common Stock = 90,000 − 12,000 = **$78,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | 36,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 54,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 12,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 78,000 |
| *Convert 1,200 preferred into 6,000 common; book value method; no gain/loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 36,000 + 54,000 = Cr 12,000 + 78,000 = 90,000. **Balanced.**  
Net equity is **unchanged** (preferred equity reclassified to common equity).

**f. Maturity / settlement of mandatorily redeemable preferred — December 31, Year 3**

After the Year 3 period-end accretion AJE, liability carrying amount = **$212,960**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 212,960 | |
| Cash | | 212,960 |
| *Redeem mandatorily redeemable preferred at fixed contractual amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 212,960 = Cr 212,960. **Balanced.**

**g. Cumulative dividend preference**  
If preferred is **cumulative**, undeclared preferred dividends **accumulate** as **dividends in arrears**. Arrears are **not a liability** until the board **declares** a dividend, but they must be **disclosed** and paid before any common dividends. (Declaration accounting is LO 19-5.)

**Key insight:** Equity preferred (callable/convertible) is recognized at par + APIC and has **no** interest accretion AJE. Mandatorily redeemable preferred is a **liability** with a recurring **period-end adjusting JE** that accretes interest until the fixed redemption date. Call excess hits RE; conversion only reclassifies within equity.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end AJEs only for outstanding MRPS; mid-year call; conversion with RE plug; classification matrix

**Concept:** Multi-year period-end adjusting JEs for already-outstanding mandatorily redeemable preferred; current/noncurrent presentation; issuer call of equity preferred; book-value conversion when common par exceeds preferred carrying amount; classification of callable vs holder-redeemable vs mandatory

**Westfield Brewing Group** (calendar-year) has the following preferred activity.

- Mandatorily redeemable preferred liability with **beginning carrying amount $250,000** on January 1, Year 1.  
- Fixed redemption amount **$314,928** due **December 31, Year 3**.  
- Implicit rate **8%** (verified: $250,000 × 1.08³ = $314,928).  
- No new MRPS issued in Years 1–3.

**Year 1–Year 3 new activity**  
1. **March 1, Year 1:** Issue **1,500** shares of **callable** preferred, **$20 par**, at **$60** cash per share. Call price **$62**.  
2. **July 1, Year 2:** Call **all 1,500** callable preferred shares at $62.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 20,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | | 5,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 15,000 |

Common Stock at par = 5,000 × $5 = **$25,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | 5,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 15,000 | |

**Check:** Dr 5,000 + 15,000 + 5,000 = Cr 25,000. **Balanced.**  
Total equity is unchanged (RE decrease offsets the larger Common Stock credit relative to preferred removed).

**e. MRPS settlement — December 31, Year 3**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 314,928 | |
| Cash | | 314,928 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 314,928. **Balanced.**

**f. Classification matrix**

| Instrument | Balance-sheet section | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| (i) Callable preferred | **Stockholders’ equity** | Call is **issuer’s option**; no unconditional obligation to transfer assets on a date certain |
| (ii) Redeemable at **stockholder’s option** only (not mandatory) | **Stockholders’ equity** (core textbook) | Not an ASC 480 **mandatorily** redeemable instrument; no fixed unconditional issuer settlement date |
| (iii) Mandatorily redeemable preferred | **Liability** (noncurrent → current as maturity nears) | Unconditional obligation to transfer assets on a **specified date** (ASC 480-10-25-4); subsequent measurement accretes interest when amount and date are fixed |

**Key insight:** The recurring **period-end adjusting JE** is the hallmark of **mandatorily redeemable** preferred measured at PV with a fixed settlement. Equity preferred (callable or optionally redeemable) does not generate interest accretion AJEs. Conversion can require a RE debit when common par exceeds preferred’s equity carrying amount.

### Q4 — MC (period-end adjusting entry)
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE for mandatorily redeemable preferred stock with fixed amount and fixed date  

**Question:** On January 1, Year 1, Driftwood Logistics Inc. issues mandatorily redeemable preferred stock for cash of $90,000. The instrument is mandatorily redeemable for a fixed $108,900 on December 31, Year 2. The implicit rate at inception is 10%. Which entry should Driftwood record at **December 31, Year 1**?

- A) Dr Preferred Stock Dividends 9,000; Cr Dividends Payable 9,000  
- B) Dr Interest Expense 9,000; Cr Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability 9,000  
- C) Dr Retained Earnings 9,000; Cr Preferred Stock 9,000  
- D) No entry; mandatorily redeemable preferred is always measured at issue proceeds until cash is paid  

**Answer:** **B.** When both the amount to be paid and the settlement date are fixed, ASC 480 requires subsequent measurement at the present value of the amount to be paid, **accruing interest cost** using the rate implicit at inception. Year 1 interest = $90,000 × 10% = **$9,000**, increasing the liability to $99,000. Year 2 interest will be $99,000 × 10% = $9,900, bringing the liability to $108,900. Option A confuses undeclared dividends (LO 19-5) with interest accretion. Option C incorrectly treats accretion as an equity reclassification. Option D contradicts ASC 480 subsequent measurement.

### Q5 — MC (call excess treatment)
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Accounting for call/recall of equity preferred when cash paid exceeds carrying amount  

**Question:** Quill Harbor Media calls all of its outstanding callable preferred stock. Preferred Stock $40,000 and Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock $55,000 are removed. Cash paid at the call price is $100,000. The correct treatment of the $5,000 difference is:

- A) Debit Loss on Retirement of Preferred Stock $5,000 (income statement loss)  
- B) Debit Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock $5,000  
- C) **Debit Retained Earnings $5,000**  
- D) Credit Gain on Retirement of Preferred Stock $5,000  

**Answer:** **C.** On call/recall of preferred, the issuer derecognizes preferred stock and related PIC—PS and reduces cash. Any **debit difference** (cash paid > equity carrying amount of preferred) is recorded in **retained earnings**; any credit difference is additional paid-in capital. No gain or loss is recognized in net income for retirement of equity preferred.
