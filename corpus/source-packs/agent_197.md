# Agent 197 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-1

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Determine costs to capitalize for land, land improvements, equipment, buildings, and construction in process  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize costs necessary to acquire and bring each plant asset to the condition and location for its intended use (historical cost); period costs (training, post-ready interest, annual property taxes, ordinary repairs) are expensed
- **Classification by category:** **Land** (no depreciation)—purchase price, commissions, title/legal, permanent grading/clearing, net razing costs; **Land improvements** (depreciate)—parking lots, fencing, driveways, sprinklers, lighting; **Equipment**—invoice net of cash discounts taken, sales tax, freight, installation, testing; **Buildings**—purchase or construction costs of the structure (not land); **Construction in process (CIP)**—accumulate self-construction / progress billings / excavation / permits / architecture until ready, then reclassify
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** cost rollforward by PPE category; straight-line depreciation of depreciable assets after placed in service; **no depreciation on land**; **no depreciation on CIP** until completion and placement into service
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** record depreciation for assets in service; reclass CIP → Building (or Equipment) upon completion
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove cost and accumulated depreciation on disposal (gain/loss); **settle CIP** by transferring accumulated construction costs into the completed asset account (and, if self-constructed cost > fair value, recognize a current-period loss for the excess)

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Self-constructed equipment, fair-value ceiling, subsequent dep schedule, disposal
**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Self-constructed asset: accumulate CIP (materials, labor, overhead); completion JE with fair-value ceiling (cost > FV → loss); subsequent measurement schedule and period-end dep; disposal settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironridge Fabrication Inc.** (calendar year-end) constructs specialized production equipment for its own use during the first half of **Year 1**. Construction costs accumulated in **Construction in Process** are:

| CIP component | Amount |
|---|---:|

| Direct labor | 210,000 |
| Incremental (variable) overhead | 35,000 |
| Applied general (fixed) overhead | 25,000 |
| **Total CIP** | **$365,000** |

Construction is completed and the equipment is ready for use on **June 30, Year 1**. The fair value of a similar asset of equal capacity and quality on that date is **$350,000**. Ironridge uses straight-line depreciation, salvage **$20,000**, useful life **10 years**. Depreciation begins when the asset is placed in service (**July 1, Year 1**); Year 1 takes **6/12** of a full year.

On **December 31, Year 3**, after recording Year 3 depreciation, Ironridge sells the equipment for **$255,000** cash.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **summary JE** to accumulate all construction costs in CIP (credit Cash / various for simplicity, or credit Cash).  
b. Prepare the **June 30, Year 1 completion / settlement JE** transferring CIP to Equipment, applying the **fair-value ceiling**.  
c. Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** of cost, annual depreciation, and end-of-year carrying amount for Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 (before disposal).  
d. Prepare the **period-end depreciation JEs** for December 31, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 disposal JE**.  
f. Briefly explain why equipment is **not** recorded at the full $365,000 construction cost.

**Key insight:** CIP is a **temporary holding account**. At completion, settle CIP into Equipment (or Building). If cost > FV, the settlement JE both **capitalizes at FV** and **recognizes a construction loss**—then the subsequent measurement schedule runs only on the capitalized amount.

### Q4 — MC — Capitalize to Land vs expense vs land improvement
**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Classification — which acquisition-related costs are capitalized as Land (nondepreciable) versus Land Improvements or period expense  
**Question:**  
On March 1, **Northlake Foods Inc.** purchases a tract of land to use as the site for a new distribution warehouse. Which of the following costs should be **capitalized as Land** (not Land Improvements and not period expense)?

- A) Annual property taxes for the first year of Northlake’s ownership after the land is acquired  
- B) Cost of razing an existing structure on the land, reduced by proceeds from salvaged materials  
- C) Cost of paving a parking lot and installing fencing after the warehouse is built  
- D) Interest on a note payable used to finance the land purchase after the land is ready for its intended use as a building site  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Razing costs necessary to prepare land as a building site are part of the **historical cost of land**, reduced by salvage proceeds. Land is not depreciated.  
A is a **period expense** (ownership-period property tax).  
C is **Land Improvements** (depreciable; temporary site enhancements).  
D is **Interest Expense** once the land is already in the condition/location for intended use (not a qualifying construction period under this LO’s basic acquisition rules; contrast interest during active construction of a qualifying asset under LO 11-4).
