# Agent 328 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-1

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Determine costs to capitalize for land, land improvements, equipment, buildings, and construction in process  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Historical cost / capitalize:** costs necessarily incurred to bring PP&E to the condition and location for its intended use (ASC 360 / ASC 835 guidance in chapter)
- **Land (no depreciation):** purchase price, commissions, title/legal/closing, permanent clearing/grading/surveying, net razing costs (razing − salvage), government assessments for permanent improvements
- **Land improvements (depreciate):** parking lots, fencing, driveways, sprinklers, lighting, landscaping that must be replaced
- **Equipment:** invoice net of cash discount taken, sales tax, freight/insurance in transit, installation, setup/testing/trial runs — **exclude** employee training and interest after the asset is ready for use
- **Buildings vs CIP:** construction costs (architecture, excavation, foundation, progress billings, self-construction materials/labor/overhead) accumulate in **Construction in process** until complete; reclassify CIP → Building (or Equipment) when ready; **no depreciation on CIP**
- **Self-constructed assets:** materials, labor, incremental and/or applied overhead; if cost > fair value of similar asset, excess is a **current-period loss**
- **Period expenses:** training, interest on debt for assets already ready, annual property taxes/insurance after acquisition, ordinary repairs
- **Initial recognition JE:** debit asset categories and expenses; credit cash (or AP)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** cost, accumulated depreciation, carrying amount by category after placed in service
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** straight-line depreciation only after placement into service; partial-year fractions; no dep on land or CIP
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove cost and AD on sale (G/L); settle CIP by transfer to completed asset account (and loss if cost > FV)

Capitalized construction cost = \(120{,}000 + 280{,}000 + 35{,}000 + 25{,}000 = \mathbf{\$460{,}000}\).

**b. July 1, Year 1 — CIP settlement with FV ceiling**

Construction cost \(460{,}000\) **exceeds** FV \(440{,}000\) by \(\mathbf{\$20{,}000}\). Equipment is recorded at **fair value** (maximum); excess is a current-period **loss**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 440,000 | |
| Loss on Construction of Equipment | 20,000 | |
| Construction in Process | | 460,000 |
| *Settle CIP: capitalize at FV; expense excess over FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(440{,}000 + 20{,}000 = 460{,}000\) = Cr 460,000. **Balanced.**  
*(If FV had been ≥ $460,000, Equipment would be debited for full $460,000 with no loss.)*

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

Depreciable base: \(440{,}000 - 40{,}000 = \$400{,}000\).  
Annual SL: \(400{,}000 / 8 = \$50{,}000\).  
Year 1 (placed July 1): \(50{,}000 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$25{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 25,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 25,000 |
| *Period-end: half-year SL depreciation after placement into service* | | |

**Check:** Dr 25,000 = Cr 25,000. **Balanced.**  
*Depreciation begins only when the asset is ready and placed in service — not while costs sit in CIP.*

BV at disposal: \(440{,}000 - 75{,}000 = \mathbf{\$365{,}000}\).  
Proceeds \(355{,}000\) → **loss \(10{,}000\)**.

After settlement from CIP, **period-end adjusting entries** allocate cost (capped at FV less salvage) over useful life starting only after placement into service.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of acquisition costs (Land vs Land Improvements vs expense)

**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Classification — which costs are capitalized as **Land** (nondepreciable) versus land improvements or period expense  
**Question:**  
**Ridgeway Packaging Co.** incurs the following costs when acquiring a plant site for a new facility. Which cost is capitalized entirely as **Land** (not Land Improvements and not a period expense)?

- A) Cost of asphalt paving for the employee parking lot  
- B) Cost to demolish an existing warehouse on the site, net of salvage proceeds from materials  
- C) First-year property taxes for the period after the land was acquired and placed in use as a building site  
- D) Cost of a chain-link fence around the perimeter of the property  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Razing (demolition) costs of structures on land acquired for redevelopment or as a building site are capitalized to **Land**, reduced by salvage proceeds. These costs prepare the land for general use and are not depreciated.  
A and D are **land improvements** (subject to depreciation). C is a **period expense** (annual property tax maintains/protects the asset for the current period only, after acquisition).

### Q5 — MC — CIP vs Building capitalization timing

**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Classification / method — when costs move from CIP to Building and when depreciation begins  
**Question:**  
On October 1, Year 1, **Sable Ridge Foods** begins constructing a warehouse. Through December 31, Year 1, it pays architecture, excavation, foundation, and progress billings totaling $480,000, all charged to Construction in Process. The warehouse is completed and ready for use on **March 1, Year 2**. Straight-line depreciation is used. Which statement is correct?

- A) Depreciation on the warehouse should begin on October 1, Year 1, when construction expenditures first occur  
- B) At December 31, Year 1, the $480,000 should be reclassified from CIP to Building even though construction is incomplete  
- C) The $480,000 remains in CIP at December 31, Year 1; on March 1, Year 2, CIP is reclassified to Building and depreciation begins when the asset is placed in service  
- D) Architecture and excavation are period expenses; only progress billings are capitalized to CIP  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Costs accumulate in **Construction in process** while the asset is under construction and are reported as a long-term (PPE) asset. Upon completion and placement into service, costs shift from CIP to **Building**, and **depreciation begins only then**. A is wrong (no dep while under construction). B is wrong (reclass only upon completion). D is wrong (architecture, excavation, and related construction costs are capitalized to CIP/Building).
