# Agent 256 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-2

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for change in accounting principle  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Change in accounting principle:** voluntary substitution of one acceptable GAAP principle for another (preferability required), or mandated change when prior principle is no longer GAAP
- **Retrospective application (default):** restate all comparative periods under the **new** principle; adjust opening assets/liabilities and **retained earnings** for cumulative direct effects of periods before those presented
- **Initial recognition JE (books catch-up):** on the first day of the year of change, Dr/Cr **Inventory** (or other BS account) and **Retained Earnings** for the full cumulative direct effect through the day before the change (prior P&L accounts are closed)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year **COGS rollforward** (Beg inv + Purchases − End inv) under the new method; period-specific NI bridge (old method vs new method); comparative income, inventory, and RE presentation schedules
- **Period-end / year-of-change measurement:** after the change date, measure inventory and COGS under the **new** principle only
- **Direct vs indirect effects:** only **direct** effects (and related tax effects) enter the cumulative RE entry; **indirect** effects (bonus, royalties based on earnings) are recognized in the **current period as incurred**, not restated into prior periods
- **Settlement JE:** pay (settle) any current-period indirect-effect liability (e.g., Bonus Payable, Royalties Payable)
- **Prospective exception:** when retrospective application is **impracticable** (classic case: change **to LIFO** and prior layers cannot be reconstructed), apply the new principle as of the earliest practicable date; **no** cumulative RE catch-up; beginning inventory becomes the LIFO base layer
- **Not a change in principle:** initial adoption for new/immaterial transactions; adoption because transaction type changed; correction of non-GAAP application (error — LO 21-4)
- **Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-1):** nature and preferability reason; method of applying the change; effects on income/line items/EPS for current and restated prior periods; cumulative effect on RE as of beginning of earliest period presented; impracticability reasons and alternative if applicable; indirect effects if recognized

### Q1 — CORE — LIFO→FIFO retrospective: COGS/NI/RE schedules (emphasis), cumulative JE, indirect settlement
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Retrospective inventory principle change — subsequent measurement schedules (COGS rollforward, period-specific NI bridge, comparative IS/RE/inventory); January 1 cumulative Inventory/RE JE; Year 3 measurement under FIFO; current-period indirect bonus JE and cash settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Wholesale Corp.** (calendar year-end) began operations on **January 1, Year 1** using **LIFO** for financial reporting. On **January 1, Year 3**, Riverbend voluntarily changes to **FIFO** because FIFO more faithfully represents physical flow after a new warehouse management system (preferable). Ignore income taxes.

Inventory and operating data:

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory — LIFO | $0 | $55,000 | $62,000 |
| Beginning inventory — FIFO | 0 | 63,000 | 78,000 |
| Purchases | 240,000 | 270,000 | 295,000 |
| Ending inventory — LIFO | 55,000 | 62,000 | 70,000 |
| Ending inventory — FIFO | 63,000 | 78,000 | 95,000 |
| Sales | 280,000 | 360,000 | 390,000 |
| Operating expenses (excl. bonus) | 45,000 | 48,000 | 50,000 |

As originally issued under LIFO: Year 1 net income **$50,000**; Year 2 net income **$49,000**; retained earnings end of Year 2 under LIFO **$99,000**.

Riverbend’s executive plan pays a bonus based on income. After the change, the board agrees that the **Year 2** bonus (to be paid in Year 3) would have been **$1,200** higher under FIFO. The original Year 2 bonus has already been recorded; the **$1,200** incremental amount will be recognized and paid in **Year 3**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 initial recognition JE** for the retrospective change from LIFO to FIFO (direct effect only; ignore taxes). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement COGS schedule** under both LIFO and FIFO for Years 1–3 (Beg + Purchases − End).  
c. Prepare a **period-specific net income bridge schedule** (NI under LIFO vs FIFO) for Years 1–2 and the cumulative effect at the change date.  
d. Prepare **three-year comparative income statements** as reported **after** the change (all years under FIFO).  
e. Prepare the **comparative inventory presentation** (Dec 31 Year 2 and Year 3) and a **two-year comparative statement of retained earnings** (Year 2 and Year 3) after the change, including the cumulative-effect line on the earliest year presented.  
f. Prepare the **Year 3 JE for the indirect effect** ($1,200 bonus) and the **settlement JE** when the incremental bonus is paid in cash.  
g. **Classification / disclosure:** Is this retrospective or prospective? List key ASC 250 disclosure elements for the year of change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — cumulative direct effect (January 1, Year 3)**

Inventory under FIFO at Jan 1, Year 3 = **$78,000**  
Inventory under LIFO at Jan 1, Year 3 = **$62,000**  
Cumulative difference (direct effect) = $78,000 − $62,000 = **$16,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 16,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 16,000 |
| *Retrospective cumulative effect of change from LIFO to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,000 = Cr 16,000. **Balanced.**

Cross-check via income differences (parts b–c): Y1 +$8,000 + Y2 +$8,000 = **$16,000** ✓  
RE end Y2: LIFO $99,000; FIFO $115,000; difference **$16,000** ✓

**b. Subsequent measurement — COGS schedule (emphasis)**

**COGS under LIFO (as originally computed)**

| Year | Beg inv (LIFO) | Purchases | End inv (LIFO) | COGS (LIFO) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 2 | 55,000 | 270,000 | 62,000 | **263,000** |
| 3 | 62,000 | 295,000 | 70,000 | **287,000** |

**COGS under FIFO (restated / going forward)**

| Year | Beg inv (FIFO) | Purchases | End inv (FIFO) | COGS (FIFO) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | $240,000 | $63,000 | **$177,000** |
| 2 | 63,000 | 270,000 | 78,000 | **255,000** |

Checks:  
Y1: $0 + $240,000 − $63,000 = $177,000  
Y2: $63,000 + $270,000 − $78,000 = $255,000

- Year 2 NI as restated (**$57,000**) ≠ Year 2 NI as originally issued (**$49,000**).  
- Full books catch-up on Jan 1 Y3 is still **$16,000** (covers both Y1 and Y2 effects).

**f. Indirect effect and settlement (Year 3)**

*Recognize incremental Year 2 bonus as current-period cost (not retrospective to Year 2)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |

| Compensation Expense | 1,200 | |

*Settlement when paid*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonus Payable | 1,200 | |

**Check:** Dr 1,200 = Cr 1,200. **Balanced.**

**g. Classification / disclosure**

- **Type:** Voluntary **change in accounting principle** (LIFO → FIFO).  
- **Application:** **Retrospective** (not prospective; not a change in estimate).  
- **Direct effects only** in the cumulative RE/inventory entry; **indirect** bonus is current-period.  
- **Disclosures (ASC 250-10-50-1):** nature of and reason for the change, including **why FIFO is preferable**; method of applying the change; description of prior-period information retrospectively adjusted; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, affected line items, and EPS for current and restated prior periods; cumulative effect on retained earnings as of the beginning of the earliest period presented; if indirect effects recognized — description and amounts (and per-share if applicable).

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedules** (COGS rollforward and NI bridge) prove that Δ Inventory at the change date equals the cumulative pretax income difference closed to RE. Comparative statements then restate every period shown under the **new** principle so users can compare apples to apples; the books catch-up is a single opening Inventory/RE entry.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Average-cost→FIFO retrospective schedules twin
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: cumulative direct-effect JE; subsequent COGS and NI schedules under FIFO; comparative RE and inventory; Year-of-change measurement; indirect royalty current JE and settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Northvale Supply Co.** (calendar year-end) used the **weighted-average cost** method for inventory since inception (**January 1, Year 1**). On **January 1, Year 3**, Northvale changes to **FIFO** for financial reporting (preferable for matching more recent costs to ending inventory in a rising-price environment). Ignore taxes.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory — Average | $0 | $40,000 | $48,000 |
| Beginning inventory — FIFO | 0 | 52,000 | 70,000 |
| Purchases | 180,000 | 210,000 | 240,000 |
| Ending inventory — Average | 40,000 | 48,000 | 55,000 |
| Ending inventory — FIFO | 52,000 | 70,000 | 88,000 |
| Sales | 220,000 | 300,000 | 335,000 |
| Operating expenses (excl. royalties) | 35,000 | 38,000 | 40,000 |

As issued under average cost: Year 1 NI = **$45,000**; Year 2 NI = **$60,000**; RE end Year 2 under average = **$105,000**.

A supplier royalty agreement pays a percentage of net income. After restatement, the Year 2 royalty payable (settled in Year 3) is **$250** higher under FIFO than originally recorded. Record and pay the incremental amount in Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (average → FIFO).  
b. Prepare the **FIFO COGS schedule** for Years 1–3 and a **NI schedule** under FIFO for each year.  
c. Prepare **comparative retained earnings** for Year 2 and Year 3 after the change.  
d. State December 31, Year 2 inventory as presented on the **Year 3 comparative balance sheet**.  
e. Prepare the **indirect-effect JE** and **cash settlement JE** for the $250 incremental royalty in Year 3.  
f. Briefly state why the cumulative RE adjustment equals the inventory difference at the change date (ignore taxes).

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 3 — initial cumulative JE**

FIFO inventory Jan 1 Y3 = **$70,000**  
Average inventory Jan 1 Y3 = **$48,000**  
Cumulative direct effect = **$22,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 22,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 22,000 |
| *Retrospective change from average cost to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 22,000 = Cr 22,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement — FIFO COGS and NI schedule**

| Year | Beg (FIFO) | Purchases | End (FIFO) | COGS (FIFO) | Sales | OpEx | **NI (FIFO)** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | $180,000 | $52,000 | **$128,000** | $220,000 | $35,000 | **$57,000** |
| 2 | 52,000 | 210,000 | 70,000 | **192,000** | 300,000 | 38,000 | **$70,000** |
| 3 | 70,000 | 240,000 | 88,000 | **222,000** | 335,000 | 40,000 | **$73,000** |

Average-cost NI check (as given / recomputed):  
Y1: 220,000 − (0 + 180,000 − 40,000) − 35,000 = 220 − 140 − 35 = **$45,000**  
Y2: 300,000 − (40,000 + 210,000 − 48,000) − 38,000 = 300 − 202 − 38 = **$60,000**  
Period-specific income increases: Y1 **+$12,000**; Y2 **+$10,000**; cumulative **+$22,000** ✓

**c. Comparative retained earnings**

| | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning balance, as previously reported (average) | $45,000 | $127,000 |
| Add: Cumulative effect of change to FIFO on prior years | 12,000 | — |
| Beginning balance, as adjusted | 57,000 | 127,000 |
| Net income (FIFO) | 70,000 | 73,000 |
| **Ending balance** | **$127,000** | **$200,000** |

(Year 2 ending under FIFO = 57k + 70k = 127k; RE under average end Y2 was 105k; 127 − 105 = 22k cumulative at change date.)

**d. Comparative inventory presentation**

December 31, Year 2 inventory on Year 3 comparative balance sheet (FIFO) = **$70,000** (not the $48,000 average amount originally reported).

**e. Indirect royalty — current period + settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Royalty Expense | 250 | |
| Royalties Payable | | 250 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 250. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Royalties Payable | 250 | |
| Cash | | 250 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 250. **Balanced.**

**f. Why inventory difference = RE adjustment**

Ignoring taxes, every dollar of higher ending inventory under the new principle is a dollar of lower cumulative COGS and higher cumulative pretax income closed to RE. At the change date, **Δ Inventory = cumulative Δ NI = Δ Retained Earnings** (direct effects only).

**Key insight:** Same retrospective path as LIFO→FIFO with fully changed company and amounts: the **schedules** quantify period-specific effects; the books entry on day one is Inventory/RE equal to the full cumulative inventory difference.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Change to LIFO when retrospective is impracticable (prospective COGS schedule)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Prospective application when retrospective is impracticable; LIFO base layer = beginning inventory; subsequent COGS schedule with mixed methods across comparative years; no cumulative RE JE; period-end Year 3 measurement under LIFO  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Retail Partners Inc.** reported inventory under **FIFO** for Year 1 and Year 2. On **January 1, Year 3**, Lakeshore changes to **LIFO**. After every reasonable effort, management **cannot reconstruct LIFO layers** for prior periods (purchase-price history for old layers is unavailable). Lakeshore can apply LIFO only from Year 3 forward. Year 3 ending inventory under LIFO is determined to be **$64,000**.

Data:

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO going forward) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $58,000 | $72,000 |
| Purchases | 200,000 | 235,000 | 260,000 |
| Ending inventory | 58,000 (FIFO) | 72,000 (FIFO) | 64,000 (LIFO) |
| Sales | 250,000 | 310,000 | 340,000 |
| Operating expenses | 42,000 | 45,000 | 47,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Is retrospective application required? Explain using ASC 250 impracticability.  
b. Prepare any **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (or state that none is made).  
c. Prepare the **three-year comparative COGS subsequent measurement schedule** under the approach Lakeshore actually uses.  
d. Compute **Year 3 net income** under the prospective LIFO approach (period-end measurement).  
e. Optional contrast: If Year 3 ending inventory under FIFO would have been **$90,000**, what would Year 3 COGS and NI have been under continued FIFO?  
f. **Presentation / disclosure:** How do comparative Year 2 vs Year 3 principles differ on the Year 3 report? What must be disclosed about impracticability?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Retrospective not required**

Change **to LIFO** with inability to reconstruct prior layers meets the **impracticability** exception (ASC 250-10-45-9): after every reasonable effort, the entity cannot apply the requirement (and/or would need unverifiable assumptions/estimates about prior periods). Therefore apply the new principle **prospectively** as of the earliest practicable date (beginning of Year 3).

**b. Cumulative-effect JE**

**No journal entry** on January 1, Year 3 to restate Inventory or Retained Earnings for prior years. Beginning inventory of **$72,000** (FIFO carrying amount) becomes the **LIFO base layer**.

**c. Comparative COGS schedule (prospective — subsequent measurement emphasis)**

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $58,000 | $72,000 |
| Purchases | 200,000 | 235,000 | 260,000 |
| Less ending inventory | 58,000 | 72,000 | 64,000 |
| **Cost of goods sold** | **$142,000** | **$221,000** | **$268,000** |

Years 1–2 COGS remain as previously computed under FIFO; only Year 3 uses LIFO ending inventory.  
Checks: Y1 $200k − $58k = $142k; Y2 $58k + $235k − $72k = $221k; Y3 $72k + $260k − $64k = $268k.

**d. Year 3 net income (prospective LIFO — period-end)**

NI = Sales − COGS − OpEx = $340,000 − $268,000 − $47,000 = **$25,000**

**e. Continued FIFO contrast (Year 3 only)**

COGS if FIFO EI $90,000: $72,000 + $260,000 − $90,000 = **$242,000**  
NI if FIFO: $340,000 − $242,000 − $47,000 = **$51,000**  
Difference in Year 3 NI: LIFO NI is **$26,000 lower** than continued FIFO ($51,000 − $25,000), equal to the $26,000 lower ending inventory ($90,000 − $64,000).

**f. Presentation / disclosure**

- Year 3 comparative statements show **Year 2 still under FIFO** and **Year 3 under LIFO** — principles are **not uniform** across periods (comparability impaired).  
- Disclose: nature and reason for the change (preferability); that retrospective application is **impracticable** and **why**; description of the **alternative method** used (prospective from Year 3; base layer = beginning inventory).  
- Do **not** restate prior inventory or RE for unavailable LIFO layers.

**Key insight:** Retrospective is the default for voluntary principle changes, but a change **to LIFO** is the textbook case where **prospective** application applies because prior-period layers cannot be rebuilt — the COGS schedule shows mixed methods across comparative years.

### Q4 — CORE — Period-end / presentation focus: average→FIFO change-date JE, inventory schedule, Year 3 rollforward
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Period-of-change cumulative JE from ending inventory differences; comparative BS inventory presentation; Year 3 subsequent inventory rollforward under new method; earliest-period cumulative effect on RE  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironwood Goods LLC** changes from **average cost to FIFO** on **January 1, Year 3**. Ignore taxes. Inventory balances at prior year-ends:

| Inventory at December 31 | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ending inventory, FIFO | $82,000 | $100,000 |
| Ending inventory, average cost | 60,000 | 72,000 |

Year 3 activity under FIFO: beginning inventory (FIFO) **$100,000**; purchases **$340,000**; ending inventory (FIFO) **$112,000**; sales **$520,000**; operating expenses **$62,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 entry** to record the accounting change.  
b. Show how the **December 31, Year 2 inventory** is presented on the comparative December 31, Year 3 balance sheet (amounts material).  
c. Prepare a **Year 3 inventory rollforward schedule** under FIFO and compute Year 3 COGS and net income.  
d. **Disclosure:** What cumulative effect amount is disclosed as of the beginning of the earliest period presented if Year 2 and Year 3 are comparative (i.e., earliest year presented is Year 2)?  
e. Reconcile: how does the $28,000 books entry on Jan 1 Year 3 relate to the Year 1-only cumulative effect in part d?

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 3 cumulative JE**

Difference at Dec 31 Year 2 / Jan 1 of change year:  
FIFO EI Year 2 $100,000 − Average EI Year 2 $72,000 = **$28,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 28,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 28,000 |
| *Retrospective change from average cost to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,000 = Cr 28,000. **Balanced.**

(Of the $28,000, $22,000 arose by end of Year 1 [$82k − $60k] and an additional $6,000 in Year 2 [($100k − $72k) − ($82k − $60k)].)

**b. Comparative inventory presentation**

On the Year 3 comparative balance sheet, December 31, Year 2 inventory is reported at the **FIFO** amount **$100,000** (restated), not the $72,000 average amount from the original Year 2 report.

**c. Year 3 inventory rollforward (FIFO) — subsequent measurement schedule**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory (FIFO, after cumulative adj.) | $100,000 |
| + Purchases | 340,000 |
| Goods available for sale | 440,000 |
| − Ending inventory (FIFO) | 112,000 |
| **COGS** | **$328,000** |

Year 3 NI = $520,000 − $328,000 − $62,000 = **$130,000**

**d. Cumulative effect disclosed for earliest period presented (Year 2)**

If Year 2 is the earliest year presented, the cumulative effect **prior to Year 2** (i.e., through Year 1) is:  
FIFO Y1 EI $82,000 − Average Y1 EI $60,000 = **$22,000**  
That **$22,000** adjusts beginning RE of Year 2 on the comparative RE statement.

**e. Reconciliation of books entry vs presentation cumulative line**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Effect arising in Year 1 (prior to earliest year presented) | $22,000 |
| Effect arising in Year 2 (period-specific restatement of Year 2 NI) | 6,000 |
| **Full books catch-up Jan 1 Year 3 (Δ Inventory)** | **$28,000** |

The ledger entry on Jan 1 Year 3 still brings the books from average to FIFO as of the change date (full $28,000). Presentation splits that total: $22,000 as an opening RE cumulative line for the earliest year presented (Year 2), and $6,000 embedded in restated Year 2 net income.

**Key insight:** Presentation restates comparative prior-year balances under the new principle; the **ledger** catch-up is a single opening entry equal to the **full** cumulative inventory difference at the change date. The Year 3 rollforward schedule then measures inventory and COGS under FIFO only.

### Q5 — MC — Method of applying a principle change (classification)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Classification of retrospective vs prospective application for voluntary change in inventory method  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 4, **Copperline Distributors** voluntarily changes its inventory costing method from FIFO to LIFO. After every reasonable effort, Copperline cannot determine LIFO inventory amounts for any year before Year 4. How should Copperline report the change?

- A) Restate all prior periods presented under LIFO and debit Retained Earnings for the cumulative decrease in inventory  
- B) Apply LIFO prospectively from Year 4, using beginning Year 4 inventory as the LIFO base layer; do not restate prior periods  
- C) Report the cumulative effect as a separate line on the Year 4 income statement (below income from continuing operations)  
- D) Treat the change as a change in accounting estimate effected by a change in principle and revise prior depreciation-style schedules  

**Answer:** **B.** When retrospective application is impracticable (typical for a change **to LIFO**), the new principle is applied **prospectively** from the earliest practicable date. Beginning inventory becomes the LIFO base; prior periods remain under the old method. A is the retrospective inventory treatment used when prior amounts **can** be determined (e.g., LIFO→FIFO). C is the obsolete “cumulative effect on income” presentation (pre-ASC 250 style), not current GAAP for voluntary principle changes. D confuses this with depreciation method changes (LO 21-1), which are prospective as estimate-effected-by-principle — different fact pattern.

### Q6 — MC — Direct vs indirect effects (classification)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Direct effects in cumulative RE entry; indirect bonus effects recognized currently, not restated  
**Question:**  
**Maplecrest Appliances** retrospectively changes from LIFO to FIFO on January 1, Year 5. The change increases inventory and retained earnings by $50,000 for periods before Year 5 (ignore taxes). Maplecrest’s board also retroactively increases the Year 4 profit-sharing bonus by $4,000 because restated Year 4 income is higher; the extra $4,000 will be paid in Year 5. Which statement is correct?

- A) Debit Inventory $54,000 and credit Retained Earnings $54,000 on January 1, Year 5  
- B) Debit Inventory $50,000 and credit Retained Earnings $50,000; record Compensation Expense $4,000 in Year 5 when the bonus is recognized  
- C) Restate Year 4 bonus expense upward by $4,000 in the comparative income statement and reduce opening RE by only $46,000  
- D) Report both the $50,000 and the $4,000 as a cumulative-effect line on the Year 5 income statement  

**Answer:** **B.** Retrospective application includes only **direct** effects (inventory and related tax effects). **Indirect** effects (bonus, profit sharing, royalties based on earnings) are **not** pushed into prior periods; if actually incurred, they are recognized in the **period of the change**. A incorrectly folds the indirect bonus into the cumulative RE entry. C incorrectly restates prior bonus expense for an indirect effect. D uses obsolete income-statement cumulative-effect presentation and mixes direct/indirect.
