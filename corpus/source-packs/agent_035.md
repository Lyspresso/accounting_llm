# Agent 35 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-3

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Prepare the investing activities section of the statement of cash flows  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Investing activities report **gross** cash inflows and outflows from changes in long-lived assets, nontrading investments, and nontrade loans
- **T-account / rollforward** analysis of PPE and Accumulated Depreciation to solve for cash purchases and cost/AD removed on disposal
- Reconstruct **cash proceeds** from sale of PPE from book value ± gain/loss
- **Initial recognition JEs** for cash purchases of PPE, investments, and loans made
- **Disposal / settlement JEs** for sale of equipment, sale of investments, and insurance recovery on destroyed assets
- Only cash paid at (or soon before/after) purchase of productive assets is an investing outflow; seller-financed portion is **noncash** (disclosed, not in SCF investing)
- Collection of nontrade loan principal = investing inflow; interest = operating (not investing)
- Equity/debt investments **not held specifically for resale** → purchase and sale cash flows are investing (gains/losses reconciling items in operating under the indirect method)

### Q1 — CORE — Lakeshore Industrial: full investing section with PPE T-accounts, purchase/sale JEs
**LO:** LO 22-3  
**Concept:** Prepare cash flows from investing activities — solve PPE purchases via T-accounts; record purchase and disposal JEs; report gross investing inflows and outflows  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Industrial Corp.** is preparing its statement of cash flows for the year ended **December 31, 2025**. Selected comparative balances and additional information follow.

| Account | Dec 31, 2024 | Dec 31, 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | $480,000 | $620,000 |
| Accumulated depreciation—equipment | 150,000 | 185,000 |
| Equity investments (FV through NI; **not** held for trading/resale) | 40,000 | 55,000 |
| Long-term notes receivable (nontrade) | 80,000 | 50,000 |

**Additional information for 2025:**
1. Equipment that cost **$70,000** with accumulated depreciation of **$45,000** was sold for **$32,000** cash.  
2. Depreciation expense on equipment was **$80,000**.  
3. All equipment purchases were for **cash** (no notes or stock issued for equipment).  
4. Equity investments were purchased for **$35,000** cash. Investments with a carrying amount of **$20,000** were sold for **$18,000** cash. No unrealized fair-value adjustment affected the year-end carrying amount beyond purchases and sales.  
5. Lakeshore made a new nontrade loan of **$15,000** cash and collected **$45,000** of principal on existing long-term notes receivable. Interest collected is **not** included in the $45,000.  
6. There were no other investing activities.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **December 31, 2025** depreciation adjusting entry.  
(b) Reconstruct the **Equipment** and **Accumulated depreciation—equipment** T-accounts (or schedules). Solve for **cash purchases of equipment**.  
(c) Prepare the journal entry to record the **sale of equipment**.  
(d) Prepare the journal entries for the **purchase of equipment**, **purchase of equity investments**, **sale of equity investments**, **loan advanced**, and **principal collection** on notes receivable.  
(e) Prepare the **Cash flows from investing activities** section of the 2025 statement of cash flows.  
(f) Briefly state how the **$7,000 gain** on equipment sale and the **$2,000 loss** on investment sale are treated under the **indirect** operating section (do not prepare the full operating section).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Period-end adjusting entry — depreciation**  
```
Dr Depreciation Expense ..................... 80,000
   Cr Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment .......... 80,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 80,000)

**(b) PPE rollforward schedules (subsequent measurement / analysis)**

**Equipment**

| | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beg. bal. | 480,000 | |
| Purchases (plug) | **210,000** | |
| Cost of equipment sold | | 70,000 |
| End. bal. | 620,000 | |

Check: \(480{,}000 + 210{,}000 - 70{,}000 = 620{,}000\).

**Accumulated depreciation—equipment**

| | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beg. bal. | | 150,000 |
| Depreciation expense | | 80,000 |
| AD removed on sale | 45,000 | |
| End. bal. | | 185,000 |

Check: \(150{,}000 + 80{,}000 - 45{,}000 = 185{,}000\).

**Cash purchases of equipment = $210,000.**

**(c) Sale of equipment (disposal JE)**  
Book value = \(70{,}000 - 45{,}000 = 25{,}000\); cash proceeds \(32{,}000\); **gain = $7,000**.  
```
Dr Cash ..................................... 32,000
Dr Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment ...... 45,000
   Cr Equipment ...................................... 70,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Equipment ......................  7,000
```
(Dr 77,000 = Cr 77,000)

**(d) Other investing-related journal entries**

Purchase of equipment (initial recognition — cash):  
```
Dr Equipment ............................... 210,000
   Cr Cash ......................................... 210,000
```

Purchase of equity investments:  
```
Dr Equity Investments ...................... 35,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 35,000
```

Sale of equity investments (carrying amount $20,000; proceeds $18,000):  
```
Dr Cash ..................................... 18,000
Dr Loss on Sale of Investments ..............  2,000
   Cr Equity Investments ............................ 20,000
```
(Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000)

Loan advanced (nontrade):  
```
Dr Long-Term Notes Receivable .............. 15,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 15,000
```

Principal collection:  
```
Dr Cash ..................................... 45,000
   Cr Long-Term Notes Receivable .................... 45,000
```

**Investment rollforward check:** \(40{,}000 + 35{,}000 - 20{,}000 = 55{,}000\).  
**Notes receivable rollforward check:** \(80{,}000 + 15{,}000 - 45{,}000 = 50{,}000\).

**(e) Cash flows from investing activities — 2025**

```
Cash flows from investing activities
  Proceeds from sale of equipment ............... $  32,000
  Proceeds from sale of equity investments ......    18,000
  Collection of long-term notes receivable ......    45,000
  Purchase of equipment .........................  (210,000)
  Purchase of equity investments ................   (35,000)
  Loans to others ...............................   (15,000)
Net cash used by investing activities .......... $(165,000)
```

Inflows \(32{,}000 + 18{,}000 + 45{,}000 = 95{,}000\); outflows \(210{,}000 + 35{,}000 + 15{,}000 = 260{,}000\); net \(95{,}000 - 260{,}000 = \mathbf{\$(165{,}000)}\).

**(f) Indirect operating treatment**  
- **Gain on sale of equipment $7,000:** deducted from net income in the operating section (full cash proceeds appear in investing).  
- **Loss on sale of investments $2,000:** added back to net income in the operating section (full cash proceeds appear in investing).

**Key insight:** Investing activities list **gross cash** amounts, not net book-value changes. Use PPE and AD T-accounts to solve for purchases when only beginning/ending balances and disposal facts are known; report **full cash proceeds** (not the gain) as the investing inflow.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Ridgeway Components twin
**LO:** LO 22-3  
**Concept:** Prepare cash flows from investing activities — all numbers changed (PPE T-account plug; investment and loan rollforwards)  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeway Components LLC** is preparing its investing activities section for the year ended **December 31, 2026**.

| Account | Dec 31, 2025 | Dec 31, 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | $360,000 | $510,000 |
| Accumulated depreciation—equipment | 95,000 | 120,000 |
| Equity investments (not held for trading) | 25,000 | 48,000 |
| Long-term notes receivable (nontrade) | 100,000 | 70,000 |

**Additional information for 2026:**
1. Equipment costing **$55,000** (accumulated depreciation **$40,000**) was sold for **$22,000** cash.  
2. Depreciation expense on equipment was **$65,000**.  
3. All equipment purchases were cash purchases.  
4. Equity investments purchased for cash **$40,000**; investments with carrying amount **$17,000** sold for **$19,000** cash. Ending balance reflects only purchases and sales (no residual FV adjustment needed for the plug).  
5. New nontrade loan advanced **$20,000** cash; principal collections on notes receivable **$50,000**.  
6. No other investing activities.

**Required:**  
(a) Solve for cash equipment purchases using Equipment and AD schedules.  
(b) Prepare the equipment **sale** JE and the equipment **purchase** JE.  
(c) Prepare investment purchase/sale JEs and the loan advanced / principal collection JEs.  
(d) Prepare the **Cash flows from investing activities** section and compute net cash provided (used).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Schedules**

**Equipment:** \(360{,}000 + \text{Purchases} - 55{,}000 = 510{,}000\) → **Purchases = $205,000**.

**Accumulated depreciation:** \(95{,}000 + 65{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 120{,}000\) (checks).

**(b) Disposal and purchase JEs**

Sale (BV \(55{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 15{,}000\); gain \(22{,}000 - 15{,}000 = 7{,}000\)):  
```
Dr Cash ..................................... 22,000
Dr Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment ...... 40,000
   Cr Equipment ...................................... 55,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Equipment ......................  7,000
```
(Dr 62,000 = Cr 62,000)

Purchase:  
```
Dr Equipment ............................... 205,000
   Cr Cash ......................................... 205,000
```

**(c) Investment and loan JEs**

```
Dr Equity Investments ...................... 40,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 40,000

Dr Cash ..................................... 19,000
   Cr Equity Investments ............................ 17,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Investments ...................  2,000

Dr Long-Term Notes Receivable .............. 20,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 20,000

Dr Cash ..................................... 50,000
   Cr Long-Term Notes Receivable .................... 50,000
```

Investment check: \(25{,}000 + 40{,}000 - 17{,}000 = 48{,}000\).  
Notes check: \(100{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 50{,}000 = 70{,}000\).

**(d) Cash flows from investing activities — 2026**

Cash flows from investing activities
  Proceeds from sale of equipment ............... $  22,000

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Copperline Metals: insurance settlement, partial cash PPE, patent purchase, noncash note
**LO:** LO 22-3  
**Concept:** Investing section with insurance recovery on destroyed PPE, land sale, cash-only portion of equipment purchase, and patent purchase; disclose noncash financing of PPE  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperline Metals Inc.** had the following investing-related events in **2025** (fiscal year-end December 31):

1. A fire completely destroyed a warehouse building. Original cost **$400,000**; accumulated depreciation at the date of the fire **$280,000**. Insurance paid Copperline **$150,000** cash in full settlement.  
2. Copperline purchased new production equipment with a cash price of **$275,000**. Payment: **$200,000** cash down and a **$75,000** three-year note payable to the seller.  
3. Purchased a patent for **$40,000** cash.  
4. Sold vacant land (cost **$60,000**) for **$85,000** cash.  
5. No other investing or PPE transactions. Depreciation on remaining assets is ignored for this problem (not needed).

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the journal entry to remove the destroyed building and record the **insurance settlement** (settlement/disposal JE).  
(b) Prepare the **initial recognition** journal entry for the equipment purchase (cash + note).  
(c) Prepare the land-sale JE and the patent-purchase JE.  
(d) Prepare the **Cash flows from investing activities** section for 2025.  
(e) Prepare the **noncash investing and financing activities** disclosure related to the equipment purchase.  
(f) Explain why the **$75,000 note** is **not** reported as an investing cash outflow (and is not a financing cash inflow) on the face of the SCF.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Insurance settlement / disposal of building**  
Carrying amount = \(400{,}000 - 280{,}000 = 120{,}000\); cash proceeds \(150{,}000\); **gain = $30,000**.  
```
Dr Cash .................................... 150,000
Dr Accumulated Depreciation—Building ...... 280,000
   Cr Building ...................................... 400,000
   Cr Gain on Insurance Settlement ..................  30,000
```
(Dr 430,000 = Cr 430,000)

**(b) Equipment purchase — initial recognition (mixed cash / noncash)**  
```
Dr Equipment ............................... 275,000
   Cr Cash ......................................... 200,000
   Cr Notes Payable—Seller .........................  75,000
```
(Dr 275,000 = Cr 275,000)

**(c) Land sale and patent**  
```
Dr Cash ..................................... 85,000
   Cr Land ........................................... 60,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Land ........................... 25,000

Dr Patent ................................... 40,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 40,000
```
(Land: Dr 85,000 = Cr 85,000; Patent: Dr = Cr = 40,000)

**(d) Cash flows from investing activities — 2025**

```
Cash flows from investing activities
  Proceeds from insurance settlement ............ $ 150,000
  Proceeds from sale of land ....................    85,000
  Purchase of equipment .........................  (200,000)
  Purchase of patent ............................   (40,000)
Net cash used by investing activities .......... $  (5,000)
```

Inflows \(150{,}000 + 85{,}000 = 235{,}000\); outflows \(200{,}000 + 40{,}000 = 240{,}000\); net \(\mathbf{\$(5{,}000)}\).

**Note:** Only the **$200,000 cash** paid for equipment is an investing outflow. Insurance proceeds are investing inflows because they compensate for loss of a productive asset (the related gain is removed from NI in the operating section under the indirect method).

**(e) Noncash disclosure**

```
Noncash investing and financing activities
  Purchased equipment by paying cash and issuing a long-term note:
    Cost of equipment ........................... $275,000
    Cash paid ...................................  200,000
    Note payable issued to seller ............... $ 75,000
```

**(f) Classification rationale**  
ASC 230 reports as investing outflows only amounts paid **at the time of purchase or soon before or after** for PPE. The seller-financed **$75,000** is a noncash exchange (asset acquired by assuming a liability). Subsequent principal payments on that note are **financing** outflows, not investing. Including $275,000 as investing would overstate the cash effect of the acquisition.

**Key insight:** The investing section is a **cash** statement: full insurance/land sale proceeds in, cash portion of PPE purchases out, and **seller financing disclosed as noncash**—never grossed into investing as if cash had changed hands.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of investing cash flows
**LO:** LO 22-3  
**Concept:** Identify which items are included as investing inflows or outflows (gross presentation)  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **correctly** reported in the **investing activities** section of the statement of cash flows under U.S. GAAP (ASC 230)?

- A) Depreciation expense of $50,000 is subtracted as an investing cash outflow.  
- B) Purchase of equipment for $100,000 cash is reported as a $100,000 investing outflow; a contemporaneous sale of different equipment for $30,000 cash is netted, showing a single $70,000 investing outflow.  
- C) Cash proceeds of $45,000 from sale of equipment (book value $38,000; gain $7,000) are reported as a **$45,000** investing inflow.  
- D) Principal collection of $12,000 and interest collection of $1,500 on a nontrade note receivable are both reported as investing inflows totaling $13,500.

**Answer:** **C.** Investing activities report the **full cash proceeds** from sale of PPE as an inflow; the gain is not the investing amount (the gain is removed from NI in the operating section under the indirect method).  

- A is wrong: depreciation is a noncash expense adjusted in **operating** (indirect method), not an investing flow.  
- B is wrong: ASC 230 requires **gross** presentation of investing inflows and outflows (purchase and sale reported separately).  
- D is wrong: **interest** collected is an **operating** inflow; only principal collection is investing.

### Q5 — MC — Net cash used by investing (insurance + rebuild)
**LO:** LO 22-3  
**Concept:** Compute net cash used by investing activities when insurance proceeds and cash construction of a replacement asset both occur  
**Question:**  
A tornado destroyed a building with original cost **$900,000** and carrying amount **$320,000**. Insurance paid the company **$280,000** cash. The company then constructed a replacement building for **$1,050,000** cash. Ignoring all other transactions, what is **net cash used by investing activities** related to these events?

- A) $1,050,000  
- B) $770,000  
- C) $730,000  
- D) $1,370,000  

**Answer:** **B. $770,000.**  
Investing inflow: insurance proceeds **$280,000**.  
Investing outflow: cash paid to construct building **$1,050,000**.  
Net cash used = \(1{,}050{,}000 - 280{,}000 = \mathbf{\$770{,}000}\).  

(The carrying amount and any loss of $40,000 affect the income statement / operating reconciling items, not the investing cash totals. Choice A ignores the insurance inflow; C incorrectly nets loss against construction; D adds cost instead of proceeds.)
