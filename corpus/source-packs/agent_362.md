# Agent 362 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-10

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Describe financing disclosures and analyses using leverage ratios  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Debt note disclosures:** nature of debt, interest rates, maturity dates, call/conversion, restrictions, pledged collateral, fair value of debt (when practical), unused lines/commitments
- **Five-year maturity disclosure (Demo 16-10A style):** combined aggregate principal maturities (and sinking-fund requirements) for each of the five years following the balance-sheet date, plus amount thereafter
- **Financial leverage ratios (Demo 16-10B style):** total liabilities-to-equity; total liabilities-to-total assets; times interest earned (income before taxes and interest / interest expense)
- **Initial recognition JE:** issue term notes, installment notes (often with PPE), and bonds at par
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** remaining-principal rollforward and five-year maturity table that reconciles to total long-term debt outstanding
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** record year-end interest; reclassify **current portion of long-term debt**; present current vs. noncurrent correctly for disclosure and ratios
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement JE:** principal installment payment; cash retirement of term debt at maturity
- **SCF tie-in (classification):** LTD proceeds and principal retirements → financing; cash interest → operating (indirect method starts with NI, which already includes interest expense)

### Q4 — MC — Five-year maturity disclosure requirement
**LO:** LO 16-10  
**Concept:** Identify required five-year aggregate maturity (and sinking-fund) disclosure for long-term borrowings  
**Question:**  
At December 31, Year 1, a company has several long-term borrowings with staggered principal payments. Which disclosure is **required** in the notes (GAAP)?

- A) Only the total long-term debt due after one year, as a single amount, with no annual breakdown  
- B) The combined aggregate amount of maturities and sinking-fund requirements for each of the **five years** following the latest balance-sheet date  
- C) A schedule of interest expense by year for the next five years only (principal optional)  
- D) Fair value of each individual loan, always measured using Level 1 inputs only  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 470 requires disclosure of the combined aggregate amount of maturities and sinking-fund requirements for all long-term borrowings for each of the five years following the date of the latest balance sheet presented. A is incomplete; C confuses interest with principal maturities; D overstates both the always-required nature of loan-by-loan FV and the Level 1-only claim (FV is disclosed when practical, with hierarchy level as applicable).

### Q5 — MC — Times interest earned components
**LO:** LO 16-10  
**Concept:** Correct formula for the times interest earned ratio  
**Question:**  
Which formula correctly computes the **times interest earned** ratio used in leverage analysis?

- A) Net income ÷ Interest expense  
- B) (Net income + Interest expense) ÷ Interest expense  
- C) Income before taxes and interest expense ÷ Interest expense  
- D) Total liabilities ÷ Interest expense  

**Answer:** **C.** Times interest earned = income before taxes and interest expense ÷ interest expense. It measures how many times current earnings can cover required interest. A uses after-interest, after-tax net income (understates coverage capacity relative to the standard definition); B is an incomplete pretax add-back (omits tax add-back structure / does not match the LO definition); D is not a coverage ratio.
