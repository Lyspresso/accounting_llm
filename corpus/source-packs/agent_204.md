# Agent 204 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-8

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for contributed property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Unconditional contribution of PPE recognized at fair value in the period received
- Contribution revenue = fair value of assets received − cash costs paid by recipient (Demo 11-8 pattern)
- Land portion of a donation is not depreciated; building/equipment is
- Subsequent measurement: straight-line depreciation on depreciable contributed assets
- Multi-period depreciation schedule (cost, annual dep, accum. dep, NBV)
- Period-end adjusting JE for depreciation
- Disposal of a previously contributed asset (remove cost and accum. dep; gain/loss)
- Donor-imposed conditions: defer contribution revenue until barrier is met (overview exception)

### Q1 — CORE — Lakeshore Packaging: donated plant, dep schedule, period-end JE, disposal
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of contributed land and building at FV; multi-year depreciation schedule; period-end dep JE; disposal of donated building  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, the City of Port Harbor unconditionally donated a warehouse building and the land under it to **Lakeshore Packaging Co.** to induce local expansion. A reliable appraisal fixed total fair value at **$720,000**, of which **25% is land** and **75% is building**. Lakeshore paid **$8,000 cash** for legal transfer costs. The city received no goods or services of equivalent value.  

Building estimates: remaining useful life **20 years**, residual value **$20,000**, **straight-line**, full year of depreciation in Year 1. Lakeshore’s fiscal year ends December 31.  

On **December 31, Year 4**, after recording Year 4 depreciation, Lakeshore **sold only the building** for **$430,000 cash** and retained the land.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry for the contribution received (and cash transfer costs).  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement (depreciation) schedule** for the building for **Years 1–4** showing: cost, annual depreciation, end-of-year accumulated depreciation, and end-of-year NBV.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting entry for depreciation (state whether the same entry repeats each year through Year 4).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 4** disposal entry for the building sale.  
e. State the **carrying amount of land** on December 31, Year 4 after the sale.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

Land FV = \(720{,}000 \times 0.25 = 180{,}000\)  
Building FV = \(720{,}000 \times 0.75 = 540{,}000\)  
Contribution revenue = \(720{,}000 - 8{,}000 = 712{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 540,000 | |
| Land | 180,000 | |
| Cash | | 8,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 712,000 |
| *To record unconditional contribution of plant at FV; transfer costs reduce contribution revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(540{,}000 + 180{,}000 = 720{,}000\); Cr \(8{,}000 + 712{,}000 = 720{,}000\). Balanced.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — building (Years 1–4)**  

Depreciable base = \(540{,}000 - 20{,}000 = 520{,}000\)  
Annual depreciation = \(520{,}000 / 20 = 26{,}000\)

| Year | Cost | Annual dep. | Accum. dep. (EOY) | NBV (EOY) |
|-----:|-----:|------------:|------------------:|----------:|
| 1 | $540,000 | $26,000 | $26,000 | **$514,000** |
| 2 | 540,000 | 26,000 | 52,000 | **488,000** |
| 3 | 540,000 | 26,000 | 78,000 | **462,000** |
| 4 | 540,000 | 26,000 | **104,000** | **$436,000** |

Rollforward checks:  
- Accum. after 4 years: \(26{,}000 \times 4 = 104{,}000\)  
- NBV end of Year 4: \(540{,}000 - 104{,}000 = 436{,}000\)  
- Also: \(514{,}000 - 3 \times 26{,}000 = 436{,}000\)

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 26,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 26,000 |
| *To record Year 1 SL depreciation on contributed building* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 26,000. Balanced.  
The **same $26,000** adjusting entry is recorded on December 31 of Years 2, 3, and 4 (straight-line, full years).

**d. December 31, Year 4 — disposal of building**  

Cash proceeds $430,000; NBV $436,000 → **loss $6,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 430,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | 104,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Building | 6,000 | |
| Building | | 540,000 |
| *To remove donated building and recognize loss on sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(430{,}000 + 104{,}000 + 6{,}000 = 540{,}000\) = Cr 540,000. Balanced.

**e. Land carrying amount after sale**  
Land remains on the books at **$180,000** (nondepreciable; not sold).

**Key insight:** Contributed PPE is capitalized at **fair value**; cash costs paid by the recipient **reduce Contribution Revenue** (not added to asset cost under the Demo 11-8 pattern). Subsequent measurement is ordinary depreciation on the **building FV**; land is not depreciated. Disposal uses the same cost/accum. dep. removal rules as purchased PPE.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Riverton Foods: donated plant, schedule, disposal
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of contributed land and building (number variant); multi-year dep schedule; period-end dep JE; disposal with gain  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, the Town of Millbrook unconditionally donated a processing plant (building and land) to **Riverton Foods Inc.** Appraised fair value of the property is **$960,000**, of which **30% is land** and **70% is building**. Riverton paid **$12,000 cash** for transfer and recording fees. No reciprocal goods or services were provided to the town.  

Building: remaining life **25 years**, residual value **$32,000**, **straight-line**, full-year convention. Year-end is December 31.  

On **December 31, Year 3**, after Year 3 depreciation, Riverton **sold the building only** for **$600,000 cash** (land retained).

**Required:**  
a. Record the January 1, Year 1 contribution JE.  
b. Prepare the building depreciation schedule for Years 1–3 (cost, annual dep, accum. dep, NBV).  
c. Record the December 31, Year 1 depreciation adjusting entry.  
d. Record the December 31, Year 3 disposal JE for the building.  
e. Compute total **contribution revenue** and total **depreciation expense** recognized for Years 1–3 related to this plant (building only for dep).

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

Land = \(960{,}000 \times 0.30 = 288{,}000\)  
Building = \(960{,}000 \times 0.70 = 672{,}000\)  
Contribution revenue = \(960{,}000 - 12{,}000 = 948{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 672,000 | |
| Land | 288,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 948,000 |
| *Unconditional contribution at FV; fees reduce contribution revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(672{,}000 + 288{,}000 = 960{,}000\); Cr \(12{,}000 + 948{,}000 = 960{,}000\). Balanced.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — building (Years 1–3)**  

Depreciable base = \(672{,}000 - 32{,}000 = 640{,}000\)  
Annual depreciation = \(640{,}000 / 25 = 25{,}600\)

| Year | Cost | Annual dep. | Accum. dep. (EOY) | NBV (EOY) |
|-----:|-----:|------------:|------------------:|----------:|
| 1 | $672,000 | $25,600 | $25,600 | **$646,400** |
| 2 | 672,000 | 25,600 | 51,200 | **620,800** |
| 3 | 672,000 | 25,600 | **76,800** | **$595,200** |

Checks: \(25{,}600 \times 3 = 76{,}800\); \(672{,}000 - 76{,}800 = 595{,}200\).

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 25,600 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 25,600 |
| *Year 1 SL depreciation on contributed building* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 25,600. Same amount each year through Year 3.

**d. December 31, Year 3 — disposal of building**  

Proceeds $600,000; NBV $595,200 → **gain $4,800**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 600,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | 76,800 | |
| Building | | 672,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Building | | 4,800 |
| *To remove donated building and recognize gain on sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(600{,}000 + 76{,}800 = 676{,}800\); Cr \(672{,}000 + 4{,}800 = 676{,}800\). Balanced.

**e. Totals (Years 1–3)**  
- Contribution revenue (Year 1 only): **$948,000**  
- Depreciation expense (building): \(25{,}600 \times 3 = \) **$76,800**  
- Land still on books: **$288,000**

**Key insight:** Changing the land/building split and residual changes the **depreciation schedule** and the disposal gain/loss, but the recognition rule is unchanged: **FV assets + cash costs reducing Contribution Revenue**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — equipment donation mid-year; partial-year schedule; scrap disposal
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Contributed equipment at FV; partial-year depreciation schedule; period-end adjusting JEs; disposal at scrap value  
**Scenario:**  
On **April 1, Year 1**, a nonprofit foundation unconditionally donated specialized packaging equipment to **Cedar Ridge Bottling LLC**. Reliable fair value of the equipment is **$180,000**. Cedar Ridge paid **$3,000 cash** for legal title transfer. The foundation received nothing of equivalent value.  

Equipment: estimated life **6 years**, residual value **$12,000**, **straight-line**, calendar year-end. Depreciation is taken for the **fraction of the year held**.  

On **December 31, Year 3**, after recording Year 3 depreciation, Cedar Ridge **scrapped** the equipment and received **$9,500 cash** from a salvage dealer.

**Required:**  
a. Record the April 1, Year 1 contribution JE.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing annual depreciation expense, year-end accum. dep., and year-end NBV (use months held for Year 1).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** period-end depreciation adjusting entries.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 3** depreciation adjusting entry and the **scrap disposal** entry (two entries).

**Answer key:**  

**a. April 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

Contribution revenue = \(180{,}000 - 3{,}000 = 177{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 180,000 | |
| Cash | | 3,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 177,000 |
| *Unconditional contribution of equipment at FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 180,000 = Cr \(3{,}000 + 177{,}000\). Balanced.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 1–3)**  

Full-year depreciation = \((180{,}000 - 12{,}000) / 6 = 168{,}000 / 6 = 28{,}000\)  
Year 1 (Apr 1–Dec 31 = 9 months): \(28{,}000 \times 9/12 = 21{,}000\)  
Years 2 and 3: full \(28{,}000\) each

| Year | Months | Dep. expense | Accum. dep. (EOY) | NBV (EOY) |
|-----:|-------:|-------------:|------------------:|----------:|
| 1 | 9 | **$21,000** | $21,000 | **$159,000** |
| 2 | 12 | **28,000** | 49,000 | **131,000** |
| 3 | 12 | **28,000** | **77,000** | **$103,000** |

Checks:  
- Total months by end of Y3: \(9 + 12 + 12 = 33\); \(28{,}000 \times 33/12 = 77{,}000\)  
- NBV: \(180{,}000 - 77{,}000 = 103{,}000\)

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs**  

December 31, Year 1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 21,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 21,000 |

December 31, Year 2:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 28,000 |

**Check:** Each JE balances (Dr = Cr).

**d. December 31, Year 3 — depreciation then disposal**  

Year 3 depreciation:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 28,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000.

Disposal (scrap): cash $9,500; NBV $103,000 → **loss $93,500**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 9,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 77,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 93,500 | |
| Equipment | | 180,000 |
| *To scrap contributed equipment and recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(9{,}500 + 77{,}000 + 93{,}500 = 180{,}000\) = Cr 180,000. Balanced.

**Key insight:** Mid-year contributions still use **FV at receipt** for the asset and **net contribution revenue**. Subsequent measurement prorates the first year’s depreciation; disposal removes **gross cost and all accum. dep.** built on the contributed basis.

### Q4 — MC — Measurement and revenue amount for donated PPE
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Contributed PPE measurement basis and contribution revenue net of recipient cash costs  

**Question:**  
On December 31, Summit Forge Co. unconditionally received a vacant building as a donation. The donor’s original cost was $250,000 with $90,000 of accumulated depreciation on the donor’s books. An independent appraisal values the building at **$175,000**. Summit Forge paid **$5,000 cash** for legal transfer costs and provided **no** goods or services to the donor. What amount of **Contribution Revenue** should Summit Forge recognize on December 31?

- A) $250,000  
- B) $160,000  
- C) $175,000  
- D) $170,000  

**Answer:** **D.**  
Recipient recognizes the building at **fair value $175,000**, not the donor’s cost or book value. Cash transfer costs **reduce** contribution revenue: \(175{,}000 - 5{,}000 = \) **$170,000** (Demo 11-8 pattern).  
- A is donor’s original cost (incorrect basis for recipient).  
- B is donor’s book value \(250{,}000 - 90{,}000\) (incorrect).  
- C is FV before subtracting recipient cash costs.

### Q5 — MC — Land vs building subsequent measurement
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Which portion of a contributed plant is depreciated after initial recognition at FV  

**Question:**  
On January 1, a municipality unconditionally donates land and a building (combined FV $400,000; 20% land / 80% building) to Orion Labs. Orion pays $0 transfer costs. The building’s remaining life is 40 years with no residual value (SL). Which statement is correct for the **year of receipt** (full year of depreciation taken)?

- A) Depreciate the entire $400,000 over 40 years → dep. expense $10,000.  
- B) Record Contribution Revenue $400,000; dep. expense on the building only = \(400{,}000 \times 0.80 / 40 = \$8{,}000\).  
- C) Record land and building at zero because no cash was paid; no depreciation.  
- D) Record Contribution Revenue only for the land; expense the building immediately.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Assets are recorded at FV even when no cash is paid: Land $80,000, Building $320,000, Contribution Revenue $400,000. Only the **building** is depreciated: \(320{,}000 / 40 = 8{,}000\). Land is not depreciated. A incorrectly depreciates land; C and D violate FV recognition of unconditional contributions.
