# Agent 92 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-7

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Account for research and development costs  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** expense most R&D as incurred; capitalize materials/equipment/facilities (and related intangibles) that have **alternative future uses**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** depreciate multi-use PPE over useful life; each period’s depreciation is classified as **R&D expense**
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** depreciation (as R&D) on multi-use assets; inventory release of materials with alternative uses when consumed in R&D
- **Disposal JE:** derecognize multi-use R&D-related PPE at carrying amount; recognize gain or loss vs cash proceeds
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** R&D vs non-R&D (commercial production, routine modifications, unrelated G&A); patent registration legal fees capitalized (not R&D); **disclose total R&D expense** in the notes (ASC 730-10-50-1)
- **Number-variant twin:** same structure, all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Full R&D cost cycle (emphasis: initial recognition)
**LO:** LO 13-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of R&D costs (expense vs capitalize multi-use assets); Year-1 R&D depreciation; multi-year depreciation schedule; disposal of multi-use equipment; R&D expense disclosure total  
**Scenario:**  
**ApexNova Therapeutics Inc.**, a calendar-year company, conducts extensive research and development. During **Year 1**, ApexNova incurred the following (all amounts paid in cash unless noted):

| 2 | Laboratory equipment purchased **January 1, Year 1** with **significant alternative future uses** (multiple future R&D projects and production support); residual value **$0**; **straight-line** over **6 years**; placed in service Jan 1 for R&D | **$480,000** |
| 3 | Specialized testing apparatus purchased **April 1, Year 1** for a **single** R&D project only; **no** alternative future use | **$96,000** |

| 5 | Fees paid to an outside university lab for contract research services | **$85,000** |
| 6 | Reasonable allocation of indirect facility costs clearly related to R&D (utilities, insurance, security of R&D wing) | **$72,000** |
| 7 | Corporate headquarters G&A (executive, HR, central accounting) **not** clearly related to R&D | **$310,000** |
| 8 | Troubleshooting and repair of breakdowns on an **existing commercial production** line | **$41,000** |
| 9 | Design and construction of **preproduction prototypes** for a new product under development | **$55,000** |

**e. January 1, Year 4 — disposal of multi-use laboratory equipment**

Accumulated depreciation after 3 years = 3 × $80,000 = **$240,000**  
Carrying amount = $480,000 − $240,000 = **$240,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$250,000**  
Gain on disposal = $250,000 − $240,000 = **$10,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 250,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Laboratory Equipment | 240,000 | |
| Laboratory Equipment | | 480,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 + 240,000 = 490,000; Cr 480,000 + 10,000 = 490,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Presentation / disclosure**  
Under ASC 730-10-50-1, the company must **disclose the total research and development costs charged to expense** for each period an income statement is presented (here **$1,778,000** for Year 1). R&D is an **operating expense**. Multi-use PPE appears in PPE (gross) with accumulated depreciation; the patent appears among intangible assets.

**Key insight:** R&D is **generally expensed as incurred**. The main **initial recognition** exception is materials, equipment, facilities, and intangibles with **alternative future uses** — capitalize those assets and charge **R&D expense** only as they are **used or depreciated**. Commercial production, routine alterations, and unrelated G&A are **not** R&D. Patent **registration** legal fees are capitalized; internal development effort remains R&D expense.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of R&D costs
**LO:** LO 13-7  
**Concept:** Classification of expenditures as R&D expense vs non-R&D vs capitalized multi-use asset  

**Question 1:**  
During the year, OrionParts Co. incurred: (i) lab research salaries for successful and unsuccessful design attempts **$100,000**; (ii) cost of creating prototypes during the design phase **$45,000**; (iii) minor design update after commercial production began **$12,000**; (iv) seasonal design updates to existing products **$25,000**. What amount should OrionParts report as **research and development costs**?

- A) \$182,000  
- B) \$157,000  
- C) \$145,000  
- D) \$45,000  

**Answer:** **C. $145,000.**  
R&D includes planned research salaries (successful **and** unsuccessful) **$100,000** and design-phase prototype costs **$45,000** = **$145,000**. Minor design updates after commercial production and seasonal updates to existing products are **non-R&D** operating expenses (\$12,000 + \$25,000 excluded).

**Question 2:**  
Hyland-style facts for one year: materials used in R&D **$400,000**; equipment acquired with significant alternative future uses **$2,000,000**; depreciation on that equipment for the year **$500,000**; R&D personnel **$1,000,000**; outside consulting for R&D **$100,000**; indirect costs reasonably allocable to R&D **$200,000**. What amount of **research and development expense** is reported on the current-year income statement?

- A) \$4,200,000  
- B) \$2,200,000  
- C) \$1,700,000  
- D) \$2,700,000  

**Answer:** **B. $2,200,000.**  
Include materials used \$400,000 + depreciation of multi-use equipment \$500,000 + personnel \$1,000,000 + consulting \$100,000 + allocable indirect \$200,000 = **\$2,200,000**. The **\$2,000,000 equipment purchase is capitalized**, not expensed as R&D in full.
