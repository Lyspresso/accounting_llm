# Agent 363 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-11

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Accounting for Bonds with Stock Warrants  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Nondetachable warrants:** entire package proceeds allocated to debt (straight-debt issue); no separate equity for warrants
- **Detachable warrants — incremental method:** assign known fair value to the security with known FV; residual proceeds to the other
- **Detachable warrants — proportional method:** allocate package proceeds by relative fair values when both debt-without-warrants and warrants FVs are known
- **Initial recognition JE:** Cash; Bonds Payable; Discount or Premium on Bonds Payable; Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants (detachable only)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortize only the **debt** discount/premium over the bond term; warrant equity is **not** amortized
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** accrue/record interest expense = cash coupon ± discount/premium amortization (including partial-period year-end accruals when interest dates ≠ fiscal year-end)
- **Exercise of warrants:** Cash + remove PIC—Stock Warrants → Common Stock + PIC in Excess of Par
- **Expiration of warrants:** reclassify remaining PIC—Stock Warrants → PIC in Excess of Par—Common Stock (no P&L)
- **Maturity / settlement JE:** retire Bonds Payable at face for cash after premium/discount is fully amortized

### Q1 — CORE — Detachable warrants (proportional), premium amort schedule, period-end interest, exercise, expiration, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Proportional allocation of detachable bond-with-warrants proceeds; subsequent straight-line premium amortization schedule; period-end interest adjusting JE; warrant exercise and expiration; bond maturity  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Packaging Co.** (calendar-year company) issues bonds with **detachable** stock purchase warrants on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Face amount | **$600,000** of 7%, 5-year nonconvertible bonds |
| Interest | Payable **annually each December 31** |
| Bond unit | Each **$1,000** bond carries **10** detachable warrants |
| Total warrants | \(600 \times 10 = \mathbf{6{,}000}\) warrants |
| Exercise terms | Each warrant: one share of **$5** par common for **$22** cash |
| Package issue price | **105** (bonds + warrants) → cash proceeds **$630,000** |
| Fair value shortly after issuance | Bonds **without** warrants quoted at **102**; warrants trade at **$3** each |
| Debt amortization | **Straight-line** amortization of premium over **5 years** |

On **March 1, Year 3**, holders exercise **4,500** warrants. The remaining **1,500** warrants expire unexercised on **January 15, Year 4**. Bonds are held to maturity and settled on **December 31, Year 5** (after the Year-5 interest payment).

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the **$630,000** package proceeds to bonds and warrants using the **proportional method**. Compute the initial bond carrying amount and premium.  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 1** initial recognition journal entry.  
c. Prepare a complete **subsequent measurement amortization schedule** for Years 1–5 (beginning carrying amount, cash interest, premium amortization, interest expense, ending carrying amount).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end interest adjusting journal entry.  
e. Record the **March 1, Year 3** exercise of 4,500 warrants.  
f. Record the **January 15, Year 4** expiration of the remaining 1,500 warrants.  
g. Record the **December 31, Year 5** maturity (settlement) entry for the bonds (interest already recorded separately).  
h. In one or two sentences, explain why Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants is **not** amortized over the bond term.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Proportional allocation**

| Component | Fair value | Weight |
|---|---:|---|
| Bonds without warrants (\(600{,}000 \times 1.02\)) | **$612,000** | \(612{,}000 / 630{,}000\) |
| Warrants (\(6{,}000 \times \$3\)) | **$18,000** | \(18{,}000 / 630{,}000\) |
| **Total fair value** | **$630,000** | |

Package proceeds = **$630,000** (equal to total FV).

- Allocation to bonds = \(630{,}000 \times 612/630 = \mathbf{\$612{,}000}\)  
- Allocation to warrants = \(630{,}000 \times 18/630 = \mathbf{\$18{,}000}\)  
- Premium on bonds = \(612{,}000 - 600{,}000 = \mathbf{\$12{,}000}\)  
- Initial bond carrying amount = **$612,000**

**b. Initial recognition**

*January 1, Year 1 — Issue bonds with detachable warrants (proportional method)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 630,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 600,000 |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 12,000 |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | | 18,000 |
| *Allocate package proceeds by relative FV; warrants recorded in equity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 630,000 = Cr 600,000 + 12,000 + 18,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — straight-line premium amortization**

Annual cash interest = \(600{,}000 \times 7\% = \mathbf{\$42{,}000}\).  
Annual premium amortization = \(12{,}000 / 5 = \mathbf{\$2{,}400}\).  
Annual interest expense = \(42{,}000 - 2{,}400 = \mathbf{\$39{,}600}\).

| Year | Beginning CA | Cash interest | Premium amort. | Interest expense | Ending CA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 612,000 | 42,000 | 2,400 | 39,600 | **609,600** |
| 2 | 609,600 | 42,000 | 2,400 | 39,600 | **607,200** |
| 3 | 607,200 | 42,000 | 2,400 | 39,600 | **604,800** |
| 4 | 604,800 | 42,000 | 2,400 | 39,600 | **602,400** |
| 5 | 602,400 | 42,000 | 2,400 | 39,600 | **600,000** |
| **Totals** | | **210,000** | **12,000** | **198,000** | |

**Roll-forward check:** Premium fully amortized \(5 \times 2{,}400 = 12{,}000\); ending CA = face **$600,000**. Equity warrant balance is **outside** this debt schedule.

**d. Period-end adjusting / interest entry — Year 1 (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 39,600 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,400 | |
| Cash | | 42,000 |
| *Cash coupon less SL premium amortization* | | |

**Check:** Dr 39,600 + 2,400 = Cr 42,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Exercise of 4,500 warrants**

PIC—Stock Warrants removed = \(18{,}000 \times 4{,}500/6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$13{,}500}\).  
Cash received = \(4{,}500 \times 22 = \mathbf{\$99{,}000}\).  
Common stock (par) = \(4{,}500 \times 5 = \mathbf{\$22{,}500}\).  
PIC in excess of par (plug) = \(99{,}000 + 13{,}500 - 22{,}500 = \mathbf{\$90{,}000}\).

*March 1, Year 3 — Exercise warrants*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 99,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 13,500 | |
| Common Stock | | 22,500 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 90,000 |

**Check:** Dr 99,000 + 13,500 = Cr 22,500 + 90,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Expiration of remaining 1,500 warrants**

PIC—Stock Warrants remaining = \(18{,}000 \times 1{,}500/6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$4{,}500}\).

*January 15, Year 4 — Expire unexercised warrants*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 4,500 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 4,500 |
| *Reclassify expired warrant equity within equity; no gain or loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,500. **Balanced.**  
**Check:** PIC—Stock Warrants after e–f = \(18{,}000 - 13{,}500 - 4{,}500 = \mathbf{\$0}\).

**g. Maturity / settlement**

After Year-5 interest and full premium amortization, carrying amount = face **$600,000**.

*December 31, Year 5 — Retire bonds at maturity*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 600,000 | |
| Cash | | 600,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 600,000. **Balanced.**

**h. Why warrants equity is not amortized**  
Detachable warrant proceeds are **paid-in capital**, not a valuation adjustment of the debt. Only the amount allocated to the **bonds** (premium or discount) is amortized as part of interest; the warrant equity remains until **exercise** or **expiration**.

**Key insight:** For detachable warrants with both FVs known, use the **proportional method** to split the package price. The **subsequent measurement schedule** and **period-end interest adjusting JE** apply only to the **debt carrying amount**; equity stays put until exercise/expiration, and maturity simply clears face Bonds Payable once the premium is gone.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Detachable warrants (incremental), discount schedule, period-end interest, full exercise, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: incremental allocation when only warrant FV is known; subsequent straight-line **discount** amortization schedule; period-end interest adjusting JE; exercise of all warrants; bond maturity  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Forge Electronics** (calendar year) issues bonds with **detachable** stock purchase warrants on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Face amount | **$350,000** of 6%, 4-year nonconvertible bonds |
| Interest | Payable **annually each December 31** |
| Bond unit | Each **$1,000** bond carries **20** detachable warrants |
| Total warrants | \(350 \times 20 = \mathbf{7{,}000}\) warrants |
| Exercise terms | Each warrant: one share of **$1** par common for **$12** cash |
| Package issue price | **101** → cash proceeds **$353,500** |
| Fair values | Warrants trade at **$2** each immediately after issuance; **FV of bonds without warrants is not determinable** |
| Debt amortization | **Straight-line** over **4 years** |

On **July 1, Year 2**, all **7,000** warrants are exercised. Bonds are held to maturity and retired for cash on **December 31, Year 4** (after Year-4 interest).

**Required:**  
a. Allocate proceeds using the **incremental method**. Compute discount and initial bond carrying amount.  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance entry.  
c. Prepare the **4-year subsequent measurement amortization schedule**.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end interest adjusting entry.  
e. Record the **July 1, Year 2** exercise of all warrants.  
f. Record the **December 31, Year 4** bond maturity settlement.  
g. Compute **total interest expense** over the four-year life and reconcile to cash interest + total discount amortized.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Incremental allocation**

| Step | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Selling price of bonds with warrants (\(350{,}000 \times 1.01\)) | **$353,500** |
| Fair value of warrants (\(7{,}000 \times \$2\)) | **14,000** |
| **Allocation to bonds (residual)** | **$339,500** |
| Discount on bonds (\(350{,}000 - 339{,}500\)) | **$10,500** |
| Initial bond carrying amount | **$339,500** |

**b. Initial recognition**

*January 1, Year 1 — Issue bonds with detachable warrants (incremental method)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 353,500 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 10,500 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 350,000 |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | | 14,000 |
| *Known FV assigned to warrants; residual to debt creates discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 353,500 + 10,500 = Cr 350,000 + 14,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — straight-line discount amortization**

Annual cash interest = \(350{,}000 \times 6\% = \mathbf{\$21{,}000}\).  
Annual discount amortization = \(10{,}500 / 4 = \mathbf{\$2{,}625}\).  
Annual interest expense = \(21{,}000 + 2{,}625 = \mathbf{\$23{,}625}\).

| Year | Beginning CA | Cash interest | Discount amort. | Interest expense | Ending CA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 339,500 | 21,000 | 2,625 | 23,625 | **342,125** |
| 2 | 342,125 | 21,000 | 2,625 | 23,625 | **344,750** |
| 3 | 344,750 | 21,000 | 2,625 | 23,625 | **347,375** |
| 4 | 347,375 | 21,000 | 2,625 | 23,625 | **350,000** |
| **Totals** | | **84,000** | **10,500** | **94,500** | |

**Roll-forward check:** Discount fully amortized \(4 \times 2{,}625 = 10{,}500\); ending CA = face **$350,000**.

**d. Period-end adjusting / interest entry — Year 1 (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 23,625 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,625 |
| Cash | | 21,000 |
| *Cash coupon plus SL discount amortization* | | |

**Check:** Dr 23,625 = Cr 2,625 + 21,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Exercise of all 7,000 warrants**

Cash received = \(7{,}000 \times 12 = \mathbf{\$84{,}000}\).  
PIC—Stock Warrants removed = **$14,000**.  
Common stock (par) = \(7{,}000 \times 1 = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\).  
PIC in excess of par = \(84{,}000 + 14{,}000 - 7{,}000 = \mathbf{\$91{,}000}\).

*July 1, Year 2 — Exercise all warrants*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 84,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 14,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 7,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 91,000 |

**Check:** Dr 84,000 + 14,000 = Cr 7,000 + 91,000. **Balanced.**  
**Note:** Exercise does **not** affect Bonds Payable or the remaining discount amort schedule.

**f. Maturity / settlement**

*December 31, Year 4 — Retire bonds at maturity (after Year-4 interest)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 350,000 | |
| Cash | | 350,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 350,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Total interest expense reconciliation**

Total interest expense = \(4 \times 23{,}625 = \mathbf{\$94{,}500}\).  
Reconcile: total cash interest \(84{,}000\) + total discount amortized \(10{,}500\) = **$94,500**. ✓

**Key insight:** When only warrant FV is known, the **incremental method** assigns that FV to equity and residual proceeds to debt—often creating a **discount**. Period-end interest expense exceeds cash coupon by the discount amortization; warrant exercise is pure equity and does not rewrite the debt amort schedule.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Mid-year issuance; year-end period-end adjusting JE (partial period); interest-date payment; exercise; maturity
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE emphasis — partial-period interest accrual and discount amortization when bonds with detachable warrants are issued off fiscal year-end; full interest-date payment; warrant exercise; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Brewing Holdings** (calendar-year company) issues bonds with **detachable** stock purchase warrants on **April 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Face amount | **$200,000** of 9%, 3-year nonconvertible bonds |
| Interest | Payable **annually each April 1** (first payment April 1, Year 2) |
| Bond unit | Each **$1,000** bond carries **5** detachable warrants |
| Total warrants | \(200 \times 5 = \mathbf{1{,}000}\) warrants |
| Exercise terms | Each warrant: one share of **$10** par common for **$30** cash |
| Package issue price | **98** → cash proceeds **$196,000** |
| Fair values | Warrants trade at **$8** each; **FV of bonds without warrants is not determinable** |
| Debt amortization | **Straight-line** over **36 months** (3 years) |
| Fiscal year-end | **December 31** |

On **October 1, Year 2**, all **1,000** warrants are exercised. Bonds are held to maturity and settled on **April 1, Year 4** (after the final interest payment).

**Required:**  
a. Allocate proceeds (incremental method) and record the **April 1, Year 1** issuance entry.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting entry for **9 months** of accrued interest and discount amortization.  
c. Prepare the **April 1, Year 2** entry to pay annual interest and complete amortization for the remaining **3 months** of the first interest year.  
d. Prepare a **3-year interest-period amortization schedule** (April 1 interest years).  
e. Record the **October 1, Year 2** exercise of all warrants.  
f. Record the **April 1, Year 4** maturity settlement (final interest already recorded separately that day).  
g. Briefly state how the **April 1, Year 1** entry would differ if the warrants were **nondetachable**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Incremental allocation and issuance**

| Step | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Selling price (\(200{,}000 \times 0.98\)) | **$196,000** |
| Fair value of warrants (\(1{,}000 \times \$8\)) | **8,000** |
| Allocation to bonds (residual) | **$188,000** |
| Discount (\(200{,}000 - 188{,}000\)) | **$12,000** |

*April 1, Year 1 — Issue bonds with detachable warrants*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 196,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 12,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 200,000 |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | | 8,000 |

**Check:** Dr 196,000 + 12,000 = Cr 200,000 + 8,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 1 (emphasis)**

Time elapsed since issuance: **9 months** (April 1 → December 31).  
Annual cash coupon = \(200{,}000 \times 9\% = \mathbf{\$18{,}000}\).  
Annual discount amort. = \(12{,}000 / 3 = \mathbf{\$4{,}000}\) (or \(12{,}000 / 36 \times 12\)).

| Component (9/12 of annual) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accrued cash interest \(18{,}000 \times 9/12\) | **$13,500** |
| Discount amortization \(4{,}000 \times 9/12 = 12{,}000 \times 9/36\) | **$3,000** |
| **Interest expense** | **$16,500** |

*December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 16,500 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,000 |
| Interest Payable | | 13,500 |
| *Accrue 9 months interest and amortize 9 months of discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,500 = Cr 3,000 + 13,500. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount after accrual date amort. = \(188{,}000 + 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$191{,}000}\).

**c. April 1, Year 2 — interest payment (complete first interest year)**

Remaining **3 months** (Jan 1 – Mar 31):  
Cash interest portion = \(18{,}000 \times 3/12 = \mathbf{\$4{,}500}\).  
Discount amort. = \(4{,}000 \times 3/12 = \mathbf{\$1{,}000}\).  
Interest expense this entry = \(4{,}500 + 1{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Payable | 13,500 | |
| Interest Expense | 5,500 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,000 |
| Cash | | 18,000 |
| *Pay full annual coupon; clear accrual; finish first-year amort* | | |

**Check:** Dr 13,500 + 5,500 = Cr 1,000 + 18,000. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount after full first interest year = \(191{,}000 + 1{,}000 = \mathbf{\$192{,}000}\).

**d. Three-year interest-period amortization schedule**

| Interest year (ending Apr 1) | Beginning CA | Cash interest | Discount amort. | Interest expense | Ending CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 (to Apr 1, Y2) | 188,000 | 18,000 | 4,000 | 22,000 | **192,000** |
| Year 2 (to Apr 1, Y3) | 192,000 | 18,000 | 4,000 | 22,000 | **196,000** |
| Year 3 (to Apr 1, Y4) | 196,000 | 18,000 | 4,000 | 22,000 | **200,000** |
| **Totals** | | **54,000** | **12,000** | **66,000** | |

**Check:** Total interest expense \(66{,}000 = \) cash interest \(54{,}000\) + discount \(12{,}000\).

**e. Exercise of all warrants — October 1, Year 2**

Cash = \(1{,}000 \times 30 = \mathbf{\$30{,}000}\).  
PIC—Stock Warrants = **$8,000**.  
Common stock = \(1{,}000 \times 10 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\).  
PIC in excess of par = \(30{,}000 + 8{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$28{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 30,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 8,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 10,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 28,000 |

**Check:** Dr 30,000 + 8,000 = Cr 10,000 + 28,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Maturity settlement — April 1, Year 4**

After final interest and full discount amortization, CA = face **$200,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000. **Balanced.**

**g. If warrants were nondetachable**  
Entire package proceeds **$196,000** would be allocated to **debt only** (no PIC—Stock Warrants). Issuance would debit Cash 196,000 and Discount 4,000; credit Bonds Payable 200,000. There would be no separate equity measurement for warrants.

**Key insight:** When interest dates do not align with fiscal year-end, the **period-end adjusting JE** accrues interest payable and amortizes a **time-proportionate** slice of the debt discount/premium created by detachable-warrant allocation. The warrant equity balance is independent of that accrual.

### Q4 — MC (method and classification)
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Classification of allocation method for detachable warrants; accounting contrast for nondetachable warrants  

**Question 1:**  
**Copperline Rail Systems** issues bonds with **detachable** stock purchase warrants. Shortly after issuance, both (i) the market price of identical bonds **without** warrants and (ii) the market price of the warrants are **reliably observable**. Which method should Copperline use to allocate package proceeds?

- A) Incremental method only (assign residual to equity)  
- B) Proportional (relative fair value) method  
- C) Allocate entire proceeds to Bonds Payable with no equity  
- D) Fair value option for the warrants with changes through earnings each period  

**Answer:** **B.** When fair values of **both** the debt instrument without warrants and the warrants are known, ASC 470-20 requires allocation of proceeds based on **relative fair values** (proportional method). The incremental method applies when only **one** of the two fair values is known. Nondetachable warrants (not detachable) receive no separate equity allocation. Detachable warrant equity is not remeasured through earnings under the LO 16-11 core model.

**Question 2:**  
**Oakmont Dental Supply** issues nonconvertible bonds with **nondetachable** stock purchase warrants for a package price of 103. What is the correct initial measurement?

- A) Allocate package proceeds between debt and Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants using proportional method  
- B) Record warrants at zero; record bonds at face only  
- C) Record the **entire** package proceeds as debt (straight-debt issue); no separate warrant equity  
- D) Expense the estimated warrant value immediately as debt issue cost  

**Answer:** **C.** Nondetachable warrants cannot be sold separately and have no separate market; the issuer treats the issue as **straight debt** and allocates **all** proceeds to the bonds (premium/discount vs face only). Separate PIC—Stock Warrants arises only for **detachable** warrants.
