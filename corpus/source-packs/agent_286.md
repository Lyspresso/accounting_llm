# Agent 286 — CORE DEMO — LO 17-4

**Chapter:** 17  
**LO title:** Account for complex finance leases for a lessee  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Lease payments for classification vs liability:** fixed payments, purchase options (if reasonably certain), and residual guarantees (100% for classification criterion #4; only the **probable amount owed** for measuring the lease liability)
- **Initial recognition (complex):** Lease liability = PV of lease payments (rate implicit if known); ROU asset = liability − lease incentives + initial direct costs (+ prepayments)
- **IDC and lease incentive pre-commencement JEs:** capitalize IDC; credit Lease Incentive Liability for cash incentives received before commencement; reclassify both into the ROU asset at commencement
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** effective-interest amortization of the lease liability (annuity-due payments); straight-line amortization of the ROU asset
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** accrue interest on the lease liability and amortize the ROU asset at reporting date (including **partial-period** accruals when commencement is not year-end)
- **Settlement / maturity:** final payment; residual-guarantee deficiency payment; reclassification when a purchase option is exercised
- **Classification / presentation:** finance lease (interest expense separate from ROU amortization); current vs noncurrent lease liability; ROU asset presentation

### Q1 — CORE — Finance lease with initial direct costs and lease incentive; partial-period period-end adjusting JEs
**LO:** LO 17-4  
**Concept:** Complex finance lease initial recognition (IDC + incentive); liability schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs** for interest and ROU amortization when year-end falls mid-lease-year  
**Scenario:**  
**Crestview Forming Co.** (calendar-year reporter) enters a **4-year noncancellable** lease with Harbor Equipment Finance for a specialized forming line. Commencement is **April 1, Year 1**. Terms:

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Annual lease payment (due **April 1** of Year 1, 2, 3, and 4) | $42,500 |
| Economic life of equipment | 4 years |
| Fair value of equipment at commencement | $156,103 |
| Estimated residual / guaranteed residual | $0 / none |
| Purchase or renewal options | None; asset reverts to lessor |
| Rate implicit in the lease (known to Crestview) | 6% |
| Crestview incremental borrowing rate | 7% (not used — implicit rate known) |
| Initial direct costs (legal fees to **execute** the lease), paid **March 15, Year 1** | $2,100 |
| Cash lease incentive from lessor, received **March 20, Year 1** | $4,800 |

Payments are at the **beginning** of each lease year (annuity-due). Crestview classifies the lease as a **finance lease** and rounds the liability schedule to whole dollars (final period plugs residual principal).

**Required:**

f. Show the **December 31, Year 1** balance sheet excerpt (ROU asset; current and noncurrent lease liability) and Year 1 income statement lease-related expenses.  
g. Prepare the **April 1, Year 2** entries to (1) accrue the remaining **3 months** of Year-1-cycle interest and (2) record the second lease payment.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Guaranteed residual value: classification vs liability, schedule, period-end accruals, settlement
**LO:** LO 17-4  
**Concept:** Finance lease with **lessee residual value guarantee** — include 100% of GRV for classification PV test but only the **probable deficiency** in the liability; full schedule; period-end adjusting; settlement payment at return  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Calibration LLC** (calendar-year) leases precision testing equipment under a **3-year noncancellable** lease commencing **January 1, Year 1**.

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Annual payment due January 1 each year (Y1–Y3) | $36,000 |
| Economic life | 3 years |
| Fair value at commencement | $120,000 |
| Guaranteed residual value (GRV) at lease end | $8,000 |
| Expected residual value at lease end (Redwood’s estimate) | $5,000 |
| Probable amount owed under residual guarantee | $3,000 |
| Purchase option | None; asset returns to lessor |
| Implicit rate (known) | 5% |
| IDC / incentives | None |

**Required:**  
a. Classify the lease; compute **classification PV** (using **100% of GRV**) and compare to 90% of fair value.  
b. Compute the **lease liability** and **ROU asset** (using only the **$3,000** probable residual payment). Record commencement and first payment.  
c. Prepare the **lease liability schedule** including the residual settlement row.  
d. **(Emphasis)** December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JEs (interest + ROU amortization).  
e. December 31, Year 1 current/noncurrent presentation of the lease liability.  
f. Entries for Year 2 payment and Year 2 period-end adjusting.  
g. Year 3 payment, Year 3 interest accrual, final ROU amortization, and **settlement** when the equipment is returned and fair value is **$5,000 as expected**.  
h. **What-if:** If fair value at return is only **$4,000**, prepare the settlement JE (amount owed = $4,000 deficiency vs $8,000 GRV).

Classification PV includes **100%** of the $8,000 GRV as a final amount:

\[
\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{class}} = \mathrm{PV}(5\%,3,-36{,}000,-8{,}000,\mathrm{type}=1) = \mathbf{\$109{,}849}

90% of FV = $120,000 × 90% = **$108,000**.  
$109,849 > $108,000 → criterion #4 met.

**Finance lease.**

\[
\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{liab}} = \mathrm{PV}(5\%,3,-36{,}000,-3{,}000,\mathrm{type}=1) = \mathbf{\$105{,}530}

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement method)
**LO:** LO 17-4  
**Concept:** Distinguishing residual guarantee amounts for classification vs liability; ROU amortization period when a purchase option is reasonably certain  

**Question 1:**  
On commencement of a finance lease, the lessee guarantees a residual value of $20,000 and expects the asset’s residual value at lease end to be $17,500. For **lease classification** criterion #4 (PV of lease payments vs fair value) and for **initial measurement of the lease liability**, respectively, the residual-related amounts included in the present value are:

- A) $20,000 for classification; $20,000 for the liability  
- B) $20,000 for classification; $2,500 for the liability  
- C) $2,500 for classification; $2,500 for the liability  
- D) $0 for classification; $2,500 for the liability  

**Answer:** **B.** Classification uses **100% of the guaranteed residual** ($20,000). The lease liability includes only the amount **probable of being owed**, $20,000 − $17,500 = **$2,500**. Unguaranteed residuals are excluded from the lessee’s liability entirely.

---

**Question 2:**  
A lessee has a 4-year finance lease that includes a **purchase option the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise**. The underlying asset’s remaining economic life is **7 years**. The right-of-use asset (ignoring any IDC split) should be amortized:

- A) Over 4 years (the lease term only)  
- B) Over 7 years (the economic life of the underlying asset)  
- C) Over 4 years to a residual equal to the purchase-option price  
- D) Not amortized until the purchase option is exercised  

**Answer:** **B.** When ownership transfers or a purchase option is **reasonably certain** to be exercised, the lessee amortizes the ROU asset over the **useful life of the underlying asset**, not merely the contractual lease term. (By contrast, without transfer/PO, amortize over the lease term.)
