# Agent 135 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-3

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-OCI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase AFS debt at acquisition cost (par); debit Investment in AFS Securities
- **Interest revenue in NI:** cash coupon (semiannual or annual) recognized in net income for AFS (same as all debt categories)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortized cost, fair value, required FVA, existing FVA, and **increase/(decrease) to FVA** rollforward across period-ends and sale date
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** update FVA through **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI** (not NI); BS carrying amount = amortized cost ± FVA = fair value
- **Disposal / sale JE (FV-OCI path):** (1) adjust sold security to FV through OCI at sale date; (2) record cash, remove investment at amortized cost, eliminate FVA, **reclassify** cumulative holding G/L from AOCI into NI as realized G/L
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** AFS unrealized holding G/L in OCI/AOCI until sale; reclassification amount disclosed; interest in NI
- **Portfolio subsequent measurement:** partial sale clears sold security’s FVA; remaining securities remeasured at year-end
- **Number-variant twin:** same FV-OCI life-cycle skill path with fully changed amounts

### Q1 — CORE — AFS life cycle with subsequent FVA measurement schedule
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule for AFS FVA (period-end and sale-date); initial recognition; interest in NI; period-end FV-OCI adjust; disposal with AOCI reclassification  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Investments Corp.** is a calendar-year investor. On **July 1 of Year 1**, Harborline purchased for **par $120,000** of **Crestview Corp. 6%** bonds that mature on **June 30 of Year 8**. Cash interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Harborline classifies the bonds as **available-for-sale (AFS)** and measures them under **FV-OCI**. Beginning AOCI related to AFS is **zero**. No other AFS activity occurs except as stated below.

**Fair values and sale:**
| Date | Event / fair value of Crestview bonds |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$114,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Fair value **$123,600** (no purchases/sales in Year 2) |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Entire holding sold for cash **$124,800** |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **July 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (purchase at par).  
(b) Record receipt of **cash interest** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for the Fair Value Adjustment (FVA) account covering: Dec 31 Y1, Dec 31 Y2, and Jan 1 Y3 sale date. Columns: amortized cost, fair value (or sale price), required FVA (debit = positive), existing FVA before adjust, **increase/(decrease) to FVA**, FVA after adjust.  
(d) Using the schedule, record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**.  
(e) Report Year 1 effects on the **income statement**, **statement of comprehensive income**, and **December 31 Year 1 balance sheet** for this investment (ignore taxes and all other items).  
(f) Record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE**.  
(g) On January 1 of Year 3: (i) sale-date FV-OCI adjust; (ii) sale with reclassification of holding gain into net income and elimination of FVA.  
(h) Prepare the Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation** (beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclassification to NI, ending AOCI).

**Answer key:**  

**(a) July 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (purchase at par)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Crestview Bonds | 120,000 | |
| Cash | | 120,000 |
| *Purchase Crestview 6% bonds at par; classify AFS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 120,000 = Cr 120,000. **Balanced.** Amortized cost basis = **$120,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest revenue in net income**  
Cash interest = \(120{,}000 \times 0.06 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$3{,}600}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,600 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 3,600 |
| *Semiannual interest on Crestview AFS bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,600 = Cr 3,600. **Balanced.** Interest is recognized in **net income** for AFS debt.

**(c) Subsequent measurement schedule — FVA rollforward (emphasis)**

Sign convention: **positive FVA = debit** (unrealized holding gain); **negative FVA = credit** (unrealized holding loss).

| Measurement date | Amortized cost | Fair value / sale price | Required FVA | Existing FVA (before adj.) | Increase/(decrease) to FVA | FVA after adj. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | 120,000 | 114,000 | (6,000) | 0 | **(6,000)** | (6,000) |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 120,000 | 123,600 | 3,600 | (6,000) | **9,600** | 3,600 |
| Jan 1, Year 3 (sale) | 120,000 | 124,800 | 4,800 | 3,600 | **1,200** | 4,800 |

**Schedule math checks:**  
- Y1 required FVA = 114,000 − 120,000 = **(6,000)**; existing 0 → decrease FVA (credit FVA) **6,000**.  
- Y2 required FVA = 123,600 − 120,000 = **3,600 debit**; existing **(6,000)** → increase = 3,600 − (−6,000) = **9,600**.  
- Sale-date required FVA = 124,800 − 120,000 = **4,800 debit**; existing **3,600** → increase = **1,200**.  
- After sale, FVA is eliminated (see part g); ending FVA for Crestview = **0**.

**(d) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE (FV-OCI)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 6,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 6,000 |
| *Adjust AFS to FV; unrealized holding loss through OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**  
BS carrying amount = 120,000 − 6,000 = **$114,000** = fair value.

**(e) Year 1 financial statement presentation**

**Income statement (excerpt):**  
- Interest revenue **$3,600**  
- Unrealized AFS holding loss is **not** reported in net income.

**Statement of comprehensive income (excerpt):**  
- Net income: includes interest revenue $3,600 (other items ignored)  
- Other comprehensive income: Unrealized holding loss on AFS securities **$(6,000)**  
- Comprehensive income = NI + OCI

**Balance sheet, December 31, Year 1:**  
- Assets: Investment in available-for-sale securities **$114,000** (amortized cost $120,000 net of FVA credit $6,000)  
- Stockholders’ equity: Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) **$(6,000)**

**(f) December 31, Year 2 — Period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 9,600 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 9,600 |
| *Roll FVA from $6,000 credit to $3,600 debit* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,600 = Cr 9,600. **Balanced.**  
FVA ending = −6,000 + 9,600 = **$3,600 debit**. AOCI related = **$3,600**. Carrying amount = 120,000 + 3,600 = **$123,600**.

**(g) January 1, Year 3 — Sale under FV-OCI**

**(i) Sale-date adjust to fair value through OCI**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 1,200 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 1,200 |
| *Bring FVA to $4,800 debit before sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,200 = Cr 1,200. **Balanced.** FVA now = **$4,800 debit**.

**(ii) Sale + reclassify AOCI holding gain to NI + eliminate FVA**  
Realized gain = cash proceeds $124,800 − amortized cost $120,000 = **$4,800**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 124,800 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 4,800 | |
| Investment in AFS—Crestview Bonds | | 120,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 4,800 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 4,800 |
| *Sell AFS; reclass cumulative holding gain from AOCI to NI; clear FVA* | | |

**Proof:** Debits \(124{,}800 + 4{,}800 = \mathbf{\$129{,}600}\); Credits \(120{,}000 + 4{,}800 + 4{,}800 = \mathbf{\$129{,}600}\). **Balanced.**  
Investment and FVA for Crestview are zero. Realized **gain $4,800** is in **net income**; the debit to Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI **reclassifies** the cumulative holding gain out of AOCI.

**(h) Year 3 AOCI reclassification disclosure**

| Reconciliation of Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, January 1, Year 3 | $3,600 |
| Current period unrealized gain on AFS securities (sale-date FV adj) | 1,200 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (4,800) |
| Net OCI for Year 3 | (3,600) |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, December 31, Year 3 | **$0** |

**Holding-period AOCI volatility check (must net to zero after full disposal):**  
Y1 OCI loss \((6{,}000)\) + Y2 OCI gain \(9{,}600\) + Y3 OCI gain \(1{,}200\) − reclass to Y3 NI \((4{,}800)\) = **$0**.

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the control document for AFS under FV-OCI: each period (and at sale) you set **required FVA = FV − amortized cost**, then book only the **change** from existing FVA through **OCI**. Interest always hits **NI**; only the **sale-date reclassification** moves cumulative holding G/L into **NI** and clears FVA.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same AFS FV-OCI path (purchase, interest, FVA schedule, sale reclass) with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Riverton Mutual Fund Services** purchased for **par $55,000** of **Oakmont Industries 9%** bonds that mature on **December 31 of Year 4**. Cash interest is paid **annually** each December 31. Riverton classifies the bonds as **AFS** (FV-OCI). Year-end is December 31. Beginning AOCI related to AFS is zero. No other AFS activity except as stated.

**Fair values and sale:**
| Date | Fair value / event |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$52,250** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Fair value **$57,200** (no sales in Year 2) |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Sold entire holding for **$57,750** cash |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Cash interest entry on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Subsequent measurement (FVA) schedule for Dec 31 Y1, Dec 31 Y2, and Jan 1 Y3 sale date (same columns as Q1).  
(d) Period-end FV-OCI adjusting JE for Year 1.  
(e) Year 1 IS / SCI / BS presentation for this investment.  
(f) Period-end FV-OCI adjusting JE for Year 2.  
(g) January 1 Year 3: (i) sale-date FV-OCI adjust; (ii) sale + reclass + eliminate FVA.  
(h) Year 3 AOCI reconciliation after the sale.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Purchase**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Oakmont Bonds | 55,000 | |
| Cash | | 55,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$55,000**. **Balanced.**

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest**  
Interest = \(55{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 12/12 = \mathbf{\$4{,}950}\)

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — AFS portfolio: partial sale + year-end FVA on remainder
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Portfolio subsequent measurement schedule; disposal of one AFS security with sale-date FV-OCI adjust and reclass; period-end FVA on remaining AFS  
**Scenario:**  
On **December 31 of Year 2**, **Silverpeak Asset Management** held the following **AFS** debt portfolio (purchased at the amortized cost amounts shown; no remaining discount or premium):

| AFS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value (12/31/Y2) | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Glacier bonds | $60,000 | $57,000 | $(3,000) |
| Harbor bonds | 90,000 | 96,000 | 6,000 |
| **Total** | **$150,000** | **$153,000** | **$3,000** |

The **$3,000** net unrealized gain equals the December 31, Year 2 balances in both **FVA—AFS** (debit) and **AOCI** related to AFS.

On **January 1 of Year 3**, Silverpeak sells the **Glacier** bonds for **$62,400**. The remaining Harbor bonds have a fair value of **$93,000** on December 31 of Year 3. No other AFS purchases or sales occur in Year 3. Year-end is December 31.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Glacier at the sale date (required FVA, existing FVA for Glacier, increase needed) and for Harbor at December 31 Year 3 (required FVA, existing FVA after Glacier sale, increase/decrease needed).  
(b) Adjust Glacier to fair value at the sale date (FV-OCI), then record the sale including reclassification of AOCI holding gain into net income and elimination of Glacier’s FVA.  
(c) Record the December 31, Year 3 entry to adjust FVA for **Harbor only**.  
(d) Prepare the December 31, Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation**.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule (portfolio)**

**Glacier — sale date January 1, Year 3**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost | 60,000 |
| Sale price / fair value | 62,400 |
| Required FVA (debit) | 2,400 |
| Existing FVA for Glacier (credit) | (3,000) |
| **Increase to FVA needed** | **5,400** |

**After Glacier sale (FVA T-account rollforward):**  
Beg portfolio FVA Dr **3,000** + sale-date adj Dr **5,400** − sale elimination Cr **2,400** = **Dr $6,000** (equals Harbor’s pre-existing unrealized gain only).

**Harbor — December 31, Year 3**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost | 90,000 |
| Fair value YE | 93,000 |
| Required FVA (debit) | 3,000 |
| Existing FVA (after Glacier sale) | 6,000 debit |
| **Decrease to FVA needed** | **(3,000)** |

**(b) January 1, Year 3 — Glacier sale (FV-OCI path)**

**Sale-date FVA adjust for Glacier:**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 5,400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 5,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$5,400**. **Balanced.**

**Sale of Glacier (realized gain $2,400 = $62,400 − $60,000):**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 62,400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 2,400 | |
| Investment in AFS—Glacier Bonds | | 60,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 2,400 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 2,400 |

**Proof:** Debits \(62{,}400 + 2{,}400 = \mathbf{\$64{,}800}\); Credits \(60{,}000 + 2{,}400 + 2{,}400 = \mathbf{\$64{,}800}\). **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 3 — Adjust Harbor (remaining AFS) to FV**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 3,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 3,000 |
| *Roll Harbor FVA from $6,000 debit to $3,000 debit* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$3,000**. **Balanced.** Ending FVA = **$3,000 debit** = ending AOCI related to remaining AFS.

**(d) Year 3 AOCI reclassification disclosure**  
Current-period unrealized (from Y3 entries affecting Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI):  
- Glacier sale-date gain: **+$5,400**  
- Harbor year-end decline: **−$3,000**  
- Net current-period unrealized = **+$2,400**  
Reclassification adjustment for **gain** included in NI (Glacier): **$(2,400)**

| Reconciliation of AOCI | Amount |
|---|---:|
| AOCI, January 1, Year 3 | $3,000 |
| Current period unrealized gain on AFS securities | 2,400 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (2,400) |
| Net OCI for Year 3 | 0 |
| AOCI, December 31, Year 3 | **$3,000** |

**Check:** Ending AOCI $3,000 = remaining Harbor FVA debit $3,000.

**Key insight:** In a portfolio, **schedule the sold security separately**: adjust it to FV through OCI, reclass its cumulative unrealized into NI, clear its FVA; then **remeasure remaining AFS** at year-end. AOCI ending must equal the remaining FVA balance.

### Q4 — MC — Classification / measurement channel (AFS FV-OCI vs trading FV-NI)
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — where AFS unrealized holding gains/losses are reported  
**Question:**  
On December 31, Year 1, **Northridge Capital** holds a debt security classified as **available-for-sale**. Amortized cost is $200,000 and fair value is $209,000. No sale has occurred. Ignoring tax, how should Northridge report the $9,000 unrealized holding gain for Year 1?

- A) As a realized gain in net income of $9,000  
- B) As an unrealized gain in **other comprehensive income** of $9,000, with a corresponding debit to Fair Value Adjustment—AFS  
- C) No recognition until the security is sold  
- D) As a direct credit to retained earnings of $9,000, bypassing both NI and OCI  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-3 / ASC 320, AFS debt is measured at fair value with unrealized holding gains and losses **excluded from earnings** and reported in **OCI** until realized. The FVA account is **debited** for holding gains (offset **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI**). Option A describes **trading (FV-NI)**. Option C ignores required period-end FV measurement for AFS. Option D is not GAAP for AFS holding gains.

### Q5 — MC — Sale sequence under FV-OCI
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** disposal with sale-date FV adjust and AOCI reclassification  
**Question:**  
When an AFS debt security is sold under the LO 14-3 approach (fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end), which sequence is correct?

- A) Record cash and remove the investment at amortized cost only; never touch FVA or OCI  
- B) First adjust the investment to fair value through **OCI** (update FVA); then record the sale, **reclassify** the cumulative holding gain/loss from AOCI into **net income**, and eliminate the FVA balance  
- C) First recognize the entire difference between cash and amortized cost in OCI only, with no effect on net income at sale  
- D) Adjust fair value through **net income** (as for trading), then sell with no AOCI reclassification  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Textbook LO 14-3 sale path: **(1)** adjust to FV via FVA and Unrealized Gain or Loss—**OCI**; **(2)** record cash, remove investment at amortized cost, eliminate FVA, and **reclassify** AOCI holding gain/loss into **NI** (realized gain/loss). A is incomplete; C never realizes the gain/loss in NI; D is the **trading / FV-NI** model (LO 14-2), not AFS FV-OCI.
