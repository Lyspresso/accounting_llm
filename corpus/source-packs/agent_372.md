# Agent 372 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-5

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Record dividend distributions, including cash, property, and liquidating  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash dividends:** declaration (Dr Retained Earnings / Cr Dividends Payable); no entry on ex-dividend or record date; payment settles the liability
- **Cumulative preferred allocation schedule:** arrears + current preferred before any common; split Dividends Payable by class
- **Property dividends:** remeasure noncash asset to **fair value** on declaration date (holding gain/loss in income); debit RE and credit Property Dividends Payable at FV; distribute asset against the payable; ignore FV changes after declaration
- **Liquidating dividends:** return of capital — debit **APIC** (not RE) for the liquidating portion; inform shareholders of the split
- **Period-end adjusting / year-end presentation:** declared but unpaid dividends remain **current liabilities**; RE rollforward; property FV remeasurement at declaration near year-end; no post-declaration remeasurement
- **Settlement (payment/distribution) JEs** for cash, property, and mixed liquidating distributions

### Q1 — CORE — Cash (cumulative preferred), property gain, and partially liquidating cash
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JEs for cash, property, and liquidating dividends; preferred allocation schedule; settlement (payment/distribution)  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarvale Industrial Corp.** has the following equity at the start of Year 1:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, **8%**, **$25 par**, **20,000 shares** issued and outstanding | $500,000 |
| Common stock, **$1 par**, **300,000 shares** issued and outstanding | 300,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common | 2,400,000 |
| Retained earnings | 1,650,000 |

Preferred stock is **cumulative** and **nonparticipating**. No preferred or common dividends were declared in the **three** prior years (dividends in arrears for three years).

**Transaction 1 — Cash dividend with cumulative preferred**  
On **February 12, Year 1**, the board declares total cash dividends of **$220,000**, payable **March 15** to stockholders of record on **February 28**.

**Transaction 2 — Property dividend (FV > CV)**  
On **May 5, Year 1**, the board declares a property dividend of Cedarvale’s investment in **4,000 shares** of Lakeshore REIT common stock. Carrying value of the investment is **$72,000**. Fair value on May 5 is **$96,000**. The property dividend is payable **May 28** to stockholders of record on **May 15**. Fair value on May 28 is **$98,500** (post-declaration change).

**Transaction 3 — Partially liquidating cash distribution**  
On **September 20, Year 1**, the board declares a cash distribution of **$150,000** and informs shareholders that **35%** is a **liquidating dividend** (return of capital) and **65%** is a distribution of retained earnings. Payable **October 10**; record date **October 1**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare an **allocation schedule** for the February 12 cash dividend (preferred arrears, preferred current, common remainder) and record the **February 12 declaration**, note the **February 28 record date**, and record the **March 15 payment**.  
b. Record all **May 5** entries for the property dividend (remeasurement and declaration) and the **May 28 distribution**. State the effect of the May 28 fair-value change.  
c. Record the **September 20 declaration** and **October 10 payment** of the partially liquidating distribution.  
d. Prepare a **Year 1 equity-reduction schedule** showing the debit to RE vs APIC for each distribution (exclude holding gains/losses from the equity-reduction columns; show the gain separately).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cash dividend — allocation, declaration, record date, settlement**  
Annual preferred entitlement = $500,000 × 8% = **$40,000**.

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred arrears (3 × $40,000) | 120,000 | — | 120,000 |
| Preferred current (Year 1) | 40,000 | — | 40,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 60,000 | 60,000 |
| **Total** | **160,000** | **60,000** | **220,000** |

Check: 120,000 + 40,000 + 60,000 = **220,000**.

February 12 — declaration (initial recognition):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 220,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 160,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 60,000 |
| *To record cash dividends declared; preferred arrears + current before common* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 220,000.

February 28 — date of record: **No entry.**

March 15 — payment (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 160,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 220,000 |
| *To record payment of cash dividends* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 220,000.

**b. Property dividend at fair value (gain)**  
Holding gain = $96,000 − $72,000 = **$24,000**.

May 5 — remeasure investment to fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Lakeshore REIT | 24,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income (holding gain) | | 24,000 |
| *To adjust investment to FV before property dividend declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000. Investment CV after remeasurement = **$96,000**.

May 5 — declaration at fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 96,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 96,000 |
| *To record property dividend at FV on declaration date* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 96,000.

May 15 — date of record: **No entry.**

May 28 — distribution (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 96,000 | |
| Investment in Lakeshore REIT | | 96,000 |
| *To distribute investment shares to shareholders* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 96,000.

**Post-declaration FV change:** The increase from $96,000 to $98,500 on May 28 is **ignored**. Property dividends are measured at FV on the **declaration** date only; subsequent changes do not remeasure the payable or the dividend amount.

**c. Partially liquidating cash distribution**  
RE portion = $150,000 × 65% = **$97,500**.  
APIC (liquidating) portion = $150,000 × 35% = **$52,500**.

September 20 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 97,500 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 52,500 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 150,000 |
| *To record cash distribution; 35% liquidating return of capital* | | |

**Check:** Dr 97,500 + 52,500 = Cr 150,000.

October 1 — date of record: **No entry.**

October 10 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 150,000 | |
| Cash | | 150,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 150,000.

APIC remaining after liquidating debit = 2,400,000 − 52,500 = **$2,347,500**.

**d. Year 1 equity-reduction schedule (distributions)**

| Distribution | Debit to RE | Debit to APIC | Total equity reduction from distribution | Holding G/L in income (separate) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Feb 12 cash | 220,000 | 0 | 220,000 | — |
| May 5 property | 96,000 | 0 | 96,000 | Gain **24,000** |
| Sept 20 mixed | 97,500 | 52,500 | 150,000 | — |
| **Totals** | **413,500** | **52,500** | **466,000** | **Gain 24,000** |

**Key insight:** Cash and property dividends reduce **retained earnings** (property at **fair value** after a holding gain/loss at declaration). A **liquidating** portion is a return of capital and reduces **APIC**, not RE. Payment/distribution settles the payable; record and ex-dividend dates have **no entries**. FV changes after property-dividend declaration are ignored.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cash, property loss, and liquidating mix
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — preferred allocation, property at FV with loss, liquidating split, settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Westbrook Packaging Inc.** capital structure (relevant accounts):

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, **6%**, **$50 par**, **12,000 shares** outstanding | $600,000 |
| Common stock, **$2 par**, **150,000 shares** outstanding | 300,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common | 1,100,000 |
| Retained earnings | 980,000 |

Preferred is **cumulative** and **nonparticipating**. Dividends are in arrears for **two** preceding years (no dividends declared in those years).

**Cash:** On **June 1**, the board declares total cash dividends of **$125,000**, payable **June 30** to record holders on **June 15**.

**Property:** On **July 8**, Westbrook declares a property dividend of **600 shares** of an investment in Coral Bay Foods. Carrying value is **$18,000**; fair value on July 8 is **$15,000**. Distribution date is **July 25** (record date July 18).

**Liquidating mix:** On **August 1**, Westbrook declares a cash distribution of **$90,000**; management announces that **70%** is liquidating (return of capital) and **30%** is from retained earnings. Payable **August 20**; record date **August 8**.

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the June 1 cash dividend; record declaration and June 30 payment (note record date).  
b. Record July 8 remeasurement and declaration, and July 25 distribution of the property dividend.  
c. Record August 1 declaration and August 20 payment of the mixed liquidating distribution.  
d. Compute ending APIC—Common after the August liquidating debit (ignore other activity).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cash allocation and JEs**  
Annual preferred = $600,000 × 6% = **$36,000**.

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred arrears (2 × $36,000) | 72,000 | — | 72,000 |
| Preferred current | 36,000 | — | 36,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 17,000 | 17,000 |
| **Total** | **108,000** | **17,000** | **125,000** |

Check: 72,000 + 36,000 + 17,000 = **125,000**.

June 1 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 125,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 108,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 17,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 125,000.

June 15 — record date: **No entry.**

June 30 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 108,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 17,000 | |
| Cash | | 125,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 125,000.

**b. Property dividend with fair-value loss**  
Loss = $18,000 − $15,000 = **$3,000**.

July 8 — remeasure to FV:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on Asset Held for Distribution (or Unrealized Holding Loss—Income) | 3,000 | |
| Investment in Coral Bay Foods | | 3,000 |
| *Write investment down to FV before property dividend declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,000. Investment CV after write-down = **$15,000**.

July 8 — declaration at FV:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 15,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000.

July 18 — record date: **No entry.**

July 25 — distribution:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 15,000 | |
| Investment in Coral Bay Foods | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000.

**c. Mixed liquidating distribution**  
RE portion = $90,000 × 30% = **$27,000**.  
APIC portion = $90,000 × 70% = **$63,000**.

August 1 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 27,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 63,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 90,000 |

**Check:** Dr 27,000 + 63,000 = Cr 90,000.

August 8 — record date: **No entry.**

August 20 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 90,000 | |
| Cash | | 90,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 90,000.

**d. Ending APIC—Common**  
1,100,000 − 63,000 = **$1,037,000**.

**Key insight:** Same core pattern as Q1 with all new numbers: allocate cash to preferred (arrears + current) before common; remeasure property to FV (here a **loss**) then debit RE at FV; split liquidating distributions between RE and APIC; settle payables with cash or the asset.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Year-end declared unpaid dividends (period-end adjusting emphasis)
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting recognition of declared dividends; RE rollforward; year-end liability presentation; January settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Specialty Foods Inc.** has a December 31 fiscal year-end. Selected equity and income data for **Year 2**:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, **5%**, **$100 par**, **4,000 shares** outstanding (cumulative, nonparticipating) | $400,000 |
| Common stock, **$0.50 par**, **250,000 shares** outstanding | 125,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common (beginning Year 2) | 875,000 |
| Retained earnings, January 1, Year 2 | 2,100,000 |
| Net income Year 2 (**before** any dividend-related holding gains/losses and **before** dividend declarations) | 480,000 |

No preferred or common dividends were declared in **Year 1** (one year of preferred dividends in arrears at the start of Year 2).

**December Year 2 declarations (all still unpaid at December 31):**

1. **December 12:** Board declares total cash dividends of **$140,000**, payable **January 15, Year 3**, to stockholders of record **January 5, Year 3**.  
2. **December 18:** Board declares a property dividend of Pinecrest’s investment in **2,000 shares** of Redwood Spice Co. Carrying value **$55,000**; fair value on December 18 **$64,000**. Distribution scheduled **January 20, Year 3**. On **December 31**, the investment’s market value is **$67,000** (still held pending distribution).  
3. **December 28:** Board declares a cash distribution of **$80,000** and notifies shareholders that **25%** is **liquidating** (return of capital) and **75%** is from retained earnings. Payable **January 31, Year 3**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **preferred/common allocation schedule** for the December 12 cash dividend.  
b. Record all **December** journal entries (period-end / year-end recognition): cash declaration, property remeasurement and declaration, and mixed liquidating declaration. Explicitly address the December 31 market value of the Redwood investment.  
c. Prepare a **December 31, Year 2** presentation of (i) all dividend-related **current liabilities** and (ii) the **APIC** balance after the liquidating debit.  
d. Prepare a **retained earnings rollforward** for Year 2 (beg RE → end RE).  
e. Record the **January Year 3 settlement** entries for all three distributions (assume cash paid and property distributed as scheduled; no further FV changes before distribution).

**Answer key:**  

**a. December 12 cash allocation**  
Annual preferred entitlement = $400,000 × 5% = **$20,000**.

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred arrears (Year 1) | 20,000 | — | 20,000 |
| Preferred current (Year 2) | 20,000 | — | 20,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| **Total** | **40,000** | **100,000** | **140,000** |

Check: 20,000 + 20,000 + 100,000 = **140,000**. End-of-Year-2 preferred arrears after this declaration = **$0**.

**b. December period-end recognition entries**

December 12 — cash dividend declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 140,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 40,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 100,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 140,000.

December 18 — remeasure investment to FV:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Redwood Spice Co. | 9,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income (holding gain) | | 9,000 |
| *FV $64,000 − CV $55,000 = $9,000 gain at declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000. Investment CV after remeasurement = **$64,000**.

December 18 — property dividend declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 64,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 64,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 64,000.

**December 31 market value $67,000:** **No entry.** Fair-value changes **after** the property-dividend declaration date are ignored; the payable and the RE reduction remain at declaration-date FV of **$64,000**.

December 28 — partially liquidating cash distribution:  
RE portion = $80,000 × 75% = **$60,000**.  
APIC portion = $80,000 × 25% = **$20,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 60,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 20,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr 60,000 + 20,000 = Cr 80,000.

Record dates in January: **No entries** on those dates.

**c. December 31, Year 2 presentation**

**(i) Current liabilities — dividend-related**

| Liability | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 40,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 100,000 |
| Property Dividends Payable | 64,000 |
| Dividends Payable (Dec 28 mixed distribution) | 80,000 |
| **Total current liabilities from declared dividends** | **284,000** |

All are **current liabilities** (not equity). Declaration creates an enforceable obligation even though cash/asset settlement occurs in Year 3.

**(ii) APIC—Common**  
Beginning 875,000 − liquidating debit 20,000 = **$855,000** at December 31, Year 2 (before other APIC activity).

**d. Retained earnings rollforward — Year 2**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning RE, Jan 1, Year 2 | 2,100,000 |
| Net income before dividend-related G/L | 480,000 |
| Property holding gain (Dec 18) | 9,000 |
| Subtotal: earnings available | 2,589,000 |
| Less: Dec 12 cash dividend (RE) | (140,000) |
| Less: Dec 18 property dividend at FV | (64,000) |
| Less: Dec 28 nonliquidating portion | (60,000) |
| **Ending RE, Dec 31, Year 2** | **2,325,000** |

Checks:  
- RE debits total = 140,000 + 64,000 + 60,000 = **264,000**.  
- 2,100,000 + 480,000 + 9,000 − 264,000 = **2,325,000**.  
- Liquidating $20,000 reduced **APIC**, not RE.

**e. January Year 3 settlement (disposal / maturity of the obligation)**

January 5 — record date (cash dividend): **No entry.**

January 15 — pay December 12 cash dividend:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 40,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 140,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 140,000.

January 20 — distribute property dividend:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 64,000 | |
| Investment in Redwood Spice Co. | | 64,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 64,000. (Still measured at declaration FV; Dec 31 $67,000 market is irrelevant.)

January 31 — pay December 28 mixed distribution:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 80,000.

**Key insight (period-end emphasis):** Once the board **declares**, the full accounting hit to RE/APIC and the credit to a **payable** occur in the declaration period—even if payment falls in the next fiscal year. Year-end workpapers must accrue **Dividends Payable** / **Property Dividends Payable** as current liabilities and complete the RE rollforward. Property is remeasured only at **declaration**; subsequent year-end market moves do not adjust the payable.

### Q4 — MC — Declaration vs record date; year-end liability
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Classification — timing of entries; liquidating vs earnings distribution; year-end presentation  

**Question 1:**  
On December 20, a calendar-year company declares a cash dividend payable January 25 to stockholders of record January 10. Which statement is correct?

- A) No liability is recorded until the date of record, when ownership of the dividend right is fixed.  
- B) Debit Retained Earnings and credit Dividends Payable on **December 20**; no entry on the date of record; settle the liability on January 25.  
- C) Debit Dividends Payable and credit Cash on December 20; reverse on January 25.  
- D) The entire dividend reduces APIC because payment is after year-end.

**Answer:** **B.** Declaration creates the enforceable obligation and reduces RE (for a nonliquidating cash dividend). The **date of record** identifies who is paid but generates **no journal entry**. Payment in the following year settles the **current liability** recognized at declaration (period-end adjusting / year-end presentation issue).

**Question 2:**  
A company declares a cash distribution of $200,000 and informs shareholders that **$50,000** is a liquidating dividend and **$150,000** is from retained earnings. At declaration, the company should:

- A) Debit Retained Earnings $200,000 only.  
- B) Debit APIC $200,000 only.  
- C) Debit Retained Earnings $150,000 and debit APIC $50,000; credit Dividends Payable $200,000.  
- D) Debit Retained Earnings $50,000 and debit APIC $150,000; credit Dividends Payable $200,000.

**Answer:** **C.** The **liquidating** portion ($50,000) is a return of capital and reduces **APIC**; the earnings distribution ($150,000) reduces **RE**. The full $200,000 is credited to **Dividends Payable** until cash settlement.
