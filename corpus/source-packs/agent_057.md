# Agent 57 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-1

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Apply lower of cost or net realizable value rule to inventory  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- LCNRV applies to inventory cost methods **other than LIFO and the retail inventory method** (e.g., FIFO, average cost)
- NRV = estimated selling price − reasonably predictable costs of completion, disposal, and transportation
- Three application levels: **individual item** (most common; lowest inventory), **classification/category**, and **inventory in total**
- Period-end write-down when NRV < cost: reduce inventory (directly or via **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to NRV**) and charge **COGS** or **Holding Loss on Inventory**
- No subsequent write-up if NRV recovers; reduced amount becomes “cost” for later periods
- Year-end **allowance rollforward** to required balance after inventory turns
- On sale of written-down inventory: close allowance into COGS so net COGS equals net carrying amount
- Balance sheet presentation: Inventory gross less allowance = inventory, net; income statement: COGS vs separate holding loss

### Q1 — CORE — LCNRV by item/category/total, initial recognition JE, Year-2 allowance adjust, sale close-out
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Initial LCNRV write-down (item approach) with allowance; subsequent year-end allowance adjustment; close allowance on disposal/sale  
**Scenario:**  
Riverbend Outfitters Co. measures merchandise inventory using **FIFO** (not LIFO or retail). At **December 31, Year 1**, cost and net realizable value (NRV) of ending inventory are:

| Item | Category | Cost | NRV |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Ridge Tent | Tents | $12,000 | $11,000 |
| Mesa Tent | Tents | 9,500 | 10,800 |
| Glacier Bag | Sleeping Bags | 26,000 | 27,500 |
| Frost Bag | Sleeping Bags | 38,000 | 34,000 |
| **Total** | | **$85,500** | **$83,300** |

Additional facts:
- Cost of goods sold **before** any LCNRV adjustment for Year 1: **$160,000**.
- Riverbend applies LCNRV using the **individual-item** approach and maintains an **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value**.
- All **January 1, Year 2** inventory (the Year 1 ending inventory) is sold during Year 2. The company adjusts the allowance only at **year-end**.
- At **December 31, Year 2**, cost of ending inventory is **$92,000** and NRV is **$84,500**.
- On **March 20, Year 3**, Riverbend sells **all** Year 2 ending inventory for cash of **$88,000**. Ending inventory cost on the books (before removing the sale) remains **$92,000**, and the allowance still has its Dec 31, Year 2 credit balance until closed at sale.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a schedule computing inventory at LCNRV applied to **(1) individual items**, **(2) each category**, and **(3) inventory in total**. Identify the write-down under the item approach.  
b. Record the **December 31, Year 1** adjusting entry under the item approach **two ways**: (1) charge **Cost of Goods Sold**; (2) charge **Holding Loss on Inventory**. Credit the **allowance** in both cases.  
c. Show Year 1 **balance sheet** inventory presentation and **income statement** impact under both equity treatments in (b).  
d. Compute the **December 31, Year 2** allowance adjustment and record the period-end JE (charge COGS).  
e. Record the **March 20, Year 3** sale of all inventory and the entry to **close the allowance** into COGS. State net COGS related to this inventory layer.

**Answer key:**  

**a. LCNRV schedule**

| Item | Category | Cost | NRV | LCNRV — Item | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Ridge Tent | Tents | 12,000 | 11,000 | **11,000** | NRV < cost |
| Mesa Tent | Tents | 9,500 | 10,800 | **9,500** | Cost lower |
| Glacier Bag | Sleeping Bags | 26,000 | 27,500 | **26,000** | Cost lower |
| Frost Bag | Sleeping Bags | 38,000 | 34,000 | **34,000** | NRV < cost |
| **Totals** | | **85,500** | **83,300** | **80,500** | |

**By category:**  
- Tents: cost \(12{,}000 + 9{,}500 = 21{,}500\); NRV \(11{,}000 + 10{,}800 = 21{,}800\) → LCNRV **$21,500**  
- Sleeping Bags: cost \(26{,}000 + 38{,}000 = 64{,}000\); NRV \(27{,}500 + 34{,}000 = 61{,}500\) → LCNRV **$61,500**  
- Category total LCNRV = \(21{,}500 + 61{,}500 =\) **$83,000**

**By total inventory:** \(\min(85{,}500,\ 83{,}300) =\) **$83,300**

| Application level | Inventory at LCNRV | Write-down vs cost $85,500 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Individual items | **$80,500** | **$5,000** |
| Categories | $83,000 | $2,500 |
| Total inventory | $83,300 | $2,200 |

Item approach produces the **lowest** inventory (no offsetting of Mesa/Glacier “surpluses” against Ridge/Frost deficits).

**b. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition (item approach write-down $5,000)**

(1) Charge COGS:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 5,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 5,000 |
| *To reduce FIFO inventory to LCNRV (item approach)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000.

(2) Charge separate holding loss:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Holding Loss on Inventory | 5,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 5,000 |
| *To recognize inventory holding loss; allowance contra to inventory* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000.

**c. Financial statement impact — Year 1**

| Balance sheet (both methods) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory (at cost) | $85,500 |
| Less: Allowance to reduce inventory to NRV | (5,000) |
| **Inventory, net** | **$80,500** |

| Income statement | (1) via COGS | (2) via Holding Loss |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of goods sold | $165,000 | $160,000 |
| Holding loss on inventory | 0 | 5,000 |
| Effect on pretax income | −5,000 | −5,000 |

**d. December 31, Year 2 — subsequent measurement / period-end allowance adjust**

Required allowance = cost − NRV = \(92{,}000 - 84{,}500 =\) **$7,500** credit  
Unadjusted allowance balance (from Year 1) = **$5,000** credit  
Additional credit needed = \(7{,}500 - 5{,}000 =\) **$2,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 2,500 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 2,500 |
| *To adjust allowance to Year 2 required LCNRV balance* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,500. Ending allowance **$7,500** credit. Inventory, net = \(92{,}000 - 7{,}500 =\) **$84,500**.

**Allowance rollforward schedule**

| Date | Description | Allowance Dr | Allowance Cr | Balance (Cr) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 | Initial LCNRV write-down | | 5,000 | 5,000 |
| 12/31/Y2 | Period-end top-up | | 2,500 | 7,500 |
| 3/20/Y3 | Close on sale of inventory | 7,500 | | 0 |

**e. March 20, Year 3 — sale and disposal/settlement of allowance**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 88,000 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 88,000 |
| *Cash sale of remaining inventory* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 92,000 | |
| Inventory | | 92,000 |
| *Remove inventory at cost* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | 7,500 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 7,500 |
| *Close allowance; reduces COGS to net carrying amount* | | |

**Checks:** Each JE balances. Net COGS on this layer = \(92{,}000 - 7{,}500 =\) **$84,500** (equals prior net inventory). Gross profit = \(88{,}000 - 84{,}500 =\) **$3,500**.

**Key insight:** LCNRV write-downs are recognized in the period utility declines. The item approach forbids offsetting; the allowance is maintained and remeasured at each reporting date; when goods are sold, closing the allowance into COGS aligns expense with the reduced carrying amount already recognized.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — LCNRV schedule and initial recognition JE
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — LCNRV by item/category/total and initial allowance JE  
**Scenario:**  
Cedarline Home Goods LLC uses **average cost** for inventory (not LIFO/retail). December 31 data:

| Item | Category | Cost | NRV |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Oak Desk | Furniture | $45,000 | $42,000 |
| Pine Desk | Furniture | 22,000 | 26,000 |
| Linen Set | Textiles | 18,000 | 19,000 |
| Wool Throw | Textiles | 31,000 | 28,500 |
| **Total** | | **$116,000** | **$115,500** |

COGS before LCNRV adjustment: **$240,000**. Policy: individual-item LCNRV; allowance account; charge **COGS**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute LCNRV by item, by category, and in total; state the write-down under the item approach.  
b. Record the Dec 31 adjusting entry (item approach).  
c. Present Inventory, net on the balance sheet and COGS after adjustment.

**Answer key:**  

**a.**

| Item | Cost | NRV | LCNRV — Item |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Oak Desk | 45,000 | 42,000 | **42,000** |
| Pine Desk | 22,000 | 26,000 | **22,000** |
| Linen Set | 18,000 | 19,000 | **18,000** |
| Wool Throw | 31,000 | 28,500 | **28,500** |
| **Total** | **116,000** | **115,500** | **110,500** |

**By category:**  
- Furniture: cost \(45{,}000 + 22{,}000 = 67{,}000\); NRV \(42{,}000 + 26{,}000 = 68{,}000\) → LCNRV **$67,000**  
- Textiles: cost \(18{,}000 + 31{,}000 = 49{,}000\); NRV \(19{,}000 + 28{,}500 = 47{,}500\) → LCNRV **$47,500**  
- Category total = \(67{,}000 + 47{,}500 =\) **$114,500**

**By total:** \(\min(116{,}000,\ 115{,}500) =\) **$115,500**

| Level | LCNRV inventory | Write-down |
|---|---:|---:|
| Item | **$110,500** | **$5,500** |
| Category | $114,500 | $1,500 |
| Total | $115,500 | $500 |

**b. Initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 5,500 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 5,500 |
| *LCNRV write-down, individual-item approach* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,500.

**c. Presentation**  
Inventory, net = \(116{,}000 - 5{,}500 =\) **$110,500**  
COGS after adjustment = \(240{,}000 + 5{,}500 =\) **$245,500**

**Key insight:** Same LCNRV mechanics as Q1 with all new numbers; item-level measurement still yields the most conservative inventory.

### Q3 — CORE — Unit-level NRV from selling price; period-end JE; classification of methods
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Compute NRV from SP − disposal costs; item-level LCNRV; period-end JE; method scope (FIFO/avg vs LIFO/retail)  
**Scenario:**  
Copperfield Electronics Inc. uses **FIFO**. It applies LCNRV to **each inventory item**. Data at fiscal year-end **June 30**:

| Item No. | Quantity | Est. selling price per unit | Disposal cost per unit | Cost per unit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A-10 | 100 | $48 | $3 | $40 |
| B-20 | 200 | 52 | 4 | 55 |
| C-30 | 150 | 38 | 2 | 30 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute NRV per unit and total inventory cost vs total LCNRV (item approach).  
b. Record the June 30 entry using an allowance and COGS.  
c. State whether Copperfield would use **LCNRV** or **lower of cost or market (LCM)** if it switched to **LIFO**, and why (classification of measurement rule by cost method).  
d. If Copperfield instead **credited Inventory directly** (no allowance) for the same write-down, prepare that alternative JE.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Unit NRV and totals**

| Item | Qty | Cost/u | SP/u | Disp./u | **NRV/u** = SP − Disp. | LCNRV/u | Extended cost | Extended LCNRV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A-10 | 100 | 40 | 48 | 3 | **45** | **40** | 4,000 | 4,000 |
| B-20 | 200 | 55 | 52 | 4 | **48** | **48** | 11,000 | 9,600 |
| C-30 | 150 | 30 | 38 | 2 | **36** | **30** | 4,500 | 4,500 |
| **Total** | | | | | | | **19,500** | **18,100** |

Write-down = \(19{,}500 - 18{,}100 =\) **$1,400** (only B-20 is impaired: \(200 \times (55 - 48) = 1{,}400\)).

**b. Period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 1,400 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 1,400 |
| *Reduce inventory to LCNRV for item B-20* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,400. Inventory, net = **$18,100**.

**c. Method classification**  
If Copperfield used **LIFO** (or the **retail inventory method**), it would apply **lower of cost or market (LCM)** under ASC 330, **not** LCNRV. LCNRV applies only to methods **other than** LIFO and retail (e.g., FIFO, average cost).

**d. Direct write-down alternative**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 1,400 | |
| Inventory | | 1,400 |
| *Direct reduction of inventory to NRV (no allowance)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,400. Inventory carrying amount **$18,100** either way.

**Key insight:** NRV is not always given as a bulk figure—derive it as selling price less completion/disposal/transport costs, compare to cost unit-by-unit, and charge the period of decline. Cost-method classification (FIFO/avg vs LIFO/retail) determines LCNRV vs LCM.

### Q4 — MC (classification / presentation)
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Classification of LCNRV application level and income-statement presentation  

**Question 1:**  
Under ASC 330, which statement about applying lower of cost or net realizable value is **correct**?

- A) LCNRV is required for LIFO inventory; FIFO inventory uses lower of cost or market with a ceiling and floor.  
- B) Applying LCNRV to **individual items** generally produces a **higher** inventory carrying amount than applying it to inventory in total because write-downs can be offset within the pool.  
- C) Applying LCNRV to **individual items** generally produces the **lowest** inventory carrying amount because units with NRV above cost cannot offset units with NRV below cost.  
- D) After a write-down, if NRV later recovers above the reduced carrying amount, the firm must reverse the allowance up to original cost.

**Answer:** C.  
Item-level application is most common and most conservative: no cross-offsetting. A reverses the LCNRV/LCM method mapping. B is backwards. D is incorrect—write-downs are not reversed for subsequent NRV recoveries; the reduced amount is “cost” going forward.

**Question 2:**  
At year-end, Harbor Point Co. (FIFO) must reduce inventory to NRV by $12,000. Management concludes the decline is **usual** for its product cycle. Which presentation is **most consistent** with common practice illustrated for ordinary declines?

- A) Debit Holding Loss on Inventory $12,000; credit Sales Revenue $12,000.  
- B) Debit Cost of Goods Sold $12,000; credit Allowance to Reduce Inventory to NRV $12,000.  
- C) Debit Retained Earnings $12,000; credit Inventory $12,000 (prior-period restatement).  
- D) Debit Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income $12,000; credit Inventory $12,000.

**Answer:** B.  
Ordinary LCNRV declines are typically charged to **COGS** (Nike-style “cost of sales”) with either an allowance or a direct inventory credit. A separate holding-loss line is used when the decline is unusual/infrequent. LCNRV adjustments are **current-period earnings**, not AOCI or automatic prior-period restatements.
