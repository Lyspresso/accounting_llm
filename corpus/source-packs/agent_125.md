# Agent 125 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-2

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for change in accounting principle  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (cumulative effect):** on the first day of the year of a voluntary principle change applied **retrospectively**, adjust the **asset/liability** (e.g., Inventory) and **opening Retained Earnings** for the cumulative direct effect of periods prior to the year of change (prior years are closed — no restatement of prior-period P&L accounts in the books)
- **Retrospective application (ASC 250):** restate all comparative periods presented under the **new** principle; adjust opening balances of assets/liabilities and RE as of the beginning of the earliest period presented for effects of periods before that earliest period
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** recompute **COGS** and ending inventory under the new method for each year; inventory rollforward (Beg + Purchases − End = COGS)
- **Period-end / year-of-change measurement:** after the change date, measure inventory and COGS under the **new** principle only
- **Indirect effects:** nondiscretionary items based on earnings (e.g., bonus) that would have differed under the new principle are **not** restated into prior periods; recognize in the **current period as incurred** when actually adjusted
- **Settlement JE:** pay (settle) any current-period indirect-effect liability (e.g., Bonus Payable)
- **Prospective exception:** when retrospective application is **impracticable** (classic case: change **to LIFO** and prior layers cannot be reconstructed), apply the new principle as of the earliest practicable date; **no** cumulative RE catch-up for unavailable prior periods; beginning inventory becomes the LIFO base layer
- **Not a change in principle:** initial adoption for new/immaterial transactions; adoption because transaction type changed; correction of a non-GAAP error (error correction — LO 21-4)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** nature and reason (preferability); method of applying the change; effects on income, line items, and EPS for current and restated prior periods; cumulative effect on RE as of beginning of earliest period presented; reasons and alternative if retrospective is impracticable; indirect effects if recognized

### Q1 — CORE — LIFO to FIFO retrospective: cumulative JE, COGS schedule, comparative statements, indirect effect, settlement
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of cumulative direct effect (Inventory / Retained Earnings); subsequent COGS schedule under FIFO; comparative income, inventory, and RE presentation; period-end Year 3 measurement under FIFO; current-period indirect bonus JE and settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Merchandising Inc.** (calendar year-end) began operations on **January 1, Year 1** using **LIFO** for financial reporting. On **January 1, Year 3**, Cedarline voluntarily changes to **FIFO** because FIFO more faithfully represents inventory flows given new warehouse systems (preferable). Ignore income taxes.

Inventory and related data:

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory — LIFO | $0 | $36,000 | $40,000 |
| Beginning inventory — FIFO | 0 | 42,000 | 52,000 |
| Purchases | 160,000 | 200,000 | 220,000 |
| Ending inventory — LIFO | 36,000 | 40,000 | 45,000 |
| Ending inventory — FIFO | 42,000 | 52,000 | 65,000 |
| Sales | 190,000 | 270,000 | 290,000 |
| Operating expenses (excl. bonus) | 30,000 | 32,000 | 33,000 |

As originally issued under LIFO, Year 1 net income was **$36,000** and Year 2 net income was **$42,000** (RE end of Year 2 under LIFO = **$78,000**).

Cedarline’s executive plan pays a bonus based on income. After the change, the board agrees that the **Year 2** bonus (to be paid in Year 3) would have been **$800** higher under FIFO. The original Year 2 bonus has already been recorded; the **$800** incremental amount will be recognized and paid in **Year 3**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 initial recognition JE** for the retrospective change from LIFO to FIFO (direct effect only; ignore taxes).  
b. Prepare a **COGS subsequent measurement schedule** under FIFO for Years 1–3 (Beg inv + Purchases − End inv). Also show LIFO COGS for comparison.  
c. Prepare **three-year comparative income statements** as they would be reported **after** the change (all years under FIFO).  
d. Prepare the **comparative inventory (asset) presentation** for December 31, Year 2 and Year 3 after the change.  
e. Prepare **two-year comparative statements of retained earnings** (Year 2 and Year 3) after the change, showing the cumulative-effect line on the earliest year presented.  
f. Prepare the **Year 3 JE for the indirect effect** ($800 bonus) and the **settlement JE** when the incremental bonus is paid in cash.  
g. **Classification / disclosure:** Is this retrospective or prospective? List the key ASC 250 disclosure elements for the year of change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — cumulative direct effect (January 1, Year 3)**

Inventory under FIFO at Jan 1, Year 3 = **$52,000**  
Inventory under LIFO at Jan 1, Year 3 = **$40,000**  
Cumulative difference (direct effect) = $52,000 − $40,000 = **$12,000**

Cross-check via income differences before Year 3:  
Y1: NI FIFO − NI LIFO = (see part b/c) $42,000 − $36,000 = **$6,000**  
Y2: $48,000 − $42,000 = **$6,000**  
Cumulative = **$12,000** ✓  
RE end Y2: LIFO $78,000; FIFO $90,000; difference **$12,000** ✓

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 12,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 12,000 |
| *Retrospective cumulative effect of change from LIFO to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr 12,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement — COGS schedule**

**COGS under FIFO**

| Year | Beg inv (FIFO) | Purchases | End inv (FIFO) | COGS (FIFO) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | $160,000 | $42,000 | **$118,000** |
| 2 | 42,000 | 200,000 | 52,000 | **190,000** |
| 3 | 52,000 | 220,000 | 65,000 | **207,000** |

**COGS under LIFO (as originally computed)**

| Year | Beg inv (LIFO) | Purchases | End inv (LIFO) | COGS (LIFO) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | $160,000 | $36,000 | **$124,000** |
| 2 | 36,000 | 200,000 | 40,000 | **196,000** |
| 3 | 40,000 | 220,000 | 45,000 | **215,000** |

**c. Comparative income statements — after change (FIFO for all years)**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sales | $190,000 | $270,000 | $290,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | 118,000 | 190,000 | 207,000 |
| Gross profit | 72,000 | 80,000 | 83,000 |
| Operating expenses | 30,000 | 32,000 | 33,000 |
| **Net income** | **$42,000** | **$48,000** | **$50,000** |

(Indirect $800 bonus is a Year 3 current-period expense if recognized — see part f; the schedule above matches the core Demo 21-2A style before the bonus line. If the $800 is included in Year 3 operating expenses, Year 3 NI = $50,000 − $800 = **$49,200**.)

**d. Comparative balance sheet — inventory only (after change)**

| Assets at Dec. 31 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (FIFO) | **$52,000** | **$65,000** |

**e. Comparative statements of retained earnings (Year 2 and Year 3 presented with Year 3 report)**

| | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning balance, as previously reported (LIFO) | $36,000 | $90,000 |
| Add: Adjustment for cumulative effect on prior years of changing to FIFO | 6,000 | — |
| Beginning balance, as adjusted | 42,000 | 90,000 |
| Net income (FIFO) | 48,000 | 50,000 |
| **Ending balance** | **$90,000** | **$140,000** |

Notes:  
- Year 2 beginning under LIFO as previously reported = Year 1 NI LIFO = **$36,000**.  
- Cumulative effect **prior to Year 2** = Year 1 only = **$6,000**.  
- Year 3 beginning is already the restated FIFO ending of Year 2 (**$90,000**); no second cumulative line.  
- Year 2 NI as restated (**$48,000**) ≠ Year 2 NI as originally issued (**$42,000**).

**f. Indirect effect and settlement (Year 3)**

*Recognize incremental Year 2 bonus as current-period cost (not retrospective to Year 2)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 800 | |
| Bonus Payable | | 800 |
| *Indirect effect of principle change — current period* | | |

**Check:** Dr 800 = Cr 800. **Balanced.**

*Settlement when paid*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonus Payable | 800 | |
| Cash | | 800 |
| *Pay incremental bonus* | | |

**Check:** Dr 800 = Cr 800. **Balanced.**

**g. Classification / disclosure**

- **Type:** Voluntary **change in accounting principle** (LIFO → FIFO).  
- **Application:** **Retrospective** (not prospective; not a change in estimate).  
- **Direct effects only** in the cumulative RE/inventory entry; **indirect** bonus is current-period.  
- **Disclosures (ASC 250-10-50-1):** nature of and reason for the change, including **why FIFO is preferable**; method of applying the change; description of prior-period information retrospectively adjusted; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, affected line items, and EPS for current and restated prior periods; cumulative effect on retained earnings as of the beginning of the earliest period presented; if indirect effects recognized — description and amounts (and per-share if applicable).

**Key insight:** The January 1 books entry captures only the **cumulative direct effect** through the day before the change (Dr Inventory / Cr RE). Comparative statements then restate every period shown under the **new** principle so users can compare apples to apples.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Average-cost to FIFO retrospective (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: cumulative direct-effect JE (Inventory / RE); COGS schedule under new method; comparative RE and inventory presentation; Year-of-change measurement; indirect royalty settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborpoint Trading LLC** (calendar year-end) used the **weighted-average cost** method for inventory since inception (January 1, Year 1). On **January 1, Year 3**, Harborpoint changes to **FIFO** for financial reporting (preferable for matching current costs with current revenues in a rising-price environment). Ignore taxes.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory — Average | $0 | $28,000 | $32,000 |
| Beginning inventory — FIFO | 0 | 35,000 | 48,000 |
| Purchases | 140,000 | 185,000 | 205,000 |
| Ending inventory — Average | 28,000 | 32,000 | 38,000 |
| Ending inventory — FIFO | 35,000 | 48,000 | 60,000 |
| Sales | 175,000 | 250,000 | 275,000 |
| Operating expenses (excl. royalties) | 28,000 | 30,000 | 31,000 |

As issued under average cost: Year 1 NI = **$35,000**; Year 2 NI = **$39,000**; RE end Year 2 under average = **$74,000**.

A supplier royalty agreement pays **2% of net income**. After restatement, the Year 2 royalty payable (settled in Year 3) is **$180** higher under FIFO than originally recorded. Record and pay the incremental amount in Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (average → FIFO).  
b. Prepare the **FIFO COGS schedule** for Years 1–3 and recompute net income under FIFO for each year.  
c. Prepare **comparative retained earnings** for Year 2 and Year 3 after the change.  
d. State December 31, Year 2 inventory as presented on the **Year 3 comparative balance sheet**.  
e. Prepare the **indirect-effect JE** and **cash settlement JE** for the $180 incremental royalty in Year 3.  
f. Briefly state why the cumulative RE adjustment equals the inventory difference at the change date (ignore taxes).

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 3 — initial cumulative JE**

FIFO inventory Jan 1 Y3 = **$48,000**  
Average inventory Jan 1 Y3 = **$32,000**  
Cumulative direct effect = **$16,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 16,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 16,000 |
| *Retrospective change from average cost to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,000 = Cr 16,000. **Balanced.**

**b. FIFO COGS schedule and net income**

| Year | Beg (FIFO) | Purchases | End (FIFO) | COGS (FIFO) | Sales | OpEx | **NI (FIFO)** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | $140,000 | $35,000 | **$105,000** | $175,000 | $28,000 | **$42,000** |
| 2 | 35,000 | 185,000 | 48,000 | **172,000** | 250,000 | 30,000 | **$48,000** |
| 3 | 48,000 | 205,000 | 60,000 | **193,000** | 275,000 | 31,000 | **$51,000** |

Average-cost NI check (as given):  
Y1: 175,000 − (0+140,000−28,000) − 28,000 = 175 − 112 − 28 = **$35,000**  
Y2: 250 − (28+185−32) − 30 = 250 − 181 − 30 = **$39,000**  
Period-specific income increases: Y1 **+$7,000**; Y2 **+$9,000**; cumulative **+$16,000** ✓

**c. Comparative retained earnings**

| | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning balance, as previously reported (average) | $35,000 | $90,000 |
| Add: Cumulative effect of change to FIFO on prior years | 7,000 | — |
| Beginning balance, as adjusted | 42,000 | 90,000 |
| Net income (FIFO) | 48,000 | 51,000 |
| **Ending balance** | **$90,000** | **$141,000** |

(Year 2 ending under FIFO = 42k + 48k = 90k; RE under average end Y2 was 74k; 90 − 74 = 16k cumulative at change date.)

**d. Comparative inventory presentation**

December 31, Year 2 inventory on Year 3 comparative balance sheet (FIFO) = **$48,000** (not the $32,000 average amount originally reported).

**e. Indirect royalty — current period + settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Royalty Expense | 180 | |
| Royalties Payable | | 180 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Royalties Payable | 180 | |
| Cash | | 180 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180. **Balanced.**

**f. Why inventory difference = RE adjustment**

Ignoring taxes, every dollar of higher ending inventory under the new principle is a dollar of lower cumulative COGS and higher cumulative pretax income closed to RE. At the change date, **Δ Inventory = cumulative Δ NI = Δ Retained Earnings** (direct effects only).

**Key insight:** Same retrospective mechanics as LIFO→FIFO: books catch-up on day one via **Inventory / RE**; comparative statements rewrite history under the new method.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Change to LIFO when retrospective is impracticable (prospective)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Prospective application when retrospective is impracticable; LIFO base layer = beginning inventory; COGS schedule with mixed methods across comparative years; no cumulative RE JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Peak Outfitters Inc.** reported inventory under **FIFO** for Year 1 and Year 2. On **January 1, Year 3**, Summit changes to **LIFO**. After every reasonable effort, management **cannot reconstruct LIFO layers** for prior periods (purchase-price history for old layers is unavailable). Summit can apply LIFO only from Year 3 forward. Year 3 ending inventory under LIFO is determined to be **$70,000**.

Data:

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO going forward) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $50,000 | $62,000 |
| Purchases | 175,000 | 200,000 | 230,000 |
| Ending inventory | 50,000 (FIFO) | 62,000 (FIFO) | 70,000 (LIFO) |
| Sales | 220,000 | 295,000 | 320,000 |
| Operating expenses | 40,000 | 42,000 | 44,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Is retrospective application required? Explain using ASC 250 impracticability.  
b. Prepare any **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (or state that none is made).  
c. Prepare the **three-year comparative COGS schedule** under the approach Summit actually uses.  
d. Compute **Year 3 net income** under the prospective LIFO approach.  
e. Optional contrast: If Year 3 ending inventory under FIFO would have been **$85,000**, what would Year 3 COGS and NI have been under continued FIFO?  
f. **Presentation / disclosure:** How do comparative Year 2 vs Year 3 principles differ on the Year 3 report? What must be disclosed about impracticability?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Retrospective not required**

Change **to LIFO** with inability to reconstruct prior layers meets the **impracticability** exception (ASC 250-10-45-9): after every reasonable effort, the entity cannot apply the requirement (and/or would need unverifiable assumptions/estimates about prior periods). Therefore apply the new principle **prospectively** as of the earliest practicable date (beginning of Year 3).

**b. Cumulative-effect JE**

**No journal entry** on January 1, Year 3 to restate Inventory or Retained Earnings for prior years. Beginning inventory of **$62,000** (FIFO carrying amount) becomes the **LIFO base layer**.

**c. Comparative COGS schedule (prospective)**

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $50,000 | $62,000 |
| Purchases | 175,000 | 200,000 | 230,000 |
| Less ending inventory | 50,000 | 62,000 | 70,000 |
| **Cost of goods sold** | **$125,000** | **$188,000** | **$222,000** |

Years 1–2 COGS remain as previously computed under FIFO; only Year 3 uses LIFO ending inventory.

**d. Year 3 net income (prospective LIFO)**

NI = Sales − COGS − OpEx = $320,000 − $222,000 − $44,000 = **$54,000**

**e. Continued FIFO contrast (Year 3 only)**

COGS if FIFO EI $85,000: $62,000 + $230,000 − $85,000 = **$207,000**  
NI if FIFO: $320,000 − $207,000 − $44,000 = **$69,000**  
Difference in Year 3 NI: LIFO NI is **$15,000 lower** than continued FIFO ($69,000 − $54,000), equal to the $15,000 lower ending inventory ($85,000 − $70,000).

**f. Presentation / disclosure**

- Year 3 comparative statements show **Year 2 still under FIFO** and **Year 3 under LIFO** — principles are **not uniform** across periods (comparability impaired).  
- Disclose: nature and reason for the change (preferability); that retrospective application is **impracticable** and **why**; description of the **alternative method** used (prospective from Year 3; base layer = beginning inventory).  
- Do **not** restate prior inventory or RE for unavailable LIFO layers.

**Key insight:** Retrospective is the default for voluntary principle changes, but a change **to LIFO** is the textbook case where **prospective** application applies because prior-period layers cannot be rebuilt.

### Q4 — CORE — Period-end / presentation focus: average → FIFO with comparative BS only + Year 3 measurement
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Period-of-change cumulative JE from ending inventory differences; classification of comparative inventory on BS; Year 3 period-end COGS under new method  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverbrook Goods Co.** changes from **average cost to FIFO** on **January 1, Year 3**. Ignore taxes. Inventory balances:

| Inventory at December 31 | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ending inventory, FIFO | $70,000 | $85,000 |
| Ending inventory, average cost | 55,000 | 60,000 |

Year 3 activity under FIFO: beginning inventory (FIFO) **$85,000**; purchases **$310,000**; ending inventory (FIFO) **$95,000**; sales **$480,000**; operating expenses **$55,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 entry** to record the accounting change.  
b. Show how the **December 31, Year 2 inventory** is presented on the comparative December 31, Year 3 balance sheet (amounts material).  
c. Compute **Year 3 COGS and net income** under FIFO (period-end measurement after the change).  
d. Prepare a brief **inventory rollforward schedule** for Year 3 under FIFO.  
e. **Disclosure:** What cumulative effect amount is disclosed as of the beginning of the earliest period presented if Year 2 and Year 3 are comparative (i.e., earliest year presented is Year 2)?

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 3 cumulative JE**

Difference at Dec 31 / Jan 1 of change year:  
FIFO EI Year 2 $85,000 − Average EI Year 2 $60,000 = **$25,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 25,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 25,000 |
| *Retrospective change from average cost to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 25,000 = Cr 25,000. **Balanced.**

(Of the $25,000, $15,000 arose by end of Year 1 [$70k − $55k] and an additional $10,000 in Year 2 [($85k − $60k) − ($70k − $55k)].)

**b. Comparative inventory presentation**

On the Year 3 comparative balance sheet, December 31, Year 2 inventory is reported at the **FIFO** amount **$85,000** (restated), not the $60,000 average amount from the original Year 2 report.

**c. Year 3 COGS and NI under FIFO**

COGS = $85,000 + $310,000 − $95,000 = **$300,000**  
NI = $480,000 − $300,000 − $55,000 = **$125,000**

**d. Year 3 inventory rollforward (FIFO)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory (FIFO, after cumulative adj.) | $85,000 |
| + Purchases | 310,000 |
| Goods available | 395,000 |
| − Ending inventory (FIFO) | 95,000 |
| **COGS** | **$300,000** |

**e. Cumulative effect disclosed for earliest period presented (Year 2)**

If Year 2 is the earliest year presented, the cumulative effect **prior to Year 2** (i.e., through Year 1) is:  
FIFO Y1 EI $70,000 − Average Y1 EI $55,000 = **$15,000**  
That $15,000 adjusts beginning RE of Year 2 on the comparative RE statement. The full $25,000 books entry on Jan 1 Year 3 still brings the ledger from average to FIFO as of the change date (covering both Y1 and Y2 effects).

**Key insight:** Presentation restates comparative prior-year balances under the new principle; the **ledger** catch-up is a single opening entry equal to the **full** cumulative inventory difference at the change date.

### Q5 — MC — Method of applying a principle change
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Classification of retrospective vs prospective application for voluntary change in inventory method  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 4, **Oakridge Distributors** voluntarily changes its inventory costing method from FIFO to LIFO. After every reasonable effort, Oakridge cannot determine LIFO inventory amounts for any year before Year 4. How should Oakridge report the change?

- A) Restate all prior periods presented under LIFO and debit Retained Earnings for the cumulative decrease in inventory  
- B) Apply LIFO prospectively from Year 4, using beginning Year 4 inventory as the LIFO base layer; do not restate prior periods  
- C) Report the cumulative effect as a separate line on the Year 4 income statement (below income from continuing operations)  
- D) Treat the change as a change in accounting estimate effected by a change in principle and revise prior depreciation-style schedules  

**Answer:** **B.** When retrospective application is impracticable (typical for a change **to LIFO**), the new principle is applied **prospectively** from the earliest practicable date. Beginning inventory becomes the LIFO base; prior periods remain under the old method. A is the retrospective inventory treatment used when prior amounts **can** be determined (e.g., LIFO→FIFO). C is the obsolete “cumulative effect on income” presentation (pre-ASC 250 style), not current GAAP for voluntary principle changes. D confuses this with depreciation method changes (LO 21-1), which are prospective as estimate-effected-by-principle — different fact pattern.

### Q6 — MC — Direct vs indirect effects
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Direct effects restated retrospectively; indirect effects recognized currently if incurred  
**Question:**  
**Pinecrest Appliances** retrospectively changes from LIFO to FIFO on January 1, Year 5. The change increases inventory and retained earnings by $40,000 for periods before Year 5 (ignore taxes). Pinecrest’s board also retroactively increases the Year 4 profit-sharing bonus by $3,000 because restated Year 4 income is higher; the extra $3,000 will be paid in Year 5. Which statement is correct?

- A) Debit Inventory $43,000 and credit Retained Earnings $43,000 on January 1, Year 5  
- B) Debit Inventory $40,000 and credit Retained Earnings $40,000; record Compensation Expense $3,000 in Year 5 when the bonus is recognized  
- C) Restate Year 4 bonus expense upward by $3,000 in the comparative income statement and reduce opening RE by only $37,000  
- D) Report both the $40,000 and the $3,000 as a cumulative-effect line on the Year 5 income statement  

**Answer:** **B.** Retrospective application includes only **direct** effects (inventory and related tax effects). **Indirect** effects (bonus, profit sharing, royalties based on earnings) are **not** pushed into prior periods; if actually incurred, they are recognized in the **period of the change**. A incorrectly folds the indirect bonus into the cumulative RE entry. C incorrectly restates prior bonus expense for an indirect effect. D uses obsolete income-statement cumulative-effect presentation and mixes direct/indirect.
