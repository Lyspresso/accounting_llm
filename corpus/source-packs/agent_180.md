# Agent 180 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-2

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting in a periodic inventory system  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** record purchases and freight-in in temporary accounts under the gross method (Purchases, Freight-In); no COGS at sale
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-period COGS schedule / inventory roll-forward — BI + Purchases, net = CGAS; CGAS − EI (physical count) = residual COGS; EI becomes next period’s BI
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** close temporary purchase accounts and beginning inventory; record ending inventory from physical count; plug residual COGS
- **Settlement JE:** pay AP within discount (Purchase Discount) or after discount (full cash); purchase returns/allowances; under net method, lost discount → Interest Expense
- **Classification:** temporary contra-purchase accounts; Inventory as BS current asset; lost discount as financing cost (not inventoriable)
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Two-month subsequent measurement schedule; gross-method cycle; period-end close
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement of inventory/COGS under periodic system — multi-period COGS schedule and inventory roll-forward; initial purchase/freight JEs (gross); settlement of AP; period-end adjusting entries  
**Scenario:**

**April transactions**

| Apr 3 | Paid freight **$400** cash on the Apr 3 purchase (f.o.b. shipping point). |
| Apr 8 | Returned defective goods; supplier credit **$500** (purchase return). |
| Apr 12 | Paid **40%** of the **remaining** accounts payable on the Apr 3 invoice **within** the discount period. |
| Apr 20 | Paid the **remaining** Apr 3 accounts payable **after** the discount period. |
| Apr 25 | Purchased additional merchandise on account, **$7,500**, terms **2/10, n/30**. |
| Apr 28 | Paid the entire Apr 25 invoice **within** the discount period. |
| Apr 29 | Sold merchandise on account, **$18,000**. |
| Apr 30 | Physical inventory count: ending inventory cost = **$8,200**. |

**May transactions**

|---|---|

| | April | May |

| Cost of goods sold (income statement) | $15,774 | $13,220 |

**Key insight:** Under the periodic system, **subsequent measurement** of inventory and COGS is an **end-of-period allocation**: CGAS is split between ending inventory (from the physical count) and residual COGS. Temporary purchase accounts accumulate activity; one closing entry each period replaces BI with EI. Multi-period schedules must roll forward (prior EI = next BI).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Net method; lost-discount settlement; period-end only from temp balances
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Periodic net method — initial recognition at net; settlement with lost discount as Interest Expense; subsequent COGS schedule; period-end adjusting JE from temporary-account balances  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperfield Distributors LLC** uses a **periodic inventory system** and the **net method** for purchase discounts. Month of **November**. Beginning inventory November 1: **$9,600**.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Nov 4 | Purchased merchandise, list invoice **$14,000**, terms **2/10, n/30**. Record under the **net method**. |
| Nov 4 | Paid freight-in **$420** cash (f.o.b. shipping point). |
| Nov 7 | Returned goods; supplier credit **$700** of list (record net-of-discount return). |
| Nov 13 | Paid **half** of the remaining net AP **within** the discount period. |
| Nov 22 | Paid the **remaining** half **after** the discount period (lost discount). |
| Nov 26 | Sold merchandise on account for **$19,200**. |
| Nov 30 | Physical inventory count: ending inventory = **$8,450**. |

**Required:**  
a. Journal entries for Nov 4 through Nov 26 under the **net method** (include settlement of the lost discount).  
b. Prepare the **subsequent measurement COGS schedule** for November (lost discount is **not** part of purchases, net).  
c. Prepare the **Nov 30 period-end adjusting entry**.  
d. **Classification:** where COGS, Interest Expense (lost discount), and Inventory appear; state the three amounts.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Net-method journal entries**

*Nov 4 — Purchase recorded net of 2%*  
Net purchase = \(14{,}000 \times 0.98 = \mathbf{\$13{,}720}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchases | 13,720 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 13,720 |
| *Initial recognition at net-of-discount* | | |

**Check:** 13,720 = 13,720.

*Nov 4 — Freight-in*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Freight-In | 420 | |
| Cash | | 420 |

**Check:** 420 = 420.

*Nov 7 — Return at net*  
Net return = \(700 \times 0.98 = \mathbf{\$686}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 686 | |
| Purchase Returns and Allowances | | 686 |
| *Return reduces net AP and contra-purchase* | | |

**Check:** 686 = 686.  
AP remaining: \(13{,}720 - 686 = \mathbf{\$13{,}034}\).

*Nov 13 — Pay half within discount*  
Half of remaining net AP = \(13{,}034 / 2 = \mathbf{\$6{,}517}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 6,517 | |
| Cash | | 6,517 |
| *AP already net; no separate Purchase Discount* | | |

**Check:** 6,517 = 6,517.  
AP remaining: \(\mathbf{\$6{,}517}\).

*Nov 22 — Pay remaining after discount (lost discount)*  
List half remaining after return: remaining list AP after return = \(14{,}000 - 700 = 13{,}300\); half of list = \(13{,}300 / 2 = \mathbf{\$6{,}650}\)  
Alternatively: net remaining \(6{,}517\) + lost discount \(6{,}650 - 6{,}517 = \mathbf{\$133}\) (check: \(6{,}650 \times 0.02 = 133\)).  
Cash paid = **$6,650**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 6,517 | |
| Interest Expense | 133 | |
| Cash | | 6,650 |
| *Lost discount = finance charge, not inventory cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(6{,}517 + 133 = 6{,}650\) = Cr 6,650. AP = 0.

*Nov 26 — Sale (revenue only)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 19,200 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 19,200 |

**Check:** 19,200 = 19,200.

**(b) Subsequent measurement COGS schedule**

Purchases, net = Purchases (already net) + Freight-In − Purchase R&A  
= \(13{,}720 + 420 - 686 = \mathbf{\$13{,}454}\)  
(Lost discount **$133** is Interest Expense — excluded from purchases, net.)

| Cost of Goods Sold — November | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $9,600 |
| + Purchases, net | 13,454 |
| = Cost of goods available for sale | 23,054 |
| − Ending inventory | (8,450) |
| = **Cost of goods sold** | **$14,604** |

**(c) Nov 30 — Period-end adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (ending) | 8,450 | |
| Purchase Returns and Allowances | 686 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 14,604 | |
| Purchases | | 13,720 |
| Freight-In | | 420 |
| Inventory (beginning) | | 9,600 |

**Check:** Dr \(8{,}450 + 686 + 14{,}604 = \mathbf{23{,}740}\); Cr \(13{,}720 + 420 + 9{,}600 = \mathbf{23{,}740}\). Balanced.  
(No Purchase Discount temporary account under net method when discounts taken leave no balance and lost discounts go to Interest Expense.)

**(d) Classification / presentation amounts**  
1. **Cost of goods sold $14,604** — income statement, cost of sales (gross profit).  
2. **Interest Expense $133** — income statement **financing** cost (not inventoriable; not COGS).  
3. **Inventory $8,450** — balance sheet current asset (physical-count cost).

**Key insight:** Net method measures Purchases/AP at the net invoice from day one. Timely payment clears net AP with cash only; late payment books **Interest Expense** for the lost discount. Subsequent measurement of COGS still uses BI + purchases, net − EI after the physical count — financing charges never enter the COGS schedule.

### Q4 — MC — Periodic subsequent measurement and temporary accounts (optional)
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Classification of residual COGS measurement and temporary accounts in a periodic system  

**Question 1:**  
Under a **periodic** inventory system, after purchases, freight-in, discounts, and returns are accumulated in temporary accounts and ending inventory is determined by physical count, cost of goods sold is measured as:

- A) Debit to Inventory for each sale times unit cost under FIFO  
- B) Beginning inventory + Purchases, net − Ending inventory (residual)  
- C) Ending inventory + Purchases, net − Beginning inventory  
- D) Cash paid to suppliers during the period  

**Answer:** **B.** COGS is the residual after allocating cost of goods available for sale (BI + Purchases, net) between ending inventory (physical count) and expense. A is perpetual/FIFO unit tracking. C reverses BI and EI. D confuses cash payments with inventoriable cost flows.

---

**Question 2:**  
Northvale (gross method) took purchase discounts of $226 in April. In the **period-end adjusting entry**, Purchase Discount is:

- A) Credited to increase net purchases  
- B) Debited to close the temporary contra-purchase account (reduces CGAS / COGS)  
- C) Debited to Interest Expense  
- D) Left open as a permanent balance-sheet account  

**Answer:** **B.** Under the gross method, Purchase Discount is a temporary **credit-balance** contra-purchase account during the period; at period-end it is **debited** (closed) so that net purchases and residual COGS reflect discounts taken. A confuses direction of the close. C is net-method lost-discount treatment. D is incorrect — purchase temps are closed each period.
