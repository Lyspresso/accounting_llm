# Agent 257 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-3

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Describe the accounting for a change in reporting entity  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Definition / scope:** A change in reporting entity produces financial statements that are, in effect, those of a **different reporting entity** (ASC Glossary). Limited mainly to: (a) consolidated or combined FS in place of individual-entity FS; (b) changing subsidiaries in the consolidated group; (c) changing entities in combined FS
- **Not a change in reporting entity:** Business combination under the **acquisition method**; consolidation of a **VIE** under Topic 810
- **Retrospective approach:** Restate **all prior periods presented** as if the new reporting entity had always been in place (ASC 250-10-45-21); restate previously issued interim information; do **not** change interest cost previously capitalized under Subtopic 835-20
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** Multi-year schedule of previously reported (separate / parent-only) NI and EPS vs restated consolidated or combined NI and EPS; period-specific effects; RE (equity) cumulative-effect bridge at the beginning of the earliest period presented
- **Initial recognition / conversion JE:** Acquisition of the investee at book value; cost-to-equity conversion catch-up (Investment and beginning Retained Earnings) for post-acquisition undistributed earnings before consolidating
- **Period-end adjusting / eliminating JE:** Eliminate reciprocal intercompany receivable/payable (and investment vs sub equity) so consolidated totals are not overstated
- **Disposal / deconsolidation (composition change):** When an entity previously consolidated or combined **no longer meets criteria**, remove it from the reporting entity and apply the change **retrospectively**
- **Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-6):** Nature and reason; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, OCI, and related **EPS** for **all periods presented**; cumulative effect on RE (or other equity) as of beginning of **earliest period presented**; subsequent periods need not repeat the note
- **Number-variant twin:** Same consolidation-from-separate path with fully changed amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Begin consolidating a previously unconsolidated subsidiary: restatement schedule (emphasis), conversion JE, elims, disclosure
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement / restatement schedule of NI and EPS under the new reporting entity (emphasis); initial acquisition and equity-method conversion JEs; RE cumulative-effect bridge; consolidation and period-end intercompany eliminating entries; ASC 250-10-50-6 disclosure; contrast with acquisition method  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgewater Industries Inc.** (calendar year-end) has owned **100%** of **Maplefield Components LLC** since **January 1, Year 1**. Through Year 2, Ridgewater issued **parent-only** financial statements and accounted for Maplefield under the **cost method** (dividend income only). Maplefield was not consolidated.

Effective for the **Year 3** annual report (with **comparative Year 2**), Ridgewater determines that Maplefield meets the criteria for consolidation and will present **consolidated** financial statements. There is **no** goodwill (acquisition was at book value). **Ignore income taxes.** Weighted-average common shares outstanding: **100,000** in all years (no preferred stock).

**Acquisition and Maplefield equity data:**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash paid for 100% of Maplefield, January 1, Year 1 (= book value) | $320,000 |
| Maplefield common stock (constant) | 240,000 |
| Maplefield retained earnings, January 1, Year 1 | 80,000 |

| Maplefield | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $60,000 | $80,000 | $100,000 |
| Dividends declared and paid | 20,000 | 25,000 | 30,000 |

**Ridgewater separate-entity results (cost method):**

| Ridgewater | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating income (excludes dividend income) | $500,000 | $560,000 | $620,000 |
| Dividend income from Maplefield | 20,000 | 25,000 | 30,000 |
| Separate-entity net income | 520,000 | 585,000 | 650,000 |

**Additional facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Ridgewater recorded the acquisition of Maplefield.
2. At **December 31, Year 3**, Ridgewater has a **$42,000** receivable from Maplefield; Maplefield has the corresponding payable (open account; will be settled in Year 4).
3. No intercompany inventory profits or other intercompany sales remain in ending inventory.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for Ridgewater’s acquisition of Maplefield.  
b. Classify the Year 3 change. State the required **accounting approach** (retrospective vs prospective) and what periods are affected.  
c. Prepare the **subsequent measurement / restatement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing: (i) previously reported parent-only NI and EPS; (ii) restated consolidated NI and EPS; (iii) period-specific effect of the change on NI and EPS. *(Emphasis angle.)*  
d. Compute the **cumulative effect on retained earnings** as of the beginning of the **earliest period presented** (January 1, Year 2) and as of January 1, Year 3. Show the undistributed-earnings bridge.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 conversion JE** that places the investment on the **equity method** for post-acquisition undistributed earnings through December 31, Year 2 (retrospective catch-up to beginning RE).  
f. Prepare the **basic consolidation eliminating entry** against Maplefield’s equity and the investment account **as of January 1, Year 3** (after the conversion in e).  
g. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end eliminating JE** for the intercompany receivable/payable.  
h. **Classification / disclosure:** List the ASC 250-10-50-6 disclosures required in the Year 3 statements (include Year 2 NI and EPS effects as dollar amounts).  
i. **Contrast:** If instead Ridgewater had **first purchased** Maplefield on January 1, Year 3 in an acquisition-method business combination, would that be a change in reporting entity? How would prior periods be treated?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — acquisition of Maplefield (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Maplefield Components LLC | 320,000 | |
| Cash | | 320,000 |
| *Acquire 100% of Maplefield at book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 320,000 = Cr 320,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Classification and approach**

- **Type:** **Change in reporting entity** — presenting **consolidated** financial statements in place of **individual-entity** (parent-only) statements, and changing the composition of entities for which consolidated statements are presented (ASC Glossary; Demo 21-3 item 1).  
- **Approach:** **Retrospective.** Restate **all prior periods presented** (here Year 2 comparative, and any Year 1 history used in multi-year schedules/notes) as if Maplefield had always been consolidated. Previously issued **interim** information is also presented retrospectively.  
- **Not** prospective; **not** a prior-period error correction. Interest cost previously capitalized under Subtopic 835-20 is **not** changed when restating.

**c. Restatement schedule (subsequent measurement of reported results under the new entity) — EMPHASIS**

Maplefield RE rollforward (support schedule):

| Date | Computation | Maplefield RE |
|---|---|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | given | 80,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 80,000 + 60,000 − 20,000 | 120,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 120,000 + 80,000 − 25,000 | 175,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 175,000 + 100,000 − 30,000 | 245,000 |

Consolidated NI (no unrealized intercompany profits)  
= Ridgewater **operating** income + Maplefield NI  
(equivalently: parent separate NI − dividend income + Maplefield NI)

| Year | Parent-only NI (as previously reported) | Parent-only EPS | Consolidated NI (restated) | Consolidated EPS | Effect on NI | Effect on EPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $520,000 | $5.20 | $500,000 + $60,000 = **$560,000** | **$5.60** | **+$40,000** | **+$0.40** |
| 2 | 585,000 | 5.85 | 560,000 + 80,000 = **640,000** | **6.40** | **+55,000** | **+0.55** |
| 3 (current) | 650,000 | 6.50 | 620,000 + 100,000 = **720,000** | **7.20** | **+70,000** | **+0.70** |

EPS checks: \(560{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 5.60\); \(640{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 6.40\); \(720{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 7.20\).  
Period effects: consolidated NI replaces dividend income with the sub’s full NI, so period effect = Maplefield NI − dividends = undistributed earnings of that year.

**d. Cumulative effect on beginning retained earnings**

Post-acquisition **undistributed** Maplefield earnings = Maplefield NI − dividends:

| Period | NI − Div | Undistributed |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | 60,000 − 20,000 | 40,000 |
| Year 2 | 80,000 − 25,000 | 55,000 |
| Years 1–2 cumulative | | **95,000** |

- **As of January 1, Year 2** (beginning of earliest period presented in the Year 3 comparative report): cumulative effect on RE = **$40,000** increase (Year 1 undistributed earnings of the sub not previously in consolidated RE).  
- **As of January 1, Year 3:** cumulative effect on RE = **$95,000** increase.

**e. January 1, Year 3 — equity-method conversion (retrospective catch-up / consolidation setup on parent books)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Maplefield Components LLC | 95,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 95,000 |
| *Recognize post-acquisition undistributed earnings of Maplefield for Years 1–2 (cost → equity conversion for consolidation)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 95,000 = Cr 95,000. **Balanced.**  
Investment carrying amount after conversion = \(320{,}000 + 95{,}000 = \mathbf{\$415{,}000}\).

**f. January 1, Year 3 — basic consolidation eliminating entry**

Maplefield equity at January 1, Year 3: CS \(240{,}000\) + RE \(175{,}000\) = **$415,000** (= investment after conversion).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock—Maplefield | 240,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Maplefield | 175,000 | |
| Investment in Maplefield Components LLC | | 415,000 |
| *Eliminate 100% investment against Maplefield’s equity at beginning of Year 3* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(240{,}000 + 175{,}000 = 415{,}000\) = Cr 415,000. **Balanced.**

**g. December 31, Year 3 — period-end intercompany eliminating JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intercompany Payable (Due to Ridgewater) | 42,000 | |
| Intercompany Receivable (Due from Maplefield) | | 42,000 |
| *Eliminate reciprocal intercompany balances in consolidation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 42,000 = Cr 42,000. **Balanced.**

**h. Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-6) — Year 3 (period of change)**

1. **Nature** of the change (Maplefield now consolidated; previously parent-only) and **reason**.  
2. Effect of the change on **income from continuing operations**, **net income**, **OCI** (none here), and related **per-share amounts** for **all periods presented** (at least Year 2 restated and Year 3). Example: Year 2 NI higher by **$55,000**; EPS higher by **$0.55** (\(6.40 - 5.85\)). Year 3 NI higher by **$70,000**; EPS higher by **$0.70**.  
3. **Cumulative effect** on retained earnings as of the beginning of the earliest period presented: **+$40,000** at January 1, Year 2.  
4. Financial statements of **subsequent periods need not repeat** these disclosures.  
5. If the change is immaterial in the period of change but reasonably certain to be material later, still disclose nature and reason when presenting the period of change.

**i. Contrast — acquisition on January 1, Year 3**

**No.** A business combination accounted for by the **acquisition method** is **not** a change in reporting entity. Results are combined **prospectively from the acquisition date**; **prior periods are not restated** as if the acquiree had always been included (though certain pro forma disclosures may be required under other guidance). That is the opposite of the retrospective CRE treatment in parts b–h.

**Key insight:** Changing **who** is inside the reporting boundary (consolidate a sub that was out, or change the combined group) is a **change in reporting entity** applied **retrospectively**. The **subsequent measurement schedule** recasts each prior period’s NI and EPS as if the new entity always existed; undistributed sub earnings drive both the RE cumulative-effect bridge and the investment conversion debit. Buying a company under the acquisition method is **not** a CRE—prior periods stay as previously reported.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Restatement schedule twin with conversion, elims, disclosure
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: subsequent measurement restatement schedule of NI/EPS (emphasis); acquisition JE; cost-to-equity conversion; RE cumulative effect; consolidation elim; period-end IC elim; ASC 250 disclosure amounts  
**Scenario:**  
**Northpeak Capital Inc.** has owned **100%** of **Fernwood Logistics LLC** since **January 1, Year 1**, accounted for under the **cost method**, and historically issued **parent-only** statements. Beginning with **Year 3** comparative statements (Year 2 restated), Northpeak will **consolidate** Fernwood — a **change in reporting entity**. Acquisition at book value; **ignore taxes**. WASO: **100,000** shares all years.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash paid for 100% of Fernwood, January 1, Year 1 | $210,000 |
| Fernwood common stock (constant) | 150,000 |
| Fernwood RE, January 1, Year 1 | 60,000 |

| Fernwood | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $50,000 | $70,000 | $90,000 |
| Dividends | 15,000 | 20,000 | 25,000 |

| Northpeak (cost method) | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Dividend income from Fernwood | 15,000 | 20,000 | 25,000 |
| Separate-entity net income | 465,000 | 530,000 | 605,000 |

At **December 31, Year 3**, Northpeak has a **$33,000** receivable from Fernwood (reciprocal payable on Fernwood’s books). No unrealized intercompany inventory profit.

**Required:**  
a. January 1, Year 1 **initial recognition JE** for the acquisition.  
b. Classify the change and state the accounting approach.  
c. **Restatement schedule** of parent-only vs consolidated NI and EPS for Years 1–3; show period effects on NI and EPS. *(Emphasis.)*

e. **January 1, Year 3** equity-method conversion JE (undistributed earnings Years 1–2).  
f. **January 1, Year 3** basic consolidation eliminating entry after conversion.  
g. **December 31, Year 3** intercompany receivable/payable eliminating JE.  
h. State the **minimum disclosure** elements for Year 3 under ASC 250-10-50-6 (include the Year 2 NI and EPS effects as dollar amounts).

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Fernwood Logistics LLC | 210,000 | |
| Cash | | 210,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 210,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Classification / approach**  
**Change in reporting entity**; **retrospective** application to all prior periods presented (and prior interim information). Not prospective; not an acquisition-method combination.

**c. Restatement schedule (emphasis)**

Fernwood RE: Jan 1 Y1 **60,000** → Dec 31 Y1 \(60{,}000 + 50{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \mathbf{95{,}000}\) → Dec 31 Y2 \(95{,}000 + 70{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{145{,}000}\) → Dec 31 Y3 \(145{,}000 + 90{,}000 - 25{,}000 = \mathbf{210{,}000}\).

Consolidated NI = Northpeak operating income + Fernwood NI:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 3 | 605,000 | 6.05 | 580,000 + 90,000 = **670,000** | **6.70** | **+65,000** | **+0.65** |

Undistributed: Y1 \(50{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \mathbf{35{,}000}\); Y2 \(70{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{50{,}000}\); cumulative Y1–Y2 = **$85,000**.

- January 1, Year 2 (earliest period if Y2–Y3 comparative): **+$35,000**

| Retained Earnings | | 85,000 |

**f. January 1, Year 3 — eliminating entry**

Fernwood equity Jan 1 Y3: \(150{,}000 + 145{,}000 = 295{,}000\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock—Fernwood | 150,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Fernwood | 145,000 | |
| Investment in Fernwood Logistics LLC | | 295,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Common-control combination schedule, deconsolidation settlement JE, acquisition contrast
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Combined financial statements under common control as a change in reporting entity; multi-year combined results schedule (subsequent measurement emphasis); deconsolidation removing an entity from the reporting group (settlement JE); distinguish acquisition-method combination (not CRE); ASC 250 disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Redline Manufacturing Co.** and **Copperfield Distribution LLC** have been under **common control** of the same family holding company since Year 1. Through Year 3 each issued **separate** financial statements only. In **Year 4**, after a reorganization, management presents **combined** financial statements of Redline and Copperfield for Year 4 with **comparative Year 3** (and discloses Year 2 for the multi-year note). No intercompany transactions. **Ignore taxes.**

| Separate NI | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Redline Manufacturing Co. | $300,000 | $340,000 | $390,000 |
| Copperfield Distribution LLC | 120,000 | 145,000 | 165,000 |

**Part B — Deconsolidation / composition change (Year 5):**  
On **January 1, Year 5**, Copperfield **no longer meets the criteria** for inclusion in the combined group (common-control combination criteria fail). Redline will report **without** Copperfield. Copperfield remains a separate legal entity (not sold). At January 1, Year 5, Copperfield’s stand-alone balances are:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Identifiable assets (various) | $520,000 |
| Liabilities (various) | 140,000 |
| Equity (net assets) | 380,000 |

Assume the combined reporting entity had been keeping a single set of “combined books” that included Copperfield’s accounts. Management records a **deconsolidation entry** on January 1, Year 5, establishing an investment (or removing net assets) at carrying amount.

**Part C — Contrast fact:** On **March 1, Year 5**, Redline **acquires** unrelated **Ironwave Packaging Inc.** for cash in an acquisition-method business combination.

**Required:**  
a. Classify the Year 4 change to combined reporting. State the accounting approach.  
b. Prepare a **combined results schedule** for Years 2–4 (combined NI) and the **period-specific effect** vs Redline-only NI. *(Emphasis on subsequent measurement schedule.)*  
c. Cumulative effect on equity of the combined entity at **January 1, Year 3** (beginning of earliest comparative period in the Year 4 report) attributable to including Copperfield — assume Copperfield RE at Jan 1 Y2 was **$200,000** and Copperfield paid **no dividends** in Years 2–3 (so Y2–Y3 NI all retained). Show the bridge.  
d. Prepare the **January 1, Year 5 deconsolidation (settlement of combination relationship) JE** that removes Copperfield’s assets and liabilities and records Investment in Copperfield at net carrying amount.  
e. After the Year 5 composition change, how are **Year 4 comparative** figures presented in the Year 5 report?  
f. Is the **March 1, Year 5 acquisition** of Ironwave a change in reporting entity? Explain prior-period treatment.  
g. **Disclosure:** What must Redline disclose in Year 4 regarding the change to combined statements?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**  
**Change in reporting entity** — presenting **combined** financial statements in place of financial statements of **individual** entities, and changing entities included in combined FS.  
**Retrospective** application to all prior periods presented.

**b. Combined results schedule (subsequent measurement — emphasis)**

| Year | Redline-only NI | Copperfield NI | Combined NI | Effect vs Redline-only |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | $300,000 | $120,000 | **$420,000** | **+$120,000** |
| 3 | 340,000 | 145,000 | **485,000** | **+145,000** |
| 4 | 390,000 | 165,000 | **555,000** | **+165,000** |

Checks: \(300{,}000 + 120{,}000 = 420{,}000\); \(340{,}000 + 145{,}000 = 485{,}000\); \(390{,}000 + 165{,}000 = 555{,}000\).

**c. Cumulative effect on equity at January 1, Year 3**

Copperfield RE at Jan 1 Y2 given **$200,000**. No dividends Years 2–3; Year 2 NI **$120,000** all retained.  
Equity of Copperfield at Jan 1 Y3 = \(200{,}000 + 120{,}000 = \mathbf{\$320{,}000}\).

When the combined entity first presents comparative Year 3, the **cumulative effect on equity at the beginning of the earliest period presented (Jan 1, Year 3)** from including Copperfield equals Copperfield’s equity at that date (**$320,000**), assuming Redline-only equity is the other component of combined equity and there is no new basis under common control (carryover basis).

(If the note focuses only on **earnings** retained after a chosen start date: Year 2 Copperfield NI included in restated combined RE = **$120,000** incremental vs Redline-only.)

**d. January 1, Year 5 — deconsolidation / settlement JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Copperfield Distribution LLC | 380,000 | |
| Liabilities (various—Copperfield) | 140,000 | |
| Assets (various—Copperfield) | | 520,000 |
| *Remove Copperfield from the combined reporting entity; record investment at carrying amount of net assets* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(380{,}000 + 140{,}000 = 520{,}000\) = Cr 520,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Year 5 comparative presentation**  
Because removing Copperfield from the reporting entity is itself a **change in reporting entity**, Year 4 (and any other prior periods presented) are **restated retrospectively** to **exclude** Copperfield — as if the new (Redline-only) reporting entity had been in place in those periods (Demo 21-3 item 2). Combined Year 4 NI of $555,000 would be restated to Redline-only **$390,000** when presented comparatively in Year 5 (absent other adjustments).

**f. Acquisition of Ironwave**  
**Not** a change in reporting entity. Acquisition-method business combinations are **excluded** from the CRE definition. Ironwave’s results are included from **March 1, Year 5 forward**; **prior periods are not restated** to include Ironwave.

**g. Year 4 disclosures (ASC 250-10-50-6)**  
Nature and reason for presenting combined statements of Redline and Copperfield; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, OCI, and EPS for all periods presented (e.g., Year 3 NI higher by **$145,000**); cumulative effect on equity/RE at beginning of earliest period presented (e.g., **+$320,000** equity of Copperfield at Jan 1, Year 3, or the earnings bridge disclosed in the note); subsequent periods need not repeat.

**Key insight:** CRE is about the **reporting boundary** (who is in the statements), applied **retrospectively**. The multi-year **combined results schedule** shows how each period’s performance is remeasured under the new entity. **Common-control combination** and **criteria-based deconsolidation** are CREs; **acquisition-method purchases** are not—even though the group’s legal composition changes.

### Q4 — MC — Accounting treatment for a change in reporting entity
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Retrospective application is required for a change in reporting entity (not prospective; not merely a prior-period adjusting entry without restatement)  
**Question:**  
A change in reporting entity is accounted for through:

- A) A prior-period adjusting entry only, with no restatement of comparative financial statements  
- B) Retrospective application of the new reporting entity to all prior periods presented  
- C) Restatement and reissuance of prior statements solely because an error was found  
- D) Prospective application of the new reporting entity to future periods only  

**Answer:** **B.**  
ASC 250-10-45-21 requires that when an accounting change results in financial statements that are, in effect, those of a **different reporting entity**, the change is **retrospectively applied** to the financial statements of **all prior periods presented**. Option A understates the requirement (comparative periods must be recast). Option C confuses CRE with **error correction**. Option D is the treatment for **changes in estimate**, not CRE.

### Q5 — MC — Identifying which event is a change in reporting entity
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Classify events as CRE vs acquisition-method combination vs estimate/principle; only CRE items get retrospective entity restatement under LO 21-3  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **both** a change in reporting entity **and** accounted for retrospectively?

- A) Parent acquires 100% of an unrelated company for cash on July 1 and consolidates from the acquisition date under the acquisition method  
- B) Parent changes from FIFO to weighted-average inventory costing for all inventories  
- C) Parent begins presenting consolidated financial statements that include a wholly owned subsidiary that previously issued only separate, unconsolidated parent-only statements (subsidiary now meets consolidation criteria)  
- D) Parent revises the useful life of manufacturing equipment from 10 years to 7 years  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Presenting consolidated statements in place of individual-entity statements (or changing which subsidiaries are consolidated) is a classic **change in reporting entity** and is applied **retrospectively**.  

- **A is wrong:** Acquisition-method business combinations are **explicitly not** changes in reporting entity; prior periods are **not** recast as if the acquiree were always consolidated.  
- **B is wrong:** Inventory method change is a **change in accounting principle** (LO 21-2), not a change in reporting entity.  
- **D is wrong:** Useful-life revision is a **change in estimate** (prospective) under LO 21-1.
