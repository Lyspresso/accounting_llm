# Agent 354 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-7

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Account for research and development costs  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **General rule (ASC 730):** research and development costs are **charged to expense when incurred**
- **Initial recognition:** materials/equipment/facilities/intangibles with **no alternative future use** → R&D expense immediately; with **alternative future uses** → **capitalize** as assets
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-use PPE depreciated SL; multi-project intangibles amortized; **period charges classified as R&D expense**
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end R&D depreciation/amortization; materials with alt. uses expensed as R&D **when consumed**; accrued R&D salaries/services; building depreciation allocated to R&D vs non-R&D
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** sell multi-use R&D-related PPE at carrying amount (gain/loss ≠ R&D); write off single-use items still on books to R&D
- **Classification / disclosure:** R&D vs non-R&D (commercial production QC, seasonal/routine updates, unrelated G&A); external patent registration legal fees → **patent asset** (not R&D); **disclose total R&D expense** (ASC 730-10-50-1)
- **Number-variant twin:** same framework, all amounts changed

**d. Total Year 3 R&D expense (disclosure)**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Already recorded cash R&D during Year 3 | $520,000 |
| Multi-use equipment depreciation | 55,000 |
| Multi-project license amortization | 6,000 |
| Materials used | 59,000 |
| Accrued R&D salaries | 21,000 |
| Building depreciation (R&D 40%) | 12,800 |
| Write-off of single-use prototype fixture | 45,000 |
| **Total R&D expense disclosed** | **$718,800** |

**Arithmetic check:** AJE R&D = 55 + 6 + 59 + 21 + 12.8 + 45 = **198,800**; 520,000 + 198,800 = **718,800**.

**Excluded from R&D:** building admin depreciation $19,200; ending materials inventory $18,000 (asset until used); multi-use equipment remaining book value (only current-year dep is R&D).

**e. Brief explanations**  
- **Admin building portion:** G&A not clearly related to R&D is **excluded** from R&D under ASC 730 (only a reasonable allocation of costs clearly related to R&D is included).  
- **Prototype write-off:** equipment acquired for a **specific** research project with **no alternative future use** is R&D; leaving it capitalized was incorrect — write-off settles the asset into R&D expense (disposal/settlement of an R&D-related single-use asset).

**Key insight (emphasis):** Most R&D “recognition” at year-end is **adjusting entries** — depreciation, amortization, materials used, accruals, and space allocation — that convert multi-use assets and unpaid costs into the **disclosed R&D expense** total.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 13-7  
**Concept:** Classification of expenditures as R&D expense vs non-R&D vs capitalized multi-use asset; computation of disclosed R&D total  

**Question 4-1:**  
HarborWave Instruments summarized the following for its annual reporting period:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Materials used in R&D projects | $80,000 |
| Equipment acquired with significant alternative future uses (including future R&D); annual depreciation on this equipment | purchase $300,000; depreciation **$50,000** |
| Personnel costs of R&D projects | 200,000 |
| Consulting fees paid to outsiders for R&D | 40,000 |
| Indirect costs reasonably allocable to R&D | 30,000 |
| Quality control during commercial production | 25,000 |
| Equipment acquired for a single R&D project with **no** alternative future use | 100,000 |

What amount should HarborWave report as research and development expense?

- A) $450,000  
- B) $500,000  
- C) $525,000  
- D) $750,000  

**Answer:** **B. $500,000.**  
R&D = materials used $80,000 + depreciation of multi-use equipment $50,000 + personnel $200,000 + consulting $40,000 + indirect $30,000 + single-use equipment $100,000 = **$500,000**.  
Exclude: multi-use equipment **purchase** $300,000 (capitalized); commercial production QC $25,000 (non-R&D).  
Check: 80 + 50 = 130; +200 = 330; +40 = 370; +30 = 400; +100 = **500,000**.  
(D wrongly includes the $300,000 purchase minus dep add-back confusion; C includes QC; A omits single-use equipment.)

**Question 4-2:**  
Which of the following is **initially capitalized as an asset** rather than expensed immediately as R&D under ASC 730?

- A) Salaries of scientists performing laboratory research aimed at a new product  
- B) Cost of a specialized testing unit usable only on one current R&D project with no alternative future use  
- C) Cost of laboratory equipment that will be used on many present and future R&D projects over several years  
- D) Design and construction of preproduction prototypes before commercial production begins  

**Answer:** **C.** Multi-use equipment with **alternative future uses** is capitalized and subsequently depreciated to R&D expense. A, B, and D are charged to R&D expense as incurred (personnel; single-use equipment; preproduction prototypes before commercial production).
