# Agent 395 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-10

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Explain the accounting for transfers of investments  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase debt classified AFS (or HTM); equity measured at FV-NI before significant influence
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** after AFS → HTM transfer, amortize transfer-date AOCI unrealized G/L as a yield adjustment over remaining life (offsetting premium/discount amortization); roll-forward HTM carrying value and AOCI
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** AFS fair-value adjustment through OCI; post-transfer interest (coupon + discount/premium amort + AOCI yield amort); TS fair-value adjustment through NI
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement JE (emphasis):** full multi-entry maturity day (final coupon, final amorts, face collection); sale/disposal of trading securities after transfer into TS; settlement of reclassified investment at face
- **Transfer JEs at fair value:** AFS → HTM (no NI); HTM → AFS (FV through OCI, no NI); AFS → TS (holding G/L to NI); equity FV-NI → equity method (FV = new cost basis)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** describe circumstances of transfer; transfers among debt categories should be rare (especially into/out of HTM and TS); no automatic AFS→TS solely because maturity is within one year
- **Number-variant twin:** same AFS→HTM life cycle through maturity with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — AFS purchase, FV adjust, transfer AFS→HTM, amort schedule, period-end JEs, **maturity settlement (emphasis)**
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Initial AFS recognition; period-end OCI fair-value adjustment; AFS→HTM transfer at fair value with no NI effect; subsequent measurement schedule amortizing AOCI unrealized loss as yield adjustment; period-end interest JEs; **full maturity-day settlement JEs (emphasis)**; transfer disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborview Partners LLC** is a calendar-year investor.

**Facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Harborview purchases **$300,000** face of **Cascade Rail Authority** 5% bonds for cash of **$300,000** (at par). Interest is paid annually each **December 31**. Bonds mature **December 31, Year 5**. Management classifies the bonds as **available-for-sale (AFS)**.
2. On **December 31, Year 1**, the fair value of the Cascade bonds is **$282,000**. Harborview records the year-end fair-value adjustment. (Amortized cost remains **$300,000** because the bonds were purchased at par.)
3. On **January 1, Year 2**, management documents a change in intent and ability and **transfers** the Cascade bonds from **AFS to held-to-maturity (HTM)**. Fair value on the transfer date is still **$282,000**. The transfer-date unrealized holding loss in AOCI is **$18,000**. Remaining life to maturity is **4 years**. Harborview uses **straight-line** amortization of the transfer-date discount and of the AOCI unrealized loss as a yield adjustment.
4. Harborview holds the bonds to maturity and settles for face on **December 31, Year 5**.

Ignore taxes, credit losses (ACL), and brokerage fees.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for the AFS purchase.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** cash interest JE and the **period-end fair-value adjusting JE** (AFS through OCI).  
c. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 transfer JE(s)** from AFS to HTM. State the effect on **net income** and on **OCI** on the transfer date.  
d. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 2–5 showing: beginning HTM carrying value; cash coupon; discount amortization; AOCI amortization; net interest revenue; ending HTM carrying value; ending AOCI unrealized loss balance.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 period-end interest-related JEs** (cash coupon, discount amortization, AOCI yield amortization).  
f. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **complete December 31, Year 5 maturity / settlement package**: final cash coupon, final discount amortization, final AOCI amortization, and collection of face. Show that HTM CV and AOCI are fully cleared.  
g. **Classification / disclosure:** What note disclosure is required regarding the transfer? Why are transfers into/out of HTM expected to be rare?

**g. Classification / presentation / disclosure**

- Notes should **describe the circumstances** leading to the transfer of a security to a different classification.
- Transfers **from HTM** should be **rare** except for the limited changes in circumstances in ASC 320-10-25-6 (e.g., significant deterioration in issuer creditworthiness, major business combination/disposition necessitating sale/transfer to maintain interest-rate or credit-risk policy, statutory/regulatory changes, etc.). An unjustified sale/transfer of HTM can **taint** intent to hold other HTM securities to maturity.
- Transfers **into or from trading** should also be **rare**.
- AFS securities are **not** automatically transferred to trading merely because maturity is within one year or management intends to sell within one year.
- **AFS → HTM transfer date:** no effect on **net income**.

**Key insight:** AFS→HTM is measured at **fair value on the transfer date** with **no NI hit**. The transfer-date unrealized G/L stays in **AOCI** and is **amortized as a yield adjustment** over remaining life. On **maturity settlement day**, record final coupon, final discount and AOCI amorts (so CV = face and AOCI = 0), then **Dr Cash / Cr HTM** for face — a clean settlement with no residual gain/loss.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — AFS→TS transfer then **disposal by sale**; HTM→AFS; equity FV-NI→equity method
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Transfer AFS→TS with holding loss to NI; subsequent TS FV adjust; **disposal/sale settlement JE (emphasis)**; transfer HTM→AFS through OCI (no NI); equity transfer FV-NI→equity method (FV = new cost basis); classification/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Westfall Industrial Co.** (calendar year-end) has several investment reclassifications and a sale during Year 2.

**Facts — Part A (debt AFS → trading, then disposal):**
1. Westfall holds **Summit Chemical** bonds classified as **AFS**. Amortized cost = **$90,000**. At **December 31, Year 1**, fair value was **$84,000** and the related FVA and OCI unrealized loss of **$6,000** were already recorded.
2. On **January 1, Year 2**, management reclassifies the Summit bonds from **AFS to trading (TS)** because it now intends to sell them in the near term for working-capital needs. Fair value on the transfer date remains **$84,000**. Assume the Dec 31 Year 1 FV adjust is still in place.
3. On **March 20, Year 2**, just before sale, fair value is **$86,500**. Westfall updates the TS fair-value adjustment through NI, then **sells** the entire TS position for cash of **$86,500** (disposal / settlement).

**Facts — Part B (debt HTM → AFS):**
4. Westfall also holds **Prairie Gas** bonds classified as **HTM** with amortized cost **$55,000**. On **April 1, Year 2**, due to a significant deterioration in Prairie Gas’s published credit rating, Westfall transfers the bonds from **HTM to AFS**. Fair value on the transfer date is **$51,500**. (This change in circumstances does not taint other HTM holdings.)

**Facts — Part C (equity FV-NI → equity method):**
5. Westfall owns **2,400** shares (24% of voting stock) of **Oakridge Components Inc.**, originally purchased for **$18** per share and measured at **FV-NI** (no significant influence initially). On **July 1, Year 2**, after board representation and other indicators of influence, Westfall concludes it now has **significant influence** and must apply the **equity method**. Fair value on the transfer date is **$27** per share. The investment was already marked to fair value at the prior reporting date; current FV still equals $27/share. (If equity method had always been used, the investment balance would have been $58,000 — this amount is **not** used; no retrospective catch-up.)

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 2 transfer JE** from AFS to TS for Summit Chemical bonds. State the **NI** effect on the transfer date.  
b. Record the **March 20, Year 2 period-end-style FV adjusting JE** for TS (to $86,500) and the **disposal / sale settlement JE**.  
c. Record the **April 1, Year 2 transfer JE** from HTM to AFS for Prairie Gas bonds. State the **NI** effect.  
d. Record the **July 1, Year 2 transfer JE** from FV-NI equity to the equity method for Oakridge. What is the **new cost basis**?  
e. **Classification / disclosure:** Which of these debt transfers should be rare? What disclosure is required?

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 2 — transfer AFS → TS at fair value (holding loss to NI)**

Unrecognized holding loss at transfer = $90,000 − $84,000 = **$6,000** (already in AOCI via FVA).  
On transfer to TS, that holding loss is recognized in **net income**, and FV **$84,000** becomes the TS cost basis.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Summit Chemical Bonds | 84,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 6,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 6,000 | |
| Investment in AFS—Summit Chemical Bonds | | 90,000 |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 6,000 |
| *Transfer AFS→TS at FV; reclassify AOCI unrealized loss into NI; clear AFS and FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 84,000 + 6,000 + 6,000 = Cr 90,000 + 6,000. **Balanced.**  
**NI on transfer date:** **$6,000 unrealized loss** recognized in net income (ASC 320-10-35-10b).  
TS new cost basis = **$84,000**.

**b. March 20, Year 2 — TS FV update then disposal / sale settlement (EMPHASIS)**

Unrealized holding gain since transfer = $86,500 − $84,000 = **$2,500**.

*Mark TS to fair value through NI before sale*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 2,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,500 |
| *Adjust TS Summit bonds to FV just before disposal* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,500. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount of TS (cost + FVA) = 84,000 + 2,500 = **$86,500**.

*Disposal / sale settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 86,500 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 2,500 |
| Investment in TS—Summit Chemical Bonds | | 84,000 |
| *Sell TS Summit bonds; remove cost basis and FVA; cash equals carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 86,500 = Cr 2,500 + 84,000. **Balanced.**

**Alternative combined view of disposal economics:**  
Total NI from transfer through sale = transfer-date loss **$(6,000)** + post-transfer unrealized gain **$2,500** = **net $(3,500)** versus original AFS amortized cost of $90,000 sold for $86,500. After the pre-sale FV adjust, the sale itself has **no additional gain or loss**.

**c. April 1, Year 2 — transfer HTM → AFS at fair value (no NI)**

Carrying amount (amortized cost) = **$55,000**. Fair value = **$51,500**.  
Unrealized holding loss of **$3,500** recognized in **OCI** (not NI). Thereafter account under AFS rules.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS—Prairie Gas Bonds | 55,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 3,500 | |

| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 3,500 |

**NI on transfer date:** **none** (ASC 320-10-35-10c).  
Net AFS carrying amount on BS = 55,000 − 3,500 FVA = **$51,500**.

**d. July 1, Year 2 — transfer equity FV-NI → equity method**

Fair value on transfer date = 2,400 × $27 = **$64,800**.  
FV becomes the **new cost basis** under the equity method. No retrospective application of the equity method; the $58,000 “as-if equity method” balance is **ignored**.

Assume the FV-NI investment account already equals fair value **$64,800** after prior mark-to-market:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Oakridge—Equity Method | 64,800 | |

| *Transfer from FV-NI to equity method; FV = new cost basis* | | |

**Check:** Dr 64,800 = Cr 64,800. **Balanced.**  
**New cost basis** = **$64,800**. Any previously recognized FV-NI gains/losses remain in retained earnings (already in NI of prior periods). Going forward, apply equity-method rules (share of earnings, dividends as reduction of investment, etc.).

**e. Classification / presentation / disclosure**

- Transfers **into or from trading (TS)** should be **rare**. Transfers **from HTM** should also be **rare**, except for the limited ASC 320-10-25-6 circumstances (here: significant deterioration in issuer creditworthiness for Prairie Gas — acceptable and does not taint other HTM securities).
- Financial statement notes should **describe the circumstances** leading to each transfer.
- AFS is **not** automatically moved to trading merely because maturity is within one year.
- **Summary of transfer-date NI effects:** AFS→TS: holding G/L to **NI**; HTM→AFS: FV adjust through **OCI**, no NI; equity FV-NI→equity method: any remaining unrealized G/L through transfer date already in / to **NI**; FV = new basis.

**Key insight:** Transfer **into trading** forces any previously unrecognized holding G/L into **net income**, and FV becomes the TS basis. On **disposal**, update TS to FV through NI, then settle **Dr Cash / Cr TS cost / Cr (or Dr) FVA** so sale cash equals carrying amount with no residual plug. Maturity of post-transfer HTM (Q1–Q2) and sale of post-transfer TS (Q3) are the two primary **settlement** paths after reclassification.

### Q4 — MC — Where holding G/L is recognized on transfer / at maturity settlement
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Classification — which transfer recognizes holding gains/losses in net income on the transfer date; maturity settlement of HTM after AFS→HTM transfer  
**Question:**  
Which statement is **correct** under US GAAP (ASC 320 / ASC 323) regarding transfers of investments and subsequent settlement?

- A) Transfer from AFS to HTM recognizes any transfer-date unrealized holding gain or loss immediately in **net income**.
- B) Transfer from HTM to AFS recognizes the fair-value adjustment on the transfer date in **net income**.
- C) Transfer from AFS to trading (TS) recognizes any unrecognized holding gain or loss existing as of the transfer date in **net income**; fair value becomes the new TS cost basis. When an HTM bond (after an earlier AFS→HTM transfer) is held to maturity, settlement is at face with no residual gain/loss after final discount and AOCI yield amortizations.
- D) Transfer of an equity investment from FV-NI to the equity method requires retrospective restatement of the investment account as if the equity method had always been applied, using the as-if equity-method carrying amount as the new basis.

**Answer:** **C.**  
- A is wrong: AFS→HTM has **no NI effect**; AOCI is amortized as a yield adjustment (ASC 320-10-35-10d).  
- B is wrong: HTM→AFS adjusts carrying amount to FV through **OCI**, not NI (ASC 320-10-35-10c).  
- C is correct: AFS→TS puts holding G/L in **NI** (ASC 320-10-35-10b); post-transfer HTM maturity settlement collects face after amorts clear the transfer-date discount and AOCI.  
- D is wrong: **no retrospective** equity-method catch-up; **fair value** on the transfer date becomes the new cost basis (ASC 323-10-35-33).
