# Agent 114 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-1

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Accounting for Restricted Stock Plans  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (restricted stock awards):** At grant, debit Unearned Compensation—Equity (contra-equity) at grant-date fair value; credit Common Stock and Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par for shares issued into trust
- **Grant-date fair value lock:** Post-grant changes in market price do **not** remeasure total compensation for equity-classified awards
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** Straight-line amortization of total grant-date fair value over the requisite service (vesting) period
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Debit Compensation Expense; credit Unearned Compensation—Equity (RSA) or Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock (RSU)
- **Restricted stock units (RSUs):** No equity stock issuance at grant; expense builds Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock until shares are issued at vesting
- **Disposal / forfeiture JE (as incurred):** Reverse Common Stock, PIC, remaining Unearned Compensation, and previously recognized Compensation Expense
- **Settlement at vesting (RSUs):** Debit PIC—Restricted Stock; credit Common Stock and PIC in Excess of Par for shares issued
- **Estimated forfeitures:** Reduce total expected compensation at grant; cumulative-catch-up if estimate changes
- **Classification / presentation:** Unearned Compensation—Equity is a **contra-equity** (not an asset); Compensation Expense on the income statement
- **Number-variant twin:** Same RSA mechanics with fully changed quantities, par, fair value, and service period

### Q1 — CORE — Restricted stock awards: grant through amortization, presentation, and forfeiture
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE at grant-date fair value; subsequent straight-line amortization schedule; period-end adjusting JE; balance sheet presentation; forfeiture (as incurred) settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Cedarline Analytics Inc.** grants **2,400** shares of its **$2 par** common stock as **restricted stock awards** to one senior officer. The shares are issued on the grant date and held in trust; they vest only if the officer remains employed for **three years** (requisite service period = vesting period).  

Grant-date market price (fair value) = **$28** per share.  
During Year 1 the stock trades between $24 and $33; the December 31, Year 1 closing price is **$31**.  
Cedarline elects to account for forfeitures **as incurred** (not estimated at grant).  

On **January 1, Year 2**, before any Year 2 amortization, the officer resigns and forfeits the entire award.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **total compensation cost** at the grant date. Explain whether Year 1 market-price changes affect that amount.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** (issuance of restricted stock).  
c. Prepare a **three-year straight-line amortization schedule** of Unearned Compensation—Equity (assuming the officer stays and no forfeitures).  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** to recognize compensation expense.  
e. Show the **December 31, Year 1 stockholders’ equity presentation** related to this award and the Year 1 income statement amount.  
f. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 journal entry** for the forfeiture.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Total compensation at grant**  
Total compensation = 2,400 shares × $28 = **$67,200**.  

Grant-date fair value is locked under ASC 718 for equity-classified awards. The Year 1 trading range ($24–$33) and Dec. 31 price of $31 are **irrelevant** to remeasurement of total compensation cost.

**b. January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (issuance of restricted stock)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unearned Compensation—Equity (2,400 × $28) | 67,200 | |
| Common Stock (2,400 × $2) | | 4,800 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock ($67,200 − $4,800) | | 62,400 |
| *Record restricted shares at grant-date FV; unearned compensation is contra-equity* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **67,200**; Cr 4,800 + 62,400 = **67,200**. Balanced.

**c. Subsequent measurement — amortization schedule (no forfeiture path)**  

| Year | Beginning unearned | Compensation expense | Ending unearned |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $67,200 | $22,400 | $44,800 |
| Year 2 | 44,800 | 22,400 | 22,400 |
| Year 3 | 22,400 | 22,400 | 0 |
| **Total** | | **$67,200** | |

Annual expense = $67,200 ÷ 3 years = **$22,400**.

**d. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 22,400 | |
| Unearned Compensation—Equity ($67,200 / 3) | | 22,400 |
| *Amortize unearned compensation over requisite service period* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **22,400**. Balanced.

**e. December 31, Year 1 presentation**

*Stockholders’ equity (related amounts):*

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock | $4,800 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common stock | 62,400 |
| Unearned compensation—equity ($67,200 − $22,400) | **(44,800)** |
| Net equity effect of award (before expense closed to RE) | **$22,400** |

*Income statement — Year 1:*  
Compensation expense **$22,400**.

**f. January 1, Year 2 — Forfeiture (as incurred)**  
Reverse the stock accounts, reverse remaining unearned compensation, and reverse compensation expense previously recognized.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock | 4,800 | |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 62,400 | |
| Unearned Compensation—Equity ($67,200 − $22,400) | | 44,800 |
| Compensation Expense | | 22,400 |
| *Derecognize award and reverse prior compensation cost upon forfeiture* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,800 + 62,400 = **67,200**; Cr 44,800 + 22,400 = **67,200**. Balanced.

*After this entry:* Common Stock, PIC, Unearned Compensation, and Comp Expense related to the award are all zero for this grant.

**Key insight:** For **restricted stock awards**, stock is issued at grant but restricted; **Unearned Compensation—Equity** (contra-equity) captures unearned cost and is amortized **straight-line** over the requisite service period. Forfeiture (as incurred) **reverses** both the equity accounts and previously recognized expense.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Restricted stock awards twin
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of RSA initial recognition, multi-year amortization schedule, period-end expense, full-period vesting settlement of unearned balance, and equity presentation  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Harborpoint Systems Corp.** grants **3,000** shares of **$1 par** common stock as restricted stock awards to its CFO. Shares are issued into trust on the grant date and vest after a **four-year** requisite service period if the CFO remains employed.  

Grant-date fair value = **$42** per share.  
No forfeitures are expected or occur. Harborpoint records forfeitures as incurred (policy irrelevant here because none occur).  
Market price at Dec. 31, Year 1 is $48; ignore for measurement.

**Required:**  
a. Compute total compensation cost and prepare the **January 1, Year 1 grant (initial recognition) JE**.  
b. Prepare the **four-year amortization schedule** of Unearned Compensation—Equity.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4** period-end adjusting entries (or state the annual amount if identical).  
d. After the Year 4 amortization entry, state the balances in Unearned Compensation—Equity, Common Stock (from this award), and PIC in Excess of Par (from this award).  
e. Briefly explain why no additional “issuance” entry is needed at the vesting date for an RSA (contrast conceptually with RSUs).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Total compensation and grant JE**  
Total compensation = 3,000 × $42 = **$126,000**.

*January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unearned Compensation—Equity | 126,000 | |
| Common Stock (3,000 × $1) | | 3,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock ($126,000 − $3,000) | | 123,000 |

**Check:** Dr = **126,000**; Cr 3,000 + 123,000 = **126,000**. Balanced.

**b. Amortization schedule**  
Annual expense = $126,000 ÷ 4 = **$31,500**.

| Year | Beginning unearned | Expense | Ending unearned |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $126,000 | $31,500 | $94,500 |
| Year 2 | 94,500 | 31,500 | 63,000 |
| Year 3 | 63,000 | 31,500 | 31,500 |
| Year 4 | 31,500 | 31,500 | 0 |
| **Total** | | **$126,000** | |

**c. Period-end adjusting entries (each of Dec. 31, Year 1–Year 4)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 31,500 | |
| Unearned Compensation—Equity | | 31,500 |

**Check each year:** Dr = Cr = **31,500**.  
Four years × $31,500 = **$126,000** total expense.

**d. Balances after Year 4 amortization**  
- Unearned Compensation—Equity: **$0**  
- Common Stock (this award): **$3,000** (remains outstanding; restrictions lift)  
- Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—CS (this award): **$123,000**  

Net equity from the award after full vesting and full expense recognition equals the original $126,000 of common equity issued, with expense of $126,000 having reduced retained earnings over the four years.

**e. RSA vs RSU at vesting**  
Under an **RSA**, shares were already issued at grant (offset by unearned compensation). At vesting, restrictions lapse—no re-issuance entry is required once unearned compensation has been fully amortized. Under an **RSU**, shares are **not** issued until vesting; a settlement entry reclassifies Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock into Common Stock and PIC in Excess of Par.

**Key insight:** Changing share count, par, fair value, and service years scales the same RSA pattern: **one grant-date equity issuance**, then **straight-line amortization** until unearned compensation is zero.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Restricted stock units: expense schedule, estimated forfeitures, change in estimate, and settlement at vesting
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** RSU period-end adjusting JEs; subsequent measurement with estimated forfeitures; cumulative catch-up for change in estimate; disposal/settlement JE when units vest and shares are issued; classification of PIC—Restricted Stock  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Meridian Peak Foods Inc.** grants a total of **6,000 restricted stock units** to six key managers (**1,000 RSUs each**). Each unit exchanges for **one share** of **$0.50 par** common stock after a **three-year** requisite service period.  

Grant-date fair value = **$20** per share.  
No shares are issued at grant.  

**Policy:** Meridian **estimates forfeitures** (entity-wide election under ASC 718).  
On January 1, Year 1, Meridian estimates that **1,200 units** (20%) will be forfeited due to turnover over the three-year period.  

**Additional facts:**  
- **December 31, Year 1:** No actual forfeitures; original estimate still appropriate.  
- **December 31, Year 2:** No actual forfeitures have yet occurred, but Meridian **revises** the estimated total forfeitures upward to **1,800 units** (30% of 6,000).  
- **December 31, Year 3:** Actual cumulative forfeitures for the award are **1,800 units** (matches revised estimate). Remaining **4,200 units** vest.  
- **January 1, Year 4:** Meridian issues **4,200** common shares for the vested units.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **grant-date total fair value** before forfeitures and **expected compensation cost** using the Year 1 forfeiture estimate. State whether a journal entry is required on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** compensation expense entry.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** entry reflecting the **change in forfeiture estimate** (cumulative catch-up). Show the target cumulative Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock after two years.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** entry to complete expense recognition under the revised estimate. Prepare a **three-year expense schedule**.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4 settlement (issuance) journal entry** for the vested shares.  
f. Classify: Is Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock an asset, liability, or equity account during the vesting period?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Measurement at grant; no grant-date JE for RSUs**  
Grant-date FV of all units = 6,000 × $20 = **$120,000**.  
Expected to vest = 6,000 − 1,200 = **4,800 units**.  
Expected compensation = 4,800 × $20 = **$96,000** (equivalently $120,000 × 80%).  

**No journal entry** on January 1, Year 1 for RSUs (rights only; stock not yet issued).

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE**  
Annual expense (original estimate) = $96,000 ÷ 3 = **$32,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 32,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | | 32,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **32,000**. Balanced.

**c. December 31, Year 2 — Change in estimate (cumulative catch-up)**  
Revised expected forfeitures = 1,800 units → expected to vest = 6,000 − 1,800 = **4,200 units**.  
Revised total compensation = 4,200 × $20 = **$84,000** (or $120,000 × 70%).  

After **2 of 3** years, cumulative PIC—Restricted Stock **should be**  
$84,000 × (2/3) = **$56,000**.  

Already recognized Year 1: **$32,000**.  
Year 2 expense (catch-up) = $56,000 − $32,000 = **$24,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 24,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | | 24,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **24,000**. Balanced.  
Cumulative PIC—RS after Year 2 = 32,000 + 24,000 = **$56,000**.

**d. December 31, Year 3 — Complete recognition**  
Target cumulative after full service = full revised total = **$84,000**.  
Already recognized = $56,000.  
Year 3 expense = $84,000 − $56,000 = **$28,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 28,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | | 28,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **28,000**. Balanced.

*Three-year expense schedule (estimated-forfeiture policy with Year 2 revision):*

| Year | Computation | Compensation expense | Cumulative PIC—RS |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $96,000 × 1/3 | $32,000 | $32,000 |
| Year 2 | Target $84,000 × 2/3 − $32,000 | 24,000 | 56,000 |
| Year 3 | $84,000 − $56,000 | 28,000 | 84,000 |
| **Total** | | **$84,000** | |

Check: 32,000 + 24,000 + 28,000 = **84,000**.

**e. January 1, Year 4 — Settlement / issuance of vested shares**  
Issue 4,200 shares at $0.50 par; reclassify entire PIC—Restricted Stock balance of $84,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | 84,000 | |
| Common Stock (4,200 × $0.50) | | 2,100 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock ($84,000 − $2,100) | | 81,900 |
| *Issue common shares for vested RSUs* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **84,000**; Cr 2,100 + 81,900 = **84,000**. Balanced.

**f. Classification**  
Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock is an **equity** (stockholders’ equity / paid-in capital) account—not an asset and not a liability—during the vesting period for equity-classified RSUs.

**Key insight:** RSUs delay share issuance until vesting, so period-end entries credit **PIC—Restricted Stock**. Estimated forfeitures reduce the base; a later estimate change uses a **cumulative catch-up** so cumulative equity equals revised total compensation × portion of service completed. At settlement, PIC—RS is reclassified into legal capital accounts.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of grant-date accounts
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation difference between restricted stock awards and restricted stock units at the grant date  
**Question:**  
On the **grant date** of an equity-classified award, which statement is correct?

- A) Restricted stock **awards** debit Compensation Expense immediately for the full grant-date fair value; restricted stock **units** debit Unearned Compensation—Equity.  
- B) Restricted stock **awards** debit Unearned Compensation—Equity (contra-equity) and credit Common Stock (and PIC); restricted stock **units** generally require **no** grant-date journal entry (compensation cost is measured but expensed over the service period with credit to PIC—Restricted Stock).  
- C) Both awards and units credit a liability at grant equal to grant-date fair value.  
- D) Both awards and units remeasure total compensation each reporting date to current stock price.

**Answer:** **B.**  
RSA: shares issued at grant → Dr Unearned Compensation—Equity, Cr Common Stock / PIC.  
RSU: rights only at grant → no issuance entry; expense over service with Cr PIC—Restricted Stock.  
A reverses the accounts. C is wrong for equity-classified plans (credit is equity, not liability). D is wrong—grant-date FV is not remeasured for equity-classified awards for market price changes.

### Q5 — MC — Forfeiture policy timing
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Classification of forfeiture accounting methods (estimate vs as incurred) and effect on period-end expense  
**Question:**  
Under ASC 718, an entity elects a policy to **recognize forfeitures as incurred** (not estimate them). For a three-year cliff-vesting RSU award with no forfeitures in Year 1, Year 1 compensation expense equals:

- A) Zero, because expense begins only when vesting is certain.  
- B) Grant-date fair value of **all** units granted ÷ 3.  
- C) Grant-date fair value of units granted × 50%, because “as incurred” means half each of the first two years.  
- D) Only the cash dividends on the underlying shares.

**Answer:** **B.**  
As-incurred policy uses full grant-date fair value of awards outstanding (not reduced for estimated turnover) and amortizes straight-line over the requisite service period; actual forfeitures reverse previously recognized cost when they occur. Year 1 with no forfeitures = total FV ÷ 3. A is incorrect (expense accrues as service is rendered). C invents a 50% rule. D confuses dividends with share-based compensation cost.
