# Agent 342 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-4

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for changes in estimate as they relate to depreciation  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Revisions of **useful life** and/or **residual (salvage) value** of depreciable PPE are **changes in accounting estimates**
- **Prospective** treatment only: **no** restatement of prior periods; **no** prior-period adjustment to Retained Earnings
- At the date of change, compute **carrying amount** = cost − accumulated depreciation to date
- **Updated annual depreciation** = (carrying amount − **new** residual value) ÷ **remaining** useful life (in years or units)
- The period-end adjusting entry form does **not** change: Dr **Depreciation Expense**, Cr **Accumulated Depreciation** — only the **amount** changes prospectively
- **Subsequent measurement schedule** should show pre-change and post-change columns so the rollforward of AD and carrying amount is transparent
- A later **disposal** uses carrying amount after any current-period depreciation up to the disposal date (including partial-year if mid-year disposal)
- (Contrast only: a **change in depreciation method** is a “change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle” — also prospective — but is LO 12-5, not this pack’s primary focus)

### Q1 — CORE — Purchase, multi-year SL schedule, dual estimate revision, period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis), mid-year disposal

**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of equipment; original SL schedule; change in useful life **and** residual (prospective); **period-end adjusting JE** at revised amount (emphasis); subsequent measurement schedule; disposal JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Fabrication Co.** (calendar year) purchases specialized CNC equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$95,000 cash**. Management originally estimates:

| Item | Original estimate |
|---|---|
| Residual value | **$5,000** |
| Useful life | **10 years** |
| Depreciation method | **Straight-line** |

Depreciation is recorded only at each **December 31** year-end (period-end adjusting entry).  

At the **start of Year 4** (January 1, Year 4), after three full years of use, management revises its estimates based on experience with the machine:

| Item | Revised estimate |
|---|---|
| Total useful life from acquisition date | **8 years** (was 10) |
| Residual value | **$3,000** (was $5,000) |

On **July 1, Year 7**, Redwood sells the equipment for **$28,000 cash**. Depreciation is taken through the disposal date (half-year in Year 7 under the revised annual amount). No residual or life change occurs after January 1, Year 4.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1** initial recognition journal entry.  
(b) Compute original annual straight-line depreciation. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 (depreciation expense, accumulated depreciation, carrying amount). Record the **December 31, Year 3** period-end adjusting entry.  
(c) At **January 1, Year 4**, compute carrying amount and the **updated annual depreciation** after the dual change in estimates. Explain the accounting treatment (prospective vs. retrospective).  
(d) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 4–8 under the revised estimates (assuming the asset is held to the end of revised life). Record the **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting entry** (emphasis).  
(e) Record **July 1, Year 7** depreciation through disposal and the **disposal** journal entry. Compute gain or loss.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 95,000 | |
| Cash | | 95,000 |
| *Acquire CNC equipment for cash* | | |

**Check:** Dr 95,000 = Cr 95,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Original annual depreciation and Years 1–3 schedule**

\[
\text{Annual SL depreciation} = \frac{\$95{,}000 - \$5{,}000}{10} = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}
\]

| Year | Beginning carrying amount | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation (EOY) | Ending carrying amount |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 95,000 | 9,000 | 9,000 | 86,000 |
| 2 | 86,000 | 9,000 | 18,000 | 77,000 |
| 3 | 77,000 | 9,000 | 27,000 | 68,000 |

*December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting entry (last year under original estimates)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |
| *SL depreciation under original life/residual* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) January 1, Year 4 — change in estimates (prospective)**

Carrying amount at change date:  
\[
\$95{,}000 - \$27{,}000 = \mathbf{\$68{,}000}
\]

Remaining useful life = revised total life − years already used = \(8 - 3 = \mathbf{5}\) years.

\[
\text{Updated annual depreciation} = \frac{\$68{,}000 - \$3{,}000}{5} = \mathbf{\$13{,}000}
\]

**Treatment:** Revisions of useful life and residual value are **changes in accounting estimates**. Apply **prospectively** to Year 4 and remaining years. **Do not** restate Years 1–3 depreciation, accumulated depreciation, or beginning retained earnings. No cumulative catch-up entry is recorded at January 1, Year 4; the only entry is the normal period-end depreciation at the new amount.

**(d) Subsequent measurement schedule under revised estimates (held to end of life)**

| Year | Beginning carrying amount | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation (EOY) | Ending carrying amount |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 68,000 | 13,000 | 40,000 | 55,000 |
| 5 | 55,000 | 13,000 | 53,000 | 42,000 |
| 6 | 42,000 | 13,000 | 66,000 | 29,000 |
| 7 | 29,000 | 13,000 | 79,000 | 16,000 |
| 8 | 16,000 | 13,000 | 92,000 | 3,000 (= residual) |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Depreciable base after change: \(68{,}000 - 3{,}000 = 65{,}000\); \(65{,}000 / 5 = 13{,}000\).  
- Total AD at end of Year 8: \(27{,}000 + 5 \times 13{,}000 = 27{,}000 + 65{,}000 = 92{,}000\).  
- Ending CV = residual **$3,000**.

*December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry (emphasis; first year under new estimates)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 13,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 13,000 |
| *Prospective SL depreciation after life/residual revision* | | |

**Check:** Dr 13,000 = Cr 13,000. **Balanced.**

Same form and amount (**$13,000**) at December 31, Year 5 and Year 6 if the asset is held those full years.

**(e) July 1, Year 7 — partial-year depreciation and disposal**

Year 7 depreciation through July 1 (½ year):  
\[
\$13{,}000 \times \frac{6}{12} = \mathbf{\$6{,}500}
\]

Accumulated depreciation at disposal:  
\[
\$27{,}000 + (3 \times \$13{,}000) + \$6{,}500 = \$27{,}000 + \$39{,}000 + \$6{,}500 = \mathbf{\$72{,}500}
\]  
(Years 4–6 full years under revised amount, plus half of Year 7.)

Carrying amount at disposal:  
\[
\$95{,}000 - \$72{,}500 = \mathbf{\$22{,}500}
\]

Gain on disposal:  
\[
\$28{,}000 - \$22{,}500 = \mathbf{\$5{,}500}
\]

*July 1, Year 7 — depreciation through disposal date*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 6,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 6,500 |
| *Half-year depreciation under revised annual amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,500 = Cr 6,500. **Balanced.**

*July 1, Year 7 — dispose of equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 72,500 | |
| Equipment | | 95,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 5,500 |
| *Remove cost and AD; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(28{,}000 + 72{,}500 = 100{,}500\); Cr \(95{,}000 + 5{,}500 = 100{,}500\). **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** A change in estimated life or residual is **prospective**. Recompute from **current carrying amount** using the **new residual** and **remaining life**; the period-end JE still debits Depreciation Expense and credits Accumulated Depreciation — only the **dollar amount** changes. Prior years are left untouched.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Purchase, dual estimate revision, period-end JEs, year-end disposal

**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; original SL schedule; change in total useful life and residual (prospective); **period-end adjusting JEs**; subsequent measurement schedule; disposal at year-end  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Tooling LLC** (calendar year) purchases production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** on account for **$128,000**. Original estimates:

| Item | Original estimate |
|---|---|
| Residual value | **$8,000** |
| Useful life | **12 years** |
| Depreciation method | **Straight-line** |

Depreciation is recorded only at each **December 31**.

At the **start of Year 5** (January 1, Year 5), after four full years, management revises:

| Item | Revised estimate |
|---|---|
| Total useful life from acquisition | **10 years** (was 12) |
| Residual value | **$4,000** (was $8,000) |

On **December 31, Year 7**, after recording Year 7 depreciation, Harborline sells the equipment for **$52,000 cash**. No further estimate changes after January 1, Year 5.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1** purchase on account.  
(b) Compute original annual depreciation; prepare the Years 1–4 measurement schedule; record **December 31, Year 4** period-end depreciation.  
(c) Compute carrying amount and **updated annual depreciation** at January 1, Year 5. State the accounting treatment.  
(d) Prepare the post-change measurement schedule for Years 5–10 (if held). Record **December 31, Year 5** and **December 31, Year 6** period-end adjusting entries (emphasis).  
(e) Record **December 31, Year 7** depreciation and the **disposal** entry. Compute gain or loss.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 128,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 128,000 |
| *Acquire production equipment on account* | | |

**Check:** Dr 128,000 = Cr 128,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Original depreciation and Years 1–4 schedule**

\[
\text{Annual SL depreciation} = \frac{\$128{,}000 - \$8{,}000}{12} = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}
\]

| Year | Beginning CV | Depreciation | AD (EOY) | Ending CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 128,000 | 10,000 | 10,000 | 118,000 |
| 2 | 118,000 | 10,000 | 20,000 | 108,000 |
| 3 | 108,000 | 10,000 | 30,000 | 98,000 |
| 4 | 98,000 | 10,000 | 40,000 | 88,000 |

*December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 10,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 = Cr 10,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) January 1, Year 5 — change in estimates**

Carrying amount: \(128{,}000 - 40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$88{,}000}\).  
Remaining life: \(10 - 4 = \mathbf{6}\) years.

\[
\text{Updated annual depreciation} = \frac{\$88{,}000 - \$4{,}000}{6} = \mathbf{\$14{,}000}
\]

**Treatment:** Prospective change in accounting estimate. No restatement of Years 1–4; no entry at January 1, Year 5 other than normal subsequent period-end depreciation at **$14,000**.

**(d) Post-change schedule (held to revised end of life) and period-end JEs**

| Year | Beginning CV | Depreciation | AD (EOY) | Ending CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | 88,000 | 14,000 | 54,000 | 74,000 |
| 6 | 74,000 | 14,000 | 68,000 | 60,000 |
| 7 | 60,000 | 14,000 | 82,000 | 46,000 |
| 8 | 46,000 | 14,000 | 96,000 | 32,000 |
| 9 | 32,000 | 14,000 | 110,000 | 18,000 |
| 10 | 18,000 | 14,000 | 124,000 | 4,000 (= residual) |

**Checks:** Depreciable base \(88{,}000 - 4{,}000 = 84{,}000\); \(84{,}000 / 6 = 14{,}000\). End-of-life AD \(40{,}000 + 84{,}000 = 124{,}000\).

*December 31, Year 5 — period-end adjusting entry (emphasis)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 14,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 14,000 |
| *First full year under revised life and residual* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,000 = Cr 14,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 6 — period-end adjusting entry (emphasis)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 14,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 14,000 |

**Check:** Dr 14,000 = Cr 14,000. **Balanced.**

**(e) December 31, Year 7 — depreciation and disposal**

*December 31, Year 7 — period-end depreciation*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 14,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 14,000 |

**Check:** Dr 14,000 = Cr 14,000. **Balanced.**

AD at disposal: \(40{,}000 + 3 \times 14{,}000 = \mathbf{\$82{,}000}\).  
Carrying amount: \(128{,}000 - 82{,}000 = \mathbf{\$46{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |

**Key insight:** Same prospective mechanics as Q1 with all numbers changed. Shortening life and reducing residual both **increase** future annual depreciation relative to the original $10,000 path; the period-end JE amount jumps to $14,000 with **no** catch-up to prior AD.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end adjusting JEs for residual-only vs. life-only revisions (same asset, separate cases)

**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Isolate residual-value change vs. useful-life change; compute updated depreciation from carrying amount; record **period-end adjusting JEs** only (emphasis); no prior-period restatement  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Conveyor Systems Inc.** acquired equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$80,000 cash**. Original estimates: residual **$8,000**, useful life **8 years**, **straight-line**. Depreciation is recorded only at year-end.

Through **December 31, Year 2**, Summit correctly recorded two years of original depreciation. On **January 1, Year 3**, management is considering three **independent** estimate revisions (analyze each case separately from the **same** January 1, Year 3 carrying amount):

| Case | What changes on Jan 1, Year 3 |
|---|---|
| **A — Residual only** | Residual revised to **$2,000**; total useful life remains **8 years** |
| **B — Life only** | Total useful life revised to **12 years**; residual remains **$8,000** |
| **C — Both** | Residual revised to **$5,000** and total useful life revised to **10 years** |

On **December 31, Year 3**, Summit records only the normal period-end depreciation entry under whichever case applies. The company does **not** dispose of the asset in Year 3.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1** purchase. Compute original annual depreciation and carrying amount at **January 1, Year 3**. Record the **December 31, Year 2** period-end entry (last year under original estimates in all cases).  
(b) For **Case A**, compute Year 3 depreciation and record the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting entry**.  
(c) For **Case B**, compute Year 3 depreciation and record the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting entry**.  
(d) For **Case C**, compute Year 3 depreciation and record the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting entry**. Prepare a brief subsequent measurement schedule for Years 3–10 under Case C (end of revised life).  
(e) For all cases, state what entry (if any) is made on **January 1, Year 3** and whether Years 1–2 financials are restated.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Purchase, original depreciation, pre-change carrying amount**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr 80,000 = Cr 80,000. **Balanced.**

\[
\text{Original annual depreciation} = \frac{\$80{,}000 - \$8{,}000}{8} = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}
\]

After 2 years: AD = \(2 \times 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$18{,}000}\); carrying amount = \(80{,}000 - 18{,}000 = \mathbf{\$62{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Case A — residual only (decrease residual → higher future depreciation)**

Remaining life = \(8 - 2 = \mathbf{6}\) years.  
\[
\text{Year 3 depreciation} = \frac{\$62{,}000 - \$2{,}000}{6} = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}
\]

*December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting entry (Case A)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 10,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 10,000 |
| *Prospective effect of lower residual only* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 = Cr 10,000. **Balanced.**  
(Original residual $8,000 → new $2,000 increases annual expense from $9,000 to $10,000.)

**(c) Case B — life only (extend life → lower future depreciation)**

Remaining life = \(12 - 2 = \mathbf{10}\) years.  
\[
\text{Year 3 depreciation} = \frac{\$62{,}000 - \$8{,}000}{10} = \mathbf{\$5{,}400}
\]

*December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting entry (Case B)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,400 |
| *Prospective effect of longer useful life only* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,400 = Cr 5,400. **Balanced.**

**(d) Case C — both residual and life change**

Remaining life = \(10 - 2 = \mathbf{8}\) years.  
\[
\text{Year 3 depreciation} = \frac{\$62{,}000 - \$5{,}000}{8} = \mathbf{\$7{,}125}
\]

*December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting entry (Case C)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 7,125 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 7,125 |
| *Prospective dual change in residual and total life* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,125 = Cr 7,125. **Balanced.**

**Case C subsequent measurement schedule (Years 3–10)**

Depreciable base after change: \(62{,}000 - 5{,}000 = 57{,}000\); \(57{,}000 / 8 = 7{,}125\).

| Year | Beginning CV | Depreciation | AD (EOY) | Ending CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 62,000 | 7,125 | 25,125 | 54,875 |
| 4 | 54,875 | 7,125 | 32,250 | 47,750 |
| 5 | 47,750 | 7,125 | 39,375 | 40,625 |
| 6 | 40,625 | 7,125 | 46,500 | 33,500 |
| 7 | 33,500 | 7,125 | 53,625 | 26,375 |
| 8 | 26,375 | 7,125 | 60,750 | 19,250 |
| 9 | 19,250 | 7,125 | 67,875 | 12,125 |
| 10 | 12,125 | 7,125 | 75,000 | 5,000 (= residual) |

**Checks:** End AD \(18{,}000 + 8 \times 7{,}125 = 18{,}000 + 57{,}000 = 75{,}000\); ending CV = residual **$5,000**.

**(e) January 1, Year 3 and restatement**

- **January 1, Year 3 entry:** **None** for the estimate change itself (no catch-up to AD or Retained Earnings).  
- **Restatement of Years 1–2:** **No.** Prospective application only.  
- The estimate change is first reflected in the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** at the new annual amount.

**Key insight (emphasis):** Whether residual alone, life alone, or both change, the **mechanics are identical**: start from **current carrying amount**, subtract the **current residual estimate**, divide by **remaining life**, and record the result only in the **period-end adjusting entry** going forward. Lower residual raises expense; longer life lowers expense; combined effects net in the updated fraction.

### Q4 — MC (classification / computation of estimate change)

**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Identify prospective change-in-estimate treatment; compute period depreciation after dual revision  

**Question 1:**  
On January 1, Year 5, after several years of straight-line depreciation, **Northridge Plastics Inc.** revises the **estimated residual value** and **remaining useful life** of a machine still in use. Which statement is correct under U.S. GAAP for this LO 12-4 situation?

- A) Restate prior-year depreciation and adjust beginning Retained Earnings for the cumulative effect.  
- B) Treat the revision prospectively: recompute depreciation from current carrying amount using the new residual and remaining life; do not restate prior periods.  
- C) Treat the revision as an error correction (prior-period adjustment) because original estimates proved incorrect.  
- D) Capitalize a cumulative catch-up amount to the Equipment account on the date of change.

**Answer:** **B.** Revisions of residual value and useful life are **changes in accounting estimates**, applied **prospectively**. Prior periods are not restated; there is no prior-period adjustment and no catch-up capitalization to the asset account. (A describes retrospective application / cumulative effect inappropriate here; C confuses estimate revision with error; D invents an invalid debit to Equipment.)

---

**Question 2:**  
**Pinnacle Die Cast Co.** purchased equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$200,000**. Original estimates: residual **$20,000**, useful life **10 years**, straight-line. On **January 1, Year 5**, after four full years of depreciation, Pinnacle revises residual value to **$8,000** and total useful life from acquisition to **12 years**. What amount of **depreciation expense** should Pinnacle recognize for the year ended **December 31, Year 5**?

- A) $15,000  
- B) $16,000  
- C) $18,000  
- D) $20,000  

**Answer:** **A. $15,000.**  

Original annual depreciation: \((200{,}000 - 20{,}000)/10 = 18{,}000\).  
AD after 4 years: \(4 \times 18{,}000 = 72{,}000\).  
Carrying amount Jan 1, Year 5: \(200{,}000 - 72{,}000 = 128{,}000\).  
Remaining life: \(12 - 4 = 8\) years.  
Year 5 depreciation: \((128{,}000 - 8{,}000)/8 = \mathbf{15{,}000}\).  

(B is \((128{,}000 - 0)/8\); C is the **original** annual amount — wrong after the change; D has no support.)
