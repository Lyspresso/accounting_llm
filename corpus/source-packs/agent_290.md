# Agent 290 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-3

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Recognize deferred tax assets attributable to deductible temporary differences  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Deductible temporary difference:** future deductible amounts when (1) GAAP asset basis < tax asset basis, or (2) GAAP liability basis > tax liability basis
- **Deferred tax asset (DTA):** cumulative deductible temporary difference × enacted tax rate expected to apply when the difference reverses
- **Initial recognition JE:** at period-end, establish DTA (Dr Deferred Tax Asset) with Income Tax Payable (current tax on the return) and net Income Tax Expense
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** roll forward ending cumulative deductible TD and required DTA each year-end
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** income-tax entry is made **only at year-end** to true up DTA to (ending cumulative deductible TD × rate); mid-year operations do not adjust DTA daily
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement of TD:** when the temporary difference fully reverses (warranty paid, performance satisfied, write-off complete, inventory sold), required DTA = $0 and the period-end JE eliminates remaining DTA
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** DTAs classified **noncurrent** under current ASC 740; notes disclose current vs deferred components of income tax expense
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO 18-3 path with all different amounts and rate

### Q1 — CORE — Warranty DTA: initial recognition, multi-year schedule, period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis), full reverse, presentation

**LO:** LO 18-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of DTA from warranty deductible temporary difference; subsequent DTA schedule; **period-end adjusting tax JEs** (emphasis); settlement when liability is fully paid; BS/note presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironbark Power Tools Inc.** (calendar year) sells equipment with multi-year warranties. Warranty costs are accrued for **GAAP when sales occur** but are **deductible for tax only when paid**. Enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. Deferred tax account balances are **zero** on January 1 of Year 1. Accounting and tax years both end December 31. No permanent differences and no other temporary differences. Ironbark records **income tax adjusting entries only at each December 31** (period-end).

**Facts:**  
In **Year 1**, Ironbark accrues warranty expense of **$48,000** (ending GAAP warranty liability **$48,000**; tax basis of the liability **$0**). Cash settlements of the warranty liability occur as follows (no new warranty accruals after Year 1):

| Year paid | Amount settled |
|---|---:|
| Year 2 | $16,000 |
| Year 3 | $20,000 |
| Year 4 | $12,000 |

Taxable income from the tax return:

| Year | Taxable income |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | $280,000 |
| Year 2 | $250,000 |
| Year 3 | $265,000 |
| Year 4 | $290,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Explain why the warranty creates a **deductible** temporary difference (basis comparison).  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of ending cumulative deductible temporary difference, required DTA, and change in DTA for Dec 31 of Years 1–4.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** income tax **period-end adjusting JE** (initial recognition of the DTA).  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** and **December 31, Year 3** period-end adjusting JEs.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 4** period-end adjusting JE that **settles** (fully eliminates) the DTA.  
f. Show Year 1 **balance sheet** classification of the DTA and Income Tax Payable, and the Year 1 **note disclosure** of current vs deferred tax expense.  
g. Reconcile pretax GAAP income to total income tax expense for Year 1 (show total tax expense = pretax GAAP × 25%).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Why deductible (basis analysis)**  
Warranty expense is recognized for **GAAP when sales occur**, but is deductible for **tax only when paid**. At year-end, **GAAP liability basis ($48,000 initially) > tax liability basis ($0)** → future deductible amounts when the liability is settled → **deductible temporary difference** → **deferred tax asset**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule**  
Cumulative deductible temporary difference = ending GAAP warranty liability (tax basis remains $0 until paid).  
\(\text{Required DTA} = \text{Cumulative deductible TD} \times 25\%\).

| Dec. 31 | Ending warranty liability (cum. deductible TD) | Tax rate | Required DTA | Change in DTA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $48,000 | 25% | **$12,000** | +$12,000 (originate) |
| Year 2 | $32,000 | 25% | **$8,000** | −$4,000 |
| Year 3 | $12,000 | 25% | **$3,000** | −$5,000 |
| Year 4 | $0 | 25% | **$0** | −$3,000 (full reverse) |

**Roll-forward check:** \(12{,}000 - 4{,}000 - 5{,}000 - 3{,}000 = 0\). Settlements \(16{,}000 + 20{,}000 + 12{,}000 = 48{,}000\).

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (initial recognition; emphasis)**  
Current tax on tax return: \(280{,}000 \times 0.25 = \mathbf{\$70{,}000}\).  
Increase DTA from $0 to required $12,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 58,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 12,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 70,000 |
| *Period-end only: current tax on TI + establish DTA for warranty deductible TD* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(58{,}000 + 12{,}000 = 70{,}000\) = Cr 70,000. **Balanced.**  
Total income tax expense \(= \$70{,}000\) current \(-\) \(\$12{,}000\) deferred benefit \(= \$58{,}000\).

**d. Period-end adjusting JEs — Years 2 and 3 (emphasis)**  

**December 31, Year 2**  
Income Tax Payable: \(250{,}000 \times 0.25 = \$62{,}500\).  
DTA required $8,000 vs beginning $12,000 → decrease DTA **$4,000** (increases deferred tax expense).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 66,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 4,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 62,500 |
| *Period-end only: current tax + reverse portion of DTA as warranty settles* | | |

**Check:** Dr 66,500 = Cr \(4{,}000 + 62{,}500\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3**  
Income Tax Payable: \(265{,}000 \times 0.25 = \$66{,}250\).  
DTA required $3,000 vs beginning $8,000 → decrease DTA **$5,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 71,250 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 66,250 |
| *Period-end only: true up DTA to ending cumulative deductible TD × rate* | | |

**Check:** Dr 71,250 = Cr \(5{,}000 + 66{,}250\). **Balanced.**

**e. December 31, Year 4 — settlement / full reverse of temporary difference**  
Warranty liability and cumulative deductible TD = $0 → required DTA = $0.  
Income Tax Payable: \(290{,}000 \times 0.25 = \$72{,}500\).  
Clear remaining DTA of **$3,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 75,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 72,500 |
| *Period-end only: eliminate DTA when deductible TD fully reverses* | | |

**Check:** Dr 75,500 = Cr \(3{,}000 + 72{,}500\). **Balanced.**

**f. Year 1 financial statement presentation**  

**Balance sheet (Dec. 31, Year 1 excerpt)**  
- **Noncurrent assets** — Deferred tax asset: **$12,000** (DTAs classified noncurrent under current ASC 740)  
- **Current liabilities** — Income tax payable: **$70,000**

**Income statement (Year 1):** Income tax expense **$58,000**

**Notes — components of income tax expense (Year 1)**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Current | $70,000 |
| Deferred | (12,000) |
| **Total income tax expense** | **$58,000** |

**g. Pretax GAAP ↔ tax expense check (Year 1)**  
Only temporary difference: warranty accrual $48,000 (GAAP expense not yet tax-deductible) →  
Taxable income \(280{,}000 =\) pretax GAAP \(+ 48{,}000\) → pretax GAAP income \(= \mathbf{\$232{,}000}\).  
\(232{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$58{,}000}\) = total income tax expense. ✓

**Key insight:** A deductible temporary difference creates a **future tax benefit** measured as cumulative deductible TD × enacted rate. The **period-end adjusting JE** is the only tax entry that establishes or trues up the DTA: an **increase** in DTA reduces total income tax expense; as the difference **reverses** (warranty paid), the DTA is reduced and deferred tax expense rises. When the cumulative difference reaches zero, the period-end JE **settles** the DTA fully.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Warranty DTA multi-year path (all numbers changed)

**LO:** LO 18-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin—initial DTA from warranty; subsequent schedule; period-end adjusting JEs; full reverse at settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Quarry Peak Fabrication LLC** (calendar year) accrues warranties for GAAP and deducts warranty costs for tax only when paid. Enacted tax rate is **30%** for all years. Deferred tax balances are zero on January 1 of Year 1. Income tax entries are recorded **only at each December 31**. No other temporary or permanent differences.

**Facts:**  
Year 1 warranty accrual (ending GAAP liability): **$80,000** (tax basis $0). Settlements (no further accruals):

| Year paid | Amount settled |
|---|---:|
| Year 2 | $30,000 |
| Year 3 | $30,000 |
| Year 4 | $20,000 |

Taxable income:

| Year | Taxable income |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | $500,000 |
| Year 2 | $420,000 |
| Year 3 | $440,000 |
| Year 4 | $460,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the DTA **schedule** (ending cumulative deductible TD, required DTA, Δ DTA) for Years 1–4.  
b. Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end tax JE (initial recognition).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 2** period-end tax JE.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 4** period-end tax JE that eliminates the DTA.  
e. State Year 1 pretax GAAP income and prove total tax expense = pretax GAAP × 30%.  
f. Classify the DTA on the Dec. 31, Year 1 balance sheet.

**Answer key:**  

**a. DTA schedule (30% rate)**

| Dec. 31 | Ending warranty liability (cum. deductible TD) | Required DTA (×30%) | Change in DTA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $80,000 | **$24,000** | +$24,000 |
| Year 2 | $50,000 | **$15,000** | −$9,000 |
| Year 3 | $20,000 | **$6,000** | −$9,000 |
| Year 4 | $0 | **$0** | −$6,000 |

**Check:** \(24{,}000 - 9{,}000 - 9{,}000 - 6{,}000 = 0\); settlements \(30{,}000 + 30{,}000 + 20{,}000 = 80{,}000\).

**b. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**  
ITP: \(500{,}000 \times 0.30 = \$150{,}000\). DTA increase $24,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 126,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 24,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 150,000 |
| *Period-end: current tax + originate DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(126{,}000 + 24{,}000 = 150{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE**  
ITP: \(420{,}000 \times 0.30 = \$126{,}000\). Decrease DTA $9,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 135,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 9,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 126,000 |
| *Period-end: current tax + reverse DTA for settlements* | | |

**Check:** Dr 135,000 = Cr \(9{,}000 + 126{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**d. December 31, Year 4 — full settlement of DTA**  
ITP: \(460{,}000 \times 0.30 = \$138{,}000\). Clear remaining DTA $6,000 (after Y3 ending DTA $6,000; if preparing only Y4, beginning Y4 DTA = $6,000).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 144,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 138,000 |
| *Period-end: eliminate DTA when TD fully reverses* | | |

**Check:** Dr 144,000 = Cr \(6{,}000 + 138{,}000\). **Balanced.**

*(For completeness — Year 3 period-end, not required but roll-forward: ITP \(440{,}000 \times 0.30 = \$132{,}000\); Δ DTA −$9,000; ITE $141,000.)*

**e. Year 1 pretax GAAP and tax expense proof**  
TI \(500{,}000 =\) pretax GAAP \(+ 80{,}000\) warranty → pretax GAAP \(= \mathbf{\$420{,}000}\).  
\(420{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$126{,}000}\) = total income tax expense. ✓

**f. Classification**  
Deferred tax asset **$24,000** reported as a **noncurrent** asset at Dec. 31, Year 1.

**Key insight:** Same LO 18-3 mechanics as Q1 with a different rate and amounts: **period-end** always sets DTA = ending cumulative deductible TD × enacted rate; full settlement zeros the DTA when the warranty liability is gone.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Deferred subscription revenue: multi-year DTA, period-end true-ups, presentation

**LO:** LO 18-3  
**Concept:** Deductible temporary difference from revenue taxed before GAAP recognition (customer advances); multi-year DTA schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs**; settlement as performance obligations are satisfied; classification/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Harborline Learning Systems Inc.** collects **$160,000** cash from customers for multi-year online subscriptions and records **deferred revenue** for GAAP. For **tax** purposes, the entire **$160,000** is included in taxable income in Year 1 (taxed on cash receipt). Harborline recognizes the $160,000 as **GAAP revenue evenly** over Year 1 through Year 4 (**$40,000** per year). Enacted tax rate is **25%** each year. Deferred tax account balances are zero at the beginning of Year 1. No other temporary or permanent differences. Harborline records the income tax entry **only at December 31** each year.

Pretax **GAAP** income (already includes the $40,000 subscription revenue recognized each year):

| Year | Pretax GAAP income |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | $220,000 |
| Year 2 | $235,000 |
| Year 3 | $240,000 |
| Year 4 | $228,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Explain why remaining deferred revenue is a **deductible** temporary difference.  
b. For each year-end Years 1–4, compute: ending deferred revenue (cumulative deductible TD), required DTA, taxable income, income tax payable, change in DTA, and total income tax expense. Present as a **schedule**.  
c. Record the **period-end** income tax JEs for **Year 1** (originate DTA), **Year 2** (partial reverse), and **Year 4** (final settlement to zero).  
d. Present the Dec. 31, Year 1 balance sheet classification of the DTA and the Year 1 note disclosure of current vs deferred tax expense.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Why deductible**  
Cash advances are **taxable when received** but **deferred for GAAP** until performance. Ending deferred revenue → **GAAP liability basis > tax liability basis ($0)** → future periods will recognize GAAP revenue without taxable income (all else constant) → **future deductible amounts** relative to pretax GAAP → **deductible temporary difference** → **DTA**.

**b. Multi-year schedule**  
Ending deferred revenue = cumulative deductible temporary difference.  
Taxable income:  
- Y1: pretax GAAP \(+ \) unearned portion taxed up front \(= 220{,}000 + 120{,}000 = 340{,}000\)  
  *(Cash \(160{,}000\) taxed − GAAP revenue recognized \(40{,}000\) = net \(+120{,}000\) addition to TI vs pretax)*  
- Y2–Y4: pretax GAAP \(-\) \(40{,}000\) (GAAP revenue already taxed in Year 1)

| Year | Ending deferred rev. (cum. deductible TD) | Required DTA (×25%) | Pretax GAAP | Taxable income | ITP (×25%) | Δ DTA | Total tax expense |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $120,000 | **$30,000** | $220,000 | $340,000 | $85,000 | +$30,000 | **$55,000** |
| 2 | $80,000 | **$20,000** | $235,000 | $195,000 | $48,750 | −$10,000 | **$58,750** |
| 3 | $40,000 | **$10,000** | $240,000 | $200,000 | $50,000 | −$10,000 | **$60,000** |
| 4 | $0 | **$0** | $228,000 | $188,000 | $47,000 | −$10,000 | **$57,000** |

**Proofs:**  
- Total tax expense each year = pretax GAAP × 25% (\(220{,}000 \times 0.25 = 55{,}000\); \(235{,}000 \times 0.25 = 58{,}750\); \(240{,}000 \times 0.25 = 60{,}000\); \(228{,}000 \times 0.25 = 57{,}000\)).  
- Y2 TI: \(235{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 195{,}000\); Y3: \(240{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 200{,}000\); Y4: \(228{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 188{,}000\).  
- DTA roll-forward: \(30{,}000 - 10{,}000 - 10{,}000 - 10{,}000 = 0\).

**c. Period-end adjusting journal entries**  

**December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 55,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 30,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 85,000 |
| *Period-end: current tax on cash-basis taxable advances + originate DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(55{,}000 + 30{,}000 = 85{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 2 — subsequent measurement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 58,750 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 10,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 48,750 |
| *Period-end: reverse DTA as $40,000 of deferred revenue is earned for GAAP* | | |

**Check:** Dr 58,750 = Cr \(10{,}000 + 48{,}750\). **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 4 — settlement (DTA to zero)**  
Beginning Y4 DTA = $10,000; required ending = $0.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 57,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 10,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 47,000 |
| *Period-end: eliminate remaining DTA when deferred revenue fully earned* | | |

**Check:** Dr 57,000 = Cr \(10{,}000 + 47{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**d. Classification and disclosure (Year 1)**  

**Balance sheet (Dec. 31, Year 1):** Deferred tax asset **$30,000** — **noncurrent** asset.  
Income tax payable **$85,000** — current liability.

**Notes — income tax expense components (Year 1)**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Current | $85,000 |
| Deferred | (30,000) |
| **Total income tax expense** | **$55,000** |

**Key insight:** When cash advances are **taxable before** GAAP revenue recognition, remaining deferred revenue is a **deductible temporary difference**. The **period-end adjusting JE** always sets DTA to ending deferred revenue × enacted rate; the DTA declines as performance obligations are satisfied and is **settled to zero** when deferred revenue is fully earned.

### Q4 — MC — Balance-sheet classification of deferred tax assets

**LO:** LO 18-3  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation of deferred tax assets under ASC 740  
**Question:** Under current U.S. GAAP (ASC 740), how should a deferred tax asset arising solely from a deductible temporary difference (e.g., warranty liability or deferred revenue taxed up front) be classified on a classified balance sheet?

- A) Always current, because income taxes are settled annually  
- B) Always noncurrent  
- C) Split current/noncurrent based on when the temporary difference is expected to reverse  
- D) Net only against income tax payable; never shown as an asset  

**Answer:** **B.** Current GAAP requires deferred tax assets and deferred tax liabilities to be classified as **noncurrent**. (Older rules that split based on reversal timing no longer apply.)

### Q5 — MC — Identifying which item creates a deferred tax asset

**LO:** LO 18-3  
**Concept:** Identifying deductible temporary differences that create DTAs (vs taxable temporary or permanent)  
**Question:** Which of the following items, existing at year-end, creates a **deferred tax asset** (positive enacted tax rate; ignore valuation allowance)?

- A) Tax depreciation to date exceeds GAAP depreciation to date (tax basis of PPE lower than GAAP basis)  
- B) Prepaid insurance deducted in full for tax when paid; still an asset for GAAP  
- C) Product warranty liability recognized for GAAP; costs not yet deductible for tax  
- D) Municipal bond interest recognized in pretax GAAP income  

**Answer:** **C.** A warranty liability has GAAP basis > tax basis (typically $0 until paid) → future deductible amounts → **DTA**.  
A creates a **taxable** temporary difference (**DTL**). B tends to create a **taxable** temporary difference (GAAP asset basis > tax basis). D is a **permanent** difference (never creates DTA/DTL).
