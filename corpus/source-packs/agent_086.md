# Agent 86 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-1

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Identify and classify intangible items  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Classification:** finite-life intangible asset vs indefinite-life intangible asset (other than goodwill) vs goodwill vs expense
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize acquired intangibles and external registration/defense costs; expense internally generated development and R&D
- **Useful life:** finite (amortize) vs indefinite (do not amortize); legal life may exceed useful life
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** straight-line amortization of finite-life intangibles over useful life (≤ legal life)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** amortization expense (debit) with credit to asset (or Accumulated Amortization)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** update amortization to disposal date; remove carrying amount; gain or loss vs proceeds
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** intangibles vs goodwill on the balance sheet; finite (amortizing) vs non-amortizing; crypto assets (when criteria met) reported separately from other intangibles
- **Number-variant twin:** same structure, all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Classify and initially recognize a portfolio of intangible items
**LO:** LO 13-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE after classification (finite IA / indefinite IA / goodwill / expense); Year-1 amortization schedule; period-end adjusting JE; BS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northbridge Precision Labs** (calendar year) enters the following transactions in **Year 1**. All purchases of identifiable assets occur on **January 1** unless noted. Management expects residual values of zero for all finite-life intangibles.

| # | Intangible item / transaction | Amount / terms |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cash purchase of a **patent** from a third party; remaining legal life 16 years; **estimated useful life 8 years** | **$48,000** |
| 2 | Cash purchase of a **franchise** with a non-renewable **10-year** contract term | **$90,000** |
| 3 | Cash purchase of a **customer list**; benefit period estimated at **6 years** | **$36,000** |
| 4 | Cash payment of **external legal/filing fees** to register a **trademark** that management expects to renew indefinitely | **$6,200** (March 15) |
| 5 | **Internal R&D salaries** of staff engineers developing a new process expected to yield a patent | **$92,000** (incurred evenly during Year 1) |
| 6 | **Business combination:** cash paid for 100% of a target; fair value of **identifiable net assets** acquired = **$625,000** | Cash paid **$800,000** |
| 7 | **In-house marketing salaries** spent maintaining brand recognition (internal “goodwill”) | **$28,000** |
| 8 | **State operating license** cash fee covering **3 years** of operating rights | **$18,000** (January 1) |

**Required:**  
a. For each item (1–8), classify the **initial recording** as **(i)** finite-life intangible asset, **(ii)** indefinite-life intangible asset (other than goodwill), **(iii)** goodwill, or **(iv)** expense.  
b. Prepare **initial recognition** journal entries for items 1–8 (combine cash outflows only where shown as a single transaction; use separate entries per item).  
c. Prepare a **Year-1 amortization schedule** for all finite-life intangibles (asset, cost, useful life, annual amortization). State which assets are **not** amortized and why.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** adjusting entry for amortization.  
e. Briefly describe **balance-sheet presentation** of the intangible-related balances immediately after the Year-1 adjusting entry (ignore taxes).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**

| # | Item | Initial recording | Life (if asset) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purchased patent | **Finite-life intangible asset** | Finite (useful life 8 yrs < legal 16) |
| 2 | Franchise (10-year term) | **Finite-life intangible asset** | Finite (contractual 10 yrs) |
| 3 | Purchased customer list | **Finite-life intangible asset** | Finite (6-year benefit) |
| 4 | External trademark registration | **Indefinite-life intangible asset** | Indefinite (continual renewal expected) |
| 5 | Internal R&D salaries | **Expense** | — |
| 6 | Excess purchase price over FV of identifiable net assets | **Goodwill** | Indefinite |
| 7 | Internal brand-maintenance salaries | **Expense** | — |
| 8 | State license (3 years) | **Finite-life intangible asset** | Finite (3-year license) |

Goodwill amount (item 6):  
\$800,000 − \$625,000 = **\$175,000**

**c. Year-1 amortization schedule (finite-life assets only)**

- **Goodwill $175,000** — indefinite useful life (no amortization; subject to impairment testing under other LOs).

**d. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE (amortization)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

**Check:** Dr 27,000 = Cr 6,000 + 9,000 + 6,000 + 6,000. **Balanced.**

Carrying amounts at December 31, Year 1:  
- Patent: 48,000 − 6,000 = **$42,000**  
- Franchise: 90,000 − 9,000 = **$81,000**  
- Customer List: 36,000 − 6,000 = **$30,000**  
- License: 18,000 − 6,000 = **$12,000**  
- Trademark: **$6,200** (unamortized)  
- Goodwill: **$175,000** (unamortized)

**e. Balance-sheet presentation (after Year-1 amortization)**  
- **Intangible assets (other than goodwill), net:** patent, franchise, customer list, license, and trademark reported as intangible assets (finite-life amounts net of amortization; trademark non-amortizing). Aggregate finite-life net = 42,000 + 81,000 + 30,000 + 12,000 = **$165,000**; plus indefinite trademark **$6,200**.  
- **Goodwill** presented **separately** from other intangibles: **$175,000**.  
- Internally generated R&D and brand costs appear only as **expenses** on the income statement, not as assets.

**Key insight:** Classification drives measurement. **Acquired** intangibles (and external registration costs) are capitalized and then split into **finite** (amortize) vs **indefinite** (do not amortize). **Internally developed** costs and internal “goodwill” are **expensed**. **Goodwill** is recognized only as the residual in a **business combination**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Classification + initial recognition twin
**LO:** LO 13-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: classify items; initial recognition JEs; amortization schedule; period-end adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Robotics** (calendar year) has the following **Year 1** activity. Finite-life assets acquired **January 1**; residual values zero.

| # | Intangible item / transaction | Amount / terms |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cash purchase of a **copyright** (printing rights); useful life **12 years** (legal life much longer) | **$72,000** |
| 2 | Cash purchase of a **franchise**; renewable annually, management expects renewals **indefinitely** with no foreseeable limit | **$110,000** |
| 3 | Cash purchase of a **patent**; remaining legal life 14 years; **useful life 7 years** | **$56,000** |
| 4 | **External counsel fees** to successfully **register** an internally developed **trade name** expected to be renewed indefinitely | **$8,400** (April 1) |
| 5 | **Internal engineer salaries** and lab supplies creating an invention later patented | **$145,000** |
| 6 | Acquisition of a competitor: cash paid **$1,250,000**; FV of **identifiable net assets** **$980,000** | Residual = goodwill |
| 7 | **External legal fees** to **successfully defend** the purchased patent (added to patent cost on **July 1**) | **$11,200** |
| 8 | **Two-year municipal license** paid in cash January 1 | **$24,000** |

**Required:**  
a. Classify each item as finite-life IA, indefinite-life IA (not goodwill), goodwill, or expense. For item 7, state the effect on the patent’s classification and subsequent measurement.  
b. Record **initial recognition** (and the July 1 patent defense) journal entries.  
c. Prepare the **Year-1 amortization schedule** (prorate partial-year amortization for the patent defense costs added July 1; full year for Jan 1 acquisitions).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1** amortization adjusting entry.  
e. Compute **December 31, Year 1 carrying amounts** for the patent (including defense costs), copyright, franchise, trade name, license, and goodwill.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**

| # | Item | Classification | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Copyright | **Finite-life intangible asset** | 12-year useful life |
| 2 | Franchise (continual renewal, no foreseeable limit) | **Indefinite-life intangible asset** | Not amortized |
| 3 | Purchased patent | **Finite-life intangible asset** | Useful life 7 yrs |
| 4 | External trade-name registration | **Indefinite-life intangible asset** | External fees capitalized |
| 5 | Internal R&D / invention costs | **Expense** | Internally generated |
| 6 | Excess over FV of identifiable net assets | **Goodwill** | \$1,250,000 − \$980,000 = **\$270,000** |
| 7 | Successful external patent defense | **Capitalize to patent** (still finite-life) | Amortize over remaining useful life |
| 8 | 2-year municipal license | **Finite-life intangible asset** | 2-year life |

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Disposal JE + classification / presentation
**LO:** LO 13-1  
**Concept:** Period-end and disposal-date amortization; disposal (derecognition) JE; classification/presentation of remaining intangibles and goodwill  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakewood Components Inc.** purchased a **patent** on **January 1, Year 1** for **$80,000** cash. Estimated useful life **10 years**, residual **zero**, straight-line. The company also holds:

| Asset (already on books at Jan 1, Year 3, *before* Year-3 amortization) | Classification | Carrying amount Jan 1, Year 3 |
|---|---|---:|
| Patent (from above) | Finite-life IA | **\$64,000** (after 2 years × \$8,000) |
| Trademark (renewed indefinitely) | Indefinite-life IA | **\$22,500** |
| Goodwill from a prior acquisition | Goodwill | **\$310,000** |
| Customer list (5 years original life; 3 years remain) | Finite-life IA | **\$27,000** |

On **April 1, Year 3**, Lakewood **sells the patent** for **\$61,000** cash. Year-end is December 31. Customer-list annual amortization is \$9,000 (\$27,000 / 3 remaining years). Trademark and goodwill are not amortized.

**Required:**  
a. Record amortization of the **patent** from January 1 to April 1, Year 3 (update to disposal date).  
b. Record the **April 1, Year 3 disposal** of the patent.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 3** amortization adjusting entry for the **customer list** only.  
d. Prepare a brief **classification / presentation** schedule of intangible-related balances on the December 31, Year 3 balance sheet (after Year-3 amortization). Indicate finite vs indefinite and whether amortized.  
e. State whether each of the following would be **capitalized or expensed** if incurred in Year 3: (1) internal design salaries refining the trademark logo; (2) external counsel fees to renew the trademark registration.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Patent amortization to disposal date (3/12 of Year 3)**  
Annual patent amortization = \$80,000 / 10 = **\$8,000**  
Jan 1–Apr 1 (3 months): \$8,000 × 3/12 = **\$2,000**

*April 1, Year 3 — Amortize patent to date of sale*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 2,000 | |
| Patent | | 2,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,000. **Balanced.**

Carrying amount at disposal: \$64,000 − \$2,000 = **\$62,000**

**b. Disposal of patent**  
Cash proceeds \$61,000 − carrying amount \$62,000 = **Loss \$1,000**

*April 1, Year 3 — Sell patent*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 61,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Patent | 1,000 | |
| Patent | | 62,000 |

**Check:** Dr 61,000 + 1,000 = Cr 62,000. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 3 — Customer list amortization**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 9,000 | |
| Customer List | | 9,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000. **Balanced.**

(Trademark and goodwill: **no** amortization entry.)

**d. Classification / presentation — December 31, Year 3**

| Line item | Classification | Amortized? | Carrying amount |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Customer list | Finite-life intangible asset | Yes | 27,000 − 9,000 = **18,000** |
| Trademark | Indefinite-life intangible asset (other than goodwill) | No | **22,500** |
| **Total intangible assets (other than goodwill)** | | | **40,500** |
| Goodwill | Goodwill (indefinite) | No | **310,000** |
| Patent | — | — | **0** (sold) |

Presentation notes:  
- Report **goodwill separately** from other intangible assets.  
- Finite-life intangibles may be shown gross with accumulated amortization in the notes; indefinite-life trademarks are non-amortizing.  
- Loss on sale of patent is a component of **income from continuing operations**.

**e. Year-3 cost classification**  
1. **Internal design salaries** refining the logo → **Expense** (internally generated / maintenance of intangible inherent in the business).  
2. **External counsel fees** to renew trademark registration → **Capitalize** to the trademark (or capitalize renewal costs consistent with policy for indefinite-life marks acquired via external fees).

**Key insight:** Before disposal, **amortize to the sale date**, then remove the **updated carrying amount**. Classification on the balance sheet separates **finite-life intangibles**, **indefinite-life intangibles**, and **goodwill**. Internal brand work is expensed; external registration/renewal legal fees may be capitalized.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of intangible items
**LO:** LO 13-1  
**Concept:** Finite vs indefinite vs goodwill vs expense; crypto asset classification when criteria met  

**Question 1:**  
A company incurs the following costs. Which item is recognized as **goodwill**?

- A) Excess of cash paid in a business combination over the fair value of identifiable net assets acquired  
- B) Excess of appraised “brand value” from an internal marketing study over book equity  
- C) Legal fees paid to outside counsel to register a patent on an internally developed process  
- D) Purchase price of a customer list expected to benefit the company for five years  

**Answer:** **A.** Goodwill is recognized only from a **business combination** as the residual excess of consideration over the FV of identifiable net assets. Internal brand studies do not create recognized goodwill (B). External patent registration is a finite-life intangible asset (C). A purchased customer list is a finite-life intangible asset (D).

---

**Question 2:**  
Which combination correctly classifies the items?

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| I | Purchased trademark management will renew indefinitely with no foreseeable limit on cash-flow contribution |
| II | Cost of in-house engineers’ salaries developing a patentable process |
| III | Bitcoin held as an investment that meets ASC 350-60 crypto-asset criteria |

- A) I = indefinite-life IA; II = finite-life IA; III = inventory  
- B) I = indefinite-life IA; II = expense; III = intangible asset (presented separately from other intangibles when criteria met)  
- C) I = goodwill; II = expense; III = finite-life IA  
- D) I = expense; II = indefinite-life IA; III = financial asset  

**Answer:** **B.** Continual-renewal trademarks with no foreseeable limit are **indefinite-life intangibles**. Internally generated development salaries are **expensed**. Qualifying **crypto assets** are classified as **intangible assets** and stated **separately** from other intangibles (not inventory or financial assets under the stated criteria).
