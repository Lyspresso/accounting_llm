# Agent 148 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-7

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for extinguishment of debt  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of bonds issued at a discount (issue JE)
- **Subsequent measurement:** full effective-interest amortization schedule through early retirement and remaining life
- Period-end / retirement-date interest and discount amortization **update before** extinguishment
- Partial early extinguishment JE (pro-rata face and unamortized discount; reacquisition price; current gain/loss)
- Accounting for remaining bonds after partial extinguishment (scaled schedule amounts through maturity)
- Mid-period straight-line update (accrued interest + amort) then partial retirement
- Extinguishment at maturity (carrying amount = face → no gain/loss)
- Bond refunding (retire old issue; issue new debt; extinguishment gain/loss in current income)
- Presentation: gain or loss on extinguishment recognized currently in net income as a separate item (ASC 470-50-40-2)

### Q1 — CORE — Copper Canyon Fabricators: schedule + partial early extinguishment (effective interest)
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule + initial issue JE + interest JEs + partial early extinguishment and remaining maturity path  
**Scenario:** On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Copper Canyon Fabricators Inc.** issued **$500,000** face of **3-year, 5%** bonds for **$461,344** (priced to yield **8%**). Cash interest is payable **annually** each **December 31**. Copper Canyon uses the **effective-interest method**, rounds interest expense to the **nearest dollar**, and plugs final-period amortization so the carrying amount equals face at maturity.

On **December 31 of Year 2**, **after** recording interest expense, amortizing the discount, and paying cash interest for Year 2, Copper Canyon **recalls 40%** of the bonds at **101**.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance entry.  
(b) Prepare the **complete effective-interest amortization schedule** for the full $500,000 issue (Years 1–3). Emphasize roll-forward of unamortized discount and carrying amount.  
(c) Record the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** interest payment entries.  
(d) Record the **December 31, Year 2** entry to **extinguish 40%** of the bonds.  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 3** interest entry on the **remaining 60%**.  
(f) Record the **December 31, Year 3** maturity retirement of the remaining bonds.  
(g) In one sentence, state how the extinguishment gain or loss is **presented**.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Issue bonds at a discount**  
Discount = $500,000 − $461,344 = **$38,656**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 461,344 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 38,656 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 500,000 |
| **Totals** | **500,000** | **500,000** |

*Check:* Dr = Cr = $500,000.

**(b) Effective-interest amortization schedule (full issue) — subsequent measurement**  
Cash interest each year = $500,000 × 5% = **$25,000**.  
Interest expense ≈ beginning carrying amount × 8% (nearest dollar). Year 3 amortization plugs remaining unamortized discount.

| Date | Cash interest | Interest expense | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | 38,656 | 461,344 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | 25,000 | 36,908 | 11,908 | 26,748 | 473,252 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 25,000 | 37,860 | 12,860 | 13,888 | 486,112 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 25,000 | 38,888 | 13,888 | 0 | 500,000 |
| **Totals** | **75,000** | **113,656** | **38,656** | | |

*Computation notes:*  
- Year 1 IE = $461,344 × 8% = $36,907.52 → **$36,908**; amort = $36,908 − $25,000 = **$11,908**.  
- Year 2 IE = $473,252 × 8% = $37,860.16 → **$37,860**; amort = **$12,860**; unamortized discount = $38,656 − $11,908 − $12,860 = **$13,888**.  
- Year 3 amort plugs remaining **$13,888**; IE = $25,000 + $13,888 = **$38,888**.  
- Check: total IE $113,656 − total cash $75,000 = total discount $38,656.

**(c) Interest entries**

|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 36,908 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 11,908 |
| Cash | | 25,000 |
| **Totals** | **36,908** | **36,908** |

December 31, Year 2:  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 37,860 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 12,860 |
| Cash | | 25,000 |
| **Totals** | **37,860** | **37,860** |

**(d) December 31, Year 2 — Early redemption of 40%**  
Reacquisition price = $500,000 × 40% × 1.01 = **$202,000**.  
Face removed = **$200,000**.  
Unamortized discount removed = $13,888 × 40% = $5,555.20 → **$5,555** (nearest dollar).  
Carrying amount extinguished = $200,000 − $5,555 = **$194,445**.  
**Loss** = reacquisition price − carrying amount = $202,000 − $194,445 = **$7,555**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |

| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |

*Check:* Dr = Cr = $207,555. Remaining face = $300,000; remaining unamortized discount = $13,888 − $5,555 = **$8,333**.

**(e) December 31, Year 3 — Interest on remaining 60%**  
Use remaining unamortized discount **$8,333** (and 60% cash interest):  
Cash interest = $25,000 × 60% = **$15,000**.  
Discount amortization (remaining) = **$8,333**.  
Interest expense = $15,000 + $8,333 = **$23,333**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 23,333 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 8,333 |
| Cash | | 15,000 |
| **Totals** | **23,333** | **23,333** |

**(f) December 31, Year 3 — Maturity of remaining bonds**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 300,000 | |
| Cash | | 300,000 |
| **Totals** | **300,000** | **300,000** |

No gain or loss (discount fully amortized on the remaining portion; reacquisition price = face).

**(g) Presentation**  
Under ASC 470-50-40-2, the difference between reacquisition price and net carrying amount is recognized **currently in net income** of the extinguishment period as a **separate** gain or loss item.

**Key insight:** The **amortization schedule is the measurement backbone**—early extinguishment is measured only **after** carrying amount is updated through the retirement date; partial retirement removes a **pro-rata slice** of face and unamortized discount, with any difference versus cash paid hitting current earnings.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Mid-period SL partial retirement, maturity, and refunding
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting (accrued interest + SL amort to retirement date); mid-period partial extinguishment; maturity settlement; refunding with current-period loss  
**Scenario — Part A (mid-period partial retirement, straight-line):** On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Redwood Harbor Marine Co.** issued **$600,000** of **4-year, 6%** bonds at **98**. Cash interest is payable **semiannually** on **June 30** and **December 31**. Redwood Harbor amortizes the discount using the **straight-line method**.

On **September 30 of Year 2**, Redwood Harbor retires **$200,000** face of the bonds when they are selling at **94**, and pays **accrued interest** on the retired portion through that date.

**Scenario — Part B (maturity):** Separately, Redwood Harbor holds **$90,000** face of another bond issue to maturity on **December 31 of Year 7**; any discount or premium on that issue is **fully amortized** by maturity.

**Scenario — Part C (refunding):** On **January 1 of Year 5**, **Glacier Peak Energy Corp.** has **$280,000** of **8%** bonds outstanding that were issued at **par** on January 1 of Year 1 (interest dates unchanged). Glacier Peak retires the entire issue at **105** and immediately issues at **par** **$294,000** of **20-year, 5%** bonds (same interest payment dates).

**Required:**  
**Part A**  
(a) Entry for bond issuance on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Entry for the June 30, Year 1 interest payment (pattern for a full semiannual period).  
(c) Entry on September 30 of Year 2 to recognize interest expense and amortization for the **retired portion only** (3 months since June 30).  
(d) Entry on September 30 of Year 2 to record the **bond retirement** (exclude accrued interest already paid in (c)).  

**Part B**  
(e) Entry at maturity to retire the $90,000 bonds.  

**Part C**  
(f) Entry to retire the 8% bonds on January 1 of Year 5.  
(g) Entry to issue the new 5% bonds on January 1 of Year 5.  
(h) State how the extinguishment result is presented and whether refunding changes that presentation rule.

**Answer key:**

**Part A — Setup**  
Issue price = $600,000 × 0.98 = **$588,000**.  
Total discount = **$12,000**.  
Semiannual periods = 4 × 2 = **8**.  
Straight-line amort per full semiannual period = $12,000 / 8 = **$1,500**.  
Cash interest per full period = $600,000 × 6% × 6/12 = **$18,000**.

Interest dates completed through June 30, Year 2: **3** full semiannual periods (Jun 30 Y1; Dec 31 Y1; Jun 30 Y2).  
July 1–September 30, Year 2: **3 months** = half of a semiannual period.  
Amortization for 3 months (entire issue) = $1,500 × 3/6 = **$750**.  
Portion retired = $200,000 / $600,000 = **1/3**.

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Issuance**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 588,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 12,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 600,000 |
| **Totals** | **600,000** | **600,000** |

**(b) June 30, Year 1 — Full-period interest (pattern)**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense ($18,000 + $1,500) | 19,500 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,500 |
| Cash | | 18,000 |
| **Totals** | **19,500** | **19,500** |

**(c) September 30, Year 2 — Interest update on retired portion only**  
Accrued cash interest on retired face = $200,000 × 6% × 3/12 = **$3,000**.  
Discount amort on retired portion for 3 months = $750 × 1/3 = **$250**.  
Interest expense = $3,000 + $250 = **$3,250**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 3,250 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 250 |
| Cash | | 3,000 |
| **Totals** | **3,250** | **3,250** |

**(d) September 30, Year 2 — Extinguishment of $200,000 face**  
Unamortized discount **after** the (c) update, entire issue:  
Amortized to date = (3 × $1,500) + $750 = **$5,250**.  
Unamortized discount remaining (entire) = $12,000 − $5,250 = **$6,750**.  
Unamortized discount on 1/3 retired = $6,750 × 1/3 = **$2,250**.  
*(Equivalently: remaining life after Sept. 30 = 3 months + 2 years = 27 months; monthly amort = $12,000 / 48 = $250; unamortized on retired = $250 × 27 × 1/3 = $2,250.)*

Reacquisition price (bond price only) = $200,000 × 0.94 = **$188,000**.  
Carrying amount of retired bonds = $200,000 − $2,250 = **$197,750**.  
**Gain** on redemption = $197,750 − $188,000 = **$9,750**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,250 |
| Cash | | 188,000 |
| Gain on Redemption of Bonds | | 9,750 |
| **Totals** | **200,000** | **200,000** |

**(e) Part B — Extinguishment at maturity**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 90,000 | |
| Cash | | 90,000 |
| **Totals** | **90,000** | **90,000** |

**No gain or loss** when discount/premium is fully amortized and cash paid equals face.

**(f) Part C — Retire 8% bonds (refunding)**  
Reacquisition price = $280,000 × 1.05 = **$294,000**.  
Carrying amount = face (issued at par) = **$280,000**.  
**Loss** = $294,000 − $280,000 = **$14,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 280,000 | |
| Loss on Redemption of Bonds | 14,000 | |
| Cash | | 294,000 |
| **Totals** | **294,000** | **294,000** |

**(g) Part C — Issue new 5% bonds at par**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 294,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 294,000 |
| **Totals** | **294,000** | **294,000** |

**(h) Presentation**  
ASC 470-50-40-2: difference between reacquisition price and net carrying amount is recognized **currently in income** as a **separate** gain or loss. **Refunding does not change the measurement or presentation**—the old issue’s extinguishment result is **not** deferred and amortized over the life of the new issue.

**Key insight:** Before a mid-period extinguishment, **update interest and amortization to the retirement date** for the portion retired; only then measure gain/loss as reacquisition price versus updated net carrying amount. Maturity (fully amortized) is face-for-cash. Refunding is still a current-period extinguishment event.

### Q4 — MC — Measuring and reporting extinguishment gain/loss
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation — measuring gain or loss on early debt extinguishment and reporting it currently in net income  

**Question:** On December 31 (an interest payment date, after interest and amortization entries), **Thornfield Media Group** extinguishes bonds with face **$90,000**, unamortized discount **$2,700**, by paying **$91,800** cash (call price 102). How should Thornfield report the extinguishment result, and what is the amount?

- A) Gain of $1,800, deferred and amortized over the remaining original term of the bonds  
- B) Loss of $4,500, recognized currently in net income as a separate item  
- C) Loss of $1,800, recognized currently in net income as a separate item  
- D) Loss of $4,500, reported only in OCI and never reclassified to earnings  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Net carrying amount = $90,000 − $2,700 = **$87,300**.  
Reacquisition price = **$91,800**.  
Loss = $91,800 − $87,300 = **$4,500**.  
ASC 470-50-40-2 requires the difference to be recognized **currently in income** of the extinguishment period as a **separate** gain or loss item—not deferred into remaining/new debt, and not OCI-only.

**Key insight:** Loss (gain) = reacquisition price − net carrying amount, where net carrying amount = face − unamortized discount (or face + unamortized premium). Report currently in earnings as a separate item.
