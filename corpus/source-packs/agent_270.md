# Agent 270 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-7

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase of trading debt securities at par (between interest dates: Investment + Interest Receivable)
- **Interest revenue:** full coupon cash vs interest earned for the holding period; reverse Interest Receivable on coupon collection
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortized-cost basis retained in Investment account; **FVA worksheet** bridges amortized cost → fair value
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** unrealized holding G/L through **net income** (Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income); FVA debited for net unrealized gains / credited for net unrealized losses; **no sale-date FV remeasurement** under Appendix 14A / LO 14-7
- **Disposal JE:** realized G/L = cash proceeds − **amortized cost**; **leave FVA untouched** until period-end
- **Period-end after disposal:** eliminate / true up FVA for securities sold during the period (portfolio-level period-end only)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** TS typically current assets at FV (AC ± FVA); unrealized and realized G/L in **other income (NI)**, not OCI; fair value option on otherwise HTM debt uses same FV-NI period-end mechanics
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO 14-7 path with all different amounts

**Method note (exam gap vs LO 14-2):** LO 14-2 remeasures FVA **at sale date and period-end**. LO 14-7 (Appendix 14A) adjusts FVA **only at period-end**. On sale, recognize **realized** G/L vs amortized cost; clean up FVA later at year-end so cumulative NI equals economic profit or loss.

### Q1 — CORE — Between-interest TS purchase, period-end FVA (emphasis), sale vs AC, year-end FVA cleanup

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of trading debt securities; cash interest; **period-end-only** FV-NI adjusting JE (emphasis); disposal with realized G/L vs amortized cost; subsequent period-end FVA elimination  
**Scenario:**  
**Larkspur Trading Partners** (calendar year) buys debt for short-term price appreciation and classifies it as **trading securities (TS)** measured at **FV-NI**. Per company policy (Appendix 14A / LO 14-7), Larkspur records **Fair Value Adjustment (FVA)** entries **only at each December 31 period-end**—never on sale dates.

**Facts:**
1. On **November 1, Year 1**, Larkspur purchases **at par** **$60,000** face of **Cascade Biologics** **9%** bonds (bonds dated **July 1, Year 1**; mature **June 30, Year 7**). Cash interest is paid **semiannually on June 30 and December 31**. No brokerage fees.
2. Fair value of the Cascade bonds on **December 31, Year 1** is **$57,000**.
3. On **March 15, Year 2**, Larkspur sells the entire holding for **$57,600** cash (clean price; ignore any March accrued-interest complexity beyond the facts given).
4. After the sale, Larkspur holds **no other TS**. It next adjusts FVA only on **December 31, Year 2**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **November 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (include accrued interest paid to seller).  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** cash interest receipt JE.  
c. Prepare a **period-end FVA worksheet** and the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis).  
d. Show **Year 1 balance-sheet carrying amount** and **income-statement** effects (interest + unrealized G/L).  
e. Prepare the **March 15, Year 2 disposal JE** under LO 14-7 (realized G/L vs amortized cost; **do not** adjust FVA at sale).  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE** that eliminates leftover FVA, and a **holding-period G/L reconciliation** schedule.

**Answer key:**  

**a. November 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (between interest dates)**  
Accrued interest July 1–Nov 1 (4 months) = \(60{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 4/12 = \$1{,}800\)  
Cash paid = \(60{,}000 + 1{,}800 = \$61{,}800\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Cascade Bonds | 60,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 1,800 | |
| Cash | | 61,800 |
| *Purchase $60,000 face 9% TS at par plus 4 months accrued interest* | | |

**Check:** Dr 61,800 = Cr 61,800. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Semiannual cash interest**  
Cash (6 months) = \(60{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 6/12 = \$2{,}700\)  
Interest revenue earned (2 months, Nov–Dec) = \(60{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 2/12 = \$900\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,700 | |
| Interest Receivable | | 1,800 |
| Interest Revenue | | 900 |
| *Collect full coupon; reverse receivable; recognize 2 months interest earned* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,700 = Cr 1,800 + 900 = 2,700. **Balanced.**

**c. Period-end FVA worksheet + December 31, Year 1 adjusting JE (emphasis)**  

| TS Investment | Amortized cost | Fair value YE | Unrealized holding G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cascade bonds | $60,000 | $57,000 | $(3,000)$ |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$(3,000)$** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 3,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 3,000 |
| *Period-end only: mark TS to FV through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,000 = Cr 3,000. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = amortized cost − credit FVA = \(60{,}000 - 3{,}000 = \$57{,}000\) = FV.

**d. Year 1 financial statement presentation**  

**Balance sheet (Dec 31, Year 1) — Current assets**  
Investment in trading securities ………… **$57,000**  
*(Investment in TS $60,000 less Fair Value Adjustment—TS credit $3,000)*

**Income statement (Year ended Dec 31, Year 1) — Other revenues (expenses)**  
Interest revenue ……………………………… **$900**  
Unrealized holding loss on trading securities … **$(3,000)**

**e. March 15, Year 2 — Disposal (LO 14-7: realized G/L vs amortized cost; no sale-date FVA)**  
Realized loss = amortized cost − proceeds = \(60{,}000 - 57{,}600 = \$2{,}400\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 57,600 | |
| Loss on Sale of Investment | 2,400 | |
| Investment in TS—Cascade Bonds | | 60,000 |
| *Sale of TS; G/L = cash − amortized cost; FVA not adjusted at sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr 57,600 + 2,400 = 60,000 = Cr 60,000. **Balanced.**  
FVA still carries a **$3,000 credit** until the next period-end.

**f. December 31, Year 2 — Period-end FVA elimination (emphasis) + holding-period reconciliation**  

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required FVA ending balance (no TS remaining) | $0 |
| Less existing FVA account balance (credit) | (3,000) |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$3,000** (debit FVA) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 3,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 3,000 |
| *Period-end only: reverse FVA related to securities sold earlier in the year* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,000 = Cr 3,000. **Balanced.**

**Holding-period G/L reconciliation**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 — Unrealized holding loss (period-end FVA) | $(3,000)$ |
| Year 2 — Realized loss on sale (vs amortized cost) | (2,400) |
| Year 2 — Unrealized G/L from eliminating leftover FVA | 3,000 |
| **Net loss over ownership period** | **$(2,400)$** |
| Check: Cost $60,000 − proceeds $57,600 | **$(2,400)** ✓ |

**Key insight:** Under LO 14-7, **fair-value true-ups hit earnings only through period-end adjusting entries**. Sale day records **realized G/L vs amortized cost** and **ignores FVA**; the **period-end adjusting JE** later eliminates residual FVA so cumulative NI equals the economic loss on the position.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Between-interest TS, period-end FVA, sale, FVA cleanup

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin—initial recognition of trading debt + period-end-only FV-NI adjusting JE + disposal vs amortized cost  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Brokerage LLC** (calendar year) measures trading debt at **FV-NI** and adjusts **FVA only at December 31** (LO 14-7 / Appendix 14A).

**Facts:**
1. On **September 1, Year 1**, Northwind purchases **at par** **$90,000** face of **Riverstone Energy** **5%** bonds (dated **July 1, Year 1**; mature **June 30, Year 10**). Cash interest paid **semiannually on June 30 and December 31**.
2. Fair value on **December 31, Year 1** is **$92,400**.
3. On **January 5, Year 2**, Northwind sells the bonds for **$91,800** cash.
4. No other TS remain after the sale; FVA is adjusted only on **December 31, Year 2**.

**Required:**  
a. September 1, Year 1 purchase JE.  
b. December 31, Year 1 interest receipt JE.  
c. Year-end FVA worksheet and **period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis).  
d. Year 1 BS carrying amount and IS effects.  
e. January 5, Year 2 sale JE (realized G/L; no sale-date FVA).  
f. December 31, Year 2 FVA elimination and holding-period reconciliation.

**Answer key:**  

**a. September 1, Year 1**  
Accrued interest (2 months, July 1–Sep 1) = \(90{,}000 \times 0.05 \times 2/12 = \$750\)  
Cash paid = \(90{,}000 + 750 = \$90{,}750\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Riverstone Bonds | 90,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 750 | |
| Cash | | 90,750 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 90,750. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Interest**  
Cash (6 months) = \(90{,}000 \times 0.05 \times 6/12 = \$2{,}250\)  
Interest revenue (4 months earned, Sep–Dec) = \(90{,}000 \times 0.05 \times 4/12 = \$1{,}500\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,250 | |
| Interest Receivable | | 750 |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,500 |

**Check:** Dr 2,250 = Cr 750 + 1,500 = 2,250. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end FVA only (emphasis)**  

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amortized cost | $90,000 |
| Fair value | 92,400 |
| Required FVA (debit) | 2,400 |
| Existing FVA | 0 |
| **Increase to FVA** | **$2,400** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 2,400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,400 |
| *Period-end only: mark TS to FV through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,400. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = \(90{,}000 + 2{,}400 = \$92{,}400\).

**d. Presentation (Year 1)**  
**BS (current assets):** Investment in trading securities **$92,400**  
**IS other income:** Interest revenue **$1,500**; Unrealized holding gain on trading securities **$2,400**

**e. January 5, Year 2 — Sale (realized G/L; FVA untouched)**  
Realized gain = \(91{,}800 - 90{,}000 = \$1{,}800\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 91,800 | |
| Investment in TS—Riverstone Bonds | | 90,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 1,800 |

**Check:** Dr 91,800 = Cr 90,000 + 1,800 = 91,800. **Balanced.**

|---|---:|

When FV rose above cost at year-end, the **period-end adjusting JE** creates a **debit FVA adjunct**; sale records a **realized gain vs amortized cost**; the next **period-end adjusting JE** reverses leftover FVA so cumulative NI still equals cash profit.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Portfolio period-end FVA after mid-year sale; FVO period-end; presentation

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Portfolio-level **period-end adjusting JE** when beginning FVA exists and one security was sold mid-year (no sale-date FVA); fair value option period-end FV-NI; classification/presentation/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Quarry Peak Investments Inc.** (calendar year) measures trading debt at **FV-NI** and adjusts fair value **only at period-end** (LO 14-7 / Appendix 14A). It maintains one **portfolio-level** Fair Value Adjustment—TS account.

**Beginning balances — January 1, Year 1 (after prior-year close):**

| Security | Classification | Amortized cost | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| D — DeltaForge Metals 6% bonds | Trading (TS) | $40,000 | Still held at 1/1 |
| E — Evergreen Transit 4% bonds | Trading (TS) | 100,000 | Still held at 1/1 |
| F — Frostline Utilities 3% bonds | Classified HTM, but **fair value option** elected at acquisition | 150,000 | Separate FVA—Fair Value Option |

Beginning **Fair Value Adjustment—TS** (portfolio, for D+E only) = **$1,500 debit**.  
Beginning **Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option** (security F) = **$0**.

**Year 1 activity:**
1. On **June 10, Year 1**, Quarry Peak sells **all of security D** for **$41,200** cash. Under LO 14-7, record realized G/L vs amortized cost; **do not** adjust FVA at the sale date.
2. Security E remains held all year. At **December 31, Year 1**, amortized cost of E is still **$100,000** (purchased at par; ignore amortization complexity) and fair value of E is **$97,500**.
3. At **December 31, Year 1**, amortized cost of F is still **$150,000** and fair value of F is **$153,750**. Interest on all positions has already been recorded correctly.
4. No other TS purchases or sales in Year 1.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **June 10, Year 1 disposal JE** for security D (LO 14-7; no sale-date FVA).  
b. Prepare the **TS portfolio period-end FVA worksheet** for December 31, Year 1 (required ending FVA from remaining securities only; roll from beginning FVA) and the single **period-end adjusting JE** for TS (**emphasis**).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1 FVO period-end adjusting JE** for security F.  
d. State **balance-sheet carrying amounts** at Dec 31, Year 1 for (i) trading securities and (ii) the FVO debt investment.  
e. For D’s Year 1 realized G/L, E’s Year 1 unrealized G/L effect through the portfolio entry, and F’s Year 1 unrealized G/L: state whether each hits **net income** or **OCI**.  
f. **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** List three reporting points (BS presentation of TS, income-statement location of unrealized G/L, and one SCF or note disclosure point).  
g. **Contrast:** If security F had been pure HTM **without** the fair value option, what period-end FV journal entry (if any) would be required?

**Answer key:**  

**a. June 10, Year 1 — Disposal of D (realized G/L; FVA untouched)**  
Realized gain = \(41{,}200 - 40{,}000 = \$1{,}200\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 41,200 | |
| Investment in TS—DeltaForge Bonds | | 40,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 1,200 |
| *Sale of TS D; G/L vs amortized cost; no period-end-style FVA entry at sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr 41,200 = Cr 40,000 + 1,200 = 41,200. **Balanced.**  
Portfolio FVA still sits at **$1,500 debit** until December 31.

**b. TS portfolio period-end FVA worksheet + adjusting JE (emphasis)**  

Only **security E** remains in the TS portfolio at year-end:

| Security | Amortized cost | Fair value | Required unrealized G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| E — Evergreen | $100,000 | $97,500 | $(2,500)$ |
| **Portfolio total (remaining)** | **$100,000** | **$97,500** | **$(2,500)$** |

| FVA rollforward | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required ending FVA (FV − AC for remaining TS) | $(2,500)$ credit |
| Beginning FVA | 1,500 debit |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$(4,000)$** |

Computation: move from **$1,500 debit** to **$2,500 credit** → credit FVA by \(1{,}500 + 2{,}500 = \$4{,}000\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 4,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 4,000 |
| *Period-end only: true up portfolio FVA to required ending balance (includes cleanup related to D sold mid-year)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,000. **Balanced.**  
Ending FVA—TS = 1,500 debit − 4,000 = **$2,500 credit**.  
TS carrying amount = \(100{,}000 - 2{,}500 = \$97{,}500\) = FV of remaining security E.

**c. Fair value option — Frostline (period-end FV-NI)**  
Unrealized holding gain = \(153{,}750 - 150{,}000 = \$3{,}750\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option | 3,750 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 3,750 |
| *FVO: period-end FV-NI adjustment (same NI treatment as TS)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,750. **Balanced.**

**d. Balance-sheet carrying amounts (Dec 31, Year 1)**  
- **Trading securities (E only):** **$97,500** (typically current assets).  
- **Frostline FVO debt:** **$153,750** (AC $150,000 + FVA debit $3,750; current/noncurrent by maturity and intent).

**e. NI vs OCI**  

| Item | Destination |
|---|---|
| Realized gain on sale of D ($1,200) | **Net income** |
| Portfolio period-end unrealized effect on TS ($4,000 debit to Unrealized G/L—Income) | **Net income** |
| Unrealized holding gain on F ($3,750) | **Net income** (not OCI) |

None of these LO 14-7 FV-NI measurements use OCI for fair-value changes.

**f. Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
1. **Balance sheet:** Report TS at **fair value** (amortized cost ± FVA), usually as **current assets**. FVA is a valuation account that preserves amortized-cost records while the face of the BS shows FV.  
2. **Income statement:** Interest revenue, **unrealized** holding G/L—Income from period-end FVA, and **realized** G/L on sale all affect **net income** (other income/expense)—**not OCI**.  
3. **SCF / notes:** Purchases and sales of trading debt held to profit on short-term price differences are often classified as **operating** cash flows (purpose-based judgment under ASC 230); notes disclose aggregate FV, gross unrealized holding G/L, and FVO election (instrument-by-instrument, generally irrevocable, elected at acquisition).

**g. Contrast — pure HTM without FVO**  
**No period-end fair value journal entry.** Security F would remain at amortized cost **$150,000**; the $3,750 unrealized gain is **not recognized** in NI or OCI (FV may appear only in required footnotes).

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** is the only place LO 14-7 remeasures FV. When a security is sold mid-year, the sale uses **realized G/L vs amortized cost** and leaves FVA alone; the year-end worksheet sets **required FVA from remaining securities only** and rolls from the **beginning FVA balance**, automatically cleaning up amounts related to sold positions.

### Q4 — MC — Timing of FVA under LO 14-7 (period-end only)

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Classification of method—when fair value is adjusted for debt measured at FV-NI under Appendix 14A  
**Question:** **Cobalt Rim Capital** holds a trading debt security purchased at par for **$80,000**. At the prior December 31, fair value was **$78,000** and Cobalt recorded a **credit FVA of $2,000**. On **April 12** of the current year, Cobalt sells the bonds for **$79,100** cash. Cobalt’s policy is **LO 14-7 (Appendix 14A): fair value adjustments only at period-end**. Which description of the **April 12** accounting is correct?

- A) First debit FVA $1,100 and credit Unrealized G/L—Income $1,100 (bring carrying amount to $79,100); then debit Cash $79,100, debit FVA $900, credit Investment $80,000.  
- B) Debit Cash $79,100; debit Loss on Sale $900; credit Investment $80,000; leave the $2,000 credit FVA outstanding until the next period-end adjusting entry.  
- C) Debit Cash $79,100; debit FVA $2,000; credit Investment $80,000; credit Gain on Sale $1,100 (eliminate FVA as part of the sale entry).  
- D) No entry until December 31; both the sale and all fair value changes are recorded only at year-end.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-7, the sale records **realized loss = amortized cost − cash** = \(80{,}000 - 79{,}100 = \$900\) and **does not** remeasure or eliminate FVA on the sale date. The preexisting FVA remains until the **period-end** true-up (which will also eliminate FVA related to sold securities). Option A is the **LO 14-2** sale-date remeasurement pattern. C incorrectly folds FVA elimination into the sale entry. D is wrong because the **sale itself** is recorded when it occurs—only the **FVA fair-value adjustment** waits until period-end.

### Q5 — MC — Where unrealized holding gains on FV-NI debt are reported

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation—unrealized holding G/L on TS / FVO debt through net income at period-end  
**Question:** At year-end, **Harborglen Advisors** records its **only** debt investment—classified as **trading** and measured at **FV-NI**—from amortized cost **$125,000** to fair value **$129,800** using a period-end-only FVA entry (LO 14-7). How is the **$4,800** unrealized holding gain reported?

- A) Other comprehensive income (OCI), net of tax, until the security is sold.  
- B) Net income (other income) in the current period via the period-end adjusting entry.  
- C) Directly to retained earnings, bypassing both NI and OCI.  
- D) No recognition until sale; amortized cost remains the BS carrying amount at year-end.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Trading securities (and FVO debt) measured at FV-NI recognize unrealized holding gains and losses **in earnings** at each **period-end** adjusting entry. OCI is the AFS (FV-OCI) model, not LO 14-7. Carrying amount becomes fair value via FVA; the gain is not deferred to sale or booked straight to RE.
