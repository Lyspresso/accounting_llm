# Agent 335 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-8

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for contributed property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Contributed (donated) PPE** is recognized by the **recipient** at **fair value** when the transfer is **unconditional** and is **not** an exchange of goods/services of approximately equal value
- Fair value is measured by appraisal or similar-asset market evidence
- Recipient records **Contribution Revenue** equal to fair value of assets received **minus any cash costs paid** (e.g., legal/transfer costs) — assets are still debited at full fair value
- **Donor-imposed conditions** (barrier + right of return) → **defer** contribution revenue until the condition is met; then recognize at period-end (or when met)
- After recognition, contributed **buildings/equipment** are depreciated like other PPE; **land** is not depreciated
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end depreciation on contributed depreciable assets; recognition of previously deferred contribution revenue when a condition is met at year-end
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year depreciation / book-value rollforward of contributed PPE
- **Disposal:** remove cost and accumulated depreciation of contributed assets; recognize gain or loss vs proceeds (land often retained when only the building is sold)
- **CORE only** (recipient path from Demo 11-8 / Review 11-8); donor accounting is Expanding Your Knowledge and is not the primary pack path

### Q1 — CORE — Unconditional donation of land and building; multi-year dep schedule; period-end dep JEs; building disposal

**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of contributed land and building at FV with transfer costs netted against Contribution Revenue; subsequent multi-year depreciation schedule; **period-end depreciation adjusting JEs** (emphasis); disposal of the contributed building  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, the **City of Riverton** unconditionally donated a manufacturing building and the land under it to **Northbridge Components Inc.** to induce Northbridge to expand local employment. The transfer is **not** an exchange of goods or services of approximately equal value. Independent appraisal of the property is **$600,000**, of which **20%** is assignable to land.

Northbridge paid **$8,000** cash for legal and title transfer costs on January 1, Year 1. The building has an estimated remaining useful life of **25 years**, **no residual value**, and is depreciated using the **straight-line** method. Northbridge records a **full year** of depreciation in the year of acquisition and adjusts depreciation only at each **December 31** year-end.

**Additional facts:**
- Land is not depreciated.
- On **March 31, Year 4**, Northbridge sells **only the building** (land is retained) for **$405,000** cash. Depreciation is recorded through the disposal date (straight-line; partial year = months/12).
- Accumulated depreciation on the building before any Year 4 entry equals the balance after three full years of depreciation.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute the fair values allocated to building and land. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for the contribution and transfer costs.  
(b) Compute annual depreciation on the contributed building. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (cost, accum. dep., book value of building; land cost; total net PPE) at each December 31 of Years 1–3.  
(c) Record the **December 31, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 period-end adjusting JEs** for depreciation (**emphasis**).  
(d) Record all entries on **March 31, Year 4** related to the building disposal (partial-year depreciation through disposal date, then removal of building cost and accum. dep., cash proceeds, and gain or loss).  
(e) State the **carrying amount of land** still on the books after the building sale.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Allocation and January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

Land FV = \(600{,}000 \times 0.20 =\) **$120,000**  
Building FV = \(600{,}000 \times 0.80 =\) **$480,000**  
Contribution Revenue = \(600{,}000 - 8{,}000 =\) **$592,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 480,000 | |
| Land | 120,000 | |
| Cash | | 8,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 592,000 |
| *Unconditional contribution at FV; transfer costs reduce contribution revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(480{,}000 + 120{,}000 = 600{,}000\); Cr \(8{,}000 + 592{,}000 = 600{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**(b) Annual depreciation and subsequent measurement schedule**

Annual SL depreciation (building only) = \(480{,}000 / 25 =\) **$19,200** per year.

| Date | Building cost | Accum. dep. (end) | Building BV | Land cost | Total net PPE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1/1/Y1 (acquisition) | 480,000 | 0 | 480,000 | 120,000 | 600,000 |
| 12/31/Y1 | 480,000 | 19,200 | 460,800 | 120,000 | 580,800 |
| 12/31/Y2 | 480,000 | 38,400 | 441,600 | 120,000 | 561,600 |
| 12/31/Y3 | 480,000 | 57,600 | 422,400 | 120,000 | 542,400 |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Acc. dep. Y2: \(19{,}200 \times 2 = 38{,}400\).  
- Acc. dep. Y3: \(19{,}200 \times 3 = 57{,}600\).  
- Building BV Y3: \(480{,}000 - 57{,}600 = 422{,}400\).  
- Total net Y3: \(422{,}400 + 120{,}000 = 542{,}400\).

**(c) Period-end adjusting JEs — depreciation (emphasis)**

**December 31, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 19,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 19,200 |
| *Period-end SL dep. on contributed building (\(480{,}000/25\))* | | |

**Check:** Dr 19,200 = Cr 19,200. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 2**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 19,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 19,200 |
| *Period-end SL dep. Year 2* | | |

**Check:** Dr 19,200 = Cr 19,200. **Balanced.** Ending accum. dep. **$38,400**.

**December 31, Year 3**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 19,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 19,200 |
| *Period-end SL dep. Year 3* | | |

**Check:** Dr 19,200 = Cr 19,200. **Balanced.** Ending accum. dep. **$57,600**.

**(d) March 31, Year 4 — partial-year depreciation and disposal**

Partial-year dep. Y4 (3 months): \(19{,}200 \times 3/12 =\) **$4,800**  
Accum. dep. at disposal = \(57{,}600 + 4{,}800 =\) **$62,400**  
Building BV at disposal = \(480{,}000 - 62{,}400 =\) **$417,600**  
Loss on disposal = \(417{,}600 - 405{,}000 =\) **$12,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 4,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 4,800 |
| *Depreciation through disposal date (3/12 of annual)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,800 = Cr 4,800. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 405,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | 62,400 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Building | 12,600 | |
| Building | | 480,000 |
| *Remove contributed building; cash proceeds; recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(405{,}000 + 62{,}400 + 12{,}600 = 480{,}000\); Cr 480,000. **Balanced.**

**(e) Land after building sale**

Land remains on the books at **$120,000** (not depreciated; not sold).

**Key insight:** Contributed PPE is capitalized at **fair value**. Cash transfer costs reduce **Contribution Revenue**, not the asset bases. After recognition, depreciable contributed assets follow normal PPE subsequent measurement (period-end dep.) and disposal accounting.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Unconditional donation; multi-year schedule; period-end dep; equipment disposal

**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition of contributed land and building at FV net of transfer costs; subsequent measurement schedule; **period-end depreciation adjusting JEs**; disposal of contributed building  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, a major shareholder of **Cascade Precision Tools Corp.** unconditionally donated a warehouse building and land to Cascade. The property was reliably appraised at **$840,000**, of which **30%** relates to land. Cascade paid **$12,000** cash for transfer and recording fees. The building’s remaining useful life is **30 years**, residual value **$0**, straight-line, full year in year of acquisition. Depreciation is recorded only at each **December 31**.

On **June 30, Year 4**, Cascade sells the **building only** for **$520,000** cash (land retained). Update depreciation through the disposal date.

**Required:**  
(a) Allocate FV to land and building; record the **January 1, Year 1** contribution entry.  
(b) Compute annual depreciation; prepare the **Years 1–3 subsequent measurement schedule** (building cost, accum. dep., BV; land; total net).  
(c) Record **December 31 Year 1–3 period-end depreciation adjusting JEs**.  
(d) Record **June 30, Year 4** partial-year depreciation and building disposal (gain/loss).  
(e) State land’s remaining carrying amount.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Allocation and initial recognition**

Land FV = \(840{,}000 \times 0.30 =\) **$252,000**  
Building FV = \(840{,}000 \times 0.70 =\) **$588,000**  
Contribution Revenue = \(840{,}000 - 12{,}000 =\) **$828,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 588,000 | |
| Land | 252,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 828,000 |
| *Unconditional contribution at FV; transfer costs reduce revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(588{,}000 + 252{,}000 = 840{,}000\); Cr \(12{,}000 + 828{,}000 = 840{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule**

Annual dep. = \(588{,}000 / 30 =\) **$19,600**

| Date | Building cost | Accum. dep. (end) | Building BV | Land cost | Total net PPE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1/1/Y1 | 588,000 | 0 | 588,000 | 252,000 | 840,000 |
| 12/31/Y1 | 588,000 | 19,600 | 568,400 | 252,000 | 820,400 |
| 12/31/Y2 | 588,000 | 39,200 | 548,800 | 252,000 | 800,800 |
| 12/31/Y3 | 588,000 | 58,800 | 529,200 | 252,000 | 781,200 |

**Checks:** Acc. dep. Y3 \(= 19{,}600 \times 3 = 58{,}800\); BV \(= 588{,}000 - 58{,}800 = 529{,}200\); total net \(= 529{,}200 + 252{,}000 = 781{,}200\).

**(c) Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

**December 31, Year 1 / Year 2 / Year 3** (identical form each year):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 19,600 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 19,600 |
| *Period-end SL dep. on contributed building (\(588{,}000/30\))* | | |

**Check each year:** Dr 19,600 = Cr 19,600. **Balanced.**  
Ending accum. dep.: Y1 **$19,600**; Y2 **$39,200**; Y3 **$58,800**.

**(d) June 30, Year 4 — partial-year dep and disposal**

Partial-year dep. (6 months): \(19{,}600 \times 6/12 =\) **$9,800**  
Accum. dep. at disposal = \(58{,}800 + 9{,}800 =\) **$68,600**  
Building BV = \(588{,}000 - 68{,}600 =\) **$519,400**  
Gain on disposal = \(520{,}000 - 519{,}400 =\) **$600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 9,800 |
| *Depreciation through 6/30 (6/12 of annual)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,800 = Cr 9,800. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 520,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | 68,600 | |
| Building | | 588,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Building | | 600 |
| *Remove contributed building; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(520{,}000 + 68{,}600 = 588{,}600\); Cr \(588{,}000 + 600 = 588{,}600\). **Balanced.**

**(e) Land remaining**

Land carrying amount **$252,000**.

**Key insight:** Changing appraisal totals, land percentages, transfer costs, useful lives, and disposal timing does not change the model: debit assets at FV, credit contribution revenue net of cash costs, depreciate only the building, and clear cost/accum. dep. at disposal.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Conditional contribution (deferral) and period-end recognition; equipment life cycle to disposal

**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** **Period-end adjusting JE emphasis** — defer contribution revenue until a donor-imposed condition is met; recognize equipment at FV when condition is satisfied at year-end; subsequent depreciation schedule; disposal at maturity of use  
**Scenario:**  
On **March 1, Year 1**, the **Port Authority of Lakeshore** transferred specialized production equipment (fair value **$150,000**) to **Harborline Marine Systems Inc.** under a **donor-imposed condition**: Harborline must create and maintain **at least 40 full-time manufacturing jobs** in Lakeshore County by **December 31, Year 1**. If the headcount target is not met, the equipment must be returned (right of return). The arrangement is not an exchange of goods or services of equal value.

Harborline paid **no** transfer costs. Legal title is held in escrow pending the condition.

**Facts:**
- On **December 31, Year 1**, Harborline documents that it employs **43** qualifying workers. The condition is **met**; title transfers unconditionally.
- Equipment useful life from January 1, Year 2: **10 years**, residual **$10,000**, straight-line. (Depreciation begins when the asset is placed in service after condition is met; assume placed in service **January 1, Year 2** and full years thereafter.)
- Harborline adjusts depreciation only at **December 31**.
- On **December 31, Year 5**, after recording that year’s depreciation, Harborline retires the equipment for scrap proceeds of **$12,000** cash (no further residual guarantee).

**Required:**  
(a) Explain why **no** contribution revenue is recognized on March 1, Year 1. Record any entry on March 1 if books track the escrow receipt (memo/no entry is acceptable if disclosed); state the correct recognition date.  
(b) Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** recognizing the unconditional contribution (**emphasis**).  
(c) Compute annual depreciation. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for equipment book value at each December 31 of Years 2–5 (after period-end dep.).  
(d) Record the **December 31, Year 2** period-end depreciation adjusting JE (representative of Years 2–5).  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 5** retirement (after that day’s depreciation has already been recorded per the schedule): remove cost and accum. dep.; record scrap cash and gain or loss.

**Answer key:**

**(a) March 1, Year 1 — condition not yet met**

A donor-imposed condition requires both (1) a **barrier** (here, the 40-job threshold) and (2) a **right of return**. Contribution revenue is **deferred** until the barrier is overcome.  
**No recognition entry** on March 1, Year 1 (or a memorandum only). Recognition date = **December 31, Year 1**, when the condition is met.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — period-end recognition when condition is met (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 150,000 | |
| Contribution Revenue | | 150,000 |
| *Condition met at year-end; record contributed equipment at FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 150,000 = Cr 150,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Subsequent measurement schedule**

Depreciable base = \(150{,}000 - 10{,}000 =\) **$140,000**  
Annual dep. = \(140{,}000 / 10 =\) **$14,000**

| Date | Equipment cost | Accum. dep. (end) | Book value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 (recognition; not yet in service for dep.) | 150,000 | 0 | 150,000 |
| 12/31/Y2 | 150,000 | 14,000 | 136,000 |
| 12/31/Y3 | 150,000 | 28,000 | 122,000 |
| 12/31/Y4 | 150,000 | 42,000 | 108,000 |
| 12/31/Y5 (after Y5 dep.) | 150,000 | 56,000 | 94,000 |

**Checks:** Acc. dep. Y5 \(= 14{,}000 \times 4 = 56{,}000\) (four years of service: Y2–Y5). BV \(= 150{,}000 - 56{,}000 = 94{,}000\).

**(d) December 31, Year 2 — period-end depreciation adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 14,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 14,000 |
| *Period-end SL dep. (\(150{,}000 - 10{,}000)/10\)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,000 = Cr 14,000. **Balanced.**  
(Same entry form at each December 31 of Years 3, 4, and 5.)

**(e) December 31, Year 5 — disposal / settlement after Y5 depreciation**

Book value at disposal = **$94,000**  
Scrap proceeds = **$12,000**  
Loss on disposal = \(94{,}000 - 12{,}000 =\) **$82,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 12,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 56,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 82,000 | |
| Equipment | | 150,000 |
| *Retire contributed equipment; scrap proceeds; loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(12{,}000 + 56{,}000 + 82{,}000 = 150{,}000\); Cr 150,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Conditional contributions are **not** revenue when received under a barrier + return right. The **period-end** moment when the condition is met is when the recipient records the asset and Contribution Revenue at fair value; thereafter, normal depreciation and disposal complete the PPE life cycle.

### Q4 — MC — Measuring contribution revenue (recipient)

**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Classification/measurement of Contribution Revenue when recipient pays cash in a nonreciprocal transfer of equipment  

**Question 1:**  
**Summit Health Clinic** received medical imaging equipment from a vendor that donated the asset to support free community screening. The equipment’s fair value is **$72,000**. Summit paid the donor **$3,000** cash as a token of appreciation and paid **$1,500** cash in separate legal transfer fees. The donor did not receive goods or services of approximately equal value. What amount of **Contribution Revenue** should Summit report?

- A) $72,000  
- B) $69,000  
- C) $67,500  
- D) $70,500  

**Answer:** C. Contribution Revenue = fair value of asset received − cash paid related to the transfer = \(72{,}000 - 3{,}000 - 1{,}500 = 67{,}500\). Assets are recorded at full FV ($72,000); cash outflows reduce contribution revenue, not the equipment base under the Demo 11-8 pattern.

**Question 2:**  
Which statement correctly describes **recipient** accounting for contributed PPE under LO 11-8?

- A) Record donated land and buildings at the donor’s historical cost; credit Common Stock.  
- B) Record donated PPE at fair value when the transfer is unconditional and not an equal-value exchange; credit Contribution Revenue (net of any costs paid).  
- C) Defer all contribution revenue until the asset is fully depreciated.  
- D) Capitalize transfer costs into the building and land accounts and credit Gain on Contribution for the full fair value.  

**Answer:** B. Core rule: unconditional, non-exchange transfers → recipient assets at FV and Contribution Revenue (reduced by cash costs paid). Donor cost is irrelevant to the recipient. Conditions defer revenue only until the barrier is met, not for the asset’s entire life. Transfer costs reduce contribution revenue in the textbook demo rather than being added to PPE cost in that illustration.
