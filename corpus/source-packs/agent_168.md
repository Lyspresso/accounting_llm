# Agent 168 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-5

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Describe required disclosures including that for noncash transactions  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Noncash investing and financing** transactions are omitted from the body of the SCF (no cash exchanged) but must be **disclosed** (narrative or schedule)
- Partial noncash asset acquisitions: report only the **cash portion** in investing; exclude noncash consideration from financing inflows
- Adjusting draft investing/financing sections when stock, notes, or assumed liabilities were mixed into “cash” lines
- **Equipment (PPE) additions schedule** that separates cash purchases from noncash purchases (subsequent analysis of account activity)
- **Note payable subsequent-measurement schedule** (interest accrual path; principal amortization; cash vs noncash periods)
- Period-end interest **adjusting JE** feeding the interest-paid disclosure
- Settlement / conversion without cash (debt to equity) — disclosure, not financing cash outflow
- Supplemental disclosures under the **indirect method**: **interest paid** (net of amounts capitalized) and **income taxes paid**
- **Restricted cash** nature disclosure
- Classification of partial noncash purchases and finance-lease commencement on the SCF

**(h) LO 22-5 reporting/disclosure checklist**  
1. Reconciliation of net income to operating cash flows (on face or note under indirect method; major classes of operating accruals/deferrals).  
2. Report investing and financing **inflows and outflows separately** (gross).  
3. Disclose **interest paid** (indirect method; net of capitalized interest) and **income taxes paid** (either method).  
4. Disclose **noncash investing and financing** transactions.  
5. Disclose the **nature of restrictions** on cash, cash equivalents, and amounts generally described as restricted cash/equivalents.

**Key insight:** A PPE additions **schedule** must split cash vs noncash so investing is not overstated. Noncash inception (note, stock, lease) is **disclosed**, not reported as cash. Later **cash** interest and principal hit the SCF when paid; period-end **accruals** bridge expense to the interest-paid disclosure. Matched noncash reclasses preserve inv+fin combined totals; unmatched phantom lines do not.

### Q4 — MC — Partial cash / partial note equipment purchase
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Classification — cash investing outflow only; note issuance excluded from financing cash inflows  
**Question:** **Fieldstone Appliances Co.** purchased equipment for **$520,000**. The seller accepted **$340,000 cash** and a **$180,000 long-term note** for the balance. How should Fieldstone report this transaction on its statement of cash flows?

| | Investing activities | Financing activities |
|---|---|---|
| A) | $(520,000) | $180,000 |
| B) | $(340,000) | $180,000 |
| C) | $(520,000) | $0 |
| D) | $(340,000) | $0 |

**Answer:** **D.** Only the **$340,000 cash** paid is an investing outflow. Issuing a note for equipment is a **noncash investing and financing** transaction disclosed in the notes (or on the same page as the SCF)—it is **not** a financing cash inflow. Reporting $(520,000) investing would overstate the cash used to acquire the asset.

### Q5 — MC — Required SCF disclosures (indirect method)
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Required supplemental disclosures — interest paid, taxes paid, noncash, restricted cash  
**Question:** Which of the following best describes **required** statement-of-cash-flows disclosures under U.S. GAAP when the **indirect method** is used for operating activities?

- A) Interest paid and income taxes paid may be omitted if interest expense and tax expense appear on the income statement.  
- B) Material noncash investing and financing transactions may be omitted because they do not affect cash.  
- C) Interest paid (net of amounts capitalized) and income taxes paid must be disclosed; material noncash investing and financing transactions must also be disclosed; the nature of restrictions on cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash must be disclosed.  
- D) Only companies using the direct method must disclose income taxes paid and noncash transactions.

**Answer:** **C.** Under ASC 230, the indirect method requires disclosure of **interest paid** (net of capitalized interest). **Income taxes paid** is disclosed regardless of method. Material **noncash investing and financing** activities must be disclosed (narrative or schedule). Entities also disclose the **nature of restrictions** on cash, cash equivalents, and amounts generally described as restricted cash. Income-statement expense lines do **not** replace cash-paid disclosures; noncash items are omitted from the body of the SCF **precisely because** they require separate disclosure when material.
