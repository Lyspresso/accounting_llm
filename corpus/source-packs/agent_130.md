# Agent 130 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-8

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Prepare a statement of cash flows using the cash T-account approach  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash T-account approach (core):** recreate balance-sheet/income-statement activity as reconstructed journal entries; post cash effects into a **cash T-account** organized by **operating, investing, and financing**; build the statement of cash flows from that T-account (Demo 22-8 style; direct-method operating section)
- **Algebraic identity:** \(\Delta\)Cash (including cash equivalents and restricted cash) \(= -\Delta\)Noncash assets \(+ \Delta\)Liabilities \(+ \Delta\)Equity
- **Initial recognition / reconstructed cash JEs:** collections from customers; payments to suppliers, employees, and for interest; cash purchases of PPE; cash issuances of debt and equity
- **Subsequent measurement schedules:** AR, inventory/AP, salaries payable, equipment, accumulated depreciation, and retained earnings rollforwards used to solve for cash and noncash amounts
- **Period-end adjusting JE (noncash):** depreciation (and similar accruals) posted to noncash T-accounts only — **not** a line in the cash T-account
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement JE:** PPE sale (cash proceeds + remove cost/AD + gain or loss); principal repayment or maturity of notes/bonds as financing cash outflow
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** O/I/F sections from the cash T-account; report investing and financing inflows and outflows **separately**; reconcile beginning to ending **cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash**; gains/losses affect operating under indirect method conceptually but under the T-account/direct approach only **cash proceeds** appear in investing

**f. Classification / presentation of the equipment disposal**  
The **loss** is a noncash income-statement amount used only to reconstruct the disposal entry. Under the cash T-account / direct approach, the statement reports only the **cash proceeds of $100,000** as an **investing inflow**. The loss does **not** appear as a separate cash line. (Under the indirect method, the loss would be **added back** to net income in operating activities so that the full proceeds can be shown in investing — same economic result.)

**Key insight:** The cash T-account is a worksheet that forces every reconstructed cash debit/credit into O, I, or F. Noncash period-end entries (depreciation) and noncash portions of disposal entries (AD removal, gain/loss) never hit the cash T-account; only true cash legs do. Beginning and ending cash on the SCF include **restricted cash**.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 22-8  
**Concept:** Classification — which reconstructed items appear in the cash T-account; treatment of restricted cash in the SCF reconciliation  

**Question 1:**  
Under the **cash T-account approach** to preparing the statement of cash flows (Demo 22-8), which reconstructed entry is **posted only to noncash T-accounts** and does **not** appear as a line in the cash T-account?

- A) Cash collections from customers equal to sales plus the decrease in accounts receivable  
- B) Cash purchase of equipment  
- C) Period-end entry debiting Depreciation Expense and crediting Accumulated Depreciation  
- D) Cash principal repayment of bonds at maturity  

**Answer:** **C.** Depreciation is a noncash expense. The reconstructed period-end JE updates expense and accumulated depreciation T-accounts so that PPE disposal entries can be reconstructed correctly, but it has **no cash leg** and therefore does not appear in the cash T-account. Choices A, B, and D all include cash and are classified as operating, investing, and financing, respectively.

**Question 2:**  
When the cash T-account is completed and the statement of cash flows is prepared, the beginning and ending “cash” amounts used to reconcile the net change should include:

- A) Cash only; cash equivalents and restricted cash are always excluded  
- B) Cash and cash equivalents only; restricted cash is always reported separately outside the SCF  
- C) Cash, cash equivalents, and amounts generally described as restricted cash or restricted cash equivalents  
- D) Cash plus the current portion of long-term debt  

**Answer:** **C.** ASC 230 requires the SCF to reconcile the change in the total of cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash (Demo 22-8 groups cash and cash equivalents with restricted cash in the cash T-account and in the bottom reconciliation).
