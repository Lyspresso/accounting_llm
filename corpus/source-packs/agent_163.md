# Agent 163 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-7

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Describe accounting for net operating loss carryforwards and loss carryback/carryforwards  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- NOL carryforward creates a **deferred tax asset** measured as remaining NOL × enacted future tax rate
- Initial recognition JE: Dr Deferred Tax Asset / Cr Income Tax Expense (credit balance presented as **Income Tax Benefit**)
- **80% of taxable income** utilization limit on NOL carryforwards (default in all assignments unless stated otherwise)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule** (emphasis): multi-year rollforward of remaining NOL, max use, NOL used, ending DTA
- Period-end tax JE: reduce DTA for NOL used; tax payable on residual taxable income (≥20% of TI when 80% limit binds)
- Valuation allowance when it is not **more likely than not** (>50%) that some/all of the DTA will be realized
- NOL **carryback|carryforward**: carry back **earliest year first** within the window → Income Tax Refund Receivable; excess loss → DTA; rates of the **prior years** apply to refunds
- Settlement / full utilization: remaining NOL and gross DTA driven to zero
- Classification: DTA from NOL is **noncurrent** (ASC 740); disclose tax effect of carryforwards and amounts/expiration

### Q1 — CORE — Northstar Optics: multi-year NOL subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of NOL DTA; multi-year subsequent measurement / utilization schedule; period-end utilization and VA adjusting JEs; full settlement of remaining NOL/DTA; IS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northstar Optics Inc.** has no temporary differences other than those created by tax loss carryforwards. Pretax GAAP income (loss) equals taxable income (loss) each year. The enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. Accounting and tax years end December 31. NOL carryforwards may offset a **maximum of 80%** of taxable income in each future year and may be carried forward indefinitely. **No carryback** is allowed for Northstar under applicable law.

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Notes |
|------|----------------------:|-------|
| 1 (current) | **$(96,000)** | Actual NOL |
| 2 | 25,000 | Management estimate at 12/31/Y1; **actual** TI in Y2 also **$25,000** |
| 3 | 20,000 | Estimate at 12/31/Y1; **actual** TI in Y3 is **$30,000** |
| 4 | 45,000 | Estimate at 12/31/Y1; **actual** TI in Y4 is **$80,000** |
| 5 | 55,000 | Estimate at 12/31/Y1 |

**Realization evidence:**  
- At December 31, Year 1, it is **more likely than not** that the **full** deferred tax asset will be realized (VA beginning balance = $0).  
- At December 31, Year 2, after recording utilization, management concludes it is MLTN that **only 70%** of the **remaining gross DTA** will be realized.  
- At December 31, Year 3, revise the valuation allowance so that VA equals **20% of ending gross DTA** (80% expected to be realized).  
- At December 31, Year 4, remaining NOL is fully used; reverse any remaining valuation allowance.

**Required:**  
(a) Measure the **deferred tax asset** at December 31, Year 1 related to the NOL carryforward. Record the **initial recognition** income tax JE.  
(b) Show the **Year 1 partial income statement** presentation of the NOL carryforward benefit.  
(c) Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** of expected NOL utilization (80% limit) based on Year 1 estimates, and the related expected DTA roll-forward by year. Confirm cumulative utilization equals the $96,000 carryforward. *(Emphasis angle.)*  
(d) Record the Year 2 **period-end tax JE** using **actual** taxable income of $25,000 (utilization + taxes payable). Then record the **valuation allowance** adjusting JE at December 31, Year 2.  
(e) Record the Year 3 tax JE for **actual** TI of $30,000. Update the valuation allowance so VA = 20% of ending gross DTA.  
(f) Record the Year 4 tax JE for **actual** TI of $80,000 that **settles** the remaining NOL/DTA. Reverse any remaining valuation allowance. State ending gross DTA, VA, and remaining NOL.  
(g) **Classification:** Is the DTA from the NOL carryforward current or noncurrent under ASC 740?

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition — December 31, Year 1**

Deferred tax asset = \(96{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$24{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Asset | 24,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 24,000 |
| *Recognize full NOL carryforward DTA; credit reported as Income Tax Benefit* | | |

**Check:** Dr 24,000 = Cr 24,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Year 1 partial income statement**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(96,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 24,000 |
| Net loss | **$(72,000)** |

**(c) Expected NOL utilization schedule (from 12/31/Y1 estimates) and DTA roll-forward — emphasis**

| Future year | Est. TI | Max NOL use (80% of TI) | NOL used | Remaining NOL | Ending DTA (×25%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Start (end Y1) | — | — | — | 96,000 | 24,000 |
| Y2 | 25,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | 76,000 | 19,000 |
| Y3 | 20,000 | 16,000 | 16,000 | 60,000 | 15,000 |
| Y4 | 45,000 | 36,000 | 36,000 | 24,000 | 6,000 |
| Y5 | 55,000 | 44,000 | 24,000* | 0 | 0 |
| **Total used** | | | **96,000** | | |

\*Utilization cannot exceed remaining NOL even when 80% of TI is larger.  
Cumulative NOL used = \(20{,}000 + 16{,}000 + 36{,}000 + 24{,}000 = \mathbf{\$96{,}000}\) (matches carryforward).

**Schedule math checks:**  
- Y2 DTA = \(76{,}000 \times 25\% = 19{,}000\); reduction from start = \(24{,}000 - 19{,}000 = 5{,}000\).  
- Y3 DTA = \(60{,}000 \times 25\% = 15{,}000\).  
- Y4 DTA = \(24{,}000 \times 25\% = 6{,}000\).  
- Y5 settles remaining \(24{,}000\) NOL → DTA **$0**.

**(d) Year 2 actual utilization + valuation allowance**

NOL used = \(25{,}000 \times 80\% = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\).  
Remaining NOL = \(96{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$76{,}000}\).  
Ending gross DTA = \(76{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$19{,}000}\).  
DTA reduction = \(24{,}000 - 19{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\).  
Income tax payable = \(25{,}000 \times 20\% \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$1{,}250}\).  
Income tax expense (before VA) = \(5{,}000 + 1{,}250 = \mathbf{\$6{,}250}\).

December 31, Year 2 — record income tax expense:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 6,250 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 1,250 |
| *Utilize 80% of TI against NOL; tax payable on residual 20%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,250 = Cr 5,000 + 1,250. **Balanced.**

Valuation allowance needed = \(19{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$5{,}700}\) (30% of remaining DTA not expected to be realized).

December 31, Year 2 — adjust valuation allowance:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 5,700 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,700 |
| *Establish VA for 30% of ending gross DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,700 = Cr 5,700. **Balanced.**  
Net DTA reported = \(19{,}000 - 5{,}700 = \mathbf{\$13{,}300}\). Combined tax expense Y2 = \(6{,}250 + 5{,}700 = \mathbf{\$11{,}950}\).

**(e) Year 3 actual TI $30,000**

Max NOL use = \(30{,}000 \times 80\% = 24{,}000\); remaining NOL was $76,000 → use **$24,000**.  
Remaining NOL = \(76{,}000 - 24{,}000 = \mathbf{\$52{,}000}\).  
Ending gross DTA = \(52{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$13{,}000}\).  
DTA reduction = \(19{,}000 - 13{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).  
Income tax payable = \((30{,}000 - 24{,}000) \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$1{,}500}\).  
Tax expense (before VA adj.) = \(6{,}000 + 1{,}500 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 7,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 1,500 |
| *Utilize NOL up to 80% of actual TI; reduce DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,500 = Cr 6,000 + 1,500. **Balanced.**

Target VA = \(13{,}000 \times 20\% = \mathbf{\$2{,}600}\). Beginning VA $5,700 → **reduce VA by $3,100**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 3,100 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 3,100 |
| *Revise VA to 20% of ending gross DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,100 = Cr 3,100. **Balanced.**  
Net DTA = \(13{,}000 - 2{,}600 = \mathbf{\$10{,}400}\).

**Actual subsequent measurement rollforward (Y1–Y3 actual path):**

| Year-end | NOL used this year | Remaining NOL | Gross DTA | VA | Net DTA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Y1 | — | 96,000 | 24,000 | 0 | 24,000 |
| Y2 | 20,000 | 76,000 | 19,000 | 5,700 | 13,300 |
| Y3 | 24,000 | 52,000 | 13,000 | 2,600 | 10,400 |

**(f) Year 4 settlement of remaining NOL**

Max use = \(80{,}000 \times 80\% = 64{,}000\); remaining NOL only **$52,000** → use all **$52,000** (full settlement).  
Remaining NOL = **$0**; ending gross DTA = **$0**.  
DTA reduction = **$13,000**.  
Taxable income after NOL = \(80{,}000 - 52{,}000 = 28{,}000\); tax payable = \(28{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\).  
Tax expense (before VA reverse) = \(13{,}000 + 7{,}000 = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 20,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 13,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 7,000 |
| *Fully utilize remaining NOL; settle gross DTA to zero* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 13,000 + 7,000. **Balanced.**

Reverse remaining VA of **$2,600**:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 2,600 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 2,600 |
| *Clear VA when related DTA is settled* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,600 = Cr 2,600. **Balanced.**  
Ending: gross DTA **$0**, VA **$0**, NOL carryforward **fully utilized**.

**(g) Classification**  
Deferred tax assets (including those from NOL carryforwards) are classified as **noncurrent** under ASC 740.

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** drives every later-year entry: recompute **max NOL use** (80% of TI, not to exceed remaining NOL), reduce the DTA by remaining-NOL × rate, and still pay tax on residual TI. A valuation allowance is a **separate** period-end judgment that rolls with evidence and is cleared when the related DTA is settled.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Glacier Bay Outfitters: NOL carryforward twin

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Copperline Railworks: NOL carryback|carryforward, refund, multi-rate, settlement
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Two-year carryback (earliest year first) with different historical rates; residual carryforward DTA at current/future rate; refund receivable; IS presentation; VA alternate; later full utilization settlement; classification/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperline Railworks Inc.** began operations in Year 1. There are no temporary differences other than those created by tax loss carryforwards/carrybacks. Copperline **qualifies for a two-year loss carryback** (starting with the **earliest** year in the window first) and an **indefinite** carryforward subject to the **80%** of taxable income limit. Management believes any carryforward DTA is **more likely than not** to be realized unless an alternate fact is stated.

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Tax rate |
|------|----------------------:|---------:|
| 1 | $55,000 | 25% |
| 2 | 70,000 | 25% |
| 3 | 25,000 | 30% |
| 4 (current) | **$(150,000)** | 30% |
| 5 | 100,000 actual | 30% |

**Required:**  
(a) Compute the **tax refund receivable** for Year 4 and the **deferred tax asset** for the residual NOL carryforward (use the **rates of the years to which loss is carried back** for the refund; use the **Year 4/future rate of 30%** for the carryforward DTA).  
(b) Record the **single compound JE** for the Year 4 NOL carryback|carryforward effect.  
(c) Show the Year 4 **partial income statement**.  
(d) **Alternate evidence:** If instead it is MLTN that the **entire** carryforward DTA will **not** be realized, record the VA adjusting entry and show revised IS presentation (carryback benefit still recognized).  
(e) **Settlement (Year 5):** Using the original assumption in (b) (no VA), actual Year 5 TI is $100,000. Record the tax JE that utilizes the remaining NOL (settle DTA as appropriate). State remaining NOL and DTA after the entry.  
(f) **Classification/disclosure:** Is the DTA from the NOL carryforward classified current or noncurrent? Name two disclosure items typically required for operating loss carryforwards.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Measurement**

Two-year carryback window for Year 4 NOL = **Year 2 and Year 3** (Year 1 is outside the window).  
Carry back to **Year 2 first** (earlier year): \(70{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$17{,}500}\) refund.  
Then Year 3: \(25{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\) refund.  
**Tax refund receivable** = \(17{,}500 + 7{,}500 = \mathbf{\$25{,}000}\).  
Loss absorbed by carryback = \(70{,}000 + 25{,}000 = \mathbf{\$95{,}000}\).  
NOL carryforward remaining = \(150{,}000 - 95{,}000 = \mathbf{\$55{,}000}\).  
**Deferred tax asset** = \(55{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$16{,}500}\).

**(b) December 31, Year 4 — initial recognition (carryback + carryforward)**

Total income tax benefit = \(25{,}000 + 16{,}500 = \mathbf{\$41{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Refund Receivable | 25,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 16,500 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 41,500 |
| *Refund for 2-year carryback (earliest first) + DTA for residual NOL* | | |

**Check:** Dr 25,000 + 16,500 = Cr 41,500. **Balanced.**

**(c) Year 4 partial income statement (full realization of DTA)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(150,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 41,500 |
| Net loss | **$(108,500)** |

**(d) Full valuation allowance on carryforward DTA only**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 16,500 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 16,500 |

**Check:** Dr 16,500 = Cr 16,500. **Balanced.**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(150,000) |
| Income tax benefit ($41,500 − $16,500) | 25,000 |
| Net loss | **$(125,000)** |

(Only the **refund** benefit remains when the carryforward DTA is fully reserved; the receivable is not subject to a valuation allowance.)

**(e) Year 5 settlement / utilization of remaining NOL**

Max NOL use = \(100{,}000 \times 80\% = 80{,}000\); remaining NOL = $55,000 → use all **$55,000**.  
Remaining NOL after Y5 = **$0**; DTA reduction = **$16,500** (settles DTA to zero).  
Income after NOL offset = \(100{,}000 - 55{,}000 = 45{,}000\).  
Income tax payable = \(45{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$13{,}500}\).  
Income tax expense = \(16{,}500 + 13{,}500 = \mathbf{\$30{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 30,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 16,500 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 13,500 |
| *Full utilization of residual carryforward; settle DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 30,000 = Cr 16,500 + 13,500. **Balanced.**  
Ending: NOL carryforward **$0**, gross DTA **$0**.

**(f) Classification and disclosure**

- Deferred tax assets (including those from NOL carryforwards) are classified as **noncurrent** under ASC 740 (all deferred tax assets/liabilities presented as noncurrent).  
- Typical disclosures: (1) **approximate tax effect** of each major source of temporary difference and carryforward; (2) **amount and expiration dates** (if any) of operating loss carryforwards; also valuation allowance rollforward information as applicable.

**Key insight:** When carryback is allowed, **first** secure the refund at the **prior years’ rates** (earliest year in the window first) as a **receivable**, then capitalize only the **excess** loss as a DTA at the **future** enacted rate. A VA can wipe out only the uncertain **future** piece, not the already-refundable carryback. Later full use of residual NOL **settles** the DTA.

### Q4 — MC — Carryback order, 80% limit, and presentation
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Carryback sequencing (earliest year first); residual taxes payable under 80% NOL limit; labeling of tax benefit  

**Question 1:**  
A company has a Year 4 NOL and qualifies for a **two-year** loss carryback, using the earliest year first. Taxable income was Year 1 $40,000, Year 2 $50,000, and Year 3 $10,000. Which years’ income is offset by the Year 4 carryback, and in what order?

- A) Year 3, then Year 2, then Year 1 (most recent first, then all prior years)  
- B) Year 2, then Year 3 only (earliest year in the two-year window first; Year 1 is outside the window)  
- C) Year 1, then Year 2 only (earliest years of operations first, regardless of window)  
- D) Year 3 only (carryback limited to one prior year under ASC 740)

**Answer:** **B.** The two-year window for a Year 4 loss is **Years 2 and 3**. Losses are carried back **starting with the earliest year first** (Year 2, then Year 3). Year 1 is outside the window.

**Question 2:**  
In a profitable year, a company has taxable income of $50,000 before NOL and a large NOL carryforward (enough to cover more than 80% of TI). Enacted rate is 25%. Ignoring valuation allowances and other temporary differences, income tax **payable** for the year is:

- A) $0, because the NOL fully offsets taxable income  
- B) $12,500 ($50,000 × 25%), because NOL carryforwards do not affect current taxes payable  
- C) **$2,500** ($50,000 × 20% residual × 25%), because carryforwards may offset only 80% of taxable income  
- D) $10,000 ($50,000 × 80% × 25%), the tax on the offset portion

**Answer:** **C.** Under the default **80% limitation**, only $40,000 of NOL may offset TI; tax is still paid on the residual **$10,000** × 25% = **$2,500**. Income tax expense also includes the reduction of the DTA for the $40,000 used, but the **payable** is only on the non-offset slice.
