# Agent 397 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-3

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-OCI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase AFS debt at cost (par path); debit Investment in AFS Securities / credit Cash
- **Interest in net income:** coupon interest recognized in **net income** (not OCI) for AFS debt
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortized cost, fair value, required FVA, existing FVA, increase/(decrease) to FVA across period-ends **and sale date**
- **Period-end adjusting JE (FV-OCI):** Dr/Cr Fair Value Adjustment—AFS with offset **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI** (not NI)
- **Disposal / sale JE (emphasis):** (1) adjust sold security to fair value through **OCI** at sale date; (2) record cash, remove investment at amortized cost, eliminate associated FVA, **reclassify** cumulative holding G/L from AOCI into **net income** as realized G/L
- **AOCI reclassification disclosure:** beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclassification of realized G/L into earnings, ending AOCI (= remaining FVA)
- **Classification / presentation:** BS at fair value (AC ± FVA); unrealized in AOCI until sale; interest and realized G/L in NI
- **Portfolio disposal:** partial sale—sale-date FV-OCI for sold security only; year-end FVA for securities still held
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO 14-3 skill path with all facts and amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — AFS full life cycle with sale-date FV-OCI adjust, AOCI reclass, and FVA elimination (emphasis: disposal)
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Disposal of AFS debt under FV-OCI—sale-date fair value adjustment through OCI, reclassification of holding gain into net income, and elimination of FVA; full purchase → period-end → sale path  
**Scenario:**  
On **July 1 of Year 1**, **Cedarpoint Holdings LLC** purchased for **par $90,000** face of **Falcon Ridge Industries 7%** bonds that mature on **June 30 of Year 6**. Cash interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Cedarpoint classifies the investment as **available-for-sale (AFS)** and measures it under **FV-OCI** (fair value adjustments at **period-end and at sale date**). Cedarpoint’s annual reporting period ends **December 31**. Beginning FVA—AFS and AOCI related to these bonds are **zero**. Cedarpoint holds **no other** AFS debt during Years 1–3.

**Additional facts:**
| Date | Event / fair value |
|---|---|
| December 31, Year 1 | Fair value of Falcon Ridge bonds = **$86,400** |
| Year 2 | No purchases or sales of AFS |
| December 31, Year 2 | Fair value = **$92,700** |
| January 1, Year 3 | Sells entire Falcon Ridge holding for **$93,600** cash (sale on interest date; no accrued-interest complexity) |

**Required:**  
a. Record the **July 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Record the **December 31, Year 1 cash interest JE**.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / FVA schedule** for Dec 31 Y1, Dec 31 Y2, and Jan 1 Y3 sale date. Columns: amortized cost, fair value (or sale price), required FVA (debit = positive; credit = negative), existing FVA before adj., increase/(decrease) to FVA, FVA after adj.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JEs** (FV-OCI).  
e. Show December 31, Year 1 **balance sheet** carrying amount and AOCI, and list Year 1 **interest revenue** and the **OCI unrealized holding** amount.  
f. **[Emphasis — disposal]** On January 1 of Year 3: (i) adjust the investment to fair value at the sale date using **FV-OCI**; (ii) record the sale, including reclassification of the holding gain from AOCI into net income and elimination of the associated FVA.  
g. Prepare the Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation** (beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclassification to NI, ending AOCI).  
h. Reconcile the **total realized gain** in Year 3 NI to (proceeds − amortized cost) and to the sum of all OCI amounts over the holding period (including the reclassification).

**Answer key:**  

**a. July 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (purchase at par)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Falcon Ridge Bonds | 90,000 | |
| Cash | | 90,000 |
| *Purchase Falcon Ridge 7% bonds at par; classify AFS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 90,000 = Cr 90,000. **Balanced.** Amortized cost basis = **$90,000**.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Interest revenue in net income**  
Cash interest = \(90{,}000 \times 0.07 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$3{,}150}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,150 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 3,150 |
| *Semiannual interest on Falcon Ridge AFS bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,150 = Cr 3,150. **Balanced.** Interest is recognized in **net income** for AFS debt.

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — FVA rollforward**

Sign convention: **positive FVA = debit** (unrealized holding gain); **negative FVA = credit** (unrealized holding loss).

| Measurement date | Amortized cost | Fair value / sale price | Required FVA | Existing FVA (before adj.) | Increase/(decrease) to FVA | FVA after adj. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | 90,000 | 86,400 | (3,600) | 0 | **(3,600)** | (3,600) |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 90,000 | 92,700 | 2,700 | (3,600) | **6,300** | 2,700 |
| Jan 1, Year 3 (sale) | 90,000 | 93,600 | 3,600 | 2,700 | **900** | 3,600 |

**Schedule math checks:**  
- Y1 required FVA = 86,400 − 90,000 = **(3,600)**; existing 0 → decrease FVA (credit FVA) **3,600**.  
- Y2 required FVA = 92,700 − 90,000 = **2,700 debit**; existing **(3,600)** → increase = 2,700 − (−3,600) = **6,300**.  
- Sale-date required FVA = 93,600 − 90,000 = **3,600 debit**; existing **2,700** → increase = **900**.  
- After sale, FVA is eliminated (part f); ending FVA for Falcon Ridge = **0**.

**d. Period-end adjusting JEs (FV-OCI)**

*December 31, Year 1:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 3,600 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 3,600 |
| *Adjust AFS to FV; unrealized holding loss through OCI (not NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,600 = Cr 3,600. **Balanced.** BS carrying amount = 90,000 − 3,600 = **$86,400**.

*December 31, Year 2:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 6,300 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 6,300 |
| *True-up FVA from credit $3,600 to debit $2,700* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,300 = Cr 6,300. **Balanced.** FVA after adj. = **$2,700 debit**; carrying amount = 90,000 + 2,700 = **$92,700**.

**e. Year 1 financial statement presentation**

**Balance sheet (December 31, Year 1):**  
- Investment in available-for-sale securities …… **$86,400**  
- Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) …… **$(3,600)**

**Income statement (year ended December 31, Year 1):**  
- Interest revenue …… **$3,150**  
- Unrealized AFS holding loss is **not** in net income.

**Statement of comprehensive income (excerpt):**  
- Unrealized holding loss on available-for-sale securities …… **$(3,600)**

**f. January 1, Year 3 — Disposal (emphasis): sale-date FV-OCI + reclass + FVA elimination**

*Step 1 — Adjust investment to fair value at sale date (FV-OCI):*  
Required FVA at sale\* = \(93{,}600 - 90{,}000 = \$3{,}600\) debit  
Less existing FVA = \(2{,}700\) debit  
**Increase to FVA needed** = **$900**

\*$93,600 fair value − $90,000 amortized cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 900 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 900 |
| *Sale-date FV-OCI adjust before recording the sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr 900 = Cr 900. **Balanced.** FVA now **$3,600 debit**; carrying amount = **$93,600**.

*Step 2 — Record sale: reclassify holding gain from AOCI to NI; eliminate investment and FVA:*  
Realized gain = \(93{,}600 - 90{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3{,}600}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 93,600 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 3,600 | |
| Investment in AFS—Falcon Ridge Bonds | | 90,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 3,600 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 3,600 |
| *Cash proceeds; reclass cumulative holding gain into NI; clear AC and FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(93{,}600 + 3{,}600 = 97{,}200\) = Cr \(90{,}000 + 3{,}600 + 3{,}600 = 97{,}200\). **Balanced.**

**g. Year 3 AOCI reclassification reconciliation**

| AOCI rollforward | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, January 1, Year 3 | $2,700 |
| Current period unrealized gain on AFS (sale-date adjust) | 900 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (3,600) |
| Net OCI for Year 3 | (2,700) |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, December 31, Year 3 | **$0** |

**Holding-period OCI summary (net effect in AOCI = $0 after sale):**  
- Loss recognized in Year 1 OCI …… $(3,600)  
- Gain recognized in Year 2 OCI …… 6,300  
- Gain recognized in Year 3 OCI (sale-date) …… 900  
- Reclassification adjustment to Year 3 NI …… (3,600)  
- **Net effect in AOCI** …… **$0**

**h. Realized gain reconciliation**  
Gain in Year 3 NI = **$3,600** = proceeds $93,600 − amortized cost $90,000.  
Sum of OCI before reclass over life = −3,600 + 6,300 + 900 = **+3,600** = amount reclassified into NI. ✓

**Key insight (disposal emphasis):** Under **LO 14-3**, the sale is **two steps**: (1) bring the security to exit fair value through **OCI** using the same FVA worksheet as period-end; (2) convert to cash, eliminate AC and FVA, and **reclassify** the cumulative unrealized amount out of AOCI into **realized gain/loss in net income**. Only at sale (or impairment, later LO) does the economic G/L hit earnings.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — AFS life cycle with disposal reclass (all new numbers)

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Portfolio disposal of one AFS security (sale-date FV-OCI) + year-end for remaining
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Disposal of one security from an AFS portfolio—sale-date FV-OCI adjust and reclass for the sold security only; subsequent measurement schedule for remaining security at year-end; AOCI disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **December 31 of Year 2**, **Harborline Partners** holds the following **AFS debt** portfolio (both bonds originally purchased at par; no premium/discount amortization remaining):

| AFS Investment | Amortized cost | Fair value Dec 31 Y2 | Unrealized holding G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Pine Corp.** bonds | $40,000 | $37,500 | $(2,500)$ |
| **Maple Industries** bonds | 75,000 | 82,000 | 7,000 |
| **Portfolio total** | **$115,000** | **$119,500** | **$4,500** |

The **$4,500** net unrealized holding gain is the December 31, Year 2 balance in **AOCI** and in **Fair Value Adjustment—AFS**. Harborline’s year-end is December 31.

**Year 3 activity:**  
- On **January 15 of Year 3**, Harborline sells its **entire Pine Corp.** holding for **$39,200** cash.  
- No other AFS purchases or sales occur in Year 3.  
- On **December 31 of Year 3**, Maple Industries bonds have a fair value of **$79,800**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **sale-date FVA worksheet for Pine only** (required FVA at sale, existing FVA for Pine, increase/(decrease) needed). Record the **sale-date FV-OCI adjusting JE**.  
b. Record the **January 15, Year 3 sale** of Pine (reclassification of realized G/L into NI and elimination of Pine’s associated FVA).  
c. After the sale, state the **remaining FVA balance** (Maple only).  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 subsequent measurement schedule** for Maple and record the **year-end FV-OCI adjusting JE**.  
e. Prepare the December 31, Year 3 **AOCI reclassification disclosure** (beg AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclass for amount in NI, ending AOCI).  
f. Briefly classify: if management intends and is able to sell Maple within one year, is the investment **current** or **noncurrent**? Where is the unrealized holding amount reported until sale?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Sale-date FVA worksheet — Pine only**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Fair value (sale price) | $39,200 |
| Amortized cost | 40,000 |
| **Required FVA at sale date** | **$(800)** credit |
| Existing FVA—Pine (from Dec 31 Y2) | (2,500) |
| **Increase/(decrease) to FVA needed** | **$1,700** |

\(39{,}200 - 40{,}000 = (800)\); increase = \((800) - (-2{,}500) = 1{,}700\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 1,700 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 1,700 |
| *Bring Pine to exit FV through OCI before sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,700 = Cr 1,700. **Balanced.**  
Portfolio FVA after adjust: \(4{,}500 + 1{,}700 = \mathbf{\$6{,}200}\) = Pine \((800)\) + Maple \(7{,}000\).

**b. January 15, Year 3 — Sale of Pine (realized loss $800)**  
Realized loss = \(39{,}200 - 40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$800}\).  
Pine’s associated FVA after step a is a **credit of $800** (eliminated by debiting FVA).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 39,200 | |
| Loss on Sale of Investment | 800 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 800 | |
| Investment in AFS—Pine Corp. Bonds | | 40,000 |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 800 |
| *Reclass cumulative holding loss into NI; eliminate AC and Pine FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(39{,}200 + 800 + 800 = 40{,}800\) = Cr \(40{,}000 + 800 = 40{,}800\). **Balanced.**

**c. Remaining FVA after Pine sale**  
FVA after sale = \(6{,}200 + 800\ \text{(debit to clear credit FVA)} = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\) debit — equals Maple’s Dec 31 Y2 unrealized gain still in FVA/AOCI for securities held.

**d. December 31, Year 3 — Maple subsequent measurement**

| Maple bonds | Amortized cost | Fair value YE | Required FVA | Existing FVA | Increase/(decrease) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Still held | 75,000 | 79,800 | 4,800 | 7,000 | **(2,200)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 2,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 2,200 |
| *Year-end FV-OCI true-up for Maple remaining* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,200 = Cr 2,200. **Balanced.** Ending FVA = **$4,800**; BS carrying amount of Maple = **$79,800**.

**e. Year 3 AOCI reclassification disclosure**

| AOCI rollforward | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, January 1, Year 3 | $4,500 |
| Current period unrealized G/(L) on AFS: Pine sale-date +$1,700; Maple YE $(2,200) | (500) |
| Reclassification adjustment for **loss** included in net income | 800 |
| Net change in AOCI for Year 3 | 300 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, December 31, Year 3 | **$4,800** |

**Tie-out:** Ending AOCI **$4,800** = remaining Maple required FVA. ✓  
**NI Year 3 (this portfolio):** Loss on sale of Pine **$800** (interest omitted as not given).  
**OCI Year 3 components:** unrealized net \((500)\) + reclass of loss \(+800\) = net OCI **+$300**.

**f. Classification / presentation**  
- If management intends and is able to sell Maple within one year (or operating cycle, if longer), classify as a **current** asset; otherwise **noncurrent**.  
- Carrying amount on the BS is **fair value**. Unrealized holding gains/losses remain in **AOCI (equity)** until sale (or certain impairments), when they are reclassified into **net income**. Interest revenue is in **net income** throughout.

**Key insight:** Portfolio disposals under LO 14-3 isolate the sold security’s FVA for the sale-date adjust and reclass; the residual FVA/AOCI equals the unrealized amount on securities **still held**, which is then true-up again at the next period-end.

### Q4 — MC — Classification / presentation of AFS FV changes
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Classification_presentation_or_disclosure — where unrealized holding gains/losses on AFS debt securities are reported before sale  
**Question:**  
Under U.S. GAAP, **unrealized holding gains and losses** on **available-for-sale debt securities** measured at FV-OCI (before sale or credit-loss impairment) are reported:

- A) In net income as part of other revenues and expenses each period  
- B) Only when the security is sold; no fair value adjustments are recorded while held  
- C) In other comprehensive income, with the cumulative amount in accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI) until reclassified into earnings on sale  
- D) As a direct adjustment to retained earnings, bypassing comprehensive income entirely  

**Answer:** **C.**  
AFS debt is measured at fair value on the balance sheet, but unrealized holding G/L go to **OCI/AOCI**, not net income, until realized on sale (when a reclassification adjustment moves the amount into earnings). Option A describes **trading / FV-NI**. Option B describes neither category correctly (HTM ignores most FV changes; AFS does adjust). Option D is incorrect presentation.

### Q5 — MC — Disposal sequence under LO 14-3
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE — correct sale-date sequence for AFS measured with FV adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Question:**  
On the date an investor sells an AFS debt security that has been measured under the **LO 14-3** approach (FV-OCI adjustments at period-end **and** at sale date), which sequence is correct?

- A) Record cash and a realized G/L equal to cash minus original cost; leave any FVA balance on the books until year-end  
- B) First adjust the investment to fair value through **OCI**; then record the sale, reclassify the cumulative holding G/L from AOCI into **net income**, and eliminate the associated FVA  
- C) First adjust the investment to fair value through **net income**; then eliminate the investment and FVA with **no** separate realized G/L account  
- D) Reverse all prior OCI amounts to retained earnings and record the sale at amortized cost with no gain or loss  

**Answer:** **B.**  
LO 14-3 requires a **sale-date FV-OCI** true-up, then sale with **reclassification** of AOCI into realized G/L in NI and elimination of FVA. Option A is incomplete (FVA and reclass). Option C is the **FV-NI / trading (LO 14-2)** path. Option D is not GAAP for AFS.
