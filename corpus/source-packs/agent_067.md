# Agent 67 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-2

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Determine costs to capitalize for lump-sum purchases of property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Lump-sum (basket) purchase: allocate a single negotiated price to individual PPE assets
- **Proportional method:** allocate using relative fair values (appraised values, tax assessments, market prices of similar assets)
- **Incremental method:** assign known fair values first; residual of the lump-sum price goes to the asset with indeterminable FV
- Directly attributable costs (e.g., land title/appraisal, equipment freight/install) assigned **in full** only to the related asset before or after proportional allocation of the remaining lump-sum price
- Subsequent measurement: each allocated cost base depreciates separately (land is not depreciated)
- Period-end adjusting entries for depreciation on basket-acquired assets
- Disposal of one asset acquired in a basket purchase (remove cost and related A/D; gain/loss)
- Classification/presentation: land vs depreciable PPE; capitalized cost vs expense

### Q1 — CORE — Proportional basket allocation, direct costs, multi-year depreciation, and disposal
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Initial proportional allocation of lump-sum PPE cost (with direct attribution), subsequent depreciation schedule, period-end AJE, and disposal of one basket asset  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, Redline Fabrication Co. acquires a package of operating assets from a retiring competitor for a single negotiated cash price of **$680,000**. Independently paid the same day:

| Direct cost | Amount | Attribution |
|---|---:|---|
| Land title insurance and survey | $6,000 | Land only |
| Equipment freight and installation | $14,000 | Equipment only |

Independent appraisals (best available fair-value indicators) at acquisition:

| Asset | Appraised fair value |
|---|---:|
| Land | $240,000 |
| Building | $400,000 |
| Equipment | $160,000 |
| **Total appraised FV** | **$800,000** |

Depreciation policy (straight-line; half-year conventions **not** used — full year in Year 1 because assets are ready January 1):

| Asset | Useful life | Residual value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | n/a (not depreciated) | n/a |
| Building | 20 years | $40,000 |
| Equipment | 5 years | $10,000 |

On **December 31, Year 3**, after recording Year 3 depreciation, Redline sells **all** of the equipment for **$85,000** cash. The building and land remain in use.

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the $680,000 lump-sum price under the **proportional method**. Compute final capitalized cost of land, building, and equipment (include direct costs).  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry for the acquisition (all cash).  
c. Compute annual depreciation for building and equipment. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting entry (or entries).  
d. Prepare a **three-year** depreciation / carrying-value schedule for building and equipment (Years 1–3).  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** disposal entry for the equipment sale.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Proportional allocation of $680,000**

| Asset | FV | Ratio | Allocation of $680,000 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 240,000 | 240/800 = **30%** | 0.30 × 680,000 = **204,000** |
| Building | 400,000 | 400/800 = **50%** | 0.50 × 680,000 = **340,000** |
| Equipment | 160,000 | 160/800 = **20%** | 0.20 × 680,000 = **136,000** |
| **Total** | **800,000** | **100%** | **680,000** |

Final capitalized costs (direct attribution):

| Asset | Basket allocation | Direct costs | **Capitalized cost** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 204,000 | 6,000 | **210,000** |
| Building | 340,000 | — | **340,000** |
| Equipment | 136,000 | 14,000 | **150,000** |
| **Total** | **680,000** | **20,000** | **700,000** |

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 210,000 | |
| Building | 340,000 | |
| Equipment | 150,000 | |
| Cash | | 700,000 |
| *To record lump-sum PPE purchase allocated on relative fair values; direct costs assigned to related assets* | | |

**Check:** Dr 210,000 + 340,000 + 150,000 = 700,000 = Cr. Balanced.

**c. Annual depreciation and Year 1 period-end AJE**

Building: \((340{,}000 - 40{,}000) / 20 = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\) per year  
Equipment: \((150{,}000 - 10{,}000) / 5 = \mathbf{\$28{,}000}\) per year  
Land: **$0** (not depreciated)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 15,000 |
| *Year 1 building depreciation* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 28,000 |
| *Year 1 equipment depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Each entry balances. Combined depreciation expense Year 1 = **$43,000**.

**d. Three-year schedule (building and equipment)**

| Year | Asset | Beg. cost | Beg. A/D | Depreciation | End. A/D | End. CV |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Building | 340,000 | 0 | 15,000 | 15,000 | 325,000 |
| 1 | Equipment | 150,000 | 0 | 28,000 | 28,000 | 122,000 |
| 2 | Building | 340,000 | 15,000 | 15,000 | 30,000 | 310,000 |
| 2 | Equipment | 150,000 | 28,000 | 28,000 | 56,000 | 94,000 |
| 3 | Building | 340,000 | 30,000 | 15,000 | 45,000 | 295,000 |
| 3 | Equipment | 150,000 | 56,000 | 28,000 | 84,000 | 66,000 |

Land remains at **$210,000** cost (and CV) throughout.

**e. December 31, Year 3 — disposal of equipment (after Y3 depreciation)**

Equipment CV = 150,000 − 84,000 = **$66,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$85,000**  
Gain on disposal = 85,000 − 66,000 = **$19,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 85,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 84,000 | |
| Equipment | | 150,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 19,000 |
| *To record sale of equipment acquired in Year 1 basket purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr 85,000 + 84,000 = 169,000; Cr 150,000 + 19,000 = 169,000. Balanced.

**Key insight:** In a basket purchase, the **single** cash price is split using **relative fair values**; costs that relate only to one asset (title insurance, install) are **not** allocated—they go entirely to that asset. After allocation, each asset’s own cost base drives subsequent depreciation and disposal accounting. Land’s allocated cost is never depreciated.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Proportional allocation with tax-assessment indicators
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — proportional lump-sum allocation using assessed values; direct costs; initial JE and first-year depreciation AJE  
**Scenario:**  
On **March 1, Year 1**, HarborPoint Logistics LLC buys a closed distribution site for a lump-sum cash price of **$450,000**. The package includes land, a warehouse building, and a fleet of forklifts. The most recent property-tax assessments (accepted as relative fair-value indicators) are:

| Asset | Assessed value |
|---|---:|
| Land | $180,000 |
| Warehouse | $270,000 |
| Forklifts | $90,000 |
| **Total** | **$540,000** |

Separately paid in cash on the acquisition date:

- County transfer and recording fees related **only to land**: **$3,000**
- Forklift safety inspection and calibration (required before use): **$5,000**

HarborPoint places all assets in service on March 1. Fiscal year ends **December 31**. Depreciation (straight-line; residual values zero):

| Asset | Useful life | Residual |
|---|---|---:|
| Land | not depreciated | n/a |
| Warehouse | 15 years | $0 |
| Forklifts | 5 years | $0 |

Company policy: take a **full year** of depreciation in the year of acquisition for PPE placed in service on or before March 31 (simplifying assumption for this problem).

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the $450,000 lump-sum price proportionally; compute final capitalized costs.  
b. Prepare the acquisition journal entry.  
c. Compute Year 1 depreciation and prepare the December 31, Year 1 adjusting entry (entries).  
d. State the carrying amounts of land, warehouse, and forklifts at December 31, Year 1.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Proportional allocation**

| Asset | Assessed FV | Ratio | Allocation of $450,000 | Direct | **Capitalized** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 180,000 | 180/540 = 1/3 | 150,000 | 3,000 | **153,000** |
| Warehouse | 270,000 | 270/540 = 1/2 | 225,000 | — | **225,000** |
| Forklifts | 90,000 | 90/540 = 1/6 | 75,000 | 5,000 | **80,000** |
| **Total** | **540,000** | **100%** | **450,000** | **8,000** | **458,000** |

**b. Acquisition JE (March 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 153,000 | |
| Building—Warehouse | 225,000 | |
| Equipment—Forklifts | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 458,000 |
| *Lump-sum purchase allocated on relative assessed values; land fees and forklift calibration capitalized to related assets* | | |

**Check:** 153,000 + 225,000 + 80,000 = 458,000. Balanced.

**c. Year 1 depreciation**

Warehouse: \(225{,}000 / 15 = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\)  
Forklifts: \(80{,}000 / 5 = \mathbf{\$16{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Warehouse | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Warehouse | | 15,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Forklifts | 16,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Forklifts | | 16,000 |

**Check:** Each JE balances. Total dep. expense Year 1 = **$31,000**.

**d. Carrying amounts at December 31, Year 1**

| Asset | Cost | A/D | **CV** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 153,000 | 0 | **153,000** |
| Warehouse | 225,000 | 15,000 | **210,000** |
| Forklifts | 80,000 | 16,000 | **64,000** |

**Key insight:** Relative fair-value indicators need not be identical to the purchase price (here assessments total $540,000 vs. cash $450,000). Ratios of those indicators still govern allocation of the **actual** lump-sum cost. Direct costs never get spread across unrelated assets.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Incremental method, subsequent measurement, classification
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Incremental method when one asset’s FV is indeterminable; period-end depreciation; classification of nondepreciable vs depreciable basket assets  
**Scenario:**  
On **July 1, Year 1**, Summit Chem Labs Inc. pays **$500,000** cash for a single package of assets:

| Asset | Fair value indicator |
|---|---|
| Land | Appraised at **$175,000** |
| Building (lab shell) | Appraised at **$280,000** |
| Specialized process equipment | **Indeterminable** — one-of-a-kind custom fabrication; no reliable market or appraisal |

No additional direct costs. Summit’s year-end is December 31. Depreciation policy:

- Land: not depreciated  
- Building: 25-year life, residual **$30,000**, straight-line  
- Specialized equipment: 8-year life, residual **$5,000**, straight-line  
- **Half-year convention** for Year 1 (assets acquired July 1): take **½ year** of annual depreciation in Year 1

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the $500,000 lump-sum price using the **incremental method**. Prepare the July 1 acquisition JE.  
b. Compute Year 1 depreciation (half-year) for building and equipment; prepare the December 31, Year 1 adjusting entries.  
c. Prepare a brief carrying-value schedule at December 31, Year 1 for all three assets.  
d. **Classification / presentation:** On the Year 1 balance sheet, how should the three assets be presented (or grouped)? Which amount is **nondepreciable** PPE?  
e. On **January 2, Year 4**, after three full calendar years of depreciation have been recorded under the half-year convention for Year 1 and full years for Years 2–3 (i.e., **2.5 years** of annual depreciation accumulated through Dec 31, Year 3), Summit scraps the specialized equipment with **no salvage recovery**. Prepare the disposal entry on January 2, Year 4 (no Year 4 depreciation yet).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Incremental allocation and acquisition JE**

| Asset | Capitalized cost | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Land | **175,000** | Known FV assigned in full |
| Building | **280,000** | Known FV assigned in full |
| Specialized equipment | **45,000** | Residual: \(500{,}000 - 175{,}000 - 280{,}000\) |
| **Total** | **500,000** | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 175,000 | |
| Building | 280,000 | |
| Equipment—Specialized | 45,000 | |
| Cash | | 500,000 |
| *Incremental method: known FVs to land and building; remainder to specialized equipment* | | |

**Check:** 175,000 + 280,000 + 45,000 = 500,000. Balanced.

**b. Annual depreciation and Year 1 half-year AJE**

Building annual: \((280{,}000 - 30{,}000) / 25 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\)  
Year 1 building: \(10{,}000 \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\)

Equipment annual: \((45{,}000 - 5{,}000) / 8 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\)  
Year 1 equipment: \(5{,}000 \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$2{,}500}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 5,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 5,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 2,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 2,500 |

**Check:** Each balances. Total Year 1 dep. expense = **$7,500**.

**c. Carrying values at December 31, Year 1**

| Asset | Cost | A/D | **CV** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 175,000 | 0 | **175,000** |
| Building | 280,000 | 5,000 | **275,000** |
| Specialized equipment | 45,000 | 2,500 | **42,500** |

**d. Classification / presentation**

- **Land** — noncurrent asset, typically separate line “Land” (or within PPE with disclosure that land is not depreciated). **Nondepreciable cost = $175,000**.  
- **Building** and **Equipment** — PPE, reported at cost less accumulated depreciation (gross cost and A/D presented or netted with parenthetical/footnote detail per company policy).  
- Do **not** expense the residual $45,000 merely because FV was hard to measure; under the incremental method it is still **capitalized** PPE cost.  
- Income statement Year 1: depreciation expense **$7,500** (not the acquisition cash outflow).

**e. Disposal of specialized equipment January 2, Year 4**

Accumulated depreciation through Dec 31, Year 3 = 2.5 × annual = \(2.5 \times 5{,}000 = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\)  
CV at disposal = 45,000 − 12,500 = **$32,500**  
No proceeds → **loss $32,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 12,500 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 32,500 | |
| Equipment—Specialized | | 45,000 |
| *Write off specialized equipment scrapped with no residual recovery* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,500 + 32,500 = 45,000 = Cr. Balanced.

**Key insight:** When only some assets in a basket have reliable FVs, use the **incremental method**: book known FVs first; the **remainder of the lump-sum price** becomes the cost of the asset with unknown FV. That residual cost is still depreciated (if the asset is depreciable) and later disposed of like any other PPE.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method choice)
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Relative fair-value allocation vs recording at individual appraisal totals; when incremental method applies  

**Question 1:**  
Cedar Ridge Realty acquires land and a small commercial building in one cash transaction for **$360,000**. Independent appraisals: land **$250,000**, building **$150,000**. No other costs. The correct initial recording is:

- A) Land $250,000; Building $150,000; Cash $400,000  
- B) Land $250,000; Building $110,000; Cash $360,000  
- C) Land $225,000; Building $135,000; Cash $360,000  
- D) Land $180,000; Building $180,000; Cash $360,000  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Total FV = 250,000 + 150,000 = **400,000**.  
Land: \(250/400 \times 360{,}000 = \mathbf{\$225{,}000}\).  
Building: \(150/400 \times 360{,}000 = \mathbf{\$135{,}000}\).  
Assets are **not** recorded at raw appraisal amounts when the negotiated lump-sum differs (A overstates assets). Equal split (D) ignores relative FVs. B incorrectly assigns the entire discount to the building only (that would be incremental only if land’s FV were known and building’s FV were **indeterminable**—here both are known, so use proportional).

**Question 2:**  
Which statement about lump-sum (basket) PPE purchases is **correct**?

- A) Directly attributable land appraisal costs should be allocated to all assets in the basket using relative fair values.  
- B) If fair values of all assets in the group are determinable, the incremental method is preferred over the proportional method.  
- C) Under the proportional method, each asset’s capitalized cost equals its individual fair value regardless of the cash paid.  
- D) If only the land’s fair value is reliably determinable and other assets in the package lack reliable FVs, land is recorded at its FV and the remaining purchase price is allocated to the other assets (incremental approach).  

**Answer:** **D.**  
That is the textbook **incremental method**. A is wrong: costs directly attributable to one asset (e.g., land appraisal) are assigned **only** to that asset. B is wrong: when all FVs are known, use **proportional**. C is wrong: proportional scales the **actual lump-sum cost** by relative FV ratios; individual FVs are weights, not automatic book values.
