# Agent 368 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-1

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe and report key components of stockholders’ equity  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Five key components of stockholders’ equity:** paid-in capital (capital stock + APIC), retained earnings, accumulated OCI (AOCI), treasury stock (contra equity), noncontrolling interest
- **Initial recognition JE:** issuance of common and preferred stock (par to capital stock; excess to APIC)
- **Share terminology:** authorized vs issued vs outstanding; treasury reduces outstanding but not issued
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** statement of stockholders’ equity roll-forward (beginning → activity → ending) for each major equity component
- **Period-end adjusting/closing JEs (emphasis):** close net income to retained earnings; close current OCI into AOCI; cash dividends reduce RE; year-end equity presentation after closes
- **Disposal / reacquisition JE:** treasury stock purchase (cost method) and reissuance (above or below cost)
- **Classification / presentation:** equity section of the balance sheet with par, authorized, issued, and outstanding disclosures; APIC aggregated; AOCI and treasury deducted when debit balances; NCI presented separately

### Q1 — CORE — Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis), midyear issuances, treasury cycle, SOE & equity section

**Concept:** Period-end adjusting/closing JEs for RE and AOCI (emphasis); initial recognition of CS/PS; treasury reacquisition and partial reissuance; subsequent SOE schedule and equity-section presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Helix Harbor Systems Inc.** is a calendar-year corporation. Selected **January 1, 2026** equity balances (all credit unless noted):

| Account | Balance |
|---|---:|
| Common stock, $3 par, 300,000 shares authorized, 50,000 issued | $150,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common | 600,000 |
| Preferred stock, 6%, $25 par, 40,000 shares authorized, 8,000 issued and outstanding | 200,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—preferred | 48,000 |
| Retained earnings | 275,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) | 22,000 Dr. |
| Treasury stock | 0 |

**2026 transactions:**

1. **February 10 — Initial recognition:** Issued **15,000** additional common shares for cash at **$22** per share.  
2. **May 5 — Initial recognition:** Issued **2,000** additional preferred shares for cash at **$30** per share.  
3. **Year 2026:** Net income **$168,000** (all temporary accounts still open until year-end close).  
4. **December 12 — Period-end:** Declared and paid cash dividends equal to the full **annual preferred preference** on shares outstanding at the declaration date **plus** common dividends of **$45,000**.  
5. **December 18 — Period-end OCI:** Other comprehensive **income** (unrealized holding gain on AFS debt securities, **net of tax**) **$14,000**. No other OCI.  
6. **December 20 — Reacquisition:** Purchased **2,500** common shares as treasury stock at **$21** per share (cost method).  
7. **December 29 — Partial settlement/reissuance:** Reissued **750** of the treasury shares for cash at **$24** per share.  
8. No noncontrolling interest.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **February 10** and **May 5** initial recognition journal entries.  
b. Prepare the **December 12 period-end** cash dividend journal entry (compute preferred preference).  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting/closing entries** to (i) close net income into retained earnings and (ii) close current OCI into AOCI.  
d. Prepare the **December 20 treasury purchase** JE and the **December 29 treasury reissuance** JE.  
e. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (statement of stockholders’ equity) for 2026 with beginning, changes, and ending balances for: Preferred stock, Common stock, APIC (combined), Retained earnings, AOCI, Treasury stock, and Total equity.  
f. Prepare the **stockholders’ equity section** of the December 31, 2026 balance sheet with par, authorized, issued, and outstanding share disclosures.  
g. Identify which of the **five key equity components** appear at year-end.

**Beginning total check:** 200,000 + 150,000 + 648,000 + 275,000 − 22,000 = **1,251,000**.

| Component | Present? | Amount / note |
|---|---|---|

| 2. Retained earnings | Yes | $383,000 |
| 3. Accumulated OCI | Yes | $(8,000) accumulated loss |
| 4. Treasury stock | Yes | $(36,750) contra equity |

**Key insight:** At **period-end**, net income is closed into **retained earnings** and current OCI is closed into **AOCI**—these adjusting/closing entries update two of the five equity components without new owner contributions. Dividends reduce RE. Paid-in capital arises only at **initial recognition** of stock issues. Treasury is a **contra-equity** deduction that lowers outstanding (not issued) shares. The SOE schedule rolls every component; the balance sheet equity section reports ending balances with authorized / issued / outstanding disclosures.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin pack (all numbers changed; period-end emphasis retained)

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end RE/AOCI closes from trial data; classify five components; equity section with NCI

**Concept:** Period-end adjusting entries for RE and AOCI from open balances; classification into the five equity components; prepare complete equity section including noncontrolling interest  
**Scenario:**  
**Vesper Lattice Group** consolidates a subsidiary. The following **December 31, 2026** data are available **before** period-end equity closes for NI and OCI (capital stock and treasury already final):

| Account / item | Amount |

**Required:**

### Q4 — MC (classification / method fit)
**LO:** LO 19-1  
**Concept:** Classification of treasury stock within the five key components of stockholders’ equity  

**Question:** Under U.S. GAAP presentation of the stockholders’ equity section, how is **treasury stock** (cost method) classified among the key equity components?

- A) As an asset (investment in the company’s own shares)  
- B) As a component of paid-in capital that increases total equity  
- C) As a **contra-equity** deduction that reduces total stockholders’ equity and outstanding shares  
- D) As a reduction of retained earnings only, with no separate treasury stock line  

**Answer:** **C.** Treasury stock is one of the five key equity components: a **contra equity** account deducted to arrive at total stockholders’ equity. The purchase reduces outstanding shares but not issued shares. It is not an asset, and it is not merely buried in paid-in capital as an increase.

### Q4b — MC
**LO:** LO 19-1  
**Concept:** Computing outstanding shares for equity-section disclosure and EPS  

**Question:** **Pike Meadow Corp.** has 80,000 common shares **authorized**, 50,000 shares **issued**, and holds 4,000 shares as **treasury stock**. How many common shares are **outstanding**, and which count is used in basic EPS?

- A) Outstanding = 80,000; EPS uses authorized shares  
- B) Outstanding = 50,000; EPS uses issued shares  
- C) Outstanding = 46,000; EPS uses outstanding shares  
- D) Outstanding = 54,000; EPS uses authorized minus treasury  

**Answer:** **C.** Outstanding = issued − treasury = 50,000 − 4,000 = **46,000**. Basic EPS uses **weighted-average outstanding** shares (not authorized or issued). Authorized (80,000) is a charter ceiling disclosed on the face or in notes; issued remains 50,000 until retirement.
