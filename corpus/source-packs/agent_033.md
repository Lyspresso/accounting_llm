# Agent 33 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-1

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Identify operating, investing, and financing activities, and the statement of cash flows format  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Classify cash inflows/outflows as **operating**, **investing**, or **financing** (ASC 230)
- Record underlying cash transactions (stock issue, debt issue/retire, PPE/intangible disposal, treasury stock, equipment purchase)
- **Gross** presentation of investing and financing inflows and outflows
- SCF **format**: operating → investing → financing; reconcile beginning to ending cash (cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash)
- Edge classifications: trade-related short-term notes (operating); interest paid (operating); dividends paid (financing); nontrade loans (investing)
- Cash rollforward / change-in-cash schedule from section subtotals
- Noncash investing/financing **disclosure** (not on face of SCF)

### Q1 — CORE — Classify cash flows, record initial recognition JEs, cash rollforward, SCF format
**LO:** LO 22-1  
**Concept:** Operating / investing / financing classification; initial recognition of cash events; reconcile change in cash  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Co.** had the following **cash** transactions during the year ended **December 31, 2025**. Beginning cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash on January 1, 2025 was **$25,000**. The company uses US GAAP (ASC 230).

| # | Cash transaction | Cash amount |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Issued 4,000 shares of $5-par preferred stock for cash at $30 per share | $120,000 |
| 2 | Paid cash to retire long-term bonds early (carrying amount of bonds retired: $78,000) | 80,000 |
| 3 | Collected cash from customers for sale of merchandise | 400,000 |
| 4 | Made a **nontrade** loan (cash advanced) to an affiliate | 50,000 |
| 5 | Sold a patent for cash (carrying amount of patent: $22,000) | 30,000 |
| 6 | Purchased equity securities **not** held for trading (available for long-term investment) | 45,000 |
| 7 | Issued bonds payable at **par** for cash | 200,000 |
| 8 | Purchased treasury stock for cash | 35,000 |
| 9 | Sold land for cash (land cost / carrying amount: $55,000) | 70,000 |
| 10 | Issued a short-term **trade** note payable related to inventory / operating financing (cash proceeds) | 20,000 |
| 11 | Declared and **paid** cash dividends to preferred shareholders | 25,000 |
| 12 | Paid cash operating expenses (salaries, rent, utilities, etc.) | 280,000 |
| 13 | Purchased equipment for cash | 90,000 |
| 14 | Collected **principal** on a nontrade note receivable | 12,000 |
| 15 | Paid **interest** on bonds payable | 16,000 |

No other cash transactions occurred. Equity securities were acquired for investment (not trading). The short-term note in item 10 is related to operating/inventory activities (not nontrade financing).

**Required:**  
(a) For each item **1–15**, state whether the cash effect is an **inflow** or **outflow** and classify it as **operating (O)**, **investing (I)**, or **financing (F)**.  
(b) Prepare **balanced journal entries** for items **1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, and 13** (initial recognition / settlement of those cash events).  
(c) Prepare a **cash rollforward schedule**: compute net cash from operating, investing, and financing activities; net change in cash; and prove ending cash.  
(d) Present the **statement of cash flows format** (sections and subtotals only—list each cash line by description and amount; show reconciliation of beginning to ending cash). State what “cash” includes under ASC 230.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Classification**

| # | Description | In / Out | Section | Reason (brief) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issue preferred stock | Inflow | **F** | Resources from owners |
| 2 | Early extinguishment of LT debt | Outflow | **F** | Principal settlement with creditors |
| 3 | Collections from customers | Inflow | **O** | Primary revenue cash effect |
| 4 | Nontrade loan to affiliate | Outflow | **I** | Making loans (nontrade) |
| 5 | Sale of patent | Inflow | **I** | Disposal of productive intangible |
| 6 | Purchase equity securities (not trading) | Outflow | **I** | Acquiring investments not held for trading |
| 7 | Issue bonds at par | Inflow | **F** | Borrowing from creditors |
| 8 | Purchase treasury stock | Outflow | **F** | Return of capital to owners |
| 9 | Sale of land | Inflow | **I** | Disposal of PPE |
| 10 | ST trade note (ops) proceeds | Inflow | **O** | Trade/operating financing (not nontrade debt) |
| 11 | Cash dividends paid | Outflow | **F** | Distribution to owners |
| 12 | Pay operating expenses | Outflow | **O** | Cash effects entering NI determination |
| 13 | Purchase equipment | Outflow | **I** | Acquire PPE |
| 14 | Collect nontrade principal | Inflow | **I** | Collecting loans (interest would be O) |
| 15 | Pay bond interest | Outflow | **O** | Interest paid is operating under US GAAP |

**(b) Journal entries (all balance)**

**1 — Issue preferred stock (4,000 × $5 par = $20,000; APIC = $100,000)**  
```
Dr Cash ........................................ 120,000
   Cr Preferred Stock ...................................... 20,000
   Cr APIC—Preferred Stock ................................ 100,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 120,000)

**2 — Early extinguishment of bonds (CV $78,000; cash paid $80,000)**  
```
Dr Bonds Payable ................................ 78,000
Dr Loss on Early Extinguishment of Debt .......... 2,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 80,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 80,000)  
*SCF note:* Entire **$80,000 cash** is financing outflow; the $2,000 loss affects NI (later adjusted in indirect operating section—LO 22-2).

**5 — Sale of patent**  
```
Dr Cash ......................................... 30,000
   Cr Patent ............................................... 22,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Patent ................................ 8,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 30,000)  
*SCF:* **$30,000** investing inflow (gross proceeds).

**7 — Issue bonds at par**  
```
Dr Cash ........................................ 200,000
   Cr Bonds Payable ....................................... 200,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 200,000)

**8 — Purchase treasury stock**  
```
Dr Treasury Stock ............................... 35,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 35,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 35,000)

**9 — Sale of land**  
```
Dr Cash ......................................... 70,000
   Cr Land ................................................ 55,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Land ................................. 15,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 70,000)  
*SCF:* **$70,000** investing inflow.

**13 — Purchase equipment**  
```
Dr Equipment .................................... 90,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 90,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 90,000)

**(c) Cash rollforward schedule**

| Section | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| **Operating inflows** | Collections $400,000 + ST trade note $20,000 | 420,000 |
| **Operating outflows** | Op. expenses $280,000 + Interest $16,000 | (296,000) |
| **Net cash provided by operating activities** | | **124,000** |
| **Investing inflows** | Patent $30,000 + Land $70,000 + Nontrade principal $12,000 | 112,000 |
| **Investing outflows** | Nontrade loan $50,000 + Equity securities $45,000 + Equipment $90,000 | (185,000) |
| **Net cash used by investing activities** | | **(73,000)** |
| **Financing inflows** | Preferred stock $120,000 + Bonds $200,000 | 320,000 |
| **Financing outflows** | Debt retire $80,000 + Treasury stock $35,000 + Dividends $25,000 | (140,000) |
| **Net cash provided by financing activities** | | **180,000** |
| **Net increase in cash** | 124,000 − 73,000 + 180,000 | **231,000** |
| Cash, Jan 1, 2025 | | 25,000 |
| **Cash, Dec 31, 2025** | 25,000 + 231,000 | **256,000** |

**(d) Statement of cash flows format (Meridian Forge Co. — year ended Dec 31, 2025)**

```
Cash flows from operating activities
  Cash collected from customers ..........................  400,000
  Proceeds from short-term trade note payable .............   20,000
  Cash paid for operating expenses ........................ (280,000)
  Cash paid for interest ..................................  (16,000)
  Net cash provided by operating activities ...............           124,000

Cash flows from investing activities
  Proceeds from sale of patent ............................   30,000
  Proceeds from sale of land ..............................   70,000
  Collection of nontrade note receivable—principal ........   12,000
  Loan to affiliate (nontrade) ............................  (50,000)
  Purchase of equity securities ...........................  (45,000)
  Purchase of equipment ...................................  (90,000)
  Net cash used by investing activities ...................           (73,000)

Cash flows from financing activities
  Proceeds from issuance of preferred stock ...............  120,000
  Proceeds from issuance of bonds .........................  200,000
  Payment to retire long-term bonds .......................  (80,000)
  Purchase of treasury stock ..............................  (35,000)
  Cash dividends paid .....................................  (25,000)
  Net cash provided by financing activities ...............           180,000

Net increase in cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash          231,000
Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, beginning ..............  25,000
Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, ending ................. 256,000
```

**“Cash” on the SCF** includes **cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash** (and restricted cash equivalents). Investing and financing **inflows and outflows are reported separately** (gross), not netted.

**Key insight:** Classification follows the **nature** of the cash flow (ops vs long-term asset strategy vs capital structure), not merely whether the item touches net income. Interest **paid** is operating; principal on nontrade debt/loans is financing/investing; owner distributions and treasury stock are financing. The three section totals must reconcile beginning cash to ending cash.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Classify, JEs, cash rollforward twin

**Concept:** Operating / investing / financing classification and cash reconciliation (all numbers changed)  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Ridge Industries** had the following cash transactions for the year ended **December 31, 2026**. Beginning cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash = **$41,500**.

| # | Cash transaction | Cash amount |
|---|---|---:|

| 4 | Made a nontrade loan to a supplier (cash advanced) | 36,000 |
| 5 | Sold equipment for cash (carrying amount of equipment sold: $48,000) | 42,000 |
| 6 | Purchased debt securities held for **long-term investment** (not trading) | 68,000 |
| 7 | Issued bonds at par for cash | 250,000 |
| 8 | Purchased treasury stock | 55,000 |

| 12 | Paid cash operating expenses | 360,000 |

| 14 | Collected principal on nontrade note receivable | 20,000 |
| 15 | Paid interest on bonds | 21,000 |

**Required:**  
(a) Classify each item 1–16 (inflow/outflow and O / I / F).  
(b) Journal entries for items **1, 2, 5, 7, 9, and 13**.  
(c) Cash rollforward: net O, I, F; change in cash; ending cash.

### Q3 — CORE — Disposal/settlement JEs, edge classifications, noncash disclosure, reverse cash reconciliation
**LO:** LO 22-1  
**Concept:** Disposal and debt-settlement cash classification; interest vs principal; noncash investing/financing disclosure; solve for beginning cash  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Packaging Ltd.** reports the following for the year ended **December 31, 2027**:

**Selected cash and noncash events:**

1. Sold machinery for **$58,000** cash. Original cost $95,000; accumulated depreciation at sale **$42,000**.  
2. On maturity date, paid **$100,000** cash to settle bonds payable at face (no gain/loss).  
3. During the year, paid **$7,500** cash interest on those bonds (before maturity).  
4. Purchased a delivery truck for **$40,000** cash.  
5. Acquired land by issuing a **$75,000** long-term note payable (no cash exchanged).  
6. Borrowed **$60,000** cash on a long-term bank note (nontrade).  
7. Paid **$15,000** cash principal on a finance lease liability.  
8. Paid **$9,000** cash dividends.  
9. Cash collections from customers **$310,000**; cash paid to suppliers and employees **$255,000**; cash paid for income taxes **$18,000**.  
10. Sold short-term **trading** equity securities (held specifically for resale / trading profits) for **$22,000** cash (cost basis $20,000; $2,000 gain already in NI).

**Additional data:**  
Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash at **December 31, 2027** = **$87,200**.  
Net cash from operating, investing, and financing is **not** given—compute each section from the events above.

**Required:**  
(a) Journal entries for items **1** (disposal), **2** (maturity settlement), **4** (PPE purchase), and **6** (debt issuance).  
(b) Classify the **cash** effect of items 1–4 and 6–10 as O / I / F (inflow or outflow). State how item **5** is reported.  
(c) Compute net cash from **operating**, **investing**, and **financing**; net change in cash; solve for **beginning** cash.  
(d) Present SCF section order and the three-line cash reconciliation (change + beginning = ending).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Journal entries**

**1 — Disposal of machinery**  
Book value = \(95{,}000 - 42{,}000 = 53{,}000\); gain = \(58{,}000 - 53{,}000 = 5{,}000\).  
```
Dr Cash ......................................... 58,000
Dr Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery ........... 42,000
   Cr Machinery ............................................ 95,000
   Cr Gain on Sale of Machinery ............................. 5,000
```
(Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000)

**2 — Maturity settlement of bonds**  
```
Dr Bonds Payable ............................... 100,000
   Cr Cash ............................................... 100,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 100,000)

**4 — Purchase delivery truck**  
```
Dr Delivery Truck / Equipment ................... 40,000
   Cr Cash ................................................ 40,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 40,000)

**6 — Issue long-term bank note**  
```
Dr Cash ......................................... 60,000
   Cr Notes Payable—Long-term .............................. 60,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 60,000)

**(b) Cash classification**

| Item | Cash effect | Class |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Sale of machinery | Inflow $58,000 | **Investing** |
| 2 Bond principal at maturity | Outflow $100,000 | **Financing** |
| 3 Interest paid | Outflow $7,500 | **Operating** |
| 4 Purchase truck | Outflow $40,000 | **Investing** |
| 5 Land for LT note | **No cash** | Disclose as **noncash investing and financing** activity (supplemental) |
| 6 LT bank borrowing | Inflow $60,000 | **Financing** |
| 7 Finance lease principal paid | Outflow $15,000 | **Financing** |
| 8 Dividends paid | Outflow $9,000 | **Financing** |
| 9 Customer collections | Inflow $310,000 | **Operating** |
| 9 Suppliers/employees | Outflow $255,000 | **Operating** |
| 9 Income taxes paid | Outflow $18,000 | **Operating** |
| 10 Sale of **trading** securities | Inflow $22,000 | **Operating** (trading / held for resale) |

**(c) Section nets and beginning cash**

**Operating:**  
Inflows: customer collections $310,000 + trading securities sale $22,000 = **$332,000**  
Outflows: suppliers/employees $255,000 + income taxes $18,000 + interest $7,500 = **$280,500**  
Net cash provided by operating activities = \(332{,}000 - 280{,}500 = \mathbf{\$51{,}500}\).

**Investing:**  
Inflow: machinery sale $58,000  
Outflow: truck $40,000  
Net cash provided by investing activities = \(58{,}000 - 40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$18{,}000}\).

**Financing:**  
Inflow: bank note $60,000  
Outflows: bond principal $100,000 + lease principal $15,000 + dividends $9,000 = **$124,000**  
Net cash used by financing activities = \(60{,}000 - 124{,}000 = \mathbf{(\$64{,}000)}\).

**Change in cash:**  
\(51{,}500 + 18{,}000 - 64{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}500}\) increase.

**Beginning cash:**  
Ending $87,200 − increase $5,500 = **$81,700**.  
Check: \(81{,}700 + 5{,}500 = 87{,}200\).

**(d) Format / reconciliation**

Order on SCF: **(1) Operating → (2) Investing → (3) Financing**, then:

```
Net increase in cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash .....   5,500
Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, beginning ..........  81,700
Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, ending .............  87,200
```

Item 5 appears in a **noncash investing and financing activities** schedule/note: “Acquired land by issuing long-term note payable, $75,000.”

**Key insight:** Split **interest** (operating) from **principal** (financing). PPE **disposals** and **purchases** are investing on a **gross** basis. Trading securities cash flows are **operating**; noncash asset-for-debt exchanges never appear as cash inflows/outflows on the face of the SCF.

### Q4 — MC — Classification choice (trading vs investment; SCF line)
**LO:** LO 22-1  
**Concept:** Classification of securities cash flows by intent; format of SCF sections  
**Question 4A:**  
Under US GAAP, cash paid to purchase **equity securities that management intends to hold for long-term capital appreciation** (not for short-term trading profits) is classified on the statement of cash flows as:

- A) Operating activity—outflow  
- B) Investing activity—outflow  
- C) Financing activity—outflow  
- D) Noncash investing and financing disclosure only  

**Answer:** **B.** Purchases of debt/equity instruments **not** acquired specifically for trading/resale are **investing** outflows. Trading securities held for short-term gains are classified as **operating**.

**Question 4B:**  
Which presentation is **required** on a complete statement of cash flows?

- A) Net investing cash flows may combine equipment purchases and sales into one net line  
- B) Operating section must always use the direct method  
- C) Totals of operating, investing, and financing activities must reconcile beginning cash (including cash equivalents and restricted cash) to ending cash  
- D) Dividends paid are always classified as operating activities  

**Answer:** **C.** ASC 230 requires the SCF to report net cash from O/I/F and reconcile beginning to ending totals of cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash. Investing/financing **gross** inflows and outflows are reported separately (not netted). Dividends **paid** are **financing** (US GAAP). Operating may be direct or indirect.
