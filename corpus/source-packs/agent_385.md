# Agent 385 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-9

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS given multiple securities and describe EPS financial statement presentation  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Multiple potentially dilutive securities (ASC 260-10-45-18 / Demo 20-9):** rank by **earnings per incremental share** (numerator effect ÷ incremental common shares), **most dilutive first** (lowest ratio); include sequentially until a security is antidilutive
- **Options/warrants first in ranking practice:** treasury-stock method usually has **zero numerator** effect → incremental EPS of **$0.00**
- **If-converted method:** convertible preferred (add back preferred dividends; no tax) and convertible debt (add back **after-tax** interest)
- **Initial recognition JE:** cash issuance of common, convertible preferred, and convertible bonds that establish the capital structure used in EPS
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** ranking table + sequential tentative diluted EPS roll-forward + basic/diluted EPS amounts
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end accrual of convertible bond interest, income tax, cumulative preferred dividend declaration/accrual, and closing of net income—entries that **finalize the EPS numerator** before ranking and presentation
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** conversion of convertible bonds (or exercise of options) after the reporting date—settles the dilutive instrument; does not rewrite prior-period diluted EPS
- **Financial statement presentation:** complex capital structure → **basic and diluted EPS with equal prominence** on the face of the income statement for income from continuing operations and net income; discontinued operations per-share on face **or** in notes; disclose antidilutive securities excluded
- **Control number:** dilutiveness tested against **income from continuing operations** available to common (not net income alone)

### Q1 — CORE — Rank four dilutives; period-end AJs finalize NI; sequential diluted EPS; conversion settlement
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JEs for common, convertible preferred, and two series of convertible bonds; period-end adjusting JEs that finalize interest, tax, and preferred claim (emphasis); ranking by earnings per incremental share; sequential diluted EPS schedule with one antidilutive exclusion; period-end closing; dual EPS presentation; post-period conversion settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironridge Analytics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company with a **complex capital structure**. On **January 1, Year 1**, Ironridge establishes the following capital structure (all instruments outstanding **the entire year**):

| Instrument | Terms |
|---|---|
| Common stock | Issues **120,000** shares of **$2 par** common for **$15** cash per share |
| Convertible preferred | Issues **4,000** shares of **6%, $100 par cumulative** preferred for **$105** cash per share; each share converts into **3** common shares (**12,000** common total) |
| Series B convertible bonds | Issues **$500,000** face of **5%** bonds **at par**; each **$1,000** bond converts into **40** common shares (**20,000** common total) |
| Series A convertible bonds | Issues **$300,000** face of **7%** bonds **at par**; each **$1,000** bond converts into **20** common shares (**6,000** common total) |
| Employee stock options | **3,000** options outstanding all year; exercise price **$10**; Year 1 **average market price $15** |

**Additional facts**

- Income tax rate is **20%**.
- Before **period-end adjusting entries**, Ironridge’s books show **pretax income of $480,000** that **does not yet include** Year 1 interest on Series A and Series B convertible bonds or income tax.
- Cumulative preferred dividends for Year 1 have **not yet** been declared or accrued.
- No share issuances, buybacks, stock dividends, or splits during Year 1.
- On **January 8, Year 2**, all Series B bondholders convert their bonds into common stock (settlement of the convertible).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entries** for (1) common stock, (2) convertible preferred, (3) Series B bonds, and (4) Series A bonds. Show Dr = Cr on each.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting journal entries** for: Series B interest, Series A interest, income tax, and declaration of the full Year 1 cumulative preferred dividend. Show Dr = Cr on each.  
c. Prepare an **income-finalization schedule** ending in Year 1 **net income** and **income available to common stockholders**.  
d. Prepare the **ranking schedule** of potentially dilutive securities by **earnings per incremental share** (most dilutive → least dilutive). Show numerator effect, incremental shares, and the ratio for each.  
e. Prepare the **sequential diluted EPS schedule** (basic row, then each security in rank order, mark dilutive/antidilutive). State final basic and diluted EPS (nearest cent).  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end closing entry** that transfers net income to retained earnings.  
g. Show **Year 1 income-statement presentation** of basic and diluted EPS and identify the antidilutive security disclosed as excluded.  
h. Prepare the **January 8, Year 2 settlement (conversion) journal entry** for Series B bonds. Show Dr = Cr.

|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|

| 3 | Convertible preferred stock | 24,000 | 12,000 | **$2.00** |
| 4 (least) | Series A convertible bonds | 16,800 | 6,000 | **$2.80** |

**e. Sequential diluted EPS schedule**

| Step | Net income available to common | Weighted-avg common shares | Per share | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Basic EPS** | $323,200 | 120,000 | **$2.69** | — |
| Effect of stock options | +0 → 323,200 | +1,000 → 121,000 | $2.67 | **dilutive** |
| Effect of Series B convertibles | +20,000 → 343,200 | +20,000 → 141,000 | $2.43 | **dilutive** |
| Effect of convertible preferred | +24,000 → 367,200 | +12,000 → 153,000 | **$2.40** | **dilutive** |
| Effect of Series A convertibles | +16,800 → 384,000 | +6,000 → 159,000 | $2.42 | **antidilutive** (exclude) |
| **Diluted EPS (final)** | **$367,200** | **153,000** | **$2.40** | — |

Computations:  
Basic EPS = $323,200 ÷ 120,000 = **$2.693333… → $2.69**  
After options: $323,200 ÷ 121,000 = **$2.671074… → $2.67** (tentative)  
After Series B: $343,200 ÷ 141,000 = **$2.434043… → $2.43** (tentative)  
After preferred: $367,200 ÷ 153,000 = **$2.40** exactly (tentative = final diluted)  
After Series A: $384,000 ÷ 159,000 = **$2.415094… → $2.42**, which **exceeds** $2.40 → **antidilutive**; stop.

**Reported:** Basic EPS **$2.69**; Diluted EPS **$2.40**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **347,200**. **Balanced.**

*Note:* EPS is a **presentation** measure derived after finalizing NI and ranking dilutives; there is **no** ledger account for “EPS.”

**g. Financial statement presentation (complex capital structure)**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $347,200 |
| | |
| **Basic earnings per share** | **$2.69** |
| **Diluted earnings per share** | **$2.40** |

Basic and diluted are presented with **equal prominence**.  
**Antidilutive disclosure:** Series A convertible bonds (potential **6,000** common shares; after-tax interest effect **$16,800**) were **excluded** from diluted EPS because inclusion would have been antidilutive.

**h. January 8, Year 2 — settlement / conversion of Series B bonds**

Common shares issued on conversion = **20,000**  
Common stock (par) = 20,000 × $2 = **$40,000**  
APIC = $500,000 − $40,000 = **$460,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable—Series B (5% convertible) | 500,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 40,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 460,000 |
| *Convert Series B bonds into 20,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **500,000**. **Balanced.**

This Year 2 settlement changes Year 2 outstanding shares; it does **not** restate Year 1 diluted EPS (Year 1 already reflected Series B under the if-converted method).

**Key insight:** With multiple dilutives, **order matters**. Rank by **earnings per incremental share** (options typically first at $0) and add securities **only while tentative EPS keeps falling**. Series A looked dilutive in isolation relative to basic $2.69, but was **antidilutive after** more dilutive securities were already included—exactly ASC 260-10-45-18 / Demo 20-9.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Four-security ranking twin with different amounts
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of multi-security ranking and sequential diluted EPS; initial recognition JEs; period-end interest/tax/preferred AJs (emphasis); subsequent measurement ranking schedule; dual presentation; option-exercise settlement JE

**MesaForge Instruments Inc.** (calendar year) establishes the following on **January 1, Year 1** (all outstanding the entire year):

**Required:**

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Continuing / discontinued / net EPS presentation with multi-security ranking
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Period-end ranking and sequential dilution using **income from continuing operations** as the control number; dual basic/diluted presentation for continuing operations, discontinued operations, and net income; period-end preferred dividend AJ and closing JE; warrant settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Media Group** has a complex capital structure. For the year ended **December 31, Year 1**:

| Item | Amount / terms |
|---|---|
| Common shares outstanding all year | **80,000** |
| Income from continuing operations (already after tax and after interest on convertible debt) | **$240,000** |
| Gain from discontinued operations, net of tax | **$60,000** |
| Net income | **$300,000** |
| 5%, $40 par cumulative convertible preferred (outstanding all year) | **5,000** shares; annual dividend claim **$10,000**; converts into **8,000** common |
| 6% convertible debt, face **$200,000** (outstanding all year; interest **already** in continuing income) | After-tax interest add-back for diluted EPS = **$9,000**; converts into **10,000** common |
| Warrants outstanding all year | **4,000** warrants; exercise price **$9**; average market price **$12** → incremental shares = **1,000**; numerator effect **$0** |

No other potential common shares. Preferred dividends for Year 1 are **declared at December 31** (period-end adjusting entry) but unpaid. On **January 20, Year 2**, all warrants are exercised for cash.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** declaring the preferred dividend.  
b. Compute **basic EPS** for (1) continuing operations, (2) discontinued operations, and (3) net income.  
c. Prepare the **ranking schedule** and **sequential diluted EPS schedule using income from continuing operations available to common as the control number**. State diluted EPS for continuing, discontinued, and net.  
d. Show **income-statement presentation** of basic and diluted EPS for all three lines (equal prominence for basic vs diluted).  
e. Prepare the **period-end closing entry** for net income.  
f. Prepare the **January 20, Year 2 warrant exercise settlement JE** (common $1 par).

**Answer key:**

**a. Period-end preferred dividend declaration**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Preferred Dividends) | 10,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred | | 10,000 |
| *Declare Year 1 cumulative preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **10,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Basic EPS (preferred deducted from continuing and from net)**

| Line item | Numerator | Denominator | Basic EPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing operations | $240,000 − $10,000 = **$230,000** | 80,000 | **$2.88** |
| Discontinued operations | **$60,000** | 80,000 | **$0.75** |
| Net income | $300,000 − $10,000 = **$290,000** | 80,000 | **$3.63** |

Checks: $2.875 → **$2.88**; $0.75; $3.625 → **$3.63**.  
$2.88 + $0.75 = $3.63 ✓

**c. Ranking and sequential diluted EPS (control = continuing ops)**

Warrants: proceeds = 4,000 × $9 = $36,000; shares repurchased at $12 = 3,000; incremental = **1,000**.

| Rank | Security | Δ Income | Δ Shares | Earn. per incr. share |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Warrants | $0 | 1,000 | **$0.00** |
| 2 | Convertible debt | 9,000 | 10,000 | **$0.90** |
| 3 | Convertible preferred | 10,000 | 8,000 | **$1.25** |

Sequential test on **continuing operations available to common** ($230,000 / 80,000 = $2.875):

| Step | Cont. num. | Shares | Cont. EPS | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Basic (control) | 230,000 | 80,000 | $2.875 | — |
| + Warrants | 230,000 | 81,000 | $2.840 | dilutive |
| + Convertible debt | 239,000 | 91,000 | $2.626 | dilutive |
| + Convertible preferred | **249,000** | **99,000** | **$2.515** | dilutive |

All three are dilutive under the control number → include **all** in diluted denominators.

| Line item | Diluted numerator | Diluted shares | Diluted EPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing operations | $249,000 | 99,000 | **$2.52** |
| Discontinued operations | $60,000 | 99,000 | **$0.61** |
| Net income | $300,000 − $10,000 + $9,000 + $10,000 = **$309,000** | 99,000 | **$3.12** |

Checks: $249,000 ÷ 99,000 = **$2.515152… → $2.52**; $60,000 ÷ 99,000 = **$0.606061… → $0.61**; $309,000 ÷ 99,000 = **$3.121212… → $3.12**.  
$2.52 + $0.61 = $3.13 (penny rounding); unrounded $2.515152 + $0.606061 = $3.121213 = net ✓

**d. Financial statement presentation**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | Basic | Diluted |
|---|---:|---:|
| Earnings per share — continuing operations | $2.88 | $2.52 |
| Earnings per share — discontinued operations | 0.75 | 0.61 |
| Earnings per share — net income | $3.63 | $3.12 |

| *Close Year 1 net income* | | |

| Cash | 36,000 | |

**Key insight:** Presentation under LO 20-9 is not just one diluted number. ### Q4 — MC — Ranking order for maximum dilution
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Classification of the correct inclusion order for multiple potentially dilutive securities  
**Question:**  
A company has four potentially dilutive securities with the following earnings per incremental share: stock options $0.00; convertible preferred $1.10; Series X convertible bonds $0.75; Series Y convertible bonds $2.05. To compute diluted EPS under ASC 260, in which order should the securities be considered for inclusion?

- A) Convertible preferred, Series X, Series Y, stock options  
- B) Stock options, Series X, convertible preferred, Series Y  
- C) Series Y, convertible preferred, Series X, stock options  
- D) Series X, stock options, convertible preferred, Series Y  

**Answer:** **B.**  
ASC 260-10-45-18 requires inclusion **from most dilutive to least dilutive** (lowest earnings per incremental share first). Options ($0.00) → Series X ($0.75) → preferred ($1.10) → Series Y ($2.05). Options/warrants are typically first because the treasury-stock method adds no numerator.

### Q5 — MC — Antidilutive exclusion after sequential inclusion
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Classification of when a convertible is excluded from diluted EPS even if it appears dilutive versus basic EPS alone  
**Question:**  
Basic EPS is $3.00. After including options and convertible preferred in rank order, tentative diluted EPS is $2.40. Adding Series Z convertible bonds would change the computation to $2.55. Which statement is correct?

- A) Include Series Z because $2.55 is still below basic EPS of $3.00.  
- B) Exclude Series Z because it is antidilutive relative to the current tentative diluted EPS of $2.40.  
- C) Include Series Z only in basic EPS, not diluted EPS.  
- D) Exclude all convertibles whenever options are outstanding.  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Dilutiveness of each next security is tested against the **current tentative diluted EPS**, not solely against basic EPS. If tentative EPS **rises**, the security is **antidilutive** and is excluded (and disclosed as such), even if the result would still be below basic EPS.
