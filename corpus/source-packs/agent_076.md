# Agent 76 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-10

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Record acquisition and depletion of natural resources  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize acquisition, successful exploration, development, and PV of restoration (ARO) into the natural resource (Mine Property / Oil Reserve)
- **Depletion base:** total capitalized natural-resource cost **less residual value** of the property after extraction
- **Units-of-production depletion:** rate = depletion base ÷ estimated recoverable units; annual depletion = rate × units **extracted**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year depletion, accumulated depletion, carrying amount of the natural resource, inventory of extracted units
- **Period-end adjusting JEs:** (1) Dr Inventory / Cr Mine Property (or Accumulated Depletion) for units extracted; (2) Dr COGS / Cr Inventory for units **sold**
- Movable equipment with **alternative uses** is **PPE**, depreciated separately — **not** in the depletion base
- **Disposal / settlement:** remove residual land (gain/loss vs residual carrying amount); settle ARO vs cash (gain/loss)
- **Classification / presentation:** natural resources within PPE (or separate line), gross cost less accumulated depletion; method disclosure (successful-efforts vs full-cost for oil & gas exploration)
- **Successful-efforts vs full-cost** for exploration costs (oil & gas): only successful wells capitalized vs all exploration costs capitalized (if ≤ estimated reserve value)

### Q1 — CORE — Acquisition, depletion base, multi-year schedule, period-end JEs, closeout (emphasis: initial recognition)
**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of natural resource (acquisition + exploration + development + ARO PV); units-of-production depletion schedule; period-end inventory/COGS JEs; equipment separate from depletion base; residual-land disposal and ARO settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperridge Mining LLC**, a calendar-year company, acquires and opens a copper mine on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Cash paid for mineral rights (acquisition) | **$3,600,000** |
| Cash exploration costs (successful — reserves confirmed) | **$400,000** |
| Cash development costs (mineshaft, haul roads; no alternative use off-site) | **$1,000,000** |
| PV of legal restoration / reclamation obligation (ARO) | **$250,000** |
| Estimated residual value of land after full extraction | **$250,000** |
| Estimated recoverable copper ore | **2,000,000 tons** |
| Movable equipment (alternative uses outside this mine) | **$180,000** cash; 9-year SL; **$0** residual |
| Year 1: tons extracted / tons sold | **220,000 / 200,000** |
| Year 2: tons extracted / tons sold | **280,000 / 290,000** |
| Year 3: tons extracted / tons sold | **250,000 / 240,000** |
| Year 4: tons extracted / tons sold | **300,000 / 310,000** |
| Year 5: tons extracted / tons sold | **950,000 / 960,000** (mine fully worked) |

Copperridge credits **Mine Property** directly for depletion (no Accumulated Depletion account). Equipment is depreciated with the full-year convention. Ignore ARO accretion (covered in LO 11-5); the ARO liability remains at its initial PV until settlement. Inventory flow for extracted ore is **FIFO**; the depletion rate is constant until estimates change (none here).

On **December 31, Year 5**, after Year-5 depletion and after all ore inventory is sold:  
- Residual land is sold for **$240,000** cash.  
- The restoration obligation is settled by paying a contractor **$255,000** cash.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **capitalized cost of Mine Property**, the **depletion base**, and the **unit depletion rate**. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entries for (1) the mine (including ARO) and (2) the equipment.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** period-end entries for (1) depletion of ore extracted, (2) cost of goods sold for ore sold, and (3) depreciation of equipment.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–5 showing tons extracted, depletion expense (cost transferred to inventory), accumulated depletion, carrying amount of Mine Property, ending inventory tons, and ending inventory dollars. Confirm total tons extracted = 2,000,000 and residual carrying amount = $250,000 before land sale.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 5 disposal/settlement** entries for (1) sale of residual land and (2) settlement of the ARO.  
e. Show **balance sheet presentation** of Mine Property and related inventory immediately after initial recognition (Jan 1, Year 1) and at December 31, Year 1 (after adjusting entries).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Depletion base, rate, and initial recognition**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Acquisition (mineral rights) | $3,600,000 |
| Exploration (successful) | 400,000 |
| Development | 1,000,000 |
| PV of restoration (ARO) | 250,000 |
| **Capitalized Mine Property** | **$5,250,000** |
| − Residual value of land | (250,000) |
| **Depletion base** | **$5,000,000** |
| ÷ Estimated recoverable tons | ÷ 2,000,000 |
| **Unit depletion rate** | **$2.50 per ton** |

*January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition of mine property (acquisition, exploration, development, ARO)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mine Property | 5,250,000 | |
| Cash ($3,600,000 + $400,000 + $1,000,000) | | 5,000,000 |
| Asset Retirement Obligation | | 250,000 |
| *Capitalize acquisition, exploration, development, and PV of restoration into natural resource* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,250,000 = Cr 5,000,000 + 250,000. **Balanced.**

*January 1, Year 1 — Equipment with alternative uses (PPE, not in depletion base)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—Mining (movable) | 180,000 | |
| Cash | | 180,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180,000.

**b. December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entries**

Year 1 depletion = 220,000 tons × $2.50 = **$550,000**  
Year 1 COGS = 200,000 tons × $2.50 = **$500,000**  
Equipment depreciation = $180,000 ÷ 9 = **$20,000**

*December 31, Year 1 — Depletion of ore extracted*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory—Copper Ore | 550,000 | |
| Mine Property | | 550,000 |
| *Transfer resource cost to inventory for units extracted (UOP)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 550,000.

*December 31, Year 1 — Cost of goods sold for ore sold*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 500,000 | |
| Inventory—Copper Ore | | 500,000 |
| *Depletion becomes COGS when ore is sold* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 500,000.

*December 31, Year 1 — Depreciation of movable equipment*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 20,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 20,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 20,000.

Ending Inventory—Copper Ore, Dec 31, Year 1: 20,000 tons × $2.50 = **$50,000**  
Carrying amount of Mine Property, Dec 31, Year 1: $5,250,000 − $550,000 = **$4,700,000**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule (units-of-production @ $2.50/ton)**

| Year | Tons extracted | Depletion (→ Inventory) | Accum. depletion | Carrying amt of Mine Property | Ending inv. (tons) | Ending inv. ($) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 (1/1/Y1) | — | — | 0 | 5,250,000 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 220,000 | 550,000 | 550,000 | 4,700,000 | 20,000 | 50,000 |
| 2 | 280,000 | 700,000 | 1,250,000 | 4,000,000 | 10,000 | 25,000 |
| 3 | 250,000 | 625,000 | 1,875,000 | 3,375,000 | 20,000 | 50,000 |
| 4 | 300,000 | 750,000 | 2,625,000 | 2,625,000 | 10,000 | 25,000 |
| 5 | 950,000 | 2,375,000 | **5,000,000** | **250,000** | 0 | 0 |
| **Total** | **2,000,000** | **5,000,000** | | | | |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
- Tons: 220 + 280 + 250 + 300 + 950 = **2,000,000**.  
- Depletion total = depletion base = **$5,000,000**.  
- Final Mine Property carrying amount before land sale = residual = **$250,000**.  
- Inventory tons: Y1 20; Y2 20+280−290=10; Y3 10+250−240=20; Y4 20+300−310=10; Y5 10+950−960=0.

COGS by year (sold × $2.50): Y1 $500,000; Y2 $725,000; Y3 $600,000; Y4 $775,000; Y5 $2,400,000.  
Total COGS = 2,000,000 × $2.50 = **$5,000,000** = depletion base (all resource cost flows through inventory to COGS; residual stays in Mine Property until land sale).

**d. December 31, Year 5 — disposal of residual land and settlement of ARO**

(After Year-5 depletion and COGS already recorded; Mine Property BV = $250,000; ARO BV = $250,000.)

*Sale of residual land for $240,000*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 240,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Land (Natural Resource Residual) | 10,000 | |
| Mine Property | | 250,000 |
| *Remove residual carrying amount; cash shortfall → loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 240,000 + 10,000 = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

*Settlement of asset retirement obligation*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Asset Retirement Obligation | 250,000 | |
| Loss on Settlement of ARO | 5,000 | |
| Cash | | 255,000 |
| *Remove ARO; excess cash paid → loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 + 5,000 = Cr 255,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Classification / presentation**

**Immediately after initial recognition (Jan 1, Year 1):**  
- **PPE / Natural resources:** Mine Property **$5,250,000** (or shown gross within oil/gas/mineral properties).  
- **PPE:** Equipment **$180,000**.  
- **Liabilities (noncurrent):** Asset Retirement Obligation **$250,000**.  
- No inventory of ore yet.

**December 31, Year 1 (after adjusting entries):**  
- Mine Property **$4,700,000** ($5,250,000 cost − $550,000 depletion credited directly), or alternatively Cost $5,250,000 less Accumulated Depletion $550,000 if a contra is used.  
- Inventory—Copper Ore (current asset) **$50,000**.  
- Equipment net **$160,000** ($180,000 − $20,000 AD).  
- ARO **$250,000** (still noncurrent if reclamation is years away).  
- Depletion cost of sold tons is in **COGS** on the income statement (not a separate “depletion expense” line when inventory is used); equipment depreciation is operating expense (or product cost if manufacturing).

**Key insight:** At **initial recognition**, all costs that create the right to extract (acquisition, exploration that finds reserves, development, and PV of restoration) are **capitalized** into the natural resource. Residual land value is **excluded from the depletion base**. Each period, UOP moves cost from Mine Property → Inventory (extracted) → COGS (sold). Movable multi-use equipment stays outside the depletion base.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Gravel quarry twin
**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial recognition of quarry cost and ARO; depletion base and rate; multi-year UOP schedule; period-end depletion and COGS JEs; residual land sale  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironwood Aggregates Co.** (calendar year) opens a gravel quarry on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Cash paid for quarry rights (acquisition) | **$4,500,000** |
| Cash development costs | **$600,000** |
| PV of restoration obligation (ARO) | **$150,000** |
| Estimated residual land value after gravel removed | **$300,000** |
| Estimated recoverable gravel | **3,000,000 cubic yards** |
| Year 1: yards extracted / sold | **360,000 / 340,000** |
| Year 2: yards extracted / sold | **420,000 / 430,000** |
| Year 3: yards extracted / sold | **510,000 / 500,000** |

No separate movable equipment. Credits to **Quarry Property** for depletion are direct. ARO remains at initial PV until settlement (ignore accretion). After Year 3 operations, remaining recoverable units and residual land are **not** fully exhausted; **do not** close the quarry in Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Compute capitalized Quarry Property cost, depletion base, and unit rate. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare **Year 1 period-end** depletion and COGS journal entries.  
c. Prepare a **Years 1–3 subsequent measurement schedule** (extraction, depletion, accum. depletion, carrying amount, ending inventory tons/yards and dollars).  
d. Compute COGS for Years 2 and 3.  
e. Briefly state how Quarry Property and gravel inventory are **classified** on the Year-1 year-end balance sheet.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Base, rate, initial recognition**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Acquisition | $4,500,000 |
| Development | 600,000 |
| PV of restoration (ARO) | 150,000 |
| **Capitalized Quarry Property** | **$5,250,000** |
| − Residual land | (300,000) |
| **Depletion base** | **$4,950,000** |
| ÷ Estimated recoverable yards | ÷ 3,000,000 |
| **Unit rate** | **$1.65 per cubic yard** |

*January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Quarry Property | 5,250,000 | |
| Cash ($4,500,000 + $600,000) | | 5,100,000 |
| Asset Retirement Obligation | | 150,000 |

**Check:** Dr 5,250,000 = Cr 5,100,000 + 150,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Year 1 period-end**

Depletion = 360,000 × $1.65 = **$594,000**  
COGS = 340,000 × $1.65 = **$561,000**  
EI = 20,000 × $1.65 = **$33,000**

*Depletion*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory—Gravel | 594,000 | |
| Quarry Property | | 594,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 594,000.

*COGS*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 561,000 | |
| Inventory—Gravel | | 561,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 561,000.

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule @ $1.65/yd**

| Year | Yards extracted | Depletion | Accum. depletion | Carrying amt of Quarry Property | EI (yds) | EI ($) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | — | — | 0 | 5,250,000 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 360,000 | 594,000 | 594,000 | 4,656,000 | 20,000 | 33,000 |
| 2 | 420,000 | 693,000 | 1,287,000 | 3,963,000 | 10,000 | 16,500 |
| 3 | 510,000 | 841,500 | 2,128,500 | 3,121,500 | 20,000 | 33,000 |

**Checks:**  
- Y2 EI: 20,000 + 420,000 − 430,000 = **10,000** yds.  
- Y3 EI: 10,000 + 510,000 − 500,000 = **20,000** yds.  
- Carrying amount Y3: $5,250,000 − $2,128,500 = **$3,121,500** (includes $300,000 residual + undepleted resource cost $2,821,500).

**d. COGS Years 2 and 3**  
- Year 2: 430,000 × $1.65 = **$709,500**  
- Year 3: 500,000 × $1.65 = **$825,000**

**e. Classification (Dec 31, Year 1)**  
- **Quarry Property** (PPE / natural resources), carrying amount **$4,656,000**, noncurrent.  
- **Inventory—Gravel** **$33,000**, current asset.  
- **ARO** **$150,000**, typically noncurrent liability.  
- Method note: units-of-production depletion of mineral property (and, if oil/gas, successful-efforts or full-cost for exploration).

**Key insight:** Same core path as Q1 with all new numbers: capitalize → compute base net of residual → UOP on **extraction** → COGS on **sales** → schedule rolls forward.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Oil & gas exploration methods + depletion under each
**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Successful-efforts vs full-cost initial recognition of exploration costs; subsequent UOP depletion and COGS under each method; classification/disclosure of method  
**Scenario:**  
**Horizon Energy Corp.** drills **8** exploratory wells during Year 1 at a cash cost of **$1,500,000 each** (total exploration outlay **$12,000,000**).  
- **2** wells are successful (recoverable oil discovered).  
- **6** wells are dry (unsuccessful).  
- Estimated fair value of reserves in the successful wells: **$25,000,000** (so full-cost capitalization is supportable).  
- Estimated recoverable reserves from the successful wells: **800,000 barrels**.  
- Year 1 production from successful wells: **80,000 barrels extracted**; **70,000 barrels sold**.  
- For simplicity, the depletion base under each method consists **only** of capitalized exploration costs (no separate acquisition/development/restoration in this problem).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **initial recognition** journal entry for Year-1 exploration under the **successful-efforts** method.  
b. Prepare the **initial recognition** journal entry for Year-1 exploration under the **full-cost** method.  
c. Under **successful-efforts**, compute the unit depletion rate, Year-1 depletion, COGS, and ending oil inventory; prepare the depletion and COGS entries.  
d. Under **full-cost**, compute the unit depletion rate, Year-1 depletion, COGS, and ending oil inventory (entries parallel to part c — amounts only required if not rewritten).  
e. **Classification / disclosure:** Where do oil and gas properties typically appear, and what method disclosure is required?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Successful-efforts — initial recognition**  
Capitalize only successful wells: 2 × $1,500,000 = **$3,000,000**  
Expense dry holes: 6 × $1,500,000 = **$9,000,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Oil Reserve (or Oil and Gas Properties) | 3,000,000 | |
| Exploration Expense (dry holes) | 9,000,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000,000 |
| *Successful-efforts: only successful exploration capitalized* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,000,000 + 9,000,000 = Cr 12,000,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Full-cost — initial recognition**  
All exploration capitalized because $12,000,000 ≤ estimated reserve value $25,000,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Oil Reserve (or Oil and Gas Properties) | 12,000,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000,000 |
| *Full-cost: all exploration costs in depletion base (within reserve value ceiling)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,000,000.

**c. Successful-efforts — depletion and sale**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Depletion base | $3,000,000 |
| ÷ Estimated barrels | ÷ 800,000 |
| **Rate** | **$3.75 per barrel** |
| Depletion (80,000 × $3.75) | **$300,000** |
| COGS (70,000 × $3.75) | **$262,500** |
| Ending inventory (10,000 × $3.75) | **$37,500** |

*Depletion*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory—Crude Oil | 300,000 | |
| Oil Reserve | | 300,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 300,000.

*COGS*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 262,500 | |
| Inventory—Crude Oil | | 262,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 262,500.

Carrying amount of Oil Reserve after Year 1 (SE): $3,000,000 − $300,000 = **$2,700,000**.

**d. Full-cost — depletion and sale**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Depletion base | $12,000,000 |
| ÷ Estimated barrels | ÷ 800,000 |
| **Rate** | **$15.00 per barrel** |
| Depletion (80,000 × $15) | **$1,200,000** |
| COGS (70,000 × $15) | **$1,050,000** |
| Ending inventory (10,000 × $15) | **$150,000** |

(Entries same form as part c with these amounts.)  
Carrying amount of Oil Reserve after Year 1 (FC): $12,000,000 − $1,200,000 = **$10,800,000**.

**e. Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
- Oil and gas properties are typically reported within **Property, plant, and equipment** (gross cost less accumulated DD&A), sometimes with a separate “Oil and Gas Properties” line (see EOG-style presentation).  
- Companies must **disclose** whether they use **successful-efforts** or **full-cost**, treatment of extraction costs, and capitalization of exploratory well costs (ASC 932-235).  
- Successful-efforts yields **higher Year-1 expense** ($9,000,000 exploration expense + higher unit rate is actually lower capitalized base → lower per-unit COGS than full-cost’s large base) and **lower assets**; full-cost capitalizes dry holes into the asset and amortizes them through future depletion/COGS.

**Key insight:** Exploration accounting method is an **initial measurement** choice that permanently changes the depletion base and unit rate. Depletion still follows UOP on units extracted; COGS follows units sold.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Successful-efforts vs full-cost — amount capitalized to the natural resource  
**Question:**  
Driftwood Petroleum drills four exploratory wells at $800,000 cash each. Three are dry; one is successful. Estimated value of reserves discovered is $5,000,000. Under the **successful-efforts** method, what amount is capitalized to Oil and Gas Properties for these wells?

- A) $0  
- B) $800,000  
- C) $2,400,000  
- D) $3,200,000  

**Answer:** **B.** Only the successful well’s $800,000 is capitalized; $2,400,000 of dry-hole costs are expensed as exploration expense. Full-cost would capitalize $3,200,000 (all four wells) because $3,200,000 < $5,000,000 reserve value.  
**Check:** not a JE; method classification.

### Q5 — MC (cost classification in depletion base)
**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Which costs enter the depletion base vs separate PPE  
**Question:**  
On acquisition of a mineral property, which item is **least** likely to be included in the **depletion base** of the natural resource?

- A) Cost of mineral rights (acquisition cost)  
- B) Development cost of a permanent mineshaft with no alternative use  
- C) Present value of legally required site restoration  
- D) Cost of movable haul trucks that will be used on other projects after this mine is exhausted  

**Answer:** **D.** Movable assets with **alternative uses** are recorded as **PPE** and depreciated over their own useful lives; they are not added to the natural-resource depletion base. A–C are classic components of the depletion cost base (acquisition, development without alternative use, restoration PV). Residual value would **reduce** the base but is not asked here.  
**Check:** classification of cost, not a multi-line JE pack.
