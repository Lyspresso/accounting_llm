# Agent 383 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-7

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS using treasury stock method for options, warrants, and restricted stock  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Treasury stock method (options/warrants):** assume exercise at beginning of period (or issuance if later); use assumed proceeds to repurchase shares at the **average market price**; add only **incremental shares** (shares issued − treasury shares assumed purchased) to the diluted EPS denominator
- **Numerator effect:** assumed exercise of options/warrants generally has **no numerator adjustment**
- **Dilutive vs antidilutive:** if average market price **≤** exercise (strike) price, assumed exercise is **antidilutive** (exclude; calculations unnecessary when market is below strike)
- **Unvested restricted stock:** excluded from **basic** EPS; included in **diluted** EPS under the treasury stock method using **unearned compensation** (compensation not yet recognized) as assumed proceeds to repurchase shares at average market price
- **Initial recognition JE:** cash issuance of common (equity base); grant-date recognition of restricted stock awards (Unearned Compensation / Common / APIC); grant-date recognition of fully vested option compensation (Comp Expense / PIC—Stock Options)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** treasury stock method incremental-shares schedule; restricted-stock compensation amortization schedule (total compensation → annual expense → unearned remaining)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end AJs that finalize pretax income and **net income** before EPS is computed; year-end **compensation expense** on restricted stock (reduces unearned compensation used in the treasury stock method)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** cash exercise of stock options (and warrants); completion of restricted-stock vesting (final amortization / shares unrestricted)
- **Presentation:** complex capital structure reports **basic and diluted EPS** when options/warrants/unvested RS are dilutive; report basic only (or basic = diluted) when all such instruments are antidilutive

### Q1 — CORE — Stock options: period-end NI AJs (emphasis), treasury stock method, exercise settlement
**LO:** LO 20-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of fully vested employee stock options; period-end adjusting JEs that finalize net income (emphasis); treasury stock method incremental-shares schedule; basic and diluted EPS; option exercise settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverstone Diagnostics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company. Its only potentially dilutive instruments are fully vested employee stock options (complex capital structure). Common stock has a **$1 par** value. No preferred stock, convertibles, or warrants.

**Facts:**
1. On **January 1, 20X4** (prior year), Riverstone granted fully vested options allowing employees to purchase **12,000** shares at an exercise price of **$20** per share. Grant-date fair value was **$4** per option (**$48,000** total). The entire amount was recognized as compensation cost in 20X4. The options remain outstanding throughout 20X5. Balance in **Paid-in Capital—Stock Options** at January 1, 20X5: **$48,000**.
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding during **20X5** (basic denominator): **300,000**. No share issuances or buybacks during 20X5.
3. Average market price of Riverstone common stock during 20X5: **$30**.
4. Before **December 31, 20X5 period-end adjusting entries**, pretax income (credit balance of temporary income accounts) is **$1,080,000**. The only remaining items needed to finalize 20X5 net income are:

| Adjustment | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Depreciation expense | **$50,000** |
| Accrued warranty expense | **$30,000** |
| Income tax expense (**25%** of pretax income **after** the two adjustments above) | *compute* |

5. On **January 20, 20X6**, employees exercise **all 12,000** options for cash at the $20 exercise price.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, 20X4 initial recognition journal entry** for the fully vested option grant (compensation cost recognized at grant because options are fully vested). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the three **December 31, 20X5 period-end adjusting journal entries** (depreciation, warranty accrual, and income tax). Show Dr = Cr on each entry.  
c. Compute **20X5 net income** after the period-end adjustments. Present an **income-finalization schedule**.  
d. Prepare the **treasury stock method incremental common shares schedule** for the options.  
e. Prepare **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS reconciliation schedules**. State whether the options are **dilutive** or **antidilutive**.  
f. **Classification / presentation:** What EPS amount(s) does Riverstone report on the face of its 20X5 income statement?  
g. Prepare the **January 20, 20X6 settlement journal entry** when all options are exercised. Show Dr = Cr.

**Answer key:**

**a. January 1, 20X4 — initial recognition of fully vested stock options**

Total compensation = \( 12{,}000 \times \$4 = \mathbf{\$48{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 48,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Stock Options | | 48,000 |
| *Recognize grant-date FV of fully vested employee stock options* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **48,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, 20X5 — period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

*(1) Depreciation*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 50,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation | | 50,000 |
| *Record 20X5 depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **50,000**. **Balanced.**

*(2) Accrued warranty*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warranty Expense | 30,000 | |
| Warranty Liability | | 30,000 |
| *Accrue warranty cost at year-end* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **30,000**. **Balanced.**

*(3) Income tax*

Pretax income after adjustments (1) and (2):  
\( \$1{,}080{,}000 - \$50{,}000 - \$30{,}000 = \mathbf{\$1{,}000{,}000} \)  
Income tax expense = \( \$1{,}000{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$250{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 250,000 | |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 250,000 |
| *Accrue income tax at 25% of pretax income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **250,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Income-finalization schedule → 20X5 net income**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Income before year-end adjustments | $1,080,000 |
| Less: Depreciation expense | (50,000) |
| Less: Warranty expense | (30,000) |
| **Pretax income** | **1,000,000** |
| Less: Income tax expense (25%) | (250,000) |
| **Net income** | **$750,000** |

**d. Treasury stock method — incremental common shares schedule (options)**

Assume exercise of all options as of **January 1, 20X5** (options outstanding all year).

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| New shares upon assumed exercise of options | 12,000 |
| Assumed proceeds (\(12{,}000 \times \$20\)) | $240,000 |
| Treasury shares assumed purchased (\( \$240{,}000 \div \$30 \) average market price) | (8,000) |
| **Incremental common shares** | **4,000** |

*Check:* Average market price **$30** > exercise price **$20** → options are **in-the-money**; proceed with treasury stock method.

**e. Basic and diluted EPS schedules**

**Basic EPS**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income available to common stockholders (numerator) | $750,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (denominator) | 300,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$2.50** |

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$750{,}000}{300{,}000} = \mathbf{\$2.50}
\]

**Diluted EPS — treasury stock method reconciliation**

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $750,000 | 300,000 | $2.50 |
| Effect of stock options: | | | |
| Numerator adjustment | *none* | | |
| Add incremental common shares | | 4,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$750,000** | **304,000** | **$2.47** |

Computation:  
\[
\text{Diluted EPS} = \frac{\$750{,}000}{304{,}000} = \$2.467105\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.47}
\]

**Dilutive test:** Diluted EPS **$2.47** < basic EPS **$2.50** → options are **dilutive**. Include in diluted EPS.

**f. Presentation**

Riverstone has a **complex capital structure**. It reports **both**:
- Basic EPS **$2.50**
- Diluted EPS **$2.47**

on the face of the 20X5 income statement.

*Note:* EPS is a **presentation** measure computed from finalized net income and share data; there is no journal entry that “books” EPS itself. Period-end AJs in part (b) are what finalize the numerator.

**g. January 20, 20X6 — option exercise settlement JE**

Cash proceeds = \( 12{,}000 \times \$20 = \mathbf{\$240{,}000} \)  
Common stock (par) = \( 12{,}000 \times \$1 = \mathbf{\$12{,}000} \)  
APIC—Common = \( \$240{,}000 + \$48{,}000 - \$12{,}000 = \mathbf{\$276{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 240,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Stock Options | 48,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 12,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 276,000 |
| *Employees exercise 12,000 options at $20; reclassify PIC—Stock Options* | | |

**Check:** Dr \( 240{,}000 + 48{,}000 = 288{,}000 \); Cr \( 12{,}000 + 276{,}000 = 288{,}000 \). **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Under the **treasury stock method**, diluted EPS does **not** add all option shares—only **incremental** shares after assuming proceeds repurchase stock at the **average market price**. Period-end AJs finalize **net income** (the EPS numerator); option exercise is a later **settlement** that issues real shares and clears PIC—Stock Options.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Options + warrants; antidilutive market-price case
**LO:** LO 20-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant treasury stock method for options and warrants; period-end AJs finalizing NI; combined diluted EPS; antidilutive classification when average market price is below exercise price; warrant exercise settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarpeak Instruments Inc.** is a calendar-year public company with a complex capital structure due to **employee stock options** and **warrants**. Common stock **$2 par**. No convertibles or preferred stock.

**Facts for 20X5:**
1. Weighted-average common shares outstanding (basic): **480,000**.
2. **Employee stock options** outstanding all year: options on **18,000** shares; exercise price **$25**. Options were granted and fully vested in a prior year; PIC—Stock Options balance related to these options: **$72,000** (for settlement reference only).
3. **Warrants** outstanding all year: warrants to purchase **6,000** shares; exercise price **$30**. Warrants were issued for cash in a prior period (equity-classified); no remaining unearned compensation.

| Adjustment | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Depreciation expense | **$100,000** |
| Accrued interest expense | **$60,000** |

6. **Alternate fact (part f only):** Same instruments, but average market price for 20X5 is **$20** (below both exercise prices). Net income is still the amount from part (c).
7. On **February 1, 20X6**, all **6,000 warrants** are exercised for cash at $30. (Options remain outstanding.)

**Required:**  
a. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the three **December 31, 20X5 period-end adjusting JEs**. Prove each balances.  
b. Income-finalization schedule → **net income**.  
c. Separate **treasury stock method schedules** for (i) options and (ii) warrants; then a **combined incremental shares** total.

|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 100,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation | | 100,000 |

No potentially dilutive securities enter the diluted computation.

| Cash | 180,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 12,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 168,000 |
| *Exercise of 6,000 warrants at $30* | | |

**Check:** Dr 180,000 = Cr 12,000 + 168,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Warrants use the **same treasury stock method** as options. If average market price is **below** the exercise price, the instrument is **antidilutive** and is **omitted** from diluted EPS—even though it might become dilutive in a future period if the market rises.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Unvested restricted stock: grant JE, period-end compensation AJ (emphasis), amortization schedule, treasury stock method, vesting
**LO:** LO 20-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for restricted stock awards; period-end adjusting JE for compensation expense (emphasis); subsequent measurement amortization schedule; unearned compensation as treasury-method proceeds; basic vs diluted EPS; vesting completion  
**Scenario:**  
**Mapleford Analytics Inc.** is a calendar-year public company. Besides common stock, its only potentially dilutive instrument is **unvested restricted stock** granted to executives (complex capital structure for diluted EPS). Common stock **$1 par**.

**Facts:**
1. On **January 1, 20X5**, Mapleford grants **5,000** shares of restricted common stock to its CEO. Grant-date fair value is **$24** per share. The shares vest after **3 years** of continuous service (requisite service period = 3 years). Shares are issued at grant and held with restrictions until vesting.
2. Average market price of Mapleford common during 20X5: **$20**.
3. Weighted-average **vested** common shares outstanding for **basic EPS** during 20X5: **250,000**. (Unvested restricted shares are **excluded** from basic EPS.)
4. Before the **restricted-stock compensation** period-end AJ and income tax, pretax income for 20X5 is **$840,000**. Remaining period-end items:

| Adjustment | Amount |

b. Prepare the **subsequent measurement compensation amortization schedule** for Years 20X5–20X7 (total compensation, annual expense, cumulative expense, unearned compensation remaining).

Pretax income after compensation: \( \$840{,}000 - \$40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$800{,}000} \)

|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 200,000 | |

| *Accrue income tax at 25% of pretax income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **200,000**. **Balanced.**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Pretax income before RS compensation | $840,000 |
| Less: Compensation expense — restricted stock | (40,000) |

| Less: Income tax expense (25%) | (200,000) |
| **Net income** | **$600,000** |

**f. Treasury stock method — unvested restricted stock (end of 20X5)**

Assumed issuance of restricted shares as of beginning of period; assumed proceeds = **unearned compensation remaining**.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| New shares upon assumed vesting of restricted stock | 5,000 |
| Unearned compensation (assumed proceeds) (\( \$120{,}000 - \$40{,}000 \)) | $80,000 |
| Treasury shares assumed purchased (\( \$80{,}000 \div \$20 \) average market price) | (4,000) |
| **Incremental common shares** | **1,000** |

**g. Basic and diluted EPS — 20X5**

**Basic EPS** (unvested RS excluded from denominator)

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$600{,}000}{250{,}000} = \mathbf{\$2.40}
\]

**Diluted EPS reconciliation**

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $600,000 | 250,000 | $2.40 |
| Effect of unvested restricted stock: | | | |
| Numerator adjustment | *none* | | |
| Add incremental common shares | | 1,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$600,000** | **251,000** | **$2.39** |

\[
\text{Diluted EPS} = \frac{\$600{,}000}{251{,}000} = \$2.390438\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.39}
\]

**Dilutive test:** $2.39 < $2.40 → restricted stock is **dilutive**.

**Presentation:** Report **basic EPS $2.40** and **diluted EPS $2.39**.

**h. December 31, 20X7 — final amortization / vesting settlement**

Final year compensation (same form as each year-end AJ):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 40,000 | |
| Unearned Compensation | | 40,000 |
| *Recognize final year of restricted stock compensation; shares fully vest* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **40,000**. **Balanced.**

After this entry, **Unearned Compensation = $0**. The **5,000** shares remain in Common Stock / APIC (already issued at grant) and are now **unrestricted / vested**. Beginning in periods after vesting, the shares are included in the **basic EPS** weighted-average shares (no longer only a diluted-EPS treasury-method item).

**Key insight:** For **unvested restricted stock**, diluted EPS uses the treasury stock method with **unearned compensation** (not a cash exercise price) as the assumed repurchase proceeds. The **period-end compensation AJ** both (1) recognizes expense that reduces net income and (2) reduces unearned compensation, which **increases** incremental dilutive shares over time as more compensation is earned.

### Q4 — MC — Dilutive vs antidilutive under treasury stock method
**LO:** LO 20-7  
**Concept:** Classification — when stock options are dilutive vs antidilutive under the treasury stock method  
**Question:**  
For diluted EPS, a company has fully vested employee stock options outstanding all year. Under the **treasury stock method**, which statement is correct?

- A) Options are always included in diluted EPS because exercise is assumed regardless of market price.  
- B) Options are dilutive only when the average market price of the common stock **exceeds** the exercise price (producing positive incremental shares).  
- C) Options adjust the **numerator** of diluted EPS by adding back after-tax compensation expense.  
- D) Options are included in **basic EPS** once granted, whether or not exercised.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under ASC 260 / the treasury stock method, exercise is assumed and incremental shares equal option shares minus shares repurchaseable with proceeds at the **average market price**. If average market price is **below** the exercise price, assumed repurchase would exceed shares issued (negative incremental shares), which would **increase** EPS — that is **antidilutive**, so the options are **excluded**. There is generally **no numerator** adjustment for options/warrants. Unexercised options are **not** in basic EPS.

### Q5 — MC — Unvested restricted stock in EPS
**LO:** LO 20-7  
**Concept:** Classification — treatment of unvested restricted stock in basic vs diluted EPS  
**Question:**  
A company has time-vesting unvested restricted stock outstanding. How does that restricted stock affect EPS in the current period (assuming it is dilutive under the treasury stock method)?

- A) Include the full number of restricted shares in **both** basic and diluted EPS denominators.  
- B) Include the full number of restricted shares in **basic EPS** only; ignore for diluted EPS.  
- C) Exclude from **basic EPS**; include **incremental shares** (restricted shares minus treasury shares assumed purchased with unearned compensation) in **diluted EPS**.  
- D) Exclude from both basic and diluted EPS until the shares vest.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Unvested (nonvested) restricted stock is **not** outstanding for **basic EPS**. For **diluted EPS**, it is included under the **treasury stock method**: assumed issued, with **unearned compensation** used as proceeds to buy treasury shares at average market price; only **incremental** shares enter the diluted denominator (and only if dilutive).
