# Agent 154 — CORE DEMO — LO 17-3

**Chapter:** 17  
**LO title:** Account for a basic operating lease for a lessee  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Classification:** operating lease when **none** of the five finance-lease criteria are met (ownership transfer, reasonably certain purchase option, major part of remaining economic life, substantially all of fair value via PV of payments, no alternative use)
- **Initial recognition JE:** at commencement, lessee records **Right-of-Use (ROU) Asset** and **Lease Liability** equal to the PV of lease payments (basic case: no IDCs, incentives, or prepayments)
- **Subsequent measurement — lease liability schedule:** effective-interest roll-forward; first payment at commencement has **zero** “interest”; later “interest” = rate × beginning liability after prior payment
- **Subsequent measurement — ROU schedule:** single **straight-line lease expense** each period; ROU reduction = SL lease expense − period “interest” (so only one expense line on the income statement)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr Lease Expense; Cr Lease Liability (accrued “interest”); Cr ROU Asset (plug so expense = SL amount)
- **Payment JE:** Dr Lease Liability; Cr Cash (payment at beginning of period after commencement is 100% principal reduction of the liability **after** prior year-end accrual has been recorded)
- **Maturity / settlement:** final payment zeros the liability; final year-end entry zeros remaining ROU with no liability accrual
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** single **lease expense** in income from continuing operations; ROU asset and lease liability (current vs noncurrent) on the balance sheet; **full cash payment** in operating activities on the SCF
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all amounts, rates, and terms changed

### Q1 — CORE — Marbledale Logistics: full basic operating lease (initial JE, dual schedules, multi-year JEs)
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE; subsequent measurement lease-liability and ROU schedules (effective interest + SL expense); period-end adjusting JEs; maturity settlement; BS/IS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1** (lease commencement), **Marbledale Logistics Co.** (calendar year-end) leases specialized **warehouse handling equipment** under a **3-year noncancellable** lease from a third-party lessor. Facts:

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Annual lease payment (annuity-due: Jan. 1 of Year 1, 2, and 3) | **$32,000** |
| Discount rate (lessor’s implicit rate, **known** to Marbledale) | **6%** |
| Fair value of equipment at commencement | **$250,000** |
| Estimated remaining economic life | **8 years** |
| Residual / end of term | Asset **reverts** to lessor; **no** purchase or renewal option; residual **not** guaranteed by lessee |
| Alternative use | Equipment is routinely leased to other customers (has alternative use) |
| Other | No initial direct costs, incentives, or prepaid rent. Accounting year ends **December 31**. |

Marbledale measures the lease liability and ROU asset at the **present value of the three lease payments** (annuity due). PV at 6%, n = 3, PMT = $32,000, type = beginning → **$90,669** (nearest dollar). Round periodic “interest” to the **nearest dollar**.

**Required:**  
a. **Classify** the lease for the lessee. Support with a brief five-criteria analysis.  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** and the **January 1, Year 1 payment JE**.  
c. Prepare the full **lease liability schedule** and the full **right-of-use asset schedule** (subsequent measurement emphasis).  
d. Prepare **all Year 1** and **Year 2** journal entries (payment dates and December 31 adjusting entries).  
e. Prepare **Year 3** journal entries through maturity/settlement (show that ROU and liability end at **zero**).  
f. Show **December 31, Year 1** balance sheet (ROU; current and noncurrent lease liability) and income statement (lease expense) amounts.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification — operating lease**

| Criterion | Analysis | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ownership transfer | Asset reverts to lessor at end of term | No |
| 2. Purchase option reasonably certain | No purchase option | No |
| 3. Lease term = major part of remaining economic life | 3 ÷ 8 = **37.5%** < 75% | No |
| 4. PV of payments ≈ substantially all of FV | PV **$90,669** vs 90% × $250,000 = **$225,000** | No |
| 5. No alternative use to lessor | Routinely leased to others — alternative use exists | No |

**None** of the criteria are met → **operating lease** for Marbledale (lessee).

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition and first payment**

*Commencement — record ROU asset and lease liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Right-of-Use Asset | 90,669 | |
| Lease Liability | | 90,669 |
| *Record ROU asset and lease liability at PV of lease payments* | | |

**Check:** Dr 90,669 = Cr 90,669. **Balanced.**

*January 1, Year 1 — first lease payment (at commencement → 100% principal)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,000 | |
| Cash | | 32,000 |
| *Payment at commencement reduces liability; no interest yet* | | |

**Check:** Dr 32,000 = Cr 32,000. **Balanced.**  
Liability after payment: $90,669 − $32,000 = **$58,669**.

**c. Subsequent measurement schedules**

**Lease Liability Schedule** (effective interest; payments at beginning of period)

| Date | Lease payment | Interest on liability | Liability decrease | Lease liability balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 (commencement) | — | — | — | **90,669** |
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | 32,000 | **0** | 32,000 | 58,669 |
| Jan. 1, Year 2 | 32,000 | **3,520** | 28,480 | 30,189 |
| Jan. 1, Year 3 | 32,000 | **1,811** | 30,189 | **0** |
| **Totals** | **96,000** | **5,331** | **90,669** | |

*Interest computations:*  
- Year 1 “interest” (accrues during Year 1; on Jan. 1, Year 2 row): $58,669 × 6% = $3,520.14 → **$3,520**  
- Year 2 “interest”: $30,189 × 6% = $1,811.34 → **$1,811**  
- First payment interest = **$0** (payment at commencement).  
- Schedule checks: total payments − total interest = total principal reduction: $96,000 − $5,331 = **$90,669** = initial liability.

**Right-of-Use Asset Schedule** (straight-line lease expense; ROU plug)

Straight-line lease expense each year = total undiscounted payments ÷ 3 = $96,000 ÷ 3 = **$32,000**  
(equals the periodic cash payment when only fixed payments and no other lease costs).

| Date | Lease expense (SL) | Interest on liability | ROU asset decrease | ROU asset balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | **90,669** |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | 32,000 | 3,520 | **28,480** | 62,189 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 32,000 | 1,811 | **30,189** | 32,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 32,000 | **0** | **32,000** | **0** |
| **Totals** | **96,000** | **5,331** | **90,669** | |

*ROU decrease each year = SL lease expense − that year’s “interest”* (ensures a **single** lease expense equal to the SL amount).  
*After Year 3, both ROU and liability = $0.*

**d. Year 1 and Year 2 journal entries**

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting entry (single lease expense)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Expense | 32,000 | |
| Lease Liability | | 3,520 |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 28,480 |
| *SL lease expense; accrue “interest”; reduce ROU for residual* | | |

**Check:** Dr 32,000 = Cr 3,520 + 28,480. **Balanced.**  
Liability at Dec. 31, Year 1: $58,669 + $3,520 = **$62,189**. ROU = **$62,189**.

*January 1, Year 2 — lease payment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,000 | |
| Cash | | 32,000 |

**Check:** Balanced. Liability after payment: $62,189 − $32,000 = **$30,189**.

*December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting entry*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Expense | 32,000 | |
| Lease Liability | | 1,811 |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 30,189 |

**Check:** Dr 32,000 = Cr 1,811 + 30,189. **Balanced.**  
Liability at Dec. 31, Year 2: $30,189 + $1,811 = **$32,000**. ROU = **$32,000**.

**e. Year 3 — maturity / settlement**

*January 1, Year 3 — final lease payment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,000 | |
| Cash | | 32,000 |

**Check:** Balanced. Liability after payment = **$0**.

*December 31, Year 3 — final lease expense (no remaining liability to accrue)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Expense | 32,000 | |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 32,000 |

**Check:** Dr 32,000 = Cr 32,000. **Balanced.**  
ROU after entry = **$0**. Lease fully settled.

**f. December 31, Year 1 financial statement amounts**

**Balance sheet — Dec. 31, Year 1**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Right-of-use asset (noncurrent asset) | **$62,189** |
| Lease liability — **current** (next payment within one year) | **$32,000** |
| Lease liability — **noncurrent** ($62,189 − $32,000) | **$30,189** |
| Total lease liability | **$62,189** |

**Income statement — Year 1**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Lease expense (single operating expense; income from continuing operations) | **$32,000** |

**Key insight:** For a basic **operating** lease, the liability is measured with effective interest (same machinery as a finance lease), but the income statement shows **only one** straight-line **lease expense**. ROU amortization is **plugged** so that “interest” + ROU reduction always equals that SL expense — never two separate expense lines.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Harborview Labs: period-end adjusting JEs, maturity settlement, presentation
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE for operating lease expense; maturity-year settlement; current/noncurrent liability split; IS and SCF presentation vs finance lease  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborview Labs Inc.** (calendar year-end) entered a **3-year** basic operating lease of lab freezers on **January 1, Year 1**. Payments of **$35,000** are due each **January 1** (Years 1–3). Discount rate **6%**. Initial PV of payments (and initial ROU = liability) = **$99,169**. No IDCs, incentives, or residual guarantee. Asset reverts to lessor; economic life **7 years**; FV **$220,000**; alternative use exists.

After the **January 1, Year 1** payment, the lease liability balance is **$64,169**. Harborview has **not yet** recorded any Year 1 adjusting entry.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **Year 1** and **Year 2** “interest” amounts and complete a condensed **liability + ROU** roll-forward (subsequent measurement).  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting JE only.  
c. State the **current** and **noncurrent** lease liability on the **December 31, Year 1** balance sheet.  
d. Prepare **all Year 3** entries (final payment on Jan. 1 and final adjusting entry on Dec. 31) that settle the lease.  
e. **Presentation:** (1) How is Year 1 expense labeled and classified on the income statement? (2) How is the **$35,000** cash payment classified on the statement of cash flows? (3) Contrast in one sentence with a finance lease’s IS presentation.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Subsequent measurement roll-forward**

Interest Year 1 (accrues on $64,169): $64,169 × 6% = $3,850.14 → **$3,850**  
After Dec. 31, Y1 accrual, liability = $64,169 + $3,850 = **$68,019**  
Jan. 1, Y2 payment reduces liability to $68,019 − $35,000 = **$33,019**  
Interest Year 2: $33,019 × 6% = $1,981.14 → **$1,981**  
After Dec. 31, Y2 accrual, liability = $33,019 + $1,981 = **$35,000**  
Jan. 1, Y3 payment → liability **$0**; Year 3 interest = **$0**

SL lease expense each year = $35,000 × 3 / 3 = **$35,000**

| Year | SL lease expense | Interest | ROU decrease | Ending ROU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Start | — | — | — | 99,169 |
| 1 | 35,000 | 3,850 | 31,150 | **68,019** |
| 2 | 35,000 | 1,981 | 33,019 | **35,000** |
| 3 | 35,000 | 0 | 35,000 | **0** |

*Checks:* ROU decreases sum to $99,169; ending ROU and liability both zero after Year 3.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Expense | 35,000 | |
| Lease Liability | | 3,850 |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 31,150 |
| *Record single SL lease expense; accrue interest; reduce ROU* | | |

**Check:** Dr 35,000 = Cr 3,850 + 31,150. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 1 liability classification**

Total lease liability = **$68,019**  
- **Current:** next cash payment due within one year = **$35,000**  
- **Noncurrent:** $68,019 − $35,000 = **$33,019**

(ROU asset reported as noncurrent asset: **$68,019**.)

**d. Year 3 maturity / settlement JEs**

*January 1, Year 3 — final payment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 35,000 | |
| Cash | | 35,000 |

**Check:** Balanced. Liability = **$0**.

*December 31, Year 3 — final expense / ROU settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Expense | 35,000 | |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 35,000 |

**Check:** Balanced. ROU = **$0**. Lease fully disposed/matured with no residual asset or liability on Harborview’s books.

**e. Presentation / disclosure**

1. **Income statement:** one line — **Lease expense $35,000** — included in **income from continuing operations** (operating expense). Do **not** split into interest expense and amortization expense.  
2. **Statement of cash flows (lessee operating lease):** the **entire** $35,000 cash payment is an **operating** cash outflow.  
3. **Contrast with finance lease:** a finance lease presents **interest expense** (other expense / financing) and **ROU amortization** (operating) as **two** amounts, with a front-loaded total expense pattern; operating lease keeps a flat single lease expense.

**Key insight:** The period-end entry is the operating-lease “engine”: it forces **straight-line** P&L while still updating the liability for effective interest and writing down ROU so both books stay in sync and finish at zero.

### Q4 — MC — Operating vs finance presentation and classification
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — single lease expense and SCF treatment for lessee operating leases  
**Question:**  
Which statement is **correct** for a lessee’s **basic operating lease** under ASC 842 (as illustrated for LO 17-3)?

- A) The lessee records no ROU asset or lease liability if the lease term is longer than 12 months.  
- B) Each period the lessee recognizes **interest expense** and **amortization expense** as two separate income statement amounts, totaling more than the cash payment in early years.  
- C) Each period the lessee recognizes a **single lease expense** on a straight-line basis in income from continuing operations, while still measuring the lease liability with the effective-interest method for balance sheet purposes.  
- D) Cash lease payments are split on the SCF: interest portion in operating activities and principal portion in financing activities.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Operating leases still put ROU assets and lease liabilities on the balance sheet (A is false for terms > 12 months). The dual interest + amortization presentation and SCF split of principal/interest describe **finance** leases (B and D), not operating leases. Operating leases report one SL **lease expense** in continuing operations, with the full cash payment in operating cash flows.
