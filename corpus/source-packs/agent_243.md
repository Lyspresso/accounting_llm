# Agent 243 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-7

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe the components of comprehensive income and prepare a statement of comprehensive income and a statement of stockholders’ equity.  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Comprehensive income** = net income + other comprehensive income (OCI), from nonowner sources
- **OCI components (common):** unrealized holding G/L on AFS debt securities; cash-flow hedge G/L; foreign-currency translation; certain pension adjustments — all **net of tax**
- **Presentation:** single continuous statement of comprehensive income **or** two separate statements (income statement + separate SCI starting with net income)
- **Closing:** NI closes to **retained earnings**; OCI closes to **accumulated OCI (AOCI)** (equity), not to RE
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-period **AOCI rollforward** and **statement of stockholders’ equity** (columnar: CS, PIC, RE, AOCI, Total)
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase of AFS debt; issuance of common stock; period-end fair-value mark of AFS / cash-flow hedges through OCI (net of deferred tax)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** tax-effected unrealized OCI; close temporary OCI accounts to AOCI; close NI to RE; dividends declared
- **Disposal / reclassification:** sale of AFS → realized G/L in **net income**; **reclassification adjustment** in OCI removes prior unrealized amounts from AOCI so comprehensive income is not double-counted
- **Classification:** NI vs OCI items; accumulate in RE vs AOCI
- **Number-variant twin:** same SCI + SOE + AOCI path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-period AOCI schedule, AFS lifecycle, SCI, SOE, and closing JEs

**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeway Components Inc.** (calendar year-end) invests in **available-for-sale (AFS) debt securities** and reports comprehensive income under U.S. GAAP. ### Q4 — MC — Classification of comprehensive income components
**LO:** LO 19-7  
**Concept:** Classification — whether items enter comprehensive income as NI or OCI, and whether they accumulate in RE or AOCI  

**Question 1:**  
Which of the following is included in **other comprehensive income** (not net income) and accumulates in **accumulated other comprehensive income**?

- A) Realized gain on sale of equipment used in operations  
- B) Unrealized holding gain on available-for-sale debt securities  
- C) Dividend revenue on equity securities  
- D) Gain on sale of a discontinued operation  

**Answer:** **B.** Unrealized holding gains/losses on **AFS debt securities** are classic OCI items (net of tax) and accumulate in **AOCI** until reclassified (e.g., on sale). Realized operating gains, dividend revenue, and discontinued-operation gains are components of **net income** and accumulate in **retained earnings**.

**Question 2:**  
At year-end, a company closes **net income** of $70,000 and **OCI** consisting of a pretax unrealized AFS loss of $15,000 and a pretax cash-flow-hedge gain of $5,000. Tax rate is 25%. Beginning AOCI is an **$18,000 debit**. What is **ending AOCI**, and what is **comprehensive income**?

- A) AOCI $10,500 debit; comprehensive income $62,500  
- B) AOCI $25,500 debit; comprehensive income $62,500  
- C) AOCI $25,500 debit; comprehensive income $60,000  
- D) AOCI $28,000 debit; comprehensive income $70,000  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Net pretax OCI = \(-15{,}000 + 5{,}000 = -10{,}000\); after tax \(= -10{,}000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{-\$7{,}500}\).  
Ending AOCI (debit) = \(18{,}000 + 7{,}500 = \mathbf{\$25{,}500}\) debit.  
Comprehensive income = \(70{,}000 - 7{,}500 = \mathbf{\$62{,}500}\).
