# Agent 264 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-10

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Explain the accounting for transfers of investments  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Transfers at fair value** on the transfer date; reclassify investment to the new category; apply new-category rules prospectively (no retrospective restatement)
- **AFS → HTM:** no NI effect on transfer; new HTM amortized-cost basis = amortized cost ± unrealized G/L remaining in AOCI (i.e., fair value at transfer); AOCI remains and is amortized to interest income as a yield adjustment; premium/discount created on HTM is amortized as yield; the two amortizations **offset** for interest income
- **HTM → AFS:** no NI effect; adjust carrying amount to fair value through **OCI** at transfer; thereafter AFS FV-OCI rules
- **Transfer into Trading (TS / FV-NI debt):** any unrecognized holding G/L at transfer recognized in **net income**; FV becomes TS cost basis
- **Transfer out of Trading:** NI already reflects FV; account under new category rules
- **Equity FV-NI → equity method:** recognize unrealized G/L in NI through transfer date; **fair value becomes new cost basis**; no retrospective equity-method catch-up
- **Equity method → FV-NI (or other):** investment account balance at change date is new cost basis
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** cash coupon interest; amortize HTM premium/discount; amortize AOCI frozen at AFS→HTM transfer; year-end FV adjustment for AFS/TS after transfer
- **Disclosure:** circumstances leading to the transfer; transfers into/out of HTM and trading should be rare

### Q1 — CORE — AFS purchase, year-end FV adjust, transfer AFS→HTM, amort schedules, period-end yield JEs, maturity
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Transfer of debt securities from AFS to HTM at fair value; AOCI frozen and amortized as yield; period-end adjusting JEs for cash interest, premium amortization, and AOCI amortization (emphasis); maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Holdings Corp.** (calendar year-end) invests in **Meridian Industrial 6% bonds**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Purchase date | **January 1, Year 1** |
| Face amount | **$100,000** |
| Coupon | **6%** paid annually each **December 31** |
| Purchase price | **$100,000** (at **par**) |
| Initial classification | **Available-for-sale (AFS)** |
| Maturity | **December 31, Year 5** |
| Fair value, December 31, Year 1 | **$106,000** |
| Transfer date | **January 1, Year 2** — management has positive ability and intent to hold to maturity (credit-quality / business-combination exception circumstances documented) |
| Fair value on transfer date | **$106,000** (unchanged from Dec 31) |
| Remaining term at transfer | **4 years** |
| Amortization method (for this problem) | **Straight-line** for AOCI runoff and for any HTM premium/discount created at transfer |

Ignore taxes, credit losses (ACL), and brokerage fees. Coupon cash is received each December 31.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry.  
b. Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting** entry to mark the AFS portfolio to fair value (assume FVA beginning balance = $0). Also record cash interest for Year 1.  
c. Record the **January 1, Year 2 transfer** entry from AFS to HTM (reclassify investment and close FVA; leave unrealized gain in AOCI). State the **new HTM amortized-cost basis**.  
d. Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 2–5 showing: beginning HTM carrying amount, premium amortization, ending HTM carrying amount, beginning AOCI unrealized gain, AOCI amortization, and ending AOCI.  
e. Record all **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting / interest** journal entries (cash coupon, HTM premium amortization, and AOCI amortization) — **emphasis**. Compute **net interest revenue** for Year 2.  
f. Record the **December 31, Year 5 maturity / settlement** entries (final cash interest, final premium amort, final AOCI amort, and face collection).  
g. **Classification / disclosure:** What note disclosure is required regarding the transfer? Do transfers AFS→HTM affect net income on the transfer date?

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (AFS at par)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS—Meridian Bonds | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 100,000 |
| *Purchase $100,000 face 6% Meridian bonds at par; classify AFS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — cash interest and period-end FV adjusting JE**

Cash coupon = \(100{,}000 \times 0.06 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 6,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 6,000 |
| *Receive annual 6% coupon* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**

Unrealized holding gain = \(106{,}000 - 100{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 6,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 6,000 |
| *Mark AFS Meridian bonds to FV; gain in OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**  
Net AFS carrying amount on BS = \(100{,}000 + 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$106{,}000}\). AOCI (credit) = **$6,000**.

**c. January 1, Year 2 — transfer AFS → HTM at fair value (no NI effect)**

New HTM amortized-cost basis = amortized cost ± AOCI unrealized G/L = \(100{,}000 + 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$106{,}000}\) (equals FV on transfer date).  
Premium created relative to face = \(106{,}000 - 100{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).  
AOCI unrealized gain of **$6,000 remains** and will be amortized as a yield adjustment over 4 years.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Meridian Bonds | 106,000 | |
| Investment in AFS—Meridian Bonds | | 100,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 6,000 |
| *Transfer AFS to HTM at FV; close FVA; AOCI gain remains for amortization* | | |

**Check:** Dr 106,000 = Cr 100,000 + 6,000. **Balanced.**  
**NI impact on transfer date: none.** AOCI still holds unrealized gain **$6,000**.

**d. Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 2–5) — premium and AOCI amortize $6,000 ÷ 4 = $1,500 per year**

| Year | Beg. HTM CA | Premium amort. | End HTM CA | Beg. AOCI gain | AOCI amort. | End AOCI |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 106,000 | 1,500 | 104,500 | 6,000 | 1,500 | 4,500 |
| 3 | 104,500 | 1,500 | 103,000 | 4,500 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| 4 | 103,000 | 1,500 | 101,500 | 3,000 | 1,500 | 1,500 |
| 5 | 101,500 | 1,500 | **100,000** | 1,500 | 1,500 | **0** |
| **Total** | | **6,000** | | | **6,000** | |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
HTM: \(106{,}000 - 4 \times 1{,}500 = 100{,}000\) (face). ✓  
AOCI: \(6{,}000 - 4 \times 1{,}500 = 0\). ✓

**e. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting / interest JEs (emphasis)**

*(1) Cash coupon*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 6,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 6,000 |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**

*(2) Amortize HTM premium (yield adjustment)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Revenue | 1,500 | |
| Investment in HTM—Meridian Bonds | | 1,500 |
| *SL amortize $6,000 premium over 4 years* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500. **Balanced.** Ending HTM CA = **$104,500**.

*(3) Amortize AOCI unrealized gain into interest (offsets premium amort)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,500 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,500 |
| *Amortize frozen AOCI gain as yield adjustment* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500. **Balanced.** Ending AOCI gain = **$4,500**.

**Net interest revenue Year 2** = \(6{,}000 - 1{,}500 + 1{,}500 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\) (coupon; premium amort and AOCI amort **offset**).

**f. December 31, Year 5 — maturity / settlement**

*(1) Cash coupon*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 6,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 6,000 |

*(2) Final premium amort*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Revenue | 1,500 | |
| Investment in HTM—Meridian Bonds | | 1,500 |

*(3) Final AOCI amort*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,500 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,500 |

*(4) Collect face at maturity*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 100,000 | |
| Investment in HTM—Meridian Bonds | | 100,000 |
| *HTM CA after final premium amort = face* | | |

Each entry: Dr = Cr. **Balanced.** HTM and AOCI both zero after settlement.

**g. Classification / disclosure**  
- Transfer AFS → HTM has **no effect on net income** on the transfer date (ASC 320-10-35-10d).  
- Notes should describe the **circumstances** leading to the transfer.  
- Transfers from AFS into HTM (and into/out of trading) should be **rare** and supportable.  
- After transfer, present as **HTM** (amortized cost); remaining AOCI amortizes through interest over the remaining life.

**Key insight:** On AFS→HTM transfer, fair value becomes the new HTM amortized-cost basis and the existing unrealized G/L stays in AOCI. Each period’s **period-end** work is three-fold: record coupon cash, amortize the new premium/discount, and amortize AOCI — the last two offset for interest income while driving HTM CA to face and AOCI to zero.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — AFS→HTM with unrealized loss; 3-year remaining life
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: AFS→HTM transfer when FV < amortized cost (unrealized loss in AOCI); new HTM basis at FV; discount + AOCI amort schedules; period-end adjusting JEs  
**Scenario:**  
**Northspire Financial Group** (calendar year) holds **Summit County 5% municipal bonds**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Purchase | **January 1, Year 1** — face **$150,000** at **par** (**$150,000**); classify **AFS** |
| Coupon | **5%** paid annually **December 31** → cash **$7,500**/year |
| Maturity | **December 31, Year 4** |
| Fair value Dec 31, Year 1 / transfer date Jan 1, Year 2 | **$141,000** |
| Transfer | **January 1, Year 2** AFS → HTM (documented change in ability and intent) |
| Remaining term at transfer | **3 years** |
| Amortization | Straight-line for discount and AOCI |

Ignore taxes and credit losses. FVA beginning balance before Dec 31, Year 1 adjust = $0.

**Required:**  
a. Record **December 31, Year 1** cash interest and the **period-end FV adjusting JE** (AFS).  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 2 transfer** AFS → HTM. State new HTM amortized-cost basis and discount.  
c. Prepare the **Years 2–4 subsequent measurement schedule** (HTM CA and AOCI unrealized loss runoff).  
d. Record **December 31, Year 2 period-end** cash interest, discount amortization, and AOCI amortization. Compute net interest revenue.  
e. Briefly contrast NI impact of this AFS→HTM transfer with a transfer of the **same security into Trading** on the same date.

**Answer key:**  

**a. December 31, Year 1 — cash interest and FV adjust**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 7,500 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 7,500 |
| *5% × $150,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,500 = Cr 7,500. **Balanced.**

Unrealized loss = \(150{,}000 - 141{,}000 = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 9,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 9,000 |
| *Mark AFS to FV; loss in OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**  
Net AFS BS amount = \(150{,}000 - 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$141{,}000}\).

**b. January 1, Year 2 — transfer AFS → HTM**

New HTM amortized-cost basis = **$141,000** (FV).  
Discount to face = \(150{,}000 - 141{,}000 = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}\).  
AOCI unrealized **loss** of **$9,000** remains for amortization over 3 years. **No NI on transfer.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Summit Bonds | 141,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 9,000 | |
| Investment in AFS—Summit Bonds | | 150,000 |
| *Transfer at FV; close credit FVA; AOCI loss remains* | | |

**Check:** Dr 141,000 + 9,000 = Cr 150,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — $9,000 ÷ 3 = $3,000 per year**

| Year | Beg. HTM CA | Discount amort. | End HTM CA | Beg. AOCI loss | AOCI amort. | End AOCI loss |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 141,000 | 3,000 | 144,000 | 9,000 | 3,000 | 6,000 |
| 3 | 144,000 | 3,000 | 147,000 | 6,000 | 3,000 | 3,000 |
| 4 | 147,000 | 3,000 | **150,000** | 3,000 | 3,000 | **0** |
| **Total** | | **9,000** | | | **9,000** | |

**Checks:** \(141{,}000 + 9{,}000 = 150{,}000\); \(9{,}000 - 9{,}000 = 0\). ✓

**d. December 31, Year 2 — period-end JEs (emphasis)**

*(1) Cash coupon*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 7,500 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr 7,500 = Cr 7,500. **Balanced.**

*(2) Accrete HTM discount*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Summit Bonds | 3,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 3,000 |

**Check:** Dr 3,000 = Cr 3,000. **Balanced.** Ending HTM CA = **$144,000**.

*(3) Amortize AOCI unrealized loss (offsets discount accretion on interest)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Revenue | 3,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 3,000 |
| *Reduce AOCI debit balance; offset yield effect of discount amort* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,000 = Cr 3,000. **Balanced.** Ending AOCI unrealized loss = **$6,000**.

**Net interest revenue Year 2** = \(7{,}500 + 3{,}000 - 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\) (coupon; discount amort and AOCI amort **offset**).

**e. Contrast with transfer into Trading**  
AFS→HTM: **no NI** on transfer date; AOCI remains and amortizes.  
Same facts transferred **into Trading**: unrecognized holding **loss of $9,000** is recognized in **net income** on the transfer date (reclassify from OCI to earnings); thereafter FV-NI rules apply. Transfers into/out of trading should be rare.

**Key insight:** Unrealized **loss** cases create a **discount** on HTM; period-end discount accretion increases interest revenue while AOCI loss amortization decreases it by the same amount — net coupon — while HTM CA climbs to face.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angles — AFS→TS, HTM→AFS, FV-NI→equity method, post-transfer TS FV adjust
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Transfer accounting across categories: AFS to trading (NI on transfer); HTM to AFS (OCI only); equity FV-NI to equity method (FV = new cost basis); period-end FV adjusting JE after transfer into TS  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironbridge Capital LLC** has three independent investment situations at **January 1, Year 2** (after any prior year-end FV entries already posted). Calendar year-end.

**Situation 1 — Debt AFS → Trading**  
- **Redwood Corp. 4% bonds:** amortized cost **$48,000**; FVA—AFS credit balance **$3,600** (FV = **$44,400**); related unrealized loss already in AOCI.  
- Transfer to **Trading** on January 1, Year 2.  
- December 31, Year 2 fair value of the TS position = **$45,900**.

**Situation 2 — Debt HTM → AFS**  
- **Lakeshore Utility bonds:** HTM amortized cost **$72,000** (purchased at amounts that fully amortized to this AC; no separate unamortized premium/discount left).  
- Transfer to **AFS** on January 1, Year 2 because of a documented major disposition that changes interest-rate risk policy (ASC 320-10-25-6-type circumstance).  
- Fair value on transfer date = **$75,600**.

**Situation 3 — Equity FV-NI → equity method**  
- **HarborTech Inc.** common: 18% ownership, measured at **FV-NI**. Carrying amount (already at FV after Dec 31, Year 1 adjust) = **$216,000**.  
- On January 1, Year 2, Ironbridge obtains significant influence (board representation); begins **equity method**.  
- If equity method had been used from acquisition, the investment balance would have been $198,000 — **do not** use that amount.

**Required:**  
a. **Situation 1:** Record the **January 1, Year 2 transfer** AFS → TS. Then record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end FV adjusting JE** for the trading security (cost basis after transfer = FV at transfer).  
b. **Situation 2:** Record the **January 1, Year 2 transfer** HTM → AFS (including FV adjustment through OCI). State NI impact.  
c. **Situation 3:** Record the **January 1, Year 2 transfer** FV-NI → equity method. What is the **new cost basis**? Is retrospective equity-method application required?  
d. Complete the **classification / impact table** (transfer type → NI on transfer date? → OCI on transfer date?).  
e. Assume Situation 1 bonds are **sold** on March 1, Year 3 for **$46,200** cash (after the Dec 31, Year 2 FV adjust in part a). Record the **disposal** entry (no separate FVA remains if the portfolio method was zeroed at sale for this single security — treat carrying amount = last FV of $45,900).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Situation 1 — AFS → Trading; then period-end TS FV adjust**

**b. Situation 2 — HTM → AFS**

Gain = \(46{,}200 - 45{,}900 = \mathbf{\$300}\).

**Key insight:** Transfers **into trading** pull unrecognized holdings into **earnings** immediately; HTM↔AFS moves are **OCI-only** on the transfer date; equity-method adoption uses **current FV as cost** with **no retrospective** equity pickup.

### Q4 — MC — NI impact by transfer type
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Classification of transfer-date income effects (AFS→HTM vs AFS→TS vs HTM→AFS)  
**Question:**  
On the transfer date, which reclassification of a debt security produces a gain or loss in **net income** (assuming unrecognized holding G/L exists and AFS/HTM had not already been marked through NI)?

- A) Transfer from AFS to HTM  
- B) Transfer from HTM to AFS  
- C) Transfer from AFS to Trading  
- D) Both A and B  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Transfers into **Trading** recognize any unrecognized holding gain or loss in **net income** on the transfer date (ASC 320-10-35-10b). AFS→HTM and HTM→AFS have **no NI effect** on the transfer date; HTM→AFS adjusts through **OCI**, and AFS→HTM leaves unrealized G/L in AOCI to amortize as yield.

### Q5 — MC — Equity method transfer cost basis
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Cost basis when transferring an equity investment from FV-NI to the equity method  
**Question:**  
An investor has measured an equity investment at FV-NI. On the date significant influence is obtained, fair value is $90,000; original cost was $70,000; and the “as-if equity method” carrying amount from acquisition would have been $82,000. The investor should begin the equity method using a new cost basis of:

- A) $70,000  
- B) $82,000  
- C) $90,000  
- D) $0, with full retrospective restatement to the equity method  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Fair value on the transfer date becomes the **new cost basis** under the equity method. Unrealized G/L through that date is already in NI (FV-NI). **No retrospective** equity-method application; the $82,000 as-if amount is not used.
