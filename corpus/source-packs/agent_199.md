# Agent 199 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-3

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for acquisition of property, plant, and equipment through debt and equity issuances  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition (debt):** PPE purchased with a note is measured at the **more clearly evident** of (1) fair value of the fixed asset or (2) **present value** of future debt payments discounted at the **prevailing (market) rate**
- **Noninterest-bearing / below-market note:** Face − initial measurement = **Discount on Note Payable** (contra liability); equipment is **not** recorded at face
- **Market-rate interest-bearing note:** acquisition recorded at **face** of the note (no discount/premium from rate mismatch)
- **Initial recognition (equity):** PPE measured at the more clearly evident of **FV of securities issued** (e.g., publicly traded stock) or **FV of the fixed asset**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** **effective interest method**—Interest expense = beginning net carrying amount × market (effective) rate; cash interest = face × stated rate (often **$0** on 0% notes); discount amortization = interest expense − cash interest; net CV rolls up to face at maturity
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** debit Interest Expense, credit Discount on Note Payable each reporting date
- **Maturity / settlement JE:** pay face cash; remove Note Payable (discount fully amortized so net CV = face)
- **Equity issuance JE:** debit Equipment, credit Common Stock (par × shares) and APIC (excess of measurement over par)

### Q1 — CORE — Noninterest note (FV of PPE unknown): initial JE, full effective-interest schedule, accruals, maturity
**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of specialized equipment at PV of a noninterest-bearing note (market rate known); cash down payment; full subsequent measurement schedule (effective interest); period-end discount amortization JEs; maturity settlement of face  
**Scenario:**  
**Harbor Forge Co.** (calendar year-end) acquires **specialized production equipment** on **January 1, Year 1**. The equipment’s fair value is **not** reliably determinable (custom-built; no active market). Harbor pays **$30,000 cash** and issues a **three-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$133,100**, due in a lump sum on **December 31, Year 3**. The prevailing market rate for liabilities of similar risk and duration is **10%**. Harbor amortizes discounts on notes payable using the **effective interest method**. **Ignore depreciation.**

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **present value** of the note, the **discount**, and the **initial cost of the equipment**. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **full subsequent measurement (effective interest) schedule** for the life of the note (columns: date/period, cash interest, interest expense, discount amortization, ending carrying amount of net note).  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting JEs** on **December 31, Year 1**, **Year 2**, and **Year 3** for interest and discount amortization.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 maturity / settlement JE** for payment of the face amount (after the Year 3 interest entry in part c).  
e. Briefly explain why equipment is recorded at **$130,000** rather than cash + face (**$163,100**), and what the discount represents.

**Answer key:**  

**a. PV, discount, equipment cost, and initial recognition**

\[
PV = \frac{\$133{,}100}{(1.10)^3} = \$133{,}100 \times \frac{1}{1.331} = \$100{,}000
\]

(Equivalently: \( \$100{,}000 \times (1.10)^3 = \$100{,}000 \times 1.331 = \$133{,}100 \).)

Discount on Note Payable = Face − PV = $133,100 − $100,000 = **$33,100**  
Initial cost of equipment = Cash + PV of note = $30,000 + $100,000 = **$130,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 130,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 33,100 | |
| Notes Payable | | 133,100 |
| Cash | | 30,000 |
| *Acquire specialized equipment: cash + 3-year 0% note at PV (market 10%)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 130,000 + 33,100 = 163,100; Cr 133,100 + 30,000 = 163,100. **Balanced.**  
Initial net carrying amount of note = $133,100 − $33,100 = **$100,000**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (effective interest; market rate 10%) — emphasis**

Cash interest each period = Face × Stated rate = $133,100 × 0% = **$0**  
Interest expense each period = Beginning net carrying amount × **10%**  
Discount amortization = Interest expense − Cash interest = Interest expense (cash = 0)

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest expense (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $100,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $10,000 | $10,000 | 110,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 11,000 | 11,000 | 121,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 0 | 12,100 | 12,100 | 133,100 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$33,100** | **$33,100** | |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Total amortization = total discount = $33,100  
- Ending CV at maturity = face $133,100  
- Interest Y1: $100,000 × 10% = $10,000; Y2: $110,000 × 10% = $11,000; Y3: $121,000 × 10% = $12,100  
- Cash outflows for the note: $0 during term + $133,100 at maturity; financing cost = $33,100 (all interest expense)

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 10,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 10,000 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount (0% stated → amort = interest exp.)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 = Cr 10,000. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $33,100 − $10,000 = $23,100; net CV = $133,100 − $23,100 = **$110,000**.

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 11,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 11,000 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,000 = Cr 11,000. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $23,100 − $11,000 = $12,100; net CV = **$121,000**.

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 12,100 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 12,100 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize remaining discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,100 = Cr 12,100. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $0; net CV = **$133,100** (face).

**d. December 31, Year 3 — maturity settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Payable | 133,100 | |
| Cash | | 133,100 |
| *Pay face amount of note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 133,100 = Cr 133,100. **Balanced.**

**e. Conceptual**  
Equipment is measured under the **cost principle** at the cash-equivalent price: cash paid plus the **PV of deferred payments** at the market rate—not cash + face. Recording face as equipment would **overstate the asset** and **omit the financing component**. The $33,100 discount is **implicit interest** recognized as **interest expense** over three years under the effective interest method as the net liability accretes to face.

**Key insight:** When FV of the specialized asset is unknown, **market-rate PV of the note** sets both the equipment cost and the initial net liability. The **amortization schedule** is the engine: market rate × beginning CV drives interest expense and rolls the net note up to face by maturity; cash coupon is zero throughout.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cash + 0% note (FV unknown), 2-year schedule, accruals, settlement
**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: acquisition of equipment with cash down payment and noninterest-bearing note measured at PV (market 8%); full effective-interest subsequent measurement schedule; period-end amortization JEs; face payment at maturity  
**Scenario:**  
**Alpine Robotics Inc.** (calendar year-end) acquires equipment on **January 1, Year 1**. Fair value of the equipment is **unknown**. Alpine pays **$25,000 cash** and issues a **two-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$116,640**, due **December 31, Year 2**. The market rate for similar risk and duration is **8%**. Use the **effective interest method**. **Ignore depreciation.**

**Required:**  
a. Compute PV of the note, discount, and equipment cost. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** acquisition JE.  
b. Prepare the **2-year effective interest amortization schedule**.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** period-end interest / amortization JE.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** interest JE and the **maturity payment JE**.  
e. State the **balance sheet presentation** of the note at December 31, Year 1 (face, unamortized discount, net liability).

**Answer key:**  

**a. PV, discount, equipment cost, initial JE**

\[
PV = \frac{\$116{,}640}{(1.08)^2} = \frac{\$116{,}640}{1.1664} = \$100{,}000
\]

(Check: \( \$100{,}000 \times 1.08^2 = \$100{,}000 \times 1.1664 = \$116{,}640 \).)

Discount = $116,640 − $100,000 = **$16,640**  
Equipment cost = $25,000 + $100,000 = **$125,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 125,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 16,640 | |
| Notes Payable | | 116,640 |
| Cash | | 25,000 |
| *Acquire equipment: cash + 2-year 0% note at PV (market 8%)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 125,000 + 16,640 = 141,640; Cr 116,640 + 25,000 = 141,640. **Balanced.**  
Initial net CV of note = **$100,000**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (effective interest; 8%)**

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest expense (8% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $100,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $8,000 | $8,000 | 108,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 8,640 | 8,640 | 116,640 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$16,640** | **$16,640** | |

**Schedule checks:** Y1: $100,000 × 8% = $8,000; Y2: $108,000 × 8% = $8,640; totals = discount $16,640; maturity CV = face.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 8,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 8,000 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr 8,000. **Balanced.**  
Net CV at Dec. 31, Y1 = **$108,000**.

**d. December 31, Year 2 — interest and maturity settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 8,640 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 8,640 |
| *Accrue interest; amortize remaining discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,640 = Cr 8,640. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = $0; net CV = face **$116,640**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Payable | 116,640 | |
| Cash | | 116,640 |
| *Pay face of note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 116,640 = Cr 116,640. **Balanced.**

**e. Balance sheet at December 31, Year 1**

| Presentation | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Notes Payable (face) | $116,640 |

| **Net liability** | **$108,000** |

(Remaining life is **one year** at Dec. 31, Year 1 → typically **current** liability presentation of the net amount, subject to company policy on classification of the current portion.)

**Key insight:** Same skill path as Q1 with a shorter term and different rate/amounts—the schedule still forces interest expense to equal beginning CV × market rate and fully extinguishes the discount by settlement.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angles — Imputed rate (FV of PPE known) + equity issuance + settlement
**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** (1) Noninterest note when FV of equipment is determinable and market rate is unknown—impute effective rate from FV and face; full subsequent measurement schedule; period-end JEs; maturity. (2) Acquisition of equipment by issuing publicly traded common stock (FV of securities more clearly evident).  
**Scenario:**  
**Part A — Debt, FV of asset known (impute rate)**  
**Cedarline Packaging LLC** (calendar year-end) acquires a machine on **January 1, Year 1** by issuing a **two-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$48,400**, due **December 31, Year 2**. The **fair value of the machine is $40,000** and is clearly evident. The market rate for similar notes is **not** quoted. Cedarline uses the **effective interest method**. **Ignore depreciation.**

**Part B — Equity**  
On **March 1, Year 1**, Cedarline acquires used equipment that is **not** normally sold before end of life (no verifiable FV for the used unit). In payment, Cedarline issues **4,000 shares** of its **$1 par** common stock. The stock is **publicly traded** at **$18 per share** on March 1. Cedarline has millions of shares outstanding.

**Required:**  
a. **Part A:** Compute the **imputed effective interest rate**, the **discount**, and prepare the **January 1, Year 1** acquisition JE.  
b. **Part A:** Prepare the **2-year effective interest subsequent measurement schedule**.  
c. **Part A:** Prepare **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** interest JEs and the **Year 2 maturity settlement JE**.  
d. **Part B:** Prepare the **March 1** JE to acquire equipment with common stock.  
e. Briefly state the measurement rule when **both** FV of the asset and FV of publicly traded stock are clearly evident (which amount is preferred for the equity case in the chapter?).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Imputed rate, discount, initial JE (Part A)**

Because the note is noninterest-bearing and the market rate is unknown, PV is set equal to the **fair value of the machine** ($40,000).

\[
(1 + r)^2 = \frac{\$48{,}400}{\$40{,}000} = 1.21 \implies 1 + r = 1.10 \implies r = \mathbf{10\%}
\]

Discount = Face − FV = $48,400 − $40,000 = **$8,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 40,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 8,400 | |
| Notes Payable | | 48,400 |
| *Acquire machine at FV; impute 10% effective rate on 0% note* | | |

**Check:** Dr 40,000 + 8,400 = 48,400; Cr 48,400. **Balanced.**  
Initial net CV = **$40,000**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (Part A) — emphasis**

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest expense (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $40,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $4,000 | $4,000 | 44,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 4,400 | 4,400 | 48,400 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$8,400** | **$8,400** | |

**Schedule checks:** Y1: $40,000 × 10% = $4,000; Y2: $44,000 × 10% = $4,400; total amort = discount; maturity CV = face.

**c. Period-end and maturity JEs (Part A)**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 4,000 |
| *Period-end interest; amortize discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,000 = Cr 4,000. **Balanced.**  
Net CV = **$44,000**.

*December 31, Year 2 — interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,400 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 4,400 |
| *Accrue remaining interest; clear discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,400 = Cr 4,400. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2 — maturity settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Notes Payable | 48,400 | |
| Cash | | 48,400 |
| *Pay face of note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 48,400 = Cr 48,400. **Balanced.**

**d. March 1 equity issuance (Part B)**

Measurement = 4,000 shares × $18 market price = **$72,000** (FV of publicly traded securities is clearly evident; equipment FV not verifiable).

Common stock (par) = 4,000 × $1 = **$4,000**  
APIC = $72,000 − $4,000 = **$68,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 72,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 4,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 68,000 |
| *Acquire used equipment by issuing 4,000 shares at $18 market* | | |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 = Cr 4,000 + 68,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Measurement hierarchy (equity)**  
Record at **FV of securities issued** when clearly evident (e.g., public market price); if securities FV is **not** clearly evident (e.g., closely held stock), use **FV of the fixed asset**. When both are clearly evident, practice/chapter guidance emphasizes the amount that is **more clearly evident**—for publicly traded shares that is typically the stock’s market price.

**Key insight:** Two initial-measurement forks for LO 11-3: (1) **debt**—more clearly evident of asset FV vs PV of payments (impute rate when asset FV is known and market rate is not); (2) **equity**—more clearly evident of stock FV vs asset FV. After a 0% note is on the books, the **effective-interest schedule** still governs subsequent measurement through maturity.

### Q4 — MC — Measurement basis for PPE acquired with debt or equity
**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** Classification / measurement — when equipment acquired with a note is recorded at face vs PV; when stock is used, measurement uses more clearly evident FV  

**Question 1:** On January 1, **Northlake Tools** acquires equipment by issuing a **two-year note** with face **$80,000** that **requires annual interest payments at the market rate of 9%**. Principal is due at maturity. The equipment’s cash price is not separately quoted. How should Northlake initially measure the **equipment** and the **note**?

- A) Equipment $80,000; note net of a discount computed at an imputed rate different from 9%  
- B) Equipment at the present value of principal only, ignoring contractual interest payments  
- C) Equipment **$80,000** and Notes Payable **$80,000** (face), because the stated rate equals the market rate  
- D) Equipment only at the sum of future interest payments, with no liability for principal until paid  

**Answer:** **C.** When a fixed asset is purchased with a note that **bears interest at the market rate**, the acquisition is recorded at the **face value** of the note; there is no rate-driven discount. (Interest expense arises later from the contractual cash interest, not from discount amortization of a below-market note.)

---

**Question 2:** **Pinnacle Fabricators** issues **1,500 shares** of its **$5 par** common stock for equipment. The stock is **publicly traded at $22** per share. The equipment’s stand-alone fair value is **not** known. What is the **debit to Equipment** and the **credit to Common Stock**?

- A) Equipment $7,500; Common Stock $33,000  
- B) Equipment $33,000; Common Stock $7,500  
- C) Equipment $33,000; Common Stock $33,000  
- D) Equipment $7,500; Common Stock $7,500  

**Answer:** **B.** Measurement = 1,500 × $22 = **$33,000** (FV of publicly traded shares). Common Stock is credited for **par only**: 1,500 × $5 = **$7,500**; the excess **$25,500** is APIC (not asked but implied). Equipment is **not** limited to par.
