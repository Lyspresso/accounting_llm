# Agent 241 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-5

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Record dividend distributions, including cash, property, and liquidating  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash dividends — declaration (initial recognition):** Dr Retained Earnings / Cr Dividends Payable (enforceable liability)
- **Timeline:** no entry on ex-dividend date or date of record; **settlement** Dr Dividends Payable / Cr Cash
- **Subsequent measurement / allocation schedule:** cumulative preferred arrears first, then current preferred, remainder to common (nonparticipating); multi-period arrears rollforward
- **Property dividends:** remeasure asset to **fair value** at declaration (holding gain/loss in income); declare at FV; distribute asset against Property Dividends Payable; post-declaration FV changes ignored
- **Liquidating dividends:** return of capital — debit **Additional Paid-In Capital** (not RE) for the liquidating portion
- **Period-end presentation:** unpaid declared dividends = current liability; RE reduced only for earnings distributions; APIC reduced for liquidating portion
- **Equity effect schedule:** roll forward RE and APIC for all distribution types in a period

### Q1 — CORE — Cash (cumulative preferred schedule), property, and liquidating dividends with settlement
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Preferred allocation schedule; initial recognition of cash/property/liquidating dividends; property FV remeasurement; settlement (payment/distribution); period-end unpaid dividends  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Trail Outfitters Inc.** (calendar year) has the following capital structure at January 1, Year 1:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 7%, $20 par, 25,000 shares issued and outstanding | $500,000 |
| Common stock, $5 par, 80,000 shares issued and outstanding | 400,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 2,200,000 |
| Retained earnings | 3,100,000 |

Preferred stock is **cumulative and nonparticipating**. At January 1, Year 1, preferred dividends are **in arrears for three full years** (nothing was declared in the three preceding years).

**Transaction 1 — Cash dividend with preferred priority (declared late year; paid next year)**  
On **December 10, Year 1**, the board declares total cash dividends of **$200,000**, payable **January 25, Year 2**, to stockholders of record on **January 8, Year 2**.

**Transaction 2 — Property dividend**  
On **March 5, Year 1**, Redwood holds **2,500 shares** of **Harborlight Labs** common stock as an investment. Carrying value is **$42,000**. Fair value on March 5 is **$51,000**. The board declares a property dividend of all 2,500 Harborlight shares, payable **March 28** to holders of record **March 15**. Fair-value changes after the declaration date are ignored.

**Transaction 3 — Partially liquidating cash distribution**  
On **August 20, Year 1**, the board declares a cash distribution of **$90,000** and informs shareholders that **two-thirds** is a **liquidating dividend** (return of capital) and **one-third** is a distribution of retained earnings. Payable **September 10**; record date **August 31**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **preferred vs common cash-dividend allocation schedule** for the December 10 declaration (show arrears, current preferred, and common).  
b. Record the **December 10 declaration**, state the entry (if any) on the **January 8 record date**, and record the **January 25, Year 2 payment**.  
c. Record all **March 5** entries related to the property dividend and the **March 28 distribution**.  
d. Record the **August 20 declaration** and **September 10 payment** of the partially liquidating distribution.  
e. Prepare a **Year 1 equity-effects schedule** showing total reductions to (i) retained earnings and (ii) additional paid-in capital from the three distributions. At **December 31, Year 1**, state the **balance-sheet presentation** of any unpaid dividends from these transactions.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Allocation schedule (annual preferred dividend = $500,000 × 7% = $35,000)**

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred dividends in arrears (3 × $35,000) | 105,000 | — | 105,000 |
| Preferred current-year dividend | 35,000 | — | 35,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 60,000 | 60,000 |
| **Total declared** | **140,000** | **60,000** | **200,000** |

Check: 105,000 + 35,000 + 60,000 = 200,000. Preferred total = 105,000 + 35,000 = **140,000**.

**b. Cash dividend — declaration, record date, settlement**

December 10, Year 1 — declaration (initial recognition):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 200,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 140,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 60,000 |
| *To record cash dividends declared* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000.

January 8, Year 2 — date of record: **No entry.**

January 25, Year 2 — payment (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 140,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |
| *To record cash dividend payment* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000.

**c. Property dividend — fair-value remeasurement, declaration, distribution**

Holding gain = $51,000 − $42,000 = **$9,000**.

March 5 — remeasure investment to fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Harborlight Labs | 9,000 | |
| Unrealized Holding Gain—Income | | 9,000 |
| *To recognize holding gain before property dividend declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000. Carrying value after remeasurement = 42,000 + 9,000 = **$51,000**.

March 5 — declaration at fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 51,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 51,000 |
| *To record property dividend at fair value of asset to be distributed* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 51,000.

March 15 — date of record: **No entry.**

March 28 — distribution (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 51,000 | |
| Investment in Harborlight Labs | | 51,000 |
| *To distribute Harborlight shares to shareholders* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 51,000. Investment balance removed: 51,000 − 51,000 = 0.

**d. Partially liquidating cash distribution**

Liquidating portion = (2/3) × $90,000 = **$60,000** (debit APIC)  
Earnings portion = (1/3) × $90,000 = **$30,000** (debit RE)

August 20 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 30,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 60,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 90,000 |
| *To record cash distribution; two-thirds liquidating return of capital* | | |

**Check:** Dr 30,000 + 60,000 = Cr 90,000.

August 31 — date of record: **No entry.**

September 10 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 90,000 | |
| Cash | | 90,000 |
| *To pay cash distribution* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 90,000.

**e. Year 1 equity-effects schedule and period-end presentation**

| Equity reduction from Year 1 distributions | Amount |
|---|---:|
| RE — cash (Dec 10) | 200,000 |
| RE — property (Mar 5 at FV) | 51,000 |
| RE — nonliquidating portion (Aug 20) | 30,000 |
| **Total reduction to Retained Earnings** | **281,000** |
| APIC—Common — liquidating portion (Aug 20) | 60,000 |
| **Total reduction to Additional Paid-In Capital** | **60,000** |

(Note: the $9,000 holding gain increased income and therefore ultimately RE; it is **not** a dividend reduction.)

Unpaid dividends at December 31, Year 1 (declared Dec 10, paid next year):

| Liability (current) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 140,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 |
| **Total current Dividends Payable** | **200,000** |

Property and August cash distributions were fully settled in Year 1 (no remaining payable).

**Key insight:** Cash and property dividends reduce **retained earnings** at the distribution amount (property at **fair value** after a holding gain/loss at declaration). A **liquidating** portion is a return of capital and reduces **APIC**, not RE. Payment/distribution settles the payable; record and ex-dividend dates have no entries. Cumulative preferred requires an **allocation schedule** (arrears → current preferred → common).

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cash, property (loss case), and liquidating dividends
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — preferred allocation schedule; property remeasurement when FV < CV; liquidating split; settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Lumenbridge Logistics Co.** (calendar year) reports:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 5%, $100 par, 8,000 shares issued and outstanding | $800,000 |
| Common stock, $1 par, 500,000 shares issued and outstanding | 500,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 1,200,000 |
| Retained earnings | 2,000,000 |

Preferred is **cumulative and nonparticipating**. Preferred dividends are **in arrears for two years** at the start of Year 1.

**Cash:** On **November 12, Year 1**, Lumenbridge declares total cash dividends of **$175,000**, payable **December 18, Year 1**, to stockholders of record **December 1, Year 1**.

**Property:** On **June 8, Year 1**, Lumenbridge holds an investment in **Solstice Rail** stock with carrying value **$75,000**. Fair value on June 8 is **$68,000**. The board declares a property dividend of the entire investment, payable **June 30** to holders of record **June 18**.

**Liquidating mix:** On **September 5, Year 1**, Lumenbridge declares a cash distribution of **$50,000**; management announces that **80%** is liquidating (return of capital) and **20%** is from retained earnings. Payable **September 28**; record date **September 15**.

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the $175,000 cash dividend between preferred and common; record declaration (Nov 12) and payment (Dec 18).  
b. Record June 8 property-dividend entries (including any holding loss) and the June 30 distribution.  
c. Record September 5 declaration and September 28 payment of the mixed liquidating distribution.  
d. Prepare a **Year 1 equity-effects schedule** of total reductions to RE and to APIC from these three distributions.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cash dividend allocation and JEs**  
Annual preferred dividend = $800,000 × 5% = **$40,000**.

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred dividends in arrears (2 × $40,000) | 80,000 | — | 80,000 |
| Preferred current-year dividend | 40,000 | — | 40,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 55,000 | 55,000 |
| **Total** | **120,000** | **55,000** | **175,000** |

Check: 80,000 + 40,000 + 55,000 = 175,000.

November 12 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 175,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 120,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 55,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 175,000.

December 1 — record date: **No entry.**

December 18 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 120,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 55,000 | |
| Cash | | 175,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 175,000.

**b. Property dividend (fair value below carrying value — holding loss)**  
Holding loss = $75,000 − $68,000 = **$7,000**.

June 8 — remeasure:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Holding Loss—Income | 7,000 | |
| Investment in Solstice Rail | | 7,000 |
| *To recognize holding loss before property dividend declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,000. Carrying value after remeasurement = 75,000 − 7,000 = **$68,000**.

June 8 — declaration at fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 68,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 68,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 68,000.

June 18 — record date: **No entry.**

June 30 — distribution:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 68,000 | |
| Investment in Solstice Rail | | 68,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 68,000.

**c. Partially liquidating distribution**  
Liquidating = 80% × $50,000 = **$40,000**  
Earnings portion = 20% × $50,000 = **$10,000**

September 5 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 10,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 40,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 50,000 |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 + 40,000 = Cr 50,000.

September 15 — record date: **No entry.**

September 28 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 50,000 | |
| Cash | | 50,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 50,000.

**d. Year 1 equity-effects schedule**

| Equity reduction from Year 1 distributions | Amount |
|---|---:|
| RE — cash (Nov 12) | 175,000 |
| RE — property (June 8 at FV) | 68,000 |
| RE — nonliquidating portion (Sep 5) | 10,000 |
| **Total reduction to Retained Earnings** | **253,000** |
| APIC—Common — liquidating portion (Sep 5) | 40,000 |
| **Total reduction to Additional Paid-In Capital** | **40,000** |

(Note: the $7,000 holding loss reduced income/RE separately; it is **not** part of the dividend declaration amount.)

**Key insight:** Same core path as Q1 with new numbers. When investment FV is **below** CV, record a **holding loss** before declaring the property dividend at **fair value**. Liquidating portion still hits APIC; earnings distributions hit RE.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-period preferred arrears schedule, property CV rollforward, period-end unpaid liability
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent multi-period preferred dividend arrears schedule; property investment carrying-value rollforward through declaration and distribution; period-end current liability for unpaid cash dividends  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpine Chemtech Corp.** has outstanding:

- Preferred stock, **6%**, **$50 par**, **10,000 shares** outstanding → legal capital **$500,000**  
- Common stock, **$2 par**, **100,000 shares** outstanding  
- Preferred is **cumulative and nonparticipating**  
- At **January 1, Year 1**, there are **no preferred dividends in arrears**

Annual preferred dividend requirement = $500,000 × 6% = **$30,000**.

**Multi-period cash dividend history (common + preferred as allocated):**

| Year | Total cash dividends **declared** (year-end) | Notes |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | $0 | Board declares nothing |
| 2 | $30,000 | Only enough for **one** year of preferred (not full arrears + current) |
| 3 | $120,000 | Large declaration; payable **January 15, Year 4**; record date **January 2, Year 4** |

**Property dividend (Year 3):**  
On **April 1, Year 3**, Silverpine declares a property dividend of its entire investment in **Oakridge Sensors** common stock:

| Date | Event | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Jan 10, Year 2 | Purchased Oakridge Sensors shares for cash | $28,000 |
| Apr 1, Year 3 | Fair value at property-dividend **declaration** | $33,500 |
| Apr 20, Year 3 | Distributed to shareholders | — |

No other fair-value adjustments were recorded between purchase and April 1, Year 3. Post-declaration FV changes are ignored.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **three-year preferred dividend arrears / allocation schedule** showing, for each year: beginning arrears, current-year preference, total preferred claim before declaration, amount declared to preferred, amount declared to common, and **ending arrears**.  
b. Record the **Year 3 cash dividend declaration** (use the schedule), state any entry on the Year 4 record date, and record the **January 15, Year 4 payment**.  
c. Prepare a **property investment carrying-value rollforward** from purchase through distribution, and record all related Year 3 JEs (remeasurement, declaration, distribution).  
d. At **December 31, Year 3**, identify the **current liability** related to the Year 3 cash dividend and compute the **Year 3 reduction to retained earnings** from (i) the cash dividend declaration and (ii) the property dividend declaration.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Multi-period preferred arrears / allocation schedule**

Annual preference = **$30,000**.

| Year | Beg. arrears | Current preference | Preferred claim before decl. | Declared to preferred | Declared to common | End. arrears |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 30,000 | 30,000 | 0 | 0 | **30,000** |
| 2 | 30,000 | 30,000 | 60,000 | 30,000 | 0 | **30,000** |
| 3 | 30,000 | 30,000 | 60,000 | 60,000 | 60,000 | **0** |

**Year 2 detail:** Total declared $30,000 goes entirely to preferred (reduces claim but does not clear full $60,000 claim → $30,000 still in arrears after Year 2). Common receives **$0**.

**Year 3 detail:** Total declared $120,000:  
- Preferred: arrears $30,000 + current $30,000 = **$60,000**  
- Common: $120,000 − $60,000 = **$60,000**  
- Ending arrears = **$0**

Check Year 3: 60,000 + 60,000 = 120,000.

**b. Year 3 cash dividend declaration and Year 4 settlement**

December 31, Year 3 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 120,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 60,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 60,000 |
| *To record Year 3 cash dividends per allocation schedule* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 120,000.

January 2, Year 4 — date of record: **No entry.**

January 15, Year 4 — payment (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 60,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 120,000.

**c. Property investment CV rollforward and Year 3 JEs**

| Date | Event | Dr/(Cr) to Investment | End. CV |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 10, Y2 | Purchase | 28,000 | 28,000 |
| Apr 1, Y3 | Remeasure to FV (gain $5,500) | 5,500 | 33,500 |
| Apr 1, Y3 | Declaration (no change to asset yet) | — | 33,500 |
| Apr 20, Y3 | Distribution to shareholders | (33,500) | **0** |

Holding gain = $33,500 − $28,000 = **$5,500**.

April 1, Year 3 — remeasure:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Oakridge Sensors | 5,500 | |
| Unrealized Holding Gain—Income | | 5,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,500.

April 1, Year 3 — declaration at FV:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 33,500 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 33,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 33,500.

April 20, Year 3 — distribution:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 33,500 | |
| Investment in Oakridge Sensors | | 33,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 33,500.

**d. Period-end presentation and Year 3 RE reductions**

At December 31, Year 3:

| Current liability | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 60,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 |
| **Total Dividends Payable (current)** | **120,000** |

Property Dividends Payable = **$0** (settled April 20).

| Year 3 reduction to Retained Earnings | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash dividend declaration | 120,000 |
| Property dividend declaration (at FV) | 33,500 |
| **Total Year 3 RE reduction from these distributions** | **153,500** |

**Key insight:** Cumulative preferred arrears are a **priority claim**, not a liability until declared. A multi-period **arrears schedule** tracks how partial declarations leave residual arrears. Property dividends require a **CV → FV** rollforward at declaration, then removal of the asset at settlement. Unpaid cash dividends declared before year-end are **current liabilities**.

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement)
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Classification of which equity account is reduced; measurement basis for property dividends  

**Question 4A:**  
On the declaration date, a corporation informs shareholders that $48,000 of a $60,000 cash distribution is a **liquidating dividend** and $12,000 is from earnings. Which accounts are debited at declaration?

- A) Retained Earnings $60,000  
- B) Paid-In Capital (APIC) $60,000  
- C) Retained Earnings $12,000 and Paid-In Capital (APIC) $48,000  
- D) Dividends Expense $60,000  

**Answer:** **C.** The earnings portion ($12,000) reduces **retained earnings**; the liquidating portion ($48,000) is a return of capital and reduces **additional paid-in capital**. Dividends are not recorded as an expense.

**Question 4B:**  
When a property dividend is declared, the distribution is measured at:

- A) Historical cost of the asset only, with no gain or loss recognized  
- B) Fair value of the asset at the **declaration** date (after recognizing any holding gain or loss to remeasure the asset)  
- C) Fair value of the asset at the **distribution** date, with gains/losses after declaration recognized through equity  
- D) Book value of the asset at the distribution date only  

**Answer:** **B.** Property dividends are recorded at the **fair value** of the assets transferred. Any difference between carrying value and fair value is recognized as a holding gain or loss **at the declaration date** before recording the dividend; subsequent FV changes are ignored.
