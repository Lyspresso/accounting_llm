# Agent 240 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-4

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe and account for preferred stock  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition (equity preferred):** Dr Cash; Cr Preferred Stock at par; Cr Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock for excess over par (callable, convertible, or redeemable solely at holder option — non-mandatory)
- **Initial recognition (mandatorily redeemable):** classified as a **liability** at issue proceeds / present value of fixed redemption (ASC 480-10); **not** equity Preferred Stock
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** when both redemption amount and settlement date are fixed, measure the liability subsequently at PV of the amount to be paid, **accruing interest cost** using the rate implicit at inception; roll beginning CV → interest expense → ending CV each period until redemption equals fixed settlement amount
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr Interest Expense; Cr Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability for the period’s accretion
- **Settlement / maturity JE:** remove the fully accreted liability; pay cash equal to fixed redemption amount
- **Disposal — call (equity preferred):** remove Preferred Stock + related PIC—PS; cash at call price; excess of cash over equity carrying amount → **debit Retained Earnings**; shortfall → credit additional paid-in capital
- **Disposal — conversion (book value method):** remove Preferred Stock + PIC—PS; credit Common Stock at par of shares issued; remainder to PIC—Common Stock (or debit RE if common par exceeds preferred carrying amount)
- **Classification:** mandatorily redeemable → **liability**; callable by issuer → **permanent equity**; redeemable solely at stockholder option (not mandatory) → equity (core textbook treatment)

### Q1 — CORE — Mandatorily redeemable preferred: initial recognition, subsequent accretion schedule, period-end JE, and maturity settlement (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule for mandatorily redeemable preferred stock (fixed amount and fixed date); initial liability recognition; period-end interest accretion; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Rivertide Precision Tools** (calendar-year) issues **mandatorily redeemable preferred stock** on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cash proceeds at issuance | **$150,000** |
| Shares / formal description | 3,000 shares of $40 par preferred (form of shares; measured as liability) |
| Redemption | **Mandatory** cash redemption of **$199,650** on **December 31, Year 3** (fixed amount and fixed date; not solely upon liquidation) |
| Implicit effective interest rate at inception | **10%** |
| Periodic preferred dividends | Ignore for this problem (focus on ASC 480 subsequent measurement of the liability) |

No other preferred stock is outstanding. The instrument is not callable early.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry. State the balance-sheet **classification** immediately after issuance.  
b. Prepare the complete **subsequent measurement (accretion) schedule** for Years 1–3 showing beginning carrying amount, interest expense, and ending carrying amount.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting** journal entries for interest accretion.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 3** interest accretion entry **and** the **maturity/settlement** entry (two entries, in order).  
e. Reconcile total interest expense over the three-year life to redemption amount − issue proceeds.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition and classification**

*January 1, Year 1 — Issue mandatorily redeemable preferred stock*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 150,000 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 150,000 |
| *Initial recognition at issue proceeds / PV of fixed redemption (ASC 480)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 150,000 = Cr 150,000. **Balanced.**

**Classification:** **Noncurrent liability** (not equity). Do **not** credit Preferred Stock equity or PIC—Preferred Stock for a mandatorily redeemable instrument.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis) — 10% effective interest, fixed $199,650 at end of Year 3**

| Year | Beginning carrying amount | Interest expense (10%) | Ending carrying amount |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 150,000 | 15,000 | 165,000 |
| 2 | 165,000 | 16,500 | 181,500 |
| 3 | 181,500 | 18,150 | **199,650** |
| **Total** | | **49,650** | |

**Roll-forward check:** 150,000 + 15,000 + 16,500 + 18,150 = **199,650** (equals fixed redemption). ✓  
**Math:** Year 1: \(150{,}000 \times 0.10 = 15{,}000\); Year 2: \(165{,}000 \times 0.10 = 16{,}500\); Year 3: \(181{,}500 \times 0.10 = 18{,}150\).

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs (Years 1 and 2)**

*December 31, Year 1 — Accrete liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 15,000 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.** Ending liability = **$165,000**.

*December 31, Year 2 — Accrete liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 16,500 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 16,500 |

**Check:** Dr 16,500 = Cr 16,500. **Balanced.** Ending liability = **$181,500**.

**d. Year 3 accretion and maturity settlement**

*December 31, Year 3 — Final accretion*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 18,150 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 18,150 |

**Check:** Dr 18,150 = Cr 18,150. **Balanced.** Carrying amount now = **$199,650**.

*December 31, Year 3 — Redeem / settle at maturity*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 199,650 | |
| Cash | | 199,650 |
| *Mandatory cash redemption at fixed settlement amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 199,650 = Cr 199,650. **Balanced.**

**e. Interest total reconciliation**  
Total interest expense = \(15{,}000 + 16{,}500 + 18{,}150 = \mathbf{\$49{,}650}\).  
Redemption − proceeds = \(199{,}650 - 150{,}000 = \mathbf{\$49{,}650}\). ✓

**Key insight:** When amount and date are fixed, the **subsequent measurement schedule** is an effective-interest rollforward: each period’s interest increases the liability until the carrying amount equals the fixed cash redemption. Core preferred equity (callable/convertible) does **not** accrete this way after issuance.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Four-year mandatorily redeemable accretion and settlement
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: subsequent measurement schedule for mandatorily redeemable preferred (fixed amount/date); initial JE; multi-year period-end accretion; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Glacier Peak Components** (calendar year) issues mandatorily redeemable preferred stock on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cash proceeds at issuance | **$250,000** |
| Formal description | 5,000 shares of $25 par preferred (liability measurement applies) |
| Redemption | **Mandatory** cash redemption of **$303,877** on **December 31, Year 4** (fixed amount and date) |
| Implicit effective interest rate | **5%** |
| Periodic preferred dividends | Ignore for accretion focus |

**Required:**  
a. Record **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition**.  
b. Prepare the full **Years 1–4 subsequent measurement schedule** (beginning CA, interest, ending CA).  
c. Record **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 3** period-end accretion entries.  
d. Record **December 31, Year 4** final accretion and cash settlement (two entries).  
e. Compute cumulative interest expense over the four-year holding period and reconcile to redemption − proceeds.  
f. Immediately before December 31, Year 4 settlement (after Year-4 accretion), is the liability **current** or **noncurrent**? Briefly explain.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

*January 1, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 250,000 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 250,000 |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.** Classified as **liability**, not equity.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis) — 5% for 4 years**

| Year | Beginning CA | Interest expense (5%) | Ending CA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 250,000 | 12,500 | 262,500 |
| 2 | 262,500 | 13,125 | 275,625 |
| 3 | 275,625 | 13,781 | 289,406 |
| 4 | 289,406 | **14,471** | **303,877** |
| **Total** | | **53,877** | |

**Computations:**  
- Y1: \(250{,}000 \times 0.05 = 12{,}500\); end \(262{,}500\)  
- Y2: \(262{,}500 \times 0.05 = 13{,}125\); end \(275{,}625\)  
- Y3: \(275{,}625 \times 0.05 = 13{,}781.25\) → round interest **$13,781**; end \(275{,}625 + 13{,}781 = 289{,}406\)  
- Y4: plug to fixed redemption: \(303{,}877 - 289{,}406 = \mathbf{\$14{,}471}\) interest (avoids $1 rounding drift; \(289{,}406 \times 0.05 = 14{,}470.30\))

**Roll-forward check:** \(250{,}000 + 12{,}500 + 13{,}125 + 13{,}781 + 14{,}471 = 303{,}877\). ✓

**c. Selected period-end adjusting JEs**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 12,500 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 12,500 |

**Check:** Dr 12,500 = Cr 12,500. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 13,781 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 13,781 |

**Check:** Dr 13,781 = Cr 13,781. **Balanced.** Ending CA after Y3 = **$289,406**.

**d. Year 4 accretion and settlement**

*December 31, Year 4 — Final accretion*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 14,471 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 14,471 |

**Check:** Dr 14,471 = Cr 14,471. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 4 — Maturity settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 303,877 | |
| Cash | | 303,877 |

**Check:** Dr 303,877 = Cr 303,877. **Balanced.**

**e. Interest reconciliation**  
Total interest = \(12{,}500 + 13{,}125 + 13{,}781 + 14{,}471 = \mathbf{\$53{,}877}\).  
Redemption − proceeds = \(303{,}877 - 250{,}000 = \mathbf{\$53{,}877}\). ✓

**f. Current vs noncurrent**  
After Year-4 accretion and **immediately before** cash settlement on the maturity date, the full **$303,877** is a **current liability** (due within one year / on the reporting date). In Years 1–3, the instrument is **noncurrent** unless reclassified in the year of maturity under the entity’s current/noncurrent presentation policy.

**Key insight:** Extending the life (4 years vs 3) and changing the rate (5% vs 10%) only changes the **schedule arithmetic**; the measurement model is unchanged: accrete at the **implicit rate** until carrying amount equals the **fixed** settlement cash.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Callable preferred (equity) issuance, call at premium; convertible preferred issuance and conversion
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Equity preferred initial recognition; call/recall settlement with RE charge; convertible preferred conversion (book value method) as disposal/settlement of preferred equity  
**Scenario:**  
**Northshore Alloy Corp.** (calendar year) has **no** preferred stock outstanding at the start of Year 1. Two equity preferred issuances and two later settlements occur.

**Issue 1 — Callable preferred**  
- On **March 1, Year 1**, issues **4,000** shares of **7%** callable preferred stock, **$50 par**, at **$62** per share cash.  
- Call price: **$65** per share at the issuer’s option.  
- On **August 20, Year 2**, Northshore **calls all 4,000 shares** at $65 cash per share.

**Issue 2 — Convertible preferred**  
- On **June 15, Year 1**, issues **1,500** shares of **6%** convertible preferred stock, **$20 par**, at **$48** per share cash.  
- Each preferred share is convertible into **3** shares of **$2 par** common stock at the holder’s option.  
- On **November 10, Year 2**, holders convert **all 1,500** preferred shares into common (book value method; no gain/loss).

**Required:**  
a. Record the **March 1, Year 1** and **June 15, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entries.  
b. Record the **August 20, Year 2 call/recall** journal entry for Issue 1.  
c. Record the **November 10, Year 2 conversion** journal entry for Issue 2.  
d. For each issue, state the **balance-sheet equity (or liability) classification** immediately after issuance.  
e. Compute the **net change in total stockholders’ equity** from (i) the call alone and (ii) the conversion alone.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

*March 1, Year 1 — Callable preferred*  
Cash = \(4{,}000 \times 62 = \mathbf{\$248{,}000}\)  
Preferred Stock = \(4{,}000 \times 50 = \mathbf{\$200{,}000}\)  
PIC in Excess of Par—PS = \(248{,}000 - 200{,}000 = \mathbf{\$48{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 248,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Callable ($50 par) | | 200,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 48,000 |
| *Issue 4,000 callable preferred at $62* | | |

**Check:** Dr 248,000 = Cr 200,000 + 48,000. **Balanced.**

*June 15, Year 1 — Convertible preferred*  
Cash = \(1{,}500 \times 48 = \mathbf{\$72{,}000}\)  
Preferred Stock = \(1{,}500 \times 20 = \mathbf{\$30{,}000}\)  
PIC—PS = \(72{,}000 - 30{,}000 = \mathbf{\$42{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 72,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Convertible ($20 par) | | 30,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 42,000 |
| *Issue 1,500 convertible preferred at $48* | | |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 = Cr 30,000 + 42,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Call / recall (disposal of equity preferred)**

Carrying amount of Issue 1 equity = \(200{,}000 + 48{,}000 = \mathbf{\$248{,}000}\).  
Cash paid = \(4{,}000 \times 65 = \mathbf{\$260{,}000}\).  
Excess cash over carrying amount = \(260{,}000 - 248{,}000 = \mathbf{\$12{,}000}\) → **debit Retained Earnings** (not an expense/loss in the P&L sense for equity transactions).

*August 20, Year 2 — Call preferred*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Callable | 200,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 48,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | 12,000 | |
| Cash | | 260,000 |
| *Recall 4,000 shares at $65 call price* | | |

**Check:** Dr 200,000 + 48,000 + 12,000 = 260,000 = Cr Cash. **Balanced.**

**c. Conversion (book value method)**

Preferred carrying amount removed = \(30{,}000 + 42{,}000 = \mathbf{\$72{,}000}\).  
Common shares issued = \(1{,}500 \times 3 = \mathbf{4{,}500}\) shares.  
Common Stock at par = \(4{,}500 \times 2 = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}\).  
PIC in Excess of Par—Common Stock = \(72{,}000 - 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$63{,}000}\).

*November 10, Year 2 — Convert preferred to common*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | 30,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 42,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 9,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 63,000 |
| *Convert 1,500 preferred into 4,500 common (book value method)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 = Cr 9,000 + 63,000. **Balanced.**  
**No net change to total equity** (reclassification within equity only).

**d. Classification after issuance**  
- Issue 1 (callable by **issuer**): **permanent stockholders’ equity** (Preferred Stock + PIC—PS).  
- Issue 2 (convertible at **holder** option; not mandatorily redeemable): **permanent stockholders’ equity**.

**e. Equity impact of settlements**  
(i) **Call:** assets down $260,000; equity accounts removed $248,000 + RE debit $12,000 → **total equity decreases by $260,000**.  
(ii) **Conversion:** **total equity change = $0** (PS and PIC—PS replaced by CS and PIC—CS of equal total).

**Key insight:** Equity preferred is recorded at par + APIC and is removed at call or conversion without interest accretion. A **call price above carrying amount** reduces **retained earnings**; conversion at book value merely reclassifies equity.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of preferred stock features
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Balance-sheet classification of mandatorily redeemable vs callable vs optionally redeemable preferred stock  
**Question:**  
Which preferred stock instrument is reported as a **liability** (not equity) on the balance sheet under U.S. GAAP (ASC 480), assuming redemption is **not** solely upon liquidation of the entity?

- A) 8% preferred stock callable solely at the **issuer’s** option at a fixed call price  
- B) 6% preferred stock convertible into common stock solely at the **holder’s** option  
- C) Preferred stock that is **mandatorily redeemable** for a fixed cash amount on a fixed future date  
- D) Preferred stock redeemable solely at the **stockholder’s** option at a fixed price (not mandatory; redemption not required)

**Answer:** **C.** Mandatorily redeemable preferred stock embodies an unconditional obligation to transfer assets at a specified or determinable date and is classified as a **liability** and measured subsequently (when amount and date are fixed) at the present value of the amount to be paid, with interest accretion. Callable-by-issuer preferred (A) and ordinary convertible preferred (B) are equity. Preferred redeemable only at the holder’s option (D) is treated as equity in the core textbook presentation (not mandatorily redeemable). Temporary equity for SEC registrants is Expanding Your Knowledge and is not required here.

### Q5 — MC — Conversion book value method
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Book-value conversion of convertible preferred into common stock  
**Question:**  
On January 1, **Lakeview Ortho** issued 800 shares of convertible preferred stock ($10 par) for $40,000 cash. Each preferred share is convertible into 5 shares of $1 par common. On July 1, holders convert all 800 preferred shares. Using the **book value method**, what is the credit to **Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock**?

- A) $0  
- B) $4,000  
- C) $32,000  
- D) $36,000  

**Answer:** **D.**  
Preferred carrying amount = $40,000 (par $8,000 + PIC—PS $32,000).  
Common shares issued = \(800 \times 5 = 4{,}000\); Common Stock credit = \(4{,}000 \times \$1 = \$4{,}000\).  
PIC—Common = \(40{,}000 - 4{,}000 = \mathbf{\$36{,}000}\).  
(Entry: Dr Preferred Stock 8,000; Dr PIC—PS 32,000; Cr Common Stock 4,000; Cr PIC—CS 36,000.)
