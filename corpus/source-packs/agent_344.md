# Agent 344 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-6

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for errors in reporting property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (wrong vs correct):** capital asset must be capitalized to Equipment (not expensed as maintenance); ordinary repairs must be expensed (not capitalized)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** year-by-year **as recorded** vs **as correct** depreciation (or omitted depreciation); cumulative AD and retained-earnings misstatement
- **Prior period adjustment JE:** after financial statements are released, correct Equipment / Accumulated Depreciation against **Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment**; restate prior periods
- **Same-period correction:** reverse through current-period accounts if discovered before books close / FS release (no PPA)
- **Period-end adjusting JE after correction (emphasis):** resume correct straight-line depreciation on the corrected carrying amount and remaining life
- **Disposal JE after error correction:** remove cost and **corrected** AD; gain/loss = proceeds vs corrected book value; also correct a misrecorded disposal via PPA
- **Classification:** PPE reporting **errors** → retrospective restatement + PPA; **changes in estimate** → prospective only (no PPA)
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Capital asset incorrectly expensed: schedule, PPA, period-end dep, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-6  
**Concept:** Initial recognition error (equipment expensed as maintenance); multi-year subsequent measurement of omitted depreciation; prior period adjustment; period-end adjusting depreciation after correction; disposal using corrected book value  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironvale Packaging Co.** (calendar year-end) purchased packaging equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$96,000** cash. Management estimated residual value of **$6,000** and a useful life of **10 years**. Ironvale uses **straight-line** depreciation. The bookkeeper **incorrectly expensed the entire $96,000 as maintenance** in Year 1 and never placed the asset on the books. **No depreciation** was recorded in any year for this equipment.

The error is detected on **January 1, Year 3**, after financial statements for Years 1–2 have been released. The error is **material**. **Ignore income taxes.**

After correction, Ironvale records correct depreciation for Years 3 and 4. On **January 1, Year 5**, it sells the equipment for **$55,000** cash.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **incorrect initial recognition** journal entry recorded on January 1, Year 1, and the **correct** entry that should have been recorded.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement comparison schedule** for Years 1–2 showing depreciation as recorded (none), correct depreciation, annual understatement of depreciation, and cumulative AD and book value at January 1, Year 3 (as if correctly accounted). Also compute the cumulative effect on retained earnings.  
c. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 prior period adjustment** journal entry. Prove Dr = Cr.  
d. Compute post-correction carrying amount, remaining life, and annual depreciation. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting** entry for depreciation (emphasis).  
e. Roll Accumulated Depreciation and book value through Year 4; prepare the **January 1, Year 5 disposal** journal entry (sale for $55,000). Prove the gain or loss.  
f. **Classification:** In 2–3 sentences, explain why this is an **accounting error** (retrospective / PPA) rather than a **change in estimate**.

**Answer key:**

**a. Initial recognition — wrong vs correct**

*January 1, Year 1 — Incorrect entry as recorded*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Maintenance Expense | 96,000 | |
| Cash | | 96,000 |
| *Capital equipment incorrectly expensed* | | |

**Check:** Dr 96,000 = Cr 96,000. **Balanced.**

*January 1, Year 1 — Correct entry that should have been recorded*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 96,000 | |
| Cash | | 96,000 |
| *Capitalize equipment at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 96,000 = Cr 96,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — Years 1–2**

Correct annual straight-line depreciation:

\[
\frac{\$96{,}000 - \$6{,}000}{10} = \$9{,}000
\]

| Year | Dep recorded | Correct dep | Understatement of dep | Ending AD (correct) | Correct book value |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 9,000 | 9,000 | 9,000 | 87,000 |
| 2 | 0 | 9,000 | 9,000 | 18,000 | 78,000 |
| **Cumulative at 1/1/Y3** | **0** | **18,000** | **18,000** | **18,000** | **78,000** |

*Correct period-end entry that should have been made each of Years 1 and 2:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000 each year.

**Cumulative income / RE effect through December 31, Year 2:**

| Component | Amount | Direction |
|---|---:|---|
| Maintenance expense recorded in error (Y1) | $96,000 | Expense **overstated** → income/RE **understated** $96,000 |
| Depreciation never recorded (2 × $9,000) | 18,000 | Expense **understated** → income/RE **overstated** $18,000 |
| **Net overstatement of expense (= understatement of RE)** | **$78,000** | |

Balance-sheet misstatements at January 1, Year 3 **before** correction:

| Account | Misstatement |
|---|---|
| Equipment | **Understated $96,000** |
| Accumulated Depreciation | **Understated $18,000** |
| Net assets | **Understated $78,000** |
| Retained earnings | **Understated $78,000** |

**c. Prior period adjustment — January 1, Year 3**

*January 1, Year 3 — To capitalize equipment previously expensed and catch up correct AD*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 96,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,000 |
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | | 78,000 |
| *Place asset on books at cost; establish AD for 2 years; restore beginning RE for net prior understatement of income* | | |

**Check:** Dr 96,000 = Cr 18,000 + 78,000 = **96,000**. **Balanced.**

After the entry: Equipment $96,000; AD $18,000; carrying amount **$78,000**; RE increased by $78,000.

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 3 (emphasis)**

Remaining useful life from January 1, Year 3 = 10 − 2 = **8 years**  
Remaining depreciable base = $78,000 − $6,000 residual = **$72,000**  
Annual depreciation (Y3 onward) = $72,000 / 8 = **$9,000** (matches original correct annual amount)

*December 31, Year 3 — Depreciation after correction*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |
| *Period-end adjusting JE: correct SL depreciation on corrected BV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**

AD at December 31, Year 3 = $18,000 + $9,000 = **$27,000**  
Book value = $96,000 − $27,000 = **$69,000**

**e. Roll-forward through Year 4 and disposal — January 1, Year 5**

Year 4 depreciation (same $9,000):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**

| Date | AD balance | Book value ($96,000 − AD) |
|---|---:|---:|
| After PPA, 1/1/Y3 | 18,000 | 78,000 |
| 12/31/Y3 | 27,000 | 69,000 |
| 12/31/Y4 (= 1/1/Y5 before sale) | 36,000 | 60,000 |

Sale proceeds $55,000 − book value $60,000 = **Loss $5,000**

*January 1, Year 5 — Sale of equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 55,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 36,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 5,000 | |
| Equipment | | 96,000 |

**Check:** Dr 55,000 + 36,000 + 5,000 = **96,000** = Cr 96,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Classification — error vs change in estimate**  
Expensing a capital acquisition that should have been capitalized is a **mistake in applying GAAP** to facts known at acquisition—an **accounting error**, not a revision based on new information. **Errors** require **retrospective restatement** of prior periods and a **PPA to beginning Retained Earnings** (plus correction of Equipment and Accumulated Depreciation). A **change in estimate** (e.g., revising remaining life because of new usage data) is applied **prospectively only**—no PPA and no restatement of prior depreciation.

**Key insight:** When a capital asset is incorrectly expensed, the PPA **puts cost on the books**, **establishes AD for prior years that should have been depreciated**, and **credits RE** for the net prior understatement of income (cost − cumulative correct dep). After correction, the period-end adjusting JE uses the **corrected BV and remaining life**. Disposal gain/loss must use the **corrected** book value.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin: capital asset incorrectly expensed
**LO:** LO 12-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: equipment expensed as maintenance; multi-year omitted-depreciation schedule; PPA; period-end adjusting JE; disposal with corrected BV  
**Scenario:**  
**Bayside Labeling Inc.** (calendar year-end) purchased production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$165,000** cash. Residual value **$15,000**; useful life **8 years**; **straight-line**. The entire cost was **incorrectly charged to Maintenance Expense** in Year 1; the asset was never capitalized and **no depreciation** was recorded.

The error is discovered on **January 1, Year 4**, after Years 1–3 financial statements have been released. The error is **material**. **Ignore income taxes.**

After correction, Bayside records correct depreciation for Years 4 and 5. On **January 1, Year 6**, it sells the equipment for **$70,000** cash.

**Required:**  
a. Incorrect vs correct **initial recognition** entries on January 1, Year 1.  
b. **Subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 (recorded vs correct depreciation; cumulative AD and RE effect at January 1, Year 4).  
c. **January 1, Year 4 prior period adjustment** JE; prove Dr = Cr.  
d. Post-correction annual depreciation and **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting** entry.  
e. Roll AD/BV through Year 5; **January 1, Year 6 disposal** JE; prove gain or loss.  
f. Prove every JE in (c), (d), and (e) balances.

**Answer key:**

**a. Initial recognition**

*Incorrect (as recorded):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Maintenance Expense | 165,000 | |
| Cash | | 165,000 |

*Correct (should have been):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 165,000 | |
| Cash | | 165,000 |

**Check:** both Dr = Cr = 165,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — Years 1–3**

Correct annual depreciation:

\[
\frac{\$165{,}000 - \$15{,}000}{8} = \$18{,}750
\]

| Year | Dep recorded | Correct dep | Understatement of dep | Ending AD (correct) | Correct book value |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 18,750 | 18,750 | 18,750 | 146,250 |
| 2 | 0 | 18,750 | 18,750 | 37,500 | 127,500 |
| 3 | 0 | 18,750 | 18,750 | 56,250 | 108,750 |
| **Cumulative at 1/1/Y4** | **0** | **56,250** | **56,250** | **56,250** | **108,750** |

**Cumulative RE effect at January 1, Year 4:**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Maintenance expense recorded in error (Y1) | $165,000 overstated expense |
| Depreciation never recorded (3 × $18,750) | 56,250 understated expense |
| **Net overstatement of expense (= understatement of RE)** | **$108,750** |

Net assets understated by $165,000 − $56,250 = **$108,750**.

**c. Prior period adjustment — January 1, Year 4**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 165,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 56,250 |
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | | 108,750 |

**Check:** Dr 165,000 = Cr 56,250 + 108,750 = **165,000**. **Balanced.**

Correct carrying amount after PPA = $165,000 − $56,250 = **$108,750**.

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 4**

Remaining life = 8 − 3 = **5 years**  
Remaining depreciable base = $108,750 − $15,000 = **$93,750**  
Annual depreciation = $93,750 / 5 = **$18,750**

*December 31, Year 4 — Depreciation after correction*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 18,750 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,750 |

**Check:** Dr 18,750 = Cr 18,750. **Balanced.**

AD at 12/31/Y4 = $56,250 + $18,750 = **$75,000**  
Book value = $165,000 − $75,000 = **$90,000**

**e. Roll-forward through Year 5 and disposal — January 1, Year 6**

Year 5 depreciation ($18,750):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 18,750 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,750 |

**Check:** Dr 18,750 = Cr 18,750. **Balanced.**

| Date | AD balance | Book value ($165,000 − AD) |
|---|---:|---:|
| After PPA, 1/1/Y4 | 56,250 | 108,750 |
| 12/31/Y4 | 75,000 | 90,000 |
| 12/31/Y5 (= 1/1/Y6 before sale) | 93,750 | 71,250 |

Sale proceeds $70,000 − book value $71,250 = **Loss $1,250**

*January 1, Year 6 — Sale of equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 70,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 93,750 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 1,250 | |
| Equipment | | 165,000 |

**Check:** Dr 70,000 + 93,750 + 1,250 = **165,000** = Cr 165,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Balance proof summary**

| Entry | Debits | Credits | Balanced? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| PPA 1/1/Y4 | 165,000 | 165,000 | Yes |
| Dep 12/31/Y4 | 18,750 | 18,750 | Yes |
| Dep Y5 | 18,750 | 18,750 | Yes |
| Disposal 1/1/Y6 | 165,000 | 165,000 | Yes |

**Key insight:** Same path as Q1 with all numbers changed. Net RE credit on the PPA = **cost − cumulative correct depreciation omitted**. Post-correction period-end dep returns to the original correct annual amount when residual and total life are unchanged.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Wrong salvage value + misrecorded disposal
**LO:** LO 12-6  
**Concept:** Depreciation system error (salvage entered wrong) with subsequent measurement schedule and PPA; period-end adjusting JE after correction; separate prior-period correction of a misrecorded disposal of fully depreciated equipment  
**Scenario:**  
**Part A — Wrong salvage value**  
**Hollowbrook Millworks** purchased equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$280,000** cash. Intended residual value **$10,000**; useful life **12 years**; **straight-line**. When the asset was entered into the depreciation system, salvage was inadvertently entered as **$70,000** instead of **$10,000**. Depreciation for Years 1–3 was computed using the wrong salvage. The error is discovered on **January 1, Year 4**, after Years 1–3 financial statements have been released. The error is **material**. **Ignore income taxes.**

**Part B — Misrecorded disposal**  
Also on **January 1, Year 4**, Hollowbrook discovers that in **Year 3**, fully depreciated equipment with original cost **$40,000** and **no salvage** was sold for **$3,500** cash. The company’s only entry for the sale was:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,500 | |
| Equipment | | 3,500 |

(The related Accumulated Depreciation of $40,000 was never removed, and no gain was recorded.) Year 3 statements have already been released. **Ignore income taxes.**

**Required:**  
**Part A**  
a. Compute incorrect annual depreciation, correct annual depreciation, and under-depreciation per year.  
b. Prepare a **Years 1–3 subsequent measurement schedule** (dep recorded vs correct; cumulative excess/deficiency of AD).  
c. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4 PPA** for the salvage-value error.  
d. Compute post-correction annual depreciation and prepare the **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting** entry.  

**Part B**  
e. State the misstatements in Equipment, Accumulated Depreciation, net assets, and Retained Earnings immediately before correction of the disposal error.  
f. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4 PPA** to correct the misrecorded disposal. Prove Dr = Cr.  
g. **Alternate same-period timing (Part A only):** Assume instead the salvage error is discovered on **December 28, Year 1**, after Year-1 depreciation of $17,500 has been posted but **before** Year 1 books close and FS release. Prepare the Year 1 correcting entry (no PPA) to bring depreciation to the correct amount.

**Answer key:**

**Part A**

**a. Depreciation amounts**

Incorrect annual dep (salvage entered $70,000):

\[
\frac{\$280{,}000 - \$70{,}000}{12} = \$17{,}500
\]

Correct annual dep (intended salvage $10,000):

\[
\frac{\$280{,}000 - \$10{,}000}{12} = \$22{,}500
\]

Under-depreciation per year = $22,500 − $17,500 = **$5,000**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — Years 1–3**

| Year | Dep recorded (wrong salvage) | Correct dep | Under-dep | Ending AD (as recorded) | Ending AD (correct) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 17,500 | 22,500 | 5,000 | 17,500 | 22,500 |
| 2 | 17,500 | 22,500 | 5,000 | 35,000 | 45,000 |
| 3 | 17,500 | 22,500 | 5,000 | 52,500 | 67,500 |
| **Cumulative at 1/1/Y4** | **52,500** | **67,500** | **15,000** | **52,500** | **67,500** |

*Illustrative period-end entry each of Years 1–3 (as incorrectly recorded):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 17,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 17,500 |

**Check:** Dr 17,500 = Cr 17,500 each year.

Misstatements at January 1, Year 4 **before** correction:
- Accumulated Depreciation **understated $15,000**
- Retained earnings **overstated $15,000** (three years of under-depreciation)
- Carrying amount **overstated $15,000** ($280,000 − $52,500 = $227,500 recorded vs correct $212,500)

**c. Prior period adjustment — January 1, Year 4 (salvage error)**

*January 1, Year 4 — To correct depreciation error (wrong salvage entered)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 15,000 |
| *Increase AD to correct cumulative under-depreciation; reduce beginning RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

Correct AD after entry = $52,500 + $15,000 = **$67,500**  
Correct carrying amount = $280,000 − $67,500 = **$212,500**

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 4**

Remaining useful life from January 1, Year 4 = 12 − 3 = **9 years**  
Remaining depreciable base = $212,500 − $10,000 = **$202,500**  
Annual depreciation (Y4 onward) = $202,500 / 9 = **$22,500**

*December 31, Year 4 — Depreciation after correction*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 22,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 22,500 |

**Check:** Dr 22,500 = Cr 22,500. **Balanced.**

AD at December 31, Year 4 = $67,500 + $22,500 = **$90,000**  
Book value = $280,000 − $90,000 = **$190,000**

**Part B**

**e. Misstatements from the Year 3 disposal error (before correction)**

Correct disposal entry would have been:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 40,000 | |
| Equipment | | 40,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 3,500 |

**Check:** Dr 3,500 + 40,000 = 43,500; Cr 40,000 + 3,500 = 43,500. **Balanced.**

As recorded vs correct:

| Account | Misstatement |
|---|---|
| Equipment | **Overstated $36,500** (only $3,500 removed; $36,500 cost remains) |
| Accumulated Depreciation | **Overstated $40,000** (AD never removed) |
| Net assets (PPE net) | **Understated $3,500** ($36,500 equip overstatement − $40,000 AD overstatement) |
| Retained earnings | **Understated $3,500** (gain never recognized) |

**f. Prior period adjustment — January 1, Year 4 (disposal error)**

*January 1, Year 4 — To correct misrecorded disposal of fully depreciated equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 40,000 | |
| Equipment | | 36,500 |
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | | 3,500 |
| *Remove remaining cost and related AD; recognize previously unrecorded gain through beginning RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr 40,000 = Cr 36,500 + 3,500 = **40,000**. **Balanced.**

After the entry: the fully depreciated asset is fully off the books; beginning RE is increased by the $3,500 gain that belonged in Year 3.

**g. Same-period discovery of salvage error (before FS release) — December 28, Year 1**  
Year-1 depreciation already posted at $17,500; correct amount is $22,500. Difference = **$5,000**. Use current-period accounts (no PPA):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,000 |
| *Catch up Year-1 depreciation to correct salvage; books not yet closed* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,000 = Cr 5,000. **Balanced.**  
Net Year-1 depreciation after correction = $17,500 + $5,000 = **$22,500**.

**Key insight:** Wrong salvage **under-depreciates** → **debit RE—PPA, credit AD** for cumulative deficiency; then period-end dep uses correct residual and remaining life. A botched disposal that only credits Equipment for proceeds leaves cost and AD stranded—correct with a **PPA** that clears AD and residual cost and restores the unrecorded gain to beginning RE. Same-period discovery uses **current expense/AD accounts**, not PPA.

### Q4 — MC (classification / effect)
**LO:** LO 12-6  
**Concept:** MC classification and quantitative effect of PPE reporting errors on retained earnings  

**Question 1:**  
On January 1, Year 1, **Pinecrest Tools** acquired equipment for **$240,000** with a **6-year** useful life and **$0** residual, using straight-line depreciation. In Year 1, the company **recorded annual depreciation twice** ($40,000 × 2 = $80,000 total). Separately, equipment purchased on **December 1, Year 1** for **$180,000** (6-year life, $0 residual, SL) was **not depreciated** in Year 1. Both errors are discovered in Year 2 after Year 1 statements were released. Ignoring taxes, what is the net prior period adjustment to **beginning Retained Earnings** on January 1, Year 2?

- A) $43,000 increase  
- B) $37,500 increase  
- C) $37,500 decrease  
- D) $42,500 increase  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Double-recording depreciation overstated Year-1 expense by **$40,000** → RE understated $40,000.  
Missed depreciation on Dec 1 purchase: ($180,000 / 6) × (1/12) = **$2,500** understated expense → RE overstated $2,500.  
Net RE understatement before correction = $40,000 − $2,500 = **$37,500**.  
PPA **credits** RE—PPA **$37,500** (increase beginning RE). Choice A uses $43,000 (as if full-year missed dep of $30,000 with different figures). Choice C reverses the direction. Choice D incorrectly treats missed dep as $2,500 full-year or mixes amounts.

**Question 2:**  
Which of the following is accounted for as an **accounting error** requiring **retrospective restatement** and a **prior period adjustment**, rather than as a change in estimate applied prospectively?

- A) Revising remaining useful life of equipment because production volume is higher than originally expected  
- B) Changing from declining-balance to straight-line depreciation for existing equipment based on a new company policy  
- C) Discovering that ordinary repairs of $25,000 paid two years ago were capitalized to Equipment and depreciated  
- D) Reducing residual value of a machine because of newly observed wear patterns  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Improper capitalization of ordinary repairs is a **prior-period error** in applying the capitalization vs expense rules → restate prior periods and record a **PPA** (remove remaining cost net of AD against beginning RE). Choices A and D are **changes in estimate** (prospective only). Choice B is a **change in depreciation method**, treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle and applied **prospectively** (not as an error correction).
