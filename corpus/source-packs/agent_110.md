# Agent 110 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-5

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Record dividend distributions, including cash, property, and liquidating  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash dividends — initial recognition:** declaration debits Retained Earnings and credits Dividends Payable (enforceable liability)
- **Timeline:** no entry on ex-dividend date or date of record; settlement debits Dividends Payable and credits Cash
- **Cumulative preferred allocation schedule:** arrears first, then current preferred, remainder to common (nonparticipating)
- **Property dividends:** remeasure noncash asset to fair value at declaration (gain/loss in income); debit RE at FV; distribute asset against Property Dividends Payable
- **Liquidating dividends:** return of capital — debit Additional Paid-In Capital (not RE) for the liquidating portion
- **Period-end / presentation:** unpaid declared dividends = current liability; RE reduced only for earnings distributions; APIC reduced for liquidating portion
- **Settlement (payment/distribution) JEs** for cash, property, and liquidating distributions

### Q1 — CORE — Cash (cumulative preferred), property, and liquidating dividends
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JEs for cash, property, and liquidating dividends; preferred allocation schedule; settlement (payment/distribution)  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Manufacturing Corp.** (calendar year) has the following capital structure at January 1, Year 1:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 8%, $25 par, 20,000 shares issued and outstanding | $500,000 |
| Common stock, $1 par, 200,000 shares issued and outstanding | 200,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 1,800,000 |
| Retained earnings | 2,400,000 |

Preferred stock is **cumulative and nonparticipating**. At January 1, Year 1, preferred dividends are **in arrears for two full years** (nothing was declared in the two preceding years).

**Transaction 1 — Cash dividend with preferred priority**  
On **December 15, Year 1**, the board declares total cash dividends of **$180,000**, payable **January 20, Year 2**, to stockholders of record on **January 5, Year 2**.

**Transaction 2 — Property dividend**  
On **July 1, Year 1**, Cedarline holds **4,000 shares** of **Northwind Equity** common stock as an investment. Carrying value is **$48,000**. Fair value on July 1 is **$56,000**. The board declares a property dividend of all 4,000 Northwind shares, payable **July 28** to holders of record **July 12**. Fair-value changes after the declaration date are ignored.

**Transaction 3 — Partially liquidating cash distribution**  
On **October 1, Year 1**, the board declares a cash distribution of **$100,000** and informs shareholders that **40%** is a **liquidating dividend** (return of capital) and **60%** is a distribution of retained earnings. Payable **October 25**; record date **October 10**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **preferred vs common cash-dividend allocation schedule** for the December 15 declaration (show arrears, current preferred, and common).  
b. Record the **December 15 declaration**, state the entry (if any) on the **January 5 record date**, and record the **January 20, Year 2 payment**.  
c. Record all **July 1** entries related to the property dividend and the **July 28 distribution**.  
d. Record the **October 1 declaration** and **October 25 payment** of the partially liquidating distribution.  
e. At **December 31, Year 1**, state the **balance-sheet presentation** of any unpaid dividends from these transactions, and compute the **total Year 1 reduction** to (i) retained earnings and (ii) additional paid-in capital from the three distributions.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Allocation schedule (annual preferred dividend = $500,000 × 8% = $40,000)**

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred dividends in arrears (2 × $40,000) | 80,000 | — | 80,000 |
| Preferred current-year dividend | 40,000 | — | 40,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 60,000 | 60,000 |
| **Total declared** | **120,000** | **60,000** | **180,000** |

Check: 80,000 + 40,000 + 60,000 = 180,000.

**b. Cash dividend — declaration, record date, settlement**

December 15, Year 1 — declaration (initial recognition):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 180,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 120,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 60,000 |
| *To record cash dividends declared* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180,000.

January 5, Year 2 — date of record: **No entry.**

January 20, Year 2 — payment (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 120,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 180,000 |
| *To record cash dividend payment* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180,000.

**c. Property dividend — fair-value remeasurement, declaration, distribution**

Holding gain = $56,000 − $48,000 = **$8,000**.

July 1 — remeasure investment to fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Northwind Equity | 8,000 | |
| Unrealized Holding Gain—Income | | 8,000 |
| *To recognize holding gain before property dividend declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. Carrying value after remeasurement = 48,000 + 8,000 = **$56,000**.

July 1 — declaration at fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 56,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 56,000 |
| *To record property dividend at fair value of asset to be distributed* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 56,000.

July 12 — date of record: **No entry.**

July 28 — distribution (settlement):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 56,000 | |
| Investment in Northwind Equity | | 56,000 |
| *To distribute Northwind shares to shareholders* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 56,000. Investment balance removed: 56,000 − 56,000 = 0.

**d. Partially liquidating cash distribution**

Liquidating portion = 40% × $100,000 = **$40,000** (debit APIC)  
Earnings portion = 60% × $100,000 = **$60,000** (debit RE)

October 1 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 60,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 40,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 100,000 |
| *To record cash distribution; 40% liquidating return of capital* | | |

**Check:** Dr 60,000 + 40,000 = Cr 100,000.

October 10 — date of record: **No entry.**

October 25 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 100,000 |
| *To pay cash distribution* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 100,000.

**e. Period-end presentation and equity effects (December 31, Year 1)**

Unpaid dividends at year-end (declared Dec 15, paid next year):

| Liability (current) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 120,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 60,000 |
| **Total current Dividends Payable** | **180,000** |

Property and October cash distributions were fully settled in Year 1 (no remaining payable).

| Equity reduction from Year 1 distributions | Amount |
|---|---:|
| RE — cash (Dec 15) | 180,000 |
| RE — property (July 1 at FV) | 56,000 |
| RE — nonliquidating portion (Oct 1) | 60,000 |
| **Total reduction to Retained Earnings** | **296,000** |
| APIC—Common — liquidating portion (Oct 1) | 40,000 |
| **Total reduction to Additional Paid-In Capital** | **40,000** |

(Note: the $8,000 holding gain increased income and therefore ultimately RE; it is **not** a dividend reduction.)

**Key insight:** Cash and property dividends reduce **retained earnings** at the amount of the distribution (property at **fair value** after a holding gain/loss at declaration). A **liquidating** portion is a return of capital and reduces **APIC**, not RE. Payment/distribution settles the payable; record and ex-dividend dates have no entries.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cash, property, and liquidating dividends
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition, preferred allocation, property at FV, liquidating split, settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Inc.** (calendar year) reports:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Preferred stock, 6%, $50 par, 12,000 shares issued and outstanding | $600,000 |
| Common stock, $2 par, 150,000 shares issued and outstanding | 300,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 950,000 |
| Retained earnings | 1,750,000 |

Preferred is **cumulative and nonparticipating**. Preferred dividends are **in arrears for one year** at the start of Year 1.

**Cash:** On **November 20, Year 1**, Meridian declares total cash dividends of **$150,000**, payable **December 15, Year 1**, to stockholders of record **December 1, Year 1**.

**Property:** On **May 15, Year 1**, Meridian holds an investment in **Solstice Instruments** stock with carrying value **$30,000**. Fair value on May 15 is **$36,500**. The board declares a property dividend of the entire investment, payable **May 31** to holders of record **May 22**.

**Liquidating mix:** On **August 1, Year 1**, Meridian declares a cash distribution of **$80,000**; management announces that **55%** is liquidating (return of capital) and **45%** is from retained earnings. Payable **August 20**; record date **August 8**.

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the $150,000 cash dividend between preferred and common; record declaration (Nov 20) and payment (Dec 15).  
b. Record May 15 property-dividend entries and the May 31 distribution.  
c. Record August 1 declaration and August 20 payment of the mixed liquidating distribution.  
d. Compute total Year 1 reductions to RE and to APIC from these three distributions.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cash dividend allocation and JEs**  
Annual preferred dividend = $600,000 × 6% = **$36,000**.

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred dividends in arrears (1 × $36,000) | 36,000 | — | 36,000 |
| Preferred current-year dividend | 36,000 | — | 36,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 78,000 | 78,000 |
| **Total** | **72,000** | **78,000** | **150,000** |

Check: 36,000 + 36,000 + 78,000 = 150,000.

November 20 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 150,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 72,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 78,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 150,000.

December 1 — record date: **No entry.**

December 15 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 72,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 78,000 | |
| Cash | | 150,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 150,000.

**b. Property dividend**  
Holding gain = $36,500 − $30,000 = **$6,500**.

May 15 — remeasure:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Solstice Instruments | 6,500 | |
| Unrealized Holding Gain—Income | | 6,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,500. New CV = 36,500.

May 15 — declare at FV:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 36,500 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 36,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 36,500.

May 22 — record date: **No entry.**

May 31 — distribute:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 36,500 | |
| Investment in Solstice Instruments | | 36,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 36,500.

**c. Mixed liquidating distribution**  
Liquidating = 55% × $80,000 = **$44,000**  
From RE = 45% × $80,000 = **$36,000**

August 1 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 36,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 44,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 80,000 |

**Check:** 36,000 + 44,000 = 80,000.

August 8 — record date: **No entry.**

August 20 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 80,000.

**d. Year 1 equity reductions from distributions**

| Item | RE | APIC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash dividend (Nov 20) | 150,000 | — |
| Property dividend (May 15 at FV) | 36,500 | — |
| Mixed distribution (Aug 1) | 36,000 | 44,000 |
| **Totals** | **222,500** | **44,000** |

**Key insight:** Same core pattern as Q1 with all new numbers: allocate cash to preferred (arrears + current) before common; remeasure property to FV then debit RE at FV; split liquidating distributions between RE and APIC; settle payables with cash or the asset.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-year preferred arrears schedule, property loss, full liquidating settlement
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent allocation schedule over multiple years; property dividend when FV < CV; pure liquidating settlement; classification/presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Brightwell Logistics LLC** has:

- Preferred stock, **7%**, **$100 par**, **5,000 shares** issued and outstanding (par total **$500,000**); **cumulative, nonparticipating**
- Common stock, **$5 par**, **80,000 shares** issued and outstanding
- Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common: **$1,200,000**
- Retained earnings (beginning Year 1): **$900,000**

No preferred or common dividends were declared in **Year 1** or **Year 2**. On **March 1, Year 3**, the board declares total cash dividends of **$140,000**, payable **March 31, Year 3**, to holders of record **March 15, Year 3**.

Separately, on **June 10, Year 3**, Brightwell declares a property dividend of a parcel of **land** held for investment. Land carrying value is **$90,000**; fair value on June 10 is **$78,000**. The land is distributed on **June 30, Year 3**.

On **December 1, Year 3**, as part of a partial wind-down of one product line, Brightwell declares a **fully liquidating** cash distribution of **$200,000** (entirely a return of capital). Payable **December 20, Year 3**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **three-year preferred dividend schedule** (Years 1–3) showing annual preferred entitlement, amount declared to preferred each year, and end-of-year arrears. Then allocate the March 1, Year 3 $140,000 declaration between preferred and common.  
b. Record the March 1 declaration and March 31 payment.  
c. Record June 10 entries for the property dividend (including the loss) and the June 30 distribution.  
d. Record December 1 declaration and December 20 payment of the fully liquidating distribution.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** For each of the three Year 3 distributions, state whether equity is reduced through RE, APIC, or both, and the amount. Also state how **Dividends Payable** (if any) would appear if the March cash dividend were still unpaid at a March 31 quarter-end **before** payment (i.e., after declaration but before cash outlay)—classify the line item.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Subsequent preferred-dividend schedule and Year 3 allocation**  
Annual preferred entitlement = $500,000 × 7% = **$35,000**.

| Year | Annual pref. entitlement | Declared to preferred | Arrears, end of year |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 35,000 | 0 | 35,000 |
| 2 | 35,000 | 0 | 70,000 |
| 3 | 35,000 | 105,000 | 0 |

Year 3 cash allocation of $140,000:

| Component | Preferred | Common | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Preferred arrears (Years 1–2) | 70,000 | — | 70,000 |
| Preferred current (Year 3) | 35,000 | — | 35,000 |
| Remainder to common | — | 35,000 | 35,000 |
| **Total** | **105,000** | **35,000** | **140,000** |

Check: 70,000 + 35,000 + 35,000 = 140,000. End-of-Year-3 preferred arrears = **$0**.

**b. Cash dividend declaration and settlement**

March 1, Year 3 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 140,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | | 105,000 |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | | 35,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 140,000.

March 15 — record date: **No entry.**

March 31 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred Stock | 105,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common Stock | 35,000 | |
| Cash | | 140,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 140,000.

**c. Property dividend with fair-value loss**  
Loss = $90,000 − $78,000 = **$12,000**.

June 10 — remeasure land to fair value:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on Asset Held for Distribution (or Unrealized Holding Loss—Income) | 12,000 | |
| Land | | 12,000 |
| *To write land down to fair value before property dividend declaration* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,000. Land CV after write-down = 90,000 − 12,000 = **$78,000**.

June 10 — declaration at FV:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 78,000 | |
| Property Dividends Payable | | 78,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 78,000.

June 30 — distribution:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Property Dividends Payable | 78,000 | |
| Land | | 78,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 78,000.

**d. Fully liquidating cash distribution (return of capital only)**

December 1 — declaration:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 200,000 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 200,000 |
| *Entirely liquidating — return of capital; no debit to RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000. APIC remaining after debit = 1,200,000 − 200,000 = **$1,000,000**.

December 20 — payment:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000.

**e. Classification / presentation summary**

| Distribution | Debit equity account(s) | Amount | Nature |
|---|---|---:|---|
| March 1 cash | Retained Earnings only | 140,000 | Distribution of earnings |
| June 10 property | Retained Earnings only (at FV) | 78,000 | Distribution of earnings (after $12,000 loss in income) |
| December 1 cash | APIC—Common only | 200,000 | **Liquidating** return of capital |

If the March cash dividend were still unpaid after declaration: report **Dividends Payable $140,000** as a **current liability** on the balance sheet (not as equity). Stockholders must be informed of any liquidating (return-of-capital) portion.

**Key insight:** Cumulative preferred arrears roll forward until declared; catch-up is scheduled before common receives anything. Property dividends can produce a **loss** when FV < CV—still measure the dividend (and payable) at **fair value**. A **100% liquidating** distribution bypasses RE entirely and reduces contributed capital (APIC).

### Q4 — MC — Classification of dividend types and accounts
**LO:** LO 19-5  
**Concept:** Classification — cash vs property vs liquidating; which equity account is debited at declaration  

**Question 1:**  
On the date a corporation declares a **property dividend**, the fair value of the noncash asset exceeds its carrying value. Which sequence is correct under U.S. GAAP (core LO 19-5 treatment)?

- A) Debit Retained Earnings for carrying value; no gain is recognized; distribute asset at carrying value.  
- B) First remeasure the asset to fair value (recognize gain in income), then debit Retained Earnings and credit Property Dividends Payable for **fair value**; later remove the asset against the payable.  
- C) Debit Additional Paid-In Capital for fair value; never touch Retained Earnings for property dividends.  
- D) Accrue the property dividend only on the date of record, measured at original cost.

**Answer:** **B.** Property dividends are measured at the **fair value** of the asset on the **declaration** date. Any difference between FV and CV is recognized as a holding gain or loss in income before (or as part of) recording the dividend at FV. Subsequent FV changes are ignored. Property dividends reduce **retained earnings**, not APIC (unless the distribution is characterized as liquidating).

**Question 2:**  
Oakridge Corp. declares a **$50,000** cash distribution and informs shareholders that the **entire** amount is a **liquidating dividend** (return of capital). The correct declaration entry debits:

- A) Retained Earnings $50,000  
- B) Dividends Expense $50,000  
- C) Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par (Additional Paid-In Capital) $50,000  
- D) Common Stock $50,000 always, regardless of APIC balance

**Answer:** **C.** Liquidating dividends are a **return of stockholders’ investment**, not a distribution of earnings. The declaration debits **APIC** (contributed capital) for the liquidating amount and credits Dividends Payable. Retained earnings is debited only for the nonliquidating (earnings) portion, if any. Common stock (legal capital) is reduced only if APIC is insufficient or when par is formally retired—APIC is the normal first debit for the liquidating portion illustrated in Demo 19-5C.
