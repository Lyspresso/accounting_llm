# Agent 198 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-2

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Determine costs to capitalize for lump-sum purchases of property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Lump-sum (basket) purchase:** single price for two or more dissimilar PP&E items must be **allocated** to each asset (identical units would split evenly)
- **Proportional method:** allocate lump-sum cost by **relative fair values** (appraised value, market prices of similar assets, assessed values, etc.)
- **Incremental method:** assign known fair value(s) to the measurable asset(s); **remainder** of lump-sum price goes to the asset with indeterminable FV
- **Directly attributable costs:** assign in full to the related asset first (e.g., land title/survey to land only; installation/testing to equipment only), then allocate the **remaining** lump-sum price
- **Initial recognition JE:** debit each asset for allocated + attributable cost; credit cash (or other consideration)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** land is **not** depreciated; building/equipment SL depreciation uses allocated capitalized cost as depreciable base
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** annual (or partial-year) depreciation on depreciable assets from the basket
- **Disposal JE:** remove cost and accumulated depreciation of disposed asset; recognize gain/loss vs proceeds (carrying amount depends on prior allocation)

### Q1 — CORE — Proportional lump-sum allocation, multi-year subsequent schedule, period-end dep, equipment disposal
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Proportional relative-FV allocation of a basket purchase (with directly attributable add-ons); multi-year subsequent measurement schedule; period-end depreciation; disposal of one allocated asset  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborview Logistics Inc.** has a **December 31** year-end and uses **straight-line** depreciation.

On **January 2, Year 1**, Harborview acquires a **land** parcel, a **warehouse building**, and **conveyor equipment** from one seller for a single **lump-sum cash price of $720,000**. Independently appraised fair values (best available relative FV indicators) on the acquisition date:

| Asset | Appraised fair value |
|---|---:|
| Land | $200,000 |
| Warehouse building | 500,000 |
| Conveyor equipment | 100,000 |
| **Total appraised FV** | **$800,000** |

Harborview also pays **cash** for costs **directly attributable** to specific assets (not part of the $720,000 negotiated package):

| Directly attributable cost | Amount | Assign to |
|---|---:|---|
| Land survey, title search, and closing costs | $8,000 | Land only |
| Equipment freight, installation, and testing | 12,000 | Equipment only |

All assets are placed in service on **January 2, Year 1**. Depreciation data:

| Asset | Useful life | Residual (salvage) value |
|---|---|---:|
| Land | n/a (not depreciated) | n/a |
| Warehouse building | 25 years | $50,000 |
| Conveyor equipment | 10 years | $2,000 |

On **January 1, Year 4**, Harborview sells the **conveyor equipment** for **$68,000 cash**. No depreciation is recorded on the disposal date (full years already recorded for Years 1–3).

**Required:**  
(a) Allocate the **$720,000** lump-sum price using the **proportional method**. Then compute **total capitalized cost** of each asset after assigning directly attributable costs.  
(b) Prepare the **initial recognition journal entry** on January 2, Year 1 (single combined entry is acceptable).  
(c) Compute **annual straight-line depreciation** for the building and equipment.  
(d) Prepare the **period-end adjusting entry** for depreciation at **December 31, Year 1**.  
(e) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (emphasis) for Years 1–3 showing, for each asset: cost, annual depreciation, accumulated depreciation, and **carrying amount** at each December 31.  
(f) Prepare the **disposal journal entry** on January 1, Year 4 for the equipment sale.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Proportional allocation of $720,000 + attributable costs**

Relative FV ratios (total appraised FV = $800,000):

| Asset | FV | Ratio | Allocated lump-sum cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | $200,000 | 200/800 = 25% | \(0.25 \times 720{,}000 = \mathbf{\$180{,}000}\) |
| Building | 500,000 | 500/800 = 62.5% | \(0.625 \times 720{,}000 = \mathbf{\$450{,}000}\) |
| Equipment | 100,000 | 100/800 = 12.5% | \(0.125 \times 720{,}000 = \mathbf{\$90{,}000}\) |
| **Total** | **800,000** | **100%** | **720,000** |

**Total capitalized cost (after attributable add-ons):**

| Asset | Allocated package | + Directly attributable | **Capitalized cost** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 180,000 | 8,000 | **188,000** |
| Building | 450,000 | 0 | **450,000** |
| Equipment | 90,000 | 12,000 | **102,000** |
| **Total** | **720,000** | **20,000** | **740,000** |

**Check:** Cash outlay = \(720{,}000 + 8{,}000 + 12{,}000 = \mathbf{\$740{,}000}\).

**(b) January 2, Year 1 — Initial recognition JE (lump-sum purchase)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 188,000 | |
| Building | 450,000 | |
| Equipment | 102,000 | |
| Cash | | 740,000 |
| *Record lump-sum basket purchase allocated by relative FV; capitalize attributable land and equipment costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(188{,}000 + 450{,}000 + 102{,}000 = 740{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**(c) Annual SL depreciation**

Building: \(\dfrac{450{,}000 - 50{,}000}{25} = \dfrac{400{,}000}{25} = \mathbf{\$16{,}000}\) per year  

Equipment: \(\dfrac{102{,}000 - 2{,}000}{10} = \dfrac{100{,}000}{10} = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\) per year  

Land: **$0** (not depreciated)

**(d) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 16,000 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 10,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 16,000 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 10,000 |
| *Record Year 1 SL depreciation on assets from lump-sum purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(16{,}000 + 10{,}000 = 26{,}000\) = Cr \(16{,}000 + 10{,}000\). **Balanced.**

(Same annual adjusting entry form is used at Dec 31, Year 2 and Year 3.)

**(e) Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 1–3) — emphasis**

| Asset | Cost | Annual dep. | AD 12/31/Y1 | CA 12/31/Y1 | AD 12/31/Y2 | CA 12/31/Y2 | AD 12/31/Y3 | CA 12/31/Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 188,000 | 0 | 0 | **188,000** | 0 | **188,000** | 0 | **188,000** |
| Building | 450,000 | 16,000 | 16,000 | **434,000** | 32,000 | **418,000** | 48,000 | **402,000** |
| Equipment | 102,000 | 10,000 | 10,000 | **92,000** | 20,000 | **82,000** | 30,000 | **72,000** |
| **Total CA** | | | | **714,000** | | **688,000** | | **662,000** |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
- Building CA Y3: \(450{,}000 - 3 \times 16{,}000 = 450{,}000 - 48{,}000 = 402{,}000\)  
- Equipment CA Y3: \(102{,}000 - 3 \times 10{,}000 = 102{,}000 - 30{,}000 = 72{,}000\)  
- Total CA Y1: \(740{,}000 - 26{,}000 = 714{,}000\); Y2: \(714{,}000 - 26{,}000 = 688{,}000\); Y3: \(688{,}000 - 26{,}000 = 662{,}000\)

**(f) January 1, Year 4 — Disposal of equipment**

Carrying amount of equipment = \(102{,}000 - 30{,}000 = \mathbf{\$72{,}000}\)  
Cash proceeds = **$68,000**  
**Loss on disposal** = \(72{,}000 - 68{,}000 = \mathbf{\$4{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 68,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 30,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 4,000 | |
| Equipment | | 102,000 |
| *Sell conveyor equipment from original basket purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(68{,}000 + 30{,}000 + 4{,}000 = 102{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** The lump-sum price is **not** recorded as one “plant assets” total forever—relative FVs allocate cost so that **depreciation bases**, **carrying amounts**, and **gains/losses on disposal** are correct for each asset. Directly attributable costs attach only to the related asset **after** (or in addition to) the proportional split of the package price.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Proportional allocation, partial-year start, two-year schedule, mid-life disposal
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Proportional basket allocation with attributable costs; partial-year first depreciation; subsequent measurement schedule; disposal after multiple periods  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Ridge Manufacturing** (December 31 year-end; straight-line depreciation) purchases land, a production building, and specialized machinery on **April 1, Year 1** for a **lump-sum cash price of $540,000**. Appraised fair values:

| Asset | Appraised fair value |
|---|---:|
| Land | $120,000 |
| Production building | 360,000 |
| Machinery | 120,000 |
| **Total** | **$600,000** |

Additional cash costs paid April 1, Year 1 (directly attributable):

| Cost | Amount | Assign to |
|---|---:|---|
| Legal fees and title insurance | $5,000 | Land only |
| Machinery freight, foundation bolts, installation, trial runs | 7,000 | Machinery only |

Placed in service **April 1, Year 1**. Depreciation data:

| Asset | Useful life | Residual value |
|---|---|---:|
| Land | n/a | n/a |
| Production building | 20 years | $24,000 |
| Machinery | 5 years | $5,000 |

On **December 31, Year 2**, immediately **after** recording Year 2 depreciation, Summit Ridge sells the **machinery** for **$48,500 cash**.

**Required:**  
(a) Allocate the lump-sum price (proportional method) and compute capitalized cost of each asset.  
(b) Initial recognition JE on April 1, Year 1.  
(c) Annual full-year SL depreciation for building and machinery; compute **Year 1 partial-year** depreciation (9/12).  
(d) Period-end adjusting entries at **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2**.  
(e) **Subsequent measurement schedule** at 12/31/Y1 and 12/31/Y2 (cost, AD, carrying amount) for all three assets.  
(f) Disposal JE for machinery on December 31, Year 2 (after Year 2 depreciation).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Proportional allocation + attributable costs**

Total FV = $600,000. Package = $540,000.

| Asset | Ratio | Allocated package | + Attributable | **Capitalized cost** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 120/600 = 20% | \(0.20 \times 540{,}000 = 108{,}000\) | 5,000 | **113,000** |
| Building | 360/600 = 60% | \(0.60 \times 540{,}000 = 324{,}000\) | 0 | **324,000** |
| Machinery | 120/600 = 20% | \(0.20 \times 540{,}000 = 108{,}000\) | 7,000 | **115,000** |
| **Total** | 100% | **540,000** | **12,000** | **552,000** |

**Check:** \(108{,}000 + 324{,}000 + 108{,}000 = 540{,}000\); \(113{,}000 + 324{,}000 + 115{,}000 = 552{,}000 = 540{,}000 + 12{,}000\).

**(b) April 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 113,000 | |
| Building | 324,000 | |
| Machinery | 115,000 | |
| Cash | | 552,000 |
| *Lump-sum purchase allocated by relative FV; capitalize attributable land and machinery costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(113{,}000 + 324{,}000 + 115{,}000 = 552{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**(c) Depreciation amounts**

Building full year: \(\dfrac{324{,}000 - 24{,}000}{20} = \dfrac{300{,}000}{20} = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\)  

Machinery full year: \(\dfrac{115{,}000 - 5{,}000}{5} = \dfrac{110{,}000}{5} = \mathbf{\$22{,}000}\)  

Year 1 (in service April 1 → **9/12**):  
Building Y1: \(15{,}000 \times 9/12 = \mathbf{\$11{,}250}\)  
Machinery Y1: \(22{,}000 \times 9/12 = \mathbf{\$16{,}500}\)  

Year 2 (full year): Building **$15,000**; Machinery **$22,000**.

**(d) Period-end adjusting JEs**

**December 31, Year 1:**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 11,250 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Machinery | 16,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 11,250 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery | | 16,500 |
| *Partial-year (9/12) depreciation on basket assets* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(11{,}250 + 16{,}500 = 27{,}750\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 2:**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 15,000 | |
| Depreciation Expense—Machinery | 22,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 15,000 |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery | | 22,000 |
| *Full-year depreciation Year 2* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(15{,}000 + 22{,}000 = 37{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**(e) Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Asset | Cost | AD 12/31/Y1 | CA 12/31/Y1 | AD 12/31/Y2 | CA 12/31/Y2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 113,000 | 0 | **113,000** | 0 | **113,000** |
| Building | 324,000 | 11,250 | **312,750** | 26,250 | **297,750** |
| Machinery | 115,000 | 16,500 | **98,500** | 38,500 | **76,500** |
| **Total CA** | | | **524,250** | | **487,250** |

**AD checks:**  
Building AD Y2: \(11{,}250 + 15{,}000 = 26{,}250\); CA \(324{,}000 - 26{,}250 = 297{,}750\)  
Machinery AD Y2: \(16{,}500 + 22{,}000 = 38{,}500\); CA \(115{,}000 - 38{,}500 = 76{,}500\)  
Total CA Y1: \(552{,}000 - 27{,}750 = 524{,}250\); Y2: \(524{,}250 - 37{,}000 = 487{,}250\)

**(f) December 31, Year 2 — Disposal of machinery (after Year 2 dep)**

BV of machinery = **$76,500**  
Proceeds = **$48,500**  
**Loss** = \(76{,}500 - 48{,}500 = \mathbf{\$28{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 48,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery | 38,500 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Machinery | 28,000 | |
| Machinery | | 115,000 |
| *Dispose of machinery acquired in lump-sum purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(48{,}500 + 38{,}500 + 28{,}000 = 115{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Changing only the numbers still requires the same sequence: **allocate → capitalize bases → depreciate only depreciable assets → schedule carrying amounts → dispose using AD built from those bases**. Partial-year service affects the **first** period’s adjusting entry and the subsequent schedule opening AD.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Incremental method when one FV is indeterminable; schedule; period-end dep; settlement/disposal
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Incremental (residual) allocation when one asset’s fair value cannot be determined; multi-period carrying schedule; year-end depreciation; cash disposal of residual-cost asset  
**Scenario:**  
**Northbay Cold Storage Co.** (December 31 year-end; straight-line) acquires **land** and a **highly specialized refrigeration plant** (equipment) on **January 1, Year 1** for a **lump-sum cash price of $350,000**.  

- Appraised fair value of **land** (determinable): **$210,000**  
- Fair value of the **specialized refrigeration plant** is **indeterminable** (no active market; custom-built for the site)  
- Additional cash **closing costs on land**: **$4,000** (directly attributable to land only)

Depreciation: refrigeration plant useful life **7 years**, residual value **$0**. Land is not depreciated. Placed in service January 1, Year 1.

On **January 1, Year 4**, Northbay sells the **refrigeration plant** for **$175,000 cash** (three full years of depreciation already recorded).

**Required:**  
(a) Allocate the $350,000 lump-sum price using the **incremental method** and compute total capitalized costs (include land closing costs).  
(b) Initial recognition JE on January 1, Year 1.  
(c) Annual depreciation of the refrigeration plant; period-end adjusting entry at December 31, Year 1.  
(d) **Subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–3 (cost, AD, carrying amount of land and plant).  
(e) Disposal/settlement JE on January 1, Year 4.  
(f) Briefly contrast the **incremental** result for the plant with what a **proportional** method would require (and why proportional cannot be applied here).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Incremental allocation**

| Asset | Capitalized amount | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Land (from package) | 210,000 | Known FV assigned in full |
| Refrigeration plant | \(350{,}000 - 210{,}000 = \mathbf{140{,}000}\) | Residual of lump-sum price |
| Land closing costs | 4,000 | Directly attributable to land |
| **Land total** | **214,000** | \(210{,}000 + 4{,}000\) |
| **Plant total** | **140,000** | Remainder of package |
| **Combined cash outlay** | **354,000** | \(350{,}000 + 4{,}000\) |

**Check:** \(214{,}000 + 140{,}000 = 354{,}000\).

**(b) January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 214,000 | |
| Equipment—Refrigeration Plant | 140,000 | |
| Cash | | 354,000 |
| *Lump-sum purchase: incremental allocation; land FV known; plant residual* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(214{,}000 + 140{,}000 = 354{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**(c) Annual depreciation and Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**

Annual SL dep: \(\dfrac{140{,}000 - 0}{7} = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 20,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 20,000 |
| *Year 1 depreciation on plant capitalized at residual lump-sum cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000. **Balanced.**

**(d) Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 1–3)**

| Asset | Cost | Annual dep. | AD Y1 | CA Y1 | AD Y2 | CA Y2 | AD Y3 | CA Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Land | 214,000 | 0 | 0 | **214,000** | 0 | **214,000** | 0 | **214,000** |
| Refrigeration plant | 140,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | **120,000** | 40,000 | **100,000** | 60,000 | **80,000** |
| **Total CA** | | | | **334,000** | | **314,000** | | **294,000** |

**Checks:** Plant CA Y3: \(140{,}000 - 3 \times 20{,}000 = 80{,}000\); Total CA Y1: \(354{,}000 - 20{,}000 = 334{,}000\).

**(e) January 1, Year 4 — Disposal of refrigeration plant**

BV = \(140{,}000 - 60{,}000 = \mathbf{\$80{,}000}\)  
Proceeds = **$175,000**  
**Gain** = \(175{,}000 - 80{,}000 = \mathbf{\$95{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 175,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 60,000 | |
| Equipment—Refrigeration Plant | | 140,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 95,000 |
| *Sell specialized plant recorded at residual of lump-sum price* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(175{,}000 + 60{,}000 = 235{,}000\); Cr \(140{,}000 + 95{,}000 = 235{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**(f) Method contrast**

Proportional allocation needs a **relative FV for every** asset in the basket. Here the plant’s FV is **indeterminable**, so relative weights cannot be formed. The **incremental method** assigns the measurable land FV first and capitalizes the **remainder** of the package price as plant cost. That residual plant cost then drives **all** subsequent depreciation and the disposal gain/loss.

**Key insight:** When one asset lacks a reliable FV, **do not invent** a ratio—use **incremental (residual) allocation**. The residual cost still becomes the depreciable base for subsequent measurement and disposal accounting.

### Q4 — MC — Allocation method selection and capitalized amount
**LO:** LO 11-2  
**Concept:** Choose proportional vs incremental method; compute allocated land cost when one FV is unknown  

**Question 1:**  
On March 1, **Lakeshore Foods** pays **$400,000** cash for land and a building in a single package. An independent appraisal values the **land at $150,000**. The building is a unique cold-storage structure with **no reliable stand-alone fair value**. Which statement is correct?

- A) Allocate $200,000 to land and $200,000 to building because equal split is required when FV is incomplete  
- B) Record land at $150,000 and building at $250,000 (incremental method)  
- C) Record the entire $400,000 as land until the building’s FV becomes available  
- D) Record land at $150,000 and building at $150,000, with $100,000 expensed as a bargain purchase  

**Answer:** **B.** Under the **incremental method**, assign the known land FV of $150,000 and capitalize the **remainder** of the lump-sum price, \(400{,}000 - 150{,}000 = \$250{,}000\), as the building. Equal split is not appropriate for dissimilar assets; the full package is capitalized to PP&E (no immediate bargain-purchase expense of the residual).  

---

**Question 2:**  
**Pinecrest Dental** buys three identical autoclave units for a **combined cash price of $9,000**. Individually the vendor lists each unit at $3,400. Using the correct allocation approach for **identical** items, capitalized cost **per autoclave** is:

- A) $3,400  
- B) $3,000  
- C) $2,800  
- D) $9,000  

**Answer:** **B.** When goods in a lump-sum purchase are **identical**, allocate the package price **evenly**: \(9{,}000 / 3 = \$3{,}000\) per unit. Relative list prices are unnecessary when units are the same nature; do not capitalize at the higher individual list price.
