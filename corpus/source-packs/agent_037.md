# Agent 37 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-5

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Describe required disclosures including that for noncash transactions  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Noncash investing and financing** transactions are omitted from the body of the SCF (no cash exchanged) but must be **disclosed** (narrative or schedule)
- Partial noncash asset acquisitions: report only the **cash portion** in investing; exclude noncash consideration from financing inflows
- Adjusting draft investing/financing sections when stock, notes, or assumed liabilities were mixed into “cash” lines
- **Initial recognition JEs** that create noncash investing/financing events (equipment/land for stock or note; bond conversion; ROU/lease liability)
- Supplemental disclosures under the **indirect method**: **interest paid** (net of amounts capitalized) and **income taxes paid** (required regardless of method)
- Reconciling interest/tax expense to cash paid using beginning/ending payables
- Equipment (or PPE) rollforward schedule that separates cash purchases vs noncash purchases
- Period-end interest accrual (adjusting JE) feeding the interest-paid disclosure
- Settlement without cash (debt converted to equity) — disclosure, not financing cash outflow
- Classification of partial noncash purchases on the SCF (investing cash only; noncash in notes)

### Q1 — CORE — Partial noncash PPE acquisitions, bond conversion, and supplemental cash disclosures (initial-recognition emphasis)
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of noncash investing/financing transactions; correct SCF investing/financing; noncash disclosure; interest and taxes paid  
**Scenario:** **Cedarline Manufacturing Co.** is finalizing its statement of cash flows for the year ended **December 31, 20X5** (indirect method for operating activities). A staff draft of investing and financing activities treated several noncash items as if they were cash. Facts discovered in review:

**Transactions during 20X5 (all original):**

1. **Equipment** with a total cost of **$285,000** was acquired. Payment: **$210,000 cash** and **1,500 shares** of common stock with a fair value of **$75,000**.  
2. **Land** with a fair value of **$90,000** was acquired by signing a **$90,000 long-term note payable** (no cash down payment).  
3. On December 15, holders of **$50,000 face** of Cedarline’s bonds (carrying amount equals face; no unamortized premium/discount) converted the bonds into common stock under the bond indenture.  
4. Other **cash** investing/financing items that belong on the SCF:  
   - Proceeds from sale of used equipment: **$40,000**  
   - Cash issuance of common stock (unrelated to the equipment deal): **$50,000**  
   - Cash retirement of other bonds: **$100,000**  
   - Cash dividends paid: **$30,000**  
   - Principal payments on mortgage payable: **$15,000**

**Staff draft (incorrect — includes noncash amounts as cash):**

| Cash flows from investing activities | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Purchase of equipment | $(285,000) |
| Purchase of land | (90,000) |
| Proceeds from sale of equipment | 40,000 |
| **Net cash used by investing activities** | **$(335,000)** |

| Cash flows from financing activities | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Issuance of common stock | $125,000 |
| Issuance of long-term note payable | 90,000 |
| Payment to retire bonds | (100,000) |
| Dividends paid | (30,000) |
| Principal payments on mortgage | (15,000) |
| **Net cash provided by financing activities** | **$70,000** |

**Supplemental data for cash paid disclosures:**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Interest expense for 20X5 | $28,000 |
| Interest payable, Jan 1, 20X5 | 4,000 |
| Interest payable, Dec 31, 20X5 | 6,500 |
| Capitalized interest during 20X5 | 0 |
| Income tax expense for 20X5 | 52,000 |
| Income taxes payable, Jan 1, 20X5 | 12,000 |
| Income taxes payable, Dec 31, 20X5 | 9,000 |
| Income tax refunds received | 0 |

No restricted cash is reported.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **initial recognition journal entries** for transactions 1–3 (equipment, land, bond conversion). Prove each entry balances.  
(b) Prepare the **corrected** investing and financing sections of the statement of cash flows (cash only).  
(c) Show that the **sum of net investing + net financing** is unchanged by the noncash reclassification (before vs after).  
(d) Prepare the **noncash investing and financing activities disclosure** (schedule form), clearly relating cash and noncash aspects of similar items.  
(e) Compute and report the **required supplemental disclosures** of **interest paid** and **income taxes paid**.  
(f) List the five general SCF reporting/disclosure requirements highlighted for LO 22-5 (overview checklist).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition journal entries**

Equipment (partial cash / partial stock):  
```
Dr Equipment ................................ 285,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 210,000
   Cr Common Stock (or CS + APIC) ...................  75,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 285,000)

Land for note (fully noncash investing/financing):  
```
Dr Land ...................................... 90,000
   Cr Notes Payable (long-term) .....................  90,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 90,000)

Bond conversion (noncash financing exchange):  
```
Dr Bonds Payable ............................. 50,000
   Cr Common Stock (or CS + APIC) ...................  50,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 50,000)

**(b) Corrected investing and financing sections**

Cash flows from **investing** activities:  
| Line | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Purchase of equipment (cash portion only) | $(210,000) |
| Proceeds from sale of equipment | 40,000 |
| **Net cash used by investing activities** | **$(170,000)** |

*Land acquisition and the $75,000 stock portion of equipment are excluded (noncash).*

Cash flows from **financing** activities:  
| Line | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Issuance of common stock (cash only) | $50,000 |
| Payment to retire bonds | (100,000) |
| Dividends paid | (30,000) |
| Principal payments on mortgage | (15,000) |
| **Net cash used by financing activities** | **$(95,000)** |

*Remove $75,000 stock-for-equipment, $90,000 note-for-land, and do **not** report the $50,000 bond conversion as a cash financing item.*

**(c) Totals unchanged**

| | Draft (incorrect) | Corrected |
|---|---:|---:|
| Net investing | $(335,000) | $(170,000) |
| Net financing | 70,000 | (95,000) |
| **Combined** | **$(265,000)** | **$(265,000)** |

Noncash reclassification shifts amounts between sections (or off the face) but does **not** change the net effect on the cash reconciliation.

**(d) Noncash investing and financing activities disclosure**

**Noncash investing and financing activities**

Purchased equipment for $285,000 through payment of cash and issuance of common stock:  
| | |
|---|---:|
| Cost of equipment | $285,000 |
| Cash payment for equipment | (210,000) |
| Issuance of common stock for equipment | $75,000 |

Purchased land by issuance of a long-term note payable:  
| | |
|---|---:|
| Cost of land / note issued | $90,000 |

Converted bonds payable into common stock:  
| | |
|---|---:|
| Carrying amount of bonds converted | $50,000 |
| Common stock issued on conversion | $50,000 |

**(e) Interest paid and income taxes paid**

Interest paid (net of capitalized interest = 0):  
\[
\text{Interest paid} = \text{Interest expense} + \text{Beg. interest payable} - \text{End. interest payable}
\]  
\[
= 28{,}000 + 4{,}000 - 6{,}500 = \mathbf{\$25{,}500}
\]  
(Equivalently: expense − increase in payable = \(28{,}000 - 2{,}500 = 25{,}500\).)

Income taxes paid:  
\[
\text{Taxes paid} = \text{Tax expense} + \text{Beg. taxes payable} - \text{End. taxes payable}
\]  
\[
= 52{,}000 + 12{,}000 - 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$55{,}000}
\]

**Supplemental disclosures of cash flow information**  
Cash paid during the year for:  
- Interest (net of amount capitalized) …… **$25,500**  
- Income taxes …………………………… **$55,000**

**(f) LO 22-5 reporting/disclosure checklist**  
1. Reconciliation of net income to operating cash flows (on face or note under indirect method; major classes of operating accruals/deferrals).  
2. Report investing and financing **inflows and outflows separately** (gross).  
3. Disclose **interest paid** (indirect method; net of capitalized interest) and **income taxes paid** (either method).  
4. Disclose **noncash investing and financing** transactions.  
5. Disclose the **nature of restrictions** on cash, cash equivalents, and amounts generally described as restricted cash/equivalents.

**Key insight:** Only **cash** changes belong in investing and financing sections. Signing a note for land or issuing stock for equipment is a **noncash investing and financing** event—record the full asset/liability/equity on the books, put only cash on the SCF, and **disclose** the noncash portion. Interest and taxes paid are separate supplemental disclosures and are **not** the same as interest/tax expense.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Partial noncash acquisitions + supplemental disclosures (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Noncash disclosure and SCF correction (number-variant twin of Q1)  
**Scenario:** **Northshore Fabrication LLC** prepares its December 31, **20X6** SCF (indirect method). Reviewers find that noncash items were mixed into the draft cash sections.

**Transactions:**

1. Machinery costing **$460,000** acquired for **$310,000 cash** and **$150,000** of common stock (fair value).  
2. Building site (land) acquired by assuming a seller’s **$125,000 mortgage note** (no cash at closing).  
3. Convertible notes with carrying amount **$80,000** converted into common stock (no gain/loss).  
4. Cash items: sale of equipment proceeds **$55,000**; cash stock issuance **$90,000**; cash bond retirement **$200,000**; dividends paid **$48,000**; long-term debt principal repayments **$22,000**.

**Staff draft (incorrect):**

Investing: purchase of machinery \((460{,}000)\); purchase of land \((125{,}000)\); proceeds from sale of equipment \(55{,}000\); net used **\((530{,}000)\)**.  
Financing: issuance of common stock \(240{,}000\) [= 90k cash + 150k noncash]; issuance of mortgage/note \(125{,}000\); retire bonds \((200{,}000)\); dividends \((48{,}000)\); principal repayments \((22{,}000)\); net provided **\(95{,}000\)**.

**Supplemental data:** Interest expense **$41,000**; interest payable beg **$7,200**, end **$5,100**; capitalized interest **$3,000**. Tax expense **$68,000**; taxes payable beg **$18,500**, end **$21,000**.

**Required:**  
(a) Initial recognition JEs for the three noncash / partial-noncash events.  
(b) Corrected investing and financing sections.  
(c) Prove combined investing + financing total is unchanged.  
(d) Noncash disclosure schedule.  
(e) Interest paid (net of capitalized) and income taxes paid.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Journal entries**

```
Dr Machinery ................................ 460,000
   Cr Cash .......................................... 310,000
   Cr Common Stock (CS/APIC) ........................ 150,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 460,000)

```
Dr Land ..................................... 125,000
   Cr Mortgage/Note Payable ......................... 125,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 125,000)

```
Dr Notes Payable (convertible) .............. 80,000
   Cr Common Stock (CS/APIC) ........................  80,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 80,000)

**(b) Corrected SCF sections**

**Investing**  
| Line | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Purchase of machinery (cash) | $(310,000) |
| Proceeds from sale of equipment | 55,000 |
| **Net cash used by investing** | **$(255,000)** |

**Financing**  
| Line | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Issuance of common stock (cash) | $90,000 |
| Payment to retire bonds | (200,000) |
| Dividends paid | (48,000) |
| Principal repayments on long-term debt | (22,000) |
| **Net cash used by financing** | **$(180,000)** |

**(c) Combined totals**

| | Draft | Corrected |
|---|---:|---:|
| Net investing | $(530,000) | $(255,000) |
| Net financing | 95,000 | (180,000) |
| **Combined** | **$(435,000)** | **$(435,000)** |

**(d) Noncash disclosure**

- Machinery purchased for $460,000: cash $310,000 + common stock $150,000.  
- Land acquired by assuming $125,000 mortgage note.  
- Convertible notes with carrying amount $80,000 converted into common stock.

**(e) Cash paid disclosures**

Interest paid (net of capitalized interest):  
Cash interest before capitalization concept under ASC 230: disclose interest paid **net of amounts capitalized**.  
Interest incurred = expense + capitalized = \(41{,}000 + 3{,}000 = 44{,}000\).  
Cash interest paid (gross of capitalization presentation basis) from payable rollforward on **total interest cost**:  
Change in interest payable: beg 7,200 − end 5,100 = decrease 2,100 → cash interest related to accrual accounts = incurred interest cost + decrease in payable = \(44{,}000 + 2{,}100 = 46{,}100\).  
Interest paid **net of capitalized**: \(46{,}100 - 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$43{,}100}\).

Simpler equivalent used in exam settings when capitalized interest was paid in cash and expense excludes it:  
\[
\text{Interest paid (net of capitalized)} = \text{Interest expense} + \text{Beg. IP} - \text{End. IP}
\]  
\[
= 41{,}000 + 7{,}200 - 5{,}100 = \mathbf{\$43{,}100}
\]  
(Capitalized interest is excluded from the “net of amounts capitalized” disclosure amount when the problem’s interest expense is the income-statement amount after capitalization; the payable rollforward on expense alone yields the same net figure here.)

Income taxes paid:  
\[
68{,}000 + 18{,}500 - 21{,}000 = \mathbf{\$65{,}500}
\]

**Supplemental disclosures:** Interest paid (net of capitalized) **$43,100**; Income taxes paid **$65,500**.

**Key insight:** Same structure as Q1 with new magnitudes—always strip noncash consideration out of investing/financing, disclose it, and convert expense + payable changes into **cash paid** for the supplemental note.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Equipment rollforward schedule, note issued for PPE, period-end interest accrual, principal settlement, multi-item noncash disclosure
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** PPE cash vs noncash purchase schedule; note subsequent measurement; period-end interest AJE; cash principal on SCF; noncash + interest-paid disclosures  
**Scenario:** **Bluffside Packaging Inc.** reports the following equipment account activity for **20X7**. Fiscal year-end is December 31.

**Equipment (gross) T-account analysis:**

| | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Balance, Jan 1, 20X7 | 520,000 | |
| Purchases during year (total additions) | 180,000 | |
| Cost of equipment sold | | 40,000 |
| Balance, Dec 31, 20X7 | 660,000 | |

Additional information:

1. Of the **$180,000** equipment additions, **$48,000** was acquired by issuing a **3-year, 6% note payable** on **April 1, 20X7** (interest payable annually each March 31; principal due at maturity). The remainder of additions was paid in **cash**.  
2. Equipment sold had cost **$40,000** and accumulated depreciation **$27,000**. Cash proceeds were **$11,000** (loss of $2,000 already in net income).  
3. On **December 31, 20X7**, Bluffside records accrued interest on the April 1 note for 9 months.  
4. On **March 31, 20X8**, Bluffside pays the first annual interest on the note in cash.  
5. Separately on **January 1, 20X7**, Bluffside commenced a finance lease and recorded Right-of-Use Asset and Lease Liability of **$95,000** (no cash at commencement; first payment occurs later).  
6. Interest expense on other obligations (excluding the April 1 note) was **$19,400**. Related interest payable: beg **$3,100**, end **$2,800**. No capitalized interest. (For the April 1 note, use the accrual in (3)/(4).)

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **April 1, 20X7 initial recognition JE** for the equipment note purchase and the **January 1, 20X7** lease commencement JE.  
(b) Prepare an **equipment additions schedule** separating cash purchases from noncash purchases; state the investing cash outflow for equipment purchases.  
(c) Prepare the **December 31, 20X7 period-end adjusting JE** for interest on the equipment note; compute 20X7 interest expense on that note.  
(d) Prepare the **March 31, 20X8 cash interest payment JE** (settlement of accrued interest + current period interest if any through March 31).  
(e) For the year **20X7** SCF: identify investing presentation of the equipment sale and purchases; state whether the lease commencement and the note-for-equipment appear on the face of the SCF.  
(f) Prepare the **20X7 noncash investing and financing disclosure**.  
(g) Compute **total interest paid** for 20X7 related only to “other obligations” in item 6 (payable rollforward). Then compute **total 20X7 interest expense** on the April 1 note and explain why **$0 cash interest** was paid on that note in 20X7.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition**

April 1, 20X7 — equipment for note:  
```
Dr Equipment ................................ 48,000
   Cr Notes Payable ...............................  48,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 48,000)

January 1, 20X7 — finance lease commencement:  
```
Dr Right-of-Use Asset ....................... 95,000
   Cr Lease Liability .............................  95,000
```
(Dr = Cr = 95,000)

**(b) Equipment additions schedule (subsequent measurement / analysis schedule)**

| Component of 20X7 equipment additions | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total additions (from T-account) | $180,000 |
| Less: Noncash — note payable issued April 1 | (48,000) |
| **Cash purchases of equipment (investing outflow)** | **$132,000** |

Proof of T-account: \(520{,}000 + 180{,}000 - 40{,}000 = 660{,}000\).

**(c) Period-end adjusting JE — interest on equipment note**

Interest for 9 months (Apr 1–Dec 31): \(48{,}000 \times 6\% \times 9/12 = \mathbf{\$2{,}160}\).

December 31, 20X7:  
```
Dr Interest Expense ......................... 2,160
   Cr Interest Payable ............................   2,160
```
(Dr = Cr = 2,160)

**(d) March 31, 20X8 — cash interest payment (first annual coupon)**

Annual contractual interest = \(48{,}000 \times 6\% = 2{,}880\).  
Of that, $2,160 was accrued in 20X7; remaining $720 is Jan 1–Mar 31, 20X8 expense.

March 31, 20X8:  
```
Dr Interest Expense .........................   720
Dr Interest Payable ......................... 2,160
   Cr Cash ........................................   2,880
```
(Dr 2,880 = Cr 2,880)

**(e) 20X7 SCF presentation (classification)**

**Investing activities (cash):**  
- Purchase of equipment ………………………… **$(132,000)**  
- Proceeds from sale of equipment …………… **$11,000**

**Not on the face as cash flows (disclose as noncash):**  
- Equipment acquired by issuing $48,000 note  
- ROU asset secured in exchange for $95,000 lease liability  

Sale loss of $2,000 is an **operating** reconciling item under the indirect method (add back), not an investing line.

**(f) 20X7 noncash investing and financing disclosure**

**Noncash investing and financing activities**  
- Purchased equipment for $180,000 through payment of cash and issuance of a note payable:  

| | |
|---|---:|
| Cost of equipment acquired | $180,000 |
| Cash payment for equipment | (132,000) |
| Issuance of note payable for equipment | $48,000 |

- Secured a right-of-use asset in exchange for a lease liability of **$95,000**.

**(g) Interest paid vs expense (20X7)**

Other obligations — interest paid:  
\[
19{,}400 + 3{,}100 - 2{,}800 = \mathbf{\$19{,}700}
\]

April 1 note — 20X7 interest **expense** = **$2,160**; 20X7 interest **paid** on that note = **$0** (first cash coupon is March 31, 20X8).  
If the company discloses a single interest-paid amount for all debt, 20X7 interest paid = \(19{,}700 + 0 = \mathbf{\$19{,}700}\) (assuming no other items), while total interest expense includes the $2,160 accrual.

**Key insight:** A PPE rollforward must split **cash** vs **noncash** purchases so the investing section is not overstated. Noncash inception (note, lease) is disclosed once; later **cash** interest and principal hit the SCF when paid. Period-end **accruals** create the bridge between expense and the interest-paid disclosure.

### Q4 — MC — Partial noncash land purchase on the SCF
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Classification — cash investing outflow only; noncash stock excluded from financing  
**Question:** **Ironridge Materials Corp.** purchased land for **$410,000**. The seller accepted **$275,000 cash** and the remainder in Ironridge common stock with a fair value of **$135,000**. How should Ironridge report this transaction on its statement of cash flows?

| | Investing activities | Financing activities |
|---|---|---|
| A) | $(410,000) | $(135,000) |
| B) | $(275,000) | $(135,000) |
| C) | $(410,000) | $0 |
| D) | $(275,000) | $0 |

**Answer:** **D.** Only the **$275,000 cash** paid is an investing outflow. Issuing stock for land is a **noncash investing and financing** transaction disclosed in the notes (or on the same page as the SCF)—it is **not** a financing cash inflow (and not a financing outflow). Reporting $(410,000) would double-count economic activity that did not use $410,000 of cash.

### Q5 — MC — Required disclosures when using the indirect method
**LO:** LO 22-5  
**Concept:** Required supplemental disclosures — interest paid, taxes paid, noncash, restricted cash  
**Question:** Which of the following best describes **required** statement-of-cash-flows disclosures under U.S. GAAP when the **indirect method** is used for operating activities?

- A) Interest paid and income taxes paid may be omitted if interest expense and tax expense appear on the income statement.  
- B) Noncash investing and financing transactions may be omitted if they are material, because they do not affect cash.  
- C) Interest paid (net of amounts capitalized) and income taxes paid must be disclosed; material noncash investing and financing transactions must also be disclosed; the nature of restrictions on cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash must be disclosed.  
- D) Only companies using the direct method must disclose income taxes paid.

**Answer:** **C.** Under ASC 230, the indirect method requires disclosure of **interest paid** (net of capitalized interest). **Income taxes paid** is disclosed regardless of method. Material **noncash investing and financing** activities must be disclosed (narrative or schedule). Entities also disclose the **nature of restrictions** on cash, cash equivalents, and amounts generally described as restricted cash. Expense line items on the income statement do **not** replace the cash-paid disclosures; noncash items are omitted from the body of the SCF **precisely because** they need separate disclosure when material.
