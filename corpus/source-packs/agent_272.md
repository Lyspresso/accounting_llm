# Agent 272 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-9

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** equity investment at purchase price + commissions/brokerage (insignificant influence; ASC 321 FV-NI)
- **Dividend revenue:** recognize only when investee declares (not equity-method share of earnings)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Fair Value Adjustment (FVA) updated **only** at reporting dates; unrealized holding G/L in **net income**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** cost vs year-end fair value vs required FVA balance; multi-period FVA rollforward
- **Disposal JE (period-end-only method):** realized G/L = cash proceeds − **original cost**; **no** FVA entry on the sale date
- **Year-end FVA cleanup:** remeasure FVA for securities still held (automatically removes amounts associated with securities sold during the period)
- **Classification / presentation:** current vs noncurrent by intent/ability; BS at fair value (cost ± FVA); dividends and unrealized G/L in NI
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-period period-end FVA (purchase → Y1 FVA → Y2 FVA rollforward → sale → Y3 FVA elimination)
**LO:** LO 14-9  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting entries for equity securities measured at FV-NI (FVA only at reporting dates; multi-year schedule)  
**Scenario:**  
**Prairie Ridge Capital LLC** has a December 31 year-end. On **October 1, Year 1**, Prairie Ridge purchased **5,000** shares of **Cascade Forge Inc.** common stock at **$32** per share and paid commissions of **$1,000**. Cascade has **50,000** shares outstanding. Prairie Ridge has **insignificant influence**, fair value is readily determinable, and Prairie Ridge measures the investment at **FV-NI** using the **Appendix 14C / LO 14-9** approach: fair value is adjusted **only at period-end** (not on the sale date). Beginning FVA—Equity Securities = **$0**.

**Additional facts:**
- **December 20, Year 1:** Cascade declares and pays total dividends of **$20,000**.
- **December 31, Year 1:** Cascade fair value is **$30** per share.
- **Year 2:** No purchase or sale of Cascade shares.
- **June 15, Year 2:** Cascade declares and pays total dividends of **$25,000**.
- **December 31, Year 2:** Cascade fair value is **$35** per share.
- **February 28, Year 3:** Prairie Ridge sells the **entire** Cascade holding for **$170,500** cash (ignore selling costs).
- **December 31, Year 3:** Prairie Ridge holds **no** equity securities measured at FV-NI.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **October 1, Year 1** purchase.  
b. Record the **December 20, Year 1** dividend.  
c. Prepare a **December 31, Year 1 subsequent measurement schedule** and the **period-end adjusting JE**.  
d. Record the **June 15, Year 2** dividend.  
e. Prepare a **December 31, Year 2 subsequent measurement schedule** (showing existing FVA and the **increase/(decrease) needed**) and the **period-end adjusting JE**.  
f. Record the **February 28, Year 3** sale under the period-end-only method (**no** sale-date FVA).  
g. Record the **December 31, Year 3** entry to eliminate the FVA account.  
h. Prepare a **multi-period FVA rollforward** and reconcile cumulative net income from fair-value changes and sale (exclude dividends) to economic G/L. Show Year 1–3 BS presentation of the investment (when held).

Sales use **cost-basis** realized G/L and leave FVA alone until the next year-end, when the schedule forces cleanup of amounts related to sold securities.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial disposal + portfolio period-end FVA for securities still held

**g. Classification and presentation / SCF**  
- **Balance sheet:** Classify equity investments as **current** if management intends and is able to sell within one year (or operating cycle, if longer); otherwise **noncurrent**. Reported amount is always **fair value** (cost ± FVA).  
- **Income statement:** Dividend revenue, realized G/L on sale, and unrealized holding G/L (period-end AJ) all run through **net income**.  
- **Statement of cash flows:** Purchases and sales of equity securities are **investing** activities unless the entity’s purpose is active short-term trading for profits, in which case cash flows may be classified as **operating** (ASC 230 / ASC 321 presentation guidance).

**Key insight:** After a partial sale, the **period-end adjusting JE** is built only from securities **still held**. Allocated original cost drives mid-year realized G/L; any FVA that “belonged” to sold shares is not removed until the next period-end schedule forces the required FVA to equal FV − cost of remaining holdings.

### Q4 — MC — Sale measurement under period-end-only FV-NI
**LO:** LO 14-9  
**Concept:** Classification of sale measurement — period-end-only FVA (LO 14-9) versus sale-date FVA (LO 14-4)  
**Question:**  
Under the **Appendix 14C / LO 14-9** approach for equity securities measured at FV-NI (fair value adjustments **only at period-end**), which statement is correct when the investor sells an equity security mid-year?

- A) First adjust the investment to fair value through Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income on the sale date, then remove the investment at its updated fair value and eliminate the related FVA.  
- B) Record cash and remove the investment at **original cost**; recognize realized G/L as proceeds − original cost; **do not** adjust FVA on the sale date—adjust FVA only in the period-end adjusting entry.  
- C) Recognize the entire difference between proceeds and original cost in OCI, with FVA remaining until maturity of the equity security.  
- D) Eliminate the entire FVA account on the sale date for all equity securities in the portfolio, whether or not other securities remain.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-9, sale-date accounting uses **original cost** for realized G/L and leaves FVA alone. The next **period-end adjusting JE** resets FVA to (FV − cost) of securities still held, which automatically clears amounts associated with securities sold during the period. Choice A describes the **LO 14-4** sale-date-and-period-end approach. Choice C confuses FV-NI with FV-OCI. Choice D incorrectly zeros the whole portfolio FVA on a single-security sale.
