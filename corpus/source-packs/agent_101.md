# Agent 101 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-11

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Accounting for Bonds with Stock Warrants  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Nondetachable stock warrants:** no separate market → **entire proceeds** allocated to the debt (straight-debt issue; premium/discount = proceeds − face)
- **Detachable stock warrants:** allocate proceeds between **debt** and **equity (Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants)** under ASC 470-20
- **Incremental method:** fair value known for **only one** security → assign FV to that security; residual proceeds to the other
- **Proportional method:** fair value known for **both** bonds (without warrants) and warrants → allocate package proceeds by **relative fair values**
- **Initial recognition JE (emphasis):** Cash; Discount/Premium on Bonds Payable; Bonds Payable; Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants (detachable only)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortize bond discount/premium over the term (straight-line used here when not materially different from effective interest)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** cash interest ± amortization of discount/premium → Interest Expense
- **Exercise of warrants:** reclassify prorated PIC—Stock Warrants + cash exercise price into Common Stock and PIC in Excess of Par
- **Expiration of warrants:** reclassify remaining PIC—Stock Warrants to PIC in Excess of Par—Common Stock (no gain/loss)
- **Maturity / settlement JE:** pay face; carrying amount equals face after full amortization
- **Classification / presentation:** Bonds Payable (net of unamortized discount or plus unamortized premium) as long-term liability; PIC—Stock Warrants in **equity** (paid-in capital), not a liability
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with fully changed facts and amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Detachable warrants, proportional allocation, discount schedule, exercise/expiration
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for bonds with detachable warrants (proportional method) and nondetachable contrast; subsequent discount amortization schedule; period-end interest JE; warrant exercise and expiration settlement; classification  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Ironwood Forge Corp.** (calendar year-end) issues **$150,000** face of **6%, 5-year** nonconvertible bonds. Interest is payable **annually each December 31**. Each **$1,000** bond carries **8 detachable** stock purchase warrants. Each warrant entitles the holder to purchase **one share** of Ironwood **$10 par** common stock for an exercise price of **$25** at any time through January 31, Year 3.

The bonds **with warrants** sell as a package for **102** (proceeds **$153,000**). Shortly after issuance:
- The **detachable warrants** trade for **$6** each.
- The bonds are quoted at **104 without** the warrants attached.

Ironwood uses **straight-line** amortization of bond discount/premium (results not materially different from effective interest).

On **January 15, Year 3**, holders exercise **800** of the **1,200** warrants. The remaining **400** warrants expire unexercised on **January 31, Year 3**. The market price of Ironwood common on the exercise date is **$32** per share (market price does **not** affect the issuer’s exercise entry).

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **proportional-method allocation** of the $153,000 package proceeds to (1) the bonds and (2) the warrants. State the resulting **discount or premium** on the bonds.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for issuance with **detachable** warrants (proportional method). Show Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare the alternative **January 1, Year 1** entry **if the warrants were nondetachable** instead (same package price of 102).  
d. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of straight-line discount amortization and bond carrying amount for **Years 1–5** (annual periods ending December 31).  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end** interest payment and discount amortization journal entry.  
f. Prepare the **January 15, Year 3** entry for exercise of 800 warrants and the **January 31, Year 3** entry for expiration of the remaining 400 warrants.  
g. State **December 31, Year 1** balance-sheet **classification/presentation** of (1) the bonds (net) and (2) Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Proportional allocation**

| Item | Fair value | Proportion |
|---|---:|---|
| Bonds without warrants ($150,000 × 1.04) | $156,000 | 156,000 / 163,200 |
| Warrants (150 bonds × 8 × $6) | 7,200 | 7,200 / 163,200 |
| **Total fair value** | **$163,200** | |

Package proceeds = $150,000 × 1.02 = **$153,000**

| Allocation | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| To bonds | $153,000 × 156,000 / 163,200 | **$146,250** |
| To warrants | $153,000 × 7,200 / 163,200 | **$6,750** |
| **Total** | | **$153,000** |

Discount on Bonds Payable = Face − Allocated bond proceeds = $150,000 − $146,250 = **$3,750**

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (detachable; proportional) — EMPHASIS**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 153,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 3,750 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 150,000 |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | | 6,750 |
| *Allocate package proceeds to debt and equity; residual creates bond discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 153,000 + 3,750 = **156,750**; Cr 150,000 + 6,750 = **156,750**. **Balanced.**

**c. January 1, Year 1 — if warrants were nondetachable**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash ($150,000 × 1.02) | 153,000 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 3,000 |
| Bonds Payable | | 150,000 |
| *Nondetachable: entire package price to debt; no separate equity for warrants* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 153,000. **Balanced.**  
(Contrast: detachable → equity slice **$6,750** and **discount $3,750**; nondetachable → **premium $3,000**, no PIC—Stock Warrants.)

**d. Subsequent measurement schedule — straight-line discount (5 years)**  
Annual discount amortization = $3,750 ÷ 5 = **$750**  
Annual cash interest = $150,000 × 6% = **$9,000**  
Annual interest expense = $9,000 + $750 = **$9,750**

| Date | Cash interest | Discount amort. | Interest expense | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Y1 (issue) | — | — | — | 3,750 | 146,250 |
| Dec. 31, Y1 | 9,000 | 750 | 9,750 | 3,000 | 147,000 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 9,000 | 750 | 9,750 | 2,250 | 147,750 |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 9,000 | 750 | 9,750 | 1,500 | 148,500 |
| Dec. 31, Y4 | 9,000 | 750 | 9,750 | 750 | 149,250 |
| Dec. 31, Y5 | 9,000 | 750 | 9,750 | 0 | 150,000 |
| **Totals** | **45,000** | **3,750** | **48,750** | | |

**Schedule check:** Ending carrying amount at maturity = face **$150,000**; total amort. = original discount **$3,750**.

**e. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting / interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 9,750 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 750 |
| Cash | | 9,000 |
| *Cash coupon + SL amortization of warrant-driven discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,750 = Cr 750 + 9,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Warrant exercise and expiration (settlement of equity component)**

**January 15, Year 3 — exercise of 800 warrants**  
PIC—Stock Warrants reclassified = $6,750 × 800/1,200 = **$4,500**  
Cash = 800 × $25 = **$20,000**  
Common Stock = 800 × $10 = **$8,000**  
PIC in Excess of Par—CS = $20,000 + $4,500 − $8,000 = **$16,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 20,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 4,500 | |
| Common Stock | | 8,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 16,500 |
| *Exercise: cash + prorated warrant equity → common equity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 + 4,500 = Cr 8,000 + 16,500 = **24,500**. **Balanced.**

**January 31, Year 3 — expiration of 400 warrants**  
Remaining PIC—Stock Warrants = $6,750 − $4,500 = **$2,250** (or $6,750 × 400/1,200)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 2,250 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 2,250 |
| *Expiration reclassifies warrant equity within paid-in capital; no gain or loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,250. **Balanced.**  
After expiration, PIC—Stock Warrants balance = **$0**.

**g. Classification / presentation (December 31, Year 1)**

| Item | Amount | Classification | Support |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Bonds Payable | $150,000 | Long-term liability (gross) | Due Dec. 31, Year 5 (not within one year) |
| Less: Discount on Bonds Payable | (3,000) | Contra-liability | Unamortized after Y1 amort. of $750 |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$147,000** | **Long-term liability** | Carrying amount = face − unamortized discount |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | **$6,750** | **Equity — additional paid-in capital** | Detachable warrant proceeds; **not** a liability |

**Key insight:** Detachable warrants force a **split of package proceeds** between **debt** and **equity**. The proportional method uses **relative fair values** of bonds-without-warrants and warrants; the equity slice usually creates (or increases) a **bond discount**. That discount is amortized into interest expense over the term. Warrant **exercise** and **expiration** only reclassify **equity** accounts—they do not affect the bond liability.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin — Proportional method with premium; full life path
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition of bonds with detachable warrants (proportional); subsequent premium amortization schedule; period-end interest JE; warrant exercise/expiration; classification  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Cedarpoint Rail Inc.** issues **$400,000** face of **7%, 5-year** nonconvertible bonds with **detachable** stock purchase warrants. Interest is payable **annually each December 31**. Each **$1,000** bond carries **12** detachable warrants. Each warrant allows purchase of **one share** of Cedarpoint **$1 par** common for **$15** through February 28, Year 2.

The package sells at **104** (proceeds **$416,000**). Shortly after issuance:
- Bonds are quoted at **100 without** warrants.
- Warrants trade for **$2** each.

Straight-line amortization of premium is used. On **February 10, Year 2**, holders exercise **3,200** of the **4,800** warrants; the remaining **1,600** expire on **February 28, Year 2**.

**Required:**  
a. Allocate package proceeds under the **proportional method**; compute premium (or discount).  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
c. Prepare the **5-year straight-line premium amortization schedule** (carrying amount each December 31).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end** interest/amortization JE.  
e. Record exercise of 3,200 warrants and expiration of 1,600 warrants.  
f. Present bonds payable, net, and PIC—Stock Warrants at **December 31, Year 1**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Proportional allocation**

| Item | Fair value |
|---|---:|
| Bonds without warrants ($400,000 × 1.00) | $400,000 |
| Warrants (400 bonds × 12 × $2) | 9,600 |
| **Total fair value** | **$409,600** |

Proceeds = $400,000 × 1.04 = **$416,000**

| Allocation | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| To bonds | $416,000 × 400,000 / 409,600 | **$406,250** |
| To warrants | $416,000 × 9,600 / 409,600 | **$9,750** |
| **Total** | | **$416,000** |

Premium on Bonds Payable = $406,250 − $400,000 = **$6,250**

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (detachable; proportional)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 416,000 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 6,250 |
| Bonds Payable | | 400,000 |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | | 9,750 |
| *Package proceeds allocated to debt (with premium) and warrant equity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 416,000 = Cr 6,250 + 400,000 + 9,750. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — straight-line premium**  
Annual premium amortization = $6,250 ÷ 5 = **$1,250**  
Annual cash interest = $400,000 × 7% = **$28,000**  
Annual interest expense = $28,000 − $1,250 = **$26,750**

| Date | Cash interest | Premium amort. | Interest expense | Unamortized premium | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Y1 (issue) | — | — | — | 6,250 | 406,250 |
| Dec. 31, Y1 | 28,000 | 1,250 | 26,750 | 5,000 | 405,000 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 28,000 | 1,250 | 26,750 | 3,750 | 403,750 |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 28,000 | 1,250 | 26,750 | 2,500 | 402,500 |
| Dec. 31, Y4 | 28,000 | 1,250 | 26,750 | 1,250 | 401,250 |
| Dec. 31, Y5 | 28,000 | 1,250 | 26,750 | 0 | 400,000 |
| **Totals** | **140,000** | **6,250** | **133,750** | | |

**Schedule check:** Maturity carrying amount = face **$400,000**; total premium amortized = **$6,250**.

**d. December 31, Year 1 — period-end interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 26,750 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 1,250 | |
| Cash | | 28,000 |
| *Cash coupon reduced by SL premium amortization* | | |

**Check:** Dr 26,750 + 1,250 = Cr 28,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Exercise and expiration**

**February 10, Year 2 — exercise of 3,200 warrants**  
PIC—SW reclassified = $9,750 × 3,200 / 4,800 = **$6,500**  
Cash = 3,200 × $15 = **$48,000**  
Common Stock = 3,200 × $1 = **$3,200**  
PIC in Excess of Par—CS = $48,000 + $6,500 − $3,200 = **$51,300**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 48,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 6,500 | |
| Common Stock | | 3,200 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 51,300 |

**Check:** Dr 48,000 + 6,500 = Cr 3,200 + 51,300 = **54,500**. **Balanced.**

**February 28, Year 2 — expiration of 1,600 warrants**  
Remaining PIC—SW = $9,750 − $6,500 = **$3,250**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 3,250 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 3,250 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,250. **Balanced.**

**f. Classification (December 31, Year 1)**

| Item | Amount | Presentation |
|---|---:|---|
| Bonds Payable | $400,000 | Long-term liability |
| Add: Premium on Bonds Payable | 5,000 | Adjunct liability (after Y1 amort.) |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$405,000** | **Long-term liability** |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | **$9,750** | **Equity (APIC)** |

**Key insight:** Same LO 16-11 proportional path as Q1, but different face, rates, warrant counts, and fair values produce a **premium** (allocated bond proceeds > face) rather than a discount. Interest expense is **below** the cash coupon while the premium amortizes; warrant exercise/expiration still only move amounts **within equity**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Incremental method; discount life cycle; maturity settlement
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of bonds with detachable warrants using the **incremental method** (only warrant FV known); subsequent discount schedule; period-end JE; warrant exercise/expiration; bond maturity settlement JE; nondetachable contrast; classification  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Ridgeway Materials LLC** issues **$300,000** of **6%, 5-year** nonconvertible bonds with **detachable** stock purchase warrants. Interest is paid **annually on December 31**. Each **$1,000** bond carries **15** detachable warrants. Each warrant is for **one share** of Ridgeway **$5 par** common at an exercise price of **$20**.

The package sells at **102** (proceeds **$306,000**). Shortly after issuance, the warrants trade for **$6** each. **Fair value of the bonds selling without warrants is not determinable.** Ridgeway therefore uses the **incremental method**. Straight-line amortization applies.

Warrants expire January 31, Year 2. On **January 12, Year 2**, holders exercise **3,600** of the **4,500** warrants; **900** expire on **January 31, Year 2**. The bonds are held to **maturity on December 31, Year 5** and settled in cash at face.

**Required:**  
a. Allocate proceeds under the **incremental method** and compute the bond discount (or premium).  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (detachable; incremental).  
c. Record the alternative issuance entry if the warrants were **nondetachable**.  
d. Prepare the **5-year discount amortization schedule** and the **December 31, Year 1** period-end interest JE.  
e. Record warrant **exercise** and **expiration** entries in Year 2.  
f. Record the **December 31, Year 5 maturity settlement JE** for the bonds (after full amortization).  
g. Explain how **Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants** and **Bonds Payable, net** should be **classified** immediately after issuance.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Incremental method allocation**  
Only the warrants have a known fair value.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Package proceeds ($300,000 × 1.02) | $306,000 |
| Fair value of warrants (300 × 15 × $6) | 27,000 |
| **Allocation to bonds (residual)** | **$279,000** |

Discount = $300,000 − $279,000 = **$21,000**

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (detachable; incremental) — EMPHASIS**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 306,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 21,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 300,000 |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | | 27,000 |
| *Incremental: assign known FV to warrants; residual to debt* | | |

**Check:** Dr 306,000 + 21,000 = Cr 300,000 + 27,000 = **327,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. If warrants were nondetachable**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 306,000 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 6,000 |
| Bonds Payable | | 300,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 306,000. **Balanced.**  
(Nondetachable → **premium $6,000** and **no** equity for warrants; detachable incremental → **discount $21,000** and **PIC—SW $27,000**.)

**d. Subsequent measurement schedule and Year 1 period-end JE**  
Annual discount amortization = $21,000 ÷ 5 = **$4,200**  
Cash interest = $300,000 × 6% = **$18,000**  
Interest expense = $18,000 + $4,200 = **$22,200**

| Date | Cash interest | Discount amort. | Interest expense | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 21,000 | 279,000 |
| Dec. 31, Y1 | 18,000 | 4,200 | 22,200 | 16,800 | 283,200 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 18,000 | 4,200 | 22,200 | 12,600 | 287,400 |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 18,000 | 4,200 | 22,200 | 8,400 | 291,600 |
| Dec. 31, Y4 | 18,000 | 4,200 | 22,200 | 4,200 | 295,800 |
| Dec. 31, Y5 | 18,000 | 4,200 | 22,200 | 0 | 300,000 |
| **Totals** | **90,000** | **21,000** | **111,000** | | |

**Schedule check:** CV at maturity = face; total amort. = $21,000.

**December 31, Year 1 — period-end JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 22,200 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 4,200 |
| Cash | | 18,000 |

**Check:** Dr 22,200 = Cr 4,200 + 18,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Exercise and expiration (Year 2)**

**January 12, Year 2 — exercise of 3,600 warrants**  
PIC—SW = $27,000 × 3,600 / 4,500 = **$21,600**  
Cash = 3,600 × $20 = **$72,000**  
Common Stock = 3,600 × $5 = **$18,000**  
PIC in Excess of Par—CS = $72,000 + $21,600 − $18,000 = **$75,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 72,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 21,600 | |
| Common Stock | | 18,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 75,600 |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 + 21,600 = Cr 18,000 + 75,600 = **93,600**. **Balanced.**

**January 31, Year 2 — expiration of 900 warrants**  
Remaining PIC—SW = $27,000 − $21,600 = **$5,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | 5,400 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 5,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,400. **Balanced.**

**f. December 31, Year 5 — maturity settlement of bonds**  
After Year 5 amortization, unamortized discount = **$0**; carrying amount = face **$300,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 300,000 | |
| Cash | | 300,000 |
| *Settle face at maturity; discount fully amortized* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 300,000. **Balanced.**  
(Interest for Year 5, including final $4,200 discount amort., is recorded in the same period’s interest entry as in part d pattern.)

**g. Classification immediately after issuance (Jan. 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Amount | Classification |
|---|---:|---|
| Bonds Payable | $300,000 | Long-term liability |
| Less: Discount on Bonds Payable | (21,000) | Contra-liability |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$279,000** | **Long-term liability** |
| Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants | **$27,000** | **Stockholders’ equity (APIC)** — never presented as a liability |

**Key insight:** When only **one** security’s fair value is known, the **incremental method** assigns that FV first and leaves a **residual** for the other security—often producing a larger bond discount than a simple “package over face” view. Debt then follows normal discount amortization through **maturity settlement**; the warrant equity is cleared only by **exercise** or **expiration** reclassifications.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method choice)
**LO:** LO 16-11  
**Concept:** Method selection for detachable warrants and accounting difference vs nondetachable warrants  

**Question 1:**  
On the issue date of bonds sold with stock purchase warrants, the issuer knows the market price of the **warrants** but **cannot** determine a fair value for identical bonds **without** warrants. The warrants are **detachable**. Which allocation approach is appropriate?

- A) Allocate the entire package proceeds to Bonds Payable (and premium/discount); ignore the warrants until exercise  
- B) **Incremental method** — measure warrants at fair value; allocate residual proceeds to the bonds  
- C) Proportional method — must impute a bond fair value equal to face before allocating  
- D) Record warrants as a liability at fair value and bonds at residual proceeds  

**Answer:** **B.** When only one security’s fair value is known, ASC 470-20 practice (and Demo 16-11) uses the **incremental method**: assign known FV to that security (here, warrants → PIC—Stock Warrants) and residual package proceeds to the debt.  
A describes **nondetachable** treatment. C is the proportional method, which requires **both** fair values. D is incorrect: detachable stock warrants are **equity**, not a liability.

---

**Question 2:**  
**Harborline Co.** issues nonconvertible bonds with **nondetachable** stock warrants at a package price of 103. Compared with an otherwise identical issue of bonds with **detachable** warrants (where warrant FV is positive and known), Harborline’s issuance entry for the **nondetachable** issue will:

- A) Credit Paid-In Capital—Stock Warrants for the fair value of the warrants  
- B) Allocate package proceeds between debt and equity using relative fair values  
- C) Allocate **all** package proceeds to the bonds (debt), with any excess over face as premium (or shortfall as discount)  
- D) Debit a warrant liability equal to the intrinsic value of the warrants  

**Answer:** **C.** Nondetachable warrants have **no separate market**, so the entire bond price is treated as a **straight-debt issue**—all proceeds to the bonds. Detachable warrants require splitting proceeds to **PIC—Stock Warrants** (A/B apply only to detachable). No warrant liability is recorded for these equity-classified stock purchase warrants (D).
