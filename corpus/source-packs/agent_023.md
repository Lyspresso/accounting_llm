# Agent 23 — CORE DEMO — LO 17-3

**Chapter:** 17  
**LO title:** Account for a basic operating lease for a lessee  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** initial_recognition_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Operating lease classification (none of five finance criteria met)
- Initial recognition: ROU asset = lease liability = PV of lease payments (annuity due)
- Lease liability amortization schedule (effective interest)
- ROU asset schedule: straight-line lease expense − “interest” = ROU reduction
- Period-end adjusting JE: single **Lease expense** (not separate interest + amortization on IS)
- Balance sheet current / noncurrent lease liability split
- Final-year / maturity settlement (liability and ROU to zero)
- Income statement presentation: single operating lease expense
- Number-variant twin (different term, rate, payment)

### Q1 — CORE — CedarPeak Outfitters full operating lease life cycle
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE + liability/ROU schedules + subsequent and maturity JEs (basic operating lease lessee)  
**Scenario:**  
On January 1, Year 1 (commencement), CedarPeak Outfitters leases specialized warehouse racking from Alpine Equipment Leasing under a 3-year noncancellable lease. Facts:

| Item | Amount / detail |
|------|-----------------|
| Annual lease payment (annuity due) | $31,764.04 on January 1 of each year (Years 1–3) |
| Discount rate | 6% (lessor’s implicit rate, known to lessee; equals incremental borrowing rate) |
| Fair value of racking at commencement | $250,000 |
| Remaining economic life | 8 years |
| Ownership transfer | No — asset reverts to lessor |
| Purchase / renewal option | None |
| Residual value guarantee | None |
| Alternative use to lessor | Yes — equipment is routinely re-leased |
| CedarPeak fiscal year-end | December 31 |

No initial direct costs, incentives, or prepaid rent. Basic operating lease (fixed payments only).

**Required:**  
a. Classify the lease for CedarPeak and support with all five classification criteria.  
b. Compute the lease liability and right-of-use (ROU) asset at commencement.  
c. Prepare the **lease liability schedule** and **ROU asset schedule** for the full term.  
d. Record **all** lessee journal entries for Year 1 (emphasize initial recognition).  
e. Show Year 1 balance sheet (ROU; current and noncurrent lease liability) and income statement impact.  
f. Record lessee journal entries for Year 2 and Year 3 (including maturity / settlement).  
g. Confirm that ROU and lease liability are zero after the Year 3 entries.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification — operating lease** (no finance criterion met)

| Criterion | Analysis | Met? |
|-----------|----------|------|
| 1. Ownership transfer | Asset reverts to lessor | No |
| 2. Purchase option | None | No |
| 3. Lease term length | 3 / 8 = 37.5% < 75% of remaining economic life | No |
| 4. PV of lease payments | PV = $90,000 < 90% × $250,000 = $225,000 | No |
| 5. No alternative use | Lessor routinely re-leases this class of equipment | No |

→ **Operating lease.**

**b. Initial measurement**  
Annuity-due PV (TYPE = 1):  
`PV(0.06, 3, −31764.04, 0, 1) = $90,000.00`

| Account | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Lease liability | $90,000 |
| ROU asset (basic case: equals liability) | $90,000 |

**c. Lease liability schedule**

| Date | Lease payment | Interest on liability (6%) | Liability reduction | Lease liability bal. |
|------|---------------|----------------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Jan. 1, Y1 (commence) | | | | **90,000.00** |
| Jan. 1, Y1 | 31,764.04 | 0.00 | 31,764.04 | 58,235.96 |
| Jan. 1, Y2 | 31,764.04 | 3,494.16 | 28,269.88 | 29,966.08 |
| Jan. 1, Y3 | 31,764.04 | 1,797.96 | 29,966.08 | 0.00 |
| **Totals** | **95,292.12** | **5,292.12** | **90,000.00** | |

Interest check: 58,235.96 × 0.06 = 3,494.1576 → **3,494.16**; 29,966.08 × 0.06 = 1,797.9648 → **1,797.96**.

**ROU asset schedule**  
Straight-line lease expense = total undiscounted payments ÷ periods = 95,292.12 ÷ 3 = **31,764.04** (equals cash payment when only fixed level payments).  
ROU reduction each period = SL lease expense − period “interest” from liability schedule.

| Date | Lease expense (SL) | Interest on liability | ROU reduction | ROU balance |
|------|--------------------|----------------------|---------------|-------------|
| Jan. 1, Y1 (commence) | | | | **90,000.00** |
| Dec. 31, Y1 | 31,764.04 | 3,494.16 | 28,269.88 | 61,730.12 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 31,764.04 | 1,797.96 | 29,966.08 | 31,764.04 |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 31,764.04 | 0.00 | 31,764.04 | 0.00 |
| **Totals** | **95,292.12** | **5,292.12** | **90,000.00** | |

**d. Year 1 journal entries (all balance)**

**January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (emphasis)**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Right-of-use asset | 90,000.00 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lease liability | | 90,000.00 |
*Dr 90,000 = Cr 90,000*

**January 1, Year 1 — First lease payment**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease liability | 31,764.04 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 31,764.04 |
*Dr 31,764.04 = Cr 31,764.04*

**December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting (single lease expense)**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease expense | 31,764.04 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lease liability | | 3,494.16 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Right-of-use asset | | 28,269.88 |
*Dr 31,764.04 = Cr 3,494.16 + 28,269.88 = 31,764.04*

(Optional two-entry form: Dr ROU / Cr Lease liability 3,494.16; then Dr Lease expense / Cr ROU 31,764.04 — same net effect.)

**e. Year 1 financial statement impact**

**Balance sheet — Dec. 31, Year 1**  
| | Amount |
|--|-------:|
| Right-of-use asset (operating) | $61,730.12 |
| Lease liability — current | $31,764.04 |
| Lease liability — noncurrent ($61,730.12 − $31,764.04) | $29,966.08 |
| Total lease liability (58,235.96 + 3,494.16) | $61,730.12 |

**Income statement — Year 1**  
| | Amount |
|--|-------:|
| Lease expense (single amount in income from continuing operations) | $31,764.04 |

*Not* presented as separate interest expense + ROU amortization (that presentation is for finance leases).

**f. Year 2 entries**

**January 1, Year 2 — Payment**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease liability | 31,764.04 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 31,764.04 |

**December 31, Year 2 — Adjusting**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease expense | 31,764.04 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lease liability | | 1,797.96 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Right-of-use asset | | 29,966.08 |
*Dr 31,764.04 = Cr 1,797.96 + 29,966.08*

**Year 3 (maturity / settlement)**

**January 1, Year 3 — Final payment**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease liability | 31,764.04 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 31,764.04 |

After this payment, lease liability balance = **$0**.

**December 31, Year 3 — Final ROU write-down (no remaining “interest”)**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease expense | 31,764.04 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Right-of-use asset | | 31,764.04 |
*Dr 31,764.04 = Cr 31,764.04*

**g. Post-maturity**  
ROU asset = **$0**; lease liability = **$0**. Underlying asset returned; no purchase.

**Key insight:** At commencement the lessee capitalizes an operating lease (ROU + liability) exactly as for a finance lease, but **subsequent** measurement puts a **single straight-line lease expense** on the income statement. “Interest” is computed only to drive the ROU reduction so that Expense = Interest-component + ROU-reduction each period.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — SummitBrite Media five-year operating lease
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — full schedules and multi-year JEs with different n, rate, and payment  
**Scenario:**  
On January 1, Year 1, SummitBrite Media leases a broadcast production trailer for **5 years**. Annual payments of **$25,000** are due at the **beginning** of each year. SummitBrite’s incremental borrowing rate is **5%** (implicit rate unknown). Fair value of the trailer is **$280,000**; remaining economic life is **12 years**. No ownership transfer, no purchase option, no residual guarantee; the lessor re-leases similar trailers routinely. Calendar year-end. No IDC/incentives/prepayments.

**Required:**  
a. Classify the lease and compute initial ROU asset and lease liability.  
b. Prepare the full lease liability and ROU asset schedules.  
c. Record commencement, first payment, and December 31 Year 1 adjusting entries.  
d. Record January 1 Year 2 payment and December 31 Year 2 adjusting entry.  
e. State Year 2 income statement lease expense and Dec. 31 Year 2 BS amounts (ROU; current and noncurrent liability).  
f. Record the Year 5 (final year) payment and final adjusting entry.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification & initial measurement**  
- Term: 5/12 ≈ 41.7% < 75% → criterion 3 not met  
- PV of payments (below) $113,648.76 ≪ 90% × $280,000 = $252,000 → criterion 4 not met  
- No ownership transfer, purchase option, or no-alternative-use → **operating lease**

`PV(0.05, 5, −25000, 0, 1) = $113,648.76`

| | Amount |
|--|-------:|
| Lease liability | $113,648.76 |
| ROU asset | $113,648.76 |

**b. Lease liability schedule**

| Date | Payment | Interest (5%) | Reduction | Balance |
|------|--------:|--------------:|----------:|--------:|
| Jan. 1, Y1 commence | | | | **113,648.76** |
| Jan. 1, Y1 | 25,000.00 | 0.00 | 25,000.00 | 88,648.76 |
| Jan. 1, Y2 | 25,000.00 | 4,432.44 | 20,567.56 | 68,081.20 |
| Jan. 1, Y3 | 25,000.00 | 3,404.06 | 21,595.94 | 46,485.26 |
| Jan. 1, Y4 | 25,000.00 | 2,324.26 | 22,675.74 | 23,809.52 |
| Jan. 1, Y5 | 25,000.00 | 1,190.48 | 23,809.52 | 0.00 |
| **Totals** | **125,000.00** | **11,351.24** | **113,648.76** | |

Interest checks: 88,648.76×0.05=4,432.438→**4,432.44**; 68,081.20×0.05=3,404.06; 46,485.26×0.05=2,324.263→**2,324.26**; 23,809.52×0.05=1,190.476→**1,190.48**.

**ROU asset schedule** (SL expense = 125,000 ÷ 5 = **25,000** each year)

| Date | Lease expense | Interest | ROU reduction | ROU balance |
|------|-------------:|---------:|--------------:|------------:|
| Jan. 1, Y1 commence | | | | **113,648.76** |
| Dec. 31, Y1 | 25,000.00 | 4,432.44 | 20,567.56 | 93,081.20 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 25,000.00 | 3,404.06 | 21,595.94 | 71,485.26 |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 25,000.00 | 2,324.26 | 22,675.74 | 48,809.52 |
| Dec. 31, Y4 | 25,000.00 | 1,190.48 | 23,809.52 | 25,000.00 |
| Dec. 31, Y5 | 25,000.00 | 0.00 | 25,000.00 | 0.00 |

**c. Year 1 journal entries**

Jan. 1, Y1 — Recognition:  
Dr Right-of-use asset **113,648.76** / Cr Lease liability **113,648.76**

Jan. 1, Y1 — Payment:  
Dr Lease liability **25,000** / Cr Cash **25,000**

Dec. 31, Y1 — Adjusting:  
Dr Lease expense **25,000.00**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cr Lease liability **4,432.44**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cr Right-of-use asset **20,567.56**  
*(25,000.00 = 4,432.44 + 20,567.56)*

**d. Year 2**

Jan. 1, Y2: Dr Lease liability **25,000** / Cr Cash **25,000**

Dec. 31, Y2:  
Dr Lease expense **25,000.00**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cr Lease liability **3,404.06**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cr Right-of-use asset **21,595.94**  
*(25,000.00 = 3,404.06 + 21,595.94)*

- Income statement: Lease expense **$25,000**  
- ROU asset: **$71,485.26**  
- Total lease liability: 68,081.20 + 3,404.06 = **$71,485.26**

- Noncurrent lease liability: 71,485.26 − 25,000 = **$46,485.26**

**f. Year 5 maturity**

Jan. 1, Y5: Dr Lease liability **25,000** / Cr Cash **25,000** → liability **$0**

Dec. 31, Y5: Dr Lease expense **25,000** / Cr Right-of-use asset **25,000** → ROU **$0**

**Key insight:** Changing term, rate, and payment changes PV and the interest/ROU-split each year, but the **operating lease pattern** is unchanged: level single lease expense, liability rolled with effective interest, ROU plugged so expense stays straight-line.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — period-end adjusting JE, BS split, and maturity
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE; current/noncurrent presentation; final-year settlement  
**Scenario:**  
Rivergate Labs entered a **4-year** operating lease of lab benches on January 1, Year 1. Annual payments of **$32,670.73** are due each January 1. Discount rate **6%**. Initial PV of lease payments (and initial ROU) was **$120,000** (rounded; exact PV of the four payments at 6% annuity-due is $119,999.98 — use **$120,000.00** for commencement measurement as management’s rounded initial recognition).

After the January 1, Year 2 payment, the lease liability balance is **$59,898.28** and the ROU asset balance is **$92,569.01**. Rivergate has a December 31 year-end. The lease remains an operating lease; no modifications.

(Supporting commencement rollforward for graders — not required of student unless asked:)

| Date | Payment | Interest | Reduction | Liab. bal. | YE int. | ROU red. | ROU bal. |
|------|--------:|---------:|----------:|-----------:|--------:|---------:|---------:|
| 1/1/Y1 commence | | | | 119,999.98 | | | 119,999.98 |
| 1/1/Y1 | 32,670.73 | 0 | 32,670.73 | 87,329.25 | | | |
| 12/31/Y1 | | | | | 5,239.76 | 27,430.97 | 92,569.01 |
| 1/1/Y2 | 32,670.73 | 5,239.76 | 27,430.97 | 59,898.28 | | | 92,569.01 |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** adjusting journal entry only. Show that the entry balances.  
b. Compute Dec. 31, Year 2 **current** and **noncurrent** lease liability and the ROU carrying amount.  
c. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4** final payment entry and the **December 31, Year 4** final adjusting entry (maturity settlement). After 1/1/Y4 payment the liability is zero; after 12/31/Y4 the ROU is zero.  
d. Briefly state how Year 2 lease cost appears on the income statement versus how a finance lease would present cost.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Dec. 31, Year 2 adjusting JE**  
Interest for Year 2 = 59,898.28 × 0.06 = **3,593.8968 → 3,593.90**  
SL lease expense = **32,670.73**  
ROU reduction = 32,670.73 − 3,593.90 = **29,076.83**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease expense | 32,670.73 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lease liability | | 3,593.90 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Right-of-use asset | | 29,076.83 |
*Dr 32,670.73 = Cr 3,593.90 + 29,076.83 = 32,670.73* ✓

**b. Dec. 31, Year 2 BS amounts**  
- ROU: 92,569.01 − 29,076.83 = **$63,492.18**  
- Total lease liability: 59,898.28 + 3,593.90 = **$63,492.18**  
- Current lease liability (next payment due 1/1/Y3): **$32,670.73**  
- Noncurrent lease liability: 63,492.18 − 32,670.73 = **$30,821.45**

**c. Maturity year (Year 4)**  

After 12/31/Y3 adjusting, ROU = liability total = **32,670.73**, and after 1/1/Y4 payment liability is cleared:

**January 1, Year 4**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease liability | 32,670.73 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 32,670.73 |

**December 31, Year 4** (no remaining interest; settle ROU)  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Lease expense | 32,670.73 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Right-of-use asset | | 32,670.73 |

Both ROU and lease liability end at **$0**.

**d. Presentation**  
Operating lease: one **lease expense** of $32,670.73 in income from continuing operations.  
Finance lease (contrast): **interest expense** on the liability plus **amortization** of the ROU asset (front-loaded total expense early in the term).

**Key insight:** The period-end operating-lease entry always balances as Dr Lease expense (SL) = Cr Liability (effective interest) + Cr ROU (plug). Current liability equals the next cash payment for a plain annuity-due structure; noncurrent is residual total liability after that split.

### Q4 — MC — Classification and presentation
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Operating vs finance classification outcome and income-statement presentation for the lessee  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 1, Pinnacle Floors leases a forklift for 3 years. Payments of $18,000 are due at the beginning of each year. Economic life of the forklift is 10 years; fair value is $95,000. Implicit rate is unknown; incremental borrowing rate is 7%. No ownership transfer, no purchase option, residual not guaranteed; forklift has alternative use to the lessor. PV of the three payments at 7% (annuity due) is $50,412. Which statement is **correct** for Pinnacle?

- A) Finance lease; Year 1 expense equals interest on the liability plus straight-line ROU amortization totaling more than $18,000 in Year 1  
- B) Operating lease; Pinnacle records no ROU asset or lease liability because the lease is operating  
- C) Operating lease; Pinnacle recognizes ROU asset and lease liability of $50,412 at commencement and reports a single Year 1 lease expense of $18,000  
- D) Operating lease; Pinnacle expenses the $18,000 payment immediately with no year-end adjusting entry to the ROU asset  

**Answer:** **C.**  
None of the five classification criteria are met (term 30% of life; PV $50,412 < 90% × $95,000 = $85,500; no transfer/option/specialized asset) → **operating**. Lessees still recognize ROU + liability at PV. Subsequent cost is **straight-line total lease cost** (= $18,000 here), reported as a single lease expense. A is the finance-lease expense pattern. B confuses old off-balance-sheet treatment with ASC 842. D omits the required period-end entry that reduces ROU and accretes the liability.

### Q5 — MC — Statement of financial position classification
**LO:** LO 17-3  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation of operating lease ROU asset and liability on the balance sheet  
**Question:**  
At December 31, Year 1, HarborPoint Catering has one operating lease of kitchen equipment. After the year-end adjusting entry, the ROU asset is $48,200 and total lease liability is $48,200, of which the next annual payment due January 1, Year 2 is $16,500. HarborPoint presents a classified balance sheet. How should HarborPoint report these amounts?

- A) ROU asset $48,200 current; lease liability $48,200 current  
- B) ROU asset $48,200 noncurrent (separately from PPE); lease liability $16,500 current and $31,700 noncurrent  
- C) ROU asset netted against lease liability; only a net liability of $0 reported  
- D) ROU asset $48,200 in PPE; entire lease liability $48,200 noncurrent  

**Answer:** **B.**  
ASC 842 requires operating lease ROU assets and liabilities to be presented **separately** from each other and from other assets/liabilities (or disclosed). ROU assets are nonfinancial assets (typically noncurrent, subject to the same current/noncurrent considerations as similar assets). Lease liabilities are split current/noncurrent like other financial liabilities: next payment **$16,500** current; remainder **$31,700** noncurrent. They are not netted to zero (C), not automatically all current (A), and ROU is not lumped into owned PPE (D).
