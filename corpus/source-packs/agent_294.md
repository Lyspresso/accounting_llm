# Agent 294 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-7

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Describe accounting for net operating loss carryforwards and loss carryback/carryforwards  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **NOL carryforward:** tax loss creates future deductible amounts → measure **Deferred Tax Asset** = remaining NOL × enacted rate; credit **Income Tax Expense** (presented as **Income Tax Benefit** when credit balance)
- **80% limitation:** unless stated otherwise, NOL carryforwards may offset only **80% of taxable income** in each future year; residual 20% is taxed currently
- **Initial recognition JE:** Dr Deferred Tax Asset / Cr Income Tax Expense (benefit) when MLTN that DTA will be realized
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** NOL carryforward balance and DTA roll-forward (beginning → utilization → ending); expected utilization schedule vs actual
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end income tax entry when profitable after NOL year — reverse DTA for NOL used, record Income Tax Payable on residual taxable income, adjust **Valuation Allowance**
- **Utilization / settlement JE:** realization of carryforward benefit in a profitable year; carryback **Tax Refund Receivable** when carryback is allowed
- **Carryback|carryforward:** when allowed, carry back first (earliest year first within window), then carry residual forward indefinitely (subject to 80% limit on forward use)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** DTA (and VA) from NOL are **noncurrent**; IS shows income tax benefit; disclose benefits and expiration of operating loss carryforwards

### Q1 — CORE — NOL carryforward initial recognition, utilization schedule, period-end JE (emphasis), presentation
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of DTA for NOL carryforward; expected utilization schedule (80% limit); subsequent measurement; period-end adjusting JEs for utilization and valuation allowance (emphasis); IS/BS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**CedarPeak Fabrication Co.** has **no temporary differences** other than those arising from net operating loss (NOL) carryforwards. CedarPeak’s accounting and tax years both end **December 31**. The enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. **NOL carryforwards may offset a maximum of 80% of taxable income** in each future year and may be carried forward indefinitely. No loss carrybacks are allowed.

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 (actual) | **$(80,000)** | First-year NOL |
| Year 2 (management estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 20,000 | Estimated |
| Year 3 (estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 15,000 | Estimated |
| Year 4 (estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 45,000 | Estimated |
| Year 5 (estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 55,000 | Estimated |
| Year 2 (actual) | **$25,000** | Actual taxable income before NOL use |

At **December 31, Year 1**, management concludes it is **more likely than not** that the **full** deferred tax asset related to the NOL will be realized (positive evidence of sufficient future taxable income within the carryforward period).

At **December 31, Year 2**, after recording utilization of the NOL, management revises its conclusion: it is **more likely than not** that only **65%** of the **remaining** deferred tax asset will be realized (i.e., a **35%** valuation allowance is required). The Valuation Allowance account had a **zero** beginning balance.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **Deferred Tax Asset** balance at December 31, Year 1 related to the NOL carryforward.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the NOL carryforward. Show Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare an **expected NOL utilization schedule** as of December 31, Year 1 (years of expected future taxable income, 80% ceiling, cumulative NOL used, remaining NOL). Confirm whether a valuation allowance is needed at Year 1 based on that schedule.  
d. Show **Year 1 income statement** presentation of the NOL carryforward benefit.  
e. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for the **NOL carryforward** and **Deferred Tax Asset** from December 31, Year 1 through December 31, Year 2 (after utilization).  
f. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting journal entries** for (1) income tax expense / utilization of the NOL carryforward and (2) the valuation allowance. Show Dr = Cr.  
g. Show **Year 2** income statement tax line and December 31, Year 2 **balance sheet** presentation of net deferred tax assets related to the NOL.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Deferred Tax Asset at December 31, Year 1**  
Full Year 1 NOL is carried forward indefinitely.  
\[
\text{DTA} = \$80{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}
\]

**b. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Asset | 20,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 20,000 |
| *Record DTA for NOL carryforward; MLTN full realization — no valuation allowance* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$20,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Expected NOL utilization schedule (as of Dec 31, Year 1)**  
Max use each year = 80% × estimated taxable income. Cumulative use cannot exceed $80,000.

| Year | Est. taxable income | 80% ceiling | NOL used | Remaining NOL after year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Begin Y2 | — | — | — | 80,000 |
| Year 2 | 20,000 | 16,000 | 16,000 | 64,000 |
| Year 3 | 15,000 | 12,000 | 12,000 | 52,000 |
| Year 4 | 45,000 | 36,000 | 36,000 | 16,000 |
| Year 5 | 55,000 | 44,000 | 16,000 | **0** |
| **Total** | | | **80,000** | |

Expected utilization consumes the **entire** $80,000 NOL within the forecast horizon → **no valuation allowance** at December 31, Year 1 (consistent with management’s MLTN conclusion).

**d. Year 1 income statement presentation**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(80,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 20,000 |
| **Net loss** | **$(60,000)** |

**e. Subsequent measurement schedule — NOL and DTA**

**Year 2 actual utilization**  
Max NOL usable = \(80\% \times \$25{,}000 = \$20{,}000\).  
NOL used = **$20,000** (less than remaining $80,000).  
Remaining NOL = \(80{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \$60{,}000\).  
DTA after utilization = \(60{,}000 \times 25\% = \$15{,}000\).  
DTA reduction = \(20{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \$5{,}000\).

| Date | Event | NOL carryforward | DTA (gross) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 | Initial recognition | 80,000 | **20,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Utilization ($20,000 × 25%) | (20,000) | (5,000) |
| 12/31/Y2 | Ending before VA | **60,000** | **15,000** |

**Roll-forward check:** \(80{,}000 - 20{,}000 = 60{,}000\); \(20{,}000 - 5{,}000 = 15{,}000\).

**f. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

Income Tax Payable on residual 20% of taxable income:  
\[
\$25{,}000 \times 20\% \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$1{,}250}
\]  
Income Tax Expense (utilization entry) = DTA reduction + payable = \(5{,}000 + 1{,}250 = \mathbf{\$6{,}250}\).

*Entry (1) — utilize NOL carryforward and record current tax*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 6,250 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 1,250 |
| *Use $20,000 NOL (80% of $25,000 TI); tax residual 20% of TI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,250 = Cr 5,000 + 1,250. **Balanced.**

*Entry (2) — valuation allowance (35% of remaining DTA not MLTN)*  
\[
\$15{,}000 \times 35\% = \mathbf{\$5{,}250}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 5,250 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,250 |
| *Establish VA; MLTN only 65% of remaining DTA will be realized* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$5,250**. **Balanced.**

**g. Year 2 presentation**

**Income statement — tax line**  
Income tax expense = \(6{,}250 + 5{,}250 = \mathbf{\$11{,}500}\).

| | |
|---|---:|
| Pretax income | $25,000 |
| Income tax expense | (11,500) |
| **Net income** | **$13,500** |

**Balance sheet — December 31, Year 2 (noncurrent)**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Deferred tax asset | $15,000 |
| Less: Valuation allowance for deferred tax asset | (5,250) |
| **Deferred tax asset, net (noncurrent)** | **$9,750** |
| Income tax payable (current liability) | 1,250 |

**Key insight:** An NOL creates a **deferred tax asset** equal to the loss × enacted rate. Realization is limited by the **80% ceiling** each year; when profitable, the **period-end tax entry** reduces the DTA for NOL used, records **Income Tax Payable** on residual taxable income, and may establish or adjust a **valuation allowance** if full realization is no longer more likely than not.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — NOL carryforward twin (different rate, amounts, VA %)
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial DTA for NOL; 80% utilization schedule; period-end adjusting JE for utilization and partial valuation allowance; presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northvale Components Inc.** has no temporary differences other than NOL carryforwards. Tax and books end December 31. Enacted tax rate is **30%** for all years. NOL carryforwards offset a **maximum of 80%** of taxable income in each future year; no carrybacks.

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 (actual) | **$(120,000)** | NOL year |
| Year 2 (estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 40,000 | Estimated |
| Year 3 (estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 30,000 | Estimated |
| Year 4 (estimate at 12/31/Y1) | 80,000 | Estimated |
| Year 2 (actual) | **$50,000** | Actual TI before NOL |

At December 31, Year 1, management concludes it is **more likely than not** the full DTA will be realized (no VA).  
At December 31, Year 2, after utilization, management concludes it is **more likely than not** that only **80%** of the **remaining** DTA will be realized (**20%** VA required). Beginning VA = $0.

**Required:**  
a. Compute Year 1 DTA and record the **initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare the **expected utilization schedule** at December 31, Year 1.  
c. Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** for NOL and DTA through December 31, Year 2.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Record Year 2 **period-end adjusting JEs** for utilization and valuation allowance.  
e. Compute net DTA at December 31, Year 2 and Year 2 total income tax expense.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 1 DTA and initial recognition**  
\[
\text{DTA} = \$120{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$36{,}000}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Asset | 36,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 36,000 |
| *NOL carryforward DTA; full realization MLTN* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$36,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Expected utilization schedule (Dec 31, Year 1)**

| Year | Est. TI | 80% ceiling | NOL used | Remaining NOL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Begin Y2 | — | — | — | 120,000 |
| Year 2 | 40,000 | 32,000 | 32,000 | 88,000 |
| Year 3 | 30,000 | 24,000 | 24,000 | 64,000 |
| Year 4 | 80,000 | 64,000 | 64,000 | **0** |
| **Total** | | | **120,000** | |

Full expected consumption → **no VA** at Year 1.

**c. Subsequent measurement (actual Year 2)**  
NOL used = \(80\% \times \$50{,}000 = \$40{,}000\).  
Remaining NOL = \(120{,}000 - 40{,}000 = \$80{,}000\).  
Ending gross DTA = \(80{,}000 \times 30\% = \$24{,}000\).  
DTA reduction = \(36{,}000 - 24{,}000 = \$12{,}000\).

| Date | Event | NOL | Gross DTA |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 | Initial recognition | 120,000 | 36,000 |
| 12/31/Y2 | Utilization | (40,000) | (12,000) |
| 12/31/Y2 | Ending before VA | **80,000** | **24,000** |

**d. December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

Income Tax Payable:  
\[
\$50{,}000 \times 20\% \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$3{,}000}
\]  
Tax expense (utilization) = \(12{,}000 + 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 15,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 12,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 3,000 |
| *Utilize $40,000 NOL; tax residual 20% of TI at 30%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 12,000 + 3,000. **Balanced.**

Valuation allowance (20% of remaining DTA):  
\[
\$24{,}000 \times 20\% = \mathbf{\$4{,}800}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 4,800 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 4,800 |
| *VA for portion of remaining DTA not MLTN of realization* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$4,800**. **Balanced.**

**e. Net DTA and total Year 2 tax expense**  
Net DTA = \(24{,}000 - 4{,}800 = \mathbf{\$19{,}200}\) (noncurrent).  
Total income tax expense Year 2 = \(15{,}000 + 4{,}800 = \mathbf{\$19{,}800}\).

**Key insight:** Same structure as Q1 with different loss, rate, and VA percentage — measure DTA at the enacted rate, apply the **80% utilization ceiling**, and adjust the **valuation allowance** at period-end when realizability changes.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — NOL carryback|carryforward, refund receivable, residual DTA, utilization settlement
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Loss carryback (earliest year first) then carryforward residual; Tax Refund Receivable; residual DTA; optional full VA; subsequent utilization (settlement) of remaining carryforward  
**Scenario:**  
**Hearthstone Mutual Casualty Co.** (a non-life insurance company) **qualifies under applicable tax law for a two-year NOL carryback** (using the **earliest year first**) and for **indefinite carryforward** of any excess loss, subject to the **80% of taxable income** limitation on carryforwards. Books and tax both end December 31. There are **no temporary differences** other than NOL effects. Management tracks losses by year. Tax rates and taxable income (loss):

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Enacted tax rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $45,000 | 25% |
| Year 2 | 60,000 | 25% |
| Year 3 | 20,000 | 25% |
| Year 4 (current) | **$(100,000)** | 25% |
| Year 5 (actual, later) | **$30,000** | 25% |

At December 31, Year 4, management initially concludes it is **more likely than not** that any residual deferred tax asset from the carryforward will be fully realized.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **Income Tax Refund Receivable** from the two-year carryback (identify which years and amounts are offset).  
b. Compute the **residual NOL carryforward** and related **Deferred Tax Asset** at December 31, Year 4.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 4 journal entry** for the carryback|carryforward (MLTN full residual DTA realized). Show Dr = Cr.  
d. Show **Year 4 income statement** presentation.  
e. **Alternate assumption:** At December 31, Year 4, evidence indicates it is **more likely than not** that **none** of the residual DTA will be realized (beginning VA = $0). Record the **period-end adjusting JE** for the valuation allowance and recompute net tax benefit and net loss.  
f. Return to the original assumption (no VA). In **Year 5**, actual taxable income is **$30,000**. Record the **Year 5 period-end JE** that **utilizes / settles** the remaining NOL carryforward and records current tax. Show Dr = Cr.  
g. State how the NOL-related deferred tax asset is **classified** on the balance sheet and name **one required disclosure** related to operating loss carryforwards.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Tax refund receivable (two-year carryback; earliest year first)**  
Carryback window for Year 4 loss = **Year 2 and Year 3** only (Year 1 is outside the two-year window).  
Order: **Year 2 first** (earlier), then Year 3.

| Carryback year | Taxable income absorbed | Refund at 25% |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 2 | $60,000 | $15,000 |
| Year 3 | 20,000 | 5,000 |
| **Total refund** | **$80,000** of NOL | **$20,000** |

Income Tax Refund Receivable = **$20,000**.

**b. Residual NOL carryforward and DTA**  
NOL residual = \(100{,}000 - 80{,}000 = \$20{,}000\).  
\[
\text{DTA} = \$20{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}
\]

**c. December 31, Year 4 — carryback|carryforward JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Refund Receivable | 20,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 5,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 25,000 |
| *Carry back $80,000 (Y2 then Y3); carry forward residual $20,000 × 25%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 + 5,000 = Cr 25,000. **Balanced.**  
Total benefit = \(100{,}000 \times 25\% = \$25{,}000\) (full tax-effected loss).

**d. Year 4 income statement**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(100,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 25,000 |
| **Net loss** | **$(75,000)** |

**e. Full valuation allowance alternate (period-end adjusting JE)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 5,000 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,000 |
| *MLTN residual DTA will not be realized — full VA* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$5,000**. **Balanced.**

Net income tax benefit Year 4 = \(25{,}000 - 5{,}000 = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\) (equals carryback refund only).

| | |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(100,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 20,000 |
| **Net loss** | **$(80,000)** |

Net DTA on BS = \(5{,}000 - 5{,}000 = \$0\).

**f. Year 5 utilization / settlement of residual carryforward (no VA case)**  
Max offset at 80% of $30,000 = **$24,000**. Residual NOL is only **$20,000** → use **all $20,000**.  
Taxable income after NOL = \(30{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \$10{,}000\).  
Income Tax Payable = \(10{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$2{,}500}\).  
DTA reduction = **$5,000**.  
Income Tax Expense = \(5{,}000 + 2{,}500 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\)  
(check: pretax $30,000 × 25% = $7,500 when full NOL benefit is realized through prior DTA).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 7,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 2,500 |
| *Settle remaining NOL carryforward; tax residual TI after full use of $20,000 NOL* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,500 = Cr 5,000 + 2,500. **Balanced.**  
Ending NOL and gross DTA related to this carryforward = **$0**.

**g. Classification and disclosure**  
- Deferred tax assets (and related valuation allowances) arising from NOL carryforwards are classified as **noncurrent** on the balance sheet (offset with other deferred taxes of the same tax-paying component and jurisdiction into a single noncurrent amount).  
- Required disclosure includes the **benefits of and expiration dates of operating loss carryforwards** (indefinite federal carryforward still disclosed as to amount/benefits).

**Key insight:** When **carryback** is allowed, shift the loss to prior years first (earliest within the window) and record a **tax refund receivable** at prior-year rates; any residual creates a **DTA** for future years. Realization uncertainty is handled with a **valuation allowance**. When residual NOL is later used, the period-end entry **settles** the DTA against current tax on residual taxable income.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)

#### Q4-1
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Balance sheet classification of deferred tax asset from NOL carryforward  
**Question:**  
Under current U.S. GAAP (ASC 740 presentation), how is a deferred tax asset arising solely from a federal NOL carryforward classified on the classified balance sheet?

- A) Current asset, because the carryforward may be used in the next year  
- B) Noncurrent asset (and any related valuation allowance is also noncurrent)  
- C) Contra-equity, as a reduction of retained earnings until utilized  
- D) Off-balance-sheet memo item until taxable income is earned  

**Answer:** **B.** Deferred tax assets and liabilities (and related valuation allowances) for a tax-paying component within a jurisdiction are offset and presented as a **single noncurrent** amount. NOL carryforwards do not create a current classification exception.

#### Q4-2
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Identify correct period-end accounts for NOL carryback|carryforward with residual DTA  
**Question:**  
A company incurs a large tax NOL in Year 4, qualifies for a two-year carryback (earliest year first) that generates a refund, and has residual NOL to carry forward that is more likely than not to be realized. Which journal entry structure is correct at December 31 of Year 4?

- A) Debit Income Tax Expense; credit Income Tax Payable for the full tax-effected NOL  
- B) Debit Deferred Tax Asset only; no income statement effect until future years  
- C) Debit Income Tax Refund Receivable and Deferred Tax Asset; credit Income Tax Expense (benefit)  
- D) Debit Income Tax Refund Receivable; credit Deferred Tax Liability  

**Answer:** **C.** Carryback creates a **refund receivable**; residual carryforward creates a **deferred tax asset**; the combined tax-effected amount credits **Income Tax Expense** (income tax benefit). No current payable arises in a full NOL year, and the residual is an asset, not a liability.
