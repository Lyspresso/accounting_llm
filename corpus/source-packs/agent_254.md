# Agent 254 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-9

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS given multiple securities and describe EPS financial statement presentation  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Ranking (most dilutive first):** Earnings per incremental share = numerator effect ÷ denominator effect; include securities from **lowest** ratio to **highest**
- **Options/warrants first:** Treasury-stock-method securities usually rank first because the numerator effect is **$0**
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** Sequential diluted EPS schedule—add one ranked security at a time; keep only while tentative EPS **falls** (dilutive); stop when EPS **rises** (antidilutive)
- **If-converted numerator effects:** Add back convertible preferred dividends; add back convertible bond interest **net of tax**
- **Treasury stock method denominator:** Incremental shares = options assumed exercised − shares repurchasable with proceeds at **average** market price
- **Initial recognition JE:** Issuance of convertible preferred and convertible bonds at par that create potentially dilutive securities
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Preferred dividend declaration/payment; cash interest on convertible bonds
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** Actual conversion of convertible bonds into common stock
- **Financial statement presentation:** Complex capital structure → **basic and diluted** EPS with equal prominence; discontinued operations EPS on face or in notes; control number = **income from continuing operations**
- **Antidilutive exclusion:** Securities that raise tentative EPS are **excluded** from diluted EPS but still **disclosed**

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-security ranking schedule, sequential diluted EPS, initial recognition & period-end JEs (emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule)
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of convertible securities; subsequent measurement ranking and sequential diluted EPS schedules; period-end preferred dividend and bond interest JEs; dual face presentation of basic and diluted EPS  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Cascade Holdings Inc.** is a calendar-year public company with a **complex capital structure**. For the year ended December 31, Year 1:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $380,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (entire year) | 200,000 |
| Income tax rate | 25% |

There are **no** discontinued operations. On **January 1, Year 1**, Meridian issued the following securities (all outstanding the entire year; none exercised or converted during Year 1). Common stock has a **$1 par** value.

1. **Employee stock options:** 10,000 options to purchase one common share each at an exercise price of **$20**. Average market price of common stock during Year 1: **$40**.
2. **5% cumulative convertible preferred stock:** 4,000 shares, **$100 par**, issued at par. Each preferred share is convertible into **3** common shares. Preferred dividends for the full year were declared and paid on December 31.
3. **Series X convertible bonds:** **$500,000** face, **4%** coupon, issued at par on January 1. Each **$1,000** bond is convertible into **25** common shares. Interest was paid in cash for the full year.
4. **Series Y convertible bonds:** **$300,000** face, **10%** coupon, issued at par on January 1. Each **$1,000** bond is convertible into **8** common shares. Interest was paid in cash for the full year.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entries** for (1) the convertible preferred stock issuance and (2) the Series X convertible bond issuance. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Compute the **numerator effect**, **denominator effect**, and **earnings per incremental share** for each potentially dilutive security. **Rank** the securities from most dilutive to least dilutive.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** for Year 1.  
d. Prepare the **sequential diluted EPS subsequent measurement schedule** (most dilutive → least dilutive). Identify which securities are dilutive vs antidilutive and state **diluted EPS**.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end journal entries** for (1) the annual preferred dividend (cash paid same day) and (2) full-year cash interest on Series X bonds. Show Dr = Cr.  
f. Show the **financial statement presentation** of basic and diluted EPS on the face of the income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition JEs**

**(1) Convertible preferred stock issued at par**

Cash proceeds = 4,000 × $100 = **$400,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 400,000 | |
| Convertible Preferred Stock ($100 par) | | 400,000 |
| *Issue 4,000 shares of 5% cumulative convertible preferred at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **400,000**. **Balanced.**

**(2) Series X convertible bonds issued at par**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 500,000 | |
| Bonds Payable—Series X (convertible) | | 500,000 |
| *Issue $500,000 of 4% convertible bonds at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **500,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Incremental effects and ranking (setup for subsequent measurement)**

**Stock options (treasury stock method)**  
Proceeds if exercised = 10,000 × $20 = **$200,000**  
Shares assumed repurchased = $200,000 ÷ $40 = **5,000**  
Incremental shares = 10,000 − 5,000 = **5,000**  
Numerator effect = **$0**  
Earnings per incremental share = $0 ÷ 5,000 = **$0.00**

**Series X convertible bonds (if-converted)**  
Pretax interest = $500,000 × 4% = **$20,000**  
After-tax interest add-back = $20,000 × (1 − 0.25) = **$15,000**  
Conversion shares = 500 bonds × 25 = **12,500**  
Earnings per incremental share = $15,000 ÷ 12,500 = **$1.20**

**Convertible preferred (if-converted)**  
Preferred dividends add-back = 4,000 × $100 × 5% = **$20,000**  
Conversion shares = 4,000 × 3 = **12,000**  
Earnings per incremental share = $20,000 ÷ 12,000 = **$1.67** (exactly $1.666…; rank uses $1.67)

**Series Y convertible bonds (if-converted)**  
Pretax interest = $300,000 × 10% = **$30,000**  
After-tax interest add-back = $30,000 × 0.75 = **$22,500**  
Conversion shares = 300 bonds × 8 = **2,400**  
Earnings per incremental share = $22,500 ÷ 2,400 = **$9.375**

**Ranking (most dilutive → least dilutive)**

| Rank | Security | Δ Income | Δ Shares | Earnings per incremental share |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Stock options | $0 | 5,000 | **$0.00** |
| 2 | Series X convertible bonds | 15,000 | 12,500 | **1.20** |
| 3 | Convertible preferred stock | 20,000 | 12,000 | **1.67** |
| 4 | Series Y convertible bonds | 22,500 | 2,400 | **9.375** |

**c. Basic EPS**

Income available to common = $380,000 − $20,000 = **$360,000**

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$360{,}000}{200{,}000} = \mathbf{\$1.80}
\]

**d. Sequential diluted EPS subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**

| Step | Net income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Basic EPS | $360,000 | 200,000 | **$1.80** | — |
| + Stock options | 360,000 | 205,000 | **1.76** | Dilutive ($1.7561 < $1.80) |
| + Series X bonds | 375,000 | 217,500 | **1.72** | Dilutive ($1.7241 < $1.7561) |
| + Convertible preferred | 395,000 | 229,500 | **1.72** | Dilutive ($1.7211 < $1.7241) |
| + Series Y bonds | 417,500 | 231,900 | **1.80** | **Antidilutive** ($1.8003 > $1.7211) |

Detail:
- Options: $360,000 / 205,000 = **$1.7561** → dilutive  
- Series X: ($360,000 + $15,000) / (205,000 + 12,500) = $375,000 / 217,500 = **$1.7241** → dilutive  
- Preferred: ($375,000 + $20,000) / (217,500 + 12,000) = $395,000 / 229,500 = **$1.7211** → dilutive  
- Series Y: ($395,000 + $22,500) / (229,500 + 2,400) = $417,500 / 231,900 = **$1.8003** → **antidilutive** (exclude)

**Diluted EPS** uses income **$395,000** and shares **229,500**:

\[
\text{Diluted EPS} = \frac{\$395{,}000}{229{,}500} = \mathbf{\$1.72}
\]

Series Y is **excluded** from diluted EPS (disclose as potentially dilutive / antidilutive for the period).

**e. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting / cash settlement JEs**

**(1) Annual preferred dividend**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Dividends (or Retained Earnings) | 20,000 | |
| Cash | | 20,000 |
| *Declare and pay annual 5% cumulative convertible preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **20,000**. **Balanced.**

**(2) Full-year cash interest on Series X bonds**

Interest = $500,000 × 4% = **$20,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 20,000 | |
| Cash | | 20,000 |
| *Pay annual interest on Series X 4% convertible bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **20,000**. **Balanced.**

*Note:* EPS is a **presentation** measure derived from net income and share counts; there is no journal entry that “books” EPS itself. Preferred dividends reduce income available to common for **basic** EPS; bond interest is already in net income and is added back **net of tax** only in the diluted EPS computation when the bonds are dilutive.

**f. Income statement presentation (face)**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Basic earnings per share | **$1.80** |
| Diluted earnings per share | **1.72** |

(Complex capital structure → dual presentation with **equal prominence**.)

**Key insight:** Maximum dilution requires ranking by **lowest earnings per incremental share** first, then building a **subsequent measurement schedule** that adds securities **only while** tentative EPS continues to fall. A high-coupon, low-conversion Series Y bond can be **antidilutive after** more dilutive securities are already in the schedule—even if a student might assume every convertible is always included.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Multi-security ranking and sequential diluted EPS twin
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: recompute incremental effects, rank most-to-least dilutive, sequential subsequent measurement schedule, period-end preferred JE, and dual EPS presentation with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Circuit Partners Ltd.** (complex capital structure) reports for Year 1:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $525,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (entire year) | 280,000 |
| Income tax rate | 25% |

No discontinued operations. Securities outstanding all year (none exercised/converted):

1. **Stock options:** 15,000 options to buy one share each at **$12**; average market price **$20**.
2. **4% cumulative convertible preferred:** 6,000 shares, **$50 par**; each convertible into **2** common shares. Full-year dividends declared and paid.
3. **Series M convertible bonds:** **$700,000** face, **3%** coupon, issued at par; each **$1,000** bond converts into **20** common shares.
4. **Series N convertible bonds:** **$400,000** face, **8%** coupon, issued at par; each **$1,000** bond converts into **10** common shares.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **period-end JE** for the annual preferred dividend (cash paid same day).  
b. Rank all potentially dilutive securities (show Δ income, Δ shares, incremental EPS).  
c. Compute basic EPS.  
d. Complete the sequential diluted EPS subsequent measurement schedule; state diluted EPS and which security is antidilutive.  
e. Present basic and diluted EPS as they would appear on the income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Preferred dividend JE**

Preferred dividends = 6,000 × $50 × 4% = **$12,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Dividends (or Retained Earnings) | 12,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000 |
| *Annual 4% cumulative convertible preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **12,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Ranking**

| Rank | Security | Δ Income | Δ Shares | Incremental EPS |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Stock options | $0 | 6,000 | **$0.00** |
| 2 | Convertible preferred | 12,000 | 12,000 | **1.00** |
| 3 | Series M bonds | 15,750 | 14,000 | **1.125** |
| 4 | Series N bonds | 24,000 | 4,000 | **6.00** |

Supporting computations:
- Options: proceeds $180,000; repurchase $180,000 ÷ $20 = 9,000; incremental = 15,000 − 9,000 = **6,000**
- Preferred: add-back **$12,000**; shares = 6,000 × 2 = **12,000**
- Series M: after-tax interest = $700,000 × 3% × 0.75 = **$15,750**; shares = 700 × 20 = **14,000**
- Series N: after-tax interest = $400,000 × 8% × 0.75 = **$24,000**; shares = 400 × 10 = **4,000**

**c. Basic EPS**

Income available to common = $525,000 − $12,000 = **$513,000**

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$513{,}000}{280{,}000} = \mathbf{\$1.83}
\]

(Exactly $1.8321…; report **$1.83**.)

**d. Sequential schedule (subsequent measurement — emphasis)**

| Step | Income | Shares | EPS | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Basic | $513,000 | 280,000 | **$1.83** | — |
| + Options | 513,000 | 286,000 | **1.79** | Dilutive |
| + Preferred | 525,000 | 298,000 | **1.76** | Dilutive |
| + Series M | 540,750 | 312,000 | **1.73** | Dilutive |
| + Series N | 564,750 | 316,000 | **1.79** | **Antidilutive** |

Detail:
- Options: $513,000 / 286,000 = **$1.7937**  
- Preferred: ($513,000 + $12,000) / (286,000 + 12,000) = $525,000 / 298,000 = **$1.7617**  
- Series M: ($525,000 + $15,750) / (298,000 + 14,000) = $540,750 / 312,000 = **$1.7332**  
- Series N: ($540,750 + $24,000) / (312,000 + 4,000) = $564,750 / 316,000 = **$1.7872** → **antidilutive**

**Diluted EPS** = $540,750 / 312,000 = **$1.73**  
**Series N excluded** as antidilutive.

**e. Presentation**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Basic earnings per share | **$1.83** |
| Diluted earnings per share | **1.73** |

**Key insight:** Changing every input (NI, shares, coupons, conversion ratios, option terms) still follows the **same** LO 20-9 algorithm: rank → sequential include while EPS falls → dual presentation. The **subsequent measurement schedule** is where antidilution is detected, not at the ranking step alone.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Bond conversion settlement JE; discontinued operations control number; multi-security diluted EPS presentation
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Settlement JE for conversion of convertible bonds; sequential diluted EPS using income from continuing operations as control number; full basic/diluted presentation for continuing operations, discontinued operations, and net income  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakebridge Precision Mfg.** reports the following for Year 1 (complex capital structure; tax rate **25%**):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations | $240,000 |
| Gain from discontinued operations, net of tax | 48,000 |
| Net income | $288,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding | 80,000 |

Common stock has a **$2 par** value. Potentially dilutive securities outstanding all year (until any conversion noted below):

1. **Options:** 6,000 options to buy one share at **$15**; average market price **$25**.
2. **6% cumulative convertible preferred:** 2,000 shares, **$100 par**; each converts into **4** common shares. Full-year preferred dividends declared and paid (**$12,000**).
3. **5% convertible bonds:** **$200,000** face at par; each **$1,000** bond converts into **18** common shares. Interest paid for the full year. On **December 31, Year 1**, holders convert **all** of the bonds into common stock (settlement). The conversion does not change Year 1 weighted-average common shares for basic EPS (converted at day-end). For diluted EPS, apply the if-converted method as if conversion were assumed for the full year when the bonds are dilutive.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 settlement journal entry** to record conversion of all convertible bonds into common stock (ignore any accrued interest on the conversion date; bonds were already paid interest for the year). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare the **period-end JE** for the $12,000 preferred dividend (assume paid in cash).  
c. Rank the three potentially dilutive securities by earnings per incremental share (treat the bonds as outstanding for the diluted EPS test).  
d. Using **income from continuing operations available to common** as the control number, complete the sequential diluted EPS subsequent measurement schedule and the final diluted share count.  
e. Prepare the **full EPS presentation** (basic and diluted) for continuing operations, discontinued operations, and net income—as required for a complex capital structure.  
f. Briefly state what would change if Lakebridge had a **loss from continuing operations** but overall net income because of the discontinued gain.

**Answer key:**  

**a. December 31, Year 1 — disposal / settlement JE (bond conversion)**

Bonds face = **$200,000**  
Common shares issued = 200 bonds × 18 = **3,600**  
Common stock (par) = 3,600 × $2 = **$7,200**  
APIC—Common = $200,000 − $7,200 = **$192,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable (convertible) | 200,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 7,200 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 192,800 |
| *Convert $200,000 of 5% convertible bonds into 3,600 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **200,000**; Cr = 7,200 + 192,800 = **200,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Preferred dividend JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Dividends (or Retained Earnings) | 12,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000 |
| *Annual 6% cumulative convertible preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **12,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Ranking**

| Rank | Security | Δ Income | Δ Shares | Incremental EPS |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Options | $0 | 2,400 | **$0.00** |
| 2 | Convertible preferred | 12,000 | 8,000 | **1.50** |
| 3 | Convertible bonds | 7,500 | 3,600 | **2.083** |

Supporting:
- Options: proceeds $90,000; repurchase $90,000 ÷ $25 = 3,600; incremental = 6,000 − 3,600 = **2,400**
- Preferred: add-back **$12,000**; shares = 2,000 × 4 = **8,000**
- Bonds: after-tax interest = $200,000 × 5% × 0.75 = **$7,500**; shares = 200 × 18 = **3,600**

**d. Sequential dilution using continuing operations as control number**

Income from continuing operations available to common (basic) = $240,000 − $12,000 = **$228,000**

| Step | Cont. ops. available | Shares | Cont. ops. EPS | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Basic | $228,000 | 80,000 | **$2.85** | — |
| + Options | 228,000 | 82,400 | **2.77** | Dilutive ($2.7670) |
| + Preferred | 240,000 | 90,400 | **2.65** | Dilutive ($2.6549) |
| + Bonds | 247,500 | 94,000 | **2.63** | Dilutive ($2.6330) |

All three securities are dilutive.  
**Diluted weighted-average shares** = **94,000**  
**Diluted EPS — continuing operations** = $247,500 / 94,000 = **$2.63**

**e. Full face presentation of EPS**

Basic numerators:  
- Continuing ops available = **$228,000**  
- Discontinued ops = **$48,000** (no preferred allocation)  
- Net income available = $288,000 − $12,000 = **$276,000**

Diluted numerators (same dilutive share count **94,000**):  
- Continuing ops = **$247,500** (after preferred add-back + after-tax interest)  
- Discontinued ops = **$48,000**  
- Net income = $288,000 − $12,000 + $12,000 + $7,500 = **$295,500**  
  (equivalently: $288,000 + $7,500 interest add-back = $295,500, because preferred dividend subtract and add-back cancel)

| | Basic | Diluted |
|---|---:|---:|
| Earnings per share — continuing operations | **$2.85** | **$2.63** |
| Earnings per share — discontinued operations | **0.60** | **0.51** |
| Earnings per share — net income | **$3.45** | **$3.14** |

Checks:  
Basic: $2.85 + $0.60 = $3.45; $228,000 / 80,000 = $2.85; $48,000 / 80,000 = $0.60; $276,000 / 80,000 = $3.45  
Diluted: $2.63 + $0.51 = $3.14; $48,000 / 94,000 ≈ $0.5106 → **$0.51**; $295,500 / 94,000 = **$3.14**

**f. Loss from continuing operations**  
If continuing operations available to common were a **loss**, potential common shares would be **antidilutive** for the control number. Diluted EPS for continuing operations (and typically all EPS lines using that diluted share set) would equal **basic** EPS even if net income is positive after a discontinued gain. Antidilutive securities would still be **disclosed**. The Dec 31 conversion settlement JE would still be recorded; it does not by itself force inclusion of potential shares when the control number is a loss.

**Key insight:** Dilutiveness is tested against **income from continuing operations**, not net income. The **settlement JE** removes the convertible liability and issues common stock, but the diluted EPS **subsequent measurement schedule** still applies the if-converted effects for the period the bonds were outstanding (here, assumed full year when dilutive). Once the diluted share count is set, apply it (with appropriate numerator adjustments) to discontinued operations and net income for dual presentation with equal prominence.

### Q4 — MC — Ranking and antidilutive cutoff with multiple securities
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Classification of which securities enter diluted EPS after ranking by earnings per incremental share on the sequential subsequent measurement schedule  
**Question:**  
**Coralpoint Logistics Co.** had **160,000** weighted-average common shares and net income available to common of **$256,000** for the year (no preferred outstanding for basic). Two potentially dilutive securities, already ranked most dilutive → least dilutive:

| Rank | Security | Increase in income | Increase in shares |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Security A | $4,000 | 12,000 |
| 2 | Security B | $28,000 | 14,000 |

What **diluted EPS** should Coralpoint report?

- A) $1.60  
- B) $1.51  
- C) $1.55  
- D) $1.48  

**Answer:** **B. $1.51**  

**Explanation:**  
Basic EPS = $256,000 / 160,000 = **$1.60**  

+ Security A: ($256,000 + $4,000) / (160,000 + 12,000) = $260,000 / 172,000 = **$1.5116** ≈ **$1.51** (dilutive vs $1.60)  

+ Security B: ($260,000 + $28,000) / (172,000 + 14,000) = $288,000 / 186,000 = **$1.5484** ≈ **$1.55** → **antidilutive** vs $1.51 (exclude Security B)

Diluted EPS stops after Security A: **$1.51**.  
Trap A is basic EPS only.  
Trap C incorrectly includes antidilutive Security B.  
Trap D would understate if both securities were forced into the calculation without sequential testing ($288,000 / 186,000 is not $1.48; $256,000 / 172,000 ≈ $1.49 is another common error).

### Q5 — MC — EPS presentation for complex capital structure
**LO:** LO 20-9  
**Concept:** Classification of required EPS financial statement presentation under ASC 260 for complex vs simple capital structures  
**Question:**  
Which statement about **EPS financial statement presentation** is correct under ASC 260 for an entity with **convertible preferred stock, convertible bonds, and stock options** outstanding (complex capital structure)?

- A) Only diluted EPS is presented on the face of the income statement; basic EPS is disclosed only in the notes.  
- B) Basic and diluted per-share amounts for income from continuing operations and for net income are presented on the face of the income statement with **equal prominence**.  
- C) Basic EPS is presented only if it equals diluted EPS; otherwise only diluted EPS is shown.  
- D) Discontinued operations per-share amounts must always appear on the face of the income statement and may never be disclosed only in the notes.

**Answer:** **B.**  

**Explanation:**  
ASC 260 requires entities with complex capital structures to present **basic and diluted** per-share amounts for income from continuing operations and for net income on the **face** of the income statement with **equal prominence**. Simple structures present basic only. Discontinued operations EPS may be on the face **or** in the notes. Option A omits basic EPS; C invents a conditional rule; D is incorrect because discontinued operations EPS may be disclosed in the notes.
