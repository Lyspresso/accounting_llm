# Agent 206 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-1

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Calculate depreciation using straight-line, sum-of-the-years’-digits, declining-balance, and units-of-production methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Depreciation is allocation, not valuation:** systematic and rational allocation of depreciable cost (cost − residual) over useful life; credit to **Accumulated Depreciation** (contra asset)
- **Terminology:** carrying amount (book value) = cost − accumulated depreciation; residual/salvage; useful life; depreciable cost (allocation base)
- **Straight-line (uniform rate):** annual expense = (cost − residual) ÷ useful life in years; rate = 1/n of depreciable cost each year
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year rollforward of depreciation expense, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount to residual
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize PPE at acquisition cost
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Debit Depreciation Expense, Credit Accumulated Depreciation
- **Balance sheet presentation:** Equipment at cost less Accumulated Depreciation = Equipment, net
- **Accelerated methods (core LO):** sum-of-the-years’-digits (fraction of depreciable cost); declining-balance (rate × beginning carrying amount; residual is a floor, not subtracted from base)
- **Units-of-production:** rate per unit × actual output; total expense still limited to depreciable cost
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** update depreciation if needed, then remove cost and related AD; recognize gain or loss = cash proceeds − carrying amount

### Q1 — CORE — Straight-line full life schedule, purchase, period-end dep, BS, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Straight-line subsequent measurement schedule over full useful life; initial recognition JE; period-end depreciation adjusting JE; balance sheet presentation; disposal settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar Ridge Fabrication Co.** (calendar year-end) acquires a specialized packaging machine on **January 1, Year 1** for **$48,000 cash**. Management estimates **residual value of $3,000** and a **5-year** useful life. The company uses the **straight-line** method and records depreciation once annually on December 31. Ignore income taxes.

On **January 1, Year 4** (immediately after three full years of use, before any Year 4 depreciation), Cedar Ridge **sells** the machine for **$19,500 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **depreciable cost**, **annual straight-line depreciation expense**, and the **straight-line rate** (as a percent of depreciable cost).  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
c. Prepare the full **straight-line subsequent measurement (depreciation) schedule** for Years 1–5 showing, for each year-end: depreciable cost, depreciation rate, depreciation expense, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount. Confirm ending carrying amount equals residual value.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for depreciation.  
e. Show the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet presentation** of the machine (cost, AD, net).  
f. Compute carrying amount on **January 1, Year 4** and record the **disposal settlement JE** (sale for $19,500 cash). State gain or loss.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Depreciable cost, annual expense, rate**

Depreciable cost = $48,000 − $3,000 = **$45,000**  
Annual straight-line depreciation = $45,000 ÷ 5 = **$9,000**  
Straight-line rate = $9,000 / $45,000 = **20%** (also 1/5)

**b. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 48,000 | |
| Cash | | 48,000 |
| *Acquire packaging machine for cash* | | |

**Check:** Dr 48,000 = Cr 48,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — straight-line (emphasis)**

| Reporting period | Depreciable cost | Rate | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 1 | | | | | $48,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $45,000 | 0.20 | $9,000 | $9,000 | 39,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 45,000 | 0.20 | 9,000 | 18,000 | 30,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 45,000 | 0.20 | 9,000 | 27,000 | 21,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 45,000 | 0.20 | 9,000 | 36,000 | 12,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 5 | 45,000 | 0.20 | 9,000 | 45,000 | 3,000 residual |

**Schedule checks:** Total depreciation over 5 years = 5 × $9,000 = **$45,000** = depreciable cost. Ending carrying amount = $48,000 − $45,000 = **$3,000** residual. Expense constant each year.

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |
| *Straight-line depreciation, Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Balance sheet presentation — December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Equipment | $48,000 |
| Less: Accumulated depreciation | (9,000) |
| **Equipment, net** | **$39,000** |

**f. Disposal — January 1, Year 4**

Carrying amount = $48,000 − $27,000 AD = **$21,000**  
Cash proceeds $19,500 − carrying amount $21,000 = **Loss on disposal $1,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 19,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 27,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 1,500 | |
| Equipment | | 48,000 |
| *Sell machine; remove cost and related AD* | | |

**Check:** Dr $19,500 + $27,000 + $1,500 = $48,000; Cr $48,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Straight-line **subsequent measurement** is a constant annual charge of depreciable cost; the multi-year schedule proves total allocation equals cost − residual and feeds both period-end AD balances and disposal gain/loss.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Straight-line schedule, JEs, mid-life disposal
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — straight-line subsequent measurement schedule; initial recognition; period-end depreciation JE; disposal settlement after two years  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Logistics LLC** purchases delivery-line equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$82,000 cash**. Residual value is estimated at **$7,000**; useful life is **5 years**. Depreciation is **straight-line**, recorded each December 31.

On **December 31, Year 2**, after recording Year 2 depreciation, Northwind **sells** the equipment for **$55,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute depreciable cost and annual straight-line depreciation.  
b. Prepare the **straight-line subsequent measurement schedule** for the full 5-year life (as if held to residual).  
c. Record the purchase JE, the **December 31, Year 1** depreciation adjusting JE, and the **December 31, Year 2** depreciation adjusting JE.  
d. After the Year 2 depreciation entry, compute carrying amount and record the **disposal settlement JE** for the $55,000 sale. State gain or loss.  
e. Verify that total depreciation recorded through disposal date equals AD removed on disposal.

**Answer key:**  

**a.**  
Depreciable cost = $82,000 − $7,000 = **$75,000**  
Annual SL depreciation = $75,000 ÷ 5 = **$15,000**  
Rate = 20% of depreciable cost per year.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — straight-line**

| Reporting period | Depreciable cost | Rate | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 1 | | | | | $82,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $75,000 | 0.20 | $15,000 | $15,000 | 67,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 75,000 | 0.20 | 15,000 | 30,000 | 52,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 75,000 | 0.20 | 15,000 | 45,000 | 37,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 75,000 | 0.20 | 15,000 | 60,000 | 22,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 5 | 75,000 | 0.20 | 15,000 | 75,000 | 7,000 residual |

**Check:** Total dep $75,000; end CA = residual $7,000.

**c. Journal entries**

*January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 82,000 | |
| Cash | | 82,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 82,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000. **Balanced.**

**d. Disposal — December 31, Year 2 (after Y2 depreciation)**

Carrying amount = $82,000 − $30,000 = **$52,000**  
Cash $55,000 − BV $52,000 = **Gain on disposal $3,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 55,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 30,000 | |
| Equipment | | 82,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 3,000 |
| *Sell equipment after two years of SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr $55,000 + $30,000 = $85,000; Cr $82,000 + $3,000 = $85,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Verification**  
AD removed on disposal = $15,000 + $15,000 = **$30,000**, which matches cumulative depreciation on the schedule through Year 2.

**Key insight:** Changing only the numbers leaves the same SL engine: constant expense, AD rollforward, and disposal gain/loss driven by proceeds versus schedule carrying amount.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — DDB schedule (residual floor) + SYD Year 1 + UOP Year 1 + disposal
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Double-declining-balance subsequent measurement schedule with residual-value floor (final-year plug); SYD and units-of-production Year 1 expense; period-end dep JE; disposal after three years under DDB  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverton Machine Co.** buys production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$50,000 cash**. Residual value **$5,000**; useful life **5 years**; estimated total productive output **10,000 units**.

Actual production Year 1: **1,800 units**.

Riverton wants to understand all four LO 12-1 methods, then elects **double-declining-balance (DDB)** for books.

On **December 31, Year 3**, after recording Year 3 DDB depreciation, Riverton **sells** the equipment for **$12,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute Year 1 depreciation under **straight-line**, **sum-of-the-years’-digits**, **double-declining-balance**, and **units-of-production**. Record the **Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** assuming DDB is used.  
b. Prepare the full **DDB subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–5. Show the residual-value floor / plug in the final year.  
c. Record the **initial recognition JE** and the **December 31, Year 3 disposal settlement JE** under DDB (after Year 3 depreciation). State gain or loss.  
d. In one sentence, explain why DDB does **not** subtract residual value when applying the rate, yet still ends at residual.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 1 depreciation — four methods**

Depreciable cost = $50,000 − $5,000 = **$45,000**

| Method | Computation | Year 1 expense |
|---|---|---:|
| Straight-line | $45,000 ÷ 5 | **$9,000** |
| Sum-of-the-years’-digits | $45,000 × 5/15; denom = 5(5+1)/2 = 15 | **$15,000** |
| Double-declining-balance | Rate = 2 × (1/5) = 40%; $50,000 × 40% | **$20,000** |
| Units-of-production | Rate = $45,000 / 10,000 = $4.50 per unit; 1,800 × $4.50 | **$8,100** |

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting (DDB elected)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 20,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 20,000 |
| *DDB depreciation Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — double-declining-balance (emphasis alternate)**

DDB rate = **40%**. Residual floor = **$5,000** (do not depreciate below residual).

| Reporting period | Beginning CA | Rate | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Ending CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 1 | | | | | $50,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $50,000 | 0.40 | $20,000 | $20,000 | 30,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 30,000 | 0.40 | 12,000 | 32,000 | 18,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 18,000 | 0.40 | 7,200 | 39,200 | 10,800 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 10,800 | 0.40 | 4,320 | 43,520 | 6,480 |
| Dec 31, Year 5 | 6,480 | plug* | 1,480* | 45,000 | 5,000 residual |

\*Year 5: 40% × $6,480 = $2,592 would push CA below residual; expense limited to $6,480 − $5,000 = **$1,480**.

**Schedule checks:**  
Y1–Y5 expense: $20,000 + $12,000 + $7,200 + $4,320 + $1,480 = **$45,000** = depreciable cost.  
Ending AD $45,000; ending CA $5,000 residual.

**c. Initial recognition and disposal**

*January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 50,000 | |
| Cash | | 50,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 50,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3 — disposal after Y3 DDB depreciation*

Carrying amount = $50,000 − $39,200 = **$10,800**  
Cash $12,000 − BV $10,800 = **Gain on disposal $1,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 12,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 39,200 | |
| Equipment | | 50,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 1,200 |
| *Sell equipment; remove cost and DDB accumulated depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr $12,000 + $39,200 = $51,200; Cr $50,000 + $1,200 = $51,200. **Balanced.**

**d. Conceptual**  
DDB applies a constant rate to **declining carrying amount** without subtracting residual from the base; residual acts as a **floor** so the final period is plugged so total AD equals depreciable cost.

**Key insight:** Accelerated methods front-load expense relative to straight-line; DDB’s subsequent measurement schedule must still reconcile to residual via the final-year plug, and that same AD balance is removed on disposal.

### Q4 — MC (method / residual treatment classification)
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Classify how residual value enters each depreciation method and identify the straight-line rate  

**Question 1:**  
Which statement about **residual (salvage) value** is correct under GAAP depreciation methods covered in LO 12-1?

- A) Residual value is subtracted from cost to compute the allocation base under straight-line, SYD, and units-of-production, but is **not** subtracted when applying the declining-balance rate (it acts as a floor on carrying amount).  
- B) Residual value is ignored under all methods until the final year.  
- C) Residual value is always subtracted from beginning carrying amount before applying the double-declining rate.  
- D) Residual value increases annual straight-line depreciation.

**Answer:** **A.** Straight-line, SYD, and UOP allocate **depreciable cost = cost − residual**. Declining-balance multiplies a rate by **gross carrying amount** each year and stops when carrying amount would fall below residual (plug the last period).

**Question 2:**  
Equipment costs $90,000, residual $10,000, useful life 8 years, straight-line. What is the annual straight-line **rate applied to depreciable cost**, and what is Year 1 depreciation expense?

- A) Rate 12.5%; expense $11,250  
- B) Rate 12.5%; expense $10,000  
- C) Rate 10%; expense $9,000  
- D) Rate 8%; expense $10,000  

**Answer:** **B.** Depreciable cost = $90,000 − $10,000 = $80,000. SL rate = 1/8 = **12.5%** of depreciable cost. Expense = $80,000 × 12.5% = **$10,000** (also $80,000 ÷ 8). Option A incorrectly uses 12.5% of gross cost.
