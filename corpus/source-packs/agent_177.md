# Agent 177 — CORE DEMO — LO 8-8

**Chapter:** 8  
**LO title:** Apply cash controls  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Internal cash controls:** cash drawer / POS reconciliation; petty cash fund; bank reconciliation; separation of duties
- **Cash Over and Short:** expense (debit) or other revenue (credit) for drawer/fund differences; other revenues and expenses on the income statement
- **Initial recognition JE:** establish petty cash (Dr Petty Cash, Cr Cash); cash-drawer short/over adjusting entry
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-date petty cash fund rollforward (authorized balance, cash on hand, vouchers, shortage, replenishment); bank reconciliation schedule proving one correct cash balance (bank side + book side)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** book-side bank recon items only — note collections, NSF, service charges, EFT/payments by bank, book errors, cash shortage; **no** AJEs for deposits in transit, outstanding checks, or undeposited cash
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** close or decrease petty cash (Dr Cash / expenses as needed, Cr Petty Cash); replenishment settles vouchers by restoring fund cash via check (expenses hit then, not when custodian pays)
- **Presentation:** petty cash is part of **cash**; fund balance stays at authorized amount until increased/decreased/closed; replenishment does **not** change the Petty Cash account

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 8-8  
**Concept:** Classification of bank-reconciling items — which require book adjusting entries  

**Question:**  
In preparing a bank reconciliation, which set of items **requires adjusting journal entries** on the company’s books?

- A) Deposits in transit and outstanding checks  
- B) Undeposited cash on hand and deposits in transit  
- C) Bank collection of a note receivable, NSF checks, bank service charges, and unrecorded EFT disbursements  
- D) Outstanding checks and bank errors that the bank will reverse next month  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Book-side reconciling items reflect amounts the company did not yet know or record; each requires an AJE so the Cash ledger equals correct cash. Deposits in transit, outstanding checks, and undeposited cash are bank-side **timing** items and do not require book entries (assuming books already recorded the deposits/checks). Bank errors are corrected by the bank, not by company AJEs (unless the company also misrecorded).

- D) Debit expenses $1,640 and credit Petty Cash $1,640; report Petty Cash at $360  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Replenishment restores cash in the box to the authorized amount and records expenses equal to vouchers (here $1,640 = $2,000 − $340). The **Petty Cash** control account stays at the authorized **$2,000** and is included in **cash** on the balance sheet. Expenses are **not** charged to the Petty Cash account on ordinary replenishment.
