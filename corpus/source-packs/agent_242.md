# Agent 242 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-6

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Account for Stock Dividends and Stock Splits  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Small stock dividend (< ~20–25%): capitalize **fair value** of new shares from retained earnings
- Declaration JE: Dr Retained Earnings (FV), Cr Common Stock Dividends Distributable (par), Cr PIC—Common (excess)
- Distribution JE: Dr Common Stock Dividends Distributable, Cr Common Stock (par)
- Fractional shares: cash paid at FV; reduce shares issued and PIC accordingly
- Large stock dividend / stock split effected in form of a dividend (≥ ~20–25%): capitalize **par (or stated) value** only
- Pure stock split / reverse stock split: **no journal entry**; change shares and par per share only; total SE unchanged
- Stockholders’ equity **schedule / rollforward** before declaration, after declaration, and after distribution
- Common Stock Dividends Distributable is **equity** (not a liability) until shares are issued
- Total stockholders’ equity unchanged by stock dividends and stock splits (except cash paid for fractional shares)

### Q1 — CORE — Small stock dividend declaration, equity schedule, distribution, and large-dividend alternative
**LO:** LO 19-6  
**Concept:** Small stock dividend at fair value; subsequent equity measurement schedule; distribution settlement; large stock dividend at par  
**Scenario:**  
CedarForge Industries Inc. had the following stockholders’ equity balances on November 15, Year 5, immediately before any dividend action:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock, $2 par; 500,000 shares authorized; 80,000 shares issued and outstanding | $160,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common stock | 480,000 |
| Retained earnings | 920,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,560,000** |

On **November 15, Year 5**, the board declares a **10%** common stock dividend. The market price of CedarForge common stock on the declaration date is **$18** per share. The dividend is payable on **December 10, Year 5**, to shareholders of record on November 28. All dividend shares will be whole shares (no fractional-share cashout).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the journal entry on the **date of declaration** (November 15).  
b. Prepare a **stockholders’ equity schedule** showing balances (1) immediately **before** declaration, (2) **after** declaration but **before** distribution, and (3) **after** distribution on December 10. Include shares outstanding and total SE.  
c. Prepare the journal entry on the **date of distribution** (December 10).  
d. **Alternate measurement:** Assume instead that on November 15 the board declared a **40%** stock split effected in the form of a stock dividend (par remains $2; market price still $18). Prepare declaration and distribution journal entries, and compute ending common stock, PIC, retained earnings, and total SE after distribution.

**Answer key:**  

**a. November 15 — declaration of small stock dividend (at fair value)**  
New shares = 80,000 × 10% = **8,000** shares  

| Computation | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Retained earnings capitalized (8,000 × $18 FV) | $144,000 |
| Common stock dividends distributable (8,000 × $2 par) | 16,000 |
| PIC in excess of par (8,000 × [$18 − $2]) | 128,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 144,000 | |
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | | 16,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 128,000 |
| *To record 10% small stock dividend at fair value on declaration date* | | |

**Check:** Dr 144,000 = Cr 16,000 + 128,000. Balanced. Total SE unchanged ($1,560,000).

**b. Stockholders’ equity schedule (subsequent measurement / rollforward)**  

| Component | (1) Before declaration | (2) After declaration, before distribution | (3) After distribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Common stock ($2 par) | $160,000 | $160,000 | $176,000 |
| Common stock dividends distributable | — | 16,000 | — |
| PIC in excess of par—CS | 480,000 | 608,000 | 608,000 |
| Retained earnings | 920,000 | 776,000 | 776,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,560,000** | **$1,560,000** | **$1,560,000** |
| Shares issued & outstanding | 80,000 | 80,000* | 88,000 |

\*Common stock dividends distributable of 8,000 shares is outstanding equity capital, but shares are not yet formally issued until distribution. Total SE is identical in all three columns.

Rollforward math:  
- RE after declaration: 920,000 − 144,000 = **776,000**  
- PIC after declaration: 480,000 + 128,000 = **608,000**  
- CS after distribution: 160,000 + 16,000 = **176,000**  
- Column totals: 160+480+920 = 1,560,000; 160+16+608+776 = 1,560,000; 176+608+776 = 1,560,000. ✓

**c. December 10 — distribution / settlement**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | 16,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 16,000 |
| *To issue 8,000 shares of $2 par common stock for stock dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 16,000. Balanced.

**d. Alternate — 40% large stock dividend (stock split effected in form of dividend) at par**  
New shares = 80,000 × 40% = **32,000**  
Capitalize at par only: 32,000 × $2 = **$64,000**  
(Fair value $18 is **not** used.)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 64,000 | |
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | | 64,000 |
| *Declaration of 40% stock split effected as dividend at par* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | 64,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 64,000 |
| *Distribution of 32,000 shares* | | |

**Ending balances after distribution (40% large):**  

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock (80,000 + 32,000 = 112,000 × $2) | $224,000 |
| PIC in excess of par—CS (unchanged) | 480,000 |
| Retained earnings (920,000 − 64,000) | 856,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,560,000** |

**Check:** 224,000 + 480,000 + 856,000 = 1,560,000. Dr = Cr on both entries.

**Key insight:** Small stock dividends reclassify retained earnings to common stock and PIC at **fair value**; large stock dividends (stock splits effected as dividends) reclassify only **par**. A pure equity schedule shows total SE unchanged while components and share counts change. Distribution settles the temporary **Common Stock Dividends Distributable** equity account into Common Stock.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Small stock dividend with fractional-share cash settlement
**LO:** LO 19-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — small stock dividend at FV with fractional shares paid in cash  
**Scenario:**  
Ridgeway Precision LLC had the following equity on April 1, Year 6:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock, $1 par; 120,000 shares issued and outstanding | $120,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common stock | 360,000 |
| Retained earnings | 540,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,020,000** |

On **April 1**, Ridgeway declares an **8%** stock dividend when the market price is **$25** per share. Distribution is scheduled for **April 20**. Of the theoretical stock-dividend shares, **9,100** will be issued as whole shares and **500** represent fractional-share interests. Company policy is to **pay cash** for fractional shares at fair value on the declaration date. Cash for fractionals is paid on the distribution date.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the number of theoretical dividend shares and the cash payable for fractional shares.  
b. Prepare the April 1 declaration journal entry.  
c. Prepare the April 20 distribution entries (share issuance and cash payment for fractionals).  
d. Prepare a brief equity schedule: total SE immediately after declaration (before any cash payment) and immediately after distribution (after cash payment).  
e. Compute common stock (par) and shares outstanding after distribution.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Computations**  
Theoretical dividend shares = 120,000 × 8% = **9,600**  
Whole shares to issue = **9,100**  
Fractional shares (cash) = **500**  
Cash for fractionals = 500 × $25 = **$12,500**  
RE total charge = 9,600 × $25 = **$240,000**

**b. April 1 — declaration**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (9,600 × $25) | 240,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Fractional Shares (500 × $25) | | 12,500 |
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable (9,100 × $1) | | 9,100 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock (9,100 × [$25 − $1]) | | 218,400 |
| *Small stock dividend at FV; cash for fractionals* | | |

**Check:** Dr 240,000 = Cr 12,500 + 9,100 + 218,400 = 240,000. Balanced.

**c. April 20 — distribution / settlement**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | 9,100 | |
| Common Stock | | 9,100 |
| *Issue 9,100 whole shares for stock dividend* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Fractional Shares | 12,500 | |
| Cash | | 12,500 |
| *Pay cash for fractional-share interests* | | |

**Check:** Each entry balances (9,100; 12,500).

**d. Equity / total SE schedule**  

| Point in time | CS | CS Div. Dist. | PIC | RE | Div. Pay. (liability) | Total SE | Cash effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Before declaration | 120,000 | — | 360,000 | 540,000 | — | **1,020,000** | — |
| After declaration (before cash) | 120,000 | 9,100 | 578,400 | 300,000 | 12,500 liab. | **1,007,500** | none yet |
| After distribution | 129,100 | — | 578,400 | 300,000 | — | **1,007,500** | −12,500 cash |

After-declaration math:  
- PIC = 360,000 + 218,400 = **578,400**  
- RE = 540,000 − 240,000 = **300,000**  
- Total SE = 120,000 + 9,100 + 578,400 + 300,000 = **1,007,500**  
- Decrease in SE = fractional cash obligation **12,500** (assets will fall when paid; equity already reduced via RE).  
- After distribution: 129,100 + 578,400 + 300,000 = **1,007,500**. ✓

**e. After distribution**  
Common stock (par) = 120,000 + 9,100 = **$129,100**  
Shares outstanding = 120,000 + 9,100 = **129,100** shares

**Key insight:** The full fair-value stock dividend (including fractionals) reduces RE. Only whole shares increase CS and PIC; fractional interests create a **liability** settled in cash, which is the sole reason total SE declines.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Pure stock split presentation, reverse split, and period-end large dividend settlement
**LO:** LO 19-6  
**Concept:** Stock split and reverse split (no JE); large stock dividend declared at period-end with settlement after year-end  
**Scenario:**  
Harborline Media Corp. reports the following on December 1, Year 7:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock, $3 par; 400,000 shares authorized; 60,000 shares issued and outstanding | $180,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common stock | 420,000 |
| Retained earnings | 750,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,350,000** |

**Independent situations** (reset to December 1 balances for each):

**Situation A — Pure stock split**  
On December 5, Harborline effects a **3-for-1** stock split. Par per share is reduced proportionally. No shares are “distributed” as a dividend; authorized shares are also tripled for consistency with the split (optional disclosure practice).

**Situation B — Reverse stock split**  
On December 5, Harborline effects a **1-for-2** reverse stock split (two old shares become one new share).

**Situation C — Large stock dividend declared at year-end, settled next period**  
On **December 28, Year 7**, the board declares a **100%** stock split effected in the form of a stock dividend (one new share for each share outstanding). Par remains **$3**. Market price on December 28 is **$42** per share. Shares will be distributed on **January 18, Year 8**. Harborline’s fiscal year ends December 31.

**Required:**  
a. **Situation A:** State whether any journal entry is required. Prepare the stockholders’ equity section **after** the 3-for-1 split (shares, par per share, dollar balances).  
b. **Situation B:** State whether any journal entry is required. Compute shares outstanding and par per share after the reverse split; confirm total SE.  
c. **Situation C:** Prepare the December 28 declaration entry. Explain how Common Stock Dividends Distributable is presented at **December 31, Year 7**. Prepare the January 18, Year 8 distribution entry. Compute Year-7 ending RE and total SE after the declaration.  
d. Briefly contrast Situation A (pure 3-for-1 split) with Situation C (100% stock dividend) regarding (i) journal entries, (ii) par per share, and (iii) retained earnings.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Situation A — 3-for-1 pure stock split**  
**No journal entry** is required. Total dollar amounts in CS, PIC, and RE are unchanged.  

| Stockholders’ Equity after 3-for-1 split | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock, **$1** par; 1,200,000 shares authorized; **180,000** shares issued and outstanding | $180,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common stock | 420,000 |
| Retained earnings | 750,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,350,000** |

Shares: 60,000 × 3 = **180,000**  
Par per share: $3 ÷ 3 = **$1**  
Total common stock dollars: 180,000 × $1 = **$180,000** (same as before). ✓

**b. Situation B — 1-for-2 reverse stock split**  
**No journal entry.**  

| Item | After reverse split |
|---|---|
| Shares outstanding | 60,000 ÷ 2 = **30,000** |
| Par per share | $3 × 2 = **$6** |
| Common stock dollars | 30,000 × $6 = **$180,000** (unchanged) |
| PIC | $420,000 (unchanged) |
| RE | $750,000 (unchanged) |
| **Total SE** | **$1,350,000** |

**c. Situation C — 100% large stock dividend; period-end then settlement**  
New shares = 60,000 × 100% = **60,000**  
Capitalize at **par** (not FV): 60,000 × $3 = **$180,000**

**December 28, Year 7 — declaration (initial recognition)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 180,000 | |
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | | 180,000 |
| *100% stock split effected as dividend at par; market $42 ignored* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180,000. Balanced.

**December 31, Year 7 presentation:**  
Common Stock Dividends Distributable **$180,000** is reported in **stockholders’ equity** (with capital stock), **not** as a liability. No additional period-end adjusting entry is required solely for the stock dividend beyond the declaration entry already recorded.

| Year-7 ending equity (after declaration, before Jan distribution) | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock, $3 par; 60,000 shares outstanding | $180,000 |
| Common stock dividends distributable (60,000 shares) | 180,000 |
| PIC in excess of par—CS | 420,000 |
| Retained earnings (750,000 − 180,000) | 570,000 |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$1,350,000** |

**January 18, Year 8 — distribution / settlement**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock Dividends Distributable | 180,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 180,000 |
| *Issue 60,000 shares for large stock dividend* | | |

After distribution: CS = **$360,000** (120,000 shares × $3); PIC = 420,000; RE = 570,000; total SE still **$1,350,000**.

**d. Contrast pure split vs large stock dividend**  

| Feature | Pure 3-for-1 stock split (A) | 100% stock dividend (C) |
|---|---|---|
| (i) Journal entries | **None** | Declaration + distribution |
| (ii) Par per share | **Decreases** ($3 → $1) | **Unchanged** ($3) |
| (iii) Retained earnings | **Unchanged** | **Decreases** by par of new shares ($180,000) |
| Shares outstanding | Triple | Double (100% dividend) |
| Total SE | Unchanged | Unchanged |

**Key insight:** A pure stock split (or reverse split) changes only share count and par per share—no accounts move. A stock split **effected in the form of a dividend** keeps par constant, capitalizes legal capital from RE, and uses Common Stock Dividends Distributable (equity) until shares are issued—often straddling period-end.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 19-6  
**Concept:** Classification of small vs large stock dividend measurement basis  

**Question 1:**  
On June 1, Quill & Bind Corp. has 50,000 shares of $1 par common stock outstanding. It declares a **22%** stock dividend when the market price is $12 per share. Management concludes the issuance is large enough to materially reduce unit market price and treats the event as a **stock split effected in the form of a dividend**. What amount is debited to Retained Earnings on the declaration date?

- A) $132,000  
- B) $11,000  
- C) $0  
- D) $121,000  

**Answer:** **B.** New shares = 50,000 × 22% = 11,000. Large stock dividend / stock split effected as dividend is recorded at **par**: 11,000 × $1 = **$11,000**. Fair value (11,000 × $12 = $132,000) would apply only if classified as a **small** stock dividend. Choice A is the small-dividend FV amount; C would be a pure stock split with par change; D is FV − par and is not a RE debit by itself.

---

**Question 2:**  
Which statement about a **2-for-1 pure stock split** (par reduced by half; no “dividend” form) is correct?

- A) Retained earnings is debited for the market value of the additional shares.  
- B) Retained earnings is debited for the par value of the additional shares.  
- C) No formal journal entry is required; only the number of shares and par per share change.  
- D) Common Stock is credited and PIC is debited for the increase in shares.  

**Answer:** **C.** Pure stock splits and reverse stock splits require **no journal entry** because total capital stock dollars, PIC, and RE are unchanged; share count and par per share move inversely.
