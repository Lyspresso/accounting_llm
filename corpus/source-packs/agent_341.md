# Agent 341 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-3

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Calculate depreciation using group and composite depreciation methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Group vs composite:** group = homogeneous (similar) assets; composite = heterogeneous (dissimilar) assets; **calculations and journal entries are identical**
- **Composite (group) annual depreciation rate** = Total annual straight-line depreciation ÷ Total acquisition cost (round to three decimals when required)
- **Composite (group) useful life** = Total depreciable cost ÷ Total annual straight-line depreciation
- **Composite annual depreciation** = Rate × Total acquisition cost (control-account approach; one AD account)
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize group/composite assets at total acquisition cost
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Dr Depreciation Expense / Cr Accumulated Depreciation—control; each year reapply **rate × current total acquisition cost** after non-material changes
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** composite/group schedule of totals; AD control roll-forward across periods
- **Disposal JE:** **no gain or loss**; Cash + AD plug = original cost removed from asset control account
- After non-material addition/disposal, **retain original rate** × **revised** total acquisition cost
- Presentation/disclosure: gross PPE less AD control; disclose straight-line group/composite method

### Q1 — CORE — Composite schedule, period-end dep (emphasis), disposal, AD roll-forward
**LO:** LO 12-3  
**Concept:** Composite depreciation schedule; rate and useful life; initial recognition; **period-end adjusting JEs** (Year 1 and Year 2 emphasis); disposal with AD plug (no G/L); subsequent measurement at old rate × new cost; AD control roll-forward  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarvale Precision Tools Inc.** (calendar year) places the following **dissimilar** plant assets into a single **composite** early in **Year 1** (full-year depreciation policy). All assets are purchased for cash on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Asset class | Quantity | Unit acquisition cost | Unit residual value | Unit useful life (years) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CNC lathes | 5 | $48,000 | $3,000 | 10 |
| Drill presses | 8 | $15,000 | $1,000 | 8 |
| Surface grinders | 10 | $9,000 | $0 | 6 |
| Tool cabinets | 20 | $2,500 | $250 | 5 |

On **March 15, Year 2**, Cedarvale sells **one drill press** for **$9,500** cash. Management judges the disposal **not material** to the makeup of the composite (continue the original composite rate).

**Required:**  
a. Complete a **Year 1 composite depreciation schedule** (total acquisition cost, total residual, total annual SL depreciation by class and in total).  
b. Compute the **composite annual depreciation rate** (three decimal places), **composite useful life** (two decimal places), and **Year 1 composite annual depreciation**.  
c. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry for the composite purchase.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for composite depreciation.  
e. Prepare the **March 15, Year 2 disposal** journal entry for the one drill press.  
f. **(Emphasis)** Compute and record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE** for composite depreciation (old rate × revised total acquisition cost).  
g. Prepare a **subsequent-measurement AD control roll-forward** from Jan 1, Year 1 through Dec 31, Year 2 (beginning AD, period dep, disposal plug, ending AD; ending gross cost and carrying amount).  
h. Briefly state balance-sheet **presentation/disclosure** for composite PPE and why **no gain or loss** is recognized on the Year 2 disposal.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 1 composite depreciation schedule**

| Asset class | Qty | Unit cost | Unit residual | Life | Total acquisition cost | Total residual | Total annual SL depreciation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CNC lathes | 5 | $48,000 | $3,000 | 10 | $240,000 | $15,000 | ($240,000 − $15,000) / 10 = **$22,500** |
| Drill presses | 8 | 15,000 | 1,000 | 8 | 120,000 | 8,000 | ($120,000 − $8,000) / 8 = **14,000** |
| Surface grinders | 10 | 9,000 | 0 | 6 | 90,000 | 0 | $90,000 / 6 = **15,000** |
| Tool cabinets | 20 | 2,500 | 250 | 5 | 50,000 | 5,000 | ($50,000 − $5,000) / 5 = **9,000** |
| **Totals** | | | | | **$500,000** | **$28,000** | **$60,500** |

Total depreciable cost = $500,000 − $28,000 = **$472,000**

**b. Rate, life, and Year 1 depreciation**

\[
\text{Composite annual depreciation rate} = \frac{\$60{,}500}{\$500{,}000} = \mathbf{0.121}
\]

\[
\text{Composite useful life} = \frac{\$472{,}000}{\$60{,}500} = \mathbf{7.80\ \text{years}}
\]

\[
\text{Composite annual depreciation (Year 1)} = 0.121 \times \$500{,}000 = \mathbf{\$60{,}500}
\]

(Composite life is **not** a simple average of unit lives; it weights classes by contribution to total depreciable cost.)

**c. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (composite purchase)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Machinery and Equipment (composite control) | 500,000 | |
| Cash | | 500,000 |
| *Capitalize heterogeneous plant assets in one composite control account at total acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 500,000 = Cr 500,000. **Balanced.**

**d. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Machinery and Equipment | 60,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery and Equipment | | 60,500 |
| *Period-end: composite annual depreciation = rate × total acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 60,500 = Cr 60,500. **Balanced.**

**e. March 15, Year 2 — disposal of one drill press (no G/L)**

Original cost of one drill press = **$15,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$9,500**  
Accumulated depreciation (plug) = $15,000 − $9,500 = **$5,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 9,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery and Equipment | 5,500 | |
| Machinery and Equipment (composite control) | | 15,000 |
| *Remove asset cost; difference vs cash plugged to AD — no gain or loss recognized* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,500 + 5,500 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

**f. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

Revised total acquisition cost = $500,000 − $15,000 = **$485,000**  
(Original rate retained — disposal not material.)

\[
\text{Year 2 composite depreciation} = 0.121 \times \$485{,}000 = \mathbf{\$58{,}685}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Machinery and Equipment | 58,685 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery and Equipment | | 58,685 |
| *Period-end: old composite rate × revised total acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 58,685 = Cr 58,685. **Balanced.**

**g. Subsequent measurement — AD control roll-forward**

| Date / event | Gross cost (control) | AD (control) | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 (purchase) | $500,000 | $0 | $500,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 period-end dep | 500,000 | 60,500 | 439,500 |
| Mar 15, Y2 disposal (cost −$15,000; AD plug −$5,500) | 485,000 | 55,000 | 430,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 period-end dep | 485,000 | 113,685 | 371,315 |

**Roll-forward proofs:**  
AD end Y1: \(0 + 60{,}500 = 60{,}500\). ✓  
AD after disposal: \(60{,}500 - 5{,}500 = 55{,}000\). ✓  
AD end Y2: \(55{,}000 + 58{,}685 = 113{,}685\). ✓  
Carrying amount end Y2: \(485{,}000 - 113{,}685 = 371{,}315\). ✓

**h. Presentation / disclosure**  
- **Balance sheet:** Machinery and equipment at **gross composite cost** less a **single accumulated depreciation control account** (not unit-by-unit AD).  
- **Notes:** disclose use of the **straight-line composite method** and estimated lives/categories as appropriate.  
- **Disposal:** under group/composite methods, **gains and losses are not recognized**; the difference is absorbed in AD so unrecorded gains tend to offset unrecorded losses over time (ASC 360-10-S99-1 style group/composite treatment).

**Key insight:** Composite methods replace unit tracking with **rate × cost**. The **period-end adjusting JE** each year is always Dr Depreciation Expense / Cr AD-control using the composite rate applied to **current** total acquisition cost after non-material changes. Disposals never produce a separate G/L — AD is the balancing plug.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Composite twin (different company and numbers)
**LO:** LO 12-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: composite schedule, rate/life, initial recognition, **period-end adjusting JEs**, disposal with AD plug, Year-2 dep at old rate × new cost  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Marine Fabricators LLC** (calendar year) combines the following **heterogeneous** production assets into a composite at the start of **Year 1** (full-year depreciation). Purchased for cash on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Asset class | Quantity | Unit acquisition cost | Unit residual value | Unit useful life (years) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hull welders | 5 | $48,000 | $4,800 | 8 |
| CNC plasma cutters | 8 | $20,000 | $2,000 | 8 |
| Paint booths | 10 | $12,000 | $0 | 6 |
| Staging racks | 20 | $4,000 | $0 | 5 |

On **August 8, Year 2**, Harborline sells **one CNC plasma cutter** for **$7,000** cash. The disposal is **not material**; continue the original composite rate.

**Required:**  
a. Complete the composite schedule (totals and annual SL depreciation by class).  
b. Compute composite rate (three decimals), composite useful life (two decimals), and Year 1 depreciation.  
c. Journal entries: (1) **initial recognition**, (2) **Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**, (3) **disposal**, (4) **Year 2 period-end adjusting JE**. Prove Dr = Cr on every entry.  
d. State revised total acquisition cost used for Year 2 depreciation.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Composite schedule**

| Asset class | Qty | Total acquisition cost | Total residual | Total annual SL depreciation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hull welders | 5 | $240,000 | $24,000 | ($240,000 − $24,000) / 8 = **$27,000** |
| CNC plasma cutters | 8 | 160,000 | 16,000 | ($160,000 − $16,000) / 8 = **18,000** |
| Paint booths | 10 | 120,000 | 0 | $120,000 / 6 = **20,000** |
| Staging racks | 20 | 80,000 | 0 | $80,000 / 5 = **16,000** |
| **Totals** | | **$600,000** | **$40,000** | **$81,000** |

Total depreciable cost = $600,000 − $40,000 = **$560,000**

**b. Rate, life, Year 1 depreciation**

\[
\text{Rate} = \frac{\$81{,}000}{\$600{,}000} = \mathbf{0.135}
\]

\[
\text{Composite useful life} = \frac{\$560{,}000}{\$81{,}000} = \mathbf{6.91\ \text{years}}
\]

\[
\text{Year 1 composite depreciation} = 0.135 \times \$600{,}000 = \mathbf{\$81{,}000}
\]

**c–d. Journal entries (all balanced)**

*(1) January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment (composite control) | 600,000 | |
| Cash | | 600,000 |

**Check:** Dr 600,000 = Cr 600,000. **Balanced.**

*(2) December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 81,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 81,000 |
| *0.135 × $600,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 81,000 = Cr 81,000. **Balanced.**

*(3) August 8, Year 2 — dispose of one CNC plasma cutter (cost $20,000; cash $7,000; AD plug $13,000)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 7,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 13,000 | |
| Equipment (composite control) | | 20,000 |

**Check:** Dr 7,000 + 13,000 = Cr 20,000. **No gain/loss.** **Balanced.**

*(4) December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE on revised cost*

Revised total acquisition cost = $600,000 − $20,000 = **$580,000**  
Depreciation = 0.135 × $580,000 = **$78,300**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 78,300 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 78,300 |
| *Old rate × revised total acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 78,300 = Cr 78,300. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Changing every input (company, costs, lives, residual, disposal proceeds) still follows the same pipeline: **build schedule → rate/life → capitalize total cost → period-end dep = rate × cost → dispose with AD plug → reapply old rate to new cost at next period-end**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Group depreciation (homogeneous fleet): period-end AJs, multi-unit disposal, addition
**LO:** LO 12-3  
**Concept:** Group depreciation for similar assets; group rate and life; **period-end adjusting JEs** across years; multi-unit disposal with AD plug; non-material addition; subsequent group depreciation at old rate × new cost  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinnacle Courier Services** acquires a **homogeneous fleet** of **25 identical delivery vans** on **January 1, Year 1** for cash (full-year depreciation; **group** method).

| Fact | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unit cost | $36,000 |
| Unit residual value | $3,600 |
| Estimated useful life | 6 years |
| Quantity | 25 vans |
| Depreciation system | **Group** (similar assets) |

On **April 12, Year 2**, Pinnacle sells **4 vans** for **$16,000 cash each** ($64,000 total). On **January 1, Year 3**, Pinnacle purchases **2 identical vans** for **$36,000 each** cash and adds them to the same group. Management judges both the Year 2 retirement and the Year 3 addition **not material** to the group’s overall composition; **retain the original group rate**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute total acquisition cost, total residual, total depreciable cost, **annual group depreciation**, **group depreciation rate** (three decimals), and **group useful life**.  
b. Prepare the **initial recognition** JE and the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**.  
c. Prepare the **April 12, Year 2** multi-unit disposal JE.  
d. Compute and record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE**.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3** addition JE and the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE**.  
f. Explain in one sentence how **group** differs from **composite** (classification concept).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Group measures**

| Item | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Total acquisition cost | 25 × $36,000 | **$900,000** |
| Total residual | 25 × $3,600 | **$90,000** |
| Total depreciable cost | $900,000 − $90,000 | **$810,000** |
| Annual group depreciation | $810,000 / 6 | **$135,000** |
| Group depreciation rate | $135,000 / $900,000 | **0.150** |
| Group useful life | $810,000 / $135,000 | **6.00 years** |

(For identical assets, group life equals each unit’s useful life.)

**b. Initial recognition and Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**

*January 1, Year 1 — purchase fleet*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Delivery Equipment (group control) | 900,000 | |
| Cash | | 900,000 |

**Check:** Dr 900,000 = Cr 900,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end group depreciation (emphasis)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Delivery Equipment | 135,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Equipment | | 135,000 |
| *0.150 × $900,000 = $135,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 135,000 = Cr 135,000. **Balanced.**

**c. April 12, Year 2 — dispose of 4 vans**

Cost removed = 4 × $36,000 = **$144,000**  
Cash = 4 × $16,000 = **$64,000**  
AD plug = $144,000 − $64,000 = **$80,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 64,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Equipment | 80,000 | |
| Delivery Equipment (group control) | | 144,000 |
| *No gain or loss; difference absorbed in AD* | | |

**Check:** Dr 64,000 + 80,000 = Cr 144,000. **Balanced.**

**d. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE**

Revised group cost = $900,000 − $144,000 = **$756,000**  
Depreciation = 0.150 × $756,000 = **$113,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Delivery Equipment | 113,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Equipment | | 113,400 |

**Check:** Dr 113,400 = Cr 113,400. **Balanced.**

**e. Year 3 addition and period-end adjusting JE**

*January 1, Year 3 — add 2 vans*

Cost added = 2 × $36,000 = **$72,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Delivery Equipment (group control) | 72,000 | |
| Cash | | 72,000 |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 = Cr 72,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3 — period-end group depreciation*

Revised group cost = $756,000 + $72,000 = **$828,000**  
Depreciation = 0.150 × $828,000 = **$124,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Delivery Equipment | 124,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Equipment | | 124,200 |
| *Old group rate × cost after non-material addition* | | |

**Check:** Dr 124,200 = Cr 124,200. **Balanced.**

**f. Group vs composite**  
**Group** depreciation combines **homogeneous/similar** assets (e.g., a fleet of like vans); **composite** depreciation combines **heterogeneous/dissimilar** assets (e.g., lathes, presses, and cabinets) — **computations and journal entries are otherwise identical**.

**Key insight:** Group method is the practical fleet application of the same control-account logic: average rate on total cost, **period-end dep = rate × current cost**, and **retirements never trigger G/L** — only cost removal and an AD plug. Non-material additions also keep the original rate.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of disposal accounting under group/composite
**LO:** LO 12-3  
**Concept:** Classification of disposal accounting under group/composite methods (no separate G/L; AD plug)  
**Question:**  
Under the **composite (or group) depreciation method**, when an individual asset is sold before the end of the composite/group life for cash less than its original cost, the company typically:

- A) Recognizes a loss equal to original cost minus cash proceeds.  
- B) Recognizes a gain or loss equal to cash proceeds minus the asset’s carrying amount computed with unit-level accumulated depreciation.  
- C) Debits Cash for proceeds, credits the asset control account for original cost, and plugs **Accumulated Depreciation** for the difference (no gain or loss).  
- D) Leaves the asset cost on the books and continues depreciating it until the composite life ends.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Group/composite methods maintain cost in a control account and a single AD control account. On disposal, remove original cost, record cash, and balance the entry through AD so that **unrecorded gains and losses offset within AD over time**. Unit-level book value and separate G/L are not used.

### Q5 — MC — Period-end measurement after non-material disposal
**LO:** LO 12-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting measurement after non-material change — old rate × revised total acquisition cost  
**Question:**  
A company uses the **straight-line composite method** with a composite annual depreciation rate of **0.121**. Total acquisition cost of the composite was **$500,000** at the start of Year 1. Early in Year 2, an asset with **$15,000** original cost is sold; the change is **not material**. What amount of **composite depreciation expense** should the company record in its **Year 2 period-end adjusting entry**?

- A) $60,500  
- B) $58,685  
- C) $60,500 × (11/12) = $55,458  
- D) Recalculate a new composite rate using remaining assets only, then apply that new rate to $485,000

**Answer:** **B.**  
After a non-material disposal, **retain the original rate** and apply it to the **revised** total acquisition cost: \(0.121 \times (\$500{,}000 - \$15{,}000) = 0.121 \times \$485{,}000 = \$58{,}685\). Full-year composite depreciation is typically recorded (not partial-year proration of the composite rate merely because a unit was sold mid-year). A material change in makeup would be needed before recomputing the rate.
