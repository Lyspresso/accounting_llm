# Agent 139 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-7

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase of trading debt securities (including between interest dates: Investment at cost + Interest Receivable for accrued coupon)
- **Interest revenue:** cash coupon vs interest earned; reverse Interest Receivable when coupon is collected
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** amortized-cost rollforward (par or after discount amortization) and multi-period **Fair Value Adjustment (FVA)** bridge from amortized cost to fair value
- **Period-end adjusting JE only:** unrealized holding G/L through **net income** (Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income); FVA is an adjunct/contra to the investment—**no sale-date FV remeasurement** under Appendix 14A / LO 14-7
- **Disposal JE:** realized gain or loss = cash proceeds − **amortized cost**; leave FVA untouched until period-end
- **Period-end after disposal:** true up / eliminate FVA for securities sold during the period (portfolio-level FVA adjustment)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** TS usually current assets; unrealized and realized holding G/L in other income (NI, not OCI); fair value option produces same FV-NI period-end mechanics
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO 14-7 path with all different amounts

**Method note (exam gap vs LO 14-2):** LO 14-2 remeasures FVA **at sale date and period-end**. LO 14-7 (Appendix 14A) adjusts FVA **only at period-end**. On sale, recognize **realized** G/L vs amortized cost; clean up FVA later at year-end.

### Q1 — CORE — TS between interest dates, period-end FVA, sale vs amortized cost, year-end FVA cleanup

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of trading debt securities; interest; period-end-only FV-NI FVA; disposal with realized G/L; subsequent FVA elimination schedule  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpine Asset Management** (calendar year) classifies debt investments bought for short-term price appreciation as **trading securities (TS)** measured at **FV-NI**. Under its accounting policy (Appendix 14A / LO 14-7), Silverpine adjusts the **Fair Value Adjustment** account **only at each December 31 period-end**—not on sale dates.

**Facts:**
1. On **November 1, Year 1**, Silverpine purchases **at par** **$45,000** face of **Crestview Manufacturing** **8%** bonds (bonds dated **July 1, Year 1**; mature **June 30, Year 8**). Cash interest is paid **semiannually on June 30 and December 31**. Brokerage fees are zero.
2. Fair value of the Crestview bonds on **December 31, Year 1** is **$42,000**.
3. On **February 1, Year 2**, Silverpine sells the entire holding for **$42,500** cash (treat as clean price; no additional accrued-interest complexity beyond facts given).
4. Silverpine holds **no other TS** after the sale. It adjusts FVA only on **December 31, Year 2**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **November 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (include accrued interest).  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** cash interest receipt JE.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement (FVA) worksheet** and the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**.  
d. Show **Year 1 balance-sheet carrying amount** and **income-statement** effects (interest + unrealized G/L).  
e. Prepare the **February 1, Year 2 disposal JE** under LO 14-7 (realized G/L vs amortized cost; **do not** adjust FVA at sale).  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** FVA adjusting JE and a **holding-period G/L reconciliation** schedule (Year 1 unrealized + Year 2 realized + Year 2 FVA true-up = economic result).

**Answer key:**  

**a. November 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (between interest dates)**

Cash paid = \(45{,}000 + 1{,}200 = \$46{,}200\)

**Balance sheet (Dec 31, Year 1) — Current assets**  
Investment in trading securities ………… **$42,000**  
*(Investment in TS $45,000 less Fair Value Adjustment—TS credit $3,000)*

**e. February 1, Year 2 — Disposal (LO 14-7: realized G/L vs amortized cost; no sale-date FVA)**  
Realized loss = amortized cost − proceeds = \(45{,}000 - 42{,}500 = \$2{,}500\)

**Key insight:** Under LO 14-7, **all fair-value true-ups hit earnings only at period-end**. Sale day uses a classic **realized G/L vs amortized cost** entry and leaves FVA alone; year-end FVA cleanup then reclassifies/nets residual valuation accounts so cumulative NI still equals economic profit or loss on the position.

### Q3 — CORE — Discount TS: amortization schedule, period-end FVA only, disposal vs amortized cost

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule (effective-interest amortized cost + multi-row FVA) with period-end-only fair value adjustments and disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Meadowbrook Capital Corp.** (calendar year) purchases debt classified as **trading securities** and uses the **effective interest method**. Fair value is adjusted **only at each December 31** (LO 14-7).

**Facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Meadowbrook purchases **$50,000** face of **Timberline Packaging** **4%** bonds for **$46,534.89**. Cash interest is paid **annually each December 31**. The bonds mature **December 31, Year 4** (four years remaining). Market (effective) rate at purchase = **6%**.
2. Fair value on **December 31, Year 1** is **$48,000**.
3. On **January 1, Year 2**, Meadowbrook sells the entire investment for **$47,800** cash.
4. No other TS remain; FVA is cleaned up on **December 31, Year 2**.

**Required:**  
a. Verify the purchase price is the PV of the bonds at 6%. Record the purchase JE.  
b. Prepare the **Year 1 amortization schedule row** (cash interest, effective interest, discount amortization, ending amortized cost). Record the December 31, Year 1 interest JE.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end FVA** (use **amortized cost after amortization**, not original cost).  
d. Record the **January 1, Year 2 sale** under LO 14-7 (realized G/L vs amortized cost; no sale-date FVA).  
e. Record the **December 31, Year 2** FVA elimination and reconcile cumulative G/L to (proceeds − amortized cost at sale).  
f. **Classification:** Where do interest revenue, unrealized holding G/L, and realized sale G/L appear? Is FVA reported separately or netted against the investment for BS presentation?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Purchase price verification and initial recognition**  
Annual cash interest = \(50{,}000 \times 0.04 = \$2{,}000\)  
\[
PV = 50{,}000 \times (1.06)^{-4} + 2{,}000 \times \frac{1-(1.06)^{-4}}{0.06}
\]  
\((1.06)^4 = 1.26247696\)  
\(PV_{\text{principal}} = 50{,}000 / 1.26247696 = 39{,}604.68\)  
\(PVAF(6\%,4) = [1 - (1.06)^{-4}] / 0.06 = 3.465106\)  
\(PV_{\text{interest}} = 2{,}000 \times 3.465106 = 6{,}930.21\)  
**Combined PV = $46,534.89** ✓

|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Timberline Bonds | 46,534.89 | |

| *Purchase TS at discount; recorded at amortized-cost basis* | | |

**b. Subsequent measurement — amortized cost schedule (Year 1) + interest JE**

|---|---:|---:|

BS carrying amount = \(47{,}326.98 + 673.02 = \$48{,}000\).

**f. Classification / presentation**  
- **Interest revenue**, **unrealized holding G/L—Income**, and **realized gain/loss on sale** all affect **net income** (typically other income/expense)—**not OCI**.  
- On the balance sheet, TS are usually **current assets**. Companies present the investment **net of FVA** (amortized cost ± FVA) so the reported carrying amount equals fair value at the last period-end measurement date.  
- FVA itself is a **valuation account**, not a separate freestanding “cash” line; it preserves the amortized-cost subledger while reporting FV on the face of the BS.

**Key insight:** Effective interest still updates **amortized cost** every period; LO 14-7 FVA only bridges **current amortized cost → fair value at period-end**. Disposal does **not** remeasure FVA—realized G/L uses amortized cost—then year-end FVA cleanup finishes the measurement cycle.

### Q4 — CORE — Portfolio FVA schedule, fair value option, and presentation/disclosure

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Subsequent multi-security FVA schedule at period-end only; fair value option on otherwise HTM debt; classification/presentation/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Redcedar Holdings Inc.** (calendar year) holds the following **debt** positions at **December 31, Year 1**. Redcedar adjusts fair value **only at period-end** (LO 14-7).

| Security | Classification / election | Amortized cost 12/31/Y1 | Fair value 12/31/Y1 | Beginning FVA (1/1/Y1) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A — Solstice Foods 5% bonds | Trading (TS) | $120,000 | $123,500 | $1,200 debit |
| B — Alpine Transit 4% bonds | Trading (TS) | 80,000 | 76,400 | 900 credit |
| C — Northshore Utilities 3.5% bonds | HTM classification, but **fair value option** elected at acquisition | 200,000 | 205,000 | 0 |

During Year 1, Redcedar **sold no** securities. Interest on all positions has already been recorded correctly. FVA for TS is maintained in one **portfolio-level** Fair Value Adjustment—TS account. FVO uses a separate Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option account.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **TS portfolio subsequent measurement (FVA) schedule** for December 31, Year 1 and the single **period-end adjusting JE** for TS.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 FVO adjusting JE** for Northshore.  
c. State the **balance-sheet carrying amounts** for (i) trading securities and (ii) the FVO debt investment.  
d. For each of A, B, and C, state whether the Year 1 unrealized holding G/L is in **net income** or **OCI**.  
e. **Disclosure / classification:** List **three** items Redcedar should address in notes or SCF classification related to these investments.  
f. **Contrast:** If security C had been pure HTM **without** the fair value option, what period-end FV entry (if any) would be required?

**Answer key:**  

**a. TS portfolio FVA subsequent measurement schedule (period-end only)**  

| Security | Amortized cost | Fair value | Required unrealized G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A — Solstice | $120,000 | $123,500 | $3,500 |
| B — Alpine | 80,000 | 76,400 | (3,600) |
| **Portfolio total** | **$200,000** | **$199,900** | **$(100)** |

| FVA rollforward | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required ending FVA (portfolio FV − AC) | $(100)$ credit |
| Beginning FVA (1,200 debit − 900 credit) = **net debit** | 300 debit |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | **$(400)$** |

Computation: to move from **$300 debit** to **$100 credit**, credit FVA by **$400**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 400 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 400 |
| *Period-end portfolio mark of TS to FV through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400. **Balanced.**  
Ending FVA—TS = 300 debit − 400 = **$100 credit**.  
Portfolio carrying amount = \(200{,}000 - 100 = \$199{,}900\) = aggregate FV.

**b. Fair value option — Northshore (FV-NI at period-end)**  
Unrealized holding gain = \(205{,}000 - 200{,}000 = \$5{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option | 5,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 5,000 |
| *FVO: period-end FV-NI adjustment (same NI treatment as TS)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Balance-sheet carrying amounts (Dec 31, Year 1)**  
- **Trading securities (A+B):** **$199,900** (typically current assets).  
- **Northshore FVO debt:** **$205,000** (current or noncurrent based on maturity/intent; carrying amount = AC + FVA debit).

**d. NI vs OCI**  

| Security | Unrealized G/L destination |
|---|---|
| A (TS) | **Net income** |
| B (TS) | **Net income** |
| C (FVO) | **Net income** (not OCI) |

None of these LO 14-7 FV-NI measurements use OCI for fair-value changes.

**e. Classification / presentation / disclosure (examples)**  
1. **Balance sheet:** Report TS (and AFS, if any) separately from assets measured on another basis—either separate line items or aggregate line with parenthetical FV disclosure (ASC 320-10-45-1).  
2. **Income / notes:** Disclose gross unrealized holding gains and losses and the change in net unrealized holding G/L for the period; FVO election is instrument-by-instrument, generally irrevocable, elected at acquisition.  
3. **Statement of cash flows:** Purchases/sales of trading debt bought to profit on short-term price differences are often **operating** cash flows; other debt investment cash flows are typically **investing** (purpose-based judgment under ASC 230).

**f. Contrast — pure HTM without FVO**  
**No period-end fair value journal entry.** Security C would remain at amortized cost **$200,000**; the $5,000 unrealized gain is **not recognized** in NI or OCI (fair value may appear only in footnotes as required).

**Key insight:** LO 14-7 period-end FVA is often applied at the **portfolio** level for TS: compute required FVA from **aggregate** amortized cost vs **aggregate** fair value, then adjust the **existing** FVA balance. The fair value option puts otherwise HTM debt on the **same FV-NI period-end path** as trading securities.

### Q5 — MC — Timing of FVA under LO 14-7 (period-end only)

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Classification of method—when fair value is adjusted for debt measured at FV-NI under Appendix 14A  
**Question:** **Cobalt Trading Desk** holds a trading debt security purchased at par for **$60,000**. At the prior December 31, fair value was **$58,500** and Cobalt recorded a credit FVA of **$1,500**. On **March 20** of the current year, Cobalt sells the bonds for **$59,200** cash. Cobalt’s policy is **LO 14-7 (Appendix 14A): fair value adjustments only at period-end**. Which description of the **March 20** accounting is correct?

- A) First debit FVA $700 and credit Unrealized G/L—Income $700 (bring carrying amount to $59,200); then debit Cash $59,200, debit FVA $800, credit Investment $60,000.  
- B) Debit Cash $59,200; debit Loss on Sale $800; credit Investment $60,000; leave the $1,500 credit FVA outstanding until the next period-end adjusting entry.  
- C) Debit Cash $59,200; debit FVA $1,500; debit Loss $300? No—credit Investment $60,000 and credit Gain $700 without a separate realized loss account.  
- D) No entry until December 31; both the sale and all fair value changes are recorded only at year-end.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-7, the sale records **realized loss = amortized cost − cash** = \(60{,}000 - 59{,}200 = \$800\) and **does not** remeasure FVA on the sale date. The preexisting FVA remains until the **period-end** true-up (which will also eliminate FVA related to sold securities). Option A is the **LO 14-2** sale-date remeasurement pattern. C mixes FVA elimination into the sale without the LO 14-7 realized G/L approach. D is incorrect because the **sale itself** is recorded when it occurs—only the **FVA fair-value adjustment** waits until period-end.

### Q6 — MC — Where unrealized holding gains on FV-NI debt are reported

**LO:** LO 14-7  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation—unrealized holding G/L on TS / FVO debt through net income  
**Question:** At year-end, **Lumenport Advisors** adjusts its only debt investment—classified as **trading** and measured at **FV-NI**—from amortized cost **$90,000** to fair value **$93,400**. Lumenport uses period-end-only FVA (LO 14-7). How is the **$3,400** unrealized holding gain reported?

- A) Other comprehensive income (OCI), net of tax, until the security is sold.  
- B) Net income (other income) in the current period.  
- C) Directly to retained earnings, bypassing both NI and OCI.  
- D) No recognition until sale; amortized cost remains the BS carrying amount.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Trading securities (and FVO debt) measured at FV-NI recognize unrealized holding gains and losses **in earnings** each period-end. OCI is the AFS (FV-OCI) model, not LO 14-7. Carrying amount becomes fair value via the FVA account; the gain is not deferred to sale or booked straight to RE.
