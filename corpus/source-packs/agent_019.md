# Agent 019 — CORE DEMO — LO 17-1

**Chapter:** 17  
**LO title:** Identify a lease, determine lease types for lessee, and classify leases using lease criteria  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** initial_recognition_JE (with full lessee life-cycle angles)

## Concept list for this pack
- Identify whether a contract contains a lease (identified asset + right to control)
- Lessee lease types: finance vs operating (short-term exception noted only)
- Five ASC 842 classification criteria (ownership, purchase option, lease term ≥75%, PV ≥90%, no alternative use)
- Initial measurement: lease liability = PV of lease payments; ROU asset = liability (basic, no incentives)
- Lease liability schedule (effective interest; annuity-due payments)
- Period-end adjusting entries (interest accrual; ROU amortization for finance lease)
- Maturity/settlement: final payment clears liability; ROU fully amortized
- Classification presentation: current vs noncurrent lease liability

### Q1 — CORE — Classify lease, initial recognition, liability schedule, full finance-lease JEs
**LO:** LO 17-1  
**Concept:** Lease identification & classification → finance-lease initial recognition and subsequent measurement  
**Scenario:** On **January 1, Year 1** (lease commencement), **Northline Logistics Corp.** enters a **4-year noncancellable** lease with **Cascade Equipment Partners** for a delivery-dock conveyor system. Facts:

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Annual lease payment | **$32,500**, due **beginning** of each year (Jan 1 Year 1–4) |
| Lessor’s implicit rate | **5%**, **known** by Northline |
| Northline’s incremental borrowing rate | 6% (not used when implicit rate is known) |
| Fair value of conveyor at commencement | **$125,000** |
| Economic life of conveyor | **4 years** |
| Residual value / guaranteed residual | **$0** / none |
| Ownership transfer at end of term | **No** — asset reverts to Cascade |
| Purchase / renewal options | **None** |
| Specialized asset / alternative use | Conveyor is **standard** equipment Cascade re-leases to others |
| Identified asset | Serial number **CLX-4419** is specified; Northline directs use and obtains substantially all economic benefits during the term |
| Northline’s year-end | December 31 |

**Required:**  
(a) Does the contract contain a **lease**? Cite the two control criteria briefly.  
(b) Apply all **five** lessee classification criteria (use 75% and 90% bright lines). Classify as **finance** or **operating**.  
(c) Compute the **lease liability** and **right-of-use (ROU) asset** at commencement (no incentives, no initial direct costs).  
(d) Prepare the complete **lease liability schedule** (payment, interest, liability change, ending liability).  
(e) Prepare Northline’s journal entries for **Year 1** (commencement, first payment, Dec 31 adjusting).  
(f) Report Dec 31, Year 1 **current** and **noncurrent** lease liability and net ROU asset.  
(g) Prepare journal entries for **Year 2** and **Year 3** (payment + Dec 31 interest and amortization).  
(h) Prepare **Year 4** settlement entries (final payment Jan 1; Dec 31 final amortization). Confirm ending ROU and liability balances are zero.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Lease identification — Yes, the contract contains a lease.**  
- **Identified asset:** conveyor serial CLX-4419 is explicitly specified.  
- **Right to control:** throughout the period of use Northline (1) obtains substantially all economic benefits from use and (2) directs how and for what purpose the conveyor is used. Cascade has no substantive substitution right described.  
→ ASC 842: contract conveys the right to control the use of identified PPE for a period of time in exchange for consideration.

**(b) Classification — Finance lease** (at least one criterion met; here two are met).

| Criterion | Analysis | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ownership transfer | Asset reverts to Cascade; no automatic title transfer | No |
| 2. Purchase option reasonably certain | No purchase option | No |
| 3. Lease term = major part of remaining economic life | Term 4 ÷ life 4 = **100% ≥ 75%** | **Yes** |
| 4. PV of lease payments ≥ substantially all of FV | PV **$121,005.56** ≥ 90% × $125,000 = **$112,500** | **Yes** |
| 5. No alternative use to lessor | Standard equipment with alternative uses | No |

Lessee type: **finance lease**.

**(c) Initial measurement**  
Rate = **5%** implicit (known). Payments are annuity-due (TYPE = 1).

\[
PV = 32{,}500 + 32{,}500 \times \frac{1-(1.05)^{-3}}{0.05} = \$121{,}005.56
\]

- Lease liability (commencement, before first payment) = **$121,005.56**  
- ROU asset (basic) = **$121,005.56**

**(d) Lease liability schedule** (amounts to nearest cent; final row clears to zero)

| Date | Lease payment | Interest on liability (5%) | Liability reduction | Lease liability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 (commencement) | — | — | — | 121,005.56 |
| Jan 1, Y1 | 32,500.00 | 0.00 | 32,500.00 | 88,505.56 |
| Jan 1, Y2 | 32,500.00 | 4,425.28 | 28,074.72 | 60,430.84 |
| Jan 1, Y3 | 32,500.00 | 3,021.54 | 29,478.46 | 30,952.38 |
| Jan 1, Y4 | 32,500.00 | 1,547.62 | 30,952.38 | 0.00 |
| **Totals** | **130,000.00** | **8,994.44** | **121,005.56** | |

Interest checks: \(88{,}505.56 \times 0.05 = 4{,}425.28\); \(60{,}430.84 \times 0.05 = 3{,}021.54\); \(30{,}952.38 \times 0.05 = 1{,}547.62\).

Annual ROU amortization (straight-line over **lease term** = 4 years; no ownership transfer / purchase option):  
\(121{,}005.56 / 4 = \$30{,}251.39\) per year.

**(e) Year 1 journal entries**

Jan 1, Year 1 — record ROU asset and lease liability:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Right-of-Use Asset | 121,005.56 | |
| Lease Liability | | 121,005.56 |

Dr 121,005.56 = Cr 121,005.56.

Jan 1, Year 1 — first lease payment:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,500.00 | |
| Cash | | 32,500.00 |

Dr 32,500.00 = Cr 32,500.00.

Dec 31, Year 1 — accrue interest (effective interest):
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,425.28 | |
| Lease Liability | | 4,425.28 |

Dr 4,425.28 = Cr 4,425.28.

Dec 31, Year 1 — amortize ROU asset (finance lease):
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 30,251.39 | |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 30,251.39 |

Dr 30,251.39 = Cr 30,251.39.

**(f) Dec 31, Year 1 presentation**  
- Total lease liability = \(88{,}505.56 + 4{,}425.28 = \$92{,}930.84\)  
- **Current** lease liability = next payment = **$32,500.00** (accrued interest $4,425.28 + next principal reduction $28,074.72)  
- **Noncurrent** lease liability = \(92{,}930.84 - 32{,}500.00 = \$60{,}430.84\)  
- ROU asset (net) = \(121{,}005.56 - 30{,}251.39 = \$90{,}754.17\)

**(g) Years 2 and 3**

**Year 2**  
Jan 1, Y2 — payment:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,500.00 | |
| Cash | | 32,500.00 |

Dec 31, Y2 — interest (\(60{,}430.84 \times 0.05\)):
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 3,021.54 | |
| Lease Liability | | 3,021.54 |

Dec 31, Y2 — amortization:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 30,251.39 | |

**Year 3**  
Jan 1, Y3 — payment:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,500.00 | |

Dec 31, Y3 — interest (\(30{,}952.38 \times 0.05\)):
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 1,547.62 | |
| Lease Liability | | 1,547.62 |

Dec 31, Y3 — amortization:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 30,251.39 | |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 30,251.39 |

**(h) Year 4 maturity / settlement**

Jan 1, Y4 — final payment (clears liability: accrued interest $1,547.62 + remaining principal $30,952.38):
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 32,500.00 | |
| Cash | | 32,500.00 |

Dec 31, Y4 — final ROU amortization (no interest remains after final payment on Jan 1):
| Account | Debit | Credit |

**Key insight:** After identifying a lease, meet **any one** of the five criteria and the lessee has a **finance** lease. Initial recognition always books **ROU asset = PV of lease payments = lease liability** (basic case). Subsequent measurement separates **interest** (effective interest on liability) from **amortization** (straight-line ROU) for finance leases.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Same life cycle, all numbers changed
**LO:** LO 17-1  
**Concept:** Lease classification + finance-lease initial recognition / schedule (number twin)  
**Scenario:** On **January 1, Year 1**, **Summit Fabrication LLC** leases a CNC finishing cell from **Helix Capital Leasing**. Facts:

| Item | Amount / fact |
|---|---|
| Annual lease payment | **$30,000**, due **beginning** of each year (4 payments) |
| Lease term | **4 years**, noncancellable |
| Implicit rate (known by Summit) | **6%** |
| Incremental borrowing rate | 7% (not used) |
| Fair value at commencement | **$115,000** |
| Economic life | **4 years** |
| Ownership transfer / purchase option | **None**; asset reverts to Helix |
| Residual / GRV | **$0** / none |
| Alternative use | Helix routinely re-leases similar cells → alternatives exist |
| Identified asset | Explicit machine ID **HX-900**; Summit directs use and takes substantially all benefits |
| Year-end | December 31 |

**Required:**  
(a) Classify the lease for Summit (show all five criteria; 75%/90% tests).  
(b) Compute commencement lease liability and ROU asset.  
(c) Prepare the full lease liability schedule.  
(d) Journal entries for **Year 1** only (commencement, payment, Dec 31 interest and amortization).  
(e) Dec 31, Year 1 current/noncurrent liability split and net ROU.  
(f) Journal entries for **final Year 4** payment and final amortization (assume Years 2–3 were recorded correctly per the schedule).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Finance lease**

| Criterion | Analysis | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership transfer | Reverts to Helix | No |
| Purchase option ROC | None | No |
| Lease term major part | 4/4 = **100% ≥ 75%** | **Yes** |
| PV ≥ 90% of FV | PV **$110,190.36** ≥ 0.90 × $115,000 = **$103,500** | **Yes** |
| No alternative use | Alternatives exist | No |

**(b)** Implicit 6% known; annuity due:

\[
PV = 30{,}000 + 30{,}000 \times \frac{1-(1.06)^{-3}}{0.06} = \$110{,}190.36
\]

ROU asset = lease liability = **$110,190.36**.

**(c) Lease liability schedule**

| Date | Payment | Interest (6%) | Reduction | Liability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 commence | — | — | — | 110,190.36 |
| Jan 1, Y1 | 30,000.00 | 0.00 | 30,000.00 | 80,190.36 |
| Jan 1, Y2 | 30,000.00 | 4,811.42 | 25,188.58 | 55,001.78 |
| Jan 1, Y3 | 30,000.00 | 3,300.11 | 26,699.89 | 28,301.89 |
| Jan 1, Y4 | 30,000.00 | 1,698.11 | 28,301.89 | 0.00 |
| **Totals** | **120,000.00** | **9,809.64** | **110,190.36** | |

Interest: \(80{,}190.36 \times 0.06 = 4{,}811.42\); \(55{,}001.78 \times 0.06 = 3{,}300.11\); \(28{,}301.89 \times 0.06 = 1{,}698.11\).

ROU amortization: \(110{,}190.36 / 4 = \$27{,}547.59\) per year.

**(d) Year 1 JEs**

Jan 1, Y1 — commencement:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Right-of-Use Asset | 110,190.36 | |
| Lease Liability | | 110,190.36 |

Jan 1, Y1 — payment:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 30,000.00 | |
| Cash | | 30,000.00 |

Dec 31, Y1 — interest:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,811.42 | |
| Lease Liability | | 4,811.42 |

Dec 31, Y1 — amortization:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 27,547.59 | |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 27,547.59 |

All four entries balance.

**(e) Dec 31, Year 1**  
- Total liability = \(80{,}190.36 + 4{,}811.42 = \$85{,}001.78\)  
- Current = **$30,000.00**; noncurrent = **$55,001.78**  
- ROU net = \(110{,}190.36 - 27{,}547.59 = \$82{,}642.77\)

**(f) Year 4 settlement**

Jan 1, Y4 — final payment:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 30,000.00 | |
| Cash | | 30,000.00 |

Dec 31, Y4 — final amortization:
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense | 27,547.59 | |
| Right-of-Use Asset | | 27,547.59 |

Liability and ROU both **$0** after Year 4 entries (and prior years’ amortization totaling \(3 \times 27{,}547.59\) before the final year).

**Key insight:** Changing payment, rate, and fair value does not change the model: **classify first** (term and/or PV tests often trigger finance), measure PV with the **correct rate and payment timing**, then run the **effective-interest schedule** and finance-lease expense split.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-scenario classification + operating contrast + initial JE
**LO:** LO 17-1  
**Concept:** Apply each classification criterion; contrast finance vs operating; initial recognition when operating  
**Scenario:** **Prairie Retail Group** is negotiating four **separate** equipment leases (each is an identified asset Prairie will control). Prairie’s incremental borrowing rate is **6%** unless an implicit rate is stated as known. Use 75% and 90% bright lines. Round PV to nearest cent.

**Lease A — Ownership transfer**  
5-year lease of a walk-in freezer; legal title **automatically transfers** to Prairie at end of term for no additional payment. Economic life 8 years. Annual payments $18,000 beginning of year. Implicit rate unknown.

**Lease B — Purchase option**  
4-year lease of point-of-sale hardware. Option to buy at end for **$4,000** when expected end-of-term FV is **$12,000**; Prairie is **reasonably certain** to exercise. Economic life 6 years. No automatic title transfer without exercise.

**Lease C — Term and PV both tested (finance)**  
Lease term **8 years**; economic life **10 years**. No title transfer, no purchase option, asset has alternative uses. Annual payment **$28,000** at **beginning** of each year. Implicit rate **unknown**; IBR **6%**. Fair value **$180,000**.

**Lease D — Operating (no criteria met)**  
Lease term **3 years**; economic life **10 years**. No title transfer, no purchase option, nonspecialized asset. Annual payment **$12,000** at beginning of each year. Implicit rate unknown; IBR **7%**. Fair value **$100,000**.

**Required:**  
(a) For **each** lease A–D, state finance or operating and **which criterion/criteria** (if any) are met. Show PV math for C and D.  
(b) For **Lease D only**, compute the commencement lease liability / ROU asset and prepare the **initial recognition** JE and the **first payment** JE on commencement date (ignore subsequent operating-lease single lease expense for this LO).  
(c) Briefly explain why Lease D is still on the balance sheet even though it is operating (terms > 12 months).

**Answer key:**

**(a)**

**Lease A — Finance.** Criterion **1 Ownership transfer** met (title transfers automatically). (Term 5/8 = 62.5% fails 75% alone, but one met criterion is enough.)

**Lease B — Finance.** Criterion **2 Purchase option** met (significant bargain / economic incentive → reasonably certain to exercise).

**Lease C — Finance.**  
- Term: \(8/10 = 80\% \geq 75\%\) → Criterion **3 met**.  
- PV (annuity due, 6%, 8 payments of $28,000):

\[
PV = 28{,}000 + 28{,}000 \times \frac{1-(1.06)^{-7}}{0.06} = \$184{,}306.68
\]

90% of FV = \(0.90 \times 180{,}000 = \$162{,}000\).  
\(184{,}306.68 \geq 162{,}000\) → Criterion **4 also met**.  
Criteria 1, 2, 5 not met. Classification: **finance**.

**Lease D — Operating.**  
- Ownership: No  
- Purchase option: No  
- Term: \(3/10 = 30\% < 75\%\) → fail  
- PV (annuity due, 7%, 3 × $12,000):

\[
PV = 12{,}000 + 12{,}000 \times \frac{1-(1.07)^{-2}}{0.07} = \$33{,}696.22
\]

90% of FV = $90,000. \(33{,}696.22 < 90{,}000\) → fail  
- Alternative use: exists → fail  
**No criteria met → operating lease.**

**(b) Lease D initial recognition** (measurement same model as finance at day one):

Commencement liability / ROU = **$33,696.22**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Right-of-Use Asset | 33,696.22 | |
| Lease Liability | | 33,696.22 |

First payment (beginning of term):
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lease Liability | 12,000.00 | |
| Cash | | 12,000.00 |

Both balance. (After payment, liability carrying amount = \(33{,}696.22 - 12{,}000 = \$21{,}696.22\).)

**(c)** ASC 842 requires lessees to recognize a **ROU asset and lease liability for leases with terms greater than 12 months**, whether finance or operating. Classification changes **subsequent expense pattern** (interest + amortization vs single lease expense), not the existence of day-one balance-sheet recognition (short-term exception aside).

**Key insight:** Walk **all five criteria**; stop at the first “yes” for finance classification, but exam work often requires documenting every test. Operating leases still get **initial ROU + liability** when term > 12 months.

### Q4 — MC (classification)
**LO:** LO 17-1  
**Concept:** Which fact pattern forces finance-lease classification  

**Question:** **Oak & Iron Brewing Co.** leases a specialized stainless fermentation tank. The tank was custom-built to Oak & Iron’s floor plan and piping; at lease end the lessor would have to spend more to reconfigure the tank for another brewer than the expected re-lease proceeds, and the lessor has no practical alternative use. The lease term is 40% of the tank’s economic life, the PV of lease payments is 55% of fair value, title does not transfer, and there is no purchase option. How should **Oak & Iron (lessee)** classify the lease?

- A) Operating lease, because the term and PV tests both fail  
- B) Finance lease, because the underlying asset has **no alternative use** to the lessor at end of term  
- C) Operating lease, because specialized assets are always operating for the lessee  
- D) Short-term lease exception automatically, because term is less than half the economic life  

**Answer:** **B.** Criterion 5 (no alternative use) is met independently of the 75% and 90% tests. Meeting **any one** of the five criteria classifies the lease as **finance** for the lessee. A is wrong because criterion 5 still applies. C is false. D confuses the short-term (≤12 months) exception with the economic-life ratio.

### Q5 — MC (rate selection for classification / measurement)
**LO:** LO 17-1  
**Concept:** Discount rate for PV of lease payments (implicit vs incremental)  

**Question:** **Cedar Transit Authority** leases buses. The lessor’s **implicit rate is 4.5%** and is **readily determinable** by Cedar. Cedar’s incremental borrowing rate for a similar collateralized loan is **6%**. For purposes of the **PV of lease payments** classification test and initial measurement of the lease liability, which rate must Cedar use?

- A) 6%, because lessees always use the incremental borrowing rate  
- B) 4.5%, the rate implicit in the lease, because it is readily determinable  
- C) A midpoint rate of 5.25%  
- D) 0%, because classification ignores discounting when payments are fixed  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 842: lessee uses the **rate implicit in the lease when readily determinable**; otherwise the **incremental borrowing rate**. Here 4.5% is known, so it is used for both the PV classification test and initial liability measurement.
