# Agent 140 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-8

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-OCI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase available-for-sale (AFS) debt at cost (par path); debit Investment in AFS / credit Cash
- **Interest in net income:** coupon cash interest (and amortization if discount/premium) recognized in **net income**, not OCI
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-period rollforward of amortized cost, year-end fair value, required FVA, existing FVA, and period adjustment (emphasis angle)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (FV-OCI):** Dr/Cr Fair Value Adjustment—AFS with offset **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI** only at reporting dates
- **Disposal under period-end-only method:** realized G/L = cash proceeds − amortized cost in **net income**; **do not** adjust FVA at the sale date
- **Year-end FVA cleanup:** remeasure FVA for securities still held (or eliminate FVA if none remain)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** BS at fair value; unrealized in AOCI (equity); reclassification of realized G/L out of AOCI into earnings; interest in NI
- **Number-variant twin:** same LO 14-8 sequence with all facts and amounts changed
- **Method contrast:** LO 14-8 (Appendix 14B, period-end only) vs LO 14-3 (sale-date and period-end FV-OCI)

### Q1 — CORE — Silverbrook full AFS life cycle with subsequent measurement schedule (period-end-only FV-OCI)

**Concept:** Initial recognition; interest in NI; multi-year subsequent measurement / FVA schedule; period-end FV-OCI entries; disposal without sale-date FVA; year-end FVA elimination; AOCI reclassification disclosure  
**Scenario:**

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-security AFS portfolio, partial sale, period-end-only FVA schedule
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** Portfolio subsequent measurement schedule after disposing of one AFS holding; period-end FVA only; reclassification disclosure when remaining securities continue  
**Scenario:**  
At **December 31 of Year 2**, **Redwood Reserve Fund** holds the following **AFS** debt portfolio (all purchased at par; amortized cost = face). Redwood uses **period-end-only** fair value adjustments (LO 14-8 / Appendix 14B). Accounting year ends December 31.

| AFS investment | Amortized cost | Fair value 12/31/Y2 | Unrealized holding G/(L) |

The **$5,000** unrealized net gain equals the December 31, Year 2 balances in **FVA—AFS** (debit) and **AOCI** (credit).

During Year 3:
- **January 1 of Year 3:** Redwood sells the entire **Atlas** holding for **$78,500** cash.  
- No other AFS purchases or sales.  
- **December 31 of Year 3:** remaining **Vale** bonds have fair value **$126,500** (amortized cost still **$120,000**).

**Required:**  
a. Record the January 1, Year 3 sale of Atlas (period-end-only method).  
b. Prepare a **portfolio subsequent measurement / FVA schedule** for December 31, Year 3: remaining securities’ amortized cost and FV, required FVA, existing FVA, and period adjustment needed.  
c. Record the December 31, Year 3 FVA adjusting entry.  
d. Prepare the Year 3 AOCI reconciliation (beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized on sold and remaining securities, reclassification for the Atlas realized loss, ending AOCI).  
e. **Classification / presentation:** At December 31, Year 3, what amount is reported for the AFS investment on the balance sheet, and where do unrealized holding amounts related to Vale appear?

- **OCI Year 3:** net **$1,500** as in part d (current-period FV changes net of reclassification).

**Key insight:** In a multi-security AFS portfolio under **period-end-only** FV-OCI, a mid-year sale records **only** cash vs amortized cost in NI. The **subsequent measurement schedule** at year-end resets **one portfolio FVA** to FV − AC of **securities still held**, automatically clearing sold positions and capturing remeasurement plus reclassification through OCI.

### Q4 — MC — Period-end-only FV-OCI vs sale-date adjustment; where unrealized vs realized go
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** Distinguishing Appendix 14B (LO 14-8 period-end only) from LO 14-3 (sale-date and period-end); routing of unrealized vs realized amounts  
**Question:**  
**Timberline Portfolio Co.** holds AFS debt securities and follows **LO 14-8 (Appendix 14B)**: fair value adjustments **only at period-end**. On June 15, Timberline sells an AFS bond that has amortized cost **$40,000**, last year-end fair value **$41,200**, and cash proceeds **$42,500**. Which statement is **correct** under LO 14-8?

- A) On June 15, debit FVA **$1,300**, credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI **$1,300**, then record sale with no gain/loss because carrying value already equals fair value.  
- B) On June 15, record **Gain on Sale of Investment $2,500** (cash $42,500 − amortized cost $40,000) and make **no** fair value adjusting entry; FVA is updated only at the next period-end.  
- C) On June 15, record **Gain on Sale $1,300** (cash $42,500 − last year-end FV $41,200) through net income and reverse the prior FVA of $1,200 through OCI on the sale date.  
- D) On June 15, record **Gain on Sale $2,500** through **OCI**, because AFS unrealized and realized amounts always bypass net income.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-8, realized G/L = **cash − amortized cost** in **net income** at sale; the Fair Value Adjustment account is **not** touched until **period-end**. Choice A describes a sale-date FV-OCI update (closer to LO 14-3). Choice C wrongly uses last FV as the cost basis and adjusts FVA at sale. Choice D wrongly routes the realized gain to OCI instead of NI.

### Q5 — MC — Balance-sheet and comprehensive-income presentation of AFS under FV-OCI
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — AFS at FV on BS; interest in NI; unrealized in OCI/AOCI  
**Question:**  
At year-end, **Quarrystone Holdings** holds a single AFS debt security (amortized cost **$100,000**, fair value **$96,000**) accounted for under FV-OCI with period-end-only adjustments. Beginning AOCI related to the security was zero. Which presentation is correct for the year just ended?

- A) Balance sheet investment **$100,000**; unrealized loss **$4,000** in net income.  
- B) Balance sheet investment **$96,000**; interest revenue in net income; unrealized holding loss **$4,000** in other comprehensive income (AOCI **$(4,000)**).  
- C) Balance sheet investment **$96,000**; both interest revenue and the **$4,000** unrealized loss reported only in OCI.  
- D) Balance sheet investment **$100,000**; fair value of **$96,000** disclosed only in the notes (no FVA on the books).

**Answer:** **B.**  
AFS debt is reported at **fair value** on the balance sheet via the FVA valuation account. **Interest** (and realized G/L on sale) affect **net income**. **Unrealized** holding G/L on AFS go to **OCI** and accumulate in **AOCI** until reclassified upon sale (or impairment). Choice A treats AFS like trading (FV-NI). Choice C misroutes interest. Choice D describes HTM amortized-cost presentation without the fair value option.
