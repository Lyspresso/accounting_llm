# Agent 323 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-5

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for purchase commitments  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Firm purchase commitments** (noncancelable and material): disclose; recognize an **unrealized loss** when contract price exceeds **NRV** (FIFO / average / other non-LIFO) or **market** (LIFO / retail)
- **Contract initiation:** ordinarily **no journal entry** if contract price ≤ NRV/market (executory); disclose material terms
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Dr Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment; Cr Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment for (remaining contract $ − NRV/market of **open** units)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** roll remaining units, contract $, NRV/market $, estimated liability, and cumulative loss after each event
- **Subsequent recovery of an estimated loss is not recognized** before settlement
- **Settlement / purchase JE:** Inventory at current NRV/market; remove Estimated Liability; additional Loss if further decline; credit Cash (or AP) for **contract** price
- **Partial deliveries:** measure the period-end liability only on **remaining** committed units
- **No loss** when firm sales contracts (or similar) ensure disposition without price concession; cancelable commitments do not support loss recognition

### Q1 — CORE — Full cycle with period-end loss recognition and settlement purchase
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE for estimated loss on firm purchase commitment; settlement JE when goods are acquired; commitment measurement schedule  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperfield Alloys Co.** uses a **perpetual** inventory system and the **FIFO** inventory method (measure purchase-commitment declines against **net realizable value**). Year-end is **December 31**. Assume no selling costs, so stated unit selling prices equal NRV. The commitment is **noncancelable and material**. No firm sales contracts protect Copperfield from price declines on the committed goods.

Events:

1. **November 12, Year 1:** Enters a noncancelable contract to purchase **90,000** units of specialty billet at **$22** per unit (**$1,980,000** total) for delivery in Year 2. NRV on the contract date is **$23.50** per unit.
2. **December 31, Year 1:** NRV of billet under the open commitment is **$20.50** per unit.
3. **April 8, Year 2:** Purchases all **90,000** units for cash at the contract price. NRV on that date is **$19.75** per unit.

**Required:**  
(a) Journal entry, if any, on **November 12, Year 1** (contract initiation).  
(b) Compute the estimated loss and record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis).  
(c) Journal entry on **April 8, Year 2** (purchase / settlement).  
(d) Prepare a **commitment measurement schedule** (units remaining, remaining contract $, NRV of open units, estimated liability balance, cumulative loss recognized) for Nov 12 Y1 → Dec 31 Y1 → Apr 8 Y2 (after settlement).  
(e) Briefly state Year 1 **classification / presentation / disclosure** for the estimated loss and liability.

**Answer key:**

**(a) November 12, Year 1 — contract initiation**  
Contract unit price **$22** < NRV **$23.50**. The commitment is executory; **no journal entry**.  
Material noncancelable commitment is **disclosed** (quantity, fixed price, timing).

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**  
Contract total = 90,000 × $22 = **$1,980,000**  
NRV of inventory under contract = 90,000 × $20.50 = **$1,845,000**  
Estimated loss = $1,980,000 − $1,845,000 = **$135,000**  
(Equivalently: 90,000 × ($22 − $20.50) = 90,000 × $1.50 = **$135,000**)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 135,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 135,000 |
| *Recognize unrealized loss; contract price exceeds NRV at year-end* | | |

**Check:** Dr 135,000 = Cr 135,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) April 8, Year 2 — purchase / settlement**  
Inventory at current NRV = 90,000 × $19.75 = **$1,777,500**  
Remove liability recognized at YE = **$135,000**  
Additional loss since YE = 90,000 × ($20.50 − $19.75) = 90,000 × $0.75 = **$67,500**  
Cash paid = 90,000 × $22 = **$1,980,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 1,777,500 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 135,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 67,500 | |
| Cash | | 1,980,000 |
| *Acquire inventory under commitment; clear liability; further NRV decline* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,777,500 + 135,000 + 67,500 = **1,980,000** = Cr Cash. **Balanced.**

**(d) Commitment measurement schedule**

| Date | Event | Units rem. | Remaining contract $ | NRV of open units $ | Est. liability bal. | Cumulative loss recognized |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nov 12, Y1 | Contract signed | 90,000 | 1,980,000 | 2,115,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | Year-end adj. | 90,000 | 1,980,000 | 1,845,000 | **135,000** | **135,000** |
| Apr 8, Y2 | Before settlement | 90,000 | 1,980,000 | 1,777,500 | 135,000 | 135,000 |
| Apr 8, Y2 | After settlement JE | 0 | 0 | n/a | **0** | **202,500** |

Cumulative loss check: Year 1 $135,000 + Year 2 $67,500 = **$202,500** = contract $1,980,000 − inventory recorded $1,777,500.

**(e) Classification / presentation / disclosure (Year 1)**  
- **Income statement:** Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment **$135,000** in other expenses (or with inventory holding losses).  
- **Balance sheet (Dec 31, Y1):** Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment **$135,000** (typically **current**, given Year 2 delivery).  
- **Notes:** Disclose the material firm purchase commitment (units, price, period) and **net losses recognized on firm purchase commitments** (ASC 330-10-50).

**Key insight:** Signing a firm purchase commitment is usually not recorded. The **first recognition JE is the period-end adjusting entry** when contract price exceeds NRV (or market). At settlement, inventory is recorded at **current** NRV, the liability is cleared, and any **further** decline is an additional loss.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Full cycle with different quantities and prices
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: period-end estimated-loss adjusting JE and settlement of purchase commitment  
**Scenario:**  
**Summitline Paper Products Inc.** uses perpetual inventory and **FIFO** (declines measured vs **NRV**). Year-end December 31. No selling costs; stated unit prices = NRV. Commitment is noncancelable and material; no firm sales contracts protect against declines.

- **October 3, Year 1:** noncancelable contract to purchase **55,000** units at **$40** per unit (**$2,200,000**). NRV on that date: **$41**.  
- **December 31, Year 1:** NRV **$37** per unit.  
- **March 12, Year 2:** purchases all 55,000 units for cash at the contract price; NRV that day **$36** per unit.

**Required:**  
(a) Entries (or state none) on Oct 3 Y1, Dec 31 Y1 (period-end adjusting JE), and Mar 12 Y2.  
(b) What inventory cost is recorded on Mar 12 Y2?  
(c) Total purchase-commitment losses recognized in Year 1 and Year 2.  
(d) One-line measurement schedule at each date (remaining contract $, NRV of open units, est. liability).

**Answer key:**

**(a)**

**October 3, Year 1:** Contract $40 < NRV $41 → **no journal entry** (disclose commitment).

**December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE:**  
Contract = 55,000 × $40 = **$2,200,000**  
NRV = 55,000 × $37 = **$2,035,000**  
Loss = **$165,000** (55,000 × $3)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 165,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 165,000 |
| *Year-end unrealized loss; contract exceeds NRV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 165,000 = Cr 165,000. **Balanced.**

**March 12, Year 2 — settlement:**  
Inventory = 55,000 × $36 = **$1,980,000**  
Liability removed = **$165,000**  
Additional loss = 55,000 × ($37 − $36) = **$55,000**  
Cash = **$2,200,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 1,980,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 165,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 55,000 | |
| Cash | | 2,200,000 |

**Check:** 1,980,000 + 165,000 + 55,000 = 2,200,000. **Balanced.**

**(b)** Inventory recorded at **$1,980,000** (current NRV), not at contract cost.

**(c)** Year 1 loss **$165,000**; Year 2 additional loss **$55,000**; **total $220,000** (= $2,200,000 − $1,980,000).

**(d) Measurement schedule**

| Date | Remaining contract $ | NRV of open units $ | Est. liability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Oct 3, Y1 | 2,200,000 | 2,255,000 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 2,200,000 | 2,035,000 | **165,000** |
| Mar 12, Y2 (after) | 0 | n/a | **0** |

**Key insight:** Same period-end measurement pattern as Q1 with all new numbers: YE loss = remaining contract $ − NRV; at purchase, inventory at then-current NRV and any incremental decline is recognized.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial take, period-end liability on remaining units, recovery not recognized, LIFO market
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE measured only on open units after partial delivery; no reverse for recovery; settlement of residual; cancelable vs protected commitments  
**Scenario:**  
**Granite Peak Bottling LLC** uses a perpetual inventory system and the **LIFO** inventory method (measure purchase-commitment declines against **market**). Year-end is December 31. Noncancelable, material contract; unprotected by firm sales contracts unless stated otherwise.

Events:

1. **August 1, Year 1:** Contract to purchase **120,000** units of aluminum can stock at **$9.00** per unit through March 31, Year 2. Market on Aug 1 = **$9.00**.
2. **October 22, Year 1:** Purchases and pays cash for **40,000** units under the contract when market is **$9.25**.
3. **December 31, Year 1:** Market for remaining units is **$8.40**.
4. **January 31, Year 2 (interim):** Market recovers to **$8.70** (still below contract). Management prepares interim statements.
5. **February 20, Year 2:** Purchases the remaining **80,000** units for cash at **$9.00**; market that day is **$8.15**.

**Required:**  
(a) Journal entry for the **October 22, Year 1** partial purchase.  
(b) Prepare a **remaining-commitment schedule** at Dec 31, Year 1 and record the **period-end adjusting JE**.  
(c) Journal entry, if any, on **January 31, Year 2** for the market recovery.  
(d) Journal entry on **February 20, Year 2** for settlement of the remaining commitment.  
(e) How would the **December 31, Year 1** answer change if (i) the commitment were **cancelable** at will with no penalty, or (ii) Granite Peak held **firm sales contracts** ensuring sale of the future inventory without price concession?  
(f) Roll-forward measurement schedule after each event in (a)–(d).

**Answer key:**

**(a) October 22, Year 1 — partial purchase under contract**  
Units purchased under the firm commitment are recorded at **contract cost**. Market $9.25 > contract $9.00; no loss on the units acquired.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 360,000 | |
| Cash | | 360,000 |
| *40,000 × $9.00 contract price* | | |

**Check:** Dr 360,000 = Cr 360,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Remaining-commitment schedule and Dec 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Original committed units | 120,000 |
| Units purchased in Year 1 | (40,000) |
| **Units remaining under commitment** | **80,000** |
| Remaining contract $ (80,000 × $9.00) | 720,000 |
| Market of remaining units (80,000 × $8.40) | 672,000 |
| **Estimated liability / unrealized loss** | **48,000** |

Equivalently: 80,000 × ($9.00 − $8.40) = 80,000 × $0.60 = **$48,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 48,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 48,000 |
| *Period-end loss on remaining open units only* | | |

**Check:** Dr 48,000 = Cr 48,000. **Balanced.**  
Liability is measured only on **remaining** units, not the full original 120,000.

**(c) January 31, Year 2 — market recovery to $8.70**  
Implied “improved” position vs YE: 80,000 × ($8.70 − $8.40) = **$24,000** reduction in economic exposure.  
Per GAAP / text: **subsequent recovery of an estimated loss is not recognized**.

**No journal entry.** Estimated liability remains **$48,000**.

**(d) February 20, Year 2 — settle remaining 80,000 units**  
Inventory at current market = 80,000 × $8.15 = **$652,000**  
Liability removed = **$48,000**  
Additional loss since YE (from last recognized market $8.40 to $8.15) = 80,000 × $0.25 = **$20,000**  
Cash = 80,000 × $9.00 = **$720,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 652,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 48,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 20,000 | |
| Cash | | 720,000 |

**Check:** 652,000 + 48,000 + 20,000 = 720,000. **Balanced.**

**(e) Cancelable / protected commitments**  
- **(i) Cancelable without material penalty:** not a firm purchase commitment for loss recognition → **no estimated loss or liability** at Dec 31 (may still disclose significant planned purchases if useful).  
- **(ii) Firm sales contracts (or similar) ensure disposition without price concession:** utility of the commitment is not impaired → **no loss recognized** even if market < contract (ASC 330-10-35-18). Disclose as appropriate.

**(f) Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Event | Units rem. | Remaining contract $ | Market of open units $ | Est. liability bal. | Cumulative loss recognized |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aug 1, Y1 | Contract signed | 120,000 | 1,080,000 | 1,080,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Oct 22, Y1 | Partial take 40,000 | 80,000 | 720,000 | 740,000* | 0 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | Year-end adj. | 80,000 | 720,000 | 672,000 | **48,000** | **48,000** |
| Jan 31, Y2 | Recovery (ignored) | 80,000 | 720,000 | 696,000 | **48,000** | **48,000** |
| Feb 20, Y2 | After residual settlement | 0 | 0 | n/a | **0** | **68,000** |

\*Market of remaining 80,000 at Oct 22 = 80,000 × $9.25 = $740,000 (above contract; no liability).  
Cumulative loss check: YE $48,000 + settlement addl $20,000 = **$68,000** (= $720,000 − $652,000 on residual tranche).

**Key insight (period-end emphasis):** After partial delivery, the **period-end adjusting JE** uses only **open** units. Do **not** reverse the liability for interim recoveries. At final settlement, clear the liability and pick up any **further** market decline since the last recognized measurement.

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement choice)
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Measuring estimated liability at period-end on remaining units; when period-end adjusting JE applies  

**Question 1:**  
On May 1, Year 1, **Bayridge Polymer Supply** entered a two-year **noncancelable** contract to purchase **200,000** units of raw materials at **$55** per unit (equal to market on that date). On September 10, Year 1, Bayridge purchased and paid for **50,000** units at **$55**. On December 31, Year 1, market was **$53** per unit. Bayridge uses LIFO. What **estimated liability on purchase commitments**, if any, should Bayridge report at December 31, Year 1?

- A) $0  
- B) $400,000  
- C) $300,000  
- D) $7,950,000  

**Answer:** **C. $300,000.**  
Remaining units = 200,000 − 50,000 = **150,000**.  
Liability = 150,000 × ($55 − $53) = **$300,000**.  
A is wrong because the remaining firm commitment is underwater. B uses 200,000 × $2 and ignores the partial purchase. D is remaining units × full market price, not the per-unit decline.

---

**Question 2:**  
On June 15, Year 1, **Northline Ceramics** signs a **noncancelable** material purchase commitment at a fixed unit price **equal to** then-current NRV. Northline uses FIFO. At December 31, Year 1, NRV has fallen below the contract price and Northline correctly records Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment of **$72,000**. On March 31, Year 2 (interim), NRV partially recovers so economic exposure is only **$40,000**. No units have been purchased yet. Which is correct for March 31, Year 2?

- A) Debit Estimated Liability $32,000; credit Gain (or reverse Loss) $32,000.  
- B) Debit Estimated Liability $72,000; credit Estimated Loss $72,000 to reverse fully.  
- C) **No journal entry**; leave Estimated Liability at $72,000.  
- D) Debit Inventory $32,000; credit Estimated Liability $32,000.  

**Answer:** **C.** Subsequent recovery of an estimated purchase-commitment loss is **not recognized**. The liability remains until settlement (or further decline that increases the recognized loss under the measurement approach used). A/B incorrectly reverse part or all of the YE loss. D invents an inventory debit with no purchase.
