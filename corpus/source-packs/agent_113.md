# Agent 113 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-8

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Explain stockholders’ equity disclosures and key ratios  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Equity disclosures (ASC 505):** shares authorized/issued/outstanding/par; changes in equity categories and share counts; rights and privileges of securities (dividend and liquidation preferences, participation, call prices/dates, conversion rates, sinking funds, unusual voting rights)
- **Preferred-specific disclosures:** liquidation preference (when significantly greater than par) in the equity section; call/redemption amounts; cumulative preferred dividends in arrears (aggregate and per share); redemption requirements for the next five years
- **Restricted / appropriated retained earnings:** legal or contractual restrictions disclosed in notes; formal appropriation reclassifies unappropriated RE to appropriated RE within equity (total equity unchanged)
- **Book value per common share:** common stockholders’ equity ÷ common shares outstanding; common equity = total equity − preferred claims (liquidation preference + cumulative preferred dividends in arrears)
- **Payout ratio:** cash common dividends ÷ net income available to common (NI − preferred dividends / cumulative claim)
- **Return on equity (ROE):** NI available to common ÷ average common stockholders’ equity
- **Price-to-earnings (P/E):** market price per share ÷ earnings per share
- **Initial recognition JE (this LO):** board appropriation of retained earnings
- **Settlement JE:** release of appropriation when restriction purpose is completed
- **Classification / presentation:** appropriated vs unappropriated RE clearly identified within shareholders’ equity; liquidation preference of preferred shown when significantly above par

### Q1 — CORE — Appropriation JE + four equity ratios (two-year comparative)
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for appropriation of retained earnings; multi-year book value per share, payout, ROE, and P/E schedule; preferred claim for common equity; equity disclosure list  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Dynamics Inc.** (calendar-year public company) reports the following stockholders’ equity balances ($ amounts):

| Account | Dec. 31, Year 3 | Dec. 31, Year 2 | Dec. 31, Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5% cumulative preferred stock, $50 par, 8,000 shares outstanding | $400,000 | $400,000 | $400,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—preferred | 40,000 | 40,000 | 40,000 |
| Common stock, $1 par | 120,000 | 120,000 | 100,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital—common | 2,480,000 | 2,480,000 | 1,900,000 |
| Retained earnings (all unappropriated before any Year 3 reclass) | 1,860,000 | 1,420,000 | 1,150,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive loss | (80,000) | (50,000) | (40,000) |
| Treasury stock (at cost) | (150,000) | (150,000) | — |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$4,670,000** | **$4,260,000** | **$3,550,000** |

Additional facts:
- Preferred stock has a **liquidation preference of $55 per share** ($440,000 aggregate). No preferred dividends are in arrears at any year-end (current preferred dividends are paid each year).
- Common shares outstanding: **115,000** at Dec. 31, Year 3 and Year 2 (120,000 issued − 5,000 treasury); **100,000** at Dec. 31, Year 1.
- Net income: Year 3 **$520,000**; Year 2 **$380,000**.
- Annual preferred dividend requirement: 5% × $400,000 = **$20,000**.
- Cash common dividends paid: Year 3 **$104,000**; Year 2 **$76,000**.
- Market price per common share: Year 3 **$48.00**; Year 2 **$36.00**.
- Basic earnings per share: Year 3 **$4.35**; Year 2 **$3.13**.
- On **December 31, Year 3**, the board formally **appropriates $250,000** of retained earnings for a plant-expansion reserve required under a bank loan covenant (reclassification only; no cash movement).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 journal entry** to record the appropriation of retained earnings. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Compute **book value per common share** for Year 3 and Year 2.  
c. Compute the **payout ratio** for Year 3 and Year 2.  
d. Compute **return on equity** for Year 3 and Year 2.  
e. Compute the **price-to-earnings ratio** for Year 3 and Year 2.  
f. Present a **two-year ratio schedule** summarizing (b)–(e).  
g. List **at least five equity disclosures** ASC 505 requires that are relevant to Lakeshore’s capital structure.  
h. Does the appropriation change total stockholders’ equity or the Year 3 book value per share? Explain.

**Answer key:**  

**a. December 31, Year 3 — appropriation of retained earnings (initial recognition of appropriation within equity)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 250,000 | |
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Plant Expansion | | 250,000 |
| *Reclassify RE for bank-covenant plant expansion reserve* | | |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

After reclass (total equity unchanged):  
Unappropriated RE = $1,860,000 − $250,000 = **$1,610,000**  
Appropriated RE = **$250,000**  
Total RE still **$1,860,000**; total SE still **$4,670,000**.

**b. Book value per common share**

Preferred claim (liquidation preference; no arrears) = **$440,000** each year.

| | Year 3 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total stockholders’ equity | $4,670,000 | $4,260,000 |
| Less: preferred liquidation claim | (440,000) | (440,000) |
| **Common stockholders’ equity** | **$4,230,000** | **$3,820,000** |
| Common shares outstanding | 115,000 | 115,000 |
| **Book value per share** | **$36.78** | **$33.22** |

\[
\text{BVPS}_{Y3} = \frac{\$4{,}230{,}000}{115{,}000} = \$36.78 \quad;\quad
\text{BVPS}_{Y2} = \frac{\$3{,}820{,}000}{115{,}000} = \$33.22
\]

**c. Payout ratio**

NI available to common:  
Year 3: $520,000 − $20,000 = **$500,000**  
Year 2: $380,000 − $20,000 = **$360,000**

\[
\text{Payout}_{Y3} = \frac{\$104{,}000}{\$500{,}000} = \mathbf{0.208}\ (20.8\%)
\]
\[
\text{Payout}_{Y2} = \frac{\$76{,}000}{\$360{,}000} = \mathbf{0.211}\ (21.1\%)
\]

**d. Return on equity**

Common equity Year 1 = $3,550,000 − $440,000 = **$3,110,000**

Average common SE Year 3 = ($4,230,000 + $3,820,000) / 2 = **$4,025,000**  
Average common SE Year 2 = ($3,820,000 + $3,110,000) / 2 = **$3,465,000**

\[
\text{ROE}_{Y3} = \frac{\$500{,}000}{\$4{,}025{,}000} = \mathbf{0.124}\ (12.4\%)
\]
\[
\text{ROE}_{Y2} = \frac{\$360{,}000}{\$3{,}465{,}000} = \mathbf{0.104}\ (10.4\%)
\]

**e. Price-to-earnings ratio**

\[
\text{P/E}_{Y3} = \frac{\$48.00}{\$4.35} = \mathbf{11.03}
\]
\[
\text{P/E}_{Y2} = \frac{\$36.00}{\$3.13} = \mathbf{11.50}
\]

**f. Subsequent measurement / ratio schedule (comparative)**

| Ratio | Year 3 | Year 2 | Direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Book value per common share | $36.78 | $33.22 | ↑ ~10.7% |
| Payout ratio | 0.208 | 0.211 | slightly ↓ |
| Return on equity | 0.124 | 0.104 | ↑ |
| Price-to-earnings | 11.03 | 11.50 | slightly ↓ |

**g. Relevant equity disclosures (examples)**  
1. Number of preferred and common shares **authorized, issued, and outstanding**, and par value.  
2. **Rights and privileges** of preferred stock (5% cumulative dividend; $55 liquidation preference).  
3. **Liquidation preference** of preferred ($440,000 aggregate / $55 per share) in the equity section when significantly greater than par.  
4. Aggregate and per-share amounts of **cumulative preferred dividends in arrears** (here: none — still state if material/policy).  
5. Changes in each equity category and in **share counts** during the period (including treasury activity).  
6. Nature and amount of **retained earnings restriction / appropriation** ($250,000 plant expansion under loan covenant) in the notes (and appropriated RE shown separately within equity).  
7. Call/redemption prices and dates, conversion features, or sinking-fund requirements if applicable.  
8. Redemption requirements for capital stock redeemable at fixed or determinable prices/dates for each of the **next five years** (if applicable).

**h. Effect of appropriation**  
Appropriation is a **reclassification within equity**. Total stockholders’ equity and common stockholders’ equity are **unchanged**, so **Year 3 BVPS is unchanged**. Payout, ROE, and P/E are also unaffected (they use NI, dividends, market price, and equity totals — not the unappropriated vs appropriated split).

**Key insight:** Demo 19-8-style analysis pairs **required equity disclosures** with four core ratios. Preferred **liquidation preference** (and any dividends in arrears) must be removed before computing common equity for BVPS and ROE; appropriation of RE is a balanced reclass that does **not** change total equity or the ratios.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin ratio pack + appropriation JE
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: appropriation JE; BVPS, payout, ROE, P/E with preferred claim; comparative schedule  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Outdoor Brands Co.** reports:

| Account | Dec. 31, Year 3 | Dec. 31, Year 2 | Dec. 31, Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 6% cumulative preferred stock, $100 par, 3,000 shares outstanding | $300,000 | $300,000 | $300,000 |
| Common stock, $2 par | 240,000 | 240,000 | 200,000 |
| Additional paid-in capital (common) | 1,760,000 | 1,760,000 | 1,200,000 |
| Retained earnings (unappropriated before Year 3 reclass) | 2,100,000 | 1,650,000 | 1,280,000 |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income (gain) | 25,000 | 15,000 | 10,000 |
| Treasury stock (at cost) | (90,000) | (90,000) | — |
| **Total stockholders’ equity** | **$4,335,000** | **$3,875,000** | **$2,990,000** |

Additional facts:
- Preferred **liquidation preference $110 per share** = **$330,000** aggregate. No preferred dividends in arrears.
- Common shares outstanding: **115,000** (Year 3 and Year 2); **100,000** (Year 1). Issued common Year 3/2 = 120,000 shares; treasury = 5,000 shares.
- Net income: Year 3 **$640,000**; Year 2 **$450,000**.
- Annual preferred dividend: 6% × $300,000 = **$18,000**.
- Cash common dividends: Year 3 **$128,000**; Year 2 **$90,000**.
- Market price: Year 3 **$52.00**; Year 2 **$41.00**.
- Basic EPS: Year 3 **$5.41**; Year 2 **$3.76**.
- On Dec. 31, Year 3, board appropriates **$180,000** of RE for a legal capital restriction under state law related to treasury stock / dividend capacity (formal appropriation).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **appropriation journal entry** on Dec. 31, Year 3.  
b. Compute BVPS, payout ratio, ROE, and P/E for **Year 3 and Year 2**.  
c. Prepare a comparative **ratio schedule**.  
d. After appropriation, report **unappropriated RE**, **appropriated RE**, and **total equity**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Dec. 31, Year 3 — appropriation JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 180,000 | |
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Legal Restriction | | 180,000 |
| *Formal appropriation for legal dividend-capacity restriction* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Ratio computations**

Preferred claim = **$330,000**

| | Year 3 | Year 2 | Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total equity | $4,335,000 | $3,875,000 | $2,990,000 |
| Less preferred liquidation claim | (330,000) | (330,000) | (330,000) |
| **Common stockholders’ equity** | **$4,005,000** | **$3,545,000** | **$2,660,000** |

**Book value per share**  
Y3: $4,005,000 / 115,000 = **$34.83**  
Y2: $3,545,000 / 115,000 = **$30.83**

**NI available to common**  
Y3: $640,000 − $18,000 = **$622,000**  
Y2: $450,000 − $18,000 = **$432,000**

**Payout ratio**  
Y3: $128,000 / $622,000 = **0.206** (20.6%)  
Y2: $90,000 / $432,000 = **0.208** (20.8%)

**Return on equity**  
Avg CSE Y3 = ($4,005,000 + $3,545,000) / 2 = **$3,775,000**  
ROE Y3 = $622,000 / $3,775,000 = **0.165** (16.5%)  

Avg CSE Y2 = ($3,545,000 + $2,660,000) / 2 = **$3,102,500**  
ROE Y2 = $432,000 / $3,102,500 = **0.139** (13.9%)

**Price-to-earnings**  
Y3: $52.00 / $5.41 = **9.61**  
Y2: $41.00 / $3.76 = **10.90**

**c. Comparative ratio schedule**

| Ratio | Year 3 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Book value per common share | $34.83 | $30.83 |
| Payout ratio | 0.206 | 0.208 |
| Return on equity | 0.165 | 0.139 |
| Price-to-earnings | 9.61 | 10.90 |

**d. Post-appropriation equity presentation (Dec. 31, Year 3)**  
Unappropriated RE = $2,100,000 − $180,000 = **$1,920,000**  
Appropriated RE = **$180,000**  
Total RE = **$2,100,000**  
**Total stockholders’ equity remains $4,335,000.**

**Key insight:** Changing every dollar amount does not change the model: strip **preferred claims**, use **NI to common** for payout and ROE, average beginning/ending common equity for ROE, and treat appropriation as a **zero-sum equity reclass**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Dividends in arrears + appropriation settlement cycle + reverse payout
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Period-end appropriation JE; settlement (release) JE when restriction lifts; BVPS with liquidation preference and cumulative preferred dividends in arrears; reverse payout; disclosure classification  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Specialty Chemicals LLC** (calendar year) has the following facts at **December 31, Year 5** (before any appropriation entry for Year 5):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total stockholders’ equity | $3,200,000 |
| 8% cumulative preferred stock, $25 par, 20,000 shares outstanding | 500,000 |
| Preferred **liquidation preference** | $28 per share ($560,000 aggregate) |
| Cumulative preferred **dividends in arrears** (2 full years) | $80,000 |
| Common shares outstanding | 200,000 |
| Net income, Year 5 | $280,000 |
| Preferred dividend requirement for Year 5 (8% × $500,000) | 40,000 |
| Cash common dividends paid, Year 5 | 72,000 |
| Market price per common share, Dec. 31, Year 5 | $18.50 |
| Basic EPS, Year 5 | $1.20 |

Events:
1. **December 31, Year 5:** Board appropriates **$400,000** of retained earnings because a term loan covenant restricts dividends until a plant is completed.  
2. **March 15, Year 6:** Plant is completed and the related loan covenant restriction is released; board **releases the entire $400,000 appropriation**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **common stockholders’ equity** and **book value per common share** at Dec. 31, Year 5 (include preferred arrears in the preferred claim).  
b. Compute what BVPS **would have been** if there were **no** dividends in arrears (liquidation preference only).  
c. Compute Year 5 **payout ratio**, **ROE** (use average common equity of **$2,400,000**), and **P/E**.  
d. Prepare the **Dec. 31, Year 5 appropriation JE** and the **March 15, Year 6 release (settlement) JE**. Both must balance.  
e. A peer company reports a payout ratio of **0.30** and NI available to common of **$240,000**. Compute cash common dividends.  
f. Classify each item as **required equity disclosure**, **ratio analysis metric**, or **neither**:  
   (i) liquidation preference significantly above par;  
   (ii) book value per share;  
   (iii) shares authorized/issued/outstanding;  
   (iv) fair value of PP&E;  
   (v) aggregate preferred dividends in arrears.

**Answer key:**  

**a. BVPS with liquidation preference + arrears**

Preferred claim = liquidation preference + dividends in arrears  
= $560,000 + $80,000 = **$640,000**

Common stockholders’ equity = $3,200,000 − $640,000 = **$2,560,000**

\[
\text{BVPS} = \frac{\$2{,}560{,}000}{200{,}000} = \mathbf{\$12.80}
\]

**b. BVPS without arrears (liquidation only)**

Common equity = $3,200,000 − $560,000 = **$2,640,000**  
BVPS = $2,640,000 / 200,000 = **$13.20**  

Difference: arrears reduce BVPS by **$0.40** per share ($80,000 / 200,000).

**c. Payout, ROE, P/E — Year 5**

NI available to common = $280,000 − $40,000 = **$240,000**  
(Note: the cumulative arrears affect the **balance-sheet claim** for BVPS; the annual preferred **requirement** of $40,000 is still subtracted for income available to common when computing payout/ROE for the period under the chapter model.)

\[
\text{Payout} = \frac{\$72{,}000}{\$240{,}000} = \mathbf{0.30}\ (30\%)
\]
\[
\text{ROE} = \frac{\$240{,}000}{\$2{,}400{,}000} = \mathbf{0.10}\ (10\%)
\]
\[
\text{P/E} = \frac{\$18.50}{\$1.20} = \mathbf{15.42}
\]

**d. Appropriation and settlement journal entries**

*December 31, Year 5 — period-end appropriation:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | 400,000 | |
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Debt Covenant | | 400,000 |
| *Restrict RE under term-loan covenant* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400,000. **Balanced.**

*March 15, Year 6 — release / settlement of appropriation:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Appropriated Retained Earnings—Debt Covenant | 400,000 | |
| Retained Earnings (Unappropriated) | | 400,000 |
| *Release appropriation; plant complete; covenant restriction lifted* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400,000. **Balanced.**  
Neither entry changes total equity; both are pure equity reclassifications.

**e. Reverse payout**

\[
\text{Cash common dividends} = 0.30 \times \$240{,}000 = \mathbf{\$72{,}000}
\]

**f. Classification**

| Item | Classification |
|---|---|
| (i) Liquidation preference significantly above par | **Required equity disclosure** (equity section / notes) |
| (ii) Book value per share | **Ratio analysis metric** (not a required “line item” disclosure formula, but used in analysis) |
| (iii) Shares authorized/issued/outstanding | **Required equity disclosure** |
| (iv) Fair value of PP&E | **Neither** (not an equity disclosure requirement under LO 19-8) |
| (v) Aggregate preferred dividends in arrears | **Required equity disclosure** |

**Key insight:** Cumulative preferred **dividends in arrears** increase the preferred claim and **lower** common BVPS. Appropriation and its later **release** are balanced reverse reclassifications—useful when a restriction is imposed at period-end and “settled” when the covenant purpose is completed.

### Q4 — MC — Preferred claim in book value per share
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Classification of amounts subtracted from total equity to measure common equity for BVPS  
**Question:**  
In computing **book value per common share**, which set of amounts is subtracted from total stockholders’ equity to arrive at common stockholders’ equity when preferred stock is outstanding?

- A) Par value of preferred stock only, even if liquidation preference is substantially higher  
- B) Liquidation preference of preferred stock and cumulative preferred dividends in arrears  
- C) Market value of preferred stock and all future preferred dividends expected over five years  
- D) Additional paid-in capital on common stock and treasury stock at cost  

**Answer:** **B.** Common stockholders’ equity equals total equity less preferred stockholder claims such as **liquidation value** and **dividends in arrears** on cumulative preferred stock. Par alone understates the claim when liquidation preference exceeds par; market value and forecasted future dividends are not the chapter’s preferred claim for BVPS.

### Q5 — MC — Equity disclosure vs ratio
**LO:** LO 19-8  
**Concept:** Classification — required ASC 505 equity disclosure versus equity ratio metric  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is a **required financial statement disclosure** related to stockholders’ equity (as opposed to solely an analytical ratio computed by users)?

- A) Price-to-earnings ratio for each of the last two years  
- B) Return on equity using average common stockholders’ equity  
- C) Aggregate and per-share amounts of cumulative preferred dividends in arrears  
- D) Book value per share rounded to the nearest cent  

**Answer:** **C.** ASC 505 requires disclosure of **aggregate and per-share cumulative preferred dividends in arrears**, rights/privileges, share counts, liquidation preferences (when significantly above par), and related terms. P/E, ROE, and BVPS are **equity analysis ratios** illustrated in Demo 19-8, not the required disclosure items themselves.
