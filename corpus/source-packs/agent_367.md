# Agent 367 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-9

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Apply intraperiod tax allocation  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Intraperiod tax allocation:** assign total income tax expense (benefit) within the same period to continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** December 31 entry that records Income taxes payable, any deferred tax asset/liability, and allocates tax expense/benefit among statement components so **Dr = Cr**
- **Continuing operations:** pretax amount shown **before tax**; separate **Income tax expense** line
- **Discontinued operations (incl. disposal):** pretax gain/loss presented **net of tax**
- **OCI (AFS debt securities):** unrealized holding gains/losses shown **net of tax**; related DTL (gain) or DTA (loss) when amount is not yet in taxable income
- **Tax allocation / subsequent measurement schedule:** pretax × enacted rate → tax (benefit) → net-of-tax by component; ties to payable and deferred tax
- **Initial recognition:** first-time recognition of current tax, deferred tax on OCI temporary difference, and intraperiod splits when no beginning balances
- **Disposal / settlement of discontinued component:** remove net assets, recognize pretax gain or loss in disc. ops, then allocate tax to that component at period end

### Q1 — CORE — Period-end adjusting tax JE with full intraperiod allocation (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting journal entry for income taxes under intraperiod allocation; tax allocation schedule; combined statement of comprehensive income (continuing ops, disc. ops loss, OCI unrealized gain)  
**Scenario:**  
**Mossvale Tools LLC** (calendar year-end) is closing its books for Year 4. Management has already posted all pretax operating and investing entries. The only remaining year-end tax work is the **period-end adjusting entry** for income taxes, using **intraperiod tax allocation**.

Pretax amounts for the year ended December 31, Year 4:

| Item | Pretax amount |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations | $320,000 |
| Loss from discontinued operations (fully tax-deductible in Year 4) | (56,000) |
| Unrealized holding **gain** on available-for-sale debt securities (OCI; not taxable until sold) | 20,000 |

**Facts:**  
- Enacted tax rate for all years: **25%**.  
- Taxable income equals pretax GAAP **net income** (continuing + discontinued).  
- The AFS unrealized gain is **not** in taxable income; it creates a **taxable temporary difference** and a **deferred tax liability**.  
- No permanent differences; no valuation allowance; no beginning deferred tax balances related to these items.  
- Simple structure: no state/foreign taxes or tax credits.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **intraperiod tax allocation schedule** (pretax, tax expense/benefit @ 25%, net of tax) for continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI—AFS.  
b. Compute **income taxes payable** and the **deferred tax liability** on the AFS unrealized gain.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting journal entry** to record income taxes with intraperiod allocation. Prove **Dr = Cr**.  
d. Prepare a **combined statement of comprehensive income** for Year 4 applying intraperiod tax allocation.  
e. In 2–3 sentences, explain what the **period-end adjusting JE** accomplishes relative to presentation of continuing operations vs. disc. ops and OCI.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Intraperiod tax allocation schedule**

| Component | Pretax | Tax rate | Tax expense (benefit) | Net of tax |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing operations | $320,000 | 25% | $80,000 expense | $240,000 |
| Discontinued operations | (56,000) | 25% | (14,000) benefit | (42,000) |
| OCI—unrealized gain on AFS debt securities | 20,000 | 25% | 5,000 expense (to OCI) | 15,000 |

*Checks:* Pretax NI = \(320{,}000 - 56{,}000 = \$264{,}000\). Net tax affecting NI = \(80{,}000 - 14{,}000 = \$66{,}000\). NI = \(264{,}000 - 66{,}000 = \$198{,}000\). Comprehensive income = \(198{,}000 + 15{,}000 = \$213{,}000\).

**b. Payable and deferred tax**

\[
\text{Taxable income} = \$320{,}000 - \$56{,}000 = \$264{,}000
\]
\[
\text{Income taxes payable} = \$264{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$66{,}000}
\]
\[
\text{Deferred tax liability (AFS)} = \$20{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}
\]

**c. December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense—Continuing Operations | 80,000 | |
| Other Comprehensive Income—Tax Effect on AFS Gain | 5,000 | |
| Income Tax Benefit—Discontinued Operations | | 14,000 |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 66,000 |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 5,000 |
| *Period-end adjusting entry: current tax, DTL on AFS, intraperiod allocation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = \(80{,}000 + 5{,}000 = \mathbf{85{,}000}\)  
Cr = \(14{,}000 + 66{,}000 + 5{,}000 = \mathbf{85{,}000}\). **Balanced.**

*Acceptable alternatives:* (1) credit **OCI** directly net of tax and still recognize DTL $5,000; (2) show disc. ops tax only through net-of-tax presentation on the face of the income statement while still allocating $14,000 of tax benefit in the JE.

**d. Combined statement of comprehensive income**

**Mossvale Tools LLC**  
**Statement of Comprehensive Income**  
**For the Year Ended December 31, Year 4**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations before income tax | $320,000 |
| Income tax expense | 80,000 |
| Income from continuing operations | 240,000 |
| Discontinued operations | |
| Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax benefit of $14,000 | (42,000) |
| **Net income** | **$198,000** |
| Other comprehensive income | |
| Unrealized holding gain on AFS debt securities, net of tax of $5,000 | 15,000 |
| **Comprehensive income** | **$213,000** |

**e. Role of the period-end adjusting JE**  
The adjusting entry does **not** change total tax for the period; it **assigns** the $66,000 current tax (and $5,000 deferred tax) so that continuing operations reports a **gross** income tax expense line of $80,000, discontinued operations reports a **net-of-tax** loss of $(42,000), and OCI reports the unrealized AFS gain **net of tax** of $15,000. Without this JE, payable, DTL, and the intraperiod presentation would be incomplete.

**Key insight:** Period-end intraperiod allocation is a **presentation and measurement assignment** step—same total tax, different lines—so users can see after-tax disc. ops and OCI while still seeing tax expense on continuing operations as a separate line.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Period-end JE with disc. ops gain and OCI unrealized loss
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: period-end adjusting tax JE and SCI when discontinued operations has a pretax **gain** and OCI has an unrealized **loss** (DTA)  
**Scenario:**  
**Thornridge Fabricators Inc.** reports the following pretax amounts for the year ended December 31, Year 6:

| Item | Pretax amount |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations | $550,000 |
| Gain from discontinued operations (taxable in Year 6) | 80,000 |
| Unrealized holding **loss** on available-for-sale debt securities (OCI; not deductible for tax until realized) | (32,000) |

Enacted tax rate: **25%**. Taxable income equals pretax GAAP net income (continuing + discontinued). The AFS unrealized loss creates a **deductible temporary difference** and a **deferred tax asset**. No valuation allowance. No other temporary or permanent differences. Beginning deferred taxes related to AFS: zero.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **tax allocation schedule** (pretax, tax, net of tax) for continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI.  
b. Compute **income taxes payable** and the **deferred tax asset** related to AFS.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 6 period-end adjusting journal entry** to record income taxes with intraperiod allocation. Prove Dr = Cr.  
d. Prepare the **combined statement of comprehensive income**.  
e. Compute **comprehensive income** and prove it equals NI + net OCI.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Tax allocation schedule**

| Component | Pretax | Tax (25%) | Net of tax |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing operations | $550,000 | $137,500 expense | $412,500 |
| Discontinued operations | 80,000 | 20,000 expense | 60,000 |
| OCI—unrealized loss on AFS | (32,000) | (8,000) benefit | (24,000) |

**b. Payable and DTA**

\[
\text{Taxable income} = \$550{,}000 + \$80{,}000 = \$630{,}000
\]
\[
\text{Income taxes payable} = \$630{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$157{,}500}
\]
\[
\text{Deferred tax asset (AFS)} = \$32{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$8{,}000}
\]

**c. December 31, Year 6 — period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense—Continuing Operations | 137,500 | |
| Income Tax Expense—Discontinued Operations | 20,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 8,000 | |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 157,500 |
| Other Comprehensive Income—Tax Benefit on AFS Loss | | 8,000 |
| *Period-end: current tax on TI; DTA and tax benefit to OCI; tax allocated to disc. ops gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr = \(137{,}500 + 20{,}000 + 8{,}000 = \mathbf{165{,}500}\)  
Cr = \(157{,}500 + 8{,}000 = \mathbf{165{,}500}\). **Balanced.**

**d. Combined statement of comprehensive income**

**Thornridge Fabricators Inc.**  
**Statement of Comprehensive Income**  
**For the Year Ended December 31, Year 6**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations before income tax | $550,000 |
| Income tax expense | 137,500 |
| Income from continuing operations | 412,500 |
| Discontinued operations | |
| Gain from discontinued operations, net of tax of $20,000 | 60,000 |
| **Net income** | **$472,500** |
| Other comprehensive income (loss) | |
| Unrealized holding loss on AFS debt securities, net of tax benefit of $8,000 | (24,000) |
| **Comprehensive income** | **$448,500** |

**e. Comprehensive income proof**  
Net income \(472{,}500\) + OCI (loss) \((24{,}000)\) = **$448,500**.

**Key insight:** A disc. ops **gain** is reduced by allocated **tax expense**; an OCI **unrealized loss** is reduced (made less negative) by a **tax benefit** and typically a DTA—still intraperiod allocation, opposite signs from Q1.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Disposal of discontinued component + permanent & temporary differences; period-end tax JE
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Disposal (settlement) journal entry for a discontinued component; intraperiod tax allocation when continuing operations includes a permanent difference and an originating taxable temporary difference; period-end tax adjusting JE and income statement  
**Scenario:**  
**QuarryBend Foods Co.** provides the following information for the year ended December 31, Year 3:

- Revenues $900,000; cost of goods sold $400,000; operating expenses $150,000.  
- Operating expenses include **regulatory fines of $3,000** that are **never tax-deductible** (permanent difference).  
- One originating timing difference produces a **future taxable amount of $36,000** during Year 3 (book income currently higher than taxable income by $36,000 for this item).  
- On **October 1, Year 3**, QuarryBend **sold** a component that meets the discontinued-operations criteria. Cash proceeds were **$156,000**. The component’s net assets (carrying amount) removed at disposal totaled **$180,000**. The **$24,000 pretax loss on disposal** is **fully tax-deductible** in Year 3 and is **not** included in the revenue/expense amounts above. No other disc. ops results.  
- Enacted tax rate for all years: **25%**. No beginning deferred tax balances. No valuation allowance issues.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **disposal journal entry** on October 1, Year 3 for the sale of the discontinued component (ignore tax in this entry).  
b. Compute **pretax income from continuing operations**.  
c. Compute **taxable income** and **income taxes payable**.  
d. Compute the **deferred tax liability** originating in Year 3 and the **intraperiod split** of tax between continuing operations and discontinued operations.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting journal entry** to record income taxes (intraperiod allocation). Prove Dr = Cr.  
f. Prepare an **income statement** (through net income) using intraperiod tax allocation.  
g. Briefly explain how tax related to the **disposal loss** is presented.

**Answer key:**  

**a. October 1, Year 3 — disposal (settlement) JE (pretax)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 156,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Discontinued Component | 24,000 | |
| Net Assets of Discontinued Component (various BS accounts, net) | | 180,000 |
| *Sale of discontinued component; pretax loss $24,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr = \(156{,}000 + 24{,}000 = 180{,}000\) = Cr. **Balanced.**

**b. Pretax income from continuing operations**

\[
\$900{,}000 - \$400{,}000 - \$150{,}000 = \mathbf{\$350{,}000}
\]

**c. Taxable income and payable**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Pretax income from continuing operations | $350,000 |
| Add: nondeductible fines (permanent) | 3,000 |
| Less: originating future taxable amount (temporary) | (36,000) |
| Less: loss from discontinued operations (deductible) | (24,000) |
| **Taxable income** | **$293,000** |

\[
\text{Income taxes payable} = \$293{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$73{,}250}
\]

**d. Deferred tax and intraperiod allocation**

\[
\text{DTL} = \$36{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}
\]

*Tax expense—continuing operations:*  
Permanent-adjusted pretax base for continuing = \(350{,}000 + 3{,}000 = \$353{,}000\).  
Tax expense—continuing = \(353{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$88{,}250}\).

*Verify current vs. deferred for continuing:*  
Taxable income from continuing only (before disc. ops) = \(350{,}000 + 3{,}000 - 36{,}000 = \$317{,}000\).  
Current tax—continuing = \(317{,}000 \times 25\% = \$79{,}250\).  
Deferred tax—continuing = **$9,000**.  
Tax expense—continuing = \(79{,}250 + 9{,}000 = \$88{,}250\) ✓.

Tax benefit—discontinued operations (disposal loss) = \(24{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).

*Roll-forward check:*  
Tax expense net of benefit = \(88{,}250 - 6{,}000 = \$82{,}250\).  
Payable + DTL increase = \(73{,}250 + 9{,}000 = \$82{,}250\) ✓.

**e. December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense—Continuing Operations | 88,250 | |
| Income Tax Benefit—Discontinued Operations | | 6,000 |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 73,250 |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 9,000 |
| *Intraperiod allocation: permanent + temporary in continuing; tax benefit to disc. ops disposal loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **88,250**  
Cr = \(6{,}000 + 73{,}250 + 9{,}000 = \mathbf{88{,}250}\). **Balanced.**

**f. Income statement (intraperiod allocation)**

**QuarryBend Foods Co.**  
**Income Statement**  
**For the Year Ended December 31, Year 3**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Revenues | $900,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | 400,000 |
| Gross margin | 500,000 |
| Operating expenses | 150,000 |
| Income from continuing operations before income tax | 350,000 |
| Income tax expense | 88,250 |
| Income from continuing operations | 261,750 |
| Discontinued operations | |
| Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax benefit of $6,000 | (18,000) |
| **Net income** | **$243,750** |

*Check:* Pretax total affecting NI = \(350{,}000 - 24{,}000 = 326{,}000\); after-tax NI = \(326{,}000 - 82{,}250 = \mathbf{243{,}750}\).  
Also: \(261{,}750 - 18{,}000 = 243{,}750\).

**g. Disposal / disc. ops tax presentation**  
The **$24,000 pretax loss on disposal** of the discontinued component is reported in the discontinued operations section **net of its $6,000 tax benefit** as **$(18,000)**. That tax benefit is **not** part of the continuing-operations income tax expense line of $88,250.

**Key insight:** Measure permanent differences and deferred taxes for **continuing operations** first; then allocate the tax (benefit) of the disposal loss so discontinued operations is always **net of tax**—the period-end adjusting JE is where that assignment is recorded.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of intraperiod tax components
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — which financial-statement components receive intraperiod tax allocation  
**Question:**  
Under U.S. GAAP, intraperiod tax allocation requires that income tax expense (benefit) be allocated among which set of items?

- A) Only income from continuing operations; discontinued operations and OCI are always shown pretax  
- B) Income (loss) from continuing operations, income (loss) from discontinued operations, and items of other comprehensive income  
- C) Only deferred tax assets and deferred tax liabilities on the balance sheet  
- D) Only permanent differences and temporary differences within pretax accounting income  

**Answer:** **B.** Intraperiod tax allocation assigns tax expense (benefit) to continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI so that disc. ops and OCI are reported **net of tax**, while continuing operations presents pretax amount and a separate income tax expense line. (A) reverses the presentation rules. (C) concerns balance-sheet classification of deferred taxes, not intraperiod allocation. (D) concerns measurement of total tax, not allocation among statement components.

### Q5 — MC — Measure net-of-tax discontinued operations and net income
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Compute income tax allocated to continuing operations and discontinued operations, and net income under intraperiod allocation  
**Question:**  
**Larkspur Ridge Co.** reports pretax income before discontinued operations of $300,000 and a pretax **gain** from discontinued operations of $20,000. The enacted tax rate is 25%. Taxable income equals pretax GAAP net income; there are no temporary differences. How much income tax should be allocated to **continuing operations** and to **discontinued operations**, and what is **net income**?

- A) Tax—continuing $75,000; tax—disc. ops $5,000; net income $240,000  
- B) Tax—continuing $80,000; tax—disc. ops $0; net income $240,000  
- C) Tax—continuing $75,000; tax—disc. ops $5,000; net income $245,000  
- D) Tax—continuing $75,000; tax—disc. ops $5,000; net income $320,000  

**Answer:** **A.**  
Tax on continuing = \(300{,}000 \times 25\% = \$75{,}000\) → income from continuing operations = **$225,000**.  
Tax on disc. ops gain = \(20{,}000 \times 25\% = \$5{,}000\) → disc. ops net of tax = **$15,000**.  
Net income = \(225{,}000 + 15{,}000 = \$240{,}000\).  
(Also: pretax NI \(320{,}000\) − total tax payable \(80{,}000\) = \(240{,}000\).)  
(B) fails to allocate tax to disc. ops. (C) misstates NI as $245,000. (D) reports pretax NI as if no tax were deducted.
