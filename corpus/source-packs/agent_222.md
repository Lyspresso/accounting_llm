# Agent 222 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-6

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Account for impairment of indefinite life intangible assets and goodwill  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize purchased indefinite-life intangible at cost; record goodwill as residual of business-combination purchase price over FV of identifiable net assets
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year carrying-amount roll-forwards for indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill — **zero amortization** (public company), impairment write-downs only, ending CA becomes next period’s beginning CA
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** annual (or interim if indicators) quantitative impairment tests — indefinite-life asset: CA vs FV; goodwill: RU CA (incl. GW) vs RU FV, loss **capped** at allocated goodwill
- **Disposal / derecognition JE:** remove remaining post-impairment CA of indefinite-life intangible; gain/loss = proceeds − CA (income from continuing operations)
- Optional qualitative assessment: more-likely-than-not screen; management may **bypass** and go straight to quantitative
- No subsequent recovery of prior impairment under US GAAP; goodwill separate BS line
- Contrast (process only): finite-life recoverability test (LO 13-3) does **not** apply to indefinite-life assets or goodwill under LO 13-6

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-year subsequent measurement schedules; trade name + RU goodwill; period-end impairments; disposal
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedules for indefinite-life trade name and reporting-unit goodwill (zero amortization; impairment-only write-downs); initial recognition; period-end quantitative impairment JEs; disposal after impairment  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverbrook Specialty Foods Inc.** is a **public** calendar-year company. It does **not** elect the private-company goodwill amortization alternative. Management **bypasses** qualitative assessments and proceeds directly to **quantitative** tests at each December 31 test date. No impairments were recorded before Year 1.

**January 2, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Transaction | Facts |
|---|---|
| Purchase of trade name **“Vesper”** | Paid **$360,000 cash**. Management concludes the trade name has an **indefinite** useful life (renewable; no foreseeable limit on cash-flow contribution). **Not amortized.** |
| Acquisition of **Lakeside ColdPack Division** (a reporting unit) | Paid **$12,000,000 cash**. Fair value of **identifiable net assets** acquired = **$10,500,000**. Residual = goodwill allocated entirely to the Lakeside RU. |

**Quantitative impairment data (period-end)**

| Date | Vesper trade name | Lakeside RU |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | CA **$360,000**; FV **$365,000** | CA of RU **including goodwill** **$12,400,000**; FV of RU **$12,800,000**. Allocated goodwill still **$1,500,000**. Other (non-goodwill) long-lived assets of the RU already tested under ASC 360; **no** further write-downs required before the goodwill test. |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | CA **$360,000** (pre-test); FV **$240,000** | CA of RU **including goodwill** **$13,000,000**; FV of RU **$12,200,000**. Allocated goodwill **$1,500,000** (pre-test). Other assets already tested; no additional ASC 360 losses. |
| Mar 20, Year 3 | Silverbrook **sells** the Vesper trade name for **$255,000 cash**. Selling costs negligible. No Year-3 impairment test is performed before the sale. | — |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | (Disposed) | CA of RU **including goodwill** **$12,900,000**; FV of RU **$12,100,000**. Allocated goodwill after Year-2 write-down = **$700,000** (pre-test). Other assets already tested. |

Assume no other activity affects the carrying amounts of Vesper or Lakeside goodwill except the impairments and disposal described.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 2, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entries for (i) the Vesper trade name and (ii) the Lakeside acquisition (use a single net “Identifiable Net Assets” control account for the acquiree’s net assets at FV). Compute goodwill.  
b. For **each** of Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3, compute any **period-end impairment loss** on (i) Vesper and (ii) Lakeside goodwill. Prepare the **December 31 period-end adjusting JE(s)** (or state none). Explicitly apply the **goodwill cap** when relevant.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent-measurement carrying-amount schedule** for (i) Vesper and (ii) Lakeside goodwill from **January 2, Year 1 through December 31, Year 3** (show amortization, impairment, disposal, and ending CA each year). Prove roll-forwards.  
d. Prepare the **March 20, Year 3 disposal** journal entry for Vesper.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** (i) Where are impairment losses reported? (ii) How is aggregate goodwill presented on the balance sheet after Year-3 testing? (iii) May Silverbrook reverse the Year-2 goodwill impairment in Year 3 if the RU recovers?

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition — January 2, Year 1**

Goodwill = Purchase price − FV of identifiable net assets  
\[
\$12{,}000{,}000 - \$10{,}500{,}000 = \$1{,}500{,}000
\]

*Jan 2, Y1 — Purchase indefinite-life trade name*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trade Name—Vesper | 360,000 | |
| Cash | | 360,000 |
| *Capitalize purchased indefinite-life intangible at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 360,000. **Balanced.**

*Jan 2, Y1 — Acquire Lakeside ColdPack Division*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Identifiable Net Assets (at FV) | 10,500,000 | |
| Goodwill—Lakeside RU | 1,500,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000,000 |
| *Residual purchase price = goodwill; not amortized (public company)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,500,000 + 1,500,000 = Cr 12,000,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Period-end quantitative tests and adjusting JEs**

**December 31, Year 1**

*Vesper:* FV **$365,000** ≥ CA **$360,000** → **not impaired**. **No entry.**

*Lakeside goodwill:* RU FV **$12,800,000** ≥ RU CA **$12,400,000** → goodwill **not impaired**. **No entry.**

**December 31, Year 2**

*Vesper:*  
\[
\text{Impairment} = \$360{,}000 - \$240{,}000 = \$120{,}000
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Trade Name (Vesper) | 120,000 | |
| Trade Name—Vesper | | 120,000 |
| *Indefinite-life quantitative test: write down to FV; no recoverability step* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 120,000. **Balanced.** New CA = **$240,000**.

*Lakeside goodwill:*  
\[
\text{RU excess of CA over FV} = \$13{,}000{,}000 - \$12{,}200{,}000 = \$800{,}000
\]  
Allocated goodwill = **$1,500,000**  
Impairment loss = **min(\$800,000, \$1,500,000) = \$800,000**  
Remaining goodwill = 1,500,000 − 800,000 = **$700,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Goodwill | 800,000 | |
| Goodwill—Lakeside RU | | 800,000 |
| *Loss limited to RU excess; does not exceed allocated goodwill* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 800,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3**

*Vesper:* disposed March 20 — **no** Dec 31 impairment test on Vesper.

*Lakeside goodwill:*  
\[
\text{RU excess} = \$12{,}900{,}000 - \$12{,}100{,}000 = \$800{,}000
\]  
Allocated goodwill (pre-test) = **$700,000**  
Impairment loss = **min(\$800,000, \$700,000) = \$700,000** (**cap binds**)  
Remaining goodwill = **$0**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Goodwill | 700,000 | |
| Goodwill—Lakeside RU | | 700,000 |
| *Cap: write goodwill to zero; leftover RU gap is not allocated to other assets via the GW test* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 700,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Subsequent-measurement carrying-amount schedules (emphasis)**

**Vesper trade name**

| Period | Beg. CA | Amortization | Impairment | Disposal | End. CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 (from 1/2) | 360,000 | 0 | 0 | — | **360,000** |
| Year 2 | 360,000 | 0 | 120,000 | — | **240,000** |
| Year 3 (to 3/20) | 240,000 | 0 | 0 | 240,000 | **0** |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
360,000 − 0 = 360,000; 360,000 − 120,000 = 240,000; 240,000 − 240,000 (disposal) = 0.

**Lakeside goodwill**

| Period | Beg. CA | Amortization | Impairment | End. CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 (from 1/2) | 1,500,000 | 0 | 0 | **1,500,000** |
| Year 2 | 1,500,000 | 0 | 800,000 | **700,000** |
| Year 3 | 700,000 | 0 | 700,000 | **0** |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
1,500,000 − 0 = 1,500,000; 1,500,000 − 800,000 = 700,000; 700,000 − 700,000 = 0.  
**Public company:** goodwill and other indefinite-life intangibles are **not amortized** — subsequent measurement is impairment testing only.

**(d) Disposal of Vesper — March 20, Year 3**

CA at disposal = **$240,000** (post Year-2 impairment basis)  
Cash proceeds = **$255,000**  
Gain on disposal = 255,000 − 240,000 = **$15,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 255,000 | |
| Trade Name—Vesper | | 240,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Trade Name | | 15,000 |
| *Derecognize post-impairment CA; gain in continuing operations* | | |

**Check:** Dr 255,000 = Cr 240,000 + 15,000. **Balanced.**

**(e) Classification / presentation**  
(i) Impairment losses (Y2: **$120,000** trade name + **$800,000** goodwill; Y3: **$700,000** goodwill) are recognized in **net income** as part of **income from continuing operations** (often a separate impairment line when material).  
(ii) After Year-3 testing, Lakeside goodwill is **$0**. Aggregate goodwill on the balance sheet equals goodwill of any **other** reporting units only (Lakeside contributes nothing). Goodwill is still presented as a **separate line item** if other RUs have remaining goodwill.  
(iii) **No.** Subsequent **reversal** of a previously recognized goodwill (or indefinite-life intangible) impairment loss is **prohibited** under US GAAP.

**Key insight:** The core subsequent-measurement model for indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill (public company) is a **carrying-amount schedule with zero amortization**. Each year the only downward adjustments are **period-end impairment write-downs** measured by FV vs CA (asset-level for other indefinite-life intangibles; **RU-level**, **capped at allocated goodwill**, for goodwill). Disposal uses the **post-impairment** basis.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin multi-year schedule path
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — multi-year subsequent measurement schedules for indefinite-life trademark and RU goodwill; initial recognition; period-end impairment JEs; disposal with loss

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end only: cap binds, no-impairment indefinite-life asset, qualitative path
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting impairment tests — goodwill loss **capped** at allocated goodwill; indefinite-life asset with FV ≥ CA (no loss); qualitative assessment avoids quantitative test; brief presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinnacle Broadcast Holdings Inc.** is a **public** calendar-year company. At **December 31, Year 2**, it performs **period-end** impairment analysis for the items below. Other long-lived assets of each RU have already been tested under ASC 360 with no further write-downs required before goodwill testing.

| Item | Facts at Dec 31, Year 2 |
|---|---|
| Indefinite-life **broadcast license** (not amortized) | Carrying amount **$175,000**. Fair value **$190,000**. Management elects to **bypass** qualitative assessment and perform the quantitative test. |
| Indefinite-life **trade name “SignalBay”** | Carrying amount **$420,000**. Management performs a **qualitative** assessment and concludes it is **not** more likely than not that SignalBay is impaired (no adverse indicators; strong brand metrics). |
| **Streaming** reporting unit | CA of RU **including goodwill** **$15,600,000**. Goodwill allocated to RU **$950,000**. Fair value of RU **$14,200,000**. Management bypasses qualitative assessment. |
| **Linear TV** reporting unit | CA of RU **including goodwill** **$22,000,000**. Goodwill allocated to RU **$2,000,000**. Fair value of RU **$23,500,000**. Management bypasses qualitative assessment. |

**Required:**  
a. For the **broadcast license**, determine the period-end impairment loss (if any) and prepare the JE or state none.  
b. For **SignalBay**, what further quantitative testing is required at Dec 31, Year 2? What is the carrying amount after testing?  
c. Compute and record the **Streaming** goodwill impairment (show cap). State remaining Streaming goodwill.  
d. Determine whether **Linear TV** goodwill is impaired; prepare JE or state none.  
e. Prepare a **one-line subsequent-measurement summary schedule** at Dec 31, Year 2 for: broadcast license, SignalBay, Streaming goodwill, Linear TV goodwill (beg. CA = pre-test amounts; show amort., impairment, end. CA).  
f. **Classification:** Where is any Year-2 impairment loss reported? If Streaming’s fair value fully recovers in Year 3, may Pinnacle reverse the Year-2 goodwill write-down?

**Answer key:**

**(a) Broadcast license — quantitative; no impairment**

FV **$190,000** > CA **$175,000** → **not impaired**.  
**No journal entry.** Ending CA remains **$175,000**.

**(b) SignalBay — qualitative pass**

Because management concludes it is **not** more likely than not that the asset is impaired, **no quantitative test is required**.  
Carrying amount remains **$420,000**. **No entry.**

**(c) Streaming goodwill — cap binds**

\[
\text{RU excess} = \$15{,}600{,}000 - \$14{,}200{,}000 = \$1{,}400{,}000
\]  
Allocated GW = **$950,000**  
Impairment = min(1,400,000, 950,000) = **$950,000**  
Remaining Streaming goodwill = **$0**.

*Dec 31, Year 2 — Period-end goodwill impairment (capped)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Impairment Loss—Goodwill | 950,000 | |
| Goodwill—Streaming RU | | 950,000 |
| *Loss limited to allocated goodwill; RU gap beyond GW is not written off under the GW test* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 950,000. **Balanced.**

**(d) Linear TV goodwill — no impairment**

RU FV **$23,500,000** > RU CA **$22,000,000** → **not impaired**.  
**No journal entry.** Remaining Linear TV goodwill = **$2,000,000**.

**(e) Subsequent-measurement summary schedule — Dec 31, Year 2**

| Asset | Beg. CA (pre-test) | Amort. | Impairment | End. CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Broadcast license | 175,000 | 0 | 0 | **175,000** |
| Trade name—SignalBay | 420,000 | 0 | 0 | **420,000** |
| Goodwill—Streaming | 950,000 | 0 | 950,000 | **0** |
| Goodwill—Linear TV | 2,000,000 | 0 | 0 | **2,000,000** |

**Roll-forwards:** 175,000; 420,000; 950,000 − 950,000 = 0; 2,000,000.  
**Total Year-2 impairment loss** = **$950,000** (goodwill only).

**(f) Classification**  
Impairment loss of **$950,000** is reported in **income from continuing operations**.  
**No** — US GAAP **prohibits** reversing a previously recognized goodwill impairment loss if the RU later recovers.

**Key insight:** Period-end LO 13-6 testing can yield **four different outcomes** in one close: (1) quantitative test with FV ≥ CA → no loss; (2) qualitative pass → no quantitative work; (3) RU gap **larger than** allocated goodwill → write GW to **zero** (cap); (4) RU FV ≥ CA → no GW loss. The subsequent-measurement schedule still shows **Amort. = 0** throughout.

### Q4 — MC — Cap on goodwill impairment (classification of measured loss)
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Quantitative goodwill impairment measurement and cap at allocated goodwill  
**Question:**  
At year-end, a public company’s **Outdoor Gear** reporting unit has a carrying amount **including goodwill of $48 million**, of which **goodwill is $2.2 million**. Fair value of the RU is **$45.1 million**. Required impairment tests of other long-lived assets in the RU are complete. The goodwill impairment loss is:

- A) **$2.9 million** (full excess of RU CA over RU FV).  
- B) **$2.2 million** (excess limited to goodwill allocated to the RU).  
- C) **$0**, because a qualitative assessment was not performed.  
- D) **$2.9 million**, with the remaining **$0.7 million** automatically reducing other identifiable assets of the RU under the goodwill test.

**Answer:** **B.**  
RU excess = \(48.0 - 45.1 = \$2.9\) million, but goodwill impairment is **limited to the \$2.2 million** of goodwill allocated to the RU. Qualitative assessment is **optional** (may be bypassed). The goodwill test does **not** push the leftover gap onto other assets.

### Q5 — MC — Subsequent measurement of indefinite-life intangible (vs finite-life recoverability)
**LO:** LO 13-6  
**Concept:** Quantitative impairment model for indefinite-life intangible assets other than goodwill (no undiscounted recoverability step)  
**Question:**  
A renewable **franchise right** is accounted for as an **indefinite-life** intangible (not amortized). At the annual test date, management determines it is more likely than not that the franchise is impaired and proceeds to the quantitative test. Carrying amount **$280,000**; fair value **$195,000**; expected **undiscounted** future net cash flows **$310,000**. Impairment loss, if any, is:

- A) **$0**, because undiscounted cash flows exceed carrying amount.  
- B) **$85,000** (\(280{,}000 - 195{,}000\)).  
- C) **$280,000** (full write-off whenever qualitative factors fail).  
- D) **$115,000** (\(310{,}000 - 195{,}000\)).

**Answer:** **B.**  
Indefinite-life intangibles use a **fair-value versus carrying-amount** quantitative test. The **undiscounted recoverability test applies to finite-life** (amortized) intangibles under LO 13-3 / ASC 360, **not** to indefinite-life assets under LO 13-6. Loss = CA − FV = **$85,000**. New subsequent-measurement basis = **$195,000**.
