# Agent 134 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-2

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase of trading (TS) debt at amortized cost (par); accrued interest when bought between interest dates
- **Interest in NI:** cash interest, interest receivable reverse, interest revenue in net income (all debt categories)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortized cost vs fair value; required FVA balance; existing FVA; adjusting amount (required − existing)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** ↔ Fair Value Adjustment—TS (FV-NI)
- **Disposal / sale JE:** sale-date FV-NI adjust first; then remove investment at amortized cost, eliminate FVA; **no separate realized G/L** on sale once FV-adjusted
- **Holding-period income rollforward:** period unrealized gains/losses in NI sum to (sale proceeds − amortized cost) over ownership
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** TS typically **current assets**; unrealized holding G/L and interest in **net income** (other income); FVA valuation account; fair value option election for non-TS debt is accounting-similar to TS
- **Number-variant twin:** same FV-NI path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — TS full life cycle (purchase between interest dates → interest → YE FVA schedule → multi-year FVA → sale-date FV-NI → sale)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE; subsequent_measurement_schedule; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **November 1 of Year 1**, **Ridgewater Advisors Inc.** purchased at **par $60,000** of **SierraTech 9%** bonds (dated **July 1 of Year 1**) that mature on June 30 of Year 7. Cash interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Ridgewater classifies the investment as **trading securities (TS)** and measures it under **FV-NI**. Ridgewater’s accounting period ends on December 31. No other TS activity occurs except as stated. Beginning FVA—TS is zero.

**Fair values / activity:**
| Date | Event / fair value |
|---|---|
| Nov 1, Year 1 | Purchase at par (plus accrued interest) |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value of SierraTech bonds = **$57,600** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Still held; fair value = **$61,500** (interest continues each June 30 / Dec 31) |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Entire position sold for **$62,000** (clean price; no accrued interest on Jan 1 interest date) |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **purchase** on November 1 of Year 1 (include accrued interest).  
(b) Record receipt of **cash interest** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Prepare the **subsequent measurement (FVA) schedule** and record the **period-end fair value adjustment** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(d) Report the investment on the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet** and the effects on the **Year 1 income statement** (interest and unrealized holding G/L only).  
(e) Assume the bonds are still held all of Year 2. Prepare the **FVA rollforward schedule** and adjusting entry on December 31 of Year 2 (fair value $61,500). (You may omit Year 2 interest entries for this part.)  
(f) On January 1 of Year 3, sell the entire position for $62,000: **(i)** adjust to fair value at the sale date using **FV-NI**; **(ii)** record the sale, eliminating the associated Fair Value Adjustment balance.  
(g) Prepare a **holding-period income schedule** showing Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 unrealized G/L in net income and prove the cumulative amount equals sale proceeds minus amortized cost.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) November 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (purchase between interest dates)**  

Accrued interest from July 1 to Nov 1 = 4 months:  
\( \$60{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 4/12 = \mathbf{\$1{,}800} \)  
Cash paid = $60,000 + $1,800 = **$61,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—SierraTech Bonds | 60,000 | |
| Interest Receivable | 1,800 | |
| Cash | | 61,800 |
| *Purchase TS at par plus 4 months accrued interest* | | |

**Check:** Dr 60,000 + 1,800 = Cr 61,800. **Balanced.** Amortized cost basis of the bond investment = **$60,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Cash interest (6 months received; 2 months earned)**  

Full coupon received: \( \$60{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$2{,}700} \)  
Interest revenue earned Nov 1–Dec 31: \( \$60{,}000 \times 0.09 \times 2/12 = \mathbf{\$900} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,700 | |
| Interest Receivable | | 1,800 |
| Interest Revenue | | 900 |
| *Receive semiannual coupon; reverse prepaid accrued interest; recognize 2 months revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,700 = Cr 1,800 + 900. **Balanced.** Interest revenue is recognized in **net income**.

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Subsequent measurement schedule and period-end FV-NI adjust**  

**Subsequent measurement schedule (Year 1 YE)**

| TS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SierraTech bonds | $60,000 | $57,600 | $(2,400)$ |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$(2,400)$** |

Required FVA = FV − AC = $57,600 − $60,000 = **$2,400 credit**  
Existing FVA = **$0**  
Adjustment = **credit FVA $2,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 2,400 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 2,400 |
| *Adjust TS to fair value through net income (FV-NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,400. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount on BS = amortized cost $60,000 − FVA credit $2,400 = **$57,600**.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**  

**Balance sheet excerpt — December 31, Year 1**  
Current assets  
 Investment in trading securities ……………… **$57,600**  
(presentation = amortized cost $60,000 net of FVA credit $2,400; TS typically current)

**Income statement excerpt — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
Other revenues (expenses)  
 Interest revenue ……………………………… **$900**  
 Unrealized holding loss on trading securities … **$(2,400)**

**(e) December 31, Year 2 — FVA rollforward (subsequent measurement schedule)**  

| | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) / Required FVA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SierraTech bonds | 60,000 | 61,500 | 1,500 (required **debit** FVA) |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | (2,400) credit |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **3,900** |

Required FVA = $61,500 − $60,000 = **$1,500 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$2,400 credit**  
Increase to FVA = $1,500 − (−$2,400) = **$3,900**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 3,900 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 3,900 |
| *Roll FVA from $2,400 Cr to $1,500 Dr through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,900. **Balanced.**  
Ending FVA = −2,400 + 3,900 = **$1,500 debit**. BS carrying amount = **$61,500**.

**(f) January 1, Year 3 — Sale under FV-NI**  

**(i) Sale-date fair value adjustment (FV-NI)**  

Required FVA at sale* = $62,000 − $60,000 = **$2,000 debit**  
Less existing FVA = **1,500 debit**  
Increase needed = **$500**

\*$62,000 sale price/fair value − $60,000 amortized cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 500 |
| *Bring carrying amount to sale-date fair value through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 500. **Balanced.** FVA now = **$2,000 debit**.

**(ii) Record sale — eliminate FVA; no separate gain/loss on sale**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 62,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 2,000 |
| Investment in TS—SierraTech Bonds | | 60,000 |
| *Remove investment at amortized cost; clear FVA; cash = fair value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 62,000 = Cr 2,000 + 60,000. **Balanced.**  
No Gain/Loss on Sale account is used: net income already reflects the difference between fair value and amortized cost through the FV-NI adjustments.

**(g) Holding-period income schedule (unrealized G/L in NI)**  

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in net income | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 | $(2,400)$ | YE FV $57,600 |
| Year 2 | 3,900 | YE FV $61,500 (from $57,600 path via FVA roll) |
| Year 3 (sale date) | 500 | Sale FV $62,000 |
| **Cumulative over ownership** | **$2,000** | Equals $62,000 − $60,000 |

**Proof:** −2,400 + 3,900 + 500 = **2,000**. Total economic gain recognized in NI over the holding period = sale proceeds − amortized cost.

**Key insight:** Under **FV-NI (trading)**, interest and **unrealized** fair value changes hit **net income** each period via **FVA**. At sale, **first** update FVA to current FV through NI, **then** remove the investment and FVA for cash—typically **without a separate sale G/L**—because the holding-period NI already captures cost-to-sale FV. The multi-period **required FVA − existing FVA** schedule is the exam control for subsequent measurement.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same TS FV-NI path with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Harborpeak Capital LLC** purchased for **par $40,000** of **Redwood Corp. 6%** bonds that mature on December 31 of Year 8. Cash interest is paid **annually** each December 31. Harborpeak classifies the bonds as **trading securities (TS)** under **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA—TS is zero. No other TS activity except as stated.

| Date | Fair value / activity |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$38,500** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Still held; fair value **$41,200** |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Sold entire holding for **$41,000** |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Cash interest entry on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Fair value adjustment on December 31 of Year 1 (include FVA schedule).  
(d) Year 1 BS / IS presentation for this investment.  
(e) December 31, Year 2 FVA rollforward schedule and adjusting entry.  
(f) January 1, Year 3 sale: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust; (ii) sale eliminating FVA.  
(g) Holding-period unrealized G/L schedule proving cumulative NI = proceeds − cost.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Purchase at par**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Redwood Bonds | 40,000 | |
| Cash | | 40,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 40,000. **Balanced.** Amortized cost = **$40,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest**  

Interest = $40,000 × 0.06 = **$2,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,400 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 2,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,400. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — FV-NI loss**  

| | Amortized Cost | Fair Value | Unrealized G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Redwood bonds | 40,000 | 38,500 | (1,500) |
| Existing FVA | | | 0 |
| **Decrease to FVA needed** | | | **(1,500)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 1,500 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 1,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,500. **Balanced.** FVA = **$1,500 credit**. BS carrying amount = **$38,500**.

**(d) Year 1 presentation**  
- **BS (current assets):** Investment in trading securities **$38,500**  
- **IS (other income):** Interest revenue **$2,400**; Unrealized holding loss on TS **$(1,500)**

**(e) December 31, Year 2 — FVA rollforward**  

| | Amortized Cost | Fair Value | Required FVA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Redwood bonds | 40,000 | 41,200 | 1,200 debit |
| Existing FVA | | | (1,500) credit |
| **Increase to FVA needed** | | | **2,700** |

Required FVA = $41,200 − $40,000 = **$1,200 debit**; existing **$1,500 credit** → increase **$2,700**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 2,700 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,700 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,700. **Balanced.** Ending FVA = −1,500 + 2,700 = **$1,200 debit**.

**(f) January 1, Year 3 — Sale**  

**(i) Sale-date adjust**  
Required FVA = $41,000 − $40,000 = **$1,000 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$1,200 debit**  
Decrease needed = **$200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 200 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200. **Balanced.** FVA now = **$1,000 debit**.

**(ii) Sale — eliminate FVA; no separate sale G/L**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 41,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 1,000 |
| Investment in TS—Redwood Bonds | | 40,000 |

**Check:** Dr 41,000 = Cr 1,000 + 40,000. **Balanced.**

**(g) Holding-period unrealized G/L in NI**  

| Period | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | $(1,500)$ |
| Year 2 | 2,700 |
| Year 3 sale-date | (200) |
| **Cumulative** | **$1,000** |

Proof: −1,500 + 2,700 − 200 = **1,000** = $41,000 − $40,000.

**Key insight:** Same **FV-NI** mechanics as Q1 with fully changed face, coupon pattern, fair values, and sale price—drill **required FVA − existing FVA** when fair value rises above cost after a prior credit FVA, and when sale price is **below** the latest carrying FV (sale-date **debit** to Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — TS portfolio: partial sale + subsequent measurement schedule on remainder (+ fair value option note)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** subsequent_measurement_schedule (portfolio FVA emphasis); disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; period_end_adjusting_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On December 31 of Year 2, **Northline Trading Co.** held the following **trading (TS)** debt portfolio (all purchased at amounts equal to amortized cost shown; no discount/premium remaining). The portfolio is measured at **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31.

| TS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value (12/31/Y2) | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Alloy Corp. bonds | $25,000 | $24,000 | $(1,000)$ |
| Beacon Inc. bonds | 35,000 | 36,500 | 1,500 |
| **Total** | **$60,000** | **$60,500** | **$500** |

The **$500** net unrealized gain equals the December 31, Year 2 debit balance in **Fair Value Adjustment—TS**. Unrealized holding gains/losses to date have already been recognized in **net income**.

On **January 1 of Year 3**, Northline sells the **Alloy** bonds for **$24,400**. The remaining **Beacon** bonds (still held) have a fair value of **$37,200** on December 31 of Year 3. No other TS purchases or sales occur in Year 3.

**Optional fact for part (d):** Northline also holds a separate **HTM** debt security for which, on acquisition, management **irrevocably elected the fair value option** (ASC 825). At a later year-end that HTM-elect security has amortized cost $50,000 and fair value $51,800 (interest already recorded).

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 portfolio subsequent measurement schedule** (already reflected in the table) and state the BS carrying amount of the TS portfolio.  
(b) On January 1 of Year 3: **(i)** adjust **Alloy** to fair value at the sale date using **FV-NI**; **(ii)** record the sale of Alloy, eliminating the associated FVA for Alloy (no separate realized G/L account). Show FVA T-account after the sale.  
(c) Record the December 31, Year 3 entry to adjust FVA for **Beacon only** (remaining portfolio). Show the subsequent measurement schedule.  
(d) **Classification / fair value option:** Prepare the year-end FV-NI adjusting entry for the HTM security under the fair value option election (using the optional fact). Briefly state how this election compares to TS accounting and what must be disclosed.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) December 31, Year 2 — Portfolio subsequent measurement (given)**  

| TS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value | Required FVA (Dr = gain) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Alloy Corp. | 25,000 | 24,000 | (1,000) |
| Beacon Inc. | 35,000 | 36,500 | 1,500 |
| **Portfolio** | **60,000** | **60,500** | **500 debit** |

BS carrying amount of TS portfolio = **$60,500** (AC $60,000 + FVA debit $500). Reported as **current assets** when held for near-term trading.

**(b) January 1, Year 3 — Alloy sale (FV-NI path)**  

**(i) Sale-date FVA adjust for Alloy only**  

Required FVA at sale* for Alloy = $24,400 − $25,000 = **$600 credit**  
Existing FVA allocated to Alloy = **$(1,000)**  
Increase to FVA needed = −600 − (−1,000) = **$400** (debit FVA / credit Unrealized G/L—Income)

\*$24,400 sale price − $25,000 amortized cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 400 |
| *Bring Alloy from YE FV $24,000 to sale FV $24,400 through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400. **Balanced.**

**(ii) Sale of Alloy — eliminate Alloy FVA; no separate sale G/L**  

Alloy FVA balance after sale-date adj = **$600 credit**.  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 24,400 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 600 | |
| Investment in TS—Alloy Corp. Bonds | | 25,000 |
| *Remove Alloy at amortized cost; clear its FVA; cash = FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 24,400 + 600 = Cr 25,000. **Balanced.**

**FVA—TS T-account after Alloy sale:**  
Sale entry **debits** FVA $600 to clear Alloy’s post-adjust **credit** FVA of $600.

| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beg (portfolio, 12/31/Y2) | 500 | |
| Sale-date Alloy adj | 400 | |
| Eliminate Alloy FVA credit on sale | 600 | |
| **Balance after Alloy sale** | **1,500** | |

Ending FVA debit **$1,500** = 500 + 400 + 600 = Beacon’s pre-existing unrealized gain only (from Y2 table). Investment account for Alloy is zero.

**Holding check for Alloy alone:** Cumulative unrealized in NI over Alloy ownership:  
Y2 YE loss $(1,000) + Y3 sale-date gain $400 = **$(600)** = $24,400 − $25,000.

**(c) December 31, Year 3 — Subsequent measurement schedule (Beacon remaining)**  

| | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Required FVA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beacon Inc. bonds | 35,000 | 37,200 | 2,200 debit |
| Existing FVA (after Alloy sale) | | | 1,500 debit |
| **Increase to FVA needed** | | | **700** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 700 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 700 |
| *Remeasure remaining TS portfolio to FV through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 700. **Balanced.** Ending FVA = 1,500 + 700 = **$2,200 debit** = $37,200 − $35,000.  
BS: Investment in trading securities **$37,200**.

**(d) Fair value option (HTM-elect) — period-end FV-NI adjust**  

Unrealized holding gain = $51,800 − $50,000 = **$1,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Fair Value Option | 1,800 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 1,800 |
| *FV option: adjust debt investment to FV through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,800. **Balanced.**

**Comparison / disclosure:** Accounting treatment under the **fair value option** is **similar to TS (FV-NI)**: fair value changes through **net income** via an FVA account. The election is made **at acquisition**, applied **instrument-by-instrument**, and is generally **irrevocable**. Disclose the fair value option election (ASC 825-10-50). The FVO is **not** elected for securities already classified as trading (they are already FV-NI).

**Key insight:** Portfolio FVA is a **control total**. On partial disposal under FV-NI: **(1)** adjust the sold security to sale-date FV through **NI**, **(2)** remove its amortized cost and **clear its FVA slice**, **(3)** at next period-end remeasure **only remaining** securities with the same **required − existing** FVA schedule. Fair value option debt is measured like TS for subsequent FV changes.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — where TS unrealized holding gains/losses are reported  
**Question:**  
On December 31, Year 1, Meridian Capital holds a debt security classified as a **trading security**. Amortized cost is $100,000 and fair value is $106,500. No sale has occurred. Ignoring tax, how should Meridian report the $6,500 unrealized holding gain for Year 1?

- A) As an unrealized gain in **other comprehensive income** of $6,500, with a corresponding debit to Fair Value Adjustment—AFS  
- B) As an unrealized gain in **net income** of $6,500, with a corresponding debit to Fair Value Adjustment—TS  
- C) No recognition until the security is sold  
- D) As a direct credit to retained earnings of $6,500, bypassing the income statement  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-2 / ASC 320, **trading** debt is measured at fair value with unrealized holding gains and losses **included in earnings (net income)**. The FVA—TS account is **debited** for holding gains (offset **Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income**). Option A describes **AFS (FV-OCI)** under LO 14-3. Option C ignores required period-end FV measurement for TS. Option D is not GAAP for trading holding gains.

### Q5 — MC (sale sequence under FV-NI)
**LO:** LO 14-2  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE — sale-date FV adjust then eliminate FVA (no separate sale G/L)  
**Question:**  
When a trading debt security is sold under the LO 14-2 approach (fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end), which sequence is correct?

- A) First adjust the investment to fair value through **OCI**, then reclassify AOCI into net income at sale  
- B) Record cash and remove the investment at amortized cost only; never update FVA at sale date  
- C) First adjust the investment to fair value through **net income** (update FVA); then record the sale, eliminate the FVA balance, and generally **do not** record a separate gain/loss on sale  
- D) Always record only a realized gain/loss equal to cash minus amortized cost, with no FVA accounts  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Textbook LO 14-2 sale path: **(1)** adjust to FV via FVA and Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income**; **(2)** record cash, remove investment at amortized cost, and **eliminate FVA**—so a separate sale G/L is usually **unnecessary** because NI already reflects FV. A is **AFS FV-OCI**. B omits the sale-date FV update. D ignores the FVA model used throughout the chapter demos.
