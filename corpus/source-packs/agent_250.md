# Agent 250 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-5

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS given share issuances, buybacks, dividends, and splits  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Basic EPS formula with LO 20-5 adjustments:** (net income − preferred dividend claim) ÷ weighted-average common shares outstanding
- **Preferred dividends — numerator:** noncumulative — subtract only dividends **declared**; cumulative — subtract **one year’s claim** whether or not declared (current-year claim only; no double-counting prior arrears)
- **Common cash dividends — numerator:** **no** effect on basic EPS (earnings available to common are the same whether retained or paid)
- **Share issuances and buybacks — denominator:** weight shares for the **fraction of the period** outstanding (months/12)
- **Stock dividends and stock splits — denominator:** apply **retroactive restatement** as if the dividend/split occurred at the beginning of the earliest period presented (equivalent shares × fraction of year)
- **Initial recognition JE:** mid-year cash issuance of common stock
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-row weighted-average common shares schedule (issuance + buyback + stock dividend/split restatement) and basic EPS computation schedule
- **Period-end adjusting / closing JE:** close net income to retained earnings; preferred dividend declaration when declared
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** treasury stock repurchase (cost method); cash settlement of declared preferred (or common) dividends
- **Classification / presentation:** simple capital structure still reports **basic EPS only**; stock split often memo only (no formal equity reclassification when par-per-share is adjusted or when memorandum only)

### Q1 — CORE — WA shares with buyback, issuance, 2-for-1 split + cumulative preferred (emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule)
**LO:** LO 20-5  
**Concept:** Weighted-average common shares schedule with mid-year buyback and issuance and retroactive 2-for-1 stock-split restatement; numerator reduction for cumulative preferred (undeclared); initial recognition JE for cash common issuance; treasury repurchase settlement JE; period-end closing of net income  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Logistics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company. Its capital structure for Year 1 is **simple** other than **nonconvertible preferred** (no options, warrants, or convertibles). No noncontrolling interest exists.

**Beginning of Year 1 (January 1):**
- **100,000** shares of **$2 par** common stock outstanding
- **5,000** shares of **8%, $20 par cumulative** preferred stock outstanding (issued in prior years; still outstanding all Year 1)

**Year 1 share activity (common):**
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| March 31 | Purchased **4,000** common shares for the **treasury** at **$15** cash per share (cost method) |
| July 1 | Issued **20,000** new shares of **$2 par** common for **$12** cash per share |
| October 1 | Effected a **2-for-1** common stock **split** (par adjusted; memorandum treatment only — no formal equity transfer JE) |

**Other Year 1 facts:**
- Net income = **$436,000**
- **No** preferred dividends were **declared** during Year 1
- **No** common cash dividends were declared
- After the October 1 split, **232,000** common shares are outstanding (116,000 pre-split × 2)

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **March 31 treasury stock repurchase** journal entry (settlement of shares from the outstanding pool). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare the **July 1 initial recognition** journal entry for the cash common stock issuance. Show Dr = Cr.  
c. State the **October 1 stock-split** accounting treatment (entry or memorandum).  
d. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule** for Year 1, applying the **retroactive restatement factor of 2.0** to all periods **before** the split (**subsequent measurement schedule — emphasis**).  
e. Prepare a **basic EPS computation schedule** (numerator, denominator, per-share). Show the preferred dividend claim.  
f. Prepare the **December 31 period-end closing entry** that transfers net income to retained earnings (use Income Summary).  
g. Present **net income** and **basic earnings per share** as they would appear on the Year 1 income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**a. March 31, Year 1 — treasury stock repurchase (settlement)**

Cost = 4,000 × $15 = **$60,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Treasury Stock | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 60,000 |
| *Reacquire 4,000 common shares at $15 (cost method)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **60,000**. **Balanced.**

Shares outstanding after repurchase: 100,000 − 4,000 = **96,000**.

**b. July 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of common stock issuance**

Cash proceeds = 20,000 × $12 = **$240,000**  
Common stock (par) = 20,000 × $2 = **$40,000**  
APIC—Common = $240,000 − $40,000 = **$200,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 240,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 40,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 200,000 |
| *Issue 20,000 shares of $2 par common at $12* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **240,000**; Cr = 40,000 + 200,000 = **240,000**. **Balanced.**

Shares outstanding after issuance: 96,000 + 20,000 = **116,000**.

**c. October 1, Year 1 — 2-for-1 stock split**

**Memorandum only** (no formal journal entry transferring retained earnings or APIC when the split is effected by adjusting par and doubling shares outstanding). Common shares outstanding become 116,000 × 2 = **232,000**.

*For basic EPS, the split is applied **retroactively** to all periods presented (restatement factor **2.0**), not weighted only from October 1.*

**d. Weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule (Year 1) — emphasis**

Retroactive restatement factor for the 2-for-1 split = **2.0** applied to actual shares outstanding in periods **before** October 1.

| Inclusive dates | Actual shares outstanding | Retroactive factor | Equivalent shares | Months | Fraction of year | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1 – Mar. 31 | 100,000 | 2.0 | 200,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 50,000 |
| Apr. 1 – June 30 | 96,000 | 2.0 | 192,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 48,000 |
| July 1 – Sept. 30 | 116,000 | 2.0 | 232,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 58,000 |
| Oct. 1 – Dec. 31 | 232,000 | — | 232,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 58,000 |
| **Totals** | | | | **12** | **12/12** | **214,000** |

**Weighted-average common shares outstanding = 214,000**

*Roll-forward of actual shares (pre-split then post-split):*  
100,000 − 4,000 (Mar. 31) = 96,000; + 20,000 (July 1) = 116,000; × 2 (Oct. 1 split) = 232,000.

**e. Basic EPS computation schedule**

Annual cumulative preferred dividend **claim** (even though **not declared**):  
8% × 5,000 × $20 = **$8,000**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $436,000 |
| Less: Cumulative preferred dividend claim (current year) | (8,000) |
| **Net income available to common stockholders (numerator)** | **$428,000** |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (denominator) | 214,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$2.00** |

Computation:  
\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$436{,}000 - \$8{,}000}{214{,}000} = \frac{\$428{,}000}{214{,}000} = \mathbf{\$2.00}
\]

*Check:* \(214{,}000 \times \$2.00 = \$428{,}000\).

*Note:* Prior years’ cumulative preferred arrears (if any) are **not** subtracted again; only the **current-year** claim enters the Year 1 numerator.

**f. December 31, Year 1 — period-end closing of net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary | 436,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 436,000 |
| *Close Year 1 net income to retained earnings* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **436,000**. **Balanced.**

*No JE books basic EPS itself.* Undeclared cumulative preferred dividends create **no liability** until declared; the **EPS numerator** still deducts the current-year claim.

**g. Financial statement presentation (Year 1)**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $436,000 |
| Basic earnings per share | **$2.00** |

**Key insight:** LO 20-5 separates **cash capital transactions** (issuances and buybacks), which are **time-weighted** in the WA denominator, from **stock dividends/splits**, which are applied with **full retroactive restatement**. Cumulative preferred stock reduces the numerator by the **current-year claim** even when undeclared. The **subsequent measurement schedules** (WA shares + EPS computation) are the core exam skill.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Buyback, issuance, 50% stock dividend + noncumulative preferred
**LO:** LO 20-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: WA shares schedule with mid-year issuance, treasury buyback, and retroactive large stock-dividend restatement; noncumulative preferred dividends declared; stock-dividend capitalization JE; preferred declare-and-pay settlement JEs; basic EPS computation schedule  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeview Media Inc.** is a calendar-year company with a simple capital structure except for **nonconvertible preferred**. No noncontrolling interest.

**Beginning of Year 1 (January 1):**
- **80,000** shares of **$1 par** common stock outstanding
- **4,000** shares of **5%, $25 par noncumulative** preferred stock outstanding all year

**Year 1 share activity (common):**
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| April 1 | Issued **40,000** shares of **$1 par** common for **$10** cash per share |
| July 1 | Purchased **8,000** common shares for the **treasury** at **$12** cash per share (cost method) |
| October 1 | Distributed a **50%** common **stock dividend** (large stock dividend; capitalize at **par**) |

**Other Year 1 facts:**
- Net income = **$482,000**
- Preferred dividends of **$5,000** were **declared on December 15** and **paid on December 28**
- No common cash dividends
- After the 50% stock dividend, common shares outstanding = 112,000 × 1.50 = **168,000** (treasury shares do not receive the stock dividend; only shares outstanding participate)

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **April 1** common stock **issuance** journal entry.  
b. Prepare the **July 1 treasury** repurchase journal entry.  
c. Prepare the **October 1 large stock dividend** journal entry (capitalize **par** of new shares).  
d. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares schedule** with **1.5** retroactive restatement for the stock dividend (**emphasis**).  
e. Prepare preferred dividend **declaration** and **payment** journal entries.  
f. Prepare the **basic EPS computation schedule** and compute basic EPS.  
g. Prepare the **December 31** closing entry for net income.

**Answer key:**  

**a. April 1, Year 1 — common stock issuance**

Cash = 40,000 × $10 = **$400,000**  
Common stock (par) = 40,000 × $1 = **$40,000**  
APIC—Common = **$360,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 400,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 40,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 360,000 |
| *Issue 40,000 shares of $1 par common at $10* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **400,000**. **Balanced.**  
Outstanding after issuance: 80,000 + 40,000 = **120,000**.

**b. July 1, Year 1 — treasury repurchase**

Cost = 8,000 × $12 = **$96,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Treasury Stock | 96,000 | |
| Cash | | 96,000 |
| *Reacquire 8,000 common shares at $12* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **96,000**. **Balanced.**  
Outstanding after buyback: 120,000 − 8,000 = **112,000**.

**c. October 1, Year 1 — 50% large stock dividend (at par)**

New shares issued = 112,000 × 50% = **56,000**  
Par transferred = 56,000 × $1 = **$56,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 56,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 56,000 |
| *Distribute 50% stock dividend (large; capitalize at par)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **56,000**. **Balanced.**  
Outstanding after stock dividend: 112,000 + 56,000 = **168,000**.

**d. Weighted-average common shares schedule (Year 1) — emphasis**

Retroactive restatement factor for the 50% stock dividend = **1.5** applied to periods **before** October 1.

| Inclusive dates | Actual shares outstanding | Retroactive factor | Equivalent shares | Months | Fraction of year | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1 – Mar. 31 | 80,000 | 1.5 | 120,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 30,000 |
| Apr. 1 – June 30 | 120,000 | 1.5 | 180,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 45,000 |
| July 1 – Sept. 30 | 112,000 | 1.5 | 168,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 42,000 |
| Oct. 1 – Dec. 31 | 168,000 | — | 168,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 42,000 |
| **Totals** | | | | **12** | **12/12** | **159,000** |

**Weighted-average common shares outstanding = 159,000**

**e. Preferred dividends — declaration and settlement**

Annual noncumulative preferred maximum = 5% × 4,000 × $25 = **$5,000** (matches amount declared).

**December 15 — declaration**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Preferred Dividends) | 5,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred | | 5,000 |
| *Declare $5,000 noncumulative preferred dividends* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **5,000**. **Balanced.**

**December 28 — payment (settlement)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable—Preferred | 5,000 | |
| Cash | | 5,000 |
| *Pay preferred dividends* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **5,000**. **Balanced.**

**f. Basic EPS computation schedule**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $482,000 |
| Less: Noncumulative preferred dividends **declared** | (5,000) |
| **Net income available to common stockholders** | **$477,000** |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding | 159,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$3.00** |

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$482{,}000 - \$5{,}000}{159{,}000} = \frac{\$477{,}000}{159{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.00}
\]

*Check:* \(159{,}000 \times \$3.00 = \$477{,}000\).

*Had the noncumulative preferred declared **nothing**, the numerator would remain **$482,000** (no subtraction).*

**g. December 31, Year 1 — closing net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary | 482,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 482,000 |
| *Close Year 1 net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **482,000**. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** A **large stock dividend** requires a **capitalization JE** (RE → Common at par here) **and** retroactive restatement in the WA schedule. **Noncumulative** preferred affects the EPS numerator **only when declared**. Issuances and buybacks remain **time-weighted** only.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Issuance + buyback only; cumulative preferred undeclared; common cash dividend does **not** change EPS
**LO:** LO 20-5  
**Concept:** WA shares with mid-year issuance and buyback (no stock dividend/split); cumulative preferred current-year claim reduces numerator even when undeclared; common cash dividend declaration and year-end liability do **not** adjust basic EPS; period-end closing  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Packaging Inc.** (calendar year) has only common stock and **nonconvertible cumulative preferred** outstanding (simple capital structure for EPS presentation purposes — basic EPS only). No stock dividends or splits in Year 1.

**Beginning January 1, Year 1:**
- **180,000** shares of common stock outstanding (remain the base until later activity)
- **3,000** shares of **6%, $100 par cumulative** preferred outstanding all year

**Year 1 common share activity:**
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| April 1 | Issued **36,000** common shares for cash |
| October 1 | Reacquired **12,000** common shares for the treasury |

**Other facts:**
- Net income = **$528,000**
- **No** preferred dividends declared in Year 1
- On **December 20**, the board declared a **common cash dividend** of **$0.25** per share on shares then outstanding (**204,000** shares), payable January 15, Year 2
- Common stock has **$1 par**; April 1 issue price was **$8** per share; October 1 treasury cost was **$9** per share

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **April 1** common issuance journal entry.  
b. Prepare the **October 1** treasury repurchase journal entry.  
c. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares schedule** (no retroactive stock dividend/split factor).  
d. Compute the **common cash dividend** amount; prepare the **December 20 declaration** JE; explain the effect (if any) on the basic EPS **numerator**.  
e. Prepare the **basic EPS computation schedule** (include cumulative preferred claim).  
f. Prepare the **December 31** closing entry for net income.

**Answer key:**  

**a. April 1 — common issuance**

Cash = 36,000 × $8 = **$288,000**  
Common stock = 36,000 × $1 = **$36,000**  
APIC = **$252,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 288,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 36,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 252,000 |
| *Issue 36,000 shares at $8* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **288,000**. **Balanced.**  
Outstanding: 180,000 + 36,000 = **216,000**.

**b. October 1 — treasury repurchase**

Cost = 12,000 × $9 = **$108,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Treasury Stock | 108,000 | |
| Cash | | 108,000 |
| *Reacquire 12,000 shares at $9* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **108,000**. **Balanced.**  
Outstanding: 216,000 − 12,000 = **204,000**.

**c. Weighted-average common shares schedule (Year 1)**

| Inclusive dates | Actual shares outstanding | Months | Fraction of year | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1 – Mar. 31 | 180,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 45,000 |
| Apr. 1 – Sept. 30 | 216,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 108,000 |
| Oct. 1 – Dec. 31 | 204,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 51,000 |
| **Totals** | | **12** | **12/12** | **204,000** |

**Weighted-average common shares = 204,000**

**d. Common cash dividend — amount, JE, EPS impact**

Dividend = 204,000 × $0.25 = **$51,000**

**December 20 — declaration**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Common Dividends) | 51,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Common | | 51,000 |
| *Declare $0.25 common cash dividend on 204,000 shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **51,000**. **Balanced.**

**Effect on basic EPS numerator:** **None.** Common cash dividends are a **distribution of** earnings already included in net income; they do **not** reduce “income available to common stockholders” for EPS. (They reduce retained earnings / create a liability when declared.)

**e. Basic EPS computation schedule**

Cumulative preferred current-year claim = 6% × 3,000 × $100 = **$18,000** (subtracted even though **not declared**).

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $528,000 |
| Less: Cumulative preferred dividend claim | (18,000) |
| Less: Common cash dividends | **0** (no EPS adjustment) |
| **Net income available to common stockholders** | **$510,000** |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding | 204,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$2.50** |

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$528{,}000 - \$18{,}000}{204{,}000} = \frac{\$510{,}000}{204{,}000} = \mathbf{\$2.50}
\]

*Check:* \(204{,}000 \times \$2.50 = \$510{,}000\).

**f. December 31 — close net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary | 528,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 528,000 |
| *Close Year 1 net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **528,000**. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Without stock dividends/splits, the WA schedule is a pure **time-weight** of issuances and buybacks. **Cumulative preferred** still reduces the numerator by the current-year claim when undeclared; **common cash dividends** never adjust the EPS numerator.

### Q4 — MC — Retroactive restatement vs time-weighting
**LO:** LO 20-5  
**Concept:** Classification of how stock splits/stock dividends versus cash share issuances affect the weighted-average common shares denominator  
**Question:**  
For **basic EPS**, how should a mid-year **2-for-1 common stock split** and a mid-year **cash issuance of new common shares** enter the weighted-average common shares denominator?

- A) Both the split and the cash issuance are weighted only for the fraction of the year after each event occurs.  
- B) Both the split and the cash issuance are applied retroactively as if they occurred at the beginning of the earliest period presented.  
- C) The stock split is applied retroactively to all periods presented; the cash issuance is weighted only for the portion of the period the new shares were outstanding.  
- D) The cash issuance is applied retroactively; the stock split is weighted only from the split date.

**Answer:** **C.** Stock dividends and stock splits do not raise new capital and are restated **retroactively** for all periods presented. Shares issued for cash are resources available only after issuance, so they are **time-weighted** (months outstanding ÷ 12).

### Q5 — MC — Preferred dividend numerator rule
**LO:** LO 20-5  
**Concept:** Classification of the correct preferred-dividend adjustment to income available to common for basic EPS  
**Question:**  
A company has **noncumulative** preferred stock outstanding. **No** preferred dividends were declared this year. Separately, another company has **cumulative** preferred stock and also declared **no** preferred dividends this year. Which statement is correct for **basic EPS** numerators (assuming both companies report net income)?

- A) Both companies subtract the full annual preferred dividend claim from net income.  
- B) Neither company subtracts any preferred amount, because nothing was declared.  
- C) The noncumulative issuer subtracts nothing; the cumulative issuer subtracts the **current-year** preferred dividend claim.  
- D) The noncumulative issuer subtracts the annual claim; the cumulative issuer subtracts nothing until paid in cash.

**Answer:** **C.** Noncumulative preferred reduces the EPS numerator **only for dividends declared** in the period. Cumulative preferred requires subtraction of the **current-year claim** whether or not declared (prior-year arrears already hit prior periods’ EPS and are not deducted again).
