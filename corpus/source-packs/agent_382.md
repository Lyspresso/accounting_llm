# Agent 382 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-6

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS using if-converted method for convertible securities  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Complex capital structure:** convertible debt or convertible preferred that can increase common shares and potentially dilute EPS
- **If-converted method (convertible debt):** assume conversion at beginning of period (or issue date if later); **add back interest expense net of tax** to the numerator; **add potential common shares** to the denominator
- **If-converted method (convertible preferred):** add back preferred dividends deducted in basic EPS (**no tax adjustment**); add potential common shares
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end **interest accrual** on convertible debt when cash interest is paid on a date other than year-end; year-end **preferred dividend** declaration; diluted EPS uses **interest expense recognized** (including accrued amounts), not merely cash paid
- **Initial recognition JE:** issue convertible bonds at par; issue convertible preferred stock
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** contractual interest roll-forward; diluted EPS reconciliation schedule (basic → effect of convertibles → diluted)
- **Partial-year weighting:** if convertibles issued mid-year, weight **both** after-tax interest add-back **and** incremental shares by time outstanding
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** actual conversion of bonds or preferred into common stock; pay accrued interest at or before conversion when applicable
- **Dilutive vs antidilutive:** include only if diluted EPS **decreases**; with a **loss from continuing operations**, potential shares are **excluded** (always antidilutive to a loss)
- **Presentation / disclosure:** complex structure reports **basic and diluted EPS**; disclose antidilutive convertibles omitted from diluted EPS

### Q1 — CORE — Convertible bonds: period-end interest accrual AJ (emphasis), interest schedule, if-converted EPS, partial-year, conversion settlement
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE for convertible-bond interest accrual (emphasis); initial recognition at par; subsequent-measurement interest schedule; basic and diluted EPS under the if-converted method (full year and mid-year issuance); conversion settlement JE after paying accrued interest; income-statement EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Robotics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company. Its only potentially dilutive instrument is convertible debt (complex capital structure). Common stock has a **$2 par** value. No preferred stock and no options/warrants.

**Facts for 20X5 (full-year bonds):**
1. On **January 1, 20X5**, Harborline issued **1,200** convertible bonds, each with **$1,000** face (**$1,200,000** total face), **7%** stated annual interest, for **cash at par**. Each bond is convertible into **25** shares of common stock. Maturity is December 31, 20Y4. Interest is paid **annually every January 1** for the **preceding calendar year** (so 20X5 interest is paid January 1, 20X6).
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding during 20X5 (before if-converted assumptions): **350,000**.
3. Net income for 20X5: **$980,000** (already after interest expense on the convertible bonds and after income taxes).
4. Enacted tax rate: **25%**.

**Alternate fact (part g only):** Same bonds are issued on **October 1, 20X5** (not January 1). Interest for the three months outstanding is accrued at December 31, 20X5, and paid January 1, 20X6. Net income for 20X5 under this alternate fact is **$1,027,250** (higher than $980,000 because only three months of after-tax interest is incurred). Weighted-average common shares remain **350,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, 20X5 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible bonds at par. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, 20X5 period-end adjusting JE** to accrue a full year of interest on the bonds. Prove the entry balances.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement interest schedule** for 20X5 (face, rate, interest expense, cash paid in 20X5, interest payable end, carrying amount).  
d. Compute **basic EPS** for 20X5 (full-year bonds facts).  
e. Prepare the **diluted EPS reconciliation schedule** using the **if-converted method**. State whether the bonds are **dilutive** or **antidilutive**.  
f. **Classification / presentation:** What EPS amount(s) does Harborline report on the face of its 20X5 income statement?  
g. Using the **October 1 issuance alternate facts**, recompute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** under the if-converted method with **partial-year weighting**. Show the reconciliation schedule and the **December 31 period-end AJ** for three months of interest.  
h. On **January 1, 20X6**, Harborline (i) pays the accrued interest from the full-year facts and (ii) all bondholders convert their bonds into common stock. Prepare both JEs (payment then conversion).

**Answer key:**

**a. Initial recognition — convertible bonds at par (January 1, 20X5)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,200,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 1,200,000 |
| *Issue 1,200 of $1,000 face 7% convertible bonds at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,200,000 = Cr 1,200,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Period-end adjusting JE — interest accrual (emphasis) (December 31, 20X5)**

Annual contractual interest = \( \$1{,}200{,}000 \times 7\% = \mathbf{\$84{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 84,000 | |
| Interest Payable | | 84,000 |
| *Year-end AJ: accrue annual interest on convertible bonds (cash paid next January 1)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 84,000 = Cr 84,000. **Balanced.**

*If-converted note:* The diluted EPS numerator add-back uses **interest expense recognized** for the period (**$84,000** pretax), which equals the amount in this period-end AJ. Cash interest paid in 20X5 is **$0** under these payment terms.

**c. Subsequent measurement — interest schedule (20X5)**

| Period | Face outstanding | Rate | Interest expense | Cash paid in 20X5 | Interest payable end | Carrying amount end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 issue | $1,200,000 | — | — | — | $0 | $1,200,000 |
| Year 20X5 | 1,200,000 | 7% | 84,000 | 0 | 84,000 | 1,200,000 |
| **Year total** | | | **84,000** | **0** | **84,000** | |

(Bonds at par: no premium/discount amortization. Carrying amount remains face.)

**d. Basic EPS (full-year bonds)**

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$980{,}000}{350{,}000} = \mathbf{\$2.80}
\]

**e. Diluted EPS — if-converted method reconciliation schedule**

Assume conversion as of **January 1, 20X5**:

- **Numerator add-back (interest, net of tax):**  
  \( \$84{,}000 \times (1 - 0.25) = \$84{,}000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{\$63{,}000} \)
- **Denominator add (new common shares):**  
  \( 1{,}200 \text{ bonds} \times 25 \text{ shares} = \mathbf{30{,}000} \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $980,000 | 350,000 | $2.80 |
| Effect of convertible bonds: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 63,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 30,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$1,043,000** | **380,000** | **$2.74** |

Math check: \( 1{,}043{,}000 \div 380{,}000 = 2.744736\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.74} \) (nearest cent).

**Dilutive test:** Diluted EPS **$2.74** < basic EPS **$2.80** → bonds are **dilutive**. Include in diluted EPS.

**f. Presentation**

Harborline has a **complex capital structure**. It reports **both**:
- Basic EPS **$2.80**
- Diluted EPS **$2.74**

on the face of the 20X5 income statement.

**g. Partial-year issuance (October 1) — period-end AJ + if-converted reconciliation**

Bonds outstanding only **3/12** of the year under the if-converted assumption (issue date if later).

Interest for three months = \( \$84{,}000 \times 3/12 = \mathbf{\$21{,}000} \)

*December 31, 20X5 period-end AJ (alternate facts):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 21,000 | |
| Interest Payable | | 21,000 |
| *Accrue 3 months interest on convertible bonds issued Oct 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 21,000. **Balanced.**

If-converted adjustments (prorated):

- After-tax interest add-back = \( \$21{,}000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{\$15{,}750} \)  
  (equivalently \( \$63{,}000 \times 3/12 = \$15{,}750 \))
- Incremental shares weighted = \( 30{,}000 \times 3/12 = \mathbf{7{,}500} \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $1,027,250 | 350,000 | $2.94 |
| Effect of convertible bonds (3/12): | | | |
| Add back prorated interest, net of tax | 15,750 | | |
| Add prorated common shares | | 7,500 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$1,043,000** | **357,500** | **$2.92** |

Basic: \( 1{,}027{,}250 \div 350{,}000 = 2.935 \approx \mathbf{\$2.94} \)  
Diluted: \( 1{,}043{,}000 \div 357{,}500 = 2.91748\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.92} \)

**Dilutive:** $2.92 < $2.94 → include. Report basic **$2.94** and diluted **$2.92**.

*Bridge from full-year NI:* Full-year after-tax interest cost \( = \$63{,}000 \). Three-month after-tax cost \( = \$15{,}750 \). Difference \( \$47{,}250 \) added to full-year NI: \( \$980{,}000 + \$47{,}250 = \$1{,}027{,}250 \). ✓

**h. January 1, 20X6 — pay accrued interest, then convert (settlement)**

*(i) Pay accrued interest (full-year facts)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Payable | 84,000 | |
| Cash | | 84,000 |
| *Pay annual interest accrued Dec 31, 20X5* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 84,000. **Balanced.**

*(ii) Conversion settlement*

Shares issued = \( 1{,}200 \times 25 = 30{,}000 \)  
Common stock at par = \( 30{,}000 \times \$2 = \$60{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$1{,}200{,}000 - \$60{,}000 = \$1{,}140{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 1,200,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 60,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 1,140,000 |
| *Convert all bonds into 30,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,200,000 = Cr 60,000 + 1,140,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** (interest accrual) is the bookkeeping link between contractual convertible debt and the if-converted numerator: diluted EPS adds back **interest expense net of tax**, including amounts recognized only through year-end AJs when cash is paid after year-end. Mid-year issues weight **both** the add-back and the potential shares. Actual conversion is a **balance-sheet settlement**; diluted EPS already assumes conversion for the period the debt was outstanding.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Convertible bonds if-converted twin (period-end AJ emphasis)
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: period-end interest accrual AJ on convertible debt; subsequent measurement interest schedule; if-converted diluted EPS reconciliation; initial recognition at par; conversion settlement after paying accrued interest; EPS presentation

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Convertible preferred if-converted; period-end dividend AJ; conversion settlement; loss-year antidilution & mid-year bond conversion
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Period-end preferred-dividend adjusting/declaration JE; if-converted diluted EPS for convertible preferred; preferred issuance and conversion settlement; diluted EPS when company has a net loss (antidilutive exclusion and disclosure); mid-year actual bond conversion and if-converted full-year dilution logic  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarpoint Logistics Group** (calendar-year) has convertible preferred stock in a profitable year and, in separate scenarios, loss-year convertibles and mid-year bond conversion.

**Part A — Convertible preferred (Year 1, profitable)**  
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Cedarpoint issued **8,000** shares of **6%**, **$50** par **cumulative convertible preferred stock** for cash at **par** (**$400,000**). Each preferred share is convertible into **4** shares of **$1** par common stock.  
2. Net income for Year 1: **$480,000**.  
3. Weighted-average common shares outstanding: **120,000**.  
4. Preferred dividends for Year 1 are **declared at year-end** (December 31) and **paid the next day** (January 1, Year 2). Cumulative preferred — the annual dividend is deducted for basic EPS whether or not paid in cash during Year 1.  
5. Tax rate: **25%** (not needed for preferred if-converted numerator).  
6. No convertible debt in Part A.

**Part B — Actual conversion (January 1, Year 2)**  
All 8,000 preferred shares convert into common stock on January 1, Year 2 (after the cash dividend payment on that date, if any).

**Part C — Net loss year with convertible bonds (antidilutive)**  
In a different year, Cedarpoint reports a **net loss of $360,000**. Throughout that year it has outstanding **600** of **5%**, **$1,000** bonds issued at par, each convertible into **20** common shares. Weighted-average common shares: **100,000**. Tax rate **25%**. Assume sufficient taxable income so interest is deductible for tax purposes if one were to compute a hypothetical diluted figure; apply the **loss-from-continuing-operations rule** for reporting.

**Part D — Actual conversion of convertible bonds mid-year (disposal / settlement)**  
In another profitable year, Cedarpoint has **1,000** of **6%**, **$1,000** bonds (issued at par in a prior year), each convertible into **20** common shares. On **May 1**, all bondholders convert. Tax rate **25%**. Weighted-average common shares for the year **before** considering conversion-related common shares already include the post-conversion shares for May 1–December 31 in basic EPS. For diluted EPS, show the if-converted **numerator and denominator adjustments only for January 1–May 1** (the period before actual conversion), so that diluted EPS reflects a full year of dilution.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible preferred stock.  
b. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end JE** declaring the annual preferred dividend (accrual/declaration).  
c. Compute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** (if-converted) for Year 1 Part A. Show the **reconciliation schedule** and dilutive test.  
d. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 conversion settlement JE** for the preferred stock (assume dividend already paid; no dividends payable remains).  
e. For Part C (loss year): compute the **hypothetical** if-converted diluted figure, explain why it is **antidilutive**, state the **reported** basic and diluted EPS, and describe the **disclosure** for the omitted convertibles.  
f. For Part D: compute the if-converted **numerator add-back** and **denominator share add** for the pre-conversion period (Jan 1–May 1). Explain why diluted EPS still reflects a **full year** of dilution relative to basic.

**Answer key:**

**a. Initial recognition — convertible preferred at par (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 400,000 | |
| Preferred Stock ($50 par) | | 400,000 |
| *Issue 8,000 shares of 6%, $50 par cumulative convertible preferred at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 400,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Period-end preferred dividend declaration (emphasis) (December 31, Year 1)**

Annual preferred dividend = \( 8{,}000 \times \$50 \times 6\% = \mathbf{\$24{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Cash Dividends—Preferred) | 24,000 | |
| Dividends Payable—Preferred | | 24,000 |
| *Year-end declaration of annual cumulative preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 24,000. **Balanced.**

(Optional next-day payment January 1, Year 2: Dr Dividends Payable—Preferred 24,000 / Cr Cash 24,000 — balances.)

**c. Basic and diluted EPS — convertible preferred if-converted schedule**

Income available to common (basic) = \( \$480{,}000 - \$24{,}000 = \mathbf{\$456{,}000} \)

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$456{,}000}{120{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.80}
\]

If-converted assumptions (conversion as of January 1, Year 1):

- **Numerator:** add back preferred dividends **$24,000** → income for diluted = \( \$456{,}000 + \$24{,}000 = \$480{,}000 \) (equivalently, start from NI and do not deduct preferred dividends)  
- **Denominator:** add \( 8{,}000 \times 4 = 32{,}000 \) common shares → \( 120{,}000 + 32{,}000 = 152{,}000 \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $456,000 | 120,000 | $3.80 |
| Effect of convertible preferred: | | | |
| Add back preferred dividends | 24,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 32,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$480,000** | **152,000** | **$3.16** |

Diluted: \( 480{,}000 \div 152{,}000 = 3.15789\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$3.16} \)

**Dilutive test:** $3.16 < $3.80 → **dilutive**. Include.  
**No tax effect** on preferred dividend add-back (dividends are not tax-deductible).

**Presentation:** Report basic EPS **$3.80** and diluted EPS **$3.16**.

**d. Conversion settlement JE — preferred (January 1, Year 2)**

Shares issued = \( 8{,}000 \times 4 = 32{,}000 \)  
Common stock at par = \( 32{,}000 \times \$1 = \$32{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$400{,}000 - \$32{,}000 = \$368{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock ($50 par) | 400,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 32,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 368,000 |
| *Convert 8,000 preferred shares into 32,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 400,000 = Cr 32,000 + 368,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Part C — net loss year; convertible bonds antidilutive**

Pretax annual interest = \( 600 \times \$1{,}000 \times 5\% = \$30{,}000 \)  
After-tax interest (hypothetical add-back) = \( \$30{,}000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{\$22{,}500} \)  
Potential shares = \( 600 \times 20 = \mathbf{12{,}000} \)

| | Income (loss) available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $(360,000) | 100,000 | $(3.60) |
| Hypothetical effect of convertible bonds: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 22,500 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 12,000 | |
| Hypothetical diluted EPS | $(337,500) | 112,000 | $(3.01) |

Hypothetical: \( -337{,}500 \div 112{,}000 = -3.01339\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$(3.01)} \)

**Antidilutive reason:** \( \$(3.01) \) is **less negative** than \( \$(3.60) \) (loss per share improves). Including potential shares reduces the loss per share → **antidilutive**.

**ASC 260 loss rule:** When there is a **loss from continuing operations**, **no** potentially dilutive securities are included in diluted EPS, even if they would appear dilutive to other subtotals.

**Reported amounts:** Basic EPS **$(3.60)** and diluted EPS **$(3.60)** (same).  
**Disclosure:** Disclose the terms of the convertible bonds and that **12,000** potential common shares (and related interest effects) were **excluded** from diluted EPS because they were antidilutive.

**f. Part D — mid-year actual conversion; if-converted pre-conversion adjustments**

Face outstanding = \( 1{,}000 \times \$1{,}000 = \$1{,}000{,}000 \)  
Annual pretax interest = \( \$1{,}000{,}000 \times 6\% = \$60{,}000 \)  
Period before conversion = 4/12 (January 1–May 1)

- **Numerator add-back (Jan 1–May 1):**  
  \( \$1{,}000{,}000 \times 6\% \times 0.75 \times 4/12 = \$60{,}000 \times 0.75 \times 4/12 = \$45{,}000 \times 4/12 = \mathbf{\$15{,}000} \)
- **Denominator add (Jan 1–May 1):**  
  \( 1{,}000 \times 20 \times 4/12 = 20{,}000 \times 4/12 = \mathbf{6{,}667} \) shares (rounded)

**Actual conversion settlement JE (May 1)** — ignore interest on May 1 for this entry:

Shares issued = \( 1{,}000 \times 20 = 20{,}000 \)  
Assume common **$1** par: CS = $20,000; APIC = $980,000

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 1,000,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 20,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 980,000 |
| *Convert all bonds into 20,000 common shares on May 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,000,000 = Cr 20,000 + 980,000. **Balanced.**

**Full-year dilution logic:** Basic EPS already includes the new common shares from May 1 onward (weighted 8/12). Diluted EPS adds the **if-converted** share and interest effects **only for January 1–May 1**. Combined, diluted EPS reflects the economic effect of conversion for the **entire year**, whether conversion occurred on May 1, another date, or not at all.

**Key insight:** Convertible **preferred** uses if-converted by adding back **dividends** (no tax shield) and potential shares; the **period-end declaration AJ** is what books the dividend that basic EPS deducts. Convertible **debt** uses after-tax **interest**. A **loss from continuing operations** makes all potential shares antidilutive for reporting. **Actual mid-year conversion** still produces full-year dilution in diluted EPS via pre-conversion if-converted adjustments plus post-conversion shares already in basic weighted-average shares.

### Q4 — MC (method / dilutive classification)
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Classification — which computational method applies to convertible securities; identifying dilutive vs antidilutive outcomes under if-converted  

**Question 4-1:**  
Which method is used to reflect the dilutive effect of convertible bonds and convertible preferred stock in diluted EPS?

- A) Treasury stock method  
- B) If-converted method  
- C) Two-class method only (no other method applies)  
- D) Reverse treasury stock method  

**Answer:** **B.** Convertible securities use the **if-converted method** (assume conversion at period start or issue date if later; adjust numerator for after-tax interest or preferred dividends; add potential common shares). The treasury stock method applies to options and warrants, not convertibles.

**Question 4-2:**  
A company reports a **net loss from continuing operations**. It has outstanding dilutive-looking convertible bonds. For diluted EPS reporting under ASC 260:

- A) Include the bonds because they would increase the absolute amount of the loss per share  
- B) Include the bonds only if the tax add-back is material  
- C) Exclude the bonds (and all potential common shares); report basic and diluted EPS as the same loss per share amount  
- D) Include the bonds in diluted EPS but exclude them from basic EPS only if conversion is probable  

**Answer:** **C.** With a **loss from continuing operations**, including potential common shares is always **antidilutive** to that loss; **no** potential common shares enter any diluted EPS amount for the period. Basic and diluted EPS are reported as the **same** loss-per-share amount, with disclosure of omitted antidilutive securities.
