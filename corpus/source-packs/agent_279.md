# Agent 279 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-7

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for extinguishment of debt  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Extinguishment at maturity: carrying amount equals face; remove Bonds Payable for cash (no gain/loss)
- Early extinguishment steps: (1) update interest expense and amortize discount/premium to retirement date, (2) derecognize face and related unamortized discount/premium (or issue costs), (3) record cash (reacquisition price), (4) recognize gain or loss
- Gain/loss = reacquisition price − net carrying amount of debt extinguished (ASC 470-50-40-2)
- Effective-interest amortization schedule feeding carrying amount used in extinguishment
- Partial early retirement: apply retirement percentage to face, unamortized discount/premium, and schedule thereafter
- Period-end / to-date adjusting JE before extinguishment (interest + amortization), including mid-period retirement
- Bond refunding: retire old issue and issue new issue; gain/loss still recognized currently
- Classification/presentation: gain or loss on extinguishment reported as a separate item in income of the period (not deferred)
- Number-variant twin with different face, rates, call price, and retirement fraction

### Q1 — CORE — Harborline Marine: issue, schedule, period-end interest, partial early extinguishment, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition + effective-interest schedule + period-end adjusting interest JE + partial early extinguishment + remaining maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On January 1, Year 1, Harborline Marine Corp. issued **$250,000** face of **3-year, 5%** bonds. Cash interest is payable **annually each December 31**. The bonds were priced to yield **7%** and sold for **$236,878** (discount **$13,122**). Harborline uses the **effective interest method**. Fiscal year-end is December 31.

On **December 31, Year 2**, after recording the year-end interest and amortization entry and paying cash interest, Harborline **recalls 40%** of the bonds at **102** (cash paid equals 40% × face × 1.02). The remaining 60% is held to maturity and retired on December 31, Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance (initial recognition).  
b. Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** for the original issue (Years 1–3).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting / interest payment entry.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 2** period-end interest entry, then the **partial early extinguishment** entry. Compute the loss.  
e. Record **December 31, Year 3** interest on the remaining bonds and **maturity settlement**.  
f. Briefly state how the Year 2 loss on extinguishment is **presented**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Cash | 236,878 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 13,122 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bonds Payable | | 250,000 |

*Dr 236,878 + 13,122 = Cr 250,000. Carrying amount = $236,878.*

**b. Effective-interest amortization schedule**  
Cash interest each year = $250,000 × 5% = **$12,500**. Market interest = 7% × beginning carrying amount. Year 3 plugs amortization so carrying amount reaches face.

| Date | Cash interest (5%) | Interest expense (7% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|------|-------------------:|--------------------------------:|----------------------:|---------------------:|----------------:|
| Jan. 1, Y1 | | | | **13,122** | **236,878** |
| Dec. 31, Y1 | 12,500 | 16,581 | 4,081 | 9,041 | 240,959 |
| Dec. 31, Y2 | 12,500 | 16,867 | 4,367 | 4,674 | 245,326 |
| Dec. 31, Y3 | 12,500 | 17,174 | 4,674 | 0 | 250,000 |
| **Totals** | **37,500** | **50,622** | **13,122** | | |

Checks: 236,878 × 0.07 = 16,581.46 → **16,581**; 240,959 × 0.07 = 16,867.13 → **16,867**; Year 3 interest expense = 12,500 + 4,674 = **17,174** (plug). Sum of amortizations = 13,122.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end interest (adjusting + payment)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 16,581 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 4,081 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 12,500 |

*Dr 16,581 = Cr 4,081 + 12,500. Ending CV = $240,959.*

**d. December 31, Year 2 — Period-end interest, then partial extinguishment**  

**Interest (full issue, before recall)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 16,867 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 4,367 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 12,500 |

*Dr 16,867 = Cr 4,367 + 12,500. CV after entry = $245,326; unamortized discount = $4,674.*

**Partial early extinguishment — 40% at 102**  

| Item | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Face retired (40% × $250,000) | 100,000 |
| Unamortized discount retired (40% × $4,674) | 1,870 |
| Carrying amount retired ($100,000 − $1,870) | 98,130 |
| Cash paid (reacquisition price) ($100,000 × 1.02) | 102,000 |
| **Loss on extinguishment** ($102,000 − $98,130) | **3,870** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Bonds Payable | 100,000 | |
| Loss on Extinguishment of Debt | 3,870 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,870 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 102,000 |

*Dr 100,000 + 3,870 = 103,870; Cr 1,870 + 102,000 = 103,870.*

Remaining after extinguishment: face **$150,000**; unamortized discount **$2,804** (60% × $4,674); carrying amount **$147,196**.

**e. December 31, Year 3 — Remaining 60% interest and maturity**  

Use 60% of the Year 3 schedule row:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense (60% × $17,174) | 10,304 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable (60% × $4,674) | | 2,804 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash (60% × $12,500) | | 7,500 |

*Dr 10,304 = Cr 2,804 + 7,500. Discount fully amortized on remaining issue.*

**Maturity / settlement of remaining bonds**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Bonds Payable | 150,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 150,000 |

*No gain or loss at maturity (carrying amount = face). Dr = Cr = 150,000.*

**f. Presentation**  
The **$3,870 loss** is recognized **currently in net income** of Year 2 and identified as a **separate item** (debt extinguishment), per ASC 470-50-40-2. It is **not** deferred or amortized as part of a new issue.

**Key insight:** Always update the carrying amount (period-end interest + amortization) **before** measuring gain/loss. Reacquisition price vs. net carrying amount of **only the portion extinguished** drives the gain or loss.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Cascade Timber: mid-period update (period-end style adjusting) + full extinguishment + refunding + presentation
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Period-end / to-date adjusting JE on mid-period retirement; full extinguishment loss; concurrent bond refunding; income-statement presentation  
**Scenario:**  
On January 1, Year 1, Cascade Timber Co. issued **$480,000** of **5-year, 6%** bonds at **98** (**$470,400** cash; discount **$9,600**). Cash interest is payable **annually each December 31**. Cascade uses **straight-line** amortization of the discount. Fiscal year-end is December 31.

On **April 1, Year 4**, Cascade retires the **entire** issue in the open market at **102** plus accrued interest. On the same date, Cascade issues **$500,000** of new **10-year, 4%** bonds at **par** (cash interest also annually each December 31) and uses the new proceeds, plus other cash, to fund the retirement.

Assume books are current through December 31, Year 3 (three full years of SL amortization already recorded).

**Required:**  
a. Compute annual straight-line discount amortization and the unamortized discount / carrying amount on January 1, Year 4.  
b. Record the **April 1, Year 4 adjusting entry** that updates interest expense and discount amortization **from January 1 to April 1** for the bonds being retired (accrued interest may be credited to Interest Payable or paid in the retirement cash; use Interest Payable here).  
c. Record the **April 1, Year 4 extinguishment** entry (reacquisition price excludes accrued interest for gain/loss; settle Interest Payable with cash in a separate clear entry or combine — show both approaches’ net cash).  
d. Record issuance of the new 4% bonds on April 1, Year 4.  
e. Classify/present the extinguishment gain or loss and contrast economic motivation when market rates have fallen.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Carrying amount at start of Year 4**  

| Item | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Original discount | $9,600 |
| Annual SL amortization ($9,600 ÷ 5) | **$1,920** |
| Amortized through Dec. 31, Y3 (3 × $1,920) | 5,760 |
| Unamortized discount, Jan. 1, Y4 | **$3,840** |
| Carrying amount, Jan. 1, Y4 ($480,000 − $3,840) | **$476,160** |

**b. April 1, Year 4 — To-date adjusting (emphasis: period-end style update to retirement date)**  
Elapsed fraction of Year 4 before retirement = **3/12**.

| Item | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Discount amortization for 3 months ($1,920 × 3/12) | **$480** |
| Accrued cash interest for 3 months ($480,000 × 6% × 3/12) | **$7,200** |
| Interest expense for 3 months ($7,200 + $480) | **$7,680** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 7,680 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 480 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest Payable | | 7,200 |

*Dr 7,680 = Cr 480 + 7,200.*  
After this entry: unamortized discount = 3,840 − 480 = **$3,360**; carrying amount = 480,000 − 3,360 = **$476,640**.

**c. April 1, Year 4 — Full extinguishment**  

| Item | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Reacquisition price (excluding accrued interest) ($480,000 × 1.02) | **$489,600** |
| Net carrying amount extinguished | **$476,640** |
| **Loss on extinguishment** ($489,600 − $476,640) | **$12,960** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Bonds Payable | 480,000 | |
| Loss on Extinguishment of Debt | 12,960 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,360 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 489,600 |

*Dr 480,000 + 12,960 = 492,960; Cr 3,360 + 489,600 = 492,960.*

**Settle accrued interest**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Payable | 7,200 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 7,200 |

*Total cash out related to retirement = $489,600 + $7,200 = **$496,800** (call price plus accrued interest). Accrued interest is **not** part of the extinguishment gain/loss.*

**d. New bond issue (refunding)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Cash | 500,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bonds Payable | | 500,000 |

*Refunding does **not** change measurement of the loss on the old issue; the new issue is recorded independently at its own proceeds.*

**e. Classification / presentation and motivation**  
- Report the **$12,960 loss** as a **separate item in income** of Year 4 (period of extinguishment) under ASC 470-50-40-2 — **not** as an adjustment to the new bonds’ carrying amount and **not** deferred.  
- Market rates have **fallen** (new 4% debt vs. old 6%): Cascade accepts a **loss** on buyback (price above CV) to replace higher-rate debt with lower-rate debt — classic early extinguishment / refunding economics.  
- If rates had **risen**, open-market reacquisition price would often be **below** CV → **gain** on extinguishment, with less incentive to refinance for interest savings.

**Key insight (emphasis):** Mid-period retirement requires the same work as a period-end adjusting entry: amortize discount/premium and accrue interest **to the extinguishment date** before removing the liability. Skipping that step misstates both interest expense and the gain/loss.

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement choice)
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Classification of extinguishment difference and correct early-retirement sequence  

**Question 1:**  
On an interest payment date, after the books are fully updated, Summit Brass retires bonds with face **$100,000**, unamortized **premium $2,400**, and cash paid to extinguish of **$101,500**. What gain or loss is recognized, and how is it reported?

- A) $1,100 loss, deferred and amortized over the remaining original term  
- B) $3,900 loss, deferred as a reduction of a concurrent new bond issue  
- C) $900 gain, recognized currently in income as a separate item  
- D) $900 gain, recognized only in OCI  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Carrying amount = $100,000 + $2,400 = **$102,400**. Reacquisition price = **$101,500**. Gain = $102,400 − $101,500 = **$900**, recognized **currently in net income** as a separate extinguishment item (ASC 470-50-40-2). Not deferred against a new issue; not OCI.

---

**Question 2:**  
Which sequence correctly accounts for **early** extinguishment of bonds **between** interest dates?

- A) Remove Bonds Payable at face only; ignore unamortized discount and accrued interest  
- B) Update amortization and accrue interest **to the retirement date**, then remove face and remaining unamortized discount/premium, record cash paid for the reacquisition price (accrued interest settled separately and excluded from the gain/loss base), and recognize gain or loss  
- C) Recognize gain or loss only at original maturity  
- D) Charge any call premium entirely to Additional Paid-in Capital  

**Answer:** **B.** Core LO 16-7 sequence: update carrying amount to date → derecognize liability accounts → record consideration transferred → gain/loss = reacquisition price versus net carrying amount of debt extinguished.
