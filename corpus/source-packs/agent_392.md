# Agent 392 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-8

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Prepare a statement of cash flows using the cash T-account approach  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash T-account approach (core Demo 22-8):** reconstruct income-statement and balance-sheet activity as journal entries; post only **cash legs** into a cash T-account organized by **operating / investing / financing**; build the SCF (**direct method** for operating activities) from that T-account
- **Algebraic identity:** \(\Delta\)Cash (including cash equivalents and restricted cash) \(= -\Delta\)Noncash assets \(+ \Delta\)Liabilities \(+ \Delta\)Equity
- **Initial recognition / reconstructed cash JEs:** collections from customers; payments to suppliers, employees, and for interest; cash PPE purchases; cash debt/equity issuances
- **Subsequent measurement schedules:** AR, inventory/AP, salaries payable, equipment, accumulated depreciation, and retained earnings rollforwards used to solve for cash and noncash amounts
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** depreciation (and similar noncash accruals) posted to **noncash** T-accounts only — **never** a line in the cash T-account; contrast with cash-settled operating payments
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement JE:** PPE sale (cash proceeds + remove cost/AD + gain or loss); principal repayment or bond maturity as **financing** cash outflow
- **Classification / presentation:** O/I/F sections from the cash T-account; report investing and financing inflows and outflows **separately**; reconcile beginning to ending **cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash**; gains/losses are not cash lines under the direct/T-account approach (only proceeds appear in investing)

**f. Classification / presentation of the equipment disposal**  
The **loss** is a noncash income-statement amount used only to reconstruct the disposal entry. Under the cash T-account / direct approach, the statement reports only the **cash proceeds of $390,000** as an **investing inflow**. The loss does **not** appear as a separate cash line. (Under the indirect method, the loss would be **added back** to net income in operating activities so that the full proceeds can be shown in investing — same economic result.)

**Key insight:** Period-end adjusting entries such as depreciation complete the **subsequent measurement** of AD and explain the income statement, but they never enter the cash T-account. The cash T-account only receives true cash debits/credits classified as O, I, or F; beginning and ending cash on the SCF include **restricted cash**.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 22-8  
**Concept:** Classification — which reconstructed items appear in the cash T-account; treatment of period-end depreciation AJE and restricted cash in the SCF reconciliation  

**Question 4a.**  
Under the cash T-account approach (Demo 22-8), which of the following reconstructed items is posted to the **cash T-account**?

- A) December 31 period-end depreciation adjusting entry of $78,000  
- B) Loss on sale of equipment of $7,000  
- C) Cash proceeds of $38,000 from sale of equipment  
- D) Removal of $75,000 accumulated depreciation on equipment sold  

**Answer:** C.  
Only true cash legs post to the cash T-account. Period-end depreciation (A), the loss (B), and AD removal (D) are noncash portions of reconstructed entries; they update noncash T-accounts only. Cash proceeds of $38,000 are an **investing inflow**.

**Question 4b.**  
Northpine Logistics reports beginning cash and cash equivalents of $140,000 and restricted cash of $30,000, and ending cash and cash equivalents of $95,000 and restricted cash of $45,000. On the statement of cash flows prepared from the cash T-account, the reconciliation of the change in cash should show:

- A) Beginning $140,000 and ending $95,000 only (exclude restricted cash)  
- B) Beginning $170,000 and ending $140,000 (cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash)  
- C) Beginning $30,000 and ending $45,000 (restricted cash only)  
- D) No beginning/ending cash amounts — only the net change of $(30,000)

**Answer:** B.  
ASC 230 requires the SCF to reconcile the change in **cash, cash equivalents, and amounts generally described as restricted cash**. Beginning \(140{,}000 + 30{,}000 = 170{,}000\); ending \(95{,}000 + 45{,}000 = 140{,}000\); net decrease $30,000.
