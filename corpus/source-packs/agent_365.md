# Agent 365 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-9

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Apply the fair value option for liabilities  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Election of the fair value option (FVO):** irrevocable, instrument-by-instrument, on the election date (generally initial recognition); entire instrument only
- **Initial recognition JE:** issue bonds/notes at proceeds (face, discount, or premium); election itself has no separate JE
- **Subsequent measurement — amortized cost track:** effective-interest amortization of discount/premium continues so amortized cost (AC) is known each period
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** adjust from **amortized cost to fair value** through **Fair Value Adjustment—Bond/Note Payable** (contra or adjunct to the liability)
- **General (base market) risk** (risk-free / benchmark rate changes) → **Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income** (net income)
- **Instrument-specific credit risk** (own credit / covenant risk) → **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI** (other comprehensive income)
- **FVA rollforward schedule:** cumulative FVA so that AC ± FVA = fair value each reporting date; period entry = change in cumulative FVA
- **Settlement / maturity / early extinguishment:** remove face, remaining discount/premium, and FVA; reclassify any remaining **AOCI** credit-risk amounts to net income when the liability is settled or matures

### Q1 — CORE — Discount bonds under FVO: issue, amort schedule, period-end FVA, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition at discount; effective-interest subsequent measurement schedule; **period-end fair value option adjusting JE** (general risk → NI); maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar Ridge Logistics Inc.** (calendar year-end) issues bonds and elects the **fair value option** on the issuance date.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| **January 1, Year 1** — issue date | Face **$250,000**, 3-year term |
| Stated (coupon) rate / payment | **6%** annual cash interest each **December 31** |
| Market yield at issuance | **7%** (annual) |
| Amortization method | **Effective interest** |
| Fair value option | **Elected January 1, Year 1** (irrevocable) |
| **Dec 31, Year 1** fair value | **$238,500** — change entirely from a **general** increase in benchmark rates |
| **Dec 31, Year 2** fair value | **$249,800** — change entirely from **general** market rate movements |
| **Dec 31, Year 3** | Bonds mature; repaid at face after recording interest and clearing FVA |

Cash interest each December 31 = \(250{,}000 \times 0.06 = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\).

Issue price (present value at 7%):  
\[
PV = \$250{,}000 \times PV(7\%,3) + \$15{,}000 \times PVA(7\%,3) = \$243{,}439
\]
(Discount on bonds payable = \(250{,}000 - 243{,}439 = \$6{,}561\).)

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance (initial recognition).  
b. Prepare the **amortized-cost schedule** for Years 1–3 (beginning CV, interest expense, cash, amortization, ending CV).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** interest payment/amortization entry **and** the **period-end FVO adjusting JE** (general risk).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 2** interest entry and the **period-end FVO adjusting JE** (use a cumulative FVA approach). State the **net liability** reported at Dec 31, Year 1 and Dec 31, Year 2.  
e. Record the **December 31, Year 3** interest entry, the final **FVA clearing entry**, and the **maturity settlement** of the bonds.  
f. Briefly state why Year 1–3 unrealized amounts hit **net income** (not OCI).

**Answer key:**

|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 243,439 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 6,561 | |

**Check:** Dr \(243{,}439 + 6{,}561 = 250{,}000\) = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Amortized-cost (effective interest) schedule**

Interest expense = beginning CV × 7%. Cash = $15,000. Amortization increases CV (discount amortization). Final period forced to face.

| Year | Beg. CV (AC) | Interest exp. (7%) | Cash interest | Discount amort. | End. CV (AC) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 243,439 | 17,041 | 15,000 | 2,041 | 245,480 |
| 2 | 245,480 | 17,184 | 15,000 | 2,184 | 247,664 |
| 3 | 247,664 | 17,336 | 15,000 | 2,336 | 250,000 |

**Roll-forward check:** \(243{,}439 + 2{,}041 + 2{,}184 + 2{,}336 = 250{,}000\). Discount fully amortized. **OK.**

**c. December 31, Year 1 — interest and period-end FVO adjustment (emphasis)**

*Interest (amortized-cost track continues under FVO)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 17,041 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,041 |
| Cash | | 15,000 |
| *Effective-interest amortization; cash coupon* | | |

**Check:** Dr 17,041 = Cr \(2{,}041 + 15{,}000\). **Balanced.**

*Period-end FVO adjusting JE — general market risk → NI*

Amortized cost Dec 31, Y1 = **$245,480**  
Fair value = **$238,500**  
Required **debit** balance in Fair Value Adjustment = \(245{,}480 - 238{,}500 = \mathbf{\$6{,}980}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 6,980 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 6,980 |
| *Adjust liability from AC to FV; general risk → earnings* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,980 = Cr 6,980. **Balanced.**

Net liability Dec 31, Y1 = AC − FVA debit = \(245{,}480 - 6{,}980 = \mathbf{\$238{,}500}\) (= FV).

**d. December 31, Year 2 — interest and cumulative FVA update**

*Interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 17,184 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,184 |
| Cash | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr 17,184 = Cr \(2{,}184 + 15{,}000\). **Balanced.**

*Period-end FVO adjusting JE*

Amortized cost Dec 31, Y2 = **$247,664**  
Fair value = **$249,800**  
Target **credit** balance in FVA = \(249{,}800 - 247{,}664 = \mathbf{\$2{,}136}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 9,116 | |

**Check:** Dr 9,116 = Cr 9,116. **Balanced.**

FVA after entry: credit balance **$2,136**.  
Net liability Dec 31, Y2 = \(247{,}664 + 2{,}136 = \mathbf{\$249{,}800}\) (= FV).

| Date | Amortized cost | Fair value | Cumulative FVA (Dr+/Cr−) | Period FVA entry (Dr FVA / Cr FVA) | Unrealized G/L—Income |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Dec 31, Y1 | 245,480 | 238,500 | Dr 6,980 | Dr 6,980 | Gain 6,980 |

| Dec 31, Y3 | 250,000 | 250,000 | 0 | Dr 2,136 (clear) | Gain 2,136 |

**e. December 31, Year 3 — interest, clear FVA, maturity settlement**

*Interest (final amortization to face)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 2,136 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,136 |
| *Remove adjunct FVA so net liability = face at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,136 = Cr 2,136. **Balanced.**

*Maturity settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 250,000 | |
| Cash | | 250,000 |
| *Repay face at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 250,000 = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

**f. NI vs OCI**  
All FV changes in this problem are attributed to **general (benchmark) interest-rate risk**, so unrealized gains/losses are recognized in **net income**. Instrument-specific credit-risk changes would go to **OCI** (see Q3).

**Key insight:** Under the FVO the company still runs the **effective-interest amortized-cost track**, then each reporting date records a **period-end adjusting JE** so that AC ± Fair Value Adjustment equals fair value. General risk → **Income**; the FVA entry amount is the **change in cumulative** FVA, not simply FV − face.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Larger discount issue, multi-period FVA, early extinguishment
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; subsequent AC schedule; **period-end FVO adjusting JEs**; disposal/early settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Fabrication Corp.** (calendar year-end) issues bonds and elects the fair value option at issuance.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| **January 1, Year 1** | Face **$400,000**, 3-year bonds |
| Stated rate / payment | **5%** annual cash interest each **December 31** |
| Market yield at issuance | **6%** |
| Amortization | Effective interest |
| FVO | Elected at issuance |
| **Dec 31, Year 1** FV | **$385,000** (general rate increase) |
| **Dec 31, Year 2** FV | **$401,000** (general rate decrease) |
| **January 1, Year 3** | Entire issue retired for **$399,500** cash (immediately after Dec 31, Year 2 books are closed; no Y3 interest yet) |

Cash interest = \(400{,}000 \times 0.05 = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\).  
Issue price at 6%:  
\[
PV = \$400{,}000 \times PV(6\%,3) + \$20{,}000 \times PVA(6\%,3) = \$389{,}308
\]
Discount = \(400{,}000 - 389{,}308 = \$10{,}692\).

**Required:**  
a. Record issuance on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the 3-year amortized-cost schedule (even though retirement occurs at the start of Year 3).  
c. Record Dec 31, Year 1 interest **and** period-end FVO adjusting JE.  
d. Record Dec 31, Year 2 interest **and** period-end FVO adjusting JE. Present a cumulative FVA schedule through Year 2.  
e. Record the **January 1, Year 3 early extinguishment** (remove BP, remaining discount, and FVA). Compute gain or loss.  
f. State the Dec 31, Year 2 balance-sheet presentation of the bonds under the FVO.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Issuance — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 389,308 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 10,692 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 400,000 |

**Check:** Dr \(389{,}308 + 10{,}692 = 400{,}000\) = Cr 400,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Amortized-cost schedule**

| Year | Beg. CV | Interest exp. (6%) | Cash | Amort. | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 389,308 | 23,358 | 20,000 | 3,358 | 392,666 |
| 2 | 392,666 | 23,560 | 20,000 | 3,560 | 396,226 |
| 3 (planned) | 396,226 | 23,774 | 20,000 | 3,774 | 400,000 |

**Check:** \(389{,}308 + 3{,}358 + 3{,}560 + 3{,}774 = 400{,}000\). **OK.**

**c. December 31, Year 1**

*Interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |

| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,358 |
| Cash | | 20,000 |

*Period-end FVO adjustment (general risk → NI)*  
AC = 392,666; FV = 385,000; FVA **debit** needed = \(392{,}666 - 385{,}000 = \mathbf{\$7{,}666}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 7,666 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 7,666 |

**Check:** Dr 7,666 = Cr 7,666. **Balanced.**  
Net liability = \(392{,}666 - 7{,}666 = \$385{,}000\).

**d. December 31, Year 2**

*Interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 23,560 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,560 |
| Cash | | 20,000 |

*Period-end FVO adjustment*  
Target FVA **credit** = \(401{,}000 - 396{,}226 = \mathbf{\$4{,}774}\)

|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 12,440 | |

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 400,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 4,774 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,774 |
| Cash | | 399,500 |

| *Retire FVO bonds; remove face, remaining discount, and FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(400{,}000 + 4{,}774 = 404{,}774\); Cr \(3{,}774 + 399{,}500 + 1{,}500 = 404{,}774\). **Balanced.**

**f. Balance-sheet presentation (Dec 31, Year 2)**  
Long-term liabilities (or current if due within a year — here still long-term before Jan 1 Y3 decision): bonds reported at **fair value $401,000**, often shown as face less unamortized discount plus/minus fair value adjustment (net $401,000). Unrealized losses of the period are already in **earnings**.

**Key insight:** Early settlement under the FVO removes **face, remaining discount/premium, and the Fair Value Adjustment** so the net liability eliminated equals the last reported fair value; any difference versus cash paid is a **realized** extinguishment gain or loss.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end FVA: general risk (NI) vs instrument-specific credit risk (OCI); multi-year note schedule; settlement with AOCI reclassification
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE emphasis — **general risk → NI** vs **instrument-specific credit risk → OCI**; multi-period FVA schedule; disposal/settlement with **AOCI reclassification to NI**  
**Scenario:**  
**Part A — Meridian Forge Co.** issued **$200,000** face, 3-year, **6%** annual bonds **at face** on **January 1, Year 1** (market rate also 6%). Cash interest = **$12,000** each December 31. Meridian **elected the fair value option** at issuance. Amortized cost remains **$200,000** each year (no discount/premium).

| Reporting date | Fair value | Attribution of FV change vs face |
|---|---:|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | **$185,000** | Entirely **instrument-specific credit risk** (covenant stress; own credit worsened) |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | **$192,000** | Still entirely **instrument-specific credit risk** (partial recovery of credit standing) |
| Jan 5, Year 3 | Retires entire issue for **$191,000** cash (no Year 3 interest accrued yet) |

**Part B — Harborline Packaging LLC** issued a **$500,000** face long-term note on January 1, Year 1 and elected the FVO. After issuance, amortized cost (AC) and fair value (FV) at each year-end were:

| Year-end | Amortized cost | Fair value | Cause of AC–FV difference |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Year 1 | $470,024 | $485,000 | **General** interest-rate changes only |
| Year 2 | $472,326 | $476,000 | **General** interest-rate changes only |
| Year 3 | $474,835 | $474,000 | **General** interest-rate changes only |

(Interest entries are already recorded; focus on FVO adjustments and presentation.)

**Required:**  
a. **Part A:** Record the Dec 31, Year 1 **period-end FVO adjusting JE**.  
b. **Part A:** Record the Dec 31, Year 2 **period-end FVO adjusting JE** (cumulative FVA). State AOCI balance after Year 2.  
c. **Part A:** Record the **January 5, Year 3 settlement**, including reclassification of remaining **AOCI** related to instrument-specific credit risk into net income.  
d. **Part B:** Prepare the **period-end FVO adjusting JE** for Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 (general risk → NI). Include a cumulative FVA schedule.  
e. **Part B:** Show the **balance-sheet carrying amount** of the note at each year-end under the FVO.  
f. Contrast in one sentence when unrealized FVO amounts go to **NI** versus **OCI**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Part A — December 31, Year 1 period-end FVO adjustment (instrument-specific → OCI)**

AC = face = $200,000; FV = $185,000; FVA **debit** = **$15,000**.  
Increase in own credit risk lowers FV of the liability → **unrealized gain in OCI** (not NI).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | 15,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 15,000 |
| *FVO period-end adj.; instrument-specific credit risk → OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**  
Net liability = \(200{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \$185{,}000\). AOCI credit (unrealized gain) = **$15,000**.

**b. Part A — December 31, Year 2 period-end FVO adjustment**

Target FVA **debit** = \(200{,}000 - 192{,}000 = \mathbf{\$8{,}000}\)  
Period **credit** to FVA = \(15{,}000 - 8{,}000 = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\)  
(Credit standing improved → FV rose → **unrealized loss in OCI**.)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 7,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 7,000 |
| *Update cumulative FVA for instrument-specific credit risk* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,000 = Cr 7,000. **Balanced.**  
Cumulative FVA debit = **$8,000**. Cumulative **AOCI credit** = \(15{,}000 - 7{,}000 = \mathbf{\$8{,}000}\).  
Net liability = \(200{,}000 - 8{,}000 = \$192{,}000\).

**Part A FVA / AOCI schedule (instrument-specific only)**

| Date | AC | FV | Cum. FVA Dr | Period entry | AOCI (Cr = net unreal. gain) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | 200,000 | 185,000 | 15,000 | Dr FVA 15,000 / Cr OCI 15,000 | 15,000 Cr |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 200,000 | 192,000 | 8,000 | Dr OCI 7,000 / Cr FVA 7,000 | 8,000 Cr |

**c. Part A — January 5, Year 3 settlement with AOCI reclassification**

Net FVO carrying amount = **$192,000**. Cash paid **$191,000** → **$1,000** economic gain on extinguishment relative to FV carrying amount.  
Also reclassify remaining **AOCI credit $8,000** into net income (instrument-specific amounts reclassified when the liability is redeemed).

*Remove liability and FVA; pay cash*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Bond Payable | | 8,000 |
| Cash | | 191,000 |

**Check:** Dr 200,000 = Cr \(8{,}000 + 191{,}000 + 1{,}000\). **Balanced.**

*Reclassify AOCI (instrument-specific credit risk) to net income*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI (AOCI) | 8,000 | |
| Reclassification to Earnings—FVO Credit Risk (or Gain—Income) | | 8,000 |

After this entry, AOCI related to these bonds = **$0**.

**d. Part B — Period-end FVO adjustments (general risk → NI)**

| Year | AC | FV | Target cum. FVA (Cr+/Dr−) | Prior cum. | Period adj. (Cr FVA if +) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 470,024 | 485,000 | Cr 14,976 | 0 | Cr 14,976 |
| 2 | 472,326 | 476,000 | Cr 3,674 | Cr 14,976 | Dr 11,302 |
| 3 | 474,835 | 474,000 | Dr 835 | Cr 3,674 | Dr 4,509 |

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 14,976 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | | 14,976 |
| *FV > AC; general risk → unrealized loss in NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,976 = Cr 14,976. **Balanced.**  
(Why a **loss**? Liability fair value **increased** → economically more onerous → debit loss / credit adjunct FVA.)

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | 11,302 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 11,302 |
| *Reduce adjunct FVA; general risk → unrealized gain in NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,302 = Cr 11,302. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Note Payable | 4,509 | |

**e. Part B — Balance-sheet carrying amounts under FVO**

| Year-end | AC | Cum. FVA | Reported liability (= FV) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|

**f. NI vs OCI**

**Key insight (emphasis):** The period-end FVO entry always bridges **amortized cost to fair value** via Fair Value Adjustment; the **offset account** (Income vs OCI) depends on **why** fair value moved—benchmark rates versus the issuer’s own credit.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of FVO unrealized amounts
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** General risk → net income; instrument-specific credit risk → OCI  
**Question:**  
On the election date, **Solstice Rail Co.** irrevocably elects the fair value option for a newly issued note payable. At year-end, the note’s fair value is **below** amortized cost solely because **risk-free interest rates rose**. Separately, another FVO bond’s fair value fell solely because Solstice’s **own credit rating deteriorated**. Where are the unrealized gains recognized?

- A) Both unrealized gains in net income  
- B) Both unrealized gains in OCI  
- C) General-rate gain in net income; own-credit gain in OCI  
- D) General-rate gain in OCI; own-credit gain in net income  

**Answer:** **C.**  
A decrease in the liability’s fair value produces an unrealized **gain**. The portion from a change in **general / benchmark rates** is reported in **net income**. The portion from a change in **instrument-specific credit risk** (own credit) is reported in **OCI** (and later reclassified to earnings when the instrument matures or is settled). Election is irrevocable and does not change this split.

### Q5 — MC — Mechanics of the period-end FVO entry
**LO:** LO 16-9  
**Concept:** Fair Value Adjustment bridges amortized cost to fair value; election is instrument-by-instrument and irrevocable  
**Question:**  
Which statement about accounting for a bond liability under the fair value option is **correct**?

- A) After electing the FVO, the company stops amortizing any discount or premium.  
- B) The year-end adjusting entry always debits or credits Bonds Payable directly for the full change in fair value.  
- C) The company may elect the FVO for only the interest-rate risk portion of a bond while leaving credit risk at amortized cost.  
- D) The company continues the effective-interest amortized-cost track and records a Fair Value Adjustment (contra/adjunct) so that AC ± FVA equals fair value each reporting date.  

**Answer:** **D.**  
Amortized cost is still measured with effective interest; the FVO is implemented through a **Fair Value Adjustment** account. The election applies to the **entire instrument** (not selected risks only) and is **irrevocable** on an instrument-by-instrument basis. Bonds Payable face is not overwritten each period.
