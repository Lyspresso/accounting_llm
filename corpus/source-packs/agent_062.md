# Agent 62 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-6

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Describe the accounting treatment for changes in inventory methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Consistency presumption; voluntary change in inventory costing principle
- **Retrospective application** (typical): cumulative effect JE — Inventory ↔ Retained Earnings (ignore tax unless stated)
- Restate prior periods presented; adjust beginning RE for periods before those presented
- **Prospective application** when changing **to LIFO** (layers impracticable to reconstruct): no change-date JE; current beginning inventory becomes LIFO base layer
- Comparative income and inventory **schedules** under restated vs mixed methods
- **Initial recognition JE** of the principle change (debit or credit Inventory depending on direction)
- Classification / presentation / disclosure: method used for each year presented; nature and justification of change
- Distinguish change in principle (inventory method) from inventory errors (LO 10-7)

### Q1 — CORE — Retrospective change LIFO → FIFO (initial recognition JE emphasis)
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for voluntary change from LIFO to FIFO; cumulative inventory–RE adjustment; restated comparative NI and inventory schedules  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar Peak Distributors Inc.** began operations on January 1 of Year 1 and elected the **LIFO** inventory method. Accounting period ends December 31. On January 1 of Year 3, management voluntarily changes to **FIFO** (preferable for matching and external comparability). Taxes are **ignored**. Year 3 results under FIFO: net income **$46,000**; December 31 Year 3 inventory (FIFO) **$90,000**.

Prior-year data:

| | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Net income reported under **LIFO** | $22,000 | $50,000 |
| Net income **if** under **FIFO** | 29,000 | 58,000 |
| Dec. 31 inventory under **LIFO** | 55,000 | 60,000 |
| Dec. 31 inventory **if** under **FIFO** | 62,000 | 75,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3** journal entry to record the change in accounting principle (retrospective). Show how the cumulative amount is measured.  
b. Prepare a **schedule** of net income reported in three-year comparative income statements **after** the change (Year 1–Year 3).  
c. Prepare a **schedule** of inventory reported in three-year comparative balance sheets **after** the change (Dec. 31 Year 1–Year 3).  
d. Briefly state the **balance-sheet classification** of the offsetting credit (or debit) in the change entry and why Inventory is adjusted.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cumulative effect and initial recognition JE**

Difference in inventory at **January 1, Year 3** (= Dec. 31, Year 2 balances):  
FIFO inventory − LIFO inventory = $75,000 − $60,000 = **$15,000**

(Check via income path: cumulative pretax income difference through Year 2  
= (Y1 FIFO − Y1 LIFO) + (Y2 FIFO − Y2 LIFO)  
= ($29,000 − $22,000) + ($58,000 − $50,000) = $7,000 + $8,000 = **$15,000**.  
Also equals Dec. 31 Y2 FIFO − LIFO inventory difference when operations started with zero inventory under both methods.)

*January 1, Year 3 — Retrospective adjustment for change from LIFO to FIFO*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 15,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 15,000 |
| *Increase Inventory to FIFO carrying amount; cumulative effect to beginning RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000. Balanced.

**b. Comparative net income schedule (all years under FIFO after restatement)**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income reported | $29,000 | $58,000 | $46,000 |

**c. Comparative inventory schedule (all years under FIFO)**

| | Dec. 31, Y1 | Dec. 31, Y2 | Dec. 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inventory | $62,000 | $75,000 | $90,000 |

**d. Classification / presentation**  
- **Inventory** remains a **current asset**; the change entry remeasures its carrying amount under the new principle.  
- The offset is to **Retained Earnings** (equity), not current-period income — retrospective principle changes do **not** flow the cumulative prior effect through current net income.  
- Comparative statements present prior years **as if FIFO had always been used**.

**Key insight:** A voluntary change **from** LIFO (or average cost) **to** FIFO is applied **retrospectively**: one **opening** JE equals the difference between old and new inventory at the change date (Dr Inventory / Cr RE when FIFO inventory is higher), and all comparative years are restated under FIFO.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Retrospective LIFO → FIFO twin
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of retrospective LIFO-to-FIFO change: cumulative JE, restated comparative NI and inventory  
**Scenario:**  
**Northridge Components LLC** began operations January 1 of Year 1 using **LIFO**. On January 1 of Year 3 it changes to **FIFO**. Ignore taxes. Year 3 under FIFO: net income **$31,000**; ending inventory **$70,000**.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Net income under **LIFO** | $18,000 | $35,000 |
| Net income **if** under **FIFO** | 23,000 | 40,000 |
| Dec. 31 inventory **LIFO** | 40,000 | 48,000 |
| Dec. 31 inventory **FIFO** | 45,000 | 58,000 |

**Required:**  
a. January 1, Year 3 retrospective change entry.  
b. Three-year comparative net income amounts after the change.  
c. Three-year comparative December 31 inventory amounts after the change.  
d. Verify that the Year 2 inventory difference equals the cumulative NI difference through Year 2.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cumulative difference**  
$58,000 − $48,000 = **$10,000**

*January 1, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 10,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,000. Balanced.

**b. Comparative net income (FIFO for all years presented)**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $23,000 | $40,000 | $31,000 |

**c. Comparative inventory (FIFO)**

| | Dec. 31, Y1 | Dec. 31, Y2 | Dec. 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inventory | $45,000 | $58,000 | $70,000 |

**d. Verification schedule (subsequent measurement / rollforward of cumulative effect)**

| Period | NI FIFO − NI LIFO | Cumulative effect on pretax income |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $5,000 | $5,000 |
| Year 2 | 5,000 | 10,000 |
| = EI FIFO − EI LIFO at Dec. 31, Y2 | | **$10,000** ✓ |

**Key insight:** Same structure as Q1 with all numbers changed: the change-date Inventory/RE adjustment always equals the **cumulative** difference in ending inventory (and cumulative NI) under the two methods through the day before the change.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Prospective change FIFO → LIFO (no change JE)
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Prospective application when changing to LIFO; no change-date JE; mixed-method comparative presentation; LIFO base layer  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Appliance Co.** used **FIFO** in Year 1 and Year 2. On January 1 of Year 3 it changes to **LIFO**. Reconstructing prior LIFO layers is impracticable. Ignore taxes.

Reported / measured amounts:

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $29,000 | $58,000 | $38,000 |
| Inventory, Dec. 31 | 62,000 | 75,000 | 70,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare any **January 1, Year 3** journal entry required for the change to LIFO. If none, explain why and identify the **LIFO base layer**.  
b. Schedule of net income in three-year comparative income statements **after** the change.  
c. Schedule of inventory in three-year comparative balance sheets **after** the change.  
d. Explain the **comparability** limitation and the usual **disclosure** emphasis for a prospective inventory method change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Change-date entry**  
**No journal entry** is recorded on January 1, Year 3.

Reason: Retrospective reconstruction of LIFO layers for prior periods is typically **impracticable** (historical purchase costs by layer unavailable). Under the **prospective** approach, the **December 31, Year 2 / January 1, Year 3 inventory balance of $75,000** (measured under FIFO) becomes the **base layer** (beginning balance) for LIFO going forward. Subsequent layers are built only from Year 3 onward under LIFO cost flow.

**b. Comparative net income (mixed methods — not restated)**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income reported | $29,000 | $58,000 | $38,000 |
| Method underlying | FIFO | FIFO | LIFO |

**c. Comparative inventory**

| | Dec. 31, Y1 | Dec. 31, Y2 | Dec. 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inventory | $62,000 | $75,000 | $70,000 |
| Method underlying | FIFO | FIFO | LIFO |

**d. Comparability / disclosure**  
- Prior years remain under **FIFO**; only Year 3 (and later) use **LIFO** → year-to-year **comparability is impaired**.  
- Disclose the **nature** of the change, that the retrospective approach was **impracticable**, the **effect** of the change on current-period income (and inventory), and that prior periods were **not** restated.  
- No retrospective restatement of Year 1–Year 2 FIFO amounts.

**Key insight:** A change **to LIFO** is generally **prospective**: **zero** opening JE; beginning inventory under the old method becomes the LIFO base; comparative statements show **different methods** across years, so disclosure of limited comparability is critical.

### Q4 — CORE — Average cost → FIFO with inventory decrease; presentation on comparative BS
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Retrospective change when new method yields **lower** inventory (Dr RE / Cr Inventory); restated prior-year inventory on comparative balance sheet  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Trail Outfitters** changed its inventory method from **weighted-average cost** to **FIFO** on January 1 of Year 3. Year-end is December 31. Ignore taxes. Inventory balances:

| Inventory at December 31 | Year 2 | Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Weighted-average cost | $150,000 | $110,000 |
| FIFO | 90,000 | 75,000 |

Assume amounts are material and Summit presents two prior years for comparison with Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the January 1, Year 3 entry to record the change in accounting principle.  
b. State the **December 31, Year 2** inventory amount presented on the **comparative** December 31, Year 3 balance sheet (and the Year 1 comparative amount).  
c. In which direction does the cumulative effect move **assets** and **equity**? Provide the accounting equation effect.  
d. (Optional extension) If weighted-average Year 2 NI had been $80,000 and the only difference between methods through Year 2 is the inventory difference, what would restated Year 2 pretax net income under FIFO be? (Use cumulative inventory difference at Dec. 31 Y2 vs the path of annual differences as needed — show Year 1 and Year 2 NI effects.)

**Answer key:**  

**a. Cumulative effect at Jan. 1, Year 3**  
FIFO EI Y2 − Average EI Y2 = $90,000 − $150,000 = **$(60,000)**  
Inventory must be **reduced** by $60,000; cumulative prior earnings were **overstated** relative to FIFO by $60,000.

*January 1, Year 3 — Retrospective adjustment, average cost → FIFO*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 60,000 | |
| Inventory | | 60,000 |
| *Reduce Inventory to FIFO; cumulative effect decreases beginning RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 60,000. Balanced.

**b. Comparative balance-sheet presentation (restated under FIFO)**  
- Inventory, December 31, Year 2 (as presented with Year 3 statements): **$90,000**  
- Inventory, December 31, Year 1 (if presented): **$75,000**

**c. Accounting equation**  
Assets **−60,000** (Inventory); Equity **−60,000** (Retained Earnings).  
Assets = Liabilities + Equity still holds; no liability is affected by this pure principle change (taxes ignored).

**d. NI effect path (subsequent measurement schedule)**  
Cumulative inventory difference Dec. 31 Y1: $75,000 − $110,000 = **$(35,000)** → Year 1 NI under FIFO lower by $35,000.  
Cumulative inventory difference Dec. 31 Y2: **$(60,000)** → additional Year 2 NI effect = $(60,000) − $(35,000) = **$(25,000)**.  
If average-cost Year 2 NI = $80,000, restated FIFO Year 2 NI = $80,000 − $25,000 = **$55,000**.

**Key insight:** Retrospective inventory method changes can **decrease** Inventory (Cr Inventory, Dr RE) when the new method produces a **lower** carrying amount (e.g., average cost → FIFO in a declining cost environment, or as given by the dual balances). Comparative BS inventory is always the **new-method** amount for periods restated.

### Q5 — MC — Retrospective vs prospective classification
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Classify whether a voluntary inventory method change is applied retrospectively or prospectively  
**Question:**  
Which of the following voluntary inventory method changes is **most likely** applied **prospectively**, with **no** journal entry at the beginning of the year of change?

- A) Change from LIFO to FIFO when FIFO inventory at the change date exceeds LIFO inventory by a material amount  
- B) Change from weighted-average cost to FIFO when both methods’ historical inventory balances are fully available  
- C) Change from FIFO to LIFO when reconstructing prior LIFO layers is impracticable  
- D) Change from FIFO to weighted-average cost when dual ending-inventory balances exist for all prior years presented  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Changes **to LIFO** are generally prospective because past purchase prices needed to rebuild LIFO layers are often unavailable; the pre-change ending inventory becomes the LIFO base layer and **no** cumulative Inventory/RE entry is made. A, B, and D are voluntary changes **from** LIFO or between methods for which dual data exist — those follow **retrospective** application with an Inventory ↔ Retained Earnings cumulative adjustment when practicable.

### Q6 — MC — Effect of retrospective entry on current-year income
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Classification of cumulative effect of retrospective inventory principle change (RE vs current income)  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 5, a company changes from LIFO to FIFO. FIFO inventory exceeds LIFO inventory by $40,000 at that date (taxes ignored). The company correctly records:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 40,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 40,000 |

How should the $40,000 cumulative effect appear in the **Year 5** income statement?

- A) As a $40,000 increase in gross profit (lower COGS)  
- B) As a $40,000 cumulative-effect gain in other comprehensive income  
- C) As a $40,000 separate line item after income from continuing operations  
- D) It should **not** appear in Year 5 net income; prior periods are restated and the cumulative effect adjusts beginning retained earnings  

**Answer:** **D.**  
Under retrospective application, the cumulative effect on periods **before** the earliest year presented (or before the year of change for the books) adjusts **beginning Retained Earnings**. Comparative prior-period financial statements are **recast** under the new method. The $40,000 is **not** a current-period gain, COGS adjustment, or OCI item.
