# Agent 212 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-5

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for changes in depreciation methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition JE for PPE acquired for cash (asset that later undergoes a method change)
- Multi-period **subsequent measurement schedule** under the **original** method (SYD or DDB)
- Change in depreciation method = **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**
- **Prospective** treatment only: no restatement of prior periods; no cumulative catch-up to beginning RE
- At change date: new depreciation from **carrying amount − residual** over **remaining useful life** under the **new** method
- Period-end adjusting JE in the year of (and after) the method change
- Disposal JE after a method change (remove cost and accumulated depreciation; recognize gain/loss)
- Classification / disclosure: nature of the change and effect on current-period income; prior years unchanged

### Q1 — CORE — SYD → SL: purchase, subsequent schedule, change, full rollforward, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule under SYD; prospective change to straight-line; period-end dep JE; disposal after change  
**Scenario:**  
Pinecrest Packaging Inc. purchased specialized packing equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$60,000 cash**. Management estimated a **5-year** useful life and a **$6,000** residual value. The company adopted the **sum-of-the-years’-digits (SYD)** depreciation method.

On **January 1, Year 4**, management changes the depreciation method for this asset to **straight-line**. Residual value and total useful life estimates are **unchanged**. Pinecrest’s year-end is December 31.

On **December 31, Year 5**, after recording Year 5 depreciation, Pinecrest sells the equipment for **$5,200 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** purchase journal entry.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for **Years 1–3 under SYD** (fraction, depreciation expense, ending AD, ending CV). Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end depreciation adjusting entry (format same each SYD year; amounts differ).  
c. Compute **straight-line depreciation for Year 4** after the method change. Record the **December 31, Year 4** period-end adjusting entry.  
d. Prepare a full **rollforward schedule for Years 1–5** (method used, depreciation expense, ending AD, ending CV).  
e. Record **Year 5 depreciation** and the **December 31, Year 5 disposal** journal entry.  
f. In one or two sentences, explain whether prior-year depreciation is restated and how the change is classified under GAAP.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 60,000 |
| *To record purchase of packing equipment* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 60,000.

**b. Subsequent measurement — SYD Years 1–3**  
Sum of years = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = **15**  
Depreciable base = $60,000 − $6,000 = **$54,000**

| Year | Beg. CV | SYD fraction | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 60,000 | 5/15 | **18,000** | 18,000 | 42,000 |
| 2 | 42,000 | 4/15 | **14,400** | 32,400 | 27,600 |
| 3 | 27,600 | 3/15 | **10,800** | 43,200 | 16,800 |

Computations:  
- Year 1: \(5/15 \times 54{,}000 = 18{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(4/15 \times 54{,}000 = 14{,}400\)  
- Year 3: \(3/15 \times 54{,}000 = 10{,}800\)

December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting entry (illustrative of SYD years):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 18,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,000 |
| *Year 1 SYD depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 18,000.  
(Year 2 JE: Dr/Cr **14,400**; Year 3 JE: Dr/Cr **10,800**.)

Carrying amount on **January 1, Year 4** (change date) = **$16,800**.

**c. Prospective straight-line after method change — Year 4**  
Remaining useful life = 2 years. Residual still $6,000.  
\[
\text{Annual SL depreciation} = \frac{\$16{,}800 - \$6{,}000}{2} = \$5{,}400
\]

December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,400 |
| *Year 4 SL depreciation after prospective method change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,400.  
Ending CV Dec 31, Year 4 = 16,800 − 5,400 = **$11,400**.  
Ending AD Dec 31, Year 4 = 43,200 + 5,400 = **$48,600**.

**d. Full rollforward Years 1–5 (subsequent measurement schedule emphasis)**

| Year | Method | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | SYD | 18,000 | 18,000 | 42,000 |
| 2 | SYD | 14,400 | 32,400 | 27,600 |
| 3 | SYD | 10,800 | 43,200 | 16,800 |
| 4 | SL (post-change) | 5,400 | 48,600 | 11,400 |
| 5 | SL | 5,400 | 54,000 | 6,000 |

Total depreciation over life = 18,000 + 14,400 + 10,800 + 5,400 + 5,400 = **$54,000** = depreciable base. Ending CV = residual **$6,000**.

**e. Year 5 depreciation and disposal**

December 31, Year 5 — depreciation:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,400 |

December 31, Year 5 — disposal (sale for $5,200):  
Book value at sale = $6,000; proceeds $5,200 → **loss $800**.  
AD at disposal = **$54,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 5,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 54,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 800 | |
| Equipment | | 60,000 |
| *To record sale of equipment below book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,200 + 54,000 + 800 = **60,000** = Cr 60,000.

**f. Classification / prospective treatment**  
A change in depreciation method is a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**. It is accounted for **prospectively** (current and future periods only). Prior years are **not** restated; beginning retained earnings is **not** adjusted for the cumulative difference between methods.

**Key insight:** After a method change, the **subsequent measurement schedule** restarts from **current carrying amount**, residual, and remaining life under the **new** method. Prior SYD amounts stay as recorded; only the path forward changes.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — DDB → SL twin with multi-period schedule
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; DDB subsequent measurement schedule; prospective change to SL; period-end JE  
**Scenario:**  
Ironvale Machine Works LLC acquired production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$120,000 cash**. Estimated useful life **5 years**; residual value **$15,000**. Ironvale used the **double-declining-balance (DDB)** method for Years 1 and 2.

On **January 1, Year 3**, Ironvale changes to the **straight-line** method for this equipment. Residual value and total useful life remain **$15,000** and **5 years**. Year-end is December 31.

**Required:**  
a. Record the purchase entry on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare a **DDB subsequent measurement schedule for Years 1–2** (beginning BV, rate, expense, ending AD, ending BV). Record each year-end adjusting entry.  
c. Compute Year 3, Year 4, and Year 5 straight-line depreciation after the change; record the **Year 3 period-end JE**.  
d. Show the full **rollforward schedule Years 1–5** and confirm carrying amount at December 31, Year 5 equals residual.  
e. State whether Year 1–2 depreciation would be restated under the change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 120,000 | |
| Cash | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 120,000.

**b. DDB subsequent measurement — Years 1–2**  
DDB rate = \(2 / 5 = 40\%\). Residual is **not** subtracted when applying the DDB rate to beginning book value (but book value is not driven below residual over the life).

| Year | Beg. BV | Rate | Depreciation | End. AD | End. BV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 120,000 | 40% | **48,000** | 48,000 | 72,000 |
| 2 | 72,000 | 40% | **28,800** | 76,800 | 43,200 |

Computations:  
- Year 1: \(120{,}000 \times 0.40 = 48{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(72{,}000 \times 0.40 = 28{,}800\)

December 31, Year 1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 48,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 48,000 |

December 31, Year 2:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 28,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 28,800 |

**Check:** Each JE balances. CV on change date **January 1, Year 3** = **$43,200**.

**c. Prospective SL after change**  
Remaining life = 3 years.  
\[
\text{Annual SL} = \frac{\$43{,}200 - \$15{,}000}{3} = \$9{,}400
\]

December 31, Year 3:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,400 |
| *Year 3 SL depreciation after prospective DDB→SL change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,400.  
Year 4 and Year 5 depreciation are also **$9,400** each (same JE amounts).

**d. Full rollforward Years 1–5**

| Year | Method | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | DDB | 48,000 | 48,000 | 72,000 |
| 2 | DDB | 28,800 | 76,800 | 43,200 |
| 3 | SL (post-change) | 9,400 | 86,200 | 33,800 |
| 4 | SL | 9,400 | 95,600 | 24,400 |
| 5 | SL | 9,400 | 105,000 | 15,000 |

Total depreciation = 48,000 + 28,800 + 9,400 + 9,400 + 9,400 = **$105,000**.  
CV end Year 5 = 120,000 − 105,000 = **$15,000** (equals residual).

**e.** No restatement of Years 1–2. The change is **prospective** only (treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle).

**Key insight:** Under DDB, residual is ignored in the rate application until late in life; after switching to SL, residual is subtracted from **current carrying amount** and allocated evenly over remaining years—this is visible on the **subsequent measurement schedule** at the change date.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end JE in year of change; disposal; classification
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting entry in year of method change; multi-period SYD schedule; disposal after change; classification/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
Redrock Foundry Co. purchased a industrial kiln on **January 1, Year 1** for **$200,000 cash**. Useful life **5 years**; residual value **$20,000**. Depreciation method: **sum-of-the-years’-digits**.

On **January 1, Year 4**, Redrock changes to **straight-line** depreciation. Residual and total life estimates are unchanged.

On **December 31, Year 4**, after recording Year 4 depreciation, Redrock sells the kiln for **$40,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **SYD subsequent measurement schedule for Years 1–3** (expense, AD, CV).  
b. Compute Year 4 depreciation under the new SL method and record the **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting entry only**.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 4 disposal** entry.  
d. **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** (1) How is this change classified? (2) Are comparative prior-year financial statements restated for depreciation? (3) What amount of Year 4 depreciation expense is reported for this asset, and how does that amount compare to what SYD would have been for Year 4?

**Answer key:**  

**a. SYD subsequent measurement — Years 1–3**  
Sum of years = **15**. Depreciable base = $200,000 − $20,000 = **$180,000**.

| Year | SYD fraction | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5/15 | **60,000** | 60,000 | 140,000 |
| 2 | 4/15 | **48,000** | 108,000 | 92,000 |
| 3 | 3/15 | **36,000** | 144,000 | 56,000 |

Computations:  
- Year 1: \(5/15 \times 180{,}000 = 60{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(4/15 \times 180{,}000 = 48{,}000\)  
- Year 3: \(3/15 \times 180{,}000 = 36{,}000\)

Carrying amount January 1, Year 4 = **$56,000**.

**b. Year 4 SL depreciation (period-end adjusting JE)**  
Remaining life = 2 years.  
\[
\frac{\$56{,}000 - \$20{,}000}{2} = \$18{,}000
\]

December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 18,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,000 |
| *Year 4 SL depreciation after prospective SYD→SL change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 18,000.  
CV after Year 4 dep = 56,000 − 18,000 = **$38,000**.  
AD after Year 4 = 144,000 + 18,000 = **$162,000**.

*(If SYD had continued in Year 4: \(2/15 \times 180{,}000 = 24{,}000\). The new SL amount is **$18,000**, which is $6,000 lower because the method—not a prior-period correction—drives the current expense.)*

**c. Disposal December 31, Year 4**  
Proceeds $40,000; book value $38,000 → **gain $2,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 40,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 162,000 | |
| Equipment | | 200,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 2,000 |
| *To record sale of kiln above book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 40,000 + 162,000 = **202,000** = Cr 200,000 + 2,000.

**d. Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
1. Classified as a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**.  
2. **No** — prior years are **not** restated; Years 1–3 SYD amounts remain as reported.  
3. Year 4 depreciation expense for this asset = **$18,000** (new method applied to carrying amount at the change date). Disclosures typically describe the **nature of the change** and the **effect on income** in the period of change (here, relative to continuing SYD, expense is $6,000 lower: 24,000 − 18,000).

**Key insight:** The period-end JE in the year of change uses the **new method only** from the change date forward. Disposal still clears gross cost and full accumulated depreciation; gain/loss is proceeds vs. post-change book value.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of a change in depreciation method
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Classification — change in estimate effected by a change in principle; prospective application  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 3, a company changes from double-declining-balance to straight-line depreciation for equipment acquired in Year 1. Residual value and total useful life are unchanged. Under U.S. GAAP as applied in this chapter’s CORE treatment, how should the company account for the change?

- A) Retrospective restatement of all prior periods presented, with a cumulative catch-up to beginning retained earnings  
- B) Prospective application as a change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle; no restatement of prior depreciation  
- C) Treat as a correction of an error with a prior-period adjustment to retained earnings  
- D) Prospective application only if the cumulative effect on prior years is immaterial; otherwise restate  

**Answer:** **B.** A change in depreciation method is treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle and is applied **prospectively**. Prior financial statements are not restated, and there is no error-correction PPA. Choice A describes a typical voluntary change in accounting principle (not method-of-depreciation treatment). Choice C confuses method changes with errors (LO 12-6). Choice D invents a materiality gate that does not change the CORE prospective rule.

### Q5 — MC — Compute depreciation in year of method change from schedule
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Compute new-method depreciation from carrying amount at change date using subsequent measurement schedule  
**Question:**  
Equipment cost $84,000; residual $4,000; useful life 4 years. SYD was used for Years 1–2. On January 1, Year 3, the company switches to straight-line (estimates unchanged). What is Year 3 depreciation expense?

Sum of years = 10. Depreciable base = $80,000.  
Year 1 SYD = \(4/10 \times 80{,}000 = 32{,}000\).  
Year 2 SYD = \(3/10 \times 80{,}000 = 24{,}000\).

- A) $16,000  
- B) $20,000  
- C) $12,000  
- D) $14,000  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Carrying amount Jan 1, Year 3 = 84,000 − 32,000 − 24,000 = **$28,000**.  
Remaining life = 2 years.  
\[
\text{Year 3 SL} = \frac{\$28{,}000 - \$4{,}000}{2} = \$12{,}000
\]  
Distractors: **A** = continuing SYD for Year 3 (\(2/10 \times 80{,}000\)) instead of applying the new method; **B** = original full-life SL as if used from day one (\(80{,}000 / 4\)); **D** = residual ignored after the change (\(28{,}000 / 2\)).
