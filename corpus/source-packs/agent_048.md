# Agent 48 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-1

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Determine the initial recognition and measurement of inventory  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Control-based recognition of inventory (location vs control)
- Goods in transit: f.o.b. shipping point vs f.o.b. destination
- Consigned inventory (consignor includes; consignee excludes)
- Firm repurchase agreements (seller retains inventory)
- Initial measurement: cash-equivalent acquisition cost + costs to prepare for sale
- Inventoriable vs period/abnormal costs (freight-in vs freight-out; abnormal spoilage)
- Period-end reconciling schedule from physical count to adjusted ending inventory
- Period-end adjusting JEs for unrecorded purchases and count errors
- Consignment settlement (sale by consignee; remove inventory / record COGS)
- Classification / presentation: current asset inventory composition

### Q1 — CORE — Meridian Trail Supply: initial measurement JE, recognition schedule, period-end adj, consignment settlement
**LO:** LO 9-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition and measurement of merchandise inventory (costs + control tests + year-end adj + consignment settlement)  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Trail Supply Co.** is a wholesale merchandiser that uses a **perpetual** inventory system. Year-end is **December 31**.

**Transaction set A — March 5 purchase (initial measurement)**  
Meridian acquires a shipment of camping gear:

| Cost element | Amount | Cash / liability |
|---|---:|---|
| Invoice price from vendor | $60,000 | Accounts payable (n/30) |
| Sales tax on purchase | 3,600 | Accounts payable |
| Freight-in (f.o.b. shipping point) | 1,850 | Cash |
| Transit insurance on the purchase | 450 | Cash |
| Wages to assemble display units for resale | 2,100 | Wages payable |
| Allocated warehouse storage cost before sale | 400 | Cash |
| Abnormal rush freight to replace goods damaged in a flood at the warehouse | 780 | Cash |
| Advertising for the product line (selling) | 1,200 | Cash (already paid separately; **do not inventoriable**) |

**Transaction set B — December 31 physical inventory & control items**  
The unadjusted inventory balance from the **physical count** is **$385,000**. Management reviews the following items before finalizing inventory:

| # | Description | Cost | Treatment note |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Merchandise purchased Dec 28, **f.o.b. shipping point**, still in transit; **not counted**; purchase **not yet recorded** | $32,000 | Purchaser controls |
| 2 | Merchandise purchased Dec 29, **f.o.b. destination**, still in transit; not counted; not recorded | 14,000 | Supplier controls |
| 3 | Goods of **Lumen Decor** held by Meridian on **consignment**; **included** in the physical count | 8,200 | Consignee does not control |
| 4 | Meridian’s goods held by **Alpine Outfitters** on **consignment**; **excluded** from count | 21,500 | Consignor controls |
| 5 | Goods sold Dec 30, **f.o.b. shipping point**, still in transit to customer; excluded from count; sale recorded Dec 30 | 11,000 | Customer controls |
| 6 | Goods sold Dec 31, **f.o.b. destination**, still in transit; **excluded** from count; sale was **incorrectly recorded** Dec 31 (title has not passed) | 7,400 | Meridian still controls; reverse sale |
| 7 | Goods “sold” Dec 15 under a **firm repurchase agreement** (Meridian **obligated** to buy back at a fixed price). Cost $19,000 was removed from Inventory and a sale was recorded. Cash of $22,000 was received. | 19,000 | Financing; inventory remains Meridian’s |

Selling price on item 6 was **$11,100** (on account). Repurchase item 7 cash proceeds **$22,000** (treat original entry as Dr Cash / Cr Sales; restore inventory and reclassify cash as a liability).

**Transaction set C — January 8 consignment settlement**  
Alpine Outfitters sells **$6,000** cost of Meridian’s consigned goods for **$9,500** cash to end customers, deducts a **10%** commission, and remits the net cash to Meridian on January 8.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **inventoriable cost** of the March 5 shipment. Record the **journal entry(ies)** on March 5 for inventoriable costs and for the abnormal freight (exclude advertising already paid). Show Dr = Cr.  
(b) Prepare a **schedule** reconciling unadjusted physical inventory to **adjusted ending inventory** at December 31 (recognition only).  
(c) Record **period-end adjusting journal entries** at December 31 for items 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7 (state any assumptions).  
(d) Record Meridian’s **January 8** entries when the consignee sells and remits (sales, commission, COGS/inventory removal).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial measurement — March 5**

Inventoriable costs:  
\[
60{,}000 + 3{,}600 + 1{,}850 + 450 + 2{,}100 + 400 = \mathbf{\$68{,}400}
\]

Excluded from inventory:  
- Abnormal rush freight **$780** → period expense  
- Advertising **$1,200** → selling expense (already paid; no entry required here)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 68,400 | |
| Abnormal Freight Expense (or Loss — Abnormal Shipping) | 780 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 63,600 |
| Cash | | 3,480 |
| Wages Payable | | 2,100 |

Proof: Dr \(68{,}400 + 780 = 69{,}180\); Cr \(63{,}600 + 3{,}480 + 2{,}100 = 69{,}180\) (Cash = \(1{,}850 + 450 + 780 + 400 = 3{,}480\)). **Balances.**

**(b) Adjusted ending inventory schedule — December 31**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Unadjusted physical inventory count | $385,000 |
| Add: FOB shipping point purchases in transit (item 1) | 32,000 |
| Less: Consignment goods of others included in count (item 3) | (8,200) |
| Add: Own goods out on consignment (item 4) | 21,500 |
| Add: FOB destination sales still in transit (item 6) | 7,400 |
| Add: Inventory under firm repurchase obligation (item 7) | 19,000 |
| **Adjusted ending inventory** | **$456,700** |

No adjustment for item 2 (FOB destination purchases — supplier’s inventory) or item 5 (FOB shipping sales — customer’s inventory).

Checks:  
\(385{,}000 + 32{,}000 = 417{,}000\);  
\(417{,}000 - 8{,}200 = 408{,}800\);  
\(408{,}800 + 21{,}500 = 430{,}300\);  
\(430{,}300 + 7{,}400 = 437{,}700\);  
\(437{,}700 + 19{,}000 = \mathbf{456{,}700}\).

**(c) Period-end adjusting JEs — December 31**

*Item 1 — unrecorded FOB shipping purchase in transit*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 32,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 32,000 |

*Item 3 — remove consignee goods incorrectly included*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory errors / COGS (or do not record if count-only worksheet) | 8,200 | |
| Inventory | | 8,200 |

(If books were set equal to the flawed physical count of $385,000 that **included** Lumen’s goods, credit Inventory $8,200. If books never included consigned goods of others, no book entry—only worksheet exclusion. **Assume count was booked → remove $8,200.**)

*Item 4 — restore own consigned goods omitted from count*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 21,500 | |
| Inventory in transit / COGS (or Consignment-out clearing) | | 21,500 |

(Assume perpetual books dropped the goods when shipped to Alpine without a sale; restore inventory.)

*Item 6 — reverse premature FOB destination sale; restore inventory*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sales Revenue | 11,100 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 11,100 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 7,400 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 7,400 |

Each JE balances.

*Item 7 — firm repurchase: restore inventory; reclassify proceeds as liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sales Revenue | 22,000 | |
| Inventory | 19,000 | |
| Liability under Repurchase Agreement | | 22,000 |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 19,000 |

Proof: Dr \(22{,}000 + 19{,}000 = 41{,}000\); Cr \(22{,}000 + 19{,}000 = 41{,}000\). **Balances.**  
(Inventory remains on Meridian’s balance sheet; cash already received is a financing liability.)

Net inventory effect of (c) under the stated assumptions:  
\(+32{,}000 - 8{,}200 + 21{,}500 + 7{,}400 + 19{,}000 = +71{,}700\), so book inventory moves from $385,000 toward **$456,700**.

**(d) Consignment settlement — January 8 (disposal / settlement)**

Commission = \(9{,}500 \times 10\% = \mathbf{\$950}\). Cash remitted = \(9{,}500 - 950 = \mathbf{\$8{,}550}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,550 | |
| Commission Expense (or Selling Expense) | 950 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 9,500 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 6,000 | |
| Inventory | | 6,000 |

Both entries balance. After settlement, remaining consigned inventory on Meridian’s books = \(21{,}500 - 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$15{,}500}\).

**Key insight:** Inventory is recognized when the company **controls** the goods (title/FOB, consignment, firm repurchase), measured at **cash-equivalent outlays to acquire and prepare for sale**—not selling costs or abnormal losses. Year-end work is a control reconciling schedule plus balanced adjusting JEs; consignment sale removes inventory only when the consignee sells to the end customer.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cascade Ridge Outfitters twin
**LO:** LO 9-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition and measurement (number-variant twin of Q1)  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Ridge Outfitters Inc.** uses a perpetual inventory system. Year-end is December 31.

**Purchase — April 12 (initial measurement)**

| Cost element | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Invoice price | $95,000 |
| Import tariff on goods | 4,750 |
| Freight-in | 2,900 |
| Transit insurance | 650 |
| Labor to package goods for resale | 3,400 |
| Normal handling in receiving | 800 |
| Abnormal spoilage (flood damage to this lot after receipt) | 5,200 |
| Freight-out to deliver a sample to a sales prospect | 1,100 |

**December 31 physical count (unadjusted):** **$512,000**

| # | Item | Cost | Include / Exclude |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Purchases in transit, **f.o.b. shipping point**, not counted, not recorded | $47,500 | Include |
| 2 | Purchases in transit, **f.o.b. destination**, not counted | 18,000 | Exclude |
| 3 | Goods held on consignment for **Northline Brands** (in count) | 11,600 | Exclude |
| 4 | Own goods on consignment at **Summit Gear Shops** (not in count) | 28,000 | Include |
| 5 | Sales in transit, **f.o.b. shipping point** (not in count; sale OK) | 15,200 | Exclude (correct) |
| 6 | Sales in transit, **f.o.b. destination**, sale recorded early @ SP $16,800, cost $10,500 (not in count) | 10,500 | Include; reverse sale |
| 7 | Firm repurchase “sale,” proceeds $30,000 cash, cost $25,000 removed | 25,000 | Include; reverse sale / book liability |

**Consignment settlement Jan 12:** Summit sells $9,000 cost of Cascade’s goods for $14,000; 12% commission; remits net cash.

**Required:**  
(a) Inventoriable cost of April 12 purchase and balanced JE (include abnormal spoilage as expense in same compound entry if paid/recognized with the lot).  
(b) Adjusted EI schedule.  
(c) Period-end JEs for items 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 (same assumptions as Q1).  
(d) January 12 consignment settlement entries.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Inventoriable cost**  
\[
95{,}000 + 4{,}750 + 2{,}900 + 650 + 3{,}400 + 800 = \mathbf{\$107{,}500}
\]  
Exclude: abnormal spoilage **$5,200**; freight-out **$1,100**.

Assume invoice + tariff on AP; freight-in, insurance, packaging wages, normal handling, and abnormal spoilage on cash/payables as follows: AP = \(95{,}000 + 4{,}750 = 99{,}750\); wages payable packaging = 3,400; cash for freight + insurance + handling + spoilage = \(2{,}900 + 650 + 800 + 5{,}200 = 9{,}550\).

| Wages Payable | | 3,400 |
| Cash | | 9,550 |

Proof: Dr \(107{,}500 + 5{,}200 = 112{,}700\); Cr \(99{,}750 + 3{,}400 + 9{,}550 = 112{,}700\). **Balances.**  
(Freight-out $1,100, if paid, is Dr Delivery/Freight-out Expense Cr Cash—separate selling cost, not inventory.)

**(b) Adjusted EI**

Check: \(512{,}000 + 47{,}500 = 559{,}500\); \(559{,}500 - 11{,}600 = 547{,}900\); \(547{,}900 + 28{,}000 = 575{,}900\); \(575{,}900 + 10{,}500 = 586{,}400\); \(586{,}400 + 25{,}000 = \mathbf{611{,}400}\).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — period-end adjusting schedule & classification presentation
**LO:** LO 9-1  
**Concept:** Period-end inventory recognition adjustments and balance-sheet classification/presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinnacle Housewares LLC** took a physical inventory on **December 31** and recorded the count of **$274,000** to the perpetual Inventory account (books now equal the raw count). The controller later compiles control and measurement corrections **before** statements are issued:

| Item | Detail | $ |
|---|---|---:|
| A | Goods purchased **f.o.b. shipping point** left vendor’s dock Dec 30; arrived Jan 4; purchase invoice $16,800 **already recorded** Dec 30 but goods **not in count** | 16,800 |
| B | Goods purchased **f.o.b. destination**; left vendor Dec 29; arrived Jan 2; **neither recorded nor counted** | 9,300 |
| C | Pinnacle is **consignee** for $5,400 of SilverOak goods **in the warehouse and in the count** | 5,400 |
| D | Pinnacle is **consignor**; $12,750 of its goods sit at a dealer; **not counted** | 12,750 |
| E | Customer shipment **f.o.b. destination** left Pinnacle Dec 31; cost $4,200; **removed from inventory and sold** on books Dec 31 | 4,200 |
| F | Inventory under **obligatory repurchase**; cost $8,000 remains off books after a “sale” | 8,000 |
| G | **Measurement add-on:** freight-in and import fees on item A’s shipment **not yet recorded** | 1,150 |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare a **reconciling schedule** from booked physical count to correct inventory for the balance sheet.  
(b) Record **all adjusting journal entries** needed so Inventory and related accounts are correct (assume sale on E was Dr AR $6,500 / Cr Sales $6,500 and Dr COGS $4,200 / Cr Inventory $4,200).  
(c) **Classification / presentation:** State the **line-item presentation** of the corrected inventory on the classified balance sheet and list which of A–G (if any) would appear as a **purchase commitment disclosure** rather than as on-balance-sheet inventory. Briefly note how firm repurchase liability (if cash was received) is presented.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Reconciling schedule**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory per books (physical count) | $274,000 |
| + A: FOB shipping goods in transit (control passed; not counted) | 16,800 |
| + G: Freight-in / import fees on A (inventoriable) | 1,150 |
| No effect B: FOB destination purchases (exclude) | — |
| − C: Consignee goods in count | (5,400) |
| + D: Own goods on consignment | 12,750 |
| + E: FOB destination sale — restore inventory | 4,200 |
| + F: Firm repurchase — restore inventory | 8,000 |
| **Correct inventory for BS** | **$311,500** |

Check: \(274{,}000 + 16{,}800 = 290{,}800\); \(+1{,}150 = 291{,}950\); \(-5{,}400 = 286{,}550\); \(+12{,}750 = 299{,}300\); \(+4{,}200 = 303{,}500\); \(+8{,}000 = \mathbf{311{,}500}\).

**(b) Adjusting journal entries**

*A + G — goods and freight already purchased but not in count*  
(Purchase of $16,800 already on books → only add omitted goods to Inventory via count correction and record unrecorded freight:)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 16,800 | |
| Inventory count adjustment / COGS | | 16,800 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 1,150 | |
| Accounts Payable (or Cash) | | 1,150 |

*B — no entry* (control not transferred; do not record purchase or inventory).

*C — remove consignee goods*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory count adjustment / COGS | 5,400 | |
| Inventory | | 5,400 |

*D — add consignor goods*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 12,750 | |
| Inventory count adjustment / COGS | | 12,750 |

*E — reverse premature sale; restore inventory*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sales Revenue | 6,500 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 6,500 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 4,200 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 4,200 |

*F — restore inventory under firm repurchase*  
(If sale/COGS previously recorded and cash received as “sales,” reverse and book liability—amounts for cash/sales depend on original proceeds. **Inventory side:**)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 8,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 8,000 |

(And reverse Sales / establish Liability under Repurchase for the cash proceeds amount when known.)

All listed JEs balance (Dr = Cr on each).

**(c) Classification / presentation**

- Corrected inventory **$311,500** is reported as a **current asset** — **Inventory** (merchandise) on the classified balance sheet; often one line, sometimes with note breakdown (e.g., merchandise on hand, in transit, on consignment).  
- **Item B** ($9,300 FOB destination in-transit purchases) is **not** inventory of Pinnacle; if material noncancelable purchase commitments exist for goods not yet controlled, they may require **note disclosure of purchase commitments**, not balance-sheet inventory.  
- Inventory under **firm repurchase** stays in **Inventory (current asset)**; the related **Liability under Repurchase Agreement** is a **liability** (typically current if repurchase is within one year)—not a sale.  
- Consignment: Pinnacle as consignor includes D; as consignee excludes C (may disclose goods held for others, but not as its asset).

**Key insight:** Period-end inventory is a **control reconciling schedule** plus balanced adjustments; presentation is **current-asset inventory** for goods controlled, while noncontrolled purchase commitments are **disclosure**, and repurchase financings keep inventory on the seller’s books with a liability.

### Q4 — MC — classification of inventoriable costs and goods
**LO:** LO 9-1  
**Concept:** Classification — include in inventory vs expense / exclude  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **correctly measured as inventoriable cost** of a merchandiser (included in Inventory when the related goods are controlled)?

- A) Freight-out on a delivery to a customer under f.o.b. destination terms  
- B) Abnormal spoilage of merchandise caused by a warehouse flood  
- C) Import tariffs and freight-in on goods purchased f.o.b. shipping point that are still in transit at year-end  
- D) General administrative salaries of the accounting department  

**Answer:** **C.** Import tariffs and freight-in are part of the cash-equivalent cost to acquire inventory and bring it to condition and location for sale; FOB shipping point goods in transit are controlled by the purchaser and included in inventory. Freight-out is a selling cost; abnormal spoilage is a period charge; G&A salaries are period expenses (ASC 330 product vs period cost logic).

### Q5 — MC — control recognition  
**LO:** LO 9-1  
**Concept:** Classification — goods in transit / consignment control test  
**Question:**  
On December 31, which item should **Bayfront Merchandising** **include** in its inventory?

- A) Goods Bayfront holds as **consignee** for a supplier, ready for sale in Bayfront’s store  
- B) Goods Bayfront purchased, still in transit, shipped **f.o.b. destination**, not yet received  
- C) Goods Bayfront sold, still in transit to the customer, shipped **f.o.b. shipping point**  
- D) Goods Bayfront owns that are held by a dealer under a **consignment** arrangement (Bayfront is consignor)

**Answer:** **D.** Consignor retains control/title until the consignee sells to the end customer, so consigned-out goods remain in the consignor’s inventory. Consignee goods (A) are excluded; FOB destination purchases (B) remain the seller’s until receipt; FOB shipping sales (C) belong to the customer once released to the carrier.
