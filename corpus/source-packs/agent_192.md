# Agent 192 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-5

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for purchase commitments  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Firm purchase commitments** that are **noncancelable and material**: disclose; recognize an **unrealized loss** when contract price exceeds **NRV** (FIFO / average / other non-LIFO) or **market** (LIFO / retail inventory method)
- **Initial recognition (contract date):** ordinarily **no JE** if contract price ≤ NRV/market (executory contract); disclose material terms
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** roll forward remaining units, contract dollars, NRV/market dollars, estimated liability balance, and cumulative loss recognized after each event (partial take, year-end, interim recovery, settlement)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment; Cr Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment for (remaining contract $ − NRV/market of open units)
- **Subsequent recovery of an estimated loss is not recognized** (liability stays until settlement or further decline)
- **Settlement / purchase JE:** Inventory (current NRV or market) + remove Estimated Liability + additional Loss if further decline; credit Cash (or AP) for **contract** price
- **Partial deliveries:** measure liability only on **remaining** open units; allocate prior liability to units taken at settlement of that tranche
- **No loss** when firm sales contracts (or similar) ensure disposition of future inventory **without price concession** (ASC 330-10-35-18); cancelable commitments do not support loss recognition

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-date subsequent measurement schedule with partial take, year-end loss, recovery ignored, and residual settlement
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule for remaining committed units; period-end loss JE; no reverse for recovery; settlement of residual commitment  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Catalysts Inc.** uses a **perpetual** inventory system and the **FIFO** inventory method (measure purchase-commitment declines against **net realizable value**). Year-end is **December 31**. Assume no selling costs, so stated unit selling prices equal NRV. Commitments are **noncancelable and material**. No firm sales contracts protect Redwood from price declines on the committed goods.

Events:

1. **October 1, Year 1:** Enters a noncancelable contract to purchase **75,000** units of specialty resin at **$48** per unit (**$3,600,000** total) for delivery no later than **April 30, Year 2**. NRV on the contract date is **$50** per unit.
2. **November 20, Year 1:** Purchases and pays cash for **15,000** units under the contract. NRV that day is **$49** per unit.
3. **December 31, Year 1:** NRV of resin still under the open commitment is **$45.50** per unit.
4. **January 31, Year 2 (interim reporting date):** NRV recovers to **$46.50** per unit (still below contract). Management prepares interim statements.
5. **February 15, Year 2:** Purchases the remaining **60,000** units for cash at the contract price. NRV that day is **$44.80** per unit.

**Required:**  
(a) Journal entry, if any, on **October 1, Year 1** (contract initiation / initial recognition).  
(b) Journal entry on **November 20, Year 1** for the partial purchase under the commitment.  
(c) Compute remaining units and record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**.  
(d) Journal entry, if any, on **January 31, Year 2** for the NRV recovery.  
(e) Journal entry on **February 15, Year 2** for settlement of the residual commitment.  
(f) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (emphasis) after each date in (a)–(e) with columns: Date / Event; Units remaining; Remaining contract $; NRV of open units $; Estimated liability balance; Cumulative loss recognized.  
(g) Briefly state Year 1 classification/presentation/disclosure for the estimated loss and liability.

**Answer key:**

**(a) October 1, Year 1 — contract initiation**  
Contract unit price **$48** < NRV **$50**. The commitment is executory; **no journal entry**.  
Material noncancelable commitment is **disclosed** (quantity, fixed price, timing).

**(b) November 20, Year 1 — partial purchase under commitment**  
Units acquired under the firm commitment are recorded at **contract cost**. NRV ($49) > contract ($48); no loss on units taken.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 720,000 | |
| Cash | | 720,000 |
| *15,000 × $48 contract price* | | |

**Check:** Dr 720,000 = Cr 720,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**  
Units remaining = 75,000 − 15,000 = **60,000**  
Remaining contract $ = 60,000 × $48 = **$2,880,000**  
NRV of open units = 60,000 × $45.50 = **$2,730,000**  
Estimated loss / liability = $2,880,000 − $2,730,000 = **$150,000**  
(Equivalently: 60,000 × ($48 − $45.50) = 60,000 × $2.50 = **$150,000**)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 150,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 150,000 |
| *Recognize unrealized loss on remaining firm commitment; contract > NRV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 150,000 = Cr 150,000. **Balanced.**  
Liability is measured only on **open** units, not the original 75,000.

**(d) January 31, Year 2 — NRV recovery to $46.50**  
Economic exposure improves by 60,000 × ($46.50 − $45.50) = **$60,000**, but **subsequent recovery of an estimated purchase-commitment loss is not recognized**.

**No journal entry.** Estimated liability remains **$150,000**.

**(e) February 15, Year 2 — settle residual 60,000 units**  
Inventory at current NRV = 60,000 × $44.80 = **$2,688,000**  
Remove liability recognized at YE = **$150,000**  
Additional loss since YE (from last recognized NRV $45.50 to $44.80) = 60,000 × $0.70 = **$42,000**  
Cash paid = 60,000 × $48 = **$2,880,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 2,688,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 150,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 42,000 | |
| Cash | | 2,880,000 |
| *Acquire remaining inventory under commitment; clear liability; further NRV decline* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,688,000 + 150,000 + 42,000 = **2,880,000** = Cr Cash. **Balanced.**

**(f) Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**

| Date | Event | Units rem. | Remaining contract $ | NRV of open units $ | Est. liability bal. | Cumulative loss recognized |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Oct 1, Y1 | Contract signed | 75,000 | 3,600,000 | 3,750,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Nov 20, Y1 | After partial purchase (15,000) | 60,000 | 2,880,000 | 2,940,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | Year-end measurement | 60,000 | 2,880,000 | 2,730,000 | **150,000** | **150,000** |
| Jan 31, Y2 | Interim NRV recovery (no reverse) | 60,000 | 2,880,000 | 2,790,000 | **150,000** | **150,000** |
| Feb 15, Y2 | After residual settlement | 0 | 0 | n/a | **0** | **192,000** |

Cumulative loss check: Year 1 **$150,000** + Year 2 additional **$42,000** = **$192,000** = total contract paid on residual tranche **$2,880,000** − inventory recorded **$2,688,000**. (The 15,000 units taken Nov 20 had no purchase-commitment loss.)

**(g) Classification / presentation / disclosure (Year 1)**  
- **Income statement:** Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment **$150,000** in other expenses (or with inventory holding losses).  
- **Balance sheet (Dec 31, Y1):** Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment **$150,000** (typically **current**, given delivery by April Y2).  
- **Notes:** Disclose the material firm purchase commitment (units, price, period) and **net losses recognized on firm purchase commitments** (ASC 330-10-50).

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** tracks remaining units and rolls the estimated liability only when contract price exceeds NRV of **open** units. Interim recoveries do not reverse the liability. At settlement, inventory is booked at **then-current NRV**, the liability is cleared, and any **further** decline since the last recognized measurement is additional loss.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Full commitment cycle with all new quantities and prices
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — subsequent measurement schedule; YE loss recognition; settlement with further NRV decline  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Extrusions Co.** uses perpetual inventory and **FIFO** (declines vs **NRV**). Year-end December 31. No selling costs; stated unit prices = NRV. Noncancelable, material commitment; no firm sales contracts protecting against declines.

- **September 8, Year 1:** Noncancelable contract to purchase **90,000** units at **$22** per unit (**$1,980,000**). NRV on that date: **$23** per unit. Delivery through May of Year 2.
- **December 31, Year 1:** NRV **$20** per unit on the full open commitment (no units taken yet in Year 1).
- **April 2, Year 2:** Purchases all **90,000** units for cash at the contract price; NRV that day **$19.25** per unit.

**Required:**  
(a) Entries (or state none) on Sep 8 Y1, Dec 31 Y1, and Apr 2 Y2.  
(b) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Sep 8 Y1 → Dec 31 Y1 → Apr 2 Y2 (after settlement): remaining units, contract $, NRV $, liability balance, cumulative loss.  
(c) Inventory cost recorded on Apr 2 Y2; total purchase-commitment losses by year.

**Answer key:**

**(a)**

**September 8, Year 1:** Contract $22 < NRV $23 → **no journal entry** (disclose material commitment).

**December 31, Year 1:**  
Contract = 90,000 × $22 = **$1,980,000**  
NRV = 90,000 × $20 = **$1,800,000**  
Estimated loss = **$180,000** (= 90,000 × $2)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 180,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 180,000 |

**Check:** Dr 180,000 = Cr 180,000. **Balanced.**

**April 2, Year 2:**  
Inventory = 90,000 × $19.25 = **$1,732,500**  
Liability removed = **$180,000**  
Additional loss = 90,000 × ($20 − $19.25) = 90,000 × $0.75 = **$67,500**  
Cash = **$1,980,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 1,732,500 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 180,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 67,500 | |
| Cash | | 1,980,000 |

**Check:** 1,732,500 + 180,000 + 67,500 = **1,980,000**. **Balanced.**

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Event | Units rem. | Contract $ | NRV $ | Est. liability | Cum. loss |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sep 8, Y1 | Contract signed | 90,000 | 1,980,000 | 2,070,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | Year-end | 90,000 | 1,980,000 | 1,800,000 | **180,000** | **180,000** |
| Apr 2, Y2 | After settlement | 0 | 0 | n/a | **0** | **247,500** |

**(c)** Inventory recorded = **$1,732,500** (current NRV, not contract cost).  
Year 1 loss **$180,000**; Year 2 additional loss **$67,500**; **total $247,500** (= $1,980,000 − $1,732,500).

**Key insight:** Same measurement path as Q1 with all new numbers: YE loss = remaining contract $ − NRV; settlement books inventory at **current** NRV and picks up any incremental decline since YE.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-period LIFO (market) schedule: YE loss, partial settlement allocating liability, second YE further decline, final settlement
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Multi-period subsequent measurement under LIFO (market); allocate prior liability on partial take; second year-end further decline; final settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Tundra Steel Works** uses a **perpetual** inventory system and the **LIFO** inventory method (measure purchase-commitment declines against **market**). Year-end is **December 31**. Noncancelable, material commitment; unprotected by firm sales contracts.

Events:

1. **June 1, Year 1:** Noncancelable contract to purchase **200,000** units of billet steel at **$6.50** per unit through **March 31, Year 3**. Market on June 1 = **$6.50**.
2. **December 31, Year 1:** Market = **$6.10** per unit (no units taken yet).
3. **June 30, Year 2:** Purchases and pays cash for **80,000** units under the contract. Market that day = **$5.95** per unit.
4. **December 31, Year 2:** Market on the **remaining** open units = **$5.80** per unit.
5. **March 1, Year 3:** Purchases the remaining **120,000** units for cash at the contract price. Market that day = **$5.70** per unit.

**Required:**  
(a) Entry, if any, on **June 1, Year 1**.  
(b) **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting JE and computation.  
(c) **June 30, Year 2** partial settlement JE (inventory at market; allocate the YE1 liability to units taken; additional loss if any). State remaining liability after the take.  
(d) **December 31, Year 2** period-end adjusting JE for further market decline on remaining units.  
(e) **March 1, Year 3** final settlement JE.  
(f) Full **subsequent measurement schedule** after each of (a)–(e): units remaining, remaining contract $, market of open units $, estimated liability balance, cumulative loss recognized.  
(g) How would Dec 31, Year 1 change if Tundra held **firm sales contracts** ensuring sale of all committed units without price concession?

**Answer key:**

**(a) June 1, Year 1**  
Contract price equals market → **no journal entry**. Disclose material commitment.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — period-end**  
Contract total = 200,000 × $6.50 = **$1,300,000**  
Market of committed units = 200,000 × $6.10 = **$1,220,000**  
Estimated loss / liability = **$80,000** (= 200,000 × $0.40)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 80,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr 80,000 = Cr 80,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) June 30, Year 2 — partial settlement of 80,000 units**  
Per-unit liability from YE1 = $80,000 / 200,000 = **$0.40**  
Liability allocated to units taken = 80,000 × $0.40 = **$32,000**  
Inventory at current market = 80,000 × $5.95 = **$476,000**  
Additional loss since YE1 = 80,000 × ($6.10 − $5.95) = **$12,000**  
Cash = 80,000 × $6.50 = **$520,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 476,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 32,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 12,000 | |
| Cash | | 520,000 |

**Check:** 476,000 + 32,000 + 12,000 = **520,000**. **Balanced.**  
Remaining liability after take = $80,000 − $32,000 = **$48,000** (on 120,000 open units at $0.40).

**(d) December 31, Year 2 — further decline on remaining 120,000**  
Remaining contract $ = 120,000 × $6.50 = **$780,000**  
Market of open units = 120,000 × $5.80 = **$696,000**  
Required estimated liability = **$84,000** (= 120,000 × $0.70 decline from contract, or 120,000 × ($6.10 − $5.80) further decline **$36,000** above the $48,000 still on books)

Additional YE2 adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 36,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 36,000 |
| *Increase liability: required 120,000 × ($6.50 − $5.80) = $84,000 − carrying $48,000* | | |

Measurement basis: required liability on open units = 120,000 × ($6.50 − $5.80) = **$84,000**; carrying liability after partial take **$48,000**; additional period-end loss **$36,000** (= 120,000 × ($6.10 − $5.80) further decline from the last recognized market of $6.10).

**Check:** Dr 36,000 = Cr 36,000. **Balanced.** Liability balance after YE2 = **$84,000**.

**(e) March 1, Year 3 — final settlement of 120,000 units**  
Inventory = 120,000 × $5.70 = **$684,000**  
Liability removed = **$84,000**  
Additional loss = 120,000 × ($5.80 − $5.70) = **$12,000**  
Cash = 120,000 × $6.50 = **$780,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 684,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 84,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 12,000 | |
| Cash | | 780,000 |

**Check:** 684,000 + 84,000 + 12,000 = **780,000**. **Balanced.**

**(f) Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**

| Date | Event | Units rem. | Remaining contract $ | Market of open $ | Est. liability bal. | Cumulative loss recognized |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jun 1, Y1 | Contract signed | 200,000 | 1,300,000 | 1,300,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | Year-end #1 | 200,000 | 1,300,000 | 1,220,000 | **80,000** | **80,000** |
| Jun 30, Y2 | After partial take 80,000 | 120,000 | 780,000 | 714,000 | **48,000** | **92,000** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | Year-end #2 further decline | 120,000 | 780,000 | 696,000 | **84,000** | **128,000** |
| Mar 1, Y3 | After final settlement | 0 | 0 | n/a | **0** | **140,000** |

Cumulative loss rollforward check:  
Y1 loss **80,000** + Jun 30 Y2 addl **12,000** + YE2 addl **36,000** + Mar 1 Y3 addl **12,000** = **140,000**.  
Total cash paid under contract = 200,000 × $6.50 = **$1,300,000**.  
Inventory recorded = 476,000 + 684,000 = **$1,160,000**.  
Difference = **$140,000** total purchase-commitment losses. ✓

**(g) Firm sales contracts protecting disposition**  
If firm sales contracts (or similar circumstances) ensure sale of the future inventory **without price concession**, the utility of the commitment is **not impaired** → **no estimated loss or liability** at Dec 31, Year 1 (ASC 330-10-35-18). Material commitments may still be disclosed.

**Key insight:** Under multi-period commitments, the **subsequent measurement schedule** is the control document: after each partial take, reduce the liability for units settled; at each later reporting date, reset the liability to (remaining contract $ − current market of open units). LIFO uses **market**; FIFO would use **NRV**—same JE skeleton.

### Q4 — MC (measurement / recognition choice)
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Measuring estimated liability on remaining purchase commitments; recovery and inception recognition rules  

**Question 1:**  
On March 1, Year 1, **Northfield Alloys** entered a two-year **noncancelable** contract to purchase **150,000** units of raw materials at **$40** per unit (equal to market on that date). On August 20, Year 1, Northfield purchased and paid for **50,000** units at **$40**. On December 31, Year 1, market was **$37** per unit. Northfield uses **LIFO**. What **estimated liability on purchase commitments** should Northfield report at December 31, Year 1?

- A) $0  
- B) $450,000  
- C) $300,000  
- D) $3,700,000  

**Answer:** **C. $300,000.**  
Remaining units = 150,000 − 50,000 = **100,000**.  
Liability = 100,000 × ($40 − $37) = **$300,000**.  
A is wrong because the remaining firm commitment is underwater. B uses the original 150,000 × $3 and ignores the partial purchase. D multiplies remaining units by market price instead of the per-unit decline.

---

**Question 2:**  
**Crestline Foods** uses FIFO. On November 15, Year 1 it signed a **noncancelable** material purchase commitment at a fixed unit price **equal to** then-current NRV. At December 31, Year 1, NRV has fallen below the contract price and Crestline recorded Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment of **$45,000**. On March 31, Year 2 (interim), NRV partially recovers so the economic exposure is only **$20,000**. No units have been purchased yet. Which is correct for March 31, Year 2?

- A) Debit Estimated Liability $25,000 and credit Gain (or reverse Loss) $25,000.  
- B) Debit Estimated Liability $45,000 and credit Inventory $45,000.  
- C) **No journal entry**; leave the $45,000 liability until settlement or a further decline requiring a higher liability.  
- D) Debit Estimated Loss $20,000 and credit Estimated Liability $20,000 (replace the prior balance).  

**Answer:** **C.** Subsequent recovery of an estimated loss on a firm purchase commitment is **not recognized**. The liability remains **$45,000** until goods are acquired (or until a later measurement requires a **larger** liability). A improperly books a recovery/gain. B confuses the liability with inventory already on hand. D would reduce the liability by resetting to $20,000, which is also a prohibited recovery recognition.
