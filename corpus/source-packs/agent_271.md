# Agent 271 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-8

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-OCI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of AFS debt at acquisition cost (par path)
- Interest revenue recognized in **net income** (coupon on amortized cost)
- **Period-end-only** Fair Value Adjustment through **OCI** (Appendix 14B / LO 14-8 — no sale-date FV adjust)
- Subsequent-year FVA bridge: required FVA − existing FVA
- Sale: realized G/L = cash proceeds − amortized cost in **NI**; leave FVA until year-end
- Year-end FVA cleanup after disposal and AOCI reclassification disclosure
- Multi-security AFS portfolio: single period-end FVA plug after partial sale
- Classification / presentation: BS at FV; unrealized in AOCI; reclass out of AOCI into NI on sale

### Q1 — CORE — Full AFS life cycle (period-end-only FV-OCI; emphasis on period-end adjusting JEs)
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE + interest in NI + multi-year period_end_adjusting_JE (FVA through OCI) + disposal without sale-date FV + year-end FVA elimination + AOCI reclass disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **July 1 of Year 1**, **Silveroak Capital LLC** purchased for **par $55,000** face of **Westbrook Corp. 6%** bonds that mature on June 30 of Year 6. Cash interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Silveroak classifies the investment as **available-for-sale (AFS)** and applies the **Appendix 14B / LO 14-8 method**: fair value is adjusted **only at period-end** (not at the sale date). Silveroak’s annual reporting period ends December 31. Beginning FVA—AFS and AOCI related to these bonds are zero.

Additional facts:
- December 31, Year 1 fair value of Westbrook bonds = **$52,250**
- No purchases or sales of AFS in Year 2; December 31, Year 2 fair value = **$56,650**
- **January 1 of Year 3:** Silveroak sells the entire Westbrook holding for **$57,400** cash (sale on an interest date; ignore accrued interest)
- No other AFS holdings remain after the sale; December 31, Year 3 required FVA ending balance = **$0**

**Required:**  
(a) Record the July 1, Year 1 **purchase**.  
(b) Record receipt of **cash interest** on December 31, Year 1.  
(c) Record the December 31, Year 1 **period-end fair value adjusting entry** (FV-OCI).  
(d) Report Year 1 effects on the **income statement**, **statement of comprehensive income**, and **December 31 Year 1 balance sheet** for this investment (AOCI begins at zero).  
(e) Record the December 31, Year 2 **period-end fair value adjusting entry**. Show the FVA rollforward schedule (required vs existing).  
(f) Record the January 1, Year 3 **sale** under the **period-end-only** method (do **not** adjust FVA at the sale date).  
(g) Record the December 31, Year 3 entry to **eliminate** the Fair Value Adjustment account.  
(h) Prepare the Year 3 **Accumulated OCI reconciliation / reclassification disclosure** (beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclassification to NI, ending AOCI).

**Answer key:**

**(a) July 1, Year 1 — Purchase at par (initial recognition)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Westbrook Bonds | 55,000 | |
| Cash | | 55,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$55,000**. Amortized cost basis = **$55,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest revenue in net income**  
Cash interest = $55,000 × 0.06 × 6/12 = **$1,650**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,650 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,650 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$1,650**. Interest is recognized in **net income** for all debt categories (including AFS). Amortized cost remains **$55,000** (purchased at par).

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end FV-OCI adjusting JE (emphasis)**  
Unrealized holding loss = amortized cost $55,000 − fair value $52,250 = **$2,750**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 2,750 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 2,750 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$2,750**. Required FVA ending = **$2,750 credit**. BS carrying amount = $55,000 − $2,750 = **$52,250**. Related AOCI = **$(2,750)**.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**

**Income statement (excerpt):**  
- Interest revenue **$1,650**  
- Unrealized AFS holding loss is **not** in net income  

**Statement of comprehensive income (excerpt):**  
- Net income: includes interest revenue $1,650 (other items ignored)  
- Other comprehensive income: Unrealized holding loss on AFS securities **$(2,750)**  
- Comprehensive income = NI + OCI  

**Balance sheet, December 31, Year 1:**  
- Assets: Investment in available-for-sale securities **$52,250** (amortized cost $55,000 net of FVA credit $2,750)  
- Stockholders’ equity: Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) **$(2,750)**

**(e) December 31, Year 2 — Period-end FVA bridge and adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| AFS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Westbrook bonds | $55,000 | $56,650 | $1,650 |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | (2,750) |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$4,400** |

Required FVA = FV − AC = $56,650 − $55,000 = **$1,650 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$2,750 credit**  
Increase needed = $1,650 − (−$2,750) = **$4,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 4,400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 4,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$4,400**. FVA ending = −2,750 + 4,400 = **$1,650 debit**. AOCI related balance = **$1,650**.

**(f) January 1, Year 3 — Sale (period-end-only method — no sale-date FV adjust)**  
Realized gain = cash $57,400 − amortized cost $55,000 = **$2,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 57,400 | |
| Investment in AFS—Westbrook Bonds | | 55,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 2,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$57,400**.  
**Key LO 14-8 distinction:** FVA is **not** adjusted or eliminated at the sale date; the realized gain hits **net income** now; FVA is cleaned up only at **period-end**.

**(g) December 31, Year 3 — Eliminate FVA (no remaining AFS)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required FVA ending account balance | $0 |
| Existing FVA account balance | 1,650 debit |
| Amount of increase (decrease) to FVA needed | **$(1,650)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,650 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 1,650 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$1,650**. FVA and investment accounts are zero after year-end cleanup.

**(h) Year 3 AOCI reclassification disclosure**

Components of Year 3 OCI of **$(1,650)**:
1. Current-period fair value increase from 12/31/Y2 FV $56,650 to sale proceeds $57,400 = **$750** unrealized gain in Y3  
2. Reclassification adjustment for **gain included in net income** = **$(2,400)**

| Reconciliation of Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, January 1, Year 3 | $1,650 |
| Current period unrealized gain on AFS securities | 750 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (2,400) |
| Net OCI for Year 3 | (1,650) |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, December 31, Year 3 | **$0** |

**Holding-period AOCI volatility check (nets to zero after sale):**  
Y1 OCI loss $(2,750) + Y2 OCI gain $4,400 + Y3 OCI $(1,650) = **$0**.  
Total NI gain over holding period = **$2,400** (recognized only in Year 3 at sale).

**Key insight:** Under LO 14-8, unrealized AFS fair-value changes hit **OCI only at period-end**. At sale, compute realized G/L as **cash − amortized cost** in NI and leave FVA alone until year-end, when FVA is reset for remaining holdings (here, fully eliminated). The period-end adjusting JE is the sole FV measurement entry each year.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same period-end-only FV-OCI path with fully changed face, coupon, interest pattern, fair values, and sale price  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Ironwood Partners Inc.** purchased for **par $90,000** face of **Lakeview 5%** bonds that mature on December 31 of Year 4. Cash interest is paid **annually** each December 31. Ironwood classifies the bonds as **AFS** and uses **fair value adjustments only at period-end** (LO 14-8). Fiscal year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA and related AOCI = $0.

Additional facts:
- December 31, Year 1 fair value = **$86,400**
- No AFS activity in Year 2; December 31, Year 2 fair value = **$92,700**
- **March 15 of Year 3:** sells entire holding for **$93,600** cash (ignore accrued interest for this problem; focus on amortized cost vs cash for the gain/loss)

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Interest revenue entry on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Period-end FV-OCI entry on December 31 of Year 1.  
(d) Year 1 IS / SCI / BS presentation for this investment.  
(e) Period-end FV-OCI entry on December 31 of Year 2 (show required vs existing FVA).  
(f) Sale entry on March 15 of Year 3 (period-end-only: no FVA at sale).  
(g) December 31, Year 3 FVA elimination.  
(h) Year 3 AOCI reconciliation (beg AOCI, current-period unrealized from last YE FV to sale price, reclass of realized gain, ending AOCI).

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Purchase**

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — AFS portfolio partial sale + period-end-only FVA on remainder
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE + subsequent_measurement_schedule (portfolio period-end FVA) + classification_presentation_or_disclosure (AOCI reclass)  
**Scenario:**  
At December 31 of Year 2, **Brookstone Advisors Co.** holds the following **AFS** debt portfolio (all purchased at par; amortized cost = face). Brookstone uses **period-end-only** fair value adjustments (LO 14-8). Accounting year ends December 31.

| AFS investment | Amortized cost | Fair value 12/31/Y2 | Unrealized holding G/(L) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tundra 4% bonds | $45,000 | $43,200 | $(1,800) |
| Coral 7% bonds | 110,000 | 125,500 | 15,500 |
| **Totals** | **$155,000** | **$168,700** | **$13,700** |

The **$13,700** unrealized net gain equals the December 31, Year 2 balances in **FVA—AFS** (debit) and **AOCI** (credit).

On **January 1 of Year 3**, Brookstone sells the entire **Tundra** holding for **$44,100** cash. No other AFS purchases or sales occur in Year 3. On December 31 of Year 3, remaining Coral bonds have fair value **$121,000** (amortized cost still $110,000).

**Required:**  
(a) Record the January 1, Year 3 sale of Tundra (**period-end-only** method — no FVA at sale).  
(b) Compute the required December 31, Year 3 FVA balance for remaining AFS and the adjusting entry amount (required − existing). Record the **year-end FVA entry**.  
(c) Prepare the Year 3 **AOCI reconciliation**, including: (i) current-period unrealized G/L on Tundra from last YE FV to sale price and on Coral from last YE FV to YE3 FV; (ii) reclassification adjustment for the realized loss; (iii) ending AOCI.  
(d) State the December 31, Year 3 **balance sheet carrying amount** of the remaining AFS investment.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 1, Year 3 — Sale of Tundra (no sale-date FV adjust)**  
Realized **loss** = cash $44,100 − amortized cost $45,000 = **$900**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 44,100 | |
| Loss on Sale of Investment | 900 | |
| Investment in AFS—Tundra Bonds | | 45,000 |

**Check:** Dr 44,100 + 900 = **$45,000** = Cr **$45,000**. No FVA entry at sale.

**(b) December 31, Year 3 — Portfolio period-end FVA (emphasis)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Required FVA (Coral FV $121,000 − AC $110,000) | **$11,000** debit |
| Existing FVA (12/31/Y2 portfolio) | **$13,700** debit |
| Decrease (credit) needed | **$(2,700)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 2,700 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 2,700 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$2,700**. Ending FVA = 13,700 − 2,700 = **$11,000 debit** = ending AOCI related to remaining AFS.

**(c) Year 3 AOCI components and reconciliation**

Current-period fair value changes:

| Security | FV at sale or 12/31/Y3 | FV 12/31/Y2 | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tundra (sold) | $44,100 | $43,200 | **$900** |
| Coral (held) | 121,000 | 125,500 | **$(4,500)** |
| **Total current-period unrealized** | | | **$(3,600)** |

Reclassification adjustment for **loss** included in net income = **+$900** (removes the cumulative unrealized loss effect from AOCI as the $900 loss is now in NI).

Net Year 3 OCI = $(3,600) + $900 = **$(2,700)** (matches the debit to Unrealized G/L—OCI in part b).

| Reconciliation of AOCI | Amount |
|---|---:|
| AOCI, January 1, Year 3 | $13,700 |
| Current period unrealized loss on AFS | (3,600) |
| Reclassification adjustment for loss included in NI | 900 |
| Net OCI Year 3 | (2,700) |
| AOCI, December 31, Year 3 | **$11,000** |

*(Ending AOCI $11,000 = remaining Coral unrealized gain $121,000 − $110,000.)*

**(d)** BS carrying amount of AFS (Coral) at 12/31/Y3 = fair value **$121,000** (= AC $110,000 + FVA debit $11,000).

**Key insight:** After a partial portfolio sale under period-end-only FV-OCI, the year-end FVA entry is a **single portfolio plug** (required FVA for securities still held − existing FVA). That plug automatically embeds current-year FV moves on sold and held securities **and** the reclassification of the realized G/L out of AOCI.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — distinguishing period-end-only FV-OCI (Appendix 14B) from sale-date-and-period-end FV-OCI; routing of unrealized vs realized  
**Question:**  
**Northshore Treasury** holds AFS debt securities and elects the accounting policy under which fair value is updated **only at period-end** (LO 14-8 / Appendix 14B). On April 12 of Year 4, Northshore sells an AFS bond that had amortized cost of **$32,000**. Cash proceeds are **$33,750**. Immediately before the sale, Fair Value Adjustment—AFS related to this bond still reflected the **prior year-end** fair value (no entry has been made since December 31, Year 3). Which combination is correct for the **sale-date** entry and the **year-end** treatment of FVA?

- A) At sale: adjust FVA to current FV through OCI, then remove the investment and FVA with reclassification of AOCI into NI; year-end: no further FVA work related to this bond.  
- B) At sale: debit Cash $33,750, credit Investment $32,000, credit Gain on Sale $1,750; leave FVA unchanged at sale; at year-end, adjust FVA for remaining AFS (including elimination of FVA that related to the sold bond).  
- C) At sale: debit Cash $33,750, credit Investment $33,750; recognize no gain because AFS unrealized amounts never affect net income; year-end FVA only for remaining bonds.  
- D) At sale: debit Cash $33,750, credit Investment $32,000, credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI $1,750; year-end: reclassify the same $1,750 from AOCI to NI.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-8, realized gain = cash − amortized cost is recorded in **net income** at sale; **FVA is not adjusted at the sale date**. Period-end FVA then resets the valuation account for securities still held and clears amounts related to securities sold during the period.  
A describes the LO 14-3 “adjust at sale date and period-end” approach. C is wrong because realized G/L **does** affect NI. D misroutes the realized gain to OCI at sale instead of NI.

### Q5 — MC (presentation)
**LO:** LO 14-8  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — balance-sheet measurement and comprehensive-income presentation of AFS debt under period-end-only FV-OCI  
**Question:**  
At December 31, Year 2, **Granite Ridge Holdings** reports an AFS corporate bond portfolio with amortized cost **$180,000** and fair value **$174,200**. There were no sales of AFS during Year 2. Beginning FVA—AFS was $0. Under LO 14-8 period-end-only measurement, which statement is correct?

- A) Investment is reported at amortized cost $180,000 on the balance sheet; the $5,800 decline is disclosed only in the notes.  
- B) Investment is reported at fair value $174,200; Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income is debited $5,800 so net income is reduced.  
- C) Investment is reported at fair value $174,200 (via FVA); Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI is debited $5,800; AOCI is reduced (or shows an accumulated loss); net income is **not** reduced by the $5,800 unrealized loss.  
- D) Investment is reported at fair value $174,200; the entire $5,800 is reclassified from AOCI into net income at year-end even though no sale occurred.

**Answer:** **C.**  
AFS debt is measured at **fair value** on the BS; period-end unrealized holding losses go to **OCI → AOCI**, not net income, until realized (sale) or certain impairment exceptions. Interest revenue still flows through NI on the amortized-cost basis. A confuses HTM. B is the FV-NI / trading treatment (LO 14-7 path for TS). D incorrectly forces reclassification without a realizing event.
