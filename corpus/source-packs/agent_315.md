# Agent 315 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-6

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Explain and compute a LIFO reserve  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **LIFO reserve** = inventory at internal method (FIFO / average / standard) − inventory at LIFO
- **Initial recognition JE** (first year of LIFO external reporting): create **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis** (contra-inventory); Dr COGS for the **full** ending reserve
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** after Year 1, adjust the allowance **only for the change (Δ)** in the reserve — not the full ending reserve; Dr COGS / Cr Allowance when Δ > 0; reverse when Δ < 0
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year rollforward of internal EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve, COGS effect, and ending allowance
- **Reverse / settlement JE** when the reserve **decreases**: Dr Allowance, Cr COGS (partial reverse of prior build-up)
- **Classification / presentation:** inventory reported net of allowance at LIFO cost; note disclosure of LIFO reserve for peer comparability
- **Outside-the-books conversion:** compute and disclose reserve without a formal JE
- **COGS bridge:** LIFO COGS = internal-method COGS + increase in LIFO reserve (Δ reserve)

### Q1 — CORE — Redwood Canvas: period-end LIFO reserve adjusting JEs (create, increase, reverse)
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs for LIFO reserve (emphasis) — initial create for full reserve, subsequent Δ-only adjustments, reverse when reserve falls; multi-year schedule; BS net presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Canvas & Supply Co.** keeps **FIFO** inventory records for internal control and converts to **LIFO** at each year-end for external financial reporting and income tax. Year 1 is the company’s first year of operations. Year-end is **December 31**. Conversion to LIFO is **recognized in the accounts** using **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis** (contra-inventory).

Inventory valuations at each year-end:

| Date | Ending inventory at FIFO | Ending inventory at LIFO |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $96,000 | $62,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 112,000 | 70,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 128,000 | 81,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 105,000 | 78,000 |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–4 with columns: FIFO EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve (current − prior), effect on COGS, and ending allowance balance.  
(b) Record the **December 31, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry that creates the LIFO allowance.  
(c) **Emphasis — period-end adjusting entries:** record the **December 31** adjusting JEs for Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4. For each year, show: (i) computation of ending reserve, (ii) beginning allowance, (iii) Δ, (iv) the balanced JE.  
(d) Present **Inventory** on comparative balance sheets at December 31, Year 3 and Year 4 (FIFO gross, allowance, net LIFO).  
(e) Compute the **cumulative net debit (or credit) to COGS** from all LIFO-reserve entries over Years 1–4 and reconcile it to the Year 4 ending reserve.  
(f) Explain why the Year 2–4 **period-end adjusting entries** book only the **change** in the reserve, not the full ending reserve each year.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule**

Formula: **LIFO reserve = FIFO inventory − LIFO inventory**  
Δ reserve = current-year reserve − prior-year reserve (Year 1: full create).

| Date | FIFO EI | LIFO EI | LIFO reserve | Δ reserve | Effect on COGS | Ending allowance (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $96,000 | $62,000 | **$34,000** | +34,000 (create) | Increase COGS $34,000 | **$34,000** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 112,000 | 70,000 | **42,000** | +8,000 | Increase COGS $8,000 | **42,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 128,000 | 81,000 | **47,000** | +5,000 | Increase COGS $5,000 | **47,000** |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 105,000 | 78,000 | **27,000** | −20,000 | Decrease COGS $20,000 | **27,000** |

Checks:  
- Y1: \(96{,}000 - 62{,}000 = \mathbf{34{,}000}\)  
- Y2: \(112{,}000 - 70{,}000 = \mathbf{42{,}000}\); Δ = \(42{,}000 - 34{,}000 = \mathbf{+8{,}000}\)  
- Y3: \(128{,}000 - 81{,}000 = \mathbf{47{,}000}\); Δ = \(47{,}000 - 42{,}000 = \mathbf{+5{,}000}\)  
- Y4: \(105{,}000 - 78{,}000 = \mathbf{27{,}000}\); Δ = \(27{,}000 - 47{,}000 = \mathbf{-20{,}000}\)  
- Allowance rollforward: \(34{,}000 + 8{,}000 + 5{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{27{,}000}\)

**Allowance T-account (credit balance):**

| Debit | Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | Credit |
|---:|---|---:|
| | | 34,000 (Y1 create) |
| | | 8,000 (Y2 increase) |
| | | 5,000 (Y3 increase) |
| 20,000 (Y4 decrease) | | |
| | **Bal. after Y4: 27,000 Cr** | |

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Initial recognition (create LIFO allowance)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 34,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 34,000 |
| *Create year-end LIFO reserve: FIFO $96,000 − LIFO $62,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 34,000 = Cr 34,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Period-end adjusting entries — Years 2, 3, and 4 (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 2 — Increase in LIFO reserve*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending LIFO reserve (FIFO − LIFO) | \(112{,}000 - 70{,}000 = \mathbf{\$42{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance (Y1 reserve) | **$34,000** |
| Δ reserve (period-end adjustment amount) | \(42{,}000 - 34{,}000 = \mathbf{\$8{,}000}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 8,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 8,000 |
| *Period-end adjust allowance for Δ reserve only: $42,000 − $34,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr 8,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(34{,}000 + 8{,}000 = \mathbf{\$42{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 3 — Increase in LIFO reserve*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending LIFO reserve | \(128{,}000 - 81{,}000 = \mathbf{\$47{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$42,000** |
| Δ reserve | \(47{,}000 - 42{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 5,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 5,000 |
| *Period-end adjust for Δ reserve: $47,000 − $42,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,000 = Cr 5,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(42{,}000 + 5{,}000 = \mathbf{\$47{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 4 — Decrease in LIFO reserve (reverse / settlement of prior build-up)*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending LIFO reserve | \(105{,}000 - 78{,}000 = \mathbf{\$27{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$47,000** |
| Δ reserve | \(27{,}000 - 47{,}000 = \mathbf{\$(20{,}000)}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | 20,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 20,000 |
| *Period-end reverse for decrease in reserve: $27,000 − $47,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(47{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$27{,}000}\).

**(d) Comparative balance-sheet presentation (inventory section)**

| | Dec 31, Year 3 | Dec 31, Year 4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (at FIFO) | $128,000 | $105,000 |
| Less: Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | (47,000) | (27,000) |
| **Inventory, net (at LIFO)** | **$81,000** | **$78,000** |

(Alternatively, report a single line “Inventory (LIFO)” of $81,000 / $78,000 and disclose the LIFO reserve in the notes.)

**(e) Cumulative COGS effect (Years 1–4)**  
Net debit to COGS = \(34{,}000 + 8{,}000 + 5{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$27{,}000}\).  
Equals Year 4 ending LIFO reserve of **$27,000** (cumulative amount by which COGS has been increased relative to FIFO).

**(f)** After Year 1 the allowance already holds the prior-year full reserve. The period-end adjusting entry must bring the allowance to the **current** reserve only, so only the **Δ** is recorded. Booking the full ending reserve each year would double-count prior periods already charged to COGS.

**Key insight:** The LIFO reserve is always the **full** FIFO−LIFO gap, but the **period-end adjusting JE** (after the first-year create) books **only that year’s change**. When the reserve grows, Dr COGS / Cr Allowance; when it shrinks, reverse (Dr Allowance / Cr COGS). Ending allowance always equals the current reserve.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Meridian Forge: average-cost internal method twin
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — period-end adjusting JEs for LIFO reserve (average cost vs LIFO); initial create; subsequent Δ-only adjustments including reverse; BS presentation; COGS bridge  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Components LLC** uses **weighted-average cost** for internal inventory records and **LIFO** for external reporting and tax. Year 1 is the first year of operations. Adjustments are recognized **in the accounts**. Year-end is **December 31**.

| Date | Ending inventory at average cost | Ending inventory at LIFO |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $210,000 | $140,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 245,000 | 155,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 220,000 | 168,000 |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** (average-cost EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve, COGS effect, ending allowance).  
(b) Journal entry December 31, Year 1 (create allowance — initial recognition).  
(c) **Emphasis — period-end adjusting entries** December 31, Year 2 and Year 3 (show beginning allowance, ending reserve, Δ, and balanced JE for each year).  
(d) Show Inventory on the December 31, Year 3 balance sheet (gross internal method, allowance, net LIFO).  
(e) Compute cumulative net COGS effect over Years 1–3 from LIFO-reserve entries and reconcile to ending reserve.  
(f) Assume Year 2 **LIFO COGS** is **$1,080,000**. Using the Δ reserve, compute **average-cost COGS** for Year 2.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule**

LIFO reserve = average-cost inventory − LIFO inventory

| Date | Avg-cost EI | LIFO EI | LIFO reserve | Δ reserve | Effect on COGS | Ending allowance (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $210,000 | $140,000 | **$70,000** | +70,000 | +COGS $70,000 | **$70,000** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 245,000 | 155,000 | **90,000** | +20,000 | +COGS $20,000 | **90,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 220,000 | 168,000 | **52,000** | −38,000 | −COGS $38,000 | **52,000** |

Checks:  
- Y1: \(210{,}000 - 140{,}000 = \mathbf{70{,}000}\)  
- Y2: \(245{,}000 - 155{,}000 = \mathbf{90{,}000}\); Δ = \(90{,}000 - 70{,}000 = \mathbf{+20{,}000}\)  
- Y3: \(220{,}000 - 168{,}000 = \mathbf{52{,}000}\); Δ = \(52{,}000 - 90{,}000 = \mathbf{-38{,}000}\)  
- Allowance: \(70{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 38{,}000 = \mathbf{52{,}000}\)

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Create allowance**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 70,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 70,000 |
| *Initial LIFO reserve: $210,000 − $140,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 70,000 = Cr 70,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Period-end adjusting entries (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 2*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending reserve | \(245{,}000 - 155{,}000 = \mathbf{\$90{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$70,000** |
| Δ | \(90{,}000 - 70{,}000 = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 20,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 20,000 |
| *Period-end adjust for Δ reserve +$20,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000. **Balanced.** Allowance bal. = \(70{,}000 + 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$90{,}000}\).

*December 31, Year 3 — Decrease (reverse)*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending reserve | \(220{,}000 - 168{,}000 = \mathbf{\$52{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$90,000** |
| Δ | \(52{,}000 - 90{,}000 = \mathbf{\$(38{,}000)}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | 38,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 38,000 |
| *Period-end reverse for Δ reserve −$38,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 38,000 = Cr 38,000. **Balanced.** Allowance bal. = \(90{,}000 - 38{,}000 = \mathbf{\$52{,}000}\).

**(d) Balance sheet — December 31, Year 3**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory (at average cost) | $220,000 |
| Less: Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | (52,000) |
| **Inventory, net (at LIFO)** | **$168,000** |

**(e) Cumulative COGS effect (Years 1–3)**  
Net debit to COGS = \(70{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 38{,}000 = \mathbf{\$52{,}000}\) = ending LIFO reserve.

**(f) Year 2 COGS bridge**  
Relationship: **LIFO COGS = internal-method COGS + increase in LIFO reserve**.  
Average-cost COGS = LIFO COGS − Δ reserve = \(1{,}080{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$1{,}060{,}000}\).

**Key insight:** Whether the internal method is FIFO or average cost, period-end mechanics are identical: **reserve = internal EI − LIFO EI**; after Year 1 book **only Δ reserve**. Ending allowance always equals the current reserve, and cumulative COGS charges equal the ending reserve.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Glacier Ridge: midstream period-end adjustments only, disclosure vs in-accounts, reverse
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs from a nonzero beginning allowance (emphasis); reverse when reserve falls; in-accounts vs outside-the-books conversion; COGS bridge  
**Scenario:**  
**Glacier Ridge Outfitters Inc.** uses **FIFO** internally and **LIFO** externally. At **January 1, Year 7**, the credit balance in **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis** is **$95,000** (equal to the LIFO reserve at December 31, Year 6). Year-end is **December 31**.

Year-end inventory valuations:

| Date | Ending inventory at FIFO | Ending inventory at LIFO |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 7 | $410,000 | $300,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 8 | 435,000 | 318,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 9 | 380,000 | 330,000 |

Additional information for the COGS bridge:  
- Year 7 **LIFO-basis COGS** = **$760,000**  
- Year 9 **LIFO-basis COGS** = **$810,000**

Management is evaluating two Year 9 reporting approaches:  
**(1) In the accounts** — adjust the allowance each year-end with a formal period-end JE.  
**(2) Outside the accounts** — leave books on FIFO; compute LIFO amounts only for external statements and notes (no formal JE for the reserve).

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 7–9 starting from the January 1, Year 7 allowance of $95,000 (FIFO EI, LIFO EI, LIFO reserve, Δ reserve, COGS effect, ending allowance).  
(b) **Emphasis — period-end adjusting journal entries** for December 31 of Year 7, Year 8, and Year 9 under approach (1). For each year show beginning allowance → ending reserve → Δ → JE.  
(c) Under approach (1), show **balance-sheet classification** of inventory at December 31, Year 9.  
(d) Under approach (2) for Year 9, state: (i) whether a formal JE is made for the reserve; (ii) inventory amount on the LIFO-basis balance sheet; (iii) LIFO reserve disclosed in the notes; (iv) FIFO inventory an analyst would reconstruct.  
(e) Using the Δ reserve each year, compute **FIFO COGS** for Year 7 and Year 9 from the given LIFO COGS amounts.  
(f) Identify which year’s entry is a **reverse / settlement-type** period-end adjustment of prior LIFO-reserve build-up, and state the income-statement effect.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 7–9; nonzero beginning allowance)**

Beginning allowance (Jan 1, Y7 / Dec 31, Y6 reserve) = **$95,000**

| Date | FIFO EI | LIFO EI | LIFO reserve | Δ reserve | Effect on COGS | Ending allowance (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Jan 1, Y7 (beg.) | — | — | 95,000 | — | — | **$95,000** |
| Dec 31, Y7 | $410,000 | $300,000 | **110,000** | +15,000 | +COGS $15,000 | **110,000** |
| Dec 31, Y8 | 435,000 | 318,000 | **117,000** | +7,000 | +COGS $7,000 | **117,000** |
| Dec 31, Y9 | 380,000 | 330,000 | **50,000** | −67,000 | −COGS $67,000 | **50,000** |

Checks:  
- Y7: \(410{,}000 - 300{,}000 = \mathbf{110{,}000}\); Δ = \(110{,}000 - 95{,}000 = \mathbf{+15{,}000}\)  
- Y8: \(435{,}000 - 318{,}000 = \mathbf{117{,}000}\); Δ = \(117{,}000 - 110{,}000 = \mathbf{+7{,}000}\)  
- Y9: \(380{,}000 - 330{,}000 = \mathbf{50{,}000}\); Δ = \(50{,}000 - 117{,}000 = \mathbf{-67{,}000}\)  
- Allowance: \(95{,}000 + 15{,}000 + 7{,}000 - 67{,}000 = \mathbf{50{,}000}\)

**(b) Period-end adjusting JEs (in the accounts) — emphasis**

*December 31, Year 7*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending reserve | \(410{,}000 - 300{,}000 = \mathbf{\$110{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$95,000** |
| Δ | \(110{,}000 - 95{,}000 = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 15,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 15,000 |
| *Period-end increase LIFO reserve from $95,000 to $110,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 8*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending reserve | \(435{,}000 - 318{,}000 = \mathbf{\$117{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$110,000** |
| Δ | \(117{,}000 - 110{,}000 = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 7,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | | 7,000 |
| *Period-end increase LIFO reserve from $110,000 to $117,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,000 = Cr 7,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 9 — Reverse / decrease*

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Ending reserve | \(380{,}000 - 330{,}000 = \mathbf{\$50{,}000}\) |
| Beginning allowance | **$117,000** |
| Δ | \(50{,}000 - 117{,}000 = \mathbf{\$(67{,}000)}\) |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | 67,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 67,000 |
| *Period-end decrease LIFO reserve from $117,000 to $50,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 67,000 = Cr 67,000. **Balanced.** Ending allowance = \(117{,}000 - 67{,}000 = \mathbf{\$50{,}000}\).

**(c) Balance-sheet classification — December 31, Year 9 (approach 1)**

| Current assets | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Inventory (FIFO) | $380,000 |
| Less: Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis | (50,000) |
| **Inventory (LIFO basis)** | **$330,000** |

The allowance is a **contra-inventory (valuation)** account — not a liability.

**(d) Outside-the-accounts conversion (approach 2) — Year 9**  
(i) **No formal journal entry** is recorded for the LIFO reserve.  
(ii) External balance sheet reports inventory at LIFO: **$330,000**.  
(iii) Note disclosure of LIFO reserve: **$50,000** (for comparability with FIFO peers).  
(iv) Reconstructed FIFO inventory = LIFO inventory + disclosed reserve = \(330{,}000 + 50{,}000 = \mathbf{\$380{,}000}\).

**(e) COGS bridge**  
Formula: **FIFO COGS = LIFO COGS − Δ LIFO reserve**

| Year | LIFO COGS | Δ reserve | FIFO COGS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 7 | $760,000 | +15,000 | \(760{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \mathbf{\$745{,}000}\) |
| Year 9 | 810,000 | −67,000 | \(810{,}000 - (-67{,}000) = \mathbf{\$877{,}000}\) |

**(f) Reverse / settlement-type year**  
**Year 9** is the reverse period-end adjustment: Dr Allowance $67,000 / Cr COGS $67,000.  
Income-statement effect: **decreases COGS by $67,000**, increasing pretax income by $67,000 relative to leaving the allowance unadjusted. This partially **settles** the prior LIFO-reserve build-up that had increased COGS in earlier years.

**Key insight:** Period-end adjusting entries work whether Year 1 starts at zero or the firm is midstream with a beginning allowance. Only Δ reserve hits COGS each period; a shrinking reserve credits COGS. Users need the disclosed LIFO reserve for cross-company comparability whether conversion is booked or done off-books.

### Q4 — MC — Period-end LIFO reserve adjustment and allowance classification
**LO:** LO 9-6  
**Concept:** Compute ending LIFO reserve and Δ; identify correct period-end adjusting JE; classify Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis  

**Question:**  
**Copperline Millworks Ltd.** keeps inventory on **FIFO** for internal purposes and reports **LIFO** externally. At the end of Year 5 the LIFO reserve (credit balance in Allowance to Reduce Inventory to LIFO Basis) is **$48,000**. At the end of Year 6, FIFO ending inventory is **$198,000** and LIFO ending inventory is **$142,000**. Copperline records the conversion **in the accounts**. Which statement is **correct**?

- A) Year 6 LIFO reserve is $56,000; the Year 6 period-end adjusting entry debits Allowance $8,000 and credits COGS $8,000.  
- B) Year 6 LIFO reserve is $56,000; the Year 6 period-end adjusting entry debits COGS $56,000 and credits Allowance $56,000.  
- C) Year 6 LIFO reserve is $56,000; the Year 6 period-end adjusting entry debits COGS $8,000 and credits Allowance $8,000; the allowance is a contra-inventory account.  
- D) Year 6 LIFO reserve is $142,000; Inventory is reported at $198,000 with no allowance because LIFO is used only for tax.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Year 6 LIFO reserve = \(198{,}000 - 142{,}000 = \mathbf{\$56{,}000}\).  
Δ reserve = \(56{,}000 - 48{,}000 = \mathbf{+\$8{,}000}\) → Dr COGS $8,000 / Cr Allowance $8,000 (**only the change**, not the full reserve — the core period-end adjusting rule).  
The allowance is a **contra account to inventory**, not a liability.  
A reverses the direction of the entry. B incorrectly books the full ending reserve instead of the change. D misstates the reserve amount and presentation.
