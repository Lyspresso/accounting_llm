# Agent 203 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-7

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for disposal of property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of PPE at historical cost (invoice + freight + installation when needed for intended use)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year straight-line depreciation, accumulated depreciation, and carrying-amount rollforward through the disposal date (emphasis)
- Period-end adjusting JE for full-year depreciation
- Disposal-date adjusting JE to update depreciation for the partial year before derecognition
- Disposal JE: remove historical cost and related accumulated depreciation; record cash (gross or net of removal costs); recognize gain or loss
- Voluntary sale (gain or loss) vs involuntary conversion / abandonment (full book-value loss when no proceeds)
- Gain or loss on disposal reported in **income from continuing operations**

### Q1 — CORE — Capitalized cost through multi-year AD schedule and mid-year sale (gain)
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Initial PPE cost; multi-year subsequent measurement (AD/BV schedule); period-end dep; disposal-date dep update; sale with gain  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Manufacturing** (calendar-year) acquires a CNC milling machine that is ready for its intended use on **January 1, Year 1**. Cash costs:

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Invoice price | $178,000 |
| Freight-in | 7,000 |
| Installation and testing | 15,000 |
| **Total capitalized cost** | **$200,000** |

Estimated residual value is **$20,000**; estimated useful life is **10 years**. Cedarline uses **straight-line** depreciation and records depreciation each **December 31**.

On **October 1, Year 4**, Cedarline sells the machine for **$148,000** cash. Depreciation was last updated on **December 31, Year 3**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** acquisition journal entry (single Equipment account at total capitalized cost).  
b. Compute annual depreciation and prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (depreciation expense, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount) from acquisition through **October 1, Year 4**.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end depreciation adjusting entry.  
d. Record the **October 1, Year 4** entry to update depreciation through the disposal date.  
e. Record the **October 1, Year 4** disposal (sale) entry.  
f. State where the gain is reported on the income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**Depreciable base and rates**  
Depreciable base = $200,000 − $20,000 = **$180,000**  
Annual depreciation = $180,000 / 10 = **$18,000**  
Nine months (Jan 1–Sept 30, Year 4) = $18,000 × 9/12 = **$13,500**

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |
| *To capitalize invoice, freight, and installation for CNC mill ready for use* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**  

| Date | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount (cost − AD) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 (acquisition) | — | $0 | $200,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $18,000 | 18,000 | 182,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 18,000 | 36,000 | 164,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 18,000 | 54,000 | 146,000 |
| Oct 1, Y4 (update, 9/12 year) | 13,500 | **67,500** | **132,500** |

**Rollforward check:**  
3 full years × $18,000 = $54,000 AD at Dec 31, Y3.  
+ $13,500 partial Year 4 = **$67,500** AD at disposal.  
Carrying amount = $200,000 − $67,500 = **$132,500**.  
Gain = proceeds $148,000 − carrying amount $132,500 = **$15,500**.

**c. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 18,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,000 |
| *Straight-line depreciation for Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 18,000.

**d. October 1, Year 4 — update depreciation through disposal date**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 13,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 13,500 |
| *Update SL depreciation for Jan 1–Sept 30, Year 4* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 13,500. AD after entry = $54,000 + $13,500 = **$67,500**.

**e. October 1, Year 4 — disposal (sale with gain)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 148,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 67,500 | |
| Gain on Sale of Equipment | | 15,500 |
| Equipment | | 200,000 |
| *Derecognize cost and AD; gain = proceeds − updated carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 148,000 + 67,500 = **215,500**; Cr 15,500 + 200,000 = **215,500**. Balanced.  
Post-entry: Equipment and related AD for this asset = **$0**.

**f. Classification / presentation**  
The **$15,500 gain** is reported in the income statement as part of **income from continuing operations** (not as an extraordinary item and not deferred solely because a replacement might be purchased).

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the exam backbone for disposal problems: roll AD and book value to the disposal date **before** measuring gain or loss. Only after that schedule is complete do you record the disposal-date dep update and strip cost + AD.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Multi-year AD schedule and mid-period sale (loss)
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: acquisition, multi-year AD/BV schedule, disposal-date dep update, sale at a loss  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Bottling Co.** (calendar-year) purchases a bottle-filler ready for use on **January 1, Year 1** for **$84,000** cash. Estimated residual value is **$12,000**; useful life is **8 years**. Straight-line depreciation; year-end is December 31.

On **April 1, Year 5**, Meridian sells the filler for **$41,000** cash. Depreciation was last updated on **December 31, Year 4**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the January 1, Year 1 acquisition entry.  
b. Compute annual depreciation and prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** (dep expense, AD, carrying amount) from acquisition through April 1, Year 5.  
c. Record the April 1, Year 5 depreciation-update entry.  
d. Record the April 1, Year 5 disposal entry.  
e. Prove that Dr = Cr on the disposal entry and compute the loss.

**Answer key:**  

**Annual depreciation**  
Depreciable base = $84,000 − $12,000 = **$72,000**  
Annual depreciation = $72,000 / 8 = **$9,000**  
Three months (Jan 1–Mar 31, Year 5) = $9,000 × 3/12 = **$2,250**

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 84,000 | |
| Cash | | 84,000 |
| *To record purchase of bottle-filler ready for use* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 84,000.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule through disposal**  

| Date | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | $0 | $84,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $9,000 | 9,000 | 75,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 9,000 | 18,000 | 66,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 9,000 | 27,000 | 57,000 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 9,000 | 36,000 | 48,000 |
| Apr 1, Y5 (3/12 year) | 2,250 | **38,250** | **45,750** |

**Proof:** 4 full years × $9,000 = $36,000; + $2,250 = **$38,250** AD.  
Book value = $84,000 − $38,250 = **$45,750**.  
Cash proceeds $41,000 − book value $45,750 = **loss $4,750**.

**c. April 1, Year 5 — update depreciation**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 2,250 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 2,250 |
| *Update SL depreciation for Jan 1–Mar 31, Year 5* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,250.

**d. April 1, Year 5 — disposal (sale with loss)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 41,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 38,250 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 4,750 | |
| Equipment | | 84,000 |
| *Derecognize cost and AD; loss = carrying amount − proceeds* | | |

**e. Balance proof**  
Dr: 41,000 + 38,250 + 4,750 = **84,000**  
Cr: **84,000**  
Loss = carrying amount − proceeds = $45,750 − $41,000 = **$4,750**.

**Key insight:** Same schedule-first workflow as a gain sale. When the **subsequent measurement** carrying amount exceeds cash received, the disposal plug is a **loss** (debit), not a gain.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Three disposal outcomes from one AD schedule; net proceeds
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Shared subsequent measurement schedule; dep update; sale at loss, sale at gain with removal costs, and involuntary/abandonment disposal  
**Scenario:**  

**Harborcrest Logistics** (calendar-year) purchased specialized warehouse equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$120,000** cash. Estimated residual value **$0**; useful life **6 years**; straight-line depreciation. Depreciation is recorded each December 31 and was last updated on **December 31, Year 2**.

On **October 1, Year 3**, the equipment is disposed of under **three separate, independent scenarios** (each scenario starts from the same pre-disposal books).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** from acquisition through October 1, Year 3 (after the disposal-date depreciation update). Show annual depreciation, AD, and carrying amount at each date.  
b. Record the **common** October 1, Year 3 depreciation-update entry (same under A, B, and C).  
c. Record the disposal entry under **Scenario A**: sold for **$52,000** cash (no selling costs).  
d. Record the disposal entry under **Scenario B**: sold for **$72,000** cash; company also pays **$2,000** cash for removal/selling costs (use **net** cash proceeds).  
e. Record the disposal entry under **Scenario C**: destroyed in a flood; **no** insurance recovery; zero proceeds.  
f. For Scenario B only, state the **net proceeds**, **gain or loss**, and income-statement classification.

**Answer key:**  

**Annual depreciation (all scenarios)**  
Depreciable base = $120,000 − $0 = **$120,000**  
Annual depreciation = $120,000 / 6 = **$20,000**  
Nine months Year 3 = $20,000 × 9/12 = **$15,000**

**a. Subsequent measurement schedule**  

| Date | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | $0 | $120,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $20,000 | 20,000 | 100,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 20,000 | 40,000 | 80,000 |
| Oct 1, Y3 (update, 9/12 year) | 15,000 | **55,000** | **65,000** |

**Rollforward:** 2 full years × $20,000 = $40,000; + $15,000 = **$55,000** AD.  
Carrying amount at disposal = $120,000 − $55,000 = **$65,000**.

**b. Common depreciation update — October 1, Year 3**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 15,000 |
| *Update SL depreciation for Jan 1–Sept 30, Year 3* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000. AD after entry = $40,000 + $15,000 = **$55,000**.

**c. Scenario A — sale for $52,000 (loss)**  
Loss = $65,000 − $52,000 = **$13,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 52,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 55,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 13,000 | |
| Equipment | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr 52,000 + 55,000 + 13,000 = **120,000** = Cr Equipment. Balanced.

**d. Scenario B — sale for $72,000 with $2,000 removal costs**  
Net proceeds = $72,000 − $2,000 = **$70,000**  
Gain = $70,000 − $65,000 = **$5,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash ($72,000 − $2,000) | 70,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 55,000 | |
| Gain on Sale of Equipment | | 5,000 |
| Equipment | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr 70,000 + 55,000 = **125,000**; Cr 5,000 + 120,000 = **125,000**. Balanced.

*(Equivalent two-step presentation: Dr Cash 72,000 for sale and Cr Cash 2,000 for removal costs, with the same net cash and gain.)*

**e. Scenario C — involuntary disposal, no proceeds (flood / abandonment)**  
Loss = full carrying amount = **$65,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 55,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 65,000 | |
| Equipment | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr 55,000 + 65,000 = **120,000**. Balanced.  
Note: Gain or loss is recognized on involuntary conversion even if the firm later replaces the asset (ASC 610-30-25-3 concept from the chapter).

**f. Scenario B — presentation**  
- Net proceeds: **$70,000**  
- Gain on sale: **$5,000**  
- Classification: reported within **income from continuing operations** on the income statement.

**Key insight:** One **subsequent measurement schedule** and one dep-update entry support every disposal outcome. Only the proceeds side changes—cash (gross or net of disposal costs) versus none—which drives loss on sale, gain on sale, or full book-value loss on abandonment.

### Q4 — MC — Schedule amount and disposal sequence
**LO:** LO 11-7  
**Concept:** Reading carrying amount from the subsequent measurement schedule; required sequence before recording disposal  

**Question 1:**  
Equipment cost $90,000, residual $10,000, life 8 years, straight-line. Acquired January 1, Year 1. Depreciation was last recorded December 31, Year 3. On July 1, Year 4, after updating depreciation for the first half of Year 4, what is the carrying amount used to measure gain or loss on disposal?

- A) $90,000  
- B) $60,000  
- C) $55,000  
- D) $50,000  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Annual dep = ($90,000 − $10,000) / 8 = **$10,000**.  
AD at Dec 31, Y3 = 3 × $10,000 = **$30,000**.  
Half-year update Y4 = $5,000; AD at July 1, Y4 = **$35,000**.  
Carrying amount = $90,000 − $35,000 = **$55,000**.

**Question 2:**  
Immediately before recording the entry to remove a depreciable plant asset sold on September 30, what must the company do if depreciation was last recorded at the prior December 31 year-end?

- A) Reverse all accumulated depreciation recorded in prior years  
- B) Update (accrue) depreciation expense from January 1 through the disposal date  
- C) Revalue the asset to fair value with an offsetting revaluation surplus in OCI  
- D) Transfer the asset’s cost to a “Assets Held for Sale” equity contra account  

**Answer:** **B.** If the asset is subject to depreciation, it is depreciated **through the date of disposal** to update the carrying amount before cost and accumulated depreciation are derecognized and gain or loss is measured (Demo 11-7 / LO 11-7 overview).
