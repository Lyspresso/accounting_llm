# Agent 200 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-4

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Interest cost incurred during the construction of a fixed asset is considered to (Calculate capitalized interest)  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Qualifying asset** and **capitalization period** (expenditures made; activities in progress; interest incurred)
- **Weighted-average accumulated expenditures (WAAE)** schedule — weight each expenditure by months outstanding / 12
- **Actual interest** on specific (project) debt + general debt
- **Weighted-average interest rate on general debt** = total general interest ÷ total general principal
- **Avoidable interest** schedule: apply **specific** rate to WAAE up to specific debt first; apply **general WA rate** to excess WAAE
- Capitalize the **lesser** of actual interest and avoidable interest to **Construction in Process**
- Residual actual interest → **Interest Expense**
- **Initial recognition JEs:** cash construction expenditures → CIP
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr CIP (capitalized), Dr Interest Expense (remainder), Cr Cash (or Interest Payable) for total actual interest
- **Subsequent measurement:** CIP rollforward including capitalized interest; when complete, CIP → Building; capitalized interest is depreciated over the asset’s useful life
- **Settlement / completion:** reclassify CIP to Building; optionally repay project-specific construction debt
- **Disclosure:** total interest incurred, amount capitalized, amount expensed

### Q1 — CORE — Oakridge Precision: expenditure JEs, WAAE & avoidable schedules, period-end interest JE, CIP rollforward
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Full interest-capitalization measurement: initial CIP recognition JEs; subsequent measurement schedules (WAAE and avoidable interest with specific-then-general rates); period-end capitalization JE; CIP rollforward and interest disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Oakridge Precision Tools** (calendar year-end) is self-constructing a specialized machine shop for **its own use** (a qualifying asset). Construction began **January 1** of the current year and is **still in progress** at **December 31**. Activities necessary to ready the shop were in progress all year, and interest cost was incurred all year. All debt listed was outstanding for the **full year**. Interest is paid in cash at year-end.

**Capital expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Amount |
|---|---:|
| January 1 | $840,000 |
| March 1 | 480,000 |
| July 1 | 720,000 |
| October 1 | 360,000 |
| December 31 | 180,000 |

**Outstanding debt at year-end:**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction loan (project-specific) | $1,000,000 | 7% | Specific |
| Note payable | 500,000 | 8% | General |
| Bonds payable | 1,500,000 | 10% | General |
| Note payable | 500,000 | 12% | General |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **journal entry** for each capital expenditure date (initial recognition to Construction in Process).  
(b) Prepare the **weighted-average accumulated expenditures (WAAE)** schedule (**emphasis**).  
(c) Compute **actual interest** for the year and the **weighted-average interest rate on general debt**.  
(d) Prepare the **avoidable interest** schedule (specific debt first, then general) and determine the amount of interest to **capitalize** (lesser of actual vs avoidable).  
(e) Record the **December 31 period-end adjusting journal entry** for interest (cash payment of full actual interest).  
(f) Prepare a **Construction in Process rollforward** for the year.  
(g) Show the **interest cost disclosure** for the December 31 financial statements.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Capital expenditure JEs (initial recognition)**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 | Construction in Process | 840,000 | |
| | Cash | | 840,000 |
| Mar 1 | Construction in Process | 480,000 | |
| | Cash | | 480,000 |
| Jul 1 | Construction in Process | 720,000 | |
| | Cash | | 720,000 |
| Oct 1 | Construction in Process | 360,000 | |
| | Cash | | 360,000 |
| Dec 31 | Construction in Process | 180,000 | |
| | Cash | | 180,000 |

Each entry balances (Dr = Cr). Total cash expenditures to CIP = **$2,580,000**.

**(b) Weighted-average accumulated expenditures (subsequent measurement schedule — emphasis)**

| Date | Actual expenditure | Months outstanding | Weight | Weighted amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 | $840,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $840,000 |
| March 1 | 480,000 | 10 | 10/12 | 400,000 |
| July 1 | 720,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 360,000 |
| October 1 | 360,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 90,000 |
| December 31 | 180,000 | 0 | 0/12 | 0 |
| **Totals** | **$2,580,000** | | | **$1,690,000** |

**WAAE = $1,690,000**  
(Check: \(840{,}000 + 400{,}000 + 360{,}000 + 90{,}000 + 0 = 1{,}690{,}000\).)

**(c) Actual interest and general debt rate**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Construction loan (specific) | $1,000,000 | 7% | $70,000 |
| Note payable (general) | 500,000 | 8% | 40,000 |
| Bonds payable (general) | 1,500,000 | 10% | 150,000 |
| Note payable (general) | 500,000 | 12% | 60,000 |
| **Total actual interest** | | | **$320,000** |

General debt principal = \(500{,}000 + 1{,}500{,}000 + 500{,}000 = \mathbf{\$2{,}500{,}000}\)  
General debt interest = \(40{,}000 + 150{,}000 + 60{,}000 = \mathbf{\$250{,}000}\)  
Weighted-average interest rate on general debt = \(250{,}000 / 2{,}500{,}000 = \mathbf{10\%}\).

**(d) Avoidable interest schedule and amount capitalized**

| Debt category | Amount of WAAE applied | Rate | Avoidable interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Specific debt (construction loan) | $1,000,000 | 7% | $70,000 |
| General debt (excess WAAE) | 690,000 | 10% | 69,000 |
| **Totals** | **$1,690,000** | | **$139,000** |

Excess WAAE = \(1{,}690{,}000 - 1{,}000{,}000 = 690{,}000\).

| Measure | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Actual interest | $320,000 |
| Avoidable interest | 139,000 |
| **Capitalize (lesser)** | **$139,000** |
| **Expense (actual − capitalized)** | **$181,000** |

**(e) December 31 period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 139,000 | |
| Interest Expense | 181,000 | |
| Cash | | 320,000 |
| *Capitalize avoidable interest; expense residual actual interest* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(139{,}000 + 181{,}000 = 320{,}000\); Cr \(320{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**(f) CIP rollforward**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning CIP | $0 |
| Capital expenditures | 2,580,000 |
| Capitalized interest | 139,000 |
| **Ending CIP** | **$2,719,000** |

**(g) Interest cost disclosure**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total interest cost incurred | $320,000 |
| Interest cost capitalized | 139,000 |
| Interest cost expensed | 181,000 |

**Key insight:** Capitalized interest equals the **lesser of actual and avoidable interest**. The **WAAE schedule** time-weights expenditures; the **avoidable interest schedule** applies the **specific** project rate first, then the **weighted-average general** rate to any excess. The period-end entry increases **CIP** and reduces interest expense relative to cash interest paid.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Vesper Components: mid-year completion WAAE, capitalization, CIP→Building settlement, depreciation
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule for a partial-year capitalization period; period-end (completion-date) interest JE; settlement reclassification CIP → Building; repayment of project-specific debt; depreciation of cost that includes capitalized interest  
**Scenario:**  
**Vesper Components Inc.** (calendar year-end) began constructing a production wing in a prior year. On **January 1, Year 2**, the **Construction in Process** balance is **$1,850,000** (includes prior capitalized interest). Construction continues in Year 2 and is **completed and placed in service on June 30, Year 2**. The capitalization period therefore ends **June 30, Year 2** (asset ready for intended use).

**Year 2 capital expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Amount |
|---|---:|
| February 1 | $420,000 |
| April 1 | 280,000 |
| June 30 (final) | 150,000 |

**Debt outstanding all of Year 2** (interest paid in cash December 31 for the full year):

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction note (project-specific) | $900,000 | 8% | Specific |
| Note payable | 1,500,000 | 8% | General |
| Bonds payable | 500,000 | 16% | General |

Building useful life **20 years**, residual value **$199,000**, straight-line, **half-year** depreciation in Year 2 (placed in service June 30). On **June 30, Year 2**, Vesper also **repays the $900,000 construction note** in cash (settlement of project-specific financing). Interest for the full calendar year is still paid December 31 as usual (interest on the construction note is incurred for only the first half of the year until repaid—see note in answer).

**Simplifying assumption for exam-style measurement (match chapter Demo method):** For avoidable interest, use **annual rates applied to WAAE** with weights based on months outstanding through **June 30** (÷ 12). For **actual interest** used as the capitalization ceiling for Year 2, compute interest **incurred while each debt is outstanding**: construction note interest for **6 months**; general debt for **12 months**.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **WAAE schedule** for Year 2 (capitalization ends June 30).  
(b) Compute **actual interest incurred in Year 2**, the **WA general rate**, **avoidable interest**, and the amount to **capitalize**.  
(c) Prepare the **June 30, Year 2** journal entry to record **capitalized interest and interest expense related to the construction period** (accrue/pay pattern: record the capitalization portion of Year 2 interest at completion; remaining general interest may be recorded later—here record **full Year 2 actual interest** at December 31 as in (e), and at June 30 record only the **capitalization entry** with a credit to **Interest Payable** for the capitalized amount, **or** combine as shown in the key). Use the single combined approach in the answer key.  
(d) Prepare the **June 30 settlement JEs**: (1) transfer CIP → Building; (2) repay the construction note.  
(e) Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** entry for remaining interest cash payment (actual interest not yet paid), if using the split approach—or state the full-year interest JE if capitalization and expense are booked together December 31 before transfer. **Use the answer key’s recommended set of JEs.**

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Capitalize lesser of actual vs avoidable interest; actual-interest ceiling  
**Question:**  
During construction of a qualifying self-constructed plant asset, a company computes **avoidable interest of $210,000** and incurs **actual interest cost of $175,000** on all outstanding debt for the period. The amount of interest that should be **capitalized** is:

- A) $210,000  
- B) $175,000  
- C) $385,000  
- D) $0  

**Answer:** **B.** Capitalized interest is the **lesser** of actual interest and avoidable interest. Avoidable interest cannot exceed actual interest incurred in the period (ASC 835-20-30-6). Here actual ($175,000) < avoidable ($210,000), so capitalize **$175,000** (and expense **$0** of the residual under this comparison—all actual interest is capitalized subject to the actual-interest ceiling).

### Q4b — MC (optional second)
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Classification of interest capitalized on land held as a building site  
**Question:**  
Interest is properly capitalized during construction of a building on a site the company owns. Expenditures for the **land** (building site) are included in weighted-average accumulated expenditures while construction is in progress. The interest capitalized that is attributable to those **land** expenditures should be:

- A) Added to the **Land** account (not depreciated)  
- B) Added to the **Building** account (and depreciated over the building’s life)  
- C) Expensed immediately as interest expense  
- D) Deferred as a prepaid expense until the building is sold  

**Answer:** **B.** When land is used as a **building site**, interest capitalized on land expenditures becomes part of the **acquisition cost of the structure** (Building), not Land, and is depreciated over the building’s useful life (ASC 835-20-15-8). Idle land that is not undergoing activities is not a qualifying asset.
