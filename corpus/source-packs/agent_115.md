# Agent 115 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-10

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Describe accounting for stock appreciation rights  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (liability SARs):** when the employee can require cash settlement, classify as a liability; at each period-end during the requisite service period, accrue Compensation Expense and SARs Liability based on year-end fair value × % of service accrued
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** cumulative SARs liability = (year-end fair value per unit × number of SARs) × cumulative % of requisite service period; annual expense = change in cumulative liability (can reverse if fair value falls)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** true-up SARs Liability (or reverse) so the balance equals the cumulative liability from the schedule
- **Settlement JE (liability):** remove SARs Liability against cash paid (intrinsic value at exercise); after full vesting remeasurement, liability should equal cash settlement when fair value equals intrinsic value
- **Equity-classified SARs:** employer (not employee) has the right to settle in stock → fixed grant-date fair value; straight-line Compensation Expense to Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan; no remeasurement for later fair-value changes
- **Equity settlement:** cash settlement may require additional Compensation Expense if cash exceeds accumulated PIC—SARs; stock settlement reclassifies PIC—SARs into Common Stock (par × shares) and APIC (shares = intrinsic ÷ market price)
- **Classification / presentation:** liability vs equity under ASC 718-10-25-11; SARs liability classified current/noncurrent by expected exercise timing; compensation cost in operating income; equity credit in equity until settlement
- **Number-variant twin:** same liability-SARs measurement logic with different units, FVs, service period, and a negative-expense year

### Q1 — CORE — Liability SARs: initial recognition, measurement schedule, adjusting JEs, settlement
**LO:** LO 20-10  
**Concept:** Initial recognition and multi-year subsequent measurement of cash-settled (liability) SARs; period-end adjusting entries; cash settlement at exercise  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Analytics Corp.** (calendar-year reporting) adopts a stock appreciation rights (SARs) plan on **January 1, Year 1**.

On that date, Harborline grants **6,000 SARs** to CFO Dana Okonkwo. For each SAR, the grantee receives **cash** equal to the excess of the market price of Harborline common stock on the exercise date over the **grant-date market price of $18** per share. Continuing employment is required. The SARs may be exercised beginning **December 31, Year 3** (three years after grant) and expire at the end of Year 5 or upon termination, whichever is earlier. Exercise is highly probable on the first eligible date, so the **requisite service period is three years**.

**Key classification fact:** the **employee** has the right to settle the SARs agreement in **cash**. Therefore the awards are classified as **liabilities** (ASC 718-10-25-11).

Year-end **fair values of the SARs** (per unit) are:

| Date | Fair value per SAR |
|---|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | $4.00 |
| December 31, Year 2 | $9.00 |
| December 31, Year 3 | $7.00 |

On **December 31, Year 3**, Okonkwo exercises all 6,000 SARs when the market price is **$25** per share. Harborline pays cash equal to the intrinsic value:  
(\$25 − \$18) × 6,000 = **\$42,000**.

**Required:**  
a. State whether the SARs are classified as **equity** or **liability** and cite the deciding feature.  
b. Prepare the complete **subsequent measurement schedule** (year-end fair value aggregate, % of service accrued, cumulative SARs liability, and **annual compensation expense**) for Years 1–3.  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting journal entries** for December 31 of Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 (initial recognition / subsequent measurement). Emphasize Year 1 as initial recognition.  
d. Prepare the **settlement journal entry** on December 31, Year 3.  
e. **Presentation:** how should the SARs Liability be classified on the December 31, Year 2 balance sheet (current vs noncurrent), and where does compensation cost appear?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**

**Liability.** The employee can require settlement by transfer of cash (or other assets). Under ASC 718-10-25-11, options or similar instruments are liabilities if the entity can be required under any circumstances to settle by transferring cash or other assets. (If only the **employer** could choose stock settlement and cash settlement were not employee-mandated, equity classification would apply.)

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule (liability SARs)**

Aggregate fair value = fair value per unit × 6,000 SARs.  
Cumulative liability = aggregate fair value × cumulative % of 3-year service period.  
Annual expense = current cumulative liability − prior cumulative liability.

| Date | FV per unit | Aggregate FV (A × 6,000) | % service accrued | Cumulative SARs liability | Annual compensation expense |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $4.00 | $24,000 | 1/3 (33⅓%) | $8,000 | **$8,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | $9.00 | $54,000 | 2/3 (66⅔%) | $36,000 | **$28,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | $7.00 | $42,000 | 100% | $42,000 | **$6,000** |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Y1: 24,000 × 1/3 = 8,000; expense 8,000 − 0 = **8,000**  
- Y2: 54,000 × 2/3 = 36,000; expense 36,000 − 8,000 = **28,000**  
- Y3: 42,000 × 100% = 42,000; expense 42,000 − 36,000 = **6,000**  
- Total expense over service period = 8,000 + 28,000 + 6,000 = **\$42,000** = cash paid at exercise.

**c. Period-end adjusting journal entries**

*December 31, Year 1 — Initial recognition / accrue SARs liability (25%? No: 1/3 of aggregate FV)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 8,000 | |
| SARs Liability | | 8,000 |
| *Year 1: \$24,000 aggregate FV × 1/3 service = \$8,000 cumulative liability* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr 8,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2 — Subsequent measurement (increase liability to \$36,000)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 28,000 | |
| SARs Liability | | 28,000 |
| *True-up: cumulative liability \$36,000 − prior \$8,000 = \$28,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,000 = Cr 28,000. **Balanced.**  
Liability balance after entry: 8,000 + 28,000 = **\$36,000**.

*December 31, Year 3 — Subsequent measurement (increase liability to \$42,000)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 6,000 | |
| SARs Liability | | 6,000 |
| *True-up: cumulative liability \$42,000 − prior \$36,000 = \$6,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**  
Liability balance after entry: 36,000 + 6,000 = **\$42,000**.

**d. Settlement JE**

Cash paid = (\$25 − \$18) × 6,000 = **\$42,000** (matches fully accrued liability).

*December 31, Year 3 — Exercise / settle SARs plan*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| SARs Liability | 42,000 | |
| Cash | | 42,000 |
| *Remove liability; pay intrinsic value cash settlement* | | |

**Check:** Dr 42,000 = Cr 42,000. **Balanced.**  
Liability after settlement: **\$0**.

**e. Presentation / classification**

- **Balance sheet (Dec 31, Year 2):** SARs Liability **\$36,000**. Because first exercise is expected **one year later** (Dec 31, Year 3), classify as **current liability** at Dec 31, Year 2 if settlement is expected within one year; at Dec 31, Year 1 the liability would typically be **noncurrent** (settlement more than one year out). Practice: classify based on expected settlement timing under the plan.
- **Income statement:** Compensation Expense is **operating** compensation cost (not a financing item).
- **No equity credit** is used for liability-classified SARs (contrast with equity-classified awards that credit Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan).

**Key insight:** Cash-settled SARs are **remeasured every period** so the liability tracks expected cash; annual expense is the **change in the cumulative liability**, not a fixed grant-date amount. Initial recognition starts that pattern at the first reporting date after grant.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Liability SARs twin with expense reversal year
**LO:** LO 20-10  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: four-year liability SARs schedule including a year with **negative** compensation expense; settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**SummitRidge Logistics Inc.** grants **8,000 SARs** on **January 1, Year 1** to its COO. Each SAR pays cash equal to the excess of the market price at exercise over the **grant-date market price of \$15**. The SARs vest after **four years** (first exercise expected **December 31, Year 4**); requisite service period = **4 years**. The **employee** may require **cash** settlement → **liability** classification.

Year-end fair values per SAR:

| Date | Fair value per SAR |
|---|---:|
| December 31, Year 1 | $2.00 |
| December 31, Year 2 | $5.00 |
| December 31, Year 3 | $3.00 |
| December 31, Year 4 | $6.00 |

On December 31, Year 4, the employee exercises all SARs when the stock price is **\$21**. Cash paid = (\$21 − \$15) × 8,000 = **\$48,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **four-year subsequent measurement schedule** (aggregate FV, % accrued, cumulative liability, annual expense).  
b. Prepare **all period-end adjusting JEs** for Years 1–4 (including the Year 3 **reversal**).  
c. Prepare the **settlement JE** at exercise.  
d. Prove that total compensation expense over the service period equals cash paid.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Subsequent measurement schedule**

Aggregate FV = FV per unit × 8,000.

| Date | FV per unit | Aggregate FV | % service | Cumulative SARs liability | Annual expense |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $2.00 | $16,000 | 25% | $4,000 | **$4,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | $5.00 | $40,000 | 50% | $20,000 | **$16,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | $3.00 | $24,000 | 75% | $18,000 | **(\$2,000)** |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | $6.00 | $48,000 | 100% | $48,000 | **$30,000** |

**Math checks:**  
- Y1: 16,000 × 0.25 = 4,000; exp **4,000**  
- Y2: 40,000 × 0.50 = 20,000; exp 20,000 − 4,000 = **16,000**  
- Y3: 24,000 × 0.75 = 18,000; exp 18,000 − 20,000 = **(2,000)**  
- Y4: 48,000 × 1.00 = 48,000; exp 48,000 − 18,000 = **30,000**

**b. Period-end adjusting journal entries**

*December 31, Year 1 — Initial recognition*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 4,000 | |
| SARs Liability | | 4,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 16,000 | |
| SARs Liability | | 16,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 16,000. **Balanced.** Balance: 4,000 + 16,000 = **20,000**.

*December 31, Year 3 — Fair value decline reduces cumulative liability*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| SARs Liability | 2,000 | |
| Compensation Expense | | 2,000 |
| *Reverse portion of prior accruals; cumulative liability to \$18,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,000. **Balanced.** Balance: 20,000 − 2,000 = **18,000**.

*December 31, Year 4*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 30,000 | |
| SARs Liability | | 30,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 30,000. **Balanced.** Balance: 18,000 + 30,000 = **48,000**.

**c. Settlement JE**

*December 31, Year 4 — Exercise of SARs*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| SARs Liability | 48,000 | |
| Cash | | 48,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 48,000. **Balanced.**

**d. Total expense = cash paid**

4,000 + 16,000 − 2,000 + 30,000 = **\$48,000** = cash paid. Liability ends at **zero**.

**Key insight:** Liability SARs can produce **negative compensation expense** in a period when fair value falls enough that the cumulative liability decreases—this is core remeasurement, not an error.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Equity-classified SARs, fixed grant-date FV, cash and stock settlement
**LO:** LO 20-10  
**Concept:** Equity classification when employer controls settlement; straight-line expense without remeasurement; cash settlement with catch-up expense; stock settlement reclassification  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Instruments Co.** grants **5,000 SARs** on **January 1, Year 1**. The **employer** (Northwind)—not the employee—has the right to settle the SARs in **common stock** (or cash at the employer’s option). Accordingly the awards are classified as **equity**.

- Grant-date **fair value of the SARs award** is estimated at **\$30,000** total (\$6 per SAR).  
- Requisite service period: **3 years** (straight-line recognition).  
- Grant-date market price of common stock: **\$20** per share (\$1 par).  
- For informational purposes only, year-end fair values of the SARs are \$4, \$8, and \$9 per unit in Years 1–3—these amounts **do not** change equity-classified measurement.  
- On **December 31, Year 3**, the employee exercises. Market price is **\$32** per share. Intrinsic value = (\$32 − \$20) × 5,000 = **\$60,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Explain why these SARs are **equity**-classified and how measurement differs from liability SARs.  
b. Prepare the **annual compensation entries** for Years 1–3 (initial recognition and subsequent straight-line accruals).  
c. Assume Northwind **elects cash settlement** of \$60,000 on December 31, Year 3. Prepare the **settlement JE**.  
d. **Alternate settlement:** instead assume Northwind settles by issuing common shares equal to intrinsic value ÷ market price (same \$32 market). Prepare the **stock settlement JE**.  
e. **Disclosure / presentation:** contrast the balance-sheet line (PIC—SARs Plan vs SARs Liability) during the service period.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Equity classification and measurement**

Because the **employer** controls settlement and may settle in **stock**, a transfer of assets is not required by the employee → **equity** classification.  
- Measure total compensation at **grant-date fair value (\$30,000)** only.  
- Recognize Compensation Expense **straight-line** over the 3-year requisite service period: \$30,000 / 3 = **\$10,000 per year**.  
- Credit **Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan** (not a liability).  
- **Do not remeasure** for subsequent changes in stock price or SAR fair value (ignore the \$4 / \$8 / \$9 year-end FVs for measurement).

**b. Annual compensation entries (equity)**

*December 31, Year 1 — Initial recognition (grant-date FV fixed)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 10,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan | | 10,000 |
| *\$30,000 grant-date FV ÷ 3 years* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 10,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,000. **Balanced.** Cumulative PIC—SARs = **\$20,000**.

*December 31, Year 3*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 10,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan | | 10,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,000. **Balanced.** Cumulative PIC—SARs = **\$30,000**.

**c. Cash settlement by employer (cash > accumulated PIC)**

Cash \$60,000 > PIC—SARs \$30,000 → additional **Compensation Expense \$30,000**.

*December 31, Year 3 — Cash settlement of equity-classified SARs*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan | 30,000 | |
| Compensation Expense | 30,000 | |
| Cash | | 60,000 |
| *Clear equity credit; excess cash over grant-date FV is additional compensation cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 30,000 + 30,000 = Cr 60,000. **Balanced.**

**d. Stock settlement alternate (same market \$32)**

Shares issued = intrinsic value ÷ market price = \$60,000 ÷ \$32 = **1,875** shares.  
Reclassify the **full** PIC—SARs balance (\$30,000) into Common Stock (par) and APIC (textbook Review 20-10 pattern).

*Stock settlement JE (employer issues common stock)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan | 30,000 | |
| Common Stock (\$1 par × 1,875 shares) | | 1,875 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 28,125 |
| *Reclassify full PIC—SARs into permanent equity; shares = (\$32 − \$20) × 5,000 / \$32 = 1,875* | | |

**Check:** Dr 30,000 = Cr 1,875 + 28,125. **Balanced.**

**e. Presentation / disclosure**

| Feature | Liability SARs (Q1/Q2) | Equity SARs (Q3) |
|---|---|---|
| Credit account during service | **SARs Liability** (BS liability) | **Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan** (equity) |
| Remeasurement | Yes, each period to updated FV | No; grant-date FV only |
| Settlement | Dr Liability / Cr Cash | Dr PIC—SARs; may Dr Comp Exp if cash > PIC; or reclass to CS + APIC |
| Disclosure | Nature of plan, liability balances, FV assumptions | Nature of plan, grant-date FV, expense recognized, shares potentially issuable |

**Key insight:** Classification hinges on **who can force cash**. Equity SARs lock in grant-date FV; liability SARs continually remeasure. Cash settlement of equity awards can create **extra compensation expense** when cash exceeds accumulated PIC—SARs.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of SARs as liability vs equity
**LO:** LO 20-10  
**Concept:** ASC 718 classification: employee cash settlement feature → liability; employer stock settlement control → equity  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 1, two otherwise identical SARs plans are granted (same number of units, same grant-date fair value, same three-year requisite service period).  

- **Plan L:** The **employee** may elect settlement in **cash**.  
- **Plan E:** Only the **employer** may elect settlement, and the employer may settle in **common stock**.  

Which statement is **correct**?

- A) Both plans are measured only at grant-date fair value and credit Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan each year.  
- B) Plan L is remeasured each period as a liability; Plan E uses fixed grant-date fair value with credits to Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan.  
- C) Plan E is a liability because share-based awards always create an obligation to transfer resources.  
- D) Plan L and Plan E both reverse previously recognized expense if year-end fair value of the SARs declines.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Plan L meets the cash-settlement condition for **liability** classification (employee can require cash) → cumulative liability = current FV × % service; expense can reverse if FV falls. Plan E is **equity** because the employer controls settlement and may settle in stock → grant-date FV only, straight-line to PIC—SARs, **no** FV remeasurement. A is wrong for Plan L. C misstates equity classification. D is true only for liability-classified Plan L, not Plan E.

### Q5 — MC — Settlement of fully vested liability SARs
**LO:** LO 20-10  
**Concept:** Settlement JE after liability is fully remeasured to expected cash  
**Question:**  
At exercise date, a company has already recorded a SARs Liability of **\$55,000** equal to the cash intrinsic value payable. The employee exercises and receives **\$55,000** cash. The correct settlement entry is:

- A) Debit Compensation Expense \$55,000; Credit Cash \$55,000  
- B) Debit Paid-in Capital—SARs Plan \$55,000; Credit Cash \$55,000  
- C) Debit SARs Liability \$55,000; Credit Cash \$55,000  
- D) Debit SARs Liability \$55,000; Credit Common Stock \$55,000  

**Answer:** **C.**  
After period-end remeasurement, the liability already equals cash to be paid; settlement **removes the liability** and pays cash. No additional expense arises at settlement if the liability is already correct (contrast equity cash settlement when cash exceeds PIC). B is an equity account. A would double-count expense. D is wrong for cash-settled liability SARs.
