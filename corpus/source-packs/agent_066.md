# Agent 66 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-1

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Determine costs to capitalize for land, land improvements, equipment, buildings, and construction in process  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Historical cost principle: capitalize costs necessary to bring PP&E to condition and location for intended use
- **Land** costs (purchase, closing, razing net of salvage, grading, permanent assessments) — not depreciated
- **Land improvements** (parking lots, fencing, landscaping, sprinklers) — depreciated
- **Equipment** costs (invoice net of cash discount taken, tax, freight, installation, testing) — exclude training and post-ready interest
- **Buildings** vs **Construction in process (CIP)** while under construction; reclass CIP → Building only when complete and ready for use
- Self-constructed assets: capitalize materials, labor, incremental/allocated overhead; cap at fair value (excess → loss)
- Period costs that are **expensed**: employee training, interest after asset is ready, annual property taxes/insurance, ordinary repairs
- Subsequent measurement: straight-line depreciation begins when asset is placed in service (not while in CIP)
- Period-end adjusting JE for depreciation on newly placed assets
- Classification / balance-sheet presentation of PP&E categories
- Disposal-related salvage proceeds that reduce land cost (razing)

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-category acquisition classification, summary capitalization JE, and first-year depreciation schedule
**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Classify and capitalize land, land improvements, equipment, and CIP; expense noncapital costs; initial recognition JE; subsequent SL depreciation schedule  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Fabricators** acquired plant assets and began constructing a production building during the year. All amounts were paid in cash (equipment seller granted a cash purchase discount that Northline took). Construction of the building is **still underway** at year-end. The new equipment was placed in service on **July 1**; land improvements were placed in service on **October 1**. Northline uses straight-line depreciation, zero salvage for land improvements and equipment, and a December 31 year-end.

**e. Classification / presentation**  
Report as noncurrent assets under property, plant, and equipment (or equivalent): Land; Land improvements (often net of AD, or gross with AD disclosed); Equipment (gross less AD); Construction in process (included in PP&E even though not yet operational). Land is not depreciated. CIP is reclassified to Building when construction is complete and the asset is ready for intended use.

**Key insight:** Capitalize only costs that prepare the asset for intended use. Training, post-ready interest, and period property taxes are period expenses. Razing and grading attach to land; parking/fencing are land improvements; incomplete building costs sit in CIP—not Building—until completion.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin multi-category classification and summary JE
**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of acquisition cost classification and balanced capitalization JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperline Assembly Co.** paid cash for the following plant-related costs. A warehouse addition is under construction at year-end. No depreciation is required for this item (classification and initial recognition only).

| Cost incurred | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Invoice price of machinery | $120,000 |
| 2% cash discount taken on machinery | (2,400) |
| Sales tax on machinery (6% of invoice) | 7,200 |
| Freight-in and handling | 2,800 |
| Installation and calibration | 5,600 |
| Cost to train machine operators | 1,400 |
| Interest on note used to finance machinery after it was ready for use | 2,100 |
| Purchase price of land for plant expansion | 250,000 |
| Legal fees and closing costs on land | 12,000 |
| Demolition of obsolete warehouse on the land | 28,000 |
| Cash received for scrap lumber and fixtures from demolition | (4,000) |
| Clearing, filling, and drainage for general land use | 15,500 |
| Site excavation for new warehouse foundation | 8,000 |
| Materials and labor for poured foundation (still under construction) | 42,000 |
| Building permits and engineering fees | 9,500 |
| Contractor progress payment on warehouse | 70,000 |
| Annual property insurance for the land for the current year | 1,100 |
| Parking lot paving and striping | 19,000 |
| Outdoor lighting system for parking area | 6,500 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute totals for Land, Land Improvements, Equipment, Building, CIP, and Period Expense.  
b. Prepare the summary cash journal entry.  
c. Prove that Dr = Cr and that capitalized PP&E + expenses = net cash.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Category totals**

**Equipment:**  
$120,000 − $2,400 + $7,200 + $2,800 + $5,600 = **$133,200**

**Land:**  
$250,000 + $12,000 + $28,000 − $4,000 + $15,500 = **$301,500**

**Land improvements:**  
$19,000 + $6,500 = **$25,500**

**CIP:**  
$8,000 + $42,000 + $9,500 + $70,000 = **$129,500**

**Building:** **$0** (under construction)

**Period expense:**  
Training $1,400 + Interest $2,100 + Property insurance $1,100 = **$4,600**

| Category | Total |
|---|---:|
| Land | 301,500 |
| Land Improvements | 25,500 |
| Equipment | 133,200 |
| Building | 0 |
| Construction in Process | 129,500 |
| Period Expense | 4,600 |
| **Cash outlay** | **594,300** |

**b. Summary JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 301,500 | |
| Land Improvements | 25,500 | |
| Equipment | 133,200 | |
| Construction in Process | 129,500 | |
| Training Expense | 1,400 | |
| Interest Expense | 2,100 | |
| Insurance Expense | 1,100 | |
| Cash | | 594,300 |
| *To record plant asset acquisitions, CIP, and period costs* | | |

**c. Proof**  
Dr: $301,500 + $25,500 + $133,200 + $129,500 + $1,400 + $2,100 + $1,100 = **$594,300** = Cr Cash.  
Capitalized PP&E = $301,500 + $25,500 + $133,200 + $129,500 = **$589,700**; + expenses $4,600 = **$594,300**. Balanced.

**Key insight:** Same decision rules as Q1 with different magnitudes: cash discounts and salvage **reduce** the capitalized base; incomplete building costs accumulate in CIP; operator training and post-ready financing costs never enter the asset accounts.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Self-constructed equipment (cost vs FV), CIP completion reclass, period-end dep, and disposal of old asset
**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Self-constructed asset transfer from CIP (with optional loss if cost > FV); completion reclass CIP → Building; period-end depreciation; salvage disposal reducing land  
**Scenario:**  

**Part A — Self-constructed equipment (Demo 11-1B style)**  
**Riverton Components** completed construction of specialized production equipment on **June 1**. Accumulated costs in Construction in Process:

| CIP component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Direct materials | $175,000 |
| Direct labor | 420,000 |
| Incremental overhead | 55,000 |
| Applied general overhead | 35,000 |
| **Total CIP** | **$685,000** |

1. Record the June 1 transfer from CIP to Equipment if fair value of comparable equipment is **$710,000**.  
2. Separately, record the June 1 transfer if fair value is only **$660,000**.

**Part B — Building completion and period-end depreciation**  
On **September 1**, Riverton’s warehouse project (unrelated to Part A) is completed and placed in service. CIP balance for the warehouse is **$480,000** (includes materials, contractor billings, permits, and allocated construction overhead). Estimated useful life is **30 years**, residual value **$30,000**, straight-line, year-end December 31.

1. Record the September 1 reclassification from CIP to Building.  
2. Record the December 31 adjusting entry for building depreciation for the current year.

**Part C — Land acquisition with razing (disposal of old structure) and presentation**  
On **March 15**, Riverton purchased a parcel for **$200,000** cash for a future plant site. It paid **$15,000** to demolish an existing shell building and received **$2,500** cash for salvaged materials. Legal/closing costs were **$6,000**. No new construction started this year.

1. Record the journal entry(ies) for the land acquisition, razing, and salvage (may be combined).  
2. State the land amount reported on the year-end balance sheet and why the demolished building is **not** recorded as a Building asset.

**Required:**  
a. Part A (both fair-value cases).  
b. Part B (reclass + depreciation).  
c. Part C (land JE + presentation).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Part A — Self-constructed equipment**

**Case 1 — FV $710,000 ≥ cost $685,000**  
Record equipment at construction cost (do not write up to FV).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 685,000 | |
| Construction in Process | | 685,000 |
| *Transfer completed self-constructed equipment at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 685,000.

**Case 2 — FV $660,000 < cost $685,000**  
Cap equipment at FV; recognize loss for excess construction cost with no future benefit.

Loss = $685,000 − $660,000 = **$25,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 660,000 | |
| Loss on Construction of Equipment | 25,000 | |
| Construction in Process | | 685,000 |
| *Transfer at FV; expense excess construction cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr $660,000 + $25,000 = Cr $685,000. Balanced.

**b. Part B — CIP settlement (completion) and period-end adjusting**

**September 1 — reclassify completed building**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 480,000 | |
| Construction in Process | | 480,000 |
| *Transfer CIP to Building when ready for intended use* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 480,000.

**December 31 — partial-year depreciation**  
Depreciable base = $480,000 − $30,000 = **$450,000**  
Annual SL = $450,000 ÷ 30 = **$15,000**  
In service Sept 1 → 4/12 year → $15,000 × 4/12 = **$5,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 5,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 5,000 |
| *4/12 of annual SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000.  
Carrying amount at Dec 31: $480,000 − $5,000 = **$475,000**.

**c. Part C — Land with razing (disposal of old structure) and disclosure**

Land cost = $200,000 + $15,000 − $2,500 + $6,000 = **$218,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 218,500 | |
| Cash | | 218,500 |
| *Purchase land; capitalize razing net of salvage and closing costs* | | |

(If recorded stepwise: Dr Land 200,000 / Cr Cash 200,000; Dr Land 15,000 / Cr Cash 15,000; Dr Cash 2,500 / Cr Land 2,500; Dr Land 6,000 / Cr Cash 6,000 — same net.)

**Presentation:** Land is reported at **$218,500** historical cost under PP&E; **not depreciated**. The demolished shell is **not** capitalized as Building because the land was acquired for redevelopment as a building **site**—razing prepares the land for its intended use, so net razing cost attaches to **Land**. Salvage proceeds reduce land cost (disposal of materials from the old structure).

**Key insight:** CIP is a temporary holding account: self-constructed equipment and buildings leave CIP only when complete, measured at cost not exceeding fair value. Depreciation starts only after placement in service. Demolition for a building site is a land cost, not a building cost.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of acquisition costs (capitalize vs expense; correct PP&E account)
**LO:** LO 11-1  
**Concept:** Classification of common acquisition and holding costs among land, land improvements, equipment, CIP, and expense  

**Question 1:**  
Harbor Point Logistics purchased land with an old warehouse for a new distribution center site. Which of the following is **correctly** included in the **Land** account?

- A) Cost of paving a new employee parking lot on the site  
- B) Annual property taxes for the first year after acquisition  
- C) Cost to demolish the old warehouse, net of salvage proceeds  
- D) Interest on a note used to finance the land after the site is ready for general use  

**Answer:** C.  
Razing costs (net of salvage) incurred to prepare land for use as a building site are capitalized to Land. Parking lots are land improvements (A). Annual property taxes after acquisition are period expenses (B). Interest after the land is ready for its intended use is expensed (D)—interest capitalization (LO 11-4) applies only during active construction of a qualifying asset, not mere post-acquisition holding of ready land.

**Question 2:**  
Which cost related to a newly purchased machine is **expensed** as incurred rather than capitalized to Equipment?

- A) Freight and in-transit insurance to the plant  
- B) Sales tax on the purchase invoice  
- C) Costs of trial runs and testing before production use  
- D) Cost of training production employees to operate the machine  

**Answer:** D.  
Training enhances the value of employees, not the machine; it is a period expense. Freight, sales tax, and testing/trial runs are necessary to bring the equipment to condition and location for intended use and are capitalized (A–C).
