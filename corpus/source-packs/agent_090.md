# Agent 90 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-5

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Determine the initial and subsequent measurement of indefinite life intangible assets and goodwill  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition of goodwill:** residual = purchase price − fair value of identifiable net assets acquired in a business combination
- **Identifiable intangibles first:** recognize separable/contractual intangibles (e.g., trademark, customer list, acquired IPR&D) at fair value **before** measuring residual goodwill
- **Indefinite-life intangibles (non-crypto):** initially capitalize at cost (purchase price + directly attributable costs); **do not amortize**
- **Acquired in-process R&D:** initially recognized as an **indefinite-life** intangible until the project is complete (or abandoned)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** carrying amount roll-forward with **zero amortization** for goodwill and other indefinite-life intangibles (impairment is LO 13-6)
- **Crypto assets (core Demo 13-5A path):** indefinite-life intangibles remeasured to **fair value** each reporting date; unrealized gains/losses in **net income**
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** fair-value remeasurement of crypto holdings (not amortization)
- **Disposal JE:** remove carrying amount of an indefinite-life intangible sold; difference vs cash = gain/loss
- **Classification / presentation:** goodwill as a **separate** balance-sheet line; other indefinite-life intangibles not amortized; crypto separate from other intangibles with FV changes in income

### Q1 — CORE — Business combination: goodwill residual, indefinite-life assets, subsequent schedule, disposal
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for acquisition (goodwill residual + indefinite trademark/IPR&D); subsequent carrying-amount schedule (no amortization); disposal of indefinite-life trademark  
**Scenario:**  
**HarborPeak Foods Inc.** (calendar-year, public company) acquires **100% of Crestline Bakes LLC** for **$650,000 cash** on **January 1, Year 1**. HarborPeak obtains control and records the identifiable assets and liabilities of Crestline at fair value; any residual is goodwill.

Crestline’s balance sheet data and HarborPeak’s fair-value estimates on the acquisition date:

| Account | Book value (as reported) | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | $45,000 | $45,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 120,000 | 110,000 |
| Inventory | 85,000 | 95,000 |
| Property, plant, and equipment (net) | 310,000 | 360,000 |
| Trademark (not recognized by Crestline; renewable indefinitely) | — | 80,000 |
| Customer list (not recognized by Crestline; 5-year finite life) | — | 40,000 |
| Acquired in-process research and development (incomplete project) | — | 55,000 |
| **Total assets** | | **$785,000** |
| Accounts payable | 70,000 | 70,000 |
| Notes payable | 150,000 | 145,000 |
| **Total liabilities** | | **$215,000** |

HarborPeak does **not** elect the private-company goodwill amortization alternative. On **December 31, Year 1**, there is no impairment of any indefinite-life intangible or goodwill (impairment testing is outside this LO’s measurement focus). The customer list is amortized straight-line over 5 years with zero residual (for contrast only).

On **June 30, Year 2**, HarborPeak sells the **Crestline trademark** to an unrelated party for **$72,000 cash**. No amortization was ever recorded on the trademark.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **fair value of identifiable net assets** and the amount of **goodwill** HarborPeak records on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the acquisition of Crestline Bakes. Show that Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / carrying-amount schedule** for Year 1 for: (1) Trademark, (2) Acquired IPR&D, (3) Goodwill, and (4) Customer list (finite contrast). State the Year-1 amortization entry, if any, for each.  
d. Prepare the **June 30, Year 2 disposal journal entry** for the sale of the trademark.  
e. State how **goodwill** and the **indefinite-life intangibles** (trademark, IPR&D) are **presented** on HarborPeak’s December 31, Year 1 balance sheet relative to other assets.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Identifiable net assets and goodwill**

Fair value of identifiable assets = **$785,000**  
Fair value of identifiable liabilities = **$215,000**  
Fair value of **identifiable net assets** = $785,000 − $215,000 = **$570,000**

\[
\text{Goodwill} = \text{Purchase price} - \text{FV of identifiable net assets} = \$650{,}000 - \$570{,}000 = \mathbf{\$80{,}000}
\]

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of acquisition (emphasis: residual goodwill + indefinite-life intangibles at FV)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 45,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | 110,000 | |
| Inventory | 95,000 | |
| Property, Plant, and Equipment | 360,000 | |
| Trademark (indefinite life) | 80,000 | |
| Customer List (finite life) | 40,000 | |
| Acquired In-Process Research and Development (indefinite until complete) | 55,000 | |
| Goodwill | 80,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 70,000 |
| Notes Payable | | 145,000 |
| Cash | | 650,000 |
| *Record Crestline assets/liabilities at FV; residual to goodwill* | | |

**Check:**  
Dr = 45,000 + 110,000 + 95,000 + 360,000 + 80,000 + 40,000 + 55,000 + 80,000 = **865,000**  
Cr = 70,000 + 145,000 + 650,000 = **865,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — Year 1 (public company; no goodwill amortization)**

| Asset | Initial carrying amount (1/1/Y1) | Amortization Year 1 | Ending carrying amount (12/31/Y1) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Trademark (indefinite) | $80,000 | **$0** | $80,000 |
| Acquired IPR&D (indefinite until complete) | 55,000 | **0** | 55,000 |
| Goodwill | 80,000 | **0** | 80,000 |
| Customer list (finite, 5 years SL) | 40,000 | **8,000** | 32,000 |

*Indefinite-life items and goodwill:* **no amortization entry** in Year 1.

*Finite contrast only — December 31, Year 1 customer-list amortization:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortization Expense—Customer List ($40,000 ÷ 5) | 8,000 | |
| Accumulated Amortization—Customer List | | 8,000 |
| *Only finite-life intangible is amortized* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000.

**d. June 30, Year 2 — disposal of indefinite-life trademark**

Carrying amount of trademark = **$80,000** (never amortized)  
Cash proceeds = **$72,000**  
Loss on disposal = $80,000 − $72,000 = **$8,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 72,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Trademark | 8,000 | |
| Trademark | | 80,000 |
| *Remove indefinite-life trademark at carrying amount; recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 + 8,000 = Cr 80,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Classification / presentation (December 31, Year 1)**  
- **Goodwill $80,000** is presented as a **separate line item** on the statement of financial position (not aggregated with other intangibles).  
- **Trademark $80,000** and **Acquired IPR&D $55,000** are reported among intangible assets (other than goodwill), **unamortized** (subject to impairment testing under LO 13-6, not amortized under LO 13-5 subsequent measurement).  
- Customer list is reported net of accumulated amortization among finite-life intangibles.

**Key insight:** Goodwill is a **residual** after all identifiable assets (including previously unrecognized trademarks, customer lists, and acquired IPR&D) and liabilities are measured at fair value. Indefinite-life intangibles and goodwill stay at cost **without amortization**; disposal removes the full carrying amount against cash proceeds.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Acquisition residual goodwill twin
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial recognition of goodwill and indefinite-life intangibles; zero-amortization subsequent schedule; disposal with gain  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Industrial Corp.** (calendar-year, public) acquires **100% of Summit Forge Co.** for **$920,000 cash** on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Account | Book value | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | $60,000 | $60,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 150,000 | 140,000 |
| Inventory | 100,000 | 120,000 |
| Property, plant, and equipment (net) | 480,000 | 520,000 |
| Trade name (not on Summit’s books; indefinite life) | — | 100,000 |
| Acquired in-process R&D (incomplete) | — | 70,000 |
| Renewable operating license (indefinite life) | — | 45,000 |
| **Total assets at FV** | | **$1,055,000** |
| Accounts payable | 90,000 | 90,000 |
| Long-term debt | 200,000 | 185,000 |
| **Total liabilities at FV** | | **$275,000** |

No private-company goodwill amortization election. No impairment in Year 1. On **March 1, Year 2**, Northline sells the **renewable operating license** for **$52,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute FV of identifiable net assets and **goodwill**.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 acquisition journal entry**.  
c. Prepare a Year-1 **carrying-amount schedule** for Trade name, Acquired IPR&D, Renewable license, and Goodwill (amortization for each).  
d. Prepare the **March 1, Year 2 disposal entry** for the license.  
e. Compute **total unamortized indefinite-life intangibles other than goodwill** at December 31, Year 1.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Goodwill**

FV identifiable assets = **$1,055,000**  
FV identifiable liabilities = **$275,000**  
FV identifiable net assets = $1,055,000 − $275,000 = **$780,000**

\[
\text{Goodwill} = \$920{,}000 - \$780{,}000 = \mathbf{\$140{,}000}
\]

**b. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 60,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | 140,000 | |
| Inventory | 120,000 | |
| Property, Plant, and Equipment | 520,000 | |
| Trade Name (indefinite life) | 100,000 | |
| Acquired In-Process Research and Development | 70,000 | |
| Renewable Operating License (indefinite life) | 45,000 | |
| Goodwill | 140,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 90,000 |
| Long-Term Debt | | 185,000 |
| Cash | | 920,000 |
| *Assets/liabilities at FV; residual goodwill* | | |

**Check:**  
Dr = 60 + 140 + 120 + 520 + 100 + 70 + 45 + 140 = **1,195,000**  
Cr = 90 + 185 + 920 = **1,195,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — Year 1**

| Asset | 1/1/Y1 carrying amount | Year-1 amortization | 12/31/Y1 carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Trade name (indefinite) | $100,000 | **$0** | $100,000 |
| Acquired IPR&D (indefinite until complete) | 70,000 | **0** | 70,000 |
| Renewable operating license (indefinite) | 45,000 | **0** | 45,000 |
| Goodwill | 140,000 | **0** | 140,000 |

**No amortization journal entries** for any of the above in Year 1.

**d. March 1, Year 2 — disposal of renewable license**

Carrying amount = **$45,000**  
Proceeds = **$52,000**  
**Gain on disposal** = $52,000 − $45,000 = **$7,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 52,000 | |
| Renewable Operating License | | 45,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Intangible Asset | | 7,000 |
| *Remove license at unamortized carrying amount; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr 52,000 = Cr 45,000 + 7,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Indefinite-life intangibles other than goodwill at 12/31/Y1**  
Trade name $100,000 + IPR&D $70,000 + License $45,000 = **$215,000** (all unamortized).

**Key insight:** Changing every number still leaves the same measurement model: **FV net assets first**, residual **goodwill**, indefinite-life items and goodwill **held at cost with zero amortization** until disposal or impairment (impairment = LO 13-6).

### Q3 — CORE — Crypto asset initial recognition + period-end FV adjusting JE; standalone indefinite trademark
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Crypto indefinite-life intangible at cost then period-end fair-value remeasurement (Demo 13-5A path); standalone indefinite trademark at cost with no amortization  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeview Digital Holdings** (calendar year) engages in the following Year 1 transactions related to indefinite-life intangibles:

| Date | Transaction |
|---|---|
| January 1 | Purchases **2 units** of a fungible crypto asset meeting ASC 350-60 criteria for **$48,000 per unit** cash ($96,000 total). Classifies as indefinite-life intangible (crypto). |
| March 15 | Pays **$35,000** purchase price plus **$4,000** external legal/registration fees cash for a **trademark** management concludes has an **indefinite** useful life (renewable indefinitely; no foreseeable limit on cash-flow contribution). |
| December 31 | Still holds both crypto units. Fair value is **$55,000 per unit**. Trademark still indefinite; no impairment indicated. |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1** journal entry to record the crypto purchase (**initial recognition**).  
b. Prepare the **March 15** journal entry to record the trademark (**initial recognition at cost**).  
c. Prepare the **December 31 period-end adjusting entry** to remeasure the crypto assets to fair value.  
d. State the **December 31 carrying amounts** of (1) crypto assets and (2) the trademark, and whether either is amortized in Year 1.  
e. Briefly state **income-statement and balance-sheet presentation** of the crypto fair-value change versus the trademark.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1 — initial recognition of crypto assets**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crypto Assets—Bitcoin (or named crypto unit) | 96,000 | |
| Cash | | 96,000 |
| *2 units × $48,000; indefinite-life intangible at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 96,000.

**b. March 15 — initial recognition of indefinite-life trademark**

Cost = purchase price + directly attributable legal/registration fees  
= $35,000 + $4,000 = **$39,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trademark | 39,000 | |
| Cash | | 39,000 |
| *Capitalize acquisition cost of indefinite-life trademark* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 39,000.

**c. December 31 — period-end adjusting JE (crypto fair-value remeasurement)**

Fair value at year-end = 2 × $55,000 = **$110,000**  
Carrying amount before adjustment = **$96,000**  
Unrealized gain = $110,000 − $96,000 = **$14,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crypto Assets | 14,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain on Crypto Assets—Income | | 14,000 |
| *Remeasure crypto to FV; gain in net income (ASC 350-60)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 14,000.

**d. Carrying amounts and amortization**

| Asset | 12/31 carrying amount | Amortized in Year 1? |
|---|---:|---|
| Crypto assets | **$110,000** (FV) | **No** — remeasured to FV, not amortized |
| Trademark | **$39,000** (cost) | **No** — indefinite life; held at cost |

**e. Presentation**  
- Crypto assets reported on the balance sheet at **fair value**, **separately** from other intangible assets; the **$14,000 unrealized gain** is included in **net income** (presented separately from changes in other intangibles’ carrying amounts).  
- Trademark remains at **historical cost $39,000** among intangible assets other than goodwill; **no amortization expense** and no FV mark-up under the general indefinite-life model.

**Key insight:** Both crypto and a renewable trademark are **indefinite-life** intangibles, but crypto has a **special subsequent measurement** rule (FV through net income each reporting date), while other indefinite-life intangibles stay at **cost** without amortization.

### Q4 — MC — Subsequent measurement / classification
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Classification of subsequent measurement — which acquired items are amortized vs held at cost (or FV for crypto)  
**Question:**  
On January 1, **Lakeport Brands** completed a business combination and recorded the following assets (among others):

| Asset | Amount | Useful life conclusion |
|---|---:|---|
| Patent | $90,000 | Finite, 9 years remaining |
| Trademark | 120,000 | Indefinite (renewable; no foreseeable limit) |
| Acquired in-process R&D | 48,000 | Project incomplete; treated as indefinite until complete |
| Goodwill | 75,000 | Residual from the acquisition |

Lakeport is a public company (no private-company goodwill amortization election). Ignoring impairment and crypto, which statement correctly describes **Year-1 subsequent measurement** under LO 13-5?

- A) Amortize patent, trademark, acquired IPR&D, and goodwill over their respective useful lives.  
- B) Amortize only the patent; do **not** amortize the trademark, acquired IPR&D, or goodwill.  
- C) Amortize the patent and goodwill over 10 years; do not amortize trademark or acquired IPR&D.  
- D) Do not amortize any of the four assets because all arose in a business combination.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 13-5, an intangible asset with a **finite** useful life (patent) is amortized; an intangible with an **indefinite** useful life (trademark; acquired IPR&D while the project remains incomplete) is **not amortized**. **Goodwill is not amortized** for public companies (tested for impairment under LO 13-6). Option A incorrectly amortizes indefinite-life items and goodwill. Option C incorrectly amortizes goodwill under the public-company model. Option D incorrectly omits amortization of the finite-life patent.

### Q5 — MC — Initial goodwill measurement
**LO:** LO 13-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition — compute residual goodwill from purchase price and FV of identifiable net assets  
**Question:**  
**Cedarline Retail Inc.** pays **$480,000 cash** to acquire a competitor. Fair values of identifiable assets acquired total **$710,000**; fair values of liabilities assumed total **$290,000**. Identifiable assets include a previously unrecognized brand name valued at **$60,000** (indefinite life). What amount of **goodwill** does Cedarline record?

- A) $0  
- B) $60,000  
- C) $120,000  
- D) $190,000  

**Answer:** **B. $60,000.**  
FV of identifiable net assets = $710,000 − $290,000 = **$420,000**.  
Goodwill = $480,000 − $420,000 = **$60,000**.  
The brand name is already included in the $710,000 of identifiable assets; it is **not** added again when measuring goodwill. Option C ($120,000) would result from incorrectly treating goodwill as equal to the brand name plus something else, or from other mis-subtraction. Option D ($190,000) equals purchase price minus liabilities only (ignores assets): $480,000 − $290,000. Option A is incorrect because purchase price exceeds FV of net assets.
