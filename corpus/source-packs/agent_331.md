# Agent 331 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-4

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Interest cost incurred during the construction of a fixed asset is considered to (Calculate capitalized interest)  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Qualifying asset and capitalization period (expenditures made; activities in progress; interest cost incurred)
- Weighted-average accumulated expenditures (WAAE) schedule
- Actual interest: specific (project) debt vs general debt
- Weighted-average interest rate on general debt = general interest ÷ general principal
- Avoidable interest: apply specific rate first up to specific debt, then WA general rate on excess WAAE
- Capitalize the **lesser** of actual interest and avoidable interest
- Initial recognition JEs: capital expenditures → Construction in Process (CIP)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Dr CIP (capitalized amount), Dr Interest Expense (remainder), Cr Cash or Interest Payable for total actual interest
- Subsequent measurement: CIP rollforward including capitalized interest
- Settlement / completion: reclassify CIP → Building; capitalized interest depreciated over building life
- Land as building site: land expenditures enter WAAE; capitalized interest becomes part of **Building** (not Land)
- Disclosure: total interest incurred, amount capitalized, amount expensed

### Q1 — CORE — Meridian Robotics: expenditure JEs, WAAE & avoidable schedules, period-end interest adjusting JE, CIP rollforward, disclosure
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Calculate capitalized interest (lesser of actual vs avoidable); initial CIP recognition; period-end adjusting JE for interest capitalization; CIP rollforward and disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Robotics Corp.** (calendar year-end) is self-constructing an automated warehouse for **its own use** (a qualifying asset). Construction began **January 1** of the current year and is **still in progress** at **December 31**. Activities necessary to get the warehouse ready for intended use were in progress throughout the year, and interest cost was incurred all year. All listed debt was outstanding for the **full year**. Interest is settled in cash at year-end as part of the period-end adjusting process.

**Capital expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Amount |
|---|---:|
| January 1 | $1,800,000 |
| April 1 | 720,000 |
| July 1 | 960,000 |
| November 1 | 480,000 |
| December 31 | 240,000 |

**Outstanding debt at year-end:**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction loan (project-specific) | $1,200,000 | 9% | Specific |
| Note payable | 1,000,000 | 8% | General |
| Bonds payable | 2,000,000 | 11% | General |
| Note payable | 500,000 | 10% | General |

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **journal entry** for each capital expenditure date (initial recognition to Construction in Process).  
(b) Prepare the **weighted-average accumulated expenditures (WAAE)** schedule.  
(c) Compute **actual interest** for the year and the **weighted-average interest rate on general debt**.  
(d) Prepare the **avoidable interest** schedule (specific debt first, then general) and determine the amount of interest to **capitalize** (lesser of actual vs avoidable).  
(e) Record the **December 31 period-end adjusting journal entry** for interest (cash settlement of full actual interest) — **emphasis**.  
(f) Prepare a **Construction in Process rollforward** for the year.  
(g) Show the **interest cost disclosure** for the December 31 financial statements.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Capital expenditure JEs (initial recognition)**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 | Construction in Process | 1,800,000 | |
| | Cash | | 1,800,000 |
| Apr 1 | Construction in Process | 720,000 | |
| | Cash | | 720,000 |
| Jul 1 | Construction in Process | 960,000 | |
| | Cash | | 960,000 |
| Nov 1 | Construction in Process | 480,000 | |
| | Cash | | 480,000 |
| Dec 31 | Construction in Process | 240,000 | |
| | Cash | | 240,000 |

Each entry balances (Dr = Cr). Total cash expenditures to CIP = **$4,200,000**.

**(b) Weighted-average accumulated expenditures**

| Date | Actual expenditure | Months outstanding | Weight | Weighted amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 | $1,800,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $1,800,000 |
| April 1 | 720,000 | 9 | 9/12 | 540,000 |
| July 1 | 960,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 480,000 |
| November 1 | 480,000 | 2 | 2/12 | 80,000 |
| December 31 | 240,000 | 0 | 0/12 | 0 |
| **Totals** | **$4,200,000** | | | **$2,900,000** |

**WAAE = $2,900,000**  
(Note: the December 31 expenditure receives zero weight for the current-year WAAE.)

**(c) Actual interest and general debt rate**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Construction loan (specific) | $1,200,000 | 9% | $108,000 |
| Note payable (general) | 1,000,000 | 8% | 80,000 |
| Bonds payable (general) | 2,000,000 | 11% | 220,000 |
| Note payable (general) | 500,000 | 10% | 50,000 |
| **Total actual interest** | | | **$458,000** |

General debt principal = \(1{,}000{,}000 + 2{,}000{,}000 + 500{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3{,}500{,}000}\)  
General debt interest = \(80{,}000 + 220{,}000 + 50{,}000 = \mathbf{\$350{,}000}\)  
Weighted-average general rate = \(350{,}000 / 3{,}500{,}000 = \mathbf{10\%}\)

**(d) Avoidable interest and amount capitalized**

| Layer | WAAE applied | Rate | Avoidable interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Specific debt | $1,200,000 | 9% | $108,000 |
| Excess over specific | \(2{,}900{,}000 - 1{,}200{,}000 = 1{,}700{,}000\) | 10% | 170,000 |
| **Total avoidable interest** | **$2,900,000** | | **$278,000** |

Actual interest = **$458,000**  
Avoidable interest = **$278,000**  
**Capitalize lesser = $278,000**  
Interest expensed = \(458{,}000 - 278{,}000 = \mathbf{\$180{,}000}\)

**(e) December 31 period-end adjusting JE for interest (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 278,000 | |
| Interest Expense | 180,000 | |
| Cash | | 458,000 |
| *To record actual interest; capitalize lesser of actual vs avoidable* | | |

Proof: Dr \(278{,}000 + 180{,}000 = 458{,}000\) = Cr. **Balances.**  
This period-end adjusting entry **increases CIP** (capitalized portion) and recognizes **Interest Expense** only for the residual actual interest not capitalized.

**(f) Construction in Process rollforward**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning CIP | $0 |
| + Capital expenditures | 4,200,000 |
| + Capitalized interest | 278,000 |
| **Ending CIP** | **$4,478,000** |

Check: \(4{,}200{,}000 + 278{,}000 = \mathbf{4{,}478{,}000}\).

**(g) Interest cost disclosure (December 31)**

| Disclosure | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total interest cost incurred | $458,000 |
| Interest cost capitalized | 278,000 |
| Interest cost expensed | 180,000 |

**Key insight:** At period-end, capitalize only the **lesser of actual interest and avoidable interest**. Avoidable interest layers **specific** project debt first, then the **weighted-average general** rate on excess WAAE. The adjusting entry moves avoidable (capped) interest into **CIP** and leaves the remainder as **Interest Expense**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Northstar Alloys twin
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Calculate and record capitalized interest (number-variant twin of Q1); period-end adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Northstar Alloys LLC** is building a specialty casting plant for its own use. Construction and related activities ran the full calendar year; all debt was outstanding the full year; interest is paid in cash at December 31 as the period-end adjusting entry.

**Capital expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Amount |
|---|---:|
| January 1 | $2,400,000 |
| March 1 | 900,000 |
| June 30 | 1,200,000 |
| September 1 | 600,000 |
| December 31 | 300,000 |

**Outstanding debt:**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction note (specific) | $1,500,000 | 6% | Specific |
| Note payable | 2,000,000 | 9% | General |
| Bonds payable | 1,000,000 | 12% | General |

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **WAAE**.  
(b) Compute **actual interest** and the **weighted-average general debt rate**.  
(c) Compute **avoidable interest** and the amount to **capitalize**.  
(d) Record the **December 31 period-end adjusting journal entry** for interest (full actual interest settled in cash).  
(e) Compute **ending Construction in Process** (expenditures + capitalized interest; beginning CIP = $0).

**Answer key:**

**(a) WAAE**

| Date | Expenditure | Months | Weight | Weighted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 | $2,400,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $2,400,000 |
| March 1 | 900,000 | 10 | 10/12 | 750,000 |
| June 30 | 1,200,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 600,000 |
| September 1 | 600,000 | 4 | 4/12 | 200,000 |
| December 31 | 300,000 | 0 | 0/12 | 0 |
| **Totals** | **$5,400,000** | | | **$3,950,000** |

**WAAE = $3,950,000**

**(b) Actual interest and general rate**

| Debt | Interest |
|---|---:|
| Specific construction note \(1{,}500{,}000 \times 6\%\) | $90,000 |
| Note payable \(2{,}000{,}000 \times 9\%\) | 180,000 |
| Bonds payable \(1{,}000{,}000 \times 12\%\) | 120,000 |
| **Total actual interest** | **$390,000** |

General debt = \(2{,}000{,}000 + 1{,}000{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3{,}000{,}000}\)  
General interest = \(180{,}000 + 120{,}000 = \mathbf{\$300{,}000}\)  
WA general rate = \(300{,}000 / 3{,}000{,}000 = \mathbf{10\%}\)

**(c) Avoidable interest and capitalization**

| Layer | Base | Rate | Avoidable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Specific | $1,500,000 | 6% | $90,000 |
| Excess | \(3{,}950{,}000 - 1{,}500{,}000 = 2{,}450{,}000\) | 10% | 245,000 |
| **Total avoidable** | **$3,950,000** | | **$335,000** |

Actual **$390,000** vs avoidable **$335,000** → **capitalize $335,000**  
Interest expense = \(390{,}000 - 335{,}000 = \mathbf{\$55{,}000}\)

**(d) December 31 period-end adjusting interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 335,000 | |
| Interest Expense | 55,000 | |
| Cash | | 390,000 |

Proof: Dr \(335{,}000 + 55{,}000 = 390{,}000\) = Cr. **Balances.**

**(e) Ending CIP**

\[
5{,}400{,}000 + 335{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}735{,}000}
\]

**Key insight:** Same measurement model as Q1 with new facts: WAAE drives avoidable interest; specific debt is applied first; the period-end adjusting entry capitalizes only the lesser amount and expenses the residual.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Cascade Optical: land-as-site WAAE, period-end accrual adjusting JE, completion settlement, depreciation of capitalized interest
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE (Interest Payable accrual); interest on land-site expenditures capitalized to Building; CIP → Building settlement; subsequent depreciation of capitalized interest  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Optical Systems** acquires land on **January 1, Year 1**, as the site for a new optics lab and begins construction immediately. The building is completed and placed in service on **December 31, Year 1** (ready for intended use that day; depreciation begins **January 1, Year 2**). All debt was outstanding the full year. Interest for Year 1 is **accrued** at year-end (credit **Interest Payable**) rather than paid in cash—highlighting the period-end adjusting nature of the capitalization entry.

**Expenditures (cash):**

| Date | Description | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| January 1, Year 1 | Land (building site) | $480,000 |
| April 1, Year 1 | Construction costs | 720,000 |
| July 1, Year 1 | Construction costs | 960,000 |
| October 1, Year 1 | Construction costs | 240,000 |

**Debt (full year):**

| Debt | Amount | Rate | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Construction note (specific) | $800,000 | 10% | Specific |
| Note payable (general) | 2,400,000 | 9% | General |

Building useful life from January 1, Year 2: **25 years**, residual value **$68,400**, straight-line. Land is not depreciated.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1** land purchase and the **construction expenditure JEs** (land separate from CIP).  
(b) Compute **WAAE** (include land expenditures while construction activities are in progress).  
(c) Compute **actual interest**, **avoidable interest**, and the amount **capitalized**.  
(d) Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for interest, accruing the full actual interest to **Interest Payable** (**emphasis**).  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 1 completion/settlement JE** reclassifying CIP to Building. State the **Land** and **Building** balances immediately after completion.  
(f) Record the **Year 2 full-year depreciation** entry (capitalized interest is depreciated as part of building cost).  
(g) Briefly state the **ASC 835-20 disclosure** amounts for Year 1.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition JEs**

| Date | Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 | Land | 480,000 | |
| | Cash | | 480,000 |
| Apr 1 | Construction in Process | 720,000 | |
| | Cash | | 720,000 |
| Jul 1 | Construction in Process | 960,000 | |
| | Cash | | 960,000 |
| Oct 1 | Construction in Process | 240,000 | |
| | Cash | | 240,000 |

Each balances. Construction cash into CIP = **$1,920,000**. Land remains in **Land** at **$480,000**.

**(b) WAAE**

| Date | Expenditure | Months | Weight | Weighted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 (land) | $480,000 | 12 | 12/12 | $480,000 |
| April 1 | 720,000 | 9 | 9/12 | 540,000 |
| July 1 | 960,000 | 6 | 6/12 | 480,000 |
| October 1 | 240,000 | 3 | 3/12 | 60,000 |
| **Totals** | **$2,400,000** | | | **$1,560,000** |

**WAAE = $1,560,000** (land site expenditures are included once construction activities are underway).

**(c) Actual, avoidable, capitalize**

Actual interest:  
- Specific: \(800{,}000 \times 10\% = 80{,}000\)  
- General: \(2{,}400{,}000 \times 9\% = 216{,}000\)  
- **Total actual = $296,000**

Avoidable interest:  
- Specific layer: \(800{,}000 \times 10\% = 80{,}000\)  
- Excess: \(1{,}560{,}000 - 800{,}000 = 760{,}000 \times 9\% = 68{,}400\)  
- **Total avoidable = $148,400**

Capitalize lesser: **$148,400**  
Interest expense: \(296{,}000 - 148{,}400 = \mathbf{\$147{,}600}\)

**(d) December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE (Interest Payable accrual — emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Construction in Process | 148,400 | |
| Interest Expense | 147,600 | |
| Interest Payable | | 296,000 |
| *Period-end adjusting entry: capitalize lesser amount; accrue residual and capitalized portions* | | |

Proof: Dr \(148{,}400 + 147{,}600 = 296{,}000\) = Cr. **Balances.**  
*All* capitalized interest is assigned to the **building** (including the portion theoretically related to land-site WAAE)—not to Land. When cash is later paid on the payable: Dr Interest Payable / Cr Cash (no further P&L effect if fully accrued).

**(e) Completion / settlement JE — December 31, Year 1**

CIP before transfer = construction expenditures + capitalized interest:  
\[
1{,}920{,}000 + 148{,}400 = \mathbf{\$2{,}068{,}400}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 2,068,400 | |
| Construction in Process | | 2,068,400 |
| *To reclassify completed self-constructed building (incl. capitalized interest)* | | |

**Balances after completion:**  
- **Land** = **$480,000** (acquisition cost only; no capitalized interest)  
- **Building** = **$2,068,400**  
- **CIP** = **$0**

**(f) Year 2 depreciation (subsequent measurement of capitalized interest via depreciation)**

Depreciable base = \(2{,}068{,}400 - 68{,}400 = \mathbf{\$2{,}000{,}000}\)  
Annual SL depreciation = \(2{,}000{,}000 / 25 = \mathbf{\$80{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 80,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 80,000 |

Proof: Dr = Cr = 80,000. **Balances.**  
Capitalized interest of $148,400 is expensed over the building’s life as part of this depreciation (not as interest expense after capitalization ends).

**(g) Year 1 interest disclosure**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total interest cost incurred | $296,000 |
| Amount capitalized | 148,400 |
| Amount expensed | 147,600 |

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** is the operational vehicle for interest capitalization—whether settled in cash or accrued to Interest Payable. Land-site expenditures enter **WAAE** during construction, but capitalized interest becomes part of **Building** cost. On completion, CIP (hard costs + capitalized interest) is reclassified to Building; thereafter capitalized interest is recovered through **depreciation**.

### Q4 — MC — Capitalization ceiling and land-site classification
**LO:** LO 11-4  
**Concept:** Capitalize lesser of actual vs avoidable interest; classification of capitalized interest on land-site expenditures  

**Question 1:**  
During construction of a qualifying self-constructed plant asset, a company computes avoidable interest of $210,000 and incurs actual interest cost of $175,000 on all outstanding debt. The amount of interest that should be **capitalized** is:

- A) $210,000  
- B) $175,000  
- C) $35,000  
- D) $0  

**Answer:** **B.** Capitalized interest is the **lesser** of actual interest and avoidable interest. Avoidable interest cannot exceed total actual interest incurred in the period (ASC 835-20). Here actual ($175,000) < avoidable ($210,000), so capitalize **$175,000**.

---

**Question 2:**  
Interest is capitalized on land expenditures while a building is being constructed on that land for the company’s own use. The capitalized interest related to those land expenditures should be:

- A) Added to the **Land** account and never depreciated  
- B) Added to the **Building** account and depreciated over the building’s useful life  
- C) Expensed immediately as interest expense  
- D) Deferred as a prepaid asset until the land is sold  

**Answer:** **B.** When land is a **building site**, interest capitalized on land expenditures is a cost of acquiring the **structure** (building), not land, and is depreciated over the building’s life.
