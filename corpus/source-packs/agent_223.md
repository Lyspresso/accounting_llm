# Agent 223 — CORE DEMO — LO 13-7

**Chapter:** 13  
**LO title:** Account for research and development costs  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **General rule (ASC 730):** research and development costs are **charged to expense when incurred**
- **Initial recognition exception:** materials, equipment, facilities, and intangibles with **alternative future uses** are **capitalized** as assets
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-use PPE is depreciated over useful life; multi-use intangibles are amortized; **each period’s depreciation/amortization is classified as R&D expense**
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** R&D depreciation / amortization on multi-use assets; materials with alt. uses expensed as R&D **when consumed**
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** dispose of multi-use R&D-related PPE at **carrying amount**; gain or loss = proceeds − CV (not itself R&D)
- **Classification / disclosure:** R&D vs non-R&D (commercial production, routine/seasonal updates, unrelated G&A); single-use R&D items fully expensed; **disclose total R&D expense** (ASC 730-10-50-1)
- **Number-variant twin:** same framework, all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-use R&D assets: initial recognition, full subsequent measurement schedules, period-end R&D charges, disposal

Accumulated depreciation after 3 years = 3 × $114,000 = **$342,000**  
Carrying amount = $600,000 − $342,000 = **$258,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$240,000**  
Loss on disposal = $258,000 − $240,000 = **$18,000**

(The disposal loss is **not** classified as R&D expense; it is a disposal gain/loss in income from continuing operations.)

**g. Conceptual**  
Under ASC 730, multi-use equipment is an **asset** because of **alternative future uses**. Only the **portion used up each period** (depreciation) is R&D expense. Expensing the entire $600,000 in Year 1 would overstate current R&D expense and understate assets that will benefit future periods (including future R&D projects).

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the bridge between capitalizing multi-use R&D assets and recognizing R&D expense: cost − residual rolls off as **R&D depreciation/amortization** each period. Single-use items and pure R&D services hit expense immediately; commercial production and unrelated G&A never enter the R&D total.

| Routine quality reviews (commercial) | **Non-R&D expense** | — | After commercial production |

**Total Year 1 R&D expense**  
$126,000 + $75,000 + $260,000 + $42,000 + $24,000 + $36,000 = **$563,000**.

**Arithmetic check:** 126 + 75 = 201; +260 = 461; +42 = 503; +24 = 527; +36 = **563,000**.

**f. January 1, Year 3 — disposal of multi-use facility equipment**

Accumulated depreciation after 2 years = 2 × $75,000 = **$150,000**  
Carrying amount = $320,000 − $150,000 = **$170,000**  
Cash proceeds = **$175,000**  
Gain on disposal = $175,000 − $170,000 = **$5,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 175,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Facility Equipment | 150,000 | |
| Facility Equipment | | 320,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 5,000 |
| *Dispose of multi-use facility equipment after 2 years* | | |

**Check:** Dr 175,000 + 150,000 = 325,000; Cr 320,000 + 5,000 = 325,000. **Balanced.**  
(Gain is **not** R&D.)

**g. Year 2 R&D expense (given items)**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Materials issued to R&D | $40,000 |
| Depreciation of multi-use facility equipment | 75,000 |
| R&D salaries | 275,000 |
| Outside R&D consultants | 38,000 |
| **Total Year 2 R&D (these items)** | **$428,000** |

**Arithmetic check:** 40 + 75 = 115; +275 = 390; +38 = **428,000**.

**Key insight:** Multi-use **materials** use an **inventory rollforward** (expense as R&D when issued); multi-use **equipment** uses a **depreciation schedule** (expense as R&D as depreciated). Both are subsequent measurement tools that convert capitalized multi-use assets into period R&D expense. Disposal settles the remaining CV against proceeds — gain/loss is not R&D.

### Q4 — MC — Classification / amount of R&D expense
**LO:** LO 13-7  
**Concept:** Classification of expenditures as R&D expense vs non-R&D vs capitalized multi-use asset; computation of disclosed R&D total  

**Question 1:**  
During the year, Solstice Optics Co. incurred: (i) lab research salaries for successful and unsuccessful design attempts **$210,000**; (ii) cost of creating prototypes during the design phase **$70,000**; (iii) minor design update after commercial production began **$18,000**; (iv) seasonal design updates to existing products **$32,000**. What amount should Solstice report as **research and development costs**?

- A) \$330,000  
- B) \$280,000  
- C) \$298,000  
- D) \$70,000  

**Answer:** **B. $280,000.**  
R&D includes planned research salaries (successful **and** unsuccessful) **$210,000** and design-phase prototype costs **$70,000** = **$280,000**. Minor design updates after commercial production and seasonal updates to existing products are **non-R&D** operating expenses ($18,000 + $32,000 excluded).

**Question 2:**  
Redwood Diagnostics reports for the year: materials used in R&D **$350,000**; equipment acquired with significant alternative future uses **$1,400,000**; depreciation on that equipment for the year **$280,000**; R&D personnel **$900,000**; outside consulting for R&D **$75,000**; indirect costs reasonably allocable to R&D **$150,000**. What amount of **research and development expense** is reported on the current-year income statement?

- A) \$3,155,000  
- B) \$1,755,000  
- C) \$1,475,000  
- D) \$2,035,000  

**Answer:** **B. $1,755,000.**  
Include materials used $350,000 + depreciation of multi-use equipment $280,000 + personnel $900,000 + consulting $75,000 + allocable indirect $150,000 = **$1,755,000**. The **$1,400,000 equipment purchase is capitalized**, not expensed as R&D in full.
