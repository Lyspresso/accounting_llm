# Agent 277 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-5

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for bonds issued at a premium  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Premium condition:** stated rate > market rate → proceeds > face; record **Premium on Bonds Payable** (adjunct liability)
- **Initial recognition JE:** Dr Cash / Cr Premium on Bonds Payable / Cr Bonds Payable (face)
- **Effective interest method:** Interest expense = beginning carrying amount × original market rate (per period); **Premium amort. = cash interest − interest expense**; carrying amount **declines** toward face
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** full-period table rolling unamortized premium and carrying amount to face at maturity
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** when the reporting date falls between interest dates, accrue a pro rata share of the next semi-period’s cash interest, effective interest expense, and **premium** amortization (Dr Interest Expense, Dr Premium, Cr Interest Payable)
- **Straight-line alternative:** equal premium amortization each period when not materially different from effective interest
- **Disposal / maturity JE:** final interest + premium amort, then pay face (carrying amount = face after full amortization); early extinguishment compares reacquisition price to carrying amount
- **Classification / presentation:** Bonds payable **plus** unamortized premium = bonds payable, net (long-term liability); total interest expense over term **<** total cash interest

### Q1 — CORE — Premium issue, effective-interest schedule, interest JEs, period-end accrual (emphasis), maturity, presentation
**LO:** LO 16-5  
**Concept:** Bonds issued at a premium — initial recognition; effective-interest amortization schedule; **period-end adjusting JE** when year-end falls between interest dates; maturity settlement; BS/IS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar & Flint Ironworks Inc.** (calendar-year reporter) authorizes and issues **$250,000** face of **6%** bonds on **April 1, Year 1**. The bonds mature on **April 1, Year 4** (3-year term) and pay cash interest **semiannually** on **April 1** and **October 1**. The bonds are sold to yield a **market rate of 4%**. Cedar & Flint uses the **effective interest method** and rounds present values and schedule amounts to the nearest dollar (final period plugs residual unamortized premium). Because interest dates are April 1 and October 1, **December 31 year-end requires an adjusting accrual** for interest and premium amortization since the last payment date.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **issue price** and prepare the **initial recognition JE** on April 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** for all six semiannual periods.  
c. Prepare the **October 1, Year 1** interest payment and premium amortization JE.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** accruing interest and premium amortization for the three months October 1–December 31. Base the accrual on the next full semiannual schedule row (prorate **3/6**), or equivalently: Interest expense = carrying amount after Oct 1 × 2% × 3/6; Interest payable = face × 6% × 3/12; Premium amort. = Interest payable − Interest expense.  
e. Show the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet excerpt** (bonds payable, net, and related interest payable) and **Year 1 interest expense**.  
f. Prepare the **April 1, Year 4** entries to (1) record the final interest payment and premium amortization and (2) **derecognize** the bonds at maturity (assume no Interest Payable balance immediately before the final interest entry).

**Answer key:**  

**Setup**  
- Semiannual periods: \(3 \times 2 = 6\)  
- Semiannual market rate: \(4\% / 2 = \mathbf{2\%}\)  
- Semiannual cash interest: \(250{,}000 \times 6\% \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\)

**a. Issue price and initial recognition**  
Issue price \(= \mathrm{PV}(2\%, 6, -7{,}500, -250{,}000) = \mathbf{\$264{,}004}\).  
Premium \(= 264{,}004 - 250{,}000 = \mathbf{\$14{,}004}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 264,004 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 14,004 |
| Bonds Payable | | 250,000 |
| *Issue $250,000 6% bonds at premium to yield 4%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 264,004 = Cr 14,004 + 250,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Effective-interest amortization schedule — premium**  
Interest expense \(=\) beginning carrying amount \(\times 2\%\). Premium amort. \(=\) cash \(-\) interest expense. Carrying amount **declines** toward face. Final period plugs residual unamortized premium.

| Date | Cash (stated) | Interest expense (mkt) | Premium amort. | Unamortized premium | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr 1, Y1 (issue) | — | — | — | 14,004 | 264,004 |
| Oct 1, Y1 | 7,500 | 5,280 | 2,220 | 11,784 | 261,784 |
| Apr 1, Y2 | 7,500 | 5,236 | 2,264 | 9,520 | 259,520 |
| Oct 1, Y2 | 7,500 | 5,190 | 2,310 | 7,210 | 257,210 |
| Apr 1, Y3 | 7,500 | 5,144 | 2,356 | 4,854 | 254,854 |
| Oct 1, Y3 | 7,500 | 5,097 | 2,403 | 2,451 | 252,451 |
| Apr 1, Y4 | 7,500 | 5,049 | 2,451 | 0 | 250,000 |
| **Totals** | **45,000** | **30,996** | **14,004** | | |

**Roll-forward checks:**  
- Sum of premium amort. \(= 14{,}004 =\) initial premium.  
- Total interest expense \(= 45{,}000 - 14{,}004 = 30{,}996 <\) total cash interest (premium case).  
- Carrying amount falls each period toward face.

**c. October 1, Year 1 — interest + premium amortization**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,280 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,220 | |
| Cash | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr 5,280 + 2,220 = Cr 7,500. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount after entry: \(264{,}004 - 2{,}220 = 261{,}784\).

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 1 (emphasis)**  
Beginning carrying amount for the next semi-period (after Oct 1): **$261,784**.  
Next full semiannual row (Apr 1, Y2): Interest expense 5,236; premium amort. 2,264; cash interest 7,500.  
Accrue **3 months / 6 months = 1/2** (equivalently, expense \(= 261{,}784 \times 2\% \times 1/2\)):

- Interest expense: \(5{,}236 \times 1/2 = \mathbf{2{,}618}\)  
- Premium amort.: \(2{,}264 \times 1/2 = \mathbf{1{,}132}\)  
- Interest payable: \(7{,}500 \times 1/2 = \mathbf{3{,}750}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 2,618 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 1,132 | |
| Interest Payable | | 3,750 |
| *Accrue 3 months premium-bond interest/amort (Oct 1–Dec 31)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,618 + 1,132 = Cr 3,750. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount after accrual: \(261{,}784 - 1{,}132 = \mathbf{260{,}652}\).  
Unamortized premium: \(11{,}784 - 1{,}132 = \mathbf{10{,}652}\).

**e. Presentation — December 31, Year 1**

Balance sheet excerpt:
| Liabilities | |
|---|---:|
| Interest payable | $3,750 |
| Bonds payable | $250,000 |
| Add: Premium on bonds payable | 10,652 |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$260,652** |

Income statement (Year 1): Interest expense \(= 5{,}280 + 2{,}618 = \mathbf{\$7{,}898}\)  
(6 months outstanding Apr 1–Oct 1 full semi + 3 months accrued Oct–Dec).  
Note: Year 1 interest expense is **less than** cash interest for the same elapsed coupon time because premium amortization reduces interest cost.

**f. Maturity — April 1, Year 4**

*Final interest + premium amortization*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,049 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,451 | |
| Cash | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr 5,049 + 2,451 = Cr 7,500. **Balanced.**

*Derecognize bonds at maturity*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 250,000 | |
| Cash | | 250,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 250,000. **Balanced.** (Premium fully amortized; net carrying amount = face.)

**Key insight:** When stated > market, investors pay a **premium**. Premium is an **adjunct** to Bonds Payable and is amortized as a **reduction** of interest expense. When books close **between interest dates**, the period-end entry must accrue **effective interest expense, premium amortization, and stated interest payable** so the JE balances and carrying amount continues to roll **down** toward face.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Premium life cycle with changed face, rates, and amounts
**LO:** LO 16-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — recompute premium issue price; effective-interest schedule; **period-end adjusting JE**; interest payment and maturity; net presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Binderies Corp.** (calendar-year) issues **$480,000** face of **8%** bonds on **April 1, Year 1**. Bonds mature **April 1, Year 4** (3 years), pay interest **semiannually** on April 1 and October 1, and are priced to yield a **market rate of 6%**. Lakeshore uses the **effective interest method** (round to nearest dollar; plug residual premium amort. in the final period). December 31 year-end falls three months after the October 1 interest date, so a period-end accrual is required.

**Required:**  
a. Compute issue price and record the **April 1, Year 1** issuance JE.  
b. Prepare the **full effective-interest amortization schedule** (6 periods).  
c. Prepare the **October 1, Year 1** interest + premium amortization JE.  
d. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (3/6 of the next semiannual row; or rate-based accrual on carrying amount after Oct 1).  
e. Report **bonds payable, net** and **interest payable** at December 31, Year 1, and **Year 1 interest expense**.  
f. Prepare **April 1, Year 4** final interest + amort JE and maturity principal payment (no Interest Payable outstanding immediately before the final interest entry).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Interim period-end accrual (emphasis); straight-line contrast; early extinguishment; classification
**LO:** LO 16-5  
**Concept:** Premium bond interim **period-end adjusting JE**; straight-line premium amortization; early settlement gain/loss; classification of bonds payable net of (plus) premium  
**Scenario — Part A (effective interest + interim accrual + early extinguishment):**  
**Northfork Precision Castings Inc.** issues **$300,000** face of **7%** bonds on **January 1, Year 1**, maturing **December 31, Year 2**. Interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Market rate at issuance is **5%**. Effective interest method; round to nearest dollar; final period plugs residual premium. Management prepares **interim** statements at **August 31**, so a period-end accrual is required for two months after the June 30 payment. On **January 1, Year 2** (after all Year 1 interest/amortization entries, including the December 31 payment), Northfork **retires** the entire issue by paying bondholders **102** (ignore transaction costs).

**Scenario — Part B (straight-line contrast):**  
Using the **same** issue price and premium as Part A, assume instead that Northfork amortizes the premium using the **straight-line interest method** over the four semiannual periods (acceptable if not materially different from effective interest).

**Required:**  
a. **Part A:** Compute issue price and record the **initial recognition JE**.  
b. **Part A:** Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** (4 periods).  
c. **Part A:** Record **June 30, Year 1** interest + premium amortization.  
d. **Part A (emphasis):** Record the **August 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (prorate the next full semiannual row by **2/6**).  
e. **Part A:** State **bonds payable, net** at December 31, Year 1 (after the Dec 31 interest entry; year-end is an interest date) and Year 1 interest expense under the effective interest method.  
f. **Part A:** Record the **January 1, Year 2 early extinguishment** at 102.  
g. **Part B:** Compute straight-line premium amortization per period and prepare the **June 30, Year 1** interest JE under straight-line.  
h. **Classification:** At December 31, Year 1 (Part A), how is the obligation classified/presented on the balance sheet?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Premium issue price and JE**  
- Semiannual market rate: \(5\% / 2 = 2.5\%\)  
- Periods: 4  
- Semiannual cash interest: \(300{,}000 \times 7\% \times 1/2 = \mathbf{\$10{,}500}\)  
- Issue price \(= \mathrm{PV}(2.5\%, 4, -10{,}500, -300{,}000) = \mathbf{\$311{,}286}\)  
- Premium \(= 311{,}286 - 300{,}000 = \mathbf{\$11{,}286}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 311,286 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 11,286 |
| Bonds Payable | | 300,000 |

**Check:** Dr 311,286 = Cr 11,286 + 300,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Effective-interest amortization schedule — premium**

| Date | Cash | Interest expense | Premium amort. | Unamortized premium | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 11,286 | 311,286 |
| Jun 30, Y1 | 10,500 | 7,782 | 2,718 | 8,568 | 308,568 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 10,500 | 7,714 | 2,786 | 5,782 | 305,782 |
| Jun 30, Y2 | 10,500 | 7,645 | 2,855 | 2,927 | 302,927 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 10,500 | 7,573 | 2,927 | 0 | 300,000 |
| **Totals** | **42,000** | **30,714** | **11,286** | | |

**Checks:** Amort sum \(= 11{,}286\); total interest expense \(= 42{,}000 - 11{,}286 = 30{,}714 <\) total cash interest.

**c. June 30, Year 1 interest JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 7,782 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,718 | |
| Cash | | 10,500 |

**Check:** Dr 7,782 + 2,718 = Cr 10,500. **Balanced.**

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — August 31, Year 1 (premium, emphasis)**  
Next full semiannual row (Dec 31, Y1): Interest expense 7,714; premium amort. 2,786; cash interest 10,500.  
Prorate **2 months / 6 months = 2/6** (equivalently, \(308{,}568 \times 2.5\% \times 2/6\)):

- Interest expense: \(7{,}714 \times 2/6 = \mathbf{2{,}571}\)  
- Premium amort.: \(2{,}786 \times 2/6 = \mathbf{929}\)  
- Interest payable: \(10{,}500 \times 2/6 = \mathbf{3{,}500}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 2,571 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 929 | |
| Interest Payable | | 3,500 |
| *Accrue 2 months premium-bond interest/amort* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,571 + 929 = Cr 3,500. **Balanced.**  
(Premium amortization **debits** the adjunct liability in both cash-interest and adjusting entries.)

**e. Presentation and Year 1 interest expense (after Dec 31 interest entry)**  
(Ignore the Aug 31 interim accrual for this annual presentation question, or assume it was reversed/settled into the Dec 31 entry so year-end balances match the schedule.)

| Bonds payable | $300,000 |
| Add: Premium on bonds payable | 5,782 |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$305,782** |

Year 1 interest expense (two full semi periods) \(= 7{,}782 + 7{,}714 = \mathbf{\$15{,}496}\).

**f. Early extinguishment — January 1, Year 2**  
Reacquisition price \(= 300{,}000 \times 1.02 = \mathbf{\$306{,}000}\).  
Carrying amount \(= \mathbf{\$305{,}782}\) (unamortized premium **$5,782**).  
Loss on extinguishment \(= 306{,}000 - 305{,}782 = \mathbf{\$218}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 300,000 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 5,782 | |
| Loss on Early Extinguishment of Debt | 218 | |
| Cash | | 306,000 |

**Check:** Dr 300,000 + 5,782 + 218 = Cr 306,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Straight-line contrast (Part B)**  
Total premium \(11{,}286 \div 4 = 2{,}821.5\) → **$2,822** per period for periods 1–3; last period plug **$2,820** (\(11{,}286 - 3 \times 2{,}822\)).  
June 30, Year 1 interest expense \(= 10{,}500 - 2{,}822 = \mathbf{\$7{,}678}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 7,678 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | 2,822 | |
| Cash | | 10,500 |

**Check:** Dr 7,678 + 2,822 = Cr 10,500. **Balanced.**  
Under straight-line, interest expense is **level** each period (except a minor final-period plug); under effective interest, expense **declines** as carrying amount declines.

**h. Classification / presentation (Part A, Dec 31, Y1)**  
Report as a **long-term liability**: **Bonds payable, net $305,782** (face **plus** unamortized premium). Premium is an **adjunct** liability account — do **not** present premium as an asset. At Dec 31, Year 1 the entire principal is due in one year (Dec 31, Year 2); many presentations reclassify the **net carrying amount** as a **current** liability when due within one year (company policy / current vs noncurrent split). Regardless of current/noncurrent split, net presentation is face + unamortized premium.

**Key insight:** Premium amortization always **reduces** interest expense whether effective interest or straight-line is used. Period-end adjusting entries for premium bonds **debit Premium** (with Interest Expense) and credit Interest Payable. Early extinguishment removes face **and** remaining unamortized premium and recognizes gain/loss as reacquisition price versus **carrying amount**.

### Q4 — MC — Premium issuance outcome and interest cost relation
**LO:** LO 16-5  
**Concept:** Classification of bonds as issued at a premium and relation of total interest expense to cash interest  
**Question:** **Silverpine Robotics Ltd.** issues multi-year bonds with a **7% stated (coupon) rate** when the market yield for similar-risk bonds is **5%**. Interest is paid semiannually; issuance costs are ignored. How are the bonds issued, and what is the relation between total interest expense over the bond term and total cash interest paid?

- A) Issued at a **discount**; total interest expense **exceeds** total cash interest  
- B) Issued at a **premium**; total interest expense **is less than** total cash interest  
- C) Issued at a **premium**; total interest expense **exceeds** total cash interest  
- D) Issued at **face (par)**; total interest expense **equals** total cash interest  

**Answer:** **B.** Market rate (5%) < stated rate (7%) → issue price > face → **premium**. Amortizing the premium reduces interest expense each period, so total interest expense = total cash coupons − initial premium **<** total cash interest.

### Q5 — MC — Period-end adjusting structure (premium bond)
**LO:** LO 16-5  
**Concept:** Structure of the period-end adjusting JE when fiscal year-end falls between interest dates (premium bond)  
**Question:** A company issued bonds at a **premium**, uses the effective interest method, and pays cash interest only on interest dates. At fiscal year-end, three months have elapsed since the last interest date (and three months remain until the next). Which entry correctly accrues the period-end adjustment?

- A) Debit Interest Expense for three months of **cash (stated) interest only**; credit Interest Payable for the same amount (no premium amortization until cash is paid)  
- B) Debit Interest Expense for three months of **effective interest**; debit **Premium on Bonds Payable** for three months of amortization; credit Interest Payable for three months of **stated cash interest**  
- C) Debit Interest Expense for six months of effective interest; credit Cash for six months of stated interest; debit Premium for the difference  
- D) Debit Bonds Payable and credit Interest Payable for three months of stated interest  

**Answer:** **B.** Between interest dates, accrue **effective interest expense** on the beginning carrying amount for the elapsed fraction of the semi-period, amortize the related **premium** (debit the adjunct account), and credit **Interest Payable** (not Cash) for the accrued stated coupon. Option A omits premium amortization; C records a full cash interest entry early; D misstates Bonds Payable.
