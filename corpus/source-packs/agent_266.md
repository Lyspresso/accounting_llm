# Agent 266 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-3

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-OCI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase AFS debt at acquisition cost (par); debit Investment in AFS Securities
- **Interest revenue in NI:** coupon interest recognized in **net income** (same for all debt categories)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** amortized cost, fair value, required FVA, existing FVA, and **increase/(decrease) to FVA** across period-ends and sale date
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** update FVA through **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI** (not NI); BS carrying amount = amortized cost ± FVA = fair value
- **Disposal / sale JE (FV-OCI path):** (1) adjust sold security to FV through OCI at sale date; (2) record cash, remove investment at amortized cost, eliminate FVA, **reclassify** cumulative holding G/L from AOCI into NI as realized G/L
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** AFS unrealized holding G/L in OCI/AOCI until sale; reclassification amount disclosed; interest in NI
- **Portfolio subsequent measurement:** partial sale clears sold security’s FVA; remaining securities remeasured at year-end
- **Number-variant twin:** same FV-OCI life-cycle skill path with fully changed amounts

### Q1 — CORE — AFS life cycle with period-end FV-OCI adjustments and sale-date reclass
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE (FV-OCI); subsequent measurement FVA schedule; initial recognition; interest in NI; disposal with AOCI reclassification  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Capital Group** is a calendar-year investor. On **July 1 of Year 1**, Meridian purchased for **par $150,000** of **Thornridge Corp. 5%** bonds that mature on **June 30 of Year 7**. Cash interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Meridian classifies the bonds as **available-for-sale (AFS)** and measures them under **FV-OCI**. Beginning AOCI related to AFS is **zero**. No other AFS activity occurs except as stated below.

**Fair values and sale:**
| Date | Event / fair value of Thornridge bonds |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$142,500** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Fair value **$154,500** (no purchases/sales in Year 2) |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Entire holding sold for cash **$156,000** |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **July 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (purchase at par).  
(b) Record receipt of **cash interest** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for the Fair Value Adjustment (FVA) account covering: Dec 31 Y1, Dec 31 Y2, and Jan 1 Y3 sale date. Columns: amortized cost, fair value (or sale price), required FVA (debit = positive), existing FVA before adjust, **increase/(decrease) to FVA**, FVA after adjust.  
(d) Using the schedule, record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis).  
(e) Report Year 1 effects on the **income statement**, **statement of comprehensive income**, and **December 31 Year 1 balance sheet** for this investment (ignore taxes and all other items).  
(f) Record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE** and show FVA/AOCI after the entry.  
(g) On January 1 of Year 3: (i) sale-date FV-OCI adjust; (ii) sale with reclassification of holding gain into net income and elimination of FVA.  
(h) Prepare the Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation** (beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclassification to NI, ending AOCI).

**Answer key:**  

**(a) July 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (purchase at par)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Thornridge Bonds | 150,000 | |
| Cash | | 150,000 |
| *Purchase Thornridge 5% bonds at par; classify AFS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 150,000 = Cr 150,000. **Balanced.** Amortized cost basis = **$150,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest revenue in net income**  
Cash interest = \(150{,}000 \times 0.05 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$3{,}750}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,750 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 3,750 |
| *Semiannual interest on Thornridge AFS bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,750 = Cr 3,750. **Balanced.** Interest is recognized in **net income** for AFS debt.

**(c) Subsequent measurement schedule — FVA rollforward**

Sign convention: **positive FVA = debit** (unrealized holding gain); **negative FVA = credit** (unrealized holding loss).

| Measurement date | Amortized cost | Fair value / sale price | Required FVA | Existing FVA (before adj.) | Increase/(decrease) to FVA | FVA after adj. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | 150,000 | 142,500 | (7,500) | 0 | **(7,500)** | (7,500) |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 150,000 | 154,500 | 4,500 | (7,500) | **12,000** | 4,500 |
| Jan 1, Year 3 (sale) | 150,000 | 156,000 | 6,000 | 4,500 | **1,500** | 6,000 |

**Schedule math checks:**  
- Y1 required FVA = 142,500 − 150,000 = **(7,500)**; existing 0 → decrease FVA (credit FVA) **7,500**.  
- Y2 required FVA = 154,500 − 150,000 = **4,500 debit**; existing **(7,500)** → increase = 4,500 − (−7,500) = **12,000**.  
- Sale-date required FVA = 156,000 − 150,000 = **6,000 debit**; existing **4,500** → increase = **1,500**.  
- After sale, FVA is eliminated (see part g); ending FVA for Thornridge = **0**.

**(d) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE (FV-OCI) [emphasis]**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 7,500 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 7,500 |
| *Adjust AFS to FV; unrealized holding loss through OCI (not NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,500 = Cr 7,500. **Balanced.**  
BS carrying amount = 150,000 − 7,500 = **$142,500** = fair value.  
**Key exam point:** The period-end adjusting entry for AFS hits **OCI**, not Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income (that account is for FV-NI / trading).

**(e) Year 1 financial statement presentation**

**Income statement (excerpt):**  
- Interest revenue **$3,750**  
- Unrealized AFS holding loss is **not** reported in net income.

**Statement of comprehensive income (excerpt):**  
- Net income: includes interest revenue $3,750 (other items ignored)  
- Other comprehensive income: Unrealized holding loss on AFS securities **$(7,500)**  
- Comprehensive income = NI + OCI

**Balance sheet, December 31, Year 1:**  
- Assets: Investment in available-for-sale securities **$142,500** (amortized cost $150,000 net of FVA credit $7,500)  
- Stockholders’ equity: Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) **$(7,500)**

**(f) December 31, Year 2 — Period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 12,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 12,000 |
| *Roll FVA from $7,500 credit to $4,500 debit* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr 12,000. **Balanced.**  
FVA ending = −7,500 + 12,000 = **$4,500 debit**. AOCI related = **$4,500**. Carrying amount = 150,000 + 4,500 = **$154,500**.

**(g) January 1, Year 3 — Sale under FV-OCI**

**(i) Sale-date adjust to fair value through OCI**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 1,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 1,500 |
| *Bring FVA to required $6,000 debit before sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500. **Balanced.** FVA after adjust = 4,500 + 1,500 = **$6,000 debit**.

**(ii) Record sale, reclassify AOCI holding gain to NI, eliminate FVA**  
Realized gain = cash proceeds − amortized cost = 156,000 − 150,000 = **$6,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 156,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 6,000 | |
| Investment in AFS Securities—Thornridge Bonds | | 150,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 6,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 6,000 |
| *Sell AFS; reclass cumulative holding gain from AOCI to NI; clear FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 156,000 + 6,000 = **162,000**; Cr 150,000 + 6,000 + 6,000 = **162,000**. **Balanced.**  
After sale: Investment = 0; FVA = 0; cumulative AOCI effect for this security nets to zero (see h).

**(h) Year 3 AOCI reclassification reconciliation**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accumulated OCI, January 1, Year 3 | $4,500 |
| Current-period unrealized gain on AFS (sale-date adj.) | 1,500 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (6,000) |
| Net OCI effect for Year 3 | (4,500) |
| **Accumulated OCI, December 31, Year 3** | **$0** |

**Holding-period OCI summary (volatility stays out of NI until sale):**  
Y1 OCI loss \$(7,500) + Y2 OCI gain \$12,000 + Y3 OCI gain \$1,500 + Y3 reclass \$(6,000) = **\$0**.

**Key insight:** AFS debt is remeasured each reporting date through **FVA and OCI**. At sale, first bring FVA to current FV through OCI, then remove the investment and FVA while **reclassifying** the cumulative unrealized amount from AOCI into **net income** as a realized gain or loss. Interest always hits NI.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — AFS purchase, period-end FVA, multi-year rollforward, sale
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same AFS FV-OCI path (purchase, interest, period-end FVA, sale reclass) with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Treasury LLC** (calendar year) purchased on **January 1 of Year 1** for **par $200,000** of **Summit Ridge Inc. 4%** bonds. Interest is paid **annually each December 31**. The bonds mature on January 1 of Year 6. Riverbend classifies the investment as **AFS (FV-OCI)**. Beginning AOCI related to AFS is zero. No other AFS activity except as stated.

| Date | Fair value / event |
|---|---|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$193,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | Fair value **$206,000** (no purchases/sales in Year 2) |
| Jan 1, Year 3 | Entire holding sold for cash **$208,500** |

**Required:**  
(a) Initial recognition JE on January 1, Year 1.  
(b) Interest revenue JE on December 31, Year 1.  
(c) FVA subsequent measurement schedule (Dec 31 Y1, Dec 31 Y2, Jan 1 Y3 sale).  
(d) Period-end adjusting JEs for Dec 31 Y1 and Dec 31 Y2.  
(e) Sale-date FV-OCI adjust and sale JEs on January 1, Year 3.  
(f) Year 3 AOCI reconciliation (beg AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclass to NI, end AOCI).

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Purchase at par**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Summit Ridge Bonds | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **200,000**. Amortized cost = **$200,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest in NI**  
Cash interest = \(200{,}000 \times 0.04 = \mathbf{\$8{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 8,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **8,000**.

**(c) Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Measurement date | Amortized cost | Fair value / sale | Required FVA | Existing FVA | Increase/(decrease) | FVA after adj. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | 200,000 | 193,000 | (7,000) | 0 | **(7,000)** | (7,000) |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 200,000 | 206,000 | 6,000 | (7,000) | **13,000** | 6,000 |
| Jan 1, Year 3 (sale) | 200,000 | 208,500 | 8,500 | 6,000 | **2,500** | 8,500 |

**Math:**  
- Y1: 193,000 − 200,000 = **(7,000)**  
- Y2: required 6,000; existing (7,000); increase = 6,000 − (−7,000) = **13,000**  
- Sale: required 8,500; existing 6,000; increase = **2,500**

**(d) Period-end adjusting JEs**

**December 31, Year 1:**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 7,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 7,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **7,000**. BS carrying amount = **$193,000**.

**December 31, Year 2:**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 13,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 13,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **13,000**. FVA ending = −7,000 + 13,000 = **$6,000 debit**. AOCI = **$6,000**. Carrying amount = **$206,000**.

**(e) January 1, Year 3 — Sale**

**(i) Sale-date FV-OCI adjust**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 2,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 2,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **2,500**. FVA after = **$8,500 debit**.

**(ii) Sale and reclassification**  
Realized gain = 208,500 − 200,000 = **$8,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 208,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 8,500 | |
| Investment in AFS Securities—Summit Ridge Bonds | | 200,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 8,500 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 8,500 |

**Check:** Dr 208,500 + 8,500 = **217,000**; Cr 200,000 + 8,500 + 8,500 = **217,000**. **Balanced.**

**(f) Year 3 AOCI reconciliation**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| AOCI, January 1, Year 3 | $6,000 |
| Current-period unrealized gain (sale-date adj.) | 2,500 |
| Reclassification of gain into net income | (8,500) |
| **AOCI, December 31, Year 3** | **$0** |

Check: 6,000 + 2,500 − 8,500 = 0. Holding-period: Y1 \$(7,000) + Y2 \$13,000 + Y3 \$2,500 − reclass \$8,500 = **\$0**.

**Key insight:** Number-variant twin of the same skill path—period-end FVA always equals **required FVA − existing FVA**, and sale reclass amount equals **realized G/L** (proceeds − amortized cost).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Portfolio AFS: sale-date FV-OCI adjust, reclass, and period-end FVA on remaining securities
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** Portfolio subsequent measurement; disposal of one AFS security with sale-date FV-OCI adjust and reclass; period-end FVA on remaining AFS  
**Scenario:**  
On **December 31 of Year 2**, **Northstar Partners Inc.** holds the following **AFS** debt portfolio (all originally purchased at par; measured FV-OCI). Year-end is December 31.

| AFS investment | Amortized cost | Fair value YE2 | Unrealized holding gain (loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Helix Corp. bonds | $50,000 | $47,000 | $(3,000) |
| Quill Industries bonds | 175,000 | 189,000 | 14,000 |
| **Totals** | **$225,000** | **$236,000** | **$11,000** |

\* The **$11,000** total is the December 31, Year 2 balance in both **FVA—AFS** (debit) and **AOCI** related to AFS.

**Events in Year 3:**  
- **January 1, Year 3:** Northstar sells the **Helix** bonds for cash **$51,500**.  
- **December 31, Year 3:** Remaining **Quill** bonds (still held; amortized cost unchanged at $175,000) have fair value **$182,000**. No other AFS purchases or sales during Year 3.

**Required:**  
(a) On the sale date, **adjust Helix** to fair value through FV-OCI; then record the **sale** (reclassify holding G/L from AOCI to NI; eliminate associated FVA).  
(b) Record the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** for the remaining Quill bonds.  
(c) Prepare the Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation**.  
(d) State the **December 31, Year 3 balance-sheet carrying amount** of AFS investments.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 3 — Helix sale (FV-OCI path)**

**Sale-date FVA analysis for Helix:**  
Required FVA at sale\* = \(51{,}500 − 50{,}000 = \mathbf{\$1{,}500}\) debit  
Existing FVA related to Helix = **$(3,000)** credit  
Increase needed = \(1{,}500 − (−3{,}000) = \mathbf{\$4{,}500}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 4,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 4,500 |
| *Adjust Helix AFS to sale-date fair value through OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **4,500**. Portfolio FVA after this entry = 11,000 + 4,500 = **$15,500 debit**.

**Sale entry** (realized gain = 51,500 − 50,000 = **$1,500**):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 51,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,500 | |
| Investment in AFS Securities—Helix Bonds | | 50,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 1,500 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 1,500 |
| *Sell Helix; reclass $1,500 gain from AOCI to NI; clear Helix FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 51,500 + 1,500 = **53,000**; Cr 50,000 + 1,500 + 1,500 = **53,000**. **Balanced.**  
FVA after sale = 15,500 − 1,500 = **$14,000 debit** (equals remaining Quill unrealized gain at YE2).

**(b) December 31, Year 3 — Period-end FVA for remaining Quill bonds**

Required FVA ending\* = \(182{,}000 − 175{,}000 = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\) debit  
Less existing FVA = **14,000**  
Decrease needed = **$(7,000)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 7,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 7,000 |
| *Roll FVA on remaining AFS from $14,000 debit to $7,000 debit* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **7,000**. FVA ending = 14,000 − 7,000 = **$7,000 debit**.

**(c) Year 3 AOCI reconciliation**

Current-period unrealized (net) = Helix sale-date gain **+4,500** + Quill YE decrease **(7,000)** = **$(2,500)**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| AOCI, January 1, Year 3 | $11,000 |
| Current-period unrealized loss on AFS securities | (2,500) |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in NI | (1,500) |
| Net OCI effect for Year 3 | (4,000) |
| **AOCI, December 31, Year 3** | **$7,000** |

Check: 11,000 − 2,500 − 1,500 = **7,000** = ending FVA on remaining Quill.

**(d) Balance-sheet carrying amount, December 31, Year 3**  
Investment in AFS (Quill) = amortized cost **$175,000** + FVA debit **$7,000** = fair value **$182,000**.

**Key insight:** Portfolio FVA is one valuation account. On partial sale, adjust the **sold** security to FV through OCI first, reclass only that security’s cumulative holding G/L, eliminate its FVA, then remeasure **remaining** AFS at the next period-end through OCI.

### Q4 — MC — Where AFS unrealized holding gains/losses are reported
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — AFS FV changes through OCI until sale  
**Question:**  
Under the FV-OCI method for **available-for-sale debt securities**, unrealized holding gains and losses arising from period-end fair value adjustments (assuming no credit impairment and no sale) are reported:

- A) In net income as part of other gains and losses  
- B) Only in the notes; the balance sheet reports amortized cost only  
- C) In **other comprehensive income**, with the cumulative amount in **AOCI** within equity  
- D) As an adjustment to interest revenue using the effective interest method  

**Answer:** **C.**  
AFS debt is reported at fair value on the balance sheet via FVA, but unrealized holding G/L bypass net income and run through **OCI → AOCI** until realized on sale (or certain impairments). Option A describes **FV-NI / trading**. Option B is closer to **HTM amortized cost** (FV disclosed, not primary measurement). Option D confuses amortization of premium/discount with fair value accounting.

### Q5 — MC — Sale-date sequence under FV-OCI
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** disposal with sale-date FV adjust and AOCI reclassification  
**Question:**  
An investor holds an AFS bond (amortized cost $100,000) with an existing **FVA debit of $3,000** (AOCI unrealized gain $3,000). On the sale date the bond is sold for **$106,000** cash. Immediately before recording the sale under the textbook FV-OCI path (adjust at sale date), what is the **increase to FVA** required, and what **realized gain** is recognized in net income on the sale entry?

- A) Increase FVA **$6,000**; realized gain in NI **$6,000**  
- B) Increase FVA **$3,000**; realized gain in NI **$6,000**  
- C) Increase FVA **$3,000**; realized gain in NI **$3,000**  
- D) No FVA adjust needed; realized gain in NI **$3,000**  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Required FVA at sale = 106,000 − 100,000 = **$6,000 debit**. Existing FVA = **$3,000 debit**. Increase needed = 6,000 − 3,000 = **$3,000**. Realized gain in NI = proceeds − amortized cost = **$6,000** (reclassified from AOCI). The sale-date OCI credit of $3,000 plus the prior AOCI of $3,000 equal the $6,000 reclass debit that moves the full realized gain into NI.
