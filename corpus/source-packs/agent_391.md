# Agent 391 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-6

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Utilize a worksheet to prepare the statement of cash flows  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash flow worksheet (optional tool):** tracks every balance-sheet change and routes cash/noncash effects to operating, investing, or financing in the **lower half** (Demo 22-6)
- **Reconstructed (worksheet-only) entries:** explain BS changes; **not** posted to the general ledger; each entry must **Dr = Cr**
- **Lower-half convention:** SCF **additions = debits**; SCF **deductions = credits** (indirect method)
- **Period-end adjusting worksheet JEs (emphasis):** working-capital reconciling items (AR gross ± allowance, inventory, prepaid, AP, salaries/interest/tax payable); depreciation and patent/bond amortization reconstructed as period-end noncash adjustments
- **Initial recognition worksheet JEs:** cash purchase of equipment; cash issuance of common stock; issuance of bonds (with premium/discount)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** depreciation/AD rollforward; patent amortization; bond premium amortization schedule feeding the worksheet
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement worksheet JEs:** sale of equipment (remove cost and AD; proceeds investing; gain/loss operating); early bond retirement (remove face and premium; cash paid financing; loss operating)
- **Classification / presentation:** transfer lower-half activity sections to a formal SCF (indirect operating); reconcile beginning cash to ending cash
- **Number-variant twin:** same worksheet process with all amounts changed

| Payment for Bond Retirement—Financing | | 79,000 |

**Check:** Dr 80,000 + 1,400 = Cr 2,400 + 79,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Lower-half classification**

| Item | Lower-half amount | Classification |
|---|---:|---|
| (1) Decrease in AR, net | 14,000 | Operating **add** (debit) |
| (2) Increase in inventory | 6,500 | Operating **deduct** (credit) |
| (3) Depreciation expense | 28,000 | Operating **add** (debit) |
| (4) Proceeds from sale of equipment | 30,000 | Investing **inflow** |
| (4) Loss on sale of equipment | 5,000 | Operating **add** (debit) |
| (5) Issuance of bonds (cash) | 76,000 | Financing **inflow** |
| (6) Amortization of bond discount | 800 | Operating **add** (debit) |
| (7) Loss on bond retirement | 1,400 | Operating **add** (debit) |
| (7) Payment for bond retirement | 79,000 | Financing **outflow** |

**c. Net effect on operating (items 1–4, 6–7 lower-half O pieces only)**

Adds: \(14{,}000 + 28{,}000 + 5{,}000 + 800 + 1{,}400 = 49{,}200\)  
Deducts: \(6{,}500\)  
**Net operating contribution from these items = \(49{,}200 - 6{,}500 = \$42{,}700\)** (before starting net income and other WC items not listed).

**Key insight:** Period-end adjusting reconstructions (WC, depreciation, discount amortization) and disposal/settlement reconstructions all use the same rule: **balance the BS half and put SCF effects in the lower half** with additions as debits and deductions as credits.

### Q4 — MC — Worksheet lower-half convention (period-end WC)
**LO:** LO 22-6  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — lower-half worksheet convention (additions = debits; deductions = credits) for a period-end working-capital reconciling item  
**Question:**  
On a cash flow worksheet prepared under the **indirect method**, a company has a **period-end increase in salaries payable** of **$9,000**. Which reconstructed worksheet entry correctly reflects the lower-half convention (SCF additions = debits; SCF deductions = credits)?

- A) Debit Salaries Payable $9,000; Credit Increase in Salaries Payable—Operating $9,000  
- B) Debit Increase in Salaries Payable—Operating $9,000; Credit Salaries Payable $9,000  
- C) Debit Salaries Payable $9,000; Credit Cash $9,000  
- D) Debit Cash $9,000; Credit Salaries Payable $9,000  

**Answer:** **B.** An increase in salaries payable is an **addition** to net income in the operating section, so it is entered as a **debit** in the lower half; the credit balances the increase in the BS salaries payable account. A reverses the debit/credit sides. C and D incorrectly treat the reconciling item as a cash T-account entry rather than a worksheet reconstruction of a noncash WC change.

**Key insight:** Memorize the lower-half rule — **additions debit, deductions credit** — then the BS side is whatever is needed to balance.
