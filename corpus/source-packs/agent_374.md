# Agent 374 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-7

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe the components of comprehensive income and prepare a statement of comprehensive income and a statement of stockholders’ equity.  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Comprehensive income** = **net income** + **other comprehensive income (OCI)**; reports all nonowner changes in equity for the period
- **OCI** includes (net of tax) items excluded from net income: unrealized holding G/L on **AFS debt securities**, **cash flow hedge** adjustments, and **foreign currency translation** adjustments
- **Two presentation formats:** single continuous statement of comprehensive income, or **two separate statements** (income statement + statement of comprehensive income that starts with net income)
- **Closing differs:** net income closes to **retained earnings**; OCI closes to **accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI)**, a permanent equity account
- **Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis):** mark AFS to fair value; record tax effects (DTL/DTA) through OCI; record cash-flow-hedge FV changes; **close OCI temporary accounts to AOCI**
- **Initial recognition:** purchase of AFS debt securities at cost (no OCI until FV changes)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** AFS cost / FV / valuation allowance rollforward; AOCI and RE rollforwards
- **Disposal / reclassification:** sale of AFS removes cost and related valuation allowance; **realized G/L to net income**; **reclassification adjustment** out of OCI (so total CI is not double-counted)
- **Statement of stockholders’ equity:** columnar rollforward of CS, APIC, RE, AOCI (and treasury stock if any)
- **Equity section of the balance sheet:** AOCI added or subtracted after RE

| (vii) Interest revenue | (1) Net income | (3) Retained earnings |

**Key insight:** Period-end work must **reset the valuation allowance to current FV − cost** and **tax-effect the entire change through OCI**. An AOCI **debit** (loss) can flip to a credit when the year’s OCI gain exceeds the opening accumulated loss. Realized AFS G/L and ordinary revenues/expenses stay in **NI → RE**; unrealized AFS, effective CFH, and translation stay in **OCI → AOCI**.

### Q4 — MC — Where does the period’s OCI close?

**LO:** LO 19-7  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation—OCI closes to AOCI (not retained earnings); NI closes to RE  
**Question:** At the end of the accounting period, the process of closing **other comprehensive income** differs from closing **net income**. Which statement is correct?

- A) Both net income and other comprehensive income are closed to retained earnings.  
- B) Net income is closed to accumulated other comprehensive income; OCI is closed to retained earnings.  
- C) Net income is closed to retained earnings; other comprehensive income is closed to **accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI)**.  
- D) Neither net income nor OCI is closed; both remain as temporary accounts on the balance sheet.

**Answer:** **C.** Net income closes to the permanent account **retained earnings**. OCI closes to the permanent equity account **AOCI**. AOCI is reported as a separate component of stockholders’ equity (added if credit/income; deducted if debit/loss).

### Q5 — MC — Components included in OCI

**LO:** LO 19-7  
**Concept:** Classification—identify gains/losses commonly reported in other comprehensive income (net of tax)  
**Question:** Which of the following is **most likely** reported as a component of **other comprehensive income** (net of tax) rather than as part of net income?

- A) Gain on sale of equipment used in operations  
- B) Unrealized holding gain on **available-for-sale debt securities**  
- C) Dividend revenue on equity securities  
- D) Loss on early extinguishment of bonds payable  

**Answer:** **B.** Common OCI items include unrealized holding G/L on AFS debt securities, cash flow hedge adjustments, and foreign currency translation adjustments—all **net of tax**. Equipment sale gains, dividend revenue, and losses on bond extinguishment are components of **net income**.
