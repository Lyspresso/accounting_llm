# Agent 142 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-1

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Identify types and features of bonds  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Bond **types**: corporate vs municipal; secured vs debenture; term vs serial; callable; convertible
- Bond **features** fixed in the indenture: face value, maturity date, stated (coupon) rate, interest payment dates, authorization date
- Market-dependent features: market (effective/yield) rate, bond selling price, issue date
- Stated vs market rate → issue at par, discount, or premium; initial recognition JE
- Subsequent measurement: **effective-interest amortization schedule** (carrying amount rollforward)
- Period-end adjusting JE when reporting date ≠ interest payment date
- Maturity / settlement JE (derecognize bonds payable; repay face)
- Classification / presentation: bonds payable net of unamortized discount (or plus premium); interest expense on income statement

### Q1 — CORE — Bond types/features, discount issuance, amortization schedule, accrual, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Identify bond types and features; initial recognition at discount; effective-interest schedule; period-end accrual; maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Pinecrest Industrial Corp.** authorizes and issues bonds under the following indenture and market conditions:

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Face value | **$200,000** (200 bonds × $1,000) |
| Stated (coupon) rate | **4%** annual |
| Interest payment dates | **June 30** and **December 31** (semiannual) |
| Maturity date | **December 31, Year 3** (3-year **term** bonds) |
| Security | No liens on specific assets — general credit of issuer only |
| Call feature | Issuer may redeem after Year 1 at **102** |
| Convertibility | Not convertible |
| Market (yield) rate at issuance | **6%** annual |
| Issue date | January 1, Year 1 (same as authorization date) |
| Amortization method | **Effective interest** |

Pinecrest is a private manufacturing corporation. Fiscal year-end is **December 31**.

**Required:**  
a. Identify all applicable **bond type(s)** for this issue.  
b. Complete the **bond features** table (indenture features and market-dependent features), including face value, maturity date, stated rate **per interest period**, interest payment dates, authorization date, market rate **per interest period**, bond selling price (present value of cash interest + principal at the market rate), and issue date.  
c. Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance journal entry.  
d. Prepare the full **effective-interest amortization schedule** for the bond term (all six semiannual periods). Round intermediate interest expense to the nearest dollar; plug the final period so the unamortized discount reaches zero.  
e. Record the **June 30, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 1** interest/amortization entries. Show **balance sheet presentation** of the bonds at December 31, Year 1 and Year 1 interest expense.  
f. Pinecrest prepares **monthly** financial statements. Record the **January 31, Year 2** period-end **adjusting** entry for one month of interest and discount amortization (prorate the next full semiannual row from the schedule).  
g. Record the **December 31, Year 3** entries for the **final interest payment** (with final amortization) and **maturity settlement** of principal. Assume no Interest Payable balance remains at maturity.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Bond type(s)**  
- **Corporate** (issued by a private corporation)  
- **Debenture** (backed only by issuer’s general credit; no asset lien)  
- **Term** (entire principal due on a single maturity date)  
- **Callable** (issuer option to redeem early at 102 after Year 1)  
- *Not* municipal, *not* secured, *not* serial, *not* convertible  

**b. Bond features**

| Feature | Pinecrest bonds |
|---|---|
| Face value | $200,000 |
| Maturity date | December 31, Year 3 |
| Stated rate per interest period | 2% (4% ÷ 2) |
| Interest payment dates | June 30 and December 31 |
| Bond authorization date | January 1, Year 1 |
| Market rate per interest period | 3% (6% ÷ 2) |
| Bond selling price | **$189,166** (see computation) |
| Bond issue date | January 1, Year 1 |

**Selling price computation** (n = 6; i = 3%; cash interest = $200,000 × 2% = **$4,000**):  
\[
PV = \$4{,}000 \times \frac{1-(1.03)^{-6}}{0.03} + \$200{,}000 \times (1.03)^{-6} = \$189{,}165.62 \approx \$189{,}166
\]  
Discount at issuance = $200,000 − $189,166 = **$10,834**.

**c. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (discount)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 189,166 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 10,834 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 200,000 |
| *To record corporate term debenture bonds sold at a discount (market 6% > stated 4%)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 189,166 + 10,834 = Cr 200,000. Balanced.

**d. Effective-interest amortization schedule**  
Cash each period = $4,000. Interest expense = 3% × beginning carrying amount (rounded). Discount amort. = Interest expense − Cash. Final period plugged.

| Date | Cash (2% × face) | Interest exp. (3% × beg. CA) | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 10,834 | 189,166 |
| June 30, Y1 | 4,000 | 5,675 | 1,675 | 9,159 | 190,841 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 4,000 | 5,725 | 1,725 | 7,434 | 192,566 |
| June 30, Y2 | 4,000 | 5,777 | 1,777 | 5,657 | 194,343 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 4,000 | 5,830 | 1,830 | 3,827 | 196,173 |
| June 30, Y3 | 4,000 | 5,885 | 1,885 | 1,942 | 198,058 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 4,000 | 5,942 | 1,942 | 0 | 200,000 |
| **Totals** | **24,000** | **34,834** | **10,834** | | |

**Rollforward checks:** Total discount amortized $10,834 = initial discount. Ending CA = face. Total interest expense $34,834 = total cash interest $24,000 + discount $10,834.

**e. Year 1 interest entries and presentation**

June 30, Year 1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,675 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,675 |
| Cash | | 4,000 |

**Check:** Dr 5,675 = Cr 1,675 + 4,000.

December 31, Year 1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,725 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,725 |
| Cash | | 4,000 |

**Check:** Dr 5,725 = Cr 1,725 + 4,000.

**Balance sheet excerpt — December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $200,000 |
| Less: Discount on bonds payable | (7,434) |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$192,566** |

**Income statement — Year 1:** Interest expense = $5,675 + $5,725 = **$11,400**.

**f. January 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting (1/6 of next semiannual row)**  
Next full row (June 30, Y2): Interest exp. $5,777; discount amort. $1,777; cash interest $4,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense ($5,777 × 1/6) | 963 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable ($1,777 × 1/6) | | 296 |
| Interest Payable ($4,000 × 1/6) | | 667 |
| *One month accrual; reporting date ≠ interest payment date* | | |

**Check:** Dr 963 = Cr 296 + 667. Balanced.

**g. Maturity — December 31, Year 3**

Final interest and amortization:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,942 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,942 |
| Cash | | 4,000 |

**Check:** Dr 5,942 = Cr 1,942 + 4,000.

Derecognize bonds at maturity (settlement of face):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |
| *Pay face value; carrying amount equals face after full discount amortization* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000.

**Key insight:** LO 16-1 features drive measurement: the **stated rate** sets cash interest; the **market rate** at issuance sets the selling price and the constant effective rate used on the amortization schedule. Discount amortization increases carrying amount until it equals face at maturity.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Discount twin with full schedule and settlement
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — bond types/features; discount issuance; effective-interest schedule; mid-period accrual; maturity  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Meridian Forge Ltd.** (a public manufacturing corporation) authorizes and issues:

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Face value | **$500,000** |
| Stated rate | **5%** annual |
| Interest dates | **June 30** and **December 31** |
| Maturity | **December 31, Year 3** (term bonds) |
| Security | First lien on specified plant equipment |
| Other features | Not callable; not convertible |
| Market rate at issuance | **7%** annual |
| Authorization = issue date | January 1, Year 1 |
| Method | Effective interest |

**Required:**  
a. Identify bond type(s).  
b. List face value, stated rate per period, market rate per period, cash interest per period, selling price, and initial discount.  
c. Record issuance JE.  
d. Prepare the full 6-period effective-interest amortization schedule (round interest expense to nearest dollar; plug final period).  
e. Record both Year 1 semiannual interest entries; state Year 1 interest expense and Dec 31, Year 1 net carrying amount.  
f. Record a **February 28, Year 2** adjusting entry for **two months** of the next semiannual interest period (prorate 2/6 of the June 30, Year 2 schedule row).  
g. Record final interest + maturity settlement on December 31, Year 3 (no Interest Payable remains).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Bond type(s)**  
- **Corporate**  
- **Secured** (lien on plant equipment)  
- **Term**  
- *Not* debenture, municipal, callable, convertible, or serial  

**b. Features / pricing**  
- Face = **$500,000**  
- Stated rate per period = **2.5%** (5% ÷ 2)  
- Market rate per period = **3.5%** (7% ÷ 2)  
- Cash interest per period = $500,000 × 2.5% = **$12,500**  
- n = 6 periods  
- Selling price:  
\[
PV = \$12{,}500 \times \frac{1-(1.035)^{-6}}{0.035} + \$500{,}000 \times (1.035)^{-6} = \$473{,}357
\]  
- Initial discount = $500,000 − $473,357 = **$26,643**

**c. January 1, Year 1 issuance**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 473,357 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 26,643 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 500,000 |

**Check:** Dr 473,357 + 26,643 = Cr 500,000.

**d. Amortization schedule**

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| June 30, Y1 | 12,500 | 16,567 | 4,067 | 22,576 | 477,424 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 12,500 | 16,710 | 4,210 | 18,366 | 481,634 |
| June 30, Y2 | 12,500 | 16,857 | 4,357 | 14,009 | 485,991 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 12,500 | 17,010 | 4,510 | 9,499 | 490,501 |
| June 30, Y3 | 12,500 | 17,168 | 4,668 | 4,831 | 495,169 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 12,500 | 17,331 | 4,831 | 0 | 500,000 |
| **Totals** | **75,000** | **101,643** | **26,643** | | |

**Checks:** Discount amortized $26,643; total interest exp. $101,643 = cash $75,000 + discount $26,643; terminal CA = face.

**e. Year 1 entries and amounts**

June 30, Y1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 16,567 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 4,067 |

Dec 31, Y1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 16,710 | |

Year 1 interest expense = $16,567 + $16,710 = **$33,277**.  
Dec 31, Y1 net carrying amount = **$481,634** ($500,000 − $18,366).

**f. February 28, Year 2 adjusting (2/6 of June 30, Y2 row)**  
June 30, Y2 row: Interest $16,857; amort. $4,357; cash interest $12,500.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 17,331 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 4,831 |
| Cash | | 12,500 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 500,000 | |

**Key insight:** Changing face, stated rate, and market rate changes cash coupons, issue price, and every schedule row — but the LO 16-1 logic is unchanged: **indenture features fix cash flows; market rate prices the bond and drives effective interest.**

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Multi-issue type/feature classification and presentation
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Classify bond types across issues; extract indenture vs market features; presentation of net liability  
**Scenario:**  
**Oakridge Utilities Group** has three separate debt issues outstanding or newly authorized. None of the numbers below match textbook demos.

**Issue A — “Series 20X1 Notes”**  
City of Lakeshore issued $2,000,000 of bonds to finance a wastewater plant. Principal repays in equal installments of $400,000 each December 31 for five years. Bondholders have a lien on plant revenues. Issuer may retire any remaining principal after year 2 at 101. Interest 3% paid annually each December 31. Sold on authorization date at par.

**Issue B — “Oakridge 6s of 20X9”**  
Oakridge Utilities Corp. issued $800,000 face, 6% bonds on March 1, Year 1 (authorized March 1, Year 1). Interest payable semiannually on September 1 and March 1. Bonds mature March 1, Year 9 on a single date. No asset security; bondholders may convert each $1,000 bond into 20 shares of Oakridge common after Year 2. Sold for **$764,000** when the market yield was **7%** annual.

**Issue C — “Oakridge 4s Premium Placement”**  
Oakridge Utilities Corp. authorized and issued on January 1, Year 1: $100,000 face, 4% annual coupon paid once each December 31, 5-year term, sold at **103** (103% of face). Market conditions produced a yield below the coupon. Not callable, not convertible, unsecured.

**Required:**  
a. For **each** of Issues A, B, and C, list all applicable bond **type(s)** from: corporate, municipal, secured, debenture, term, serial, callable, convertible.  
b. For **Issue B only**, complete a features table with: face value; maturity date; stated rate per interest period; interest payment dates; authorization date; market rate per interest period; bond selling price; bond issue date; initial discount or premium amount.  
c. For **Issue C**, record the issuance JE at 103 and state the adjunct/contra account used.  
d. **Classification / presentation:** Assume at December 31, Year 1 (after two semiannual amortizations), Issue B has unamortized discount of **$33,120**. Show how Issue B should be presented in the long-term liabilities section. Also state how interest expense related to discount amortization is classified on the income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Types**

| Issue | Types |
|---|---|
| **A** | **Municipal**; **secured** (lien on plant revenues); **serial** (principal in installments); **callable** (after year 2 at 101) |
| **B** | **Corporate**; **debenture** (no asset security); **term** (single maturity); **convertible** |
| **C** | **Corporate**; **debenture**; **term** (not municipal, secured, serial, callable, or convertible) |

**b. Issue B features**

| Feature | Amount / description |
|---|---|
| Face value | $800,000 |
| Maturity date | March 1, Year 9 |
| Stated rate per interest period | 3% (6% ÷ 2) |
| Interest payment dates | September 1 and March 1 |
| Authorization date | March 1, Year 1 |
| Market rate per interest period | 3.5% (7% ÷ 2) |
| Bond selling price | $764,000 |
| Bond issue date | March 1, Year 1 |
| Initial discount | $800,000 − $764,000 = **$36,000** |

Cash interest per period = $800,000 × 3% = **$24,000**.

**c. Issue C issuance at 103**  
Selling price = 1.03 × $100,000 = **$103,000**. Premium = **$3,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 103,000 | |
| Premium on Bonds Payable | | 3,000 |
| Bonds Payable | | 100,000 |
| *Premium is an adjunct liability; market rate < stated rate* | | |

**Check:** Dr 103,000 = Cr 3,000 + 100,000.

**d. Presentation / classification — Issue B at Dec 31, Year 1**

Long-term liabilities:
| | |
|---|---:|
| Bonds payable | $800,000 |
| Less: Discount on bonds payable | (33,120) |
| **Bonds payable, net** | **$766,880** |

Income statement: Amortization of the discount is reported as **interest expense** (component of other expenses / financing cost), not as a separate extraordinary item and not as a reduction of bonds payable on the income statement. Carrying amount increases as discount amortizes toward face.

**Key insight:** LO 16-1 identification work (type labels + feature extraction) is the foundation for every later measurement step: cash coupons, PV price, discount/premium, schedule, and net BS presentation.

### Q4 — MC — Bond type and stated-vs-market classification
**LO:** LO 16-1  
**Concept:** Classify bond type and issue-price relation (discount / premium / par)  

**Question 1:**  
**Redline Transit Authority**, a governmental unit, issues bonds that mature in equal principal installments over 10 years, are backed by a pledge of farebox revenues, and may be retired early by the issuer after year 3 at 102. Which classification set is **most complete and correct**?

- A) Corporate; term; debenture; convertible  
- B) Municipal; serial; secured; callable  
- C) Municipal; term; debenture; convertible  
- D) Corporate; serial; secured; not callable  

**Answer:** **B.** Issuer is a governmental unit → **municipal**. Principal in installments → **serial**. Pledge of specific revenues → **secured**. Issuer early-retirement option → **callable**. A and D misstate the issuer as corporate; C wrongly labels term/debenture/convertible.

---

**Question 2:**  
On the bond issue date, the **market (yield) rate exceeds the stated (coupon) rate**. Investors will purchase the bonds:

- A) At a **premium**, and the issuer records Premium on Bonds Payable (adjunct liability).  
- B) At **face (par)** only, because face value is fixed in the indenture.  
- C) At a **discount**, and the issuer records Discount on Bonds Payable (contra liability).  
- D) At a premium if the bonds are callable, otherwise at a discount.  

**Answer:** **C.** When market rate > stated rate, PV of contractual cash flows < face → **discount**. Premium arises when market < stated. Face is the maturity payment, not the automatic issue price. Callability alone does not reverse the market-vs-stated pricing rule.
