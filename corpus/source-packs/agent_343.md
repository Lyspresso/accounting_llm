# Agent 343 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-5

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for changes in depreciation methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE** for PPE acquired for cash (asset that later undergoes a method change)
- Multi-period **subsequent measurement schedule** under the **original** method (SYD or DDB)
- Change in depreciation method = **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**
- **Prospective** treatment only: no restatement of prior periods; **no cumulative catch-up JE** to beginning retained earnings at the change date
- At change date: new depreciation from **(carrying amount − residual) / remaining useful life** under the **new** method
- **Period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis): Depreciation Expense Dr / Accumulated Depreciation Cr in the year of change and remaining years — this is the only year-end entry related to the method change
- **Disposal JE** after a method change (remove cost and accumulated depreciation; recognize gain/loss)
- Classification / disclosure: nature of the change and effect on current-period income; prior years unchanged

### Q1 — CORE — SYD → SL: purchase, schedules, period-end JEs each year, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Full life cycle with period-end adjusting JEs before and after prospective SYD→SL change; disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Tooling Corp.** purchased specialized production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$55,000 cash**. Management estimated a **5-year** useful life and a **$4,000** residual value. The company adopted the **sum-of-the-years’-digits (SYD)** depreciation method.

On **January 1, Year 4**, management changes the depreciation method for this asset to **straight-line**. Residual value and total useful life estimates are **unchanged**. Meridian’s year-end is December 31.

On **December 31, Year 5**, after recording Year 5 depreciation, Meridian sells the equipment for **$3,400 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** purchase journal entry.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule for Years 1–3 under SYD** (fraction, depreciation expense, ending AD, ending CV). Record the **period-end adjusting entry** for **each** of Years 1, 2, and 3.  
c. State whether any journal entry is required on **January 1, Year 4** solely because of the method change. Compute **straight-line depreciation for Year 4** after the change. Record the **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting entry** (emphasis).  
d. Prepare a full **rollforward schedule for Years 1–5** (method used, depreciation expense, ending AD, ending CV).  
e. Record **Year 5 period-end depreciation** and the **December 31, Year 5 disposal** journal entry. Prove each JE balances.  
f. In one or two sentences, classify the change under GAAP and state whether prior-year depreciation is restated.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 55,000 | |
| Cash | | 55,000 |
| *To record purchase of production equipment* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 55,000.

**b. Subsequent measurement — SYD Years 1–3; period-end adjusting JEs**  
Sum of years = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = **15**  
Depreciable base = $55,000 − $4,000 = **$51,000**

| Year | Beg. CV | SYD fraction | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 55,000 | 5/15 | **17,000** | 17,000 | 38,000 |
| 2 | 38,000 | 4/15 | **13,600** | 30,600 | 24,400 |
| 3 | 24,400 | 3/15 | **10,200** | 40,800 | 14,200 |

Computations:  
- Year 1: \(5/15 \times 51{,}000 = 17{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(4/15 \times 51{,}000 = 13{,}600\)  
- Year 3: \(3/15 \times 51{,}000 = 10{,}200\)

December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 17,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 17,000 |
| *Year 1 SYD depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 17,000.

December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 13,600 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 13,600 |
| *Year 2 SYD depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 13,600.

December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 10,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 10,200 |
| *Year 3 SYD depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,200.

Carrying amount on **January 1, Year 4** (change date) = **$14,200**.

**c. No change-date catch-up JE; Year 4 period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**  

**January 1, Year 4:** **No journal entry** for the method change. The change is **prospective** only (treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle). Prior AD and RE are **not** adjusted.

Remaining useful life = 2 years. Residual still $4,000.  
\[
\text{Annual SL depreciation} = \frac{\$14{,}200 - \$4{,}000}{2} = \$5{,}100
\]

December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry (emphasis):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,100 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,100 |
| *Year 4 SL depreciation after prospective method change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,100.  
Ending CV Dec 31, Year 4 = 14,200 − 5,100 = **$9,100**.  
Ending AD Dec 31, Year 4 = 40,800 + 5,100 = **$45,900**.

**d. Full rollforward Years 1–5**

| Year | Method | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | SYD | 17,000 | 17,000 | 38,000 |
| 2 | SYD | 13,600 | 30,600 | 24,400 |
| 3 | SYD | 10,200 | 40,800 | 14,200 |
| 4 | SL (post-change) | 5,100 | 45,900 | 9,100 |
| 5 | SL | 5,100 | 51,000 | 4,000 |

Total depreciation over life = 17,000 + 13,600 + 10,200 + 5,100 + 5,100 = **$51,000** = depreciable base. Ending CV = residual **$4,000**.

**e. Year 5 period-end depreciation and disposal**

December 31, Year 5 — depreciation:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 5,100 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 5,100 |
| *Year 5 SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,100.

December 31, Year 5 — disposal (sale for $3,400):  
Book value at sale = $4,000; proceeds $3,400 → **loss $600**.  
AD at disposal = **$51,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,400 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 51,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 600 | |
| Equipment | | 55,000 |
| *To record sale of equipment below book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,400 + 51,000 + 600 = **55,000** = Cr 55,000.

**f. Classification / prospective treatment**  
A change in depreciation method is a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**. It is accounted for **prospectively** (current and future periods only). Prior years are **not** restated; beginning retained earnings is **not** adjusted for the cumulative difference between methods.

**Key insight:** The method change does **not** generate a January 1 adjusting entry. The **only** period-end adjusting JE in the year of change is the ordinary depreciation accrual under the **new** method, computed from **current carrying amount**, residual, and **remaining** life.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — DDB → SL twin with multi-period schedule and period-end JEs
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; DDB subsequent measurement schedule; prospective change to SL; period-end JEs  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Plastics LLC** acquired production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$120,000 cash**. Estimated useful life **4 years**; residual value **$12,000**. Cascade used the **double-declining-balance (DDB)** method for Years 1 and 2.

On **January 1, Year 3**, Cascade changes to the **straight-line** method for this equipment. Residual value and total useful life remain **$12,000** and **4 years**. Year-end is December 31.

**Required:**  
a. Record the purchase entry on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare a **DDB subsequent measurement schedule for Years 1–2** (beginning BV, rate, expense, ending AD, ending BV). Record each year-end adjusting entry.  
c. Confirm there is **no** January 1, Year 3 catch-up entry. Compute Year 3 and Year 4 straight-line depreciation after the change; record the **Year 3 and Year 4 period-end JEs**.  
d. Show the full **rollforward schedule Years 1–4** and confirm carrying amount at December 31, Year 4 equals residual.  
e. State whether Year 1–2 depreciation would be restated under the change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 120,000 | |
| Cash | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 120,000.

**b. DDB subsequent measurement — Years 1–2; period-end JEs**  
DDB rate = \(2 / 4 = 50\%\). Residual is **not** subtracted when applying the DDB rate to beginning book value (but book value is not driven below residual over the life).

| Year | Beg. BV | Rate | Depreciation | End. AD | End. BV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 120,000 | 50% | **60,000** | 60,000 | 60,000 |
| 2 | 60,000 | 50% | **30,000** | 90,000 | 30,000 |

Computations:  
- Year 1: \(120{,}000 \times 0.50 = 60{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(60{,}000 \times 0.50 = 30{,}000\)

December 31, Year 1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 60,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 60,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 60,000.

December 31, Year 2:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 30,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 30,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 30,000.  
CV on change date **January 1, Year 3** = **$30,000**.

**c. No catch-up JE; prospective SL period-end JEs**  
**January 1, Year 3:** **No entry** for the method change.  
Remaining life = 2 years.  
\[
\text{Annual SL} = \frac{\$30{,}000 - \$12{,}000}{2} = \$9{,}000
\]

December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |
| *Year 3 SL depreciation after prospective DDB→SL change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000.

December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,000 |
| *Year 4 SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000.

**d. Full rollforward Years 1–4**

| Year | Method | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | DDB | 60,000 | 60,000 | 60,000 |
| 2 | DDB | 30,000 | 90,000 | 30,000 |
| 3 | SL (post-change) | 9,000 | 99,000 | 21,000 |
| 4 | SL | 9,000 | 108,000 | 12,000 |

Total depreciation = 60,000 + 30,000 + 9,000 + 9,000 = **$108,000**.  
CV end Year 4 = 120,000 − 108,000 = **$12,000** (equals residual).

**e.** No restatement of Years 1–2. The change is **prospective** only (treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle).

**Key insight:** Under DDB, residual is ignored in the rate application; after switching to SL, residual is subtracted from **current carrying amount** and allocated evenly over remaining years. The year-of-change **period-end adjusting JE** uses only the new SL amount — never a retrospective RE plug.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Year-of-change period-end JE only; prove no retrospective entry; disposal
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting entry in year of method change; no January 1 catch-up; disposal after change  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Conveyors Inc.** purchased a conveyor system on **January 1, Year 1** for **$36,000 cash**. Useful life **3 years**; residual value **$6,000**. Depreciation method: **sum-of-the-years’-digits**.

On **January 1, Year 2**, Harborline changes to **straight-line** depreciation. Residual and total life estimates are unchanged.

On **December 31, Year 3**, after recording Year 3 depreciation, Harborline sells the conveyor for **$5,800 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **SYD subsequent measurement for Year 1 only** (expense, AD, CV at Jan 1, Year 2).  
b. **(Emphasis)** State whether a journal entry is recorded on **January 1, Year 2** for the method change. If none, explain why.  
c. Compute Year 2 depreciation under the new SL method and record **only** the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting entry**.  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 3 period-end depreciation** and the **disposal** entry.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** (1) How is this change classified? (2) Are comparative prior-year financial statements restated for depreciation? (3) What amount of Year 2 depreciation expense is reported for this asset, and how does that amount compare to what SYD would have been for Year 2?

**Answer key:**  

**a. SYD Year 1 → CV at change date**  
Sum of years = 1 + 2 + 3 = **6**. Depreciable base = $36,000 − $6,000 = **$30,000**.

| Year | SYD fraction | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3/6 | **15,000** | 15,000 | 21,000 |

Computation: \(3/6 \times 30{,}000 = 15{,}000\).

Carrying amount **January 1, Year 2** = **$21,000**. Accumulated depreciation = **$15,000**.

**b. January 1, Year 2 method-change entry**  
**No journal entry.** A change in depreciation method is a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**, applied **prospectively**. There is no cumulative effect to Retained Earnings and no restatement of Year 1. Books continue with the existing cost and AD balances; only **future** depreciation amounts change.

**c. Year 2 period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**  
Remaining life = 2 years. Residual still $6,000.  
\[
\text{Annual SL} = \frac{\$21{,}000 - \$6{,}000}{2} = \$7{,}500
\]

December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting entry **only**:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 7,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 7,500 |
| *Year 2 SL depreciation after prospective method change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,500.  
End Year 2: AD = 15,000 + 7,500 = **$22,500**; CV = 21,000 − 7,500 = **$13,500**.

**d. Year 3 depreciation and disposal**

December 31, Year 3 — depreciation:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 7,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,500.  
End Year 3: AD = 22,500 + 7,500 = **$30,000**; CV = **$6,000** (residual).

December 31, Year 3 — disposal (sale for $5,800):  
Book value $6,000 − proceeds $5,800 = **loss $200**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 5,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 30,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 200 | |
| Equipment | | 36,000 |
| *To record sale of conveyor below book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,800 + 30,000 + 200 = **36,000** = Cr 36,000.

**e. Classification / presentation**  
(1) **Change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle** (prospective).  
(2) **No** — comparative prior years are **not** restated for the method change.  
(3) Year 2 depreciation reported = **$7,500**. Under continued SYD, Year 2 would have been \(2/6 \times 30{,}000 = \$10{,}000\). The $2,500 lower expense is the **current-period effect** of the prospective change (disclose as appropriate).

**Key insight (emphasis):** Students often invent a “catch-up” debit or credit to RE when methods change. Under LO 12-5, the **only** year-of-change journal entry is the ordinary **period-end adjusting JE** for depreciation under the new method.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of a change in depreciation method
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Classification — change in estimate effected by a change in principle; prospective application  
**Question:**  
On January 1 of the current year, **Northbridge Foundry Co.** changes from the double-declining-balance method to the straight-line method for equipment purchased two years earlier. Residual value and useful life estimates are unchanged. How should Northbridge account for this change?

- A) As a change in accounting principle, with retrospective restatement of prior-period financial statements  
- B) As a change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle, applied prospectively from the year of change  
- C) As a correction of an error, with a prior-period adjustment to beginning retained earnings  
- D) As a change in reporting entity, with restatement of all periods presented  

**Answer:** B.  
A change in depreciation method is specifically a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**. GAAP requires **prospective** application: recompute depreciation for the current and future periods using the asset’s **carrying amount** at the change date, residual, and remaining life under the new method. Prior periods are **not** restated, and there is no cumulative-effect entry to retained earnings (unlike most voluntary principle changes). Not an error (C) and not a change in reporting entity (D).

### Q5 — MC — Compute depreciation after SYD → SL change
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Compute new-method depreciation from carrying amount at change date  
**Question:**  
**Briarwood Assembly Inc.** purchased equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$48,000**. Residual value **$3,000**; useful life **5 years**. The company used **sum-of-the-years’-digits** for Years 1 and 2. On **January 1, Year 3**, Briarwood changes to **straight-line**. Residual and total life remain unchanged. What amount of depreciation expense should Briarwood recognize for **Year 3**?

- A) $4,500  
- B) $6,000  
- C) $9,000  
- D) $12,000  

**Answer:** B. **$6,000**  

Sum of years = 15. Depreciable base = $48,000 − $3,000 = **$45,000**.  
Year 1 SYD: \(5/15 \times 45{,}000 = 15{,}000\)  
Year 2 SYD: \(4/15 \times 45{,}000 = 12{,}000\)  
AD end Year 2 = 27,000; CV Jan 1 Year 3 = 48,000 − 27,000 = **$21,000**.  
Remaining life = 3 years.  
\[
\text{Year 3 SL} = \frac{\$21{,}000 - \$3{,}000}{3} = \$6{,}000
\]  
(A) would be if residual were ignored incorrectly or life were miscounted; (C) is Year 2 SYD; (D) is Year 1 SYD × fraction error / remaining base misuse.
