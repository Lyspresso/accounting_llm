# Agent 14 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-4

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for Bonds Issued at a Discount  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of bonds issued at a discount (Cash, Discount on Bonds Payable, Bonds Payable)
- Measuring issue proceeds at PV using market (effective) rate
- Effective interest amortization schedule (interest expense ↑ as carrying amount ↑)
- Subsequent measurement JEs each interest date (Dr Interest Expense; Cr Discount; Cr Cash)
- Period-end adjusting JE when reporting date ≠ interest payment date (prorate schedule amounts)
- Derecognition at maturity (final interest + pay face)
- Balance sheet presentation: Bonds payable net of unamortized discount
- Number-variant twin of the core discount life-cycle

### Q1 — CORE — Cedar Ridge discount bonds (issue → schedule → Year 1 → maturity)
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE and full effective-interest life cycle for discount bonds  
**Scenario:**  
Cedar Ridge Manufacturing authorizes and issues **$200,000** face of **4%** interest-bearing bonds on **January 1, Year 1**. The bonds mature **December 31, Year 3** and pay cash interest **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. The bonds are sold to yield a **market rate of 6%**. Cedar Ridge amortizes the bond discount using the **effective interest method**. Amounts are rounded to the nearest dollar (textbook style).

**Given computations (semiannual):**
- Semiannual market rate: 6% ÷ 2 = **3%**
- Semiannual periods: 3 × 2 = **6**
- Semiannual cash interest: 4% ÷ 2 × $200,000 = **$4,000**
- Issue price = PV of principal + PV of interest annuity at 3%, 6 periods = **$189,166**
- Initial discount = $200,000 − $189,166 = **$10,834**

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **effective interest amortization schedule** over the full bond term (all 6 periods).  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 issuance journal entry** (emphasis).  
c. Prepare the interest/amortization JEs for **June 30, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 1**.  
d. Show the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet** presentation of the bonds and **Year 1 interest expense**.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** entries for the final interest payment and maturity settlement (assume no Interest Payable balance).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Amortization schedule — effective interest (stated 4%, market 6%)**

| Date | Cash (2% × face) | Interest expense (3% × beg. carry) | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|------|------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 10,834 | 189,166 |
| Jun 30, Y1 | 4,000 | 5,675 | 1,675 | 9,159 | 190,841 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 4,000 | 5,725 | 1,725 | 7,434 | 192,566 |
| Jun 30, Y2 | 4,000 | 5,777 | 1,777 | 5,657 | 194,343 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 4,000 | 5,830 | 1,830 | 3,827 | 196,173 |
| Jun 30, Y3 | 4,000 | 5,885 | 1,885 | 1,942 | 198,058 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 4,000 | 5,942 | 1,942 | 0 | 200,000 |
| **Totals** | **24,000** | **34,834** | **10,834** | | |

*Notes:* Interest expense each period = beginning carrying amount × 3%. Discount amort. = Interest expense − Cash. Final period amort. clears remaining unamortized discount. Total interest expense ($34,834) exceeds total cash interest ($24,000) by the discount ($10,834).

**b. Issuance at a discount (initial recognition)**  
January 1, Year 1 — To record bonds sold at a discount  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Cash | 189,166 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 10,834 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bonds Payable | | 200,000 |

*Check:* Dr 189,166 + 10,834 = 200,000 = Cr. Carrying amount at issue = $189,166.

**c. Interest and discount amortization (Year 1)**  
June 30, Year 1 — To record payment of interest and bond discount amortization  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 5,675 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,675 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 4,000 |

*Check:* 5,675 = 1,675 + 4,000. Carry after entry = 189,166 + 1,675 = **190,841**.

December 31, Year 1 — To record payment of interest and bond discount amortization  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 5,725 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,725 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 4,000 |

*Check:* 5,725 = 1,725 + 4,000. Carry = 190,841 + 1,725 = **192,566**. Unamortized discount = **7,434**.

**d. Financial statement presentation — December 31, Year 1**  

Balance sheet (liabilities excerpt):  
- Bonds payable — $200,000  
- Less: Discount on bonds payable — 7,434  
- **Bonds payable, net — $192,566**

Income statement (Year 1):  
- Interest expense — **$(11,400)** [= 5,675 + 5,725]

**e. Maturity — December 31, Year 3**  
Final interest payment and amortization:  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 5,942 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,942 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 4,000 |

Derecognize bonds at face:  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Bonds Payable | 200,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 200,000 |

*After final amort., unamortized discount = 0; carrying amount = face.*

**Key insight:** Stated rate < market rate → proceeds < face → **Discount on Bonds Payable** (contra-liability). Effective interest: expense = market rate × **beginning carrying amount**, so expense and amortization **increase** each period as the discount is amortized and carrying amount climbs to face.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Northline period-end accrual, presentation, maturity
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE (interest date ≠ reporting date), classification on the balance sheet, and maturity settlement  
**Scenario:**  
Northline Utilities issued **$300,000** of **6%** bonds on **January 1, Year 1**, maturing **December 31, Year 5**. Interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Market rate at issuance was **8%**. Effective interest method; round to nearest dollar.

**Semiannual setup:**
- Market rate per period: 8% ÷ 2 = **4%**
- Periods: 5 × 2 = **10**
- Cash interest: 6% ÷ 2 × $300,000 = **$9,000**
- Issue price = **$275,667**; Discount = **$24,333**

Partial amortization (first three interest dates) used for this problem:

| Date | Cash | Interest expense | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|------|-----:|-----------------:|----------------:|---------------------:|----------------:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | — | — | 24,333 | 275,667 |
| Jun 30, Y1 | 9,000 | 11,027 | 2,027 | 22,306 | 277,694 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 9,000 | 11,108 | 2,108 | 20,198 | 279,802 |
| Jun 30, Y2 | 9,000 | 11,192 | 2,192 | 18,006 | 281,994 |

*(Full-term totals for reference: cash interest $90,000; interest expense $114,333; discount amort. $24,333. Final Dec 31, Y5 period: cash 9,000; expense 11,884; amort. 2,884 → carry 300,000.)*

**Required:**  
a. Issuance JE on January 1, Year 1.  
b. December 31, Year 1 balance sheet classification/presentation of the bonds (long-term liability, net of discount).  
c. Northline prepares **monthly** financial statements. Record the **January 31, Year 2** adjusting entry for one month of bond interest and discount amortization (prorate the June 30, Year 2 schedule row).  
d. Record maturity entries on **December 31, Year 5** for the final interest payment and repayment of face (no Interest Payable).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Issuance**  
January 1, Year 1  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Cash | 275,667 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 24,333 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bonds Payable | | 300,000 |

*Dr = Cr = 300,000.*

**b. Classification / presentation — December 31, Year 1**  
Long-term liabilities:  
- Bonds payable — $300,000  
- Less: Discount on bonds payable — 20,198  
- **Bonds payable, net — $279,802**

*(Discount is a contra-liability, not an asset; bonds are reported at carrying amount. Unamortized discount after two periods = 24,333 − 2,027 − 2,108 = 20,198.)*

**c. Period-end adjusting JE — January 31, Year 2**  
Prorate June 30, Year 2 schedule amounts × 1/6:  
- Interest expense: $11,192 × 1/6 = **$1,865**  
- Discount amortization: $2,192 × 1/6 = **$365**  
- Interest payable: $9,000 × 1/6 = **$1,500**

January 31, Year 2 — To record accrual of bond interest expense  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 1,865 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 365 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interest Payable | | 1,500 |

*Check:* 1,865 = 365 + 1,500.

**d. Maturity — December 31, Year 5**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Interest Expense | 11,884 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,884 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 9,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|------:|-------:|
| Bonds Payable | 300,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 300,000 |

**Key insight:** When the books close between interest dates, **prorate** the next period’s effective-interest amounts from the amortization schedule. Discount amortization still runs with the accrual (credit Discount / reduce contra-liability), and cash interest not yet paid is credited to **Interest Payable**. At maturity, carrying amount equals face and the discount is fully amortized.

### Q4 — MC — Discount bond presentation
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Classification and presentation of Discount on Bonds Payable  
**Question:**  
On the issuance date, Oak Hollow Co. sold $100,000 face of bonds for $96,490 cash when the market rate exceeded the stated rate. How should Oak Hollow report the $3,510 difference immediately after issuance?

- A) As a deferred charge (asset) amortized to interest expense over the bond term  
- B) As a reduction of Bonds Payable (contra-liability), so bonds are presented at carrying amount below face  
- C) As a component of other comprehensive income until maturity  
- D) As a direct reduction of retained earnings at issuance  

**Answer:** B.  
When bonds are issued at a discount, **Discount on Bonds Payable** is a **contra-liability** deducted from Bonds Payable so the liability is reported at **carrying amount** (proceeds initially; accretes to face under effective interest). It is not an asset, not OCI, and not a retained earnings charge. Over the term the discount is amortized as additional interest expense.

### Q5 — MC — Effective interest vs cash for discount bonds
**LO:** LO 16-4  
**Concept:** Relationship of interest expense, cash interest, and discount amortization  
**Question:**  
Under the effective interest method for bonds issued at a **discount**, which statement is correct for a typical interest period (before the final rounding period)?

- A) Interest expense equals cash interest paid each period  
- B) Interest expense is less than cash interest; the difference amortizes the discount  
- C) Interest expense equals beginning carrying amount × market rate at issuance; expense exceeds cash interest by the discount amortized  
- D) Interest expense equals face × market rate each period regardless of carrying amount  

**Answer:** C.  
Effective interest expense = **beginning carrying amount × historical market (effective) rate**. For a discount bond, carrying amount < face, but expense still exceeds contractual cash interest; the excess is the period’s **discount amortization**, which increases carrying amount toward face. Expense rises over time as carrying amount rises (A and B reverse discount vs premium; D incorrectly multiplies face by market rate each period).
