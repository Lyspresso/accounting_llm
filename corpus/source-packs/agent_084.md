# Agent 84 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-8

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for assets held for sale  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** when held-for-sale criteria are met, measure at **lower of carrying amount or fair value less costs to sell**; recognize impairment write-down immediately
- **Cease depreciation** once classified as held for sale; recoverability (undiscounted cash flows) test does **not** apply
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** remeasure each reporting date at lower of (1) carrying amount and (2) current FV less costs to sell; recovery gains limited to **cumulative prior write-downs** (and cannot exceed carrying amount at HFS decision date)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** further write-down or recovery of prior impairment (via Accumulated Depreciation)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove cost and accumulated depreciation; difference vs net proceeds = gain or loss on sale
- **Classification / presentation:** report as **other (nonoperating) assets**, not PPE; disclosure of HFS measurement and cease-depreciation policy
- **Number-variant twin:** full life cycle with different cost, AD, FV, costs to sell, recovery, and disposal amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Full HFS life cycle (emphasis: initial recognition JE)
**LO:** LO 12-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE (lower of CA or FV − costs to sell); subsequent measurement schedule; period-end recovery JE; disposal JE; presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeway Packaging Systems**, a calendar-year company, decides on **December 31, Year 1** to remove a specialized carton-sealing line from operations and sell it. Management has committed to a sale plan; the asset is available for immediate sale; an active program to locate a buyer has begun; sale is probable within one year; the asking price is reasonable relative to fair value; and significant changes to the plan are unlikely. All **ASC 360 held-for-sale** criteria are met on December 31, Year 1.

| Fact | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cost of equipment | **$180,000** |
| Accumulated depreciation immediately **before** HFS classification | **$72,000** |
| Fair value, Dec 31, Year 1 | **$95,000** |
| Estimated direct costs to sell, Dec 31, Year 1 | **$5,000** |
| Fair value, Dec 31, Year 2 (still held for sale) | **$103,000** |
| Estimated costs to sell, Dec 31, Year 2 | **$5,000** (unchanged) |
| March 20, Year 3 — cash selling price | **$100,000** |
| March 20, Year 3 — broker / selling costs paid in cash | **$4,000** |

Ridgeway records write-downs and recoveries by adjusting **Accumulated Depreciation** (textbook Demo 12-8 style). No depreciation is recorded after HFS classification.

**Required:**  
**(a)** Compute carrying amount before HFS adjustment and fair value less costs to sell on December 31, Year 1. Record the **initial recognition / impairment** journal entry (if any). State the adjusted carrying amount.  
**(b)** Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for the asset held for sale at Dec 31, Year 1 and Dec 31, Year 2 (measurement base, write-down or recovery, ending carrying amount, and ceiling).  
**(c)** Record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting** entry (if any). Confirm that **no depreciation** is recorded in Year 2.  
**(d)** Record the **disposal** journal entry on March 20, Year 3.  
**(e)** How should the asset be **classified and presented** on the December 31, Year 1 balance sheet? Briefly note the income-statement treatment of the impairment loss (continuing operations vs discontinued operations concept only if criteria for a discontinued operation are met).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition — December 31, Year 1**

Carrying amount before HFS = $180,000 − $72,000 = **$108,000**  
Fair value less costs to sell = $95,000 − $5,000 = **$90,000**  
Measurement = lower of $108,000 and $90,000 = **$90,000**  
Impairment loss = $108,000 − $90,000 = **$18,000**

*December 31, Year 1 — To record impairment of asset held for sale*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on Impairment | 18,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 18,000 |

**Check:** Dr 18,000 = Cr 18,000. **Balanced.**

Adjusted carrying amount = $180,000 − ($72,000 + $18,000) = **$90,000**.  
Going forward: **no further depreciation** while classified as held for sale.

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Cost | Accum. dep. | Carrying amount (beg.) | FV less costs to sell | Ceiling (CA at HFS decision) | Cum. prior losses | Write-down (recovery) | Ending CA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 (HFS) | 180,000 | 72,000 → 90,000 | 108,000 | 90,000 | 108,000 | — | Loss **18,000** | **90,000** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 180,000 | 90,000 → 82,000 | 90,000 | 98,000 | 108,000 | 18,000 | Recovery **8,000** | **98,000** |

Year 2 computation:  
FV − costs to sell = $103,000 − $5,000 = **$98,000**  
Allowed new CA = min($98,000, ceiling $108,000) = **$98,000**, and increase cannot exceed cumulative prior losses of $18,000.  
Recovery = $98,000 − $90,000 = **$8,000** (≤ $18,000).

**(c) December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE**

*No depreciation entry.*

*December 31, Year 2 — To record recovery of impairment on asset held for sale*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 8,000 | |
| Gain on Recovery of Impaired Asset | | 8,000 |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr 8,000. **Balanced.**  
Ending CA = $180,000 − $82,000 = **$98,000**.

**(d) Disposal — March 20, Year 3**

Net proceeds = $100,000 − $4,000 = **$96,000**  
Loss on disposal = $98,000 − $96,000 = **$2,000**  
Accumulated depreciation at sale = $82,000

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 100,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 2,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 82,000 | |
| Equipment | | 180,000 |
| Cash (selling costs) | | 4,000 |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 + 2,000 + 82,000 = **184,000**; Cr 180,000 + 4,000 = **184,000**. **Balanced.**

*(Equivalent net-cash form: Dr Cash 96,000; Dr Loss 2,000; Dr Accum. Dep. 82,000; Cr Equipment 180,000.)*

**(e) Classification / presentation**  
- Balance sheet (Dec 31, Y1): report the asset held for sale at **$90,000** within **other (nonoperating) assets**, **not** within property, plant, and equipment.  
- No depreciation while held for sale.  
- Impairment loss ($18,000) is recognized in earnings when HFS criteria are met. If the asset (or disposal group) also meets **discontinued operations** criteria (Ch. 3), the loss is presented in discontinued operations, net of tax; otherwise in continuing operations (often within other expense / impairment).

**Key insight:** Held-for-sale assets are a **valuation** model (lower of CA or FV − selling costs), not a cost-allocation model. Initial recognition often produces an immediate write-down; subsequent recoveries are capped; depreciation stops the day HFS classification begins.

### Q2 — CORE number-variant twin
**LO:** LO 12-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial HFS write-down, multi-period measurement schedule, recovery JE, disposal with small loss  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Logistics LLC**, calendar year-end, classifies a warehouse conveyor system as **held for sale** on **December 31, Year 1** after all ASC 360 criteria are met. The system is taken out of use the same day.

| Fact | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cost | **$250,000** |
| Accumulated depreciation before HFS | **$95,000** |
| Fair value, Dec 31, Year 1 | **$128,000** |
| Estimated costs to sell, Dec 31, Year 1 | **$6,000** |
| Fair value, Dec 31, Year 2 (still HFS) | **$148,000** |
| Estimated costs to sell, Dec 31, Year 2 | **$6,000** |
| August 1, Year 3 — cash selling price | **$145,000** |
| August 1, Year 3 — cash selling costs | **$3,500** |

**Required:**  
**(a)** Record the December 31, Year 1 **initial recognition** impairment JE and state the adjusted carrying amount.  
**(b)** Prepare the **subsequent measurement schedule** for Dec 31, Y1 and Dec 31, Y2.  
**(c)** Record the December 31, Year 2 **period-end recovery** JE (if any).  
**(d)** Record the August 1, Year 3 **disposal** JE.  
**(e)** State the December 31, Year 2 **balance-sheet carrying amount** and classification.

**Answer key:**

**(a) December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition**

CA before = $250,000 − $95,000 = **$155,000**  
FV − costs to sell = $128,000 − $6,000 = **$122,000**  
Impairment loss = $155,000 − $122,000 = **$33,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on Impairment | 33,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 33,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 33,000. **Balanced.**  
Adjusted CA = $250,000 − ($95,000 + $33,000) = **$122,000**.  
Accum. dep. balance after entry = **$128,000**.

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Beg. CA | FV − CTS | Ceiling | Cum. losses avail. | Adj. | End. CA | End. Accum. dep. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | 155,000 | 122,000 | 155,000 | — | Loss **33,000** | **122,000** | 128,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 122,000 | 142,000 | 155,000 | 33,000 | Recovery **20,000** | **142,000** | 108,000 |

Y2: FV − CTS = $148,000 − $6,000 = **$142,000**  
Recovery = $142,000 − $122,000 = **$20,000** (≤ $33,000 cumulative losses; ≤ ceiling $155,000).

**(c) December 31, Year 2**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 20,000 | |
| Gain on Recovery of Impaired Asset | | 20,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 20,000. **Balanced.**  
No depreciation in Year 2.

**(d) August 1, Year 3 disposal**

Net proceeds = $145,000 − $3,500 = **$141,500**  
CA at disposal = **$142,000**  
Loss on disposal = $142,000 − $141,500 = **$500**  
Accum. dep. at disposal = **$108,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 145,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 108,000 | |
| Equipment | | 250,000 |
| Cash (selling costs) | | 3,500 |

**Check:** Dr 145,000 + 500 + 108,000 = **253,500**; Cr 250,000 + 3,500 = **253,500**. **Balanced.**

**(e) Presentation at Dec 31, Year 2**  
Asset held for sale reported at **$142,000** as an **other asset** (not PPE). Fair value less costs to sell is the ongoing measurement base (with recovery ceiling).

**Key insight:** Same model as Q1 with all new numbers — write-down at HFS, recovery limited to prior cumulative losses, then disposal gain/loss against the **remeasured** carrying amount.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — multi-asset HFS classification, further write-down, recovery, presentation
**LO:** LO 12-8  
**Concept:** Multi-asset held-for-sale measurement at reporting date; period-end write-downs and recovery; no depreciation; other-asset presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northpine Industrial Components** has three plant assets that **already qualify** as held for sale at **December 31, Year 1**. None are depreciated after HFS classification. Data at Dec 31, Year 1:

| Plant asset | Original cost | Accumulated depreciation | Fair value | Selling costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| #A41 Sealer | $80,000 | $50,000 | $28,000 | $1,000 |
| #B17 Mixer | 120,000 | 40,000 | 90,000 | 4,000 |
| #C09 Cutter | 200,000 | 110,000 | 70,000 | 5,000 |

At **December 31, Year 2**, all three assets are still held for sale (no sales occurred). Updated data:

| Plant asset | Fair value | Selling costs |
|---|---:|---:|
| #A41 | $25,000 | $1,000 |
| #B17 | 78,000 | 4,000 |
| #C09 | 85,000 | 5,000 |

**Required:**  
**(a)** For each asset at Dec 31, Year 1, compute carrying amount, FV less costs to sell, impairment loss (if any), and adjusted carrying amount.  
**(b)** Record **one combined** journal entry for all Year 1 impairment losses.  
**(c)** Prepare a **Year 2 subsequent measurement** table and record the **December 31, Year 2 adjusting entries** (further write-downs and/or recoveries).  
**(d)** State the **aggregate** carrying amount of assets held for sale on Dec 31, Year 1 and Dec 31, Year 2, and how they are **classified** on the balance sheet. Confirm depreciation expense on these assets for Year 2 is zero.

**Answer key:**

**(a) December 31, Year 1 measurement**

| Asset | CA = Cost − AD | FV − CTS | Impairment loss | Adj. CA | Adj. AD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| #A41 | 80,000 − 50,000 = **30,000** | 28,000 − 1,000 = **27,000** | **3,000** | **27,000** | 53,000 |
| #B17 | 120,000 − 40,000 = **80,000** | 90,000 − 4,000 = **86,000** | **0** (FV−CTS > CA) | **80,000** | 40,000 |
| #C09 | 200,000 − 110,000 = **90,000** | 70,000 − 5,000 = **65,000** | **25,000** | **65,000** | 135,000 |
| **Totals** | | | **28,000** | **172,000** | |

Ceilings at HFS decision (for later recovery): #A41 **$30,000**; #B17 **$80,000**; #C09 **$90,000**.

**(b) Combined impairment JE — December 31, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on Impairment | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Plant Assets | | 28,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000. **Balanced.**  
(Allocation of the credit: $3,000 to #A41 AD; $25,000 to #C09 AD.)

**(c) December 31, Year 2 subsequent measurement and adjusting JEs**

| Asset | Beg. CA (Y2) | FV − CTS | Ceiling | Cum. prior loss | Required end CA | Adj. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| #A41 | 27,000 | 25,000 − 1,000 = **24,000** | 30,000 | 3,000 | **24,000** | Further loss **3,000** |
| #B17 | 80,000 | 78,000 − 4,000 = **74,000** | 80,000 | 0 | **74,000** | Loss **6,000** |
| #C09 | 65,000 | 85,000 − 5,000 = **80,000** | 90,000 | 25,000 | **80,000** | Recovery **15,000** |

*December 31, Year 2 — Further write-downs (#A41 and #B17)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on Impairment ($3,000 + $6,000) | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Plant Assets | | 9,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2 — Recovery of prior impairment (#C09)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Plant Assets | 15,000 | |
| Gain on Recovery of Impaired Asset | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000. **Balanced.**  
Recovery ≤ cumulative prior loss on #C09 ($25,000) and does not exceed ceiling ($90,000).  
**No depreciation** entries for any of the three assets in Year 2.

Ending carrying amounts Y2: #A41 **$24,000**; #B17 **$74,000**; #C09 **$80,000**.

**(d) Aggregate presentation**  
- Dec 31, Year 1 aggregate HFS carrying amount = **$172,000** → reported as **other assets** (not PPE).  
- Dec 31, Year 2 aggregate = $24,000 + $74,000 + $80,000 = **$178,000** → still **other assets**.  
- Year 2 depreciation expense on these assets = **$0**.

**Key insight:** Assets already held for sale are remeasured individually each period. #B17 had no initial loss but can still require a write-down later when FV − CTS falls below CA. Recoveries never push CA above the amount at the HFS decision date.

### Q4 — MC — Measurement base for held-for-sale impairment
**LO:** LO 12-8  
**Concept:** Held-for-sale impairment uses lower of CA or FV less costs to sell (recoverable cost / undiscounted CF is irrelevant)  
**Question:**  
At a reporting date, **Solstice Components Corp.** has equipment **classified as held for sale** with a carrying amount of **$55,000**, estimated **undiscounted future net cash flows from use (recoverable cost) of $52,000**, fair value of **$50,000**, and estimated selling costs of **$1,500**. What impairment loss, if any, should Solstice recognize?

- A) $0  
- B) $3,000 loss  
- C) $5,000 loss  
- D) $6,500 loss  

**Answer:** **D.**  
For assets held for sale, measurement is the **lower of carrying amount or fair value less costs to sell**.  
FV − costs to sell = $50,000 − $1,500 = **$48,500**.  
Impairment = $55,000 − $48,500 = **$6,500**.  
The recoverability / undiscounted-cash-flow amount ($52,000) applies to **assets held and used**, not to held-for-sale assets.

### Q5 — MC — Classification, depreciation, and presentation
**LO:** LO 12-8  
**Concept:** Cease depreciation; measure at lower of CA or FV − costs to sell; report as other assets  
**Question:**  
Which statement correctly describes accounting for a long-lived asset that meets **all** held-for-sale criteria under ASC 360?

- A) Continue depreciating the asset over remaining useful life; measure at historical cost less accumulated depreciation only.  
- B) Cease depreciation; measure at the lower of carrying amount or fair value less costs to sell; report as an other (nonoperating) asset rather than PPE.  
- C) Cease depreciation; measure only at fair value, ignoring estimated costs to sell; report within PPE until disposal.  
- D) Apply the two-step held-and-used impairment test (recoverability then fair-value measurement); continue depreciation until sale.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Per ASC 360 / Demo 12-8: discontinue depreciation; report at lower of CA or FV less selling costs; present as other assets (not PPE) because the asset is no longer in use. Subsequent recoveries of prior write-downs are allowed but limited.
