# Agent 259 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-5

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Apply tax effects to changes in accounting principle and error corrections  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (change in principle, books ≠ tax):** cumulative effect **net of tax** to Retained Earnings; **Deferred Tax Asset** when book inventory falls below tax base; **Deferred Tax Liability** when book inventory rises above tax base
- **Initial recognition JE (LIFO conformity, books = tax):** LIFO change forces tax method change; tax effect is **Income Tax Payable** (repay prior LIFO savings), not deferred tax
- **Error correction JE (error on tax return, book = tax):** **Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment** net of tax; tax effect is **Income Tax Receivable** or **Payable** (cash taxes wrong)—no new temporary difference
- **Error correction JE (books wrong, tax correct):** reverse the **DTA/DTL** that arose from the temporary difference; PPA is pretax correction net of the deferred-tax reversal
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year inventory book-vs-tax temporary-difference rollforward; deferred-tax remeasurement after a principle change as layers turn; multi-year LIFO/FIFO inventory-gap build-up feeding the cumulative effect; multi-year pretax error accumulation and net-of-tax PPA schedule; RE rollforward (as reported → cumulative effect/PPA net of tax → adjusted)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** remeasure DTA/DTL for remaining inventory temporary difference; record PPA and related tax when error discovered after prior statements issued
- **Settlement JE:** collect Income Tax Receivable (error refund); remit Income Tax Payable arising from LIFO method change
- **Classification:** DTA/DTL vs current tax receivable/payable depends on whether book and tax bases still differ after the adjustment

### Q1 — CORE — FIFO→average with DTA; multi-year temporary-difference / DTA schedule (emphasis); capitalized-repair PPA; tax-refund settlement
**LO:** LO 21-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for FIFO→average-cost with DTA; subsequent measurement schedule of inventory temporary difference and DTA remeasurement; period-end DTA adjusting JE; multi-year repair-error PPA with tax receivable; settlement of tax refund  
**Scenario:**  
**Millcreek Outdoor Gear LLC** is a calendar-year merchandiser. The enacted income tax rate is **25%** for all years presented.

**Change in accounting principle (Parts a–d):**  
On **January 1, Year 4**, Millcreek voluntarily changes its inventory costing method from **FIFO to weighted-average** for **financial reporting only**. It continues to use **FIFO for tax purposes**. Management determines that if weighted-average had been applied in all prior periods, **December 31, Year 3 inventory** would have been **$64,000 lower** than the FIFO balance on the books.

**Beginning retained earnings, January 1, Year 4, as previously reported** (before the principle change): **$512,000**.

After the change, Millcreek tracks the remaining book–tax inventory temporary difference each year-end as inventory turns (book inventory remains lower than tax inventory until the difference fully reverses):

| Measurement date | Remaining deductible temporary difference (book inv. below tax inv.) |
|---|---:|
| January 1, Year 4 (immediately after change entry) | $64,000 |
| December 31, Year 4 | $40,000 |
| December 31, Year 5 | $16,000 |
| December 31, Year 6 | $0 |

Assume the enacted rate remains 25% and that Millcreek remeasures the related deferred tax asset at each year-end for the remaining temporary difference (no valuation allowance).

**Error correction (Parts e–f):**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, Millcreek paid **$36,000** cash for ordinary repairs and **erroneously debited Equipment**. Straight-line depreciation is **10% per year** with no residual value. The **same 10% rate is used for tax**, and the **error carried over to the tax return**. Millcreek discovers the error on **January 1, Year 3** (after Year 1 and Year 2 statements were issued).

**Settlement (Part g):**  
On **April 15, Year 3**, Millcreek receives a cash refund from the taxing authority equal to the full Income Tax Receivable recorded for the repair-error correction.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 4 initial recognition JE** for the FIFO → weighted-average change, including tax effects. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of the inventory temporary difference and deferred tax asset balance at each date in the table (Jan 1 Y4; Dec 31 Y4; Dec 31 Y5; Dec 31 Y6).  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting JEs** at **December 31, Year 4** and **December 31, Year 5** to remeasure the DTA for the decline in the temporary difference (debit Income Tax Expense; credit Deferred Tax Asset).  
d. Prepare the **retained earnings rollforward** for the beginning of Year 4 reflecting the retrospective principle change.  
e. Compute the **multi-year pretax income effect** of the repair error through January 1, Year 3, and prepare the **correcting JE** including tax effects.  
f. Prepare a one-line **error-effect schedule** (Year 1 pretax, Year 2 pretax, cumulative pretax, tax, net of tax).  
g. Prepare the **April 15, Year 3 settlement JE** when the tax refund cash is received.  
h. In 2–3 sentences, explain why the principle change uses a **Deferred Tax Asset** while the repair error uses **Income Tax Receivable**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — FIFO → weighted-average (January 1, Year 4)**

Pretax cumulative effect (inventory ↓ / cumulative COGS ↑) = **$64,000**  
Deferred tax asset = \( \$64{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$16{,}000} \)  
Net-of-tax debit to retained earnings = \( \$64{,}000 - \$16{,}000 = \mathbf{\$48{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 48,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 16,000 | |
| Inventory | | 64,000 |
| *Retrospective change FIFO → weighted-average; books only; tax still FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(48{,}000 + 16{,}000 = 64{,}000\) = Cr 64,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — inventory temporary difference and DTA (emphasis)**

| Measurement date | Remaining deductible TD (book inv. < tax inv.) | DTA @ 25% | Change in DTA (period) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 4 (after change entry) | $64,000 | **$16,000** | — (established) |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 40,000 | **10,000** | ↓ $6,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 5 | 16,000 | **4,000** | ↓ $6,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 6 | 0 | **$0** | ↓ $4,000 |

Book inventory lower than tax inventory → **deductible temporary difference** → **DTA**. As layers turn, book COGS exceeds tax COGS, pretax book income is lower relative to taxable income, and the DTA **reverses through current tax expense**.

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs — DTA remeasurement**

**December 31, Year 4** (DTA from $16,000 → $10,000):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 6,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |
| *Remeasure DTA for remaining deductible TD of $40,000 × 25%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 5** (DTA from $10,000 → $4,000):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 6,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |
| *Remeasure DTA for remaining deductible TD of $16,000 × 25%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**

*(December 31, Year 6 would reverse the remaining $4,000 DTA the same way: Dr Tax Expense 4,000 / Cr DTA 4,000.)*

**d. Retained earnings rollforward (beginning Year 4)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Retained earnings, Jan 1, Year 4, as previously reported | $512,000 |
| Cumulative effect of change in accounting principle (net of $16,000 tax benefit) | (48,000) |
| **Retained earnings, Jan 1, Year 4, as adjusted** | **$464,000** |

**e. Error correction — repairs capitalized (January 1, Year 3)**

Two years of depreciation have been recorded on the improperly capitalized amount (\(2 \times \$36{,}000 \times 10\% = \$7{,}200\)).

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Correct repair expense for Year 1 (never recorded as expense) | $36,000 |
| Depreciation incorrectly recorded in Year 1 ($36,000 × 10%) | (3,600) |
| **Year 1 net understatement of pretax expense (overstatement of pretax income)** | **$32,400** |
| Depreciation incorrectly recorded in Year 2 ($36,000 × 10%) — overstatement of Year 2 expense | (3,600) |
| **Cumulative pretax income overstatement at Jan 1, Year 3** | **$28,800** |
| Income tax receivable ($28,800 × 25%) | $7,200 |
| **Prior period adjustment, net of tax ($28,800 × 75%)** | **$21,600** |

Because the error also appeared on the tax return and book = tax measurement, cash taxes were overpaid → **Income Tax Receivable**, not deferred tax. No DTA/DTL to reverse (book and tax depreciation were identical under the error).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 7,200 | |
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | 21,600 | |
| Income Tax Receivable | 7,200 | |
| Equipment | | 36,000 |
| *Correct Years 1–2 capitalization of ordinary repairs; error on books and tax return* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(7{,}200 + 21{,}600 + 7{,}200 = 36{,}000\) = Cr 36,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Multi-year error-effect schedule (subsequent measurement of cumulative PPA)**

| Period | Pretax income effect of error (overstatement / (understatement)) | Tax @ 25% | Net-of-tax effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | +$32,400 | +$8,100 | +$24,300 |
| Year 2 | −$3,600 | −$900 | −$2,700 |
| **Cumulative at Jan 1, Year 3** | **+$28,800** | **+$7,200** | **+$21,600** |

PPA debits RE for the **cumulative net-of-tax overstatement** of **$21,600**; tax receivable is the cumulative **$7,200**.

**g. Settlement — collect tax refund (April 15, Year 3)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 7,200 | |
| Income Tax Receivable | | 7,200 |
| *Collect refund for overpaid taxes related to repair-error restatement* | | |

**Check:** Dr 7,200 = Cr 7,200. **Balanced.**

**h. Why DTA vs Income Tax Receivable**

- **Principle change (books ≠ tax):** After the entry, book inventory is on a different basis than tax inventory until layers reverse → **temporary difference** → **Deferred Tax Asset**.  
- **Repair error (books = tax, error on return):** Correcting both books and tax does **not** leave a temporary difference; cash taxes were wrong → **Income Tax Receivable** for the refund.

**Key insight:** Retrospective principle changes and error PPAs hit equity **net of tax**. The tax **account** is deferred tax when book and tax bases still differ after the adjustment, and current tax receivable/payable when cash taxes were simply wrong. Subsequent measurement of a books-only inventory change is a **temporary-difference / DTA rollforward** as inventory turns.

### Q2 — CORE number variant
**LO:** LO 21-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — average-cost→FIFO with DTL at 30%; multi-year temporary-difference / DTL subsequent measurement schedule; period-end DTL adjusting JE; multi-year depreciation understatement PPA with tax receivable; RE rollforward  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Merchandising Corp.** (calendar year-end). Enacted tax rate is **30%**.

**Principle change:** On **January 1, Year 5**, Riverbend changes from **weighted-average to FIFO** for **financial reporting only**; continues **weighted-average for tax**. If FIFO had always been used, Dec 31, Year 4 inventory would be **$90,000 higher**. Beginning RE as previously reported on Jan 1, Year 5: **$740,000**.

Remaining **taxable** temporary difference (book inventory above tax inventory) after the change:

| Measurement date | Remaining taxable temporary difference |
|---|---:|
| January 1, Year 5 (after change entry) | $90,000 |
| December 31, Year 5 | $54,000 |
| December 31, Year 6 | $18,000 |
| December 31, Year 7 | $0 |

**Error:** Depreciation expense was **understated by $12,000 in Year 2 and $12,000 in Year 3** (total pretax **$24,000**) for **both accounting and income tax purposes**. The error is discovered on **January 1, Year 4**.

**Required:**  
a. January 1, Year 5 JE for the average-cost → FIFO change (with tax).  
b. Subsequent measurement schedule of remaining taxable TD and DTL at each date.  
c. Period-end adjusting JE at **December 31, Year 5** to remeasure the DTL.  
d. Beginning RE rollforward for Year 5.  
e. January 1, Year 4 correcting JE for the multi-year depreciation understatement (with tax), including pretax computation.  
f. Briefly state how Year 4 comparative statements treat the Year 2–3 depreciation error.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Change average-cost → FIFO (January 1, Year 5)**

Pretax inventory increase = **$90,000**  
DTL = \( \$90{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$27{,}000} \)  
Net credit to RE = \( \$90{,}000 - \$27{,}000 = \mathbf{\$63{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 90,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 27,000 |
| Retained Earnings | | 63,000 |
| *Retrospective change average-cost → FIFO; books only; tax still average-cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 90,000 = Cr \(27{,}000 + 63{,}000\). **Balanced.**

Book inventory higher than tax inventory → **taxable temporary difference** → **DTL**.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — taxable TD and DTL (emphasis)**

| Measurement date | Remaining taxable TD (book inv. > tax inv.) | DTL @ 30% | Change in DTL (period) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 5 (after change entry) | $90,000 | **$27,000** | — (established) |
| Dec 31, Year 5 | 54,000 | **16,200** | ↓ $10,800 |
| Dec 31, Year 6 | 18,000 | **5,400** | ↓ $10,800 |
| Dec 31, Year 7 | 0 | **$0** | ↓ $5,400 |

As the difference reverses, book COGS is lower than tax COGS; pretax book income is higher relative to taxable income; the DTL reverses through current tax accounting.

**c. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 5**

DTL from $27,000 → $16,200; decrease **$10,800**:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Liability | 10,800 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 10,800 |
| *Remeasure DTL for remaining taxable TD of $54,000 × 30%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,800 = Cr 10,800. **Balanced.**

**d. RE rollforward**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| RE, Jan 1, Year 5, as previously reported | $740,000 |
| Cumulative effect of principle change (net of $27,000 tax) | 63,000 |
| **RE, Jan 1, Year 5, as adjusted** | **$803,000** |

**e. Multi-year depreciation understatement (January 1, Year 4)**

Pretax income overstatement total = \( \$12{,}000 + \$12{,}000 = \$24{,}000 \)  
Income tax receivable = \( \$24{,}000 \times 30\% = \mathbf{\$7{,}200} \)  
PPA net of tax = \( \$24{,}000 \times 70\% = \mathbf{\$16{,}800} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | 16,800 | |
| Income Tax Receivable | 7,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation | | 24,000 |
| *Correct understated depreciation Years 2–3; error on books and tax return* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(16{,}800 + 7{,}200 = 24{,}000\) = Cr 24,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Comparative presentation**  
Restate Year 2 and Year 3 comparative amounts (increase depreciation expense; correct tax expense/net income). Cumulative income effect for any period not presented adjusts beginning RE of the earliest year presented, **net of tax**. Disclose nature of the error and effects **gross and net of applicable income tax** (ASC 250-10-50-7 and 50-8).

**Key insight:** Scaling inventory gap and tax rate scales DTL and net RE proportionally; the structure remains Dr Inventory / Cr DTL / Cr RE when book basis rises above tax basis. Subsequent measurement is the **DTL rollforward** as the taxable temporary difference reverses.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — LIFO→FIFO with Income Tax Payable; multi-year inventory difference schedule; books-only error removing DTL; settlement of tax payable
**LO:** LO 21-5  
**Concept:** LIFO→FIFO initial recognition with Income Tax Payable (conformity); multi-year LIFO/FIFO inventory difference subsequent measurement schedule feeding cumulative effect; books-only prepaid error removing DTL; period-end PPA JE; settlement of LIFO-related tax payable  
**Scenario:**  
**Stoneharbor Industrial Supply Inc.** (calendar year-end). Tax rate **25%** unless noted.

**Part A — LIFO change with multi-year inventory schedule (conformity):**  
Stoneharbor has used **LIFO for both financial reporting and tax** since inception. On **January 1, Year 4**, it changes to **FIFO for both books and tax** (LIFO conformity rule). Taxes previously saved under LIFO must be repaid; treat the entire tax effect as **currently payable** for this problem (ignore IRS installment-period detail).

Inventory under each method at year-end (only data needed for the cumulative effect):

| Date | Inventory — LIFO | Inventory — FIFO | FIFO higher by |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $210,000 | $228,000 | $18,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 235,000 | 268,000 | 33,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 250,000 | 322,000 | **72,000** |

Beginning RE as reported Jan 1, Year 4 before the change: **$605,000**.

**Part B — Books wrong, tax correct (remove DTL):**  
Also early in Year 4, Stoneharbor discovers that **$40,000** of costs that should have been expensed in prior years for books were incorrectly left in **Prepaid Expense**. For **tax**, the costs were **correctly deducted when paid**. A deferred tax liability of \( \$40{,}000 \times 25\% = \$10{,}000 \) is on the books for this taxable temporary difference (book carrying amount of the prepaid exceeds tax base of zero). Prepare the correcting entry on January 1, Year 4.

**Part C — Settlement:**  
On **March 15, Year 4**, Stoneharbor remits the full Income Tax Payable arising from the LIFO → FIFO change in a single payment.

**Required:**  
a. Using the multi-year inventory schedule, identify the pretax cumulative effect at Jan 1, Year 4 and prepare the **initial recognition JE** for LIFO → FIFO including tax.  
b. Prepare the **subsequent measurement / build-up schedule** of the LIFO–FIFO inventory difference by year-end (show how the Year 3 cumulative gap of $72,000 accumulated).  
c. Prepare the **RE rollforward** for the LIFO change.  
d. Prepare the **correcting JE** for the prepaid/expense error removing the DTL (Part B).  
e. Prepare the **settlement JE** for payment of the LIFO-related Income Tax Payable.  
f. **Classification:** For (a) and (d), identify the tax balance-sheet account used and one-sentence rationale.

**Answer key:**  

**a. LIFO → FIFO, book and tax (January 1, Year 4)**

Pretax cumulative inventory increase (FIFO − LIFO at Dec 31, Year 3) = **$72,000**  
Income tax payable = \( \$72{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$18{,}000} \)  
Net credit to RE = \( \$72{,}000 - \$18{,}000 = \mathbf{\$54{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 72,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 18,000 |
| Retained Earnings | | 54,000 |
| *Leave LIFO; restate inventory to FIFO; repay prior LIFO tax savings* | | |

**Check:** Dr 72,000 = Cr \(18{,}000 + 54{,}000\). **Balanced.**

Not DTL: under LIFO conformity the tax method changes with books, so the tax is a **current** obligation to repay prior savings—not a temporary difference that will reverse under different future tax treatment of the same inventory base.

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — multi-year LIFO vs FIFO inventory gap (emphasis)**

| Year-end | LIFO inventory | FIFO inventory | Cumulative pretax effect if FIFO used (FIFO − LIFO) | Incremental pretax effect for the year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $210,000 | $228,000 | $18,000 | $18,000 |
| Year 2 | 235,000 | 268,000 | 33,000 | 15,000 |
| Year 3 | 250,000 | 322,000 | **72,000** | 39,000 |

Incremental Year 2 = \(33{,}000 - 18{,}000 = 15{,}000\); Year 3 = \(72{,}000 - 33{,}000 = 39{,}000\).  
The **Jan 1, Year 4 cumulative effect** uses the **ending Year 3 gap ($72,000)**; the schedule shows how that cumulative pretax amount was measured across prior periods (period-specific effects for retrospective restatement of comparative years).

**c. RE rollforward (LIFO change)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| RE, Jan 1, Year 4, as previously reported | $605,000 |
| Cumulative effect of change in accounting principle (net of $18,000 tax) | 54,000 |
| **RE, Jan 1, Year 4, as adjusted** | **$659,000** |

**d. Books-only prepaid error — remove DTL (January 1, Year 4)**

Book prepaid $40,000 with tax base $0 → **taxable temporary difference** → DTL $10,000 was recorded. Correcting the error eliminates both the overstated asset and the related DTL.

Pretax write-off of prepaid = **$40,000**  
Reverse DTL = **$10,000**  
Net debit to RE—PPA = \( \$40{,}000 - \$10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$30{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings—Prior Period Adjustment | 30,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | 10,000 | |
| Prepaid Expense | | 40,000 |
| *Expense costs previously left in prepaid; tax already correct — reverse related DTL* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(30{,}000 + 10{,}000 = 40{,}000\) = Cr 40,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Settlement — pay LIFO-related Income Tax Payable (March 15, Year 4)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Payable | 18,000 | |
| Cash | | 18,000 |
| *Settle tax obligation arising from change from LIFO to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 18,000 = Cr 18,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Tax account classification**

| Scenario | Tax account | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Leave LIFO (book = tax after change) | **Income Tax Payable** | Prior LIFO tax savings repaid; no ongoing book/tax inventory base difference from a method split |
| (d) Books-only prepaid error; tax correct | **Deferred Tax Liability** (removed) | Temporary difference is eliminated when the book asset is corrected; tax return already correct so no receivable/payable |

**Key insight:** Same “net of tax to RE” pattern appears with three different tax accounts—**Payable** (LIFO repay), **Receivable** (tax-return error), **DTA/DTL** (book ≠ tax temporary difference). Choosing the wrong tax account is a common exam trap. Settlement of the payable is a pure balance-sheet cash outflow (no additional RE hit).

### Q4 — MC (classification of tax effects)
**LO:** LO 21-5  
**Concept:** Classification — DTA for books-only inventory principle change; Income Tax Payable when error overstated expenses equally for book and tax  

**Question 1:**  
On January 1, Year 3, a company changes from FIFO to weighted-average for **financial reporting only** and continues FIFO for **tax**. Inventory would have been **$48,000 lower** under weighted-average. Tax rate 25%. The tax effect of the retrospective entry is recorded as:

- A) Debit Income Tax Payable $12,000  
- B) Debit Deferred Tax Asset $12,000  
- C) Credit Deferred Tax Liability $12,000  
- D) Debit Income Tax Receivable $12,000  

**Answer:** **B.** Book inventory falls $48,000 below tax inventory → deductible temporary difference \( 48{,}000 \times 25\% = \$12{,}000 \) → **Deferred Tax Asset**. Not payable/receivable (tax method unchanged). Not DTL (book base is lower, not higher).

**Question 2:**  
An error **overstated expenses** in a prior period by the **same amount for financial reporting and tax**. When the error is corrected in a subsequent period, the tax effect is recorded as:

- A) Deferred Tax Liability  
- B) Deferred Tax Asset  
- C) Income Tax Payable  
- D) Valuation allowance on a deferred tax asset  

**Answer:** **C.** Book and tax treatments were the same → **no temporary difference**. Overstated expenses understated taxable income → too little tax was paid → correcting increases taxes owed → **Income Tax Payable** (not deferred tax).
