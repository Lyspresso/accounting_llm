# Agent 193 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-6

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Describe the accounting treatment for changes in inventory methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Voluntary change in inventory costing method is a **change in accounting principle**
- **Retrospective application** (general rule, e.g., LIFO → FIFO, average → FIFO, FIFO → average): cumulative inventory difference at the start of the change year adjusts **Inventory** and **Retained Earnings** (ignore taxes here); comparative prior periods restated under the **new** method
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year inventory / net-income bridge under old vs new method; cumulative pretax effect = ending inventory (new) − ending inventory (old)
- **Period-end measurement** after the change uses the **new** method for COGS and ending inventory for the year of change and thereafter
- **Prospective application** when changing **to LIFO** (retrospective impracticable): **no** cumulative-effect JE; prior periods stay on the old method; beginning inventory of the change year becomes the **LIFO base layer**
- Comparative presentation after a prospective change mixes methods (reduces comparability)
- Direction of Inventory/RE adjustment depends on whether new-method EI > or < old-method EI at the change date

### Q1 — CORE — Meridian Forge Distributors: LIFO→FIFO retrospective JE, multi-year schedule, restated comparatives, Year-3 period-end COGS
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Retrospective change in inventory method — cumulative Inventory/RE entry, subsequent-measurement schedule, restated comparative NI and inventory, period-end COGS under new method  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Distributors Inc.** began operations January 1, Year 1 and elected **LIFO**. Its year-end is December 31. On **January 1, Year 3**, management voluntarily changes from **LIFO to FIFO** for financial reporting (preferable method; more decision-useful). Taxes are ignored. Comparative financial statements will present Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3.

Data available:

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income **reported under LIFO** | $96,000 | $128,000 | n/a (not used after change) |
| Net income **if under FIFO** | 108,000 | 145,000 | **$162,000** (computed under FIFO) |
| Ending inventory **under LIFO** | 210,000 | 235,000 | n/a |
| Ending inventory **under FIFO** | 222,000 | 264,000 | **290,000** |

Year 3 activity under the **new (FIFO)** method (periodic inventory system):

- Beginning inventory, January 1, Year 3 **after** the retrospective adjustment equals Year 2 ending inventory under FIFO  
- Purchases during Year 3: **$980,000**  
- Ending inventory, December 31, Year 3 under FIFO: **$290,000** (as above)

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **subsequent-measurement / cumulative-effect schedule** for December 31 of Year 1 and Year 2 showing inventory under LIFO, inventory under FIFO, annual pretax income difference (FIFO − LIFO), and cumulative pretax effect on equity.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 journal entry** to record the change in accounting principle (ignore taxes). Prove the amount.  
c. After the change, what **net income** amounts appear in the three-year comparative income statements? What **inventory** amounts appear on the three comparative balance sheets (December 31 of Year 1, Year 2, Year 3)?  
d. Compute Year 3 **cost of goods sold under FIFO** and prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end** periodic closing entry that recognizes ending inventory and COGS (balanced).  
e. Briefly state where any cumulative effect on periods **prior to the earliest year presented** would be reported (assume only Years 1–3 are presented and the company began in Year 1).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Subsequent-measurement / cumulative-effect schedule**

| Date | Inv. LIFO | Inv. FIFO | Annual NI (FIFO − LIFO) | Cumulative pretax effect (FIFO − LIFO inv.) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $210,000 | $222,000 | $108,000 − $96,000 = **$12,000** | **$12,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 235,000 | 264,000 | $145,000 − $128,000 = **$17,000** | $12,000 + $17,000 = **$29,000** |

**Proof of cumulative effect:** Ending inventory difference at Dec 31, Year 2 = $264,000 − $235,000 = **$29,000** = sum of annual pretax income differences. Inventory (new − old) at the change-date opening balance is the cumulative pretax adjustment to retained earnings.

**b. January 1, Year 3 — retrospective adjustment (initial recognition of the principle change)**

Cumulative effect = FIFO EI − LIFO EI at Dec 31, Year 2 = $264,000 − $235,000 = **$29,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 29,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 29,000 |
| *To retrospectively adjust inventory and beginning RE for change from LIFO to FIFO (ignore taxes)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$29,000**. Inventory after entry = $235,000 + $29,000 = **$264,000** (FIFO carrying amount).

**c. Comparative presentation after retrospective application (all periods under FIFO)**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Net income reported** | $108,000 | $145,000 | $162,000 |
| **Inventory, December 31** | $222,000 | $264,000 | $290,000 |

Prior periods are **restated** as if FIFO had always been used. Year 3 is measured under FIFO going forward.

**d. Year 3 period-end measurement under FIFO (periodic)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory (FIFO, Jan 1 Year 3) | $264,000 |
| + Purchases | 980,000 |
| Goods available for sale | $1,244,000 |
| − Ending inventory (FIFO) | 290,000 |
| **Cost of goods sold (FIFO)** | **$954,000** |

December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting/closing entry (periodic system):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 954,000 | |
| Inventory (ending) | 290,000 | |
| Inventory (beginning) | | 264,000 |
| Purchases | | 980,000 |
| *To close purchases and beginning inventory; establish ending inventory and COGS under FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 954,000 + 290,000 = **1,244,000**; Cr 264,000 + 980,000 = **1,244,000**. Balanced.

**e. Presentation of cumulative effect for periods before those presented**  
Because the company began operations in Year 1 and Years 1–3 are all presented and restated, **no additional opening RE catch-up** is needed beyond the January 1, Year 3 Inventory/RE entry (which itself puts the books on FIFO from that date). If comparative statements had omitted earlier periods still affected, the cumulative effect of those omitted periods would adjust the **beginning retained earnings of the earliest year presented**.

**Key insight:** A voluntary change **away from** LIFO (or between FIFO/average) is applied **retrospectively**: restate comparatives under the new method and book the cumulative inventory difference to **Inventory** and **Retained Earnings** at the start of the change year. After the change, subsequent measurement (COGS, EI) uses the new method only.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Northcliff Wholesale: LIFO→FIFO retrospective twin
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — recompute cumulative Inventory/RE adjustment, restated NI and inventory schedule, and Year-of-change COGS under FIFO  
**Scenario:**  
**Northcliff Wholesale LLC** used **LIFO** from inception (January 1, Year 1). On **January 1, Year 3**, it changes to **FIFO**. Year-end December 31. Ignore taxes. Comparative statements will show Year 1–Year 3.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income under **LIFO** | $55,000 | $70,000 | n/a |
| Net income under **FIFO** | 61,000 | 82,000 | **$95,000** |
| Ending inventory **LIFO** | 40,000 | 48,000 | n/a |
| Ending inventory **FIFO** | 46,000 | 66,000 | **75,000** |

Year 3 (FIFO, periodic): purchases **$420,000**; ending inventory FIFO **$75,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Verify that the cumulative pretax effect at December 31, Year 2 equals both the inventory difference and the sum of annual NI differences.  
b. Journal entry on January 1, Year 3 for the change (ignore taxes).  
c. Comparative net income and December 31 inventory amounts for Years 1–3 after the change.  
d. Year 3 COGS under FIFO and the period-end periodic close entry.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Verification**

| Check | Computation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| Inv. difference Dec 31 Y2 | $66,000 − $48,000 | **$18,000** |
| Annual NI diffs | ($61,000 − $55,000) + ($82,000 − $70,000) = $6,000 + $12,000 | **$18,000** |
| Dec 31 Y1 inv. diff | $46,000 − $40,000 | **$6,000** (= Y1 NI diff) |

Cumulative pretax effect to open Year 3 = **$18,000**.

**b. January 1, Year 3**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 18,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 18,000 |
| *Retrospective change LIFO → FIFO; cumulative EI difference $18,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 18,000. Books inventory becomes $48,000 + $18,000 = **$66,000**.

**c. Comparative amounts (all under FIFO after restatement)**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $61,000 | $82,000 | $95,000 |
| Inventory, Dec 31 | $46,000 | $66,000 | $75,000 |

**d. Year 3 COGS and period-end entry**

BI (FIFO) $66,000 + Purchases $420,000 − EI $75,000 = **COGS $411,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 411,000 | |
| Inventory (ending) | 75,000 | |
| Inventory (beginning) | | 66,000 |
| Purchases | | 420,000 |

**Check:** Dr 411,000 + 75,000 = 486,000; Cr 66,000 + 420,000 = 486,000. Balanced.

**Key insight:** Same retrospective mechanics as Q1 with different amounts—always prove cumulative effect two ways (inventory gap vs sum of income differences) before booking the Inventory/RE entry.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angles — Prospective FIFO→LIFO; average→FIFO with inventory decrease; “settlement” of cumulative effect into RE
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Prospective application when changing **to LIFO** (no JE; base-layer schedule); retrospective average→FIFO when FIFO EI is lower; presentation of cumulative effect  
**Scenario — Part A (prospective):**  
**Pinecrest Retail Group** reported inventory on the **FIFO** basis in Year 1 and Year 2. On **January 1, Year 3**, it changes to **LIFO**. Reconstructing prior LIFO layers is impracticable. Ignore taxes.

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $200,000 | $240,000 | $210,000 |
| Ending inventory | 150,000 | 180,000 | 165,000 |

**Scenario — Part B (retrospective, opposite direction):**  
**Oakridge Parts Co.** changes from **average cost to FIFO** on **January 1, Year 3**. Inventory balances:

| Inventory at December 31 | Year 2 | Year 1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average cost | $180,000 | $140,000 |
| FIFO | 150,000 | 120,000 |

**Required:**  
a. **Part A:** Prepare any January 1, Year 3 journal entry for the change to LIFO. If none, explain why.  
b. **Part A:** Prepare a **comparative subsequent-measurement schedule** of net income and inventory for Years 1–3 **after** the change, labeling the method used each year. Identify the **LIFO base-layer** amount.  
c. **Part B:** Prepare the January 1, Year 3 retrospective entry (ignore taxes).  
d. **Part B:** What inventory amount is reported for December 31, Year 2 on Year 3 comparative balance sheets? What amount, if any, adjusts beginning retained earnings of Year 2 for periods prior to Year 2 when Years 2 and 3 are presented comparatively?  
e. **Settlement / equity path:** For Part B, show the accounting-equation settlement of the cumulative effect (assets and equity).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Change to LIFO — prospective; no entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| *No journal entry* | — | — |

Retrospective reconstruction of LIFO layers for prior periods is **impracticable**. The change is applied **prospectively**. The accounting records are **not** adjusted at the change date.

**b. Comparative schedule after prospective change to LIFO**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Method reflected | **FIFO** | **FIFO** | **LIFO** |
| Net income reported | $200,000 | $240,000 | $210,000 |
| Inventory, December 31 | $150,000 | $180,000 | $165,000 |

**LIFO base layer (beginning inventory for LIFO going forward)** = Year 2 ending inventory under the former method = **$180,000**.  
Year 3 ending inventory under LIFO ($165,000) is measured by applying LIFO only to **Year 3** layers; the $180,000 opening balance is not restated.

Comparability across years is **reduced** because two methods appear in the same comparative set.

**c. Average cost → FIFO (retrospective); FIFO EI lower**

Cumulative effect at Jan 1, Year 3 = FIFO EI − Average EI at Dec 31, Year 2  
= $150,000 − $180,000 = **$(30,000)** (decrease inventory and RE).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings | 30,000 | |
| Inventory | | 30,000 |
| *Retrospective change average → FIFO; cumulative decrease in inventory $30,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 30,000. Inventory becomes $180,000 − $30,000 = **$150,000**.

**d. Comparative presentation (Years 2 and 3 presented)**

- December 31, Year 2 inventory (restated under FIFO): **$150,000**  
- Cumulative effect on periods **prior to Year 2** (i.e., through Dec 31, Year 1): FIFO − Average at Dec 31, Year 1 = $120,000 − $140,000 = **$(20,000)**  
  → Reported as a **$20,000 decrease** to **beginning retained earnings of Year 2** (the earliest year presented), so Year 2’s restated rollforward of RE is correct.  
- The full $30,000 books adjustment at Jan 1, Year 3 is still required on the accounting records; for **reporting**, Year 2 is restated and the pre-Year-2 slice of the cumulative effect hits opening RE of Year 2.

**e. Accounting-equation settlement of Part B cumulative effect**

| | Assets | = | Liabilities | + | Equity |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 3 cumulative effect | Inventory **−30,000** | | 0 | | Retained Earnings **−30,000** |

Assets and equity both decrease by $30,000; the “settlement” of the principle change is entirely through **beginning equity**, not current-period income.

**Key insight:** **To LIFO → prospective, no JE, base layer = old-method beginning inventory.** Other voluntary inventory-method changes → **retrospective** Inventory ↔ RE, with the **sign** set by whether new-method inventory is higher or lower. Prior periods presented are restated; earlier omitted periods adjust opening RE of the earliest year shown.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method application)
**LO:** LO 10-6  
**Concept:** Classify retrospective vs prospective application and compute equity effect of inventory-method change  

**Q4A.**  
On January 1, Year 3, **Blue Harbor Foods** changes its inventory method from **FIFO to LIFO**. Reconstructing LIFO layers for prior years is impracticable. Ending inventory at December 31, Year 2 was $90,000 under FIFO. Ending inventory at December 31, Year 3 is $84,000 under LIFO. Which statement is correct?

- A) Debit Inventory and credit Retained Earnings for $6,000 on January 1, Year 3.  
- B) Debit Retained Earnings and credit Inventory for $6,000 on January 1, Year 3.  
- C) **No journal entry is recorded on January 1, Year 3; the $90,000 FIFO balance becomes the LIFO base layer.**  
- D) Restate Year 1 and Year 2 inventory and net income as if LIFO had always been used.

**Answer:** **C.** A change **to LIFO** is applied **prospectively** when retrospective application is impracticable. No cumulative-effect entry is made; beginning inventory of the change year (here the Dec 31, Year 2 FIFO amount of $90,000) is the LIFO base. Prior periods are **not** restated onto LIFO.

**Q4B.**  
**Canterbury Components** changed from **LIFO to FIFO** on January 1, Year 3. Inventory data:

| | Dec 31, Year 1 | Dec 31, Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| LIFO | $52,000 | $58,000 |
| FIFO | 71,000 | 79,000 |

Ignoring taxes, what is the effect on **equity** of the journal entry recorded January 1, Year 3?

- A) $19,000 increase  
- B) $21,000 increase  
- C) $21,000 decrease  
- D) $27,000 increase  

**Answer:** **B. $21,000 increase.**  
Cumulative effect = FIFO − LIFO at Dec 31, Year 2 = $79,000 − $58,000 = **$21,000**.  
Entry: Dr Inventory 21,000 / Cr Retained Earnings 21,000 → equity **increases** $21,000.  
(Year 1 difference $19,000 is only the portion through Year 1, not the full cumulative entry at the change date.)
