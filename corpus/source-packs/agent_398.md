# Agent 398 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-4

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** equity purchase at price **plus brokerage/commissions** capitalized in Investment cost; ownership <20% guideline with **insignificant influence** → **FV-NI** (not equity method)
- **Dividend revenue:** dividends declared/paid → **Dividend Revenue** in net income (return *on* investment); Investment account is **not** reduced
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** Cost vs fair value; required FVA; existing FVA; adjusting amount (required − existing)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities ↔ Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** (FV-NI)
- **Disposal / sale JE (emphasis):** **first** adjust FVA to sale-date fair value through NI; **then** remove Investment at cost and eliminate FVA against cash—**no separate realized G/L** on the sale JE once FV-adjusted
- **Holding-period income rollforward:** period unrealized G/L in NI sum to (sale proceeds − cost) over ownership
- **Partial disposal:** pro-rate cost of shares sold; clear only FVA tied to shares sold; remaining shares get separate YE FVA
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** current vs noncurrent by intent/ability to sell within one year; investment at FV on BS; dividend revenue and unrealized holding G/L in **net income** (not OCI); disclose portion of unrealized G/L relating to equity securities **still held** at reporting date; SCF investing vs operating by purpose
- **Number-variant twin:** same FV-NI path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Equity FV-NI full life cycle with disposal emphasis (purchase → dividend → YE FVA → sale-date FV-NI → sale)
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE; subsequent_measurement_schedule; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **March 12 of Year 1**, **Meridian Capital Partners** purchased **6,000** of **Oakridge Systems Inc.**’s **80,000** outstanding common shares for **$28 per share**. Brokerage commissions paid were **$1,500**. Meridian’s 7.5% interest does **not** give it significant influence over Oakridge. Fair value is readily determinable. Meridian measures the investment at **FV-NI** and has a December 31 year-end. Beginning Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities is **zero**. No other equity investment activity except as stated.

| Date | Event / fair value |
|---|---|
| Mar 12, Year 1 | Purchase 6,000 shares @ $28 + $1,500 commissions |
| Sep 1, Year 1 | Oakridge declares and pays total dividends of **$120,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value of Oakridge stock = **$31 per share** |
| Mar 20, Year 2 | Entire position sold for **$178,200** cash |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **purchase** on March 12 of Year 1.  
(b) Record Meridian’s share of the **dividend** on September 1 of Year 1.  
(c) Prepare the **subsequent measurement (FVA) schedule** and record the **period-end fair value adjustment** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(d) Report the investment on the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet** and the related **Year 1 income statement** effects (dividend revenue and unrealized holding G/L).  
(e) On March 20 of Year 2, sell the entire position for $178,200: **(i)** adjust to fair value at the sale date using **FV-NI**; **(ii)** record the sale, eliminating the associated Fair Value Adjustment balance. Show that Dr = Cr on both entries.  
(f) Prepare a **holding-period income schedule** for unrealized G/L in net income and prove the cumulative amount equals sale proceeds minus original cost.  
(g) Briefly state **balance sheet classification** logic, income-statement placement of unrealized G/L, ASC 321 disclosure for securities still held, and SCF classification.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) March 12, Year 1 — Initial recognition**  

Cost = (6,000 × $28) + $1,500 = $168,000 + $1,500 = **$169,500**  
Ownership = 6,000 / 80,000 = **7.5%** → insignificant influence → **FV-NI** (not equity method).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Oakridge Stock | 169,500 | |
| Cash | | 169,500 |
| *Purchase equity securities; capitalize commissions in cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 169,500 = Cr 169,500. **Balanced.**

**(b) September 1, Year 1 — Dividend revenue (insignificant influence)**  

Meridian’s share = $120,000 × 7.5% = **$9,000**  
Under FV-NI, dividends are **revenue**, not a reduction of the Investment account.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 9,000 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 9,000 |
| *Dividend revenue in net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Subsequent measurement schedule and period-end FV-NI adjust**  

Fair value YE = 6,000 × $31 = **$186,000**

**Subsequent measurement schedule (Year 1 YE)**

| Equity Investment | Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Oakridge common stock | $169,500 | $186,000 | $16,500 |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$16,500** |

Required FVA = FV − Cost = $186,000 − $169,500 = **$16,500 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$0**  
Adjustment = **debit FVA $16,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 16,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 16,500 |
| *Adjust equity investment to fair value through net income (FV-NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 16,500. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount on BS = cost $169,500 + FVA debit $16,500 = **$186,000**.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**  

**Balance sheet excerpt — December 31, Year 1**  
Assets (current or noncurrent depending on intent to sell within one year/operating cycle)  
 Investment in equity securities ……………… **$186,000**  
(presentation = cost $169,500 + FVA debit $16,500)

**Income statement excerpt — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
Other revenues (expenses)  
 Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$9,000**  
 Unrealized holding gain on equity securities … **$16,500**

**(e) March 20, Year 2 — Disposal under FV-NI (emphasis)**  

**(i) Sale-date fair value adjustment (FV-NI) — required before the sale JE**  

Required FVA at sale* = $178,200 − $169,500 = **$8,700 debit**  
Less existing FVA = **16,500 debit**  
Decrease needed = **$(7,800)**

\*$178,200 sale price/fair value − $169,500 original cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 7,800 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 7,800 |
| *Bring carrying amount to sale-date fair value through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,800. **Balanced.** FVA now = **$8,700 debit**.

**(ii) Record sale — eliminate FVA; no separate gain/loss on sale**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 178,200 | |
| Investment in Oakridge Stock | | 169,500 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 8,700 |
| *Remove investment at cost; clear FVA; cash = fair value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 178,200 = Cr 169,500 + 8,700. **Balanced.**  
No Gain/Loss on Sale account is used: net income already reflects cost-to-sale fair value changes through the FV-NI adjustments (period-end and sale-date).

**(f) Holding-period income schedule (unrealized G/L in NI)**  

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in net income | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 | $16,500 | YE FV $186,000 |
| Year 2 (sale date) | (7,800) | Sale FV $178,200 |
| **Cumulative over ownership** | **$8,700** | Equals $178,200 − $169,500 |

**Proof:** 16,500 − 7,800 = **8,700**. Total economic gain recognized in NI over the holding period = sale proceeds − cost. (Dividend revenue of $9,000 is separate return *on* investment and is not part of this FV rollforward.)

**(g) Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
- **BS:** Classify as **current** if management intends and is able to sell within one year (or operating cycle if longer); otherwise **noncurrent**. Report at **fair value**.  
- **IS:** Dividend revenue and unrealized holding gains/losses are in **net income** (other income/expense)—**not** OCI.  
- **Disclosure:** For each period presented, disclose the portion of unrealized gains and losses that relates to equity securities **still held** at the reporting date (ASC 321-10-50-4).  
- **SCF:** Purchases/sales of equity securities are **investing** unless the company actively trades them for short-term profits (then **operating**).

**Key insight:** Under **equity FV-NI**, disposal is a two-step sequence: **(1)** roll FVA to sale-date fair value through **Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income**, then **(2)** debit Cash, credit Investment at **cost**, and eliminate the **FVA** balance. Cumulative unrealized G/L in NI equals proceeds − cost; no separate “realized gain on sale” is booked after the sale-date FV adjust.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same equity FV-NI path with all new amounts (including disposal sequence)  
**Scenario:**  
On **October 1 of Year 1**, **Silverpine Investments LLC** purchased **2,000** shares of **Harborlight Media Corp.** common stock for **$45 per share**. Transfer costs and brokerage totaled **$600**. Silverpine has **insignificant influence** over Harborlight; fair value is readily determinable. Measure at **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA—Equity Securities is zero.

| Date | Fair value / activity |
|---|---|
| Nov 20, Year 1 | Harborlight declares and pays a cash dividend of **$1.80 per share** on shares held by Silverpine |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$42 per share** |
| Jan 18, Year 2 | Entire holding sold for **$93,150** cash |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on October 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Dividend entry on November 20 of Year 1.  
(c) Fair value adjustment on December 31 of Year 1 (include FVA schedule).  
(d) Year 1 BS / IS presentation for this investment.  
(e) January 18, Year 2 sale: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust; (ii) sale eliminating FVA.  
(f) Holding-period unrealized G/L schedule proving cumulative NI (FV only) = proceeds − cost.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) October 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  

Cost = (2,000 × $45) + $600 = $90,000 + $600 = **$90,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Harborlight Stock | 90,600 | |
| Cash | | 90,600 |
| *Purchase equity securities including incidental costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 90,600. **Balanced.**

**(b) November 20, Year 1 — Dividend revenue**  

Dividend = 2,000 × $1.80 = **$3,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,600 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 3,600 |
| *Dividend revenue under FV-NI (not a reduction of Investment)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,600. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end FVA**  

FV YE = 2,000 × $42 = **$84,000**

| Equity Investment | Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Harborlight common | $90,600 | $84,000 | $(6,600)$ |
| Existing FVA | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$(6,600)$** |

Required FVA = **$6,600 credit** (FV < cost)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 6,600 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 6,600 |
| *FV-NI period-end loss through net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,600. **Balanced.**  
BS carrying amount = $90,600 − $6,600 FVA credit = **$84,000**.

**(d) Year 1 presentation**  

**Balance sheet — December 31, Year 1**  
 Investment in equity securities ……………… **$84,000**

**Income statement — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
 Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$3,600**  
 Unrealized holding loss on equity securities … **$(6,600)**

**(e) January 18, Year 2 — Disposal under FV-NI**  

**(i) Sale-date FV adjust**  

Required FVA at sale* = $93,150 − $90,600 = **$2,550 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$6,600 credit**  
Increase (decrease) to FVA needed = (+2,550) − (−6,600) = **+$9,150** (move from Cr 6,600 to Dr 2,550)

\*$93,150 fair value − $90,600 cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 9,150 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 9,150 |
| *Roll FVA from $6,600 Cr to $2,550 Dr through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,150. **Balanced.** Ending FVA = **$2,550 debit**.

**(ii) Sale — eliminate FVA**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 93,150 | |
| Investment in Harborlight Stock | | 90,600 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 2,550 |
| *Remove investment at cost; clear FVA; cash = fair value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 93,150 = Cr 90,600 + 2,550. **Balanced.**

**(f) Holding-period unrealized G/L schedule**  

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in NI | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 | $(6,600)$ | YE FV $84,000 |
| Year 2 (sale date) | 9,150 | Sale FV $93,150 |
| **Cumulative** | **$2,550** | Equals $93,150 − $90,600 |

**Proof:** −6,600 + 9,150 = **2,550**. Dividend revenue of $3,600 is separate.

**Key insight:** Even when Year 1 ends in an unrealized **loss**, the sale-date FV-NI step still runs first; cumulative FV-related NI over ownership equals proceeds − cost ($2,550). The sale JE never posts a separate “realized gain” after FVA has been rolled to sale-date fair value.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial disposal mid-year + remaining YE FVA and still-held disclosure
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE (partial sale); period_end_adjusting_JE (remaining shares); subsequent_measurement_schedule; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **June 1 of Year 1**, **Westfield Advisors Inc.** purchased **5,000** shares of **PrismTech Corp.** common stock for **$250,000** cash (price includes all incidental costs). Westfield has **insignificant influence**; fair value is readily determinable. Measure at **FV-NI**. December 31 year-end. Beginning FVA—Equity Securities is **zero**.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Aug 15, Year 1 | Sold **1,500** shares for **$58 per share** cash |
| Dec 10, Year 1 | PrismTech declares and pays a cash dividend of **$2.40 per share** on shares still held by Westfield |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value of the **remaining** investment = **$192,500** |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on June 1 of Year 1 (compute cost per share).  
(b) **Disposal of 1,500 shares** on August 15: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust for the shares sold; (ii) sale JE eliminating the related FVA.  
(c) Dividend entry on December 10 of Year 1.  
(d) Subsequent measurement schedule and period-end FVA entry on December 31 of Year 1 for **shares still held**.  
(e) Year 1 income-statement effects (dividend + unrealized G/L) and balance-sheet carrying amount of the remaining investment.  
(f) Of Year 1 unrealized gains/losses recognized in NI, what portion relates to equity securities **still held** at December 31 (ASC 321-10-50-4)?

**Answer key:**  

**(a) June 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  

Cost = **$250,000**  
Cost per share = $250,000 / 5,000 = **$50**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in PrismTech Stock | 250,000 | |
| Cash | | 250,000 |
| *Purchase equity securities measured at FV-NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 250,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) August 15, Year 1 — Partial disposal (emphasis)**  

Shares sold = 1,500  
Proceeds = 1,500 × $58 = **$87,000**  
Cost of shares sold = 1,500 × $50 = **$75,000**

**(i) Sale-date FV-NI adjust (shares sold only)**  

Required FVA for shares sold* = $87,000 − $75,000 = **$12,000 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$0**  
Adjustment = **debit FVA $12,000**

\*$87,000 fair value of shares sold − $75,000 allocated cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 12,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 12,000 |
| *Adjust sold shares to sale-date fair value through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,000. **Balanced.**

**(ii) Sale — remove cost of shares sold; eliminate related FVA**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 87,000 | |
| Investment in PrismTech Stock | | 75,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 12,000 |
| *Partial disposal: cash = FV; clear cost and FVA of shares sold* | | |

**Check:** Dr 87,000 = Cr 75,000 + 12,000. **Balanced.**  
Investment cost remaining = $250,000 − $75,000 = **$175,000** (3,500 shares × $50).  
FVA balance after sale = **$0**.

**(c) December 10, Year 1 — Dividend on remaining shares**  

Remaining shares = 5,000 − 1,500 = **3,500**  
Dividend = 3,500 × $2.40 = **$8,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,400 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 8,400 |
| *Dividend revenue under FV-NI on shares still held* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,400. **Balanced.**

**(d) December 31, Year 1 — YE FVA on remaining shares**  

| Equity Investment (remaining) | Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| PrismTech common (3,500 sh.) | $175,000 | $192,500 | $17,500 |
| Existing FVA | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$17,500** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 17,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 17,500 |
| *Period-end FV-NI adjust for equity securities still held* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 17,500. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = $175,000 + $17,500 = **$192,500**.

**(e) Year 1 presentation**  

**Income statement — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
 Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$8,400**  
 Unrealized holding gain on equity securities … **$29,500**  
  (= $12,000 on shares sold + $17,500 on shares held)

**Balance sheet — December 31, Year 1**  
 Investment in equity securities ……………… **$192,500**

**(f) Still-held disclosure (ASC 321-10-50-4)**  

Of the **$29,500** unrealized holding gain recognized in Year 1 net income, the portion relating to equity securities **still held** at December 31 is **$17,500**. The remaining **$12,000** relates to securities disposed of during the period (already realized through the sale-date FV-NI step and sale).

**Key insight:** On a **partial disposal**, pro-rate **cost** of shares sold, run the **sale-date FV-NI adjust only for those shares**, clear their FVA with the sale JE, then measure **remaining** shares to YE fair value with a separate FVA entry. Disclose how much of period unrealized G/L relates to securities still held.

### Q4 — MC — Placement of unrealized holding G/L (equity FV-NI)
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — unrealized holding G/L on equity FV-NI reported in net income  
**Question:**  
An investor holds common stock with **insignificant influence** and a **readily determinable** fair value. The investment is measured at FV-NI. At year-end, fair value exceeds cost. Where is the unrealized holding gain reported?

- A) Other comprehensive income (OCI), then reclassified to net income only when sold  
- B) Net income as unrealized holding gain (via Fair Value Adjustment)  
- C) Directly in retained earnings, bypassing comprehensive income  
- D) As a reduction of the Investment account with no income-statement effect until sale  

**Answer:** **B.** Under ASC 321 / LO 14-4, equity securities with insignificant influence and readily determinable fair value are measured at fair value with unrealized holding gains and losses included in **earnings** (FV-NI)—not OCI. A valuation account (FVA) adjusts carrying amount; Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** hits the income statement. (A describes AFS debt FV-OCI, not equity FV-NI.)

### Q5 — MC — Disposal sequence under equity FV-NI
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE — sale-date FV adjust then eliminate FVA (no separate sale G/L)  
**Question:**  
On the date an equity security measured at FV-NI is sold for cash equal to current fair value, which sequence is correct under the textbook (sale-date adjust) approach?

- A) Debit Cash and credit Investment at original cost only; recognize the entire (proceeds − cost) as Gain on Sale in one entry, ignoring any existing FVA  
- B) First adjust Fair Value Adjustment to the required balance based on sale-date fair value through Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income; then debit Cash, credit Investment at cost, and eliminate the FVA balance (no separate Gain on Sale after that adjust)  
- C) Reclassify the existing FVA balance from AOCI to net income, then remove the investment at fair value  
- D) Write the Investment account directly to fair value with an offset to Dividend Revenue, then record cash equal to that amount  

**Answer:** **B.** Sale of equity securities measured at FV-NI: **first** adjust the investment to fair value through NI (update FVA), **then** record the sale by removing the Investment at cost and eliminating the associated FVA against cash. After the sale-date FV adjust, the sale JE does **not** post a separate realized gain—the holding-period NI already captures (proceeds − cost). (A skips FVA; C confuses AFS debt OCI reclassification; D misuses dividend accounts.)
