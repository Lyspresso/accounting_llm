# Agent 216 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-9

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Describe property, plant, and equipment disclosures and ratio analyses  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize invoice + freight + installation (and similar ready-for-use costs) into PPE
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year straight-line cost / accumulated depreciation / book value rollforward; AD-to-gross-cost life-expired indicator
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Depreciation Expense → Accumulated Depreciation
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** update depreciation through disposal date; remove cost and AD; recognize gain or loss
- **ASC 360-10-50-1 required disclosures:** depreciation expense; balances of major classes of depreciable assets (by nature or function); accumulated depreciation (by class or in total); description of depreciation methods by major class
- **Commonly expected note items:** fully depreciated assets still in service; significant liens disclosed with **liabilities** (not as an asset offset); postacquisition cost policy
- **Ratio analysis:** **Asset turnover** = Net sales ÷ Average total assets; **Return on assets** = Net income ÷ Average total assets; **Fixed asset turnover** = Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets (PPE net)
- **Depreciation method and asset age vs ROA:** ROA tends to rise as plant assets age (numerator reduced by one year’s dep; denominator reduced by cumulative dep); when assets are new, SL often yields higher ROA than accelerated methods; relation can reverse as assets age

**d. ROA and depreciation method over asset age**

| | Year 1 SL | Year 1 Accel. | Year 5 SL | Year 5 Accel. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NI (before dep − dep) | $280,000 | $232,000 | $280,000 | $328,000 |
| Avg total assets | 1,600,000 | 1,504,000 | 1,120,000 | 1,024,000 |
| **ROA** | **0.18** | **0.15** | **0.25** | **0.32** |

(Computations: Y1 SL 280/1,600 = 0.175 → **0.18**; Y1 Accel 232/1,504 ≈ 0.154 → **0.15**; Y5 SL 280/1,120 = 0.25; Y5 Accel 328/1,024 ≈ 0.320 → **0.32**.)

**Why ROA rises as assets age:** Numerator falls by only **one year’s** depreciation, while the denominator is reduced by **cumulative** depreciation to date (lower average assets) → ratio climbs over time regardless of method.  
**Method effect:** When assets are **new**, **straight-line** produces **higher** early earnings (and often higher ROA) than accelerated methods (0.18 vs 0.15). As assets **age**, the relation **reverses** — accelerated methods have lower remaining depreciation, so later-year ROA can exceed SL (0.32 vs 0.25). Analysts comparing firms must consider **asset age mix** and **method choice**, not only the raw ratio.

| Asset turnover | $4,200,000 ÷ $3,200,000 | **1.31** |
| Return on assets | $352,000 ÷ $3,200,000 | **0.11** |
| Fixed asset turnover | $4,200,000 ÷ $1,623,000 | **2.59** |

**Key insight:** LO 12-9 is as much about **notes and ratio interpretation** as about the journal entries that feed those numbers. A **subsequent measurement rollforward** (beg + additions − disposals + dep) supports both the disclosure schedule and AD/cost analysis; disposal losses are IS items; liens stay with liabilities; method-vs-age effects stop naive cross-company ROA comparisons.

### Q4 — MC — Required PPE disclosure under ASC 360
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification of required depreciation and PPE disclosures (ASC 360-10-50-1)  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **required** to be disclosed for each reporting period related to depreciation and depreciable assets under ASC 360-10-50-1?

- A) Fair value of each major class of plant assets  
- B) Balances of major classes of depreciable assets, by nature or function  
- C) Budgeted capital expenditures for the next five years  
- D) Replacement cost of fully depreciated assets still in service  

**Answer:** **B.** ASC 360-10-50-1 requires (1) depreciation expense, (2) balances of major classes of depreciable assets by nature or function, (3) accumulated depreciation by asset or in total, and (4) a description of depreciation methods by major class. Fair value of each PPE class, multi-year capex budgets, and replacement cost of fully depreciated assets are **not** among the required ASC 360-10-50-1 items (fully depreciated assets still in service are often disclosed at **historical cost**, not replacement cost, as useful user information).

### Q4b — MC — Ratio definitions (optional second MC)
**LO:** LO 12-9  
**Concept:** Classification of PPE-related activity and profitability ratios  
**Question:**  
**Fixed asset turnover** is best described as:

- A) Net income ÷ Average total assets  
- B) Net sales ÷ Average total assets  
- C) Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets (PPE net)  
- D) Average fixed assets ÷ Net sales  

**Answer:** **C.** Fixed asset turnover = **Net sales ÷ Average fixed assets**, an activity ratio measuring effectiveness of PPE. Asset turnover uses average **total** assets (B). Return on assets uses **net income** over average total assets (A). D is the reciprocal and is not the standard definition.
