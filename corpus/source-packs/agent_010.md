# Agent 10 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-9

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of equity securities (cost includes commissions/brokerage) under insignificant influence
- Dividend revenue (not equity-method share of earnings)
- Period-end-only fair value adjustment via Fair Value Adjustment (FVA) → Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income
- Subsequent measurement schedule (cost vs fair value vs FVA needed)
- Disposal: realized gain/loss = cash proceeds − original cost (no FVA entry at sale date)
- Year-end FVA clean-up after full or partial disposal
- Balance sheet / income statement presentation of equity securities at FV-NI

### Q1 — CORE — Full life cycle (purchase → dividend → period-end FVA → sale → FVA elimination)
**LO:** LO 14-9  
**Concept:** Equity securities FV-NI with fair value adjustments only at period-end (initial recognition emphasis)  
**Scenario:**  
Mariner Co. has a December 31 year-end. On August 1, Year 1, Mariner purchased 4,000 shares of Cedar Ltd. common stock for $35 per share and paid commissions of $800. Cedar has 40,000 shares outstanding; Mariner has insignificant influence and measures the investment at FV-NI. Mariner adjusts fair value **only at period-end** (Appendix 14C / LO 14-9 approach).

Additional facts:
- November 20, Year 1: Cedar declares and pays total dividends of $24,000.
- December 31, Year 1: Cedar’s fair value is $38 per share.
- March 10, Year 2: Mariner sells the entire investment for $156,200 cash.
- December 31, Year 2: Mariner holds no equity securities measured at FV-NI.

**Required:**  
a. Record the purchase on August 1, Year 1.  
b. Record the dividend on November 20, Year 1.  
c. Prepare a year-end measurement schedule and record the period-end fair value adjusting entry on December 31, Year 1.  
d. Show the December 31, Year 1 balance sheet presentation of the investment and the Year 1 income-statement effects.  
e. Record the sale on March 10, Year 2 (gain/loss = cash − original cost; do **not** adjust FVA at the sale date).  
f. Record the December 31, Year 2 entry to adjust/eliminate the FVA account.  
g. Reconcile total gain recognized over the ownership period.

**Answer key:**  

**a. August 1, Year 1 — Purchase (cost includes commissions)**  
Investment cost = (4,000 × $35) + $800 = $140,000 + $800 = **$140,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Cedar Stock | 140,800 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 140,800 |

Dr 140,800 = Cr 140,800 ✓

**b. November 20, Year 1 — Dividend revenue**  
Ownership % = 4,000 / 40,000 = 10%  
Dividend revenue = $24,000 × 10% = **$2,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,400 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dividend Revenue | | 2,400 |

Dr 2,400 = Cr 2,400 ✓

**c. December 31, Year 1 — Subsequent measurement schedule and period-end FVA**

| Investment | Cost | Fair value (12/31/Y1) | Unrealized holding gain (loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cedar common stock | $140,800 | $152,000* | $11,200 |
| Existing FVA balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) needed in FVA** | | | **$11,200** |

\* 4,000 × $38 = $152,000

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 11,200 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 11,200 |

Dr 11,200 = Cr 11,200 ✓

**d. Financial statement presentation — December 31, Year 1**

Balance sheet (assets):  
Investment in equity securities ……………… **$152,000**  
(cost $140,800 + FVA debit $11,200)

Income statement (Year 1 other income):  
Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$2,400**  
Unrealized holding gain on equity securities … **$11,200**

**e. March 10, Year 2 — Sale (period-end-only method: no FVA at sale date)**  
Gain on sale = cash proceeds − original cost = $156,200 − $140,800 = **$15,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 156,200 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in Cedar Stock | | 140,800 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Gain on Sale of Investment | | 15,400 |

Dr 156,200 = Cr 156,200 ✓  
(FVA is **not** touched on the sale date under LO 14-9.)

**f. December 31, Year 2 — Eliminate FVA (no equity securities remain)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 11,200 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 11,200 |

Dr 11,200 = Cr 11,200 ✓

**g. Ownership-period gain reconciliation**

| Period | Amount in net income |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 unrealized holding gain | $11,200 |
| Year 2 realized gain on sale | 15,400 |
| Year 2 reverse prior FVA (debit to income) | (11,200) |
| **Net Year 2 effect** | **4,200** |
| **Total gain over ownership** | **$15,400** |

Check: cash $156,200 − cost $140,800 = **$15,400** ✓  
(Y1 $11,200 + Y2 $4,200 = $15,400)

**Key insight:** Under LO 14-9, FVA is adjusted **only at period-end**. On sale, realized gain/loss is always **proceeds − original cost**. Prior-period unrealized amounts remaining in FVA are cleaned up at the next period-end so total earnings over the holding period still equal economic gain/loss.

### Q2 — CORE number variant
**LO:** LO 14-9  
**Concept:** Equity securities FV-NI period-end-only (number-variant twin; unrealized loss then partial recovery on sale)  
**Scenario:**  
Harbor Partners has a December 31 year-end. On April 1, Year 1, Harbor purchased 6,000 shares of Pinewood Corp. common stock for $18 per share and paid brokerage of $1,200. Pinewood has 75,000 shares outstanding (Harbor owns 8%). Harbor has insignificant influence and uses FV-NI with fair value adjustments **only at period-end**.

- June 30, Year 1: Pinewood declares and pays total dividends of $15,000.
- December 31, Year 1: fair value of Pinewood is $16.50 per share.
- February 15, Year 2: Harbor sells the entire holding for $103,500 cash.
- December 31, Year 2: Harbor holds no equity securities measured at FV-NI.

**Required:**  
a. Purchase entry (April 1, Year 1).  
b. Dividend entry (June 30, Year 1).  
c. Year-end measurement schedule and FVA adjusting entry (December 31, Year 1).  
d. Sale entry (February 15, Year 2) — proceeds vs original cost; no FVA at sale.  
e. Year-end FVA elimination (December 31, Year 2).  
f. Reconcile total gain (loss) over the ownership period.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial disposal + remaining portfolio FVA schedule
**LO:** LO 14-9  
**Concept:** Partial sale at cost with year-end FVA only for securities still held  
**Scenario:**  
Ridgeway Inc. (December 31 year-end) purchased 8,000 shares of Summit Co. common stock on January 15, Year 1 for $50 per share plus brokerage of $1,600. Summit has 100,000 shares outstanding; Ridgeway has insignificant influence and uses FV-NI with **period-end-only** fair value adjustments.

- July 1, Year 1: Ridgeway sells **3,000** shares for $54 per share cash.
- September 30, Year 1: Summit declares and pays total dividends of $40,000 (Ridgeway still holds 5,000 shares after the July sale).
- December 31, Year 1: fair value of Summit is $48 per share. Beginning FVA balance is $0.

**Required:**  
a. Record the January 15 purchase.  
b. Record the July 1 partial sale (gain/loss = proceeds − **allocated original cost**; no FVA entry at sale).  
c. Record the September 30 dividend.  
d. Prepare a December 31 measurement schedule for shares **still held** and record the period-end FVA entry.  
e. Briefly state how the investment is classified on the balance sheet (current vs noncurrent) under LO 14-9.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 15, Year 1 — Purchase**  
Cost = (8,000 × $50) + $1,600 = $400,000 + $1,600 = **$401,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Summit Stock | 401,600 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 401,600 |

Dr 401,600 = Cr 401,600 ✓

**b. July 1, Year 1 — Partial sale of 3,000 shares**  
Cost allocated to shares sold = $401,600 × (3,000 / 8,000) = **$150,600**  
Cash proceeds = 3,000 × $54 = **$162,000**  
Gain on sale = $162,000 − $150,600 = **$11,400**  
Remaining cost = $401,600 − $150,600 = **$251,000** (5,000 shares)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 162,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Investment in Summit Stock | | 150,600 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Gain on Sale of Investment | | 11,400 |

Dr 162,000 = Cr 162,000 ✓

**c. September 30, Year 1 — Dividend on remaining shares**  
Ownership after sale = 5,000 / 100,000 = 5%  
Dividend revenue = $40,000 × 5% = **$2,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dividend Revenue | | 2,000 |

Dr 2,000 = Cr 2,000 ✓

**d. December 31, Year 1 — FVA for securities still held**

| Investment (held) | Cost | Fair value (12/31/Y1) | Unrealized holding gain (loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Summit common stock (5,000 sh) | $251,000 | $240,000* | $(11,000) |
| Existing FVA balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) needed in FVA** | | | **$(11,000)** |

\* 5,000 × $48 = $240,000

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 11,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 11,000 |

Dr 11,000 = Cr 11,000 ✓

Balance sheet carrying amount of investment = $251,000 − $11,000 = **$240,000**

Year 1 income effects related to Summit:  
- Dividend revenue $2,000  
- Gain on sale $11,400  
- Unrealized holding loss $11,000  

**e. Classification / presentation**  
Equity investments measured at FV-NI are classified as **current or noncurrent** based on management’s intent and ability to sell within one year (or operating cycle, if longer). The investment is reported at **fair value** (cost ± FVA) on the balance sheet; unrealized holding gains/losses and dividends are in **net income**.

**Key insight:** Under period-end-only FV-NI, a mid-year partial sale never touches FVA. Cost is removed pro rata; any FVA at year-end is computed **only for remaining shares** (and would also eliminate FVA that had related to sold shares if a prior-period FVA balance existed).

### Q4 — MC — Method mechanics at sale date
**LO:** LO 14-9  
**Concept:** Classification of sale measurement under period-end-only FV-NI  
**Question:**  
Under the LO 14-9 approach (equity securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments **only at period-end**), how is the gain or loss recorded on the date an equity investment is sold?

- A) Adjust the investment to fair value through net income, then remove the investment at fair value and clear the related FVA.  
- B) Recognize gain or loss as cash proceeds minus **original cost**; do not adjust the Fair Value Adjustment account until period-end.  
- C) Recognize gain or loss as cash proceeds minus fair value at the last balance sheet date, and reverse FVA immediately.  
- D) Defer any gain or loss in OCI until the investment’s original acquisition year is closed.

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-9 / Appendix 14C, sale G/L = proceeds − original cost. The FVA account is adjusted only in the period-end entry (which also removes FVA amounts associated with securities sold during the period). Choice A describes the LO 14-4 sale-date-and-period-end approach, not LO 14-9.
