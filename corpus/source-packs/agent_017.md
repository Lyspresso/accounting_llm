# Agent 17 — CORE DEMO — LO 16-7

**Chapter:** 16  
**LO title:** Account for extinguishment of debt  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of bonds issued at a discount (issue JE)
- Effective-interest amortization schedule through early retirement date
- Period-end / retirement-date interest and discount amortization update
- Partial early extinguishment JE (remove pro-rata face, unamortized discount; reacquisition price; gain/loss)
- Remaining bonds after partial extinguishment (interest and maturity)
- Straight-line amortization with mid-period partial retirement (accrued interest + amort update)
- Extinguishment at maturity (no gain/loss when carrying amount equals face)
- Bond refunding (retire old issue; issue new debt; recognize extinguishment gain/loss currently)
- Presentation: gain/loss on extinguishment recognized currently in net income as a separate item (ASC 470-50-40-2)

### Q1 — CORE — Partial early extinguishment (effective interest), full path
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE + effective-interest schedule + period-end interest JE + partial early extinguishment JE  
**Scenario:** On January 1 of Year 1, **Meridian Forge Co.** issued **$200,000** of **3-year, 5%** bonds for **$189,700**. Cash interest is payable annually each **December 31**. The bonds were priced to yield **7%**. Meridian uses the **effective-interest method** and rounds interest expense to the nearest dollar; the final period plugs amortization so carrying amount equals face at maturity.

On **December 31 of Year 2**, after recording interest expense, amortizing the discount, and paying cash interest for the year, Meridian **recalls 50%** of the bonds at **102**.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance entry.  
(b) Prepare the **complete effective-interest amortization schedule** for the full $200,000 issue (Years 1–3).  
(c) Record the **December 31, Year 1** interest payment entry.  
(d) Record the **December 31, Year 2** interest payment entry.  
(e) Record the **December 31, Year 2** entry to **extinguish 50%** of the bonds.  
(f) Record the **December 31, Year 3** interest payment entry on the **remaining 50%**.  
(g) Record the **December 31, Year 3** maturity retirement of the remaining bonds.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Issue bonds at discount**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 189,700 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 10,300 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 200,000 |

*Check:* Dr 200,000 = Cr 200,000.

**(b) Effective-interest amortization schedule (full issue)**  
Cash interest each year = $200,000 × 5% = **$10,000**.  
Interest expense ≈ beginning carrying amount × 7% (nearest dollar); Year 3 amort plugs remaining discount.

| Date | Cash interest | Interest expense | Discount amort. | Unamortized discount | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | 10,300 | 189,700 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | 10,000 | 13,279 | 3,279 | 7,021 | 192,979 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 10,000 | 13,509 | 3,509 | 3,512 | 196,488 |
| Dec. 31, Year 3 | 10,000 | 13,512 | 3,512 | 0 | 200,000 |
| **Totals** | **30,000** | **40,300** | **10,300** | | |

*Notes:* Year 1 IE = $189,700 × 7% = $13,279. Year 2 IE = $192,979 × 7% = $13,508.53 → **$13,509**. Year 3 amort = remaining unamortized discount **$3,512**; IE = $10,000 + $3,512 = **$13,512**.

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Interest**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 13,279 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,279 |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

*Check:* Dr 13,279 = Cr 13,279.

**(d) December 31, Year 2 — Interest**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 13,509 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,509 |
| Cash | | 10,000 |

*Check:* Dr 13,509 = Cr 13,509.

**(e) December 31, Year 2 — Early redemption of 50%**  
Reacquisition price = $200,000 × 50% × 1.02 = **$102,000**.  
Unamortized discount removed = $3,512 × 50% = **$1,756**.  
Carrying amount extinguished = $100,000 − $1,756 = **$98,244**.  
**Loss** = reacquisition price − carrying amount = $102,000 − $98,244 = **$3,756**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable ($200,000 × 50%) | 100,000 | |
| Loss on Redemption of Bonds | 3,756 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 1,756 |
| Cash | | 102,000 |

*Check:* Dr 103,756 = Cr 103,756.

**(f) December 31, Year 3 — Interest on remaining 50%**  
Use 50% of Year 3 schedule amounts:  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense ($13,512 × 50%) | 6,756 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable ($3,512 × 50%) | | 1,756 |
| Cash ($10,000 × 50%) | | 5,000 |

*Check:* Dr 6,756 = Cr 6,756.

**(g) December 31, Year 3 — Maturity of remaining bonds**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 100,000 |

*Check:* Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000. No gain or loss (discount fully amortized; carrying amount = face).

**Key insight:** Early extinguishment first updates carrying amount through amortization, then removes face and the **pro-rata unamortized discount/premium**, records cash paid (reacquisition price), and recognizes any difference currently as **gain or loss in net income**. Partial extinguishment does not change the accounting for the bonds that remain.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Partial early extinguishment (effective interest)
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — issue JE, effective-interest schedule, interest JEs, partial early extinguishment at a premium call price

The bonds were priced to yield **8%**. Oakridge uses the **effective-interest method** (nearest-dollar IE; final period plugs remaining discount).

On **December 31 of Year 2**, after year-end interest entries and cash interest payment, Oakridge **recalls 40%** of the bonds at **103**.

(a) Record the **January 1, Year 1** issuance.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Mid-period SL partial retirement + maturity + refunding
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting (accrued interest + SL amort to retirement date); mid-period partial extinguishment; maturity vs. refunding  
**Scenario — Part A (mid-period partial retirement, straight-line):** On **January 1 of Year 1**, **Blue Harbor Logistics Corp.** issued **$480,000** of **5-year, 5%** bonds at **95**. Cash interest is payable **semiannually** on **June 30** and **December 31**. Blue Harbor amortizes the discount using the **straight-line method**.

On **October 1 of Year 2**, Blue Harbor retires **$120,000** face value of the bonds when they are selling at **91**, and pays **accrued interest** on the retired portion through that date.

**Scenario — Part B (maturity):** Same company; assume instead that Blue Harbor holds a separate **$75,000** face, 6% bond issue (issued years earlier at a discount that is **fully amortized** by maturity) to maturity on **December 31 of Year 8**.

**Scenario — Part C (refunding):** On **January 1 of Year 6**, **Pinecrest Utilities** has **$150,000** of **7%** bonds outstanding that were issued at **par** on January 1 of Year 1 (10-year term; interest dates unchanged). Pinecrest retires the entire issue at **104** and immediately issues at **par** **$156,000** of **20-year, 5%** bonds (same interest dates).

**Required:**  
**Part A**  
(a) Entry for bond issuance on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Entry for the June 30, Year 1 interest payment (pattern for a full semiannual period).  
(c) Entry on October 1 of Year 2 to recognize interest expense and amortization for the **retired portion only** (3 months since June 30).  
(d) Entry on October 1 of Year 2 to record the **bond retirement**.  

**Part B**  
(e) Entry at maturity to retire the $75,000 bonds.  

**Part C**  
(f) Entry to retire the 7% bonds on January 1 of Year 6.  
(g) Entry to issue the new 5% bonds on January 1 of Year 6.  
(h) Briefly state how the gain or loss on extinguishment is **presented** in the financial statements.

**Answer key:**

**Part A — Setup**  
Issue price = $480,000 × 0.95 = **$456,000**.  
Total discount = **$24,000**.  
Semiannual periods = 5 × 2 = **10**.  
Straight-line amort per full semiannual period = $24,000 / 10 = **$2,400**.  
Cash interest per full period = $480,000 × 5% × 6/12 = **$12,000**.

From January 1, Year 1 through June 30, Year 2: **3** full semiannual periods completed.  
July 1–October 1, Year 2: **3 months** = one-half of a semiannual period.  
Amortization for 3 months (entire issue) = $2,400 × 3/6 = **$1,200**.  
Portion retired = $120,000 / $480,000 = **25%**.

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Issuance**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 456,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | 24,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 480,000 |

*Check:* Dr 480,000 = Cr 480,000.

**(b) June 30, Year 1 — Full-period interest (pattern)**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense ($12,000 + $2,400) | 14,400 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 2,400 |
| Cash | | 12,000 |

*Check:* Dr 14,400 = Cr 14,400.

**(c) October 1, Year 2 — Interest update on retired portion only**  
Accrued cash interest on retired face = $120,000 × 5% × 3/12 = **$1,500**.  
Discount amort on retired portion for 3 months = $1,200 × 25% = **$300**.  
Interest expense = $1,500 + $300 = **$1,800**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 1,800 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 300 |
| Cash | | 1,500 |

*Check:* Dr 1,800 = Cr 1,800.

**(d) October 1, Year 2 — Extinguishment of $120,000 face**  
Unamortized discount **after** the (c) update, entire issue:  
Amortized to date = (3 × $2,400) + $1,200 = **$8,400**.  
Unamortized discount remaining (entire) = $24,000 − $8,400 = **$15,600**.  
Unamortized discount on 25% retired = $15,600 × 25% = **$3,900**.  
*(Equivalently: remaining life after Oct. 1 = 3 months + 3 years = 39 months; monthly amort = $24,000/60 = $400; unamortized on retired = $400 × 39 × 0.25 = $3,900.)*

Reacquisition price (excluding accrued interest already paid in (c)) = $120,000 × 0.91 = **$109,200**.  
Carrying amount of retired bonds = $120,000 − $3,900 = **$116,100**.  
**Gain** on redemption = $116,100 − $109,200 = **$6,900**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 120,000 | |
| Discount on Bonds Payable | | 3,900 |
| Cash | | 109,200 |
| Gain on Redemption of Bonds | | 6,900 |

*Check:* Dr 120,000 = Cr 120,000.

**(e) Part B — Extinguishment at maturity**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 75,000 | |
| Cash | | 75,000 |

*Check:* Dr 75,000 = Cr 75,000. **No gain or loss** when discount/premium is fully amortized and reacquisition price equals face.

**(f) Part C — Retire 7% bonds (refunding)**  
Reacquisition price = $150,000 × 1.04 = **$156,000**.  
Carrying amount = face (issued at par) = **$150,000**.  
**Loss** = $156,000 − $150,000 = **$6,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 150,000 | |
| Loss on Redemption of Bonds | 6,000 | |
| Cash | | 156,000 |

*Check:* Dr 156,000 = Cr 156,000.

**(g) Part C — Issue new 5% bonds at par**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 156,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 156,000 |

*Check:* Dr 156,000 = Cr 156,000.

**(h) Presentation**  
Under ASC 470-50-40-2, the difference between reacquisition price and net carrying amount of extinguished debt is recognized **currently in income** of the period of extinguishment as a **gain or loss**, identified as a **separate item** (not deferred and amortized over the new issue’s life). Refunding is accounted for like any other debt extinguishment.

**Key insight:** Before a mid-period extinguishment, **update interest and amortization to the retirement date** for the portion retired; only then measure gain/loss as reacquisition price versus updated net carrying amount. At maturity with fully amortized premium/discount, extinguishment is simply face for cash. Refunding does not change that measurement—gain/loss still hits current income.

### Q4 — MC — Gain/loss measurement and presentation
**LO:** LO 16-7  
**Concept:** Classification/presentation — measuring and reporting gain or loss on early debt extinguishment  

**Question:** On December 31 (an interest payment date, after interest and amortization entries), **Summit Rail Co.** extinguishes bonds with face **$80,000**, unamortized discount **$2,400**, by paying **$81,200** cash (call price 101.5). How should Summit report the extinguishment result, and what is the amount?

- A) Gain of $1,200, deferred and amortized over the remaining original term  
- B) Loss of $3,600, recognized currently in net income as a separate item  
- C) Loss of $1,200, recognized currently in net income as a separate item  
- D) Gain of $3,600, reported as OCI only  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Net carrying amount = $80,000 − $2,400 = **$77,600**.  
Reacquisition price = **$81,200**.  
Loss = $81,200 − $77,600 = **$3,600**.  
ASC 470-50-40-2 requires the difference to be recognized **currently in income** of the extinguishment period as a **separate** gain or loss item—not deferred into the new/remaining debt, and not OCI-only.

**Key insight:** Loss (gain) = reacquisition price − (face − unamortized discount) [or face + unamortized premium]. Report currently in earnings.
