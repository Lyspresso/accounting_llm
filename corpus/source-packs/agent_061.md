# Agent 61 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-5

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for purchase commitments  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Noncancelable, material purchase commitments: disclose; recognize unrealized loss when contract price exceeds market (LIFO/retail) or NRV (FIFO/other)
- Contract initiation: ordinarily **no** journal entry (executory contract) if contract price ≤ market/NRV
- Period-end adjusting JE: Dr Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment; Cr Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment
- Subsequent recovery of an estimated purchase-commitment loss is **not** recognized
- Settlement/purchase: Inventory at current market/NRV; remove estimated liability; recognize additional loss if market/NRV fell further; credit Cash (or AP) for contract price
- Partial deliveries: measure liability only on **remaining** committed units
- Classification/presentation: loss in other expenses (or with inventory losses); estimated liability (usually current); disclose net losses on firm purchase commitments and material commitments
- No loss when firm sales contracts (or similar) ensure disposition without price concession

### Q1 — CORE — Full commitment cycle: initiation, year-end loss, and settlement purchase
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of estimated loss on firm purchase commitment; settlement JE when goods are acquired  
**Scenario:**  
Meridian Forge Co. uses a **perpetual** inventory system and the **FIFO** inventory method (measure declines against **net realizable value**). Year-end is December 31. Assume no selling costs so stated selling prices equal NRV.

- On **November 5, Year 1**, Meridian enters a **noncancelable, material** purchase contract to buy **40,000** units of specialty alloy at **$15** per unit (**$600,000** total) for delivery in Year 2. On the contract date, NRV is **$16** per unit.
- On **December 31, Year 1**, NRV of the alloy under contract is **$14** per unit.
- On **April 10, Year 2**, Meridian takes delivery of all **40,000** units and pays cash at the contract price. NRV on that date is **$13.50** per unit.
- There are no firm sales contracts protecting Meridian from price declines.

**Required:**  
a. Journal entry, if any, on **November 5, Year 1** (contract initiation).  
b. Journal entry, if any, on **December 31, Year 1** (period-end). Show the loss computation.  
c. Journal entry on **April 10, Year 2** (purchase/settlement).  
d. Briefly state how the Year 1 loss and remaining commitment would be **classified/presented/disclosed**.  
e. Complete a **commitment measurement schedule** (contract total, NRV at each date, cumulative loss recognized, estimated liability balance) for Nov 5 Y1 → Dec 31 Y1 → Apr 10 Y2 (after settlement).

**Answer key:**  

**a. November 5, Year 1 — initiation**  
Contract price ($15) < NRV ($16). Noncancelable commitment is executory; **no journal entry**.  
**Disclosure** of the material commitment is required (nature, quantity, price, timing).

**b. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE (initial loss recognition)**  
Contract total = 40,000 × $15 = **$600,000**  
NRV of inventory under contract = 40,000 × $14 = **$560,000**  
Estimated loss = $600,000 − $560,000 = **$40,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 40,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 40,000 |
| *To recognize unrealized loss; contract price exceeds NRV* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 40,000.

**c. April 10, Year 2 — purchase / settlement**  
Inventory at current NRV = 40,000 × $13.50 = **$540,000**  
Remove liability recognized at YE = **$40,000**  
Additional loss since YE = ($14 − $13.50) × 40,000 = **$20,000**  
Cash paid = 40,000 × $15 = **$600,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 540,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 40,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 20,000 | |
| Cash | | 600,000 |
| *Acquire inventory under commitment; derecognize liability; recognize further NRV decline* | | |

**Check:** Dr 540,000 + 40,000 + 20,000 = **600,000** = Cr Cash. Balanced.

**d. Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
- **Income statement (Year 1):** Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment **$40,000** in other expenses (or with inventory holding losses).  
- **Balance sheet (Dec 31, Year 1):** Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment **$40,000** (typically **current** liability given Year 2 delivery).  
- **Notes:** Disclose the material firm purchase commitment (units, price, period) and **net losses recognized on firm purchase commitments** (ASC 330-10-50).  
- Year 2 additional Loss on Purchase Contract **$20,000** in other expenses when settled.

**e. Commitment measurement schedule**

| Date | Event | Units remaining | Contract $ | NRV (or market) $ | Est. liability bal. | Cumulative loss recognized |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nov 5, Y1 | Contract signed | 40,000 | 600,000 | 640,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | Year-end | 40,000 | 600,000 | 560,000 | **40,000** | **40,000** |
| Apr 10, Y2 | Purchase (before JE) | 40,000 | 600,000 | 540,000 | 40,000 | 40,000 |
| Apr 10, Y2 | After settlement JE | 0 | 0 | n/a | **0** | **60,000** |

**Key insight:** Signing a firm purchase commitment is usually not recorded. The **first recognition JE** is the period-end entry when contract price exceeds NRV (or market). At settlement, inventory is recorded at **current** NRV, the liability is cleared, and any **further** decline is an additional loss. Subsequent recovery of the estimated loss is not recognized before purchase.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Full commitment cycle with different quantities and prices
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of estimated loss recognition and settlement of purchase commitment  
**Scenario:**  
Lakeshore Components Inc. uses perpetual inventory and **FIFO** (declines measured vs **NRV**). Year-end December 31. No selling costs; stated unit prices = NRV. Commitment is noncancelable and material; no firm sales contracts protect against declines.

- **October 12, Year 1:** noncancelable contract to purchase **60,000** units at **$25** per unit (**$1,500,000**). NRV on that date: **$26.50**.  
- **December 31, Year 1:** NRV **$23** per unit.  
- **March 20, Year 2:** purchases all 60,000 units for cash at the contract price; NRV that day **$22** per unit.

**Required:**  
a. Entries (or state none) on Oct 12 Y1, Dec 31 Y1, and Mar 20 Y2.  
b. What is inventory cost recorded on Mar 20 Y2?  
c. Total purchase-commitment losses recognized in Year 1 and Year 2.

**Answer key:**  

**a.**  

**October 12, Year 1:** Contract $25 < NRV $26.50 → **no journal entry** (disclose commitment).

**December 31, Year 1:**  
Contract = 60,000 × $25 = **$1,500,000**  
NRV = 60,000 × $23 = **$1,380,000**  
Loss = **$120,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 120,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 120,000.

**March 20, Year 2:**  
Inventory = 60,000 × $22 = **$1,320,000**  
Liability removed = **$120,000**  
Additional loss = ($23 − $22) × 60,000 = **$60,000**  
Cash = **$1,500,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 1,320,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 120,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 1,500,000 |

**Check:** 1,320,000 + 120,000 + 60,000 = 1,500,000.

**b.** Inventory recorded at **$1,320,000** (current NRV), not at contract cost.

**c.** Year 1 loss **$120,000**; Year 2 additional loss **$60,000**; **total $180,000** (= $1,500,000 − $1,320,000).

**Key insight:** Same measurement pattern as Q1 with all new numbers: YE loss = remaining contract $ − NRV; at purchase, inventory at then-current NRV and any incremental decline is recognized.

### Q3 — CORE — Partial deliveries, remaining-unit liability schedule, and recovery not recognized
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement on remaining committed units; period-end liability; settlement of residual commitment; no reverse for recovery  
**Scenario:**  
Cedar Valley Packaging LLC uses perpetual inventory and the **LIFO** method (measure purchase-commitment declines against **market**). Year-end December 31. Noncancelable, material contract; unprotected by firm sales contracts.

- **September 1, Year 1:** contract to purchase **100,000** units of kraft liner at **$8.00** per unit through June 30, Year 2. Market on Sept 1 = **$8.00**.  
- **November 18, Year 1:** purchases and pays for **30,000** units under the contract when market is **$8.10**.  
- **December 31, Year 1:** market for remaining units is **$7.60**.  
- **January 31, Year 2 (interim):** market recovers to **$7.85** (still below contract). Management prepares interim statements.  
- **February 28, Year 2:** purchases the remaining **70,000** units for cash at **$8.00**; market that day is **$7.40**.

**Required:**  
a. Journal entry for the **November 18, Year 1** partial purchase.  
b. Prepare a **remaining-commitment schedule** at Dec 31, Year 1 (units remaining, contract $, market $, estimated liability). Record the Dec 31 adjusting JE.  
c. Journal entry, if any, on **January 31, Year 2** for the market recovery.  
d. Journal entry on **February 28, Year 2** for settlement of the remaining commitment.  
e. How would the answer to (b) change if the commitment were **cancelable** at will with no penalty, or if Cedar Valley held **firm sales contracts** ensuring sale of the future inventory without price concession?

**Answer key:**  

**a. November 18, Year 1 — partial purchase under contract**  
Units purchased under firm commitment are recorded at **contract cost** (cash paid). Market $8.10 > contract $8.00; no loss on the units acquired.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 240,000 | |
| Cash | | 240,000 |
| *30,000 × $8.00 contract price* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 240,000.

**b. Remaining-commitment schedule and Dec 31, Year 1 JE**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Original committed units | 100,000 |
| Units purchased in Year 1 | (30,000) |
| **Units remaining under commitment** | **70,000** |
| Remaining contract $ (70,000 × $8.00) | 560,000 |
| Market of remaining units (70,000 × $7.60) | 532,000 |
| **Estimated liability / unrealized loss** | **28,000** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated Loss on Purchase Commitment | 28,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | | 28,000 |
| *70,000 × ($8.00 − $7.60)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000. Liability is measured only on **remaining** units, not the full original 100,000.

**c. January 31, Year 2 — market recovery to $7.85**  
Implied “improved” position vs YE: 70,000 × ($7.85 − $7.60) = $17,500 reduction in economic exposure.  
Per GAAP/text: **subsequent recovery of an estimated loss is not recognized**.

**No journal entry.** Estimated liability remains **$28,000**.

**d. February 28, Year 2 — settle remaining 70,000 units**  
Inventory at current market = 70,000 × $7.40 = **$518,000**  
Liability removed = **$28,000**  
Additional loss since YE (recovery ignored) = market decline from YE $7.60 to $7.40: 70,000 × $0.20 = **$14,000**  
Cash = 70,000 × $8.00 = **$560,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 518,000 | |
| Estimated Liability on Purchase Commitment | 28,000 | |
| Loss on Purchase Contract | 14,000 | |
| Cash | | 560,000 |

**Check:** 518,000 + 28,000 + 14,000 = 560,000.

**e. Cancelable / protected commitments**  
- **Cancelable without material penalty:** not a firm purchase commitment for loss recognition → **no estimated loss or liability** at Dec 31 (may still disclose significant planned purchases if useful).  
- **Firm sales contracts (or similar) ensure disposition without price concession:** utility of the commitment is not impaired → **no loss recognized** even if market < contract (ASC 330-10-35-18). Disclose as appropriate.

**Key insight:** After partial delivery, measure the liability only on **open** units. Do **not** reverse the liability for interim recoveries. At final settlement, clear the liability and pick up any **further** market decline since the last recognized measurement.

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement choice)
**LO:** LO 10-5  
**Concept:** Measuring estimated liability on remaining purchase commitments; when initial recognition applies  

**Question 1:**  
On April 1, Year 1, Basil Ridge Materials entered a two-year **noncancelable** contract to purchase **125,000** units of raw materials at **$110** per unit (equal to market on that date). On October 16, Year 1, Basil purchased and paid for **25,000** units at **$110**. On December 31, Year 1, market was **$108** per unit. Basil uses LIFO. What **estimated liability on purchase commitments**, if any, should Basil report at December 31, Year 1?

- A) $0  
- B) $250,000  
- C) $200,000  
- D) $10,800,000  

**Answer:** **C. $200,000.**  
Remaining units = 125,000 − 25,000 = **100,000**.  
Liability = 100,000 × ($110 − $108) = **$200,000**.  
A is wrong because the remaining firm commitment is underwater. B uses 125,000 × $2 and ignores partial purchase. D is remaining units × full market price, not the per-unit decline.

---

**Question 2:**  
On May 1, Year 1, Orion Plastics signs a **noncancelable** contract to buy goods in Year 2 at a fixed price **equal to** then-current NRV. Orion uses FIFO. Which statement is correct regarding **May 1** accounting?

- A) Debit Inventory and credit Accounts Payable for the full contract amount.  
- B) Debit Estimated Loss and credit Estimated Liability for the full contract amount.  
- C) No journal entry; disclose if the commitment is material.  
- D) Debit Prepaid Inventory and credit Cash for the full contract amount.  

**Answer:** **C.** A firm purchase commitment is generally executory until delivery (or until a reportable loss arises). When contract price does not exceed NRV/market at inception, **no entry**; material noncancelable commitments are **disclosed**. A/D incorrectly capitalize an unshipped commitment. B invents a loss when none exists.
