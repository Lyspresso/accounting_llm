# Agent 304 — CORE DEMO — LO 8-4

**Chapter:** 8  
**LO title:** Measuring and Recording Receivables at Net Amount Expected to Be Collected  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** credit sales establish gross trade accounts receivable
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** allowance T-account and gross AR rollforwards (sales, collections, write-offs, recoveries)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** plug Bad Debt Expense so Allowance for Doubtful Accounts equals the CECL desired ending balance (aging or single-rate pool)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** write-off of specific uncollectible accounts; recovery (reinstate then collect)
- Balance-sheet presentation at the **net amount expected to be collected**
- Debit unadjusted AFDA when write-offs exceed prior allowance — period-end plug is larger
- Number-variant twin with all different facts
- Classification: CECL/allowance required under GAAP (even if remote); write-off does not change net AR

### Q1 — CORE — Full allowance cycle with aging period-end adjust (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Initial AR recognition; AFDA rollforward schedule; CECL aging period-end adjusting JE; write-off and recovery settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverglen Components Inc.** applies the **allowance method** under the **Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL)** model to trade accounts receivable. Fiscal year-end is **December 31**.

**Balances at January 1, Year 5**
| Account | Balance |
|---|---:|
| Accounts Receivable (gross) | $450,000 |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | $15,200 credit |

**Year 5 activity**
| Event | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Credit sales | $1,250,000 |
| Cash collections on account (excluding recoveries) | $1,180,000 |
| Write-offs of specific customer accounts deemed uncollectible | $13,500 |
| Recovery of a previously written-off account (cash collected after write-off) | $1,800 |

**Aging of accounts receivable at December 31, Year 5** (after all of the above activity):

| Age category | Receivable balance | Expected credit loss rate | Estimated uncollectible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current (not past due) | $380,000 | 0.8% | ? |
| 1–30 days past due | 72,000 | 3.5% | ? |
| 31–90 days past due | 38,000 | 15.0% | ? |
| Over 90 days past due | 16,500 | 50.0% | ? |
| **Total** | **$506,500** | | **?** |

Management believes historical rates, adjusted for current conditions and reasonable and supportable forecasts, are appropriate **expected credit loss rates**. Receivables are pooled by past-due status.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **initial recognition** journal entry for Year 5 credit sales.  
b. Record cash collections on account (excluding the recovery).  
c. Record the **write-off** of specific uncollectible accounts (settlement/disposal of AR components).  
d. Record the **recovery** of the previously written-off account (**two** entries).  
e. Prepare an **allowance rollforward schedule** from January 1 through the **unadjusted** December 31 balance (before the Year 5 adjusting entry). Also prove ending gross AR = $506,500.  
f. Complete the aging schedule; compute the required ending allowance; record the **December 31 period-end adjusting entry** for bad debt expense (emphasis).  
g. Show **balance-sheet presentation** of receivables at the net amount expected to be collected. State the effect of the Year 5 write-off on net accounts receivable **at the write-off date** (before recovery or YE adjust).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Credit sales — initial recognition of AR**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 1,250,000 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 1,250,000 |
| *To record Year 5 credit sales* | | |

*(Dr 1,250,000 = Cr 1,250,000)*

**b. Collections on account**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,180,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 1,180,000 |
| *To record collections on account* | | |

*(Dr 1,180,000 = Cr 1,180,000)*

**c. Write-off of specific accounts**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | 13,500 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 13,500 |
| *To write off accounts deemed uncollectible* | | |

*(Dr 13,500 = Cr 13,500)*

**d. Recovery (reinstate, then collect)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 1,800 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 1,800 |
| *To reverse prior write-off and reinstate customer account* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,800 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 1,800 |
| *To record cash collection on reinstated account* | | |

*(Each entry: Dr = Cr = 1,800)*

**e. Gross AR proof and allowance rollforward (before YE adjust)**

Gross AR rollforward:
\[
\begin{align*}
\text{Beg AR} &\quad 450{,}000 \\
+ \text{Credit sales} &\quad 1{,}250{,}000 \\
- \text{Collections} &\quad (1{,}180{,}000) \\
- \text{Write-offs} &\quad (13{,}500) \\
+ \text{Recovery reinstate} &\quad 1{,}800 \\
- \text{Recovery collection} &\quad (1{,}800) \\
\hline
\text{End AR (gross)} &\quad \mathbf{\$506{,}500}
\end{align*}
\]

Check: \(450{,}000 + 1{,}250{,}000 - 1{,}180{,}000 - 13{,}500 = 506{,}500\).

Allowance rollforward schedule (unadjusted):

| Item | Amount (Cr) / (Dr) |
|---|---:|
| Beginning allowance, Jan 1, Year 5 | $15,200 Cr |
| Write-offs | (13,500) |
| Recoveries (reinstatement) | 1,800 |
| **Unadjusted ending allowance, Dec 31, Year 5** | **$3,500 Cr** |

Check: \(15{,}200 - 13{,}500 + 1{,}800 = 3{,}500\).

**f. Aging schedule and period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| Age category | Receivable balance | Rate | Estimated uncollectible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current (not past due) | $380,000 | 0.8% | $3,040 |
| 1–30 days past due | 72,000 | 3.5% | 2,520 |
| 31–90 days past due | 38,000 | 15.0% | 5,700 |
| Over 90 days past due | 16,500 | 50.0% | 8,250 |
| **Total** | **$506,500** | | **$19,510** |

Required ending allowance (credit) = **$19,510**  
Unadjusted allowance (credit) = **$3,500**  
Bad debt expense (credit loss expense) = \(19{,}510 - 3{,}500 = \mathbf{\$16{,}010}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 16,010 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 16,010 |
| *To adjust allowance to net amount expected to be collected (aging/CECL period-end plug)* | | |

*(Dr 16,010 = Cr 16,010)*

Post-adjust allowance T-account:

| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | |
|---:|---:|
| Write-offs 13,500 | Beg 15,200 |
| | Recovery 1,800 |
| | YE adjust 16,010 |
| | **End bal. 19,510** |

Check: \(15{,}200 - 13{,}500 + 1{,}800 + 16{,}010 = 19{,}510\).

**g. Presentation and write-off effect**

Balance sheet (current assets):  
**Trade receivables, net of allowance of $19,510 — $486,990**  
(\(506{,}500 - 19{,}510 = 486{,}990\))

At the **write-off date**, both gross AR and the allowance decrease by the same amount, so **net AR is unchanged** and **total assets are unchanged**. The income-statement effect of expected losses is recognized when the allowance is established/adjusted at period-end, not when a specific account is written off.

**Key insight:** Under CECL, period-end bad debt expense is the **plug** from the unadjusted AFDA balance (after write-offs and recoveries) to the **desired ending balance** from the aging schedule. That adjusting JE is what reports receivables at the **net amount expected to be collected**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Full allowance cycle with aging YE adjust
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — AR recognition, write-off, recovery, AFDA schedule, aging period-end adjust, net presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**SummitForge Distributors LLC** applies CECL (allowance method) to trade receivables. Year-end is December 31.

**Balances at January 1, Year 4**
| Account | Balance |
|---|---:|
| Accounts Receivable (gross) | $275,000 |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | $9,800 credit |

**Year 4 activity**
| Event | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Credit sales | $740,000 |
| Cash collections on account (excluding recoveries) | $695,000 |
| Write-offs of specific accounts | $8,400 |
| Recovery of previously written-off account (cash after write-off) | $900 |

**Aging at December 31, Year 4** (after activity above; totals ending gross AR):

| Age category | Receivable balance | Expected credit loss rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current (not past due) | $245,000 | 0.6% |
| 1–45 days past due | 42,000 | 2.5% |
| 46–90 days past due | 18,200 | 12.0% |
| Over 90 days past due | 6,400 | 42.0% |
| **Total** | **$311,600** | |

**Required:**  
a. Record JEs for: (1) credit sales, (2) collections, (3) write-offs, (4) recovery (two entries).  
b. Allowance rollforward to unadjusted ending balance; prove ending gross AR.  
c. Aging computation; Year 4 **period-end adjusting entry** for bad debt expense.  
d. Net amount expected to be collected on the December 31, Year 4 balance sheet.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Journal entries**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 740,000 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 740,000 |

*(Dr = Cr = 740,000)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 695,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 695,000 |

*(Dr = Cr = 695,000)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | 8,400 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 8,400 |

*(Dr = Cr = 8,400)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 900 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 900 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 900 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 900 |

*(Each recovery entry: Dr = Cr = 900)*

**b. Rollforwards**

Gross AR:
\[
275{,}000 + 740{,}000 - 695{,}000 - 8{,}400 + 900 - 900 = \mathbf{\$311{,}600}
\]

Allowance (unadjusted):
\[
9{,}800 - 8{,}400 + 900 = \mathbf{\$2{,}300\ \text{credit}}
\]

**c. Aging and YE period-end adjust**

| Age category | Balance | Rate | Uncollectible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current | $245,000 | 0.6% | $1,470 |
| 1–45 days | 42,000 | 2.5% | 1,050 |
| 46–90 days | 18,200 | 12.0% | 2,184 |
| Over 90 days | 6,400 | 42.0% | 2,688 |
| **Total** | **$311,600** | | **$7,392** |

Required ending AFDA = **$7,392**  
BDE = \(7{,}392 - 2{,}300 = \mathbf{\$5{,}092}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 5,092 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 5,092 |
| *To adjust allowance to CECL/aging estimate at period-end* | | |

*(Dr 5,092 = Cr 5,092)*

Post-adjust check: \(2{,}300 + 5{,}092 = 7{,}392\).

**d. Net presentation**  
Trade receivables, net of allowance of **$7,392** — **$304,208**  
(\(311{,}600 - 7{,}392 = 304{,}208\))

**Key insight:** Changing every input (sales, collections, write-offs, recoveries, aging buckets and rates) changes both the unadjusted allowance and the required ending balance; bad debt expense remains the **period-end plug** to the CECL estimate—not a fixed percentage of sales.

### Q3 — CORE — Period-end adjust from debit AFDA; single-rate CECL; write-off and recovery
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE when unadjusted AFDA is a debit; single-rate expected credit loss; write-off neutrality; recovery settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Orthotics Co.** uses a **single expected credit loss rate** of **4.0%** of year-end gross accounts receivable (one pool; similar risk characteristics). At **December 31, Year 1**, before the year-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Unadjusted balance |
|---|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | $220,000 debit |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | $1,400 **debit** |

Write-offs during Year 1 exceeded the prior allowance, leaving a temporary **debit** balance in AFDA before the period-end CECL estimate is recorded.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the required ending allowance and the **December 31 period-end adjusting entry** (emphasis).  
b. Report accounts receivable at the net amount expected to be collected.  
c. On **January 20, Year 2**, Lakeshore writes off a specific customer balance of **$2,500**. Record the write-off and show that **net AR is unchanged** immediately before vs. after the write-off (use post-Year-1-adjust balances).  
d. On **February 5, Year 2**, Lakeshore collects **$1,000** on the account written off in (c) (partial recovery). Record the **two** recovery entries and compute net AR after the recovery.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Required allowance and period-end adjusting JE**  
Required AFDA (credit) = \(220{,}000 \times 0.04 = \mathbf{\$8{,}800}\)  

Unadjusted AFDA is a **$1,400 debit**, so the credit needed to reach an $8,800 credit balance is:
\[
8{,}800 + 1{,}400 = \mathbf{\$10{,}200}
\]

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 10,200 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 10,200 |
| *To establish required CECL allowance from a debit unadjusted balance* | | |

*(Dr 10,200 = Cr 10,200)*

Allowance T-account:

| | Allowance for Doubtful Accounts |
|---:|---:|
| Unadj. bal. 1,400 | |
| | YE adjust 10,200 |
| | **End bal. 8,800** |

**b. Net amount expected to be collected**  
\[
220{,}000 - 8{,}800 = \mathbf{\$211{,}200}
\]  
Balance sheet: **Accounts receivable, net of allowance of $8,800 — $211,200**

**c. Write-off neutrality (Jan 20, Year 2)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | 2,500 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 2,500 |

*(Dr 2,500 = Cr 2,500)*

| | Before write-off | After write-off |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts receivable (gross) | $220,000 | $217,500 |
| Less: Allowance for doubtful accounts | (8,800) | (6,300) |
| **Net accounts receivable** | **$211,200** | **$211,200** |

Net AR and total assets are **unchanged** by the write-off.

**d. Partial recovery (Feb 5, Year 2)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 1,000 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 1,000 |
| *To reinstate portion of previously written-off account* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable | | 1,000 |
| *To record cash collection on reinstated account* | | |

*(Each entry: Dr = Cr = 1,000)*

After recovery:

| | After write-off | After recovery |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts receivable (gross) | $217,500 | $217,500 |
| Less: Allowance for doubtful accounts | (6,300) | (7,300) |
| **Net accounts receivable** | **$211,200** | **$210,200** |

Net AR falls by the **$1,000 cash collected** (gross AR ends unchanged after reinstate-and-collect; AFDA is reinstated by $1,000).

**Key insight:** When the unadjusted allowance has a **debit** balance (write-offs > prior estimate), the period-end bad debt expense equals required ending credit **plus** the debit unadjusted amount. Write-offs never reduce net AR; recoveries reinstate the allowance so management’s overall uncollectible estimate remains intact before the next period-end plug.

### Q4 — MC — Write-off effect and CECL measurement basis
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Classification/method: write-off impact on net AR; CECL net measurement vs direct write-off  

**Question 1:**  
At year-end, after all adjusting entries, **Driftwood Outfitters Inc.** reports:

- Gross accounts receivable: $95,000  
- Allowance for doubtful accounts: $4,100 credit  

During the next period, Driftwood writes off **$1,200** of specific customer accounts (no other activity). Immediately after the write-off, what is the **net amount expected to be collected** on accounts receivable?

- A) $90,900  
- B) $89,700  
- C) $90,900 (unchanged); gross AR and allowance both fall by $1,200  
- D) $93,800  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Before: net = \(95{,}000 - 4{,}100 = 90{,}900\).  
After: gross \(93{,}800\); allowance \(2{,}900\); net \(93{,}800 - 2{,}900 = 90{,}900\).  
Write-off reduces gross AR and AFDA equally; **net AR is unchanged**. A states the correct net amount but omits the equal-reduction mechanism; C is the complete correct description. B incorrectly subtracts the write-off from net. D is post-write-off gross, not net.

**Question 2:**  
Which statement best describes measurement of trade accounts receivable under U.S. GAAP (CECL)?

- A) Report gross receivables only; recognize losses only when a specific account is written off (direct write-off).  
- B) Report receivables at the **net amount expected to be collected**, using an allowance for expected credit losses over the contractual life, adjusted each reporting date.  
- C) Report receivables at fair value through net income each period.  
- D) Establish an allowance only when a loss is probable and reasonably estimable under an incurred-loss model that still governs all trade receivables.

**Answer:** **B.**  
CECL (ASC 326-20) requires an allowance so that amortized-cost financial assets (including most trade receivables) are presented at the **net amount expected to be collected**, with expected credit losses estimated over the contractual term and updated each period. A is the direct write-off method (not GAAP for these receivables; often used for tax). C is not the general trade-receivable model. D describes superseded incurred-loss timing emphasis; CECL recognizes expected losses even if remote.
