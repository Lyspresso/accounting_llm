# Agent 317 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-8

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Apply the dollar-value LIFO method  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition / base year:** first year of DVLIFO (or adoption date) sets the **base-year inventory layer** at then-current FIFO dollars; index = 1.00; LIFO reserve typically **zero** at adoption if base FIFO = base LIFO
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (four steps):** (1) restate FIFO EI ÷ year index → base-year dollars; (2) arrange into LIFO layers (oldest first; new layer if base-year $ rise; liquidate if fall); (3) match each layer to its **originating** price index; (4) restate layers × originating indices → **dollar-value LIFO EI**
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** convert internal FIFO inventory to external DVLIFO via **LIFO reserve** — Dr **Cost of Goods Sold**, Cr **Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO** for the **increase** in (FIFO EI − DVLIFO EI); reverse if the reserve **decreases**
- **Layer liquidation (disposal of layers):** when base-year-dollar inventory **falls**, newer layers are peeled first; liquidated layers’ originating indices drop out of the schedule; COGS absorbs the LIFO effect through the reserve adjustment
- **Pooling / method choice:** DVLIFO pools inventory **dollars**, not individual unit costs; FIFO (or average) continues **internally**; conversion is a **year-end** external/tax computation only
- **Number-variant twin:** same four-step path and period-end JEs with all different amounts

**Method note (core exam path — Demo 9-8 / Review 9-8):**  
Company keeps **FIFO** books. At each **December 31**, compute DVLIFO EI with the four steps, then record only the **change** in LIFO reserve:  
\(\text{LIFO reserve} = \text{FIFO EI} - \text{DVLIFO EI}\)  
\(\text{Period-end adjustment} = \text{Current LIFO reserve} - \text{Prior LIFO reserve}\)  
Do **not** re-use the current-year index on old layers. Double-extension internal index is Expanding Your Knowledge (sidebar) — not required here.

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-year DVLIFO layers, schedule, and period-end LIFO reserve JEs

**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Establish base-year layer (initial recognition); subsequent four-step DVLIFO measurement schedule for Years 1–3; **period-end adjusting JEs** to the LIFO reserve (emphasis); presentation of inventory net of allowance  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Outdoor Co.** (calendar year-end) completed its first year of operations in **Year 1**. Management uses **FIFO** for internal perpetual records and reporting to managers. For **income tax and external financial statements**, the company applies the **dollar-value LIFO** method to one inventory pool. Price indices are externally determined and provided below. Only at year-end is FIFO converted to DVLIFO via the LIFO reserve.

**Facts — ending inventory at FIFO (year-end prices) and price indices:**

| Year | FIFO ending inventory | Price index |
|------|----------------------:|------------:|
| Year 1 (base) | $120,000 | 1.00 |
| Year 2 | 156,000 | 1.20 |
| Year 3 | 178,200 | 1.32 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute **dollar-value LIFO ending inventory** for Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 using the four-step method. Present a **layer schedule** each year.  
b. Compute the **LIFO reserve** at each December 31 and the **adjustment** to the LIFO reserve each year.  
c. Prepare the **period-end adjusting JEs** at December 31 of Year 2 and Year 3 (emphasis). State any Year 1 entry.  
d. Show **balance-sheet presentation** of inventory at December 31, Year 3 (FIFO gross, allowance, net DVLIFO).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Four-step DVLIFO computation**

**Step 1 — Restate ending inventory at base-year dollars**

| Year | FIFO EI ÷ Index | Base-year dollars |
|------|----------------:|------------------:|
| Year 1 | \(120{,}000 / 1.00\) | **$120,000** |
| Year 2 | \(156{,}000 / 1.20\) | **130,000** |
| Year 3 | \(178{,}200 / 1.32\) | **135,000** |

**Step 2–4 — Layers, matching indices, restatement to DVLIFO dollars**

**Year 1 (base — initial recognition of base layer)**  
- Layer: base \(120{,}000 \times 1.00 = \$120{,}000\)  
- **DVLIFO EI = $120,000**

**Year 2**  
Base-year dollars \(130{,}000\) = base layer \(120{,}000\) + **new Year 2 layer** \(10{,}000\):

| Layer (base-year $) | Originating index | DVLIFO $ |
|--------------------:|------------------:|---------:|
| 120,000 (Y1) | 1.00 | \(120{,}000 \times 1.00 = 120{,}000\) |
| 10,000 (Y2) | 1.20 | \(10{,}000 \times 1.20 = 12{,}000\) |
| **Total** | | **$132,000** |

**Year 3**  
Base-year dollars \(135{,}000\) = \(120{,}000 + 10{,}000 +\) **new Year 3 layer** \(5{,}000\):

| Layer (base-year $) | Originating index | DVLIFO $ |
|--------------------:|------------------:|---------:|
| 120,000 (Y1) | 1.00 | \(120{,}000 \times 1.00 = 120{,}000\) |
| 10,000 (Y2) | 1.20 | \(10{,}000 \times 1.20 = 12{,}000\) |
| 5,000 (Y3) | 1.32 | \(5{,}000 \times 1.32 = 6{,}600\) |
| **Total** | | **$138,600** |

**Summary schedule**

| Year | FIFO EI | Base-year $ | DVLIFO EI |
|------|--------:|------------:|----------:|
| Year 1 | $120,000 | $120,000 | **$120,000** |
| Year 2 | 156,000 | 130,000 | **132,000** |
| Year 3 | 178,200 | 135,000 | **138,600** |

**b. LIFO reserve and period-end adjustments**

| Date | FIFO EI | DVLIFO EI | LIFO reserve (FIFO − DVLIFO) | Adjustment (Δ reserve) |
|------|--------:|----------:|-----------------------------:|-----------------------:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $120,000 | $120,000 | **$0** | **$0** |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 156,000 | 132,000 | **24,000** | **+24,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 178,200 | 138,600 | **39,600** | **+15,600** |

Checks: \(156{,}000 - 132{,}000 = 24{,}000\); \(178{,}200 - 138{,}600 = 39{,}600\); \(39{,}600 - 24{,}000 = 15{,}600\). ✓

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

**December 31, Year 1 — Base year / initial recognition**  
No LIFO reserve adjustment (FIFO = DVLIFO). Base layer of **$120,000** is established for future DVLIFO computations only.

**December 31, Year 2 — Adjust LIFO reserve (increase)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 24,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | | 24,000 |
| *Period-end: increase LIFO reserve to $24,000 ($156,000 FIFO − $132,000 DVLIFO)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 24,000 = Cr 24,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3 — Adjust LIFO reserve (increase)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 15,600 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | | 15,600 |
| *Period-end: increase LIFO reserve by $15,600 to cumulative $39,600* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,600 = Cr 15,600. **Balanced.**

**d. Balance-sheet presentation — December 31, Year 3**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Inventory (at FIFO) | $178,200 |
| Less: Allowance to reduce FIFO inventory to LIFO | (39,600) |
| **Inventory (at dollar-value LIFO)** | **$138,600** |

(Alternatively reported as a single line at DVLIFO **$138,600**, with LIFO reserve disclosed in notes.)

**Key insight:** DVLIFO is a **year-end conversion** of FIFO dollars using price indices and **layers**. Old layers always keep their **originating** indices. The only recurring book entry is the **period-end LIFO reserve adjustment** that plugs COGS so external inventory equals DVLIFO.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin multi-year DVLIFO with reserve roll-forward

**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin—base-year establishment; four-step layer schedule; period-end LIFO reserve adjusting JEs for Years 1–3  
**Scenario:**  
**Northpeak Supply LLC** (calendar year-end) adopts **dollar-value LIFO** for external reporting and taxes from the start of operations in **Year 1**, while maintaining **FIFO** internally for one inventory pool.

**Facts:**

| Year | FIFO ending inventory | Price index |
|------|----------------------:|------------:|
| Year 1 (base) | $80,000 | 1.00 |
| Year 2 | 99,000 | 1.10 |
| Year 3 | 120,000 | 1.25 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute DVLIFO ending inventory for each year (show base-year dollars and layers).  
b. Prepare a LIFO reserve roll-forward (balance and adjustment each year).  
c. Record the **period-end adjusting JEs** at December 31 of Year 2 and Year 3.

**Answer key:**  

**a. DVLIFO computations**

| Year | FIFO EI | ÷ Index | Base-year $ | Layers (base-year $ × index) | DVLIFO EI |
|------|--------:|--------:|------------:|------------------------------|----------:|
| Y1 | $80,000 | 1.00 | $80,000 | \(80{,}000 \times 1.00\) | **$80,000** |
| Y2 | 99,000 | 1.10 | 90,000 | \(80{,}000 \times 1.00 = 80{,}000\) + \(10{,}000 \times 1.10 = 11{,}000\) | **91,000** |
| Y3 | 120,000 | 1.25 | 96,000 | \(80{,}000 \times 1.00 = 80{,}000\) + \(10{,}000 \times 1.10 = 11{,}000\) + \(6{,}000 \times 1.25 = 7{,}500\) | **98,500** |

Layer build:  
- Y2 new layer = \(90{,}000 - 80{,}000 = 10{,}000\) base-year dollars  
- Y3 new layer = \(96{,}000 - 90{,}000 = 6{,}000\) base-year dollars  

Math checks: \(99{,}000 / 1.10 = 90{,}000\); \(120{,}000 / 1.25 = 96{,}000\); \(80{,}000 + 11{,}000 + 7{,}500 = 98{,}500\). ✓

**b. LIFO reserve roll-forward**

| Date | FIFO EI | DVLIFO EI | Ending LIFO reserve | Period adjustment |
|------|--------:|----------:|--------------------:|------------------:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $80,000 | $80,000 | $0 | $0 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 99,000 | 91,000 | **8,000** | **+8,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 120,000 | 98,500 | **21,500** | **+13,500** |

Checks: \(99{,}000 - 91{,}000 = 8{,}000\); \(120{,}000 - 98{,}500 = 21{,}500\); \(21{,}500 - 8{,}000 = 13{,}500\). ✓

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs**

**December 31, Year 1:** No entry (reserve = $0).

**December 31, Year 2**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 8,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | | 8,000 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve to $8,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 8,000 = Cr 8,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 13,500 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | | 13,500 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve by $13,500 to cumulative $21,500* | | |

**Check:** Dr 13,500 = Cr 13,500. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Same core path as Q1 with different numbers—restatement isolates **quantity** change in base-year dollars; new layers are costed at the **year they form**; period-end JE moves only the **Δ LIFO reserve** into COGS.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Layer liquidation (disposal of LIFO layers) and reverse period-end adjustment

**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Adoption of DVLIFO after FIFO years; multi-year layer build; **layer liquidation** when base-year dollars fall (disposal of newer layers, then base); **period-end adjusting JE** that **decreases** the LIFO reserve (emphasis alternate); COGS effect of liquidation  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverton Hardware Inc.** used FIFO for all purposes through **Year 1**. On **January 1, Year 2**, it adopted **dollar-value LIFO** for external reporting and income taxes (FIFO continued for internal management). The December 31, Year 1 FIFO inventory becomes the **base layer**. In Year 4, physical volume declines sharply and a **LIFO layer liquidation** occurs.

**Facts:**

| Year | FIFO ending inventory | Price index |
|------|----------------------:|------------:|
| Year 1 (base at adoption) | $100,000 | 1.00 |
| Year 2 | 126,000 | 1.05 |
| Year 3 | 156,000 | 1.20 |
| Year 4 | 99,000 | 1.10 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute DVLIFO ending inventory for Years 1–4. Explicitly identify which layers exist after the **Year 4 liquidation**.  
b. Prepare the LIFO reserve schedule (balance and **period-end adjustment**) for Years 2–4.  
c. Record the **period-end adjusting JEs** at December 31 of Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4 (include the **decrease** in Year 4).  
d. Explain in one sentence how Year 4 liquidation affects older unit costs in COGS relative to pure unit-LIFO without pooling.

**Answer key:**  

**a. DVLIFO ending inventory**

**Year 1 (base / initial layer at adoption)**  
\(100{,}000 / 1.00 = 100{,}000\) base-year $ → **DVLIFO = $100,000**

**Year 2**  
\(126{,}000 / 1.05 = 120{,}000\) base-year $  
Layers: \(100{,}000 \times 1.00 = 100{,}000\) + \(20{,}000 \times 1.05 = 21{,}000\)  
**DVLIFO = $121,000**

**Year 3**  
\(156{,}000 / 1.20 = 130{,}000\) base-year $  
Layers: \(100{,}000 \times 1.00 = 100{,}000\) + \(20{,}000 \times 1.05 = 21{,}000\) + \(10{,}000 \times 1.20 = 12{,}000\)  
**DVLIFO = $133,000**

**Year 4 — layer liquidation**  
\(99{,}000 / 1.10 = 90{,}000\) base-year $  

Base-year dollars **fell** from \(130{,}000\) to \(90{,}000\) → liquidate **entire** Year 3 layer (\(10{,}000\)), **entire** Year 2 layer (\(20{,}000\)), and **$10,000** of the Year 1 base layer (\(100{,}000 - 90{,}000 = 10{,}000\) liquidated from base).

Remaining after liquidation:

| Layer (base-year $) | Originating index | DVLIFO $ |
|--------------------:|------------------:|---------:|
| 90,000 (portion of Y1 base) | 1.00 | \(90{,}000 \times 1.00 = 90{,}000\) |
| **Total** | | **$90,000** |

(Year 2 index 1.05 and Year 3 index 1.20 are **not** used — those layers are gone.)

**Summary**

| Year | FIFO EI | Base-year $ | DVLIFO EI | Event |
|------|--------:|------------:|----------:|-------|
| Y1 | $100,000 | $100,000 | **$100,000** | Base established |
| Y2 | 126,000 | 120,000 | **121,000** | New layer |
| Y3 | 156,000 | 130,000 | **133,000** | New layer |
| Y4 | 99,000 | 90,000 | **90,000** | **Liquidation** of Y3, Y2, and part of Y1 |

**b. LIFO reserve schedule**

| Date | FIFO EI | DVLIFO EI | Ending reserve | Period-end adjustment |
|------|--------:|----------:|---------------:|----------------------:|
| Dec 31, Y1 | $100,000 | $100,000 | $0 | $0 (pre-adoption / base) |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 126,000 | 121,000 | **5,000** | **+5,000** |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 156,000 | 133,000 | **23,000** | **+18,000** |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 99,000 | 90,000 | **9,000** | **−14,000** |

Checks:  
\(126{,}000 - 121{,}000 = 5{,}000\);  
\(156{,}000 - 133{,}000 = 23{,}000\); \(23{,}000 - 5{,}000 = 18{,}000\);  
\(99{,}000 - 90{,}000 = 9{,}000\); \(9{,}000 - 23{,}000 = -14{,}000\). ✓

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis — includes reverse entry)**

**December 31, Year 2**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 5,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | | 5,000 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve to $5,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,000 = Cr 5,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 3**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 18,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | | 18,000 |
| *Increase LIFO reserve by $18,000 to $23,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 18,000 = Cr 18,000. **Balanced.**

**December 31, Year 4 — Decrease LIFO reserve (liquidation year)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce FIFO Inventory to LIFO | 14,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 14,000 |
| *Decrease LIFO reserve by $14,000 to $9,000 after layer liquidation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 14,000 = Cr 14,000. **Balanced.**

**Allowance T-account roll-forward:** \(5{,}000 + 18{,}000 - 14{,}000 = 9{,}000\) ending credit balance. ✓

**d. Liquidation vs unit LIFO**  
Dollar-value LIFO **pools** inventory dollars so liquidation is measured only after stripping price change via the index; a decline in one SKU need not liquidate a low-cost base layer if other pool items replace it in base-year dollars—unlike unit-cost LIFO, where each item’s layers liquidate separately.

**Key insight:** When base-year dollars **fall**, layers are disposed **LIFO-style** (newest first). The period-end entry is still only the **change in the LIFO reserve**—and that change can be a **debit to the allowance / credit to COGS** when the reserve shrinks after liquidation (or after deflation of the FIFO–LIFO gap).

### Q4 — MC — Method / classification: originating index for a DVLIFO layer

**LO:** LO 9-8  
**Concept:** Classification/method—each inventory layer is restated using the **price index of the year the layer originated**, not the current-year index  
**Question:**  
AspenForge Co. uses dollar-value LIFO. At December 31, Year 3, its inventory in base-year dollars consists of a Year 1 base layer of $50,000, a Year 2 layer of $8,000, and a Year 3 layer of $4,000. Price indices were 1.00 (Y1), 1.12 (Y2), and 1.25 (Y3). What is dollar-value LIFO ending inventory at December 31, Year 3?

- A) $77,500  
- B) $63,960  
- C) $62,000  
- D) $55,000  

**Answer:** **B. $63,960.**  
Each layer is restated with its **originating** index (Steps 3–4):  
\(50{,}000 \times 1.00 = 50{,}000\); \(8{,}000 \times 1.12 = 8{,}960\); \(4{,}000 \times 1.25 = 5{,}000\); total \(50{,}000 + 8{,}960 + 5{,}000 = \$63{,}960\).  

- A is wrong: multiplies **all** base-year dollars by the current Year 3 index (\(62{,}000 \times 1.25\)).  
- C is wrong: stops at base-year dollars (skips restatement to LIFO dollars).  
- D is wrong: applies only the base index to every layer.
