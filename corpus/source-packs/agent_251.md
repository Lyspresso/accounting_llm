# Agent 251 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-6

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute EPS using if-converted method for convertible securities  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Complex capital structure:** convertible debt or convertible preferred that can increase common shares and potentially dilute EPS
- **If-converted method (convertible debt):** assume conversion at beginning of period (or issue date if later); **add back interest expense net of tax** to the numerator; **add potential common shares** to the denominator
- **If-converted method (convertible preferred):** add back preferred dividends deducted in basic EPS (no tax adjustment); add potential common shares
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** contractual interest schedule on convertible debt; diluted EPS reconciliation schedule (basic → effect of convertibles → diluted EPS)
- **Initial recognition JE:** issue convertible bonds at par; issue convertible preferred stock
- **Period-end adjusting / cash interest JE:** record interest expense and payment (or accrual) on convertible debt; preferred dividend declaration/payment
- **Partial-year weighting:** if convertibles issued mid-year, weight **both** after-tax interest add-back **and** incremental shares by time outstanding
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** actual conversion of bonds or preferred into common stock
- **Dilutive vs antidilutive:** include only if diluted EPS **decreases**; with a **loss from continuing operations**, potential shares are **excluded** (always antidilutive to a loss)
- **Presentation / disclosure:** complex structure reports **basic and diluted EPS**; disclose antidilutive convertibles omitted from diluted EPS

### Q1 — CORE — Convertible bonds: interest schedule, if-converted EPS reconciliation (emphasis), partial-year, conversion JE
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedules for convertible-bond interest and diluted EPS under the if-converted method; initial recognition at par; period-end interest JEs; full-year and mid-year if-converted weighting; conversion settlement JE; income-statement EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Precision Instruments Inc.** is a calendar-year public company. Its only potentially dilutive instrument is convertible debt (complex capital structure). Common stock has a **$1 par** value. No preferred stock and no options/warrants.

**Facts for Year 1 (full-year bonds):**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Riverbend issued **1,500** convertible bonds, each with **$1,000** face (**$1,500,000** total face), **6%** stated annual interest, for **cash at par**. Each bond is convertible into **30** shares of common stock. Maturity is December 31, Year 10. Interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31.
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding during Year 1 (before if-converted assumptions): **250,000**.
3. Net income for Year 1: **$750,000** (already after interest expense on the convertible bonds and after income taxes).
4. Enacted tax rate: **25%**.

**Alternate fact (part g only):** Same bonds are issued on **July 1, Year 1** (not January 1). Semiannual interest is paid December 31 for the six months outstanding. Net income for Year 1 under this alternate fact is **$783,750** (higher than $750,000 because only six months of after-tax interest is incurred). Weighted-average common shares remain **250,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible bonds at par. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement interest schedule** for Year 1 (face, rate, interest expense, cash paid, carrying amount). Prepare the **June 30** and **December 31** cash interest payment JEs.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** for Year 1 (full-year bonds facts).  
d. Prepare the **diluted EPS reconciliation schedule** using the **if-converted method** (emphasis). State whether the bonds are **dilutive** or **antidilutive**.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** What EPS amount(s) does Riverbend report on the face of its Year 1 income statement?  
f. On **January 1, Year 2**, all bondholders convert their bonds into common stock. Prepare the **conversion (settlement) JE**. (Ignore any interest on January 1.)  
g. Using the **July 1 issuance alternate facts**, recompute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** under the if-converted method with **partial-year weighting**. Show the subsequent measurement / reconciliation schedule.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — convertible bonds at par (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,500,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 1,500,000 |
| *Issue 1,500 of $1,000 face 6% convertible bonds at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500,000 = Cr 1,500,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement — interest schedule and period-end interest JEs (emphasis)**

Annual contractual interest = \( \$1{,}500{,}000 \times 6\% = \mathbf{\$90{,}000} \)  
Semiannual cash interest = \( \$90{,}000 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$45{,}000} \)

**Interest schedule (Year 1 — bonds at par; no premium/discount amortization)**

| Period | Face outstanding | Rate | Interest expense | Cash paid | Carrying amount end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 issue | $1,500,000 | — | — | — | $1,500,000 |
| Jan 1 – Jun 30 | 1,500,000 | 6% × 6/12 | 45,000 | 45,000 | 1,500,000 |
| Jul 1 – Dec 31 | 1,500,000 | 6% × 6/12 | 45,000 | 45,000 | 1,500,000 |
| **Year total** | | | **90,000** | **90,000** | |

*June 30, Year 1 — pay semiannual interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 45,000 | |
| Cash | | 45,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 45,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 1 — pay semiannual interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 45,000 | |
| Cash | | 45,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 45,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Basic EPS (full-year bonds)**

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$750{,}000}{250{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.00}
\]

**d. Diluted EPS — if-converted method reconciliation schedule (emphasis)**

Assume conversion as of **January 1, Year 1**:

- **Numerator add-back (interest, net of tax):**  
  \( \$90{,}000 \times (1 - 0.25) = \$90{,}000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{\$67{,}500} \)
- **Denominator add (new common shares):**  
  \( 1{,}500 \text{ bonds} \times 30 \text{ shares} = \mathbf{45{,}000} \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $750,000 | 250,000 | $3.00 |
| Effect of convertible bonds: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 67,500 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 45,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$817,500** | **295,000** | **$2.77** |

Math check: \( 817{,}500 \div 295{,}000 = 2.771186\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.77} \) (rounded to nearest cent).  
Cross-check: \( 295{,}000 \times \$2.77 = \$817{,}150 \) (rounding residual of $350 on last-cent rounding — report **$2.77** as \(817{,}500 / 295{,}000\) to two decimals).

**Dilutive test:** Diluted EPS **$2.77** < basic EPS **$3.00** → bonds are **dilutive**. Include in diluted EPS.

**e. Presentation**

Riverbend has a **complex capital structure**. It reports **both**:
- Basic EPS **$3.00**
- Diluted EPS **$2.77**

on the face of the Year 1 income statement.

**f. Conversion settlement JE (January 1, Year 2)**

Shares issued = \( 1{,}500 \times 30 = 45{,}000 \)  
Common stock at par = \( 45{,}000 \times \$1 = \$45{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$1{,}500{,}000 - \$45{,}000 = \$1{,}455{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 1,500,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 45,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 1,455,000 |
| *Convert all bonds into 45,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500,000 = Cr 45,000 + 1,455,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Partial-year issuance (July 1) — if-converted subsequent measurement / reconciliation schedule**

Bonds outstanding only **6/12** of the year under the if-converted assumption (issue date if later):

- After-tax interest add-back = \( \$67{,}500 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{\$33{,}750} \)  
  (equivalently \( \$45{,}000 \times 0.75 = \$33{,}750 \))
- Incremental shares weighted = \( 45{,}000 \times 6/12 = \mathbf{22{,}500} \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $783,750 | 250,000 | $3.14 |
| Effect of convertible bonds (6/12): | | | |
| Add back prorated interest, net of tax | 33,750 | | |
| Add prorated common shares | | 22,500 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$817,500** | **272,500** | **$3.00** |

Basic: \( 783{,}750 \div 250{,}000 = 3.135 \approx \mathbf{\$3.14} \)  
Diluted: \( 817{,}500 \div 272{,}500 = \mathbf{\$3.00} \) exactly.

**Dilutive:** $3.00 < $3.14 → include. Report basic **$3.14** and diluted **$3.00**.

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedules** are the core deliverable for LO 20-6: (1) the contractual interest roll on convertible debt feeds the **after-tax interest add-back**, and (2) the **diluted EPS reconciliation schedule** shows basic EPS → if-converted adjustments → diluted EPS. Mid-year issues weight **both** the numerator add-back and the potential shares. Actual conversion is a separate **balance-sheet settlement JE**; diluted EPS already assumes conversion for the period the debt was outstanding.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Convertible bonds if-converted twin
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: subsequent measurement interest schedule and if-converted diluted EPS reconciliation; initial recognition of convertible bonds at par; period-end interest JE; conversion settlement JE; EPS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Copperpeak Materials Group** (calendar-year) issued convertible debt on **January 1, Year 1**. Common stock has a **$2 par** value.

**Facts:**
1. Issued **2,500** of **4%**, **$1,000** face convertible bonds (**$2,500,000** total) **at par** for cash. Each bond converts into **25** shares of common stock. Interest is paid **annually** on December 31.
2. Weighted-average common shares outstanding in Year 1: **400,000**.
3. Net income for Year 1: **$1,200,000** (after interest and taxes).
4. Tax rate: **30%**.
5. No preferred stock; no other dilutives.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** for the January 1 bond issue.  
b. Prepare the **subsequent measurement interest schedule** for Year 1 and the **December 31 interest payment JE**.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** (if-converted). Show the **reconciliation schedule** and dilutive test.  
d. State the EPS amounts reported on the income statement.  
e. Prepare the JE if **all** bonds convert on **January 1, Year 2**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,500,000 | |
| Bonds Payable | | 2,500,000 |
| *Issue 2,500 of $1,000 face 4% convertible bonds at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,500,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement — interest schedule and December 31 interest JE**

Interest expense = \( \$2{,}500{,}000 \times 4\% = \mathbf{\$100{,}000} \)

| Period | Face outstanding | Rate | Interest expense | Cash paid | Carrying amount end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 issue | $2,500,000 | — | — | — | $2,500,000 |
| Year 1 | 2,500,000 | 4% | 100,000 | 100,000 | 2,500,000 |
| **Year total** | | | **100,000** | **100,000** | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 100,000 |
| *Pay annual interest on convertible bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 100,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Basic and diluted EPS (if-converted reconciliation schedule — emphasis)**

After-tax interest add-back = \( \$100{,}000 \times (1 - 0.30) = \$100{,}000 \times 0.70 = \mathbf{\$70{,}000} \)  
New common shares = \( 2{,}500 \times 25 = \mathbf{62{,}500} \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $1,200,000 | 400,000 | $3.00 |
| Effect of convertible bonds: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 70,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 62,500 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$1,270,000** | **462,500** | **$2.75** |

Basic: \( 1{,}200{,}000 \div 400{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3.00} \)  
Diluted: \( 1{,}270{,}000 \div 462{,}500 = 2.745945\ldots \approx \mathbf{\$2.75} \)

**Dilutive test:** $2.75 < $3.00 → **dilutive**. Include in diluted EPS.

**d. Presentation**

Report **basic EPS $3.00** and **diluted EPS $2.75** on the face of the Year 1 income statement.

**e. Conversion JE — January 1, Year 2**

Shares issued = 62,500  
Common stock at par = \( 62{,}500 \times \$2 = \$125{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$2{,}500{,}000 - \$125{,}000 = \$2{,}375{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonds Payable | 2,500,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 125,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 2,375,000 |
| *Convert all bonds into 62,500 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,500,000 = Cr 125,000 + 2,375,000. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** Same if-converted mechanics as Q1 with fully changed inputs (face, coupon, tax rate, conversion ratio, shares, income, annual vs semiannual interest). Always recompute after-tax interest as \( \text{interest} \times (1 - t) \), build the **EPS reconciliation schedule**, and test dilution against basic EPS.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Convertible preferred if-converted schedule; conversion settlement; loss-year antidilution & disclosure
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement diluted EPS reconciliation for convertible preferred (if-converted); preferred issuance and dividend JEs; conversion settlement JE; diluted EPS when company has a net loss (antidilutive exclusion and disclosure)  
**Scenario:**  
**Marigold Transit Holdings** (calendar-year) has convertible preferred stock and, in a separate scenario year, convertible bonds with a loss.

**Part A — Convertible preferred (Year 1, profitable)**  
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Marigold issued **12,000** shares of **5%**, **$100** par **cumulative convertible preferred stock** for cash at **par** (**$1,200,000**). Each preferred share is convertible into **3** shares of **$1** par common stock.  
2. Net income for Year 1: **$720,000**.  
3. Weighted-average common shares outstanding: **180,000**.  
4. Preferred dividends for Year 1 are declared and paid in cash (cumulative preferred — annual dividend is earned/deducted for basic EPS).  
5. Tax rate: **25%** (not needed for preferred if-converted numerator).  
6. No convertible debt in Part A.

**Part B — Actual conversion (January 1, Year 2)**  
All 12,000 preferred shares convert into common stock on January 1, Year 2.

**Part C — Net loss year with convertible bonds (antidilutive)**  
In a different year, Marigold reports a **net loss of $480,000**. Throughout that year it has outstanding **800** of **5%**, **$1,000** bonds issued at par, each convertible into **25** common shares. Weighted-average common shares: **160,000**. Tax rate **25%**. Assume sufficient taxable income so interest is deductible for tax purposes if one were to compute a hypothetical diluted figure; apply the **loss-from-continuing-operations rule** for reporting.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for issuance of the convertible preferred stock.  
b. Prepare the **period-end (declaration and payment) JE** for Year 1 preferred dividends.  
c. Compute **basic EPS** and **diluted EPS** (if-converted) for Year 1. Show the **subsequent measurement / reconciliation schedule** and dilutive test.  
d. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 conversion settlement JE** for the preferred stock.  
e. For Part C (loss year): compute the **hypothetical** if-converted diluted figure, explain why it is **antidilutive**, state the **reported** basic and diluted EPS, and describe the **disclosure** for the omitted convertibles.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — convertible preferred at par (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,200,000 | |
| Preferred Stock ($100 par) | | 1,200,000 |
| *Issue 12,000 shares of 5%, $100 par cumulative convertible preferred at par* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,200,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Preferred dividends Year 1 (period-end)**

Annual preferred dividend = \( 12{,}000 \times \$100 \times 5\% = \mathbf{\$60{,}000} \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Cash Dividends—Preferred) | 60,000 | |
| Cash | | 60,000 |
| *Declare and pay annual preferred dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 60,000. **Balanced.**

(If declaration and payment are split: Dr Retained Earnings / Cr Dividends Payable 60,000; then Dr Dividends Payable / Cr Cash 60,000 — each balances.)

**c. Basic and diluted EPS — convertible preferred if-converted schedule (emphasis)**

Income available to common (basic) = \( \$720{,}000 - \$60{,}000 = \mathbf{\$660{,}000} \)

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$660{,}000}{180{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.67}
\]

(\( 660{,}000 \div 180{,}000 = 3.6\overline{6} \approx \$3.67 \) rounded to nearest cent.)

If-converted assumptions (conversion as of January 1, Year 1):

- **Numerator:** add back preferred dividends **$60,000** → income for diluted = \( \$660{,}000 + \$60{,}000 = \$720{,}000 \) (equivalently, start from NI and do not deduct preferred dividends)  
- **Denominator:** add \( 12{,}000 \times 3 = 36{,}000 \) common shares → \( 180{,}000 + 36{,}000 = 216{,}000 \)

| | Income available to common | Weighted-average shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $660,000 | 180,000 | $3.67 |
| Effect of convertible preferred: | | | |
| Add back preferred dividends | 60,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 36,000 | |
| **Diluted EPS** | **$720,000** | **216,000** | **$3.33** |

Diluted: \( 720{,}000 \div 216{,}000 = 3.3\overline{3} \approx \mathbf{\$3.33} \)

**Dilutive test:** $3.33 < $3.67 → **dilutive**. Report basic **$3.67** and diluted **$3.33**.

*(Note: Preferred dividends have **no tax add-back adjustment** — dividends are not tax-deductible.)*

**d. Conversion settlement JE — January 1, Year 2**

Common shares issued = \( 12{,}000 \times 3 = 36{,}000 \)  
Common stock at par = \( 36{,}000 \times \$1 = \$36{,}000 \)  
APIC = \( \$1{,}200{,}000 - \$36{,}000 = \$1{,}164{,}000 \)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock ($100 par) | 1,200,000 | |
| Common Stock ($1 par) | | 36,000 |
| APIC—Common Stock | | 1,164,000 |
| *Convert 12,000 preferred shares into 36,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,200,000 = Cr 36,000 + 1,164,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Net loss year — antidilutive convertible bonds (Part C)**

Contractual interest = \( 800 \times \$1{,}000 \times 5\% = \$40{,}000 \)  
After-tax interest (hypothetical) = \( \$40{,}000 \times 0.75 = \$30{,}000 \)  
Potential shares = \( 800 \times 25 = 20{,}000 \)

| | “Income” (loss) available | Shares | Per share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic EPS | $(480,000) | 160,000 | $(3.00) |
| Hypothetical if-converted: | | | |
| Add back interest, net of tax | 30,000 | | |
| Add new common shares | | 20,000 | |
| Hypothetical “diluted” | $(450,000) | 180,000 | $(2.50) |

**Antidilutive analysis:** Including the convertibles **improves** (makes less negative) loss per share from **$(3.00)** to **$(2.50)**. That is **antidilutive**.

**ASC 260 rule:** When there is a **loss from continuing operations**, potential common shares are **not** included in diluted EPS (they are always antidilutive to a loss).

**Reported amounts:** Basic EPS = Diluted EPS = **$(3.00)**

**Disclosure:** Disclose the convertible bonds (terms, conversion ratio, that they were **excluded** from diluted EPS because they were **antidilutive** for the period). Full terms are required even though the securities are omitted from the diluted computation.

**Key insight:** Convertible **preferred** uses if-converted by restoring **preferred dividends** (no tax gross-up) and adding shares on the **EPS reconciliation schedule**. Convertible **debt** restores **after-tax interest**. In a **loss year**, potential shares are left out of diluted EPS; report the same loss per share for basic and diluted and **disclose** the antidilutive convertibles.

### Q4 — MC (method and dilution classification)
**LO:** LO 20-6  
**Concept:** Classification — which computational method applies to convertible securities; identifying dilutive vs antidilutive outcomes  

**Question 1:**  
In computing **diluted EPS**, the effect of **convertible bonds** and **convertible preferred stock** is incorporated using which method?

- A) Treasury stock method  
- B) If-converted method  
- C) Two-class method only (no conversion assumption)  
- D) Retrospective restatement method  

**Answer:** **B.** The **if-converted method** assumes conversion at the beginning of the period (or issuance if later), adjusts the numerator (after-tax interest for debt; preferred dividends for convertible preferred), and adds the resulting common shares to the denominator. The treasury stock method applies to options/warrants (LO 20-7), not to convertibles.

**Question 2:**  
Trestle Co. has basic EPS of **$4.10**. Including its only convertible preferred issue under the if-converted method would produce EPS of **$4.25**. How should Trestle treat the preferred stock in diluted EPS?

- A) Include the preferred; report diluted EPS of $4.25  
- B) Include the preferred; report diluted EPS of $4.10  
- C) Exclude the preferred as **antidilutive**; diluted EPS equals basic EPS of $4.10 (absent other dilutives)  
- D) Exclude the preferred and do not disclose them  

**Answer:** **C.** A security is **dilutive** only if its inclusion **reduces** EPS (or increases loss per share). Here $4.25 > $4.10, so the convertible preferred is **antidilutive** and is **excluded** from diluted EPS. Antidilutive convertibles still require **disclosure** of terms; they are not silently ignored in the notes.
