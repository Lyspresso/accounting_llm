# Agent 49 — CORE DEMO — LO 9-2

**Chapter:** 9  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting in a periodic inventory system  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Periodic system: temporary purchase accounts until physical count
- **Initial recognition** of purchases (gross method): Purchases, Freight-In, Accounts Payable
- Settlement: pay within/after discount; purchase returns and allowances
- Sales under periodic: revenue only at sale (no COGS entry until period end)
- **Period-end adjusting JE**: close temps, replace BI with EI, residual COGS
- COGS schedule: BI + Purchases, net − EI
- **Net method**: record purchases net of discount; lost discount → Interest Expense (finance charge)
- Classification/presentation: temporary contra-purchase accounts; Interest Expense vs COGS; Inventory on balance sheet

### Q1 — CORE — Periodic system, gross method: full-month cycle (initial recognition emphasis)
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of purchases/freight under gross method; AP settlement; period-end inventory adjusting entry and COGS  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Outfitters Co.** uses a **periodic inventory system** and the **gross method** for purchase discounts. Fiscal month is **July**. Beginning inventory on July 1 is **$4,200**.

Chronological transactions:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| July 3 | Purchased merchandise on account, **$12,000**, terms **2/10, n/30**. |
| July 3 | Paid freight of **$450** cash on the July 3 purchase (f.o.b. shipping point; buyer pays). |
| July 12 | Paid **half** of the July 3 invoice within the discount period. |
| July 15 | Paid **$4,000** of the remaining accounts payable **after** the discount period. |
| July 18 | Returned defective goods and received a credit of **$800** from the supplier (purchase return). |
| July 22 | Sold merchandise on account for **$9,500**. |
| July 31 | Physical inventory count: ending inventory cost = **$6,350**. |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare **all journal entries through July 22** (purchases through sale). Emphasize initial recognition under the gross method.  
b. Compute **purchases, net** and prepare the **cost of goods sold schedule** for July.  
c. Prepare the **July 31 period-end adjusting entry** that records ending inventory, closes temporary purchase accounts and beginning inventory, and records cost of goods sold.  
d. State the July 31 balance-sheet amount for **Inventory** and the income-statement amount for **Cost of goods sold**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Journal entries through July 22 (gross method)**

*July 3 — Purchase of inventory (initial recognition, gross)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchases | 12,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 12,000 |
| *Record purchase at invoice gross amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,000.

*July 3 — Freight-in (initial recognition of inventoriable transportation-in)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Freight-In | 450 | |
| Cash | | 450 |
| *Buyer pays shipping; f.o.b. shipping point* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 450.

*July 12 — Payment within discount period (half of $12,000 = $6,000)*  
Purchase discount = $6,000 × 2% = **$120**; cash = $6,000 − $120 = **$5,880**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 6,000 | |
| Purchase Discount | | 120 |
| Cash | | 5,880 |
| *Pay half within 2/10; reduce AP at gross, record discount taken* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 120 + 5,880. Balanced.

*July 15 — Payment after discount period*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 4,000 | |
| Cash | | 4,000 |
| *No discount; pay full gross amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,000.

*July 18 — Purchase return*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 800 | |
| Purchase Returns and Allowances | | 800 |
| *Return defective goods; contra-purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 800.

*AP rollforward (settlement):*  
12,000 − 6,000 − 4,000 − 800 = **$1,200** still payable (unpaid after return).

*July 22 — Sale (periodic: revenue only)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 9,500 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 9,500 |
| *Periodic system: no COGS entry at sale* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,500.

**b. Purchases, net and COGS schedule**

Purchases, net = Purchases + Freight-In − Purchase discounts − Purchase returns and allowances  
= $12,000 + $450 − $120 − $800 = **$11,530**

| Cost of Goods Sold — July | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $4,200 |
| + Purchases, net | 11,530 |
| = Cost of goods available for sale | 15,730 |
| − Ending inventory (physical count) | (6,350) |
| = **Cost of goods sold** | **$9,380** |

**c. July 31 — Period-end inventory adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (ending) | 6,350 | |
| Purchase Discount | 120 | |
| Purchase Returns and Allowances | 800 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 9,380 | |
| Purchases | | 12,000 |
| Freight-In | | 450 |
| Inventory (beginning) | | 4,200 |
| *Close temporary purchase accounts; replace BI with EI; residual = COGS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,350 + 120 + 800 + 9,380 = **16,650**; Cr 12,000 + 450 + 4,200 = **16,650**. Balanced.

**d. Presentation amounts**  
- Balance sheet — Inventory (July 31): **$6,350**  
- Income statement — Cost of goods sold: **$9,380**

**Key insight:** Under the **gross** periodic method, purchases and AP are recorded at invoice gross; discounts taken and returns use **temporary contra accounts**. COGS is **not** booked at sale; after the physical count, one adjusting entry closes temps, swaps BI for EI, and plugs COGS as the residual (CGAS − EI).

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Periodic gross method: full cycle twin
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of periodic gross-method purchases, settlements, sale, and period-end COGS adjusting entry  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverbend Supply LLC** uses a **periodic inventory system** and the **gross method**. Month of **September**. Beginning inventory September 1: **$7,500**.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Sept 4 | Purchased merchandise on account, **$18,000**, terms **3/15, n/45**. |
| Sept 4 | Paid freight **$720** cash (f.o.b. shipping point). |
| Sept 18 | Paid **two-thirds** of the Sept 4 invoice within the discount period. |
| Sept 22 | Returned damaged goods; supplier credit **$1,200**. |
| Sept 28 | Paid the **remaining** accounts payable balance **after** the discount period. |
| Sept 29 | Cash sales of merchandise, **$14,000**. |
| Sept 30 | Physical count: ending inventory = **$9,100**. |

**Required:**  
a. All journal entries from Sept 4 through Sept 29.  
b. COGS schedule for September.  
c. Sept 30 period-end adjusting entry.  
d. Ending Inventory and COGS amounts for the financial statements.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Journal entries**

*Sept 4 — Purchase (gross)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchases | 18,000 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 18,000 |

**Check:** 18,000 = 18,000.

*Sept 4 — Freight-in*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Freight-In | 720 | |
| Cash | | 720 |

**Check:** 720 = 720.

*Sept 18 — Pay 2/3 within discount*  
Gross portion paid = $18,000 × 2/3 = **$12,000**  
Discount = $12,000 × 3% = **$360**  
Cash = $12,000 − $360 = **$11,640**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 12,000 | |
| Purchase Discount | | 360 |
| Cash | | 11,640 |

**Check:** 12,000 = 360 + 11,640.

*Sept 22 — Purchase return*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 1,200 | |
| Purchase Returns and Allowances | | 1,200 |

**Check:** 1,200 = 1,200.

*Remaining AP before final payment:* 18,000 − 12,000 − 1,200 = **$4,800**

*Sept 28 — Pay remaining AP after discount period*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 4,800 | |
| Cash | | 4,800 |

**Check:** 4,800 = 4,800. AP = 0.

*Sept 29 — Cash sale (revenue only)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 14,000 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 14,000 |

**Check:** 14,000 = 14,000.

**b. COGS schedule**

Purchases, net = 18,000 + 720 − 360 − 1,200 = **$17,160**

| Cost of Goods Sold — September | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $7,500 |
| + Purchases, net | 17,160 |
| = Cost of goods available for sale | 24,660 |
| − Ending inventory | (9,100) |
| = **Cost of goods sold** | **$15,560** |

**c. Sept 30 period-end adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (ending) | 9,100 | |
| Purchase Discount | 360 | |
| Purchase Returns and Allowances | 1,200 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 15,560 | |
| Purchases | | 18,000 |
| Freight-In | | 720 |
| Inventory (beginning) | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr 9,100 + 360 + 1,200 + 15,560 = **26,220**; Cr 18,000 + 720 + 7,500 = **26,220**. Balanced.

**d.** Inventory (Sept 30) = **$9,100**; COGS = **$15,560**.

**Key insight:** Same periodic gross-method mechanics as Q1 with all new amounts: discounts and returns reduce purchases, net; EI from the physical count drives the residual COGS plug in the closing entry.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Net method: initial purchase, lost-discount settlement, period-end close
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Periodic net method — record purchases net; lost discount as Interest Expense; period-end COGS adjusting entry; classification of expense  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar Peak Hardware Co.** uses a **periodic inventory system** and the **net method** for purchase discounts. Month of **October**. Beginning inventory October 1: **$5,000**.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Oct 5 | Purchased merchandise on account, list invoice **$10,000**, terms **2/10, n/30**. Record under the **net method**. |
| Oct 6 | Paid freight-in **$300** cash (f.o.b. shipping point). |
| Oct 14 | Paid **half** of the Oct 5 payable **within** the discount period. |
| Oct 20 | Paid the **remaining** half **after** the discount period (lost discount). |
| Oct 25 | Sold merchandise on account for **$8,200**. |
| Oct 31 | Physical inventory count: ending inventory = **$4,150**. |

**Required:**  
a. Journal entries for Oct 5 through Oct 25 under the **net method**.  
b. COGS schedule for October (treat lost discount correctly — it is **not** part of purchases, net).  
c. Oct 31 period-end adjusting entry.  
d. **Classification/presentation:** Identify where (1) Cost of goods sold and (2) the lost discount appear in the financial statements, and the Oct 31 Inventory balance-sheet amount.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Net-method journal entries**

*Oct 5 — Purchase recorded net of 2% discount*  
Net purchase = $10,000 × 0.98 = **$9,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchases | 9,800 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 9,800 |
| *Initial recognition at net-of-discount amount* | | |

**Check:** 9,800 = 9,800.

*Oct 6 — Freight-in*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Freight-In | 300 | |
| Cash | | 300 |

**Check:** 300 = 300.

*Oct 14 — Pay half within discount period*  
Half of net AP = $9,800 × 1/2 = **$4,900**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 4,900 | |
| Cash | | 4,900 |
| *No separate discount account; AP already net* | | |

**Check:** 4,900 = 4,900.

*Oct 20 — Pay remaining half after discount (lost discount = finance charge)*  
Gross half of invoice = $5,000; net AP remaining = $4,900; lost discount = **$100**  
Cash paid = **$5,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Payable | 4,900 | |
| Interest Expense | 100 | |
| Cash | | 5,000 |
| *Lost discount is Interest Expense (or Purchase Discount Lost), not inventory cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,900 + 100 = Cr 5,000. Balanced. AP = 0.

*Oct 25 — Sale (periodic: revenue only)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 8,200 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 8,200 |

**Check:** 8,200 = 8,200.

**b. COGS schedule**

Purchases, net (for COGS) = Purchases (already net) + Freight-In − R&A  
= $9,800 + $300 − $0 = **$10,100**  
(Note: $100 lost discount is **Interest Expense**, not a purchase discount reduction and not added to inventory cost.)

| Cost of Goods Sold — October | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $5,000 |
| + Purchases, net | 10,100 |
| = Cost of goods available for sale | 15,100 |
| − Ending inventory | (4,150) |
| = **Cost of goods sold** | **$10,950** |

**c. Oct 31 period-end adjusting entry**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (ending) | 4,150 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 10,950 | |
| Purchases | | 9,800 |
| Freight-In | | 300 |
| Inventory (beginning) | | 5,000 |

**Check:** Dr 4,150 + 10,950 = **15,100**; Cr 9,800 + 300 + 5,000 = **15,100**. Balanced.  
(No Purchase Discount temporary account under net method when all discounts either taken or lost via Interest Expense.)

**d. Classification / presentation**  
1. **Cost of goods sold $10,950** — income statement, operating cost of sales (gross profit computation).  
2. **Interest Expense $100** (lost discount) — income statement as a **financing cost**, not inventoriable and not COGS.  
3. **Inventory $4,150** — balance sheet, current asset (physical-count cost under the company’s cost-flow method).

**Key insight:** Under the **net method**, initial AP/Purchases are already net of available discounts. Paying late does **not** change purchases; the lost discount is **Interest Expense**. Period-end still allocates CGAS between EI and residual COGS after the physical count.

### Q4 — MC — Periodic system classification (optional)
**LO:** LO 9-2  
**Concept:** Classification of temporary accounts and timing of COGS recognition in a periodic system  

**Question 1:**  
In a **periodic** inventory system using the **gross** method, which set of accounts is used during the period (before the physical-count adjusting entry) to accumulate purchase activity?

- A) Inventory (debit for purchases) and Cost of Goods Sold (debit at each sale)  
- B) Purchases, Freight-In, Purchase Discounts, and Purchase Returns and Allowances (temporary accounts)  
- C) Only Inventory, updated continuously for every purchase and sale  
- D) Purchase Discounts Lost and Interest Payable only  

**Answer:** **B.** The periodic system uses temporary purchase accounts until the period-end count; Inventory is not debited for each purchase, and COGS is not recorded at each sale. A describes a hybrid of perpetual features. C is perpetual. D relates only to net-method lost discounts, not the full set of purchase accumulators.

---

**Question 2:**  
Cedar Peak (net method) lost a $100 purchase discount by paying after the discount period. How is that $100 **classified**?

- A) Debit to Purchases (increases inventoriable cost)  
- B) Debit to Cost of Goods Sold when the invoice is paid  
- C) Debit to Interest Expense (or Purchase Discount Lost) as a finance charge  
- D) Credit to Purchase Discount (contra-purchase)  

**Answer:** **C.** Under the net method, a discount not taken is recorded as **Interest Expense** (finance charge), not as inventory cost or COGS at payment. A would incorrectly inventoriable-ize a financing cost. B confuses payment with COGS timing. D is a gross-method account used when discounts are **taken**, not lost.
