# Agent 136 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-4

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase equity with insignificant influence at cost **including brokerage/commissions** (no significant influence → **FV-NI**, not equity method)
- **Dividend revenue:** cash dividends declared → **Dividend Revenue** in net income (investment account **not** reduced)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** cost basis vs fair value rollforward; required FVA; existing FVA; period adjustment
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Dr/Cr **Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities** and **Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income** so carrying amount = fair value
- **Disposal JE:** first **adjust to fair value (FV-NI)** at sale date, then record sale eliminating **Investment** and related **FVA** (no separate “realized gain” if already at FV)
- **Partial sale:** pro-rata cost of shares sold; FVA only on portion sold then re-establish FVA on remaining portfolio at period-end
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** BS short-term vs long-term by intent/ability; unrealized G/L in **net income** (not OCI); disclose portion of unrealized G/L on equity securities **still held** at reporting date; SCF investing vs operating by purpose

### Q1 — CORE — Equity FV-NI full life cycle: purchase, dividend, YE FVA schedule, presentation, sale
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition (cost + commissions); subsequent measurement / FVA schedule; period-end adjusting JE; disposal at FV-NI; classification/presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Rivermark Advisors LLC** is a calendar-year company. On **August 1, Year 1**, Rivermark purchases **4,000** of **Riverstone Tech Inc.**’s **50,000** outstanding common shares for **$28** per share and pays **brokerage commissions of $800**. Rivermark owns **8%** of Riverstone and **does not have significant influence**. Fair value of the shares is **readily determinable**. Rivermark measures the investment at **FV-NI**.

Additional facts:
1. On **November 20, Year 1**, Riverstone declares and pays a cash dividend of **$0.40 per share**.
2. On **December 31, Year 1**, Riverstone’s common stock fair value is **$31 per share**.
3. On **January 12, Year 2**, Rivermark sells the **entire** investment for **$125,500** cash.
4. Beginning FVA balance is **zero**. Rivermark expects to hold the shares as a temporary investment of excess cash (current asset) until sold.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **August 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** (include commissions in cost).  
(b) Record the **November 20, Year 1** dividend entry.  
(c) Prepare the **subsequent measurement / FVA schedule** through the sale date (cost basis, fair value, required FVA, existing FVA, adjustment). Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**.  
(d) Report the investment on the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet** and the related **Year 1 income statement** amounts (dividend revenue; unrealized holding G/L).  
(e) Record the **January 12, Year 2 disposal**: (i) adjust to fair value at sale date using FV-NI; (ii) record the sale and eliminate the associated FVA. Reconcile total unrealized G/L recognized over the holding period to (proceeds − cost).  
(f) **Classification / presentation:** Where is the unrealized holding gain reported (NI vs OCI)? What SCF classification is typical if Rivermark is **not** an active short-term trader of equities for trading profits?

**Answer key:**  

**(a) August 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition**  
Purchase cost = \((4{,}000 \times \$28) + \$800 = \$112{,}000 + \$800 = \mathbf{\$112{,}800}\)  
Ownership = \(4{,}000 / 50{,}000 = 8\%\) → **insignificant influence** → **FV-NI**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Equity Securities—Riverstone | 112,800 | |
| Cash | | 112,800 |
| *Purchase 4,000 shares including $800 commissions* | | |

**Check:** Dr 112,800 = Cr 112,800. **Balanced.**

**(b) November 20, Year 1 — Dividend revenue**  
Cash dividend = \(4{,}000 \times \$0.40 = \mathbf{\$1{,}600}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,600 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 1,600 |
| *Dividend on equity securities measured at FV-NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,600 = Cr 1,600. **Balanced.**  
*(Under FV-NI with insignificant influence, dividends are **revenue**, not a reduction of the investment.)*

**(c) Subsequent measurement schedule and December 31, Year 1 FVA**  

Fair value Dec 31, Year 1 = \(4{,}000 \times \$31 = \mathbf{\$124{,}000}\)  
Required FVA (debit) = \(124{,}000 - 112{,}800 = \mathbf{\$11{,}200}\)

| Date / event | Cost basis | Fair value | Required FVA Dr/(Cr) | Existing FVA | Adj. to FVA Dr/(Cr) | Unrealized G/(L) in NI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aug 1, Y1 purchase | 112,800 | 112,800 | 0 | 0 | — | — |
| Dec 31, Y1 period-end | 112,800 | 124,000 | 11,200 | 0 | **11,200** | **11,200** |
| Jan 12, Y2 pre-sale (see e) | 112,800 | 125,500 | 12,700 | 11,200 | **1,500** | **1,500** |

*December 31, Year 1 — Adjust to fair value (FV-NI)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 11,200 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 11,200 |
| *Mark equity investment to $124,000 FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,200 = Cr 11,200. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = cost + FVA debit = \(112{,}800 + 11{,}200 = \mathbf{\$124{,}000}\) = FV.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**  

**Balance sheet — December 31, Year 1 (current assets)**  
Investment in equity securities ……………… **$124,000**  
*(Investment $112,800 + Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities $11,200)*

**Income statement — Year ended December 31, Year 1 (other revenues/expenses)**  
Dividend revenue ………………………………… **$1,600**  
Unrealized holding gain on equity securities …… **$11,200**

**(e) January 12, Year 2 — Disposal (FV-NI then eliminate FVA)**  

Required FVA at sale\* = \(125{,}500 - 112{,}800 = \mathbf{\$12{,}700}\) debit  
Less existing FVA = **11,200**  
**Increase to FVA needed** = **$1,500**

\*$125,500 sale price (fair value) − $112,800 original cost.

*(i) Adjust to fair value at sale date*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 1,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 1,500 |

**Check:** Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500. **Balanced.**  
FVA now = \(11{,}200 + 1{,}500 = \$12{,}700\); carrying amount = \(112{,}800 + 12{,}700 = \$125{,}500\).

*(ii) Record sale and eliminate FVA (no separate realized gain—already in earnings via FV-NI)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 125,500 | |
| Investment in Equity Securities—Riverstone | | 112,800 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 12,700 |
| *Sell entire equity position; clear cost and FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 125,500 = Cr 112,800 + 12,700. **Balanced.**

**Holding-period G/L reconciliation:**  
Unrealized gain in Year 1 income ……………… $11,200  
Unrealized gain in Year 2 income ……………… 1,500  
**Total gain recognized over ownership period** … **$12,700**  
= proceeds \(125{,}500\) − cost \(112{,}800\). ✓

**(f) Classification / presentation / SCF**  
- Unrealized holding gains/losses on equity securities measured at **FV-NI** are reported in **net income** (other income/expense), **not** in OCI.  
- Because Rivermark is not engaged in active short-term trading for profits, cash paid to acquire and cash received on sale are typically classified as **investing activities** on the statement of cash flows (if actively trading equities for short-term profits, operating classification may apply).  
- Disclosure: portion of unrealized G/L for the period related to equity securities **still held** at the reporting date (here, at Dec 31 Year 1, the entire $11,200 relates to securities still held).

**Key insight:** Equity with **insignificant influence** and readily determinable fair value is **FV-NI**: cost includes incidental purchase costs; dividends → revenue; every reporting date (and sale date) **FVA** pushes fair-value change into **earnings**; sale clears cost + FVA against cash so cumulative NI equals cash profit/loss over the life.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Equity FV-NI with unrealized loss then partial recovery at sale
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition + FVA schedule + period-end JE + disposal (number twin; loss then partial recovery)  
**Scenario:**  
On **March 1, Year 1**, **Copperline Holdings Corp.** (calendar year) purchases **6,000** of **Brookfield Media Co.**’s **80,000** outstanding common shares for **$15.50** per share and pays **commissions of $1,200**. Copperline owns **7.5%** and has **insignificant influence**. Fair value is readily determinable; measure at **FV-NI**. Beginning FVA = **0**.

Facts:
1. On **June 30, Year 1**, Brookfield declares and pays total cash dividends of **$48,000** on all outstanding shares.
2. On **December 31, Year 1**, fair value is **$14 per share**.
3. On **February 8, Year 2**, Copperline sells the entire holding for **$86,400** cash.

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase JE on March 1, Year 1.  
(b) Dividend JE on June 30, Year 1 (Copperline’s share).  
(c) Subsequent measurement / FVA schedule through sale; Dec 31 Year 1 adjusting JE.  
(d) Year 1 BS carrying amount and IS effects (dividend + unrealized G/L).  
(e) February 8 Year 2: FV-NI sale-date adjustment, then sale with FVA elimination; holding-period reconciliation.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) March 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  
Cost = \((6{,}000 \times \$15.50) + \$1{,}200 = \$93{,}000 + \$1{,}200 = \mathbf{\$94{,}200}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Equity Securities—Brookfield | 94,200 | |
| Cash | | 94,200 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 94,200. **Balanced.**

**(b) June 30, Year 1 — Dividend**  
Ownership = \(6{,}000 / 80{,}000 = 7.5\%\)  
Dividend revenue = \(0.075 \times \$48{,}000 = \mathbf{\$3{,}600}\)  
*(Equivalently: \(48{,}000 / 80{,}000 = \$0.60\) per share; \(6{,}000 \times \$0.60 = \$3{,}600\).)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,600 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 3,600 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,600. **Balanced.**

**(c) FVA schedule and December 31, Year 1 entry**  
Fair value YE = \(6{,}000 \times \$14 = \mathbf{\$84{,}000}\)  
Required FVA = \(84{,}000 - 94{,}200 = \mathbf{\$(10{,}200)}\) (credit)

| Date / event | Cost basis | Fair value | Required FVA Dr/(Cr) | Existing FVA | Adj. to FVA Dr/(Cr) | Unrealized G/(L) in NI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar 1, Y1 purchase | 94,200 | 94,200 | 0 | 0 | — | — |
| Dec 31, Y1 period-end | 94,200 | 84,000 | (10,200) | 0 | **(10,200)** | **(10,200)** |
| Feb 8, Y2 pre-sale | 94,200 | 86,400 | (7,800) | (10,200) | **2,400** | **2,400** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 10,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 10,200 |
| *Mark equity investment down to $84,000 FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,200. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount = \(94{,}200 - 10{,}200 = \mathbf{\$84{,}000}\).

**(d) Year 1 presentation**  
**BS:** Investment in equity securities **$84,000**  
**IS:** Dividend revenue **$3,600**; Unrealized holding loss on equity securities **$(10,200)**

**(e) February 8, Year 2 — Sale**  

Required FVA at sale = \(86{,}400 - 94{,}200 = \mathbf{\$(7{,}800)}\) credit  
Existing FVA = **(10,200)** credit  
Increase (decrease) to FVA = \(-7{,}800 - (-10{,}200) = \mathbf{+\$2{,}400}\)

*Adjust to fair value at sale date*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 2,400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 2,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 2,400. **Balanced.**  
FVA ending credit = \(10{,}200 - 2{,}400 = \$7{,}800\); carrying amount = \(94{,}200 - 7{,}800 = \$86{,}400\).

*Sale and eliminate FVA*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 86,400 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 7,800 | |
| Investment in Equity Securities—Brookfield | | 94,200 |

**Check:** Dr 86,400 + 7,800 = Cr 94,200. **Balanced.**

**Holding-period reconciliation:**  
Year 1 unrealized loss …… \((10{,}200)\)  
Year 2 unrealized gain …… \(2{,}400\)  
**Net loss over ownership** …… **\((7{,}800)\)** = cost \(94{,}200\) − proceeds \(86{,}400\). ✓

**Key insight:** Same FV-NI pipeline as Q1 with all new numbers. When FV falls, FVA is a **credit** valuation account; a later sale-date recovery reduces the credit FVA and books an unrealized **gain** in Year 2 even though the overall cash result may still be a net loss versus cost.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial sale mid-year + period-end FVA on remaining shares
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule with partial disposal; sale-date FV-NI on shares sold; period-end FVA on remaining portfolio; dividend on shares held  
**Scenario:**  
On **September 1, Year 1**, **Lakeshore Equity Partners** purchases **5,000** shares of **Meridian Cloud Inc.** common stock for a total cost of **$200,000** (includes all incidental costs). Lakeshore has **insignificant influence** and measures the investment at **FV-NI**. Accounting year-end is **December 31**. Beginning FVA = **0**.

Facts:
1. On **November 15, Year 1**, Lakeshore sells **2,000** shares for **$43 per share** (**$86,000** cash).  
2. On **December 15, Year 1**, Meridian declares and pays a cash dividend of **$2.50 per share** (Lakeshore still holds **3,000** shares).  
3. On **December 31, Year 1**, fair value of the **remaining** Meridian shares is **$129,000**.

**Required:**  
(a) September 1 purchase JE.  
(b) November 15 sale: (i) adjust the **shares sold** to fair value at the sale date; (ii) record the sale eliminating cost and related FVA for the portion sold. Show the cost allocated to shares sold.  
(c) December 15 dividend JE.  
(d) Prepare a **subsequent measurement / portfolio schedule** for sold vs remaining shares; record the December 31 period-end FVA on the **remaining** investment.  
(e) Summarize **Year 1 net income effects** from this investment (unrealized G/L + dividend revenue).  
(f) **Classification:** Is the remaining investment reported at cost or fair value on the Dec 31 balance sheet?

**Answer key:**  

**(a) September 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  
Cost basis total = **$200,000** → cost per share = \(200{,}000 / 5{,}000 = \$40\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Equity Securities—Meridian | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 200,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) November 15, Year 1 — Partial sale (2,000 of 5,000 shares)**  
Cost of shares sold = \(2{,}000 / 5{,}000 \times \$200{,}000 = \mathbf{\$80{,}000}\)  
*(Or \(2{,}000 \times \$40 = \$80{,}000\).)*  
Sale proceeds = \(2{,}000 \times \$43 = \mathbf{\$86{,}000}\)  
Required FVA on **sold** portion = \(86{,}000 - 80{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\) debit  
Existing FVA (portfolio) = **0** → increase FVA **$6,000**.

*(i) Adjust sold shares to fair value (FV-NI)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 6,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 6,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,000. **Balanced.**

*(ii) Record sale; eliminate cost and FVA for sold shares*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 86,000 | |
| Investment in Equity Securities—Meridian | | 80,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 6,000 |

**Check:** Dr 86,000 = Cr 80,000 + 6,000. **Balanced.**  
After sale: Investment cost balance = \(200{,}000 - 80{,}000 = \mathbf{\$120{,}000}\); FVA balance = **0**.

**(c) December 15, Year 1 — Dividend on remaining shares**  
Dividend = \(3{,}000 \times \$2.50 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 7,500 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 7,500 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,500. **Balanced.**

**(d) Portfolio subsequent measurement schedule and YE FVA**  

| Portfolio slice | Shares | Cost basis | Fair value | Required FVA Dr/(Cr) | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Sold Nov 15 | 2,000 | 80,000 | 86,000 | 6,000 | FVA recognized then eliminated at sale |
| Remaining Dec 31 | 3,000 | 120,000 | 129,000 | 9,000 | Period-end FVA |
| **Total (orig. portfolio)** | **5,000** | **200,000** | — | — | |

Required FVA on remaining = \(129{,}000 - 120{,}000 = \mathbf{\$9{,}000}\) debit  
Existing FVA after sale = **0**  
Adj. needed = **$9,000**

*December 31, Year 1 — Adjust remaining equity to fair value*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 9,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 9,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 9,000. **Balanced.**  
BS carrying amount of remaining investment = \(120{,}000 + 9{,}000 = \mathbf{\$129{,}000}\).

**(e) Year 1 net income effects**  
Unrealized gain on shares sold (to sale date) …… **$6,000**  
Dividend revenue ………………………………… **$7,500**  
Unrealized gain on shares still held …………… **$9,000**  
**Total NI impact (this investment)** …………… **$22,500**

**(f) Classification / measurement at period-end**  
The remaining investment is reported at **fair value $129,000** (not historical cost $120,000). Unrealized holding gain of $9,000 on securities **still held** at the reporting date is included in net income and is a disclosure item under ASC 321.

**Key insight:** Partial sales allocate **pro-rata cost**; FV-NI is applied to the **sold** slice at the sale date (FVA created then immediately cleared with the sale). The **remaining** cost stays on books and is re-measured with a **new** FVA at period-end so the BS always shows fair value of shares still held.

### Q4 — MC — Where unrealized equity FV-NI holding gains/losses are reported
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation of unrealized holding G/L on equity securities measured at FV-NI  
**Question:**  
**Summit Ridge Investments** holds marketable equity securities representing **12%** of an investee’s voting stock. Summit Ridge does **not** have significant influence. Fair value is readily determinable. At year-end, the securities’ fair value exceeds cost. How should Summit Ridge report the unrealized holding gain?

- A) In other comprehensive income (OCI) until the securities are sold, then reclassify to net income  
- B) In **net income** as part of earnings for the period  
- C) Directly in retained earnings, bypassing the income statement  
- D) Only as a disclosure note; no journal entry is recorded until sale  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under ASC 321 / LO 14-4, equity securities with insignificant influence and readily determinable fair value are measured at **FV-NI**. Unrealized holding gains and losses are included in **earnings (net income)**, not OCI. Option A describes **AFS debt (FV-OCI)**, not equity FV-NI. Option C is incorrect. Option D is wrong because period-end FVA entries **are** required.

### Q5 — MC — Sale sequence under equity FV-NI
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** Disposal JE mechanics—adjust FVA first, then eliminate investment and FVA  
**Question:**  
When an equity security measured at **FV-NI** is sold mid-period, what is the correct sequence?

- A) Debit Cash and credit Investment for original cost; credit Gain for proceeds minus cost; leave any existing FVA balance outstanding  
- B) First adjust the investment to fair value through **Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income** and FVA; then debit Cash, credit Investment for cost, and eliminate the related FVA (no separate realized gain if already at FV)  
- C) Reclassify the cumulative unrealized gain from AOCI to net income, then remove the investment at cost  
- D) Credit Investment Income for the investor’s share of investee earnings from last year-end to the sale date, then remove the investment  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Per Demo 14-4 / LO 14-4: at sale date, **first** remeasure to FV (FV-NI), **then** record the sale by removing cost and the associated FVA against cash. Because fair-value changes already hit earnings, the sale does not create a separate residual “realized gain” beyond the final FV-NI tweak. A leaves FVA stranded. C is AFS debt reclassification. D is **equity method** (LO 14-5), not FV-NI.
