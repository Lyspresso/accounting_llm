# Agent 248 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-3

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Accounting for Employee Stock Purchase Plans  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Noncompensatory ESPP criteria (ASC 718-50-25-1):** purchase discount ≤ 5% (safe harbor) **or** terms no more favorable than all holders of the same class; substantially all employees meeting limited qualifications may participate equitably; no option features beyond short enrollment (≤ 31 days) and refund of amounts paid before purchase
- **Initial recognition JE — noncompensatory:** Dr Cash (discounted price); Cr Common Stock (par); Cr PIC—CS to balance; **no** Compensation Expense
- **Initial recognition JE — compensatory:** Dr Cash (discounted price) + Dr Compensation Expense (employee discount); Cr Common Stock + Cr PIC—CS so equity is credited for **full market value** of shares issued
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-month **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan** accumulation; multi-offering liability / share-issuance rollforward; quarterly compensatory purchase cost schedule
- **Period-end adjusting / payroll JE:** credit Liability—ESPP each pay period for amounts withheld from net pay
- **Settlement JE:** clear Liability—ESPP when employees purchase shares (and cash residual refund if liability does not buy an exact whole-share multiple); cash purchase settlements at offering-period end
- **Classification / presentation:** compensatory vs noncompensatory; compensation cost on the income statement; Liability—ESPP as a current liability until settled or refunded

### Q1 — CORE — Dual offering liability schedules, noncompensatory vs compensatory initial recognition, settlement (emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule)
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedules for multi-period ESPP payroll liability accumulation and multi-offering share issuance; initial recognition of noncompensatory and compensatory cash purchases; period-end withholding JE; settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Northwind Analytical Labs** (calendar-year) sponsors two employee stock purchase arrangements. Common stock has a **$1 par** value.

**Plan Alpha (broad-based):** All full-time employees who meet limited service qualifications may participate on an equitable basis. The purchase price is **95% of the market price** of Northwind common stock on the last day of each six-month offering period. Employees may cancel participation before the purchase date and obtain a full refund of amounts previously paid through payroll withholdings. After the purchase price is fixed, employees have **no more than 31 days** to enroll. There are no other option features.

**Plan Beta (directors only):** Only division directors may participate. The purchase price is **85% of the market price** on the purchase date.

**Cash purchases on June 30, Year 1** (market price **$32** per share):  
- Plan Alpha: employees purchase **5,000 shares** for cash.  
- Plan Beta: directors purchase **1,500 shares** for cash.

**Payroll-funded Plan Alpha cohorts (credited to Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan each month):**

| Offering period | Monthly withholding | Months | Market on purchase date |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 – June 30, Year 1 | $12,160 | 6 | $32 |
| July 1 – December 31, Year 1 | $14,250 | 6 | $36 |

Employees purchase as many **whole shares** as the accumulated liability will buy at 95% of market. In both offerings the liability divides evenly by the purchase price (no residual refund). No employees cancel.

**Required:**  
a. Classify **Plan Alpha** and **Plan Beta** as compensatory or noncompensatory. Support with the ASC 718-50-25-1 criteria.  
b. Prepare the **June 30, Year 1 initial recognition journal entries** for (1) the 5,000-share Plan Alpha cash purchase and (2) the 1,500-share Plan Beta cash purchase. Show Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare the **subsequent measurement / accumulation schedule** of Liability—ESPP for the **January–June** payroll cohort (month-by-month withholdings and cumulative balance). Compute whole shares issuable on June 30.  
d. Prepare a **multi-offering subsequent measurement schedule** for both Year 1 offering periods showing: total withholdings, market price, purchase price, shares issued, compensation expense, Common Stock credit, and PIC—CS credit.  
e. Prepare (1) the **January 31 period-end payroll withholding entry** and (2) the **June 30 settlement journal entry** for the first payroll cohort.  
f. In one or two sentences, explain why Plan Alpha payroll settlements create **no** compensation expense even though employees pay less than market.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**

| Plan | Compensatory? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan Alpha** | **Noncompensatory** | Discount **5%** (≤ 5% safe harbor); substantially **all** qualifying employees may participate equitably; only permitted option features (refund of withholdings; enrollment ≤ 31 days). No recognizable compensation cost. |
| **Plan Beta** | **Compensatory** | Participation limited to **division directors only** (fails “substantially all employees”) **and** discount is **15% > 5%**. Recognize compensation expense for the employee discount. |

**b. June 30, Year 1 — initial recognition JEs**

**(1) Plan Alpha — noncompensatory cash issuance**

Purchase price per share = \(32 \times 0.95 = \$30.40\)  
Cash received = \(5{,}000 \times \$30.40 = \$152{,}000\)  
Common stock (par) = \(5{,}000 \times \$1 = \$5{,}000\)  
PIC—CS = \(152{,}000 - 5{,}000 = \$147{,}000\)  
Compensation expense = **$0**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (5,000 × $32 × 95%) | 152,000 | |
| Common Stock (5,000 × $1) | | 5,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 147,000 |
| *Issuance under noncompensatory ESPP — no compensation cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 152,000 = Cr 5,000 + 147,000. **Balanced.**

**(2) Plan Beta — compensatory cash issuance**

Cash = \(1{,}500 \times \$32 \times 0.85 = \$40{,}800\)  
Compensation expense = \(1{,}500 \times \$32 \times 0.15 = \$7{,}200\)  
Market value of shares issued = \(1{,}500 \times \$32 = \$48{,}000\)  
Common stock = \(1{,}500 \times \$1 = \$1{,}500\)  
PIC—CS = \(48{,}000 - 1{,}500 = \$46{,}500\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (1,500 × $32 × 85%) | 40,800 | |
| Compensation Expense (1,500 × $32 × 15%) | 7,200 | |
| Common Stock (1,500 × $1) | | 1,500 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 46,500 |
| *Issuance under compensatory ESPP — discount recognized as compensation* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(40{,}800 + 7{,}200 = 48{,}000\); Cr \(1{,}500 + 46{,}500 = 48{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement — January–June Liability—ESPP accumulation schedule (emphasis)**

| Month | Payroll withholding credited to Liability—ESPP | Cumulative liability |
|---|---:|---:|
| January | $12,160 | $12,160 |
| February | 12,160 | 24,320 |
| March | 12,160 | 36,480 |
| April | 12,160 | 48,640 |
| May | 12,160 | 60,800 |
| June | 12,160 | **72,960** |
| **Total** | **$72,960** | |

Purchase price = \(32 \times 0.95 = \$30.40\)  
Whole shares issuable = \(72{,}960 \div 30.40 = \mathbf{2{,}400}\) shares (exact).

**d. Multi-offering subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**

| Offering | Total withholdings | Market | Purchase price (95%) | Shares issued | Comp. expense | Common Stock | PIC—CS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1 – Jun 30 | $72,960 | $32 | $30.40 | 2,400 | $0 | $2,400 | $70,560 |
| Jul 1 – Dec 31 | 85,500 | 36 | 34.20 | 2,500 | 0 | 2,500 | 83,000 |
| **Year 1 totals** | **$158,460** | | | **4,900** | **$0** | **$4,900** | **$153,560** |

H2 check: monthly \(14{,}250 \times 6 = 85{,}500\); price \(36 \times 0.95 = 34.20\); shares \(85{,}500 \div 34.20 = 2{,}500\).  
PIC H1 = \(72{,}960 - 2{,}400 = 70{,}560\); PIC H2 = \(85{,}500 - 2{,}500 = 83{,}000\).

**e. Period-end withholding JE and settlement JE**

**(1) January 31 — period-end payroll withholding**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salaries Payable (or Cash — reduction of net pay) | 12,160 | |
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | | 12,160 |
| *Withhold employee contributions for ESPP* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 12,160. **Balanced.**  
*(Same amount each month of the first offering; total credits to liability for H1 = $72,960.)*

**(2) June 30 — settlement: issue 2,400 shares; clear liability**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 72,960 | |
| Common Stock (2,400 × $1) | | 2,400 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 70,560 |
| *Settle ESPP liability by issuing stock at 95% of market (noncompensatory)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 72,960 = Cr 2,400 + 70,560. **Balanced.**  
(No compensation expense: Plan Alpha is noncompensatory.)

**f. Why no compensation expense on Plan Alpha**  
Because Plan Alpha meets all noncompensatory criteria (≤ 5% discount, broad equitable participation, only permitted option features), ASC 718 treats the arrangement like a capital-raising transaction at a de minimis discount — the company records cash (or clears the liability) at the discounted price with **no** Compensation Expense.

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** is the core deliverable for payroll-funded ESPPs: lock monthly withholdings into **Liability—ESPP**, roll the cumulative balance to the purchase date, convert at the period-end purchase price into whole shares, and settle. Noncompensatory plans produce **zero** compensation cost on that schedule; compensatory plans (Plan Beta) debit **Compensation Expense** so equity equals **full market value**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — ESPP twin (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: subsequent measurement liability schedule; initial recognition of noncompensatory and compensatory ESPP issuances; period-end withholding; settlement JE; total compensation expense  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverbrook Medical Devices Inc.** (calendar-year) has **$2 par** common stock and two ESPP arrangements.

**Plan N (noncompensatory design):** Substantially all eligible employees may participate equitably; purchase price = **95% of market** on the purchase date; refunds of withholdings allowed if participation is canceled before purchase; enrollment window after price is fixed does not exceed 31 days.

**Plan C (compensatory design):** Only designated high performers may participate; purchase price = **90% of market** on the purchase date.

**Cash purchases on September 30, Year 1** (market price **$48**):  
- Plan N: employees buy **3,000 shares** for cash.  
- Plan C: employees buy **2,000 shares** for cash.

**Payroll-funded Plan N cohort (January 1 – June 30, Year 1 offering):**  
Monthly withholdings **$11,400** for six months credited to Liability—ESPP. On June 30, market price is **$40**; Plan N price = 95% of market. Whole shares only; exact fit. No cancellations.

**Required:**  
a. Classify Plan N and Plan C.  
b. Prepare the **September 30 Plan N** initial recognition JE.  
c. Prepare the **September 30 Plan C** initial recognition JE.  
d. Prepare the **six-month Liability—ESPP accumulation schedule** and compute shares issued at June 30.  
e. Prepare the **representative January 31 period-end withholding JE** and the **June 30 settlement JE** for the payroll cohort.  
f. Compute **total compensation expense** recognized in Year 1 from these ESPP transactions (cash purchases + payroll cohort).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**  
- **Plan N — noncompensatory:** 5% discount safe harbor; broad equitable participation; only permitted option features.  
- **Plan C — compensatory:** limited eligibility **and** 10% discount (> 5%).

**b. September 30 — Plan N noncompensatory issuance**

Cash = \(3{,}000 \times \$48 \times 0.95 = \$136{,}800\)  
CS = \(3{,}000 \times \$2 = \$6{,}000\)  
PIC = \(136{,}800 - 6{,}000 = \$130{,}800\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (3,000 × $48 × 95%) | 136,800 | |
| Common Stock (3,000 × $2) | | 6,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 130,800 |
| *Noncompensatory ESPP issuance* | | |

**Check:** Dr 136,800 = Cr 6,000 + 130,800. **Balanced.**

**c. September 30 — Plan C compensatory issuance**

Cash = \(2{,}000 \times \$48 \times 0.90 = \$86{,}400\)  
Compensation expense = \(2{,}000 \times \$48 \times 0.10 = \$9{,}600\)  
Market value = \(2{,}000 \times \$48 = \$96{,}000\)  
CS = \(2{,}000 \times \$2 = \$4{,}000\)  
PIC = \(96{,}000 - 4{,}000 = \$92{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (2,000 × $48 × 90%) | 86,400 | |
| Compensation Expense (2,000 × $48 × 10%) | 9,600 | |
| Common Stock (2,000 × $2) | | 4,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 92,000 |
| *Compensatory ESPP — discount to compensation expense* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(86{,}400 + 9{,}600 = 96{,}000\); Cr \(4{,}000 + 92{,}000 = 96{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**d. Liability—ESPP accumulation schedule (payroll cohort — subsequent measurement)**

| Month | Withholding | Cumulative liability |
|---|---:|---:|
| January | $11,400 | $11,400 |
| February | 11,400 | 22,800 |
| March | 11,400 | 34,200 |
| April | 11,400 | 45,600 |
| May | 11,400 | 57,000 |
| June | 11,400 | **68,400** |
| **Total** | **$68,400** | |

Purchase price = \(40 \times 0.95 = \$38\)  
Shares = \(68{,}400 \div 38 = \mathbf{1{,}800}\) shares.

**e. Period-end withholding and settlement**

**(1) January 31 — period-end withholding**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salaries Payable (or Cash — reduction of net pay) | 11,400 | |
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | | 11,400 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 11,400. **Balanced.**

**(2) June 30 — settlement JE (payroll cohort, noncompensatory)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 68,400 | |
| Common Stock (1,800 × $2) | | 3,600 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 64,800 |
| *Settle liability; issue stock at 95% of market* | | |

**Check:** Dr 68,400 = Cr 3,600 + 64,800. **Balanced.**

**f. Total Year 1 compensation expense from these ESPP items**  
Plan N cash + payroll cohort: **$0** (noncompensatory)  
Plan C cash purchase: **$9,600**  
**Total compensation expense = $9,600**

**Key insight:** Changing share counts, market prices, par value, and discount rates changes cash, PIC, and compensation amounts, but the **structure** is stable: noncompensatory → liability (or cash) settles at the discounted price with **no** expense; compensatory → expense = shares × market × discount % so equity equals full market value.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Quarterly compensatory purchase schedule; residual refund settlement
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Subsequent measurement schedule of quarterly compensatory ESPP issuances and compensation cost; period-end purchase JE; payroll liability roll-forward with residual cash refund settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborpoint Logistics Group** (calendar-year) has **$1 par** common stock and two arrangements.

**Arrangement 1 — Compensatory quarterly plan:** Only warehouse supervisors and regional managers may participate. Purchase price = **90% of market** on the last business day of each calendar quarter. Employees pay cash on the purchase date (no payroll liability for this arrangement). Year 1 purchases:

| Purchase date | Shares purchased | Market price per share |
|---|---:|---:|
| March 31 | 2,000 | $22 |
| June 30 | 2,500 | 24 |
| September 30 | 1,800 | 26 |
| December 31 | 3,200 | 30 |

**Arrangement 2 — Noncompensatory payroll plan:** Substantially all eligible employees may participate; purchase price = **95% of market** on December 31; permitted refund and enrollment features only. Employee **Chen** signed a payroll deduction agreement on January 1 for **$200 per month** for 12 months. Harborpoint credits **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan** at each payroll. On December 31, market price is **$40**. Employees cannot purchase partial shares. Any residual liability not applied to whole shares is refunded in cash on December 31. Chen does not cancel.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / compensation cost schedule** for Arrangement 1 for Year 1 showing, for each quarter: cash received, compensation expense, Common Stock, PIC—CS, and market value of shares issued. Total Year 1 compensation expense.  
b. Record the **December 31 period-end journal entry** for Arrangement 1 (the Q4 cash purchase).  
c. Prepare Chen’s **Liability—ESPP accumulation schedule** (monthly) and a **year-end liability roll-forward** (beginning balance, withholdings, amount applied to shares, residual refund, ending balance).  
d. Record (1) the **December 31 period-end withholding** for Chen’s final monthly deduction and (2) the **December 31 settlement JE** (issue whole shares + residual cash refund).  
e. How should Liability—ESPP and compensatory compensation cost be **presented**?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Arrangement 1 — quarterly subsequent measurement / compensation schedule (emphasis)**

Discount = 10% of market. Equity is credited for **full market value**.

| Date | Shares | Market | Cash (90%) | Comp. expense (10%) | Common Stock ($1) | PIC—CS | Market value check |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar 31 | 2,000 | $22 | $39,600 | $4,400 | $2,000 | $42,000 | $44,000 |
| Jun 30 | 2,500 | 24 | 54,000 | 6,000 | 2,500 | 57,500 | 60,000 |
| Sep 30 | 1,800 | 26 | 42,120 | 4,680 | 1,800 | 45,000 | 46,800 |
| Dec 31 | 3,200 | 30 | 86,400 | 9,600 | 3,200 | 92,800 | 96,000 |
| **Year 1 total** | **9,500** | | **$222,120** | **$24,680** | **$9,500** | **$237,300** | **$246,800** |

Checks (each row): Cash + Comp = Market value; CS + PIC = Market value.  
Mar: \(39{,}600 + 4{,}400 = 44{,}000\); \(2{,}000 + 42{,}000 = 44{,}000\).  
Jun: \(54{,}000 + 6{,}000 = 60{,}000\); \(2{,}500 + 57{,}500 = 60{,}000\).  
Sep: \(42{,}120 + 4{,}680 = 46{,}800\); \(1{,}800 + 45{,}000 = 46{,}800\).  
Dec: \(86{,}400 + 9{,}600 = 96{,}000\); \(3{,}200 + 92{,}800 = 96{,}000\).  
Total comp: \(4{,}400 + 6{,}000 + 4{,}680 + 9{,}600 = \mathbf{\$24{,}680}\).  
Total market value: \(222{,}120 + 24{,}680 = 246{,}800\); \(9{,}500 + 237{,}300 = 246{,}800\).

**b. December 31 — Arrangement 1 period-end purchase JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (3,200 × $30 × 90%) | 86,400 | |
| Compensation Expense (3,200 × $30 × 10%) | 9,600 | |
| Common Stock (3,200 × $1) | | 3,200 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 92,800 |
| *Compensatory ESPP quarterly purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(86{,}400 + 9{,}600 = 96{,}000\); Cr \(3{,}200 + 92{,}800 = 96{,}000\). **Balanced.**

**c. Chen — Liability accumulation and year-end roll-forward**

**Monthly accumulation schedule**

| Month | Withholding | Cumulative liability |
|---|---:|---:|
| January | $200 | $200 |
| February | 200 | 400 |
| March | 200 | 600 |
| April | 200 | 800 |
| May | 200 | 1,000 |
| June | 200 | 1,200 |
| July | 200 | 1,400 |
| August | 200 | 1,600 |
| September | 200 | 1,800 |
| October | 200 | 2,000 |
| November | 200 | 2,200 |
| December | 200 | **2,400** |
| **Total** | **$2,400** | |

Purchase price = \(40 \times 0.95 = \$38\)  
Whole shares = \(\lfloor 2{,}400 \div 38 \rfloor = \lfloor 63.1579\ldots \rfloor = \mathbf{63}\) shares  
Applied to shares = \(63 \times \$38 = \$2{,}394\)  
Residual cash refund = \(2{,}400 - 2{,}394 = \$6\)

**Year-end liability roll-forward**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning Liability—ESPP, 1/1/Y1 | $0 |

| Ending Liability—ESPP, 12/31/Y1 | **$0** |

**d. December 31 — period-end withholding and settlement**

**(1) Final monthly withholding (December 31)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salaries Payable (or Cash — reduction of net pay) | 200 | |
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | | 200 |
| *Withhold Chen’s December ESPP contribution* | | |

**(2) Settlement — issue 63 shares + residual refund (noncompensatory)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 2,400 | |
| Common Stock (63 × $1) | | 63 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 2,331 |
| Cash (residual refund) | | 6 |
| *Settle liability: issue whole shares at 95% of market; refund residual* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,400 = Cr \(63 + 2{,}331 + 6 = 2{,}400\). **Balanced.**  
(No compensation expense: Arrangement 2 is noncompensatory.)

**e. Presentation**  
- **Liability—ESPP** is a **current liability** while withholdings are outstanding; ending balance is **$0** after settlement/refund.  
- **Compensation Expense $24,680** from Arrangement 1 is reported in operating expenses (compensation cost). Arrangement 2 generates **no** compensation expense.  
- Stock issuances increase **Common Stock** and **PIC—CS** (Arrangement 1 at full market value; Arrangement 2 at the discounted cash equivalent applied to shares).

**Key insight:** A **subsequent measurement schedule** for compensatory ESPPs tracks cash, compensation cost, and equity each purchase date so totals roll forward cleanly. Payroll-funded noncompensatory plans accumulate a **liability** until settlement; if the liability is not an exact multiple of the purchase price, issue whole shares and **refund the residual cash** — still with no compensation expense when criteria are met.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of noncompensatory ESPP and measurement of the cash/equity JE
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Classification — noncompensatory ESPP (5% discount, broad participation) and correct cash/equity journal entry  

**Question:**  
**Cascade Ridge Analytics Co.** has an employee stock purchase plan open to substantially all eligible employees on an equitable basis. Purchase price is **95% of market** on the purchase date; employees may obtain a refund of withholdings if they cancel before purchase; enrollment after the price is fixed does not exceed 31 days. On March 31, employees purchase **4,000 shares** of $1 par common when market is **$25**. Which statement is correct?

- A) The plan is compensatory; Compensation Expense is $5,000.  
- B) The plan is noncompensatory; debit Cash $95,000; credit Common Stock $4,000 and PIC—CS $91,000; no compensation expense.  
- C) The plan is noncompensatory; debit Cash $100,000 and credit Common Stock $100,000.  
- D) The plan is compensatory because any discount from market requires compensation expense.

**Answer:** **B.** Discount of 5% with broad participation and only permitted option features → **noncompensatory**. Cash = \(4{,}000 \times \$25 \times 0.95 = \$95{,}000\); CS = **$4,000**; PIC = **$91,000**; compensation expense = $0. A and D incorrectly treat a qualifying 5% plan as compensatory. C ignores the 5% discount on cash and misstates equity accounts.

### Q5 — MC — Compensatory ESPP PIC measurement
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Classification/measurement — PIC credit when compensatory ESPP issues shares at full market value  

**Question:**  
Same facts as Q4, except only **regional managers** may participate and the discount is **10%** (purchase at 90% of market). Employees still buy **4,000 shares** at market **$25**, $1 par. What is the credit to **Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock**?

- A) $90,000  
- B) $100,000  
- C) $96,000  
- D) $86,000  

**Answer:** **C. $96,000**  
Plan is **compensatory** (select employees + 10% discount). Equity is credited for **full market value** of shares issued:  
Cash = \(4{,}000 \times \$25 \times 0.90 = \$90{,}000\)  
Compensation Expense = \(4{,}000 \times \$25 \times 0.10 = \$10{,}000\)  
Common Stock = **$4,000**  
**PIC—CS = \(100{,}000 - 4{,}000 = \$96{,}000\)**  
A is cash only (ignores compensation and par); B credits full market to PIC and ignores par; D is market less cash less an erroneous double-count.
