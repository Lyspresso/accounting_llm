# Agent 366 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-8

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Explain and demonstrate accounting for uncertainty in income tax decisions  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Two-step process (ASC 740):** (1) **Recognition** — more likely than not (>50%) the tax position will be sustained on technical merits (assume full examination knowledge; ignore detection risk); (2) **Measurement** — largest amount of tax benefit with **cumulative probability >50%** of being realized upon settlement
- **Initial recognition JE:** Income Tax Expense = Income Tax Payable + **Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits (UTB)** when part (or all) of the filed benefit is not recognized for financial reporting
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** remeasure the UTB liability when new information changes the cumulative-probability measurement (prospective change in estimate — not prior-period error restatement); optionally accrue interest and penalties on uncertain positions in income tax expense
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-period roll-forward of the UTB liability (beginning → additions/reductions for remeasurement → current-year positions → settlements → ending)
- **Settlement / resolution JE:** (a) full benefit lost, (b) full benefit realized, (c) partial realization matching prior measurement, (d) partial realization differing from prior measurement
- **Fails recognition threshold:** no step-two measurement of benefit; full tax effect of the position is recorded as UTB liability
- Classification / disclosure: UTB liability; note reconciliation of beginning to ending unrecognized tax benefits

### Q1 — CORE — Multi-year period-end remeasurement of UTB (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 18-8  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs for UTB remeasurement (emphasis); initial recognition after two-step process; subsequent measurement schedule; settlement JE  
**Scenario:**  
**MapleRidge Industrial LLC** (calendar-year) claims a **$200,000** cost-classification deduction on its Year 1 tax return. Management concludes the position meets the **more-likely-than-not** recognition threshold based on technical merits (assuming the taxing authority examines the position with full knowledge of all relevant information). Taxable income for Year 1, **after** taking the full $200,000 deduction, is **$1,200,000**. The enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. MapleRidge has no temporary differences other than the uncertain tax position effects illustrated here.

**Year 1** management estimates of possible settlement outcomes:

| Possible estimated outcome (allowable tax deduction) | Individual probability | Cumulative probability |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $200,000 | 10% | 10% |
| 160,000 | 20% | 30% |
| 120,000 | 35% | 65% |
| 80,000 | 25% | 90% |
| 40,000 | 10% | 100% |

At **December 31, Year 2**, new information revises the outcome table (change in estimate — **period-end adjusting** focus):

| Possible estimated outcome (allowable tax deduction) | Individual probability | Cumulative probability |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $200,000 | 5% | 5% |
| 120,000 | 15% | 20% |
| 100,000 | 25% | 45% |
| 80,000 | 30% | 75% |
| 40,000 | 25% | 100% |

At **December 31, Year 3**, further new information improves the outlook (second **period-end adjusting** remeasurement):

| Possible estimated outcome (allowable tax deduction) | Individual probability | Cumulative probability |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $200,000 | 20% | 20% |
| 160,000 | 35% | 55% |
| 100,000 | 30% | 85% |
| 40,000 | 15% | 100% |

On **March 10, Year 4**, after negotiations, MapleRidge and the taxing authority settle: **$140,000** of the original $200,000 deduction is sustained (**$60,000** disallowed). Any additional tax is currently payable. Ignore interest and penalties in this question.

**Required:**  
a. Apply the **two-step** process at December 31, Year 1: does recognition apply? What **amount of deduction benefit** is measured for financial reporting?  
b. Compute **Income Tax Payable**, **Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits**, and **Income Tax Expense** at December 31, Year 1. Prepare the **initial recognition journal entry**. Show Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare a complete **subsequent measurement schedule** for the **Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits** from December 31, Year 1 through March 10, Year 4 settlement. Show date, description, change, and ending balance after each event.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** and **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JEs** for remeasurement (emphasis).  
e. Prepare the **March 10, Year 4 settlement journal entry**.  
f. In one or two sentences, explain why Year 1 tax expense is **not** restated when later period-end remeasurements or settlement differ from the Year 1 estimate.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Two-step recognition and measurement (Year 1)**

- **Step 1 — Recognition:** Yes. Management concludes it is **more likely than not (>50%)** the position will be sustained on technical merits → proceed to measurement.  
- **Step 2 — Measurement:** Largest allowable deduction with **cumulative probability >50%** is **$120,000** (cumulative 65%).  
- Recognized benefit (deduction base) = **$120,000**  
- Unrecognized deduction base = $200,000 − $120,000 = **$80,000**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

Income Tax Payable = \(1{,}200{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$300{,}000}\)  
Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits = \(80{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\)  
Income Tax Expense = \(300{,}000 + 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$320{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 320,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 300,000 |
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | | 20,000 |
| *Record Year 1 tax expense; UTB for unrecognized portion of uncertain deduction* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$320,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits**

**Year 2 remeasurement:**  
Largest amount with cumulative probability >50% = **$80,000** (cumulative 75%).  
Unrecognized base = $200,000 − $80,000 = **$120,000**  
Required UTB = \(120{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$30{,}000}\)  
Increase = \(30{,}000 - 20{,}000 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\)

**Year 3 remeasurement:**  
Largest amount with cumulative probability >50% = **$160,000** (cumulative 55%).  
Unrecognized base = $200,000 − $160,000 = **$40,000**  
Required UTB = \(40{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\)  
Decrease = \(30{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\)

| Date | Description | Change in UTB | Ending UTB liability |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 | Initial recognition (unrecognized base $80,000 × 25%) | +20,000 | **20,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Period-end remeasurement — increase (worse estimate) | +10,000 | **30,000** |
| 12/31/Y3 | Period-end remeasurement — decrease (improved estimate) | (20,000) | **10,000** |
| 3/10/Y4 | Settlement — reverse remaining UTB | (10,000) | **0** |

**Roll-forward check:** \(20{,}000 + 10{,}000 - 20{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$0}\).

**d. Period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 2 — Increase UTB*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 10,000 | |
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | | 10,000 |
| *Period-end remeasurement of UTB for new information; prospective change in estimate* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$10,000**. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3 — Decrease UTB*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 20,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 20,000 |
| *Period-end remeasurement of UTB for improved outlook; reduce tax expense prospectively* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$20,000**. **Balanced.**

**e. March 10, Year 4 — settlement JE**

Deduction sustained = **$140,000**  
Deduction disallowed = $200,000 − $140,000 = **$60,000**  
Additional Income Tax Payable = \(60{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$15{,}000}\)  
Remove UTB liability of **$10,000**  
Difference increases Income Tax Expense: \(15{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 10,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | 5,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 15,000 |
| *Settle uncertain tax position; reverse UTB; true-up tax expense* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 + 5,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Change in estimate (not error)**  
Period-end remeasurements and settlement differences arise from **new information** and are accounted for **prospectively** as changes in accounting estimate. Prior-period tax expense and financial statements are **not** restated.

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** is the recurring exam skill after initial recognition: each reporting date, re-run the cumulative-probability measurement table with current information, lock the required UTB, and adjust tax expense prospectively. The subsequent measurement schedule documents those adjustments through settlement.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin UTB schedule with period-end remeasurement and full-benefit settlement
**LO:** LO 18-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — period-end adjusting remeasurement of UTB; initial recognition after two-step process; subsequent measurement schedule; settlement when full benefit is realized  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironvale Commerce Corp.** claims a **$140,000** deduction on its Year 1 return related to classification of certain project costs. Management determines the position is **more likely than not** to be sustained. Taxable income after the full deduction is **$560,000**. Tax rate is **25%**. No other temporary differences.

**Year 1** possible outcomes:

| Possible estimated outcome (allowable tax deduction) | Individual probability | Cumulative probability |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $140,000 | 15% | 15% |
| 100,000 | 25% | 40% |
| 84,000 | 30% | 70% |
| 50,000 | 20% | 90% |
| 20,000 | 10% | 100% |

At **December 31, Year 2**, new information revises the table so the largest amount with cumulative probability >50% is now **$100,000** (management documents cumulative probability of 58% at $100,000).  
On **August 1, Year 3**, Ironvale **fully sustains** the entire $140,000 deduction after examination.

**Required:**  
a. Measure the Year 1 recognized deduction benefit and unrecognized base; compute tax payable, UTB liability, and tax expense.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
c. Prepare a **UTB liability subsequent measurement schedule** through settlement.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 period-end remeasurement JE** (emphasis).  
e. Prepare the **August 1, Year 3 settlement JE** (full benefit realized).  
f. Compute what **Income Tax Expense** would have been in Year 1 if the individual probability of the full $140,000 outcome had been **55%** (so cumulative probability at $140,000 exceeds 50%).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 1 measurement**

Largest amount with cumulative probability >50% = **$84,000** (cumulative 70%).  
Unrecognized deduction base = $140,000 − $84,000 = **$56,000**  
Income Tax Payable = \(560{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$140{,}000}\)  
Liability for UTB = \(56{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$14{,}000}\)  
Income Tax Expense = \(140{,}000 + 14{,}000 = \mathbf{\$154{,}000}\)

**b. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 154,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 140,000 |
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | | 14,000 |
| *Record tax expense and UTB for unrecognized portion* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$154,000**. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — UTB liability**

Year 2 required liability: unrecognized base = $140,000 − $100,000 = **$40,000**; UTB = \(40{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\)

| Date | Description | Change in UTB | Ending UTB liability |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 12/31/Y1 | Initial recognition | +14,000 | **14,000** |
| 12/31/Y2 | Period-end remeasurement (decrease) | (4,000) | **10,000** |
| 8/1/Y3 | Settlement — full benefit realized | (10,000) | **0** |

**Roll-forward check:** \(14{,}000 - 4{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$0}\).

**d. December 31, Year 2 — period-end remeasurement JE (emphasis)**

Decrease in UTB = \(14{,}000 - 10{,}000 = \mathbf{\$4{,}000}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 4,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 4,000 |
| *Period-end reduction of UTB for improved measurement estimate* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$4,000**. **Balanced.**

**e. August 1, Year 3 — settlement (full tax benefit realized)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 10,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 10,000 |
| *Favorable resolution; reverse remaining UTB; reduce tax expense (change in estimate)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$10,000**. **Balanced.**  
No Income Tax Payable is recorded because the full return position is sustained (no additional tax due).

**f. Counterfactual — full $140,000 has >50% likelihood**

If individual probability of the full $140,000 outcome is **55%**, the largest amount with cumulative probability >50% is **$140,000**.  
Unrecognized base = **$0**; UTB = **$0**  
Income Tax Expense = Income Tax Payable = **$140,000** only.

**Key insight:** Once recognition is met, the **cumulative** probability column drives measurement. A favorable **period-end adjusting JE** reduces UTB and tax expense prospectively; full success at settlement clears the remaining UTB entirely against tax expense with **zero** additional payable.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — period-end UTB/interest adjustments + fails MLTN + settlement outcomes
**LO:** LO 18-8  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs for UTB remeasurement and interest/penalties (emphasis); fails-MLTN initial recognition; settlement/resolution JEs for full loss, full sustain, measured partial, and unexpected partial  
**Scenario:**  

**Part A — Period-end adjusting focus (remeasurement + interest)**  
**Northbridge Metals Co.** begins Year 2 with a **Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits of $15,000** related to a Year 1 uncertain deduction of **$180,000** (the $15,000 UTB equals an unrecognized deduction base of **$60,000** at 25%). At **December 31, Year 2**, new information revises measurement so the largest allowable deduction with cumulative probability >50% is now **$100,000**. Separately, management accrues **$2,400** of interest and penalties related to the uncertain tax position (policy: record interest and penalties within income tax expense). Also during Year 2, Northbridge takes a new current-year Position T: claims a **$60,000** deduction that meets the more-likely-than-not threshold; cumulative-probability measurement supports recognizing benefit of a **$36,000** deduction (unrecognized base **$24,000**). Tax rate is **25%** throughout. Ignore settlement of the Year 1 position in Year 2.

**Part B — Fails recognition threshold**  
**Windmere Labs Inc.** takes a **$75,000** deduction on its Year 1 tax return. Management assesses that the probability the position will be sustained on technical merits is **only 40%** (does **not** meet more-likely-than-not). Taxable income after taking the $75,000 deduction is **$500,000**. Tax rate is **25%**.

**Part C — Settlement outcomes (separate case; meets threshold previously)**  
**Harborline Products LLC** previously recorded a **Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits of $11,250** at December 31, Year 1 related to a Year 1 uncertain deduction of **$150,000**. That liability reflected an unrecognized deduction base of **$45,000** at 25% (i.e., financial statements recognized benefit of a **$105,000** deduction). In December of Year 2, consider each resolution **separately**:

1. **Full tax benefit is lost** — none of the $150,000 deduction is sustained.  
2. **Full tax benefit is realized** — the entire $150,000 deduction is sustained.  
3. **Portion realized as previously measured** — taxing authority allows **$105,000** of the deduction.  
4. **Portion realized differently** — taxing authority allows only **$75,000** of the deduction.

**Required:**  
a. (Part A) Compute the Year 2 required UTB for the Year 1 position after remeasurement; prepare the **period-end adjusting JEs** for (i) UTB remeasurement and (ii) interest/penalties. Also compute the UTB recorded for current-year Position T and prepare a **Year 2 subsequent measurement schedule** for the UTB liability (beginning → prior-year remeasurement → current-year addition → ending).  
b. (Part B) Explain why no benefit is measured under step two. Compute amounts and prepare Windmere’s **December 31, Year 1 income tax JE**.  
c. (Part C) Prepare the **Year 2 settlement JE** for each of cases 1–4.  
d. For case 3 only, explain the **income statement effect** relative to cases 1, 2, and 4.  
e. State the **balance-sheet classification** of a material UTB liability expected to be negotiated over multi-year examinations and name one typical **disclosure** for the roll-forward.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Northbridge — period-end adjusting JEs and UTB schedule (emphasis)**

Year 1 position after remeasurement:  
Recognized deduction = **$100,000**; unrecognized base = $180,000 − $100,000 = **$80,000**  
Required UTB = \(80{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\)  
Increase = \(20{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\)

*Period-end adjusting JE (i) — remeasurement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 5,000 | |
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | | 5,000 |
| *Period-end remeasurement of prior-year uncertain tax position* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$5,000**. **Balanced.**

*Period-end adjusting JE (ii) — interest and penalties*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 2,400 | |
| Accrued Interest and Penalties on Uncertain Tax Positions | | 2,400 |
| *Period-end accrual of interest/penalties on UTB (in tax expense)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$2,400**. **Balanced.**

Position T UTB = \(24{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\)

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Balance at beginning of Year 2 | **$15,000** |
| Additions for tax positions of prior years (Year 1 position remeasurement) | **5,000** |
| Additions based on tax positions related to the current year (Position T) | **6,000** |
| Settlements | **0** |
| **Balance at end of Year 2** | **$26,000** |

**Roll-forward check:** \(15{,}000 + 5{,}000 + 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$26{,}000}\).  
(Interest/penalties of $2,400 are accrued separately and are **not** part of the UTB liability roll-forward.)

**b. Windmere — fails recognition threshold (initial recognition JE)**

- **Step 1:** Probability of sustainment = 40% **≤ 50%** → does **not** meet MLTN recognition threshold.  
- **Step 2 measurement is not applied** to recognize any financial-statement benefit of the position.  
- Full tax effect of the $75,000 deduction is treated as unrecognized.

Income Tax Payable (as filed) = \(500{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$125{,}000}\)  
Liability for UTB = \(75{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$18{,}750}\)  
Income Tax Expense = \(125{,}000 + 18{,}750 = \mathbf{\$143{,}750}\)

(Equivalent view: tax expense as if the deduction is fully disallowed = \((500{,}000 + 75{,}000) \times 25\% = \$143{,}750\).)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 143,750 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 125,000 |
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | | 18,750 |
| *Position fails MLTN; no FS benefit recognized for the $75,000 deduction* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$143,750**. **Balanced.**

**c. Harborline — Year 2 settlement JEs**

**Case 1 — Full tax benefit lost**

Additional tax due on full $150,000 disallowance = \(150{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$37{,}500}\)  
Prior UTB accrual = **$11,250**  
Incremental Income Tax Expense in Year 2 = \(37{,}500 - 11{,}250 = \mathbf{\$26{,}250}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 11,250 | |
| Income Tax Expense | 26,250 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 37,500 |
| *Fail to sustain tax position; settle UTB and accrue remaining tax* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,250 + 26,250 = Cr 37,500. **Balanced.**

**Case 2 — Full tax benefit realized**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 11,250 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 11,250 |
| *Favorable resolution; reverse UTB; reduce tax expense prospectively* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$11,250**. **Balanced.**

**Case 3 — Portion realized as previously measured ($105,000 allowed)**

Additional tax due = unrecognized base \(45{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$11{,}250}\)  
Equals existing UTB → pure reclassification; **no** income-statement effect.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 11,250 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 11,250 |
| *Negotiated settlement matches prior measurement; reclassify UTB to payable* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **$11,250**. **Balanced.**

**Case 4 — Portion realized differently ($75,000 allowed)**

Deduction disallowed = $150,000 − $75,000 = **$75,000**  
Additional Income Tax Payable = \(75{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$18{,}750}\)  
Remove UTB of **$11,250**  
Incremental Income Tax Expense = \(18{,}750 - 11{,}250 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits | 11,250 | |
| Income Tax Expense | 7,500 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 18,750 |
| *Partial settlement worse than prior measurement; reverse UTB and true-up expense* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,250 + 7,500 = Cr 18,750. **Balanced.**

**d. Income statement effect — case 3 vs others**  
When settlement equals the amount previously measured (case 3), there is **no** Year 2 income-statement effect—only a reclassification from UTB to Income Tax Payable. Case 1 increases tax expense for the shortfall not previously accrued; case 2 reduces tax expense by reversing the full UTB; case 4 increases tax expense for the portion of the true-up beyond the existing UTB.

**e. Classification and disclosure**  
A material UTB expected to be resolved through multi-year examination/negotiation is generally presented as a **noncurrent liability** (unless expected to be paid within one year). Typical disclosure: a **reconciliation of the beginning and ending balances** of unrecognized tax benefits (additions for current/prior years, reductions, settlements), often with related interest and penalties.

**Key insight:** **Period-end adjusting entries** keep the UTB liability (and any interest/penalties) aligned with management’s current best judgment. Settlement then either reclassifies UTB to payable (if the estimate was exact), reduces tax expense (if better than expected), or increases tax expense (if worse)—always prospectively.

### Q4 — MC (method / recognition threshold)
**LO:** LO 18-8  
**Concept:** Measurement method for a tax position that meets the more-likely-than-not recognition threshold  

**Question:**  
Lakeview Paper Co. concludes that an uncertain tax position meets the more-likely-than-not recognition threshold. Which of the following correctly describes how Lakeview should **measure** the tax benefit recognized in the financial statements?

- A) Expected value (probability-weighted average) of all possible outcomes  
- B) The most likely single outcome only, even if its individual probability is less than or equal to 50%  
- C) The largest amount of tax benefit that is greater than 50% likely of being realized upon settlement (using cumulative probabilities)  
- D) Zero, because any uncertainty prohibits recognition of a tax benefit  

**Answer:** **C.** Under ASC 740’s two-step model, once recognition (MLTN) is met, measurement is the **largest** amount of benefit with **cumulative probability greater than 50%** of being realized upon settlement—not a pure expected-value average, and not automatic zero.

### Q4b — MC (fails recognition threshold)
**LO:** LO 18-8  
**Concept:** Effect of failing the more-likely-than-not recognition threshold on initial measurement  

**Question:**  
Pinecrest Logistics Inc. takes a $40,000 deduction on its tax return. Management concludes there is only a 30% chance the position will be sustained on technical merits if examined. Taxable income after the deduction is $200,000; the tax rate is 25%. What **Liability for Unrecognized Tax Benefits** should Pinecrest record at year-end related to this position?

- A) $0  
- B) $3,000  
- C) $7,000  
- D) $10,000  

**Answer:** **D. $10,000.** Because the position fails the more-likely-than-not recognition threshold (30% ≤ 50%), **no** financial-statement benefit is recognized for the position. Full tax effect = \(40{,}000 \times 25\% = \$10{,}000\) UTB. Income Tax Payable = \(200{,}000 \times 25\% = \$50{,}000\); Income Tax Expense = \(50{,}000 + 10{,}000 = \$60{,}000\).
