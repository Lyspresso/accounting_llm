# Agent 291 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-4

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Account for multiple tax differences and prepare a tax reconciliation disclosure  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** first-year period-end entry that simultaneously records Income Tax Payable, Deferred Tax Liability (taxable temporary differences), Deferred Tax Asset (deductible temporary differences), and Income Tax Expense when multiple book–tax differences exist
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year schedules of cumulative temporary differences × enacted rate → DTL/DTA ending balances; deferred-tax roll-forward (beg + increase/(decrease) = end)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end income-tax entry that sets payable to current tax and true-ups deferred accounts to schedule ending balances (origins and partial reversals in one compound entry)
- **Disposal / settlement / reverse JE:** prepaid (or similar asset) basis difference fully reverses; warranty (or similar) cash settlements partially reverse the DTA
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** rate reconciliation (statutory $ and % → permanent-difference reconciling items → effective tax rate); permanent items never create DTA/DTL; same-component/jurisdiction DTA and DTL presented net as one noncurrent amount
- **Number-variant twin:** same multi-difference path with all pretax amounts, temporary differences, permanent items, and rate changed

### Q1 — CORE — MesaForge: multi-year DTL + DTA + muni interest; period-end tax JEs (emphasis), schedules, rate recon
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Multiple temporary and permanent differences over three years; **period-end adjusting income-tax JEs** (emphasis); deferred tax schedules and roll-forward; partial warranty settlement; rate reconciliation disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**MesaForge Industrial Systems Inc.** is a calendar-year company. Pretax GAAP income (after all revenues and expenses, including the items below) is:

| Year | Pretax GAAP income |
|------|-------------------:|
| Year 1 | $280,000 |
| Year 2 | $320,000 |
| Year 3 | $250,000 |

The enacted tax rate is **21%** for all years. Deferred tax accounts have **zero** beginning balances on January 1, Year 1. Accounting and tax periods both end December 31.

Book–tax differences:

1. **Prepaid insurance (taxable temporary difference):** On December 31, Year 1, MesaForge paid and recorded prepaid insurance of **$36,000** for Year 2 coverage. The cost is **deductible for tax when paid** and is expensed for GAAP in Year 2 when coverage is consumed.  
2. **Warranty accrual (deductible temporary difference):** On December 31, Year 1, MesaForge accrued warranty expense of **$48,000** (GAAP liability). Warranty costs are **not deductible for tax until paid**. Settlements are **$16,000 in Year 2, $16,000 in Year 3, and $16,000 in Year 4**.  
3. **Municipal bond interest (permanent difference):** MesaForge earns **$3,000** of municipal bond interest revenue each year (Year 1–Year 3). Interest is **included in pretax GAAP income** but is **nontaxable**.

No other temporary or permanent differences exist. No valuation allowance is required.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute **taxable income** and **income tax payable** for Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3.  
(b) Prepare schedules of the **deferred tax liability** and **deferred tax asset** ending balances at December 31 of Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3. Also prepare a **deferred-tax roll-forward** (beginning, increase/(decrease), ending) for each account.  
(c) **(Emphasis)** Record the **period-end adjusting income-tax journal entries** for Year 1 (initial recognition), Year 2, and Year 3. Verify each entry balances.  
(d) Prepare the **rate reconciliation** (dollars and percentages) from the statutory rate to the effective tax rate for each of the three years.  
(e) At December 31, Year 3, state the **balance-sheet presentation** of deferred taxes (same tax-paying component and jurisdiction) and the remaining warranty temporary difference expected to reverse in Year 4.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Taxable income and income tax payable**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pretax GAAP income | $280,000 | $320,000 | $250,000 |
| Insurance (prepaid origin / reverse) | (36,000) | 36,000 | — |
| Warranty (origin / cash settlements) | 48,000 | (16,000) | (16,000) |
| Municipal bond interest (permanent) | (3,000) | (3,000) | (3,000) |
| **Taxable income** | **$289,000** | **$337,000** | **$231,000** |
| Tax rate | × 21% | × 21% | × 21% |
| **Income tax payable** | **$60,690** | **$70,770** | **$48,510** |

Checks: \(289{,}000 \times 0.21 = 60{,}690\); \(337{,}000 \times 0.21 = 70{,}770\); \(231{,}000 \times 0.21 = 48{,}510\).

**(b) Deferred tax schedules and roll-forward**

**Deferred tax liability (prepaid insurance — taxable temporary difference)**

| | Dec 31, Y1 | Dec 31, Y2 | Dec 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GAAP basis of prepaid insurance | $36,000 | $0 | $0 |
| Tax basis of prepaid insurance | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Cumulative taxable temporary difference | 36,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Enacted rate | × 21% | × 21% | × 21% |
| **DTL ending balance** | **$7,560** | **$0** | **$0** |

**Deferred tax asset (warranty — deductible temporary difference)**

| | Dec 31, Y1 | Dec 31, Y2 | Dec 31, Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GAAP basis of warranty liability | $48,000 | $32,000 | $16,000 |
| Tax basis of warranty liability | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Cumulative deductible temporary difference | 48,000 | 32,000 | 16,000 |
| Enacted rate | × 21% | × 21% | × 21% |
| **DTA ending balance** | **$10,080** | **$6,720** | **$3,360** |

**Deferred-tax roll-forward**

| Account | Beg Y1 | Δ Y1 | End Y1 | Δ Y2 | End Y2 | Δ Y3 | End Y3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Deferred tax liability | $0 | +7,560 | $7,560 | (7,560) | $0 | 0 | $0 |
| Deferred tax asset | $0 | +10,080 | $10,080 | (3,360) | $6,720 | (3,360) | $3,360 |

Prepaid fully **settles/reverses** in Year 2 (DTL → 0). Warranty **partially settles** $16,000 cash each year (DTA declines by \(16{,}000 \times 21\% = \$3{,}360\) per settlement year).

**(c) Period-end adjusting income-tax journal entries (emphasis)**

Formula: Income tax expense = income tax payable + increase in DTL − increase in DTA  
(Equivalently: payable adjusted for the change needed to hit schedule ending balances.)

**December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition / period-end adjusting JE**  
Expense \(= 60{,}690 + 7{,}560 - 10{,}080 = \mathbf{\$58{,}170}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 58,170 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 10,080 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 7,560 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 60,690 |
| *Period-end: current tax + originate DTL (prepaid) and DTA (warranty)* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(58{,}170 + 10{,}080 = 68{,}250\) = Cr \(7{,}560 + 60{,}690\). **Balanced.**  
Cross-check (permanent only): \(280{,}000 \times 21\% - 3{,}000 \times 21\% = 58{,}800 - 630 = 58{,}170\).

**December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE (settlement of prepaid + partial warranty reverse)**  
ΔDTL \(= 0 - 7{,}560 = (7{,}560)\); ΔDTA \(= 6{,}720 - 10{,}080 = (3{,}360)\)  
Expense \(= 70{,}770 + (7{,}560) - (3{,}360) = 70{,}770 - 7{,}560 + 3{,}360 = \mathbf{\$66{,}570}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 66,570 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | 7,560 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,360 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 70,770 |
| *Period-end: reverse prepaid DTL; reduce DTA for warranty cash settlement* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(66{,}570 + 7{,}560 = 74{,}130\) = Cr \(3{,}360 + 70{,}770\). **Balanced.**  
Cross-check: \(320{,}000 \times 21\% - 630 = 67{,}200 - 630 = 66{,}570\).

**December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting JE**  
ΔDTA \(= 3{,}360 - 6{,}720 = (3{,}360)\); no DTL  
Expense \(= 48{,}510 - (3{,}360) = 48{,}510 + 3{,}360 = \mathbf{\$51{,}870}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 51,870 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 3,360 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 48,510 |
| *Period-end: current tax; reduce DTA for Year 3 warranty settlement* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(51{,}870\) = Cr \(3{,}360 + 48{,}510\). **Balanced.**  
Cross-check: \(250{,}000 \times 21\% - 630 = 52{,}500 - 630 = 51{,}870\).

**(d) Rate reconciliation (statutory → effective)**

| | Year 1 $ | Year 1 % | Year 2 $ | Year 2 % | Year 3 $ | Year 3 % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Statutory tax rate | $58,800 | 21.000% | $67,200 | 21.000% | $52,500 | 21.000% |
| Tax-exempt municipal interest | (630) | (0.225)% | (630) | (0.197)% | (630) | (0.252)% |
| **Effective tax rate** | **$58,170** | **20.775%** | **$66,570** | **20.803%** | **$51,870** | **20.748%** |

Supporting math:  
- Statutory $: pretax × 21%.  
- Tax-exempt reconciling item each year: \(3{,}000 \times 21\% = \$630\); % = \(630 / \text{pretax}\).  
- Effective rate: income tax expense ÷ pretax GAAP income.  
- **Temporary differences do not appear** as reconciling items (they affect deferred tax, not the permanent gap between statutory and effective rates on pretax GAAP income).

**(e) December 31, Year 3 presentation**  
Same tax-paying component and jurisdiction → **net noncurrent deferred tax asset \(=\$3{,}360\)** (DTA $3,360 − DTL $0).  
Remaining deductible temporary difference: warranty liability **$16,000** expected to reverse (settle) in Year 4 → DTA \(16{,}000 \times 21\% = \$3{,}360\).

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** is one compound entry: current tax payable from the tax return, plus/minus deferred true-ups to schedule ending balances. Permanent differences change **tax expense vs. statutory pretax × rate** and drive the **rate reconciliation**; temporary differences drive **DTA/DTL** only.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Silverline Petrochem: multi-year multiple differences (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — same multi-difference path (DTL + DTA + permanent) with different pretax income, temporary amounts, permanent item, and enacted rate; period-end JEs and rate recon  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverline Petrochem Holdings** is a calendar-year company. Pretax GAAP income (after all items below):

| Year | Pretax GAAP income |
|------|-------------------:|
| Year 1 | $420,000 |
| Year 2 | $380,000 |
| Year 3 | $460,000 |

Enacted tax rate **25%** all years. Deferred tax beginning balances = **$0** on January 1, Year 1.

Book–tax differences:

1. **Prepaid insurance:** Paid/recorded **$50,000** on December 31, Year 1 for Year 2 coverage (tax deductible when paid; GAAP expense in Year 2).  
2. **Warranty accrual:** **$72,000** accrued December 31, Year 1; cash settlements **$24,000** in each of Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4 (deductible for tax when paid).  
3. **Municipal bond interest:** **$8,000** each year (nontaxable permanent difference; included in pretax GAAP).

No valuation allowance. Accounting and tax years end December 31.

**Required:**  
(a) Taxable income and income tax payable for Years 1–3.  
(b) DTL and DTA ending-balance schedules for Years 1–3.  
(c) **Period-end adjusting** income-tax JEs for Years 1–3 (show Dr = Cr).

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Northpine: mixed origins/settlements, nontaxable + nondeductible, single period-end JE + recon
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE when beginning DTL settles, new taxable and deductible temporary differences originate, and **both** nontaxable and nondeductible permanent items affect the rate reconciliation; net BS presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Northpine Logistics Group** (calendar year) has an enacted tax rate of **21%**. On January 1 of the current year:

- **Deferred Tax Liability** credit balance **$5,040**, entirely related to **prepaid rent** of **$24,000** that will be fully expensed for GAAP this year (already deducted for tax last year).  
- **Deferred Tax Asset** balance **$0**.

Pretax GAAP income for the year is **$360,000** and already includes all revenues and expenses below. Accounting and tax periods both end December 31. No valuation allowance.

Additional book–tax items for the year:

1. **Warranty accrual (new deductible temporary difference):** Warranty expense accrued **$35,000**; not tax-deductible until paid (no cash settlements this year).  
2. **Depreciation (new taxable temporary difference):** Tax depreciation exceeds book depreciation by **$40,000**.  
3. **Municipal bond interest (nontaxable permanent):** **$5,000** included in pretax GAAP income.  
4. **Officer life insurance premiums (nondeductible permanent):** **$8,000** included in pretax GAAP expense / reduces pretax GAAP income.  
5. **Settlement of beginning temporary difference:** The **$24,000** prepaid rent reverses fully this year (as described above).

**Required:**  
(a) Compute taxable income and income tax payable.  
(b) Compute ending DTL and DTA balances; prepare a DTL roll-forward showing settlement of prepaid and origin of excess tax depreciation.  
(c) **(Emphasis)** Record the single **December 31 period-end adjusting** income-tax journal entry.  
(d) Prepare the **rate reconciliation** in dollars and percentages (include both permanent items).  
(e) Present deferred taxes on the December 31 balance sheet (same component/jurisdiction).

**Answer key:**

**(a) Taxable income and income tax payable**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Pretax GAAP income | $360,000 |
| Prepaid rent reverse (add) | 24,000 |
| Warranty accrual (add) | 35,000 |
| Excess tax depreciation (subtract) | (40,000) |
| Municipal bond interest (subtract) | (5,000) |
| Life insurance premiums (add — nondeductible) | 8,000 |
| **Taxable income** | **$382,000** |
| Tax rate | × 21% |
| **Income tax payable** | **$80,220** |

Check: \(382{,}000 \times 0.21 = 80{,}220\).

**(b) Ending deferred balances and DTL roll-forward**

| Temporary difference | Cumulative TD | Ending deferred |
|---|---:|---:|
| Prepaid rent (settled) | $0 | DTL $0 |
| Excess tax depreciation (origin) | 40,000 taxable | DTL \(40{,}000 \times 21\% = \mathbf{\$8{,}400}\) |
| Warranty liability (origin) | 35,000 deductible | DTA \(35{,}000 \times 21\% = \mathbf{\$7{,}350}\) |

**DTL roll-forward (settlement + new origin):**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning DTL (prepaid) | $5,040 |
| Settlement: reverse prepaid (\(24{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | (5,040) |
| Origin: excess tax depreciation | 8,400 |
| **Ending DTL** | **$8,400** |

DTA: beginning $0 + origin $7,350 = **ending DTA $7,350**.

ΔDTL \(= 8{,}400 - 5{,}040 = +\$3{,}360\); ΔDTA \(= +\$7{,}350\).

**(c) Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis)**  
Expense \(= 80{,}220 + 3{,}360 - 7{,}350 = \mathbf{\$76{,}230}\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 76,230 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 7,350 | |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 3,360 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 80,220 |
| *Period-end: current tax; net DTL increase; originate DTA* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(76{,}230 + 7{,}350 = 83{,}580\) = Cr \(3{,}360 + 80{,}220\). **Balanced.**

Permanent-only cross-check (temporary items net to zero in expense):  
\(360{,}000 \times 21\% - 5{,}000 \times 21\% + 8{,}000 \times 21\% = 75{,}600 - 1{,}050 + 1{,}680 = 76{,}230\).

**(d) Rate reconciliation**

| | Dollars | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Statutory tax rate (\(360{,}000 \times 21\%\)) | $75,600 | 21.000% |
| Tax-exempt municipal interest | (1,050) | (0.292)% |
| Nondeductible life insurance premiums | 1,680 | 0.467% |
| **Effective tax rate** | **$76,230** | **21.175%** |

Checks: \(1{,}050 / 360{,}000 = 0.2917\% \approx 0.292\%\); \(1{,}680 / 360{,}000 = 0.4667\% \approx 0.467\%\); \(76{,}230 / 360{,}000 = 21.175\%\).  
Nontaxable revenue **lowers** ETR; nondeductible expense **raises** ETR. Temporary differences (prepaid settlement, depreciation, warranty) **do not** appear as reconciling lines.

**(e) Balance-sheet presentation (December 31)**  
Same component/jurisdiction → **net noncurrent deferred tax liability \(= \$8{,}400 - \$7{,}350 = \$1{,}050\)**.

**Key insight:** One **period-end** entry absorbs settlement of beginning deferreds, new origins, and current tax. Rate recon starts at statutory pretax × rate and adjusts **only** for permanent (and certain other non-temporary) items—never for ordinary temporary-difference origins/reversals.

### Q4 — MC (classification / disclosure)
**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Classification — which items enter the statutory-to-effective rate reconciliation vs. deferred tax measurement  

**Question 1:**  
In preparing a rate reconciliation from the U.S. statutory tax rate to the effective tax rate, which item is **properly included** as a reconciling item (assuming a constant enacted rate and no valuation allowance, tax credits, or foreign operations)?

- A) Origin of a deferred tax liability for prepaid insurance deductible when paid  
- B) Partial reversal of a warranty-related deferred tax asset when claims are paid  
- C) Nontaxable municipal bond interest included in pretax GAAP income  
- D) The entire increase in income tax payable for the year  

**Answer:** **C.** Permanent differences (e.g., nontaxable municipal interest) create a lasting gap between pretax GAAP × statutory rate and income tax expense; they appear in the rate reconciliation. Temporary-difference origins and reversals (A, B) affect deferred tax balances and the split of tax expense between current and deferred, but they do not change the effective rate relative to pretax GAAP when the rate is constant. Income tax payable (D) is the current tax amount, not a reconciling line by itself.

**LO:** LO 18-4  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — netting deferred tax balances for financial statement display  

**Question 2:**  
At year-end, MesaForge has a deferred tax asset of $6,720 and a deferred tax liability of $0, both related to the same U.S. tax-paying component and jurisdiction. How should deferred taxes be **classified and presented** on the balance sheet under current GAAP (ASC 740)?

- A) Current deferred tax asset $6,720  
- B) Noncurrent deferred tax asset $6,720  
- C) Current asset $3,360 and noncurrent asset $3,360  
- D) Contra equity valuation allowance $6,720  

**Answer:** **B.** Deferred tax assets and liabilities are classified as **noncurrent**. For a given tax-paying component and tax jurisdiction, DTA and DTL are **offset** and presented as a single noncurrent amount. Here net DTA = $6,720 noncurrent. There is no automatic current/noncurrent split of deferred taxes, and a valuation allowance is used only when realization is not more-likely-than-not (not indicated here).
