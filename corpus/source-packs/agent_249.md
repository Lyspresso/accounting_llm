# Agent 249 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-4

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Compute earnings per share (EPS) with a simple capital structure  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Basic EPS formula:** net income available to common stockholders ÷ weighted-average common shares outstanding
- **Simple capital structure:** equity consists only of common stock (one or more classes); no instruments that could convert into common and dilute EPS
- **Initial recognition JE:** cash issuance of common stock that establishes shares outstanding for the EPS denominator
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** weighted-average common shares schedule when shares are constant all year; multi-line basic EPS computation schedule (numerator / denominator / per-share)
- **Period-end adjusting / closing JE:** close net income (Income Summary) to retained earnings; EPS is a **presentation** measure (no separate “EPS” ledger account)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** cash common dividend declare-and-pay — **no** reduction of net income available to common for basic EPS
- **Multiple classes of common stock:** total weighted-average shares of **all** common classes enter the basic EPS denominator
- **Net loss per share:** net loss ÷ weighted-average common shares
- **Classification / presentation:** simple capital structure reports **basic EPS only** (single EPS presentation) on the face of the income statement

### Q1 — CORE — Basic EPS: WA shares schedule + EPS computation schedule (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for common stock issuance; subsequent measurement schedules (weighted-average common shares and basic EPS computation); period-end closing of net income; simple-structure income-statement presentation of basic EPS  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Robotics Corp.** is a calendar-year public company with a **simple capital structure** (only common stock; no preferred stock, convertible debt, options, warrants, or other potentially dilutive instruments). No noncontrolling interest exists.

On **January 1, Year 1**, Cedarline issues **180,000 shares** of **$5 par** common stock for **$22** cash per share. All 180,000 shares remain outstanding for the **entire year**. There are no additional share issuances, buybacks, stock dividends, or stock splits during Year 1. Net income for Year 1 is **$945,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the common stock issuance. Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule** for Year 1 (**subsequent measurement schedule — emphasis**).  
c. Prepare a **basic EPS computation schedule** showing numerator, denominator, and per-share amount.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end closing entry** that transfers net income to retained earnings (use Income Summary).  
e. Show how **net income** and **basic earnings per share** appear on the face of the Year 1 income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition of common stock issuance**

Cash proceeds = 180,000 × $22 = **$3,960,000**  
Common stock (par) = 180,000 × $5 = **$900,000**  
APIC—Common = $3,960,000 − $900,000 = **$3,060,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,960,000 | |
| Common Stock ($5 par) | | 900,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 3,060,000 |
| *Issue 180,000 shares of $5 par common at $22* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **3,960,000**; Cr = 900,000 + 3,060,000 = **3,960,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Weighted-average common shares outstanding schedule (Year 1) — emphasis**

| Period outstanding | Shares | Fraction of year (months/12) | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 – December 31 (entire year) | 180,000 | 12/12 = 1.00 | 180,000 |
| **Weighted-average common shares outstanding** | | | **180,000** |

Because no shares were issued or reacquired **during** the year after January 1, and the January 1 shares were outstanding for the full year, weighted-average common shares equal **180,000**.

**c. Basic EPS computation schedule (subsequent measurement — emphasis)**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income available to common stockholders (numerator) | $945,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (denominator) | 180,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$5.25** |

Computation:  
\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$945{,}000}{180{,}000} = \mathbf{\$5.25}
\]

*Check:* \(180{,}000 \times \$5.25 = \$945{,}000\).

**d. December 31, Year 1 — period-end closing of net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary | 945,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 945,000 |
| *Close Year 1 net income to retained earnings* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **945,000**. **Balanced.**

*Note:* Basic EPS is computed from net income and weighted-average shares for **presentation**; there is no journal entry that “books” EPS itself.

**e. Financial statement presentation (simple capital structure)**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $945,000 |
| Basic earnings per share | **$5.25** |

Only **basic** EPS is presented (single EPS amount) because the capital structure is **simple**.

**Key insight:** Under LO 20-4, **basic EPS** = net income available to common ÷ weighted-average common shares. The **subsequent measurement schedules** (WA shares and EPS computation) translate the year’s earnings and share count into a per-share amount. When only common stock is outstanding all year and no dilutive instruments exist, the company has a **simple capital structure** and reports a **single** basic EPS figure on the face of the income statement.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Basic EPS schedules twin
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: common stock issuance JE; weighted-average shares schedule and basic EPS computation schedule; period-end closing; simple-structure presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Ridge Analytics Inc.** has a **simple capital structure** (common stock only; no convertible or option-type instruments). No noncontrolling interest.

On **January 1, Year 1**, Summit Ridge issues **320,000 shares** of **$2 par** common stock for **$12** cash per share. All 320,000 shares remain outstanding **the entire year**. Net income for Year 1 is **$1,280,000**. No share activity occurs during the year after issuance.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1** common stock **initial recognition** journal entry.  
b. Prepare the **weighted-average common shares** schedule for Year 1.  
c. Prepare the **basic EPS computation schedule** and compute basic EPS.  
d. Prepare the **December 31** closing entry transferring net income to retained earnings.  
e. Present **net income** and **basic EPS** as they would appear on the Year 1 income statement.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

Cash = 320,000 × $12 = **$3,840,000**  
Common stock (par) = 320,000 × $2 = **$640,000**  
APIC—Common = $3,840,000 − $640,000 = **$3,200,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,840,000 | |
| Common Stock ($2 par) | | 640,000 |
| Additional Paid-in Capital—Common | | 3,200,000 |
| *Issue 320,000 shares of $2 par common at $12* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **3,840,000**; Cr = 640,000 + 3,200,000 = **3,840,000**. **Balanced.**

**b. Weighted-average common shares schedule**

| Period outstanding | Shares | Weight | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January 1 – December 31 | 320,000 | 12/12 | 320,000 |
| **Weighted-average common shares** | | | **320,000** |

**c. Basic EPS computation schedule**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income available to common stockholders | $1,280,000 |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding | 320,000 |
| **Basic EPS** | **$4.00** |

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$1{,}280{,}000}{320{,}000} = \mathbf{\$4.00}
\]

*Check:* \(320{,}000 \times \$4.00 = \$1{,}280{,}000\).

**d. December 31 — close net income**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Summary | 1,280,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 1,280,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **1,280,000**. **Balanced.**

**e. Income statement presentation (simple capital structure)**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net income | $1,280,000 |
| Basic earnings per share | **$4.00** |

**Key insight:** Same LO 20-4 core path as Q1 with fully changed company, par, issue price, share count, and net income. When shares are constant all year, the weighted-average denominator equals the shares outstanding for the full year, and the EPS computation schedule is a direct division of net income available to common by that denominator.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Two common classes; net loss; dividend settlement; multi-line EPS schedule
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Basic EPS with two classes of common stock (sum weighted-average shares); net loss per share; cash common dividend declaration and settlement JEs do not change the EPS numerator; simple capital structure presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Prairie Forge Materials LLC** is a calendar-year public company with a **simple capital structure**. Throughout Year 1 it has the following common stock outstanding (issued in prior years; **no** Year 1 share issuances, buybacks, stock dividends, or splits):

| Class | Shares outstanding all year | Par |
|---|---:|---|
| Class A common | **200,000** | $1 |
| Class B common | **50,000** | $1 |

There is **no** preferred stock and **no** potentially dilutive securities. No noncontrolling interest.

For Year 1, Prairie Forge reports a **net loss of $375,000**. On **December 10, Year 1**, the board declares a **cash dividend of $0.15 per share** on **all** common shares (Class A and Class B), payable **December 27, Year 1**. The dividend is paid on the payment date.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **December 10 declaration** and **December 27 payment** journal entries for the common cash dividend. Show Dr = Cr on each entry.  
b. Prepare a **weighted-average common shares schedule** that combines both common classes.  
c. Compute **basic earnings (loss) per share** for Year 1. Explicitly state the numerator and whether the common cash dividend affects it. Present a full **EPS computation schedule**.  
d. Show the **income statement presentation** of the net loss and basic EPS for Year 1.  
e. In one or two sentences, explain why Prairie Forge reports **only basic EPS** and why both common classes enter the denominator.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Common cash dividend — declaration and settlement**

Total common shares = 200,000 + 50,000 = **250,000**  
Cash dividend = 250,000 × $0.15 = **$37,500**

*December 10 — declaration*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained Earnings (or Dividends Declared) | 37,500 | |
| Dividends Payable | | 37,500 |
| *Declare $0.15 cash dividend on 250,000 common shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **37,500**. **Balanced.**

*December 27 — payment (settlement)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dividends Payable | 37,500 | |
| Cash | | 37,500 |
| *Pay common cash dividend* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **37,500**. **Balanced.**

**b. Weighted-average common shares schedule (two classes)**

| Class / period | Shares | Weight | Weighted shares |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Class A common — Jan 1 – Dec 31 | 200,000 | 12/12 | 200,000 |
| Class B common — Jan 1 – Dec 31 | 50,000 | 12/12 | 50,000 |
| **Weighted-average common shares outstanding** | | | **250,000** |

**c. Numerator and basic EPS (net loss case) — EPS computation schedule**

Net loss for the year = **$(375,000)**  
Common cash dividends **do not** change net income (loss) available to common for EPS. EPS measures earnings or loss **per common share**; distributing retained earnings as a common dividend does not increase the loss attributable to common for the EPS calculation.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Net income (loss) available to common stockholders (numerator) | $(375,000) |
| Weighted-average common shares outstanding (denominator) | 250,000 |
| **Basic earnings (loss) per share** | **$(1.50)** |

\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$(375{,}000)}{250{,}000} = \mathbf{\$(1.50)}
\]

*Check:* \(250{,}000 \times \$(1.50) = \$(375{,}000)\).

**d. Income statement presentation**

**Income Statement (partial) — Year ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Net loss | $(375,000) |
| Basic earnings (loss) per share | **$(1.50)** |

**e. Classification / presentation and multi-class denominator**  
Prairie Forge has a **simple capital structure** (only common stock; no instruments that could convert into common and dilute EPS), so a **single** EPS presentation—**basic** EPS (here, a loss per share)—is appropriate on the face of the income statement. When **several classes of common stock** exist, the **total** of the weighted-average shares of those classes is used as the basic EPS denominator.

**Key insight:** A **net loss** is still divided by weighted-average common shares to produce a **loss per share**. **Common** cash dividends reduce retained earnings when declared/settled but **do not** adjust the EPS numerator. Multiple **common** classes are aggregated in the denominator under LO 20-4’s simple-structure basic EPS path. (Preferred-dividend claims and mid-year share activity refinements are developed under related EPS LOs.)

### Q4 — MC — Simple capital structure: which EPS on the income statement
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Classification of which EPS amount(s) appear on the face of the income statement when the capital structure is simple  
**Question:**  
**Northwind Components Inc.** has only common stock outstanding for the entire year. It has no convertible debt, convertible preferred stock, stock options, warrants, or other potentially dilutive instruments. Net income is **$720,000** and weighted-average common shares outstanding are **160,000**. Which earnings-per-share presentation is appropriate on the face of Northwind’s income statement?

- A) Basic EPS of $4.50 only  
- B) Diluted EPS of $4.50 only  
- C) Both basic EPS of $4.50 and diluted EPS of $4.50 with equal prominence  
- D) No EPS disclosure is required because the company has no dilutive securities  

**Answer:** **A.** With a **simple capital structure**, the company presents **basic EPS only** (single EPS presentation) on the face of the income statement. Basic EPS = $720,000 ÷ 160,000 = **$4.50**. Diluted EPS is required when the structure is **complex** (potential common shares exist). Absence of dilutives means the LO 20-4 simple-structure presentation is **basic EPS alone**, not dual basic-and-diluted reporting.

### Q5 — MC — Elementary basic EPS formula
**LO:** LO 20-4  
**Concept:** Classification of the correct elementary basic EPS computation for a simple capital structure  
**Question:**  
**Blue Harbor Outfitters Corp.** has a simple capital structure. Common shares outstanding for the entire year: **240,000**. Net income: **$792,000**. No preferred stock. What is basic earnings per share for the year, and which description of the computation is correct?

- A) Basic EPS = $3.30; net income available to common ÷ weighted-average common shares outstanding  
- B) Basic EPS = $3.30; net income ÷ year-end common shares only when shares changed mid-year  
- C) Basic EPS = $6.60; net income ÷ (weighted-average common shares × ½)  
- D) Basic EPS = $3.30; cash dividends declared on common ÷ weighted-average common shares  

**Answer:** **A.**  
\[
\text{Basic EPS} = \frac{\$792{,}000}{240{,}000} = \mathbf{\$3.30}
\]  
Elementary basic EPS divides **net income available to common stockholders** by **weighted-average common shares outstanding**. With shares constant all year, the weighted average equals 240,000. Common cash dividends are not the numerator; there is no “half-year” factor when shares were outstanding the entire year.
