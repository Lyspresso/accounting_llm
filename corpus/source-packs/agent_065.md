# Agent 65 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-9

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Retail inventory methods have two other options: the LIFO retail method (Appendix 10A — LIFO retail and dollar-value LIFO retail)  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- LIFO retail uses the **average cost retail** approach (net markups **and** net markdowns enter the **current-year** cost ratio; ratios exclude beginning inventory from the current-year ratio)
- Separate **cost ratios** for the base (beginning inventory) layer and the current-year layer
- **Layering** ending inventory at retail into base and incremental layers; convert each layer at its own cost ratio
- Period-end **adjusting JE** to reduce goods available for sale to estimated LIFO retail ending inventory and record COGS (periodic)
- **Dollar-value LIFO retail**: convert ending inventory at retail to base-year retail dollars (÷ price index); apply **both** the layer’s price index and cost ratio
- Layer **increases** (new LIFO retail layers) and layer **liquidations** when base-year retail declines
- Change from **conventional retail** to LIFO retail: restate inventory at the change date (adoption JE)
- Classification / presentation: inventory at LIFO retail cost; disclosure of method and LIFO-related layers/index

### Q1 — CORE — LIFO retail: first-year estimation, layer schedule, and period-end JE
**LO:** LO 10-9  
**Concept:** Initial application of LIFO retail — ending inventory at retail (average cost approach), base vs current cost ratios, layer valuation, and period-end COGS/inventory JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedar Ridge Outfitters** elects the **LIFO retail** method on **January 1, Year 1**. Prices are assumed stable within the year (quantity changes only). Net markups and net markdowns relate entirely to purchases. The company uses a **periodic** inventory system and a December 31 year-end. Year 1 data:

| | At Cost | At Retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory (becomes base LIFO layer) | $50,000 | $80,000 |
| Net purchases | 300,000 | 500,000 |
| Net markups | — | 50,000 |
| Net markdowns | — | 50,000 |
| Net sales | — | 420,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute **estimated ending inventory at retail** using the average cost retail approach required for LIFO retail (include net markups and net markdowns with purchases).  
b. Compute the **beginning inventory cost ratio** and the **current-year cost ratio** (current-year ratio **excludes** beginning inventory). Round ratios to three decimal places if needed.  
c. Prepare the **LIFO retail layer schedule** and compute ending inventory at **LIFO retail cost**.  
d. Compute **cost of goods sold** for Year 1.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** that (1) establishes ending inventory at LIFO retail cost and (2) records COGS, closing beginning inventory and net purchases into COGS/EI (periodic close).  
f. Briefly explain why the LIFO retail method does **not** use the conventional retail (LCM-approximating) cost ratio.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Ending inventory at retail (average cost retail approach)**

| | Cost | Retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $50,000 | $80,000 |
| Net purchases | 300,000 | 500,000 |
| Net markups | | 50,000 |
| Net markdowns | | (50,000) |
| **Subtotal excluding beginning inventory** | **300,000** | **500,000** |
| Goods available for sale | $350,000 | $580,000 |
| Net sales | | (420,000) |
| **Estimated ending inventory at retail** | | **$160,000** |

**Check:** $80,000 + $500,000 + $50,000 − $50,000 − $420,000 = **$160,000**.

**b. Cost ratios**

Beginning inventory (base) cost ratio:  
\[
\frac{\$50{,}000}{\$80{,}000} = \mathbf{0.625}
\]

Current-year cost ratio (purchases only; markups and markdowns in retail denominator):  
\[
\frac{\$300{,}000}{\$500{,}000 + \$50{,}000 - \$50{,}000} = \frac{\$300{,}000}{\$500{,}000} = \mathbf{0.600}
\]

**c. LIFO retail layer schedule**

Ending retail of $160,000 exceeds beginning retail of $80,000 → new Year 1 layer of **$80,000** at retail.

| Layer | Inventory at retail | Cost ratio | Inventory at LIFO cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base (beginning inventory) | $80,000 | 0.625 | $50,000 |
| Year 1 increment | 80,000 | 0.600 | 48,000 |
| **Total** | **$160,000** | | **$98,000** |

Ending inventory at LIFO retail cost = **$98,000**.

**d. Cost of goods sold**  
GAFS at cost = $50,000 + $300,000 = **$350,000**  
COGS = $350,000 − $98,000 = **$252,000**

**e. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting / closing entry (periodic)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Merchandise Inventory (ending, LIFO retail) | 98,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 252,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Merchandise Inventory (beginning) | | 50,000 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Purchases (net) | | 300,000 |
| *Close BI and purchases; record EI at LIFO retail cost and COGS* | | |

**Check:** Dr $98,000 + $252,000 = $350,000; Cr $50,000 + $300,000 = $350,000. Balanced.

*(Equivalent single presentation if inventory still sits at GAFS cost $350,000 before close: Dr COGS $252,000 / Cr Merchandise Inventory $252,000 to bring inventory down to $98,000.)*

**f. Why not conventional retail for LIFO retail**  
Conventional retail **excludes net markdowns from the cost-ratio denominator**, producing a lower cost ratio that approximates **lower of cost or market**. LIFO retail is a **cost** method: it layers inventory at cost using the **average cost retail** approach (markdowns **included** in the current-year ratio) so that each LIFO layer is measured at historical cost, not LCM.

**Key insight:** Under LIFO retail, ending retail is computed like average cost retail, but ending inventory is valued in **layers**—base inventory at the beginning cost ratio and any quantity increase at the **current-year** cost ratio only.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — LIFO retail estimation and period-end JE
**LO:** LO 10-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of LIFO retail layer valuation and period-end inventory/COGS recognition  
**Scenario:**  
**Pine Hollow Mercantile** adopts the LIFO retail method on January 1 of the current year. Prices are stable within the year. Data for the year ended December 31:

| | At Cost | At Retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $72,000 | $120,000 |
| Net purchases | 414,000 | 690,000 |
| Net markups | — | 60,000 |
| Net markdowns | — | 30,000 |
| Net sales | — | 600,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Compute ending inventory at retail and both cost ratios (base and current year).  
b. Prepare the LIFO retail layer schedule and ending inventory at LIFO cost.  
c. Compute COGS.  
d. Prepare the period-end journal entry to record ending inventory and COGS (periodic close of BI and purchases).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Ending inventory at retail and cost ratios**

Goods available at retail = $120,000 + $690,000 + $60,000 − $30,000 = **$840,000**  
Ending inventory at retail = $840,000 − $600,000 = **$240,000**

Base cost ratio = $72,000 / $120,000 = **0.600**  
Current-year cost ratio = $414,000 / ($690,000 + $60,000 − $30,000) = $414,000 / $720,000 = **0.575**

**b. Layer schedule**

Retail increase = $240,000 − $120,000 = **$120,000**

| Layer | At retail | Cost ratio | At LIFO cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base | $120,000 | 0.600 | $72,000 |
| Current year | 120,000 | 0.575 | 69,000 |
| **Total** | **$240,000** | | **$141,000** |

**c. COGS**  
GAFS cost = $72,000 + $414,000 = $486,000  
COGS = $486,000 − $141,000 = **$345,000**

**d. Period-end JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Merchandise Inventory (ending, LIFO retail) | 141,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 345,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Merchandise Inventory (beginning) | | 72,000 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Purchases (net) | | 414,000 |

**Check:** Dr $141,000 + $345,000 = $486,000; Cr $72,000 + $414,000 = $486,000. Balanced.

**Key insight:** Same three-step LIFO retail process with all numbers changed: EI at retail → separate cost ratios → multiply each retail layer by its own ratio.

### Q3 — CORE — Dollar-value LIFO retail multi-year layers, liquidation, conversion JE, and presentation
**LO:** LO 10-9  
**Concept:** Dollar-value LIFO retail layer schedule (price index × cost ratio); layer liquidation when base-year retail declines; adoption JE from conventional retail; classification/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Retail Group** adopts the **dollar-value LIFO retail** method on **January 1, Year 1**. The price index at adoption (base) is **1.00**. Beginning inventory on that date: **$60,000 cost / $100,000 retail** (base cost ratio **0.60**). Ignore income taxes.

**Year 1**

| | At Cost | At Retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $60,000 | $100,000 |
| Net purchases | 429,000 | 660,000 |
| Net markups | — | 40,000 |
| Net markdowns | — | 40,000 |
| Net sales | — | 550,000 |
| Year-end price index | | **1.05** |

**Year 2**

| | At Cost | At Retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory at retail (carryforward) | | $210,000 |
| Net purchases | 504,000 | 840,000 |
| Net markups | — | 40,000 |
| Net markdowns | — | 40,000 |
| Net sales | — | 760,000 |
| Year-end price index | | **1.16** |

**Year 3**

| | At Cost | At Retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory at retail (carryforward) | | $290,000 |
| Net purchases | 450,000 | 750,000 |
| Net markups | — | 30,000 |
| Net markdowns | — | 30,000 |
| Net sales | — | 860,000 |
| Year-end price index | | **1.20** |

**Additional fact (adoption angle):** Immediately **before** January 1, Year 1, Northline’s books showed ending inventory under the **conventional retail** method of **$58,500**. Management restates inventory to LIFO retail base cost of **$60,000** on adoption (prospective LIFO base; difference is treated as an adoption adjustment to beginning retained earnings for this problem).

**Required:**  
a. For **Year 1**: compute EI at retail, current-year cost ratio, EI at base-year retail, DV LIFO layer schedule, and DV LIFO ending inventory at cost.  
b. For **Year 2**: same steps (show three layers).  
c. For **Year 3**: same steps; identify which layers are **liquidated** (partial or full).  
d. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry to restate inventory from conventional retail carrying amount to the LIFO retail base.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3** period-end entry to record Year 3 COGS and ending inventory, given Year 3 beginning inventory at **DV LIFO cost of $163,050** (end of Year 2) and Year 3 net purchases of $450,000.  
f. State balance-sheet **classification** and one **disclosure** item appropriate for dollar-value LIFO retail.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 1 — dollar-value LIFO retail**

EI at retail = $100,000 + $660,000 + $40,000 − $40,000 − $550,000 = **$210,000**  
Current-year cost ratio = $429,000 / ($660,000 + $40,000 − $40,000) = $429,000 / $660,000 = **0.650**  
EI at base-year retail = $210,000 / 1.05 = **$200,000**

| Layer (base-year retail) | Price index | Cost ratio | DV LIFO cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base $100,000 | 1.00 | 0.60 | $60,000 |
| Year 1 $100,000 | 1.05 | 0.65 | 68,250 |
| **Total base-year $200,000** | | | **$128,250** |

**b. Year 2**

EI at retail = $210,000 + $840,000 + $40,000 − $40,000 − $760,000 = **$290,000**  
Year 2 cost ratio = $504,000 / ($840,000 + $40,000 − $40,000) = $504,000 / $840,000 = **0.600**  
EI at base-year retail = $290,000 / 1.16 = **$250,000**

| Layer (base-year retail) | Price index | Cost ratio | DV LIFO cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base $100,000 | 1.00 | 0.60 | $60,000 |
| Year 1 $100,000 | 1.05 | 0.65 | 68,250 |
| Year 2 $50,000 | 1.16 | 0.60 | 34,800 |
| **Total base-year $250,000** | | | **$163,050** |

**c. Year 3 — layer liquidation**

EI at retail = $290,000 + $750,000 + $30,000 − $30,000 − $860,000 = **$180,000**  
Year 3 cost ratio = $450,000 / ($750,000 + $30,000 − $30,000) = $450,000 / $750,000 = **0.600**  
EI at base-year retail = $180,000 / 1.20 = **$150,000**

Base-year retail declined from $250,000 to $150,000 → **no Year 3 layer**. Liquidations (in LIFO order):  
- Entire **Year 2** layer of $50,000 base-year retail is liquidated.  
- **Year 1** layer reduced from $100,000 to $50,000 base-year retail (partial liquidation of $50,000).  
- Base layer of $100,000 remains intact.

| Layer (base-year retail) | Price index | Cost ratio | DV LIFO cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base $100,000 | 1.00 | 0.60 | $60,000 |
| Year 1 (remaining) $50,000 | 1.05 | 0.65 | 34,125 |
| **Total base-year $150,000** | | | **$94,125** |

**d. January 1, Year 1 — adoption / restatement to LIFO retail base**  
Increase needed = $60,000 − $58,500 = **$1,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Merchandise Inventory | 1,500 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Retained Earnings | | 1,500 |
| *Restate inventory from conventional retail carrying amount to LIFO retail base layer cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,500. Balanced.

**e. December 31, Year 3 — period-end JE after layer liquidation**  
GAFS at cost = BI (DV LIFO) $163,050 + Purchases $450,000 = **$613,050**  
COGS = $613,050 − EI $94,125 = **$518,925**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Merchandise Inventory (ending, DV LIFO retail) | 94,125 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 518,925 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Merchandise Inventory (beginning) | | 163,050 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Purchases (net) | | 450,000 |

**Check:** Dr $94,125 + $518,925 = $613,050; Cr $163,050 + $450,000 = $613,050. Balanced.

**f. Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
- **Classification:** Merchandise inventory under dollar-value LIFO retail is a **current asset** on the balance sheet at **LIFO cost** (not at current retail).  
- **Disclosure:** Accounting policy note stating that inventory is valued using the **dollar-value LIFO retail** method (cost-to-retail ratios by layer; external price indices), and often a **LIFO reserve** (or FIFO-equivalent inventory) so users can convert to a non-LIFO basis for comparability.

**Key insight:** Dollar-value LIFO retail first deflates ending retail to **base-year dollars**, assigns layers, then multiplies each layer by **both** its price index and its cost ratio. A decline in base-year retail **liquidates** the most recent layers first—raising COGS relative to maintaining those layers.

### Q4 — MC (method / classification)
**LO:** LO 10-9  
**Concept:** Distinguishing LIFO retail vs conventional retail and identifying dollar-value LIFO retail measurement elements  

**Question 1:**  
Which statement best describes the **LIFO retail** method (stable prices within the period)?  
- A) Ending inventory is valued with a single cost ratio that excludes markdowns from the denominator to approximate LCM.  
- B) Beginning inventory and current purchases share one blended cost ratio that includes markdowns; layers are not used.  
- C) Ending inventory at retail is layered; the base layer uses the beginning cost ratio and any retail increase uses a current-year cost ratio that includes markups and markdowns (average cost approach).  
- D) Ending inventory at retail is always multiplied only by the current-year price index, with no separate cost ratio by layer.

**Answer:** **C.** LIFO retail layers inventory: base at BI cost ratio; increments at the current-year average cost retail ratio (markups and markdowns in the ratio). Conventional retail (A) is the LCM approximation, not LIFO retail.

**Question 2:**  
Under the **dollar-value LIFO retail** method, a new inventory layer is measured by multiplying the layer’s base-year retail amount by:  
- A) The current-year cost ratio only.  
- B) The current-year price index only.  
- C) The base-year cost ratio and a price index of 1.00 only.  
- D) **Both** the price index for the year the layer was added **and** that year’s cost ratio.

**Answer:** **D.** Each DV LIFO retail layer = base-year retail layer × layer-year price index × layer-year cost ratio.
