# Agent 109 — CORE DEMO — LO 19-4

**Chapter:** 19  
**LO title:** Describe and account for preferred stock  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (equity preferred):** Dr Cash; Cr Preferred Stock at par; Cr Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock for excess (callable / convertible / optionally redeemable non-mandatory)
- **Initial recognition JE (mandatorily redeemable):** classified as a **liability** at issue proceeds / present value of fixed redemption (ASC 480-10); not equity
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** for mandatorily redeemable preferred with fixed amount and fixed date, accrete carrying amount to redemption using the **implicit interest rate**; period-end Dr Interest Expense / Cr Liability
- **Disposal — call/recall:** remove Preferred Stock + related PIC—PS; cash at call price; **excess of cash over equity carrying amount → Retained Earnings** (debit); shortfall → additional paid-in capital (credit)
- **Disposal — conversion (book value method):** remove Preferred Stock + PIC—PS; credit Common Stock at par of shares issued; remainder to PIC—Common Stock (or debit RE if par of common exceeds carrying of preferred)
- **Classification / presentation:** mandatorily redeemable → **noncurrent liability** (unless due within one year); callable by issuer → **permanent equity**; redeemable solely at stockholder option (not mandatory) → equity (textbook core); cumulative feature described (dividends in arrears disclosed when undeclared — LO 19-5 for declaration)

### Q1 — CORE — Preferred stock initial recognition, mandatorily redeemable accretion, call, and conversion
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of callable and convertible preferred as equity; mandatorily redeemable as liability with subsequent interest accretion schedule; call and conversion settlement JEs; classification presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Forge Industries** (calendar-year) completes three preferred-stock transactions on **January 1, Year 1**, and later settles two of the equity issues.

**Issue A — Callable preferred (equity)**  
- 3,600 shares of **8%** callable preferred stock, **$50 par**, issued at **$118** per share for cash.  
- Issuer may call the shares at **$120** per share on or after January 1, Year 2.  
- On **September 15, Year 3**, Meridian **calls all 3,600 shares** at the $120 call price.

**Issue B — Convertible preferred (equity)**  
- 2,000 shares of **6%** convertible preferred stock, **$25 par**, issued at **$95** per share for cash.  
- Each preferred share is convertible into **4** shares of **$5 par** common stock at the holder’s option.  
- On **March 1, Year 2**, holders convert **all** 2,000 preferred shares into common.

**Issue C — Mandatorily redeemable preferred (liability)**  
- On January 1, Year 1, Meridian issues mandatorily redeemable preferred stock for cash of **$86,384**.  
- The instrument is **mandatorily redeemable** for a **fixed** cash amount of **$100,000** on **December 31, Year 3** (three years; redemption not solely upon liquidation).  
- Market / implicit effective interest rate at inception is **5%**. Dividends, if any, are ignored for this accretion problem; focus on subsequent measurement of the liability under ASC 480.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JEs** for Issues A, B, and C.  
b. Prepare the complete **subsequent measurement (accretion) schedule** for Issue C for Years 1–3, and the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE**.  
c. Prepare the **March 1, Year 2 conversion JE** for Issue B (book value method).  
d. Prepare the **September 15, Year 3 call/recall JE** for Issue A.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 maturity/settlement JE** for Issue C after full accretion.  
f. **Classification / presentation:** For each of Issues A, B, and C, state the balance-sheet section immediately after issuance (and, for C, just before maturity). Briefly state what “cumulative dividend preference” means for preferred stock (no JE required).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

*Issue A — Callable preferred (equity)*  
Cash = 3,600 × $118 = **$424,800**  
Preferred Stock = 3,600 × $50 = **$180,000**  
PIC in Excess of Par—PS = 424,800 − 180,000 = **$244,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 424,800 | |
| Preferred Stock—Callable ($50 par) | | 180,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 244,800 |
| *Issue 3,600 shares callable preferred at $118* | | |

**Check:** Dr 424,800 = Cr 180,000 + 244,800. **Balanced.**

*Issue B — Convertible preferred (equity)*  
Cash = 2,000 × $95 = **$190,000**  
Preferred Stock = 2,000 × $25 = **$50,000**  
PIC—PS = 190,000 − 50,000 = **$140,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 190,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Convertible ($25 par) | | 50,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 140,000 |
| *Issue 2,000 shares convertible preferred at $95* | | |

**Check:** Dr 190,000 = Cr 50,000 + 140,000. **Balanced.**

*Issue C — Mandatorily redeemable preferred (liability at PV / issue proceeds)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 86,384 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 86,384 |
| *Initial recognition of mandatorily redeemable preferred as liability (ASC 480)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 86,384 = Cr 86,384. **Balanced.**  
(Do **not** credit equity Preferred Stock for mandatorily redeemable shares.)

**b. Subsequent measurement schedule — Issue C (fixed amount, fixed date; 5% implicit)**

| Date | Interest expense (5% × beg. CV) | Carrying amount of liability |
|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 1 (issue) | — | $86,384 |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $86,384 × 0.05 = **4,319** | 86,384 + 4,319 = **90,703** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | $90,703 × 0.05 = **4,535** | 90,703 + 4,535 = **95,238** |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | $95,238 × 0.05 = **4,762** | 95,238 + 4,762 = **100,000** |

**Rollforward check:** 86,384 + 4,319 + 4,535 + 4,762 = **100,000** (equals fixed redemption). ✓

*December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE (accretion)*  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,319 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 4,319 |
| *Accrete liability toward fixed redemption; interest cost under ASC 480* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,319 = Cr 4,319. **Balanced.**

*(Year 2 and Year 3 year-end adjusting JEs use interest of $4,535 and $4,762, respectively.)*

**c. Conversion of preferred into common — March 1, Year 2 (book value method)**

Carrying amount of convertible preferred equity = $50,000 + $140,000 = **$190,000**  
Common shares issued = 2,000 × 4 = **8,000** shares  
Common Stock = 8,000 × $5 = **$40,000**  
PIC—Common Stock = 190,000 − 40,000 = **$150,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | 50,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 140,000 | |
| Common Stock ($5 par) | | 40,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 150,000 |
| *Convert 2,000 preferred into 8,000 common; book value method; no gain/loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 50,000 + 140,000 = Cr 40,000 + 150,000 = 190,000. **Balanced.**  
Net equity is **unchanged** (preferred equity reclassified to common equity).

**d. Call/recall of callable preferred — September 15, Year 3**

Cash paid = 3,600 × $120 = **$432,000**  
Equity carrying amount removed = $180,000 + $244,800 = **$424,800**  
Excess of cash over carrying amount → **Retained Earnings** = 432,000 − 424,800 = **$7,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Callable | 180,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 244,800 | |
| Retained Earnings | 7,200 | |
| Cash | | 432,000 |
| *Call 3,600 shares at $120; excess of call price over equity carrying amount reduces RE* | | |

**Check:** Dr 180,000 + 244,800 + 7,200 = Cr 432,000. **Balanced.**  
Assets and equity both decrease by **$432,000** (or equity by 424,800 + 7,200).

**e. Maturity / settlement of mandatorily redeemable preferred — December 31, Year 3**

After Year 3 accretion, liability carrying amount = **$100,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 100,000 | |
| Cash | | 100,000 |
| *Redeem mandatorily redeemable preferred at fixed contractual amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Classification / presentation**

| Issue | Classification immediately after issuance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A Callable preferred | **Stockholders’ equity** (permanent equity) — Preferred Stock + PIC—PS | Call is **issuer’s option**; not a liability |
| B Convertible preferred | **Stockholders’ equity** — Preferred Stock + PIC—PS | Conversion option does not reclassify to liability at issuance under the core LO treatment |
| C Mandatorily redeemable | **Noncurrent liability** (at $86,384; grows with accretion) | ASC 480-10: unconditional obligation to transfer assets on a specified date → **liability**, measured subsequently at PV with interest cost when amount and date are fixed. Just before Dec 31, Year 3 redemption, present as **current liability** if still outstanding at a prior year-end within one year of settlement |

**Cumulative dividend preference:** If preferred is **cumulative**, undeclared preferred dividends **accumulate** as **dividends in arrears**. Arrears are **not a liability** until the board **declares** a dividend, but they must be **disclosed** and must be paid before any common dividends. (Declaration accounting is LO 19-5.)

**Key insight:** Non-mandatory preferred is equity at issue (par + APIC). Mandatorily redeemable preferred is a **liability** accreted with **interest expense**. Call removes equity for cash (any excess hits RE); conversion only reclassifies within equity.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Preferred stock life-cycle twin
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: equity preferred issuance, conversion, call with RE charge; mandatorily redeemable liability with 4-year accretion schedule and settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Components Co.** (calendar-year) enters the following preferred arrangements.

**Issue 1 — Callable preferred**  
- **January 1, Year 1:** Issue **5,000** shares of **7%** callable preferred, **$20 par**, at **$48** per share cash.  
- Call price = **$50** per share (issuer option).  
- **June 30, Year 2:** Call **all** 5,000 shares at $50.

**Issue 2 — Convertible preferred**  
- **January 1, Year 1:** Issue **1,500** shares of convertible preferred, **$40 par**, at **$110** per share cash.  
- Conversion ratio: **3** shares of **$10 par** common per preferred share.  
- **October 1, Year 1:** Convert **all** preferred into common.

**Issue 3 — Mandatorily redeemable preferred**  
- **January 1, Year 1:** Issue for cash **$63,367**.  
- **Mandatory** redemption for fixed **$80,000** cash on **December 31, Year 4** (four years).  
- Implicit effective rate = **6%**.

**Required:**  
a. Initial recognition JEs for Issues 1, 2, and 3 on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Full **accretion schedule** for Issue 3 (Years 1–4) and the **December 31, Year 2** period-end adjusting JE only.  
c. Conversion JE on October 1, Year 1 (Issue 2).  
d. Call JE on June 30, Year 2 (Issue 1).  
e. Settlement JE on December 31, Year 4 (Issue 3).  
f. State balance-sheet classification of each issue right after issuance.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

*Issue 1 — Callable*  
Cash = 5,000 × $48 = **$240,000**  
PS = 5,000 × $20 = **$100,000**  
PIC—PS = **$140,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 240,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Callable | | 100,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 140,000 |

**Check:** 240,000 = 100,000 + 140,000. **Balanced.**

*Issue 2 — Convertible*  
Cash = 1,500 × $110 = **$165,000**  
PS = 1,500 × $40 = **$60,000**  
PIC—PS = **$105,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 165,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | | 60,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 105,000 |

**Check:** 165,000 = 60,000 + 105,000. **Balanced.**

*Issue 3 — Mandatorily redeemable liability*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 63,367 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 63,367 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 63,367. **Balanced.**

**b. Accretion schedule — Issue 3 at 6%**

| Date | Interest (6% × beg. CV) | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, Year 1 | — | $63,367 |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | 63,367 × 0.06 = **3,802** | **67,169** |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 67,169 × 0.06 = **4,030** | **71,199** |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 71,199 × 0.06 = **4,272** | **75,471** |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 75,471 × 0.06 = **4,529** | **80,000** |

**Rollforward check:** 63,367 + 3,802 + 4,030 + 4,272 + 4,529 = **80,000**. ✓

*December 31, Year 2 — Period-end adjusting JE*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,030 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 4,030 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,030. **Balanced.** Ending liability after Year 2 entry = **$71,199**.

**c. Conversion — October 1, Year 1**

Common shares = 1,500 × 3 = **4,500**  
Common Stock = 4,500 × $10 = **$45,000**  
PIC—CS = 165,000 − 45,000 = **$120,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Convertible | 60,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 105,000 | |
| Common Stock ($10 par) | | 45,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 120,000 |

**Check:** Dr 165,000 = Cr 165,000. **Balanced.** Total equity unchanged.

**d. Call — June 30, Year 2**

Cash paid = 5,000 × $50 = **$250,000**  
Carrying equity = 100,000 + 140,000 = **$240,000**  
Debit RE = **$10,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preferred Stock—Callable | 100,000 | |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | 140,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | 10,000 | |
| Cash | | 250,000 |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 + 140,000 + 10,000 = Cr 250,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Settlement — December 31, Year 4**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 80,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Classification after issuance**

| Issue | Presentation |
|---|---|
| 1 Callable | Stockholders’ equity (Preferred Stock + PIC—PS) |
| 2 Convertible | Stockholders’ equity (Preferred Stock + PIC—PS) |
| 3 Mandatorily redeemable | **Liability** (noncurrent until within one year of Dec 31, Year 4 redemption) |

**Key insight:** Changing the share counts, prices, conversion ratio, rate, and horizon does not change the model: equity preferred = par + APIC; mandatory redeemable = liability + interest accretion; call excess → RE; conversion = equity reclass only.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Classification matrix and comparative initial recognition
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Classification presentation of callable vs redeemable-at-holder-option vs mandatorily redeemable preferred; comparative initial recognition JEs; disclosure of outstanding preferred features  
**Scenario:**  
On **April 1, Year 1**, **Harborline Packaging Inc.** raises capital by issuing three separate preferred packages for cash (each package is a separate class; ignore underwriting costs):

| Class | Shares | Par | Issue price | Key feature |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **P-Call** | 800 | $100 | $125 | **Callable** by Harborline at $130 anytime after Year 1 |
| **P-Red** | 800 | $100 | $125 | **Redeemable** at the **stockholder’s option** at $130 after Year 3 (not mandatory; issuer is not unconditionally obligated on a fixed date) |
| **P-Man** | 800 | $100 | $125 | **Mandatorily redeemable** for **$104,000** total cash on **March 31, Year 5** (fixed amount and date certain) |

Total cash received for each class = 800 × $125 = **$100,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** for **P-Call**.  
b. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** for **P-Red** under the core textbook treatment for non-mandatorily redeemable preferred (equity).  
c. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** for **P-Man** (liability).  
d. Complete the **classification / presentation table** (section of the balance sheet for each class right after issuance).  
e. Assume that on **March 31, Year 5**, P-Man is settled for the contractual **$104,000** and that the liability has already been accreted fully to $104,000. Prepare the **settlement JE**.  
f. List **preferred features** Harborline would typically **disclose** for outstanding preferred (title/class, par, shares authorized/issued/outstanding, dividend rate, call/redemption/conversion terms, and any dividends in arrears if cumulative).

**Answer key:**  

**a. P-Call initial recognition (equity)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 100,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Callable (800 × $100) | | 80,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 20,000 |

**Check:** 100,000 = 80,000 + 20,000. **Balanced.**

**b. P-Red initial recognition (equity — not mandatorily redeemable)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 100,000 | |
| Preferred Stock—Redeemable (800 × $100) | | 80,000 |
| Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Preferred Stock | | 20,000 |

**Check:** 100,000 = 80,000 + 20,000. **Balanced.**  
(Core LO: redeemable at the **holder’s option** but **not mandatorily redeemable** is still **equity**, not a liability under ASC 480. SEC “temporary equity” mezzanine presentation is an advanced / Expanding Your Knowledge path—not required for this core pack.)

**c. P-Man initial recognition (liability)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 100,000 | |
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | | 100,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 100,000. **Balanced.**  
Even though par is $100 × 800 = $80,000, **par is not used for the liability caption**—the instrument is measured as a **liability** at proceeds / subsequent PV accretion toward the fixed **$104,000** settlement (implicit rate would accrete the $4,000 difference over the remaining life as interest cost).

**d. Classification / presentation table**

| Class | Balance-sheet section after issuance | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| P-Call | **Stockholders’ equity** | Call option is the **issuer’s**; no unconditional obligation to redeem |
| P-Red | **Stockholders’ equity** (core) | Redemption is **optional** to the holder; not an unconditional fixed-date obligation of the issuer under ASC 480’s mandatory definition |
| P-Man | **Liability** (noncurrent until within one year of March 31, Year 5) | **Unconditional** obligation to transfer assets on a **specified date** → liability (ASC 480-10-25-4) |

**e. P-Man settlement — March 31, Year 5** (after full accretion to $104,000)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock Liability | 104,000 | |
| Cash | | 104,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 104,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Typical preferred disclosures**

- Title and description of each class (callable / redeemable / mandatorily redeemable / convertible / cumulative or noncumulative)  
- Par (or stated) value; dividend rate or amount  
- Shares **authorized, issued, and outstanding**  
- Call price and dates; redemption terms; conversion ratio and common par  
- Liquidation preference  
- **Dividends in arrears** on cumulative preferred (amount per share and in total), if any  
- Changes in preferred equity accounts during the period (statement of stockholders’ equity / notes)

**Key insight:** Same cash and same “preferred” label can land in **equity** or **liability** solely based on whether redemption is **mandatory** (unconditional, date-certain asset transfer). Accounting follows the **economic obligation**, not the word “stock” in the title.

### Q4 — MC (classification / method)
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Balance-sheet classification of mandatorily redeemable preferred stock  

**Question:** On January 1, Year 1, Northwind Tools Inc. issues for cash 4,000 shares of 5%, $25 par preferred stock that is **mandatorily redeemable** for a fixed price on December 31, Year 6. How should Northwind present this instrument on its December 31, Year 1 balance sheet?

- A) Preferred stock within permanent stockholders’ equity at par, with excess of proceeds over par in additional paid-in capital  
- B) Temporary equity (mezzanine) only if the company is privately held  
- C) **Noncurrent liability** (measured initially at issue proceeds / present value, with subsequent interest accretion when amount and settlement date are fixed)  
- D) Contra-equity treasury stock until the mandatory redemption date  

**Answer:** **C.** ASC 480 requires mandatorily redeemable financial instruments (other than those redeemable only upon liquidation of the entity) to be classified as **liabilities**. When both the amount and settlement date are fixed, subsequent measurement accretes the carrying amount using the rate implicit at inception, recognizing **interest cost**. Option A describes ordinary non-mandatory preferred equity. Option B confuses SEC temporary-equity guidance for instruments redeemable outside the issuer’s control but not necessarily ASC 480 mandatory liabilities. Option D is incorrect—treasury stock is reacquired equity, not a classification for newly issued mandatory redeemable preferred.

### Q5 — MC (conversion method)
**LO:** LO 19-4  
**Concept:** Book-value conversion of convertible preferred into common  

**Question:** Convertible preferred stock with a total equity carrying amount of $180,000 (par $40,000 + PIC—PS $140,000) is converted into common stock with aggregate par of $30,000. Using the book value method, the conversion entry credits Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock for:

- A) $0  
- B) $10,000  
- C) **$150,000**  
- D) $180,000  

**Answer:** **C.** Remove preferred equity of $180,000; credit Common Stock $30,000; credit PIC—Common Stock for the **remainder** $150,000. No gain or loss is recognized; total equity is unchanged.
