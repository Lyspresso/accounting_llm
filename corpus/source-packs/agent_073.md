# Agent 73 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-8

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for contributed property, plant, and equipment  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of **unconditional** contributed PPE at **fair value** (not donor carrying amount)
- Contribution revenue = aggregate fair value of assets received **minus** any cash/costs paid by the recipient
- Separate land vs. building (or equipment) fair-value allocations; only depreciable assets are depreciated
- Subsequent measurement: straight-line depreciation of contributed depreciable PPE; carrying-value schedule
- Period-end adjusting entry for depreciation on contributed assets
- Donor-imposed **conditions**: defer contribution revenue (liability) until barrier is met and right of return lapses
- Disposal of previously contributed PPE (update depreciation, remove cost and accum. dep., gain/loss)
- Classification/presentation: contribution revenue (not equity from owners); land nondepreciable; exchange vs. contribution

### Q1 — CORE — Unconditional land and building contribution: initial JE, dep schedule, year-end adjusting, carrying values
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of unconditional contributed land and building at FV net of transfer costs; subsequent SL depreciation schedule and period-end adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
On **December 31, Year 1**, the City of Riverton **unconditionally** donates a warehouse building and the land under it to **Northwind Logistics Inc.** to induce Northwind to locate operations in the city. An independent appraisal reliably measures fair values as:

| Asset | Fair value |
|---|---:|
| Building | $720,000 |
| Land | $180,000 |
| **Total** | **$900,000** |

Northwind pays **$12,000** cash for legal transfer and title costs on December 31, Year 1. The city receives **no** goods or services of approximately equal value in return. The building has an estimated remaining useful life of **25 years** and residual value of **$20,000**. Northwind uses **straight-line** depreciation and records a **full year** of depreciation in the year **following** acquisition (asset placed in service January 1, Year 2). Land is not depreciated.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the December 31, Year 1 journal entry to record the contributions received.  
b. Compute annual depreciation for the building and prepare a **carrying-value schedule** for the building for December 31 of Years 1–4 (through three full years of depreciation).  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** period-end adjusting entry for depreciation.  
d. State the amounts reported on the December 31, Year 2 **balance sheet** for Land, Building, Accumulated Depreciation—Building, and the net book value of the building.

**Answer key:**  

**a. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition (unconditional contribution)**  

Contribution revenue = ($720,000 + $180,000) − $12,000 = **$888,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 720,000 | |
| Land | 180,000 | |
| Cash | | 12,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 888,000 |
| *Record unconditional donated building and land at FV; transfer costs reduce contribution revenue* | | |

**Check:** Dr 720,000 + 180,000 = **900,000**; Cr 12,000 + 888,000 = **900,000**. Balanced.  
Assets are recorded at **fair value** (not at donor book value). Cash costs paid by the recipient reduce **Contribution Revenue**, not the capitalized land/building amounts under this LO’s core demo treatment (Demo 11-8 style).

**b. Annual depreciation and carrying-value schedule**  

Depreciable base = $720,000 − $20,000 = **$700,000**  
Annual SL depreciation = $700,000 ÷ 25 = **$28,000** per year (beginning Year 2)

| Date | Building cost | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 (acquisition) | $720,000 | $0 | $720,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 720,000 | 28,000 | 692,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 | 720,000 | 56,000 | 664,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 4 | 720,000 | 84,000 | 636,000 |

**Proof:** CV at end of Year 4 = $720,000 − 3 × $28,000 = $720,000 − $84,000 = **$636,000**.

**c. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 28,000 |
| *Full-year SL depreciation on contributed building* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000.

**d. December 31, Year 2 balance sheet amounts**  

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Land | $180,000 |
| Building | 720,000 |
| Less: Accumulated depreciation—Building | (28,000) |
| **Building, net** | **$692,000** |

**Key insight:** Unconditional contributed PPE is recognized at **fair value** when received. Contribution revenue equals total FV **minus** costs paid by the recipient. Depreciable contributed assets are depreciated like any other PPE using the FV cost base; land is not depreciated.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Appraised package with % allocation, different life and residual
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: lump-sum appraisal allocation to land/building, contribution revenue net of costs, subsequent depreciation  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Cedarfield Manufacturing** receives an **unconditional** donation of a production facility and site from a private foundation. The property is reliably appraised at a **total** fair value of **$960,000**, of which **30%** is assignable to land and **70%** to the building. Cedarfield pays **$8,000** cash for transfer costs. The donor receives nothing of equivalent value. The building has a **20-year** remaining life and **zero** residual value. Straight-line depreciation; full year in Year 1 (asset placed in service January 1).

**Required:**  
a. Allocate the appraisal to land and building; record the January 1, Year 1 receipt entry.  
b. Compute Year 1 depreciation and record the December 31, Year 1 adjusting entry.  
c. Prepare a two-year building carrying-value schedule (Dec 31 Year 1 and Dec 31 Year 2).  
d. Prove that Dr = Cr on the acquisition entry and that contribution revenue = total FV − transfer costs.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Allocation and January 1, Year 1 entry**  

Land FV = 0.30 × $960,000 = **$288,000**  
Building FV = 0.70 × $960,000 = **$672,000**  
Contribution revenue = $960,000 − $8,000 = **$952,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Building | 672,000 | |
| Land | 288,000 | |
| Cash | | 8,000 |
| Contribution Revenue | | 952,000 |
| *Unconditional contribution at FV; 30%/70% appraisal split* | | |

**Check:** Dr 672,000 + 288,000 = **960,000**; Cr 8,000 + 952,000 = **960,000**. Balanced.

**b. Year 1 depreciation**  

Annual dep = $672,000 ÷ 20 = **$33,600**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Building | 33,600 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Building | | 33,600 |

**Check:** 33,600 = 33,600.

**c. Building carrying-value schedule**  

| Date | Cost | Accum. dep. | Carrying value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Year 1 | $672,000 | $33,600 | $638,400 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 | 672,000 | 67,200 | 604,800 |

**Proof:** Year 2 CV = $672,000 − 2 × $33,600 = $672,000 − $67,200 = **$604,800**.

**d. Proofs**  
- Acquisition: 960,000 = 960,000.  
- Contribution revenue = $960,000 − $8,000 = **$952,000**.  
- Land remains **$288,000** (nondepreciable) on both year-ends.

**Key insight:** When only a **package** fair value is given, allocate by the stated percentages (or relative FVs) before recording the contribution. Changing the appraisal total, land %, transfer costs, life, and residual scales every amount but does not change the recognition pattern.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Conditional contribution deferral, later recognition, and disposal of contributed equipment
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Donor-imposed condition (defer then recognize contribution revenue); disposal of previously contributed PPE with period-end dep update  
**Scenario:**  

**Part A — Conditional contribution (land)**  
On **March 1, Year 1**, the Township of Oakridge transfers vacant industrial land (fair value **$250,000**) to **Harborline Foods Inc.** The agreement is **conditional**: Harborline must complete construction of a processing plant on the site by **February 28, Year 3**, or title reverts to the township (barrier + right of return). Harborline pays **$0** cash at transfer. On **November 15, Year 2**, Harborline completes the plant and the condition is satisfied; the township’s right of return lapses. (Ignore any construction accounting for the plant itself.)

**Part B — Disposal of previously contributed equipment**  
Separately, on **January 1, Year 1**, Harborline had received **unconditional** donated specialized equipment with fair value **$85,000** (no residual value; 5-year SL life; full year depreciation each year). On **July 1, Year 3**, Harborline sells the equipment for **$28,000** cash. Harborline’s year-end is December 31; depreciation is recorded annually at year-end unless an interim disposal requires update. Record depreciation for the first half of Year 3 before disposal.

**Required:**  
a. Part A: Journal entry on March 1, Year 1 (receipt under condition).  
b. Part A: Journal entry on November 15, Year 2 when the condition is met.  
c. Part B: Annual depreciation amount; journal entry to update depreciation on July 1, Year 3; journal entry to record the sale on July 1, Year 3.  
d. Part B: Prove the gain or loss on disposal (show cost, accum. dep. to disposal date, book value, cash, and gain/loss).

**Answer key:**  

**a. March 1, Year 1 — conditional receipt (defer revenue)**  

Because a **donor-imposed condition** exists (barrier + right of return), contribution revenue is **not** recognized until the condition is met. Record the land and a **deferred contribution** liability (or Refundable Advance / Deferred Contribution Revenue).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land | 250,000 | |
| Deferred Contribution Revenue (liability) | | 250,000 |
| *Conditional land transfer; revenue deferred until barrier overcome* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 250,000. No contribution revenue in Year 1 from this transfer.

**b. November 15, Year 2 — condition met**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Contribution Revenue | 250,000 | |
| Contribution Revenue | | 250,000 |
| *Condition satisfied; recognize contribution revenue* | | |

**Check:** 250,000 = 250,000. Land remains on the books at **$250,000** (nondepreciable).

**c. Part B — depreciation update and disposal**  

Annual depreciation = $85,000 ÷ 5 = **$17,000**  
Depreciation for Years 1 and 2 already taken by Dec 31, Year 2: 2 × $17,000 = **$34,000**  
Half-year (Jan 1–July 1, Year 3) update: 0.5 × $17,000 = **$8,500**

**July 1, Year 3 — update depreciation to disposal date**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Equipment | 8,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 8,500 |
| *Six months depreciation before disposal* | | |

**Check:** 8,500 = 8,500.

Accumulated depreciation at disposal = $34,000 + $8,500 = **$42,500**  
Book value = $85,000 − $42,500 = **$42,500**  
Cash proceeds = **$28,000**  
**Loss on disposal** = $42,500 − $28,000 = **$14,500**

**July 1, Year 3 — record sale**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 42,500 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 14,500 | |
| Equipment | | 85,000 |
| *Remove contributed equipment; recognize loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,000 + 42,500 + 14,500 = **85,000**; Cr = **85,000**. Balanced.

**d. Disposal proof**  

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cost (initial FV at contribution) | $85,000 |
| Accum. dep. (2.5 years × $17,000) | (42,500) |
| Book value at July 1, Year 3 | 42,500 |
| Cash proceeds | 28,000 |
| **Loss on disposal** | **$14,500** |

**Key insight:** **Conditional** contributions require **deferral** of revenue until the barrier is overcome and the right of return lapses; the asset may still be recorded when received (with a liability). After recognition, contributed PPE follows normal subsequent measurement and **disposal** rules (update dep, remove gross cost and accum. dep., cash vs. book value → gain/loss). Contributed cost basis is the original **fair value**, not the donor’s historical cost.

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement)
**LO:** LO 11-8  
**Concept:** Classification of contribution vs exchange; measurement of contribution revenue when recipient pays cash  

**Question 1:**  
Summit Clinic receives medical equipment from a manufacturer. The equipment’s fair value is **$45,000**. Summit pays the manufacturer **$2,000** cash. The manufacturer receives no other goods or services of approximately equal value. What amount of **contribution revenue** should Summit report?

- A) $0 (record only if purchased)  
- B) $43,000  
- C) $45,000  
- D) $47,000  

**Answer:** **B.** Contribution revenue = fair value of asset received − cash (or other costs) paid by the recipient = $45,000 − $2,000 = **$43,000**. The equipment is debited for **$45,000** (FV); cash is credited **$2,000**; contribution revenue is credited **$43,000**. (Same structure as Demo 11-8 / MC11-10 style core measurement.)

**Question 2:**  
Which statement correctly describes **recipient** accounting for contributed PPE under LO 11-8?

- A) Record the asset at the donor’s book value and credit Additional Paid-in Capital.  
- B) Record the asset at fair value and credit Contribution Revenue when the transfer is unconditional and not an exchange of approximately equal value.  
- C) Never record donated land because it has no cost to the recipient.  
- D) Always capitalize legal transfer costs into the Building account and never affect contribution revenue.  

**Answer:** **B.** Core LO 11-8: unconditional, nonreciprocal transfers are recorded at **fair value** with **Contribution Revenue** (net of any costs paid). Donor book value is irrelevant to the recipient. Land is recorded even though nondepreciable. Transfer costs reduce contribution revenue in the core demo treatment (assets stay at FV).
