# Agent 386 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-1

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for change in accounting estimate including a change in accounting estimate effected by change in accounting principle  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize PPE/intangible at cost; **no** catch-up or restatement entry when estimates later revise
- **Change in accounting estimate** (useful life, residual/salvage, warranty-type recurring estimates): **prospective** only — recompute depreciation/amortization from **carrying amount at change date** over **remaining life** to revised residual; **no** prior-period restatement (ASC 250-10-45-17)
- **Change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle** (e.g., depreciation method change): treated as a **change in estimate** — **prospective** from carrying amount at method-change date (ASC 250-10-45-18)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** record revised depreciation/amortization in the year of change and later years; multi-year AJE trail is the exam control point
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year depreciation/amortization rollforward (beginning CA, expense, accumulated, ending CA) **before and after** the estimate (or method) change
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove cost and accumulated depreciation/amortization; gain/loss = proceeds − carrying amount after revised depreciation
- **Disclosure:** effect on income from continuing operations, net income, and EPS when the change affects several future periods and is material (ASC 250-10-50-4)

### Q1 — CORE — Useful life and residual revision with multi-year period-end AJEs through disposal
**LO:** LO 21-1  
**Concept:** Prospective change in useful life and residual; multi-year period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis); subsequent measurement schedules before/after change; disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeway Packaging Systems Inc.** (calendar year-end) purchased specialized filling equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$360,000 cash**. Original estimates: **12-year** useful life, residual value **$24,000**, **straight-line** depreciation (full years; no partial-year convention issues).

On **January 1, Year 5**, after four full years of use, management revises estimates based on new line-speed data: total useful life from acquisition is now **10 years**, and residual value is **$8,000**. Depreciation method is unchanged (still SL).

On **December 31, Year 7**, after recording Year 7 depreciation under the revised estimates, Ridgeway sells the equipment for **$95,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Compute original annual SL depreciation and prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule for Years 1–4** (beginning carrying amount, depreciation expense, end-of-year accumulated depreciation, ending carrying amount).  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 through Year 4 period-end adjusting JEs** (same form each year under original estimates).  
d. Compute the **carrying amount on January 1, Year 5** and the **revised annual depreciation**. State whether any retrospective or cumulative catch-up entry is recorded.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 5 period-end adjusting JE** (year of change — emphasis).  
f. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule for Years 5–7** under the revised estimates, and state the form of the Year 6 and Year 7 period-end AJEs.  
g. Prepare the **December 31, Year 7 disposal JE** (after Year 7 depreciation).  
h. If material, what **disclosure** is required for Year 5 regarding the estimate change? Quantify the Year 5 pretax income effect relative to continuing the old estimates.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 360,000 | |
| Cash | | 360,000 |
| *Capitalize equipment at cash acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 360,000 = Cr 360,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Original SL and Years 1–4 subsequent measurement schedule**

Original depreciable base = $360,000 − $24,000 = **$336,000**  
Original annual SL depreciation = $336,000 ÷ 12 = **$28,000**

| Year | Beg. carrying amount | Depreciation expense | End. accum. dep. | End. carrying amount |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $360,000 | $28,000 | $28,000 | $332,000 |
| 2 | 332,000 | 28,000 | 56,000 | 304,000 |
| 3 | 304,000 | 28,000 | 84,000 | 276,000 |
| 4 | 276,000 | 28,000 | 112,000 | 248,000 |

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs — Years 1–4 (pre-change)**

*Each December 31 of Years 1–4:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 28,000 |
| *Original SL depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000 each year. **Balanced.** (Four identical AJEs; total accum. dep. after Year 4 = 4 × $28,000 = **$112,000**.)

**d. Carrying amount at change date and revised annual depreciation**

Carrying amount, January 1, Year 5 = $360,000 − $112,000 = **$248,000**  
Revised total useful life = 10 years; elapsed = 4 years → **remaining life = 6 years**  
Revised residual = **$8,000**  
Revised annual depreciation = ($248,000 − $8,000) ÷ 6 = **$40,000**

**Prospective application only** — **no** journal entry to restate prior Accumulated Depreciation or Retained Earnings; **no** pro forma restatement of prior financial statements (ASC 250-10-45-17).

**e. December 31, Year 5 — Period-end adjusting JE (year of change — emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 40,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 40,000 |
| *Prospective depreciation using revised life and residual* | | |

**Check:** Dr 40,000 = Cr 40,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Subsequent measurement schedule — Years 5–7 (revised estimates)**

| Year | Beg. carrying amount | Depreciation expense | End. accum. dep. | End. carrying amount |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | $248,000 | $40,000 | $152,000 | $208,000 |
| 6 | 208,000 | 40,000 | 192,000 | 168,000 |
| 7 | 168,000 | 40,000 | 232,000 | 128,000 |

Accumulated depreciation check, Dec 31 Year 7:  
$112,000 (pre-change) + 3 × $40,000 = $112,000 + $120,000 = **$232,000**  
Carrying amount: $360,000 − $232,000 = **$128,000** ✓

*December 31, Year 6 and Year 7 period-end AJEs (same form as Year 5):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 40,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 40,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 40,000 each year. **Balanced.**

**g. December 31, Year 7 — Disposal (settlement) JE**

Cash proceeds $95,000 − carrying amount $128,000 = **loss $33,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 95,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 232,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 33,000 | |
| Equipment | | 360,000 |
| *Remove cost and accum. dep.; record cash and loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr 95,000 + 232,000 + 33,000 = **360,000**; Cr **360,000**. **Balanced.**

**h. Disclosure (Year 5)**  
- **Type:** Change in **accounting estimate** (useful life and residual).  
- **Treatment:** **Prospective** only (ASC 250-10-45-17).  
- **Disclosure:** If material and the change affects several future periods, disclose the **effect on income from continuing operations, net income, and related per-share amounts** for the current period (ASC 250-10-50-4).  
- Year 5 depreciation under new estimates **$40,000** vs old rate **$28,000** → pretax income lower by **$12,000** relative to continuing the pre-change estimates.

**Key insight:** The exam control path is the **period-end adjusting JE trail**. At the change date, take **book value**, subtract the **new residual**, and allocate over **remaining** life only. Prior years’ AJEs stay as recorded; disposal uses the **post-revision** carrying amount after that year’s period-end AJE.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Useful life and residual revision with period-end AJEs and disposal

**Concept:** Number-variant twin: prospective life/residual revision; multi-year period-end adjusting JEs; subsequent measurement schedules; disposal with loss

On **January 1, Year 4**, after three full years, management revises estimates: total useful life from acquisition is now **12 years**, residual value **$15,000**. Method remains SL.

On **December 31, Year 6**, after recording Year 6 depreciation under the revised estimates, Northspire sells the machinery for **$275,000 cash**.

a. January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE.

d. Carrying amount January 1, Year 4 and revised annual depreciation.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Depreciation method change as estimate effected by principle; period-end AJEs; disposal
**LO:** LO 21-1  
**Concept:** Change from SL to DDB as change in estimate effected by change in principle; prospective DDB subsequent measurement schedule; multi-year period-end AJEs; disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Industrial Co.** (calendar year-end) purchased equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$440,000 cash**. Estimated useful life **10 years**, residual value **$40,000**, **straight-line** depreciation.

On **January 1, Year 3**, after two full years under SL, management concludes that the pattern of economic benefits is more front-loaded and changes the depreciation method from **straight-line to double-declining-balance (DDB)**. Useful life and residual remain 10 years and $40,000 from acquisition (8 years remaining). The change is inseparable from the revised estimate of the consumption pattern. (DDB is applied to carrying amount; residual is a floor only near the end of life.)

On **December 31, Year 5**, after recording Year 5 DDB depreciation, Cedarline sells the equipment for **$145,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Compute annual SL depreciation and the **Years 1–2 subsequent measurement schedule**. Prepare illustrative **December 31, Year 1–2 period-end AJEs**.  
c. Compute carrying amount on **January 1, Year 3** and the **DDB rate** based on remaining useful life. Compute **Year 3 DDB depreciation**.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** (emphasis).  
e. Prepare a **DDB subsequent measurement schedule for Years 3–5** (beginning CA, rate, depreciation expense, accum. dep., ending CA). State Year 4 and Year 5 period-end AJE amounts.  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 5 disposal JE**.  
g. How much higher is Year 3 depreciation under DDB than under continuing SL? Is that difference reported as a cumulative catch-up or restatement? What disclosure applies if material?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 440,000 | |
| Cash | | 440,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 440,000. **Balanced.**

**b. SL history — Years 1–2 and period-end AJEs**

Annual SL = ($440,000 − $40,000) ÷ 10 = **$40,000**

| Year | Beg. CA | Dep. expense | End. accum. dep. | End. CA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $440,000 | $40,000 | $40,000 | $400,000 |
| 2 | 400,000 | 40,000 | 80,000 | 360,000 |

*Each December 31 of Years 1–2:*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 40,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 40,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 40,000 each year. **Balanced.**

**c. Prospective switch to DDB at January 1, Year 3**

Carrying amount January 1, Year 3 = $440,000 − $80,000 = **$360,000**  
Remaining useful life = **8 years**  
Straight-line rate on remaining life = 1/8 = **0.125**  
DDB rate = 2 × 0.125 = **0.25**  
Year 3 DDB depreciation = $360,000 × 0.25 = **$90,000**

**Classification:** Change in accounting **estimate effected by a change in accounting principle** (ASC 250-10-45-18) — accounted for **prospectively** as a change in estimate. **No** retrospective restatement; **no** cumulative effect through retained earnings.

**d. December 31, Year 3 period-end JE (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 90,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 90,000 |
| *Year 3 DDB depreciation on carrying amount at method-change date* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 90,000. **Balanced.**

**e. DDB subsequent measurement schedule — Years 3–5**

| Year | Beg. CA | Rate | Dep. expense | End. accum. dep. | End. CA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | $360,000 | 0.25 | **$90,000** | $170,000 | $270,000 |
| 4 | 270,000 | 0.25 | **67,500** | 237,500 | 202,500 |
| 5 | 202,500 | 0.25 | **50,625** | 288,125 | 151,875 |

Accum. dep. check Year 5: pre-change $80,000 + $90,000 + $67,500 + $50,625 = **$288,125**; CA = $440,000 − $288,125 = **$151,875** ✓

*Year 4 period-end AJE:* Dr Depreciation Expense **67,500** / Cr Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment **67,500**. **Balanced.**  
*Year 5 period-end AJE:* Dr Depreciation Expense **50,625** / Cr Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment **50,625**. **Balanced.**

**f. December 31, Year 5 — Disposal JE**

Proceeds $145,000 − CA $151,875 = **loss $6,875**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 145,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 288,125 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 6,875 | |
| Equipment | | 440,000 |

**Check:** Dr 145,000 + 288,125 + 6,875 = **440,000**; Cr **440,000**. **Balanced.**

**g. Income comparison and disclosure**  
Year 3 DDB $90,000 − continuing SL $40,000 = **$50,000 higher** depreciation (lower pretax income by $50,000).  
This difference is **not** reported as a cumulative catch-up or prior-period restatement; it is simply the **current-year** effect of the new estimate/method applied prospectively via the **period-end AJE**.  
If material and the change affects several future periods, disclose the effect on **income from continuing operations, net income, and per-share amounts** for the current period (ASC 250-10-50-4).

**Key insight:** A switch in depreciation (amortization/depletion) method is **not** a retrospective principle change. Apply the **new method to the existing carrying amount** over remaining life. The multi-year **period-end AJEs** under DDB are the subsequent measurement engine after the change date.

### Q5 — MC (classification / method of application)
**LO:** LO 21-1  
**Concept:** Classification — pure change in estimate vs estimate effected by principle vs retrospective principle change; prospective application  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 4, a company changes the depreciation method for a machine from double-declining-balance to straight-line because management’s revised assessment of the pattern of future benefits is more even over the remaining life. Useful life and residual are unchanged. How should the company account for this change?

- A) As a voluntary change in accounting principle applied **retrospectively**, with a cumulative effect adjustment to beginning retained earnings  
- B) As a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**, applied **prospectively** to the carrying amount over remaining life  
- C) As a correction of an error, with restatement of all prior periods presented  
- D) As a change in estimate only if the difference in Year 4 expense is material; otherwise ignore the method change  

**Answer:** **B.** A change in depreciation (amortization/depletion) method is treated as a **change in estimate effected by a change in principle** and is applied **prospectively** (ASC 250-10-45-18). Prior financial statements are not restated, and there is no retrospective RE catch-up of the type used for most voluntary principle changes (e.g., inventory methods). A is the typical voluntary principle-change treatment but is **not** used for depreciation-method changes. C confuses method revision with error correction (unrealistic estimates or math mistakes). D invents a materiality gate that does not change the CORE prospective classification rule.

### Q6 — MC (period-end AJE implements prospective estimate — no catch-up)
**LO:** LO 21-1  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE implements pure change in estimate — no restatement or cumulative catch-up  
**Question:**  
Equipment cost $360,000; original life 12 years; residual $24,000; SL depreciation for four years ($28,000 per year). On January 1, Year 5, total useful life is revised to 10 years and residual to $8,000. Which statement correctly describes the **December 31, Year 5 period-end adjusting entry**?

- A) Debit Retained Earnings and credit Accumulated Depreciation for four years of the difference between old and new rates  
- B) Restate Years 1–4 comparative depreciation as if the 10-year life and $8,000 residual had always been used, then record Year 5 depreciation at the new rate  
- C) Debit Depreciation Expense and credit Accumulated Depreciation for **$40,000** = ($248,000 carrying amount − $8,000 residual) ÷ 6 remaining years; no prior-period adjustment  
- D) Continue depreciating at $28,000 in Year 5 and apply the new estimates only to assets acquired after January 1, Year 5  

**Answer:** **C.** Pure changes in useful life/residual are **changes in estimate** applied **prospectively** from the carrying amount at the start of the change period over remaining life (ASC 250-10-45-17). The **period-end AJE** is simply the revised depreciation amount for the current year. No RE catch-up (A), no restatement of prior years (B), and no “grandfathering” of the old rate for existing assets (D). Carrying amount at Jan 1, Year 5 = $360,000 − 4 × $28,000 = $248,000; ($248,000 − $8,000) ÷ 6 = **$40,000**.
