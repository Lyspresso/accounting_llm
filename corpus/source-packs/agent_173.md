# Agent 173 — CORE DEMO — LO 8-4

**Chapter:** 8  
**LO title:** Measuring and Recording Receivables at Net Amount Expected to Be Collected  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** record credit sales (Dr Accounts Receivable / Cr Sales) and establish or maintain the **allowance method** under the **CECL model** (Bad Debt Expense / Allowance for Doubtful Accounts)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** roll forward **Accounts Receivable** and **Allowance for Doubtful Accounts** (sales, collections, write-offs, recoveries); build an **aging schedule** applying expected credit loss rates by age category to compute the **desired ending allowance**
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** adjust the allowance **to** the desired credit balance (plug = Bad Debt Expense); report **Trade receivables, net of allowance** at the **net amount expected to be collected**
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** write off specific uncollectible accounts (Dr AFDA / Cr AR — **no effect** on net AR or income if within the allowance); recoveries (reinstate then collect)
- **Classification / presentation:** AFDA is a **valuation (contra) account**; CECL requires expected credit losses over the receivable’s contractual life, including remote risk; GAAP uses the **allowance method**, not pure direct write-off (except tax)
- **Number-variant twin:** same skill path with all balances, rates, and write-off/recovery amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Credit sales, AFDA rollforward, aging schedule, YE adjust, write-off & recovery
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of AR from credit sales; subsequent measurement via AR/AFDA schedules and aging of receivables under CECL; period-end adjusting JE to desired allowance; settlement write-off and recovery JEs; BS presentation at net amount expected to be collected  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Supply Group** is a calendar-year company that measures expected credit losses under the **CECL allowance method**. Selected balances and activity follow.

**January 1, Year 2 balances:**
- Accounts Receivable (gross): **$248,000**
- Allowance for Doubtful Accounts: **$11,200** credit

**During Year 2:**
1. Credit sales: **$1,850,000**
2. Cash collections on account (excluding recoveries below): **$1,720,000**
3. Write-offs of accounts deemed uncollectible: **$13,500**
4. Recoveries of accounts previously written off: **$2,400** (full amount of each recovered account is reinstated and then collected in cash)

**December 31, Year 2 aging of the ending receivable balance** (management concludes historical rates, adjusted for current conditions and reasonable and supportable forecasts, are appropriate expected credit loss rates):

| Age category | Receivable balance | Expected credit loss rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Not past due (current) | $219,000 | 1.0% |
| 1–30 days past due | 85,000 | 3.0% |
| 31–60 days past due | 42,000 | 12.0% |
| Over 60 days past due | 18,500 | 35.0% |
| **Total** | **$364,500** | |

**Subsequent events (Year 3):**
- **January 18, Year 3:** Meridian writes off the **$3,200** remaining balance of customer **Trent Co.** after exhausting collection efforts.
- **February 10, Year 3:** Trent unexpectedly remits **$1,250** on the account previously written off; no further collections from Trent are expected.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **initial recognition JE** for Year 2 credit sales (summary entry).  
b. Prepare **subsequent measurement schedules**: (1) Accounts Receivable rollforward for Year 2; (2) Allowance for Doubtful Accounts rollforward to the **unadjusted** December 31 balance (before YE adjusting entry); (3) **aging schedule** computing the amount estimated to be uncollectible (desired ending AFDA).  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting JE** to report receivables at the net amount expected to be collected. Show **balance sheet presentation** of trade receivables at December 31, Year 2.  
d. Prepare the **January 18, Year 3 write-off JE** and the **February 10, Year 3 recovery JEs**. Compute net accounts receivable **immediately before and after** the Trent write-off (using post–Year 2 adjusted balances).  
e. Briefly explain why the write-off does **not** reduce net income again and how CECL differs from waiting until write-off to recognize credit loss (direct write-off).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 2 — initial recognition of credit sales**

| Account | Debit | Credit |

| − Write-offs | (13,500) |

**Check:** Aging total $219,000 + $85,000 + $42,000 + $18,500 = **$364,500** matches ending AR.

**(2) Allowance for Doubtful Accounts rollforward — to unadjusted Dec 31**

|---:|---:|

| Age category | Balance | ECL rate | Amount estimated uncollectible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

**Desired ending AFDA credit balance = $16,255.**

**c. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE**

Adjustment required = Desired ending AFDA − Unadjusted AFDA  
= $16,255 − $100 = **$16,155**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 16,155 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 16,155 |
| *Adjust allowance to net amount expected to be collected (aging/CECL)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,155 = Cr 16,155. **Balanced.**

Post-adjustment AFDA = $100 + $16,155 = **$16,255 Cr**.

**Balance sheet presentation — December 31, Year 2**

Trade receivables, net of allowance of $16,255 — **$348,245**

(Computation: $364,500 − $16,255 = **$348,245**)

**d. Settlement / disposal JEs — write-off and recovery**

*January 18, Year 3 — write off Trent Co.*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | 3,200 | |
| Accounts Receivable—Trent Co. | | 3,200 |
| *Write off Trent Co. as uncollectible* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,200 = Cr 3,200. **Balanced.**

Net AR **before** Trent write-off (using Dec 31 post-adjust balances):  
Gross AR $364,500 − AFDA $16,255 = **$348,245**

Net AR **after** Trent write-off:  
Gross AR $364,500 − $3,200 = $361,300  
AFDA $16,255 − $3,200 = $13,055  
Net AR = $361,300 − $13,055 = **$348,245** (unchanged)

*February 10, Year 3 — partial recovery of Trent ($1,250)*

Reinstate:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable—Trent Co. | 1,250 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 1,250 |
| *Reinstate portion of Trent account recovered* | | |

Collect:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,250 | |
| Accounts Receivable—Trent Co. | | 1,250 |
| *Collect cash on reinstated Trent account* | | |

**Check:** Each entry Dr = Cr. **Balanced.**  
Gross AR is unchanged after the two recovery entries; AFDA increases by $1,250, so **net AR decreases by $1,250** as cash is collected (cash ↑, net AR ↓).

**e. Conceptual**

- The **Year 2 adjusting entry** already recognized expected credit losses in **Bad Debt Expense**. A later write-off merely **reclassifies** within net receivables (gross AR ↓ and AFDA ↓ by the same amount). Income and net AR are affected at estimation time, not again at write-off (unless write-offs exceed estimates and force a larger subsequent adjustment).  
- Under **CECL / GAAP allowance method**, management estimates expected credit losses over the receivable’s life (even if remote) and reports AR at **net amount expected to be collected**. The **direct write-off method** (expense only when a specific account is written off) generally **does not** comply with GAAP for financial reporting when credit-loss risk is more than a zero-loss expectation; it is commonly used for **tax** purposes.

**Key insight:** Pool receivables by similar risk (often **age**), multiply each pool by its **expected credit loss rate**, and **plug** Bad Debt Expense so AFDA ends at that target. Write-offs and recoveries move the allowance and gross AR but do not restate the CECL estimate until the next measurement date.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Aging CECL, debit unadjusted AFDA, full cycle
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: credit sales initial recognition; AR/AFDA subsequent measurement schedules; aging-based desired allowance; period-end adjust when unadjusted AFDA is a **debit**; write-off and recovery settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Cascade Appliance Distributors** (calendar year) uses the CECL allowance method with an aging analysis.

**January 1, Year 1 balances:**
- Accounts Receivable: **$312,000**
- Allowance for Doubtful Accounts: **$14,800** credit

**During Year 1:**
- Credit sales: **$2,400,000**
- Collections on account (excluding recoveries): **$2,265,000**
- Write-offs: **$18,200**
- Recoveries of previously written-off accounts: **$3,100** (reinstate and collect)

**December 31, Year 1 aging:**

| Age category | Receivable balance | Expected credit loss rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Not past due | $268,000 | 0.8% |
| 1–45 days past due | 98,000 | 2.5% |
| 46–90 days past due | 45,000 | 10.0% |
| Over 90 days past due | 17,800 | 40.0% |
| **Total** | **$428,800** | |

**Year 2 settlement events:**
- **March 5, Year 2:** Write off **Lumen Retail LLC** for **$4,600**.
- **April 12, Year 2:** Recover **$2,000** of the Lumen account previously written off (reinstate and collect).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **summary JE** for Year 1 credit sales.  
b. Prepare the **AR rollforward**, **AFDA rollforward to unadjusted YE**, and **aging schedule** for the desired ending allowance.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 adjusting JE** and report **net trade receivables**.  
d. Prepare the **March 5 write-off** and **April 12 recovery** JEs.  
e. State the unadjusted AFDA balance type (debit/credit) before adjustment and why the Bad Debt Expense amount is **larger** than the desired ending allowance.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Credit sales — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable | 2,400,000 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 2,400,000 |
| *Record Year 1 credit sales* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,400,000 = Cr 2,400,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement schedules**

**(1) AR rollforward**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning AR | $312,000 |
| + Credit sales | 2,400,000 |
| − Collections | (2,265,000) |
| − Write-offs | (18,200) |
| ± Recoveries (net) | 0 |
| **Ending AR** | **$428,800** |

**Check:** 268,000 + 98,000 + 45,000 + 17,800 = **$428,800**.

**(2) AFDA rollforward to unadjusted Dec 31**

| | Debit | Credit | Running bal. |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Beg. bal. | | 14,800 | 14,800 Cr |
| Write-offs | 18,200 | | 3,400 Dr |
| Recoveries | | 3,100 | **300 Dr** |
| **Unadjusted ending** | | | **$300 debit** |

**(3) Aging — desired ending AFDA**

| Age category | Balance | Rate | Est. uncollectible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Not past due | $268,000 | 0.8% | $2,144 |
| 1–45 days | 98,000 | 2.5% | 2,450 |
| 46–90 days | 45,000 | 10.0% | 4,500 |
| Over 90 days | 17,800 | 40.0% | 7,120 |
| **Total** | **$428,800** | | **$16,214** |

**c. December 31, Year 1 — adjusting JE**

Desired ending AFDA = **$16,214** credit  
Unadjusted AFDA = **$300** debit  
Adjustment = 16,214 + 300 = **$16,514**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 16,514 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts |  | 16,514 |
| *Adjust AFDA to desired credit balance per aging/CECL* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,514 = Cr 16,514. **Balanced.**

Net trade receivables = $428,800 − $16,214 = **$412,586**  
Presentation: *Trade receivables, net of allowance of $16,214 — $412,586*

**d. Settlement JEs**

*March 5, Year 2 — write off Lumen Retail LLC*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | 4,600 | |
| Accounts Receivable—Lumen Retail LLC | | 4,600 |
| *Write off Lumen Retail LLC* | | |

**Check:** Balanced. Net AR **unchanged** by the write-off alone.

*April 12, Year 2 — recover $2,000 of Lumen*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable—Lumen Retail LLC | 2,000 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 2,000 |
| *Reinstate recovered portion* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,000 | |
| Accounts Receivable—Lumen Retail LLC | | 2,000 |
| *Collect cash on recovered account* | | |

**Check:** Both entries balanced.

**e. Debit unadjusted AFDA**

Write-offs (**$18,200**) exceeded beginning AFDA plus recoveries (**$14,800 + $3,100 = $17,900**), leaving a **$300 debit** unadjusted balance. Bad Debt Expense must both (1) clear the debit and (2) build the **$16,214** credit target, so expense (**$16,514**) **exceeds** the desired ending allowance by the amount of the unadjusted debit.

**Key insight:** Always solve for the **plug to the desired ending AFDA**. When the allowance sits in a **debit** before adjustment, Bad Debt Expense = desired credit balance **+** unadjusted debit balance.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Single-rate CECL, multi-year establish & adjust, write-off neutrality
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE using a **single expected credit loss rate** on total receivables; first-year establishment of the allowance; multi-period AFDA schedule; demonstration that write-offs do not change net AR; recovery settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborview Medical Products Inc.** extends trade credit and applies CECL using a **single pool** rate when receivables share similar risk characteristics.

**Year 1 (first year of operations):**
- Ending gross accounts receivable (before allowance): **$95,000**
- Expected credit loss rate on total receivables (supported by history adjusted for forecasts): **2.5%**
- Beginning AFDA = **$0**

**Year 2 activity:**
- Beginning AR: **$95,000**; beginning AFDA: balance after Year 1 adjustment  
- Credit sales: **$620,000**
- Collections on account: **$575,000**
- Write-offs during Year 2: **$4,100**
- No recoveries during Year 2  
- At December 31, Year 2, management’s single expected credit loss rate is **3.0%** of ending gross AR (rate updated for current conditions and forecasts — change in estimate, prospective)

**Year 3 settlement:**
- On **January 20, Year 3**, Harborview writes off customer **Pike Dental** for **$1,800**.
- On **February 28, Year 3**, Pike remits **$600** on the written-off account (reinstate and collect).

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **Year 1 desired allowance** and prepare the **December 31, Year 1 establishing JE**. Show Year 1 net AR.  
b. Prepare the **Year 2 AR rollforward** and **AFDA rollforward to unadjusted YE**. Compute **ending gross AR** and **desired Year 2 AFDA** at 3.0%.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 adjusting JE**.  
d. Show **net AR before and after** the January 20, Year 3 Pike write-off (using post–Year 2 adjusted balances). Prepare the write-off JE and the February 28 recovery JEs.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** How is the allowance reported, and what amount is the **net amount expected to be collected** after the Year 2 adjustment?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Year 1 — establish allowance (single rate)**

Desired AFDA = $95,000 × 2.5% = **$2,375**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 2,375 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 2,375 |
| *Establish allowance — first year, single ECL rate 2.5%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,375 = Cr 2,375. **Balanced.**

Net AR, Dec 31, Year 1 = $95,000 − $2,375 = **$92,625**  
Presentation: *Trade receivables, net of allowance of $2,375 — $92,625*

**b. Year 2 — subsequent measurement schedules**

**AR rollforward**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning AR | $95,000 |
| + Credit sales | 620,000 |
| − Collections | (575,000) |
| − Write-offs | (4,100) |
| **Ending AR** | **$135,900** |

**AFDA rollforward to unadjusted Dec 31, Year 2**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning AFDA (credit) | $2,375 |
| − Write-offs | (4,100) |
| **Unadjusted ending AFDA** | **$1,725 debit** |

Desired AFDA, Dec 31, Year 2 = $135,900 × 3.0% = **$4,077** credit

**c. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE**

Adjustment = $4,077 + $1,725 = **$5,802**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bad Debt Expense | 5,802 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 5,802 |
| *Adjust AFDA to 3% of ending AR under CECL single-rate approach* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,802 = Cr 5,802. **Balanced.**  
Ending AFDA = **$4,077** credit.

**d. Write-off neutrality and recovery**

Net AR **before** Pike write-off:  
$135,900 − $4,077 = **$131,823**

*January 20, Year 3 — write off Pike Dental $1,800*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | 1,800 | |
| Accounts Receivable—Pike Dental | | 1,800 |
| *Write off Pike Dental* | | |

**Check:** Balanced.

After write-off:  
Gross AR = $135,900 − $1,800 = $134,100  
AFDA = $4,077 − $1,800 = $2,277  
Net AR = **$131,823** (same as before)

*February 28, Year 3 — recover $600 of Pike*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accounts Receivable—Pike Dental | 600 | |
| Allowance for Doubtful Accounts | | 600 |
| *Reinstate recovered portion* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 600 | |
| Accounts Receivable—Pike Dental | | 600 |
| *Collect cash on recovered account* | | |

**Check:** Both balanced. Net AR declines by **$600** after collection (AFDA up $600; cash replaces net AR).

**e. Classification / presentation**

- **Allowance for Doubtful Accounts** (allowance for credit losses) is a **contra (valuation) asset** deducted from gross accounts receivable.  
- After the Year 2 adjustment, the **net amount expected to be collected** is **$131,823** ($135,900 − $4,077).  
- Updates to the ECL rate (2.5% → 3.0%) are **changes in estimate**, accounted for **prospectively** through current-period Bad Debt Expense.

**Key insight:** Single-rate CECL still targets a **desired ending allowance**; activity during the year can drive AFDA into a **debit**, which increases the year-end Bad Debt Expense plug. Write-offs never “double count” the loss if the allowance was adequate.

### Q4 — MC — Net AR effect and CECL measurement focus
**LO:** LO 8-4  
**Concept:** Classification of write-off effects under the allowance method; measurement objective of CECL (net amount expected to be collected)  

**Question 1:**  
Under the allowance (CECL) method, when a company writes off a specific customer’s account against the Allowance for Doubtful Accounts, the **immediate** effect is:

- A) Net accounts receivable decreases and bad debt expense increases  
- B) Net accounts receivable is unchanged and total assets are unchanged  
- C) Gross accounts receivable is unchanged and the allowance increases  
- D) Bad debt expense increases by the write-off amount and net income decreases  

**Answer:** **B.** A write-off debits AFDA and credits AR by the same amount, so **gross AR and the allowance both fall equally**; **net AR and total assets are unchanged**. Bad debt expense was recognized when the allowance was adjusted, not at write-off.

**Question 2:**  
At period-end under CECL, management’s primary measurement objective for trade receivables is to report them at:

- A) Gross accounts receivable without reduction for credit risk  
- B) Historical cost of goods sold related to open invoices  
- C) The **net amount expected to be collected** (gross AR less the allowance for credit losses)  
- D) Only those invoices that are currently past due  

**Answer:** **C.** ASC 326 requires the allowance for credit losses so that financial assets measured at amortized cost are presented at the **net amount expected to be collected**. The estimate reflects expected losses over the contractual life, considering past events, current conditions, and reasonable and supportable forecasts—not merely currently past-due invoices.
