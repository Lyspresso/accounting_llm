### Q1 — CORE — AFS debt transferred to trading, then sold
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** AFS → trading transfer at fair value; reclassify AOCI to NI; new TS cost basis; subsequent sale  
**Scenario:**  
Cedar Peak Partners holds Harborline Corp. 5% bonds with amortized cost of **$48,000** (purchased at par; amortized cost equals face). The bonds have been classified as **available-for-sale (AFS)**.

- On **December 31, Year 4**, fair value of the Harborline bonds is **$43,200**. Cedar Peak already recorded the year-end fair-value adjustment through OCI (unrealized holding loss of **$4,800**). After that entry, **Fair Value Adjustment—AFS** has a **$4,800 credit** balance and **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI / AOCI** has a **$4,800 debit** balance related to Harborline.
- On **January 1, Year 5**, management reclassifies the Harborline bonds from **AFS to trading securities (TS)** because it now intends to sell them in the near term. Fair value on the transfer date remains **$43,200**.
- On **March 15, Year 5**, Cedar Peak sells the entire Harborline position for **$44,100** cash. Cedar Peak adjusts trading securities to fair value immediately before sale (sale-date fair value equals cash proceeds).

**Required:**  
a. State the December 31, Year 4 FVA entry already on the books (reference only; do not re-record).  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 5** journal entry to transfer the investment from AFS to TS.  
c. State (i) the **new cost basis** of the TS investment and (ii) the transfer-date effect on **net income** and **OCI**.  
d. Record the **March 15, Year 5** entries to adjust the TS investment to fair value and to record the sale.  
e. Compute the **total Year 5 pretax income effect** from Harborline (transfer + sale path).

**Answer key:**

a. **Dec 31, Year 4 (already recorded — reference):**  
Unrealized loss = $48,000 − $43,200 = **$4,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 4,800 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 4,800 |

*Check:* Dr 4,800 = Cr 4,800.

b. **January 1, Year 5 — Transfer AFS → TS at fair value**  
Derecognize AFS amortized cost and related FVA; recognize TS at fair value; reclassify the AOCI unrealized loss into **net income**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Harborline Bonds | 43,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 4,800 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 4,800 | |
| Investment in AFS—Harborline Bonds | | 48,000 |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 4,800 |

*Check:* Dr $43,200 + $4,800 + $4,800 = **$52,800**; Cr $48,000 + $4,800 = **$52,800**. Balances.

c. **New basis and transfer-date P&L/OCI**  
- (i) **New TS cost basis = fair value on transfer date = $43,200**.  
- (ii) **NI:** recognize **$4,800 unrealized loss** (reclassification from AOCI into earnings). **OCI:** **$4,800 credit** (clears the prior AOCI debit); **net AOCI effect on transfer = zero after reclass** (loss moves from AOCI to NI). **No effect on total comprehensive income** beyond the reclassification presentation, but **NI falls by $4,800**.

d. **March 15, Year 5 — Mark TS to FV, then sell**  
Fair value / cash = $44,100; carrying basis = $43,200 → pre-sale unrealized gain **$900**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 900 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 900 |

*Check:* Dr 900 = Cr 900.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 44,100 | |
| Investment in TS—Harborline Bonds | | 43,200 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 900 |

*Check:* Dr 44,100 = Cr $43,200 + $900 = **$44,100**. Balances. (No separate “gain on sale” once marked to market.)

e. **Total Year 5 pretax income effect**  
Transfer loss $(4,800) + pre-sale mark-up $900 = **$(3,900)** pretax loss.  
*Cross-check:* Cash received $44,100 − original amortized cost $48,000 = **$(3,900)**.

**Key insight:** Transfers into **trading** are measured at **fair value** on the transfer date; any unrealized holding gain or loss that had been in AOCI is **recognized in net income**, and fair value becomes the **new cost basis** of the TS investment.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — AFS → TS (unrealized gain case)
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** AFS → trading transfer at fair value (unrealized gain into NI); number-variant twin  
**Scenario:**  
Pinnacle Trail Capital holds Rockford Industries 4% bonds with amortized cost of **$65,000** (purchased at par), classified as **AFS**.

- On **December 31, Year 2**, fair value is **$71,500**. Pinnacle already recorded FVA through OCI: **Fair Value Adjustment—AFS** has a **$6,500 debit**; **AOCI** has a **$6,500 credit** (unrealized gain).
- On **January 1, Year 3**, Pinnacle transfers the Rockford bonds from **AFS to TS**. Fair value on the transfer date is still **$71,500**.
- On **February 20, Year 3**, Pinnacle sells the bonds for **$70,200** cash. Adjust TS to fair value immediately before sale.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 3** transfer entry (AFS → TS).  
b. State the new TS cost basis and the transfer-date NI / OCI effects.  
c. Record the **February 20, Year 3** mark-to-market and sale entries.  
d. Compute total Year 3 pretax income effect from Rockford.

**Answer key:**

a. **January 1, Year 3 — Transfer AFS → TS**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in TS—Rockford Bonds | 71,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 6,500 | |
| Investment in AFS—Rockford Bonds | | 65,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 6,500 |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 6,500 |

*Check:* Dr $71,500 + $6,500 = **$78,000**; Cr $65,000 + $6,500 + $6,500 = **$78,000**. Balances.

b. **Basis and transfer-date effects**  
- New TS cost basis = **$71,500**.  
- **NI:** **$6,500 unrealized gain** recognized.  
- **OCI:** **$6,500 debit** reclassifies the prior AOCI credit into earnings (AOCI related to Rockford returns to zero).

c. **February 20, Year 3**  
Pre-sale decline: $71,500 − $70,200 = **$1,300**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 1,300 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 1,300 |

*Check:* Dr 1,300 = Cr 1,300.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 70,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | 1,300 | |
| Investment in TS—Rockford Bonds | | 71,500 |

*Check:* Dr $70,200 + $1,300 = **$71,500**; Cr **$71,500**. Balances.

d. **Total Year 3 pretax income**  
Transfer gain $6,500 − subsequent unrealized loss $1,300 = **$5,200** pretax income.  
*Cross-check:* Cash $70,200 − original AC $65,000 = **$5,200**.

**Key insight:** Same transfer mechanics as Q1, but with an **unrealized gain**: AOCI is reclassified **into NI** as income; fair value still becomes the TS cost basis. Changing every number does not change the transfer model.

### Q3 — CORE — AFS → HTM transfer with AOCI yield-amortization schedule
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** AFS → HTM transfer (no NI on transfer); amortize transfer-date AOCI as yield adjustment; period-end adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
Summit Ridge Investments holds the following **AFS** debt securities at **December 31, Year 1**. Both were purchased at **par** (amortized cost = cost = face). Fair-value adjustments through OCI are already recorded.

| Security | Amortized cost | Fair value (Dec 31, Y1) | Unrealized gain (loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Northwood Co. bonds | $72,000 | $78,000 | $6,000 |
| Bayview Ltd. bonds | 54,000 | 49,500 | (4,500) |
| **Total** | **$126,000** | **$127,500** | **$1,500** |

On **January 1, Year 2**, Summit Ridge changes its ability and intent and **transfers both securities from AFS to held-to-maturity (HTM)**. Remaining term to maturity for **both** bonds is **5 years**. Summit Ridge **straight-line amortizes** the transfer-date unrealized gain or loss remaining in AOCI as a **yield adjustment** to interest income over the remaining life (and amortizes the related fair-value adjustment so carrying amount returns to amortized cost by maturity). No other HTM fair-value accounting applies after the transfer.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 2** transfer entries (reclassify investments AFS → HTM). State the transfer-date effect on NI and OCI.  
b. Prepare a **5-year amortization schedule** of the transfer-date AOCI amounts (by security and total), showing annual yield adjustment to interest revenue.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 2** period-end adjusting entries related to amortization of the transfer-date unrealized amounts and FVA (Year 1 of the schedule).  
d. After the Year 2 amortization entries, state ending AOCI related to these bonds and the net carrying amount of the HTM portfolio (AC ± remaining FVA).

**Answer key:**

a. **January 1, Year 2 — Reclassify investments (no NI)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Northwood Bonds | 72,000 | |
| Investment in AFS—Northwood Bonds | | 72,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Bayview Bonds | 54,000 | |
| Investment in AFS—Bayview Bonds | | 54,000 |

*Optional presentation note:* Existing **FVA** ($1,500 debit net) and **AOCI** ($1,500 credit net) **remain** at the transfer date; they are **not** reversed through NI.  
**Transfer-date impact:** **No effect on NI.** Unrealized gain/loss stays in **AOCI** and will be amortized as a yield adjustment. After transfer, account for the securities under **HTM** rules (amortized cost; no new FV-OCI marks for ordinary price changes).

*Check:* Each reclass entry Dr = Cr.

b. **AOCI / yield amortization schedule (straight-line, 5 years)**

| Security | Transfer-date AOCI G/(L) | Annual amort. | Direction of interest effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Northwood | $6,000 gain | $6,000 ÷ 5 = **$1,200** | Reduces interest revenue (with offsetting AOCI entry) |
| Bayview | $(4,500) loss | $4,500 ÷ 5 = **$900** | Increases interest revenue (with offsetting AOCI entry) |
| **Net** | **$1,500 gain** | **$300** | Net reduce interest $300; net clear AOCI $300 |

**Rollforward schedule (net AOCI credit balance):**

| Year ended | Beg. AOCI (cr) | Amort. this year | End. AOCI (cr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dec 31, Y2 | 1,500 | 300 | 1,200 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 1,200 | 300 | 900 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 900 | 300 | 600 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | 600 | 300 | 300 |
| Dec 31, Y6 | 300 | 300 | 0 |

**By security (annual):** Northwood $1,200; Bayview $900; net $300 each year for 5 years.

c. **December 31, Year 2 — Yield amortization (paired FVA + AOCI so lifetime NI nets correctly for par bonds held to maturity)**

*Northwood (unrealized gain $1,200):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Revenue | 1,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—HTM (Northwood) | | 1,200 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,200 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 1,200 |

*Bayview (unrealized loss $900):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—HTM (Bayview) | 900 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 900 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Revenue | 900 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 900 |

*Checks:*  
- Northwood pair: each entry Dr 1,200 = Cr 1,200; **net NI effect $0**; AOCI reduced $1,200; FVA reduced $1,200.  
- Bayview pair: each entry Dr 900 = Cr 900; **net NI effect $0**; AOCI increased (loss cleared) $900; FVA increased (credit FVA reduced / debit restored) $900.  
- **Net AOCI amortization for Y2 = $1,200 − $900 = $300** (matches schedule).  
- **Net NI from yield pairs = $0** (par bonds held to maturity; coupons drive cash interest; AOCI/FVA unwind offset).

*Compact net form (acceptable if instructor wants one compound for the portfolio):*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 300 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—HTM (net) | | 300 |

*Check:* Dr 300 = Cr 300. (Same net AOCI and FVA effect as the detailed pairs.)

d. **After Year 2 entries**  
- Ending AOCI related to these bonds = **$1,200 credit** (net).  
- Amortized cost still **$126,000**.  
- Remaining net FVA debit = $1,500 − $300 = **$1,200**.  
- Net carrying amount = $126,000 + $1,200 = **$127,200**.

**Key insight:** AFS → HTM has **no NI effect on the transfer date**. The transfer-date unrealized G/L stays in **AOCI** and is **amortized as a yield adjustment** over the remaining life—unlike a transfer **into trading**, which dumps AOCI into current NI immediately.

### Q4 — CORE — HTM → AFS transfer and subsequent AFS period-end adjustment
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** HTM → AFS transfer at fair value through OCI (no NI); subsequent AFS FVA period-end JE  
**Scenario:**  
Lakeshore Mutual Fund holds the following **HTM** portfolio at **December 31, Year 3**. All bonds were purchased at **par**.

| Security | Amortized cost | Fair value (Dec 31, Y3) | Unrealized gain (loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Quill Corp. bonds | $90,000 | $96,300 | $6,300 |
| Dune Partners bonds | 60,000 | 55,800 | (4,200) |
| **Total** | **$150,000** | **$152,100** | **$2,100** |

On **January 1, Year 4**, because of a significant deterioration in the issuers’ credit standings, Lakeshore changes intent and **transfers both securities from HTM to AFS**. Fair values on the transfer date equal the December 31, Year 3 amounts above.

At **December 31, Year 4**, fair values are: Quill **$94,000**; Dune **$57,000**. Lakeshore adjusts AFS securities to fair value at year-end through OCI.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 4** transfer entries (reclassify HTM → AFS and recognize transfer-date FV through OCI).  
b. State the transfer-date effect on NI and OCI.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 4** AFS fair-value adjusting entry.  
d. Briefly state what footnote disclosure should accompany a transfer of this type.

**Answer key:**

a. **January 1, Year 4 — Transfer HTM → AFS**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS—Quill Bonds | 90,000 | |
| Investment in HTM—Quill Bonds | | 90,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS—Dune Bonds | 60,000 | |
| Investment in HTM—Dune Bonds | | 60,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 2,100 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 2,100 |

*Check:* Investment reclasses balance; FVA entry Dr 2,100 = Cr 2,100.  
Combined asset after transfer: AC $150,000 + FVA $2,100 = **$152,100** (= total fair value).

b. **Transfer-date effects**  
- **NI: none.**  
- **OCI: $2,100 unrealized holding gain** (net) recognized to mark carrying amount to fair value.  
- After transfer, account for the securities under **AFS** rules (FV-OCI).

c. **December 31, Year 4 — AFS year-end FVA**  
Year-end FV total = $94,000 + $57,000 = **$151,000**.  
Required FVA ending balance = $151,000 − $150,000 AC = **$1,000 debit**.  
Existing FVA (from transfer) = **$2,100 debit**.  
Decrease needed = $2,100 − $1,000 = **$1,100**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,100 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 1,100 |

*Check:* Dr 1,100 = Cr 1,100.  
Ending AOCI (net credit related to these bonds, ignoring tax) after Y4 mark = $2,100 − $1,100 = **$1,000**.

d. **Disclosure**  
Notes should **describe the circumstances** leading to the transfer (here: significant deterioration in issuer creditworthiness / change in intent and ability that supports leaving HTM). Transfers from HTM should be **rare** except for ASC-identified circumstances.

**Key insight:** HTM → AFS adjusts carrying amount to **fair value through OCI** on the transfer date (**no NI**). Subsequent measurement follows **AFS** (FV-OCI period-end adjustments).

### Q5 — CORE — Equity securities: FV-NI → equity method
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Transfer from FV-NI equity measurement to equity method; fair value becomes new cost basis  
**Scenario:**  
Evergreen Partners owns **2,400** of the **10,000** outstanding common shares of Fable Corp. (24%). The shares were purchased years ago for **$18 per share** (**$43,200** total) and have been measured at **FV-NI** (no significant influence). On **January 1, Year 6**, after Evergreen placed two directors on Fable’s board, Evergreen concludes it now has **significant influence** and must switch to the **equity method**.

- Fair value of Fable stock on **December 31, Year 5** (and on January 1, Year 6) is **$35 per share**.  
- The investment was already adjusted to fair value at December 31, Year 5 under FV-NI.  
- If Evergreen had applied the equity method from acquisition, the investment account on January 1, Year 6 would have been **$68,000**. (Do **not** use retrospective equity-method balances; fair value at transfer is the new basis.)

**Required:**  
a. Compute the fair value / carrying amount of the investment immediately before the transfer.  
b. Record the **January 1, Year 6** transfer from FV-NI to the equity method.  
c. State the **new cost basis** under the equity method and the transfer-date NI effect (if any).  
d. On March 31, Year 6, Fable reports first-quarter net income of **$40,000** and pays a cash dividend of **$10,000**. Record Evergreen’s equity-method entries for its 24% share.

**Answer key:**

a. **Pre-transfer carrying amount**  
FV = 2,400 × $35 = **$84,000**. (FV-NI carrying amount after Dec 31 mark = **$84,000**.)

b. **January 1, Year 6 — Transfer FV-NI → equity method**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Fable—Equity Method | 84,000 | |
| Investment in Fable—FV-NI | | 84,000 |

*Check:* Dr 84,000 = Cr 84,000.  
(Any separate Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities balance related to Fable is closed with the FV-NI investment account so that the net $84,000 moves into the equity-method investment. If FVA is tracked separately with Investment still at cost $43,200 and FVA debit $40,800: Dr Investment—Equity Method 84,000; Cr Investment—FV-NI 43,200; Cr FVA—Equity Securities 40,800.)

c. **New basis / NI**  
- **New equity-method cost basis = fair value at transfer = $84,000** (not the hypothetical retrospective $68,000).  
- Unrealized G/L existing as of the transfer date has **already been recognized in NI** under FV-NI; the transfer entry itself is a **reclassification of the asset** at the already-marked amount.  
- **No additional transfer-date NI** if fair value was updated at the prior reporting date (as assumed). Going forward, adjust the **$84,000** basis under equity-method rules (share of earnings, dividends, etc.). **No retrospective restatement.**

d. **March 31, Year 6 — Equity method**  
Share of income = 24% × $40,000 = **$9,600**  
Share of dividends = 24% × $10,000 = **$2,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Fable—Equity Method | 9,600 | |
| Equity in Earnings of Fable | | 9,600 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,400 | |
| Investment in Fable—Equity Method | | 2,400 |

*Checks:* Each entry balances. Ending investment = $84,000 + $9,600 − $2,400 = **$91,200**.

**Key insight:** On a change **to** the equity method, **fair value at the transfer date becomes the new cost basis**; subsequent accounting follows equity-method rules with **no retrospective** rewrite of prior FV-NI periods.

### Q6 — MC (classification / method fit)
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Classification of transfer-date NI vs OCI effects by transfer type  
**Question:** Which transfer of a debt investment correctly describes the **transfer-date** effect on net income under U.S. GAAP (ASC 320)?

- A) HTM → AFS: recognize unrealized holding gain or loss in **net income**; new category measured at FV-NI thereafter.  
- B) AFS → HTM: recognize unrealized holding gain or loss in **net income**; AOCI is cleared immediately.  
- C) AFS → trading: recognize any unrealized holding gain or loss existing at the transfer date in **net income**; fair value becomes the new TS cost basis.  
- D) Trading → AFS: reverse all prior unrealized gains/losses out of net income into AOCI at the transfer date.

**Answer:** **C.**  
Transfers **into trading** recognize any unrecognized holding G/L in **NI** at the transfer date; FV becomes the TS basis.  
- **A is wrong:** HTM → AFS adjusts to FV through **OCI**, not NI.  
- **B is wrong:** AFS → HTM has **no NI** effect; AOCI remains and is **amortized** as a yield adjustment.  
- **D is wrong:** Transfers **from** trading do not reverse prior NI marks; NI has already reflected FV, and the security is then accounted for under the **new** category’s rules.

### Q7 — MC (presentation / rare-event classification)
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** When transfers among debt categories are appropriate; presentation/disclosure  
**Question:** Falcon Ridge Capital holds a bond currently classified as **held-to-maturity**. Management wants to move it to **available-for-sale** solely because the bond will mature in nine months and the treasurer “might sell if rates move.” Which statement is most consistent with GAAP for transfers of debt investments?

- A) Automatic reclassification to AFS is required whenever remaining maturity falls below one year.  
- B) The transfer is routine and should be recorded at amortized cost with no fair-value entry.  
- C) Transfers from HTM should be **rare**; AFS is not automatically used merely because maturity is within one year or because management intends to sell within one year. If a justified transfer occurs, adjust carrying amount to **fair value through OCI** (no NI) and disclose the circumstances.  
- D) Any HTM → AFS transfer must recognize the entire unrealized G/L in **current earnings**.

**Answer:** **C.**  
ASC guidance: transfers from HTM should be **rare** (except specified changes in circumstances); AFS is **not** automatic when maturity is within one year or sale is merely contemplated. A justified HTM → AFS transfer is at **FV through OCI**, with **disclosure** of circumstances—not an automatic NI hit.
