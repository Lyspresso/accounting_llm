# Agent 161 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-5

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Explain how to record a valuation allowance and report deferred taxes  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Valuation allowance** is a **contra asset** to the deferred tax asset when it is **more likely than not (>50%)** that some or all of the DTA will not be realized
- **Sources of taxable income** for realizability: future reversals of taxable temporary differences; future taxable income exclusive of reversing TDs; taxable income in carryback years; tax-planning strategies
- **Initial recognition JE:** Dr Income Tax Expense / Cr Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset (establishes or increases the allowance)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** multi-year rollforward of gross DTA, target VA, VA adjustment, ending VA, and net DTA
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** remeasure VA each reporting date so net DTA equals the benefit more likely than not to be realized
- **Release / settlement:** debit VA (reduce tax expense) when realizability improves or when the related deductible temporary difference fully reverses
- **Classification / presentation:** within one tax-paying component and jurisdiction, offset DTA, VA, and DTL into a single **noncurrent** net deferred tax asset or liability; compare (DTA − VA) to DTL
- **Number-variant twin:** same measurement logic with all company names, rates, and amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Northline Outdoor Gear: multi-year valuation allowance subsequent measurement schedule (full life cycle)
**LO:** LO 18-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of valuation allowance; multi-year subsequent measurement schedule (gross DTA, target VA, adjustments, net DTA); period-end current + deferred + VA JEs; full release on DTA settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Northline Outdoor Gear Co.** (calendar-year) accrues product warranties for GAAP before the costs are deductible for tax. The enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. Accounting and tax years both end December 31. Beginning balances in all deferred tax accounts at January 1, Year 1 are **zero**. Northline has **no** deferred tax liabilities and **no** permanent differences.

| Item | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|------|-------:|-------:|-------:|
| Taxable income | $520,000 | $480,000 | $550,000 |
| Cumulative deductible temporary difference (warranty) at **year-end** | $96,000 | $64,000 | $0 |
| Management estimate: portion of **ending gross DTA** more likely than not **not** realizable | **30%** of ending gross DTA | **$4,000** fixed (of ending gross DTA) | **0%** (DTA fully reversed / fully realizable) |

Additional facts:
- Year 1 originates the full $96,000 warranty temporary difference.
- Year 2 and Year 3 changes in the cumulative temporary difference arise from warranty claims paid (tax-deductible) that reverse prior accruals.
- Record **current tax**, **gross deferred tax asset** change, and **valuation allowance** adjustment at each year-end.

**Required:**  
a. Compute the **gross deferred tax asset** for Years 1–3. Prepare a **three-year subsequent measurement schedule** with columns: year; beginning gross DTA; change in gross DTA; ending gross DTA; beginning VA; **target** ending VA; VA adjustment (Dr or Cr); ending VA; ending **net** DTA.  
b. Record the **December 31, Year 1** combined income tax journal entry (current tax, initial DTA recognition, and initial valuation allowance).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 2** period-end adjusting income tax entry (current, DTA change, VA remeasurement).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 3** entry when the temporary difference fully reverses and the valuation allowance is released.  
e. State the **net noncurrent deferred tax asset** on the balance sheet at December 31 of Year 1 and Year 2.  
f. Prepare a three-year **T-account rollforward** of Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset (beginning, adjustments, ending).

**Answer key:**

**a. Gross DTA and subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis)**

Gross DTA = cumulative deductible temporary difference × 25%.

| Year | Beg. gross DTA | Change in DTA | End. gross DTA | Beg. VA | Target end VA | VA adjustment | End. VA | Net DTA (end) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | +$24,000 | **$24,000** | $0 | 30% × 24,000 = **$7,200** | Cr **$7,200** | $7,200 | **$16,800** |
| 2 | 24,000 | −$8,000 | **$16,000** | 7,200 | **$4,000** | Dr **$3,200** | 4,000 | **$12,000** |
| 3 | 16,000 | −$16,000 | **$0** | 4,000 | **$0** | Dr **$4,000** | 0 | **$0** |

Checks:  
- Year 1 DTA: $96,000 × 25% = **$24,000**.  
- Year 2 DTA: $64,000 × 25% = **$16,000**; decrease = $24,000 − $16,000 = **$8,000**.  
- Year 3 DTA: $0; decrease = **$16,000**.  
- Net DTA Year 1: $24,000 − $7,200 = **$16,800**.  
- Net DTA Year 2: $16,000 − $4,000 = **$12,000**.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition (current + DTA + VA)**

Current tax payable = $520,000 × 25% = **$130,000**.  
Gross DTA increase (deferred tax benefit) = **$24,000**.  
Valuation allowance increase = **$7,200** (increases income tax expense).  
Total income tax expense = $130,000 − $24,000 + $7,200 = **$113,200**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 113,200 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 24,000 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 7,200 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 130,000 |
| *Year 1 current tax; recognize warranty DTA; establish valuation allowance* | | |

**Check:** Dr 113,200 + 24,000 = **137,200**; Cr 7,200 + 130,000 = **137,200**. **Balanced.**

Alternatively as two entries (also acceptable):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 106,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 24,000 | |
| Income Tax Payable | | 130,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 7,200 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 7,200 |

**c. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjustment (subsequent remeasurement)**

Current tax payable = $480,000 × 25% = **$120,000**.  
Gross DTA decrease = **$8,000** → deferred tax expense.  
VA decrease (to target $4,000) = **$3,200** → reduces tax expense.  
Total income tax expense = $120,000 + $8,000 − $3,200 = **$124,800**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 124,800 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 3,200 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 8,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 120,000 |
| *Year 2 current tax; reverse portion of DTA; reduce VA from $7,200 to $4,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 124,800 + 3,200 = **128,000**; Cr 8,000 + 120,000 = **128,000**. **Balanced.**  
Ending VA = 7,200 − 3,200 = **$4,000**. Ending net DTA = **$12,000**.

**d. December 31, Year 3 — full settlement / release of DTA and VA**

Current tax payable = $550,000 × 25% = **$137,500**.  
Gross DTA decrease = **$16,000**.  
VA released in full = **$4,000**.  
Total income tax expense = $137,500 + $16,000 − $4,000 = **$149,500**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 149,500 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 4,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 16,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 137,500 |
| *Year 3 current tax; eliminate remaining DTA on full warranty reversal; release VA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 149,500 + 4,000 = **153,500**; Cr 16,000 + 137,500 = **153,500**. **Balanced.**  
Ending gross DTA = **$0**; ending VA = **$0**.

**e. Balance sheet presentation (net noncurrent)**  
- December 31, Year 1: **Noncurrent deferred tax asset, net = $16,800** ($24,000 − $7,200).  
- December 31, Year 2: **Noncurrent deferred tax asset, net = $12,000** ($16,000 − $4,000).  
- December 31, Year 3: **$0** (no deferred taxes remain).

**f. Valuation Allowance T-account rollforward**

| Date | Debit | Credit | Balance (credit) |
|------|------:|-------:|-----------------:|
| Jan 1, Year 1 | — | — | $0 |
| Dec 31, Year 1 establish | — | 7,200 | 7,200 |
| Dec 31, Year 2 adjust | 3,200 | — | 4,000 |
| Dec 31, Year 3 release | 4,000 | — | **$0** |

**Key insight:** The **subsequent measurement schedule** drives every period-end VA entry: set **target ending VA** so net DTA equals the amount more likely than not to be realized, then post the **change** from beginning VA against income tax expense. Establishing or increasing the allowance increases tax expense; decreasing or releasing it decreases tax expense. When the related deductible temporary difference fully reverses, clear both the gross DTA and any remaining allowance.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Ridgeview Medical Devices: DTA and VA remeasurement twin
**LO:** LO 18-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition of DTA and valuation allowance; subsequent measurement schedule; period-end VA remeasurement at a different enacted rate  
**Scenario:**  
**Ridgeview Medical Devices Inc.** has a deductible temporary difference from an accrued litigation contingency (recognized for GAAP; not yet deductible for tax). Enacted tax rate is **21%**. Years end December 31. Deferred tax accounts begin at **zero** on January 1, Year 1. No deferred tax liabilities. No permanent differences.

| Item | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Year 1 taxable income | $800,000 |
| Year 1 ending cumulative deductible temporary difference (litigation) | $150,000 |
| Year 1: portion of ending gross DTA more likely than not **not** realized | **40%** of ending gross DTA |
| Year 2 taxable income | $720,000 |
| Year 2 ending cumulative deductible temporary difference | $90,000 |
| Year 2: portion of ending gross DTA more likely than not **not** realized | **20%** of ending gross DTA |

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **two-year subsequent measurement schedule**: ending gross DTA, target VA, net DTA, change in gross DTA, and VA adjustment for each year.  
b. Record the **December 31, Year 1** combined income tax entry (current, DTA, VA).  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 2** combined income tax entry (current, DTA change, VA adjustment).  
d. Prepare a two-year rollforward of the Valuation Allowance account (beg, adjustments, end).  
e. State Year 1 and Year 2 **net noncurrent deferred tax asset** balance sheet amounts.

**Answer key:**

**a. Subsequent measurement schedule**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gross DTA (TD × 21%) | $150,000 × 21% = **$31,500** | $90,000 × 21% = **$18,900** |
| Target VA | 40% × 31,500 = **$12,600** | 20% × 18,900 = **$3,780** |
| Net DTA | 31,500 − 12,600 = **$18,900** | 18,900 − 3,780 = **$15,120** |
| Change in gross DTA | +31,500 (from 0) | 18,900 − 31,500 = **−$12,600** |
| VA adjustment needed | Cr **$12,600** (from 0) | Target 3,780 − beg 12,600 → Dr **$8,820** |

**b. December 31, Year 1**

Current payable = $800,000 × 21% = **$168,000**.  
Tax expense = $168,000 − $31,500 + $12,600 = **$149,100**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 149,100 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 31,500 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 12,600 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 168,000 |
| *Year 1 current tax; recognize litigation DTA; establish 40% valuation allowance* | | |

**Check:** Dr 149,100 + 31,500 = **180,600**; Cr 12,600 + 168,000 = **180,600**. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31, Year 2**

Current payable = $720,000 × 21% = **$151,200**.  
DTA decrease = **$12,600** (deferred expense).  
VA decrease = **$8,820** (deferred benefit).  
Tax expense = $151,200 + $12,600 − $8,820 = **$154,980**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 154,980 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 8,820 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 12,600 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 151,200 |
| *Year 2 current tax; reverse portion of DTA; reduce VA to 20% target* | | |

**Check:** Dr 154,980 + 8,820 = **163,800**; Cr 12,600 + 151,200 = **163,800**. **Balanced.**

**d. Valuation Allowance rollforward**

| Date | Debit | Credit | Balance (Cr) |
|------|------:|-------:|-------------:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | — | — | 0 |
| Dec 31, Y1 establish | — | 12,600 | 12,600 |
| Dec 31, Y2 adjust | 8,820 | — | **3,780** |

**e. Net noncurrent DTA on BS**  
- December 31, Year 1: **$18,900**.  
- December 31, Year 2: **$15,120**.

**Key insight:** Same LO 18-5 measurement logic as Q1 with a **21%** rate and percentage-based targets: always set VA so ending net DTA = gross DTA × (1 − unrealizable %), then plug the **change** in VA against income tax expense.

### Q3 — CORE — Lakeshore Packaging: classification, net presentation, and VA-only period-end adjust
**LO:** LO 18-5  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — offset DTA, valuation allowance, and DTL into one noncurrent net amount; period-end VA-only adjusting JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Packaging Group** has a **single** tax-paying component in **one** U.S. jurisdiction. At December 31, Year 4, temporary differences and related tax effects (enacted rate **25%**) are:

| Temporary difference | Future (taxable)/deductible amount | Deferred tax effect |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------:|--------------------:|
| Excess tax depreciation (MACRS > SL) | Taxable $240,000 | DTL **$60,000** |
| Installment gross profit (accrual vs cash) | Taxable $80,000 | DTL **$20,000** |
| Warranty accrual | Deductible $120,000 | DTA **$30,000** |
| Unearned (deferred) revenue | Deductible $48,000 | DTA **$12,000** |

Management concludes it is more likely than not that **$15,000** of the **gross deferred tax assets** will not be realized (valuation allowance required = $15,000).

**Required:**  
a. Compute total **gross DTA**, total **DTL**, valuation allowance, and the **single noncurrent amount** reported on the classified balance sheet. State whether the line is a net deferred tax **asset** or **liability**.  
b. Assume instead the valuation allowance is **$36,000** (more negative evidence). Recompute the net amount and classification.  
c. **Period-end adjusting JE only for the valuation allowance:** On January 1, Year 4 the valuation allowance was **$10,000**. At December 31, Year 4 the required allowance is **$15,000** (part a). Gross DTA and DTL accounts have already been adjusted to the balances above through other tax entries. Record the **valuation allowance adjusting entry only**.  
d. Briefly state the ASC 740 **offset rule** (same component / same jurisdiction; noncurrent presentation).  
e. **Disclosure sketch:** Using part a amounts, show a short note-style schedule: gross DTA, less valuation allowance, gross DTL, and net deferred tax liability.

**Answer key:**

**a. Base case (VA = $15,000)**

Gross DTA = $30,000 + $12,000 = **$42,000**.  
Gross DTL = $60,000 + $20,000 = **$80,000**.  
DTA net of VA = $42,000 − $15,000 = **$27,000**.  
Compare: DTL $80,000 **>** net DTA $27,000 →  
**Noncurrent deferred tax liability, net = $80,000 − $27,000 = $53,000**.

**b. Alternative (VA = $36,000)**

Net DTA = $42,000 − $36,000 = **$6,000**.  
**Noncurrent deferred tax liability, net = $80,000 − $6,000 = $74,000**.  
(Still a net liability; a larger allowance increases the net DTL reported.)

**c. VA adjusting entry only (increase $5,000)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 5,000 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 5,000 |
| *Increase valuation allowance from $10,000 to $15,000 target* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **5,000**. **Balanced.**

**d. Offset / presentation rule**  
For a **particular tax-paying component** within a **particular tax jurisdiction**, all deferred tax assets, deferred tax liabilities, and any related valuation allowance are **offset and presented as a single noncurrent amount**. Do **not** offset amounts attributable to different components or different jurisdictions.

**e. Note-style schedule (part a)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Gross deferred tax assets | $42,000 |
| Less: Valuation allowance | (15,000) |
| Deferred tax assets, net of valuation allowance | 27,000 |
| Deferred tax liabilities | (80,000) |
| **Net noncurrent deferred tax liability** | **$(53,000)** |

**Key insight:** Presentation is always **net** after the valuation allowance. Classification depends on whether (DTA − VA) exceeds DTL (net asset) or DTL is larger (net liability). Under current U.S. GAAP classified BS rules, do not report gross DTA and gross DTL separately for the same component/jurisdiction.

### Q4 — MC — Net deferred tax classification and VA adjustment
**LO:** LO 18-5  
**Concept:** Classification of net deferred taxes after valuation allowance; correct period-end VA adjusting entry  

**Question 1:**  
**Bayside Retail Partners** has deferred tax assets of $72,000, a valuation allowance of $18,000, and deferred tax liabilities of $30,000 (same tax-paying component and jurisdiction). How should deferred taxes appear on a classified balance sheet?

- A) Noncurrent deferred tax asset $72,000 and noncurrent deferred tax liability $30,000  
- B) Noncurrent deferred tax asset, net $42,000  
- C) Noncurrent deferred tax asset, net $24,000  
- D) Noncurrent deferred tax liability, net $30,000  

**Answer:** C.  
Net DTA = $72,000 − $18,000 = $54,000. DTL = $30,000. Because net DTA > DTL, report a single **noncurrent deferred tax asset, net = $54,000 − $30,000 = $24,000**. Gross presentation (A) is incorrect; ignoring the liability (B) or reporting a net liability (D) is incorrect.

**Question 2:**  
**Westbrook Components Inc.** has a December 31 deferred tax asset balance of $48,000. Management concludes it is more likely than not that **50%** of the deferred tax asset will **not** be realized. The Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset has an unadjusted credit balance of $8,000. The period-end entry to adjust the allowance to the desired ending balance includes:

- A) a debit to Income Tax Expense for $16,000  
- B) a debit to Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset for $16,000  
- C) a credit to Deferred Tax Asset for $24,000  
- D) a debit to Income Tax Expense for $24,000  

**Answer:** A.  
Target VA = 50% × $48,000 = **$24,000**. Existing VA = $8,000 credit. Increase needed = **$16,000**. Entry: **Dr Income Tax Expense $16,000 / Cr Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset $16,000**. Choice D would overstate the adjustment by ignoring the existing $8,000 balance; B would decrease (not increase) the allowance; C incorrectly reduces the gross DTA.
