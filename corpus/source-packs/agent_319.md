# Agent 319 — CORE DEMO — LO 10-1

**Chapter:** 10  
**LO title:** Apply lower of cost or net realizable value rule to inventory  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- LCNRV applies to inventory cost methods **other than LIFO and the retail inventory method** (e.g., FIFO, average cost)
- **NRV** = estimated selling price − reasonably predictable costs of completion, disposal, and transportation
- Three application levels: **individual item** (most common; lowest inventory), **classification/category**, and **inventory in total**
- Period-end write-down when NRV < cost: reduce inventory (directly or via **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to NRV**) and charge **COGS** or **Holding Loss on Inventory**
- **Emphasis — period-end adjusting JE:** allowance is typically remeasured only at each reporting date to the required credit balance (not at each sale)
- No subsequent write-up if NRV recovers on the **same** goods; reduced amount becomes “cost” for later periods
- When goods turn, year-end remeasurement sets the allowance for **current** ending inventory (may increase or decrease the allowance balance)
- On **sale/disposal** of written-down inventory: close remaining allowance into COGS so net COGS equals net carrying amount

### Q1 — CORE — LCNRV schedule, initial write-down, multi-year period-end allowance adjusts, sale close-out
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Period-end LCNRV adjusting entries (multi-year allowance remeasurement); item/category/total schedule; initial recognition; disposal close of allowance  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinnacle Outdoor Apparel Co.** measures merchandise inventory using **FIFO** (not LIFO or the retail inventory method). It applies the **lower of cost or net realizable value (LCNRV)** rule at the **individual-item** level and maintains an **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value**. The allowance is remeasured only at each **year-end** reporting date (not at each sale). Ordinary write-downs are charged to **Cost of Goods Sold**.

**January 8, Year 1 — purchases remaining at year-end:** Pinnacle purchases inventory on account that will remain in ending inventory. That ending layer costs **$87,500** in total (detail below). Other Year 1 purchases are sold before year-end and are already in pre-adjustment COGS.

At **December 31, Year 1**, cost and NRV of ending inventory:

| Item | Category | Cost | NRV |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Trail Shell | Outerwear | $24,000 | $21,500 |
| Summit Vest | Outerwear | 16,000 | 19,000 |
| Base Layer | Baselayers | 19,000 | 20,200 |
| Thermal Set | Baselayers | 28,500 | 25,000 |
| **Total** | | **$87,500** | **$85,700** |

Additional facts:
- Cost of goods sold **before** any LCNRV adjustment for Year 1: **$198,000**.
- All **January 1, Year 2** inventory (Year 1 ending inventory) is sold during Year 2. The Year 1 allowance balance remains on the books until the **December 31, Year 2** year-end adjustment.
- At **December 31, Year 2**, cost of ending inventory is **$102,000** and NRV is **$93,600**.
- All **January 1, Year 3** inventory is sold during Year 3. Allowance is again adjusted only at year-end.
- At **December 31, Year 3**, cost of ending inventory is **$68,000** and NRV is **$64,500**.
- On **April 8, Year 4**, Pinnacle sells **all** remaining Year 3 ending inventory for cash of **$66,000**. Inventory still carries **$68,000** cost on the books; the allowance still has its December 31, Year 3 credit balance until closed at sale.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **January 8, Year 1** purchase of the inventory that remains at year-end (**$87,500** on account).  
(b) Prepare an **LCNRV measurement schedule** at December 31, Year 1 for application to **(1) individual items**, **(2) each category**, and **(3) inventory in total**. Identify the write-down under the **item** approach used by Pinnacle.  
(c) Record the **December 31, Year 1** period-end adjusting entry (item approach; charge COGS; credit allowance). Also show the alternative entry charging **Holding Loss on Inventory**.  
(d) Show Year 1 **balance sheet** inventory presentation and **income statement** impact under both equity treatments in (c).  
(e) Prepare a multi-year **period-end allowance measurement schedule** for Year 1–Year 3 year-ends, showing: inventory cost, NRV, required LCNRV, required allowance, unadjusted allowance balance, and the debit or credit adjustment.  
(f) Record the **December 31, Year 2** and **December 31, Year 3** period-end adjusting entries (charge or credit COGS as needed).  
(g) Record the **April 8, Year 4** sale and the entry to **close the allowance** into COGS. Compute net COGS on this inventory layer and gross profit.

**Answer key:**

**(a) January 8, Year 1 — initial recognition of inventory at cost**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 87,500 | |
| Accounts Payable | | 87,500 |
| *Purchase ending-inventory layer at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 87,500 = Cr 87,500. **Balanced.**

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — LCNRV schedule**

| Item | Category | Cost | NRV | LCNRV — Item | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Trail Shell | Outerwear | 24,000 | 21,500 | **21,500** | NRV < cost |
| Summit Vest | Outerwear | 16,000 | 19,000 | **16,000** | Cost lower |
| Base Layer | Baselayers | 19,000 | 20,200 | **19,000** | Cost lower |
| Thermal Set | Baselayers | 28,500 | 25,000 | **25,000** | NRV < cost |
| **Totals** | | **87,500** | **85,700** | **81,500** | |

**By category:**  
- Outerwear: cost \(24{,}000 + 16{,}000 = 40{,}000\); NRV \(21{,}500 + 19{,}000 = 40{,}500\) → LCNRV **$40,000**  
- Baselayers: cost \(19{,}000 + 28{,}500 = 47{,}500\); NRV \(20{,}200 + 25{,}000 = 45{,}200\) → LCNRV **$45,200**  
- Category total LCNRV = \(40{,}000 + 45{,}200 =\) **$85,200**

**By total inventory:** \(\min(87{,}500,\ 85{,}700) =\) **$85,700**

| Application level | Inventory at LCNRV | Write-down vs cost $87,500 |
|---|---:|---:|

**(f) Period-end adjusting JEs — Year 2 and Year 3**

**December 31, Year 2** — required allow \(102{,}000 - 93{,}600 = 8{,}400\); unadj. 6,000; additional credit **2,400**:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 2,400 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 2,400 |

**December 31, Year 3** — required allow \(68{,}000 - 64{,}500 = 3{,}500\); unadj. 8,400; reduce allow by **4,900**:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | 4,900 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 4,900 |

**Check:** Dr 4,900 = Cr 4,900. Ending allow **$3,500** Cr. Inventory, net = \(68{,}000 - 3{,}500 =\) **$64,500**.

Note: The Year 3 debit to the allowance is a remeasurement for **new** ending inventory after prior goods sold—it is **not** a prohibited write-up of the original Year 1 units still held at their written-down “cost.”

**(g) April 8, Year 4 — sale and disposal/settlement of allowance**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 66,000 | |
| Sales Revenue | | 66,000 |
| *Cash sale of remaining inventory* | | |

**Check:** Dr 66,000 = Cr 66,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 68,000 | |
| Inventory | | 68,000 |
| *Remove inventory at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 68,000 = Cr 68,000.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | 3,500 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 3,500 |
| *Close allowance; reduces COGS to net carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,500 = Cr 3,500.  
Net COGS on this layer = \(68{,}000 - 3{,}500 =\) **$64,500** (equals prior net inventory).  
Gross profit = \(66{,}000 - 64{,}500 =\) **$1,500**.

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** is the operational core of LCNRV under an allowance system: remeasure only at reporting dates so the allowance equals cost − LCNRV for **current** ending inventory. Item-level measurement is most conservative; closing the allowance on sale aligns COGS with the already-reduced carrying amount.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — LCNRV schedule and initial period-end JE
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — LCNRV by item/category/total and period-end allowance JE  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Pet Supply LLC** uses **average cost** for inventory (not LIFO/retail). December 31 data:

| Item | Category | Cost | NRV |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Grain Blend | Dry Food | $36,000 | $32,500 |
| Senior Blend | Dry Food | 21,000 | 23,000 |
| Chew Rope | Accessories | 14,500 | 15,800 |
| Puzzle Toy | Accessories | 29,000 | 26,500 |
| **Total** | | **$100,500** | **$97,800** |

COGS before LCNRV adjustment: **$255,000**. Policy: individual-item LCNRV; allowance account; charge **COGS**.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute LCNRV by item, by category, and in total; state the write-down under the item approach.  
(b) Record the Dec 31 period-end adjusting entry (item approach).  
(c) Present Inventory, net on the balance sheet and COGS after adjustment.

**Answer key:**

**(a)**

| Item | Cost | NRV | LCNRV — Item |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Grain Blend | 36,000 | 32,500 | **32,500** |
| Senior Blend | 21,000 | 23,000 | **21,000** |
| Chew Rope | 14,500 | 15,800 | **14,500** |
| Puzzle Toy | 29,000 | 26,500 | **26,500** |
| **Total** | **100,500** | **97,800** | **94,500** |

**By category:**  
- Dry Food: cost \(36{,}000 + 21{,}000 = 57{,}000\); NRV \(32{,}500 + 23{,}000 = 55{,}500\) → LCNRV **$55,500**  
- Accessories: cost \(14{,}500 + 29{,}000 = 43{,}500\); NRV \(15{,}800 + 26{,}500 = 42{,}300\) → LCNRV **$42,300**  
- Category total = \(55{,}500 + 42{,}300 =\) **$97,800**

**By total:** \(\min(100{,}500,\ 97{,}800) =\) **$97,800**

| Level | LCNRV inventory | Write-down |
|---|---:|---:|
| Item | **$94,500** | **$6,000** |
| Category | $97,800 | $2,700 |
| Total | $97,800 | $2,700 |

Write-down (item) = \(100{,}500 - 94{,}500 =\) **$6,000**  
(alternatively: Grain \(36{,}000 - 32{,}500 = 3{,}500\) + Puzzle \(29{,}000 - 26{,}500 = 2{,}500\) = **$6,000**).

**(b) Period-end adjusting JE — initial recognition of write-down**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 6,000 | |
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | | 6,000 |
| *LCNRV write-down, individual-item approach* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,000 = Cr 6,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) Presentation**  
Inventory, net = \(100{,}500 - 6{,}000 =\) **$94,500**  
COGS after adjustment = \(255{,}000 + 6{,}000 =\) **$261,000**

**Key insight:** Same LCNRV mechanics as Q1 with all new numbers; the period-end entry still credits the allowance for the full item-level write-down when no prior allowance exists.

### Q3 — CORE — Unit NRV from selling price; period-end JE with preexisting allowance; direct write-down alternative
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE when an unadjusted allowance already exists; derive unit NRV; method-scope classification (LCNRV vs LCM)  
**Scenario:**  
**Brightline Electronics Distributors Inc.** uses **FIFO** and applies LCNRV to **each inventory item**. It adjusts the **Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value** only at fiscal year-end (**September 30**). Charges and credits for allowance changes flow through **Cost of Goods Sold**.

At September 30, Year 5, unit data for ending inventory:

| Item No. | Quantity | Est. selling price per unit | Disposal cost per unit | Cost per unit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| D-40 | 80 | $65 | $5 | $55 |
| E-55 | 120 | 42 | 3 | 48 |
| F-70 | 200 | 30 | 2 | 28 |

**Unadjusted** credit balance in the allowance before the year-end entry: **$2,000** (carryover from prior periods after inventory turns; company has not yet remeasured for Year 5 ending inventory).

**Required:**  
(a) Compute NRV per unit and total inventory cost vs total LCNRV (item approach). State the **required** allowance at September 30, Year 5.  
(b) Compute and record the **period-end adjusting entry** that sets the allowance to the required balance (through COGS).  
(c) State whether Brightline would use **LCNRV** or **lower of cost or market (LCM)** if it switched to **LIFO**, and why.  
(d) Ignore the preexisting allowance. If Brightline instead **credited Inventory directly** (no allowance) for the full Year 5 write-down amount, prepare that alternative JE.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Unit NRV and totals**

| Item | Qty | Cost/u | SP/u | Disp./u | **NRV/u** = SP − Disp. | LCNRV/u | Extended cost | Extended LCNRV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| D-40 | 80 | 55 | 65 | 5 | **60** | **55** | 4,400 | 4,400 |
| E-55 | 120 | 48 | 42 | 3 | **39** | **39** | 5,760 | 4,680 |
| F-70 | 200 | 28 | 30 | 2 | **28** | **28** | 5,600 | 5,600 |
| **Total** | | | | | | | **15,760** | **14,680** |

Write-down needed = \(15{,}760 - 14{,}680 =\) **$1,080**  
(only E-55 is impaired: \(120 \times (48 - 39) = 1{,}080\)).  
**Required allowance** credit = **$1,080**.

**(b) Period-end adjusting JE (preexisting unadjusted allow $2,000 Cr)**

Required allow = **1,080** Cr  
Unadjusted allow = **2,000** Cr  
Adjustment = reduce allowance by \(2{,}000 - 1{,}080 =\) **$920**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Allowance to Reduce Inventory to Net Realizable Value | 920 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | | 920 |
| *Period-end remeasurement of allowance to required LCNRV balance* | | |

**Check:** Dr 920 = Cr 920. Ending allow **$1,080** Cr. Inventory, net = \(15{,}760 - 1{,}080 =\) **$14,680**.

**(c) Method classification**  
If Brightline used **LIFO** (or the **retail inventory method**), it would apply **lower of cost or market (LCM)** under ASC 330, **not** LCNRV. LCNRV applies only to methods **other than** LIFO and retail (e.g., FIFO, average cost).

**(d) Direct write-down alternative (no allowance; full current-period impairment $1,080)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost of Goods Sold | 1,080 | |
| Inventory | | 1,080 |
| *Direct reduction of inventory to NRV (no allowance)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 1,080 = Cr 1,080. Inventory carrying amount **$14,680**.

**Key insight:** Period-end LCNRV under an allowance is a **balance-sheet remeasurement**—debit or credit COGS only for the **change** needed to reach the required allowance. Derive NRV as SP − disposal costs; only units with NRV < cost contribute to the write-down.

### Q4 — MC (classification / presentation)
**LO:** LO 10-1  
**Concept:** Classification of LCNRV application level and period-end income-statement presentation  

**Question 1:**  
Under ASC 330, which statement about applying lower of cost or net realizable value is **correct**?

- A) LCNRV is required for LIFO inventory; FIFO inventory uses lower of cost or market with a ceiling and floor.  
- B) Applying LCNRV to **individual items** generally produces a **higher** inventory carrying amount than applying it to inventory in total because write-downs can be offset within the pool.  
- C) Applying LCNRV to **individual items** generally produces the **lowest** inventory carrying amount because units with NRV above cost cannot offset units with NRV below cost.  
- D) After a write-down, if NRV later recovers above the reduced carrying amount on the **same** goods still held, the firm must reverse the allowance up to original cost.

**Answer:** C.  
Item-level application is most common and most conservative: no cross-offsetting. A reverses the LCNRV/LCM method mapping. B is backwards. D is incorrect—write-downs are not reversed for subsequent NRV recoveries on the same goods; the reduced amount is “cost” going forward.

**Question 2:**  
At year-end, **Cascade Supply Co.** (FIFO) must increase its Allowance to Reduce Inventory to NRV by **$9,500** for an ordinary product-cycle decline. Which period-end presentation is **most consistent** with common practice illustrated for ordinary declines?

- A) Debit Holding Loss on Inventory $9,500; credit Sales Revenue $9,500.  
- B) Debit Cost of Goods Sold $9,500; credit Allowance to Reduce Inventory to NRV $9,500.  
- C) Debit Retained Earnings $9,500; credit Inventory $9,500 (prior-period restatement).  
- D) Debit Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income $9,500; credit Inventory $9,500.

**Answer:** B.  
Ordinary LCNRV declines are typically charged to **COGS** (Nike-style “cost of sales”) with either an allowance or a direct inventory credit. A separate holding-loss line is used when the decline is unusual/infrequent. LCNRV adjustments are **current-period earnings**, not AOCI or automatic prior-period restatements.
