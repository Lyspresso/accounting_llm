# Agent 105 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-9

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Apply intraperiod tax allocation  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Intraperiod tax allocation:** allocate total income tax expense (benefit) among financial statement components within the same period
- **Continuing operations:** pretax income (loss) from continuing operations is presented **before tax**, with **Income tax expense** shown as a separate line
- **Discontinued operations:** income (loss) from discontinued operations (including disposal gains/losses) is presented **net of tax**
- **Other comprehensive income:** OCI items (e.g., unrealized holding gains/losses on AFS debt securities) are shown **net of the related tax effect**
- **Initial / period-end tax JE:** record current tax payable, deferred tax (when applicable), and allocate tax among continuing ops, disc. ops, and OCI so **Dr = Cr**
- **Allocation schedule:** pretax × enacted rate for each component; net-of-tax = pretax × (1 − rate) for disc. ops and OCI
- **Classification / presentation:** combined statement of comprehensive income with tax on continuing ops above the line and disc. ops / OCI net of tax

### Q1 — CORE — Intraperiod allocation: initial tax JE, allocation schedule, SCI (emphasis: initial recognition JE)
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Initial recognition journal entry for income taxes with intraperiod allocation to continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI; tax allocation schedule; combined statement of comprehensive income  
**Scenario:**  
**CedarPoint Manufacturing Co.** (calendar-year) is preparing Year 1 financial statements. Pretax amounts for the year ended December 31, Year 1:

| Item | Pretax amount |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations | $240,000 |
| Loss from discontinued operations (tax-deductible in Year 1) | (40,000) |
| Unrealized holding gain on available-for-sale debt securities (OCI; not taxable until sold) | 16,000 |

Enacted tax rate for all years: **25%**. There are **no other** temporary or permanent differences. Taxable income equals pretax GAAP **net income** (continuing + discontinued). The AFS unrealized gain is **not** included in taxable income (it creates a taxable temporary difference and deferred tax liability). No valuation allowance is needed. There were no deferred taxes at the beginning of Year 1 related to these items.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **tax allocation schedule** showing pretax amount, tax expense (benefit) at 25%, and net-of-tax amount for (1) continuing operations, (2) discontinued operations, and (3) OCI—AFS.  
b. Compute **income taxes payable** and the **deferred tax liability** related to the AFS unrealized gain.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 initial recognition (period-end adjusting) journal entry** to record income taxes using **intraperiod tax allocation**. Show that Dr = Cr.  
d. Prepare a **combined statement of comprehensive income** for Year 1 applying intraperiod tax allocation (single-step style for continuing operations is acceptable: start with pretax income from continuing operations).  
e. State how **tax related to discontinued operations** and **tax related to OCI** are **classified / presented** relative to tax on continuing operations.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Tax allocation schedule**

| Component | Pretax | Tax rate | Tax expense (benefit) | Net of tax |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing operations | $240,000 | 25% | $60,000 expense | $180,000 |
| Discontinued operations | (40,000) | 25% | (10,000) benefit | (30,000) |
| OCI—unrealized gain on AFS debt securities | 16,000 | 25% | 4,000 expense (to OCI) | 12,000 |

*Check (income statement components):* pretax NI = 240,000 − 40,000 = **$200,000**; net tax affecting NI = 60,000 − 10,000 = **$50,000**; NI = 200,000 − 50,000 = **$150,000**.  
*OCI:* 16,000 − 4,000 = **$12,000**.  
*Comprehensive income:* 150,000 + 12,000 = **$162,000**.

**b. Payable and deferred tax**

\[
\text{Taxable income} = \$240{,}000 - \$40{,}000 = \$200{,}000
\]
\[
\text{Income taxes payable} = \$200{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$50{,}000}
\]
\[
\text{Deferred tax liability (AFS)} = \$16{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$4{,}000}
\]

**c. December 31, Year 1 — initial recognition JE (intraperiod allocation)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense—Continuing Operations | 60,000 | |
| Other Comprehensive Income—Tax Effect on AFS Gain | 4,000 | |
| Income Tax Benefit—Discontinued Operations | | 10,000 |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 50,000 |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 4,000 |
| *Record current tax, deferred tax on AFS, and intraperiod allocation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = 60,000 + 4,000 = **64,000**  
Cr = 10,000 + 50,000 + 4,000 = **64,000**. **Balanced.**

*Alternative acceptable presentation of the OCI tax line:* debit **Income Tax Expense—Allocated to OCI** (or credit **OCI** directly net) with the same $4,000 amount, provided total tax on the AFS gain reduces OCI and creates the DTL.

**d. Combined statement of comprehensive income**

**CedarPoint Manufacturing Co.**  
**Statement of Comprehensive Income**  
**For the Year Ended December 31, Year 1**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations before income tax | $240,000 |
| Income tax expense | 60,000 |
| Income from continuing operations | 180,000 |
| Discontinued operations | |
| Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax benefit of $10,000 | (30,000) |
| **Net income** | **$150,000** |
| Other comprehensive income | |
| Unrealized holding gain on AFS debt securities, net of tax of $4,000 | 12,000 |
| **Comprehensive income** | **$162,000** |

**e. Classification / presentation**  
- **Tax on continuing operations ($60,000)** is presented as **Income tax expense** immediately below pretax income from continuing operations (gross presentation).  
- **Tax benefit on discontinued operations ($10,000)** is **not** a separate income-statement line for tax; it is **netted** against the pretax disc. ops loss so disc. ops appears **net of tax**.  
- **Tax on OCI ($4,000)** is **not** in net income; it is **netted** within OCI (or disclosed parenthetically) so the unrealized gain is shown **net of tax**.  
- Net income and OCI roll to **comprehensive income**.

**Key insight:** Intraperiod allocation does **not** change total tax for the period; it **assigns** current and deferred tax to continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI so each component is reported consistently (gross tax on continuing ops; net-of-tax for disc. ops and OCI).

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Intraperiod twin (gain on disc. ops + unrealized loss in OCI)
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: intraperiod tax JE and SCI when discontinued operations has a pretax **gain** and OCI has an unrealized **loss**  
**Scenario:**  
**Riverglen Retail Corp.** reports the following pretax amounts for the year ended December 31, Year 2:

| Item | Pretax amount |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations | $480,000 |
| Gain from discontinued operations (taxable in Year 2) | 60,000 |
| Unrealized holding loss on available-for-sale debt securities (OCI; not deductible for tax until realized) | (24,000) |

Enacted tax rate: **25%**. Taxable income equals pretax GAAP net income. The AFS unrealized loss creates a **deductible temporary difference** (deferred tax asset). No valuation allowance. No other temporary or permanent differences. Beginning deferred taxes related to AFS: zero.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **tax allocation schedule** (pretax, tax, net of tax) for continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI.  
b. Compute **income taxes payable** and **deferred tax asset** related to AFS.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 journal entry** to record income taxes with intraperiod allocation. Prove Dr = Cr.  
d. Prepare the **combined statement of comprehensive income**.  
e. Compute **comprehensive income**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Tax allocation schedule**

| Component | Pretax | Tax (25%) | Net of tax |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing operations | $480,000 | $120,000 expense | $360,000 |
| Discontinued operations | 60,000 | 15,000 expense | 45,000 |
| OCI—unrealized loss on AFS | (24,000) | (6,000) benefit | (18,000) |

**b. Payable and DTA**

\[
\text{Taxable income} = \$480{,}000 + \$60{,}000 = \$540{,}000
\]
\[
\text{Income taxes payable} = \$540{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$135{,}000}
\]
\[
\text{Deferred tax asset (AFS)} = \$24{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}
\]

**c. December 31, Year 2 — tax JE with intraperiod allocation**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense—Continuing Operations | 120,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense—Discontinued Operations | 15,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 6,000 | |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 135,000 |
| Other Comprehensive Income—Tax Benefit on AFS Loss | | 6,000 |
| *Current tax on TI; DTA and tax benefit allocated to OCI; tax on disc. ops gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr = 120,000 + 15,000 + 6,000 = **141,000**  
Cr = 135,000 + 6,000 = **141,000**. **Balanced.**

*Note:* Tax on discontinued operations may alternatively be presented only by showing disc. ops **net of tax** on the face of the income statement (net gain $45,000) without a separate “Income Tax Expense—Discontinued Operations” caption; the JE still allocates $15,000 of tax to that component.

**d. Combined statement of comprehensive income**

**Riverglen Retail Corp.**  
**Statement of Comprehensive Income**  
**For the Year Ended December 31, Year 2**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Income from continuing operations before income tax | $480,000 |
| Income tax expense | 120,000 |
| Income from continuing operations | 360,000 |
| Discontinued operations | |
| Gain from discontinued operations, net of tax of $15,000 | 45,000 |
| **Net income** | **$405,000** |
| Other comprehensive income (loss) | |
| Unrealized holding loss on AFS debt securities, net of tax benefit of $6,000 | (18,000) |
| **Comprehensive income** | **$387,000** |

**e. Comprehensive income**  
Net income $405,000 + OCI (loss) $(18,000) = **$387,000**.

**Key insight:** A disc. ops **gain** absorbs **tax expense** (reduces net disc. ops); an OCI **unrealized loss** generates a **tax benefit** (and often a DTA) so OCI is reported net of tax—still intraperiod allocation, opposite signs from Q1.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end tax JE with permanent difference, temporary difference, and disc. ops disposal
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JE and income statement when intraperiod allocation interacts with a permanent difference, originating taxable temporary difference, and loss on disposal of a discontinued component  
**Scenario:**  
**SummitLake Products Inc.** provides the following information for the year ended December 31, Year 3:

- Revenues $720,000; cost of goods sold $310,000; operating expenses $130,000.  
- Operating expenses include **regulatory fines of $4,000** that are **never tax-deductible** (permanent difference).  
- One originating timing difference produces a **future taxable amount of $48,000** during Year 3 (book income currently higher than taxable income by $48,000).  
- **Loss from discontinued operations** of **$28,000** (pretax) is **fully tax-deductible** in Year 3 and is **not** included in the revenue/expense amounts above (disposal of a component that meets discontinued-operations criteria).  
- Enacted tax rate for all years: **25%**. No beginning deferred tax balances. No valuation allowance issues.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **pretax income from continuing operations**.  
b. Compute **taxable income** and **income taxes payable**.  
c. Compute the **deferred tax liability** originating in Year 3.  
d. Compute **income tax expense allocated to continuing operations** and the **tax benefit allocated to discontinued operations**.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end journal entry** to record income taxes (intraperiod allocation). Prove Dr = Cr.  
f. Prepare an **income statement** (through net income) using intraperiod tax allocation.  
g. Briefly explain the **disposal / discontinued-operations** presentation of the tax benefit.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Pretax income from continuing operations**

\[
\$720{,}000 - \$310{,}000 - \$130{,}000 = \mathbf{\$280{,}000}
\]

**b. Taxable income and payable**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Pretax income from continuing operations | $280,000 |
| Add: nondeductible fines (permanent) | 4,000 |
| Less: originating future taxable amount (temporary) | (48,000) |
| Less: loss from discontinued operations (deductible) | (28,000) |
| **Taxable income** | **$208,000** |

\[
\text{Income taxes payable} = \$208{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$52{,}000}
\]

**c. Deferred tax liability**

\[
\text{DTL} = \$48{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$12{,}000}
\]

**d. Intraperiod allocation of tax**

*Tax on continuing operations (ASC 740 “with and without” / incremental approach conceptually):*  
Pretax continuing $280,000 + permanent fines $4,000 = **$284,000** permanent-adjusted “book” base for continuing.  
Tax expense—continuing = $284,000 × 25% = **$71,000**  
(Equivalently: current tax attributable to continuing + deferred tax on the $48,000 temporary difference.)

Verify split of current tax:  
Taxable income from continuing only (before disc. ops) = 280,000 + 4,000 − 48,000 = **$236,000**  
Current tax—continuing = 236,000 × 25% = **$59,000**  
Deferred tax—continuing = **$12,000**  
Tax expense—continuing = 59,000 + 12,000 = **$71,000** ✓

Tax benefit—discontinued operations = $28,000 × 25% = **$7,000**

*Roll-forward check:*  
Tax expense net of benefit = 71,000 − 7,000 = **$64,000**  
Payable + DTL increase = 52,000 + 12,000 = **$64,000** ✓

**e. December 31, Year 3 — period-end adjusting JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense—Continuing Operations | 71,000 | |
| Income Tax Benefit—Discontinued Operations | | 7,000 |
| Income Taxes Payable | | 52,000 |
| Deferred Tax Liability | | 12,000 |
| *Intraperiod allocation: permanent + temporary in continuing; tax benefit to disc. ops disposal loss* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **71,000**  
Cr = 7,000 + 52,000 + 12,000 = **71,000**. **Balanced.**

**f. Income statement (intraperiod allocation)**

**SummitLake Products Inc.**  
**Income Statement**  
**For the Year Ended December 31, Year 3**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Revenues | $720,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | 310,000 |
| Gross margin | 410,000 |
| Operating expenses | 130,000 |
| Income from continuing operations before income tax | 280,000 |
| Income tax expense | 71,000 |
| Income from continuing operations | 209,000 |
| Discontinued operations | |
| Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax benefit of $7,000 | (21,000) |
| **Net income** | **$188,000** |

*Check:* Pretax total affecting NI = 280,000 − 28,000 = 252,000; after tax NI = 252,000 − 64,000 = **188,000** ✓  
(Alternatively: 209,000 − 21,000 = 188,000.)

**g. Disposal / disc. ops tax presentation**  
The **$28,000 pretax loss on disposal / operations of the discontinued component** is reported in the discontinued operations section **net of its $7,000 tax benefit** as a single amount of **$(21,000)**. The tax benefit is **not** included in the continuing-operations income tax expense line.

**Key insight:** Permanent differences and deferred taxes from temporary differences are first measured for **continuing operations**; the tax (benefit) of discontinued operations is then allocated so that disc. ops (including disposal) is always shown **net of tax**.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of intraperiod tax components
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Classification / presentation — which financial statement components receive intraperiod tax allocation  
**Question:**  
Under U.S. GAAP (ASC 740), intraperiod tax allocation requires that income tax expense (benefit) be allocated among which of the following sets of items?

- A) Only income from continuing operations; discontinued operations and OCI are always shown pretax  
- B) Income (loss) from continuing operations, income (loss) from discontinued operations, and items of other comprehensive income  
- C) Only deferred tax assets and deferred tax liabilities on the balance sheet  
- D) Only permanent differences and temporary differences within pretax accounting income  

**Answer:** **B.** Intraperiod tax allocation assigns tax expense (benefit) to continuing operations, discontinued operations, and OCI so that disc. ops and OCI are reported **net of tax**, while continuing operations presents pretax amount and a separate income tax expense line. (A) is the opposite of the rule. (C) describes balance-sheet classification of deferred taxes, not intraperiod allocation. (D) describes measurement of tax, not allocation among statement components.

### Q5 — MC — Measure net-of-tax discontinued operations
**LO:** LO 18-9  
**Concept:** Compute loss from discontinued operations net of tax under intraperiod allocation  
**Question:**  
**PineHarbor Co.** reports pretax income from continuing operations of $90,000 and a pretax loss from discontinued operations of $12,000. The enacted tax rate is 25%. Taxable income equals pretax GAAP net income; there are no temporary differences. What amount will PineHarbor report for **loss from discontinued operations** and for **net income**?

- A) Disc. ops loss $(12,000); net income $78,000  
- B) Disc. ops loss $(9,000); net income $58,500  
- C) Disc. ops loss $(9,000); net income $66,000  
- D) Disc. ops loss $(3,000); net income $75,000  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Tax on continuing = $90,000 × 25% = $22,500 → income from continuing operations = **$67,500**.  
Tax benefit on disc. ops = $12,000 × 25% = $3,000 → disc. ops loss net of tax = **$(9,000)**.  
Net income = $67,500 − $9,000 = **$58,500**.  
(Also: pretax NI $78,000 − total tax payable $19,500 = $58,500.)  
Option A reports disc. ops pretax. Option C uses the correct net disc. ops loss but an incorrect NI of $66,000. Option D reports only the tax benefit as if it were the disc. ops loss.
