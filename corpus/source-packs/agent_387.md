# Agent 387 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-2

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Demonstrate accounting for change in accounting principle  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Change in accounting principle:** voluntary substitution of one acceptable GAAP principle for another (must be **preferable**), or mandated change when the prior principle is no longer GAAP
- **Retrospective application (default):** restate all comparative periods under the **new** principle; adjust opening assets/liabilities and **retained earnings** for cumulative **direct** effects of periods before those presented
- **Initial recognition JE (books catch-up):** on the first day of the year of change, Dr/Cr **Inventory** (or other BS account) and **Retained Earnings** for the full cumulative direct effect through the day before the change (prior-period P&L accounts are closed)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** after the change date, measure inventory and COGS under the **new** principle only; prepare **year-end periodic inventory closing/adjusting entries** that establish ending inventory and COGS under the new method
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year COGS rollforward (Beg + Purchases − End) under old vs new methods; period-specific NI bridge; comparative income, inventory, and RE presentation
- **Direct vs indirect effects:** only **direct** effects enter the cumulative Inventory/RE entry; **indirect** effects (bonus, royalties based on earnings) are recognized in the **current period as incurred**
- **Settlement JE:** pay (settle) any current-period indirect-effect liability (Bonus Payable, Royalties Payable)
- **Prospective exception:** when retrospective application is **impracticable** (classic: change **to LIFO** and prior layers cannot be reconstructed), apply the new principle as of the earliest practicable date; **no** cumulative RE catch-up; beginning inventory becomes the LIFO base layer; period-end JE still records EI/COGS under LIFO going forward
- **Not a change in principle:** initial adoption for new/immaterial transactions; adoption because transaction type changed; correction of non-GAAP application (error — LO 21-4)
- **Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-1):** nature and preferability; method of applying the change; effects on income/line items/EPS; cumulative effect on RE as of beginning of earliest period presented; impracticability reasons if applicable; indirect effects if recognized

### Q1 — CORE — LIFO→FIFO retrospective: Jan 1 cumulative JE, period-end adjusting JE (emphasis), schedules, indirect settlement
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Retrospective inventory principle change — January 1 Inventory/RE catch-up; **Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** under FIFO (periodic); COGS/NI/RE subsequent schedules; current-period indirect bonus recognition and cash settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Crestview Hardware Co.** (calendar year-end) began operations on **January 1, Year 1** using **LIFO** for financial reporting. On **January 1, Year 3**, Crestview voluntarily changes to **FIFO** because FIFO more faithfully represents physical flow after a new perpetual-scanning warehouse system (preferable). Crestview uses a **periodic inventory system** for year-end measurement. Ignore income taxes.

Inventory and operating data:

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory — LIFO | $0 | $42,000 | $50,000 |
| Beginning inventory — FIFO | 0 | 51,000 | 68,000 |
| Purchases | 195,000 | 245,000 | 275,000 |
| Ending inventory — LIFO | 42,000 | 50,000 | 57,000 |
| Ending inventory — FIFO | 51,000 | 68,000 | 86,000 |
| Sales | 250,000 | 325,000 | 360,000 |
| Operating expenses (excl. bonus) | 40,000 | 44,000 | 47,000 |

As originally issued under LIFO: Year 1 net income **$57,000**; Year 2 net income **$44,000**; retained earnings end of Year 2 under LIFO **$101,000**.

Crestview’s executive plan pays a bonus based on income. After the change, the board agrees that the **Year 2** bonus (to be paid in Year 3) would have been **$1,500** higher under FIFO. The original Year 2 bonus has already been recorded; the **$1,500** incremental amount will be recognized and paid in **Year 3**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 initial recognition JE** for the retrospective change from LIFO to FIFO (direct effect only; ignore taxes). Show Dr = Cr.  
b. Prepare a **subsequent measurement COGS schedule** under both LIFO and FIFO for Years 1–3 (Beg + Purchases − End).  
c. Prepare a **period-specific net income bridge schedule** (NI under LIFO vs FIFO) for Years 1–2 and the cumulative effect at the change date.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** under the **FIFO** method (periodic system) that establishes ending inventory and COGS for Year 3. Show Dr = Cr.  
e. Prepare **three-year comparative income statements** as reported **after** the change (all years under FIFO), before the indirect bonus.  
f. Prepare the **comparative inventory presentation** (Dec 31 Year 2 and Year 3) and a **two-year comparative statement of retained earnings** (Year 2 and Year 3) after the change, including the cumulative-effect line on the earliest year presented.  
g. Prepare the **Year 3 JE for the indirect effect** ($1,500 bonus) and the **settlement JE** when the incremental bonus is paid in cash.  
h. **Classification / disclosure:** Is this retrospective or prospective? List key ASC 250 disclosure elements for the year of change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — cumulative direct effect (January 1, Year 3)**

Inventory under FIFO at Jan 1, Year 3 = **$68,000**  
Inventory under LIFO at Jan 1, Year 3 = **$50,000**  
Cumulative difference (direct effect) = $68,000 − $50,000 = **$18,000**

RE end Y2: LIFO $101,000; FIFO $119,000; difference **$18,000** ✓

**COGS under LIFO (as originally computed)**

**g. Indirect effect and settlement (Year 3)**

*Recognize incremental Year 2 bonus as current-period cost (not retrospective to Year 2)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 1,500 | |

*Settlement when paid*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bonus Payable | 1,500 | |

**Check:** Dr 1,500 = Cr 1,500. **Balanced.**

**h. Classification / disclosure**

- **Type:** Voluntary **change in accounting principle** (LIFO → FIFO).  
- **Application:** **Retrospective** (not prospective; not a change in estimate).  
- **Direct effects only** in the cumulative RE/inventory entry; **indirect** bonus is current-period.  
- **Period-end measurement** in Year 3 uses **FIFO only** (part d).  
- **Disclosures (ASC 250-10-50-1):** nature of and reason for the change, including **why FIFO is preferable**; method of applying the change; description of prior-period information retrospectively adjusted; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, affected line items, and EPS for current and restated prior periods; cumulative effect on retained earnings as of the beginning of the earliest period presented; if indirect effects recognized — description and amounts (and per-share if applicable).

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** in the year of change is ordinary inventory measurement — but under the **new** principle only. The **Jan 1 cumulative Inventory/RE entry** rewrites the past on the books; the **year-end FIFO closing entry** then measures Year 3 COGS correctly. Schedules prove Δ Inventory at the change date equals cumulative pretax income difference closed to RE.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Average-cost→FIFO with period-end adjusting JE twin
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: cumulative direct-effect JE; subsequent COGS/NI schedules under FIFO; **Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** under FIFO; comparative RE and inventory; indirect royalty current JE and settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Summit Ridge Outfitters Inc.** (calendar year-end) used the **weighted-average cost** method for inventory since inception (**January 1, Year 1**). On **January 1, Year 3**, Summit Ridge changes to **FIFO** for financial reporting (preferable for matching more recent costs to ending inventory in a rising-price environment). The company uses a **periodic inventory system**. Ignore taxes.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory — Average | $0 | $36,000 | $45,000 |
| Beginning inventory — FIFO | 0 | 48,000 | 66,000 |
| Purchases | 165,000 | 200,000 | 230,000 |
| Ending inventory — Average | 36,000 | 45,000 | 52,000 |
| Ending inventory — FIFO | 48,000 | 66,000 | 81,000 |
| Sales | 200,000 | 285,000 | 315,000 |
| Operating expenses (excl. royalties) | 32,000 | 36,000 | 39,000 |

As issued under average cost: Year 1 NI = **$39,000**; Year 2 NI = **$58,000**; RE end Year 2 under average = **$97,000**.

A supplier royalty agreement pays a percentage of net income. After restatement, the Year 2 royalty payable (settled in Year 3) is **$800** higher under FIFO than originally recorded. Record and pay the incremental amount in Year 3.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (average → FIFO).  
b. Prepare the **FIFO COGS schedule** for Years 1–3 and a **NI schedule** under FIFO for each year.  
c. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** under FIFO (periodic) establishing ending inventory and COGS.  
d. Prepare **comparative retained earnings** for Year 2 and Year 3 after the change.  
e. State December 31, Year 2 inventory as presented on the **Year 3 comparative balance sheet**.  
f. Prepare the **indirect-effect JE** and **cash settlement JE** for the $800 incremental royalty in Year 3.  
g. Briefly state why the cumulative RE adjustment equals the inventory difference at the change date (ignore taxes).

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 3 — initial cumulative JE**

FIFO inventory Jan 1 Y3 = **$66,000**  
Average inventory Jan 1 Y3 = **$45,000**  
Cumulative direct effect = **$21,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory | 21,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 21,000 |
| *Retrospective change from average cost to FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr 21,000 = Cr 21,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Subsequent measurement — FIFO COGS and NI schedule**

| Year | Beg (FIFO) | Purchases | End (FIFO) | COGS (FIFO) | Sales | OpEx | **NI (FIFO)** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $0 | $165,000 | $48,000 | **$117,000** | $200,000 | $32,000 | **$51,000** |
| 2 | 48,000 | 200,000 | 66,000 | **182,000** | 285,000 | 36,000 | **$67,000** |
| 3 | 66,000 | 230,000 | 81,000 | **215,000** | 315,000 | 39,000 | **$61,000** |

Average-cost NI check (as given / recomputed):  
Y1: 200,000 − (0 + 165,000 − 36,000) − 32,000 = 200 − 129 − 32 = **$39,000**  
Y2: 285,000 − (36,000 + 200,000 − 45,000) − 36,000 = 285 − 191 − 36 = **$58,000**  
Period-specific income increases: Y1 **+$12,000**; Y2 **+$9,000**; cumulative **+$21,000** ✓

**c. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 3 under FIFO (emphasis)**

After Jan 1 catch-up, books carry beginning inventory at FIFO **$66,000**. Year 3 purchases **$230,000**. Ending inventory under FIFO is **$81,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (ending, FIFO) | 81,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 215,000 | |
| Inventory (beginning, FIFO) | | 66,000 |
| Purchases | | 230,000 |
| *Period-end adjustment — EI and COGS under FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr $81,000 + $215,000 = **$296,000**; Cr $66,000 + $230,000 = **$296,000**. **Balanced.**  
COGS check: $66,000 + $230,000 − $81,000 = **$215,000** ✓

**d. Comparative retained earnings**

| | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginning balance, as previously reported (average) | $39,000 | $118,000 |
| Add: Cumulative effect of change to FIFO on prior years | 12,000 | — |
| Beginning balance, as adjusted | 51,000 | 118,000 |
| Net income (FIFO) | 67,000 | 61,000 |
| **Ending balance** | **$118,000** | **$179,000** |

(Year 2 ending under FIFO = 51k + 67k = 118k; RE under average end Y2 was 97k; 118 − 97 = 21k cumulative at change date.)

**e. Comparative inventory presentation**

December 31, Year 2 inventory on Year 3 comparative balance sheet (FIFO) = **$66,000** (not the $45,000 average amount originally reported).

**f. Indirect royalty — current period + settlement**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Royalty Expense | 800 | |
| Royalties Payable | | 800 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 800. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Royalties Payable | 800 | |
| Cash | | 800 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 800. **Balanced.**

**g. Why inventory difference = RE adjustment**

Ignoring taxes, every dollar of higher ending inventory under the new principle is a dollar of lower cumulative COGS and higher cumulative pretax income closed to RE. At the change date, **Δ Inventory = cumulative Δ NI = Δ Retained Earnings** (direct effects only).

**Key insight:** Same retrospective path as LIFO→FIFO with fully changed company and amounts: the **Jan 1 Inventory/RE entry** restates the past; the **period-end FIFO adjusting JE** measures the year of change; schedules quantify period-specific effects.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Change to LIFO when retrospective is impracticable (prospective + period-end LIFO JE)
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Prospective application when retrospective is impracticable; LIFO base layer = beginning inventory; **no** cumulative RE JE; mixed-method comparative COGS schedule; **period-end Year 3 adjusting JE under LIFO**; impracticability disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecroft Merchants LLC** reported inventory under **FIFO** for Year 1 and Year 2. On **January 1, Year 3**, Pinecroft changes to **LIFO**. After every reasonable effort, management **cannot reconstruct LIFO layers** for prior periods (purchase-price history for old layers is unavailable). Pinecroft can apply LIFO only from Year 3 forward. Year 3 ending inventory under LIFO is determined to be **$69,000**. The company uses a **periodic inventory system**.

Data:

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO going forward) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $55,000 | $74,000 |
| Purchases | 185,000 | 220,000 | 250,000 |
| Ending inventory | 55,000 (FIFO) | 74,000 (FIFO) | 69,000 (LIFO) |
| Sales | 230,000 | 295,000 | 325,000 |
| Operating expenses | 38,000 | 42,000 | 44,000 |

**Required:**  
a. Is retrospective application required? Explain using ASC 250 impracticability.  
b. Prepare any **January 1, Year 3 cumulative-effect JE** (or state that none is made).  
c. Prepare the **three-year comparative COGS subsequent measurement schedule** under the approach Pinecroft actually uses.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end adjusting JE** under **LIFO** (periodic) establishing ending inventory and COGS.  
e. Compute **Year 3 net income** under the prospective LIFO approach.  
f. Optional contrast: If Year 3 ending inventory under FIFO would have been **$92,000**, what would Year 3 COGS and NI have been under continued FIFO?  
g. **Presentation / disclosure:** How do comparative Year 2 vs Year 3 principles differ on the Year 3 report? What must be disclosed about impracticability?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Retrospective not required**

Change **to LIFO** with inability to reconstruct prior layers meets the **impracticability** exception (ASC 250-10-45-9): after every reasonable effort, the entity cannot apply the requirement (and/or would need unverifiable assumptions/estimates about prior periods). Therefore apply the new principle **prospectively** as of the earliest practicable date (beginning of Year 3).

**b. Cumulative-effect JE**

**No journal entry** on January 1, Year 3 to restate Inventory or Retained Earnings for prior years. Beginning inventory of **$74,000** (FIFO carrying amount) becomes the **LIFO base layer**.

**c. Comparative COGS schedule (prospective — subsequent measurement)**

| | Year 1 (FIFO) | Year 2 (FIFO) | Year 3 (LIFO) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginning inventory | $0 | $55,000 | $74,000 |
| Purchases | 185,000 | 220,000 | 250,000 |
| Less ending inventory | 55,000 | 74,000 | 69,000 |
| **Cost of goods sold** | **$130,000** | **$201,000** | **$255,000** |

Years 1–2 COGS remain as previously computed under FIFO; only Year 3 uses LIFO ending inventory.  
Checks: Y1 $185k − $55k = $130k; Y2 $55k + $220k − $74k = $201k; Y3 $74k + $250k − $69k = $255k.

**d. Period-end adjusting JE — December 31, Year 3 under LIFO (emphasis)**

Beginning inventory carries forward as the LIFO base layer **$74,000**. Purchases Year 3 **$250,000**. Ending inventory under LIFO **$69,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inventory (ending, LIFO) | 69,000 | |
| Cost of Goods Sold | 255,000 | |
| Inventory (beginning / LIFO base layer) | | 74,000 |
| Purchases | | 250,000 |
| *Period-end adjustment — EI and COGS under prospective LIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr $69,000 + $255,000 = **$324,000**; Cr $74,000 + $250,000 = **$324,000**. **Balanced.**  
COGS check: $74,000 + $250,000 − $69,000 = **$255,000** ✓

**e. Year 3 net income (prospective LIFO — period-end)**

NI = Sales − COGS − OpEx = $325,000 − $255,000 − $44,000 = **$26,000**

**f. Continued FIFO contrast (Year 3 only)**

COGS if FIFO EI $92,000: $74,000 + $250,000 − $92,000 = **$232,000**  
NI if FIFO: $325,000 − $232,000 − $44,000 = **$49,000**  
Difference in Year 3 NI: LIFO NI is **$23,000 lower** than continued FIFO ($49,000 − $26,000), equal to the $23,000 lower ending inventory ($92,000 − $69,000).

**g. Presentation / disclosure**

- Year 3 comparative statements show **Year 2 still under FIFO** and **Year 3 under LIFO** — principles are **not uniform** across periods (comparability impaired).  
- Disclose: nature and reason for the change (preferability); that retrospective application is **impracticable** and **why**; description of the **alternative method** used (prospective from Year 3; base layer = beginning inventory).  
- Do **not** restate prior inventory or RE for unavailable LIFO layers.  
- The **period-end LIFO adjusting JE** still occurs — prospective application does not eliminate year-end measurement under the new method going forward.

**Key insight:** Retrospective is the default for voluntary principle changes, but a change **to LIFO** is the textbook case where **prospective** application applies because prior-period layers cannot be rebuilt. Even then, the **period-end adjusting JE** in Year 3 records LIFO ending inventory and COGS; only the **cumulative RE catch-up** is absent.

### Q4 — CORE — Period-end / presentation focus: average→FIFO change-date JE, Year 3 rollforward, RE disclosure

**Concept:** Change-date cumulative JE from ending inventory differences; comparative BS inventory presentation; Year 3 subsequent inventory rollforward and **period-end COGS**; earliest-period cumulative effect on RE vs full books catch-up

| Purchases | | 310,000 |
| *Period-end adjustment — EI and COGS under FIFO* | | |

**Check:** Dr $108,000 + $298,000 = **$406,000**; Cr $96,000 + $310,000 = **$406,000**. **Balanced.**

**e. Cumulative effect disclosed as of beginning of earliest period presented (Year 2)**

Earliest year presented = Year 2. Cumulative effect on periods **prior to Year 2** = Year 1 inventory difference only:  
FIFO EI Year 1 $75,000 − Average EI Year 1 $55,000 = **$20,000**

This **$20,000** appears as the adjustment to beginning retained earnings of Year 2 on the comparative statement of retained earnings.

**f. Reconcile books catch-up vs earliest-period disclosure**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Effect of Year 1 (prior to earliest presented) | $20,000 |
| Effect of Year 2 (period-specific restatement of Y2 NI) | 8,000 |
| **Full books catch-up Jan 1 Year 3** | **$28,000** |

Year 2 period-specific effect: Δ Inventory during Year 2 = ($96,000 − $68,000) − ($75,000 − $55,000) = $28,000 − $20,000 = **$8,000** (higher pretax income in restated Year 2).  
Disclosure of **$20,000** at beginning of Year 2 plus restated Year 2 NI (higher by $8,000) rolls the RE balance to the same place as the **$28,000** single books entry on Jan 1 Year 3.

**Key insight:** Presentation separates (1) the **cumulative effect prior to the earliest year shown** on the RE statement from (2) the **full books catch-up** that adjusts inventory to the new principle as of the change date. The **period-end JE** then continues measurement under FIFO only.

### Q5 — MC — Method of application: LIFO→FIFO vs change to LIFO
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Classification of application method — retrospective default vs prospective when impracticable  
**Question:**  
Which statement best describes the required application method under U.S. GAAP (ASC 250)?

- A) A voluntary change from LIFO to FIFO is applied prospectively; a change from FIFO to LIFO is always applied retrospectively.  
- B) A voluntary change from LIFO to FIFO is applied retrospectively (unless impracticable); a change to LIFO is often applied prospectively when prior layers cannot be reconstructed.  
- C) All voluntary changes in inventory method are applied only to the current year with a cumulative-effect line on the income statement.  
- D) Changes in inventory method are treated as changes in estimate and never affect retained earnings.

**Answer:** **B.** Voluntary changes in principle are generally applied **retrospectively** (restating comparative periods and adjusting opening RE for cumulative direct effects). A change **to LIFO** is the classic case where retrospective application is often **impracticable**, so the new principle is applied **prospectively** from the earliest practicable date with no cumulative RE catch-up. Choice A reverses the methods. Choice C describes obsolete cumulative-effect-on-income treatment, not current ASC 250 retrospective application. Choice D confuses principle changes with estimate changes.

### Q6 — MC — Direct vs indirect effects at period-end of change year
**LO:** LO 21-2  
**Concept:** Direct effects in cumulative RE entry; indirect bonus effects recognized currently, not restated  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 3, a company retrospectively changes from LIFO to FIFO. Inventory is increased and retained earnings is credited for the cumulative pretax inventory difference through December 31, Year 2. Separately, a profit-based bonus that would have been $2,000 higher for Year 2 under FIFO is paid in Year 3 after the board authorizes the adjustment. How should the $2,000 be accounted for?

- A) Debit Retained Earnings $2,000 and credit Bonus Payable $2,000 as part of the retrospective application.  
- B) Debit Compensation Expense $2,000 and credit Bonus Payable (or Cash) $2,000 in Year 3 — an indirect effect recognized currently.  
- C) Debit Cost of Goods Sold $2,000 in Year 3 as a period-end inventory adjustment.  
- D) No entry is required because indirect effects are ignored under ASC 250.

**Answer:** **B.** Retrospective application includes only **direct** effects (inventory and related tax effects). **Indirect** effects (nondiscretionary items based on earnings, such as bonuses) are **not** restated into prior periods; if incurred, they are recognized in the **period of the change** as current expense. Choice A incorrectly puts the bonus into the cumulative RE entry. Choice C confuses COGS measurement with the bonus. Choice D is wrong because recognized indirect effects are recorded currently and disclosed.
