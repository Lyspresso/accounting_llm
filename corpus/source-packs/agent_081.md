# Agent 81 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-5

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for changes in depreciation methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis:** initial_recognition_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition JE for PPE acquisition cost (cash purchase) that will later undergo a method change
- Subsequent measurement under the **original** depreciation method (SYD or DDB) with a multi-period schedule
- Change in depreciation method = **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle** (ASC-style treatment)
- **Prospective** treatment only: no restatement of prior periods; no catch-up to beginning RE
- At the change date: recompute depreciation from **carrying amount − residual** over **remaining useful life** under the **new** method
- Period-end adjusting JE for depreciation in the year of (and after) the change
- Disposal / settlement JE after a method change (remove cost and accumulated depreciation; gain/loss)
- Classification / presentation / disclosure: nature of the change and effect on income in the period of change; prior years unchanged

### Q1 — CORE — SYD to straight-line: purchase, schedule, change, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of equipment; subsequent SYD measurement; prospective switch to straight-line; period-end dep JE; disposal after change  
**Scenario:**  
Cedarvale Logistics Inc. purchased a specialized conveyor system on **January 1, Year 1** for **$48,000 cash**. Management estimated a **5-year** useful life and a **$3,000** residual value. The company adopted the **sum-of-the-years’-digits (SYD)** depreciation method.

On **January 1, Year 4**, management changes the depreciation method for this asset class to **straight-line**. Residual value and total useful life estimates are **unchanged**. Cedarvale’s year-end is December 31.

On **December 31, Year 5**, after recording Year 5 depreciation, Cedarvale sells the conveyor for **$2,800 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** purchase journal entry.  
b. Prepare a depreciation schedule for **Years 1–3 under SYD** (show fraction, expense, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount each year-end). Record the **December 31, Year 1** depreciation adjusting entry (format is the same each year; amount differs).  
c. Compute **straight-line depreciation for Year 4** after the method change. Record the **December 31, Year 4** period-end adjusting entry.  
d. Prepare a rollforward schedule covering **Years 1–5** (method used, depreciation expense, ending AD, ending CV).  
e. Record **Year 5 depreciation** and the **December 31, Year 5 disposal** journal entry.  
f. In one or two sentences, explain whether prior-year depreciation is restated and how the change is classified under GAAP.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — January 1, Year 1**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 48,000 | |
| Cash | | 48,000 |
| *To record purchase of conveyor system* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 48,000.

**b. SYD Years 1–3**  
Sum of years = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = **15**  
Depreciable base = $48,000 − $3,000 = **$45,000**

| Year | Beg. CV | SYD fraction | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 48,000 | 5/15 | **15,000** | 15,000 | 33,000 |
| 2 | 33,000 | 4/15 | **12,000** | 27,000 | 21,000 |
| 3 | 21,000 | 3/15 | **9,000** | 36,000 | 12,000 |

Computations:  
- Year 1: \(5/15 \times 45{,}000 = 15{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(4/15 \times 45{,}000 = 12{,}000\)  
- Year 3: \(3/15 \times 45{,}000 = 9{,}000\)

December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting entry (illustrative of SYD years):

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 15,000 |
| *Year 1 SYD depreciation* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 15,000.  
(Year 2 JE: Dr/Cr **12,000**; Year 3 JE: Dr/Cr **9,000**.)

Carrying amount on **January 1, Year 4** (change date) = **$12,000**.

**c. Prospective straight-line after method change — Year 4**  
Remaining useful life = 2 years. Residual still $3,000.  
\[
\text{Annual SL depreciation} = \frac{\$12{,}000 - \$3{,}000}{2} = \$4{,}500
\]

December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 4,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 4,500 |
| *Year 4 SL depreciation after prospective method change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,500. Ending CV Dec 31, Year 4 = 12,000 − 4,500 = **$7,500**.

**d. Full rollforward Years 1–5**

| Year | Method | Depreciation expense | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | SYD | 15,000 | 15,000 | 33,000 |
| 2 | SYD | 12,000 | 27,000 | 21,000 |
| 3 | SYD | 9,000 | 36,000 | 12,000 |
| 4 | SL (post-change) | 4,500 | 40,500 | 7,500 |
| 5 | SL | 4,500 | 45,000 | 3,000 |

Total depreciation over life = 15,000 + 12,000 + 9,000 + 4,500 + 4,500 = **$45,000** = depreciable base. Ending CV = residual **$3,000**.

**e. Year 5 depreciation and disposal**

December 31, Year 5 — depreciation:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 4,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 4,500 |

December 31, Year 5 — disposal (sale for $2,800):  
Book value at sale = $3,000; proceeds $2,800 → **loss $200**.  
AD at disposal = **$45,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 45,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Equipment | 200 | |
| Equipment | | 48,000 |
| *To record sale of conveyor below book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 2,800 + 45,000 + 200 = **48,000** = Cr 48,000.

**f. Classification / prospective treatment**  
A change in depreciation method is a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**. It is accounted for **prospectively** (current and future periods only). Prior years are **not** restated; beginning retained earnings is **not** adjusted for the cumulative difference.

**Key insight:** After a method change, depreciation is driven by **current carrying amount**, **residual**, and **remaining life** under the **new** method. Prior SYD amounts stay as recorded; only the path forward changes.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — DDB to straight-line twin
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; DDB subsequent measurement schedule; prospective change to SL; period-end JE  
**Scenario:**  
Harborline Fabricators LLC acquired production equipment on **January 1, Year 1** for **$80,000 cash**. Estimated useful life **4 years**; residual value **$8,000**. Harborline used the **double-declining-balance (DDB)** method for Years 1 and 2.

On **January 1, Year 3**, Harborline changes to the **straight-line** method for this equipment. Residual value and total useful life remain **$8,000** and **4 years**. Year-end is December 31.

**Required:**  
a. Record the purchase entry on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Compute DDB depreciation for Year 1 and Year 2; record each year-end adjusting entry. Prepare a two-year DDB schedule.  
c. Compute Year 3 and Year 4 straight-line depreciation after the change; record the Year 3 period-end JE.  
d. Show carrying amount at December 31, Year 4 (end of life).  
e. State whether Year 1–2 depreciation would be restated under the change.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 80,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 80,000.

**b. DDB Years 1–2**  
DDB rate = \(2 / 4 = 50\%\). Residual is **not** subtracted when applying the DDB rate to beginning book value (but book value is not driven below residual over the life).

| Year | Beg. BV | Rate | Depreciation | End. AD | End. BV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 80,000 | 50% | **40,000** | 40,000 | 40,000 |
| 2 | 40,000 | 50% | **20,000** | 60,000 | 20,000 |

December 31, Year 1:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 40,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 40,000 |

December 31, Year 2:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 20,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 20,000 |

**Check:** Each JE balances. CV on change date **January 1, Year 3** = **$20,000**.

**c. Prospective SL after change**  
Remaining life = 2 years.  
\[
\text{Annual SL} = \frac{\$20{,}000 - \$8{,}000}{2} = \$6{,}000
\]

December 31, Year 3:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 6,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 6,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,000. Year 4 depreciation is also **$6,000** (same JE amounts).

**d. End of Year 4**  
AD end Year 4 = 40,000 + 20,000 + 6,000 + 6,000 = **$72,000**  
CV = 80,000 − 72,000 = **$8,000** (equals residual).

**e.** No restatement of Years 1–2. The change is **prospective** only (treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle).

**Key insight:** Under DDB, residual is ignored in the rate application until late in life; after switching to SL, residual is subtracted from **current carrying amount** and allocated evenly over remaining years.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end JE after DDB→SL change; disposal; presentation
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting entry in year of method change; multi-period DDB schedule; disposal after change; classification/disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
Solstice Printworks Co. purchased a high-speed press on **January 1, Year 1** for **$125,000 cash**. Useful life **5 years**; residual value **$5,000**. Depreciation method: **double-declining-balance** (rate = \(2/5 = 40\%\)).

On **January 1, Year 4**, Solstice changes to **straight-line** depreciation. Residual and total life estimates are unchanged.

On **December 31, Year 4**, after recording Year 4 depreciation, Solstice sells the press for **$16,000 cash**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the DDB depreciation schedule for **Years 1–3** (expense, AD, CV).  
b. Compute Year 4 depreciation under the new SL method and record the **December 31, Year 4 period-end adjusting entry only**.  
c. Record the **December 31, Year 4 disposal** entry.  
d. **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** (1) How is this change classified? (2) Are comparative prior-year financial statements restated for depreciation? (3) What amount of Year 4 depreciation expense is reported for this asset, and how does that amount compare to what DDB would have been for Year 4?

**Answer key:**  

**a. DDB Years 1–3**  
Rate = 40%.

| Year | Beg. BV | Depreciation (40% × beg. BV) | End. AD | End. CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 125,000 | **50,000** | 50,000 | 75,000 |
| 2 | 75,000 | **30,000** | 80,000 | 45,000 |
| 3 | 45,000 | **18,000** | 98,000 | 27,000 |

Carrying amount January 1, Year 4 = **$27,000**.

**b. Year 4 SL depreciation (period-end adjusting JE)**  
Remaining life = 2 years.  
\[
\frac{\$27{,}000 - \$5{,}000}{2} = \$11{,}000
\]

December 31, Year 4 — period-end adjusting entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 11,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 11,000 |
| *Year 4 SL depreciation after prospective DDB→SL change* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 11,000.  
CV after Year 4 dep = 27,000 − 11,000 = **$16,000**.  
AD after Year 4 = 98,000 + 11,000 = **$109,000**.

*(If DDB had continued in Year 4: \(27{,}000 \times 40\% = 10{,}800\). The new SL amount is **$11,000**, which differs because the method—not a prior-period correction—drives the current expense.)*

**c. Disposal December 31, Year 4**  
Proceeds $16,000 = book value $16,000 → **no gain or loss**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 16,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 109,000 | |
| Equipment | | 125,000 |
| *To record sale of press at book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 16,000 + 109,000 = **125,000** = Cr 125,000.

**d. Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
1. Classified as a **change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle**.  
2. **No** — prior years are **not** restated; Years 1–3 DDB amounts remain as reported.  
3. Year 4 depreciation expense for this asset = **$11,000** (new method applied to carrying amount at the change date). Disclosures typically describe the **nature of the change** and the **effect on income** in the period of change (here, relative to continuing DDB, expense is $200 higher: 11,000 − 10,800).

**Key insight:** The period-end JE in the year of change uses the **new method only** from the change date forward. Disposal still clears gross cost and full accumulated depreciation; gain/loss is proceeds vs. post-change book value.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of a change in depreciation method
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Classification — change in estimate effected by a change in principle; prospective application  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 4, a company changes from sum-of-the-years’-digits to straight-line depreciation for equipment acquired in Year 1. Residual value and total useful life are unchanged. Under U.S. GAAP as applied in this chapter’s CORE treatment, how should the company account for the change?

- A) Retrospective restatement of all prior periods presented, with a cumulative catch-up to beginning retained earnings  
- B) Prospective application as a change in accounting estimate effected by a change in accounting principle; no restatement of prior depreciation  
- C) Treat as a correction of an error with a prior-period adjustment to retained earnings  
- D) Prospective application only if the cumulative effect on prior years is immaterial; otherwise restate  

**Answer:** **B.** A change in depreciation method is treated as a change in estimate effected by a change in principle and is applied **prospectively**. Prior financial statements are not restated, and there is no error-correction PPA. Choice A describes a typical voluntary change in accounting principle (not method-of-depreciation treatment). Choice C confuses method changes with errors (LO 12-6). Choice D invents a materiality gate that does not change the CORE prospective rule.

### Q5 — MC — Compute depreciation in year of method change
**LO:** LO 12-5  
**Concept:** Compute new-method depreciation from carrying amount at change date  
**Question:**  
Equipment cost $90,000; residual $6,000; useful life 6 years. SYD was used for Years 1–2. On January 1, Year 3, the company switches to straight-line (estimates unchanged). What is Year 3 depreciation expense?

Sum of years = 21. Depreciable base = $84,000.  
Year 1 SYD = \(6/21 \times 84{,}000 = 24{,}000\).  
Year 2 SYD = \(5/21 \times 84{,}000 = 20{,}000\).

- A) $16,000  
- B) $11,500  
- C) $12,000  
- D) $10,000  

**Answer:** **D.**  
Carrying amount Jan 1, Year 3 = 90,000 − 24,000 − 20,000 = **$46,000**.  
Remaining life = 4 years.  
\[
\text{Year 3 SL} = \frac{\$46{,}000 - \$6{,}000}{4} = \$10{,}000
\]  
Distractors: **A** = continuing SYD for Year 3 (\(4/21 \times 84{,}000\)) instead of applying the new method; **B** = $46,000 / 4 if residual is ignored; **C** mishandles base or life (e.g., rough round figure).
