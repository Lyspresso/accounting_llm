# Agent 4 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-3

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for debt securities measured at FV-OCI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- Initial recognition of AFS debt at acquisition cost (par)
- Interest revenue on AFS debt recognized in net income
- Period-end fair value adjustment through FVA and Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI (FV-OCI)
- Subsequent-year FVA rollforward (required FVA − existing FVA)
- Sale-date FV-OCI adjust, then sale with reclassification of AOCI holding gain/loss into net income and elimination of FVA
- AOCI reclassification disclosure / reconciliation
- Portfolio-level FVA with partial disposal
- Classification/presentation: unrealized AFS holding gains/losses in OCI (not NI) until sale

### Q1 — CORE — Full AFS life cycle (purchase → interest → YE FVA → next-year FVA → sale with reclass)
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE + subsequent FVA (period-end and sale date) + disposal reclassification  
**Scenario:**  
On July 1 of Year 1, Cascade Holdings purchased for **par $80,000** of Pinecrest 7% bonds that mature on June 30 of Year 6. Cash interest is paid **semiannually** on June 30 and December 31. Cascade classifies the investment as **available-for-sale (AFS)** and measures it under **FV-OCI**. Cascade’s accounting period ends on December 31. Beginning AOCI related to AFS is zero. No other AFS activity occurs except as stated.

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **purchase** of the investment on July 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Record receipt of **cash interest** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Record the **period-end fair value adjustment** on December 31 of Year 1, assuming fair value of the Pinecrest bonds is **$76,000**.  
(d) Report the Year 1 effects on the **income statement**, **statement of comprehensive income**, and **December 31 Year 1 balance sheet** (investment carrying amount and AOCI). Ignore all other items.  
(e) Assume no change in holdings during Year 2. Record the entry to adjust AFS to fair value on December 31 of Year 2 if fair value is **$82,400**. Show the FVA rollforward schedule.  
(f) On January 1 of Year 3, Cascade sells the entire position for **$83,200**. (i) Adjust the investment to fair value at the sale date using FV-OCI. (ii) Record the sale, including reclassification of holding gains/losses from AOCI to net income and elimination of the Fair Value Adjustment balance.  
(g) Prepare the Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation** (beginning AOCI, current-period unrealized, reclassification to NI, ending AOCI), assuming no other AOCI items.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) July 1, Year 1 — Purchase at par (initial recognition)**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Pinecrest Bonds | 80,000 | |
| Cash | | 80,000 |

Dr = Cr = **$80,000**. Amortized cost basis = **$80,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest revenue in net income**  
Cash interest = $80,000 × 0.07 × 6/12 = **$2,800**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 2,800 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 2,800 |

Dr = Cr = **$2,800**. Interest is recognized in **net income** for all debt categories (including AFS).

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Adjust to fair value (FV-OCI)**  
Unrealized holding loss = amortized cost $80,000 − fair value $76,000 = **$4,000**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 4,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 4,000 |

Dr = Cr = **$4,000**. FVA ending balance = **$4,000 credit**. Carrying amount on BS = $80,000 − $4,000 = **$76,000**.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**  
**Income statement (excerpt):** Interest revenue **$2,800** (unrealized AFS holding loss is **not** in NI).  

**Statement of comprehensive income (excerpt):**  
- Net income: includes interest revenue $2,800 (other items ignored)  
- Other comprehensive income: Unrealized holding loss on AFS securities **$(4,000)**  
- Comprehensive income reflects NI + OCI  

**Balance sheet, December 31, Year 1:**  
- Assets: Investment in available-for-sale securities **$76,000** (amortized cost $80,000 net of FVA credit $4,000)  
- Stockholders’ equity: Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) **$(4,000)**

**(e) December 31, Year 2 — FVA rollforward and adjusting entry**  

| AFS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pinecrest bonds | 80,000 | 82,400 | 2,400 |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | (4,000) |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **6,400** |

Required FVA = FV − AC = $82,400 − $80,000 = **$2,400 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$4,000 credit**  
Increase needed = $2,400 − (−$4,000) = **$6,400**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 6,400 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 6,400 |

Dr = Cr = **$6,400**. FVA ending = −4,000 + 6,400 = **$2,400 debit**. AOCI related balance = **$2,400**.

**(f) January 1, Year 3 — Sale under FV-OCI**  

**(i) Adjust to fair value at sale date**  
Required FVA at sale* = $83,200 − $80,000 = **$3,200 debit**  
Less existing FVA = **2,400**  
Increase needed = **$800**  

\*$83,200 fair value (sale price) − $80,000 amortized cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 800 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 800 |

Dr = Cr = **$800**. FVA now = **$3,200 debit**.

**(ii) Record sale + reclass AOCI holding gain to NI + eliminate FVA**  
Realized gain = cash proceeds $83,200 − amortized cost $80,000 = **$3,200**.  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 83,200 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 3,200 | |
| Investment in AFS—Pinecrest Bonds | | 80,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 3,200 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 3,200 |

**Proof:** Debits $83,200 + $3,200 = **$86,400**; Credits $80,000 + $3,200 + $3,200 = **$86,400**. Balances.  
FVA and investment accounts are zero after the sale. Realized **gain of $3,200** is in **net income**; the matching debit to Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI **reclassifies** the cumulative holding gain out of AOCI.

**(g) Year 3 AOCI reclassification disclosure**  

| Reconciliation of Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, January 1, Year 3 | $2,400 |
| Current period unrealized gain on AFS securities (sale-date FV adj) | 800 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (3,200) |
| Net OCI for Year 3 | (2,400) |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income, December 31, Year 3 | **$0** |

**Holding-period AOCI volatility check (nets to zero after sale):**  
Y1 OCI loss $(4,000) + Y2 OCI gain $6,400 + Y3 OCI gain $800 − reclass to Y3 NI $(3,200) = **$0**.

**Key insight:** AFS debt uses **FV-OCI**: interest hits **NI**; period-end and sale-date fair value changes hit **OCI** via FVA; only at **sale** is the cumulative holding gain/loss **reclassified** into NI, and FVA is eliminated so the investment accounts clear.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same AFS FV-OCI path with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
On January 1 of Year 1, Summit Forge LLC purchased for **par $45,000** of Larkspur Corp. 8% bonds that mature on December 31 of Year 5. Cash interest is paid **annually** each December 31. Summit classifies the bonds as **AFS** (FV-OCI). Year-end is December 31. Beginning AOCI related to AFS is zero. No other AFS activity except as stated.

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on January 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Cash interest entry on December 31 of Year 1.  
(c) Fair value adjustment on December 31 of Year 1 if bonds are quoted at fair value **$43,200**.  
(d) Year 1 IS / SCI / BS presentation for this investment (AOCI begins at zero).  
(e) No sales in Year 2; fair value on December 31 of Year 2 is **$46,800**. Prepare the FVA schedule and adjusting entry.  
(f) Sell the entire holding on January 1 of Year 3 for **$47,100**: (i) sale-date FV-OCI adjust; (ii) sale + reclass + eliminate FVA.  
(g) Year 3 AOCI reconciliation after the sale.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS Securities—Larkspur Bonds | 45,000 | |
| Cash | | 45,000 |

Dr = Cr = **$45,000**.

**(b) December 31, Year 1 — Interest**  
Interest = $45,000 × 0.08 × 12/12 = **$3,600**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,600 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 3,600 |

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — FV-OCI loss**

|---|---:|---:|

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — AFS portfolio: partial sale + year-end FVA on remainder
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE + subsequent_measurement_schedule (portfolio FVA)  
**Scenario:**  
On December 31 of Year 2, Apex Treasury Partners held the following **AFS** debt portfolio (all purchased at amounts equal to amortized cost shown; no discount/premium remaining):

| AFS Investment | Amortized Cost | Fair Value (12/31/Y2) | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Redwood bonds | $40,000 | $38,500 | $(1,500) |
| Solstice bonds | 75,000 | 79,500 | 4,500 |
| **Total** | **$115,000** | **$118,000** | **$3,000** |

The **$3,000** net unrealized gain equals the December 31, Year 2 balances in both **FVA—AFS** (debit) and **AOCI** related to AFS.  

On **January 1 of Year 3**, Apex sells the **Redwood** bonds for **$41,200**. The remaining Solstice bonds (still held) have a fair value of **$77,000** on December 31 of Year 3. No other AFS purchases or sales occur in Year 3. Year-end is December 31.

**Required:**  
(a) Adjust Redwood to fair value at the sale date (FV-OCI), then record the sale including reclassification of AOCI holding gain/loss into net income and elimination of the associated FVA for Redwood.  
(b) Record the December 31, Year 3 entry to adjust the FVA account for **Solstice only** (remaining portfolio). Show the schedule.  
(c) Prepare the December 31, Year 3 **AOCI reclassification reconciliation**.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) January 1, Year 3 — Redwood sale (FV-OCI path)**  

**Sale-date FVA adjust for Redwood:**  
Required FVA at sale* = $41,200 − $40,000 = **$1,200 debit**  
Existing FVA allocated to Redwood = **$(1,500)**  
Increase needed = $1,200 − (−$1,500) = **$2,700**  

\*$41,200 sale price/FV − $40,000 amortized cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 2,700 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 2,700 |

Dr = Cr = **$2,700**.

**Sale of Redwood (realized gain $1,200 = $41,200 − $40,000):**  
| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 41,200 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 1,200 | |
| Investment in AFS—Redwood Bonds | | 40,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 1,200 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 1,200 |

**Proof:** Debits $41,200 + $1,200 = **$42,400**; Credits $40,000 + $1,200 + $1,200 = **$42,400**.

**FVA T-account after Redwood sale:**  
Beg Dr $3,000 + sale-date adj Dr $2,700 − sale elimination Cr $1,200 = **Dr $4,500** (equals Solstice’s pre-existing unrealized gain only).

**(b) December 31, Year 3 — Adjust Solstice (remaining AFS) to FV**  

| | Amortized Cost | Fair Value YE | Required FVA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Solstice bonds | 75,000 | 77,000 | 2,000 debit |
| Existing FVA (after Redwood sale) | | | 4,500 debit |
| **Decrease to FVA needed** | | | **$(2,500)** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 2,500 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 2,500 |

Dr = Cr = **$2,500**. Ending FVA = **$2,000 debit** = ending AOCI related to remaining AFS.

**(c) Year 3 AOCI reclassification disclosure**  
Current-period unrealized (from Y3 entries affecting Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI):  
- Redwood sale-date gain: **+$2,700**  
- Solstice year-end decline: **−$2,500**  
- Net current-period unrealized = **+$200**  
Reclassification adjustment for **gain** included in NI (Redwood): **$(1,200)**

| Reconciliation of AOCI | Amount |
|---|---:|
| AOCI, January 1, Year 3 | $3,000 |
| Current period unrealized gain on AFS securities | 200 |
| Reclassification adjustment for gain included in net income | (1,200) |
| Net OCI for Year 3 | (1,000) |
| AOCI, December 31, Year 3 | **$2,000** |

**Check:** Ending AOCI $2,000 = remaining Solstice FVA debit $2,000.

**Key insight:** In a portfolio, **adjust the sold security to FV through OCI first**, record sale with **reclass of that security’s cumulative unrealized into NI** and **clear its FVA**, then **re-measure remaining AFS** at year-end through OCI. AOCI ending must equal the remaining FVA balance.

### Q4 — MC — Classification / measurement channel (AFS vs TS)
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure — where AFS unrealized holding gains/losses are reported  
**Question:**  
On December 31, Year 1, Meridian Capital holds a debt security classified as **available-for-sale**. Amortized cost is $100,000 and fair value is $106,500. No sale has occurred. Ignoring tax, how should Meridian report the $6,500 unrealized holding gain for Year 1?

- A) As a realized gain in net income of $6,500  
- B) As an unrealized gain in **other comprehensive income** of $6,500, with a corresponding debit to Fair Value Adjustment—AFS  
- C) No recognition until the security is sold  
- D) As a direct credit to retained earnings of $6,500, bypassing both NI and OCI  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Under LO 14-3 / ASC 320, AFS debt is measured at fair value with unrealized holding gains and losses **excluded from earnings** and reported in **OCI** until realized. The FVA account is **debited** for holding gains (offset **Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI**). Option A describes **trading (FV-NI)**. Option C ignores required period-end FV measurement for AFS. Option D is not GAAP for AFS holding gains.

### Q5 — MC — Sale sequence under FV-OCI
**LO:** LO 14-3  
**Concept:** disposal with sale-date FV adjust and AOCI reclassification  
**Question:**  
When an AFS debt security is sold under the LO 14-3 approach (fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end), which sequence is correct?

- A) Record cash and remove the investment at amortized cost only; never touch FVA or OCI  
- B) First adjust the investment to fair value through **OCI** (update FVA); then record the sale, **reclassify** the cumulative holding gain/loss from AOCI into **net income**, and eliminate the FVA balance  
- C) First recognize the entire difference between cash and amortized cost in OCI only, with no effect on net income at sale  
- D) Adjust fair value through **net income** (as for trading), then sell with no AOCI reclassification  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Textbook LO 14-3 sale path: **(1)** adjust to FV via FVA and Unrealized Gain or Loss—**OCI**; **(2)** record cash, remove investment at amortized cost, eliminate FVA, and **reclassify** AOCI holding gain/loss into **NI** (realized gain/loss). A is incomplete; C never realizes the gain/loss in NI; D is the **trading / FV-NI** model (LO 14-2), not AFS FV-OCI.
