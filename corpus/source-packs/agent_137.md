# Agent 137 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-5

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities following the equity method  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase of equity securities with significant influence (Dr Investment / Cr Cash) at cost
- **Significant influence presumption:** generally 20%–50% voting stock (rebuttable); equity method (ASC 323) vs FV-NI (ASC 321)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** roll-forward of Investment account = cost + share of investee NI − share of dividends − excess depreciation (basis difference)
- **Period-end adjusting JEs:** equity-method income (Dr Investment / Cr Investment Income); excess depreciation on FV > BV depreciable assets (Dr Investment Income / Cr Investment); dividends as return of investment (Dr Cash / Cr Investment)
- **Ignore fair value changes** (unless impaired under LO 14-6); no Fair Value Adjustment under equity method
- **Partial-period holding:** prorate share of investee NI and excess depreciation for months owned; dividends only for amounts declared while investor holds the shares
- **Basis difference / goodwill:** allocate excess cost over BV to FV adjustments on identifiable assets, then residual goodwill (not amortized)
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** remove carrying amount of Investment; difference vs cash proceeds = gain or loss on sale
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** typically noncurrent investment; SCF — equity income vs dividends received reconciling item (indirect method); purchase/sale cash flows usually investing
- **Number-variant twin:** same structure, all new companies and amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Equity method: purchase, multi-year investment schedule, basis-difference dep, FV ignored, partial-year, disposal
**LO:** LO 14-5  
**Concept:** Initial recognition under equity method; subsequent measurement schedule (share of NI, dividends, excess depreciation); period-end adjusting JEs; ignore fair value; partial-year proration; disposal settlement JE; SCF presentation of equity income vs dividends  
**Scenario:**  
**Horizon Peak Co.** is a calendar-year company. On **January 1, 2025**, Horizon Peak purchases **40%** of the voting common stock of **Mariner Tech Corp.** for **$240,000** cash. Horizon Peak has **significant influence** over Mariner Tech and accounts for the investment under the **equity method**. Horizon Peak intends to hold the investment long term (noncurrent).

**Acquisition-date analysis (January 1, 2025):**
| Item | Book value | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Stockholders’ equity (net assets BV) | $500,000 | — |
| Undervalued depreciable equipment (total company) | included in BV | BV + $25,000 |
| Remaining life of undervalued equipment | | **10 years**, straight-line, no salvage |
| Residual excess of cost over FV of net assets | | **goodwill** (not amortized) |

Horizon Peak’s cost equals:
- 40% of Mariner Tech’s **book value** of net assets = \(0.40 \times \$500{,}000 = \$200{,}000\)
- 40% of **FV excess** on equipment = \(0.40 \times \$25{,}000 = \$10{,}000\)
- Therefore 40% of **fair value of identifiable net assets** = \( \$200{,}000 + \$10{,}000 = \$210{,}000 \)
- **Goodwill** in the investment cost = \( \$240{,}000 - \$210{,}000 = \$30{,}000 \)

**Subsequent facts:**
| Year | Mariner Tech net income | Mariner Tech cash dividends declared & paid | Quoted FV of Horizon Peak’s 40% interest at Dec 31 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025 | $80,000 | $20,000 | $275,000 |
| 2026 | $60,000 | $15,000 | $268,000 |

On **January 1, 2027**, Horizon Peak sells its **entire** Mariner Tech investment for **$290,000** cash.

**Alternate fact (part g only):** Same facts as original except the investment is purchased on **April 1, 2025** (not January 1). Mariner Tech’s 2025 NI is earned evenly throughout the year. Dividends are still declared and paid on December 31, 2025 (while Horizon Peak owns the shares).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, 2025 initial recognition JE**.  
b. Prepare **December 31, 2025 period-end adjusting JEs**: (1) share of investee net income, (2) excess depreciation on the basis difference, (3) dividends received.  
c. Prepare a **two-year subsequent measurement (Investment account) schedule** through December 31, 2026, showing beginning balance, share of NI, excess dep, dividends, and ending balance each year.  
d. What entry (if any) does Horizon Peak make on December 31, 2025 and 2026 for the **change in fair value** of the Mariner Tech shares?  
e. Prepare the **January 1, 2027 disposal (sale) JE**.  
f. **Classification / presentation:** (1) How is the investment classified on Horizon Peak’s December 31, 2025 balance sheet? (2) For the 2025 statement of cash flows (indirect method), what reconciling adjustment is made in the **operating** section related to equity-method income vs. dividends? How is the 2025 purchase classified?  
g. Using the **April 1, 2025 purchase alternate facts**, prepare the December 31, 2025 JEs for share of NI, excess depreciation, and dividends, and compute the **December 31, 2025 Investment balance**.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — equity method purchase (January 1, 2025)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | 240,000 | |
| Cash | | 240,000 |
| *Acquire 40% voting interest; significant influence* | | |

**Check:** Dr 240,000 = Cr 240,000. **Balanced.**

**Purchase-price allocation (memo / analysis — not a separate JE):**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cost | $240,000 |
| 40% of BV of net assets | 200,000 |
| 40% of FV excess — equipment | 10,000 |
| Goodwill (residual) | 30,000 |
| **Total** | **$240,000** |

**b. Period-end adjusting JEs — December 31, 2025**

*(1) Share of investee net income*

Share of NI = \(0.40 \times \$80{,}000 = \$32{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | 32,000 | |
| Investment Income | | 32,000 |
| *40% × $80,000 investee NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 32,000. **Balanced.**

*(2) Excess depreciation on depreciable basis difference*

Annual excess dep = \(0.40 \times (\$25{,}000 \div 10) = \$1{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment Income | 1,000 | |
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | | 1,000 |
| *Amortize FV > BV on equipment; reduces equity income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 1,000. **Balanced.**

Net equity-method income for 2025 = \( \$32{,}000 - \$1{,}000 = \$31{,}000 \).

*(3) Dividends received (return of investment, not dividend revenue)*

Share of dividends = \(0.40 \times \$20{,}000 = \$8{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,000 | |
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | | 8,000 |
| *40% × $20,000 dividends declared & paid* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. **Balanced.**

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule — Investment in Mariner Tech**

| Date / item | Share of NI | Excess dep | Dividends | Investment balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, 2025 purchase | | | | **240,000** |
| 2025 activity | 32,000 | (1,000) | (8,000) | |
| **Dec 31, 2025** | | | | **263,000** |
| 2026 activity | 24,000 | (1,000) | (6,000) | |
| **Dec 31, 2026** | | | | **280,000** |

**2026 detail:**  
- Share of NI = \(0.40 \times \$60{,}000 = \$24{,}000\)  
- Excess dep = \( \$1{,}000 \) (same annual amount; equipment basis difference)  
- Dividends = \(0.40 \times \$15{,}000 = \$6{,}000\)  
- Ending = \( \$263{,}000 + \$24{,}000 - \$1{,}000 - \$6{,}000 = \$280{,}000 \)

**Roll-forward proof:**  
\( \$240{,}000 + \$32{,}000 - \$1{,}000 - \$8{,}000 + \$24{,}000 - \$1{,}000 - \$6{,}000 = \$280{,}000 \). **OK.**

**d. Fair value changes**

**No entry** on December 31, 2025 or 2026 for the increase/decrease in quoted fair value. Under the equity method, **changes in fair value are ignored** (unless an impairment loss is required under LO 14-6, which is outside this LO’s core measurement path). The Investment account is **not** adjusted to $275,000 or $268,000.

**e. Disposal JE — January 1, 2027**

Carrying amount at sale = **$280,000** (from schedule).  
Cash proceeds = **$290,000**.  
Gain on sale = \( \$290{,}000 - \$280{,}000 = \$10{,}000 \).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 290,000 | |
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | | 280,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 10,000 |
| *Sell entire equity-method investment* | | |

**Check:** Dr 290,000 = Cr 280,000 + 10,000. **Balanced.**

**f. Classification / presentation / SCF**

1. **Balance sheet (Dec 31, 2025):** Report **Investment in Mariner Tech** as a **noncurrent asset** at carrying amount **$263,000** (long-term significant-influence investment; not expected to be sold currently).

2. **Statement of cash flows (2025, indirect method):**  
   - Net equity-method income recognized = **$31,000** (\( \$32{,}000 - \$1{,}000 \)).  
   - Cash dividends received = **$8,000**.  
   - **Operating reconciling adjustment:** subtract **$23,000** (\( \$31{,}000 - \$8{,}000 \)) from net income so that operating cash reflects only the **$8,000** cash dividend inflow.  
   - **Investing section:** cash outflow of **$240,000** for purchase of the investment (not a trading-purpose acquisition).

**g. Partial-year purchase (April 1, 2025) — December 31, 2025**

Holding period = **9/12** of the year.

Share of NI = \(0.40 \times \$80{,}000 \times 9/12 = \$24{,}000\)  
Excess dep = \( \$1{,}000 \times 9/12 = \$750 \)  
Dividends (owned on declaration date Dec 31) = \(0.40 \times \$20{,}000 = \$8{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | 24,000 | |
| Investment Income | | 24,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment Income | 750 | |
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | | 750 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,000 | |
| Investment in Mariner Tech — Equity Method | | 8,000 |

All three JEs **balance**.

**Dec 31, 2025 Investment balance (April 1 purchase):**  
\( \$240{,}000 + \$24{,}000 - \$750 - \$8{,}000 = \$255{,}250 \).

**Key insight:** Under the equity method, the investor recognizes its **proportionate share of investee earnings** (not dividends) as income, reduces the investment for dividends, and amortizes **only** depreciable/amortizable **basis differences** (goodwill is not amortized). Fair value changes are ignored. A multi-period **Investment account schedule** is the core exam tool for subsequent measurement.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Equity method with basis difference, schedule, loss year, disposal
**LO:** LO 14-5  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — initial recognition; subsequent measurement schedule including share of loss and excess depreciation; period-end JEs; ignore FV; sale of equity-method investment  
**Scenario:**  
**Solstice Brands Inc.** (calendar year) acquires **30%** of the voting common stock of **Cedar Grove Packaging Co.** on **January 1, 2025** for **$195,000** cash. Solstice has significant influence and applies the equity method.

**Acquisition-date data:**
- Cedar Grove stockholders’ equity (BV of net assets) = **$500,000**  
  → Solstice’s 30% of BV = \(0.30 \times \$500{,}000 = \$150{,}000\)
- Undervalued depreciable plant (total company FV excess) = **$40,000**, remaining life **8 years**, straight-line, no salvage  
  → Solstice’s share of FV excess = \(0.30 \times \$40{,}000 = \$12{,}000\)
- 30% of FV of identifiable net assets = \( \$150{,}000 + \$12{,}000 = \$162{,}000 \)
- **Goodwill** = \( \$195{,}000 - \$162{,}000 = \$33{,}000 \) (not amortized)

**Subsequent facts:**
| Year | Cedar Grove NI (loss) | Cash dividends declared & paid | Fair value of Solstice’s 30% interest at year-end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025 | $70,000 | $20,000 | $210,000 |
| 2026 | **($16,000)** loss | $4,000 | $185,000 |

On **March 1, 2027**, after recording no 2027 equity-method activity (ignore proration for simplicity — sale is at beginning of period relative to investee results), Solstice sells the **entire** investment for **$188,000** cash. Use the **December 31, 2026** carrying amount as the book value sold.

**Required:**  
a. Initial recognition JE on January 1, 2025.  
b. All equity-method JEs for **2025** (share of NI, excess dep, dividends).  
c. All equity-method JEs for **2026** (share of loss, excess dep, dividends).  
d. Two-year **Investment account schedule** ending December 31, 2026.  
e. Entry (if any) for year-end fair value changes.  
f. Sale JE on March 1, 2027.  
g. Compute **net equity-method income (loss)** reported by Solstice for 2025 and for 2026.

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, 2025 purchase**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | 195,000 | |
| Cash | | 195,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 195,000. **Balanced.**

**b. 2025 period-end JEs**

Share of NI = \(0.30 \times \$70{,}000 = \$21{,}000\)  
Annual excess dep = \(0.30 \times (\$40{,}000 \div 8) = 0.30 \times \$5{,}000 = \$1{,}500\)  
Dividends = \(0.30 \times \$20{,}000 = \$6{,}000\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | 21,000 | |
| Investment Income | | 21,000 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment Income | 1,500 | |
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | | 1,500 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 6,000 | |
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | | 6,000 |

All **balanced**. Net equity income 2025 = \( \$21{,}000 - \$1{,}500 = \$19{,}500 \).

**c. 2026 period-end JEs**

Share of loss = \(0.30 \times \$16{,}000 = \$4{,}800\)  
Excess dep = **$1,500**  
Dividends = \(0.30 \times \$4{,}000 = \$1{,}200\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equity in Investee Loss (or Investment Income) | 4,800 | |
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | | 4,800 |
| *30% × investee net loss* | | |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment Income (or Equity in Investee Loss) | 1,500 | |
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | | 1,500 |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,200 | |
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | | 1,200 |

All **balanced**. Net equity-method loss 2026 = \( \$4{,}800 + \$1{,}500 = \$6{,}300 \).

**d. Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date / item | Share of NI (loss) | Excess dep | Dividends | Investment balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan 1, 2025 purchase | | | | **195,000** |
| 2025 | 21,000 | (1,500) | (6,000) | **208,500** |
| 2026 | (4,800) | (1,500) | (1,200) | **201,000** |

Math: \( \$195{,}000 + \$21{,}000 - \$1{,}500 - \$6{,}000 = \$208{,}500 \).  
\( \$208{,}500 - \$4{,}800 - \$1{,}500 - \$1{,}200 = \$201{,}000 \). **OK.**

**e. Fair value**

**No entry.** Year-end FVs of $210,000 and $185,000 are **ignored** under the equity method.

**f. Sale March 1, 2027**

Carrying amount = **$201,000**. Proceeds = **$188,000**.  
Loss on sale = \( \$201{,}000 - \$188{,}000 = \$13{,}000 \).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 188,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Investment | 13,000 | |
| Investment in Cedar Grove — Equity Method | | 201,000 |

**Check:** Dr 188,000 + 13,000 = Cr 201,000. **Balanced.**

**g. Net equity-method income (loss)**

| Year | Amount |
|---|---:|
| 2025 net equity income | **$19,500** |
| 2026 net equity loss | **($6,300)** |

**Key insight:** Share of investee **losses** reduces the Investment account (and income) the same way gains increase it. Excess depreciation continues even in a loss year. Dividends always **credit** the Investment account under the equity method — never Dividend Revenue when significant influence exists.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Mid-year acquisition with full purchase-price allocation; period-end adjusting only; presentation
**LO:** LO 14-5  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs after mid-year purchase; allocate cost to BV, depreciable FV excess, and goodwill; investment ending balance; equity method vs FV-NI comparison on dividends and income; balance-sheet and SCF classification  
**Scenario:**  
On **July 1, 2025**, **Northwind Logistics Corp.** purchases **6,000** of the **20,000** outstanding common shares (**30%**) of **Pinnacle Fasteners Inc.** for **$168,000** cash. Northwind obtains significant influence and uses the equity method. Both companies have December 31 year-ends.

**Pinnacle Fasteners balance sheet data on July 1, 2025:**
| | Book value | Fair value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Nondepreciable assets | $280,000 | $280,000 |
| Depreciable assets (net) | 120,000 | 160,000 |
| Total liabilities | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| Stockholders’ equity | 300,000 | — |

Depreciable assets have a **10-year** remaining life (straight-line, no salvage). Any residual excess of cost over FV of net assets is **goodwill**.

**Pinnacle Fasteners results for full year 2025:**
- Net income = **$48,000** (earned evenly throughout the year)
- Cash dividends declared and paid = **$12,000** on **December 20, 2025** (after Northwind’s purchase)

At December 31, 2025, the fair value of Northwind’s 30% interest is **$175,000**.

**Required:**  
a. Compute ownership percentage and prepare the **July 1, 2025 purchase JE**. Show allocation of the $168,000 cost among BV of net assets acquired, depreciable FV excess, and goodwill.  
b. Prepare **only the December 31, 2025 period-end equity-method adjusting JEs** (share of NI for holding period, excess depreciation for holding period, dividends).  
c. Compute the **December 31, 2025 Investment balance**.  
d. Briefly contrast: if Northwind **lacked** significant influence and used **FV-NI**, what would 2025 **dividend** and **year-end fair value** accounting look like (amounts only — no need for full FVA T-account mechanics)?  
e. **Classification / presentation:** balance-sheet classification and amount at Dec 31, 2025; SCF treatment of the July 1 cash purchase and of the equity income vs dividends difference.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Purchase and allocation (July 1, 2025)**

Ownership = \(6{,}000 / 20{,}000 = \mathbf{30\%}\)

BV of net assets = \( \$280{,}000 + \$120{,}000 - \$100{,}000 = \$300{,}000 \)  
(also equals stated stockholders’ equity)

FV of identifiable net assets = \( \$280{,}000 + \$160{,}000 - \$100{,}000 = \$340{,}000 \)

| Allocation | Computation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| 30% of BV of net assets | \(0.30 \times \$300{,}000\) | $90,000 |
| 30% of FV excess — depreciable assets | \(0.30 \times (\$160{,}000 - \$120{,}000) = 0.30 \times \$40{,}000\) | 12,000 |
| Goodwill (plug) | \( \$168{,}000 - \$90{,}000 - \$12{,}000 \) | 66,000 |
| **Cost** | | **$168,000** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Pinnacle Fasteners — Equity Method | 168,000 | |
| Cash | | 168,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 168,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Period-end adjusting JEs (December 31, 2025)**

Holding period from July 1 = **6/12**.

Share of NI = \(0.30 \times \$48{,}000 \times 6/12 = \$7{,}200\)  
Annual excess dep on full-year basis = \( \$12{,}000 \div 10 = \$1{,}200 \); for 6/12 = **$600**  
Dividends = \(0.30 \times \$12{,}000 = \$3{,}600\)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Pinnacle Fasteners — Equity Method | 7,200 | |
| Investment Income | | 7,200 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,200. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment Income | 600 | |
| Investment in Pinnacle Fasteners — Equity Method | | 600 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 600. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 3,600 | |
| Investment in Pinnacle Fasteners — Equity Method | | 3,600 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,600. **Balanced.**

Net equity-method income 2025 = \( \$7{,}200 - \$600 = \$6{,}600 \).

**c. Ending Investment balance**

\( \$168{,}000 + \$7{,}200 - \$600 - \$3{,}600 = \mathbf{\$171{,}000} \)

**d. Contrast if FV-NI (no significant influence)**

- **Dividends:** recognize **Dividend Revenue $3,600** (Dr Cash / Cr Dividend Revenue) — does **not** reduce the investment.  
- **Year-end FV:** adjust investment (via FVA) to **$175,000**; unrealized holding gain in **net income** of \( \$175{,}000 - \$168{,}000 = \$7{,}000 \) (assuming cost basis still $168,000 and no prior FVA).  
- **No** share of investee NI and **no** excess depreciation under FV-NI.

**e. Classification / presentation**

- **Balance sheet Dec 31, 2025:** noncurrent **Investment in Pinnacle Fasteners** at **$171,000** (equity-method carrying amount, not $175,000 FV).  
- **SCF:** July 1 purchase **$168,000** = **investing outflow**. Indirect-method operating section: subtract net equity income in excess of dividends = \( \$6{,}600 - \$3{,}600 = \$3{,}000 \) so only the cash dividend remains in the cash-flow story.

**Key insight:** When acquisition is mid-year, **both** equity income and excess depreciation are **time-weighted**. Dividends reduce the investment only if declared while the investor holds the shares. Cost is analyzed as BV share + FV adjustments + goodwill — only the depreciable/amortizable FV layer drives ongoing “extra” expense.

### Q4 — MC — Method selection and equity-method mechanics
**LO:** LO 14-5  
**Concept:** Classification / method choice — when equity method applies; treatment of dividends and fair value under equity method  

**Question 1:**  
An investor acquires **25%** of an investee’s voting common stock, obtains board representation, and can participate in policy decisions. The investor should generally account for the investment using:

- A) FV-NI, because equity securities are always measured at fair value through net income  
- B) The equity method, because the investor has significant influence  
- C) Consolidation, because 25% ownership is a controlling interest  
- D) Held-to-maturity amortized cost, because the investor intends to hold indefinitely  

**Answer:** **B.** Significant influence (presumed at 20%–50% voting interest and supported here by board representation/policy participation) requires the **equity method** under ASC 323. FV-NI (A) applies when influence is **insignificant**. Consolidation (C) requires **control** (typically >50%). HTM (D) is a **debt** classification, not equity.

---

**Question 2:**  
Under the equity method, which statement is correct?

- A) Dividends received are recorded as Dividend Revenue and increase net income  
- B) The investor’s share of investee net income increases the Investment account; dividends decrease the Investment account  
- C) The Investment account is adjusted to fair value each reporting date through OCI  
- D) Goodwill embedded in the equity-method investment cost is amortized straight-line over 10 years  

**Answer:** **B.** Equity method: Dr Investment / Cr Investment Income for share of NI; Dr Cash / Cr Investment for dividends. (A) describes **FV-NI**. (C) is wrong — FV changes are **ignored** (not OCI). (D) is wrong — **goodwill is not amortized** under the equity method (only depreciable/amortizable basis differences are).
