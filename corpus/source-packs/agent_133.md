# Agent 133 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-10

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Explain the accounting for transfers of investments  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** purchase debt securities classified AFS (or HTM/TS); equity securities measured at FV-NI before significant influence
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** after AFS → HTM transfer, amortize transfer-date AOCI unrealized gain/loss as a yield adjustment over remaining life (offsetting premium/discount amortization); roll-forward of HTM carrying value and AOCI balance
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** AFS fair-value adjustment through OCI; post-transfer interest (coupon + discount/premium amort + AOCI yield amort); TS fair-value adjustment through NI
- **Transfer JEs at fair value:** AFS → HTM (no NI); HTM → AFS (FV through OCI, no NI); AFS → TS (holding G/L to NI); TS → other (NI already current); FV-NI equity → equity method (FV = new cost basis)
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement JE:** cash maturity of HTM at face after schedule runs off; sale of TS after transfer
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** describe circumstances of transfer; transfers among debt categories should be rare (especially into/out of HTM and TS); no automatic AFS→TS solely because maturity is within one year
- **Number-variant twin:** same AFS→HTM life cycle with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — AFS purchase, FV adjustment, transfer to HTM, AOCI yield amortization schedule, maturity
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Initial AFS recognition; period-end OCI fair-value adjustment; AFS→HTM transfer at fair value with no NI effect; subsequent measurement schedule amortizing AOCI unrealized loss as yield adjustment (offsetting discount amortization); interest JEs; maturity settlement; transfer disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Holdings Corp.** is a calendar-year investor.

**Facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Cedarline purchases **$200,000** face of **Redwood Utilities** 5% bonds for cash of **$200,000** (at par). Interest is paid annually each **December 31**. Bonds mature **December 31, Year 5**. Management classifies the bonds as **available-for-sale (AFS)**.
2. On **December 31, Year 1**, the fair value of the Redwood bonds is **$188,000**. Cedarline records the year-end fair-value adjustment. (Amortized cost remains **$200,000** because the bonds were purchased at par.)
3. On **January 1, Year 2**, management documents a change in intent and ability and **transfers** the Redwood bonds from **AFS to held-to-maturity (HTM)**. Fair value on the transfer date is still **$188,000**. The transfer-date unrealized holding loss in AOCI is **$12,000**. Remaining life to maturity is **4 years**. Cedarline uses **straight-line** amortization of the transfer-date discount and of the AOCI unrealized loss as a yield adjustment.
4. Cedarline holds the bonds to maturity and collects face on **December 31, Year 5**.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for the AFS purchase.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** cash interest JE and the **period-end fair-value adjusting JE** (AFS through OCI).  
c. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 transfer JE(s)** from AFS to HTM. State the effect on **net income** and on **OCI** on the transfer date.  
d. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 2–5 showing: beginning HTM carrying value; cash coupon; discount amortization; AOCI amortization; net interest revenue; ending HTM carrying value; ending AOCI unrealized loss balance.  
e. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2 period-end interest-related JEs** (cash coupon, discount amortization, AOCI yield amortization).  
f. Prepare the **December 31, Year 5 maturity (settlement) JE** (after recording Year 5 interest entries, which bring carrying value to face).  
g. **Classification / disclosure:** What note disclosure is required regarding the transfer? Why are transfers into/out of HTM expected to be rare?

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition (AFS at par)**

|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS—Redwood Bonds | 200,000 | |
| Cash | | 200,000 |
| *Purchase $200,000 face 5% Redwood bonds at par; classify AFS* | | |

**Check:** Dr 200,000 = Cr 200,000. **Balanced.**

**b. December 31, Year 1 — interest and AFS fair-value adjustment**

Cash coupon = $200,000 × 5% = **$10,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 10,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 10,000 |
| *Collect annual coupon on Redwood bonds* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,000. **Balanced.**

Unrealized holding loss = $200,000 amortized cost − $188,000 FV = **$12,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | 12,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | | 12,000 |
| *Mark AFS portfolio to FV; loss to OCI (not NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr 12,000. **Balanced.**

Carrying amount on BS = $200,000 − $12,000 FVA = **$188,000**. AOCI includes unrealized loss of **$12,000**.

**c. January 1, Year 2 — transfer AFS → HTM at fair value (no NI)**

New amortized-cost basis for HTM = fair value on transfer date = **$188,000**.  
Transfer-date discount to face = $200,000 − $188,000 = **$12,000**.  
AOCI unrealized loss of **$12,000** remains in equity and will be amortized as a yield adjustment (does **not** hit NI on transfer date).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in HTM—Redwood Bonds | 188,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 12,000 | |
| Investment in AFS—Redwood Bonds | | 200,000 |
| *Transfer Redwood bonds AFS→HTM at FV; clear AFS and FVA; HTM new cost = $188,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr 188,000 + 12,000 = Cr 200,000. **Balanced.**

**Transfer-date income effects:**
- **Net income:** **no effect** (ASC 320-10-35-10d).
- **OCI:** no new OCI entry on the transfer date; the existing **$12,000** unrealized loss remains in AOCI to be amortized prospectively as a yield adjustment.

**d. Subsequent measurement schedule (Years 2–5) — emphasis**

Annual cash coupon = **$10,000**.  
Straight-line discount amortization = $12,000 ÷ 4 = **$3,000**/year (increases interest revenue; increases HTM CV).  
Straight-line AOCI unrealized-loss amortization = $12,000 ÷ 4 = **$3,000**/year (reduces interest revenue; clears AOCI).  
**Net interest revenue each year** = $10,000 + $3,000 − $3,000 = **$10,000** (coupon only; discount and AOCI amorts offset).

| 5 | 197,000 | 10,000 | 3,000 | 3,000 | 10,000 | 200,000 | 0 |
| **Total** | | **40,000** | **12,000** | **12,000** | **40,000** | | |

Y2 end CV: 188,000 + 3,000 = **191,000**. AOCI: 12,000 − 3,000 = **9,000**.  
Y5 end CV: 197,000 + 3,000 = **200,000** (face). AOCI fully amortized to **0**.  
Total discount amort $12,000 = total AOCI amort $12,000 (offsetting yield effects).

|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 200,000 | |

(AOCI already amortized to zero by Year 5 AOCI amort entry.)

**g. Classification / presentation / disclosure**

- Notes should **describe the circumstances** leading to the transfer of a security to a different classification.
- Transfers **from HTM** should be **rare** except for the limited changes in circumstances in ASC 320-10-25-6 (e.g., significant deterioration in issuer creditworthiness, major business combination/disposition necessitating sale/transfer to maintain interest-rate or credit-risk policy, statutory/regulatory changes, etc.). An unjustified sale/transfer of HTM can **taint** intent to hold other HTM securities to maturity.
- Transfers **into or from trading** should also be **rare**.
- AFS securities are **not** automatically transferred to trading merely because maturity is within one year or management intends to sell within one year.

**Key insight:** AFS→HTM is measured at **fair value on the transfer date** with **no NI hit**. The transfer-date unrealized G/L stays in **AOCI** and is **amortized as a yield adjustment** over remaining life, offsetting amortization of the transfer-date premium/discount so net interest often equals the contractual coupon when the bond was originally at par.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angles — HTM→AFS, AFS→TS, FV-NI→equity method, TS subsequent FV and sale
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Transfer HTM→AFS (FV through OCI, no NI); transfer AFS→TS (holding loss to NI); transfer equity FV-NI→equity method (FV becomes new cost basis); subsequent TS fair-value adjustment and disposal  
**Scenario:**  
**Lakeshore Partners Inc.** is a calendar-year company. Consider three independent transfer situations.

**Situation A — Debt HTM → AFS**  
On **January 1, Year 3**, Lakeshore holds **$100,000** face **Summit Rail** 6% bonds classified **HTM** at amortized cost of **$100,000** (purchased at par in a prior year; still at par). Fair value on January 1, Year 3 is **$103,500**. Management transfers the bonds to **AFS** (assume the transfer is supportable and does not taint other HTM holdings). On **December 31, Year 3**, fair value is **$102,000**. Annual coupon **$6,000** is received December 31, Year 3.

**Situation B — Debt AFS → TS (Demo 14-10 style)**  
Lakeshore holds **Quarry Brands** bonds with amortized cost **$40,000**, classified **AFS**. On **March 1**, fair value is **$36,500**. Unrealized loss of **$3,500** is already recognized in OCI with FVA—AFS credit balance **$3,500**. Management transfers the bonds to **trading securities (TS)** on March 1. On **December 31**, fair value of the TS is **$37,200**. On **February 15 of the next year**, Lakeshore sells the TS for cash of **$37,800**. (FVA is adjusted only at year-end / transfer / sale as indicated.)

**Situation C — Equity FV-NI → equity method**  
Lakeshore owns **2,400** of the **10,000** outstanding voting shares of **Harbor Tech Inc.**, originally purchased for **$18** per share and measured at **FV-NI**. On **January 1**, Lakeshore obtains significant influence (board representation). Fair value on January 1 is **$28** per share. The investment was adjusted to fair value on December 31 of the prior year. (If equity method had been used from acquisition, the investment balance would be **$52,000**—not used for the transfer entry under the FV-becomes-new-cost approach.)

**Required:**  
a. **Situation A:** Prepare the Jan 1, Year 3 HTM→AFS transfer JE. Prepare Dec 31, Year 3 cash interest JE and AFS fair-value adjusting JE. State NI vs OCI effects on the transfer date.  
b. **Situation B:** Prepare the March 1 AFS→TS transfer entry(ies) that reclassify the unrealized loss from AOCI into NI and establish TS at fair value. Prepare the Dec 31 TS fair-value adjusting JE. Prepare the Feb 15 sale JE (including clearing any FVA).  
c. **Situation C:** Prepare the January 1 transfer JE from FV-NI measurement to the **equity method**. What is the new cost basis?  
d. **Classification:** Complete the impact table (NI / OCI on transfer date; subsequent accounting) for: (1) AFS→HTM, (2) HTM→AFS, (3) any debt transfer **to** TS, (4) equity transfer **to** equity method.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Situation A — HTM → AFS**

*January 1, Year 3 — transfer at fair value through OCI (no NI)*

|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in AFS—Summit Rail Bonds | 100,000 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—AFS | 3,500 | |
| Investment in HTM—Summit Rail Bonds | | 100,000 |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 3,500 |
| *Transfer HTM→AFS; mark to FV $103,500 through OCI* | | |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 + 3,500 = Cr 100,000 + 3,500. **Balanced.**  
(Alternatively: Dr AFS 103,500 / Cr HTM 100,000 / Cr Unrealized G/L—OCI 3,500 if a single net investment account is used without a separate FVA; either presentation is acceptable if BS carrying amount = **$103,500** and AOCI credit = **$3,500**.)

**Transfer-date effects:** **No effect on NI**. Carrying value adjusted to FV through **OCI** (**$3,500** unrealized gain).

*December 31, Year 3 — cash coupon*

| Cash | 6,000 | |
| Interest Revenue | | 6,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 6,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 3 — AFS FVA (subsequent measurement as AFS)*

Required FVA balance = FV $102,000 − amortized cost $100,000 = **$2,000** debit.  
Existing FVA = **$3,500** debit. Decrease FVA by **$1,500**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 3,500 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—OCI | | 3,500 |
| *Reclassify unrealized holding loss from AOCI into net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,500 = Cr 3,500. **Balanced.**  
**NI effect on transfer date:** **$3,500 unrealized loss** recognized in earnings. New TS cost basis = **$36,500**.

*December 31 — TS fair-value adjustment through NI*

Required FVA—TS = $37,200 − $36,500 = **$700** debit.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

*February 15 next year — sale of TS*

Cash proceeds **$37,800**. Carrying amount before sale (after clearing FVA into the computation of gain/loss): amortized/cost basis $36,500 + FVA $700 = **$37,200**. Gain = 37,800 − 37,200 = **$600**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 37,800 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—TS | | 700 |
| Investment in TS—Quarry Bonds | | 36,500 |
| Gain on Sale of Investment | | 600 |
| *Sell TS; clear investment and FVA; recognize gain* | | |

**Check:** Dr 37,800 = Cr 700 + 36,500 + 600. **Balanced.**

**c. Situation C — FV-NI equity → equity method**

Fair value / new cost basis = 2,400 shares × $28 = **$67,200**.  
(Prior FVA already embedded so carrying amount equals FV.)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Harbor Tech—Equity Method | 67,200 | |
| Investment in Harbor Tech—FV-NI | | 67,200 |
| *Transfer to equity method; FV becomes new cost basis* | | |

**Check:** Dr 67,200 = Cr 67,200. **Balanced.**

**New cost basis** = **$67,200** (fair value at transfer). Prospective equity-method accounting applies (investor’s share of investee earnings, dividends as reduction of investment, etc.). No retrospective restatement of prior periods. (Any unrealized G/L already in NI under FV-NI stays in NI; ASC 323: unrealized G/L existing as of transfer is recognized in NI—already done via prior FV-NI marking.)

**d. Classification / impact table**

| Transfer type | Impact on NI at transfer | Impact on OCI at transfer | After transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| (1) Debt AFS → HTM | None | Existing AOCI G/L remains; amortized to interest as yield adjustment | HTM rules (amortized cost); amortize AOCI |
| (2) Debt HTM → AFS | None | Adjust carrying amount to FV through OCI | AFS rules (FV through OCI) |
| (3) Debt **to** TS (from AFS or HTM) | Recognize any unrecognized holding G/L in NI | Reverse related AOCI if coming from AFS | TS rules (FV through NI) |
| (4) Equity FV-NI → equity method | Unrealized G/L already (or then) in NI; FV = new cost | N/A (equity FV-NI G/L were in NI) | Equity method prospectively |

**Key insight:** Transfer accounting always starts from an **updated carrying amount through the transfer date**, then remeasures per the **new category**. Debt transfers **to TS** recycle holding G/L into **NI**; AFS↔HTM transfers do **not** affect NI on the transfer date; equity-method adoption uses **FV as the new cost basis**.

### Q4 — MC — Transfer impact classification
**LO:** LO 14-10  
**Concept:** Classification — which transfer recognizes holding gains/losses in net income on the transfer date  

**Question:**  
Which of the following transfers of investments requires recognition of any unrealized holding gain or loss in **net income** on the transfer date (rather than only through OCI or amortization of existing AOCI)?

- A) Transfer of a debt security from **AFS to HTM**
- B) Transfer of a debt security from **HTM to AFS**
- C) Transfer of a debt security from **AFS to trading (TS)**
- D) Transfer of a debt security from **trading (TS) to AFS**, where fair-value changes were already recognized in NI each period before the transfer

**Answer:** **C.**  
Under ASC 320-10-35-10b, a debt transfer **to trading** recognizes any unrecognized holding gain or loss in **net income** on the transfer date; thereafter TS accounting applies.  
A is wrong: AFS→HTM has **no NI** effect; AOCI is amortized as a yield adjustment.  
B is wrong: HTM→AFS adjusts to FV through **OCI**, not NI.  
D is wrong: transfers **from** TS do not create a new NI hit on transfer because NI was **already** adjusted for unrealized G/L while the security was trading; the security is then accounted for under the new category’s rules.
