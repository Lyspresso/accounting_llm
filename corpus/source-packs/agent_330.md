# Agent 330 — CORE DEMO — LO 11-3

**Chapter:** 11  
**LO title:** Account for acquisition of property, plant, and equipment through debt and equity issuances  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition (debt):** PPE acquired with a note is measured at the **more clearly evident** of (1) **fair value of the fixed asset** or (2) **present value** of future debt payments discounted at the **prevailing (market) rate**
- **Noninterest-bearing / below-market note:** Face − initial measurement = **Discount on Note Payable** (contra liability); equipment is **not** recorded at face; net liability = face − unamortized discount
- **Cash down payment + note:** Equipment cost = cash paid + initial PV (or FV) of the note
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** **effective interest method** — Interest expense = beginning net carrying amount × market rate; cash interest on 0% notes = **$0**; discount amortization = interest expense; net CV rolls up to face at maturity
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Debit Interest Expense, credit Discount on Note Payable at each **reporting date**; when note anniversary ≠ fiscal year-end, **allocate** note-year interest across calendar periods (time fraction)
- **Maturity / settlement JE:** Pay face cash; remove Note Payable (discount fully amortized so net CV = face)
- **Initial recognition (equity):** PPE measured at the more clearly evident of **FV of securities issued** (e.g., publicly traded stock) or **FV of the fixed asset**; credit Common Stock (par × shares) and APIC (excess)
- **Imputed rate (FV known, market rate unknown):** Record equipment at known FV; discount = face − FV; impute effective rate from FV, face, and term for amortization

### Q1 — CORE — Cash + noninterest note (asset FV unknown): initial JE, schedule, period-end interest, maturity + equity alternative

**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of specialized equipment at cash + PV of noninterest-bearing note; effective-interest subsequent measurement schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs** for discount amortization; maturity settlement; equity-issuance alternative  
**Scenario:**  
**Cedarline Precision Works Inc.** (calendar year-end) acquires **specialized CNC equipment** on **January 1, Year 1**. The equipment is custom-configured for Cedarline’s production line and has **no reliable stand-alone fair value**. Payment terms:

- Cash down payment: **$28,000** on January 1, Year 1  
- **Noninterest-bearing** note with face **$133,100**, due in a **single lump sum on December 31, Year 2** (two years; no stated interest)  
- Prevailing market interest rate for liabilities of similar risk and duration: **10%**  
- Cedarline amortizes Discount on Note Payable using the **effective interest method**  
- **Ignore depreciation** of the equipment (focus on acquisition cost and the note)

**Required:**  
(a) Compute the **present value** of the note, the **discount**, and the **initial cost of the equipment**. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1** acquisition journal entry.  
(b) Prepare a **two-year effective-interest amortization schedule** for the note (beginning carrying amount, cash interest, interest expense, discount amortization, ending carrying amount).  
(c) Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** **period-end adjusting entries** for interest (and, on Dec. 31, Year 2, the **maturity settlement** of the note).  
(d) Prove that total interest expense over the two years equals the original discount, and that equipment cost + total interest = cash outflows related to the acquisition.  
(e) **Alternative:** Assume instead that on January 1, Year 1, Cedarline issues **2,800 shares** of its own **$5 par** common stock (publicly traded at **$42** per share) **plus** the **$28,000** cash down payment for the same equipment (no note). Record the purchase.

**Answer key:**

**(a) PV, discount, equipment cost, and initial recognition**

\[
PV = \frac{\$133{,}100}{(1.10)^{2}} = \$133{,}100 \times \frac{1}{1.21} = \mathbf{\$110{,}000}
\]

Discount on Note Payable = \(133{,}100 - 110{,}000 =\) **$23,100**  
Equipment cost = cash + PV of note = \(28{,}000 + 110{,}000 =\) **$138,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 138,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 23,100 | |
| Cash | | 28,000 |
| Note Payable | | 133,100 |
| *Acquire specialized equipment: cash down payment + PV of 2-year noninterest-bearing note at 10% market rate* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(138{,}000 + 23{,}100 = 161{,}100\); Cr \(28{,}000 + 133{,}100 = 161{,}100\). **Balanced.**  
Net note liability at acquisition = \(133{,}100 - 23{,}100 =\) **$110,000**.

**(b) Subsequent measurement schedule (effective interest, market 10%; stated cash interest = $0)**

Cash interest each period = Face × 0% = **$0**  
Interest expense = Beginning net CA × **10%**  
Discount amortization = Interest expense (cash = 0)

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest expense (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount (net NP) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $110,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $11,000 | $11,000 | 121,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 12,100 | 12,100 | 133,100 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$23,100** | **$23,100** | |

**Schedule checks:**  
- Year 1: \(110{,}000 \times 0.10 = 11{,}000\); end CA \(110{,}000 + 11{,}000 = 121{,}000\)  
- Year 2: \(121{,}000 \times 0.10 = 12{,}100\); end CA \(121{,}000 + 12{,}100 = 133{,}100\) = face  
- Total amortization = original discount **$23,100**

**(c) Period-end adjusting JEs and maturity settlement (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end interest / discount amortization*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 11,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 11,000 |
| *Accrue effective interest; amortize discount on noninterest-bearing note* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,000 = Cr 11,000. **Balanced.**  
Unamortized discount = \(23{,}100 - 11{,}000 =\) **$12,100**; net NP = \(133{,}100 - 12{,}100 =\) **$121,000**.

*December 31, Year 2 — period-end interest (final year)*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 12,100 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 12,100 |
| *Final year effective interest; discount fully amortized* | | |

**Check:** Dr 12,100 = Cr 12,100. **Balanced.** Unamortized discount = **$0**; net NP = face **$133,100**.

*December 31, Year 2 — maturity / settlement of note*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 133,100 | |
| Cash | | 133,100 |
| *Pay face amount of noninterest-bearing note at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 133,100 = Cr 133,100. **Balanced.**

**(d) Economic proof**

Total interest expense = \(11{,}000 + 12{,}100 =\) **$23,100** = original discount.  
Cash outflows: down payment \(28{,}000\) + maturity \(133{,}100 =\) **$161,100**.  
Equipment cost \(138{,}000\) + total interest \(23{,}100 =\) **$161,100**. (Cash paid finances equipment + pure interest cost of deferred payment.)

**(e) Equity alternative — publicly traded stock + cash**

Measurement of stock = \(2{,}800 \times \$42 =\) **$117,600** (FV of securities more clearly evident).  
Equipment cost = \(28{,}000 + 117{,}600 =\) **$145,600**.  
Common Stock (par) = \(2{,}800 \times \$5 =\) **$14,000**.  
APIC = \(117{,}600 - 14{,}000 =\) **$103,600**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 145,600 | |
| Cash | | 28,000 |
| Common Stock | | 14,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 103,600 |
| *Acquire equipment for cash + 2,800 shares of $5 par stock trading at $42* | | |

**Check:** Dr 145,600 = Cr \(28{,}000 + 14{,}000 + 103{,}600 = 145{,}600\). **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** When asset FV is unknown, the note’s **market-rate PV** is the equipment’s initial measurement (plus any cash). Face is **not** cost. The entire discount is **interest**, recognized over the note’s life via **period-end** effective-interest adjusting entries until settlement at face. Equity financing uses the **more clearly evident** FV (usually market price of publicly traded stock).

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cash + noninterest note: initial JE, schedule, period-end JEs, maturity + equity alt

**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — cash + PV of noninterest note (asset FV unknown); effective-interest schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs**; maturity settlement; equity alternative  
**Scenario:**  
**Northglass Systems LLC** (calendar year-end) acquires **specialized packaging equipment** on **January 1, Year 1**. Fair value of the equipment is **not** determinable. Terms:

- Cash down payment: **$45,000**  
- Noninterest-bearing note face **$96,800**, due in a lump sum on **December 31, Year 2**  
- Market rate for similar risk/duration: **10%**  
- Effective interest method for discount amortization  
- Ignore depreciation  

**Required:**  
(a) Compute PV, discount, and equipment cost; record **January 1, Year 1** acquisition.  
(b) Prepare the **two-year effective-interest amortization schedule**.  
(c) Record **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** period-end interest entries and the **Year 2 maturity settlement**.  
(d) Prove total interest = original discount.  
(e) **Alternative:** Instead of the note, Northglass issues **1,200 shares** of **$10 par** common stock trading at **$55** per share **plus** the **$45,000** cash. Record the purchase.

\[
PV = \frac{\$96{,}800}{(1.10)^{2}} = \$96{,}800 \times \frac{1}{1.21} = \mathbf{\$80{,}000}
\]

Discount = \(96{,}800 - 80{,}000 =\) **$16,800**

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Mid-year noninterest note: period-end allocation emphasis + FV-known / impute-rate variant

**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** **Period-end adjusting JE emphasis** — note-year effective-interest schedule vs fiscal calendar allocation when acquisition is mid-year; maturity settlement; contrast with **asset FV known / market rate unknown** (impute rate)  
**Scenario:**  
**Emberline Machining Corp.** has a **December 31** fiscal year-end.

**Part A — FV of asset unknown; mid-year note (period-end allocation emphasis)**  
On **April 1, Year 1**, Emberline acquires a **specialized finishing machine**. Fair value is **not** determinable. Emberline issues a **two-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$72,600**, due in a single payment on **March 31, Year 3**. The market rate for similar risk and duration is **10%**. No cash is paid at acquisition. Effective interest method. Ignore depreciation.

**Part B — FV of asset known; market rate unknown**  
Independently, on **January 1, Year 1**, Emberline acquires a **standard industrial lathe** with a **clearly determinable fair value of $54,000** by issuing a **two-year, noninterest-bearing** note with face **$65,340** due December 31, Year 2. The prevailing market rate for this type of note is **unknown**. Ignore depreciation.

**Required:**  
(a) **Part A:** Compute PV, discount, and initial equipment cost. Record the **April 1, Year 1** acquisition entry.  
(b) **Part A:** Prepare the **note-year** (April 1–March 31) effective-interest amortization schedule.  
(c) **Part A (emphasis):** Allocate each note-year’s interest to **fiscal reporting periods** (9 months to Dec. 31 / 3 months to Mar. 31). Record interest accruals on **December 31, Year 1**, **March 31, Year 2**, **December 31, Year 2**, and **March 31, Year 3**. Record the **March 31, Year 3** maturity settlement. Verify total interest equals the original discount.  
(d) **Part B:** Compute the **imputed interest rate**, discount, and initial entry on January 1, Year 1. Prepare the **two-year amortization schedule** and the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** period-end interest entries and Year 2 settlement.  
(e) Briefly contrast measurement when **market rate is known / FV unknown** vs **FV known / market rate unknown**.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Part A — Initial recognition (April 1, Year 1)**

\[
PV = \frac{\$72{,}600}{(1.10)^{2}} = \$72{,}600 \times \frac{1}{1.21} = \mathbf{\$60{,}000}
\]

Discount = \(72{,}600 - 60{,}000 =\) **$12,600**  
Equipment cost = **$60,000** (PV only; no cash down)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 60,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 12,600 | |
| Note Payable | | 72,600 |
| *Acquire machine at PV of 2-year noninterest-bearing note at 10%* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(60{,}000 + 12{,}600 = 72{,}600\) = Cr 72,600. **Balanced.**  
Net NP = **$60,000**.

**(b) Part A — Note-year subsequent measurement schedule**

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest expense (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $60,000 |
| Mar. 31, Year 2 | $0 | $6,000 | $6,000 | 66,000 |
| Mar. 31, Year 3 | 0 | 6,600 | 6,600 | 72,600 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$12,600** | **$12,600** | |

**Checks:** \(60{,}000 \times 0.10 = 6{,}000\); \(66{,}000 \times 0.10 = 6{,}600\); totals = discount.

**(c) Part A — Fiscal period-end allocation and JEs (emphasis)**

Note-year interest follows **April 1–March 31** periods. With a **December 31** year-end, allocate **9/12** of each note-year amount to the Dec. 31 report and **3/12** to the following Mar. 31.

| Note-year interest (period ending) | Amount | Dec. 31 (9/12) | Mar. 31 next (3/12) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar. 31, Year 2 | $6,000 | $4,500 | $1,500 |
| Mar. 31, Year 3 | 6,600 | 4,950 | 1,650 |
| **Totals** | **$12,600** | | |

**Reporting-period interest expense:**

| Reporting date | Interest expense | Build-up |
|---|---:|---|
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | **$4,500** | \(9/12 \times 6{,}000\) |
| Mar. 31, Year 2 | **$1,500** | \(3/12 \times 6{,}000\) |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | **$4,950** | \(9/12 \times 6{,}600\) |
| Mar. 31, Year 3 | **$1,650** | \(3/12 \times 6{,}600\) |
| **Total** | **$12,600** | equals original discount |

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 4,500 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 4,500 |
| *9 months: $60,000 × 10% × 9/12 = $4,500* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,500 = Cr 4,500. **Balanced.** Net CV at Dec. 31, Y1 = \(60{,}000 + 4{,}500 =\) **$64,500**.

*March 31, Year 2 — complete note-year 1 interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 1,500 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 1,500 |
| *3 months: $60,000 × 10% × 3/12 = $1,500* | | |

*March 31, Year 3 — final interest*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

**(d) Part B — FV known; impute rate from FV and face**

Because the note is noninterest-bearing and the market rate is **unknown**, present value is **inferred from the fair value of the lathe** ($54,000).  
Discount = \(65{,}340 - 54{,}000 =\) **$11,340**.

Imputed effective rate \(r\) solves \(54{,}000 \times (1+r)^{2} = 65{,}340\):

\[
(1+r)^{2} = \frac{65{,}340}{54{,}000} = 1.21 \implies 1+r = 1.10 \implies r = \mathbf{10\%}
\]

|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 54,000 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | 11,340 | |
| Note Payable | | 65,340 |
| *Record lathe at clearly evident FV; discount = face − FV; impute 10%* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(54{,}000 + 11{,}340 = 65{,}340\) = Cr 65,340. **Balanced.** Net NP = **$54,000**.

*Amortization schedule (imputed 10%)*

| Date | Cash interest (0%) | Interest expense (10% × beg. CV) | Discount amortization | Ending carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan. 1, Year 1 | — | — | — | $54,000 |
| Dec. 31, Year 1 | $0 | $5,400 | $5,400 | 59,400 |
| Dec. 31, Year 2 | 0 | 5,940 | 5,940 | 65,340 |
| **Totals** | **$0** | **$11,340** | **$11,340** | |

*December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JE*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,400 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 5,400 |
| *Effective interest at imputed 10%* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,400 = Cr 5,400. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2 — interest and settlement*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest Expense | 5,940 | |
| Discount on Note Payable | | 5,940 |
| *Final year imputed effective interest* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,940 = Cr 5,940. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Note Payable | 65,340 | |
| Cash | | 65,340 |
| *Pay face at maturity* | | |

**Check:** Dr 65,340 = Cr 65,340. **Balanced.**  
Total interest \(5{,}400 + 5{,}940 =\) **$11,340** = discount ✓

**(e) Measurement contrast**

| Situation | Initial PPE measurement | Discount / rate |
|---|---|---|
| Market rate known; asset FV **not** determinable | **PV of note** at market rate (+ cash if any) | Discount = face − PV; amortize at **given market rate** |
| Asset FV **known**; market rate unknown | **Fair value of asset** (+ cash if any) | Discount = face − FV; **impute** effective rate from FV, face, and term |
| Publicly traded equity issued | **FV of securities** (or FV of asset if stock FV not clear) | No discount on equity; CS at par + APIC |

**Key insight (emphasis):** Interest on a deferred-payment PPE acquisition is recognized at **each reporting date**, not only on the note anniversary. When the fiscal year-end cuts a note-year, **time-allocate** effective interest so Interest Expense and the net liability are correct on the balance sheet date. Whether you start from market rate or from asset FV, equipment is **never** recorded at the face of a noninterest-bearing note.

### Q4 — MC — Measurement basis for debt vs equity PPE acquisitions

**LO:** LO 11-3  
**Concept:** Classification / method choice — more clearly evident measurement base for noninterest-bearing debt vs publicly traded equity  
**Question 1:**  
On January 1, a company acquires specialized equipment with **no reliable fair value** by issuing a **two-year noninterest-bearing** note with face $50,000. The prevailing market rate for similar notes is **8%**. At what amount should the company initially measure the **equipment** (ignore any cash paid)?

- A) $50,000 (face of the note)  
- B) Present value of $50,000 discounted at 8% for two years  
- C) Zero until the note is paid  
- D) Face of the note plus two years of 8% interest  

**Answer:** **B.** When asset FV is not determinable, PPE is recorded at the **PV of the debt payments at the market rate**. Face is the maturity amount of the liability, not acquisition cost; the difference is Discount on Note Payable (future interest).  

**Question 2:**  
A company issues **1,000 shares** of its **$1 par** common stock, which is **publicly traded at $25** per share, to acquire used equipment that is **not** regularly sold in an active market (no verifiable asset FV). At what amount is **equipment** recorded?

- A) $1,000 (par × shares)  
- B) $25,000 (market price × shares)  
- C) Seller’s book value of the equipment  
- D) $24,000 (market minus par)  

**Answer:** **B.** When equity securities are publicly traded, their **fair value is clearly evident**, so PPE is measured at market price × shares issued. Par affects only the split between Common Stock and APIC, not the asset’s cost.
