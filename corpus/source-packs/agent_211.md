# Agent 211 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-4

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for changes in estimate as they relate to depreciation  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize equipment at cash-equivalent cost
- **Original SL depreciation:** (cost − residual) ÷ useful life
- **Change in estimate (prospective):** revise useful life and/or residual value; **do not** restate prior periods or adjust beginning retained earnings
- At revision date: carrying amount = cost − accumulated depreciation to date
- **Revised annual depreciation** = (carrying amount − revised residual) ÷ **remaining** useful life
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year depreciation rollforward (original rates, then revised rates)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Depreciation Expense Dr / Accumulated Depreciation Cr after the revision
- **Disposal JE:** update depreciation through disposal date; remove cost and AD; recognize gain or loss = proceeds − carrying amount
- Classification: change in residual value or useful life = **change in accounting estimate** → prospective only (not prior-period adjustment; not retrospective restatement)

### Q1 — CORE — Equipment purchase, SL dep, shortened life + lower residual, schedule, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of equipment; multi-year SL depreciation schedule; prospective change in useful life and residual; period-end JE after revision; disposal with loss  
**Scenario:**  
**RidgeForge Fabrication Co.**, a calendar-year company, acquires production equipment on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cash purchase price of equipment | **$48,000** |
| Original estimated useful life | **10 years** |
| Original residual value | **$3,000** |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line |
| Revision date | **January 1, Year 5** (after four full years of depreciation) |
| Revised total useful life from acquisition | **7 years** (so **3 years** remain at the revision date) |
| Revised residual value | **$1,500** |
| Disposal | **January 1, Year 7**, sold for **$10,200** cash (after Year 5 and Year 6 depreciation have been recorded) |

No impairment indicators arise at any date. Accumulated depreciation is maintained in a contra-asset account.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** journal entry for **initial recognition** of the equipment.  
b. Compute original annual depreciation, accumulated depreciation after Years 1–4, and the **carrying amount on January 1, Year 5** (immediately before the estimate revision).  
c. Prepare a **subsequent-measurement depreciation schedule** for Years 1–6 showing beginning carrying amount, depreciation expense, ending accumulated depreciation, and ending carrying amount (apply the revised estimates beginning Year 5).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 5** period-end adjusting entry for depreciation under the revised estimates.  
e. Record the **January 1, Year 7** disposal entry.  
f. In one or two sentences, explain why Years 1–4 depreciation is **not** restated when useful life and residual are revised.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

*January 1, Year 1 — Purchase equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 48,000 | |
| Cash | | 48,000 |
| *Record equipment at cash-equivalent cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 48,000 = Cr 48,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Pre-revision depreciation and carrying amount**

Original annual depreciation = \((\$48{,}000 - \$3{,}000) / 10 = \$4{,}500\) per year.

Accumulated depreciation, Years 1–4 = \(4 \times \$4{,}500 = \$18{,}000\).

Carrying amount, January 1, Year 5 = \(\$48{,}000 - \$18{,}000 = \$30{,}000\).

**c. Depreciation schedule (prospective revision at start of Year 5)** — *emphasis: subsequent measurement schedule*

At revision: remaining useful life = \(7 - 4 = 3\) years; residual = $1,500.  
Revised annual depreciation (Years 5–7) = \((\$30{,}000 - \$1{,}500) / 3 = \$28{,}500 / 3 = \$9{,}500\).

| Year | Beg. CA | Dep. expense | End. AD | End. CA | Notes |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 48,000 | 4,500 | 4,500 | 43,500 | Original estimate |
| 2 | 43,500 | 4,500 | 9,000 | 39,000 | Original estimate |
| 3 | 39,000 | 4,500 | 13,500 | 34,500 | Original estimate |
| 4 | 34,500 | 4,500 | 18,000 | 30,000 | Original estimate |
| 5 | 30,000 | **9,500** | **27,500** | **20,500** | **Revised life & residual (prospective)** |
| 6 | 20,500 | **9,500** | **37,000** | **11,000** | **Revised estimates** |
| **Total through Y6** | | **37,000** | | | |

**Roll-forward check:** Cost − AD through Y6 = \$48,000 − \$37,000 = **\$11,000** ending CA.  
(If held through Y7: \$11,000 − \$9,500 = \$1,500 residual — check passes.)

**d. Period-end adjusting entry after change in estimate**

*December 31, Year 5 — Depreciation under revised remaining life and residual*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 9,500 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 9,500 |
| *Allocate remaining depreciable base over revised remaining life* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,500 = Cr 9,500. **Balanced.**

**e. Disposal**

Carrying amount on January 1, Year 7 = **\$11,000**.  
Cash proceeds = **\$10,200**.  
Loss on disposal = \$11,000 − \$10,200 = **\$800**.

*January 1, Year 7 — Sell equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 10,200 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 37,000 | |
| Loss on Sale of Equipment | 800 | |
| Equipment | | 48,000 |

**Check:** Dr 10,200 + 37,000 + 800 = 48,000 = Cr. **Balanced.**

**f. Prospective treatment**  
A revision of estimated useful life and/or residual value of a depreciable asset is a **change in accounting estimate**. Prior periods are **not** restated and beginning retained earnings is **not** adjusted; the remaining carrying amount (less revised residual) is depreciated over the **revised remaining** useful life only.

**Key insight:** At **initial recognition**, capitalize equipment at cost. When useful life is later shortened (and residual revised), leave historical depreciation alone and spread the **current carrying amount minus new residual** over the **new remaining life**—then remove cost and AD at disposal to recognize gain or loss.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Twin: longer original life, mid-life revision, disposal with gain
**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial recognition of equipment; SL schedule; prospective useful-life and residual revision; period-end JE; disposal with gain (vs Q1 loss)  
**Scenario:**  
**HarborPeak Logistics Inc.**, a calendar-year company, acquires warehouse equipment on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cash purchase price of equipment | **$75,000** |
| Original estimated useful life | **8 years** |
| Original residual value | **$5,000** |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line |
| Revision date | **January 1, Year 4** (after three full years of depreciation) |
| Revised total useful life from acquisition | **6 years** (so **3 years** remain at the revision date) |
| Revised residual value | **$3,750** |
| Disposal | **January 1, Year 6**, sold for **$20,000** cash (after Year 4 and Year 5 depreciation have been recorded) |

No impairment. Accumulated depreciation is maintained as a contra-asset.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **January 1, Year 1** initial recognition JE.  
b. Compute original annual depreciation, AD after Years 1–3, and carrying amount on **January 1, Year 4**.  
c. Prepare the **subsequent-measurement depreciation schedule** for Years 1–5 (revised estimates from Year 4).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 4** period-end depreciation adjusting entry.  
e. Record the **January 1, Year 6** disposal entry.  
f. State whether prior-year financial statements are restated for this estimate change (yes/no) and why.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition**

*January 1, Year 1 — Purchase equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 75,000 | |
| Cash | | 75,000 |

**Check:** Dr 75,000 = Cr 75,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Pre-revision amounts**

Original annual depreciation = \((\$75{,}000 - \$5{,}000) / 8 = \$70{,}000 / 8 = \$8{,}750\).

AD after Years 1–3 = \(3 \times \$8{,}750 = \$26{,}250\).

Carrying amount, January 1, Year 4 = \(\$75{,}000 - \$26{,}250 = \$48{,}750\).

**c. Depreciation schedule (prospective revision at start of Year 4)**

Remaining life = \(6 - 3 = 3\) years; residual = $3,750.  
Revised annual depreciation = \((\$48{,}750 - \$3{,}750) / 3 = \$45{,}000 / 3 = \$15{,}000\).

| Year | Beg. CA | Dep. expense | End. AD | End. CA | Notes |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 75,000 | 8,750 | 8,750 | 66,250 | Original |
| 2 | 66,250 | 8,750 | 17,500 | 57,500 | Original |
| 3 | 57,500 | 8,750 | 26,250 | 48,750 | Original |
| 4 | 48,750 | **15,000** | **41,250** | **33,750** | **Revised (prospective)** |
| 5 | 33,750 | **15,000** | **56,250** | **18,750** | **Revised** |
| **Total through Y5** | | **56,250** | | | |

**Roll-forward check:** \$75,000 − \$56,250 = **\$18,750**.  
(Through Y6 full life: \$18,750 − \$15,000 = \$3,750 residual — check passes.)

**d. Period-end adjusting entry (Year 4)**

*December 31, Year 4*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | | 15,000 |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Disposal**

Carrying amount, January 1, Year 6 = **\$18,750**.  
Cash proceeds = **\$20,000**.  
Gain on disposal = \$20,000 − \$18,750 = **\$1,250**.

*January 1, Year 6 — Sell equipment*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 20,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Equipment | 56,250 | |
| Equipment | | 75,000 |
| Gain on Sale of Equipment | | 1,250 |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 + 56,250 = 76,250; Cr 75,000 + 1,250 = 76,250. **Balanced.**

**f. Restatement?**  
**No.** A change in useful life and residual is a **change in accounting estimate** accounted for **prospectively** only—current and future periods. Prior-year statements and beginning RE are not adjusted.

**Key insight:** Same mechanics as Q1 with all new amounts: book carrying amount at the revision date, subtract the **new** residual, divide by **remaining** years, and keep the depreciation schedule rolling forward without rewriting history.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Residual-only revision (life unchanged); period-end focus; scrap disposal
**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Prospective change in residual value only (useful life unchanged); subsequent measurement schedule after residual revision; period-end adjusting JE; disposal/scrap settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**SummitCoil Metals LLC** places specialized machinery in service on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cash cost of machinery | **$60,000** |
| Original useful life | **5 years** (unchanged throughout) |
| Original residual value | **$6,000** |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line |
| Revision date | **January 1, Year 3** (after two full years) |
| Revised residual value | **$2,400** (management now expects lower scrap value) |
| Revised useful life | Still **5 years total** (**3 years** remain) |
| Disposal / settlement | **December 31, Year 5**, scrap sold for **$1,800** cash (after Year 5 depreciation is recorded) |

**Required:**  
a. Compute original annual depreciation and the carrying amount on **January 1, Year 3**.  
b. Compute **revised** annual depreciation for Years 3–5.  
c. Prepare a **full five-year subsequent-measurement schedule** (beg. CA, dep. expense, end. AD, end. CA).  
d. Record the **December 31, Year 3** period-end adjusting entry.  
e. Record Year 5 depreciation and the **December 31, Year 5** scrap disposal entry.  
f. Briefly contrast this residual-only change with a change that would require restating prior years (name the type of change that is **not** this case).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Pre-revision**

Original annual depreciation = \((\$60{,}000 - \$6{,}000) / 5 = \$54{,}000 / 5 = \$10{,}800\).

AD after Years 1–2 = \(2 \times \$10{,}800 = \$21{,}600\).

Carrying amount, January 1, Year 3 = \(\$60{,}000 - \$21{,}600 = \$38{,}400\).

**b. Revised depreciation**

Remaining life = \(5 - 2 = 3\) years.  
Revised annual depreciation = \((\$38{,}400 - \$2{,}400) / 3 = \$36{,}000 / 3 = \$12{,}000\).

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule**

| Year | Beg. CA | Dep. expense | End. AD | End. CA | Notes |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 60,000 | 10,800 | 10,800 | 49,200 | Original residual $6,000 |
| 2 | 49,200 | 10,800 | 21,600 | 38,400 | Original residual $6,000 |
| 3 | 38,400 | **12,000** | **33,600** | **26,400** | **Revised residual $2,400** |
| 4 | 26,400 | **12,000** | **45,600** | **14,400** | Revised residual |
| 5 | 14,400 | **12,000** | **57,600** | **2,400** | Ends at revised residual |
| **Total** | | **57,600** | | | |

**Roll-forward check:** \$60,000 − \$57,600 = **\$2,400** = revised residual. ✓  
**Depreciable base check:** Original base \$54,000 depreciated \$21,600 in Y1–Y2; remaining \$32,400 would have been over 3 years under old residual, but new residual is \$2,400 lower so remaining depreciable base rises to \$36,000 → \$12,000/yr.

**d. Period-end adjusting entry (Year 3)**

*December 31, Year 3*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 12,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery | | 12,000 |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr 12,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Year 5 depreciation and scrap disposal**

*December 31, Year 5 — Final year depreciation*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense | 12,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery | | 12,000 |

**Check:** Dr 12,000 = Cr 12,000. **Balanced.**

After Year 5 dep: CA = **\$2,400**; AD = **\$57,600**.  
Scrap proceeds = **\$1,800**.  
Loss = \$2,400 − \$1,800 = **\$600**.

*December 31, Year 5 — Scrap disposal*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 1,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Machinery | 57,600 | |
| Loss on Disposal of Machinery | 600 | |
| Machinery | | 60,000 |

**Check:** Dr 1,800 + 57,600 + 600 = 60,000 = Cr. **Balanced.**

**f. Contrast**  
A residual (or useful-life) revision is a **change in estimate (prospective)**. It is **not** an error correction requiring a **prior-period adjustment** to retained earnings and restatement, nor a voluntary change in accounting principle applied retrospectively. (A material depreciation **error** discovered after statements are issued would use prior-period adjustment—that is LO 12-6, not LO 12-4.)

**Key insight:** Even if **only residual** changes, recompute from **current carrying amount**: subtract the **new** residual and divide by **years left**. The schedule’s last year should land on the revised residual before disposal/scrap settlement.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of useful-life / residual revision
**LO:** LO 12-4  
**Concept:** Classify a revision of depreciable useful life or residual value as a prospective change in estimate  
**Question:**  
On January 1, Year 4, Northline Press revises the estimated useful life of a press from 12 years to 9 years and reduces residual value. The press was purchased on January 1, Year 1 and has been depreciated using straight-line. How should Northline account for this revision under GAAP?

- A) Restate prior years’ financial statements retrospectively and recompute cumulative depreciation as if the new estimates had always been used  
- B) Record a cumulative-effect adjustment in current-year income as a separate line item below income from continuing operations  
- C) Apply the new estimates **prospectively** only: allocate the carrying amount at the revision date (less new residual) over the **remaining** useful life; do not restate prior periods  
- D) Debit Retained Earnings for the cumulative under-depreciation of Years 1–3 as a prior-period adjustment  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Revisions of useful life and residual value are **changes in accounting estimates**. Under GAAP they are applied **prospectively** to the current and future periods. Prior results are not restated (eliminates A), there is no separate cumulative-effect line (eliminates B), and beginning RE is not adjusted as for an error (eliminates D). New annual depreciation = (carrying amount at revision date − revised residual) ÷ remaining life.
