# Agent 32 — CORE DEMO — LO 18-7

**Chapter:** 18  
**LO title:** Describe accounting for net operating loss carryforwards and loss carryback/carryforwards  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- NOL carryforward creates a **deferred tax asset** (NOL × enacted future rate)
- Initial recognition JE: Dr Deferred Tax Asset / Cr Income Tax Expense (**Income Tax Benefit** when credit)
- **80% of taxable income** utilization limit on NOL carryforwards (assignments default)
- Subsequent measurement: reduce DTA when NOL is used; tax payable on residual taxable income (≥20%)
- Valuation allowance when not **more likely than not** (>50%) that DTA will be realized
- NOL **carryback|carryforward**: carry back earliest year first within window → tax refund receivable; excess → DTA
- Income statement presentation of tax benefit against pretax operating loss
- Classification: DTA from NOL is **noncurrent** (ASC 740); disclosure of carryforward amounts/expiration
- Settlement/maturity: full utilization of remaining NOL drives DTA to zero

### Q1 — CORE — Redwood Trail Supply: NOL carryforward initial recognition, utilization schedule, VA, settlement
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of NOL DTA; subsequent utilization (80% limit); period-end valuation allowance; full DTA settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Redwood Trail Supply Co.** has no temporary differences other than those created by tax loss carryforwards. Pretax GAAP income equals taxable income (loss) each year. The enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. Accounting and tax years end December 31. Unless stated otherwise, NOL carryforwards may offset a **maximum of 80%** of taxable income in each future year and may be carried forward indefinitely (no carryback allowed under applicable law for Redwood).

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Notes |
|------|----------------------:|-------|
| 1 (current) | **$(80,000)** | Actual NOL |
| 2 | 20,000 | Management estimate at 12/31/Y1; **actual** TI in Y2 also $20,000 |
| 3 | 15,000 | Estimate at 12/31/Y1; **actual** TI in Y3 is **$50,000** |
| 4 | 50,000 | Estimate at 12/31/Y1; **actual** TI in Y4 is **$40,000** |
| 5 | 60,000 | Estimate at 12/31/Y1 |

At December 31, Year 1, evidence indicates it is **more likely than not** that the full deferred tax asset will be realized.  
At December 31, Year 2, after recording utilization, management concludes it is **more likely than not** that **only 85%** of the **remaining** DTA will be realized (valuation allowance beginning balance = $0).  
At December 31, Year 3 and Year 4, assume remaining DTA (net of any prior allowance) is expected to be fully realized; reverse any remaining valuation allowance when the related DTA is reduced/settled as needed so net DTA reflects expected realization. For simplicity in (e)–(f), assume management revises the allowance each year-end so that VA = **15% of ending gross DTA** after Y2, and when gross DTA reaches zero the VA is fully reversed.

**Required:**  
(a) Measure the **deferred tax asset** at December 31, Year 1 related to the NOL carryforward. Record the **initial recognition** income tax JE.  
(b) Show the **Year 1 income statement** presentation (partial) for the NOL carryforward benefit.  
(c) Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of expected NOL utilization (80% limit) based on Year 1 estimates, and the related expected DTA roll-forward by year. Confirm cumulative utilization equals the $80,000 carryforward.  
(d) Record Year 2 **period-end tax JE** using **actual** taxable income of $20,000 (utilization + taxes payable). Then record the **valuation allowance** adjusting JE at December 31, Year 2.  
(e) Record the Year 3 tax JE for **actual** TI of $50,000 (utilization of NOL and taxes payable). Update the valuation allowance so VA = 15% of ending gross DTA.  
(f) Record the Year 4 tax JE for **actual** TI of $40,000 that **settles** the remaining NOL/DTA. Reverse any remaining valuation allowance. State ending gross DTA and VA.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Initial recognition — December 31, Year 1**

Deferred tax asset = \(80{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$20{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Asset | 20,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 20,000 |

(Credit balance in Income Tax Expense is reported as **Income Tax Benefit**. Dr = Cr = $20,000.)

**(b) Year 1 partial income statement**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(80,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 20,000 |
| Net loss | **$(60,000)** |

**(c) Expected NOL utilization schedule (from 12/31/Y1 estimates) and DTA roll-forward**

| Future year | Est. TI | Max NOL use (80% of TI) | NOL used | Remaining NOL | Ending DTA (×25%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Start Y1 | — | — | — | 80,000 | 20,000 |
| Y2 | 20,000 | 16,000 | 16,000 | 64,000 | 16,000 |
| Y3 | 15,000 | 12,000 | 12,000 | 52,000 | 13,000 |
| Y4 | 50,000 | 40,000 | 40,000 | 12,000 | 3,000 |
| Y5 | 60,000 | 48,000 | 12,000 | 0 | 0 |
| **Total used** | | | **80,000** | | |

Cumulative NOL used = \(16{,}000 + 12{,}000 + 40{,}000 + 12{,}000 = \mathbf{\$80{,}000}\) (matches carryforward).  
Note: utilization cannot exceed remaining NOL even when 80% of TI is larger (Y5).

**(d) Year 2 actual utilization + valuation allowance**

NOL used = \(20{,}000 \times 80\% = \mathbf{\$16{,}000}\).  
Remaining NOL = \(80{,}000 - 16{,}000 = \mathbf{\$64{,}000}\).  
Ending DTA = \(64{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$16{,}000}\).  
DTA reduction = \(20{,}000 - 16{,}000 = \mathbf{\$4{,}000}\).  
Income tax payable = \(20{,}000 \times 20\% \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$1{,}000}\).  
Income tax expense (before VA) = \(4{,}000 + 1{,}000 = \mathbf{\$5{,}000}\).

December 31, Year 2 — record income tax expense:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 5,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 4,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 1,000 |

Valuation allowance needed = \(16{,}000 \times 15\% = \mathbf{\$2{,}400}\) (15% of remaining DTA not expected to be realized).

December 31, Year 2 — adjust valuation allowance:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 2,400 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 2,400 |

Net DTA reported = \(16{,}000 - 2{,}400 = \mathbf{\$13{,}600}\). Combined tax expense Y2 = \(5{,}000 + 2{,}400 = \mathbf{\$7{,}400}\).

**(e) Year 3 actual TI $50,000**

Max NOL use = \(50{,}000 \times 80\% = 40{,}000\); remaining NOL was $64,000 → use **$40,000**.  
Remaining NOL = \(64{,}000 - 40{,}000 = \mathbf{\$24{,}000}\).  
Ending gross DTA = \(24{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).  
DTA reduction = \(16{,}000 - 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\).  
Income tax payable = \((50{,}000 - 40{,}000) \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$2{,}500}\).  
Tax expense (before VA adj.) = \(10{,}000 + 2{,}500 = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 12,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 10,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 2,500 |

Target VA = \(6{,}000 \times 15\% = \mathbf{\$900}\). Beginning VA $2,400 → **reduce VA by $1,500**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 1,500 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 1,500 |

Net DTA = \(6{,}000 - 900 = \mathbf{\$5{,}100}\).

**(f) Year 4 settlement of remaining NOL**

Max use = \(40{,}000 \times 80\% = 32{,}000\); remaining NOL only **$24,000** → use all **$24,000** (full settlement).  
Remaining NOL = **$0**; ending gross DTA = **$0**.  
DTA reduction = **$6,000**.  
Taxable income after NOL = \(40{,}000 - 24{,}000 = 16{,}000\); tax payable = \(16{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$4{,}000}\).  
Tax expense (before VA reverse) = \(6{,}000 + 4{,}000 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 10,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 4,000 |

Reverse remaining VA of **$900**:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | 900 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 900 |

Ending: gross DTA **$0**, VA **$0**, NOL carryforward **fully utilized**.

**Key insight:** At initial recognition, book the **full** DTA (NOL × enacted rate) against income tax benefit when realization is MLTN. In later years, the 80% limit caps NOL used; tax is still paid on residual TI; DTA is reduced as NOL is consumed; a valuation allowance is a **separate** period-end judgment that can reverse as utilization and evidence change. Full use of remaining NOL **settles** the DTA to zero.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Harborline Ceramics: NOL carryforward twin
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** NOL carryforward initial recognition and Year-2 utilization with valuation allowance (number-variant twin of Q1)  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Ceramics LLC** reports a tax operating loss of **$(120,000)** in Year 1. Enacted tax rate is **25%** for all years. No temporary differences other than the NOL carryforward. Carryforward only (no carryback); **80%** utilization limit applies. At December 31, Year 1, realization of the full DTA is **more likely than not**. In Year 2, actual taxable income is **$30,000**. At December 31, Year 2 (after utilization), management concludes it is MLTN that **only 75%** of the remaining DTA will be realized (VA beginning balance $0).

**Required:**  
(a) Compute DTA at 12/31/Y1 and record the initial recognition JE.  
(b) Show Year 1 partial IS (operating loss, tax benefit, net loss).  
(c) Record the Year 2 income tax JE (utilization + payable).  
(d) Record the Year 2 valuation allowance JE. State net DTA reported.

**Answer key:**

**(a)**  
DTA = \(120{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$30{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deferred Tax Asset | 30,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 30,000 |

**(b)**  

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(120,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 30,000 |
| Net loss | **$(90,000)** |

**(c) Year 2**  
NOL used = \(30{,}000 \times 80\% = \mathbf{\$24{,}000}\).  
Remaining NOL = \(120{,}000 - 24{,}000 = \mathbf{\$96{,}000}\).  
Ending DTA = \(96{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$24{,}000}\).  
DTA reduction = \(30{,}000 - 24{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).  
Income tax payable = \(30{,}000 \times 20\% \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$1{,}500}\).  
Income tax expense = \(6{,}000 + 1{,}500 = \mathbf{\$7{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 7,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 1,500 |

**(d) Valuation allowance**  
Unrealizable portion = \(25\% \times 24{,}000 = \mathbf{\$6{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 6,000 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 6,000 |

Net DTA reported = \(24{,}000 - 6{,}000 = \mathbf{\$18{,}000}\).

**Key insight:** Same mechanics as Q1 with scaled numbers—always recompute DTA as **remaining NOL × rate**, tax payable on the **non-offset** slice of TI, and VA as a separate judgment after the gross DTA is updated.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Pinnacle Forge: NOL carryback|carryforward, refund, presentation, settlement
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Two-year carryback (earliest year first) + unlimited carryforward DTA; refund receivable; IS presentation; later full utilization settlement  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinnacle Forge Inc.** began operations in Year 1. There are no temporary differences other than those created by tax loss carryforwards/carrybacks. The enacted tax rate is **25%** in all years. Pinnacle **qualifies for a two-year loss carryback** (starting with the **earliest** year in the window first) and an **indefinite** carryforward subject to the **80%** of taxable income limit.

| Year | Taxable income (loss) | Status |
|------|----------------------:|--------|
| 1 | $40,000 | Outside 2-year carryback window for Y4 loss |
| 2 | 50,000 | Eligible for carryback |
| 3 | 12,000 | Eligible for carryback |
| 4 (current) | **$(90,000)** | NOL year |
| 5 | 40,000 actual | First profitable year after NOL |

At December 31, Year 4, evidence indicates it is **more likely than not** that the full carryforward DTA will be realized. Valuation allowance beginning balance is $0.

**Required:**  
(a) Compute the **tax refund receivable** for Year 4 and the **deferred tax asset** for the residual NOL carryforward.  
(b) Record the **single compound JE** for the Year 4 NOL carryback|carryforward effect.  
(c) Show the Year 4 **partial income statement**.  
(d) **Alternate evidence:** If instead it is MLTN that the **entire** carryforward DTA will **not** be realized, record the VA adjusting entry and show revised IS presentation.  
(e) **Settlement (Year 5):** Using the original assumption in (b) (no VA), actual Year 5 TI is $40,000. Record the tax JE that utilizes the remaining NOL (settle DTA as appropriate). State remaining NOL and DTA after the entry.  
(f) **Classification/disclosure:** Is the DTA from the NOL carryforward classified current or noncurrent? Name two disclosure items typically required for operating loss carryforwards.

**Answer key:**

**(a) Measurement**

Two-year carryback window for Year 4 NOL = **Year 2 and Year 3** (Year 1 is outside the window).  
Carry back to **Year 2 first** (earlier year): \(50{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$12{,}500}\) refund.  
Then Year 3: \(12{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$3{,}000}\) refund.  
**Tax refund receivable** = \(12{,}500 + 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$15{,}500}\).  
Loss absorbed by carryback = \(50{,}000 + 12{,}000 = \mathbf{\$62{,}000}\).  
NOL carryforward remaining = \(90{,}000 - 62{,}000 = \mathbf{\$28{,}000}\).  
**Deferred tax asset** = \(28{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$7{,}000}\).

**(b) December 31, Year 4 — initial recognition (carryback + carryforward)**

Total income tax benefit = \(15{,}500 + 7{,}000 = \mathbf{\$22{,}500}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Refund Receivable | 15,500 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | 7,000 | |
| Income Tax Expense | | 22,500 |

(Dr = Cr = $22,500.)

**(c) Year 4 partial income statement (full realization of DTA)**

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(90,000) |
| Income tax benefit | 22,500 |
| Net loss | **$(67,500)** |

**(d) Full valuation allowance on carryforward DTA**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 7,000 | |
| Valuation Allowance for Deferred Tax Asset | | 7,000 |

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Operating loss before income taxes | $(90,000) |
| Income tax benefit ($22,500 − $7,000) | 15,500 |
| Net loss | **$(74,500)** |

(Only the **refund** benefit remains when the carryforward DTA is fully reserved.)

**(e) Year 5 settlement / utilization of remaining NOL**

Max NOL use = \(40{,}000 \times 80\% = 32{,}000\); remaining NOL = $28,000 → use all **$28,000**.  
Remaining NOL after Y5 = **$0**; DTA reduction = **$7,000** (settles DTA to zero).  
Income after NOL offset = \(40{,}000 - 28{,}000 = 12{,}000\).  
Income tax payable = \(12{,}000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{\$3{,}000}\).  
Income tax expense = \(7{,}000 + 3{,}000 = \mathbf{\$10{,}000}\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Income Tax Expense | 10,000 | |
| Deferred Tax Asset | | 7,000 |
| Income Tax Payable | | 3,000 |

Ending: NOL carryforward **$0**, gross DTA **$0** (carryforward benefit fully settled through utilization).

**(f) Classification and disclosure**

- Deferred tax assets (including those from NOL carryforwards) are classified as **noncurrent** under ASC 740 (all deferred tax assets/liabilities presented as noncurrent).  
- Typical disclosures: (1) **approximate tax effect** of each major source of temporary difference and carryforward; (2) **amount and expiration dates** (if any) of operating loss carryforwards; also valuation allowance beginning/ending and the portion of VA for which subsequently recognized tax benefits would hit continuing operations / OCI / equity as applicable.

**Key insight:** When carryback is allowed, **first** secure the refund (earliest year in the window first) as a **receivable**, then capitalize only the **excess** loss as a DTA. Presentation shows total tax benefit; a VA can wipe out only the uncertain **future** piece, not the already-refundable carryback. Later full use of residual NOL **settles** the DTA.

### Q4 — MC — Classification and presentation of NOL tax effects
**LO:** LO 18-7  
**Concept:** Classification of NOL DTA; income statement labeling of tax benefit  

**Question 1:**  
Under ASC 740 presentation rules used in this course, a deferred tax asset arising solely from a federal NOL carryforward is classified on the classified balance sheet as:

- A) Current asset, because it will reduce taxes payable within one year  
- B) Noncurrent asset  
- C) Contra-equity (similar to AOCI)  
- D) Contingent asset disclosed only, not recognized  

**Answer:** **B.** All deferred tax assets and liabilities are classified as **noncurrent**. Realization timing does not create a current DTA slice under current ASC 740 balance-sheet classification.

**Question 2:**  
In a year a company recognizes a deferred tax asset for an NOL carryforward (no taxes payable; credit to Income Tax Expense), the income statement typically reports that credit as:

- A) Gain on extinguishment of tax liability within other income  
- B) Reduction of pretax operating loss (netted against revenues)  
- C) **Income tax benefit** (often shown as a negative income tax expense line)  
- D) Additional paid-in capital  

**Answer:** **C.** A credit balance in income tax expense is presented as **Income Tax Benefit**, reducing the pretax operating loss to arrive at net loss—not as a pretax gain or equity.
