# Agent 399 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-5

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities following the equity method  
**Critical gap LO:** yes  
**Emphasis angle:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** Dr Investment in Investee Stock; Cr Cash at cost when the investor has **significant influence** (presumptively 20%–50% voting stock; rebuttable) but not control
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** Investment = cost + share of investee NI − share of dividends − excess depreciation/amortization on depreciable FV–BV basis differences (land/goodwill **not** amortized)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** (1) Dr Investment / Cr Investment Income for % × investee NI; (2) Dr Investment Income / Cr Investment for excess depreciation on depreciable basis differences; (3) dividends reduce Investment (**not** dividend revenue); ignore fair-value changes (no FVA unless fair-value option elected or impairment under LO 14-6)
- **Disposal JE (emphasis):** update Investment through the sale date (prorate NI and excess dep for holding period; include dividends while held), then remove updated carrying amount vs cash; difference = **gain or loss in net income**
- **Partial disposal:** allocate carrying amount pro rata to interest sold; continue equity method on remaining interest if significant influence retained
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** typically **noncurrent** long-term investment; gain/loss on sale in **NI** (not OCI); SCF — purchase/sale usually **investing**; under indirect method, subtract (equity-method income − dividends received) from NI in operating section
- **Number-variant twin:** same equity-method engine with all different companies and amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Equity method full life cycle with disposal (emphasis)

**Concept:** Initial recognition; investment rollforward schedule; period-end equity-method adjustments; **disposal JE** after updating carrying amount through sale date  
**Scenario:**  
**Ironvale Capital Inc.** (calendar-year) acquires a long-term equity interest in **Boreal Sensors Corp.** on **January 1, Year 1**. Ironvale can exercise **significant influence** and applies the **equity method** (does **not** elect the fair value option).

| Remaining useful life of that equipment | **8 years**, straight-line, no residual |
| Remainder of Ironvale’s cost over share of fair value of identifiable net assets | Attributable to **goodwill** (not amortized) |
| Boreal net income, Year 1 | **$120,000** |

**Key insight:** Before disposal, bring the Investment account **current through the sale date** (prorated share of earnings and excess dep; dividends during the holding period). The disposal entry removes that **updated carrying amount** against cash; any difference is a **gain or loss in earnings**. Fair value is never the disposal “cost basis” under pure equity method.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial disposal of equity-method investment (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 14-5  
**Concept:** Update equity-method carrying amount through partial sale date; allocate carrying amount pro rata; record **partial disposal JE**; continue equity method on retained interest when significant influence remains; presentation  
**Scenario:**  
**Westbrook Materials Co.** has accounted for its investment in **Quarry Peak Inc.** under the **equity method** since acquisition. Westbrook owns **40%** of Quarry’s voting stock (**40,000 of 100,000** outstanding shares) and has significant influence.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Carrying amount of Investment in Quarry Peak, January 1, Year 3 | **$520,000** |
| Ownership at Jan 1, Year 3 | **40%** |
| Quarry Peak net income for **full Year 3** (earned evenly throughout the year) | **$180,000** |
| Annual excess depreciation on Westbrook’s depreciable basis difference (for the **40%** interest) | **$4,800** |
| Quarry cash dividends declared and paid **June 15, Year 3** (while Westbrook still held 40%) | **$60,000** total by Quarry |
| On **August 1, Year 3**, Westbrook sells **half** of its interest (20,000 shares = **20%** of Quarry) for cash | **$280,000** |
| After the sale, Westbrook retains **20%** and **continues significant influence** (board representation) | equity method continues on remaining interest |
| No further dividends after the sale in Year 3 | — |

Assume NI and excess depreciation are recognized on a time-proportion basis. For the retained 20% interest after August 1, annual excess depreciation is half of the original $4,800 (basis difference sold with half the investment). Ignore taxes.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **pre-disposal** equity-method updates for the **seven months** ended July 31 / August 1 (share of NI while holding 40%, 7/12 excess depreciation on the full 40% basis difference, and share of June dividends).  
b. Compute the **carrying amount immediately before the partial sale** and the **carrying amount allocated to the interest sold**.  
c. Record the **August 1, Year 3 partial disposal** journal entry (**emphasis**).  
d. Record post-sale equity-method entries for the **remaining five months** on the **retained 20%** interest (share of NI; excess dep). Compute the **December 31, Year 3** carrying amount of the remaining investment.  
e. **Classification / presentation:** (1) Is the $12,400-scale disposal result in NI or OCI? (2) SCF classification of the $280,000 cash proceeds (long-term strategic investment, not trading). (3) How should the remaining investment typically be classified on the balance sheet at year-end?

*(1) Share of NI for five months at 20%*  
Quarry NI for 5 months = \( \$180{,}000 \times 5/12 = \$75{,}000 \)

### Q4 — MC — Equity method classification and disposal measurement
**LO:** LO 14-5  
**Concept:** Classification of measurement method (equity method vs FV-NI); correct disposal gain base after equity-method updates  

**Question 1:**  
On January 1, **Larkspur Holdings** acquires **28%** of the voting common stock of **Nimbus Robotics** for cash and can exercise significant influence over Nimbus’s operating and financial policies. Larkspur intends to hold the investment indefinitely and does **not** elect the fair value option. Which measurement model applies, and how does Larkspur record its share of Nimbus’s Year 1 cash dividend?

- A) FV-NI; debit Cash, credit Dividend Revenue  
- B) Equity method; debit Cash, credit Dividend Revenue  
- C) Equity method; debit Cash, credit Investment in Nimbus Stock  
- D) Consolidation; no separate Investment account is reported  

**Answer:** **C.** Ownership of 28% with significant influence → **equity method** (presumption at ≥20%, supported by facts). Under the equity method, dividends are a **return of investment** (reduce the Investment account), not dividend revenue. Consolidation requires **control** (typically >50%). FV-NI applies when influence is **insignificant**.

---

**Question 2:**  
**Granite Peak Corp.** accounts for a 35% investee under the equity method. On January 1 the investment carrying amount is **$210,000**. Through the May 1 sale date, Granite records its share of investee NI of **$14,000**, excess depreciation of **$1,200**, and its share of dividends of **$5,000**. Granite then sells the **entire** investment for **$225,000** cash. What is the **gain (loss)** on disposal?

- A) Gain of $15,000 (cash − January 1 carrying amount)  
- B) Gain of $7,200  
- C) Gain of $6,200  
- D) Loss of $1,000  

**Answer:** **B.**  
Updated carrying amount at sale = \(210{,}000 + 14{,}000 - 1{,}200 - 5{,}000 = \mathbf{\$217{,}800}\).  
Gain = \(225{,}000 - 217{,}800 = \mathbf{\$7{,}200}\).  
Option A incorrectly uses the Jan 1 carrying amount without equity-method updates. Option C is a distractor (e.g., forgetting excess dep). Option D confuses directions of NI/dividend adjustments.
