# Agent 267 — CORE DEMO — LO 14-4

**Chapter:** 14  
**LO title:** Account for equity securities measured at FV-NI with fair value adjustments at sale date and period-end  
**Critical gap LO:** yes

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE:** equity purchase at price **plus brokerage/commissions** (capitalized in Investment cost); insignificant influence (<20% guideline) → **FV-NI**, not equity method
- **Dividend revenue:** dividends declared/paid → **Dividend Revenue** in net income (return *on* investment); Investment account **not** reduced
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** Cost vs fair value; required FVA; existing FVA; adjusting amount (required − existing)
- **Period-end adjusting JE:** Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities ↔ Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income** (FV-NI)
- **Disposal / sale JE:** **first** adjust FVA to sale-date fair value through NI; **then** remove Investment at cost and eliminate FVA for cash—**no separate realized G/L** on sale once FV-adjusted
- **Holding-period income rollforward:** period unrealized G/L in NI sum to (sale proceeds − cost) over ownership
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** current vs noncurrent by intent/ability to sell within one year; investment reported at FV on BS; dividend revenue and unrealized holding G/L in **net income**; disclose portion of unrealized G/L relating to equity securities **still held** at reporting date; SCF investing vs operating by purpose
- **Number-variant twin:** same FV-NI path with all amounts changed

### Q1 — CORE — Equity FV-NI full life cycle (purchase + commissions → dividends → YE FVA schedule → BS/IS → sale-date FV-NI → sale)
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** initial_recognition_JE; subsequent_measurement_schedule; period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **August 15 of Year 1**, **Cedarlane Holdings LLC** purchased **4,000** of **Riverstone Corp.**’s **40,000** outstanding common shares for **$35 per share**. Brokerage commissions paid were **$800**. Cedarlane’s 10% interest does **not** give it significant influence over Riverstone. Fair value is readily determinable. Cedarlane measures the investment at **FV-NI** and has a December 31 year-end. Beginning Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities is **zero**. No other equity investment activity except as stated.

| Date | Event / fair value |
|---|---|
| Aug 15, Year 1 | Purchase 4,000 shares @ $35 + $800 commissions |
| Oct 20, Year 1 | Riverstone declares and pays total dividends of **$80,000** |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value of Riverstone stock = **$38 per share** |
| Feb 10, Year 2 | Entire position sold for **$148,500** cash |

**Required:**  
(a) Record the **purchase** on August 15 of Year 1.  
(b) Record Cedarlane’s share of the **dividend** on October 20 of Year 1.  
(c) Prepare the **subsequent measurement (FVA) schedule** and record the **period-end fair value adjustment** on December 31 of Year 1.  
(d) Report the investment on the **December 31, Year 1 balance sheet** and the related **Year 1 income statement** effects (dividend revenue and unrealized holding G/L).  
(e) On February 10 of Year 2, sell the entire position for $148,500: **(i)** adjust to fair value at the sale date using **FV-NI**; **(ii)** record the sale, eliminating the associated Fair Value Adjustment balance.  
(f) Prepare a **holding-period income schedule** for unrealized G/L in net income and prove the cumulative amount equals sale proceeds minus original cost.  
(g) Briefly state **balance sheet classification** logic and what ASC 321 requires disclosed about unrealized gains/losses for equity securities still held.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) August 15, Year 1 — Initial recognition**  

Cost = (4,000 × $35) + $800 = $140,000 + $800 = **$140,800**  
Ownership = 4,000 / 40,000 = **10%** → insignificant influence → **FV-NI** (not equity method).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Riverstone Stock | 140,800 | |
| Cash | | 140,800 |
| *Purchase equity securities; capitalize commissions in cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 140,800 = Cr 140,800. **Balanced.**

**(b) October 20, Year 1 — Dividend revenue (insignificant influence)**  

Cedarlane’s share = $80,000 × 10% = **$8,000**  
Under FV-NI, dividends are **revenue**, not a reduction of the Investment account.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 8,000 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 8,000 |
| *Dividend revenue in net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Subsequent measurement schedule and period-end FV-NI adjust**  

Fair value YE = 4,000 × $38 = **$152,000**

**Subsequent measurement schedule (Year 1 YE)**

| Equity Investment | Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Riverstone common stock | $140,800 | $152,000 | $11,200 |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$11,200** |

Required FVA = FV − Cost = $152,000 − $140,800 = **$11,200 debit**  
Existing FVA = **$0**  
Adjustment = **debit FVA $11,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 11,200 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 11,200 |
| *Adjust equity investment to fair value through net income (FV-NI)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 11,200. **Balanced.**  
Carrying amount on BS = cost $140,800 + FVA debit $11,200 = **$152,000**.

**(d) Year 1 financial statement presentation**  

**Balance sheet excerpt — December 31, Year 1**  
Assets (current or noncurrent depending on intent to sell within one year/operating cycle)  
 Investment in equity securities ……………… **$152,000**  
(presentation = cost $140,800 + FVA debit $11,200)

**Income statement excerpt — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
Other revenues (expenses)  
 Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$8,000**  
 Unrealized holding gain on equity securities … **$11,200**

**(e) February 10, Year 2 — Sale under FV-NI**  

**(i) Sale-date fair value adjustment (FV-NI)**  

Required FVA at sale* = $148,500 − $140,800 = **$7,700 debit**  
Less existing FVA = **11,200 debit**  
Decrease needed = **$(3,500)**

\*$148,500 sale price/fair value − $140,800 original cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 3,500 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 3,500 |
| *Bring carrying amount to sale-date fair value through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 3,500. **Balanced.** FVA now = **$7,700 debit**.

**(ii) Record sale — eliminate FVA; no separate gain/loss on sale**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 148,500 | |
| Investment in Riverstone Stock | | 140,800 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 7,700 |
| *Remove investment at cost; clear FVA; cash = fair value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 148,500 = Cr 140,800 + 7,700. **Balanced.**  
No Gain/Loss on Sale account is used: net income already reflects cost-to-sale fair value changes through the FV-NI adjustments.

**(f) Holding-period income schedule (unrealized G/L in NI)**  

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in net income | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 | $11,200 | YE FV $152,000 |
| Year 2 (sale date) | (3,500) | Sale FV $148,500 |
| **Cumulative over ownership** | **$7,700** | Equals $148,500 − $140,800 |

**Proof:** 11,200 − 3,500 = **7,700**. Total economic gain recognized in NI over the holding period = sale proceeds − cost. (Dividend revenue of $8,000 is separate return *on* investment and is not part of this FV rollforward.)

**(g) Classification / presentation / disclosure**  
- **BS:** Classify as **current** if management intends and is able to sell within one year (or operating cycle if longer); otherwise **noncurrent**. Report at **fair value**.  
- **IS:** Dividend revenue and unrealized holding gains/losses are in **net income** (other income/expense)—**not** OCI.  
- **Disclosure:** For each period presented, disclose the portion of unrealized gains and losses that relates to equity securities **still held** at the reporting date (ASC 321-10-50-4).  
- **SCF:** Purchases/sales of equity securities are **investing** unless the company actively trades them for short-term profits (then **operating**).

**Key insight:** Under **equity FV-NI** (insignificant influence, readily determinable FV), **commissions enter cost**, **dividends hit NI as revenue**, and **every FV change hits NI via FVA**—at **period-end** and **again at sale date** before the sale JE clears Investment and FVA. Cumulative unrealized G/L in NI equals proceeds − cost.

### Q2 — CORE number variant twin
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** number_variant_twin — same equity FV-NI path with all new amounts  
**Scenario:**  
On **March 1 of Year 1**, **Northfork Partners Inc.** purchased **3,000** shares of **Tidal Bay Inc.** common stock for **$52 per share**. Transfer costs and brokerage totaled **$1,200**. Northfork has **insignificant influence** over Tidal Bay; fair value is readily determinable. Measure at **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA—Equity Securities is zero.

| Date | Fair value / activity |
|---|---|
| June 30, Year 1 | Tidal Bay declares and pays a cash dividend of **$2.50 per share** on shares held by Northfork |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value **$49 per share** |
| Apr 5, Year 2 | Entire holding sold for **$151,800** cash |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on March 1 of Year 1.  
(b) Dividend entry on June 30 of Year 1.  
(c) Fair value adjustment on December 31 of Year 1 (include FVA schedule).  
(d) Year 1 BS / IS presentation for this investment.  
(e) April 5, Year 2 sale: (i) sale-date FV-NI adjust; (ii) sale eliminating FVA.  
(f) Holding-period unrealized G/L schedule proving cumulative NI (FV only) = proceeds − cost.

**Answer key:**  

**(a) March 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  

Cost = (3,000 × $52) + $1,200 = $156,000 + $1,200 = **$157,200**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Tidal Bay Stock | 157,200 | |
| Cash | | 157,200 |
| *Purchase equity securities including incidental costs* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 157,200. **Balanced.**

**(b) June 30, Year 1 — Dividend revenue**  

Dividend = 3,000 × $2.50 = **$7,500**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 7,500 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 7,500 |
| *Dividend revenue under FV-NI (not a reduction of Investment)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 7,500. **Balanced.**

**(c) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end FVA**  

FV YE = 3,000 × $49 = **$147,000**

| Equity Investment | Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tidal Bay common | $157,200 | $147,000 | $(10,200)$ |
| Existing FVA | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$(10,200)$** |

Required FVA = **$10,200 credit** (FV < cost)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | 10,200 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 10,200 |
| *FV-NI period-end loss through net income* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 10,200. **Balanced.**  
BS carrying amount = $157,200 − $10,200 FVA credit = **$147,000**.

**(d) Year 1 presentation**  

**Balance sheet — December 31, Year 1**  
 Investment in equity securities ……………… **$147,000**

**Income statement — Year ended December 31, Year 1**  
 Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$7,500**  
 Unrealized holding loss on equity securities … **$(10,200)**

**(e) April 5, Year 2 — Sale under FV-NI**  

**(i) Sale-date FV adjust**  

Required FVA at sale* = $151,800 − $157,200 = **$(5,400)** → **$5,400 credit** FVA  
Existing FVA = **$10,200 credit**  
Increase (decrease) to FVA needed = −5,400 − (−10,200) = **+$4,800** (reduce the credit balance)

\*$151,800 fair value − $157,200 cost.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 4,800 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 4,800 |
| *Roll FVA from $10,200 Cr to $5,400 Cr through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 4,800. **Balanced.** Ending FVA = **$5,400 credit**.

**(ii) Sale — eliminate FVA**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 151,800 | |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 5,400 | |
| Investment in Tidal Bay Stock | | 157,200 |
| *Remove investment at cost; clear credit FVA; cash = FV* | | |

**Check:** Dr 151,800 + 5,400 = Cr 157,200. **Balanced.** No separate sale G/L.

**(f) Holding-period unrealized G/L schedule**  

| Period | Unrealized G/(L) in NI | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year 1 | $(10,200)$ | YE FV $147,000 |
| Year 2 (sale date) | 4,800 | Sale FV $151,800 |
| **Cumulative** | **$(5,400)** | Equals $151,800 − $157,200 |

**Proof:** −10,200 + 4,800 = **−5,400**.

**Key insight:** Same control as Q1 with a **loss-then-partial-recovery** path: required FVA is always **FV − cost**; the adjusting JE moves FVA from **existing** to **required** through Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income; sale clears the residual FVA.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Partial sale + remaining period-end FVA (emphasis: period_end_adjusting_JE)
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** period_end_adjusting_JE; disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; subsequent_measurement_schedule; classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
On **September 1 of Year 1**, **Westbridge Equity Fund** purchased **6,000** shares of **Meridian Tech Corp.** common stock for a total cash outlay of **$180,000** (including commissions). Westbridge has **insignificant influence**; measure at **FV-NI**. Year-end is December 31. Beginning FVA—Equity Securities is **zero**.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Nov 12, Year 1 | Sold **2,000** shares for **$68,000** cash |
| Dec 15, Year 1 | Meridian declares and pays a **$1.25 per share** cash dividend (on shares Westbridge still holds) |
| Dec 31, Year 1 | Fair value of the **remaining** investment = **$128,000** |

**Required:**  
(a) Purchase entry on September 1 of Year 1.  
(b) November 12 sale of 2,000 shares: **(i)** sale-date FV-NI adjust for the **sold portion**; **(ii)** record the sale and eliminate the related FVA.  
(c) Dividend entry on December 15 of Year 1.  
(d) Prepare the **period-end FVA schedule** for the **remaining** shares and record the December 31 adjusting entry (assume FVA for remaining shares starts at zero after the sale).  
(e) Year 1 income statement effects related to Meridian (dividends + all unrealized G/L in NI) and year-end BS carrying amount of the remaining investment.  
(f) State how much of Year 1’s unrealized G/L relates to equity securities **still held** at December 31 (disclosure).

**Answer key:**  

Cost per share = $180,000 / 6,000 = **$30**

**(a) September 1, Year 1 — Purchase**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Meridian Stock | 180,000 | |
| Cash | | 180,000 |
| *Initial recognition of equity securities at cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 180,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) November 12, Year 1 — Partial disposal under FV-NI**  

Cost of 2,000 shares sold = 2,000 × $30 = **$60,000**  
Sale proceeds (fair value) = **$68,000**

**(i) Sale-date FV adjust (sold portion)**  

Required FVA for sold portion = $68,000 − $60,000 = **$8,000 debit**  
Existing FVA (beginning zero; no prior adjust) = **0**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 8,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 8,000 |
| *Adjust sold shares to sale-date fair value through NI* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. **Balanced.**

**(ii) Record sale — eliminate FVA for sold shares**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 68,000 | |
| Investment in Meridian Stock | | 60,000 |
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | | 8,000 |
| *Remove cost of shares sold; clear related FVA* | | |

**Check:** Dr 68,000 = Cr 60,000 + 8,000. **Balanced.**

Remaining shares = 4,000; remaining cost = **$120,000**. FVA after eliminating sold-portion FVA = **$0**.

**(c) December 15, Year 1 — Dividend on shares still held**  

Dividend = 4,000 × $1.25 = **$5,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 5,000 | |
| Dividend Revenue | | 5,000 |
| *Dividend revenue on remaining shares* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,000. **Balanced.**

**(d) December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE (remaining shares) — EMPHASIS**  

**Subsequent measurement schedule (remaining position)**

| Equity Investment | Cost | Fair Value YE | Unrealized Holding Gain (Loss) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Meridian common (4,000 sh) | $120,000 | $128,000 | $8,000 |
| Existing FVA account balance | | | 0 |
| **Increase (decrease) to FVA needed** | | | **$8,000** |

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Value Adjustment—Equity Securities | 8,000 | |
| Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income | | 8,000 |
| *Period-end FV-NI adjust for securities still held* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 8,000. **Balanced.**  
BS carrying amount of remaining investment = **$128,000**.

**(e) Year 1 IS / BS summary**  

**Income statement — Year 1 (Meridian-related)**  
 Dividend revenue ……………………………… **$5,000**  
 Unrealized holding gain — sold shares (Nov 12) … **$8,000**  
 Unrealized holding gain — still held (Dec 31) … **$8,000**  
 **Total unrealized G/L in NI for Year 1** ……… **$16,000**

**Balance sheet — December 31, Year 1**  
 Investment in equity securities (remaining) …… **$128,000**

**(f) Disclosure — unrealized G/L on securities still held**  
Of the **$16,000** total unrealized holding gain recognized in Year 1 net income, **$8,000** relates to equity securities **still held** at the reporting date (the period-end FVA on the remaining 4,000 shares). The other **$8,000** relates to shares sold during the period (sale-date FV adjust). ASC 321 requires this still-held portion to be disclosed.

**Key insight (period-end emphasis):** After a partial sale, **recompute FVA only for remaining cost vs remaining FV**. The period-end JE is always **required FVA − existing FVA** through Unrealized Gain or Loss—**Income**. Exam traps: treating dividends as a reduction of Investment (equity method error) and recording a separate “Gain on Sale” instead of sale-date FV-NI + FVA elimination.

### Q4 — MC — Where unrealized holding G/L on equity FV-NI is reported
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** classification_presentation_or_disclosure  
**Question:**  
An investor holds common stock with **insignificant influence** and a **readily determinable fair value**, measured under **FV-NI**. At year-end the fair value of shares still held exceeds cost. Where is the unrealized holding gain reported?

- A) Other comprehensive income (OCI), then reclassified to net income only when sold  
- B) **Net income** as unrealized holding gain (other income/expense)  
- C) Directly in retained earnings, bypassing the income statement  
- D) Not recognized until the shares are sold (then as realized gain only)

**Answer:** **B.** Under ASC 321 / LO 14-4, equity securities with insignificant influence and readily determinable FV are subsequently measured at fair value with unrealized holding gains and losses **included in earnings (net income)** each period—not OCI (that is debt AFS / FV-OCI), not a direct RE plug, and not deferred until sale.

### Q5 — MC — Sale sequence under equity FV-NI
**LO:** LO 14-4  
**Concept:** disposal_maturity_or_settlement_JE; period_end_adjusting_JE  
**Question:**  
On a sale date during Year 2, an equity security measured at **FV-NI** has: cost **$50,000**, existing FVA **debit** balance **$3,000**, and cash proceeds **$54,500**. Which pair of journal entries is correct?

- A) Debit Fair Value Adjustment $1,500 / credit Unrealized Gain or Loss—Income $1,500; then debit Cash $54,500, credit Investment $50,000, credit Fair Value Adjustment $4,500; **no separate Gain on Sale**  
- B) Debit Cash $54,500, credit Investment $50,000, credit Gain on Sale $4,500; leave the existing $3,000 FVA balance untouched  
- C) Debit Cash $54,500 and debit FVA $3,000, credit Investment $50,000, credit Gain on Sale $7,500  
- D) Credit OCI $4,500 for the cumulative fair value change, then reclassify OCI into net income only at sale  

**Answer:** **A.**  
Required FVA at sale = $54,500 − $50,000 = **$4,500 debit**. Existing FVA = **$3,000 debit**. Increase needed = **$1,500** → Dr FVA / Cr Unrealized G/L—Income. Sale then removes Investment at cost and clears the **$4,500** FVA against cash; no separate sale G/L. OCI is not used for equity FV-NI.
