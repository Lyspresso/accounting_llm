# Agent 340 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-2

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Account for depreciation in partial periods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Exact fractional proration:** months-in-service ÷ 12 applied to time-based annual depreciation (SL annual amount; each SYD layer; first DDB year only)
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Dr Depreciation Expense / Cr Accumulated Depreciation for the partial-year (or full-year) amount at year-end
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize PPE at acquisition cost when placed in service mid-period
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year tables of expense, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount that roll to residual and total exactly to depreciable cost
- **SYD partial periods:** split each full-year layer across calendar years (e.g., 9/12 then 3/12)
- **DDB partial periods:** prorate Year 1 only; then apply rate to beginning carrying amount; stop so BV does not fall below residual
- **Units-of-production:** automatically partial — actual output × per-unit rate (no separate month fraction)
- **Policy conventions:** full-year (beginning / end of period), half-year, full-month — GAAP-acceptable if not materially different from exact fractional; applied consistently
- **Disposal with partial period:** update depreciation through disposal date (period-end / through-date AJ), then remove cost and AD; gain/loss = proceeds − carrying amount

### Q1 — CORE — Mid-period acquisition: multi-method Y1/Y2 dep, full schedules, initial JE, **period-end AJs** (emphasis)
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting JEs for partial-year depreciation under SL, SYD, DDB, and UOP; initial recognition; subsequent measurement schedules that total to depreciable cost  
**Scenario:**  
**Clearwater Industrial Supply Co.** (calendar-year reporter) places a vertical packaging machine into service on **April 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Acquisition cost (cash) | **$50,000** |
| Estimated residual value | **$5,000** |
| Estimated useful life | **5 years** |
| Estimated total productive output | **30,000 units** |
| Actual output, Year 1 | **2,000 units** |
| Actual output, Year 2 | **8,000 units** |
| Fiscal year-end | December 31 |
| Partial-period policy | **Exact fractional months** (prorate time-based methods) |

Depreciable base = $50,000 − $5,000 = **$45,000**.  
Service life under exact months: **April 1, Year 1 through March 31, Year 6** (exactly 5 years).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **April 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry.  
b. Compute **Year 1** and **Year 2** depreciation under (1) **straight-line**, (2) **sum-of-the-years’-digits**, (3) **double-declining-balance**, and (4) **units-of-production**. Prorate partial periods for time-based methods.  
c. Prepare the complete **straight-line subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–6 (expense, AD, carrying amount). Confirm total expense = depreciable cost.  
d. Prepare the complete **SYD subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–6, showing layer splits. Confirm total = depreciable cost.  
e. Prepare the complete **DDB subsequent measurement schedule** (stop at residual).  
f. **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** under each of the four methods (four separate balanced JEs).  
g. **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, Year 2 period-end adjusting entry** under **straight-line** only.  
h. State Year 1 balance-sheet presentation of the machine under straight-line.

**Answer key:**

**a. April 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—Packaging Machine | 50,000 | |
| Cash | | 50,000 |
| *Place packaging machine in service at acquisition cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 50,000 = Cr 50,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Year 1 and Year 2 depreciation (exact fractional periods)**

**(1) Straight-line**  
Annual SL = $45,000 / 5 = **$9,000**  
Year 1 (Apr–Dec = **9/12**): $9,000 × 9/12 = **$6,750**  
Year 2 (full year): **$9,000**

**(2) Sum-of-the-years’-digits**  
Digits sum = 5+4+3+2+1 = **15**  
Each full-year layer is allocated **9/12** to the calendar year that contains April–December of that service year and **3/12** to the following calendar year (January–March).

| Layer | Fraction | Full-year layer | 9/12 portion | 3/12 portion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5/15 | $15,000 | $11,250 (Y1) | $3,750 (Y2) |
| 2 | 4/15 | 12,000 | 9,000 (Y2) | 3,000 (Y3) |
| 3 | 3/15 | 9,000 | 6,750 (Y3) | 2,250 (Y4) |
| 4 | 2/15 | 6,000 | 4,500 (Y4) | 1,500 (Y5) |
| 5 | 1/15 | 3,000 | 2,250 (Y5) | 750 (Y6) |

**Year 1 SYD** = **$11,250**  
**Year 2 SYD** = $3,750 + $9,000 = **$12,750**

**(3) Double-declining-balance**  
DDB rate = 2 × (1/5) = **40%**  
Year 1: $50,000 × 40% × 9/12 = **$15,000**  
Carrying amount end of Year 1 = $50,000 − $15,000 = **$35,000**  
Year 2: $35,000 × 40% = **$14,000**  
Carrying amount end of Year 2 = $35,000 − $14,000 = **$21,000**

**(4) Units-of-production** (automatically partial)  
Rate = $45,000 / 30,000 units = **$1.50 per unit**  
Year 1: 2,000 × $1.50 = **$3,000**  
Year 2: 8,000 × $1.50 = **$12,000**

**Summary — Year 1 / Year 2 depreciation expense**

| Method | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Straight-line | $6,750 | $9,000 |
| Sum-of-the-years’-digits | 11,250 | 12,750 |
| Double-declining-balance | 15,000 | 14,000 |
| Units-of-production | 3,000 | 12,000 |

**c. Straight-line subsequent measurement schedule**

| End of period | Depreciable cost | Rate | Partial period | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr 1, Y1 (in service) | — | — | — | — | $0 | $50,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $45,000 | 1/5 | 9/12 | **$6,750** | 6,750 | 43,250 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 15,750 | 34,250 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 24,750 | 25,250 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 33,750 | 16,250 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | 45,000 | 1/5 | full | **9,000** | 42,750 | 7,250 |
| Dec 31, Y6 | 45,000 | 1/5 | 3/12 | **$2,250** | **45,000** | **5,000** residual |

**Total depreciation over life** = $6,750 + $9,000 × 4 + $2,250 = **$45,000** = depreciable cost. ✓  
Exactly **5 full years** of SL depreciation are recorded across Years 1–6.

**d. SYD subsequent measurement schedule**

| End of period | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr 1, Y1 | — | $0 | $50,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | **$11,250** (layer 1 × 9/12) | 11,250 | 38,750 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | **12,750** (3,750 + 9,000) | 24,000 | 26,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | **9,750** (3,000 + 6,750) | 33,750 | 16,250 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | **6,750** (2,250 + 4,500) | 40,500 | 9,500 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | **3,750** (1,500 + 2,250) | 44,250 | 5,750 |

**Total** = $11,250 + $12,750 + $9,750 + $6,750 + $3,750 + $750 = **$45,000**. ✓

**e. DDB subsequent measurement schedule**

| End of period | Beginning carrying amount | Rate | Partial | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr 1, Y1 | — | — | — | — | $0 | $50,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | $50,000 | 40% | 9/12 | **$15,000** | 15,000 | 35,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 35,000 | 40% | full | **14,000** | 29,000 | 21,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 21,000 | 40% | full | **8,400** | 37,400 | 12,600 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | 12,600 | 40% | full | **5,040** | 42,440 | 7,560 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | 7,560 | 40% | full | **$2,560**† | **45,000** | **5,000** residual |
| Dec 31, Y6 | 5,000 | — | — | **$0** | 45,000 | 5,000 |

† Uncapped 40% of $7,560 = $3,024 would drop BV below residual $5,000. Maximum Year 5 depreciation = $7,560 − $5,000 = **$2,560**. Year 6 has no remaining depreciable base.  
**Total** = $15,000 + $14,000 + $8,400 + $5,040 + $2,560 = **$45,000**. ✓

**f. December 31, Year 1 — period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

*(1) Straight-line*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Machine | 6,750 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Machine | | 6,750 |
| *Partial-year SL: $9,000 × 9/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr 6,750 = Cr 6,750. **Balanced.**

*(2) Sum-of-the-years’-digits*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Machine | 11,250 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Machine | | 11,250 |
| *Partial-year SYD: layer 1 × 9/12 = $15,000 × 9/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr 11,250 = Cr 11,250. **Balanced.**

*(3) Double-declining-balance*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Machine | 15,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Machine | | 15,000 |
| *Partial-year DDB: $50,000 × 40% × 9/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr 15,000 = Cr 15,000. **Balanced.**

*(4) Units-of-production*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Machine | 3,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Machine | | 3,000 |
| *UOP: 2,000 units × $1.50 (automatic partial period)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 3,000 = Cr 3,000. **Balanced.**

**g. December 31, Year 2 — period-end adjusting JE (straight-line emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Packaging Machine | 9,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Packaging Machine | | 9,000 |
| *Full-year SL after partial Year 1* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 = Cr 9,000. **Balanced.**  
AD after two period-end AJs (SL): $6,750 + $9,000 = **$15,750** (matches schedule). ✓

**h. Year 1 presentation (straight-line)**  
- **Income statement:** Depreciation Expense **$6,750** (from the period-end AJ).

**Key insight:** The **period-end adjusting JE** is how partial-period depreciation hits the books: same Dr/Cr form as a full-year entry, but the **amount** is the fractional (or UOP) computation. Multi-year **schedules** prove that, across partial first/last years, total depreciation still equals **depreciable cost** and ends at **residual**.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Late-year purchase: SL life schedule + policy conventions + period-end AJs
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin — exact fractional SL subsequent schedule; policy-convention comparison; initial recognition and **period-end adjusting JEs** under exact fractional and half-year  
**Scenario:**  
**Driftwood Cabinetry Inc.** (calendar year) purchases a CNC router on **October 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Acquisition cost (cash) | **$84,000** |
| Residual value | **$4,000** |
| Useful life | **4 years** |
| Placed in service | **October 1, Year 1** |
| Depreciation method for schedules | Straight-line |
| Year-end | December 31 |

Depreciable base = $84,000 − $4,000 = **$80,000**.  
Annual SL = $80,000 / 4 = **$20,000**.  
Exact service window: Oct 1, Year 1 – Sep 30, Year 5.

**Required:**  
a. Record the **October 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry.  
b. Under **exact fractional months**, prepare the **straight-line subsequent measurement schedule** for Years 1–5.  
c. **(Emphasis)** Record the **December 31, Year 1** and **December 31, Year 2** period-end adjusting entries under exact fractional SL.  
d. Prepare a **comparison schedule** of Year 1–Year 5 SL depreciation under: (1) exact fractional, (2) full-year convention—beginning of period, (3) full-year convention—end of period, (4) half-year convention, (5) full-month convention. Confirm each column totals $80,000.  
e. **(Emphasis)** Record the December 31, Year 1 adjusting entry under the **half-year convention**.  
f. Briefly state why policy conventions are acceptable under GAAP.

**Answer key:**

**a. October 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—CNC Router | 84,000 | |
| Cash | | 84,000 |
| *Place CNC router in service* | | |

**Check:** Dr 84,000 = Cr 84,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Exact fractional months — SL subsequent measurement schedule**

Months in Year 1 = Oct–Dec = **3/12**.  
Year 5 = Jan–Sep = **9/12**.

| End of period | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Oct 1, Y1 | — | $0 | $84,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | **$5,000** ($20,000 × 3/12) | 5,000 | 79,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | **20,000** | 25,000 | 59,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | **20,000** | 45,000 | 39,000 |
| Dec 31, Y4 | **20,000** | 65,000 | 19,000 |
| Dec 31, Y5 | **$15,000** ($20,000 × 9/12) | **80,000** | **4,000** residual |

**Total expense** = $5,000 + $20,000 × 3 + $15,000 = **$80,000**. ✓

**c. Period-end adjusting JEs — exact fractional SL (emphasis)**

*December 31, Year 1*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC Router | 5,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC Router | | 5,000 |
| *Partial-year SL: $20,000 × 3/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,000 = Cr 5,000. **Balanced.**

*December 31, Year 2*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC Router | 20,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC Router | | 20,000 |
| *Full-year SL* | | |

**Check:** Dr 20,000 = Cr 20,000. **Balanced.**  
AD after two AJs: $5,000 + $20,000 = **$25,000**. ✓

**d. Policy-convention comparison schedule**

| Method | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 | Year 5 | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| (1) Exact fractional (Demo 12-2A style) | **$5,000** | $20,000 | $20,000 | $20,000 | **$15,000** | **$80,000** |
| (2) Full-year — beginning of period | **0** | 20,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | **80,000** |
| (3) Full-year — end of period | **20,000** | 20,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | **0** | **80,000** |
| (4) Half-year | **10,000** | 20,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | **10,000** | **80,000** |
| (5) Full-month (Oct purchase) | **5,000** | 20,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | **15,000** | **80,000** |

**Reasoning notes:**  
- **Full-year—beginning of period:** no depreciation in year of purchase; full years in Years 2–5.  
- **Full-year—end of period:** full depreciation in year of purchase; none in final calendar year of the 4-year life.  
- **Half-year:** ½ × $20,000 in Year 1 and Year 5 regardless of October 1.  
- **Full-month:** full month in October (month of purchase), none in month of disposal → same pattern as exact 3/12 and 9/12 when purchase/disposal fall on the first of the month.

Year 1 difference, exact vs half-year: half-year records **$5,000 more** ($10,000 vs $5,000) because it ignores the late-year purchase date. All five approaches still depreciate exactly **$80,000** over the asset’s life.

**e. December 31, Year 1 — half-year convention adjusting JE (emphasis)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC Router | 10,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC Router | | 10,000 |
| *Half-year convention: ½ × $20,000 annual SL* | | |

**Check:** Dr 10,000 = Cr 10,000. **Balanced.**

**f. GAAP note**  
Policy conventions are acceptable when results are **not materially different** from exact fractional-period depreciation and the **same policy is applied consistently** from period to period.

**Key insight:** Whether you use exact months or a convention, the **period-end AJ** is always Dr Expense / Cr AD for that year’s computed amount. Changing acquisition date and amounts does not change schedule discipline: every multi-year column must still sum to **depreciable cost**.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Through-date partial depreciation before disposal + disposal JE; convention Year-1 amounts
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Period-end/through-date partial depreciation AJ before disposal; disposal JE (loss); policy-convention Year-1 amounts for mid-period purchase  
**Scenario:**  
**Red Cedar Logistics LLC** owns a delivery truck purchased and placed in service on **January 1, Year 1**.

| Fact | Amount / term |
|---|---|
| Cost of truck | **$42,000** |
| Residual value | **$6,000** |
| Useful life | **6 years** |
| Depreciation method | Straight-line |
| Accumulated depreciation, January 1, Year 4 | **$18,000** |
| Disposal date | **September 1, Year 4** |
| Cash proceeds on sale | **$18,000** |
| Partial-period policy | **Exact fractional months** through disposal date |
| Fiscal year-end | December 31 |

Annual SL = ($42,000 − $6,000) / 6 = **$6,000**.  
Check AD at Jan 1, Year 4: 3 full years × $6,000 = **$18,000**. ✓

**Required:**  
a. **(Emphasis)** Prepare the **September 1, Year 4** entry to **update depreciation** through the disposal date (8 months).  
b. Compute the **carrying amount** at September 1 and the **gain or loss** on disposal.  
c. Prepare the **September 1 disposal journal entry**. Show Dr = Cr.  
d. Independently, assume a **different** asset acquired on **May 12, Year 1** (cost $42,000, residual $6,000, 6-year SL, annual $6,000). Without computing exact days, state **Year 1** depreciation under each policy convention: (1) full-year—beginning of period, (2) full-year—end of period, (3) half-year, (4) full-month.  
e. Classify: where is the loss on disposal reported, and is Accumulated Depreciation a liability?

**Answer key:**

**a. September 1, Year 4 — update depreciation through disposal (emphasis)**  
8/12 × $6,000 = **$4,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—Delivery Truck | 4,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Truck | | 4,000 |
| *Partial-year SL through Sept 1: $6,000 × 8/12* | | |

**Check:** Dr 4,000 = Cr 4,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Carrying amount and gain/loss**  
AD at disposal = $18,000 + $4,000 = **$22,000**  
Carrying amount = $42,000 − $22,000 = **$20,000**  
Proceeds = **$18,000**  
**Loss on disposal** = $20,000 − $18,000 = **$2,000**

**c. September 1, Year 4 — disposal JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 18,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—Delivery Truck | 22,000 | |
| Loss on Disposal of PPE | 2,000 | |
| Equipment—Delivery Truck | | 42,000 |
| *Remove cost and AD; recognize loss (proceeds < BV)* | | |

**Check:** Dr $18,000 + $22,000 + $2,000 = **$42,000**; Cr **$42,000**. **Balanced.**

**d. Policy conventions — Year 1 only (May 12 acquisition; annual SL $6,000)**

| Convention | Year 1 depreciation | Reasoning |
|---|---:|---|
| (1) Full-year — **beginning of period** | **$0** | Assets purchased during the year are **not** depreciated that year |
| (2) Full-year — **end of period** | **$6,000** | Assets purchased during the year are depreciated a **full year** |
| (3) Half-year | **$3,000** | ½ × $6,000 in year of purchase regardless of May 12 |
| (4) Full-month | **$4,000** = 8/12 × $6,000 | Full month in May (month of purchase); 8 months May–Dec |

(Exact fractional from May 12 is nearly the same as full-month for practical purposes; textbook full-month = full depreciation in purchase month, none in disposal month.)

**e. Classification / presentation**  
- **Loss on Disposal of PPE $2,000** is reported on the **income statement** (typically other expense / not a cost of sales to customers).  
- **Accumulated Depreciation** is a **contra-asset** (credit balance reducing PPE), **not** a liability.  
- After disposal, both the truck’s cost and its AD are **removed**; neither remains on the balance sheet.

**Key insight:** Before any mid-period **disposal**, always **update depreciation** with a through-date adjusting entry so AD and carrying amount are current. That partial AJ is the same form as a year-end AJ—only the date and months fraction change. The disposal entry then clears cost and AD and plugs **gain or loss** so Dr = Cr.

### Q4 — MC — Half-year convention Year-1 amount
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Identify correct partial-period depreciation under the half-year convention  
**Question:**  
On **November 8, Year 1**, **Blue Mesa Printing LLC** purchased equipment for **$96,000**. Residual value **$6,000**; useful life **5 years**; straight-line. Blue Mesa’s policy is the **half-year convention**. What is **Year 1 depreciation expense**?

- A) $0  
- B) $9,000  
- C) $3,000  
- D) $18,000  

**Answer:** **B. $9,000.**  
Annual SL = ($96,000 − $6,000) / 5 = **$18,000**. Half-year convention → Year 1 = ½ × $18,000 = **$9,000**, regardless of the November 8 purchase date.  
- A would be full-year—beginning of period (no depreciation in year of purchase).  
- C is 2/12 × $18,000 (approximate exact months Nov–Dec) — not half-year.  
- D is a full year of SL — full-year—end of period, not half-year.

### Q5 — MC — First-year SYD layer proration
**LO:** LO 12-2  
**Concept:** Prorate first SYD layer when asset is acquired mid-year (exact fractional periods)  
**Question:**  
Equipment cost **$75,000**, residual **$5,000**, life **5 years**, purchased **July 1, Year 1**. Company uses **sum-of-the-years’-digits** and **exact fractional periods**. What is **Year 1 depreciation**?

- A) $23,333  
- B) $11,667  
- C) $14,000  
- D) $7,000  

**Answer:** **B. $11,667** (rounded; exact $11,666.67).  
Depreciable base = $70,000; digits sum = 15; first full layer = 5/15 × $70,000 = **$23,333.33**. July 1 → **6/12** of first layer in Year 1: $23,333.33 × 6/12 = **$11,666.67 ≈ $11,667**.  
- A is the full first layer (no proration).  
- C is half-year of SL annual ($14,000 = ½ × $28,000), wrong method.  
- D is 6/12 of annual SL ($14,000 × 6/12) or other miscalc.
