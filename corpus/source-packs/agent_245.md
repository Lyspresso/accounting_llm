# Agent 245 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-1

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Accounting for Restricted Stock Plans  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Initial recognition JE (restricted stock awards):** At grant, debit Unearned Compensation—Equity (contra-equity) for grant-date fair value; credit Common Stock and Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par for shares issued into trust
- **Grant-date fair value lock:** Post-grant market-price changes do **not** remeasure total compensation for equity-classified awards (ASC 718)
- **Subsequent measurement schedule (emphasis):** Straight-line amortization of total grant-date fair value over the requisite service (vesting) period; unearned balance rolls forward each year
- **Period-end adjusting JE (RSA):** Debit Compensation Expense; credit Unearned Compensation—Equity
- **Period-end adjusting JE (RSU):** Debit Compensation Expense; credit Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock (no share issuance at grant)
- **Disposal / forfeiture JE (as incurred):** Reverse Common Stock, PIC, remaining Unearned Compensation, and previously recognized Compensation Expense
- **Settlement at vesting (RSUs):** Debit PIC—Restricted Stock; credit Common Stock and PIC in Excess of Par for shares issued
- **Estimated forfeitures:** Reduce expected compensation at grant; cumulative catch-up when the estimate changes
- **Classification / presentation:** Unearned Compensation—Equity is a **contra-equity** (not an asset); Compensation Expense on the income statement
- **RSA vs RSU:** RSA issues stock at grant (with unearned contra); RSU delays share issuance until vesting

### Q1 — CORE — Restricted stock awards: grant JE, multi-year amortization schedule, period-end expense, presentation, and forfeiture
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition at grant-date fair value; subsequent straight-line amortization schedule (emphasis); period-end adjusting JE; equity presentation; forfeiture (as incurred) disposal JE  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Riverglen Precision Tools Corp.** grants **1,800** shares of its **$5 par** common stock as **restricted stock awards** to one senior officer. The shares are issued on the grant date and held in trust; they vest only if the officer remains employed for **three years** (requisite service period = vesting period).

- Grant-date market price (fair value) = **$36** per share.  
- During Year 1 the stock trades between $30 and $44; the December 31, Year 1 closing price is **$41**.  
- Riverglen elects to account for forfeitures **as incurred** (not estimated at grant).  
- On **January 1, Year 2**, before any Year 2 amortization, the officer resigns and forfeits the entire award.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **total compensation cost** at the grant date. Explain whether Year 1 market-price changes affect that amount.  
b. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** (issuance of restricted stock).  
c. Prepare a **three-year straight-line amortization (subsequent measurement) schedule** of Unearned Compensation—Equity, assuming the officer stays and no forfeitures. Show beginning unearned, compensation expense, and ending unearned for each year.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting entry** to recognize compensation expense.  
e. Show the **December 31, Year 1 stockholders’ equity presentation** related to this award and the Year 1 income statement amount.  
f. Prepare the **January 1, Year 2 journal entry** for the forfeiture (disposal of the award).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Total compensation at grant**  
Total compensation = 1,800 shares × $36 = **$64,800**.

Grant-date fair value is locked under ASC 718 for equity-classified awards. The Year 1 trading range ($30–$44) and Dec. 31 price of $41 are **irrelevant** to remeasurement of total compensation cost.

**b. January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition (issuance of restricted stock)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unearned Compensation—Equity (1,800 × $36) | 64,800 | |
| Common Stock (1,800 × $5) | | 9,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock ($64,800 − $9,000) | | 55,800 |
| *Record restricted shares at grant-date FV; unearned compensation is contra-equity* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **64,800**; Cr 9,000 + 55,800 = **64,800**. Balanced.

**c. Subsequent measurement — amortization schedule (no-forfeiture path; emphasis)**  
Annual expense = $64,800 ÷ 3 years = **$21,600**.

| Year | Beginning unearned | Compensation expense | Ending unearned |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $64,800 | $21,600 | $43,200 |
| Year 2 | 43,200 | 21,600 | 21,600 |
| Year 3 | 21,600 | 21,600 | 0 |
| **Total** | | **$64,800** | |

Rollforward check: 64,800 − 21,600 − 21,600 − 21,600 = **0**. Schedule balances.

**d. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 21,600 | |
| Unearned Compensation—Equity ($64,800 / 3) | | 21,600 |
| *Amortize unearned compensation straight-line over the requisite service period* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **21,600**. Balanced.

**e. December 31, Year 1 presentation**

*Stockholders’ equity (related amounts):*

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Common stock | $9,000 |
| Paid-in capital in excess of par—common stock | 55,800 |
| Unearned compensation—equity ($64,800 − $21,600) | **(43,200)** |
| Net equity effect of award (before expense closed to RE) | **$21,600** |

*Income statement — Year 1:*  
Compensation expense **$21,600**.

**f. January 1, Year 2 — Forfeiture / disposal (as incurred)**  
Reverse the stock accounts, reverse remaining unearned compensation, and reverse compensation expense previously recognized.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock | 9,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | 55,800 | |
| Unearned Compensation—Equity ($64,800 − $21,600) | | 43,200 |
| Compensation Expense | | 21,600 |
| *Derecognize award and reverse prior compensation cost upon forfeiture* | | |

**Check:** Dr 9,000 + 55,800 = **64,800**; Cr 43,200 + 21,600 = **64,800**. Balanced.

*After this entry:* Common Stock, PIC, Unearned Compensation, and Comp Expense related to this grant are all zero.

**Key insight:** For **restricted stock awards**, stock is issued at grant but restricted; **Unearned Compensation—Equity** (contra-equity) captures unearned cost and is amortized **straight-line** on a multi-year schedule. Forfeiture (as incurred) **reverses** both the equity accounts and previously recognized expense.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Restricted stock awards twin with full vesting
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin of RSA initial recognition, multi-year subsequent measurement schedule, period-end expense JEs, and balances after full vesting (restrictions lift)  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Oakridge BioSensors LLC** grants **4,000** shares of **$1 par** common stock as restricted stock awards to its CFO. Shares are issued into trust on the grant date and vest after a **four-year** requisite service period if the CFO remains employed.

- Grant-date fair value = **$55** per share.  
- No forfeitures are expected or occur. Oakridge’s forfeiture policy is “as incurred” (irrelevant here because none occur).  
- Market price at Dec. 31, Year 1 is $62; ignore for measurement.  
- The CFO remains employed through the vesting date.

**Required:**  
a. Compute total compensation cost and prepare the **January 1, Year 1 grant (initial recognition) JE**.  
b. Prepare the **four-year amortization (subsequent measurement) schedule** of Unearned Compensation—Equity.  
c. Prepare the **December 31 period-end adjusting entry** for Year 1 (state the annual amount if identical for Years 2–4).  
d. After the Year 4 amortization entry, state the balances in Unearned Compensation—Equity, Common Stock (from this award), and PIC in Excess of Par (from this award).  
e. Briefly explain why no additional “issuance” entry is needed at the vesting date for an RSA (contrast conceptually with RSUs).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Total compensation and grant JE**  
Total compensation = 4,000 × $55 = **$220,000**.

*January 1, Year 1 — Initial recognition*

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unearned Compensation—Equity | 220,000 | |
| Common Stock (4,000 × $1) | | 4,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock ($220,000 − $4,000) | | 216,000 |

**Check:** Dr = **220,000**; Cr 4,000 + 216,000 = **220,000**. Balanced.

**b. Subsequent measurement — amortization schedule**  
Annual expense = $220,000 ÷ 4 = **$55,000**.

| Year | Beginning unearned | Compensation expense | Ending unearned |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $220,000 | $55,000 | $165,000 |
| Year 2 | 165,000 | 55,000 | 110,000 |
| Year 3 | 110,000 | 55,000 | 55,000 |
| Year 4 | 55,000 | 55,000 | 0 |
| **Total** | | **$220,000** | |

Rollforward check: 220,000 − 4 × 55,000 = **0**. Schedule balances.

**c. Period-end adjusting entry (each of Dec. 31, Year 1–Year 4)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 55,000 | |
| Unearned Compensation—Equity | | 55,000 |

**Check each year:** Dr = Cr = **55,000**.  
Four years × $55,000 = **$220,000** total expense.

**d. Balances after Year 4 amortization (full vesting / restrictions lift)**  
- Unearned Compensation—Equity: **$0**  
- Common Stock (this award): **$4,000** (remains outstanding; restrictions lift)  
- Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—CS (this award): **$216,000**

Net equity from the award after full vesting and full expense recognition equals the original $220,000 of common equity issued, with expense of $220,000 having reduced retained earnings over the four years. No separate “settlement” JE is needed for an RSA once unearned compensation is fully amortized.

**e. RSA vs RSU at vesting**  
Under an **RSA**, shares were already issued at grant (offset by unearned compensation). At vesting, restrictions lapse—no re-issuance entry is required once unearned compensation has been fully amortized. Under an **RSU**, shares are **not** issued until vesting; a settlement entry reclassifies Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock into Common Stock and PIC in Excess of Par.

**Key insight:** Changing share count, par, fair value, and service years scales the same RSA pattern: **one grant-date equity issuance**, then a **straight-line subsequent measurement schedule** until unearned compensation is zero.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Restricted stock units: expense schedule, estimated forfeitures, change in estimate, and settlement at vesting
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** RSU period-end adjusting JEs; subsequent measurement schedule with estimated forfeitures; cumulative catch-up for change in estimate; settlement JE when units vest and shares are issued  
**Scenario:**  
On **January 1, Year 1**, **Silverline Logistics Inc.** grants a total of **8,000 restricted stock units** to eight key managers (**1,000 RSUs each**). Each unit exchanges for **one share** of **$2 par** common stock after a **four-year** requisite service period.

- Grant-date fair value = **$25** per share.  
- No shares are issued at grant.  

**Policy:** Silverline **estimates forfeitures** (entity-wide election under ASC 718).  
On January 1, Year 1, Silverline estimates that **1,600 units** (20%) will be forfeited due to turnover over the four-year period.

**Additional facts:**  
- **December 31, Year 1:** No actual forfeitures; original estimate still appropriate.  
- **December 31, Year 2:** No actual forfeitures have yet occurred, but Silverline **revises** the estimated total forfeitures upward to **2,400 units** (30% of 8,000).  
- **December 31, Year 3 and Year 4:** No further estimate changes. Actual cumulative forfeitures by the end of Year 4 equal **2,400 units** (matches revised estimate). Remaining **5,600 units** vest.  
- **January 1, Year 5:** Silverline issues **5,600** common shares for the vested units.

**Required:**  
a. Compute **grant-date total fair value** before forfeitures and **expected compensation cost** using the Year 1 forfeiture estimate. State whether a journal entry is required on January 1, Year 1.  
b. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1** compensation expense entry.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement schedule** of cumulative expected compensation cost and period expense for Years 1–4, incorporating the Year 2 change in estimate. Show for each year: expected total compensation (after estimate), cumulative to-date fraction, target cumulative PIC—Restricted Stock, prior cumulative expense, and **current-period expense**.  
d. Prepare the **December 31, Year 2** period-end entry (with cumulative catch-up).  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 5 settlement (issuance) journal entry** for the vested units.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Grant-date totals; no grant JE for RSUs**  
Gross grant-date fair value = 8,000 × $25 = **$200,000**.  
Expected units that will vest (Y1 estimate) = 8,000 − 1,600 = **6,400**.  
Expected compensation cost = 6,400 × $25 = **$160,000** (equivalently $200,000 × 80%).  

**No journal entry on January 1, Year 1** — RSUs do not issue stock at grant; total cost is measured at grant-date FV and recognized over the service period via Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock.

**b. December 31, Year 1 — Period-end adjusting JE**  
Annual expense (Y1 estimate) = $160,000 ÷ 4 = **$40,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 40,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | | 40,000 |
| *Recognize 1/4 of expected compensation ($160,000)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **40,000**. Balanced.

**c. Subsequent measurement schedule (with Year 2 estimate change)**  

*Year 1 basis:* expected total = **$160,000** (6,400 units × $25).  
*Year 2 revised basis:* expected units = 8,000 − 2,400 = **5,600**; expected total = 5,600 × $25 = **$140,000**.

| Year | Expected total comp | Service fraction cumulative | Target cumulative PIC—RS | Prior cumulative exp. | **Current-period exp.** | Ending PIC—RS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 160,000 | 1/4 | 40,000 | 0 | **40,000** | 40,000 |
| 2 | 140,000 *(revised)* | 2/4 | 70,000 | 40,000 | **30,000** | 70,000 |
| 3 | 140,000 | 3/4 | 105,000 | 70,000 | **35,000** | 105,000 |
| 4 | 140,000 | 4/4 | 140,000 | 105,000 | **35,000** | 140,000 |
| **Total** | | | | | **$140,000** | |

Year 2 catch-up math: target cumulative after 2/4 of revised total = $140,000 × 2/4 = **$70,000**; prior expense $40,000 → current expense **$30,000**.  
Years 3–4: remaining $70,000 over 2 years = **$35,000** each year.  
Total expense over life = **$140,000** = final expected cost. Schedule balances.

**d. December 31, Year 2 — Period-end JE (cumulative catch-up)**  

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compensation Expense | 30,000 | |
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | | 30,000 |
| *Target cumulative PIC $70,000 − prior $40,000 = $30,000* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = **30,000**. Balanced.  
Cumulative PIC—Restricted Stock after Y2 entry: **$70,000**.

**e. January 1, Year 5 — Settlement / issuance of vested shares**  
Vested units = **5,600**.  
Balance in PIC—Restricted Stock = **$140,000** (equals 5,600 × $25).  
Common stock = 5,600 × $2 = **$11,200**.  
PIC in excess of par = $140,000 − $11,200 = **$128,800**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock | 140,000 | |
| Common Stock (5,600 × $2) | | 11,200 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 128,800 |
| *Issue common shares upon RSU vesting / settlement* | | |

**Check:** Dr = **140,000**; Cr 11,200 + 128,800 = **140,000**. Balanced.

**Key insight:** RSUs build **Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock** over the service period (no stock at grant). Estimated forfeitures reduce expected cost; a change in estimate is handled with **cumulative catch-up** so that cumulative PIC equals (revised expected total × fraction of service completed). At vesting, PIC—Restricted Stock is reclassified into Common Stock and APIC.

### Q4 — MC — Classification of unearned compensation
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Classification of Unearned Compensation—Equity for restricted stock awards  
**Question:**  
On the grant date of equity-classified **restricted stock awards**, the company debits **Unearned Compensation—Equity** for the grant-date fair value of the shares issued into trust. How is Unearned Compensation—Equity presented?

- A) As a prepaid asset (other current asset) amortized like prepaid rent  
- B) As a long-term liability until the shares vest  
- C) As a **contra-equity** account within stockholders’ equity  
- D) As a temporary income-statement account closed each period before amortization begins  

**Answer:** **C.** Unearned Compensation—Equity is a **contra-equity** account. Restricted stock awards increase Common Stock and APIC at grant, offset by the unearned contra; amortization reduces the contra and recognizes Compensation Expense. It is **not** an asset or liability.

### Q4b — MC — RSU grant-date recognition
**LO:** LO 20-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition difference — restricted stock units vs restricted stock awards  
**Question:**  
On the grant date of **restricted stock units** (time-based, equity-classified), with total grant-date fair value measured but no shares issued yet, the required journal entry is:

- A) Debit Unearned Compensation—Equity; credit Common Stock and Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par  
- B) **No journal entry** on the grant date (measure total cost; begin expensing over the service period)  
- C) Debit Compensation Expense for the full grant-date fair value; credit Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock  
- D) Debit Cash; credit Unearned Compensation—Equity  

**Answer:** **B.** Unlike RSAs, **RSUs do not issue shares at grant**. Total compensation is measured at grant-date fair value, but recognition of Compensation Expense (with a credit to Paid-in Capital—Restricted Stock) occurs over the **requisite service period**. Choice A describes an RSA grant entry; choice C would front-load the entire cost, which is incorrect for a multi-year service condition.
