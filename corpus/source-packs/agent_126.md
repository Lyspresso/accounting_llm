# Agent 126 — CORE DEMO — LO 21-3

**Chapter:** 21  
**LO title:** Describe the accounting for a change in reporting entity  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Definition / scope:** A change in reporting entity produces financial statements that are, in effect, those of a **different reporting entity** (ASC Glossary). Limited mainly to: (a) consolidated or combined FS in place of individual-entity FS; (b) changing subsidiaries in the consolidated group; (c) changing entities in combined FS
- **Not a change in reporting entity:** Business combination under the **acquisition method**; consolidation of a **VIE** under Topic 810
- **Initial recognition / conversion JE:** When a previously unconsolidated investee is brought into consolidation (or when converting cost-method investment to equity for consolidation), record the retrospective catch-up to the investment and **beginning retained earnings** for post-acquisition undistributed earnings; eliminate Investment against sub equity
- **Retrospective approach:** Restate **all prior periods presented** as if the new reporting entity had always been in place (ASC 250-10-45-21); also restate previously issued interim information; do **not** change interest cost previously capitalized under Subtopic 835-20
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** Multi-year schedule of previously reported (separate) NI/EPS vs restated consolidated/combined NI/EPS; RE cumulative-effect bridge at beginning of earliest period presented
- **Period-end adjusting / eliminating JE:** Intercompany receivable/payable (and similar) eliminations so consolidated totals are not overstated
- **Disposal / deconsolidation (composition change):** When an entity previously consolidated (or combined) **no longer meets criteria**, remove it from the reporting entity and apply the change **retrospectively** (not the same as an acquisition-method business combination or a simple asset sale)
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-6):** Nature and reason for the change; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, OCI, and related **EPS** for **all periods presented**; cumulative effect on RE (or other equity) as of the beginning of the **earliest period presented**; subsequent periods need not repeat the note
- **Number-variant twin:** Same consolidation-from-separate path with fully changed amounts

### Q1 — CORE — Begin consolidating a previously unconsolidated subsidiary: conversion JE, restatement schedule, elims, disclosure
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Initial equity-method conversion and consolidation eliminating entries; retrospective restatement schedule of NI and EPS; period-end intercompany elim; ASC 250 disclosure; contrast with acquisition  
**Scenario:**  
**Pinecrest Holdings Inc.** (calendar year-end) has owned **100%** of **Oakdale Precision LLC** since **January 1, Year 1**. Through Year 2, Pinecrest issued **parent-only** financial statements and accounted for Oakdale under the **cost method** (dividend income only). Oakdale was not consolidated.

Effective for the **Year 3** annual report (with **comparative Year 2**), Pinecrest determines that Oakdale meets the criteria for consolidation and will present **consolidated** financial statements. There is **no** goodwill (acquisition was at book value). **Ignore income taxes.** Weighted-average common shares outstanding: **100,000** in all years (no preferred stock).

**Acquisition and Oakdale equity data:**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash paid for 100% of Oakdale, January 1, Year 1 (= book value) | $160,000 |
| Oakdale common stock (constant) | 120,000 |
| Oakdale retained earnings, January 1, Year 1 | 40,000 |

| Oakdale | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $60,000 | $75,000 | $90,000 |
| Dividends declared and paid | 15,000 | 20,000 | 25,000 |

**Pinecrest separate-entity results (cost method):**

| Pinecrest | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating income (excludes dividend income) | $300,000 | $340,000 | $380,000 |
| Dividend income from Oakdale | 15,000 | 20,000 | 25,000 |
| Separate-entity net income | 315,000 | 360,000 | 405,000 |

**Additional facts:**
1. On **January 1, Year 1**, Pinecrest recorded the acquisition of Oakdale.
2. At **December 31, Year 3**, Pinecrest has a **$38,000** receivable from Oakdale; Oakdale has the corresponding payable (open account; will be settled in Year 4).
3. No intercompany inventory profits or other intercompany sales remain in ending inventory.

**Required:**  
a. Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition JE** for Pinecrest’s acquisition of Oakdale.  
b. Classify the Year 3 change. State the required **accounting approach** (retrospective vs prospective) and what periods are affected.  
c. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / restatement schedule** for Years 1–3 showing: (i) previously reported parent-only NI and EPS; (ii) restated consolidated NI and EPS; (iii) period-specific effect of the change.  
d. Compute the **cumulative effect on retained earnings** as of the beginning of the **earliest period presented** (January 1, Year 2) and as of January 1, Year 3.  
e. Prepare the **January 1, Year 3 conversion JE** that places the investment on the **equity method** for post-acquisition undistributed earnings through December 31, Year 2 (retrospective catch-up to beginning RE).  
f. Prepare the **basic consolidation eliminating entry** against Oakdale’s equity and the investment account **as of January 1, Year 3** (after the conversion in e).  
g. Prepare the **December 31, Year 3 period-end eliminating JE** for the intercompany receivable/payable.  
h. **Classification / disclosure:** List the ASC 250-10-50-6 disclosures required in the Year 3 statements.  
i. **Contrast:** If instead Pinecrest had **first purchased** Oakdale on January 1, Year 3 in an acquisition-method business combination, would that be a change in reporting entity? How would prior periods be treated?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Initial recognition — acquisition of Oakdale (January 1, Year 1)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Oakdale Precision LLC | 160,000 | |
| Cash | | 160,000 |
| *Acquire 100% of Oakdale at book value* | | |

**Check:** Dr 160,000 = Cr 160,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Classification and approach**

- **Type:** **Change in reporting entity** — presenting **consolidated** financial statements in place of **individual-entity** (parent-only) statements, and changing the composition of entities for which consolidated statements are presented (ASC Glossary; Demo 21-3 item 1).  
- **Approach:** **Retrospective.** Restate **all prior periods presented** (here Year 2 comparative, and any Year 1 history used in multi-year schedules/notes) as if Oakdale had always been consolidated. Previously issued **interim** information is also presented retrospectively.  
- **Not** prospective; **not** a prior-period error correction.

**c. Restatement schedule (subsequent measurement of reported results under the new entity)**

Oakdale RE rollforward (for reference):

| Date | Computation | Oakdale RE |
|---|---|---:|
| Jan 1, Y1 | given | 40,000 |
| Dec 31, Y1 | 40,000 + 60,000 − 15,000 | 85,000 |
| Dec 31, Y2 | 85,000 + 75,000 − 20,000 | 140,000 |
| Dec 31, Y3 | 140,000 + 90,000 − 25,000 | 205,000 |

Consolidated NI (no unrealized intercompany profits)  
= Pinecrest **operating** income + Oakdale NI  
(equivalently: parent separate NI − dividend income + Oakdale NI)

| Year | Parent-only NI (as previously reported) | Parent-only EPS | Consolidated NI (restated) | Consolidated EPS | Effect on NI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $315,000 | $3.15 | $300,000 + $60,000 = **$360,000** | **$3.60** | **+$45,000** |
| 2 | 360,000 | 3.60 | 340,000 + 75,000 = **415,000** | **4.15** | **+55,000** |
| 3 (current) | 405,000 | 4.05 | 380,000 + 90,000 = **470,000** | **4.70** | **+65,000** |

EPS checks: \(360{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 3.60\); \(415{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 4.15\); \(470{,}000 / 100{,}000 = 4.70\).

**d. Cumulative effect on beginning retained earnings**

Post-acquisition **undistributed** Oakdale earnings = Oakdale NI − dividends:

| Period | NI − Div | Undistributed |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | 60,000 − 15,000 | 45,000 |
| Year 2 | 75,000 − 20,000 | 55,000 |
| Years 1–2 cumulative | | **100,000** |

- **As of January 1, Year 2** (beginning of earliest period presented in the Year 3 comparative report): cumulative effect on RE = **$45,000** increase (Year 1 undistributed earnings of the sub not previously in consolidated RE).  
- **As of January 1, Year 3:** cumulative effect on RE = **$100,000** increase.

**e. January 1, Year 3 — equity-method conversion (retrospective catch-up / “initial” consolidation setup on parent books)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Oakdale Precision LLC | 100,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 100,000 |
| *Recognize post-acquisition undistributed earnings of Oakdale for Years 1–2 (cost → equity conversion for consolidation)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000. **Balanced.**  
Investment carrying amount after conversion = \(160{,}000 + 100{,}000 = \mathbf{\$260{,}000}\).

**f. January 1, Year 3 — basic consolidation eliminating entry**

Oakdale equity at January 1, Year 3: CS \(120{,}000\) + RE \(140{,}000\) = **$260,000** (= investment after conversion).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock—Oakdale | 120,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Oakdale | 140,000 | |
| Investment in Oakdale Precision LLC | | 260,000 |
| *Eliminate 100% investment against Oakdale’s equity at beginning of Year 3* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(120{,}000 + 140{,}000 = 260{,}000\) = Cr 260,000. **Balanced.**

**g. December 31, Year 3 — period-end intercompany eliminating JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intercompany Payable (Due to Pinecrest) | 38,000 | |
| Intercompany Receivable (Due from Oakdale) | | 38,000 |
| *Eliminate reciprocal intercompany balances in consolidation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 38,000 = Cr 38,000. **Balanced.**

**h. Disclosure (ASC 250-10-50-6) — Year 3 (period of change)**

1. **Nature** of the change (Oakdale now consolidated; previously parent-only) and **reason**.  
2. Effect of the change on **income from continuing operations**, **net income**, **OCI** (none here), and related **per-share amounts** for **all periods presented** (at least Year 2 restated and Year 3). Example: Year 2 NI higher by **$55,000**; EPS higher by **$0.55** (\(4.15 - 3.60\)).  
3. **Cumulative effect** on retained earnings as of the beginning of the earliest period presented: **+$45,000** at January 1, Year 2.  
4. Financial statements of **subsequent periods need not repeat** these disclosures.  
5. If the change is immaterial in the period of change but reasonably certain to be material later, still disclose nature and reason when presenting the period of change.

**i. Contrast — acquisition on January 1, Year 3**

**No.** A business combination accounted for by the **acquisition method** is **not** a change in reporting entity. Results are combined **prospectively from the acquisition date**; **prior periods are not restated** as if the acquiree had always been included (though certain pro forma disclosures may be required under other guidance). That is the opposite of the retrospective CRE treatment in parts b–h.

**Key insight:** Changing **who** is inside the reporting boundary (consolidate a sub that was out, or change the combined group) is a **change in reporting entity** applied **retrospectively**. Buying a company under the acquisition method is **not** a CRE—prior periods stay as previously reported.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Cost-to-equity conversion, restatement schedule, elims, disclosure
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial acquisition JE; retrospective consolidation of previously separate sub; RE cumulative effect; conversion and eliminating JEs; period-end IC elim; disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverbrook Group Inc.** has owned **100%** of **Tamarack Labs Inc.** since **January 1, Year 1**, accounted for under the **cost method**, and historically issued **parent-only** statements. Beginning with **Year 3** comparative statements (Year 2 restated), Silverbrook will **consolidate** Tamarack — a **change in reporting entity**. Acquisition at book value; **ignore taxes**. WASO: **120,000** shares all years.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cash paid for 100% of Tamarack, January 1, Year 1 | $240,000 |
| Tamarack common stock (constant) | 180,000 |
| Tamarack RE, January 1, Year 1 | 60,000 |

| Tamarack | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net income | $90,000 | $110,000 | $130,000 |
| Dividends | 30,000 | 40,000 | 50,000 |

| Silverbrook (cost method) | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating income (ex dividend income) | $450,000 | $500,000 | $560,000 |
| Dividend income from Tamarack | 30,000 | 40,000 | 50,000 |
| Separate-entity net income | 480,000 | 540,000 | 610,000 |

At **December 31, Year 3**, Silverbrook has a **$52,000** receivable from Tamarack (reciprocal payable on Tamarack’s books). No unrealized intercompany inventory profit.

**Required:**  
a. January 1, Year 1 **initial recognition JE** for the acquisition.  
b. Classify the change and state the accounting approach.  
c. **Restatement schedule** of parent-only vs consolidated NI and EPS for Years 1–3; show period effects.  
d. Cumulative effect on RE at **January 1, Year 2** and **January 1, Year 3**.  
e. **January 1, Year 3** equity-method conversion JE (undistributed earnings Years 1–2).  
f. **January 1, Year 3** basic consolidation eliminating entry after conversion.  
g. **December 31, Year 3** intercompany receivable/payable eliminating JE.  
h. State the **minimum disclosure** elements for Year 3 under ASC 250-10-50-6 (include the Year 2 NI and EPS effects as dollar amounts).

**Answer key:**  

**a. January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Tamarack Labs Inc. | 240,000 | |
| Cash | | 240,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 240,000. **Balanced.**

**b. Classification / approach**  
**Change in reporting entity**; **retrospective** application to all prior periods presented (and prior interim information). Not prospective; not an acquisition-method combination.

**c. Restatement schedule**

Tamarack RE: Jan 1 Y1 **60,000** → Dec 31 Y1 \(60+90-30=\mathbf{120{,}000}\) → Dec 31 Y2 \(120+110-40=\mathbf{190{,}000}\) → Dec 31 Y3 \(190+130-50=\mathbf{270{,}000}\).

Consolidated NI = Silverbrook operating income + Tamarack NI:

| Year | Parent-only NI | Parent-only EPS | Consolidated NI | Consolidated EPS | Effect on NI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $480,000 | $4.00 | $450,000 + $90,000 = **$540,000** | **$4.50** | **+$60,000** |
| 2 | 540,000 | 4.50 | 500,000 + 110,000 = **610,000** | **5.0833… ≈ $5.08** | **+70,000** |
| 3 | 610,000 | 5.0833… ≈ 5.08 | 560,000 + 130,000 = **690,000** | **$5.75** | **+80,000** |

EPS math: \(480{,}000/120{,}000=4.00\); \(540{,}000/120{,}000=4.50\); \(610{,}000/120{,}000=5.0833\ldots\); \(690{,}000/120{,}000=5.75\).

**d. Cumulative effect on beginning RE**

Undistributed: Y1 \(90-30=\mathbf{60{,}000}\); Y2 \(110-40=\mathbf{70{,}000}\); cumulative Y1–Y2 = **$130,000**.

- January 1, Year 2 (earliest period if Y2–Y3 comparative): **+$60,000**  
- January 1, Year 3: **+$130,000**

**e. January 1, Year 3 — conversion JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Tamarack Labs Inc. | 130,000 | |
| Retained Earnings | | 130,000 |
| *Cost → equity conversion for Years 1–2 undistributed earnings* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 130,000. **Balanced.**  
Investment after conversion = \(240{,}000 + 130{,}000 = \mathbf{\$370{,}000}\).

**f. January 1, Year 3 — eliminating entry**

Tamarack equity Jan 1 Y3: \(180{,}000 + 190{,}000 = 370{,}000\).

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common Stock—Tamarack | 180,000 | |
| Retained Earnings—Tamarack | 190,000 | |
| Investment in Tamarack Labs Inc. | | 370,000 |

**Check:** Dr \(180{,}000 + 190{,}000 = 370{,}000\) = Cr 370,000. **Balanced.**

**g. December 31, Year 3 — period-end IC elim**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intercompany Payable | 52,000 | |
| Intercompany Receivable | | 52,000 |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 52,000. **Balanced.**

**h. Disclosure elements (with amounts)**  
Nature and reason for consolidating Tamarack; effect on income from continuing operations / net income / EPS for all periods presented — e.g., Year 2 NI **+$70,000**, Year 2 EPS from **$4.50** to **≈$5.08**; cumulative effect on RE at beginning of earliest period presented (**+$60,000** at Jan 1, Year 2); subsequent periods need not repeat.

**Key insight:** Same CRE mechanics as Q1 with new numbers: **undistributed sub earnings** drive both the RE cumulative effect and the investment conversion debit; consolidated NI replaces dividend income with the sub’s full results.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Common-control combination, deconsolidation “settlement,” and non-CRE acquisition contrast
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Combined financial statements under common control as a change in reporting entity; multi-year combined schedule; deconsolidation removing an entity from the reporting group; distinguish acquisition-method combination (not CRE)  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborline Brands Co.** and **Seaglass Retail LLC** have been under **common control** of the same family holding company since Year 1. Through Year 3 each issued **separate** financial statements only. In **Year 4**, after a reorganization, management presents **combined** financial statements of Harborline and Seaglass for Year 4 with **comparative Year 3** (and discloses Year 2 for the multi-year note). No intercompany transactions. **Ignore taxes.**

| Separate NI | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Harborline Brands Co. | $200,000 | $220,000 | $250,000 |
| Seaglass Retail LLC | 80,000 | 95,000 | 110,000 |

**Part B — Deconsolidation / composition change (Year 5):**  
On **January 1, Year 5**, Seaglass **no longer meets the criteria** for inclusion in the combined group (common-control combination criteria fail). Harborline will report **without** Seaglass. Seaglass remains a separate legal entity (not sold). At January 1, Year 5, Seaglass’s stand-alone balances are:

| Account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Identifiable assets (various) | $400,000 |
| Liabilities (various) | 90,000 |
| Equity (net assets) | 310,000 |

Assume the combined reporting entity had been keeping a single set of “combined books” that included Seaglass’s accounts. Management records a **deconsolidation entry** on January 1, Year 5, establishing an investment (or removing net assets) at carrying amount.

**Part C — Contrast fact:** On **March 1, Year 5**, Harborline **acquires** unrelated **Driftwood Snacks Inc.** for cash in an acquisition-method business combination.

**Required:**  
a. Classify the Year 4 change to combined reporting. State the accounting approach.  
b. Prepare a **combined results schedule** for Years 2–4 (combined NI) and the **period-specific effect** vs Harborline-only NI.  
c. Cumulative effect on equity of the combined entity at **January 1, Year 3** (beginning of earliest comparative period in the Year 4 report) attributable to including Seaglass’s post–Year 1 earnings retained — assume Seaglass RE at Jan 1 Y2 was **$150,000** and Seaglass paid **no dividends** in Years 2–3 (so Y2–Y3 NI all retained). Show the bridge.  
d. Prepare the **January 1, Year 5 deconsolidation (settlement of combination relationship) JE** that removes Seaglass’s assets and liabilities and records Investment in Seaglass at net carrying amount.  
e. After the Year 5 composition change, how are **Year 4 comparative** figures presented in the Year 5 report?  
f. Is the **March 1, Year 5 acquisition** of Driftwood a change in reporting entity? Explain prior-period treatment.  
g. **Disclosure:** What must Harborline disclose in Year 4 regarding the change to combined statements?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**  
**Change in reporting entity** — presenting **combined** financial statements in place of financial statements of **individual** entities, and changing entities included in combined FS.  
**Retrospective** application to all prior periods presented.

**b. Combined results schedule**

| Year | Harborline-only NI | Seaglass NI | Combined NI | Effect vs Harborline-only |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | $200,000 | $80,000 | **$280,000** | **+$80,000** |
| 3 | 220,000 | 95,000 | **315,000** | **+95,000** |
| 4 | 250,000 | 110,000 | **360,000** | **+110,000** |

**c. Cumulative effect on equity at January 1, Year 3**

Seaglass RE at Jan 1 Y2 given **$150,000**. No dividends Years 2–3; Year 2 NI **$80,000** all retained.  
Equity of Seaglass at Jan 1 Y3 = \(150{,}000 + 80{,}000 = \mathbf{\$230{,}000}\).

When the combined entity first presents comparative Year 3, the **cumulative effect on equity at the beginning of the earliest period presented (Jan 1, Year 3)** from including Seaglass equals Seaglass’s equity at that date (**$230,000**), assuming Harborline-only equity is the other component of combined equity and there is no new basis under common control (carryover basis).

(If the note focuses only on **earnings** retained after a chosen start date: Year 2 Seaglass NI included in restated combined RE = **$80,000** incremental vs Harborline-only.)

**d. January 1, Year 5 — deconsolidation / settlement JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investment in Seaglass Retail LLC | 310,000 | |
| Liabilities (various—Seaglass) | 90,000 | |
| Assets (various—Seaglass) | | 400,000 |
| *Remove Seaglass from the combined reporting entity; record investment at carrying amount of net assets* | | |

**Check:** Dr \(310{,}000 + 90{,}000 = 400{,}000\) = Cr 400,000. **Balanced.**

**e. Year 5 comparative presentation**  
Because removing Seaglass from the reporting entity is itself a **change in reporting entity**, Year 4 (and any other prior periods presented) are **restated retrospectively** to **exclude** Seaglass — as if the new (Harborline-only) reporting entity had been in place in those periods (Demo 21-3 item 2). Combined Year 4 NI of $360,000 would be restated to Harborline-only **$250,000** when presented comparatively in Year 5 (absent other adjustments).

**f. Acquisition of Driftwood**  
**Not** a change in reporting entity. Acquisition-method business combinations are **excluded** from the CRE definition. Driftwood’s results are included from **March 1, Year 5 forward**; **prior periods are not restated** to include Driftwood.

**g. Year 4 disclosures (ASC 250-10-50-6)**  
Nature and reason for presenting combined statements of Harborline and Seaglass; effect on income from continuing operations, net income, OCI, and EPS for all periods presented (e.g., Year 3 NI higher by **$95,000**); cumulative effect on equity/RE at beginning of earliest period presented; subsequent periods need not repeat.

**Key insight:** CRE is about the **reporting boundary** (who is in the statements), applied **retrospectively**. **Common-control combination** and **criteria-based deconsolidation** are CREs; **acquisition-method purchases** are not—even though the group’s legal composition changes.

### Q4 — MC — Accounting treatment for a change in reporting entity
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Retrospective application is required for a change in reporting entity (not prospective; not merely a prospective prior-period adjusting entry)  
**Question:**  
A change in reporting entity is accounted for through:

- A) A prior-period adjusting entry only, with no restatement of comparative financial statements  
- B) Retrospective application of the new reporting entity to all prior periods presented  
- C) Restatement and reissuance of prior statements solely because an error was found  
- D) Prospective application of the new reporting entity to future periods only  

**Answer:** **B.**  
ASC 250-10-45-21 requires that when an accounting change results in financial statements that are, in effect, those of a **different reporting entity**, the change is **retrospectively applied** to the financial statements of **all prior periods presented**. Option A understates the requirement (comparative periods must be recast). Option C confuses CRE with **error correction**. Option D is the treatment for **changes in estimate**, not CRE.

### Q5 — MC — Identifying which event is a change in reporting entity
**LO:** LO 21-3  
**Concept:** Classify events as CRE vs acquisition-method combination vs estimate/principle; only CRE items get retrospective entity restatement under LO 21-3  
**Question:**  
Which of the following is **both** a change in reporting entity **and** accounted for retrospectively?

- A) Parent acquires 100% of an unrelated company for cash on July 1 and consolidates from the acquisition date under the acquisition method  
- B) Parent changes from FIFO to weighted-average inventory costing for all inventories  
- C) Parent begins presenting consolidated financial statements that include a wholly owned subsidiary that previously issued only separate, unconsolidated parent-only statements (subsidiary now meets consolidation criteria)  
- D) Parent revises the useful life of manufacturing equipment from 10 years to 7 years  

**Answer:** **C.**  
Presenting consolidated statements in place of individual-entity statements (or changing which subsidiaries are consolidated) is a classic **change in reporting entity** and is applied **retrospectively**.  

- **A is wrong:** Acquisition-method business combinations are **explicitly not** changes in reporting entity; prior periods are **not** recast as if the acquiree were always consolidated.  
- **B is wrong:** Inventory method change is a **change in accounting principle** (LO 21-2), not a change in reporting entity.  
- **D is wrong:** Useful-life revision is a **change in estimate** (prospective) under LO 21-1.
