# Agent 261 — CORE DEMO — LO 22-8

**Chapter:** 22  
**LO title:** Prepare a statement of cash flows using the cash T-account approach  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis:** subsequent_measurement_schedule

## Concept list for this pack
- **Cash T-account approach (core Demo 22-8):** recreate income-statement and balance-sheet activity as reconstructed journal entries; post only **cash legs** into a cash T-account organized by **operating / investing / financing**; prepare the SCF (direct method for operating activities) from that T-account
- **Algebraic identity:** \(\Delta\)Cash (including cash equivalents and restricted cash) \(= -\Delta\)Noncash assets \(+ \Delta\)Liabilities \(+ \Delta\)Equity
- **Initial recognition / reconstructed cash JEs:** collections from customers; payments to suppliers, employees, and for interest; cash PPE purchases; cash debt/equity issuances
- **Subsequent measurement schedules (emphasis):** AR, inventory/AP, salaries payable, equipment, accumulated depreciation, and retained earnings rollforwards used to solve for cash and noncash amounts before building the cash T-account
- **Period-end adjusting JE (noncash):** depreciation posted to noncash T-accounts only — **never** a line in the cash T-account
- **Disposal / maturity / settlement JE:** PPE sale (cash proceeds + remove cost/AD + gain or loss); principal repayment or bond maturity as financing cash outflow
- **Classification / presentation:** O/I/F sections from the cash T-account; report investing and financing inflows and outflows **separately**; reconcile beginning to ending **cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash**; gains/losses are not cash lines under the direct/T-account approach (only proceeds appear in investing)

**f. Classification of the equipment disposal**  
The **loss** is a noncash income-statement amount used only to complete the reconstructed disposal entry and the AD/equipment schedules. Under the cash T-account / direct approach, the statement reports only the **cash proceeds of $270,000** as an **investing inflow**. The loss is **not** a separate cash line. (Under the indirect method, the loss would be **added back** to net income in operating activities so the full proceeds can be shown in investing — same economic result.)

**Key insight:** Subsequent measurement schedules solve for every unknown (collections, supplier payments, purchases, dividends) before any cash hits the T-account. Noncash period-end entries (depreciation) and noncash portions of disposals (AD removal, gain/loss) never enter the cash T-account; only true cash legs do. Beginning and ending cash on the SCF include **restricted cash**.

### Q4 — MC — What posts to the cash T-account
**LO:** LO 22-8  
**Concept:** Classification — which reconstructed items appear in the cash T-account; restricted cash in the SCF reconciliation  
**Question:**  
Under the **cash T-account approach** (Demo 22-8), a company reconstructs the following items for the year. Which set is **correct** about what appears in the **cash T-account** and how **restricted cash** is treated on the statement of cash flows prepared from that T-account?

- A) Depreciation expense is posted as an operating cash outflow in the cash T-account; restricted cash is excluded from the beginning and ending cash totals on the SCF.  
- B) Only the **cash proceeds** from a PPE sale post to the cash T-account (investing); the related loss does **not** appear as a cash line; beginning and ending cash on the SCF include **cash + cash equivalents + restricted cash**.  
- C) The full book value of equipment sold is the investing cash inflow in the cash T-account; restricted cash changes are classified as financing activities.  
- D) Bond principal repaid at maturity is an operating cash outflow in the cash T-account because interest is also operating; restricted cash is reported only in a supplemental disclosure, never in the cash reconciliation.

**Answer:** **B.**  
The cash T-account receives only **true cash legs**. Depreciation is noncash and never posts. On a PPE disposal, only **cash proceeds** hit investing; gain/loss and AD removal are noncash. Bond **principal** settlement is **financing**, not operating. ASC 230 requires the SCF change to reconcile **cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash** in the aggregate.
