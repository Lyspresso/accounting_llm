# Agent 337 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-1

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Calculate depreciation using straight-line, sum-of-the-years’-digits, declining-balance, and units-of-production methods  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Depreciation is cost allocation, not valuation:** systematic and rational transfer of depreciable cost to expense; credit **Accumulated Depreciation** (contra asset)
- **Terminology:** carrying amount (book value) = cost − AD; residual/salvage; useful life; depreciable cost (cost − residual)
- **Straight-line (uniform):** annual expense = (cost − residual) ÷ useful life in years; rate = 1/n of depreciable cost each year
- **Sum-of-the-years’-digits (accelerated):** depreciable cost × (years remaining at BOY) / [n(n+1)/2]
- **Declining-balance (accelerated):** beginning carrying amount × (multiple × 1/n); residual is a **floor**, not subtracted from the base; final year may be a **plug** to residual
- **Units-of-production (activity):** (depreciable cost ÷ estimated total units) × actual units; total expense capped at depreciable cost
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize PPE at acquisition cost when ready for intended use
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** Dr Depreciation Expense / Cr Accumulated Depreciation for the period’s computed amount under the elected method
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year rollforward of expense, AD, and carrying amount ending at residual
- **Disposal / settlement JE:** update depreciation through disposal date if needed; remove cost and related AD; gain/loss = proceeds − carrying amount
- **Presentation:** equipment at historical cost less accumulated depreciation (net PPE); manufacturing dep. may flow through inventory/COGS; nonmanufacturing dep. is period SG&A

### Q1 — CORE — Four methods, period-end AJs (emphasis), full schedules, BS, disposal
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Initial recognition; **period-end depreciation adjusting JEs** under SL, SYD, DDB, and UOP (emphasis); full subsequent-measurement schedules for all four methods; balance sheet presentation; disposal settlement JE under straight-line  
**Scenario:**  
**LumenForge Tooling Co.** (calendar year-end) purchases a CNC machining center on **January 1, Year 1** for **$90,000 cash**. The asset is ready for intended use on that date. Management estimates:

| Fact | Amount |
|---|---|
| Acquisition cost (cash) | $90,000 |
| Estimated residual (salvage) value | $6,000 |
| Estimated useful life | 5 years |
| Estimated productive output | 12,000 units |
| Actual output — Year 1 | 2,400 units |
| Actual output — Year 2 | 2,800 units |
| Actual output — Year 3 | 2,600 units |
| Actual output — Year 4 | 2,200 units |
| Actual output — Year 5 | 2,000 units |

Ignore income taxes. Depreciation is recorded once annually at **December 31**.

**Required:**  
(a) Prepare the **January 1, Year 1 initial recognition** journal entry for the purchase.  
(b) **(Emphasis)** Compute **Year 1** depreciation under each method: (i) straight-line, (ii) sum-of-the-years’-digits, (iii) double-declining-balance, (iv) units-of-production. Prepare the **December 31, Year 1 period-end adjusting JE** for each method as four **alternative** entries (assume one method is elected). Prove each balances.  
(c) Prepare complete **subsequent measurement schedules** (Years 1–5) for **all four methods**, showing depreciation expense, accumulated depreciation, and carrying amount each year-end. Confirm ending carrying amount equals residual and total expense equals depreciable cost. For DDB, show any **forced plug** in the final year.  
(d) Show the **balance sheet presentation** of the machine at December 31, Year 1 **assuming straight-line**.  
(e) Assume the company uses **straight-line**. After recording Year 3 depreciation, it **sells** the machine on **December 31, Year 3** for **$42,000 cash**. Prepare the **disposal journal entry** and compute the gain or loss.

**Answer key:**

**Depreciable cost (allocation base)** = $90,000 − $6,000 = **$84,000** for all methods that use residual in the base (SL, SYD, UOP). DDB applies the rate to **carrying amount** and stops at residual.

**(a) January 1, Year 1 — initial recognition**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment—CNC Machining Center | 90,000 | |
| Cash | | 90,000 |
| *Capitalize acquisition cost when ready for intended use* | | |

**Check:** Dr 90,000 = Cr 90,000. **Balanced.**

**(b) Year 1 depreciation computations and period-end adjusting JEs (emphasis)**

**(i) Straight-line**  
Annual SL = $84,000 ÷ 5 = **$16,800**  
SL rate on depreciable cost = $16,800 ÷ $84,000 = **20%** (also 1/5)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC | 16,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC | | 16,800 |
| *Dec 31, Y1 — SL period-end adjusting entry* | | |

**(ii) Sum-of-the-years’-digits**  
Denominator = 5(5+1)/2 = **15**  
Year 1 fraction = 5/15  
Y1 SYD expense = $84,000 × 5/15 = **$28,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC | 28,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC | | 28,000 |
| *Dec 31, Y1 — SYD period-end adjusting entry* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 28,000. **Balanced.**

**(iii) Double-declining-balance**  
DDB rate = 2 × (1/5) = **40%**  
Y1 DDB expense = $90,000 × 40% = **$36,000**  
(Residual is **not** subtracted from cost when applying the rate.)

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC | 36,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC | | 36,000 |
| *Dec 31, Y1 — DDB period-end adjusting entry* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 36,000. **Balanced.**

**(iv) Units-of-production**  
Rate per unit = $84,000 ÷ 12,000 units = **$7 per unit**  
Y1 UOP expense = $7 × 2,400 = **$16,800**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depreciation Expense—CNC | 16,800 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC | | 16,800 |
| *Dec 31, Y1 — UOP period-end adjusting entry* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 16,800. **Balanced.**

**(c) Subsequent measurement schedules**

**Checks:** 28,000 + 22,400 + 16,800 + 11,200 + 5,600 = **$84,000**; fractions sum 15/15; ending BV = residual.

| Dec. 31, Y5 | 11,664 | plug* | **5,664** | 84,000 | **6,000** (residual) |

Y5 dep = $11,664 − $6,000 residual = **$5,664**.

**Units-of-production schedule**

| Year-end | Units | $/unit | Dep. expense | Accum. dep. | Carrying amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

**Method comparison — Year 1 expense**

| Method | Year 1 dep. expense |
|---|---:|
| Straight-line | $16,800 |
| Sum-of-the-years’-digits | 28,000 |

Accelerated methods (SYD, DDB) front-load expense relative to SL; UOP tracks actual activity.

**(d) Balance sheet — December 31, Year 1 (straight-line)**

| Assets | Amount |
|---|---:|

| Less: Accumulated depreciation—CNC | (16,800) |
| **Equipment, net** | **$73,200** |

(Alternatively: “Equipment, net of accumulated depreciation of $16,800 — $73,200.”) Historical cost is preserved; AD is a **contra asset**. Depreciation is allocation, not fair-value write-down.

**(e) December 31, Year 3 — disposal under straight-line**

After Year-3 SL adjusting entry: AD = $16,800 × 3 = **$50,400**  
Carrying amount = $90,000 − $50,400 = **$39,600**  
Cash proceeds = **$42,000**  
**Gain on disposal** = $42,000 − $39,600 = **$2,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 42,000 | |
| Accumulated Depreciation—CNC | 50,400 | |
| Equipment—CNC Machining Center | | 90,000 |
| Gain on Disposal of Equipment | | 2,400 |
| *Remove cost and AD; gain when proceeds > carrying amount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 42,000 + 50,400 = **92,400**; Cr 90,000 + 2,400 = **92,400**. **Balanced.**

**Key insight:** All four systematic methods allocate the **same depreciable cost** ($84,000) over the asset’s life, but **period patterns differ**. The **period-end adjusting JE** always debits Depreciation Expense and credits Accumulated Depreciation for that period’s computed amount. On **disposal**, remove gross cost and related AD; gain/loss compares cash to book value.

### Q4 — MC — Method selection and DDB residual floor
**LO:** LO 12-1  
**Concept:** Classification / method fit — when each systematic method is most appropriate; DDB residual is a floor not subtracted from the depreciation base  

**Question 1 of 2:**  
Which depreciation method is **most appropriate** when the decline in service potential is driven primarily by **actual usage** (hours or units produced) rather than the passage of time?  
- A) Straight-line  
- B) Sum-of-the-years’-digits  
- C) Double-declining-balance  
- D) Units-of-production  

**Answer:** **D.** Units-of-production (activity method) allocates a constant depreciable cost per unit of output/input so annual expense **varies with actual usage**. SL and the accelerated time-based methods allocate by the **passage of time**, not measured activity.

---

**Question 2 of 2:**  
Under the **double-declining-balance** method, which statement is **correct**?  
- A) Residual value is subtracted from cost before multiplying by the DDB rate each year  
- B) The DDB rate is applied to beginning-of-period carrying amount; residual value is a floor so the asset is not depreciated below residual (final year may be a plug)  
- C) Total depreciation over the life always equals acquisition cost with no regard to residual  
- D) Accumulated depreciation is credited directly to the Equipment account each year  

**Answer:** **B.** DDB multiplies **beginning carrying amount** by a constant multiple of the straight-line rate; residual is **not** removed from the base when computing the annual charge, but the asset must not be driven **below residual**—often requiring a forced amount in the last period. (A) describes SL/SYD/UOP base treatment, not DDB. (C) is wrong because residual remains undepreciated. (D) is wrong because the credit is to **Accumulated Depreciation**, preserving historical cost.
