# Agent 379 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-3

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Accounting for Employee Stock Purchase Plans  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- Noncompensatory ESPP criteria (ASC 718-50-25-1): discount ≤ 5% (safe harbor), substantially all employees, limited option features → **no compensation expense**
- Compensatory ESPP: fails one or more criteria (e.g., discount > 5%, selective eligibility) → **compensation expense for the employee discount**
- Initial recognition at share issuance: Cash (and Comp Expense if compensatory) → Common Stock + PIC in Excess of Par
- Subsequent measurement: payroll-withholding liability accumulation schedule (Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan)
- Period-end adjusting / purchase-period settlement JE when offering period ends
- Settlement: clear ESPP liability, issue whole shares, refund remainder, recognize compensation if compensatory

### Q1 — CORE — Noncompensatory vs compensatory ESPP; payroll schedule; period-end settlement
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of ESPP share issuance; payroll liability schedule; period-end settlement JE (compensatory discount)  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Pulse Labs** (calendar year-end) maintains employee stock purchase arrangements for its workforce. Common stock has a **$1 par** value unless noted.

**Plan A — Noncompensatory (direct purchase, no withholdings)**  
- Substantially all employees may participate on an equitable basis.  
- Purchase price = **95%** of market price on the purchase date (discount **5%**).  
- Purchase price is based solely on market price at purchase; employees may cancel participation and receive a refund of amounts paid; enrollment window ≤ 31 days.  
- On **January 31, Year 1**, employees purchased **12,000** shares when the market price was **$20** per share.

**Plan B — Compensatory (payroll withholdings; year-end settlement)**  
- Only **designated managers** may participate (not substantially all employees).  
- Purchase price = **85%** of market price on the last day of the calendar year (discount **15%**).  
- Aggregate monthly payroll deductions for all participants combined are **$430** beginning **January 1, Year 1** (treat as a single withholding pool).  
- The company credits **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan** for each withholding.  
- On **December 31, Year 1**, the market price is **$30** per share. Participants elect to purchase as many **whole** shares as the accumulated deposit will buy at the discounted price. Any residual cash is **refunded**.  
- Partial shares are **not** issued.

**Required:**  
a. Classify Plan A and Plan B as noncompensatory or compensatory and briefly support using the ASC criteria.  
b. Record the **January 31, Year 1** journal entry for Plan A share issuance.  
c. Prepare a **quarterly subsequent-measurement schedule** of the Plan B liability for Year 1 (ending balance each quarter). Record the **summary annual** payroll-withholding entry for Year 1 (four equal quarterly postings totaling the year are acceptable in the schedule).  
d. Compute whole shares issuable under Plan B on December 31, Year 1; prepare the **period-end settlement / adjusting** journal entry (clear liability, compensation if any, stock issuance, cash refund).  
e. Show that Plan B Dr = Cr and that equity is credited for the **full fair value** of shares issued.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**  
- **Plan A — Noncompensatory.** Discount **5%** meets the safe-harbor; substantially all employees; allowed limited option features only → **no compensation cost**.  
- **Plan B — Compensatory.** Fails “substantially all employees” **and** the discount is **15% > 5%** → **recognize compensation expense for the discount**.

**b. Plan A — January 31, Year 1 initial recognition (noncompensatory)**  
Cash = 12,000 × $20 × 95% = **$228,000**  
Common stock = 12,000 × $1 = **$12,000**  
PIC in excess of par = 228,000 − 12,000 = **$216,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 228,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 12,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 216,000 |
| *Issuance under noncompensatory ESPP (5% discount; no compensation)* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 228,000. Balanced. No compensation expense.

**c. Plan B — subsequent measurement schedule (payroll liability)**  
Monthly withholding = $430; annual total = 430 × 12 = **$5,160**.

| Quarter ended | Months in quarter | Quarterly withholdings | Ending liability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar 31, Y1 | 3 | 1,290 | **1,290** |
| Jun 30, Y1 | 3 | 1,290 | **2,580** |
| Sep 30, Y1 | 3 | 1,290 | **3,870** |
| Dec 31, Y1 (before settlement) | 3 | 1,290 | **5,160** |

Summary annual (or four quarterly) withholding entry:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salaries Payable (or Cash — net pay reduction) | 5,160 | |
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | | 5,160 |
| *Year 1 payroll withholdings under ESPP* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 5,160. Liability rollforward ends at $5,160 before settlement.

**d. Plan B — December 31, Year 1 period-end settlement / adjusting JE**  
Discounted purchase price = $30 × 85% = **$25.50** per share.  
Whole shares = floor($5,160 ÷ $25.50) = floor(202.353) = **202** shares.  
Cash applied to stock = 202 × $25.50 = **$5,151**.  
Cash refund = 5,160 − 5,151 = **$9**.  
Compensation expense = 202 × $30 × 15% = **$909**.  
Common stock = 202 × $1 = **$202**.  
PIC in excess of par = (202 × $30) − $202 = **$5,858**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 5,160 | |
| Compensation Expense | 909 | |
| Common Stock | | 202 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 5,858 |
| Cash (refund of residual deposit) | | 9 |
| *Period-end settlement of compensatory ESPP; discount recognized as compensation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 5,160 + 909 = **6,069**; Cr 202 + 5,858 + 9 = **6,069**. Balanced. Liability after settlement = **$0**.

**e. Full fair value of equity issued**  
Fair value of 202 shares = 202 × $30 = **$6,060** = cash applied ($5,151) + compensation ($909).  
Equity credited (CS + PIC) = 202 + 5,858 = **$6,060**.  
Total Dr/Cr also includes the **$9** refund of unused withholdings.

**Key insight:** Noncompensatory ESPPs that meet ASC criteria record only cash and equity at the discounted cash amount. Compensatory ESPPs record **compensation expense equal to the discount** so that equity is credited for the shares’ **full market value**. Payroll plans accumulate a liability until the purchase-period settlement date.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — Direct-issue noncompensatory vs compensatory ESPP
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial recognition JE for noncompensatory vs compensatory employee stock purchase  
**Scenario:**  
**Harborwick Instruments Corp.** offers an ESPP covering substantially all employees with limited option features. Common stock is **$1 par**. On **March 15**, employees purchase **5,000** shares when the market price is **$48** per share.

**Required:**  
a. Assume the plan allows purchase at a **5%** discount and meets all noncompensatory criteria. Record the March 15 issuance entry.  
b. Assume instead the discount is **15%** (plan is compensatory; all other facts unchanged). Record the March 15 issuance entry.  
c. Compute the difference in **compensation expense** and in the **credit to PIC in Excess of Par** between (a) and (b).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Noncompensatory (5% discount)**  
Cash = 5,000 × $48 × 95% = **$228,000**  
CS = 5,000 × $1 = **$5,000**  
PIC = 228,000 − 5,000 = **$223,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 228,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 5,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 223,000 |
| *Noncompensatory ESPP issuance* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 228,000. Compensation expense = **$0**.

**b. Compensatory (15% discount)**  
Cash = 5,000 × $48 × 85% = **$204,000**  
Compensation expense = 5,000 × $48 × 15% = **$36,000**  
CS = **$5,000**  
PIC = (5,000 × $48) − 5,000 = **$235,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 204,000 | |
| Compensation Expense | 36,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 5,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 235,000 |
| *Compensatory ESPP issuance — discount as compensation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 204,000 + 36,000 = **240,000**; Cr 5,000 + 235,000 = **240,000**. Full FV = 5,000 × $48 = **$240,000**.

**c. Comparison**  
| Item | (a) Noncompensatory | (b) Compensatory | Difference (b − a) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Compensation expense | 0 | 36,000 | **+36,000** |
| PIC in Excess of Par credit | 223,000 | 235,000 | **+12,000** |
| Cash received | 228,000 | 204,000 | −24,000 |
| Total equity credit (CS + PIC) | 228,000 | 240,000 | +12,000 |

The compensatory plan increases compensation by the full discount (**$36,000**) and credits equity for full FV; PIC is higher by **$12,000** relative to noncompensatory because noncompensatory equity equals cash only, while compensatory equity equals cash + discount (and CS is the same $5,000 in both cases: PIC difference = full FV equity 240,000 − cash-basis equity 228,000 = 12,000).

**Key insight:** Same shares and market price: noncompensatory books equity at **cash received**; compensatory books equity at **fair value**, with the discount flowing through compensation expense.

### Q3 — CORE alternate angle — Period-end adjusting JE; selective eligibility; multi-period purchase schedule
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting / settlement JE for compensatory ESPP; schedule of two purchase periods; selective-eligibility compensatory treatment  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpine Logistics Inc.** (calendar year) has a semi-annual employee stock purchase plan. Common stock is **$1 par**.

**Facts — Plan design**  
- Purchase periods end **June 30** and **December 31**.  
- Employees pay **88%** of market price on the last day of each purchase period (**12% discount**).  
- Substantially all full-time employees may participate; limited cancelation/refund features only.  
- Because the discount **exceeds 5%**, the plan is **compensatory**.  
- The company accumulates payroll withholdings in **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan**.

**Purchase period 1 (ended June 30, Year 1)**  
- Accumulated withholdings at June 30: **$88,000**.  
- Market price June 30: **$50**.  
- Issue whole shares only; refund any remainder.

**Purchase period 2 (ended December 31, Year 1)**  
- Monthly aggregate withholdings July–December: **$6,600** per month.  
- Market price December 31: **$50**.  
- Same 12% discount; whole shares only.

**Additional fact for part (d)**  
On a **separate** one-time arrangement (not the semi-annual plan), Silverpine allows **only three senior executives** to buy **3,000** shares at a **5%** discount on December 31 when market price is **$22** and par is **$5**. Substantially all employees are **not** eligible.

**Required:**  
a. For the June 30 purchase period: compute shares, compensation, refund (if any); record the **period-end adjusting/settlement JE**.  
b. Prepare a **six-month liability accumulation schedule** for July–December and the December 31 settlement JE (shares, compensation, refund).  
c. Prepare a **Year 1 two-period summary schedule**: beginning liability, withholdings, settlement (cash to equity / refund), compensation expense, ending liability.  
d. Record the December 31 entry for the **selective executive** purchase (part of period-end adjusting work). Explain why compensation is recognized even though the discount is only 5%.

**Answer key:**  

**a. June 30 period-end settlement**  
Purchase price = $50 × 88% = **$44**.  
Shares = $88,000 ÷ $44 = **2,000** (exact).  
Compensation = 2,000 × $50 × 12% = **$12,000**.  
CS = 2,000 × $1 = **$2,000**.  
PIC = (2,000 × $50) − 2,000 = **$98,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 88,000 | |
| Compensation Expense | 12,000 | |
| Common Stock | | 2,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 98,000 |
| *June 30 period-end ESPP settlement (compensatory 12% discount)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 88,000 + 12,000 = **100,000**; Cr 2,000 + 98,000 = **100,000**. FV = 2,000 × $50 = **$100,000**.

**b. July–December accumulation and Dec 31 settlement**  

| Month | Withholding | Ending liability (before Dec settlement) |
|---|---:|---:|
| July | 6,600 | 6,600 |
| August | 6,600 | 13,200 |
| September | 6,600 | 19,800 |
| October | 6,600 | 26,400 |
| November | 6,600 | 33,000 |
| December | 6,600 | **39,600** |

Summary withholdings July–Dec:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salaries Payable | 39,600 | |
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | | 39,600 |

Dec 31 purchase price = $50 × 88% = **$44**.  
Shares = $39,600 ÷ $44 = **900** (exact; no residual).  
Compensation = 900 × $50 × 12% = **$5,400**.  
CS = 900 × $1 = **$900**.  
PIC = (900 × $50) − 900 = **$44,100**.  
Cash refund = **$0**.

Verify: FV = 900 × $50 = **$45,000** = cash $39,600 + compensation $5,400.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 39,600 | |
| Compensation Expense | 5,400 | |
| Common Stock | | 900 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 44,100 |
| *December 31 period-end ESPP settlement; whole shares only* | | |

**Check:** Dr 39,600 + 5,400 = **45,000**; Cr 900 + 44,100 = **45,000**. Balanced. Ending liability = **$0**.

**c. Year 1 two-period summary schedule**

| Period end | Beg. liability | + Withholdings | − Settled to stock (cash basis) | − Cash refund | End. liability | Comp. expense |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jun 30 | 0 | 88,000 | 88,000 | 0 | 0 | 12,000 |
| Dec 31 | 0 | 39,600 | 39,600 | 0 | 0 | 5,400 |
| **Year total** | | **127,600** | **127,600** | **0** | **0** | **17,400** |

**d. Selective executive purchase (period-end adjusting)**  
Even with only a **5%** discount, the plan fails the “**substantially all employees**” criterion → **compensatory**.  
Cash = 3,000 × $22 × 95% = **$62,700**.  
Compensation = 3,000 × $22 × 5% = **$3,300**.  
CS = 3,000 × $5 = **$15,000**.  
PIC = (3,000 × $22) − 15,000 = **$51,000**.  
FV = 3,000 × $22 = **$66,000**.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 62,700 | |
| Compensation Expense | 3,300 | |
| Common Stock | | 15,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 51,000 |
| *Selective executive purchase — compensatory despite 5% discount* | | |

**Check:** Dr 62,700 + 3,300 = **66,000**; Cr 15,000 + 51,000 = **66,000**.

**Key insight:** Period-end ESPP work is an **adjusting settlement**: clear the withholding liability, issue equity at full fair value when compensatory, refund residual deposits, and recognize the discount as compensation. A **5% discount alone is not enough**—eligibility must also cover substantially all employees for noncompensatory treatment.

### Q4 — MC — Classification and measurement choice
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Noncompensatory vs compensatory classification; effect on APIC credit  

**Question 1:**  
Which arrangement is **most likely noncompensatory** under ASC 718-50-25-1 (no compensation cost)?

- A) 12% discount; all full-time employees eligible; purchase price = market on purchase date  
- B) 4% discount; only the CFO and two VPs eligible; purchase price = market on purchase date  
- C) 5% discount; substantially all employees eligible; purchase price based on market at purchase; employees may cancel and obtain refund of withholdings  
- D) 5% discount; all employees eligible; employees receive a three-year option after enrollment to buy at the lower of enrollment-date or purchase-date price (lookback)

**Answer:** **C.** Meets discount safe harbor (≤ 5%), broad eligibility, and only allowed limited features. A fails discount > 5%. B fails broad eligibility. D has a lookback option feature beyond the limited exceptions → compensatory.

**Question 2:**  
**Lumen Ridge Tools** has a compensatory ESPP (15% discount). Employees purchase **10,000** shares of **$1** par common when market price is **$40**. Cash received is at 85% of market. What is the **credit to Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock**?

- A) $330,000  
- B) $340,000  
- C) $390,000  
- D) $400,000  

**Answer:** **C. $390,000.**  
Full FV equity = 10,000 × $40 = $400,000.  
CS = 10,000 × $1 = $10,000.  
PIC = 400,000 − 10,000 = **$390,000**.  
(Cash = 10,000 × 40 × 85% = $340,000; Comp exp = $60,000; Dr 340,000 + 60,000 = Cr 10,000 + 390,000.)  
A is the noncompensatory-style APIC (cash $340,000 − par $10,000). B is cash received only. D is full FV before subtracting par.
