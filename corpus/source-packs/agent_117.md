# Agent 117 — CORE DEMO — LO 20-3

**Chapter:** 20  
**LO title:** Accounting for Employee Stock Purchase Plans  
**Critical gap LO:** no

## Concept list for this pack
- **Noncompensatory ESPP criteria (ASC 718-50-25-1):** discount ≤ 5% (safe harbor) or terms no more favorable than all shareholders; substantially all employees may participate equitably; no option features beyond short enrollment (≤31 days) and refund of amounts paid before purchase
- **Initial recognition JE — noncompensatory:** Dr Cash at discounted price; Cr Common Stock (par); Cr PIC—CS to balance; **no** Compensation Expense
- **Initial recognition JE — compensatory:** Dr Cash at discounted price + Dr Compensation Expense for the employee discount; Cr Common Stock + Cr PIC—CS so equity is credited for full market value of shares issued
- **Subsequent measurement / accumulation schedule:** payroll withholdings build **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan** over the offering period
- **Period-end adjusting / payroll JE:** credit Liability—ESPP each pay period for amounts withheld
- **Settlement JE:** clear Liability—ESPP (or cash) and issue stock when employees purchase; refund liability if employee cancels
- **Classification / presentation / disclosure:** compensatory vs noncompensatory; compensation cost on income statement; equity presentation of stock issued; ASC 718 share-based arrangement disclosures

### Q1 — CORE — Noncompensatory vs compensatory ESPP initial recognition; payroll liability schedule; settlement
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Initial recognition JE for noncompensatory and compensatory employee stock purchase plans; payroll accumulation schedule; period-end withholding JE; settlement JE on purchase date; classification and disclosure  
**Scenario:**  
**Meridian Fabrication Corp.** (calendar-year) sponsors two employee stock purchase arrangements. Common stock has a **$1 par** value.

**Plan A (broad-based):** All full-time employees who meet limited service qualifications may participate on an equitable basis. The purchase price is **95% of the market price** of Meridian common stock on the last day of each six-month offering period. Employees may cancel participation before the purchase date and obtain a full refund of amounts previously paid through payroll withholdings. After the purchase price is fixed, employees have **no more than 31 days** to enroll. There are no other option features. Terms are not more favorable than those available to all holders of the same class of shares for capital-raising purposes beyond the 5% discount.

**Plan B (executives only):** Only senior managers (a select group) may participate. The purchase price is **85% of the market price** on the purchase date.

**Additional facts — Plan A cash purchase (emphasis: initial recognition):**  
On **June 30, Year 1**, employees purchase **6,000 shares** under Plan A for cash when the market price is **$28** per share.

**Plan B cash purchase (same date):**  
On **June 30, Year 1**, executives purchase **1,200 shares** under Plan B for cash when the market price is **$28** per share.

**Payroll-funded Plan A cohort (separate group of employees, same offering period):**  
For the **January 1 – June 30, Year 1** offering, this cohort elects total payroll withholdings of **$19,950 per month** for six months. Meridian credits **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan** at each payroll. On June 30, Year 1, the market price is **$28**; the Plan A purchase price is 95% of market. Employees purchase as many **whole shares** as the accumulated liability will buy at the discounted price (exact whole-share fit in this problem). No employees in this cohort cancel.

**Required:**  
a. Classify **Plan A** and **Plan B** as compensatory or noncompensatory. Support with the ASC 718-50-25-1 criteria.  
b. Prepare the **June 30, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the **6,000-share Plan A cash purchase**. Show Dr = Cr.  
c. Prepare the **June 30, Year 1 initial recognition journal entry** for the **1,200-share Plan B cash purchase**. Show Dr = Cr.  
d. Prepare a **subsequent measurement / accumulation schedule** of Liability—ESPP for the payroll-funded cohort (monthly deductions and cumulative balance).  
e. Prepare (1) a **representative monthly period-end payroll withholding entry** for January and (2) the **June 30 settlement journal entry** when the payroll-funded cohort’s shares are issued.  
f. State how Plan A and Plan B affect **presentation** (income statement and equity) and list the **disclosure** categories required for share-based payment arrangements.

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**

| Plan | Compensatory? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan A** | **Noncompensatory** | Discount **5%** (≤ 5% safe harbor); substantially **all** qualifying employees may participate equitably; only permitted option features (refund of withholdings; short enrollment ≤31 days). No recognizable compensation cost. |
| **Plan B** | **Compensatory** | Participation limited to **senior managers only** (fails “substantially all employees”) **and** discount is **15% > 5%**. Recognize compensation expense for the employee discount. |

**b. June 30, Year 1 — Plan A noncompensatory cash issuance (emphasis: initial recognition JE)**

Purchase price per share = $28 × 0.95 = **$26.60**  
Cash received = 6,000 × $26.60 = **$159,600**  
Common stock (par) = 6,000 × $1 = **$6,000**  
PIC—CS = $159,600 − $6,000 = **$153,600**  
Compensation expense = **$0**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (6,000 × $28 × 95%) | 159,600 | |
| Common Stock (6,000 × $1) | | 6,000 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 153,600 |
| *Issuance under noncompensatory ESPP — no compensation cost* | | |

**Check:** Dr 159,600 = Cr 6,000 + 153,600. **Balanced.**

**c. June 30, Year 1 — Plan B compensatory cash issuance (initial recognition JE)**

Cash = 1,200 × $28 × 0.85 = **$28,560**  
Compensation expense (discount) = 1,200 × $28 × 0.15 = **$5,040**  
Market value of shares issued = 1,200 × $28 = **$33,600**  
Common stock = 1,200 × $1 = **$1,200**  
PIC—CS = $33,600 − $1,200 = **$32,400**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash (1,200 × $28 × 85%) | 28,560 | |
| Compensation Expense (1,200 × $28 × 15%) | 5,040 | |
| Common Stock (1,200 × $1) | | 1,200 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 32,400 |
| *Issuance under compensatory ESPP — discount recognized as compensation* | | |

**Check:** Dr 28,560 + 5,040 = 33,600; Cr 1,200 + 32,400 = 33,600. **Balanced.**

**d. Subsequent measurement — Liability—ESPP accumulation schedule (payroll cohort)**

| Month | Payroll withholding credited to Liability—ESPP | Cumulative liability |
|---|---:|---:|
| January | $19,950 | $19,950 |
| February | 19,950 | 39,900 |
| March | 19,950 | 59,850 |
| April | 19,950 | 79,800 |
| May | 19,950 | 99,750 |
| June | 19,950 | **119,700** |
| **Total** | **$119,700** | |

Purchase price = $28 × 0.95 = **$26.60**  
Whole shares issuable = $119,700 ÷ $26.60 = **4,500 shares** (exact).

**e. Period-end withholding JE and settlement JE**

**(1) January 31 (representative monthly period-end adjusting / payroll withholding):**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salaries Payable (or Cash — reduction of net pay) | 19,950 | |
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | | 19,950 |
| *Withhold employee contributions for ESPP* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 19,950. *(Same amount each month for six months; total credits to liability = $119,700.)*

**(2) June 30 — settlement: issue 4,500 shares; clear liability**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 119,700 | |
| Common Stock (4,500 × $1) | | 4,500 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 115,200 |
| *Settle ESPP liability by issuing stock at 95% of market (noncompensatory)* | | |

**Check:** Dr 119,700 = Cr 4,500 + 115,200. **Balanced.**  
(No compensation expense: Plan A noncompensatory.)

**f. Presentation and disclosure**

- **Income statement:** Plan B compensation expense **$5,040** is recognized as compensation cost. Plan A generates **no** compensation expense.  
- **Equity / balance sheet:** Stock issuances increase Common Stock and PIC—CS. Payroll withholdings are reported as a **liability** (Liability—ESPP) until settlement or refund.  
- **Disclosures (ASC 718-10-50-1):** entity should enable users to understand (a) nature and terms of share-based arrangements and potential effects on shareholders; (b) effect of compensation cost on the income statement; (c) method of estimating fair value of equity instruments granted (when applicable); (d) cash flow effects of share-based payment arrangements.

**Key insight:** A **5% (or less) discount** with broad employee eligibility and only permitted option features is **noncompensatory** — cash (or liability settlement) is recorded at the discounted price with **no** compensation expense. If the plan fails the criteria (e.g., **>5% discount** or only select employees), the company debits **Compensation Expense** for the discount so that equity is credited for the **full market value** of shares issued.

### Q2 — CORE number variant — ESPP twin (all numbers changed)
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Number-variant twin: initial recognition of noncompensatory and compensatory ESPP issuances; payroll liability schedule; period-end withholding; settlement JE; classification  
**Scenario:**  
**Oakline Precision Systems Inc.** (calendar-year) has $1 par common stock and two ESPP arrangements.

**Plan N (noncompensatory design):** Substantially all eligible employees may participate equitably; purchase price = **95% of market** on the purchase date; refunds of withholdings allowed if participation is canceled before purchase; enrollment window after price is fixed does not exceed 31 days.

**Plan C (compensatory design):** Only designated high performers may participate; purchase price = **90% of market** on the purchase date.

**Cash purchases on September 30, Year 1** (market price **$15**):  
- Plan N: employees buy **10,000 shares** for cash.  
- Plan C: employees buy **3,000 shares** for cash.

**Payroll-funded Plan N cohort (January 1 – June 30, Year 1 offering):**  
Monthly withholdings **$22,800** for six months credited to Liability—ESPP. On June 30, market price is **$24**; Plan N price = 95% of market. Whole shares only; exact fit.

**Required:**  
a. Classify Plan N and Plan C.  
b. Prepare the **September 30 Plan N** initial recognition JE.  
c. Prepare the **September 30 Plan C** initial recognition JE.  
d. Prepare the **six-month Liability—ESPP accumulation schedule** and compute shares issued at June 30.  
e. Prepare the **June 30 settlement JE** for the payroll cohort.  
f. Compute **total compensation expense** recognized in Year 1 from these ESPP transactions (cash purchases + payroll cohort).

**Answer key:**  

**a. Classification**  
- **Plan N — noncompensatory:** 5% discount safe harbor; broad equitable participation; only permitted option features.  
- **Plan C — compensatory:** limited eligibility **and** 10% discount (> 5%).

**b. September 30 — Plan N noncompensatory issuance**

Cash = 10,000 × $15 × 0.95 = **$142,500**  
CS = 10,000 × $1 = **$10,000**

| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 132,500 |
| *Noncompensatory ESPP issuance* | | |

**Check:** Dr 142,500 = Cr 10,000 + 132,500. **Balanced.**

**c. September 30 — Plan C compensatory issuance**

Cash = 3,000 × $15 × 0.90 = **$40,500**  
Compensation expense = 3,000 × $15 × 0.10 = **$4,500**  
Market value = 3,000 × $15 = **$45,000**  
CS = **$3,000**  
PIC = **$42,000**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|

| Common Stock (3,000 × $1) | | 3,000 |

| *Compensatory ESPP — discount to compensation expense* | | |

**Check:** Dr 40,500 + 4,500 = 45,000; Cr 3,000 + 42,000 = 45,000. **Balanced.**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 136,800 | |

Plan N cash + payroll cohort: **$0** (noncompensatory)

**Total compensation expense = $4,500**

**Key insight:** Changing share counts, market prices, and discount rates changes cash, PIC, and compensation amounts, but the **structure** is stable: noncompensatory → no expense; compensatory → expense = shares × market × discount %.

### Q3 — CORE — Liability roll-forward, cancellation refund, period-end compensatory settlement
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Period-end adjusting and settlement path — ESPP liability roll-forward schedule; refund (cancellation) settlement JE; compensatory purchase-date recognition JE; presentation of remaining liability  
**Scenario:**  
**Crestline Audio Devices Inc.** offers a calendar-year **compensatory** employee stock purchase plan: only employees in the engineering and product groups may participate, and the purchase price is **88% of market** on December 31 (12% discount). Common stock is **$2 par**. The company uses **Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan** for payroll withholdings.

Activity for the Year 1 offering period:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Liability—ESPP balance, January 1, Year 1 | $0 |
| Payroll withholdings credited to Liability—ESPP during Year 1 | 184,080 |
| Cash refunds to employees who canceled before purchase date | 16,000 |
| Remaining liability applied to share purchases on December 31 | ? |

On **December 31, Year 1**, market price is **$40** per share. Remaining participants purchase as many **whole shares** as the liability will buy at 88% of market. In this problem the available liability divides evenly by the purchase price (no residual refund).

**Required:**  
a. Prepare a **liability roll-forward schedule** for Year 1 (beginning balance, withholdings, refunds, amount applied to shares, ending balance).  
b. Prepare the **journal entry** for the **$16,000 cancellation refunds** (settlement without share issuance).  
c. Compute the **number of whole shares** issued on December 31. Prepare the **December 31 period-end settlement / issuance journal entry** (including compensation expense).  
d. How should any remaining Liability—ESPP (if any) and the compensation cost be **classified / presented**?

**Answer key:**  

**a. Liability roll-forward schedule**

Purchase price per share = $40 × 0.88 = **$35.20**  
Liability available after refunds = $184,080 − $16,000 = **$168,080**  
Shares issued = $168,080 ÷ $35.20 = **4,775** (exact)  
Residual after issuance = **$0**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Beginning Liability—ESPP, 1/1/Y1 | $0 |
| + Payroll withholdings | 184,080 |
| − Cancellation refunds | (16,000) |
| − Applied to share purchases 12/31 | (168,080) |
| Ending Liability—ESPP, 12/31/Y1 | **$0** |

**b. Cancellation refund settlement JE**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 16,000 | |
| Cash | | 16,000 |
| *Refund withholdings to employees who canceled before purchase* | | |

**Check:** Dr = Cr = 16,000. **Balanced.**

**c. December 31 — compensatory settlement / initial recognition of shares issued**

Cash equivalent via liability = **$168,080**  
Compensation expense = 4,775 × $40 × 0.12 = **$22,920**  
Market value of shares = 4,775 × $40 = **$191,000**  
Common stock = 4,775 × $2 = **$9,550**  
PIC—CS = $191,000 − $9,550 = **$181,450**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liability—Employee Stock Purchase Plan | 168,080 | |
| Compensation Expense (4,775 × $40 × 12%) | 22,920 | |
| Common Stock (4,775 × $2) | | 9,550 |
| Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock | | 181,450 |
| *Compensatory ESPP settlement at period end* | | |

**Check:** Dr 168,080 + 22,920 = 191,000; Cr 9,550 + 181,450 = 191,000. **Balanced.**

**d. Classification / presentation**  
- **Liability—ESPP** is a **current liability** while withholdings are outstanding (settled within the offering cycle). Ending balance here is **$0**.  
- **Compensation Expense $22,920** is reported in operating expenses (compensation cost) on the income statement.  
- **Common Stock** and **PIC—CS** increase stockholders’ equity by the **full market value** of shares issued ($191,000).  
- Disclose nature/terms of the compensatory ESPP, compensation cost effect, and cash flow effects (payroll withholdings / refunds / no cash received at issuance when settled from liability).

**Key insight:** ESPP payroll activity is a **liability roll-forward** until purchase or refund. Cancellation is a **cash settlement of the liability** with no equity issuance. At purchase under a **compensatory** plan, clear the liability for the discounted amount and debit **Compensation Expense** for the discount so equity equals market value.

### Q4 — MC (classification / measurement choice)
**LO:** LO 20-3  
**Concept:** Classification of ESPP as compensatory vs noncompensatory and measurement of PIC credit on issuance  

**Question 1:**  
**Riverglen Components Co.** has an employee stock purchase plan open to substantially all eligible employees on an equitable basis. Purchase price is **95% of market** on the purchase date; employees may obtain a refund of withholdings if they cancel before purchase; enrollment after the price is fixed does not exceed 31 days. On March 31, employees purchase **8,000 shares** of $1 par common when market is **$20**. Which statement is correct?

- A) The plan is compensatory; Compensation Expense is $8,000.  
- B) The plan is noncompensatory; debit Cash $152,000; credit Common Stock $8,000 and PIC—CS $144,000; no compensation expense.  
- C) The plan is noncompensatory; debit Cash $160,000 and credit Common Stock $160,000.  
- D) The plan is compensatory because any discount from market requires compensation expense.

**Answer:** **B.** Discount of 5% with broad participation and only permitted option features → **noncompensatory**. Cash = 8,000 × $20 × 0.95 = **$152,000**; CS = **$8,000**; PIC = **$144,000**; compensation expense = $0. A and D incorrectly treat a qualifying 5% plan as compensatory. C ignores the 5% discount on cash.

---

**Question 2:**  
**Same facts as Question 1**, except only **regional managers** may participate and the discount is **8%** (purchase at 92% of market). Employees still buy **8,000 shares** at market **$20**, $1 par. What is the credit to **Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par—Common Stock**?

- A) $147,200  
- B) $160,000  
- C) $152,000  
- D) $159,200  

**Answer:** **C. $152,000**  
Plan is **compensatory** (select employees + 8% discount). Equity is credited for **full market value** of shares issued:  
Cash = 8,000 × $20 × 0.92 = $147,200  
Compensation Expense = 8,000 × $20 × 0.08 = $12,800  
Common Stock = $8,000  
**PIC—CS = $160,000 − $8,000 = $152,000**  
A is cash only; B credits full market to PIC and ignores par; D is an incorrect hybrid.
