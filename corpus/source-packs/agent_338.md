# Agent 338 — CORE DEMO — LO 12-10

**Chapter:** 12  
**LO title:** Record acquisition and depletion of natural resources  
**Critical gap LO:** no  
**Emphasis angle:** period_end_adjusting_JE

## Concept list for this pack
- **Depletion cost base** = acquisition + exploration (as allowed) + development + restoration (ARO at PV) − residual/salvage of the property after extraction
- **Initial recognition JE:** capitalize Mine Property / Natural Resource (and credit Cash and/or Asset Retirement Obligation)
- **Units-of-production depletion:** rate = depletion base ÷ estimated recoverable units; annual depletion = rate × units **extracted**
- **Transfer path:** Natural Resource → **Inventory** (depletion) → **COGS** when sold; unsold extracted units remain in ending inventory
- **Subsequent measurement schedule:** multi-year depletion rollforward; estimate changes (remaining reserves) applied **prospectively**
- **Period-end adjusting JE (emphasis):** year-end depletion (and related UOP depreciation on site-tied equipment) booked when extraction is known for the period
- **Movable / alternative-use equipment:** PP&E depreciated separately (not in depletion base); **immovable / no-alt-use** development assets are often PP&E but life limited by the shorter of asset life or resource life (UOP common)
- **Disposal / settlement:** COGS on ore sold; sale of remaining rights removes carrying amount of natural resource (buyer may assume ARO)
- **Oil & gas exploration:** **successful-efforts** capitalizes only successful wells; **full-cost** capitalizes all exploration if reserves value ≥ amount capitalized

### Q1 — CORE — Acquisition (ARO), multi-year UOP depletion schedule, period-end JEs, FIFO COGS, disposal of rights

**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Initial recognition of depletion base (incl. ARO); multi-year subsequent measurement schedule; **period-end adjusting JEs** for depletion; FIFO inventory/COGS; disposal of residual mine rights  
**Scenario:**  
**Silverpeak Minerals LLC** acquires and develops a copper mine for extraction. The company credits **Mine Property** directly for depletion (no Accumulated Depletion contra). ### Q4 — MC (classification / method)

**LO:** LO 12-10  
**Concept:** Classification of costs in the depletion base; successful-efforts vs full-cost for oil & gas exploration  

**Question 1:**  
**Red Mesa Energy** drills five exploratory wells for cash totaling **$12,000,000** ($2,400,000 each). Three wells are dry; two are successful. Estimated fair value of reserves in the successful wells is **$18,000,000**. Which statement is correct?

- A) Under successful-efforts, capitalize $12,000,000 as Oil Reserve because total cost < reserve fair value.  
- B) Under full-cost, expense $7,200,000 (dry wells) and capitalize $4,800,000 (successful wells).  
- C) Under successful-efforts, capitalize $4,800,000 as Oil Reserve and expense $7,200,000 as Exploration Expense; under full-cost, capitalize all $12,000,000 as Oil Reserve (because $12,000,000 ≤ $18,000,000 reserve value).  
- D) Under both methods, expense all $12,000,000 until production begins.

**Answer:** C.  
Successful-efforts capitalizes only successful exploration ($2 × $2,400,000 = $4,800,000) and expenses dry holes ($7,200,000). Full-cost capitalizes all exploration costs when the estimated value of reserves discovered is at least the amount capitalized ($12,000,000 ≤ $18,000,000).

**Question 2:**  
Which set of costs is **included** in the **depletion base** of a mineral property (assuming successful discovery)?

- A) Acquisition cost of mineral rights, development cost of a mineshaft with no alternative use, and the present value of required restoration; residual land value is **subtracted**.  
- B) Acquisition cost only; development and restoration are always expensed as incurred.  
- C) Acquisition, development, and residual land value are all added; restoration is never capitalized.  
- D) Only restoration costs; acquisition is expensed when paid.

**Answer:** A.  
Depletion base = acquisition + (allowed) exploration + development + restoration (ARO at PV) − residual value after extraction. Tangible assets with alternative uses are PP&E depreciated separately rather than folded into the depletion base.
